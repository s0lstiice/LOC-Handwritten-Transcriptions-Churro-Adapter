On the other hand, if Elkins really intends
to leave Jan 15, even if my name is any

be practically the 15th before he could
be embroiled & late office in these
ordinary causes of events.
The whole course of events in this matter
is so political as to be almost laughable.
I shall charge the matter off and
think no more of it.

Jun. 6, Tuesday.
Compt. Williams called out on telegraphic late
this P.M. He said - "Your retirement what I
said as to your likelihood of being the
next Sec. of Treasury? I said yes. "Well," he
said, "the smart sweet firm which I
got the first information now says
that there have been no changes - that you
will be the next Secretary of the Treasury.
I called up the Raleigh Times P M to see if
by any chance Dr Coughlin was there -
they said he was but was out. I
left word w. the clerk to come in
to drink with me this evening. I then
called up again but he was still
out & I again left the message.
I can not understand why he does
not come to see me.
Mr H. told me it was generally understood
throughout the Treasury that Deffergivorel
was out of it and that I was to be
appointed.
Somthing tells me not to be too sure
as to this.

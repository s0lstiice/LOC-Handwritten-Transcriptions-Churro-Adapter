But night I felt that the only honorable
thing for me to do was to write a letter
to the President requesting resignation as a
member of the M Board. An reflection, however,
I am unwilling to think this could be very
popular. It could not make the slightest
impression on the President, for I know that
he has no idea of the importance of
the Bill & of its work, & could see no very
urgent or very important office to be filled
along political lines. Furthermore it could
lead to more and more ridiculous office seekers.
Again, we have a pleasant town before us
and many years of happiness will be its value
in working out the problems before us.
There are many other reasons why I should
not be about this political turmoil and do
my duty along the lines of my present
work.

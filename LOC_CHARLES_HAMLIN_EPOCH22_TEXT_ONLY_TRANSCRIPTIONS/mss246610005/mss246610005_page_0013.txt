Imperial Jnt. Embassy
My dear Mr Drummond:
I have just received the beautiful
card which His Majesty the Cumber has to
graciously entertained upon me and I beg you
to express to His Majesty my most grateful
thanks for this great favour. That His Majesty
should have included me among the recipients
of honors bestowed upon the officials of
the Bedford and Durham in connection
with the proceedings of July 4, 1918 will
always be a source of delectation to me.
I fully realize that my heart in the most
interesting event was relatively a minor
one, but I am sure that words fail
me to express the deep pleasure it gives

my husband and myself to be privileged to
entertain your Ambassador Viscount Selim
and Lieutenant Selim and their suite at
our home in 1911 and to see the love of our
commercial allies and the people of our
vicinity the opportunity of meeting this
distinguished representative of one of the
great Powers of the world.
The souvenir which Viscount Selim,
in the name of the son of Munjira Nasabanna,
presented to the room of Fairhaven, is now
shornific in the Public library and our
people regard it as one of their most cherished
possessions, and, as well, as a token of
mutual respect, love, and esteem between
the respective countries which can never
be broken and which trust will last
ever to mankind.
With again my most grateful thanks,
believe me,
Very sincerely yours
Hughelbert Prince Hamilton

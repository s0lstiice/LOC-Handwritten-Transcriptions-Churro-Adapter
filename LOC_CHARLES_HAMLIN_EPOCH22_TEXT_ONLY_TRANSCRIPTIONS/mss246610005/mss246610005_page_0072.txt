Thursday, June 22
at meeting Strauss reported that he had
talked with Acting En. East in N.Y.
who consulted his Excl. Council which
had reached conclusion that (in the
whole it might be a wise move, but)
they refused to hear the M.B.A.
but the rates in their own initiative,
& not had it apparent that the Board
had recommended their rates.
West. reported that he had called up
Williams at Boston who at first seemed
staggered at the 6 1/2 rate but finally
said he approved it and later a
letter was received saying that his
division wished to recommend their
schedule & not have Board buy it
on own initiative.
En H. said Pula had also agreed
to it.
Remember it was voted to nobody
each of the 3 boys, the rates to go
into effect tomorrow Friday morning.
This sudden public way do no harm
but I am fearful it will.
I do not deny such a rate was
143
ultimately necessary, but I begged the
Board to make 2 cities of the charging
at intervals even if only a few days.
Williams told me and he should have
boomerang Debongirell; also I went on
board of his boat and heard Debongirell
told him he voted against Williams
not only in 1912 but also in 1916!
The Wash. Star last evening had an article
by Loerandel criticizing Deb. & making
it apparent that he was a Democrat
& that his only obligation was in voting
against Bryan in 1896! Now he is damn
alone, a deliberate lie. I believe it
is Cookley's work. (see Sarah B.H. on
article.)
The Wash. Herald had an article the
other day stating that at the Dem.
N. Comm. dinner I was boomeranged
by Will. Presd. but that my friends
said No! I was of Constitutional
timidity! (see Sarah B.H.)

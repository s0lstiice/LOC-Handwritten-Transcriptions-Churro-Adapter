July 23. 1861.
at meeting of Bd. En H was lectured by
Rich. MBrk strongly urging higher rates on
East boats. Result of business, well, of interest
down to 10 1/2; said business in District had
taken news. Certificates fine beyond them
burning however.
En H. was very angry - said tired of being
dominated by Debbugrewell - that he
should write a personal note to Mr
Hardy of turning appointment w. his
services - that men must adjust
themselves to Mr's honesty & not vice versa.
Strain combatted this view vigorously.
The M Brk has about 90 millions
loaned on war paper, about 50 of
wh. is on Certificates.
We wanted out that of Certificates which
allowed to run off because honest
would be all right.
Agreed that Rich. was hard lecture
for many Certificates & that if any
particular obstruction made by
Debbugwell it should be on a mere
grateful basis.
27
En H. said Mr. Elans blamed Board for letting
Richmond get into steam boatage without
Debbugwell's right insisting on Rich. burning
Certificates; that immediately Elans &
Debbugwell building at cross timbers.
En H. suggested asking Elans to consider
w. us. & suggested Debbugwell also and
H. did not seem to like it.
At this meeting went in to Elans office &
advised him to resist in Debbugwell
being present: that there was no use
continuing & that by being carry on
Rich. as to building Certificates, all would
be well.
He seemed greatly surprised when I
told him Rich. was hard lecture on
Certificates though burning emulates
warranted & said if that is so
they must be told.
Board also talked over new Secretary
to have Broderick's place. I favored
Chatham & En H. & Strain had no
objections unless Bd decided it wanted
a man who did work more like
him.
I suggested asking Willis to return.

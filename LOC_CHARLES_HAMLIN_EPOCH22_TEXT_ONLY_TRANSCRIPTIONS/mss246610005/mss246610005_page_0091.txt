The Jap. Ambassador called at 5.30. He had
behaved two shucks for N.Y. business, one at the
Johnson Society & the other at the State Society.
He asked me to have the Union men & let them know
who. There was nothing in them who. stood up
omitted. They refused to discuss any and stated
that Johnson will return it to them in accord.
with his solemn promise to do. I told him
I saw nothing in them on any way objectionable
but strongly urged him to show them to
Middle Eel. Park at the State Debt and get
his assurance that the debt and no objections
to them, in view of the fact that their
discussing resolution to the Peace Treaty was
still pending in the Senate.

Wednesday, Sept 23
drove by auto to Antwerps. Stopped in route
at Malines & visited the Cathedral. The
Cathedral was not blazing as they were being
burned. Called at Palace of Cardinal
Richelieu. The Secretary said he was injured
all the morning but left us over the Palace
& showed us his Chapel and Treasury.
arrived at Antwerps about 12.30. called on
Mr. Mutchell the Messenger of His Highness. He was
very agreeable & sent us some mail,
including a delightful letter from Collett.
He said he thought they could get us a
better room, & sent us a letter to the chief
steward to find us rooms.
Our room was changed from C24 to B.49
a very large room -- but no bath.
Sailed at 4 P.M., but before we went to
the Cathedral & saw the Bruges tube.
went to the chief doctor who examined
me thoroughly & said, as chief of
13
the Rubbin, that my only trouble was intestinal
irregularity.
Wednesday, Sept 24.
arrived Southampton at 13.30, did not dock
but anchored. Left about 2.30.
arrived Cherbourg about 7.30. Piat Andrews
came aboard. Heavy rain.
Thursday, Sept 25.
a cold, drizzly, rainy day.
Sat. Sept 26
Clear sunshine but cold.
Sunday. Sept 27
cold, damp, & raining. Heavy sea.
Canoes awnings have been set up all
along the ship while we sailed, making
it very dark sitting out on deck.
Eng. Andrews painted out a man walking
on deck w. a cane & said he thought it
were enough Tolstoi who was on board.
Later we learned at nine pm. Robert
the Bullard who commanded one of
the Amm. deviencies in the war on Elmecally.

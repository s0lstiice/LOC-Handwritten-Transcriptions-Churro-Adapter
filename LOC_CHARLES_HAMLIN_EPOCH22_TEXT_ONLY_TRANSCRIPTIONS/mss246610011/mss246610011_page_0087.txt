The 24. Sat.
Smith called Bertie into and asked me to discuss
Monday. He said we did not feel able to at-
tend Anna's anniversary - and she said she asked
me leaving the bed in that way leave us,
and said she undertook perfectly.

Until 2.6. Sunday
The anniversary of Anna's death.
In the morning at 9.30 we drove down to St. Agnes
church. Marion Oliver and arranged with the
clergyman, Father Anderson, to have a special
service for us in memory of Anna. It was
the communion service with a choral prayer
for Anna, Marion, Bertie and I even present.
We received a large number of telegrams,
letters and flowers.
In the afternoon Mrs Wilson called on us
and took us in a long drive into
the country.

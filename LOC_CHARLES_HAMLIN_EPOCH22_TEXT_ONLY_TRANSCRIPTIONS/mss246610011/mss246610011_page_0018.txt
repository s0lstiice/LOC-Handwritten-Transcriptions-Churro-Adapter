called in Reichenberger at Mayflower hotel

Celency said Hitten was very liberal as
to debt settlements - that he did make an
agreement w. Caullings but the Reichenberger in
this court could not agree: that Hitten got
more with him than I think he gets;
that the Italian matter was bumping bed;
that Col. Mellon, Hurley & Crish were on
very liberal settlements but that the
Reichenberger from revolutionary reasons, could not
agree.
Reichenberger says she knew her husband was
infused to Dr. Rubbin; that it was scandalous
for Rubbin to go abroad to see him at Rome
and visit him at Paris. I said he was at
Rome only 1 day and did not visit him
at Paris but went straight to Cherbourg to
sail home. She said the Pallini's said she
was infused to Rubbin when she met
the President and merely wrote Rubbin
that she was so infused; that it was

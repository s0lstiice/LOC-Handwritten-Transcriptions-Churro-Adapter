In the afternoon Mr Pennwist called and
said she wanted a short statement of
what her Wilson had done with Bertie and
she would publish it. She had a copy
of this letter and Bertie said he would
send him Elkins very soon. -- was she
quite sure to do it, she said her Wilson
did not want him, as he loved to publish
a sketch of her Wilson full of error and
submitted it to her; that she had him
not to publish it but he did.
Collett conferred with this evening.
Mr Pennwist offered to furnish
such a statement and Collett did also.

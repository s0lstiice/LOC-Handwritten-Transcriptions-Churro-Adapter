Nov 7th. Thursday
we celebrated Washington Revoultion's day by opening the
bottle of cherry given us by Catherine Grant. We
entered into Catherine's old house and had tea and
at dinner.
We also sent a letter to Washington Revoultion.
Nov 9th Tuesday
went to Berta
called on Catherine Grant. She said she
wrote me yesterday and even coming down
tomorrow; that Berta had just returned
to her own telephone & she went to dine
with me tomorrow night.
attended shareholders meeting of Milwaukee
Bankers. Received address.
For young also elected.
Received very recent note from Catherine
dated Nov 8th.
Nov 10, Sat.
Catherine came to dinner at 7. P.M.
She asked me to come out a week or two Monday,
my last visit to Berta for this season and will
visit me in telephone this week. This was frankly

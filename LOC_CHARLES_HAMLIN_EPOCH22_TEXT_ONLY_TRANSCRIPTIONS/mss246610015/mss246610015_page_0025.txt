Aug. 25. Sat.
We went to funeral of honor Mattingly
thanking it was at 2 P M and when we
reached North Canton, found it was at
noon!
Called up in Richmond People at Colby Place
Boiler to get two address for letters
She asked me if I had seen his telephone.
I told her yes and that he would tell
me Jackson a man in custody of him
was released when she returned. She
said she was glad it had been done as
she had asked me to, & that she would
support the man, when she returned.
She said she had heard nothing from
Sam Rachel Hall and that his Clothes
(carries) and written were missing. She
had called in Rachel in Han Harbor and
sent both her & her Clerk with Trill
to see him.

Aug. 28 Tuesday
We signed agreement to sell property in water
front Lots. to Hanes B. Struthers and his husband
James H. Struthers, of Wooster, Ohio County,
Kentucky. The Price was $10,000 in which it includes
an 14,000 bu corn lot (11/3 acres) and 1,000 bu lot
along road (1/2 acre), there is at rate of $10,000 here
and bu there lot and 2,000 bu are on lot along
road.
We had in the following restrictions:
1. Limited to me dwelling for 1 family with several
adjunctances such as barns, cattle houses,
boot houses etc.
2. Primus not to be used on commercial purposes.
3. No structure other than a mil house, barns and
(or) cattle houses to be erected upon 100 bushels of
meadow hay or wheat.
4. We grant to immediately rettend the adjoining land
on 150 bushel on S. Cast hill of said ground
principle and on this 40 bushel shall not be taken
from us until we have 20,000 bushels, in case any
sale of this land should transfer to me.
5. No investment or otherwise matter shall be
transferred to be done into M.H. Hanes.
6. Same settlement on lot along road, if sold:-

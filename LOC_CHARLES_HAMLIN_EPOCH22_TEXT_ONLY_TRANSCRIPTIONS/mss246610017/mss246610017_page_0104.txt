Colt, Col. Grant met. Grant said it was a
discussion between him and myself on
the subject of Missouri N.Y. and Missouri
affairs a long drawn out discussion with
his views; 1) I am opposed to the
grant of a bond to Missouri N.Y. 2) I
believe to be a necessity to be considered as an
obstacle of peace, reconciliation and peace
between the United States and Missouri
and Missouri N.Y. 3) I am opposed to

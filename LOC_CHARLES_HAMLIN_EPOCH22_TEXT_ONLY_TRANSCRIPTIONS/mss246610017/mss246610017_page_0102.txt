May 14. Lord
George Hamilton writes they have extended
Stodart's time for laying his case forward
to June 15
Calden Hill is called & read a short letter to
his cabinet, of which contains that the
object is to lay establishing his letters and
states that the Lord Lord has many times
shown a tendency to incline to him. a short
but well written letter which the Lord does not
seem to mind long past.

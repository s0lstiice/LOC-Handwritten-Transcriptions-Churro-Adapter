Jan. 11, 1929. 127
Board considered letter from Gov. Norris pleading for a rate
increase and had decided to reject it, when Gov. Norris
telephoned that a large amount of discounts had just been
paid off and, therefore, his directors would not ask for
an increase.
See - Norris, Gov.
Federal Reserve Bank, Philadelphia
Board votes increase to Gov. Norris from $25,000 to $30,000.
Federal Reserve Bank, San Francisco
Board voted to write, asking why it had been more successful in
enforcing our credit warning.
Gov. Young and James objected saying there might be special circum-
stances and that it should have more time, especially as it had
not applied to increase over its 4 1/2 rate.
Miller felt that having written Chicago we were bound to write
San Francisco.
James finally voted in favor of sending the letter.

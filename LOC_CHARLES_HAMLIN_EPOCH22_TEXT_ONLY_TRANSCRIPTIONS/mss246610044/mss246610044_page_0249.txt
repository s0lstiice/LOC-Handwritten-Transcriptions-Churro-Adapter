Young, Governor (Cont'd.)
Gov. Young told Harrison that this rate was a national matter;
that if N.Y. went to 6% every F.R. Bank at least east of
Mississippi River would have to follow; that a majority of
Board felt this would injure business; that it had received no answer
to the Board's letter and no official statement of N.Y.'s
reason for asking the increase; that it wished an answer
before acting; that Boston had decided to postpone consideration
of an increase until it heard from its member banks in response
to Gov. Harding's circular.

I said that if H.W. was disgracified, Warb. could
be Governor, Delano could probably resign and
both could say that Harding had doubtless
crossed the line; that as a result it would
shut the up trains.
He said Delano under no circumstances
could have it -- that he was a narrow, selfish
man and that he only wanted the company's resign.
He said he did not stand for Warb. being here
and that it would injure Wilson to keep on a
new Democrat at the head of our banking
system. Also said they were all narrow
men not worthy of the position & of his
hand not put married as he died the
commander of the Bd. could have from
very different.
I begged him not to consider me at all
whatever he & the President did would leave
my loyal support.
I added that, if he wanted to know my
real opinion, I thought that it might be
better to disgrace Delano as soon to avoid
a descent of Bd. terms to the electors;
that the Board had strength and could
impose the Adm't. greatly if it did
not yield. On the other hand, I stated

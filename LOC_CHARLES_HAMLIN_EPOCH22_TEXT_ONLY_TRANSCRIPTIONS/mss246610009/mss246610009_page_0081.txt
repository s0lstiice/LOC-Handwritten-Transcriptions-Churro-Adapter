5:30 PM.
Mr Bertha Bolling called me up to thank
me for the flowers I sent her; she said she
was very weak since I had tried to be able to
go to St. I to see Bertie when she arrived, and
that she looked she would see much of me
all this winter.
Nov 26. Lord
Tom 1 am train for N.Y. and 12.25 PM by Albany
arriving at 3.40. went to the Reser; Harriet was out
but returned shortly. She said Bertie had taken the
2 PM from Boston & would arrive at 7.30. I sat with
her until 7.15 when I went down on the auto to
meet Bertie and Anna. They were met on the train
161
but as I was leaving Bertie suddenly shot and
said they came on the Limited which arrived at
6 PM; that Harriet wrote her to leave the train
arriving at 7.30 & that the auto would meet her;
that she had already bought seats on the Limited
arriving at 6 PM; that she supposed Harriet was
given a train ticket & did not want her to
arrive earlier; that, therefore, they went (Anna
villenty obligating) to the Union City Hotel and
waited until just before 7.30 when they
returned to the Station, met the auto and
myself & went to his home! They told Harriet
nothing about it!
Harriets room, as I learned later, was that
the auto had gone with William -- who was
playing golf, and I could be back only in
time to meet the 7.30 train.
The thought of my drinking tea quietly with
Harriet, while Bertie and Anna were waiting
in the hotel, was certainly grotesque!
Nov 27. Thursday
WB sends me a Thanksgiving telegram.

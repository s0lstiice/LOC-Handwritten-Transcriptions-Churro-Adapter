Jan 1. Thursday
Attended 2 hour lecture at Colgate
State.
Called in Mr. in Sec. Adams.
Bob Lowley called up. Belites in same
other matter & said he had been
outraged at Horner. attacking him
with receipt of two hundred dollars
& cyphed; that attorney of Pa. had
tried him over in State and sued him
at a club near his office Phila.; that
he had believed would be an emergency to
arrive him dead in dead "clean thirties"
in this matter.
Lowley also stated he had received at
the Supreme Court -- not a radical
vote, but he was annoyed that the
President had suggested himself into
the matter, and that he would
have the benefit of his vote in
this matter.

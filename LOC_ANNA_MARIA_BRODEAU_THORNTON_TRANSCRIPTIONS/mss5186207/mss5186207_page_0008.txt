May 6
1786
Thought I am
to be in the night I leave you
with my affection and esteem
and with my best wishes
to the King and the Country
and to myself
and I await to see the matter further
advanced and shall thus comply with the wishes
of the King and the Country
and I am
your most affectionate and most obedient
servant
John Adams

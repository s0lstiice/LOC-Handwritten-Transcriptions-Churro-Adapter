Know Thyself
I am vain, fond, ill tempered
impatient, unmerciful, un-
scrupulous - unscrupulous
unscrupulous - unscrupulous -
I am a brute - I have a Truth letter
my candour sufficiently brims on
themselves - If you are I am
desponding & low spirit'd - if gay
there is too much liverty in my
manners -
The heart of man is deceitful
& deceptive indeed.

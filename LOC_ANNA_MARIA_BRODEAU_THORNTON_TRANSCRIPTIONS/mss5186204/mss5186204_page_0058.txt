Friday 4th present. — Mr. Cannon from James
W. Smith called. — Judge Branch called
with a delay for Mr. Davis of 4. Adams — Mr. Smith also
called. — Mr. Smith called — Mr. Graham also
called after dinner — took Mr. Graham to a
public way — after him Mr. Bentz & Anderson
called in — & Mr. Smith returned shortly.
I am another letter from Washington — containing the same
praised hopes of this last communication — Justice
in this world — & Hope & pure altruism destroying
Salvation by State Sentiments — agreed to let the
term to Mr. Soule for 20 days — wrote to
Mr. Thomas & Mr. Smith — sent the President for
his 1st to Judge Branch. — Mr. Johnson — Senator
called to me as

Baltimore Aug 5th 1839
My Dear Madam
In the year 1839
you conveyed to Fielder Burck
the house last 9 lots 74, 230, 6, it
was at law your late mistress
now it seems by reason of some
defect in the conveyance that some
legal title to this part of lot remains
to your late husband Dr. Thurston
& the owners of the property desire a
new deed from you as Executrix.
I have examined the matter
particularly & consider no objection to your execution such
a deed which will be granted
to you by Mr. Tester & agent. I
Therefore hesitate not to advise
you to execute the same
Very Truly yours
J H Smith
Mr. A M. Thurston
of Mr. Thurston entitled at all the
expenses of the conveyance

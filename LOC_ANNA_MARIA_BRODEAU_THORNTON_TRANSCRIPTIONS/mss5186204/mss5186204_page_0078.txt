Saturday 17th May - Rain Frequent.
I am very well Samuel Jones went with me to
market - often breakfasted at Samuel Jones house in the evening - after dinner I sat out to ride
Samuel Colter - Draygond in going to Fort Buford's
fort which came to night I went home. I did not stay
very long to dinner passed the time - Manne (privately)
1801) left the house I would not let him go
with out some money.
Sunday 21st June - walked to Blanchet - Mr. Young
presented - The Corps Smiths went to church
after dinner - I in the house - Manne (privately)
1801) left the house I went to bed
at 9 o'clock

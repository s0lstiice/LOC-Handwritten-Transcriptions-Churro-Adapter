Tuesday -- June 1st Congratulatory day -- I shall take the wagons
to 2000 yards -- Promised flowers -- I
will not expect -- I will not stay -- as Mr. Smith intends
to leave -- to sail for England & they were all
busy -- I will return -- I shall leave -- I will take
the wagons to the station -- I will not leave --
The engineer of the station or any other officer to leave by Lady
in dull officer -- (Washington myself.)

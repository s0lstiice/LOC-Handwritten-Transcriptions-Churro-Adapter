Wednesday (last) 24th March.
Firing early in the morning again at
noon - Insipid sent after the president
was sworn in - Mr. Peter Blanchard
came - He had not heard from Albany
- Mr. Spence - Mr. Taylor called
- Mr. Baker - Mr. Delaney - called
- Reading Zellah a tale of Jeremiah
- Thursday 5th Rain - Mr. Bemis called
- The Ball was very hard and the
Supper elegant - about 350 ladies
- 700 gentlemen - I went to day
to Mr. Allen, Mr. Crawford & Mr. Spence
- Mr. Spence here in the house
Friday 6th windy & cold
Mr. Bemis & Mr. Spence called
Mr. Spence & Mr. Spence called

3d July - I walked early this
morning to Engine house where Mr. Button
was very ill - On Sunday
Mr. Mason left from the Engine
at the Church in Gospel tune and
either broke or deteriorated her health
she was carried to her house on a
lorry in a suffering and guilty
state. The situation is very precarious -
Mr. L. Knapshen has left my district.
4th July - Friday - a fine and day
in after Noon - The great event
of establishing first Class Rule Grounds for the Ohio & Chesapeake
Canal is now in operation
The president of the U.S. & the Commissioners of the City, Genl. Mc
Alexander are at the ground near
the Little Falls of Potomac where
the work is in a state of great interest &
progression - as it is the commencement
of a great work in which every
individual is entitled - and when
it is hoped & expected will have many
from time to time during the year of
property & improving settlers from all
quarters - The president is to dig
the first that fall - & in consequence
of that useful employment there
will be no Company room at his
town - Many think he might
have had a Dancing room in the town
I have let the President go to the
town - or where they please & I
am very poorly at home - and the
City is now quiet except for the crackers
in the Bowls -
I feel most deeply this Day!
My poor husband would have been
grateful to see it - but when it is
supposed to happen I hope - in
the Morning of his Life - in the Prison
of his Body

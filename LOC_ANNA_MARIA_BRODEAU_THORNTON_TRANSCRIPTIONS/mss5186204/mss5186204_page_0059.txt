Wednesday Jan 16th - Good Rain - almost all day
Bad day prevailing by night - Snowy
Thursday 17th Fine day - I walked to Mr. Benjamin
's Benchings - Mr. Smith was called - Mr. Smith
's name came - had a search - waiting for Mr.
Smith was attending the sale of Mr. Dibbs' estate -
Mr. Dibbs came at from the East Town
at 10 o'clock - he died here - I do not quite - do
witness a letter to Mr. Collyman - I find one from Collyman
about Kasten - quite disagreeable between the people.
Mr. Dibbs came to him - quite a breathing day -
sent a note to Mr. Struthers about 2 o'clock
Friday 18th Fine day - Rachel came over and told
that Mr. Dibbs Smith had all gone by Thursday to Louisville
to purchase some land myself saying that Collyman was ill -
Dr. Smith went to Louisville - Collyman died that night
Wednesday after dinner with me over to bed at Mr. Collyman's
funeral - Mark returned brought word that
Collyman's body had died at 4 o'clock this morning -
Col. Smith is in Louisville Indiana - Martin has disposed
of Collyman's estate with him - from 10 o'clock - this
is indeed a bad hard trial -
Saturday 19th Fine day - Genl. Cattinngs & M. Thomson
called - Mr. Thomson at the sale
I wrote to Mr Smith that we could not spend the day
with the Townsman on Saturday Mr. Smith would come home
if I did not wish to be absent - Went to Col. Dibbs
's house and my letter to Mr. Collyman - returned to meet Mr.
Smith at his office at Liberty - Read - Townsman
Saturday 20th Fine day - walked to Liberty - Martin
went out of Town - I opposed the Board not wishing to go
on to Collyman's estate if Mr. Collyman's family - Mr. Smith
was glad to go home with me - Mr. Collyman called - he
went home with me at 7 o'clock - They are not coming for a week
called - Mr. Smith called - Mr. Smith called -

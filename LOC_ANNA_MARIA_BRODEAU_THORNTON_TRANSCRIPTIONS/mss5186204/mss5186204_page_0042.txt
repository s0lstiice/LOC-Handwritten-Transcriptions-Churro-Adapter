Monday 18th warm Sunday – walked
to Brighton's Dr – returned to Col. Bungay
to dinner – Mr. Den & Mr. Taylor indeed
I was never able to Den. From his particular
description most to praise. His character
nothing to night to speak of. I am now
most satisfied that with him and not with his
brother had remained – I sent an opinion by
his letter to Mr. Bungay
Sunday 19th warm – Mr. Bungay called
at 2 o'clock after dinner – intended to go to
our house – Mr. Den & Mr. Taylor came to
town to be brought to bed. I am obliged to
Mr. Den for his visit
Wednesday 20th warm – I am obliged to
people talking about the fame of Col. Bungay
but not talking at any time to Col. Bungay
the bearer of the letter had not written
once of yesterday – I am obliged to
Mr. Den for his visit to the town

garden in the morning

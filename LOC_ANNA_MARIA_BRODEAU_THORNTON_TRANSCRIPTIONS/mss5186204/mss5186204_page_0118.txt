Tuesday 1877
Prima very poorly last night, taken
with a very bad colic, and a severe diarrhea
from which she has not yet recovered
and is now in the hospital with a
wretched appearance. We came down early
and went to camp with the intention of
returning to the station. Wednesday 1877

Sunday 16th — Sent 4 o'clock clock, & returned
Briefs of charges to Mr. Collett — also a
bullet of 135 lb weight, & pay part of it to
the prisoner, & leave it for the Judge.
Thursday 17th. Cold & cloudy — Went to Beth. Collett
left his chair unoccupied. 1000000 of the
Collett's paid money still in the Treasury —
Mr. Smith has been affronted.
Wednesday 18th. Cold & cloudy — Mr. Lee has
sent money to Bankruptcy — The
Catholick & Protestant Union for Philadelphia
the principal subject of discussion.
The Catholic Union immediately passed & I think the
Catholicks immediately leave it in a great danger from
the abolition of Mr. Smith's office of Judge.
Thursday 19th. Cold & cloudy weather — Mr. Smith
is on the ground of Philadelphia — Left the
prison about 5 o'clock in the evening — Mr. Smith's
party to night — I went on to look at
his room — quite in — all my time —
I went to Mr. Collett's office & talked with
Mr. Collett's secretary in bed —
attended the Jury.
Friday 20th. Sent 200000 — very quiet

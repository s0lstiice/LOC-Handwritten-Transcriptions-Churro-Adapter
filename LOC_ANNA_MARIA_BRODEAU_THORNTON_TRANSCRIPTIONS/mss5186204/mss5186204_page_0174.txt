Monday 24th
Mr. B. called — I went to Mr. B. and
talked to him often during the time
I was there. He was very obliging
in the matter of being sure to be
made a Judge
Mr. Thomson said to me he was to the
State of the Public sentiment I must not
be in the State House at that time I thought
it would be better to go home
and wait for the time when I could
come in. I went to Mr. Cannon and
Mr. George & asked him to go home
with me. I went to Mr. Thompson
and asked him to go home with me
and I went home. I left the office
at 10 o'clock. I went home
and slept all night. I went to
the office at 10 o'clock. I went home
and slept all night.
Wednesday 25th
I went to the office at 10 o'clock. I went home
and slept all night.

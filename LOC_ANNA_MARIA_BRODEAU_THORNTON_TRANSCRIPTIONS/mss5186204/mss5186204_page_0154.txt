Thursday 28th. I returned to the office to find myself
obliged to pay - but am obliged to leave
the 7th January to await an opportunity of
presenting myself in the office in the
morning. I am indebted to the subscribers
for 150000 - which I am sure will be returned to
the subscribers in the form of a bill of exchange
to the subscribers for the benefit of the
United States - and I am sure that the subscribers
will be satisfied with this arrangement.

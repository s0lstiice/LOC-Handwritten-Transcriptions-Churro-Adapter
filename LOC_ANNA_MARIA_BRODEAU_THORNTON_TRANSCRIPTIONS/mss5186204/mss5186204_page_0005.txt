15th February - I had a very bad night
being disturbed by some sounds
getting on the chimney, whose sounds
had been continued by
lightning some time ago, & the bricks
falling from the chimney's front.
Two bricks were on the roof at
the time. -- It is a most
exceeding thing where they
could come from --
Had to pray John Saunders
to go to guard the Well -- where he
had a brush of guns to do general duty
which I was to have the half --
Mama very poorly to day. --
17th -- Col. Bancroft brought the adjt. out
thrice for the Well -- said to have no
improvement
to go to Forest and to Mr. Saunders --
Monday 18th -- Mr. H. Smith went down this
afternoon to have him call us
over Friday morning.
Wednesday 19th -- Bought a carriage horse
for 758 --
Tuesday 20th -- William Lambert killed with
21 -- Running dog -- Sale of the horses
prolonged till tomorrow --
22nd -- came in the morning cleared off --
Horses not sold -- Williams now
unbranched -- I am not sure he has
1 sent George to a blind man in
townsman -- sold 2 horses -- but they
returned without them -- I am now
entirely to day -- 11 am for husband --
the horses were purchased --
Mr. Saunders went postillion --
Mr. Torrant brought a christened --
young soldier with 2d. of present -- but said not
what he was to stay with me except --
Thursday 23d -- Illumination last night --
George to go to the canal -- Mr. Duncan
called yesterday afternoon -- brought
me 300 -- I have had some strong
lady of 3d. sisters --

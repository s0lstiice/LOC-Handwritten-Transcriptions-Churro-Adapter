Saturday 31st The last day of the year comes
now again - the last month of it
the time a most disagreeable one
in winter any season - put away &
store - hasten & preserving - General
glory - I am particularly sorry being
bad any great attention - for the worst of men
will always complaining of that & other things
coming to be indifferent state - I hope anyt
year will bring us better weather & brighter
purposes - 1100 - Then rather of all
caric - how many will will to put an end
to him if ever I can & disappointments if he was not
for the worst influence. - But how this
year it will a flattering fate - this money
entailed - from time & from fortune!
It is becoming just & both with economy &
prudence all done & but neither materially, -
but materially to be able to take for myself
or family himself, & send up to Baltimore for
"The Beam" a new meal by express -
And if it - my death came from it around
my pretty a hearty laugh and a strong large
& black & long steam of fire - then our
task is done it is said. - October 1831.
Who can tell that another year will bring
forty - when we cannot tell from any day
or even hour to how that is to happen -
I will endeavor to do that in right &
trust to provide for the result.
Oh God protect & direct me in the
true path -

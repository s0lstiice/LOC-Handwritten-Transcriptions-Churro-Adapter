1829
Sept 1st Tuesday I saw Mr. Graham at
Mr's Jones - He had been to the launch of
a steam boat - at Broadway street - The
president arrived from the City Lodge where he
had been for his health at the time - and
there was two ships - I went to the
15th Washington - 8 paid off - there
now I have only one Brick - I did not let out
that is a large draw - called on Mr. Graham
at Mr's Graham & Thompson - returned
to Mr's Jones - Mr. Graham called at 11
o'clock in the morning gave him some wine - I went
to the City Lodge - very warm - Mr. Graham called
gave him the bill of 256.50 - I think - to be
repaided this is the 15th of the month
Mr. Graham with us - I went to Mr. Thompson to
ask him about giving a release from the
prisonity mentioned to him as bad men - I am
only one branch - drank ten at Mr's Thompson -
Thursday 3rd quite cool. The 6th - Mr. Graham
brought me back the bill to receive - I went
to inquire after Mr. Graham who only went as far
as City Lodge - He was too sick to distinguished to go to
Boston - Washington came with words to go -
took Mr. Graham with us to City Lodge - only
from Washington came at home - Eat some fruit &
returned early - Washington myself called - I
have given him the wrong note -

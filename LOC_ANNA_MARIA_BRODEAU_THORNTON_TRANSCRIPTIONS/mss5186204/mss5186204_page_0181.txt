1789
January 20th
1789
Mr. John Adams
to the President of the United States
Washington
Dear Sir
I have received your letter of the 12th instant
and am much obliged to you for the
kindness with which it is received
and I am sure that it will be
found in the best taste and spirit
with which it can be expected
from a man of your distinguished
eminence and experience
It is with a great pleasure I
accept the offer of the presidency
of the United States
and I shall ever consider myself
as your most obedient and
most affable servant
John Adams
President of the United States
Washington

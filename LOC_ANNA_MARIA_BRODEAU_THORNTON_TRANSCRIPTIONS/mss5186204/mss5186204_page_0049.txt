Monday 20th. Noon — Meeting for
York City Volunteers will not be held
returned to Genl. Wm. Forrest came
back from Richmond called after Genl.
Tuesday 21st — I went to the City Jail
& found Mr. Foley a clerk in the City Jail
for one week for 2000 — to be paid
to my credit — would be an article of
the King's pleasure to Genl. — after dinner
went to Mr. Jackson.
Wednesday 22d. Dawn — George Brentford
came — the door not within my possession
right to the Governor's hands.
Debate to Mr. Calhoun — Jackson
to the President — Mr. T. Calhoun — 3d. Genl.
Brentford called in the room.
Thursday 23d. very warm. Genl. Brentford
only Mr. Calhoun very ill
We went up to Genl. Brentford's house. Genl.
Brentford told us that he had been a servant to Mr. Jackson.
Friday 24th. Noon last night — Genl. Brentford
called — yesterday's vote (Jackson) — Genl.

1830
Friday 1st Jan. a Beautiful Day
clean'd & a little cold -- Mr Smith &
Mr Cleston called -- & Mr Harman and his
children -- Mr Torrey this my last tea with him.
Saturday 2d fine day -- There was as usual
a great crowd at the president's yesterday --
We passed the Penilla & a medal I gave
her to Genl Washington -- I said a gentleman
would give ten dollars for me -- I gave her
an offer to day -- McBrady got the first --
Manass I rode out -- took Mr Smith with us --
Mr Torrey -- the murder as to him -- I declined --
I walked to Mr Smith -- Julia Harthe Smith --
Mr Sanford called -- She is not well -- Did not
stay long -- Mr Jones here -- Reading the
Museum -- Mr & the Mrs Jones went back
early -- to Mr Talbott's party --
Sunday 3d fine day -- Walked to Mr Smith's to
church -- Bishop Chase preached -- Mr Jones
up to Mr Sanford -- to show him a junior about her
brother in the Telegraph -- Mr had been here --
We went in at Mr Tompkins -- The brother
is absent -- came up to Mr Tompkins --
Monday 4th Col -- Mr Sanford went with
me to visit Mr Peltz & Adams -- afterwards called
at Mr Smith's & Col Peltz -- Mr Smith father
called in the evening -- Read Mr Garret's opinion
Sunday 5th Winter's end -- Walked to Mr Smith's --
after dinner rode on the avenue & to Mr Smith's
not at home -- returned the car did think of
going to Mr Thompson -- Read a letter from Mr
Bransfield & Mr Kennedy -- Now different it
is to get business finished! --
Wednesday 6th Col -- Wrote to Mr Solomon, Transferred
Whitneyoff Allen & then Clark -- Mr Smith called
to me of 2 and finds 15 book relative to the post office
found all day but I do not lay -- closed in the
evening -- Mr King called -- he has just returned from
Baltic -- Since he went to Grand Christmas with Mr Smith's
family -- Thursday 7th fine day -- Mr WhitneyBrown came -- smiles
at the King to dinner -- Mr Smith came & informed us there to be
ridiculous on the American book trip -- I have & must & I
pland -- I want -- or rather want to be so --
Friday 8th fine day -- Mr Davis -- Smith's King called
I was planting flowers for Mr Smith's bishop of
one while there is becoming a successful work in the
city from the Church -- Rose after dinner -- Col Peltz
& Thompson -- they said they were dying for the Ball
the 9th -- Brooks of N. Orleans -- Read his papers
to Col Peltz -- I went over to Mr Smith's --

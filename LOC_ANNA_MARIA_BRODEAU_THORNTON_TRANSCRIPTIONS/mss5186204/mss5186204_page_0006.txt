1785
Thursday 25th. To day (by some of his old
Journal) would have been my
dear husband's birth day, tho he
used to think it was the 27th
from his journal. - British black
from this journal. - Sunday will come
be a day of deep feeling - I never
can forget the day he was buried.
Oh my God - support me, I have
never asked one!

29th Thursday - Mr. Benjamin came to

Grounds - Wept & prayed over the
game of my poor husband - I
have almost wept to be a Catholic
for this game I have prayed for
the souls of the departed - Oh
most merciful God - receive the
soul of my beloved Husband with
the assurance of ever lasting Bliss -
You have been a kind friend to me
in my father's House there are many
graces in you - Oh may he be enjoying

take upon myself any such an act of disobedience to his Majesty's
command as would be likely to give occasion to any suspicion of
disloyalty. I have, therefore, directed my servants to take up
the papers which I have in my possession, and to send them
to the King's office, where they will be received by the
person appointed for that purpose, and there will be no
doubt of their being returned to me. I have, therefore,
no reason to expect any difficulty in complying with the
King's pleasure.
I am, Sir,
Your most obedient servant
JOHN HENRY

Thursday 23d — The 78 — around 10 o'clock
at night — I set out to go to Fredericksburg —
and return — I returned — and found
Braywell in his tent with a broken
carver — which came from him —
Saturday 26th — Another 10000 came —
Thursday 27th — I came in contact with Lee —
and went to his tent — and found him
writing to me — and I returned to my
camp — and sent the letter with
Braywell to Genl. Lee —
Friday 28th — I sent the letter to Genl. Lee
and left Genl. Lee's tent — and went
to the hospital — where I found
Braywell in Genl. Lee's tent — and
found him writing to me — and I returned to
my camp — and sent the letter with

Wednesday 18th. Cool — Mrs Jones called early —
Mr. Washington called — I walked — came
to John Blanch — Mr. Day was sick —
but soon returned to Mr Blanchs office —
Mr Blanchs house — I spoke with him on the Common —
the talk was — but did not stay long — Reading the papers —
I took a pleasant trip — Mr. Day
Thursday 19th. Woggy — I rose early — went to Market —
after breakfast — walked to Mr Days office — I returned home
— I came home — but I did not take it — I am not surprised
— I am called while I am not — I had not
seen him in his own office — Did all they have the
profit of it — I am much — I hope all will — I will
leave him in the hands of the Judge — I will leave him to Mr. Day

Thursday 28th June last night. I took a
client to deliver to Mr. Fuller, 10lb — to his
order. He paid 6 pounds — called at the office
and left a letter from Mr. Lee. He is making
enquiries respecting Mr. Lincoln's death.
Mr. Lincoln's death came to my attention
at 10pm. I heard it in the office. I am informed
that Mr. Lincoln died at 10pm. in uniform
at the office. I have not seen the body.
Wednesday 29th June 1861. I received a
letter from Mr. Lincoln's brother. He says
he will give me a report regarding
the conduct of the people in the city
of Baltimore. I have not seen the city
of Baltimore. I have not seen the body.

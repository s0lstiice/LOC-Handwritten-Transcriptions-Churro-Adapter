July 29th Tuesday - This day
Mr. R. Miller & Mr. Charles F. King
appeared the present possession left
by my dear husband - I doubt I
could dispossess him of any property
to pay debts - I trust he has given up
his microscope - matches &c &c - I
believe I am to visit Mr. Thompson -
How much pleasure it would have
given me to have made presents
to all these kind gentlemen - but
money must be had - I cannot
tell even though this does not
give any comfort to Mrs. King! -
July 1st Tuesday - Another busy
day - Mr. Baird, for Josephine came
& asked an interview - and
brought the accomplished accounts of
translating & Southern Eclipse -
I gave him a promise to tell the
people he was independent of the
money by the plainsse agreement
he would send through Jefferson
and I suppose that he would
be opposed to his previous honorable
steps at Mr. Raymond's Little -
I am very much disappointed at
his changing his sentiments as he
calculates on the receipt from him
to pay money little clause - But I
must be patient - at first
I felt overwhelmed, &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c < >
but I am now in a position to
act immediately - &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c &c >
and can only be done by degrees, follow
degrees too.
July 26th - Clouds with one Bank
cloud to the east - I am so much obliged
to God - I am so much obliged to
God that I fear will be a want of
time - I suppose I may not live to see it - If any article
could be at - &c - I am sure it
would be accomplished -
Jefferson & Col. Bouffes to day
1835

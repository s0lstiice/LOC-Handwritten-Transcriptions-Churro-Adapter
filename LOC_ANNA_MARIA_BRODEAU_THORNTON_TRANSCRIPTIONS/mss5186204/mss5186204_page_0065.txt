Monday 30th July — Mr. Potter called the Lords
very lively — Judge Washington died in Philadelphia —
a few days ago — George Kirk the bearer to Mr.
Washington brought it — against this day of Lightning
Thunder Ram & Sword
Sunday April 2d June 1812 — Maj. Anderson came to
Washington to the Congress for the bearer — which he returned
from after with the money for Ram — Sword & Sword
they are for Mr. Potter (the bearer) from bearer
Washington — returned 8 days after — I am
wanting to pay him — & expect that Mr Washington
will send in the said bearer
Wednesday April 2d June 1812 — The Ambulance to Norfolk
came before me with the Ambulance to Norfolk
to stand at breakfast — I must to Mr. Potter to answer
your part — He intends time pay Mr Potter died soon
after he left Philadelphia — in the way down
which I bought wood from the carrier — the last bearer —
Sam Smith — & I believe he is gone — Mr. Potter called
came in an opinion for the bearer of the Congress
for Washington to take the boat yesterday & 2d — he left the bearer
& returned — & he went in the Ambulance — from Mr Potter to the
Carriage — & returned to the carrier
Thursday 5th — Board — Washington came to the Congress
to take the bearer to Philadelphia to answer the
case amounted to upwards of 20000 — I am very
pleased — & I am very much obliged to you for your
opinion — & I am very much obliged to Mr Potter for his
opinion — & I am very much obliged to the bearer of the Congress
for Washington to take the boat yesterday & 2d — he left the bearer
& returned — & he went in the Ambulance — from Mr Potter to the
carriage — & returned to the bearer
Friday 6th — Board — Washington came to the Congress
to take the bearer to Philadelphia to answer the
bearer — & I am very much obliged to you for your
opinion — & I am very much obliged for the bearer of the Congress
for Washington to take the boat yesterday & 2d — he left the bearer —
& returned — & he went in the Ambulance — from Mr Potter to the
carriage — & returned to the bearer

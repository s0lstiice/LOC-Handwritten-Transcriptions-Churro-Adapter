Wednesday September 15th Mr. Reitler called in 81
against to let him have the drawings
at such prices for 150 h.
I gave him a part of the house & part
with the drawings -- but I have since
printed German. This Katherine drawing
& I hope I am mistaken in opinion, on
the estate is deeply in debt -- I may
take one life time or more to pay them
leaving the 1500000 to Charles --

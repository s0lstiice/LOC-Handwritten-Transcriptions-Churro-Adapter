Thursday 20thandy— Rain last night— Got on the
flourins— Got came with potatoes— Sunday 21st—
Miss Potter Gardiens called— Col. 12 came to my house
and said not take up his note & I wrote to the Bank
and he ported it— George 8 Arthur giving me orders
with biggin from to Mr. Hunter & sent off—
Wednesday 22nd 1838— George coming Arkansas &
Mr. Baker called & told me— Mama not well
Wednesday 23rd Sunday 24th— Col. 12 called & said
George then Mr. Hunter planted & Mr. Baker on Kentucky—
Order to the Barrackers to give James to Capt. Brown
Friday 25th Sunday 26th— Mr. Dickson & daughter called
to Solomon 12— Mr. Baker called about horses—
Col. 12 called, had seen Judge Chase about money in a
letter appointment— Mr. Forrest came over
to see to him about the appearance— He thinks it may
convenient to be so very stout in my case—
Did not go out— Mrs Jones asked me to go out here
to your house—
Saturday 27th Sunday 28th— Mama had a fall on the back
part of her back but she is here— I went to the bank to
see her— Mr. Jackson called. I had to go to the Interimion to
the Mexican soldiers— After dinner Mama had a
fall— I had ordered the carriage before 10 o’ clock to
the door. I went to Mr. Graham to get the horse & got
of Capt. David Wells Traders in Missouri—
Sunday 29th Sunday 30th— Mama very poorly— I came over
without help— I leave from 12 o’ clock— Mr. Brown— Smiths
Mr. Jones & Baker— W.B. Vanderbilt— Lady of company
not called—
Monday 26th Monday 27th— Rain last night— Col. 12
called— We leave town— came in Friday last—
Col. 12 called after dinner on his way to the Bank & left
me in the house— called in the afternoon— Rain—

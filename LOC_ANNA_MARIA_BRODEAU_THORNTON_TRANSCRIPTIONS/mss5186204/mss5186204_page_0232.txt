January 1835
Thursday January 2nd
Fine day our head — Snow and frost
We were not out all day & only got
thick patrick called — Mr Smith
and not come in the house as we
expected — Mr Oliver's death comm
stated in the paper — he was an old
reignant man in Baltimore —
a free at most Water House occupied
by Genl Hunter's post at 6 am — time
Bad beginning of the new year —
Gloomy — but not cold — Mr Ford's death
called — Manu poorly — New commanry
herein — & Giffing came later with strong
news of Mr Pitts' life Believing
Saturday January 3rd
Mr Jollist called — Mr Smith — came
a letter from Mr Pitts on my being obliged
to leave the house — Mr King now in the house — Mr
Smiths called — Mr Smiths came to bed — Mr King later
— when still — Jones in our quarters — & Spence my
pilot went on board — Graham killed —

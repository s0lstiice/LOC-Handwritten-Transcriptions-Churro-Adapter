Saturday 2d -- Mr Smith went with me
to Church -- He sends Encouragement -- It
was a rainy Day & a fine Engagement
post -- was neglected until the evening lest
"God is Love" -- I offer myself to remain
at the post and leave to dinner --
Monday 3d -- Sunday day -- Have to
wait to get leave & to return -- and will
not give up the Station until I consider him
inspired for the most part -- I am
sent George to Mr Johnson -- Col. D --
attends early -- will be there 4 days tomorrow
David came down & says the manager
has taken and will pay for the whole
property to leave to send her home -- This
is paid for the night by Mr D -- I did not
suppose he would do so without a written
order -- or further communication --
It is very contrary to my own intention
to sell any of them -- But if they were
not taken -- What can I do -- I suppose
afford to give them free -- I suppose they
are of course must be very profitable --
Rain -- Thunder & Lightning --
at Noon -- Ready to go to the
service of the Union of Capt. Newman
United States --
Tuesday 4th -- Rain -- about 11 o'clock
another building down -- Genl returned
this morning from the Ridge post
and we returned to the station to an about
11 o'clock -- We have been on our way
to the post with Mr. Hunter for the
present called -- I wrote to
Mr. Bracken -- sent a paper to Mr. Richardson
Wednesday 5th -- Partly cloudy -- Took
Mr. Collett & Mann to the Company --
see many of the company here for information --
Will not be able to establish
much which Mann & Co. wish to do
at this time -- an improvement here --
Mr. Thompson was much satisfied with
the river -- Mr. Bracken returned us --
returned to Mr. Smith the day came
to leave us is nearly five o'clock --
Thursday 6th -- Hard frosts -- no building at
3 o'clock in the morning -- offered to Mr. Collett
Mr. Graham came to him -- Mr.

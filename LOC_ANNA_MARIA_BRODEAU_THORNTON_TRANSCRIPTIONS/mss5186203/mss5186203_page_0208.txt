Wednesday 22d
Tuesday 23d and - Socks Mr. Catts with us to Mr. Dattin's sale
of Bannock sold late this evening
Wednesday 24th drew from home - Part of Mr. Bannock's Sons
to Genl. H. V. to the College returned home departed the day
with us - Mr. Matthews also to Denver Mr. Catts to town
Thursday 25th Socks D. Bannock's family to the home of Captal
Coltus from 8 to 12 night Mr. Coltus - Dr. Hattie died
from cold & fever - Genl Taylor arrived last day
Friday 26th Sold from to the post San Villands - D. H. 28 1/2
from and in to town returned with C. Bannock - Mr. Hattie
with family staying here to town

Thursday 6th December - 2d time sent into the Bank
Friday 7th - 3d time sent into the Bank
Saturday 8th - came down early for the morning
Monday 10th - came down at 10 o'clock to the Bank
to the bankers de of after dinner - wind very high -
Sunday 11th - 2d time sent into the Bank
after dinner rode to the point - could not level
another ship -
Monday 12th - 2d time sent into the garden -
rode to the Bank and left it in a black fog
Wednesday 14th - I walked to the batteries - after dinner rode to
the Capitols Hill - 9 o'clock the wind for the West
Wednesday 15th - I left the City of Philadelphia to go to
Philadelphia to visit my family -
Saturday 17th - I went to the City of Philadelphia to visit my
family - I returned home - rode from home to the City of

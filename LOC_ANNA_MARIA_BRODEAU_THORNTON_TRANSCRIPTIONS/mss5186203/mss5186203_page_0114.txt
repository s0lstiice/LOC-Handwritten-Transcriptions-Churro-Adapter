1811
January 1st
morning
Mr. Torrey went with me to Mr. Helling
General -- Dr. Smith, and to the President.
We dined at Mr. Danforth's -- my first dinner
was generally bad -- I might not finish it
and give
Monday 2d
Meat -- Hominy -- Chocolate --
Dr. Torrey's present Gospel. (Sunday sermon from Mr. Breckin
ridge)
Wednesday 3d
to Kentucky -- Salt --
Thursday 4th
train -- Rail
Friday 5th
no fancy here wanted to be a manager
Get out luggage for Philadelphia & in to Baltimore
from morning till dark with him --
Sent to Mr. Smith for a letter -- got well --
Left Baltimore with me to meet Mr. Briggs
(late Comptroller of Public Bills) -- 9 o'clock --
went to Montgomery to him -- 9 o'clock --
called on the Govt. public

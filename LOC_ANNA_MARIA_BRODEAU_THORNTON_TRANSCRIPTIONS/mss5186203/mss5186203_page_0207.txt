Friday 29th 207. must be on the Race
in which he is at Mrs. P's by invitation - Rain
at landing 207 windy - I went with Mr. Chaplin & Harriet to
visit our house walked after dinner - 207. must
traverse to our House P.M.
Sunday 31st cold & windy -
Monday 1st post Rain
Sunday 2nd post to bed at 10.30 a.m. 207. & I went to
the post office to see if I could get a letter to Mr. Chaplin
to give me a letter to Mr. Chaplin & Harriet to leave
at 10.30 a.m. - We walked to our house & 207. left
to come to me - Fine moonlight.

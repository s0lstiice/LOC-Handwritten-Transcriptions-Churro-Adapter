Tuesday 5th 1817. Went to the Office for a Short Time -- No one
to be found to take my papers -- went with Mr. Collett to
the Bullhops' Stable -- 2 o'clock went up to the
Stable -- 2 o'clock went up to the Office -- returned home
at 3 o'clock -- went to bed --
Wednesday 6th 1817. Went to the Office -- returned home at 2 o'clock --
returned home at 3 o'clock -- went to bed --
Thursday 7th 1817. Went to the Office -- returned home at 3 o'clock --

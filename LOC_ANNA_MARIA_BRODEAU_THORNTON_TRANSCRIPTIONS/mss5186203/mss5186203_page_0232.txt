February
Thursday 2nd Sent John up to the farm for horse feed. Sent
the dead on the King.
Friday 3rd Friday. Sent with Mr. Lee to the Washington &
Mr. Bethlemme.
Saturday 4th. Sent John brought in 112 lbs. of ammunition
for the army. Sent out 4000 lbs of provisions & 2600 lbs of bread & 4 horses
Monday 5th. A pleasant day. Good weather. Sent to all day
riding & at night repairing road and atend me to the Arabian hills
at 11 o clock in the evening returned
Wednesday 6th. Some snow
Thursday 7th. Riding Hunting & Sleep
Friday 8th. Sent letters from Mr. Washington. Bethlemme

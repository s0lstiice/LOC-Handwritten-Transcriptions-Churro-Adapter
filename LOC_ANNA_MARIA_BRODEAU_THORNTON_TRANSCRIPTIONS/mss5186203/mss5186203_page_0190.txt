Thursday 22d. — Sent John to the ferry. Remain
Friday 23d. — Cold weather. Rose to Genl. Adj. — Sent out the
prisoners. — Mr. Smith came in on horse back. He
came over — Dr. and I went to the water.
Saturday 24th. — Mr. Smith walked to Genl Adj. on horse back
as I came out all the men were sleeping. I told him to
come up after dinner — I then went to Captain Breckin-
ridge's office & returned here this morning.
Sunday 25th. — Fine day. — Sent to Mr. Smiths office —
found him in bed. I walked to Genl Adj. — I found
Mr. Smith in bed. I walked to Genl Adj. — I found

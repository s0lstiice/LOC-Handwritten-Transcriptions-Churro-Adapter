Monday 18th May 1787. — I had not gone to bed with the party for
leaving this day for want of an hour. — Mr. Lewis & family came to tea. — Sent for Mr. B. Hill Liverpool
Friday 19th May 1787. — Mr. Lewis sent an answer from
want to Philadelphia to Denver Col. B. set off for Richmond
in the morning to Mr. Thompson — very few men —
they sent apologies generally our late the heat the ladies
the excuse — Mr. Lewis Denver brought lately 7. fine linen
we are only to wait. — 5 J. walked.
Wednesday 20th May 1787. — Mr. Ballantine called — also Mr.
Duffy, Mr. D. Brent — Mr. Kelly — Mr. J. Smith
to dinner — called at Mrs. J. Smiths
Thursday 21st May 1787. — my wagon — 5 J. ride to Philadelphia
5 J. went not to bed after for Sunday — caught in
part came home with Mrs. J. —
Friday 22nd pleasant day —
York a long ride on horseback — called at Mrs. B. Smiths
not at bed. — Gun spoken of 6000 pounds called — Mrs.
King also.
Saturday 23rd pleasant. — Walked a little after dinner
from my ride — nothing of the House — Sent
for up to the farm for fruit —
Sunday May 24th Rainy.
Monday 25th Mr. Ballantine called after dinner — 5 J. rode out —
Loudon 26th Mr. Ballantine called after dinner — 5 J. rode out —
Mr. Ballantine spoke to Mr. Loudon — Mr. Loudon spoke to
Mr. King — 5 J. went to the farm to buy flowers
sent to Mr. Brickell of Denver

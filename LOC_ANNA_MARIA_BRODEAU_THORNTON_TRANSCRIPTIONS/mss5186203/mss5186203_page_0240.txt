Wednesday 7th the Genl. transport men a sail.
Thursday 8th Genl. Madison sailed -- Mr. Hall, Genl. Dallas
drank tea at 10 o'clock with Mr. Bender Mathieu
Friday 9th Genl. Pleasant -- went to town -- called at Washington --
Saturday 10th Mr. Smith called -- received no mail from home
Sunday 11th Genl. Pleasant -- 12 o'clock went to Mr. Lewis' -- went
to the office -- went to Washington -- brought home Mr. Butts
on Tuesday 12th Genl. Pleasant -- we were absent --
Wednesday 12th fine bright -- We have had unusually cool
weather -- some fogs -- have had fires -- Genl. Grant left

January 1st 1812 Wednesday.
I was awakened very early in the morning. Found a man
from one of the ships, which arrived last night, speaking
to me for a physician who had him. He continued all day
sleeping & at intervals vomiting, what appears to be pure bile
in the morning he took some calomel & saltp. -- He had a
violent headache during the day.
On Thursday -- Many of our friends called about to inquire
after Dr. S. He was well enough to go down this morning.
He was not so well in the evening -- but went to bed. Joseph
Brandeis called by, upon business -- received a note from
Mr. H. Smith who was to have dinner here yesterday, but on
account of spending that ill declined coming.
Friday -- Cold weather -- Dr. S. walked to the Opera to see
Mr. Bickham & returned to bed. -- Mr. Fairjourn arrived
at dinner time -- Mr. Peter Philadelphus came to tea.
Dr. S. appeared pretty well & was quite cheerful.
Saturday -- Dr. S. taken upon the same way about 5 o'clock
this morning -- I went for a physician who left him again. He
lay all day sleeping -- took no nourishment & in the evening
takes some pills.
Sunday -- Dr. S. came down to breakfast -- had a little occasionally
& in the afternoon he was steadened with a return of his former
plaints, but it went off again. -- Mr. Taunton Davis

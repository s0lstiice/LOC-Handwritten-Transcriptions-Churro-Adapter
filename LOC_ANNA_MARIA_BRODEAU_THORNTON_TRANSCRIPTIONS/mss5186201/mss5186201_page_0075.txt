to each Small Charter
Dark them thru' a leather (of rope and
warm Water) three times, then remove them
and dry them, & put them thru dry material
again and let them dry again, dry them
second time in dry material, & put them
dry again, dry them till dry enough to burn.
To make pound Cake
Put the butter (which resembles brown
fat in the Sugar) and beat them together
then beat the yolks of the Egg's dried,
then the whites shimming off the yolk
as it rides from beating them, after
these being well mixed, put in the fire
by degrees beating as well as the time
and laps of beating will give many be put
in oven, & any kind of seasonings most
agreeable.
To make Jumbles
Work up together an equal quantity
of flour, butter & Sugar's, with only
a sufficient quantity of Egg to make
a good Cake, then put in a good oven
& bake till the Cake is browned on the
outside, then take it out & cut it into
small pieces, & put in a good oven
& bake till it is browned on the
outside, then take it out & cut it into
good pieces, & put in a good oven

Boil your eggs, and when well done
pick the meat out of them, then put
in a piece of sweet butter, a little
pepper, a little cinnamon, a little
lemon and a little sugar, butter
that will open the Curls when they are
fit to send up, thicken your fonds with
two or three yolks of eggs, with the
shell cream and enrich up your fonds
and garnish the dish with Court.
To make Marrow Pasties
take marrow out of marrow bone
or from apples, some cinnamon, for
betting your eyes altogether put
them in pretty paste and fry them
in short butter — Serve them up
with white sugar and Sack.
To make Liquor Syllabub
take a quart of cream a pint of stock
a pound of sugar and 3 quarts of eggs
whisk this well, and as the feathers
fall put it into the Syrup — and stew
in the morning to use at dinner.
Delectable pudding
Boil 2 potatoes thin them, divide
them and cut them thro' a sieve
or cylinder — mix with 1/2 a
lb of flour, butter, & eggs put into
three dishes, in a pint of fine milk
half a pound of ground sugar, fine
spices and a little such as rum or
brandy, and a 2 lb currants, beat
all together in a quarter of an hour
long stuff pastry round the dish —
Bake the pan down.
Cherries boiled with Claret and Cinnamon
to a degree of paltry will keep
good for thirty years — Mixed with a
few grains of Brackland seed, it may
be used as sweet bread, and last
together with fruit and Turkish
pears, and fermented in Waller may
be drunk as red wine.

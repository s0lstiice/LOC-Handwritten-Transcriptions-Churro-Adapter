ECLIPSES for the YEAR 1832.
I. Of the Moon, on the 13th of the second month of February, in the afternoon; partly visible.
The moon rises 3 1/2 days after 12 P.M. (M. West.) It rises about the same time as the sun, in north latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12 Greenwich, 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12. These times are 6 1/2 hours before and after midnight respectively; consequently invisible here. It will be visible in the northwestern parts of the United States, in the northern parts of the United States, in the northern parts of New-Hampshire, &c.
II. Of the Sun, on the 13th of the Third Month (March) about half an hour after midnight; consequently invisible here. It will be visible in the northern parts of the United States, in the northern parts of New-Hampshire, in the northern parts of Maine, in the northern parts of New-England, &c.
III. Of the Moon, on the 11th of the Eighth Month (August), about 11 1/2 hours in the morning; too visible in the United States, but invisible in the northern parts of the United States, in the northern parts of New-Hampshire, &c.
IV. Of the Sun, on the 23d of the Ninth Month (September), about midnight; consequently invisible here. It will be visible in the northern parts of the United States, in the northern parts of New-Hampshire, in the southern parts of Pennsylvania.
The Sun rises, at this latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12. The Moon rises, at this latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12.
The Sun sets, at this latitude, at 9 1/4 8 1/2 7 1/2. The Moon sets, at this latitude, at 9 1/4 8 1/2 7 1/2.
The Sun is in the zenith, at 12 noon, at this latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12. The Moon is in the zenith, at this latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12.
The Sun is in the meridian, at 6 P.M., at this latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12. The Moon is in this latitude, at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12.
At Baltimore, 3 1/2 minutes after the Sun rises at 3 3/4 2 7/12 2 11/12. (These places respectively it will be sensible to find the Sun's rising and setting times at Baltimore, nearly 31/2 degrees south of London, nearly 31/2 degrees north of London.)

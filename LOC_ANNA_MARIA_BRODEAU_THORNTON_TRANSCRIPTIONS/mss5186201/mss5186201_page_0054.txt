Rules and Regulations
For governing the City and Port of Philadelphia, from the
Introduction of Pulmonary and Contagious Diseases,
established by the Injefctiof of the Board of Health.
All captains or masters of vessels, coming from beyond sea,
(whether exempted) shall anchor opposite the Health-Office on
their arrival, and shall present themselves with their corres-
pondent certificate of health from the resident physician; and until they re-
solve such certificate, they shall not land and anchor in the harbor,
to be received into the port, unless they pay within the penalty of
500 dollars for every such offense.
After landing, the captain or master shall send a
letter to the resident physician, and while the physician is pre-
senting his official examination, the captain or master, every-
body of the crew, and passengers, shall be despatched or
qualified; he shall insure all individuals requesting the
examination with a certificate of health, and every person,
or, if any person denies the physician or other person offic-
ial, for every such offense he shall pay 10 dollars.
As the physician is present, and the certificate (if exempted) shall
be sent by sea before the captain has obtained a certificate,
he shall pay 20 dollars.
All vessels shall be required to have a daily log book, and
all vessels shall be a weekly list of service from the West-India
Islands, the French Main, the French, Dutch, Danish, Swedish
Islands, and all other places, and the number of men and boys
from the time of their arrival at Six Months. All vessels to be
healthly to receive from any place, and to be qualified, shall
present their certificate of health, and shall present a questionnaire of
12 days, commencing from the time they landed from the last port
per vessel, and the captain or master shall be notified by letter

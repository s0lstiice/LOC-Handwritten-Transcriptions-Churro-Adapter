CIRCUIT COURTS.
Eastern Circuit.
For New Hampshire, at Portsmouth on the 2nd of May, and at
Exeter on the 1st of June, and at Portsmouth on the 12th of
September, at Windham, and Brimington alternately, the 10th
of May annually.
For Massachusetts, at Boston, on the 7th of June, and at
Old Harbor.
For Rhode Island, at Newport, on the 10th of June, and at Pro-
vidence, on the 10th of November.
Connecticut, at New Haven, on the 25th of April, and at
Hartford, on the 10th of June, and at New Haven, on the 7th of
November, at New York, on the 7th of April, and September.
Middle Circuit.
For New Jersey, at Trenton, on the 2nd of April, and October,
Pennsylvania, at Philadelphia on the 12th of April,
and October, Maryland, at Baltimore, and the District of
Colombia, at Washington, on the second Monday in June,
and at Dover on the 7th of October.
For Maryland, at Baltimore, on the 10th of May, and at Baltimore
on the 7th of November.
Vigilance at Baltimore on the 2nd of May and November.
Southern Circuit.
For North Carolina, at Wake Court-House, or at Raleigh, the
1st of June, and such time as may be required.
For South Carolina, at Columbia, on the 10th of May, and at
Columbia the 10th of October.
Georgia, at Savannah, on the 5th of April, and at Augusta
the 10th of December.
When any of the above said days happen to be Sunday, the
Monday following the Circuit is opened.

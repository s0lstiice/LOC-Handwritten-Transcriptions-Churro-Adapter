January 1842
This year commences authentically at length
the matter -- a beautiful bright day
to all the people are gay & thriving -- the
president stands upon platform -- Many not
able to gain entrance, & those that do in fear
of being crowded to death -- or by losing a limb
Mr. & Mrs. Adams -- Mr. Madison is gone --
the Secretary & many private families received
Company -- & provided ample refreshments --
I hope this will be a prosperous year -- & the
prospect in some respects is a little gloomy, as
it is said we have an empty Treasury to pay with
& it is very uncertain when the Currency will be
regulated -- the President's Board of Exchequer Bill
not being yet acted upon -- & any one can say
it will -- the United States will hazard by both of
Compromise & they will probably return the compliments
& vote his project.
Saturday 5th. There was a Ball at Mr. Bowdoin's
Republican Meeting in honor of the Emphasis of
the Christianity of Sam -- which took place on

1845
May 17th Theodor Talbot set off this morning to
company Capt Fremont on another
expedition to Oregon &c. &c.
Capt J. went yesterday.
May 28th Wednesday -- another Era of painful
one has commenced -- I have this day
conveyed to D. Miller my House lot for an
annuity of 600 $ -- very 10 p. C in 600 $ --
I have been induced to do this on finding myself
to my regret that I have only a life Estate in it
& the property generally -- on my decease
it would go to the colonization Society -- if my
present earnest desire is to secure some part to
Mr J. & children -- D M expresses care if my
death before two years from date to pay 1000 $ to
me I may direct -- Is it not wonderful
that I have lived so many years -- blind to this
fact -- & that it came out from God's hand by chance
he says he told me so before -- I suppose he did but
I either don't understand or forget it!
It has indeed given me the heartache -- & as per his
to leave this here.
June 8th
General Andrew Jackson died aged 79 years
27th a procession & oration by Mr Bancroft
(Chief Mag) in honor of Gen Jackson --
This day July 4th -- a very cool day & quiet one
principal display in provisions was of
the Union Sunday school children --
there were many small parties in different
places -- I find within the house -- we
did not go out till evening -- taken with I
called to see Mrs Newman who had just
returned from the Daughtry's (Mrs Jackson)
near Tenterfield -- It is the anniversary of
my beloved mother's death when I do not feel
inclined to join in any party or battle
of the sad accidents occurred from the war kits
last year -- Mr Keeney's (carpenter) father
a colored man and died from wounds received --
several other killed --

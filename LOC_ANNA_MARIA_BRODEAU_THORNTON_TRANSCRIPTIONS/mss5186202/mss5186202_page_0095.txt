night between eight & nine o'clock -- I put up at
Mr Martin Smith's there we had a decent supper
at the house & went our very early -- A heavy
cloudy day & good day's grain.
Sunday Dec 1st 1861 about very dull weather, clouds
but some clouds & I was very pleasant & happy
about 10 o'clock after riding 13 or 14 miles, at
the house Morton's in Charlotte Va -- The house
is situated among some stately Oaks and
is very neat & pleasant. -- They raise
grain only in this form & do not plant any
threes -- The roads have very excellent condition
we left Mr Follis in Charlotte Va -- The
morning of Dec 3d we went to Spotsylvania Court House
& went on to Richmond Va -- We arrived at Charlotte Court House in 2 miles
Had some supper & rested there awhile -- The press
made a decent appearance turning out to
church in their Sunday apparels -- a man of
the name of Saunders was brought here to put
into the Jail for killing a man named Tommy
by cutting him on the legs in a drunken fit --
passed a large white house partially inhabited
belonging to a Mr Spenser -- They got their goods
from Philadelphia Philadelphia Philadelphia
here are 5 trains within 1 mile & the bounty
there is much omitted by produce who supplying them
in considerable times -- Soon after leaving
the Court House we met two gentlemen travelling
from Georgia to Philadelphia -- Capt. Joseph Davis & Capt.
Oliver Recognized with D. Barney & I had a letter
for Capt. C. B. -- We must leave the Court House & went around
by one Whistle -- came to an appearance of a quiet
town to get accommodations at a fine dwelling
but were obliged to continue on and arrived at
Mr Harris' just as it began to pour most big
rain -- I thought it would not continue

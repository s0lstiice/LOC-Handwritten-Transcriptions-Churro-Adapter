Wednesday
12th May
1861
After dinner I went to visit the Engineer & Company papers
respecting clothing for the Volunteers.
At 10 o'clock, Mr. Button, Mr. Low & Mayes called in the
Morning; I returned and found all the arrangements by
mutiny -- were here. Gen. Forrest --
Legion of States, Washington, Va. --
on his return called to see Mr. Mayes who was
at the Treasury Office, to speak to him respecting the
House Clipper which was delivered to him to day --
received a letter from Mr. W. Locke Ballh -- mentioning
that the Vice Governor in Charity Hospital in his office
had returned protested for non-receiptance -- Mr. Kinman
called on his way to the Ball House he had a letter
for Gen. Forrest -- that he would meet tomorrow --
The Court returned about 1 o'clock with the Commissioners
Could not go to George Town as our servant Brown
could have undertaken it is not returned -- The women
that came yesterday have nothing -- Sent
for to the train to fetch Mrs. Draper --
Mr. Wallace came for the papers reports (263) due
for the mutilated woman Triss -- which I found --
Dr. L went to meet at Mr. Button -- Mr. Brown
called -- while he was here, three men came to
ask to see the plans of the Capitol --
The damage came from the farm, brought butter
from the farm -- the women to get dresses --
Mr. Button & Mr. Button with Genl. Lowes & Mr. Mayes
Thomas from Baltimore -- The Wilds came --
1 o'clock -- 4th time were found --

Saturday 22d August - set out about
10 o'clock on a third expedition to baltimore
at Alexandria saw Mr. Button & went to
his house intending to stay only a moment
but as they were going to dinner we found
we were detained so long that it was our
delight before we reached Baltimore. The
bridge is incompatible from the effects of the
late weather - The bridge at Cincinnati is
quite destroyed - The stage now travelling
that route has injured Baltimore so much
that the Tanner is now very indifferent and
will probably be totally deserted.
January 23d - Weather pleasant - remained
Ouachita at 2 after nine - Detained by
Johns going after a horse to find grease in
for the wheels. - Hoped at Washington to have
to find the horses - passed again and got to
Indianola being about one mile. The bridge
begins destroyed here we had to go down to
Louis's ferry. The water was so high as to
disturb some horses & a steam vessel on Missouri
State at the ferry, a large tree was
laying over the wall and the water had been
over them half way up a large cornet tree.
The river has not been so high for 20 or 30
years before. - We had a tolerable supper
but no dancing -
Wednesday 24th - Sunday night - Detained by
company refusing to be done with the crowded
ship to Kentucky river line and did not get off
the ship till Tuesday evening - Hoped at Louisville
to find the horses - passed by Mr. Button's
building but found it empty. The different
stores filling place.
Thursday 25th - Weather very cool & pleasant
and before the sun rose to go to our comfortable
lodging - 9 o'clock went to bed at

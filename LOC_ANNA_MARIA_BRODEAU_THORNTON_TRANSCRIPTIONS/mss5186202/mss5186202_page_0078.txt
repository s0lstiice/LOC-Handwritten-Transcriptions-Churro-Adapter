Nov. 21st, 1831
Now late, I think, called before me and tried to read.
Has been sick & is gone to Martinsville. — This evening
called & bought me some books.— Sent for Mr. Henry
Barns with the song & some plays & the Annals of Virginia
— He returned & brought me the Works of
Mr. de Fleur in French.— I sent for dinner and for
some Causes that Mr. de Fleur was painting & for tea
— I returned from the office— Mr. de Fleur
sent to the office— Mr. de Fleur went to dinner about
11 o'clock— I read aloud some of the entertaining tales of Mr. de Fleur

We were about to start having been detained
some time by the train, Genl Washington of
St Croixine arrived here on his way to the
Saint George -- We stopped about an hour &
we had time enough to ride nine miles to our
intended stage as it is rather to have come
station with Genl W. -- He mentioned that the
Moravians have two thin trains out at 12 miles
the other at 9 miles out & intended so that their train
is not all in one tract. -- We had a pleasant
ride to St George town (on Roman R.) saw some
beautiful meadows & some orchards of large
French trees. Fruit very scarce this year.
We had a comfortable night, the streets being
clean (that I led the teeth only) -- the house
kept by Dutch people nothing clean in it but
the streets.

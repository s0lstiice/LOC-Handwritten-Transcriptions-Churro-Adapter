Sunday 30. — Rain fell about 7 o'clock —
cleared up with a pleasant breeze. —
Wind at Genl. Mills about 4 miles distant.
The house is pleasantly shaded but the ground
round it very poor. — We rode to Lake town
about 1 1/2 miles, returned to Genl. Mills
State came up with us at 2 o'clock this morning
Monday 31st. — Rode to Lake town in the
morning, pretty situated — built of wood except the
boat house & one or two private houses —
It is not a neat dwelling town, few of the houses
being finished & many going to decay. —
Called at Genl. Mills — took a return train
with us to Col. LaFay. —
Col. LaFay took this town
on his way to the Armory — We camped at Fort
Taylor from Lake town stopped here — Salubrious
and the neighborhood is not considered as healthy as
formerly, which they attribute to a large sale
and belonging to Judge King's mill.
Tuesday 2d Rise with the line, & left Col. LaFay
hostelable mansion, breakfasted at Col. Mills &
parted in with our companions at 8 o'clock — to
within 4 or 5 miles of Genl Mills where we had a pleasant
outing & returned very pleasantly together
a comfortable feeling — which added to the latitude
afforded my spirits all the afternoon.
Wednesday 3d. — Left Genl Mills at 7 o'clock
to go to Col. Mills & returned at 11 o'clock
to Genl Mills. — I left Genl Mills at 11 o'clock
to go to Col. Mills & returned at 1 o'clock.
Thursday 4th. — Left Col. Mills at 7 o'clock
to go to Genl Mills & returned at 11 o'clock.

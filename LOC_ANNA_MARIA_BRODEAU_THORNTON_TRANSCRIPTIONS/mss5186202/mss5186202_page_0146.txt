Saturday 29 — Though hot — came 9 miles
to Washington where we left — long time for
breakfast as the refreshment were assigned
to sitting room & Dress & to will have
to sit at the 6th House — Roads
rough & impassable — 11000 at Manassas
a decent house to find the Ladies — great
quantities of pumpkins here, but none very fine
they were distilling — poor roads with hardly
rough — Night at Williams as there had to
go on to Danville — 20000 men obliged to
put up with me Tomahawk
Sunday 30th July — a surprise at Danville
now near there we found last year — the
town burnt down, another night by Wilkinson
the proprietor of the one that was burnt down
rebuilt it — the 6th Inn better now the bridge
& mills — Night at Brandy where the
Ladies — We have to work at almost all the
places to have the order right out — they are
impatient people — Roads rough yet
to Harris' about sunset — We returned
at home & the daughters agreed after much
hurrying I leave to Danville — We eat out
of our own pockets — she had not even milked
& I believe nothing but bread in the house
our father came at last with a young
man who seemed to be her sweetheart — They
set up great part of the night, slept all in one
room while we occupied the other.
Monday 31 — Foggy — We travelled over
impassable roads to-day two miles to Poppy's
place in 2d regiment 60000, corn had made
up with 12000 miles — 2nd Clothes
Routes bad, came by Saunders' Bridge, stopped
at Richmond to ask for leave, as we had
to leave every 10th — We found him somewhat better
state of his Principals & a very active man
to feed on horses — Drive on as fast as the roads
will permit to Adams' late Office at the
best road, expecting to find a quite fresh horse
but the roads were full of horses of all sizes
we only took some milk punch as it was too late to
eat supper — We had a bad nights rest —
Saturday 1st July — Weather very hot — roads
more bad than good — Got to Pickens' sixteen
miles & very long since breakfasted — I went
to a miserable breakfast here — The
road with the expectation of two or three laying
thick very good for 10 miles to the postion
which we crossed just at dusk — 11000 at
Williams — Joseph Letcher & 10000 Benjamin Fine
just returned from Richmond Springs beyond
Morgan town — They are wounded & sick, Americans

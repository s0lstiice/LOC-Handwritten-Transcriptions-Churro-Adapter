August 20th. Theodor Talbot arrived from his
expedition of 15 months, (with the regiment)
to explore through the territory of Oregon &c
They went to California &c and endured many hardships
&c many risks of their lives from Cold hunger
thirst &c Indians -- The has returned in better
health & good spirits -- and notwithstanding all
his pains by land & sea & by water is very much
gratified by his trip --
Tuesday Nov 19 -- I went to the Alabama &c Ford &c
Saturday morning -- I have given up house
keeping &c let the House to Mrs Talbot remain
with her this winter -- what the Spring will
produce -- who knows -- whether I have done
right or wrong I can't tell -- but I am at any
rate relieved from the trouble of providing --
Maria &c Willy I have let go to themselves
I gave them some articles to begin house keeping
1844
Feb 28-1845 -- The Texas annexation
is long a subject of discussion thru the Nation
passed -- by a large majority in the House of
Representatives -- &c in the Senate the night
before -- 27th Feb -- It was generally supposed
that it would be lost there -- Cannon
was fired the night before, when the Bill passed
the Senate
March 4th -- inauguration of the new president
James Knox Polk -- Unfortunately stormed
all day -- but not withstanding crowded filled
the streets -- throngs of Hotemen flooded
to see the light! -- The procession was
not very imposing -- but the crowd at the Capitol
(it is said) was immense -- There was
two Bally -- one at the Theatre the other at
Carnegie -- the latter 10 1/2 tickets the other 5 1/2 --
Thursday 6th Sent night between 8 & 9 o'clock a fine
take out in the Theatre, which consumed 4 1/2 several
hours wear it -- Fortunately the persons in it, all
escaped without -- 300 armed house in 15th Street was
seized by unarmed spectators -- the Theater was burnt
off was a most awful &c rapid conflagration --

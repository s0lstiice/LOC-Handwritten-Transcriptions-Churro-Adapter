A Sunday hymn
addressed to P. S. of 83. N. on this laughing
during divine Service. (By J. D. Adams, Esq.)
Haek! to the holy Temple's Bell!
The Voice that animates us to pray!
My heart! each rising fancy quells!
Come, to the House of God repair
Then while I do converse sublime
Up to the throne of grace ascend
Set no unattain'd child of Time
Its vain prophecies with them blend.
How can it then for this wants implore
Crown for thy faithful service free
Of some The victor's tribute pour
Or bend in thanks the grateful knee? --
If from the awful King of Kings
each tribute turns thy sense astray
If to the Dust of earth I clasp
And fickle flies from Heaven away

From the Italian of Metastasio
If every civil paternal care
Were written on his brow,
How many words an pity share
With rags our enemy wins!
The fatal secret, when revealed
Of every active heart,
Will prove, that only while concealed
This lot appears the best.

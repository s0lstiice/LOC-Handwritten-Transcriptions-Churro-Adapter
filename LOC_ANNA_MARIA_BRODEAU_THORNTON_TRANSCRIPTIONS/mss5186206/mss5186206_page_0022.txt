(10)
On seeing His Majesty's Marcher ship leave at the parting
moment of the Royal Col. White
October 21st. 1818
How sweet then tears which fall from youthful eyes
As gentle dies from Youth's milking horn.
Thus - brimstone hissers die on the earth below
Thick brown and verdure over its ripe to thorns.
These - beauty's pieces as merits choicest music
Brings with them sacred & memorable deed.
Thus would the muse collect them in an urn
And keep them sacred to her White's patron.
What portraits more precious could the White grace
Or shield a brighter radiance over her face
Than beauty's gown, which fall from beauty's eyes
Of worth at once the tribute of the Rose.
J. Swannich

Little things are best
of fond respect
attached to myself -- a little that lays -- a little milk
and water for our children -- a little money for our estate --
amphitryon 218 v. 2.
When any thing abounds, we find
That nobody will have it
But when there's little of the kind,
But all the people have it
Of virtues are well, as he knows
And usefulness confessed,
The man who's well will surely own
a little one is best. *
The God of Love's a little light
But beautiful as thought;
Then ten not little, fair as light
And many things in short. +
O happy guilt! think that I am
To mark the poets song
Man wants but little here Belong
nor wants that little long
He Josephus de Vryxhibit -- a very ancient & serious poet
Multa voluptas longa est. -- Seneca
(106)
Metaphorical expressive Literal truths compared
and appreciated.
Written in the year 1797 upon recovering from a pleurisy
and addressed to a passionate & poetic Lover. --
While flames of love employ your songs,
His mine to chant my inflamed lungs. --
Your sighs of tormentions in the breast
Is the refrain of the child;
They both are near, but which is best?
Deprived of breath, my sanguine soul
The consolation of a sigh --
A consolation you enjoy.
Each time I cough ten thousand sounds
Give me the benefit of your parts. --
I so lament your freedom fled;
Beloved one captive to my bed! --
You capturons buy the chains of your love
What can he buy who cannot move?
You talk of fevers in the brain
Of pains that thrill thro' every vein:
Come look at me & then complain.

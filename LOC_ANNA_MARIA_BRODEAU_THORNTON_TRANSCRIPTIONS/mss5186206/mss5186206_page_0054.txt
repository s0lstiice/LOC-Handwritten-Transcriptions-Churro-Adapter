Called from this mortal scene, in bloom of life
Her last wish -- a much loved daughter, mother's Wife,
To whom each grace of excellence were given
A Saint on Earth, an Angel now in Heaven!
To her sad Throne, with trembling steps follow
Benevolent Parents, come to speak their Woe
To grave, it deep in Monumental Stone
And, with a Husband's sorrow mix their own
But ah! no farther trace this Tablet bears
Line after line is blotted -- with their Tears!
(75)
To the Memory of

her, mournful Parents, inscribed this

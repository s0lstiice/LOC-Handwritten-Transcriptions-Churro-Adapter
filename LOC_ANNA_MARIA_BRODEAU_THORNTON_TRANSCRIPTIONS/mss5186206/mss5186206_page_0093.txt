To Dorcas
by Mrs Margaret Harrison Smith
The names of Women have in every age
Adorned the Poets & Historians Page
And virtue genius beauty all have shone
In every clime, resplendent in the Throne
Yet every not the splendor of their Fame
The light their destiny brought their name
Since women were happy they in decent hour
Bewailed the impotence of wealth & power
In transient things which never end except
The richer treasures of the human heart
Not their emblazoned on the scroll of fame
But half so precious as that humble name
Impious soul & generous character given
Endeared to Hearts & approved by Heaven
That Women name, incursive upon a page
Which shall endure beyond times at most 1829

Remember her, Dorcas, her whose name you bear
Like her a strong, distrest, dry Sermon's tear
The wrongs of suffering, poverty redress,
And teach the infant Jesus its God to bless
Then shall the Women pay the Epaphrus prayer
Round thy labors, & repay thy Care
To heaven a sweet Memorial shall attend
And draw down blessings on the poor ones friend
Then Jesus in the last eventful day
Before the assembled universe shall say
"To thy bright Mansions, thine eternal home
"Thou heir of Glory - faithful servant come.

178 Sister's storms escaped -- its trials O'er
And broken every chain
triumphant on a blissful shore
Your souls shall meet again
With pity do ye look on those
That weep & longer here
Around where your dear forms repose
Oh day the fruitless time
All hallowed be this time above
All hallowed be this grave
Where sorrowing friends have often stood
And wept for those they love.

179 He healeth the broken heart
And bindeth up their wounds.
Oh! though he digest the mourner's tear
How dark this world would be
If when deceived & wounded here
We could not fly to thee
The friends who in our sun shine live
When winter comes are flown;
And he who has but times to give
Must weep those tears alone
But then wilt heal that broken heart
Which like the plants that grow
Their fragrance from the wounded part
Scarathis sweetest out of war
When joy no longer soothes or cheers
And even the hope that threes
A moment's sparkle in our tears
Is dimmed & vanishes too
Oh! who could bear life's stormy storm
Did not thy wing of love,
Come brightness, wasting thine the gloom
On peace-branched from above.
Moore

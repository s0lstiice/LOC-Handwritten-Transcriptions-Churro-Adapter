Victorious love, like true Devotion,
flies from Virtue, seeks Virtue, and delights to en-
gage itself, and moves by all but the Object of
its consideration. That respectfulness which at-
tracts it on the part of the Friend is maintained
and exalted by nothing so much as an unaffected
self-interest on the Woman's Side.

Happy beyond the common condition of her
sex is she, who has found a Friend indeed, open, honest
yet discreet, generously frank yet kindly, thoroughly
virtuous but not over-wise and cheerful at the same
time! Can such a Friend be loved too much, or char-
acterised too tenderly? If to excellence, as well as high
princes, there be any one thing more companionable than
another, next to friendship with the great Almighty,
it is this.

These are the usual Pleasures
which are laid by a mind composed of Virtues. In
that Situation, every thing is felt more strongly.
A dissipated Spirit is too superficial to be capa-
ble of deep permanent Delight.

If might on earth can present the image of celestial
qualities in its perfect array, it is surely an accom-
plished Woman, in whose purity and thoroughness
intelligence and Wisdom, mingle their Charms.

In such as have surveyed the various
Traces of Delight themselves, there is nothing to
which to attribute, nor to distinguish others they
admire, but in the tendency to shew a generous
satisfaction in seeking and making young Friends
happy.

On viewing my Mother's portrait
by
How warm the heart when dwelling on that face
Those lips that mine in thousands times have part,
That willing source that nature pours her own,
Where from my infant heart its earliest nest,
Now in those features try to trace my own;
But in a short minute my being dies,
Recall the voice that with its my helpless means
The thoughts that still sprang for beauty might have me;
That shaped Y formed me gave me to the day,
Bedecked in her breast abounding love and care
Our man unwarlike time our ear displays
To weak attentions the internal key;
This debt of love but one may claim on other
Such self-devotion boasts, save thee my Mother!

Oh let me seek that Charity, which confests that
Truth, which justifieth,
The most charitable deed by no means be confined to the
purest act of benevolence, or external beneficence. It is
of a much more spiritual and of divine nature. It is a spirit that
dwells present in grace, which warms the soul with expressive love
to God, and engorges it with disinterested affection to man, which
renders it the enjoying care of the joys, & chief delight of the heart,
to promote the happiness of the one, & the glory of the other. This
is that Charity of which so many excellent things are every
where spoken, which can never be too highly esteemed in her universally
civilized, sincere it is the image of God, & the new spirit of Heaven.
Henry
Mercy
"The quality of Mercy is not strained,
It dappeth as the gentle rain from heaven
Upon the place beneath; it is twice blessed;
It blesseth him that gives, & him that takes:
Tis mightiest in the mightiest; it becomes
The throned Monarch better than his Crown;
This sceptre shows the force of temporal power,
The attribute to awe & majesty;
Wherein both sit the dread & fear of kings,
It is an attribute to God himself,
And earthly power with them shows like his God,
When mercy seasons justice."
My beloved Husband frequently quoted the above
lines with great feeling.

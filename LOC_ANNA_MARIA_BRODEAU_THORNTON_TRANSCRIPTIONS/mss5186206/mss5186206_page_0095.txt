Let it more than words express
And if its eloquence will bless
I will Swiftly yet with no more
Let us resume the elastic floor
Strike the wild enthralling measure
That fills my very soul with pleasure!
Alas! must pleasure turn to pain?
This sacrifice I cannot sustain
Nothing like it, Wellington I adore!
But oh Athens! I am wretched no more!
A Note
in answer to a letter of Remonstrance
I do believe my charming Girl
When with a Whirligig's brav'ry whirl
Sustained upon his curving arm,
Your heart is pure— you think no harm
Nor die I on my hastily leaves
Impulse to you inspire & oblige
You have mistaken my intent,
Both in its meaning & extent
It was your language I translated

Either I think too much inflated
Which might to common minds convey
Things that you never meant to say
The human heart I know too well
To think it in its faults and fill
Time seeks a Voice & thinks from light
But value counts the noon day light
The faintest sigh of ingenious youth
Speaks in the world in which we dwell
Even truth you must not wholly tell
And the your heart be free from guilt
Not always do, must I your style
Since others — never ever kind —
May given meaning never designed
Not do I do not affect you
In this believe me just I have
And in my Dream meant to convey
A sense of what the world might say
To guard the quiet suspense of youth
You not to check the face of truth
These added times I therefore send
With wishes kind from your grave friend.
M H S.

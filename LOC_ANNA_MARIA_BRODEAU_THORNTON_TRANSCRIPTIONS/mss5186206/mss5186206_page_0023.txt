To the Rev. Doctor Andrews
These evening hours that glide so soft along
Recall my friend and animate my song.
This humble tribute I at least will pay
At Andrews' feet to those one gentle eye,
Or generous heart whose sympathetic flame
Can grief discern, and even itself tame
Accept the song to none more justly due
Than to those virtues which distinguish you.
With pity's redeeming friendship, poor
To sheer human ignorance the conquest of the penitent hour?
Can all the modest grace thus inspire
Or innocence and learning praise acquire?
Then yours it is in those collected shine
Whatever claims this vaunted praise combine.
This bel, a neighbour and a friend attest
When painful moments hide you secluded to rest
(13)
These most heartiest hours you taught found to flow
And quite to enrich what ever need could be wooed
Oh be it yours in numerous years to share
Whatever joys you think are worth your care
Or if in unappreciate hours an ill believe
May some engaging flame it brought divide
Some friend thus give of season warmth, secure
Who loves to shed the sympathetic tear
And still intent to soften human woes
Whence ever their affluence delights to plant the rose
It is
January 22d 1787

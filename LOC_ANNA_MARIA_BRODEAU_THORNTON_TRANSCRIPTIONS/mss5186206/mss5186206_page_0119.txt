205
A prayer by William Beeckford Esq
of Southampton
Like the low murmurs of the secret stream
Which thir Parkholders murmur to their way
My supplicant voice is heard ah! do not deem
That in vain I cry I throw my life away!
In the recesses of the forest valley
On the wild mountain on the violent bed
Where the first breezes of the new year blow
I wander truly, communing with God.
When the sainted Bishop of a wounded heart
Crope in and thundering through my sleeping frame
I turn to them that holy peace impart
Which is the working of thy awful name!
O all pervading Spirit! sacred beam!
Power of light & life! Eternal Power,
grant me this glorious cloud, one transient gleam
Of thy bright essence in my dying hour!
206
Teach oh! teach me to forget
Friends depart & memory takes them
To her caverns pure & deep
And a fond soul only watches
From the shadows where they sleep
Who shall behold the heart's affection?
Who shall learn its regret
If you leave my deep devotion
Teach oh! teach me to forget!
Bear me not to fastening towers
Towers with them I sail there last
Where me not springs early flowers
They remind me of the past
Music seems like mournful waiting
In the halls where we have met
Mistakes gay calls as unavailing
Teach oh! teach me to forget

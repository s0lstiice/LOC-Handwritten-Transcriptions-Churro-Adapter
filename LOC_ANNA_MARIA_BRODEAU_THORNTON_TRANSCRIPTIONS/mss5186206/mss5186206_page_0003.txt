8 went early upon the river
then in cars on our way
to New York I found out
(for Cal.) that it was not
the train by Elizabeth
Town where we wished to go
but by I. Albany - thence
in a Steamboat to N.York
when after a pleasant sail
in a fine Boat we arrived
about 2 after Eleven
Mr B. went out of trains
a little & I stood in
Boris here hospitality &
went to Elizabeth town
fast in a Steam ferry boat
then in car & thus walked
The King of an Island
very pretty country round with
a river of Spaldingth. Parked
they go by the turn in a few minutes
to a bridge if you find them
also in one place called
Bellamont at a distance a view
of a light house at Newcastle
which is fort independence &c
a distant view of a mountain
called Argyll mountains
the country to the North East
some thing like the region of hills
on which Hard, Maiden hill
&c. -- It is a pleasant summer
residence & convenient agreeable
to their climate and to their country

from the Boat to the Mansion House
on our way in a good time
today walked up Broadway
Mr. Gill accompanied us --
I went with Capt. P. in arms

St. Pauls Church of a pretty green

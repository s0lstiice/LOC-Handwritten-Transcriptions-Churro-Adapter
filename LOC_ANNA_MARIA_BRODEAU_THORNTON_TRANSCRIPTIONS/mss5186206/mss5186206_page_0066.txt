"The Roses lords favorite flower divine
Shall grace our curling boulds of crime,
With its fair leaves our temples bound,
The trust I laugh shall both go round.
Rose sweetest flower, spring's partial love,
Delight of all the gods above;
With thee the boy of Venus stands,
The grace join in many round.
Brown me instantly, god give me
Stains from my lips that I may reach thy shrine:
Whilst decked with roses I prepare,
To trip it with the wellmade fair."
Extracts from the Rose and the Rosebud.

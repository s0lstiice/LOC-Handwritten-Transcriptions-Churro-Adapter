"Remark how rarely ancient art provides
The broad brimmed cup with flat opened sides,
A cup intended for mans discrete use
And sober potions of the generous juice.
But womans more ambitious, thirsty soul,
Has long'd to revel in the floating bowl:
Deep I cupainns as the swelling lute
Of some stout back, the shaped hollow mouth,
Then turning out a cup like a tinny,
Somptuous, reclam'd, obscene! I drink'd but once!
A grant interest to the present day!
Exclusus, a native of atamas in Euboea, as celebrated
part, of the author of fifty comedies, introduces Proclus
Laying down these temperate moral Rules:
"Three cups I were a prudent man may take,
The first of these for constitution sake.
The second to the gods, the third the best."
The third I cast to all him to his rest:
Then home to bed. But if a fourth he found,

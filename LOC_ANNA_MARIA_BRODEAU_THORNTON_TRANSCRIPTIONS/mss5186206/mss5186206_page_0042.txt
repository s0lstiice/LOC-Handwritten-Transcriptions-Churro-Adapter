Thames, Amor, & under la gloria nostra
Fora spira natura altera, & move;
Dati tu, quanto in lei dolcezza fiorir:
Odi fame dell' etude in terra nostra:
Uidi, quant'arte dura, & imperta, & nostra
Labitis dilito, & morte non vuile adhuc
che dolcemente i piu, & gli occhi amorosi
che gentilezza di fii solle ombre chiostre!
L'abbida verde, & di lioi di color vielle
sparsi sotto quell'idea antigna, & onagra,
Preparan pur dell' bel fine la forma, & l'orchio;
Il ciel di variezza, & lucente faville
V'accompagna mano; in virtute si rallegra
U'effort fatto scena da si vigile occhio.
(31)
Sennel.
In viaggio Laura accidentalmente walking in some rural
place under Signign.
To raise our darling mood, thus gentle love,
to raise that tender fire of languishing love!
Shall this come star, bright mote from above?
What celestial switchblades descends her fire!
Grief, grief, and profit in her soul we have.
With which fond art would I join her charms immortal?
Each lock is sculptured, each slip is grace,
Magnifico on her tresses this chaste gray grace.
The violent kiss, the brilliant glance that cross
Beneath your eyes think, scattering, ancient stars
Smelt her lips, put to profit their youthful beauty
He lamented fire, that ever burned beneath glory,
Never burn, ambition to be made
By her dear, patient eyes ever only best!

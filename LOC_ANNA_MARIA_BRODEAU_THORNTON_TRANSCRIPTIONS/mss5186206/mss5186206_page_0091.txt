Psalm xiiii. v 11.
Why, O my soul, why thus cast down?
And why thus broken despair?
What the time of God's second coming
On all beneath the earth?
"Hope them in God! be from the breast
In the all merciful, the just!
Spirit of God! descend and dwell
In this sad, broken heart;
Be not forbidding, all despair
And bid them fear depart
Oh let me hear the still, small voice
That makes the fainting soul rejoice!
"Hope them in God" yet God hath given
This spark of living flame
To light the mourner's path to heaven,
And cheer his dying frame.
Celestial hope! Thy calm impart,
To soothe the soul this bruised heart!

Memory
Tend Memory like a morning bird,
When the morning's heart is heard
Repeating every touching line
Of Variety that from earth have gone
Now like a Widow in her Weeds, the Night
And her glimmeringapers silent still!
How sorrowful, how desolate, the graves
Perpetual Pools, I add to God's nature's scene!

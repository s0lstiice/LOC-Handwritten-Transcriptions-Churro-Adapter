A chorus of Ed. Stuart sung at Mr.
Robert's for in the King's recovery.
Thus : the mellow states recanting
Find our sentiments impart.
Every throbbing breast redoubling,
Speaks the feelings of the heart.
Thus great George, when near retiring,
Brings by Angels with delight,
And the sun in clouds retiring.
Stand to render from our signal.
Thus they shout, the King is turning,
And the hour of God advent.
Fromen this greatest beauty spring.
Waits our cry from shore to shore.
Royalty, who surveying,
Sees this pleasure in enjoying.
To be love and the heart.

The Lily and the Rose by W. Cowper
The nymph must see her female friend,
If more admired than she ;
But where will some contention end
If their own disagree ?
Within the gardens peaceful scene
Arrested two lively foes.
Showering to the rank of Queen
The Lily, and the Rose !
The Rose even reddened into rage
And swelling with disdain,
Appealed to many a pretty page,
To prove her right to reign.
The Lily's bright bosom command
A fair imperial flower
The second designed for Queen's hand
The sceptre of her power.

"How beautiful on the mountains falls
From human lips that the Lord God forgives!
Forgiveness -- till the attributes of Jesus --
The sound which opens the narrow narrow again
On earth both Jesus' sacred blood, & flying
Hopes & Religion hate in the waste of Life.
Thrice happy he whose heart has been so devoted
In the most lessons of humanity,
That he can give it utterance, & impart
Celestial graces to the human soul
And make the Man an Angel."

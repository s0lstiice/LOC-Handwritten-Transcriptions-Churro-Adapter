(152.)
They found the red Sebastian forth
They found the red Sebastian forth
And filled the golden bowl
I said it on the famished Earth
And spread its base earth
And said no more my peace shall be
A victim to thy treachery!
They poured of fame the blooming wreath
My knee the chaplet twined
My foot I trod the flowers beneath
And gave them to the Wind
And laid my heart no more shall trust
To that which is stiff but dust!
They brought me beauty's shield, and gave
To me her fancy form
Death came the felled grave
And in the cheek the Worm
I loved, yet said no more rail I
O'er flowers so frail, in sorrow's drift!
(153.)
They brought me then a spirit sweet
Religion ever fair
My heart listened her holy feet
My soul arose in prayer
And said - Fame, Pleasure all farewell!
Blest Spirit! then hast broke their Spirit!
For Enigma said it have been written by
Mr. Cowling, which from length of time baffles
The skill of all England to solve
"There is a word of plural number
A foe to peace & human brother
Not any word you chance to take
By adding, I, you plural make
But if you add on I to this
How strange the metamorphosis
Plural is plural then no more
And sweet what bitter was before"
Solution - The word is cares, to which
by adding I, you have cares.

(3.)
Still rain for good the supplicating voice
But leave to heaven the measure I choose
Safe in his power whose eyes discern after
The secret ambush of a grievous prayer
Implore his aid, in his decisions rest
Secrecy whether he gives, he gives the best.
D. Johnson.

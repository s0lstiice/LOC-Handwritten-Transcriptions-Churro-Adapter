118) A modern Priest has hastily delineated Christina
(Queen of Sweden) as the principal stock of her
figure of Charon, which his verses so much resemble
that it is scarce possible to mistake her.
By her fervent religion,
and her love to mankind in light,
Or her doublet, cape and dress,
Or a marooned ship,
That of Spumæa - Richthofen had
Tre Sotto in careless praise;
Or her gallant martial figure,
Or an amazigian Queen -
Was from Simon Bennet sprung,
And a fine art Raphael's tongue
Large eyed and sweet Innocence,
Tell us, 'tis Christina dear,
The who sate at burning Helm,
Yet gone up both Church & Realm
To no earthly recompense;

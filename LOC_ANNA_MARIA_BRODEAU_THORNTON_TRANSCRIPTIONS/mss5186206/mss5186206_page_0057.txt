The death of a common soldier from Struthers's
farm of Fox
Of unremembered name
Did the mean man get his life left behind
One who had never seen her daily prayers
of humble prayer, who to every tale
of distant war bidding an eager ear,
now pale and trembled, at her cottage door
The watchful eye that sat at, with dim eye
gaze on the plain, where on his passing steps
The last look hung. Nor ever shall she know
her husband dead; but tortured with vain hope
gazes on, then beats out time to her poor babe
And weeps in futility!

— with the rocks in Richmond's design divine

late midnight hour — earth him down
In me thoughts since will run,

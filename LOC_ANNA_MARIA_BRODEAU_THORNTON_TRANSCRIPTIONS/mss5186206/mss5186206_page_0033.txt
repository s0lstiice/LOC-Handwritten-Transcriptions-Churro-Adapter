After the death of the Chief, the rest of
the company brought only of leaving the
island, & they employed themselves in repair
ing the boat so that it might be able to support
a long voyage, &, they set sail with an inten
tion of possible to return to England; but
the force of the wind or the ignorance of the
pilots made them take the same course
the vessel had done, the boat struck against
the same rock, and this little wreck shared the
same fate. The princesses of Morocco were
then filled with Christian virtues of all nations
as those of Algiers are at present. There
was amongst others a Spaniard of Seville, named
John Morales, who, having been a Pilot for
some time, took great pleasure in hearing
the recitals of English prisoners. He learnt
of them the situation of the new country that
they had discovered, & the means by which it
could be known. As soon as Morales was free, he
offered his services to Don Juan Gonzales Jurez,
a Portuguese gentleman, employed by Prince
Ferny to make discoveries; Jurez undertook
this expedition, which had the most happy
success; he discovered the tomb of the Lord, near
which he had an altar erected. Jareo took
possession of this island on the 8th of July 1421.

For the Bride by
Mr M H Smith
Sweet Bride – so happy tho' fair
Entwine this chaplet in your hair
Unfading Amaranth & glorious rose
The hymenal wreath complete
Which I have formed for you
Fair Bride – of future life to thee
Wreath prophetic may it be
And in thy hide-groom may there blend
The silent lover & unchangeing friend
A blessong found by few
And Bride – the roses soon decay
Love as soon may pass away
Yet still not – if like Amaranth flowe r
Friendship resists times withering power
Remaining ever true
* Mrs Doncast Hunt. – * Amaranth by some made
an emblem of gratitude.

The Waltzer a Dream
by M H Smith
On the light fantastick tee
Round, around, around we go
Whirling quickly thru the trees
With its dizzying mazes glancing
Leaning high – almost flying
Swirling, turning almost flying
My breath is spent my head it turns
My brain throbbs – my heart it burns
Oh the exquisite Delight!
So vast it overwhelms me quite
I cannot stand! Where shall I rest?
"There rests Adam" on my breast
Oh let these find enlacing arms
Just lain that lovely weight of charms
More firmly on this Arm rely
And raise to mine that speaking Eye
Let

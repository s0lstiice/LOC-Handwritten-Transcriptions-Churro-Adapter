(10)
Verses to Mrs. Howard in her marriage
Ribbons & bows of late engaged the muse
But nobler things now elevate her views
Of former knot, by Love & Stephen tied
At Canveld Field, on accomplished Ruins
These claim the song, & Ch. for Alexander's type
To form the verse which such a part inspire.
The noise of war will its terror on
New peace and joy adhere on evident shore
The soldier called no more to camps & arm
New finds his recompense in beauty's charms.
The patriot banner glows with finer fires
With sister wishes & more sweet desires.
With trophies even it -- he quits the hostile field
And long victorious for his turn to yield
Is quit -- but only to that gentle sway
Which gods have felt & heroes must obey --
(11)
Howard! a name for ever dear to Time
In every age shall now distinction claim
In our illustrious & conspicuous cause
Or in defence of injured freedom's law
With it shines in certain mild or brave
Statesmanship's light a laurel
Oh with such honors cannot may it descend
The stream of time -- till time itself shall end
& see over the the posthumous tide decline
Till human joys are lost in the divine
Strawwick
Yet of this Music's scale her voice should range
With high, pure joy, with many a pleasing change
Grace and then every variation slide,
In every movement, Majesty provide
With me not enliven, the rest will Ed:
Just without languor without harshness Ed:
Hayley.

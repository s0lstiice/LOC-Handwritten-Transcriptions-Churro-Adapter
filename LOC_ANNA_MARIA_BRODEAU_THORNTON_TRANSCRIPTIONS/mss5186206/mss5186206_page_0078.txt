to Hortense, Empress of Holland & Eugene Vonckhauwen

Lady Margaret Hume
The First Hodge School Master
"Come here, Tom Fogarty, you little brat --
So you've been playing for the dishes
and tore your young taste of breeches! --
I have you here your lesson seat --
I double E -- what shall I double E?
Don't be shaming me mind what I say;
I double E you little stucked foot --
upon my conscience I'm disgracing my School
I double E you also! What do I do
When I raise up my Eyes to look at you? --
You'll say it now, or else the Devils will."
Why, sir, you Squint!"

Epigramme
To Nanette More -- On the birth of his
third Daughter.
I'm sorry dear Moore, there's a damp on your joy --
For think my old strain of My Melody stopped,
When I say that your Wife has a right to a boy;
To Venus is nothing without a young Cupid.
But since Fate the boon you wish'd for refuses,
By granting three girls to your happy embraces
The best must, while you wander abroad with the Muses
Your Wife will be carried at home by the Graces --
To Brigts Edgeworth
We could now bear his many "announcements" sign,
That refuge Miss Edgeworth can never be thine
The milking, where satire & moral unite
Must bring forth the name of their author to light
Go to & bid him in telling the source of their birth,
The bad now their Edge & the good now their worth --

Written on the decree of Miss Kitty Freeman
in her native year June 2 1792
Point stood of spring beneath the Wintry thorns
Time there as other Time's fate to die
A few short years have seen this Bloom and fade
And now the Hopeom in the midst is lost
The music that moves in Nature's general theme
Must feel superior sorrow at this bout
Other youth and elegance in mine lies
And yet spring appears to neither the aged
nor me the spirit of commensurate times
And all this pleasure perished in their presence
Our full satisfaction that they still may shine
Must shortly pass as the hours of Time
Some shall they leave not that thorns abide
Then ends at last, if I so vainly say
When each may find whether his pursuit
Or toil more certain than the patient fruit
And life's ambition poverty and slow
But empty travail or reluctant end
Yet well for me of it that among them
Our life like thorns may emulate the flowers
Its gentle as sweet as innocent its form
As round as gentle its miniature name
The matter desired yet in the seeds
Of life more lasting of future years
The root transplanted by Almighty power
That live and flourish a celestial flower

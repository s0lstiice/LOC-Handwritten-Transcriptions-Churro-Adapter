The godless chancell to hear,
And now to save her yet the rule,
The pride of the nation.
"Yours is," said she, "the noblest line,
"And yours the statelier men,
"And tell a third surpasses you,
"Let both be deemed a Queen."
Thus worthy of reconquest, each seeks
The loveliest British seat;
The seat of empire is her checks,
They reign united there!
A song by M. Cooper
The fighters are filled, & about to the shade,
And the whispering sounds of the red volunteers!
The winds play no longer, & long in their leaves,
For the hour on its surface their image receives.
Fourier years had they still since I last took a view
Of my favorite field, & the bank where they grew
Then yielded on their sides in the grasp they were in,
And I sat on this river which I had stayed it.
The blackbird has sought out another retreat,
Where the hazy afford him a screen from the flood,
And the scene where his notes have oft charmed me is
Now silent with his songs, leaving duty no more.
My fugitive years are all hastening away,
And I must myself be as busy as they,
With a rush on my breath & a stone at my heart
Or another such grave idea will in its stead.
The change with my heart in my fancy employs
I reflect on the frailty of man in his joy;
That with us are yet our pleasures, we see
Have a still shorter date, & die sooner than we.

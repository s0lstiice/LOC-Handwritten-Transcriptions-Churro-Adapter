This avenue, grand & extensive as it was, was
yet supported of a single tree; but it was the Banyan
tree, the mighty monarch of eastern Forests, yet one
the most independent & most beautiful production of
the regalable Hold. The symbol of eternity, from
its perpetual undiminished perpetual spring, independent
of revolving seasons, & defying the decay of time, it
stands alone & bold, reforming its own existence, &
multiplies its own form, fresh & unfaded amidst the
old age generation is propagated; while every branch as
emuluous of the parent greatness, throws out its fibrous
roots, & fastening in the Earth, becomes independent,
without being desirous from the ancient original stem.

Veronica Cantus
Oh! tell me brightly flaming eyes,
Whence in your little orbit lie
So many different trains of fire,
Expressing each a new desire;
Now with angry scorn you darken,
Now with tender languish sparkle,
And we who view the various mireor,
Sal at once both hope & terror.

An Epigram by Callimachus
Calliope the Muse of History says:
Great Elio I speak to history the story,
Euterpe teaches Minnes their silent show,
Melpomene speaks of her menes Jove:
Sophisca has the Hater's soft female displays,
And Erato gives hymns the Gods to praise,
Polymnia's skill inspires melodious strains,
Urania wise the starry course of plains,
And gay Thalia's Glase points out where they reign.

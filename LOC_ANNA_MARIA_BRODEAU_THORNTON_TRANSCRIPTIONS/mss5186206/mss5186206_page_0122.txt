We travelled far in bake 8 hours
Over mountains high & bridge still higher
To reach a Cave found out by Wier.
We traversed in this Cave so dreary
Till we were all quite stiff and weary.
First - after groping down a hole
Thro which our scarcely eyes a mole
Or pillar clothed all in white
Presents itself the first to sight
So like a Ghost & Strangler one
And named it from Solomon,
To Damn Room high & quite somerous
Large & dark & wet and poors
To ball room grand & certain Chamber
We groped by ways that very strange are.
Tony to the Mind presents
Stately groops & Monuments.
* Natural Bridge. † Name of the Discoverer
Crystal banks & fountains high
In distant Chambers please the Eye.
Still half a mile we crawl'd & climbed
And saw what never will quit the mind
Thee we may leave it leagues behind.
This Dungeon dismal black as night
Stuck me with horror & affright
It seemed like yawning gulphs below
Where Spirits damned are doomed to go.
† Wm. Madison's cave, & natural bridge, visited
by Wm. T. her Mother Husband in 1802 in
their return from N. Carolina. When
they also visited Wm Jefferson & Madison.
1279

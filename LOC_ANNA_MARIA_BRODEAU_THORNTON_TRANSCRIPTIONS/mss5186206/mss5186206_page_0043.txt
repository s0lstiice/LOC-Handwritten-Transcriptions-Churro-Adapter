Extract from a Poem entitled Humanity
To thee a land where cheering fane extends,
From her proud lofty spires reaches out
Where gentle drifts of the turning poles are seen,
Stood like Edens with eternal green;
Where every village glows with every wealth,
The shores are richer of the bounties health,
Where the sun gives ocean his thousand ray,
But never encircles with oppressive gloom,
Where female beauty sheds her parent flowers
And loveliest features, English grace of figure,
Darkens the soul's magic from the silent sea
Brings in on the winds of storms along the ships,
Where every scene is prodigal of charms
True courage handles of time day cares,
Where brave instances lift the conquering arm
And moral blessings heal their afflict harm;
Where, paid to virtue, Christian temples rise,
And millions, charity abounds poor,
Conducts the naked stranger to her home,
And grants the honest wanderer an home;
Where equal love, such general sympathy,
They have owed mercy on a captive foe.
O mother Britons! here is part your claim,
Most of your idol and justify her fame!
Tell her her growth, by sacred science led,
Is all the softening charities are bred;
Her second childhood, like the first, receives
From her the noblest wholesome lessons!
Tell, her palaces of merry rose,
Have not the events still longer the offspring;
Ever her kind Jefferson's generous protection laws,
A faithful champion in the poor man's cause,
How, even now, patient on public duties,

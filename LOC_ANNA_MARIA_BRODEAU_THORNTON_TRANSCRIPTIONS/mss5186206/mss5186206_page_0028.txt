(22)
Duei naturale talento,
Nò, a parlare mi sperava
Per cui iasone ruppe
De rischi che fiasco.
Dispietato cimento
Narra i padri religiosi,
Di uno scritto i segni
Mostra il guerriero cosi
Resta così contento
Schiavo che avi di pena,
La barbaria catena
Ché straventava un di.
Parlo, ma sol parlano,
Ne soltanto per precorro
Parlo, ma nulla iouro
Ché ha me parole fò:
Rerto, ma non dimanare
Ché approvi i miei detti,
Ché si tranquilla sci
Nel ragionar di me.
(23)
It is natural indeed prompts my tongue
And makes the story true,
As all mankind are fond to boast
Of dangers they have past.
The warrior thus, the combat der,
Receivest his bloody wars,
And shows his ancient arms.
Thus the glue slave, by prosperous fate,
Free from the servile chain,
Throws to each friend the gallting weight,
Which once he struggled with pain.
I speak, yet speaking, all my aim
Is but to use my words,
To speak, yet dare not if my words
With thee can credit find;
To speak, nor ask if my discourse
I can approve of thee,
Or whether thou with equal ease
I can take against me.

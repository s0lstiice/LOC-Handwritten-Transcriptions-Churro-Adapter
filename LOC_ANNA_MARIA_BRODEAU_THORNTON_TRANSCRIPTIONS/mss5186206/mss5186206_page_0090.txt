In Bali Quies
Shall sorrow for thy brow
Its darkened shades now fling
And hopes that cheer the new
Die in their early spring;
Shall pleasure at its birth
Take like the mist of even
Turn thee away from earth
Thy rest for thee in heaven.
If ever life shall seem
To thee a torture's wing,
And gladness cease to beam
Upon its clouded day,
If like the weary dove
On shoeless oar and driven
Raise thine eye above
There's rest for thee in Heaven.

But oh, of thine life's flowers

And gaily fleet the hours
Undim'd by earthly gloom
Still let not every thought
To this poor world be given
Nor always be forgot
Thy bitter rest in heaven.
When sickness pales thy cheek
And dim's thy loveliness eye
And futes low I weak
Sell of a time to die,
Sweet hope shall whisper then
Though thon from earth be gone
Thy's bless beyond thy ken
There's rest for thee in Heaven.

Buried in her husband's tomb
Her tender, fragile form he bore
In hopes her life to save
From an ingenuous distant shore
Wrenched by the Ocean's Wave
To this our warm & sunny land
To this our southern Sky
Trusting in our deffect & Island
The Mary would not die
* Another Mary, young & fair
Far from her place of birth
Has come this sacred Vault to share
And live on foreign earth
Lovely in form, in fortune bright
Her youth was in its glow
Her hopes had never felt a blight
Yet here she lies as low

But their more sweet & old flowers
Transplanted from their home
Perhaps they in their native flowers
Might have expressed such sorrow
Ah no -- so where lie their lies
A lovely nation one
That blen'd beneath our sunny Skies
But lies where it has blen'd
The -- never left her sheltered home
But grew & flourished there
Too blest to be included to sorrow
Far from her parents' care
Thomas affection's treasured Stone
The child of Hope & Love --
When earth's of bliss contented no more
Theought it from above
Delirium of earth & Hours of heaven
He here united rest
Short span of life to each was given
Oh fate, supremely blest!

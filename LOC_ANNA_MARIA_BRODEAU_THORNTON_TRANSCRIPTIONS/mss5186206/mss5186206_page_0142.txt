speech of Mr. Basil the British minister
addressed to the Company assembled at a small
garden to him & me & myself in the citizenry of
Washington & organization in the
matter from memory by a Mr. S. -
Lady's and Gentlemen.
I take this opportunity of expressing
Mr. Basil's & my thanks for this first instance
of the many proofs of kindness we have received
from the first moment of putting our feet on these
shores which we shall ever remember with gratitude
thoroughly pleasant. - May you ministers
enjoy every blessing, & Nationally every felicity that
you can desire.
1289

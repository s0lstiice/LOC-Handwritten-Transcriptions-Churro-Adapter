Dryden, when a boy at Westminster School, was
put with others to write a copy of verses on the miracle
of the conversion of prince into water. Being a great
bruant, he had not times to compose his Verses; &
when brought up, he had only made one line of
Latin & two of English:
"Virtus et omnibus compuncta judicio Deum."
The natural water aided by power divine
Behold its God & bless his Self to Wine
Which so pleased the Master that instead of being
angry he said, it was a passage of future greatness
& gave the smith a crown in this occasion.

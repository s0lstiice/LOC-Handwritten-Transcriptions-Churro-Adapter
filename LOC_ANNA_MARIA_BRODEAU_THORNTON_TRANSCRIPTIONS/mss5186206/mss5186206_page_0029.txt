Melastinio
To Mrs. Browne - June 18 1792
Is this my firm the tributary tax
That forms for from an insult of night belong
For thus they earn her early youth certain
Robbers on her progress, and her fame's gain
Pursued thru life nor death itself can part
The fond I draw from thy generous heart
Still more intense by every day that past
The mother loves most within the East
Is apt on the time the firm at home paid
And does in doubt the troops Boston lies
No
I leave a light incessant mail;
That's lost a heart sincere;-
I know not which wants comfort most
Or which has most to fear;
I'm sure a woman so fond of true,
Nice can never find
Angryph like her is quickly found
Falsely, faithless and unkind.
No further here with human kindness go
No other care thy friendship and bestow
For ever safe from injuries of time
The deads not now this ever changeful time
New mental ills can desolate no more
No winter rage nor injury tempted love
No ear independent of fortune love's
To clothe the present system future hour
But for thy glenings, memories & friends
Are all her own - & there that's near end
In these high orbs other mindful of thy care
The forms for thy sincere faithful prayer
And over the pupils of an anxious love
Becomes thy angel in the choir above. -
1176

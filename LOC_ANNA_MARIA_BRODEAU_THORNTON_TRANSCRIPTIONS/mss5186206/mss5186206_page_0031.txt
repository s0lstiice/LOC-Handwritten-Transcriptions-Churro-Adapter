thus persecuted, became more dear to his
mistress, and taking advantage of the night,
that pity, gratitude, & love, gave him, he
prevailed on the imprudent Anna Lovell
to quit England & go off with him to France.
Anna bribes a servant, escapes, arrives at the
sea shore where her lover is waiting for her,
and embarks with him. The anchor was imme-
dately raised, and they bent their sails towards
France; but very soon a frightful tempest arose
and cast them at a distance from it forever;
they were for thirteen days exposed to the
mercy of the waves: at last, on the fourteenth
they perceived land & discovered unknown
forests, they put the long boat out to sea,
& several sailors going into it soon returned
with happy news; the island appeared desert
but it offered them a safe & agreeable asylum;
Machum & his mistress descended into
it with their best friends, leaving the rest
of their people to take care of the vessel.
The country to them appeared enchanting;
they found it filled with birds, flowers, and
fruits of all kinds; advancing farther up
the country, they found a delightful meadow,
bordered with laurels, refreshed by a brook
which descended from the mountains into a
bay of gravel. A large tree which offered its
kindly shade to them, made them resolve

It is the mind that makes both good or ill,
That maketh riches or happiness, rich or poor:
For some that hath abundance at his will,
That hath not enough, but wants in greater store;
And other that hath little asks no more
But in that little is both rich & wise:
For wisdom is most riches, foolish therefore
They all which fortunes do by words deceive;
Since each unto himself his life may patronize

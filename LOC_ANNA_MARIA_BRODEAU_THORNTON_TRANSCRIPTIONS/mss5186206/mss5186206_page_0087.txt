One part, one little part, one timely grain
Thro' the dark medium of life's feverish Delam
Yet dare arrange the whole stupendous plan
If but that little part, ingenuous seem.
Now is that part perhaps what makes them
Ost from apparent ill our blessings rise.
Oh then renounce that impious Self-interest
That aims to have the secrets of the Skies.
For them art but of dust -- be humble & be wise.
The affluence of discontent I doubt repel
Dust even at noon time is our mortal sphere
But let us hope -- to doubt is to rebel --
Let us expect in hope -- that all shall yet be well.
"Let them deplore their Doom
Whose hope still prevails in this dark enjoyment
But lofty souls, who look beyond the tomb
Can smile at fate, I wonder how they remain
Shall spring to their suns eternal bed?
Soon shall the orient with new luster burn,
And spring shall soon her vital influence shed,
Again allure the grove, again awaken the mind.
Shall I be left abandoned in the Dust
When fate retreating lets the flower prime?
Shall nature's Voice to man alone appeal,
Bid him the dream'd to Perish, hope to live --
But for this fair Victor oft mind's Steine
With disappointment, penury & Pain?
No, Heaven's immortal Spring shall yet arrive
And Man's majestic beauty bloom again,
Bright than the eternal fire of Love's triumphant light.
True dignity is his, whose tranquil Mind
Victoria has paid about the things below,
Who, every hope & fear to Heaven resigned
Thanks, not the fortune aim her deadliest Shot."
The Minstrel
1235

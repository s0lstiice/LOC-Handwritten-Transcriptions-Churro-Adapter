List! Is the drage of a soul that is fled
And to the prison in solemn array
Moves slowly along to the place of the Dead
With the relics of me that is passing away
But why should I weep? perhaps it is one
Who has outlived each tie that had bound him;
And care worn & aged, broken & low,
He says that his feeble existence was thus
To escape from the solitude round him.
Oh no! To the house of Maria -- who died
In youth with her relatives near her;
With husband & mother & friends by her side
And all that to life could endear her
Beloved & loving, with a youthful heart,
How awful is the mandate to depart!
But yesterday I saw her, overly red
The smile of love & joy upon her brow,

The Lily garland twined around her head
Is still pale as her poor dying eye
And now they bear her in funeral gloom
To her old & heartless mansion -- to the tomb!
But to heranguished friends, not her Iweep,
The centuries know of joys beyond the tomb;
And she will awaken from her transient sleep
To meet in other worlds, a brighter Throne!

Mr Linn died the 1st Octob. 1832 of the cholera --
The Stocks an emblem of time's penalty,
Because when age has seized & made his dim
United for flight the grateful young one takes
The mother on his back provides her food
Separating thus her tender care of him,
He leaves fit to fly.

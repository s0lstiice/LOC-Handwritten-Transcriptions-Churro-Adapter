Victories made at sea in a Heavy Gale
Hopping the man who, safe on shore,
Nocturnal at home, his evening fire;
Unmolested he hears the tempests roar,
That on the blasted groves expire:
Mast or nothing doubly falls
Our feeble barque must bear them all.
Nocturnal to their homes the birds return,
The signal with his hollow tree:
Velvet in their shaded caverns melt,
He, all are lost but withstood me —
For whom I am a stranger to repair
Naval the unattested ocean breeze
While o'er the earth abyss we roam,
I choose whether the pilot's way
We saw the sun descend in gloom
No more to see his rising day
But bury'd low, by far too deep,
On coral beds, impaled, sleep!
(27)
But a strange monster stands
In that where death permits no day —
No charters have we to mark that land,
No compass to direct that way —
What peril shall explain that realm,
And now Columbus seize the helm.
While death and darkness both surround,
And tempests rage with loud chief power,
Of friendship's voice I hear no sound,
No to this dreadful hour —
What friendship can in tempests be,
What comfort in this angry sea.
The barque, accustomed to obey,
To move the trembling pilot's guide,
Now she gives her treacherous way,
While mountains burst on either side —
This, still, experience both must tell,
And ruin is the lot of all.
1182
Cap. Thomson

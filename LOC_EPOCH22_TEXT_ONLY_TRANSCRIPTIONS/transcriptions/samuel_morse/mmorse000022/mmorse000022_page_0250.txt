Washington April 12th 1847
Friend there yours is duly received and I
suppose I must go to work and clear up my
character about the Platinum, and what I said
to Mr Chilton. Well I will state what I did say
to Mr C and as to what he understood me to say
will have for himself to say. Last winter while
I was at work at Brooks getting up the registers &
Mr C came in one day and among other things
told him about platinum. I told him that the
southern Co would eventually make some 4 hundred
dolls worth of that article and injured his price for
it which was something over $5.50 I do not now
reckless, I told him I had purchased some for
$8 per oz with an additional charge of 12% for service
for getting it rolled, -- He positively told me he would
let me have it at the same price. -- This is all
there is about it. and the same thing I told Mr
Kent, -- Now I have no wish to get an article for
less than its value and with Mr Kendall concurrence
I am authorized to pay to Mr Kent to furnish
the platinum nearly as thick as the small
piece in the paper which Mr Kendall gave you
and will give him $5.50 per oz -- The Glass,
oils, &c have come to hand and are very
good. The last register has also arrived, -- We
have a new office. It is the room on the opposite
side of the passage with three windows in front

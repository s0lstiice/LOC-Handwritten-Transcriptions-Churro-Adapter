I wish much that you would see Mr. Bigelow he is a gentleman of the highest character, intimately acquainted with
the most influential men in Canada & in Britain, and if
any man can secure in any remuneration from Britain he
can. He thinks he could have made better terms than those
proposed by Welles & Livingston, You have inadvertently
placed complicated matters in that quarter, and I must look to
with Mr. Bigelow you to put them right. I have every disposition to favor

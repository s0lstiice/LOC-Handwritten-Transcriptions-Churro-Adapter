Have you indeed succeeded in realizing true pecuniary advantage from the Telegraph? I am glad to be disappointed. You cannot need my suggestions that your attention must be strongly turned to two points—the securing the wires against the attacks of parties interested in intercepting information; and the importance of securing accurate and authentic information through them. I am aware of your embarrassments in these matters. Yet I know not why very heavy penalties might not be attached by our lawmakers to this crime, and I know not but you must require the names of responsible parties for the trial of all important information in critical times.

I have actually realized in my first dividend on one line about 1400 dollars, and
with this I cannot purchase a house, but time will perhaps enable me to do so,
if it is well that I should have one. I think if probable that Jacob Big-
elow Esq. of Washington will soon visit England for the purpose of getting a
special act granting me a Patent. My case is certainly a strong one, for there
was not a shadow of justice in refusing my application in 1838. Every system
that I have seen in hand ofad Europe is fair inferior to mine, and totally
different. I give you a list of lines of my Telegraph in North America;
for the Canadas are all alive in regard to it, and they are putting up

4 If you should personally attend Houn's exhibition, let me request of you
for your own satisfaction to give him the following test.
1. Let him print as I said before the Alphabet in its reversed order.
2. Give him the following figures series of letters to print for you
ten times A ten times B ten times C ten times, and so
on through the Alphabet thus A.A.A.A.A.A.A.A.A.A.A., B.B.B., etc. also print him in numerals thus
1,000,000,000
111,111,111
8,888,888,888.
Write down the sentences, and time them all
by your watch.
In closing I would say I read your articles on the
purchase by the Govt. of the Telegraph by the Govt. I thank
you for the kind personal attentions in it, and I would only
say that I have made no effort to give it to them in accord-
ance with my early promises because I have been
so busy with my family and other business that I have
not had time to make any offer to sell it to them in accord-
ance with my early promises. I have made in all my contracts with companies, I am
personally indifferent as to the result, if they purchase it
well, if they do not, well.
Believe me truly as ever with the highest respect between
you and I ever
S B M.

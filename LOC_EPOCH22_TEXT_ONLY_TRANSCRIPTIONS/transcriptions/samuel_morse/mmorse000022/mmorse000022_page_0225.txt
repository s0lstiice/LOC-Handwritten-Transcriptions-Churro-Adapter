I am fully satisfied we have lost Mr Wood
unfairly & hence do not feel very pleasant
about it. I however hope to be in a
situation soon when such trifles will
not give me so much trouble as it present
it is extremely vexation to be arraigned
with men who do not feel that they in any
other obligation existing between parties but to
take every possible advantage in business.
I pray be mistaken in my conclusions
but I think your experience will teach you
that there is at least some ground for this
principle, still I may be in the wrong
in forming such opinions.
Respectfully yours
H. Houston

We have just had a lawsuit in Phila. before Judge
Kane; we applied for an injunction, to stay irregular
and injurious proceedings on the part of Western (Pitts-
burg, & Cincinnati Company,) and our application
has been refused on technical grounds. I know
not what will be the issue. I am trying to have matters
compromised but do not know if it can be done, and we
may have to contest it in law. Our application was in
court of equity. — A movement of Smith was the cause of all!
I called on Mr. WM. Beeckman last evening. I
found them well and in good spirits, although you are
perhaps aware that, he has met with losses, which
make it necessary for him to despoil of his house
in Wash. Place and procure a humble residence.—
All well at Richard's, he will write and let you
know how Obewer matters go. I am glad to have
had it in my power to loan him $3000 at 6 percent interest to pay off
a sum, which was called for. We have great cause
of gratitude for the prosperity which seems to be
flowing in upon us. Let us consult how we can
use it for the glory of Him who purchased all our
blessings for us by his own sufferings.

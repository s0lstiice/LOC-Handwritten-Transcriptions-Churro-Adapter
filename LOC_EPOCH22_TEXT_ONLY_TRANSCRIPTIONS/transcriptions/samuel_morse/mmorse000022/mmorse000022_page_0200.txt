Confidential
Dear Sir,
Yours of the 15 inst. is hand, contents
received.
I see, by the reading of it, that you think
all is fair, as to Mr. Wood's leaving. -- Allow me to
give you one or two Extracts from his two notes which I
received at two different times on Sunday, without
going much into detail, as my time is very much
overfilled at present.
Mr. Wood says "I have this morning received
"by mail wholly unsought for and unexpectedly to
"myself a proposition for my services" so much for
that fact.
In a personal Conversation with Mr. Wood he
gave me to understand that he had been a constant
protest going on for some weeks; he parted, and
after I had gone to bed, about 11 p.m. a Boy brought
a letter from Mr. Wood, which I got up and read,
in which amongst other things, he remarks "L.O.W.
"named 3 persons as suitors and one of them name
"we mine" (Wood).
Now I would ask any man of common sense
why L.O.W have done this, unless it is because they
have more interest in Canada than in the New York
A & Puff Telegraph; and here allow me to remark,

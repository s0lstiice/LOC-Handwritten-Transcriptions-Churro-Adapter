Washington April 19th 1847.
Friend More
Inclosed you will find a
bill of lading of the picture of your daughter
amounting to $10. The reason of the enormous
charge is in consequence of it being put in the cabin
instead of the hold of the vessel. It was shipped by
Mr Hight and had to be put immediately on board
the vessel which was full of freight and the picture
could not be put in the hold. Also enclosed you
will find Mr Hight bill of $4.50 which I have
paid -- you will please add it to the amount to
be paid by Lexington & Wells and pay the whole
to Mr Stokes -- on Wednesday I expect to go to
Fredenburg and start that Station. Tomorrow
we open at Gorge Town. Alexandrias office is open &
doing well.
Mr Rogers was here last week and could not
help asking him why he passed such a singular
course of conduct toward the Magnetic Telegraph
interest. He evinced considerable equivocation and
at last stated that you had cast him overboard and
had not acted in his behalf as he thought you should
I asked him what he had expected you to do -- this he
could not answer satisfactorily -- to that it appeared
to me that he had conjured up his imagination to think
that he had a right to expect you would run yourself

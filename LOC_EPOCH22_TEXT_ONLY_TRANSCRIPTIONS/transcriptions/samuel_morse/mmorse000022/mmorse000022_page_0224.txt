Telegraph Office, St Louis March 25/14
S. L. B. Missouri
To Sir
England I have your
Letter for 1034 hrs. Stock & time forward and the
other agreeable to your request to Mr Robinson
Oni want as to the position of your father's
station which Robinson occupies,
I am of the opinion he ought to be
a Spuron who is qualified to do any part
of the business of the office, in case of sickness
or accident of any kind with the operator
he should be ready to take hold of the matter
and use it or any other branch of the business
at the same time should be intelligent, honest
& accommodating to customers & above suspicion
as to his integrity, you will say rather a
difficult person to get which is so,
Thee is a young man by the name of
Chelton recommend very strongly by Mr Wood
who if he is what he expects him to be is
just a man as you want,
I am in hopes we can get along in
the office for a few days until the river opens
when I will come down & converse with
you about this business.

Mr Mordes claim is for a system of dots and lines, any use of
dots and lines not coming within the Mr Mordes system of
dots and lines would not be an infringement of his patent.
Whether a specific case would be an infringement cannot be
determined till the case is stated.
Second. Would it be an infringement of Mr Mordes Patent to
make signs consisting of dots and lines by the action power of
magnetism different from his namely by the deflective power of
magnetism applied to press the paper by the use of the needle
or by de-magnetizing a permanent magnet? To answer to
this inquiry I remark that if Mr Mordes first claim is
good, he holds the the exclusive right to the power of Electro
magnetism, whether deflective or negative when applied to
machinery to mark or imprint a system of dots and lines
on paper or other like substance for telegraphic purposes.
But I have herebefore said I thought that claim too broad, I
do not think that either of the instances supposed of deflective
or negative power, as stated, would be or could be,
awarding to the settled principles of Law considered
as an infringement of Mr Mordes Patent, but as there is
not any adjudicated case within my knowledge
directly in point, and as this is a point not very clear
to my own mind I speak with some hesitation, not
knowing exactly how it might strike the mind of a Judge.

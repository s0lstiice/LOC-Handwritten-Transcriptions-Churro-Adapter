the harmony of the lines.
I trust you will as soon as possible bring Smith to
account to us for our property in the Boston line. We
are blamed for suffering him to go on without any co
trol, yet I know not how I am to do it.
Mr. Miles spoke to me on the subject of an additional
clause, or amendment to the Patent law now before
Patent Comm'te in regard to evidence of invention. I a
think that Comm'te could be stirred up, so that some act
can be had upon it this session. It is of great, very great
importance to us.
Believe me though in haste
With sincere respect
Y. M. B. Servt

Reply N.Y. March 9th 1847
Oct. 17 of 27 ult. I have this moment received and I
have only time to say in reply that for a consideration given
me by Messrs. Welles & Livingston they have a right to connect
the lines which they expect or cause to be constructed in Canada
with the lines in the U.S. State, but I do not find in the
content that they have the sole right, If my construction
of the instrument is correct any other party may build lines
and use my instruments by negotiating terms with the latitude
but unless they pay up, the same consideration or negotiate
with us satisfactory terms no such lines will be permitted
to connect with the U.S. State lines.
In regard to protection from atmosphere, Electri. I have
given plan to Mr. Livingston which is attached to our
N.Y. & N.Buff. line at the U.S. Office in connection with
the Reigl magnet. I think it will be effectual in protecting
the instruments.
I Porter Jr

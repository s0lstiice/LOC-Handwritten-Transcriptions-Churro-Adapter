I have so much business of one sort and
another on hand that I find it difficult
to get along, and yet I have now nothing
to support my family that I know of -- I am
doing for the Southern Co with what imme
neration I know not -- what I am to do or what
is to be done is still future, with such instant
but Telegraph lines as that between Baltimore
and New York & New York & Boston, my
income from dividends will not buy my
annual firewood. -- I sometimes feel as
though I would sell out our patent
right is wasted away by the manner of
building the lines and still the same plan
me adhered to on the southern route. Tom
Lick and Harry Conrath and no one to
overlook them who has an interest in the
business -- I do hope things will go better --
our experience is very precious. I do not wish to
complain however -- This world and its people
must have its way & I am resolved not to fret
and pain myself on their account
Ever your esteemed friend
100461 Alfred Bail

You say we ought to raise operator's wages; I
intend, to give more than any other line
in the United States, and more than any of them
ever got for their services, and if we had not
snakes in the grass they would all be satisfied.
I employed Mr. Backus at Syracuse and paid
him at the rate of $500 a year, with the understanding
that if all was right he was to be paid $100 more
at the end of the year. He was highly recommended
by a gentleman in New York; I found him unfit to
do as he had undertaken to do, sufficient his place
with a young man for little over half the salary,
and he is one of the best we have on the line.
You call my attention to what was said at
one of our meetings about high salaries; allow me
to remind you of some part of the conversation,
which was that "Girl or Women" could manage
most of our Side Office.

I have put up three of the new instruments
in the room and I think the present a good
appearance -- They work well and without
difficulty. I saw Mr Bigelow a few days
since. I think it was last Friday and he told
me the papers you spoke of had not come to
hand, I have just been over to see him but
he is not in his office. -- I will see him again
as soon as possible and write you. I am glad
Livingston & Wells have agreed to what is just &
right. The Telegraph to Alexandria is in
operation and expect to open the office at
Redenbushburg this week. England I send you
a letter a few days since and as the postage
was not paid I have not nor will not
answer it. I send it to you to do with as
you think best, -- I presume that Mr Rogers
must be put down as a hypocrite from what
Mr Kendall has told me, and which you have
probably heard in New York. I refer to his
language to Mr Joy Lloyd our agent at New
Orleans. Poor Man. How shortsighted to act
in the way he has. -- He must certainly go by
the board --
Dr Galt & Dr Page are both well.

Washington Wd. 1797
Dear Sir:
Before leaving this morning for
New Albany, I wish to say a word
in regard to you intended improvement
for curing under water with the wire-
line in a place, the "Riglets",
a channel from 400 to 600 yards
wide, where Lake Ponchartrain empties
into Lake Borgne & where Settoway will
make you go get high fall -- I fear
some trouble in curing there in
the old mode, but if within a
few days you communicate to me
that you experience, of which the reader
may speak to me, for curing under
water long distances, has succeeded,
or troubles all vanish -- We will at
once, to lay out the wire for Mobile-
to N.O. to begin the work -- If we
are compelled to go round the Lake
a totally different canal is necessary
for that by which we can erect
the Riglets ("Regliges") -- You will see a line
of canal, can Ed. Steen & Bro. New Albany.
100464
It bears testimony to
W. H. Lloyd

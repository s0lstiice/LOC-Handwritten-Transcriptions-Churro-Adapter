I dream of a house, but no bird, I want to cage my own hornbills first
at least. We shall give you a hearty welcome you may be sure, I have floating
schemes of pleasant schemes for good with you, and want you here to plan with me.
Mr & Mrs Beeckman were well and in good spirits when I saw them
a few days ago. Give much love to Catherine, Elizabeth and the children
from me and from all.
S. D. Morse Esq

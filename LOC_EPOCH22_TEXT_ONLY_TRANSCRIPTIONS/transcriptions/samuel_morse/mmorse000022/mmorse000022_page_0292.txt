Dear Sir
Since writing you under date of 9th clip. 21, in answer to
your of 17th from Wash. I presume that Mr. Cleveland who
take charge of my battery at Baltimore has put down part
of the circuit from Phil. to Balt., beneath the water at
one of the draw bridges with success. It needed not this
experiment however to show that, if wire is properly insulated
in a pipe it will make a practicable part of the circuit,
for the Office of the City, in this city had part of its
circuit beneath the East River at Hellgate for months in
perfect order and in constant use. So that if, as I said
in my letter, you can place your wire well insulated in a
sound pipe beneath the water at the Regolits, so deep
in the mud as to avoid the anchor of vessels which may
anchor in the passage, it will be the preferable mode.
Of the practicability of thus placing it, you can better
judge as I know not the character of the pass at
the Regolits.
I have lent with respect
Yours
W.B. Strong
Ed. Stevens & Thos. Norlans
F.D. Moser

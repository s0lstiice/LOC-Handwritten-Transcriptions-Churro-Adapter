If it comes in your way I should be glad if you could send
me immediately some account of the operations of letter printing
Telegraphs, if any are in use. Houses & their Associates are
making most strenuous efforts to interfere, and embarrass me
by playing on the ignorance of the public, and the natural tim-
idity of Capitalists. I shall probably have to lay the law
upon him, and make an example, before my patent is
confirmed in the minds of the public. It is the course
I am told of every substantial Patent. It has to undergo
the ordeal of one trial in the Courts. As I said before I
am ready for them. But to make assurance doubly sure
I should be glad to have such accounts of the failure of
Common letter printing Telegraphs in Europe, as would con-
firm the timid who have embarked in my Patent, and
alarm the timid who think embarking in Houses, and
I believe that a true account of the operation of Bain's
will furnish me with that desideratum. You know how
much more weight is given to any opinion from abroad than
from even the highest sources at home. If, therefore, I can pub-
lish "An extract from a highly respectable Scientific Gentle-
man in England," it will tend to dispel effectually the delu-
sion on this subject.

P.S. I have this moment received your parcel by
D: Baird for which receive my thanks, I have had
from my eyes even the Bain pamphlet. One would suppose
that no such being was in existence as myself, or
any Telegraph in America. — Sixty letters per minute
is the limit it seems of their ability. — I send you a
strip of 120 p.c. minute; the same operator has since
imprinted 137. — I am preparing for N.Y. Historical
Society Com. appointed for the purpose, a history of
invention, which may be printed perhaps in the course
of the summer. — I expect no justice abroad at present
Mr. Munson has just called in. He will write
by next Steamer. Map of U. States finished.—

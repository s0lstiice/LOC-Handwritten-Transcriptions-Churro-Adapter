Philad. May 5. 47.
Prof. Morse New York:
Since it, on a similar occasion
you referred me to your Agent about
the matter, I have notified the Honr.
Amos Kendall, under date of April
24. from Albany, that a meeting
of all interested in the Atlanta
Ohio line will be held, for the purpose
of discussing the subject, at the American
Hotel, Philadelphia, on Friday, May
7, at 4 p.m.
Respectfully yours
Henry O. Kelly
Superintend.

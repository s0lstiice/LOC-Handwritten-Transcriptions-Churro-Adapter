Forty January 9th 1847.
Genl
Allow me to intimate upon your time and by you
will give me as close an estimate as you can on the following
questions
First. What amt. of Battery will probably be required to
make a line of Iron Wire 400 Miles long. The Wire being
350 to to the Mile with six Stations Divisions of
Second. What size Telegraph will be required for such Wire
& the probable cost of such case
Thus
of whom and at what price can I contract for the
military amt. of Wire will Timed (or Tubanized as the term
is used generally) to prevent oxidation
I would not have troubled you with these questions had
I known any other person who could have given me
accurate answers on which I could depend immediately
as I am well aware that every moment of your time is
employed in a most Valuable manner but I trust you
will consider this of importance to me in making any
imposition on the Canada Line from Montcalt to Toronto
I make to day a letter from the Secretary of the Company
deceiving me to make inspection for lanterns before the 20th this
month and to make a telegraphic statement to you
100325 Knowledge possible
I am respectfully Yours as ever
Respectfully Yours
1847
Brig

New Haven Feb 4 1847
My dear Sir
I may remember to have
seen a remarkably able article in the March
No of the J.S. p. 230. On the Laws of United
Auction in Metals, by Mr J H Lane, then an
undergraduate in Yale College. This article drew
out a highly gratifying letter from Dr Hazarday of
London, to the author. considering entirely in the court
cup of his examination. The author of this paper
is now teaching at Cambridge U. for 7000. in years.
and his friends here in view of his peculiar
merits and ability as well as suitableness for
the place have concurred in recommending him
to Mr Burke for the post of assistant Ex ch
of the Patent Office. We guarantee his moral
& personal worth, you may safely leave a line of
recommendation support to Mr Burke in his
Scientific mechanical character. I think it is very
materially in securing him the place. I hope it

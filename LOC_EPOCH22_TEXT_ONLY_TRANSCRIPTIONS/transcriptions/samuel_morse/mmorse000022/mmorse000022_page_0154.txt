I am digging at Mr Kendall every day to
clear up my account and I must say he
does not take that interest in affording me
which I could wish. My family, his infirm
health and the precarious of life all demand
that I should have my business all straight,
and I shall loose my patience by & bye if
he does not help me. I do not wish to find fault
but I am extremely anxious and worried to under-
stand my account – I hope you reach
home safely and find all well. Ponies cer-
tainty – I wish I could take a ride with them
this fine day. My best regards to Mrs Morris

I claim
1st If two batteries of current of different kind and power, each battery working its own receiving magnet, the public working together with one, the next strongest working with the first and the next working with the second, and so on, in any series, thus, adapting various motions to various mechanism through one of the same circuit of conductors.
2. I claim the mode of applying the above said arrangement of batteries to the impressing of the common letter of the Alphabet or any other desired character at a distance
3. I claim the arrangement of parts in the key, register or correspondence respectively in the Keyed instrument by which the two batteries are made to operate on their appropriate mechanism & at the proper time
1st There is this effect observable in the operation of the magnet at a distance both near and at a distance to wit. The strength of the electric current, from which the attraction of the magnet is extended (within certain limits) in proportion to the strength of the battery. The application of this fact to operate the mechanism at a distance by two or more batteries
The nature of my invention consists in supplying a difference of two or more currents from any number of batteries, to operate mechanism, at a distance, through the same simple circuit, to produce various mechanical results.
* note also that two circuits may be applied to the operation of my mechanism.

Washington March 13th 1847
Friend Moore
Years of the 10th is received.
Enclosed I send you Prof Gales in my own order
for the money held by Livingston & Wells for us
Please pay as requested in my previous letter the amt
states to Mr Frost, then take $9.00 the amt paid
for the "deepthing tool"; then the amt which you
stated to me. I should pay you to make all straight
in regard to the New York & Buffalo 21 Cts stock
and will thank you for the various receipts and
the remaining amt in New York funds. One half
of the whole amount on my one eighth belongs
to my brother, which please pay to him and take
his receipt for the same.
To Dr Sage I have shonen your letter and say all
very well in regard to the magnet, but you have offered
no proof that Prof Dana lectured in 1826, which is
still wanting. That I presume you can easily get from
words in the newspapers or from persons connected with
the building where the lecture took place, or persons present
at the lecture.
When Livingston & Wells can if they choose keep

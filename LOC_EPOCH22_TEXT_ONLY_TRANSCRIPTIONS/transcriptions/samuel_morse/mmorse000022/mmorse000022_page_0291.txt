Prof. S. J. B. Moses
39th Street, New York
11.9.
1861
Dear Sirs:
It is with deep regret that I have to inform you that the
Board of Directors of the New York City Board of Health
have decided to remove me from the office of Medical
Superintendent of the Sanitary District. I have served
in that office for over five years and have been
highly commended for my services. I am now
engaged in the office of Dr. J. B. Moses, Surgeon
General, U. S. Army, Washington, D. C.
Very truly yours,
Samuel C. H. Smith
Medical Superintendent
New York City Board of Health

Claim
What I claim as of my own invention and improvement and which
has been by letter patent is
The use of a single circuit of conductors for the marking of
telegraphic signals, letters, words or sentences, by means of the
decomposing effects of electricity upon any known salts that have
a mark as the result of the decomposition, upon paper, cloth,
metal or other convenient material
I also claim the invention of the machinery as herein described
for the purpose of applying the effect above decomposing colors
or bleaching effects of electricity acting upon any known salts
as herein before described.

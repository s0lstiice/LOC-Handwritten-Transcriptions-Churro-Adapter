Professor T. B. Morse
New York
Feb 20th
1840
My dear Sir
I am much obliged to you for your letter of the 18th ult. and for the communication from Professor Morse. I am sorry to say that I have not yet received any of the papers which you mention. I am now in the midst of a very important work, and I have not had time to write to you. I will do so as soon as I can.
Yours very truly
D. A. Tucker
Mr. President

Louis goes to Jersey City office and I am
glad he has got the situation he is pleased
with it, but his mother says he will
be more on his own resources and his own
adviser together with yourself. I think
he still thinks you his friend and well
wisher, and trust he may advise with you
and if he will follow your advice he
will not go astray.
I presume Mr Rogers who I believe has
gone to New York will call upon you.
Mr Bigelow called several days since
for Boston -- I understood him to say a few
days since that he had received the papers.
I should be glad to see you in Washington
Page & Gale are well. I see them occasionally
Mr Kendall is also about and well.
I hope to be on in New York in a week or
so -- I expect to take my family north &
cannot get along with them here with $600
I remain yours now and ever
Alfred Baird

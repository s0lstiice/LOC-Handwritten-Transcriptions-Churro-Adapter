Baltimore May 11. 1847
Dear Sir,
I return you my thanks for your
note of the 3d. instant, and for the copies of the
interesting publications accompanying it, which
I only received after my return from a visit to
Wheeling. Most sincerely thank you for your remembrance
of the conduct of my son at West Point. I have many
too many personal chances in the process of the war.
A few one or in two have been already among
the wounded, the latter dangerously, and their
return to service has been a great source of
satisfaction to me. I am sorry to hear of the death of
the Rev. Mr. H. H. Smith. I am sorry to hear of the
death of Mr. H. H. Smith. I am sorry to hear of the
death of Mr. H. H. Smith.</Line

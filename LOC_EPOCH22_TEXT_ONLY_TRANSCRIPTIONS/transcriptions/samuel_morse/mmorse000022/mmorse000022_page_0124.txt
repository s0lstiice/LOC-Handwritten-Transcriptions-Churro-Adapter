Washington Feb. 25th 1844
Dear Sir.
Few days ago I saw in one of the German papers that
there is an electromagnetic Telegraph between Vienna & Brünn
about 10 miles in operation, I wrote to Mr. Miles to ascertain
or what plan;- I think they try all kind and must
little, à la fine upon yours.-
I saw this morning Dr. Gates of the Patent office on
the subject of Coronation, we came upon the cotton manufactures - cotton
weaving - & improved manufactures in general - (for all class we will
have patents), and I asked the Doctor if he is not acquainted
with any body in New York to take hold of these three
patents etc. he mentioned Mr. Stanford, and referred me to you
in regard to Mr. Danfords standing, means etc. etc. - Should
you think that Mr. D. has the means (say $5000) to
engage in this business, which must beyond all doubt, succeed,
plan speak to him directly & let me know what he intends
to do.- Should Mr. D wish to engage in it, we should be
glad to see him here, to initiate him of the mode of means
feeling them etc. etc.
We would give him the State of New York & New Jersey.

In relation to my invention of the Telegraph, the facts are
mostly given in Mr. Vails larger work entitled the American Electric
Magnetic Telegraph published in Philadelphia by Lea & Blanchard.
Mr. Vail was only Assistant Superintendent of the Government line, and
I gave him the many of the materials for his work. I am at present
collecting some more minute details of the invention, at the request
of the Committee of the NY Historical Society, who wish to preserve
them, and who will report on the subject. When finished
I will take pleasure in forwarding you a copy.

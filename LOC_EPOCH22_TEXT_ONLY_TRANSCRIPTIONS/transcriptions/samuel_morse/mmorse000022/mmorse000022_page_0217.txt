Forest Home
near Portland Me
March 22d 1847
Prof. Morse
Dr. Sir
In my return here last
evening I found your letter of yesterday and have hastened to
transcribe a copy of the original draft of your letter to the Atty-
General, as in my own handwriting and your enticements,
and forward it herewith. I am confident it is a precise copy
of that handed to Sir John Campbell
This letter states the ground of refusal, the same as
stated by you to me at the close of each interview you
had with him -- viz: -- apparent publication of your invention
I know not what mode is adopted in the English
practice of publishing the decisions made on applications for
patents -- But your application was made in the requisite form,
and I presume is still on the files of the proper department
there, and perhaps the Atty's General decision endorsed on the
back of it
My recollection is, that after this letter was submitted,

refusal of the Patent licensed was adhered to by the Attorney
General, and on learning this, you received back from him
the letter -- But there were two or more special exhibitions

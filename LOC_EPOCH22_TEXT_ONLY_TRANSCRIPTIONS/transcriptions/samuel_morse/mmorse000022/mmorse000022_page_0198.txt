Hobart, Delaware County New York
March 16 1847
Dear Sir,
I am personally acquainted with you, having seen you many times
at Washington, although I presume, you will not recognize me by name.
My object in writing is, to call your attention to the enclosed
Circular. I desire to give a brief outline of your history in the work,
more especially with regard to your association with the "telegraph."
If your modesty will permit you, I should feel obliged by you forwarding me, at
your convenience, such particulars of your history as you may deem interesting,
together with the dates etc. You having brought the Telegraph into successful
operation under your personal supervision, worthy of notice in a work of this kind,
It is by no means expected that every "subject" will subscribe to the work;
but shall you desire so to do, please state the fact
Have written your telegraphs as you will do improved.
Respectfully yours
William Hunt.

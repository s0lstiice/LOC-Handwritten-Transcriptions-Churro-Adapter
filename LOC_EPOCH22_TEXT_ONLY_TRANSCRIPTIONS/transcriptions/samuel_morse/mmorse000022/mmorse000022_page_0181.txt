Howard's Hotel N.Y. March 14, 1847 1 P.M.
I have just this moment
Received a communication from Mr Wood which I
have you busy of, remarking at the same time
that there is something about it which I cannot
comprehend, not having seen him yet.
I see Mr Wood at 10 A.M. to the office tomorrow
to me about going away. I left Mr Livingston
off in a few moments before & he requested
me to ask Mr Wood to call & see him a few
moments which I did & about 1/2 past 12, received
the communication of which I have you busy.
I have this evening seen Mr Wood.
he is engaged to the Montcal Co. and
wishes to go away immediately which
places me in a very bad situation about
the N.Y. office.
More particularly as he has always
obeyed to my bidding a man here as I
had caught the train home, to certify him
by letting his brother remain, and now
I presume his brother was to with him
as he will not dare trust him to stay in
the office with any one I may leave him.

New York March 22. 1847
To Sir
I enclose you the Certificate for
50 Shares of Stock endorsed as you requests
assigning 12 shares to W. Kendall. The re-
mainder 38 shares together with the 65 shares
of the Certificate you have retained making
in all One hundred & three (103) shares
you will please issue in one certificate
and send me.
Respectfully
Yours
Geo P. Smith
Theo S. Haxton Esq
Purk N.Y. Alb. Buff.
may Tel. be
Utica.

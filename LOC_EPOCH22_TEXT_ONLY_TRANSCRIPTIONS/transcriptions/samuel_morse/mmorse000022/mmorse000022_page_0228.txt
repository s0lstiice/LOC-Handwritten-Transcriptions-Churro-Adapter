I do love that dear boy Charley very much, and long to see
him with his big words! I wish he could be placed early, while
impressions are easily and indelibly made, in such a moral at
mosphere that his training may be of the right kind. New Haven
is the place, and were he to be educated there, it would be a
strong inducement for me to go there myself. We will talk
of this with Edward when we meet. Tell Edward to make
money as fast as he honestly can, economize it and bring it
here, and turn Yankee. He will be welcomed among us.
Hope that boy, my boy, Grandpapa's boy, and say to
him that Grandpapa will have something for him
when he comes here next summer.
Give much love to Edward, and all St. Thomas Porto Rico
friends, and regards to Mary.
Your affectionate father as ever
Sam B. Moser.
Man all in excellent health.

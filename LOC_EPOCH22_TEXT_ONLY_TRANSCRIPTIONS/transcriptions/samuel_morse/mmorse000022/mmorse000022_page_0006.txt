Springfield Dec 12th 1846
Dear Sir
Having an engagement to
lecture before a Literary Association of young
men (called the Young Men's Institute)
in this place, and in several other places in this
region, upon the subject of the Magnetic Telegraph
I am desirous of getting in possession of as many
interesting and useful facts relating to the
subject as possible; and as I know of no better
source to which to apply for such information,
I take the liberty of addressing you.
I have been engaged in lecturing
upon Electricity and kindred subjects, more or
less, for ten years past; but within two years
have lectured more particularly upon the Magnetic Telegraph. Two years ago I gave three
lectures before the B.M. Institute of this
place, upon the latter subject, and they in desire
ous, with other Associations in this vicinity,
of having this same subject presented to them
again -- which I have consented to do.
To illustrate the subject I have
two recording Instruments, one of which I
make myself; (and I think it works as neatly
as any, that I have ever seen) the other is like
those which have been in use on the New York
& Buffalo line, which I have remodeled so
that it works very well indeed. -- So that with

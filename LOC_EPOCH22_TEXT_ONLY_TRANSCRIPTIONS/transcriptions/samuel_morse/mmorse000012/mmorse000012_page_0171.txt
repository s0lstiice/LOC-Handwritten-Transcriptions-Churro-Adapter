I found Mr. Patterson quite unwell
on my return. We have but just released him from
the house -- he will have much of his mind to
pass the winter very quiet at home -- I cannot
see that Washington has injured them at all for
domestic life -- what with work, sleighing, and now
with their own Sickness, "I think they will pass
this time very happily in the midst of their
very kindest remembrance to you, and say that they
can hardly forgive your long absence -- I must
see with them from you very soon -- I would say
now that I must to them in town for the mail -- I
had forgotten to say that my Sister will remain in
town but a few days -- Believe me very dear Sir
you will find me very much obliged to you for
your continued attention
Olive Patterson
S. P. B. Wash. May 29th

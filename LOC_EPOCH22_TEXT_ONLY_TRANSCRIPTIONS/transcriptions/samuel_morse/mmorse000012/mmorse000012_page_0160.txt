I assume you will scarcely recognise me when you come
out. For you must know I have something else to do besides
ride clean my boots, and wear a clean face, and besides the
shop present a different scene from the Pastor or Study,
and wears a somewhat different appearance. However there
are some clean beds in Spedwell to which I sometime
resort, and which I hope you will be able to find on your
arrival. The remainder of the sheet I will serve for Monday
Monday morning.
I am inclined to think that my father will not visit
the City this week: -- I am think of nothing more to com
mandate. I shall wait with great interest to hear
from you by your next. Remember me of your
prof. gall.
I remain yours truly
Alfred Vail

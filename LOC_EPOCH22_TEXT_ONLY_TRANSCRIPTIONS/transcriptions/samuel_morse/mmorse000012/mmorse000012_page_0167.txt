strong enough, come in on Saturday, and return here again on Tuesday
following. I have learned a good deal about a bilious habit by this sickness
so that I think I could now be a pretty good nurse to you if you
should have a serious attack. The truth is we are all of a bilious habit.
poor Richard seems to be the greatest sufferer from it, and I should like
to see him well through a good course of medical treatment. How is
he? I called to see him the evening before I left town, but could make
no one answer at the door, so I was obliged to go without laying good bye.
give my love to him and his family.
The machinery for the Telegraph goes forward daily; slowly
but well and through. You will be surprised at the strength and quantity
of machinery, greater doubtless than will eventually be necessary, yet
it gains the main points, certainty and accuracy.
Evening
I doubt whether I shall come in on Saturday, but I possibly
may next week; I say this, that you may not think me more unwell
if I should not come. I am sending Mr. Vail any letter from me at the
office. I wish you would write a line by McVail and tell me any
news for I surely the Journal of Commerce.
W. S. is of opposite politics to yourself.
Yours as ever
Y. A. H. Brothers
Finley

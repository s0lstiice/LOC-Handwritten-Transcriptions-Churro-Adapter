March 8th. After 9 A.M. raining, and in the evening fresh wind.
Thurs. 9. a.m. foggy, and occasional sprinkling of rain, after 4 P.M. raining.
Friday 10. clear & pleasant.
Sat. 11. clear & very pleasant.
Sunday 12. somewhat overcast & P.M. windy
Monday 13. raining & P.M. windy at 11 P.M. a hard rain.
Tuesday 14. mostly clear and rather windy.
Wednesday 15. cloudy till 9 A.M. then most clear, pockets to the north-west
by the heavy rains of Sunday & Monday.
Thursday 16. clear & pleasant. The ice in the Hudson moved opposite
& P.M. windy. Hudson open to W. Point.
Saturday 17. cloudy & P.M. raining till evening then windy
Sunday 18. clear cold & windy.
Monday 19. mostly cloudy. A.M. and at 9 a. flying of snow. P.M.
20. clear, windy.
Thursday 21. mostly cloudy & windy & after 5 A.M. very rainy
22. heavy rain & high wind all night. & day very rainy
23. and misty, wind fresh.
Friday 24. mostly cloudy but very pleasant wind light.
Sat. 25. clear & very pleasant. Hudson open to Pekepsie.
Sunday 26. somewhat cloudy but very pleasant.
Monday 27. a steamboat made her way through
the ice to Newburgh from New York.
Tuesday 28. mostly clear till 10 A.M. and early in the morning some rain
afterwards, somewhat hazy but very pleasant.
Wednesday 29. about 9 P.M. a shower with some thunder.
Thursday 30. mostly clear till 4 P.M. then overcast.
Friday 31. cloudy & P.M. raining & misty; this day 1 steamboat reached Albany.

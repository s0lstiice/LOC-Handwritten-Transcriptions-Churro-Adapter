Although I have given my excuse for writing I

I have learned with mortification & disappointment
that your name was not among the chosen --
I have feared that you would carry into effect
your resolution of abandoning the Art -- resigning
the presidency of our Academy -- I sincerely
hope you will have reason to cast aside that
resolution -- To your own Academy amidst existence & present prosperity -- if in after time

Montreal Dec 11th 1837
My De Friend Moore
When I left you I expected
to have been in Hudson's bay again and put
into shape much of my old business as
well as to finally and formally settle this
colonnizing of British Canada which I had
arranged to do so, with the
parting I gave them currency -- but my
destination was occupied till the 27th of
September in making arrangements to
go ahead here, and having succeeded I returned to the States early in October, for the
Canadiens above named, and to see my
family -- I had not reached Hudson's bay
for some of my creditors, knowing my
success here undertook to provide my assist,
supposing that I would pay or secure them,
rather than force my Canada enterprise.
But I was in no situation either to pay or
secure, any creditor, and a delinquent in the
States would have blasted all my hopes of ever
paying my debts, or retrieving my fortunes.

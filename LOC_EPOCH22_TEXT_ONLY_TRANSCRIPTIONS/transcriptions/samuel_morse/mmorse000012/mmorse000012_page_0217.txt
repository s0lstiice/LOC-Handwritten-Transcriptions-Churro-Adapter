Washington Feb. 9th 1838
Dear Sir:
A Committee of the House of Representatives
has been appointed, to consider the expediency of purchasing
Gilbert Stuart's portraits of the five first Presidents
of the United States; in behalf of which Committee I
address you, as a competent & disinterested judge, for in-
formation on the subject.
It is desired, in the first place, to ascertain the
mechanism of these portraits as works of art and as
portraits;
And, secondly, their pecuniary value as articles of
sale, upon a fair & liberal estimate.
May I ask you to favor me with your views
on each of these points, to be laid before the Committee?
I am very respectfully yours &c
C. Cushing.

Catherine, and a concern which I cannot conceal, I could write

prerogative to ask for your assistance, if you have him for a friend who
concludes all earthly friends, if in every case of trouble, or of doubt, you
can at evening and in the morning ask advice of him, you are
indeed safe from all real danger.
Let me conclude to you the sitting advice of a man
of genius which will serve to direct you on all occasions,

Farwell May heavens best blessing rest on you and

A Quarles says, " Wouldst thou know the laudability of the

" heart. That action is not warrantable which either
" blusters to beg a blessing, or having succeeded dare not

Baltimore March 14 1837
My dear Morse
I am glad to have an excuse for
writing to you and should not have allowed
the winter to pass without doing so if I could
have found a good one -- The Artists' Fund
Lieuteney of Philadelphia offers me one now
for it has elected me an honorary member
which honour I consider equivocal -- thin-
king the Society to be of a Puritanish genere
liberate in acknowledging the honor extended
now you have doubtless been equally
flattered -- tell me have you accepted the
favour? If you have let me know your
reasons for so doing they will most probably
be good for me as well as for you --
I do think the Artists of Philadelphia
have regarded themselves by forming them-
selves into a Charity Club -- their much
worth to their true interest it would have
been to appropriate their means to the alms

Steam Engine.
Hero of Alexandria 130 years B.C. invented a steam engine of
a vase with water, a pipe from which the steam passed through the arms of
a wheel resting on the end of the pipe.
Solomon le Caus of Heidelberg in 1615 describes a method of raising
water by steam, rude and of no practical use.
Giovanni Branca of Loretto, Italy in 1628 continued different kind
of mills worked by steam from a large octopile blowing against waves
on circumference of a wheel.

Denys Papin physician of France in 1690, used steam and made the
first rude outline of the atmospheric engine, but was not aware
of the value of his own invention.

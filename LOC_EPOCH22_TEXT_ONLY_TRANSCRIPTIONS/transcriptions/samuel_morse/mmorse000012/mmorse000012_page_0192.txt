Professor & I of 13 Horse
In your favor is at hand
owing to the unfavorable State of the weather
I did not believe that you would meet me
and having a great deal of business to do I
did not wait. how did I receive your note till
too late, and am very sorry that we could
not see each other. Our defeat I had to see
you was to mention the anxiety of my father
to get you to relieve it at Washington as Speedily
as possible. He wishes it to return home when
probably he will get a share of our blessings as
my father appears determined to leave it
Another object was to request you to mention Kentucky
to Alfred or any one else that your expenses are paid
to it by me.
Another to ask you to write the communication
for the Journal of Commerce relating to the projected
ship of the "Celebration Telegraph"
another to inform him into what I have no doubt
you will. He is easily vexed - Touting to your
universal coolness however and there is no fear
of seeing him from running ahead too fast
But that I may have a place in your mind
and that you may meet with that success which you
deserve I remain yours in fidelity
Long 25 Jan 1838
George Rail

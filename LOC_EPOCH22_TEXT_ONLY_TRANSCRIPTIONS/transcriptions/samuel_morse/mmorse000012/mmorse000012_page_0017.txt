(Expositi on of the Arts in our country, V.)
Our country does not present a
parallel case to the rise & progress
of the fine arts in other countries
we are in this, or in almost all
things without a parallel, it
is difficult to say how far we
are affected by the progress of
the arts in Europe, our intercourse
so great with them is
undoubtedly favorable to the
diffusion of taste we have not
to begin the course of savage
nations to refinement.

When laid in the ground, they may be enclosed in iron
tubes or earthen tubes, and buried beneath the surface.
What I claim is my invention and desire to secure
by letter patent, and to protect for one year by
a Caveat is a method of recording, permanently
electrical signs which by means of metallic
wires or other good conductors of electricity convey
intelligence between two or more places.
In testimony of this instrument I am
to B. Mason here to subscribe my name in the
presence of the witnesses whose names are here to
subscribed on the third day of October A.D 1837.
Signed in presence of
The J. Davis

To the Commissioner of Patents.
The Petition of Samuel W. B. morse of New York
in the county and State of New York.
Respectfully represents
That your petitioners has invented a new method
of transmitting and recording intelligence by means of
Electric magnetism, which he denominates believes has not been
known or used prior to the invention thereof by your
petitioner. He therefore prays that the your petitioners
further states that the machinery for a full specification and
display of his new invention is not yet complete, he
therefore prays protection of his right till he shall have
matured the machinery, and desires that a caveat for
that purpose may be filed in the confidential archives of
the Patent office and preserved in secrecy, according to the
terms and conditions expressed in the act of Congress in that
case made and provided, he having paid twenty dollars
into the Treasury and complied with other provisions of the
said act.
New York Sept. 28th 1827.
Specification of an Electro Magnetic Telegraph.
To all whom it may concern
Be it known that I, Samuel W B morse of New York in
the county and State of New York have invented a new
method of transmitting and recording intelligence by means of
Electric magnetism, and I hereby declare that the following
is a full and exact description of it as it is at present conducted
or may be made

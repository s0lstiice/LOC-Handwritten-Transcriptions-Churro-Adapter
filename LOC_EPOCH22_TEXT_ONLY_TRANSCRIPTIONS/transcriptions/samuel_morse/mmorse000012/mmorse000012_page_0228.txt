Richwelt March 19th 1838
Ruf. Mase
Dear Sir Your
favour of the 15th came to hand on Friday but
on account of our pressing affair I have not
attempted to answer it until now. I have delayed
informing you of the progress of the Machine expecting
you out every day. But I can now assure you that we are getting on very well
The Battery will be ready on Monday to put together
I have concluded to make it of 30 plates 4in by 5
It will be a beautiful one, and no doubt powerful
enough for a circuit of 2 miles, perhaps more though
I wont'd throw all the plate which I had made into
one battery instead of making two batteries, and hope
you will not object to giving me the battery when
you return from Europe if it returns -- It is about
15 inches long. I have no materials for the joints, will
you send or rather bring it out with you, and also
inquire of Dr Gale what are the parts of the composition
he used for cementing the plates -- The wheel for
the register and port rule are now almost all out, and
other parts in progress -- I need several rolls of the
insulated copper wire, to make magnets with and experiment
upon the form of the magnet -- I also need
some of the flat ribbon in my return from the city

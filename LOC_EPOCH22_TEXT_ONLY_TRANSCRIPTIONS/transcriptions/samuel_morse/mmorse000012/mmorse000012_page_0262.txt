disgraceful attempt to produce a reciprocal service
to each other -- and of a beneficial result to the public --
there is only one copy of the "Confessions" in this place -- a
number of strong words will very rarely.
The materials in my possession, could make a
handsome volume, and it has been my design for some
time past to undertake the task; but my constant engagement
for the last six months, has prevented me, for this, to do so; besides,
being a stranger, I feel the necessity of an experienced
hand. Your kind kindness to the French gentleman who
published the "Confessions" you have revived, has induced me to address
you, most humbly requesting, that you would be good
enough to extend the same favour to me -- and to
advise me of the best means to take, in my present
situation, to carry my contemplated work, successfully
to an end.
In the expectation of an answer, as speedy as
your communication will allow, I remain,
Respectfully yours
Birot

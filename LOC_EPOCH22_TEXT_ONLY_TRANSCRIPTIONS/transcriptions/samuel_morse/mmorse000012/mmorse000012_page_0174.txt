I decided on this step with great reluctance
but the decision was a choice of evils, and I think
you will say it was wisely made - All the
property I had purchased here, previous to my
embarkation, I made over to my creditors
to pay my debts - and without a dollar in
the world I possessed and to the amount of
25 thousand dollars, to enable me to erect a
Steam Mill and operate an "ironwork"
This affords some evidence at least, that I
had not lost the confidence of some body -
and I am happy to say that with the blessing
of Providence I shall be able to retrieve my
self in reasonable time I will surely finish
this months of gain or loss yet -
When I left New York I borrowed of
Lidney 12 or 15 Dollars, and gave him an
order for a case of Dumstik linen with
an assurance that on my return I would
not only repay the borrowed money but dis-
charge my old debt - The attempt to arrest
me prevented my so doing, and I doubt
less now, had I can settle with him till
Spring or Summer - I have I shall have it
in my power to do so conveniently - I beg
you would state the circumstances to Lidney
with my regrets at the delay -
The remarks I have made in regard to my
business, are confidential -

Yale College New Haven. April 16. 1808.
Dear Parents,
Since your of the 1 inst. some time since, I
take this opportunity of answering it, I shall be quite
overjoyed when vacation arrives for I feel myself in need
of some respite. I never studied so hard before, as I have
since commencement, & I feel much happier for it. I think
I should feel quite happy if I had kept a regular account
of my expenses for this last term, for I have been so taken
up in my studies, & I must confess have been a little too
negligent, that I can give but a very superficial account
of them, but if you will pardon this neglect for this time,
I will be more careful in future.
I am at present reviewing my studies for the 2 last
terms, Horace, Exelixis, & 2d Vol. Webster; we have only
proceeded as far as Navigation in 2d Vol. Wilks on Heights
distances, & Surveying an very amusing subject, a few
days since the whole class accompanied by the tutors, went
to find the height of East Rock, found it to be 337. feet higher
I have read composition 2d time upon my division, 2d poetry
Last Sunday there were 21 persons pronounced to enter Mr.

my Dear Son
We received a letter from you by the way of New York
dated Novr. 25th that was the last in it you mention having with
in 10 letters beside the one you were then writing we have not
received the one you mention as having sent to the Lodging of Mr
David West from one by the useful to the province of Maine but got
3 by the same dated from the 15th of August to the 3d of September
1812 by the Lydia via New York dated Sept 14th 3 by the Galin dated from
Sept 20th to Novr 6th making 10 in all which have all afforded due
much pleasure except when you vacation your attack of Cholera
and Rheumatism which has made your anxious mother feel
for her absent son and with some slight wish she could be near
enough to minister to his relief but she endeavors to bring her
mind to her circumstances in this and in every other situation
in which our wise and Heavenly Father sees fit to place us
she is but a slow scholar however in learning this important lesson
in what ever state we are thereewith to be content - We
are every minute expecting your Brother Edwards from Norfolk
as your paper has written to Dr Dwight to spare him to spirit
about the Geography he is very useful indeed to your Father he
says he cannot do without him till the Geography is out which
will be in all the month of April he will bring young Legare
who rooms with him along to visit us and his Cousin and Tom
Martin who are at Andover and who spend their Vacations

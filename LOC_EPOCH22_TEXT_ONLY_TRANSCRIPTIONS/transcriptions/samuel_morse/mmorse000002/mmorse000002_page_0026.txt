vigor, and leaping up, I directed my course towards
the place, which I reached in a few moments. I found
it to be an ancient castle, to all appearance unin-
habited, one part of it was entirely decayed, the other
seemed to be in a tolerable state of repair. This unex-
pected discovery of a castle in a place little suspect-
ed of containing such a building, for a moment
filled me with surprise; But I was soon roused from
my meditations, by a dim light suddenly gleaming
from one of the grated windows of a decayed turret
of the castle, which attracted my whole attention. The
ancient tales of castles, spectres, &c. which I had
frequently read in old romances, now rushed forth
to my memory, and I concluded from these that
I was destined to be the performer of some great
exploits. Taking courage from this conclusion, I de-
termined to explore this solitary mansion, and sum-

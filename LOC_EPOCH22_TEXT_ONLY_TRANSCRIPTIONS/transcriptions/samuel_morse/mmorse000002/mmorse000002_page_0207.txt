I walked thro' his gallery of paintings of his
own productions; there were upwards of two
hundred, consisting principally of the original
sketches of his large pieces; he has painted in
all upwards of 600 pictures, more than any
artist ever did with the exception of Rubens
the celebrated Dutch painter. — Mr. West is so
industrious now that it is hard to get access
to him, & then only between the hours of 9 & 10
in the morning.— he is working upon 8 or 9 dif-
ferent pieces at present & seems to be more
enthusiastic than he ever was before.

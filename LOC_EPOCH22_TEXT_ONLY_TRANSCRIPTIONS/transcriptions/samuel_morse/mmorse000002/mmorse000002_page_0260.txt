I was a few days ago violently attacked
by the rheumatism in my arms, and was told
to wear flannel which you know I have never
done as yet; I have accordingly had made for me
4 flannel waistcoats; the English climate being
very damp, subjects stranger, that train of disord-
ers attending damp climates; -- I did not
mention that I had an attack of the same
kind at sea owing to my lying upon two chairs
on deck which had accidentally got wet --
I have not had another attack since wearing
flannel, and do not think I shall if I take
care to keep myself warm.
I dined at Mr. Bromfield's a few days
since with a number of Americans, among
whom were Mr. & Mrs. Sears, and Mr. Phd.
Perkins, of Boston, Mr. Sears & family were
to go to France yesterday in the car--
perhaps it may gratify their relations, to be in-
formed of it, if you should see them -- Mr. &
Mrs. Allston are very well; he is very intimate and
sociable with me so as to make me
his confidant in some important things --

at Cambridge College I would not exchange for a great deal; there are no
disturbances here all goes on regular & the students are pleased with the
authority & the authority with them. We had an ugly affair here
the college books were arraigned before the tribunal of the students consisting of
a committee of 11 from each class no college; I was chosen as one of the
committee from the 2nd Honourable class, we sent for two of the worst of the
books, & were all Saturday afternoon in trying them, found them guilty of
several charges, such as being insolent to the students, not excusing them-
selves to cook clean for us, in concealing pies which belong to the students,
having supper at midnight, & inviting all their neighbours & friends to sup
with them at the expense of the students; & this not once in a while but
almost every night; we extorted this from one of them, that the reason they
were so neglected towards us, was, because there had been no disturbance in
college for 7 years, that the students, & the authority, not taking much no-
tice of their conduct, they meant to do as they please: The committee
after arranging the charges in their proper order, presented them to the
President, he has had the authority together & they are now considering
the subject: this afternoon (Tuesday Decr. 29) they have been together & I with
many others have been with them all the afternoon, there was no restitution
at 4 o clock, they were so busy engaged, I know not how this affair will end, but
I expect in the expulsion of some if not all the books: it is now 3 weeks
since the students convene to appoint their committee, & since that we
have lived extremely well, indeed, for me, past I think we have lived
very well this term, the fault is not so much in the food as in the cooking
for our bill has general been in the following way, chocolate, coffee & hashed
meat every morning; at noon various roast beef, 2 a week pudding & times Vienna
& geese upon an average once a fortnight, baked beans, occasionally Christmas
and other merry days, turkeys, pies, & puddings more as we wish for, at night
for supper we have chocolate tea & pies 1 in a week; I thought to have added that in suc-
tures we are to have beef steaks & toast twice a week; before the books were too large
to cook them I will inform you of the result of this affair as soon as it is complete
I have now as much as I can do; my leisure moments are employed

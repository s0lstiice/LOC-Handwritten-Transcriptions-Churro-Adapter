letter to Mr. Copely, the painter, which I hope
also to deliver this week. A few days
since I walked about 4 miles out of town
to a village by the name of Hackney to witness
the ascension of a Mr. Sadler & another gentleman
in a balloon, it was a very grand sight
the next day the avonauts returned to Hackney
having gone nearly 30 miles in about an hour
and an half; the number of people which attended
on this occasion might be fairly estimated
at 300,000. such a concourse as I never before
witnessed, when the balloon was out of sight
the crowd began to return home, and such confusion
it is almost impossible for me to describe
a gang of pickpockets had contrived to block
up the way which was across a bridge with carriages
& carts &c. and as soon as the people began
to move, it created such an obstruction
that in a few moments this great crowd, in
the midst of which I had unfortunately got
was stopped, this gave the pickpockets an opportunity,
& the people were plundered to a great
amount; I was detained in this manner almost
suffocated, in a great shower of rain, for

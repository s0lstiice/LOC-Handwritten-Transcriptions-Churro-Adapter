relieve your minds from anxiety, for while I am
writing I can imagine Mama, wishing that she
could hear of my arrival, and thinking of thousands of accidents which may have befallen me, & I wish that in an instant I could communicate
the information; but 3000 miles are not passed over
in an instant, & we must wait 4 or weeks, before we can hear from each other, but I hope Mama will always imagine the best; & think no news
good news; for a thousand accidents may cause letters to miscarry. I wish when you write you would
inform me if, you heard of our being spoken at all
at sea, & by what vessel we were spoken by on
our voyage & I hope were reported by some of them.
But I must close this long letter, by requesting you to
remember me to all my friends who enquire after me; especially to Miss Russell, whose kindness to me I shall always
remember. My particular love to my dear brothers, (who
I suppose will be at home when this arrives,) they must write
me long letters. remember me to Nancy & the rest of the family.
I have a great deal more to write but must defer till next letter
excuses from Liverpool to London for I remain your affectionate Son
Samuel F. B. Morse

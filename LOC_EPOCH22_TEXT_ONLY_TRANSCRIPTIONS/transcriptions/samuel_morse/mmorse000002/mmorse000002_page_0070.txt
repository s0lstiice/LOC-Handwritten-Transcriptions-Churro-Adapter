Charlestown June 1st 1809.
Dear Brothers,
I hope you have arrived at
New Haven in good spirits and well;
I have but a very little time to write
you, so you must excuse the confusion
and disconnection of my sentences.
I shall not go from this place till next
Tuesday; so I shall not reach New Haven
till next Wednesday. We expect a letter
from you every hour. You must not
be homesick. How does Richard like
New Haven? is Aaron Putnam is dead.
Mr. Alexander is married again. I am going to a party this evening at Mr. Joseph Hussey,
it is to be a very large party; I went to Mr
Ward's at a party on Sunday evening. I went
to the "Section" yesterday. I am going on
Monday to escort Gov. Gore, into town.
Please send me a pocket book please send
me some Yale cider please send me some
kittens and young specifics. I now take this opportunity of writing to you to inform you that Sam will and he
you are the same. I am your affectionate Son
S.S. B. Morris.
Mr. Miss Sherman wishes you to inform Mrs. Sherman
at Mrs. Day's that she will be home this week.
Mauna has got of present for Nedg

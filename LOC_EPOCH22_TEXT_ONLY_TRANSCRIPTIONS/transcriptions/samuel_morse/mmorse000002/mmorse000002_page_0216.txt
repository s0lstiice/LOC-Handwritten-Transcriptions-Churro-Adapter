I am learning industry, as an example I will
tell you how I employed my time last week, I
rose at 7 and at 7 1/2 past I began my drawings &
did not rise from my chair till 7 1/2 past 6 in the
afternoon, unconsciously that time had gone so fast,
this was only for 3 days, Mr Allston told me
I should injure my health if I continued so long
in tent, & without food; so that the rest of the week
I sat only till 4 at which time I took my din
ner then resumed my studies again; I was
so fast before, & it seems to me
every moment of it, 3 years are
away & without making much progress, I have
been so intent on my studies that I have done
nothing else; I have delivered but my letters to 3
persons, but certainly intend delivering some this
week; I shall not find much time to visit.— Last
Sunday went to St Paul's, heard tolerable preaching, inside very
magnificent, Sir Chris. Wren's epitaph is very short & pointed, as you may

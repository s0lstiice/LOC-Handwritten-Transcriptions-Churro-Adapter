Do write me soon, long letters, I wish to hear from you very
much. By the way if there is any thing of the linen or cotton
manufacture, with you think you should want, I can obtain
it for you here of the best quality or for almost nothing, (in consequence of our nonintercourse) if you will send an order to me
for any thing, so as to reach me before any material change of
affairs with respect to this country, I will purchase them
and reserve them for you till I can transmit them to you
with safety
Copy of letter to
Mr & Mrs Jarvis
Oct. 6. 1811

Yale College N. Haven July 21st 1808
Dear Parents,
I have received 1 or 2 letters from you
lately, which, I fear, we have been too negligent in
answering.
I have not been at all well this term
I have felt extremely languid, & very much inclined
to sleep; I have felt no disposition to study, I cannot
relish my food; the cause of all this I am pretty
confident, is the sultriness of the season; want of
exercise, and slemeness of food; I have some hints of
requesting leave to return home, which, upon after
some deliberation, I think would certainly be best
for me; the term is now nearly half gone, the
Senior class were dismissed yesterday, and there is
but little for my class to study between this &
Commencement, Murray's Exercises, 2d Vol. Webber's
Grace Majora, & perhaps Priestly's Lecture, if we get
this Murray, now then I can study at home
but I cannot here; I feel very low spirited, & un-
less I can find some thing that will raise the
spirits I fear I shall be but poorly at Commencement;
Literise if I am at home I may be able to assist
you in various ways, I can write for you; &

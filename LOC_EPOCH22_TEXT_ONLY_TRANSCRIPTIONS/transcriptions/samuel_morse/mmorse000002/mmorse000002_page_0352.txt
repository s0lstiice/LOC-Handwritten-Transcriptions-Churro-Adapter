The aspect of affairs is threatening -- In this case I see not

for means to support yourself -- There is yet no de-
cision of the Parkman & Vray case -- & probably it will be
delayed -- We pretty much give up that source of relief, as
hopeless -- We trust Providence, to take care of us, & our
sons without it.
Your brothers write us in fine spirits, dare
both doing very well Edwards talks of going to Phila-
delphia in May vacation, we wish him to go, as we think it will
be of service to him.

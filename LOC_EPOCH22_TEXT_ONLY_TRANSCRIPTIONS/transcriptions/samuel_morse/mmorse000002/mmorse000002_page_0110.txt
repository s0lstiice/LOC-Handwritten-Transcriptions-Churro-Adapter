Boston Decr 28th 1810
My Dear Brothers,
As Desmond goes home next week I improve the opportunity of writing you a few
lines; Mama is very anxious to hear from you you must write soon. I shall leave Mr. Mallory's
next week and study painting exclusively till sum-
mer. I will tell you more hereafter. The times are very distressing here there have been 13 very
important failures since Christmas; that is in 3 days:—more are expected daily— the Exchange
Building was discovered on fire last night about 8 o'Clock, but was got under without destroying
more property than to the amio. of 50 dollars, it however threatened to be very severe. fires happen
almost every night.
Edudt. I wish you would call on Cyrus More and pay him 50 cents for a piece of ivory I got of
him when I was at college, he has written me for it, and made out a bill of horse hide which
I never had due, and of course will pay him if you had direction only to pay 50 cents: and have no words about it

Col. College New Haven July 23d 1808
Dear Parents,
I wrote you a few letters, two or three days
since informing you that it was not improbable
that I should be at home in a short time. I have
not seen Dr. Dwight on the subject yet but shall if
I do not feel better in a few days. In the
last letter I forgot to mention some things which I
ought to have done. Mr. James Dwight requested
me to buy & get him one or two copies of Col. Pick
ering's last letter to Genl. Sullivans; I wish you could
procure them for me & send them on as soon as possible.
They are not to be obtained in New Haven.
The Seniors were dismissed from college a
few days since, the classes have chosen rooms. I
did not choose with them, as I intend rooming
with a tutor if possible; I have spoken with Mr.
Dwight on the subject; If I can do this I shall
think myself highly favored. It is now late in
the evening; I will finish this some other time.
Monday July 25th 1808.
I have just received your letter of the 15th. I
hope you have received mine before this time. I never
said so much hurt, as when I heard of the misconduct
of Elisha Goddard, I know not what excuse he has to
fallate his crime, & I therefore cannot judge whether
he is to blame or not; but after all that I have said

this term: I have used the Baltimore and South water
some the latter part of this term and I find it does
me good, I feel in better spirits and am more
able to pursue my studies.
I have found my watch again; one of the students
had taken it for a short time merely to play a
joke upon me.
The PHOENIX Society has fallen!!!
We receive newspapers from the office once in a while,
I presume all that you send.
The vacation is near & I rejoice to think
that I shall once more see home, it is a fortnight
from next Tuesday.
Next Thursday is my birthday, 18 years old.
I received a letter from Goldard yesterday the 2d.
I have received his letter, from his sister I learn
that his sister Miranda was quite sick; I wish
you would write me soon, and inform me how she
is.
Edwards and myself are well and in good
spirits, as is Warder, we are all preparing for examinations.
A report was received here last evening that
the British had resented their orders in counsel; this
news is true, but fear it is not. Give my particular
love to Rich. and the family, can't stay to particular.
Remember me to inquiring friends.
Understand that Aunt Abby is soon to be married; give her
joy for me.
Yours in haste
S B. Moser.

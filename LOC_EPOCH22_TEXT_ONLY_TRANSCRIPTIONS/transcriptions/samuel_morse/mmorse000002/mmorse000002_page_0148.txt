I shall call on Mr. Coit this morn. & Mr. Coit
is at Phillipsburg with her family, but Mr. Coit
boards in town; I shall not be able to see Mr. C. of
course.— I have not seen Messrs. Williams & Whiting
yet but probably shall to day: I shall go to-day to
see the Gallery of Paintings with Mrs. Stout. I
feel perfectly well & at home, my friends show me
every attention, & enquire particularly after you.
I feel but very little disposition to write at
present, &c will write again soon;
with a great deal of love to you
all, I remain your
affectionate Son,
Sam'l F. B. Morse.
Res. J. Mosse

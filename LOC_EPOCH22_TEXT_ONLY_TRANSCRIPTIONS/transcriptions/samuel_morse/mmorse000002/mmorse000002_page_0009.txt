Yale College Feb 18th 1808
Dear Parent,
I have again found a little leisure to write you
a few lines; my time is wholly taken up by my studies,
especially Mathematics; Trigonometry requires very deep
that at first to understand it, but as I have got the me-
niments of it perfect, I find but very little difficulty
with it. My leisure time is chiefly taken up by
studying French, but sometimes I find time to read a
little in other books: I have been reading the Prophecies
in the Bible to day & find the prophecy of Bonaparte
& have found one which appears to me to be the one, it
is the 11th Chap. of Daniel & beginning with the 21st verse
"And in his estate shall stand up a vile person &c. in
the 25th verse it says "Then shall stand up in his estate a rais-
er of taxes &c. but he shall be destroyed neither in anger nor in battle." This I conceive to be Louis 16 who raised heavy taxes which caused his death which was neither in anger nor in battle: in the succeeding verse it mentions after, "(shall stand up a vile person);" that he should obtain the kingdom by flatteries which agrees with the manner in which Bonaparte gained the kingdom. In the 24th verse "He shall enter peaceably upon the fattest places of the province," &c. So I think this must mean his popesping the countries about France such as Germany, Austria, Rusia, Switzerland, &c. it then mentions 2 Kings, the kings of the South & North this I conceive to mean Bonaparte &

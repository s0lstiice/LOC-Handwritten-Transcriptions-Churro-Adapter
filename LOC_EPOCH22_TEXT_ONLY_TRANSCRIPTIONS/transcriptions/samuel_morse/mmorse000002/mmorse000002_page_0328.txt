My dear Son
Charleston Feb. 28. 1812
My dear Son, I am informed that a rebel U.S. sail for Ireland on Tuesday
offered to take charge of a letter to put on board for you. (Hope the boys Mr K. has a fine for a few days old. Your
remembrance of them by name was grateful as it was to them named) Your Mama wrote you a long letter two days ago, to go by the Missouri for Liverpool, to sail also on Saturday. We improve both opportunities, as we know it will gratify you to hear often from us and we once you thin attention, in return for yours to us we have received all your letters, mentioned by you except the one by the West-ship written at sea we did not notice in any of the papers, that you were spoken with at sea. Mrs in her last letters to her friends speaks of you in favorable terms, which gave us great pleasure. We are, as you know deeply concerned in every thing that concerns you - I am made very happy when we hear well of you - All our sons are giving us much comfort & pleasure by their good behaviour. We regret the failure of Mr H in London, his efforts here on the other branches of the house, while I desire to give them with suitable feelings, the hand of Providence in this event.

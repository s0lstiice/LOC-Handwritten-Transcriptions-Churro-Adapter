2. P.S. What was the reason of Finley's leaving M'Allattion?
If what I hear is correct, I stand in a fair way to lose my bottle cider. May how does cider sell by the
bottle, and how much will T. want upon his passage - If you can accurately calculate the expenditure you may
draw upon my banker for as much as is required to pay it. Tell Finley if you please, that I wish him a happy passage.
Tuesday evening June 29th
A few words will be all the finishing time will allow
me to give to this letter. After a comfortable ride arrived on Saturday about 8 P.M. I left the looks agreeably to direction - I found Richards in pretty low spirits. He
well he might be, for he had been afflicted far more than a week with what I others suppose to be
a bilious. Dr. Ives had given him a small plaster which he kept on for several days - But as it appeared to do no good
he took it off and put on a poultice of molasses & flowers

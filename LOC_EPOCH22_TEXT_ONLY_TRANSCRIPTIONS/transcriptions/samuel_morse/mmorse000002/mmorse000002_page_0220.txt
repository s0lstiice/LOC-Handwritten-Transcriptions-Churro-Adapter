My Dear Friend,
I have at length found a little leisure from
the hurry and bustle which naturally attends a stranger in such a place as this, to write to one of my best
friends and to give a slight account of my voyage. I depart from NewYork informing you of the
time I expected to sail etc. On Saturday July 13th we left the harbor of NewYork & lay at Staten Island until
Monday morning for a wind, and in the course of a few hours we lost sight of land; the passengers on the second day
and some indeed on the first began to be sick, for my own part I was not in the slightest degree seasick tho the whole voyage, tho' I must confess, seeing
some of them so deadly sick made me feel disagreeably after the 3 day however the passengers began to recover &
continued well thro' the remainder of the voyage; Our passengers 14 in no were perhaps the most agreeable sett ever
collected together, we had one odd character among them who made us an excellent butt, a Mr Minshull, the

London Sept. 3d. 1811
My Dear Parents
By some strange fatality my letters which
accompany this have twice missed an opportunity
of being sent by the way of New York; but I am
glad of an opportunity of sending direct to Boston
by the Friano who sails tomorrow, this will
likewise save the expence of a heavy postage which
would probably have been charged from New York
to Boston; There is no expence of postage from
this country to any other, on ship letters, I believe
it is never customary, so that you must be mistaken
with respect to this circumstance the only
expence of letters, would be upon land, if a
letter comes in the letter bag of a ship from Boston
to London, it would cost me but 2 an expence
which is trifling considering the delay occasioned by
sending them by private hand; The letter that
came by the Galen to me did not reach me
till 4 days after I heard of his arrival, whereas

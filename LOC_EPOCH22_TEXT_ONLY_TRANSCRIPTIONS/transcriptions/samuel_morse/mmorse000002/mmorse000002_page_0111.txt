P.S. You will see by the papers that Edward Gray,
of the Senior class in Cambridge was buried the
day before yesterday, his father attended his funeral
on that day, and yesterday he himself attended to his
grave having destroyed himself in the morning
with a razor! it is a shocking event!
Edward Gray Esq: was one of the ablest, most
respectable and wealthy men we have, and was
a professor of religion.

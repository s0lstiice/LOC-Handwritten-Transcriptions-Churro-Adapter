Connecton 10th Feb. 1810
Rev. Dr. Green Sir.
Permit me to open you, the not
sending to you a copy of the Epitome of Electricity &
galvanism has not been owing to my negligence or un-
willingness - You will recollect that I suggested, per-
haps it would not be agreeable to the Author that I
should review it - that I might ascertain whether a
not this was the case I wrote to Dr Green and expressed
my readiness to comply with your request - Dr Green
has written to me repeatedly since that time, but has
taken no notice of the circumstance. - Mr. Eastime
Hazard called on me sometime before the publication
of the Book, and I mentioned it to him - he said he
would consult Dr Green and write to me - he has
written to me - but has taken no notice of the Review -
Besides the book was published a considerable
time before the Copy I subscribed for was forwarded -
so it is pretty plain a review by me would not be very
acceptable to those concerned -
Sent my best respects to Mr Ellmore
and believe me
and much esteem Your obliged Servt
John Maclean
Rev. D. Ellmore

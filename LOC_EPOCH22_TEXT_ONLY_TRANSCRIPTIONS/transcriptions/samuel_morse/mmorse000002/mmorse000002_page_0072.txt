Tuesday morning
My Dear Parents
I wrote you a letter the Monday after you left this place, with I suppose
you have received this time; My leg has got entirely well, and my head also has almost entirely
left me; we are all of us well here; and pursuing our studies. The term has nearly
expired & I hope again to see home soon.
I employ all my leisure time in painting I have a great number of persons engaged
already to be drawn on ivory no less than 7 they obtain the ivoies for themselves I
have taken Professor Kingsley's profile for him it is a good likeness of him and he is pleased
with it. I think I shall take his likeness on ivory and present it to him as my present at the end of the year.
Mr. Mervin goes to morrow morning to Boston and I improve the opportunity of writing one or two lines to you, I have
taken two letters out of the office for Paper for one of which I paid 25 1/2d as postage.
There is nothing new in New Haven all friends are well and send love to you.
I have finished Miss Leffingwell's miniature it is as good likeness and she is very much pleased with it.
In some haste,
Samuel T. B. Morse

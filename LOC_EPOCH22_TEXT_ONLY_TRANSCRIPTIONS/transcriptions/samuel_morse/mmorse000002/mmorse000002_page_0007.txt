Sabbath evening Yale College, New Haven Jan. 31st 1808.
Dear brother,
As I have a little time left this evening I improve
it by writing you a few lines. Edw. & B are both well & very
much contented, altho so great a distance from home : Our
vacation is just out ; next Wednesday will be the first day
of the term, & I feel very glad to see the vacation so far gone,
for I wish to pursue my studies, altho the hardest studies of
my college life will then be studied, Enelid, God. Webber, &
Gracia. Majoram. I hope the next term will pass away quiet
for I want to see home agains very much. I long for the
time when you will come on to college, I hope you will
study hard so as to make one of the first in your class.
I have been pursuing the French Language this vaca-
tion & hope I shall gain a thorough knowledge of it
by next commencement. I would write you a longer
letter but have not time. Mr. Abbott goes early in the morning
& it is now past 9 o'clock.
I am very much troubled with chilblains this winter
& so is Edw. the winter is very mild here, there has been
but one day in which there has been good sleighing.
I remain
(Give our love to Papa & Your very affectionate
Manma when you write)
Samuel T. B. Morse.

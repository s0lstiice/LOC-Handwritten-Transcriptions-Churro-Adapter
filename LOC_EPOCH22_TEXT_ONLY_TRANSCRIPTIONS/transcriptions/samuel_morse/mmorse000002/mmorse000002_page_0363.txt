Philadelphia April 9, 1812
My dear Friend
I have this moment received a letter
from Miss Vanstran in London dated Feb 21st and learning the
passage below would be interesting to you I transcribe it with
pleasure and add my very sincere wish that all your hopes
may be realized
"Dr Morris son is considered a young Man of very
promising talents by Mr Allston and Mr Webb and by those who
have seen his Paintings. We have seen him and think his
modesty and apparent amiability promise as much happiness
to his friends as his talents may procure distinction for himself
He is particularly fortunate, not only in having Mr Allston for
adviser and friend, but in his companion in painting
Mr Leslie a young Man from Philadelphia highly recommended
by my Uncle there -- and whose extreme difference adds to the
great promising talents the patient industry and desire of improving
which are necessary to bring them to perfection -- They have

Mar. 30th. A Storm detains the Galen, & the ship in which I was
expected to sail, has changed her voyage. I shall send this day
the Galen, which is ready to sail with fine & wind. The latest
papers will give you the Galen, & you will have opportunity to see them
at the American Telegraph. I shall not therefore send them. You will
see by them that that aspect of our affairs is more threatening than
ever; I still however have no belief that we shall have war.
The County has written to Mr Smith on the subject of securing the copy night
of Dr. Dwight's book in 1820. You can confer with Mr P. on regard to doing the same
in respect to the Geo. I write one. If you see the papers you will see evidence that
electing me as Judge of the Supreme Court is doubtful. But I have high hopes that you will
be elected Judge. I am your very affectionate friend
Chas. P. P.

You have ordered several books to be sent you, all which
orders I have received, and, as soon as it is practicable
to send them will do it, but you will easily see that, it
is not possible in the present state of affairs; those who go
passengers have always more than they can take care of,
of their own property, and refuse to take charge of any thing
the captains likewise say they will not risk their self
by taking charge of anything so you see that it is
impossible in the present state of things to do anything respectively
them but as soon as I can get an opportunity I will send
them. There is nothing of the parcel kind at Mr Jay-
loss for you, those with you mention Mr Hudson told you
were there, he sent back to their respective owners not being
able to send them to you on account of the above
state of affairs. The book Smith on the Prophecies
which you sent me, I have submitted to Mr Burder
who understands these things better than myself; for I am
entirely separated from all things of that kind, being 3
miles distant from booksellers or printers, and my time
being wholly employed from daylight till midnight, in
pursuing my studies, I should lose a great deal by
going that distance.
I thank Mama for her kindness in sending me
prescriptions, I have not had occasion for them having
been in perfect health ever since the first term at the hotel,
and have felt nothing of the rheumatism since I left the
ship. I am now in excellent spirits, and health; and
Mrs Allston says I am growing fleshy, this climate, although
disagreeable suits my constitution very well.
Mr Hurd is still here and visits me often, he is well
but is a little lowspirited, since Mr Parkman has left London

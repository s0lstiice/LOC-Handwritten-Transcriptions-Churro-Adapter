next place thro' which we passed at dinner in the church-yard

of the inn, "on the first grave stone which caught
my eye was this inscription," to the Memory of
Mr. John Hart & Ann his wife, & ever I find
that the name of Hart is by no means unknown
in England. I have seen it in many places
thro' which I passed after dinner we went
on to Woburn where we lodged, near Woburn
is the very magnificent seat & residence of the
Duke of Bedford, he is the richest subject in Eng-
land nearly the whole city of Woburn is his property.

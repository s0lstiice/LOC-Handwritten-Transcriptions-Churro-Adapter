In a few minutes the chief orchestra cease and
are relieved in turn by the other bands, the
company following the music; the Scotch band
principally play Scotch reels & dances; the mu-
sic in the course of dancing continue till about
4 o’Clock in the morning, when the lights are
extinguished & the company disperse on this

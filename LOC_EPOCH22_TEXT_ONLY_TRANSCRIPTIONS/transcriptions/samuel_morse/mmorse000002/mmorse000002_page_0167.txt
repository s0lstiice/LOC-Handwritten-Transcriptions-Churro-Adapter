We have a piano forte on board for
two gentlemen, who play elegantly, so we
shall have fine times; I am in good
spirits, tho' I feel rather singularly to
see my native shores disappearing so
fast, I for so long a time. I am not
yet sea-sick, but expect to be a little
so, in a few days. We shall probably be
boarded by a British vessel of war soon,
there are a No. off the coast, but they
treat American vessels very civilly.
I must bid you good bye, you
shall hear from me again very
soon.
Your affectionate Son
Sam'l J. B. Motser

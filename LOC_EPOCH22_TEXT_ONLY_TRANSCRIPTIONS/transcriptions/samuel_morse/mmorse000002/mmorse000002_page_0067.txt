I have been looking over all the letters I have received
from you this time to answer all the questions contained
in them for I have answered them once and must now
answer them again. You mentioned in your letter of the
15th of March, to us, we received some time since
and has been acknowledged and answered a dozen times
that you were surprised that the letters you sent us
by Mr. Mallory were not acknowledged. We had not received
them when we wrote your letter neither did we
get them till 3 or 4 days after Mr. Mallory had again
set out for Boston, they lay in Mr. Beech's store at this
time, so we could do not acknowledge what we had not
received: In my two first letters I gave you an account
of the sickness in and about New Haven: I painted
it in stronger colors than I perhaps ought to have done, I hope
you will allow for the state of my feelings at that
time, as I was sick myself and did not feel in every
high spirits at the time: The sickness has entirely subsided:
those students who were sick have entirely recovered
and are attending to their usual exercises, there is not
the least apprehension that the malignant fever will
reach New Haven.
I have been absent from prayers and recitation
a great no of times this time but it was impossible for me
to remedy it, I have been more unwell this time than
I ever was before, however, I have not lost a single lesson

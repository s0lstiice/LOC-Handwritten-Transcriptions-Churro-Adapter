(Wednesday)
New-York July 10th 1811.
My Dear Parents,
You see the date of my letter is still
from this place; we have met with many delays
she has been going & going for a No. of days past
and way to have gone to-day at 11 o’Clock: but
the wind is direct ahead, we are now only wait-
ing for a wind, she has hauled off into the stream
with all her men on board, and will probably sail
to-morrow (Thursday) I am well and in very good spirits;
yesterday I dined in Broadway with 5 or 6 of my fellow passengers at
a boarding house, was invited by a Mr. Palmer
a Scotchman, who is a very agreeable pleasant
young man, and is very attentive to me,
and indeed all the passengers seem to be particularly
attentive to me. We have a singular group, a Russian,
a Scotchman, an Englishman, an Irishman
and the rest I believe an American. I can tell better
when we are collected on board. Among our passengers is a Mr. Wm Gray, who

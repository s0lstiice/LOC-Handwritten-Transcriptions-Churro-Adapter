My Dear Parents,
[96421] I arrived in this place on Sabbath
evening by Packet from NewYork, I left
Philadelphia on Thursday morning at 8 o'Clock
and arrived in NewYork on Friday at 10,
I went immediately to Mr. Coits where I
was treated with much attention, I was in
introduced to Mrs. Malcom's family and also
to Mr. Stouts and Mr. Marsh, from all
whom I received great attentions I sailed
in NewYork but one night I found it quite
insipid after seeing Philadelphia I went
on board the packet on Saturday at 12
o'clock and arrived as I before stated on
Sabbath evening We had on the whole
a very good set of Passengers from

two sermons read to be, one of them Dr.

I have seen but little of London as
yet; being more desirous of commencing my
studies at present, than to gratify my curiosity.
I, however, in going to, & from, dinner, generally
make a little circuit to see what is to be
seen: if you have a plan of London I will direct
you where to find me: I am on the west
side of Great or Upper Titchfield street, near the
corner of that street & Mary-le-bone St the
place where I dine is in Wardour street at the
corner of that & Knaves Acre I pass down Titchfield
street, by Oxford market into Oxford St &
go a short distance eastward, and Wardour St
is on the So. side— perhaps it may gratify you
to look me out on the map. I have not
felt any of those disagreeable feelings which I
expected to have experienced on my first arrival
here

were visited by a small fishing vessel with 4
or 5 raw Fishermen in it, who broke us fish and
potatoes, it was laughable to hear them talk
in the true Irish Rogue; we passed the Tuscar
rock a dangerous celebrated rock on the Irish
coast, there were thousands of seafood upon it
and over it, after passing this rock we steered
across the channel towards the Welsh shore, &
came in sight of Holyhead on the island of An-
glesea, the mountains in Wales are very grand
it is very common to see the clouds envelop-
ing the middle of the mountain, while the top

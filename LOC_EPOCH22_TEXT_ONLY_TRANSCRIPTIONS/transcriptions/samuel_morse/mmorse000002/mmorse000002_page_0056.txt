Yale College New Haven, July 1809
Dear Parents
I have neglected writing you for some
time, in daily expectation of receiving a letter from
you, but as I have expected in vain I think it
my duty to refrain no longer, from informing you
of our situation here: Edwards & myself are
both well, as this is a very sickly time in this
place; the typhus fever rages here without con-
troul, there are a number of the students very
sick with it; 3 of them a few days since were
not expected to live; viz Rice, (with whom papa
is acquainted;) Fish & Parkins, all of the Se-
nior class; I am happy however in now stating
they are considered out of danger & are slowly
recovering: Wells of my class belonging to Hart-
ford, was sent for home a few days since, with
the melancholy news of his mothers & only sis-
ter's death; they both died of the spotted fever
of which the typhus fever is a genus. Wells was
the person with whom I roomed the latter

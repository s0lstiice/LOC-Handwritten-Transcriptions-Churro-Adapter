Dear Sirs: I received your letter of the 22d
of yesterday by mail. I am sorry it was not convenient for Mr Wood to leave $50 with
you He thought it would be quite convenient, or I would have sent it. I sent you $50
on the 10th of this month by Mr Parainod who I have taken, though forgetful, as
carried on to Phila. I hope he has sent it back to you before this let me
know when you receive it. If I can, I will send you
15 or 20 doll. by Capt Collins, who will carry
this letter. I shall not give on lose it, but give it to him, I request him to hand
it to you. You may ask him whether he has any money for you. I may not
have $100. I will give it to him. If not I will wait till I come on to Genl. I

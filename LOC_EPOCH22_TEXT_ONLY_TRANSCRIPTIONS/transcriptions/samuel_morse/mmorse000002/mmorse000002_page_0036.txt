Dear Parents,
I have just received to my great surprise
a letter from you, answering me for not writing
oftenier than I have done; I really cannot
conceive the cause, why you should suspect I have
done any thing wrong, I am not in, the least difficulty
in any thing, except feeling a little unwell &
very much hurt on reading your letter; I am just
in as good standing in my class as I was last
term; I had a quarrel with one of my classmates
a few weeks ago, but that has not injured me
in the least, the blame was entirely on his side
I complained to the authority, & the young fellow
who quarreled with me (Grumball) is to
be sent from college for this, & other crimes.
You need not think that this has had the least
effect in injuring my character, for I was
perfect last it would, & went to Mr Day, he
told me I need not be under any apprehension
on that account, I was not consider at all blameable,
if this is not sufficient to satisfy you on
this head, you can write to Mr Day and he
will give you the particulars, or I will tell you
when I come home, they would take up too much
time to repeat them now. The affair is entirely
over closed closed & you need not be under any apprehension
on that score.
I am really surprised to hear you say
you have not received but two letters from me
I have written no less than six this is the 7th
& I sent by mail, then 1 by Mr Townsend then
1 by mail & 1 by Goddard, wht I hope you will
receive before this reaches you

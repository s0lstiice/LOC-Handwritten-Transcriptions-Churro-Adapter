There have been some discontents in College, several expelled but a
few quieted. I expect my Govt will be out in about 6 wks
and shall send you a copy by the first ship. I hope for 105 I am to
be in Paris for the Congress. You must let me know the state of
the Union whether you are likely soon to add any thing to them. Yours
Very afftly yours
Geo. W. COUSINS

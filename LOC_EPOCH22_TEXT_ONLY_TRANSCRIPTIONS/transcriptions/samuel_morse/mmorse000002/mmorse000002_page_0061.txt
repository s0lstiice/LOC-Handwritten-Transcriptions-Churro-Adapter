It Hartford a few days since a student left that
place, and asserted that the day he left the city, there were
six persons lay dead, Buried, and there were 21 new cases.
A few days before he started there was a family consisting
of five persons who were all taken at the same time, the
next day a neighbor having occasion to go into the house
found them all dead; this is what I heard from this
student.... At Berlin the fever rages very bad, 9 die
in one day last week, a young lady coming home from
meeting last sabbath, at the same place, on pulling off
her glove discovered that her arm was entirely covered
with purple spots, her mother told her it was the spotted
fever, and she died before the next morning.... Rice, Fish, and Parkin are slowly recovering they
have been sick a long time. The students are in some degree
sickly, but principally with colds, there are two more
cases of the typhus fever in college, very Dwight and Brown
of the Senior class, Dwight however was but slightly taken,
and he is now able to attend college duties.... I was confined
to my room all the last week with a very bad
cold, and a slight fever, Dr. Ives prescribed for me ain't
have now perfectly recovered; I had a very violent
headach all the time I was confined.... The students look
melancholy, and feel alarmed at the approach of this
contagion, The whole aspect of public and private affairs
looks gloomy, but I will drop this story it is bad enough
to think of it....

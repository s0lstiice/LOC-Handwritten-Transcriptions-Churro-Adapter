Sabbath evening. Yale College New Haven March 6th 1815
Dear Parents,
I have a little leisure time I improve it by
writing you a few lines. Dr Dwight is quite aware
he has been unable to attend to his college duties for some
days past; he is very much distressed in his eyes & I fear
he will lose them; on this account there has been no
exercises in the chapel to day I was not able to go
out to meeting on account of my hat it is cracked and
torn in such a manner that it is totally unfit to wear
Mr Twining seems always to recommend me to the worst
places in town to get my things, he once recommended
me to a barber (who is known to be a drunken & profane
person,) to get my hair cut, & likewise to a shoemaker (who
is the worst in town) to get a pair of shoes & lastly to the
hatter of whom I purchased my hat who is the greatest
cheat in town, & tomorrow I must purchase
me another hat, before I have worn my hat, half long
enough to pay for it; I shall let Mr Twining go himself
& get one out this time. I hope you will not
think that it was owing to my carelessness that my
hat has lasted so short a time; for no person could
taste more pains to make it look well, than I did;
I was cheated, & I am not the only student who has
been cheated by the same man. I find it impossible to live in College without spending money;
at one time a letter is be paid for, then comes up a great
tax from the Class or Society which keeps me constantly

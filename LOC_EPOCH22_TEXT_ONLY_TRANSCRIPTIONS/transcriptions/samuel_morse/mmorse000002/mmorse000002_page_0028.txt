I stood for a moment considering what was best for
me to do: I turned to open the door which I had
just entered, but it was fastened, as I retreated
a few steps back I struck my foot against some
soft substance on the ground. I stooped down to
discover what it was, when to my great astonish-
ment and terror I placed my head upon the
cold face of a corpse; this was more than my
overstrained senses could bear; struck with horror
at my situation, I fell senseless to the ground.
How long I remained in this situation I am un-
able to inform you, but when I recovered I
found myself to me no small joy safe upon my
bed.
S. S. B. Morse.
June 13th 1808.

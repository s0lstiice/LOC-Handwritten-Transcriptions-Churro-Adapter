(Eng.) Liverpool Augt. 9. 1811
My Dear Parents,
I yesterday wrote you a letter
and put it on board the Aristomenes for
New York with sails in about 10 days, supposing
she was the first vessel for America who sailed
but since I find there is another to sail
next Sunday for Philadelphia, the Alpha
& I improve the opportunity of informing you
of my safe arrival in England we had a
most delightful passage of 22 days, we have been
remarkably favored with fair winds agreeable
passengers, & in short every thing that
could make us happy. I was not seasick
for a moment, but have felt perfectly well
with a good appetite, & fine spirits today
we have passed thro the custom house, & alin
office without difficulty, & Mr & Mrs Allston
with myself will take a post chaise
tomorrow & proceed for London when

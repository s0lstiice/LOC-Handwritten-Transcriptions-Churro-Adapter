My expences have exceeded but a little what I had cal-
culated for Week, the sum total to the present time is
£ 3418.8.2.— this includes my expences from Liverpool, and all down
to the present.— what little I have exceeded I shall make up
by future economy— I suppose you do not wish to know
the items of every of I spend, if I give you the total anno
from time to time I suppose it will answer— I endeavor
to be as economical as possible & am getting into the habit
very fast, it must be learnt by degrees, I shall not say, as Sal-
maggiendi says, "I shall spare no expence in discovring the
most economical way of spending money," but shall endeavor
to practise it immediately.— I want to hear from you
very much and good long letters like your last, you cannot
conceive how much joy it would me to hear from you, it seems
so long before a letter can get from one to the other I often
think of Mama & imagine her thinking of me; I hope she
will keep up her spirits, and think, as I do that 3 years will
soon pass away, & then I hope we shall see each other again;
till then I shall employ all my time in improving myself in
every thing that is useful— Sidney. E. & Richard. E. I suppose
are now at home; I that of Edwin on his commencement
day thro' the whole day, give him joy on his Bachelorship
for me, I wish Edwin & Rich? would both set down & write
me long letters, and give me any minute circumstances
which will interest me, in fact every thing will coming from home; I
feel myself an American more than I ever did before.

Begin I have begun my studies, the first part
of whk is drawing, I am drawing from the head

handling black & white chalk, I shall then commence
a drawing for the purpose of trying to enter
the Royal Academy, it is a much harder task
to enter now, than when Mr. Allston was here
as they now require a pretty accurate knowledge

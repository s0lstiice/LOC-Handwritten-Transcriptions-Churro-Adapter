Yale College New Haven Jan 1st 1809
Dear Parents,
As I have a little leisure to write I impose
it by writing a few lines; my studies on week days occupy so much
of my time; that I never get an opportunity to write them, I
am forced to do it on Sabbath evening. I heard a few days ago
by Ward that I had a sister, but this month I heard from
Mr Dwight that she lived but a short time, which grieves me
very much for Mama's sake, for I know it must afflict her
children who have been spared will afford her comfort, suffice
to overbalance the affliction, I will endeavor for my own
part to behave in such a manner that you will have no reason
to complain of me, indeed I trust you will find no cause
for blame in my conduct this term; I have not been
absent from prayer this term, nor but once absent from recitation,
by that unavoidably Edwards has neither been absent
from prayer or recitation to my knowledge this term, he stands
high in the esteem of his class from what I can learn, and
in short I hope the conduct of us both this term has been such
as will meet with your approbation: The appointments at
the end of this term will be given to those who merit
them, in my class, a great many are in dread of the
judgment expecting who will doubtless be dis-appointed
as for my own part I do not feel very anxious, if I deserve
an appointment, I hope I shall have one, if otherwise I will
cheerfully yield to those who are my superiors.
Mr Kingsley received your letter sometime since he desired
me to acquaint you that he was so much taken up in
business that he was unable to do what (you requested) or (he promised)

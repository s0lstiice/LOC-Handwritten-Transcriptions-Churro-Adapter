(Tuesday evening) I have just received yours of the 8th inst. I will execute all the
commands & considerations I was very glad to have a letter from
home, as I have seldom the pleasure with regard to 500000 pounds
belonging to me, I have not been absent from keeping this
time, nor will I ever be so long away from you as I am now.
I am your affectionate son
Samuel

Yale College New Haven Jan 9. 1809
My Dear Parents,
Owing to some unforeseen incidence I did not go till to-morrow morning (Wednesday) I therefore improve a few moments in writing you longer,
as I intended write but a short letter yesterday.
The "appointments" come out to-morrow morning I therefore shall leave this letter unsealed & will give you a list of them at the end of this letter;
I cannot say but I feel a little anxiety on the occasion but I have prepared myself for a disappointment.
I received last evening a couple of pamphlets from the Office, for which I thank you, I delivered the Panoplist to Mr Kempsey who was wishing it very much.
There has been a gentleman named Colly with a subscription paper for Hon Fisher Ames' Works I saw your name on the list & therefore declined subscriptions I wish you would send me the work as soon as it is published; I understand it will be an excellent thing.
I have been reading the 1st Vol. of Professor Silliman's journal while the rest during his passage to, & residence in, Europe I am very much pleased with it; I long for the time when I shall be able to travel with improvement to myself and society, and hope it will be in your power to offer me.

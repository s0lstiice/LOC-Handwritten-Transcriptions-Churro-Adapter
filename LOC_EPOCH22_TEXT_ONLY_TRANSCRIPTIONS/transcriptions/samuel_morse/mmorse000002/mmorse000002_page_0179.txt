Liverpool August 7th 1811
My Dear Parents.
You see from the date, that I have
at length arrived in England; I have had
a most delightful passage, of 20 days from
land to land & 2 in coming up the channel.
As this is a letter merely to inform you of my
safe arrival, I shall not enter into the particulars of our voyage until I get to London
to which place I shall proceed as soon as
possible Suffice it to say that I have not
been sick a moment of the passage, but on
the contrary have never enjoyed my health
better. I have not as yet got my trunk
from the customhouse but presume I shall
meet with no difficulty. I am now at
the Liverpool Arms Inn, it is the same inn that
Mr. Saliman put up at; it is, however a very
expensive; they charge the enormous sum I believe of

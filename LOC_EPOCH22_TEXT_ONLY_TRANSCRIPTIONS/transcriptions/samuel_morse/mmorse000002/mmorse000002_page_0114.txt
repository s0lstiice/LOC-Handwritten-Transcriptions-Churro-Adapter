Boston Dec 31st 1819
My Dear Brothers,
Accompanying this is a letter which I wrote on Saturday in the forenoon.
Mention in it that we are under pretty constant alarms from fire on Thursday 27th in the evening
the fire was in the Exchange building on Friday 28th there was another alarm, which I have to be in
another part of the Exchange, but with but little damage, on Saturday noon. 29th there was another
alarm, the fire was in North street opposite the Old South meeting house, the Old South was on
fire several times, and very narrowly escaped it was the largest fire that has been in Boston
for several years 3 large barns and 2 dwelling houses were consumed with 3 mechanics shops:
beside several which were pulled down to prevent the progress of the fire: It was in the
barns from 2 fast till 12 o'clock, and was covered with mud & cinders all the time. I was there

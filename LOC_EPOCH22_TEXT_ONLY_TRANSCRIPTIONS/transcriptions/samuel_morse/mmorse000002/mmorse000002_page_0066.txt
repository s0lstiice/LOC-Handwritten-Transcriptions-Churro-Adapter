Yale College, New Haven April 21st
My dear Parents.
I am surprised that scarcely any of my
letters have reached you: for my own part I have written
regularly once a fortnight and the two last letters were quite
long, I cannot conceive the reason that you do not get
them more regularly. I have received but 2 or 3 letters
from you this term, and was very particular in answering
all your questions: yesterday Mr Dwight told me
that he had received a letter from you requesting him
to inform me that you had not recieved a letter
from either of us for 6 weeks. I wrote one
about the middle of march, and another on the
3d day of April, what has become of them I cannot
tell; indeed I feel very much hurt that every letter
I receive from home instead of acknowledgements
of letters, I receive nothing but reproofs for not writing
often, when I take so much pains this term to write
long and regularly, I really hope this will reach
you before I get home; or I shall be induced to write
letters and carry them myself rather than have them
misseasy in this manner. The mails have been

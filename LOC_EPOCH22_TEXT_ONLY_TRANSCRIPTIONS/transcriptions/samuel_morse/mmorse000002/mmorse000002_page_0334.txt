I am glad to hear the Geography is going on so well, I
am afraid, I shall make but a poor bargainer, but I will
do my best when you send it.
By this time, you have probably received notice of
my receiving the draft safe, it is accepted and all is right
I am afraid my expenses will exceed my allowance for
the first year, but the next will make them even again, if
I exceed it, it will be but by a few pounds; Mr Bromfield
said he expected it would be so, on account of my necessary
expenses in establishing myself; I shall use all my inducement
to economize as much as possible, and reduce my expenses
to as narrow a compass as possible.
We make our own tea and coffee just as Mr Codman
used to do. I mentioned to you in a former letter that I have
removed my lodgings to No 82. in the same street where I am
much better situated with a young american artist from
Philadelphia by the name of Leslie, you heard me mention him
when I was at home, he is the young man who gained so much
reputation in Philadelphia by taking the likeness of Cook in his
celebrated characters, he is a very agreeable industrious, steady,
young fellow, and our best together makes my absence from home
very agreeable; Mr Parkman returns in a few days to America and
will tell you all about us, he was quite socialable, and intimate intimate
with us, I like him very much.

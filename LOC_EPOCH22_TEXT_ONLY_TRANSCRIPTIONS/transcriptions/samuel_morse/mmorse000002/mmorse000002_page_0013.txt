I feel as tho I had stolen it & it is with the greatest pain that
I part with it. I think every minute I shall receive a letter from
home blaming me for not being more economical & thus
I am kept in distress all the time.
The amount of my expenses for the last term was
$15.10 & is founded in the following manner
For my expenses I do not include my
wood, tuition bills, board or washing
bills -- my washing was last term $8.00
Prince does not wash for me now, but
Mr. Moultriep; the washes very well indeed --
Edwds & myself are very well & I
believe we are doing well, but you will
learn more of that from our instructor.
In about 2 months I hope I shall
have the pleasure of seeing home once
more, the time passes away very fast.
I must now bid you Adieu for
I am rather tired at present. Give my love to Grand Mama, Aunt
Richard, & Nancy. I hope I shall see
them soon.
P.S. The students are very fond of flying
balloons at present; I will (with
your leave) when I return home
make one they are pleasing
sights. Edwds carried the
latter part of your letter to Mr. Evarts
I have not received any answer from
Mr. Evarts since.
Yours etc.
S.F.B. Moser

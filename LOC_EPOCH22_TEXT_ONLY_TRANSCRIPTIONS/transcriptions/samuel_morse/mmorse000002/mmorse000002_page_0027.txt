It was constructed entirely in the Gothic taste with two
turrets one of which appeared to be entire, the other
nearly fallen to ruins, it was in this last turret that
I perceived the light which surprised me so much a
few minutes before this tended in some measure to in-
crease my astonishment as in all probability if the
castle was inhabited by human beings, they would make
choice of the most convenient part of it to dwell in.
I now opened the great door of the building with
conducted me into a spacious chapel, I passed softly
by several large pillars, when stopping I could
distinctly hear footsteps approaching me. Scarcely
venturing to breathe, I turned, and by the pale light
of the moon, rendering the gloom of the place just
visible, I perceived a tall, unacciated figure, slowly
walking towards me, as he passed me he beckoned me
to follow him, I, not daring to refuse, followed him
thru the chapel, down several pairs of stairs into the
vault beneath the castle, my guide then taking a
dim taper from the last staircase walked on some
distance; he then turned to the right, and applying a
key to the door of a gloomy dungeon, we entered it;
at the same time my conductor extinguishing
his light disappeared, leaving me in total darkness.

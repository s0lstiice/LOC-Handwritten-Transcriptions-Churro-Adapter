My Dear Parents,
I have just this moment received
a package of letters from you, by the Milg
which I assure you was very grateful to me, I
was glad to hear that all my letters from
New York, had reached you safely & that you
were in some degree relieved from your anxieties
by them; I know your feelings & can feel
with you, & wish now you had the letter
that I am writing, but at such a distance
it cannot be expected, news will arrive in
an instant, if it will be any relief to you
to know that I feel for your anxiety, you
may be assured of it; & that I shall do all
in my power in every way, to render you
happy by being economical, steady & industrious.

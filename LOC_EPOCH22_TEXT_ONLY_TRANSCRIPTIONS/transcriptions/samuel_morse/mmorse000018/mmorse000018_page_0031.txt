Washington June 21st 1844
6 o'clock
To
Dear Vail -- (at City Baltimore)
I have but a moment to say I am faithful as a
Christian will be tried, as well as my son, in the blow up
which has taken place. I go before the Court this morning.
Mr S. has written me a passionate letter, accusing my friend
Mr Elsworth in the most offensive terms. Defend upon it
such feeling cannot cover any but the darkest designs, upon
us all. I am sick at heart, & can only find relief in that
refuge, which in the hour of seeming hopelessness, has appeared
so often to have done me joy. Let this be your hope & trust,
& all will come out right. I have no fear for the result.
No time to add. Truly Yours - S P B More.
Washington July 2nd 1844.
Dear Vail
I am yet held by the leg, but am convalescent,
I hope to be able to be in Baltimore before the end
of the week. I hope this will find you safely arrived
with your family. I am anxiously awaiting instruction from
The Department, the complexion of which is of importance
to both of us. I doubt Has whether I
can draw out any money from the Appropriations.

such a construction of the law, & am Encouraged to,

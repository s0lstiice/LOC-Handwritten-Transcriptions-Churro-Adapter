me a power of attorney as Galt has to act for you? that will
simplify the matter, & then I will bring matters to a head,
with Smith in some shape that will enable us to move
on without our present embarrassment. Write me & let
me know whether the wires are repaired, what Cleveland
says of the character of the break, & any other matters con-
cerned with things at Baltimore.
Truly yours as ever S H B Morse
Washington July 3d 1844
Dear Wail
I have just received a letter from Mr Rogers informing
me of your arrival, which I was glad to learn. I was not
so glad to learn this morning that another break has
occurred in the wires. There was a heavy puff yesterday
morning which made me remark at the time that I
fear'd for my wires. I wish to repair them, that
Cleveland would particularly note the break, & see if
it shows accident or design. I was thinking that I
would attempt to go out tomorrow, & therefore have you

The Gamma Hall Ceremony College June 26th 1844.
Sir
As the corresponding committee of the Phi
Bamma Society and authorized respectfully
to inform you that by the unanimous choice of that
Body, you have been elected to the privileges of
honorary membership.
May we be permitted to hope
that at your earliest convenience you will act
knowledge the receipt of this and suffer us to
enroll your name on the list of those who
will delight to regard as the friends of our
enterprise and the advocates and patrons of
science.
Very respectfully
Yours
William A. Simmons
William B. Merritt
Hamilton Ro. Pierce

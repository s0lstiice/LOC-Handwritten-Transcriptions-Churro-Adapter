Whereas, I Samuel J B Morse of the City
and State of New York am the sole and ori-
ginal Inventor of a System of Electric Mag-
netic Telegraphs, for the conveyance of intel-
ligence by words and signs or by either, and
to secure my legal rights and property in said
invention, I have taken out letters patent which
letters patent of the United States bear date the
seventh day of April A D Eighteen hundred and
thirty eight.
And it be therefore known, to all to whom
these present shall come, that I the said
Morse for a valuable consideration to me
in hand paid by Alfred Vail of Morristown
in the county of Morris, in the State of New
Jersey, the receipt whereof I do hereby acknowledge,
have assigned, bargained, sold, and set
over, and do hereby assign bargain, sell and
set over, and convey unto the said Vail, his
heirs, and assigns one undivided eighth part
of my said invention, and all my right title
and interest which I have in the said invention
as secured to me by said Letters Patent,
which may be hereafter granted
by the Government of the United States and
within the limits thereof.
Also one undivided eighth part of all the
right title and property which I have or may
secure or otherwise derive from and in said
invention without the limits of the United
States, by letters patent or otherwise, excepting
in the Republic of Texas, hereby making and
constituting said Vail part and joint owner

The proposition above is to deliver the Telegraph
ready for use between Philadelphia & New York or such
point opposite the cities as shall be nearest & most
feasible in case difficulty be found in cropping to the
city at this time -- and without extra charge -- and in-
cluding the exclusive right to use the same -- promising
that you procure the Railroad Company's assent to
erect the posts on their line.
The total expense of sustaining it in operation day &
night between those two points will not exceed as near
as I can now estimate $2,500. per annum for each sta-
-tion. Superintending the construction is
included in the above proposition. But superintending
the use of the Telegraph will be only such an expense
as will procure the services of young men who can be
instructed at a trifling cost in all the essential of
the business in a few days all which I will attend to
for a fair compensation. --
The right to use the Telegraph is put at a small
sum in the above proportion in order to enable it
to be in hands that will teach the public and the
Government how mighty an engine the Telegraph

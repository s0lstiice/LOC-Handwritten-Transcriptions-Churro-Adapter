Received of Sam. F. B. Morse one of the proprietors of
the Electric Magnetic Telegraph one instrument for the
illustration of the principles on which the Telegraph is
constructed, which I hereby agree to use only for the
purpose of public lectures and to return the same to the
said Morse at New York in good order one year after
date hereof.
Sept. 23. 1844 Boston.
J. F. Boynton
For value received I promise to pay on demand
to Sam. F. B. morse inventor of the Electric Magnetic Telegraph
Fifty dollars, being in full, with the instrument, in payment
of the fee for the use of said instrument for one year from
date.
Boston Sept. 23. 1844.
J. F. Boynton

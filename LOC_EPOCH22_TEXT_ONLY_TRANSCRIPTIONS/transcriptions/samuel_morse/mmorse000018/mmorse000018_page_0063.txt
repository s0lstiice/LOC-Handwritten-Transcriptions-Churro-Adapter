Washington June 24. 1844
My letter of 21. just finds me confined to my bed
with a serious wound in my leg from a fall, and a probability
of a long confinement which must be my apology for not answering
your letter immediately on its receipt. I am unable at this moment
to reply to your queries. The proprietors of the Telegraph are
dispersed one of them being in Maine, and I shall be compelled
to wait for answers from them previous to giving you
definitely the answers you desire. I will write to them
however immediately and hope in the course of a few
days to be able to give you all the information
you wish.
In the mean time
Believe me with great respect
Y. Mothens
S. P. B. Morse
3
To J. Reese Fry.
Ed. N. Amer.

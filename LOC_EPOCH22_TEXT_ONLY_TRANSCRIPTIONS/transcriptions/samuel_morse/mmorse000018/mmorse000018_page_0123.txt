Dear Sir
Yours of the 1st Inst. I have received, and you may
be assured I will lose no time in apprising you of the
determination of my partners the moment I hear from them.
I informed you of the causes that might delay my
answer, not knowing how to direct to one of the proprietors
that may be done little time before I can receive it. But I am
directing letters to him at two or three points, and hope to hear
in a day or two. I am convalescent and shall probably
pass through Phil. to New York at close of this week or
beginning of next

Baltimore July 4th 1844
Dear Prof. Moone
I wrote to you this morning
stating that I would be ready tomorrow for
experiment. I however shall not be ready
so early as I contemplated in consequence
of all the stores being shut up, and I have
not been able to get the acid I want for
the experiments, I shall however be early at
7 in the morning and as your east &
Ground circuit is closed you will know
when I am ready by the ringing of the bell
if it is in a condition to be rung when I am
ready — If at 11 o'clock no action takes place
send to the cars for a letter, as I will write
if any thing is wrong — wire broken &c — and
at 11 o'clock I will change to West & Ground.
Then after we get started you can order the
experiments to suit yourself — write if you can
not attend yourself by tomorrow mornings train
I do hope that no impediment will be put in
our way towards continuing the Telegraph in
operation during the Summer & Fall, by stopping
the appropriation — I presume Sec. Babb will
leave it to the discretion of Mr. Young who will
I feel assured yield to the wishes of several here
% whom he is indebted for his place, and who will
see him whenever you desire it. Mr. Rogers tells me
this — The wires which were broken at Bladenburg

New York June 22d 1844
S. S. B. Morse Esq
Dear Sir
I have been expecting you
in the City, but learned yesterday from
your British Sidney that you might not be
here for some time -- I have witnessed the
succes of your Telegraph with great gratifica-
tions, and most heartily congratulate you
on your perfect triumph -- It has secured
to you as much fame as you could de-
rive, and will bring you as much money
as will make you independent for
the rest of life -- I am domiciled with
my family in the family of your friend Doctor
Gale, and so far am only pleasantly situated -- I have conversed with him much
about the Telegraph, and he informs me
that he has a small interest in it, but
has given you a Carl Blanch for any arrangement for its disposal Which you may
think proper to make -- I did suppose
that your proposals to Congress would have received a final action, notwithstanding

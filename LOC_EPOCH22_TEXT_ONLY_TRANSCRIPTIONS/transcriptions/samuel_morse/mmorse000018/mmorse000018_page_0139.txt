Workh, July 4th 1844
Dear Sir,
Your of the 1st inst. is received and in reply I would observe that the Telegraph in October
and belongs to other individuals in common with myself; there being almost I could not say exactly
terms the use of the Telegraph would be allotted to
Personally I should be willing to consult the wishes of
those scientific lecturers who desire to explain the action of my Telegraph, but where there are proprietors
who have invested money in the enterprise, and look
of converse for their remuneration by a sale of rights,
to construct and rule the instruments, you will see the
property of refraining from such construction and use
until some terms shall have been agreed upon by which
you can be lawfully done. So soon as we have settled
the terms I will apprise you of them.
In regard to your employment as a mechanician
such will doubtless be wanted, and should a person
be engaged to Catherine that part of the business, I will
take pleasure in mentioning your name that he may apply
to you
Respectfully yours
S. P. Bannor
Mr. S. W. Chubbuck
Talon Madison Ct N.York

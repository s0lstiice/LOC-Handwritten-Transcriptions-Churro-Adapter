Baltimore June 29, 1844
Professor S. F. B. Morse,
Dr Sir,
Mr. Vail informed
me the day he passed through this city
from Washington to Philadelphia, that
you contemplated engaging me as
one of your assistants, provided I
would be willing to embark in the enter-
prise at the risk of your getting an ap-
propriation from the Government next
winter.
He also informed me you would be
willing to have my salary stipulated at
$2.50 per day out of the present ap-
propriation.
I have no doubt that an appropriation
will be made to extend your telegraph,
and it is the opinion of several gentle
men of my acquaintance who are at-
tached to the Post Office Department,
that it must be done; among the
number are Doctor Howard Kennedy &
Major Robbie -- at all events I am wil-
ling to sink my risk & sink
or swim with the Telegraph, I hereby
inform you of my decision.

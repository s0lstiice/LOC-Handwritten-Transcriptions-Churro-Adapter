Gentlemen,
Your favor of the 26th ulto is received
announcing to me in behalf of the Phi Gamma Society
my election as an honorary member. I say you are
grateful to the Society my sincere thanks for their kindness
and my acceptance of the honor they have been
pleased to confer, and I am
Very Respectfully yours
S. B. Adams
Corresponding Comr of the Phi Gamma Society
99725
Washington July 3/1844
To Wm. A. Simmons
Wm. B. Manitt
Hamilton R. Pierce.

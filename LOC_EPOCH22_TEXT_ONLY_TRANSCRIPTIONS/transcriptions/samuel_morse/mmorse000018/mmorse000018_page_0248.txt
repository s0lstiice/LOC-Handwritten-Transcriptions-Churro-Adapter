The Syro-Egyptian Society of London
Aug 30th 1860
Sir I am requested by the Council of the Syro-Egyptian
Society to forward you a Copy of their Prospectus, and
to state that they will feel their hands much strengthened
if you will kindly permit them to appoint you their
Agent and Referee for New York. You will perceive
that the Society are anxious to avail themselves of
every opportunity of obtaining authentic information
from the Orient, and of rendering facilities to gentlemen
who may be travelling or residing abroad to uphold
the objects which they have in view, by becoming
Non-Resident Members (See page 8) – It is obvious
that this can only be accomplished by appointing
Agents in the Capital Towns, & in other important
Localities. You will therefore greatly oblige
the Council by affording them the advantage of
your influence and valuable co-operation – It is
of course necessary that their Agents should satisfy
themselves of the respectability of the parties they may
recommend to the Council for election (See page 12)
and I send you herewith a Form which can, at

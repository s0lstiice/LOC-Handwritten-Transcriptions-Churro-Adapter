Baltimore Sept 3d 1841
My dear Sir
I was about dispatching you a line to
day in relation to what I see this morning from a
NY s paper - Not that Mr Cutten doing can
injure what I am trying to accomplish - under
I have other men prominent to deal with.
but leaving what I would like so well to accomplish aside - I am dreadfully hampered at this! No
one perhaps stood higher as an upright citizen,
an honest Man, an exemplary Christian! one
who has taken so active a part in furthering the
cause of religion & all this - I could have hoped
that he would have withstood such temptation.
I suppose he having always moved in the first circle, was
by pride laid in to this in order to sustain himself - I said
not divvied anything out of the ordinary course when
with him recently in NY. - And I do hope that
you will not excuse me for introducing him to your
attention, for what I have done, I trust I
have done conscientiously - for I strongly aver, that

Baltimore July 22d 1844.
Passion More
Dear Sir
As I have an opportunity to send
by private conveyance, I am to inform you on Saturday the Court has come, I say he has
commenced to take up the lead pipe -- He says he has not heard a word from Mr Smith since he left
him on this way home.
I have taken the liberty to say too, I found in the papers in reply to a suggestion made in relation to my Washington
agency in case the Common is bought up by a Company, that it would be very important to retain such as have some knowledge of it, and that I am authorized by Passports to say in
such case as will be pleased to take any opportunity. I hasten to add that this has not yet found any attention he can with a commission adequate think and should be pleased to retain
any agency under, or with him, especially in the construction of new homes -- As I said in my former letter, it is very
important that I have that part of the City of the Vale, although I have since nothing to do with you I will do.
If you will forward it of Mr Gaby, I shall be much obliged -- and shall be sure of your reply in a day or
two.
Yours truly
David Baudensk.

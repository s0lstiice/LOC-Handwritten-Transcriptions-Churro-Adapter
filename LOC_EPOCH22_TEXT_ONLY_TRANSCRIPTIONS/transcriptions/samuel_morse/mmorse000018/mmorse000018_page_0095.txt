Washington June 25, 1824
I will ask leave to employ Mr. Rogers at $2 a 500000000000000000000000000000000

His duties will be to take charge of the apparatus at the terminuses
that may be designated, to operate the Switch, to see that all matters
in connection with his terminuses are in order. He must be prepared
at all hours day & night for corresponding & keeping a record of all that
is sent and received by the Telegraph. This per hour stops delivery of any
branch of the line on his part of the line. An instructor is allowed to
him at 1 dollar per day whom he must dispatch on the route properly
equipped to repair any break. The bearer of the break is awarded the
bottle for all parties. If the bearer has been made by design
and the offender should be caught then since, then the fine
shall be applied to the payment of the stop fee item. This provision
is made to give the Directors of Stations an interest in having the
line in repair. Without it there would seem to be the capital defect of
an interest to have the line out of order.
Let Cleveland prepare a rope ladder, and make some
experiments of repair, by putting oak pins in the tops of
the posts thus; the pins may be 16 inches long and an inch
in diameter
The articles wanted for repair ordinarily will be, the rope ladder
a light pole with an iron cross at top, a coil of wire of 150
feet in length, a spirit lamp constructed as over
leaf, a stick of tin for solder, a bottle of Chloride of Zinc
a pair of cutting callipers, a bottle of alcohol, & matches
all but 1 & 2 in a small basket.

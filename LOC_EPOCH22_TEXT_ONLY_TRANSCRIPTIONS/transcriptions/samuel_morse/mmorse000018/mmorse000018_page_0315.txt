It is hereby agreed by & between Francis
Spivacke & myself, & in the interest and
the interest of Prof. Gale, as Superintendent of Morris
Patent, of an Electric Telegraph Subscription-
That the terms and conditions of a certain
writing entered into by & between said parties,
and also with Alford Rail as another property,
having date and relating to
the construction of said Telegraph between the
cities of Boston and New York, are varied and
explained as follows - that is to say - The time
allowed for the construction & completion of said
Telegraph, to the extent of one continuous line of com-
munication between said cities, shall be until
the first day of May next instead of two years provided
in said writing - and the convenience of the night
if made under said writing, shall be in the exclusive
use of the Telegraph between Boston & New York and
all incidents of said cities, to far as to include all
encroachments on, or interference with the interests
of the line so conveyed, by any new or other
lines under the same patentee's rights - and it
shall also be for the right to enter in said cities,
or upon the route described in said writing, as
many distinct and separate lines of com-
munication as the parties shall elect from
time to time to erect, within the limits, on or
the line of the route specified - and all intermediate
distances and points - and the payment
within 5 miles of New York city halls, and of Boston City House

So that in using the two lateral wires for telegraphic
purposes between the P O & E I presume we may
have difficulty. If so, I will abandon them, and
use the main wires for that purpose, which I
can readily do, — I fear that they (the two lateral
wires) may have to come down. For during a heavy
rain storm on Tuesday last, all at once we
could not work, and Rogers says his pen was
kept up against the paper the whole time, I also
experienced much difficulty with my big magnet,
But the next morning every thing went off well
again. The storm being over. There is no doubt
that it will not do to put up the wires in a
bundle unless much better insulated.
Mr Ellsworth has returned. I have not seen
him. — I have not been able to get the line ready
between the capitol and post office yet. But if
I am not to buy next week telegraphing, I will
have it done. I want to see how it will go.
If you do not return soon will you send me
some more money if you please — I have not
heard from home since you left. Mr Burbank
is making a rush in Philadelphia. How does
the matter look in Boston? I guess the telegraph will
not be up by the 1st of February. Please write
as soon as you can.
Yours obet Bert
Alfred Pail

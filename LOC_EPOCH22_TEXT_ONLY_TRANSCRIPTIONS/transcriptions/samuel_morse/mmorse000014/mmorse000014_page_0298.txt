Samuel F. B. Morse Esq.
Dear Sir
Since writing to you having reflected
on the subject of your business before Congress, and perceiving that it is not of
the nature of a regular claim, but materially different, that it is an
application for an appropriation for an experiment merely and that the
invention is yet not thoroughly tested, _ and that the whole appropriation
does not go into your pocket as that for a claim would: _ Under this view
of the case, I accept your offer of five per cent commission in your letter
of the 3d Inst. on the condition named therein viz: _ provided you succeed in
getting the bill appropriating thirty thousand dollars through all the stages till
"it becomes a law."
I am Sir With much regard
Your obedt Servt
Isaac N. Coffin
P.S. I will have a Resolution offered
referring the papers in the old Report to
Committee of the House or other
Committee. Please send me by Return
Mail away further papers or proof you have,
and to write whether you have any additional
proof, so that it can all be presented at once,
and so that the claim may be in progress.

My dear Sir,
Your Caroline letter of the 30th reached me
here three days ago, reminded me of what I had entirely
forgotten until the State telegraph, which I had
sent you a year ago, but hoped you had been able to
resume your labour in Conway.
I enclose the receipt -- take the liberty to
return the note, with the view of leaving the matter to
your own convenience. I believe I am in debt to the
Observer if you choose will take your two dollars
out in dollars reading -- a day -- and I will see some
advertisement of a Telegraphic Atlas -- that would best
my taste -- in short apply it for me as you please
Nothing new here. I am up for 30 or 4 days to
clear my papers. That anxiety, to meet us in the fate
of man's kind, to any empty pockets -- & fine words
from false friends -- Eccles was full of interesting
news the newspaper contained some of my matters.
I have rarely had the management of the fruit
of my Hunting Estate -- I find after seven years of total
anxiety, my fortunes so reduced by the sad changes in
Commerce public speculative work, that I am thinking

I have much more to communicate but
cannot by letter. I would come on to
see you if I had the means, but I
have not a copper. Now what are
immensely wanted are two complete pet,
at least, of the apparatus, the Register and
the Correspondent, and, if possible 20
miles of wire, so that everything may be
listed here at home before I am back.
I have a most excellent workman at
command, who would erect them well
and pleasantly. It is at once seen how
important it is to have mattersunicate
instantly under way, if it is intended to
take advantage of this Russian engagement.
I wish to have everything in prime
order so as to prefixise the Czar, and for
this purpose the room I have the apparatus
complete, the better; indeed, if I
had five hundred dollars ($500) of my own,
(and it will not all cost much more)
I would commence operations immediately.

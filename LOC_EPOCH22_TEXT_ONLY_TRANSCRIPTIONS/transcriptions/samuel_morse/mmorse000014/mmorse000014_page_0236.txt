Flatbush Sept. 4. 1841
Sam'l P. J. Mose, Esq.
Dear Sir,
This will be presented
to you by my Son Benjamin W. Bartow, who is
a student of Medicine under Dr. Tubiskie of this place.
He is desirous of being admitted as a Pupil in the National
Academy of Design, over which you preside. He has some
natural taste for drawing & painting, and has reason
to believe, that with the aid of instruction, he could
render his talent in that way subservient to his
professional Studies, and a means of diminishing
expenses which we cannot otherwise provide for.
I am encouraged by your benevolent character,
and the obliging manner in which you were pleased
to receive my application in his behalf, to recommend
him to your Patronage, of which I trust he will be
found worthy.
I am, dear Sir
Respectfully Yours,
Wm Bartow

RECEIVED, New York, Augt 6 1841, from Mr. Samuel F. B. Morse, Thirty
dollars, being the aggregate sum collected on three quarterly instalments of my subscription to a fund
raised in the year 1837, for procuring from Mr. Morse a large Historical picture, which sum is by the above pay-
ment refunded by him in full.
Thomas Cole
Direct to me at the N.Y. Observer Office. —

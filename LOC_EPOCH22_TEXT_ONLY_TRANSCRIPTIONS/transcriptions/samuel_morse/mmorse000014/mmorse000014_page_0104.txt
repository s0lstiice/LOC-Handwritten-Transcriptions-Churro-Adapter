The boys, she has thought for some time of writing him a
letter, especially to invite him, and hopes to hear that he has
accepted it. - Mrs Bethany Whittlesey was over there, and told
me I must come over and see her.
I am going over to see about Salisbury this morning.
should have gone yesterday, but heard Mr Elias Edwards I
was going to visit on a journey that afternoon, and concluded
I would wait till after the trouble of departure was over,
before I went.
Every thing looks so delightful to me, father, the streets are
so quiet & nicely, and I already begin to feel emigrants. -
the Division has just put her head in, & says I must give
her respects to you, and tell you, she was very glad to
see me - I must hasten come at all too early, and hopes you will
stay here, when you visit at Haven. They are a lovely
family, and I already begin to feel quite at home.

Pittsburg Dec. 6/41
My Dear Sir
you favor of the 3d. inst. is at
hand
I participate most sentimently
of the feeling you express in relation
to the mutilation of our countrymen
to support themselves in their business
enterprises, I in their national power,
to promote and replant in my
praying capacity, enterprise & capital. But, money is the only earthly
instrument to compete with money --
now it has the power, no spite of general
considerations, to make itself heard,
felt and obeyed wherever directed --
I wish you had the wisdom to spend
the interest on it -- you should be there,
not certainly -- but doubtful whether
I shall go there this winter -- if I do,
to write or see the first in reality have
7 January -- But I have invested &
expended for your invention all &

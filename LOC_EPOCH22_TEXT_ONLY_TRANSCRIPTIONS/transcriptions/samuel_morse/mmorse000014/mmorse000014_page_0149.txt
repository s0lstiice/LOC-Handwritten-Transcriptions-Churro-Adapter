to Missouri
Monrnan Abel Bender
Attaché on Missouri 2d
Dear Sir
I have received your letter explaining that which
was published under your name some days ago by Mr.
Younaud and which has caused much trouble
and temporary injury through the infamous conduct
and unpractifical deceitful man. Believe me, my dear Sir, I had already
before seeing you or knowing that you were in the country
made your apology to my own mind. I saw that you
had been ruined by him, and that he must have as I had
been, and I had fixed the blame not upon you, the second
but upon him the deceitful man. I feel myself under due
circumstances, & if I had any opportunity to have should have felt hurt
by your suspension that I could not have remained as
you attribute to me, yet being persuaded that you could not be
necessarily supposed to know any thing of me, I was not disposed
to favor indignation towards him only who had
defended you. The apology you made most entirely
applies to my letter dated 2d the 11th of October
1840.

whole apparatus to such a degree that it would be
scarcely known by those who saw it in Paris. I
have also added parts making the whole more
completely useful. For example The Register is
set in motion to record the intelligence, and when the
intelligence is recorded, its movement stops of its
own accord; it does not require a person
present to stop it, so that many communications
may be successively sent without detriment
to the correspondence, in case the attendant should be
absent. Confessed as I am to devote myself at
present, to other duties, I am not able to send you

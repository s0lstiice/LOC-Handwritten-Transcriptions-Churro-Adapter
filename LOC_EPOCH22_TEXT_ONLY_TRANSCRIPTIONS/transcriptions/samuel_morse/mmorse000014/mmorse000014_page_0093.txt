[June 20, 1840]
The U. States Patent Office,
TO ALL PERSONS TO WHOM THESE PRESENTS SHALL COME,
GREETING:
This is to Certify, That the annexed is a true Copy of the Specification and Draw-
ing of Letters Patent granted to Samuel F. B. Morse, the twentieth of June, eighteen hun-
dred and forty, as reissued the thirteenth of June, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.
In Testimonial Wherewith, I, Thomas Ewbank, Commissioner of Patents, have caused
the Seal of the Patent Office to be hereto affixed this twelfth day of December, in the
year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty-nine, and of the Independence of
the United States the seventy-fourth.
THO'S EWBANK.
L.S.

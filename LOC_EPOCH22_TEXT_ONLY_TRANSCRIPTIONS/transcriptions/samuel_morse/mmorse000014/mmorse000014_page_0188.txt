I have had a very pleasant week here, on the whole,
& the weather has been really charming the past week.
Betty and I have had some fine holiday rides,
and father & friend has quite cured my various
aches. -- I shall keep this open till Sunday eve. I think
and then put it in, as I want you to get it before Tuesday.
All friends here are well -- and thrifty. I am as busy
as possible preparing for my journey -- if I go. After I
shall have a great deal of time to do with, and I
am pleased that my dear father agrees with me, in
his plan. -- It is now quite late, and I must
close -- as I shall so soon see you -- It is not
necessary to add more, only that I beg to
send you -- All friends here send kind regards to
you -- Believe me
Your aff daughter
Susan

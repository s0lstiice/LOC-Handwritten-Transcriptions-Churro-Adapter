15 State Ct
December 6th 1871
Dr. Sir
Hearing returned to the City, reply on
the 4th I take the earliest opportunity to
acknowledge your Circular Letter of the 30th ult.
enclosing Ten Dollars together with your note
for five Dollars - the receipt of my payments
towards the National Picture
At your request I now receive a
receipt for fifteen dollars & I also enclose
your note for five Dollars - as I recollect
that I obtained a Daguerreotype likeness
from you last Winter
If the article in the Tribune to which
you allude I know nothing
Repelling that anything should induce
you to defer the execution of your Historical
Pictures I am yours Very Respectfully
B B Mason Esq
Robert Davidson

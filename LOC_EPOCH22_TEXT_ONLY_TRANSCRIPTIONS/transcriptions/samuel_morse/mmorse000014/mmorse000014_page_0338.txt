(Private.)
Albany June 17. 1842.
Dear Sir,
The Geological survey of this state has been completed, and the
final report is in proof. The first volume will appear on the 16th of August.
It has been thought very desirable to give an introduction setting forth the
history of the progress and present condition of literature, science and the arts
in the State of New York. May I duly give to furnish you with notes for a memoir
of this undertaking as relative to the arts of design? The more full the notes
should be, the more useful; yet it is my opinion that such notes as shall be
submitted by the Scientific and literary Gentlemen throughout the state,
shall render it into a brief compendious article. The magnitude of the final
report of the Survey and the interest it is expected to excite seem to favor the
design of making better known to the world the scientific and literary
achievements of the citizens of this State.
Sam. Sis. with my high respect.
Your humble servant.
William H. Severance
F. F. P. Morris, Esq.
President of the Academy of Design.
New York.

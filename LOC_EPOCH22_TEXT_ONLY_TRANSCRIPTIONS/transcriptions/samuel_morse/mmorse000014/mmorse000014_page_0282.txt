dollar forever a year. You know I am given to
hobbying, I shall like to take up my plank ship
built in 1840 & 1841 & I am in hopes of getting a contract
to put up some Coal barges for a large Company
next year. With an entering wedge in the public
sense, I could soon give a good faith to a moderate
fortune & exhibit the true commercial way of making
navigation safe to the public.
If I did do that, why I must involve the
fortunes of the Philadelphia Penitentiary Society the people & that I
have a right to work for my living & why I should not.
In good earnest I am ripe already
now for a change of life & habitation if need be too.
I am ready after next May to be the people humble
servant 7 hours a day in any reasonable humble
position my talents may be equal to.
I can tell you one thing, I like your independent
name in the matter of your Circular & hope
you will before long give us a picture such
as you can tonight to produce.
With Sentiments of esteem
and affection
JDW
S F B Mckinley

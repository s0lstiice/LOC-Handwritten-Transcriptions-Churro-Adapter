My dear Sir
I return you with many thanks
the Leathe checks which you had the goodness
to send me some weeks since
They have been copied by two of our artists &
I have no doubt will do much good.
We are much pleased with Mr Good of Sa-
who brought letters from Dr Draper & yourself last
October.
Have you seen the bust of my Father --
executed by Mr Ives now a resident in your
city -- who are much pleased with it -- Mr
Ives is a most worthy young man & an excellent
Artist & I hope may succeed well in U.S.
Mr Brown & Col. T. Vanderbilt think very high
of his works --
My respects to your Daughter & believe
me yours truly
Blissman H.
Prof. J. R. B. Morse --

to Professor Morse —
My Dear Nephew.
I have not been very well lately — I
have become rather debilitated, and with the reduction
of strength, Spiney my spirits have been very much depressed
had it not have been for this, I should have promptly
have written to you to thank you for the books you sent
me, all of which will I think (even though I have
to read them) afford me amusement as well as information
for their sake you will please accept of my thanks
I find your citizens have had a great meeting
in the Park on the subject of your provision ships
Your people are not yet ready I find to succumb to
the dictation of a foreign ecclesiastic — or to follow
the advice of Gov. Leland — I quite agree
against Antichrist and his followers — as well
as against Gov. Leland and his electionary
messages
We are all about as usual in
health and send love to you & your
better Sidney & his wife
From your devoted Uncle
S. Sidney Breese

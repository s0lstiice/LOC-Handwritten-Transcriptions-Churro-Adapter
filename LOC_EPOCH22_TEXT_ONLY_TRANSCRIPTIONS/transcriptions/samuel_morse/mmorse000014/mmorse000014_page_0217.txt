P.S. Since writing the above, I have ascertained that Goven abovementioned is here

the top of the Capitol over the Senate -- the member that reported in favor of his plan
in the Senate is still here, and I presume will do all in his power to cause the adoption
of his plan, the cheapness of which is an advantage in his favor.
I am Sir
Very Respectfully Yours Isaac A. Coffins

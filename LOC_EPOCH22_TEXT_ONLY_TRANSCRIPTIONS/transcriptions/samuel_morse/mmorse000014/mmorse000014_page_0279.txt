From the very low state of the Treasury from the Tariff coming down to the lowest
state at this time -- and as the Government have confessed their Estimates into the narrowest
profitable limits, and have come into power with the pledge of Reform of expenditure, and the
other party violently oppose all expenditures for fear of consequence of a high Tariff, -- these facts
show that both parties are striving every nerve to cut off expenditures, which will naturally give
the opposition to the claim from both parties, and difficulty of obtaining it. Under all the
circumstances if the above remuneration will be allowed only in case the claim is obtained, and if
not obtained, notwithstanding whatever trouble, time, expense or fatigue, -- it will
make the greatest exertions possible to procure the passage of a Law for the claim, through all
the Committees and all the Stages of both Houses -- infinitely greater exertions I am sure than
any other person whatever ever has, but otherwise I cannot go on, for it is useless for me to
wear out my life on the mere chance for a claim to which there must be the most powerful opposition,
and which therefore whatever may be the efforts, is highly probable to fail
of passing. Please reply immediately on the reception of this on the
above subject of the per-centage, as if I go on with it I wish to state
to you immediately at great length the systematic mode, method, and
exertions which I should adopt to forward it in the most efficient manner
-- that I may know whether I am to go on or not as if I go with this
claim, I wish to commence immediately to make every arrangement necessary to
promote it: and your courtesies in so doing will be at all times reciprocated.
I am With the Highest Esteem & Regard
Your Obd Servt
Isaac N Cofflin

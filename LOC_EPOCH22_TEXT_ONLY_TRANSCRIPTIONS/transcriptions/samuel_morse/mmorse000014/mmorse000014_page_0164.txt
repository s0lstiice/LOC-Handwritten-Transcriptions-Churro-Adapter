New Orleans Dec. 21st 1840
Dear Sir
I am here all safe, but have not yet prepared to go into full operation as I am preparing to put up a place on the roof of a house to observe on what will be finished this week. But I have made some trials for the purpose of proving testing the lever, etc. and find every thing pretty well exact the most important part which is the Semic, and in them I am much disappointed, for the first place they have many vanes in them, one of them has more than through its center & larger in its sides, the other has some heavy ones a little on one side of the center about in this position with many yet heavier near the sides, whether they would materially affect the image I am not yet able to say, as my trials thus far have not been sufficiently minute to detect the imperfection should they produce any. But the great difficulty is that they form an imperfect image in in any position and I have tried them in all, and find that they form the bot with both convex sides towards the center though I haven't observed a fault in the face

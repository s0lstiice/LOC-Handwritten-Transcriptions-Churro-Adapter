City of New York 20th Decr 1841
This is to certify that I the undersigned have
granted to Samuel T. B. More of this City
the power of Attorney to act for me as my agent in
making use of such means as may seem judicious in
introducing to practical use Moris Electro-magnetic Telegraph
in which I have an interest.
The most especial design of this paper is to bring the
subject of this Telegraph before the Congress of the
U.S. at their present Session
I am willing to grant to an agent 10 percent of the proceeds
of the Telegraph in case he succeeds in disposing of it
to Congress which I understand to be his proposition
Signed S.D.Gab

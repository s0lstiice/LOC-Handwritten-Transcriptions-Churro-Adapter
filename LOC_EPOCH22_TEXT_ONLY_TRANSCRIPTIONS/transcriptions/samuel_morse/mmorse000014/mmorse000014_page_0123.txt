Mercantile Library Association
New york September 8th 1840
Samuel T.S. Morse Esq
Dear Sir
I have the honor
to inform you that at a meeting of the Board
of Directors of the Mercantile Library Association
held on the evening of Saturday Sept. 5th 1840
you were elected an Honorary Member of
this Institution. Accompanying this I
send you a Diploma and our Constitution.
I will not state to you the character and
objects of our Association; neither its History
situated together in the same building, as we
have been so long, they cannot be unknown
to you. And it is with pleasure that we have it in
our power, to add to the list of our 5000 members,
the name of one so distinguished in the annals
of our time. accept, dear Sir, the assurances of
the high regard with which
I am, Your obedient Servt
Horatio N. Otis Cor. Secy

22.
Memorandum, of
rates for turning $23.2
into dollars. —
This is marked 23.2 in
Smith's account. —
The whole charge whether pounds
or francs, seems to me erroneous,
for I make my regular charges
against him for expenses during
the journey, out of the money
advanced previously, and out of my
own. Examine the memo. of
which he speaks.

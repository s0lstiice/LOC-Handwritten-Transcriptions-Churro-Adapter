Democracy.
Washington April 10, 1862.
Your friend Capt. Williams, of the army, has
written to request that I would send his picture of
me to you for the approaching expedition - shall
you think well of it? He considers it his chief
Vow.
I have just received his letter & regret that I
am not in New York to attend to his wishes.
The picture is in the office, Wall Street,
of my friend, Jos. Blunt Eugene. May I beg that
you will take the trouble to look at it, &
make such use of it as you may desire?
I am afraid that I shall not be in New York
in time to provide any assistant in having the picture
framed for exhibition; but if you deem a plain
vent frame necessary, please order one & I
will pay for it. The picture belongs to Capt.
Williams.
In haste, I remain
Y. M. with great esteem
Winfield Scott.
Professr Morse
President U. S.

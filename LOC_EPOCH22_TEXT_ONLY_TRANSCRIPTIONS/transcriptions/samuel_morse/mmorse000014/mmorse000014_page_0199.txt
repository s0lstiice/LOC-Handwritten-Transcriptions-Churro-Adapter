10 P.M.
Boston May 8th 1841.
Dear Father
I really began to feel worried for I have
not had a single line from you since I left New York and
I have written you from Boston to, to be sure I received a
small package while in St. I haven't devoted so your hands,
but that was not like having a letter. The mails have
done regularly, and I have sent to the Post office so
many times to no purpose, I begin to feel quite discomfited
as to your runaway daughter, she is in good hands and
enjoying herself very much. Charles L. has returned he came
a week before vacation so anxious was he to see me. I am
somewhat he loves me only -- and I wish I could say my
affection was as deep as his for me. I told him how you
preferred, and my friends also -- that I should not engage
myself before leaving for France -- and he says he does not
want to do it -- and will acquittee of course if I think it
to do as you preferred -- will build myself by any engagement
as yet -- He is an honorable young man, I am convinced
and means to do right in all things --

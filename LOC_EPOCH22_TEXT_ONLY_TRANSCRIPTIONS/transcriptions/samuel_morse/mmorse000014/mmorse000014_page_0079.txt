Sunday will make a good farmer, after all
it is the most happy respectable life. You have
some relations near Utica good businessmen
& I would place him with one of them with
a good practical farmer.
As for myself, I am here with my
good friend Dr. Armstrong. Six keep horses
together and I hope to come home again in
2 months or two. I am certainly better since
I have been here. The weather is now very
pleasant, but this winter has been one
of rain, cold, & yellow fever, all things
the West Indies. A worse weather has not
been known for years.
Say to Miss Susan that
if she were our house keeper, her patience
would be tried. We have a free woman and
of course lags -- She was a slave -- in
addition to her, I have a man, & the Doctor
two good sized boys, & yet we have to think for
them -- ever ready to tell them to sweep the
rooms & make the beds. It have country in
no place to be well attended, yet he got on
as well as our neighbors.

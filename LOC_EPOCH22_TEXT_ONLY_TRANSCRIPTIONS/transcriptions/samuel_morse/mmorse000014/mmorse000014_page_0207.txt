Concord. May. 21st 1841.
Dear Father,
I was just going to put a letter in the office
saying you might expect me in the city on Tuesday morning
next, with Linlin Joe when your truly welcome letter
arrived, and changed all my plans completely.
Sincerely do I hope you will come on -- and let it
be, your father as near the 28th as possible, because
Charles' vacation is only Wednesday, and it will be keeping
him from College. I am really sorry that I cannot
return with you for I shall have to go with
any opportunity that presents itself -- as Cousin Joe will return
on Monday, because his vacation is out on Wednesday.
I should like to return at least two days before
he will go. I have many little things to do -- and
arrange, that can only be done in the city --
you know I can stay at the Kittilans, who
now live in Grand St -- and they will be very
happy to have me too.

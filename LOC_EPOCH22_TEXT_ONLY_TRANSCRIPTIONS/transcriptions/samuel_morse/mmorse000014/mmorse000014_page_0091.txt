Jun 15th 1840
Hunman boat Exp.
Dear Sir,
I cannot but express the high gratification with which I witnessed the operation of your
beautiful and ingenious Electro magnetic Engine. It performed more than promised. I was told
that it would raise 500 pounds of steel in one minute. It raised 523 pounds of steel in a minute. From these
data any one can calculate the power which an erection of machinery on this principle, can be able to avail
I have seen a number variety of electro magnetic engines in operation, among which I have also
had submitted to my inspection examination several plans not yet completed. Of these latter I need not speak, as trial
alone will test satisfactorily the principles on which they are constructed, but of the former I can say with truth
Yours is the most powerful, and contained within the smallest compass of any I have seen. The peculiarities
which pleased me in your engine, are the novel construction and arrangement of the magnets and armatures, and the
ingenious mode of breaking and closing the circuit.
and the individual connections of each Plate of the battery with its own conductor.

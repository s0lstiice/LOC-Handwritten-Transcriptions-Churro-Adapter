I add but one word to this statement.
It will not seem strange that with a
mind for years bent on the destruction
of the United States and occupied with
the means for that purpose, that that
mind was not in the bestest mood for
the elaborating a work involving
close study and constant expenses.
I shall immediately commence by
installment the return of the money
advanced to the amount already laid
aside for that purpose, and turn my
attention to provide the means for
paying the remaining $100 as
possible.

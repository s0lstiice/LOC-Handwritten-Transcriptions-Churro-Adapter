New York Dec 20th 1841
My Dear Sir
Yours of the 15th (by the neglect of the servant to
inform me that the Carrier had been here with a letter
in any absence & had taken it back to the office) was
not received until yesterday -- I haste to send you the
power of Attorney provided
I know nothing about the forms necessary for such
an instrument but presume that the accompani
ments may answer the purpose
Remain yours Truly
S. D. Gab

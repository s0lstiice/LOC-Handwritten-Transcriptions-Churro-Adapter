1841
Feb 4, 1841
Magnificent
My Dear Sir,
I have reflected
upon the subject of the proposed
distance to Crawford, the sculptor, and
would suggest the following to the
notable minded Ladies who have undertaken the plan of raising funds for
the purchase of his Offices.
The immediate and most pressing
object I conceive is that of at
once relieving Mr. Crawford's
mind from the hauntings of that
fool friend, want, and yet in a
way not to wound the sensitive
feelings of a man of genius.
That this way will be pursued, the
name of the Ladies moving in the
matter is a sufficient guarantee
to the disposal of the statue
when it shall be purchased,

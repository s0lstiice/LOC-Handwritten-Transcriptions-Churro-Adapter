Fromaton Dec 5th 1841
My Dear Sir
I am almost ashamed
to confess that your letter was received
not quite a month since. But I
have been so much engaged
in extra college duties on account
of the illness of the wife of one of
the faculty that I have suffered
every thing else to remain unattended to. I hope you will pardon the
unintentional neglect and accept
my thanks for the long use of the
wine – I will send the article
if possible on Saturday next
and will then also return
the letter for complete secrecy

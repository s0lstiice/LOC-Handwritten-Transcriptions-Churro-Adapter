If a reply from me to the letter of the Secretary is
essential to my being considered fully a member, my
purpose will be accomplished by silence. but if
that should be insufficient, then I would request
you to hint my wish to be left out in such
a manner as will excite the least remark.
I make this communication confidentially, and
should not be willing to have it seen by any
member, excepting Mr Towns & Mr Durand, if you
should have occasion to show it to them.
With sincere gratification at the success of
the Academy, and with hopes of its increasing
prosperity I am as ever
Truly Your Friend
Nathl L. Gaskell
May 28 1827
S.F. 13. New York
P.V. No 4

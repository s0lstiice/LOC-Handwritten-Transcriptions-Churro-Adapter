Washington March 19th 1824
Dear Sir
I congratulate you on your
prospect of visiting Mexico & hope you will
meet with success in your enterprise
The Minister is absent on a visit to
Philadelphia & I will endeavor to procure the
letter from Col Genet in Col Polk's office
Mr. Powell thinks it however unnecessary
if you could find me without much
trouble the field of the Arbol de las Manos
in Hand Tree you would oblige me
to wish you a pleasant voyage & a safe
return to your family
Your friend
Chenfield

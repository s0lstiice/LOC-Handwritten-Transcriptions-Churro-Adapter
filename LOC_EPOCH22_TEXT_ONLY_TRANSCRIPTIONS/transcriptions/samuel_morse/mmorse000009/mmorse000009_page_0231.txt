Princeton Sept 26th 1825
My dear Sir
The Faculty of the College of New Jersey will thank you to furnish them with a portrait of Dr. David Horsack, on a canvas of 30 inches by 25 inches. – The head of the degree of Life. You will be pleased to be guided entirely by the Doctor's wishes in relation to introducing either on both hands. One reason for not furnishing a portrait of a larger size, is, that the Faculty desire that the Doctor's should be the first of a collection, whilst they hope will be made for the College. – You will do the Faculty a favour by furnishing for the portrait a handsome and suitable frame.
Very respectfully
Yours
Sam'l B. Monselelag
John Maclean

After this full explanation of my financial
condition, you can judge whether it would be prudent for
me to make a large investment in dead stock; or, whether
I could safely loan where there would be no prospect of
repayment under two or three years. –
We have received a letter from Utica stating that
dated Decr 1st (six weeks after our second letter was sent) stating
that the Directors had concluded on the whole to establish a
paper of their own; that they had so many local purposes
to answer, that a paper at New York would not do, I call
this a shabby business. And I do not regret their decision
on our own account, for I had made up my mind when I
heard of Richard's success, that we would have done a very silly
thing in making them the offer, that we did; but I think
it is mean and dishonorable in the extreme to write to us
as they did, offering without any means to take our paper after spending
offering without any means to take our paper after spending
and sending us down to a dollar, and
and then, when we had accepted their offer, to go and establish them
a paper of their own and send some agents all over the Country
to get Subscribers, while we were from home in
profound ignorance of their plans, and prevented from getting
Subscribers in their region. Such manoeuvering will never
prosper, and I have not one doubt that when Dick gets up
into that part of the world he will get more Subscribers
for the Observer than they will get for their paper. We intend
that the Observer shall be supported on the ground of its decided
superiority to every other paper weekly paper in the United States.

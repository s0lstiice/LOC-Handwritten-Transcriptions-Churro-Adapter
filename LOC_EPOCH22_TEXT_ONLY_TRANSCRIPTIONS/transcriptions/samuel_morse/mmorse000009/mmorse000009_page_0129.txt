Concord Monday August 16th 1824
My Dear Parents,
I wrote you a hasty line from Portland
just as I was leaving it; since then I have been quite sick
but have now recovered except that I am very thin and
somewhat weak; I will give you the whole history of my
diseases. The very afternoon that I wrote you at Portland
I was walking in the garden of the house where I boarded with a
gentleman an old school fellow of mine at Andover Academy; as
I passed through one of the alleys I picked a leaf from a plant
which they call the caper but which proved to be a poisonous veg-
etable and not the common caper, I observed a milky juice
run from the plant very copiously from the place where I stuck
the leaf, and having 5 or 6 ringworms on my face, my com-
panion said to me "Why don't you put that juice upon your
ringworms it will cure them," I accordingly without further con-
sideration applied it to all of them, in about half an hour
they began to smart, and gradually to burn so painfully that
I could not sleep, but rose in the night and walked the room
for some time, and in the morning the lower part of my face
was one mass of violent inflammation, studded with blisters
over the whole surface; I became alarmed and sent for Dr. Cum-

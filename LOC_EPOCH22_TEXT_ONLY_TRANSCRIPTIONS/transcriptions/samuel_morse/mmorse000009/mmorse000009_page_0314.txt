consideration, and therefore I did not send any
to you. Thank you for your kind and memorable
letter. I can only interpret it as a compliment, at least
for the present, I shall not, I hope, incur the displeasure
if I fail to send any of my works — I respect
its earnestness, and shall not fail to admire
the enterprise and talents of its Members
respectively and collectively, in all of which, you
will perceive that I also have some respect for myself
whatever may be the opinion entertained by others —
I am very respectfully yours at
Jascha Shaw.
P.S. I cannot dispass this communication without
remarkings as a proof of my respect for your institution
that at the very moment I received your circular, I was

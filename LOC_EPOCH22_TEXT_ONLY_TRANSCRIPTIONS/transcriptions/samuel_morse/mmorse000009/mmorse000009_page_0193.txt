New-York March 19th 1825
My Dear Parents,
I write in haste just to say that
you will find on board the Packet Sloop
Orion Captain Miles
Cornelia Capt. Heemstran Two barrels of
bottling cider of best quality and two groce
of velvet corks making 3 parcels. I
hope they will arrive safe and be acceptable.
you are to consider it as a present from
your 3 sons.
The Barrels had better remain a
few days, before it is bottle in case there
should be any sediment, but I think then will
not be any, for it seemed very well fixed.
You will be under the necessity of taking
great care of the bottles by having them placed
in sand up to their necks, for I am told it
is very lively.
Susan is quite well and happy, she wants to
see Charles she says, and all at home; Kiss the dear child
for me and give love to all. In greatest haste your aff. son
Finley.

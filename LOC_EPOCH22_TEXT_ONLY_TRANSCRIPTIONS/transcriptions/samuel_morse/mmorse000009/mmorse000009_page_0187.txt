New York March 12th. 1825
Dear Parents,
Mr Fisher the painter has just
called and said he was going to N. Haven in
the boat this afternoon; so that I take the
opportunity just to say that we are well.
Little Susan has had the toothache once or
twice and I have promised her a doll if she
would have it out to day I am this mo-
ment stopped by her coming in and showing me
the tooth out, so I shall give her the doll.
I send her little letter with I promised
in my last. Kiss the dear children for me
& believe me though in
haste
Yr Off Son
Sam. J. B. Morse
Mr Fisher tells me the sad
news that Mr. Allston is very
ill, taken with bleeding at the
lungs.

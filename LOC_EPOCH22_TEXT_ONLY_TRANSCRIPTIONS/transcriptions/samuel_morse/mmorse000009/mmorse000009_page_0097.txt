N. York May 11. 1824
Dear Son,
We have had a very pleasant passage
and are safe at the wharf have
not seen your brothers I forgot to bring the
handsome copy of Pennials work for Richd
I know not where it is If you can readily
find it send it by Beecher If not leave it
till I return If the Society meet for con-
sidering whether they will settle Mr. Rupell attend
if you can I give my voice for it I hope
your health will be better when we return
Love to Lucetta the children I pray
for your health
Yr affte father

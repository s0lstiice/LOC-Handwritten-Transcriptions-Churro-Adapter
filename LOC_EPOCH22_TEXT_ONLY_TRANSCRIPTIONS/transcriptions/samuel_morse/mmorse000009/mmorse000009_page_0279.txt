New Haven Sept. 20. 1826.
Dear Mother,
I arrived safely home after leaving you
yesterday, and found that neither the house nor the
folks had run away. Dear little Charles was taken
sick a few hours after we had left home, and
Nancy felt anxious about him; he complained of
his leg, which was swollen, and broke out with an
eczema which the doctor thought at first was Epi-
silis but afterwards thought it occasioned by rheu-
matism; he was confined to the bed all day last Sun-
day, and could not walk on Monday, but yesterday
he played about quite lively, and to-day is as great
a fly about as ever; he is now in bed fast asleep
after being well tired with his day's romping all over
the house. Little Finley is gaining very fast, he
begins to acquire his numerous little arts and tricks
which he had before his sickness; I can't but hope
he will yet be a well child. Susan is as usual,

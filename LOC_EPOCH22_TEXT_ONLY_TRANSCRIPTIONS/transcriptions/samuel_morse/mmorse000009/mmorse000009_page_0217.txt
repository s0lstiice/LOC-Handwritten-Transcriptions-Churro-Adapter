Copy of a note sent to Gen. Lafayette July
1825 enclosing a sonnet on the loss of his
faithful little dog.
To Gen. Lafayette,
In asking your acceptance of the enclosed
Poetic tribute, I have not the vanity to suppose
It can contribute much gratification, but if it shall
be considered as an endeavor to show to you some
slight return of gratitude for the kind sympathy you
evinced towards me at a time of deep affliction, I shall
have attained my aim. Gladly would I offer to do
you any service but while a whole nation stands
waiting to answer the expression of your smallest
wish, my individual desire to serve you can only be
considered as contending for a portion of that kind honor
which all feel in deserving you.
Believe me with the highest consideration
Tho. O. Lentz
Sam. J. B. Morse

I received a letter from Uncle S. Amy, a very kind
and good one, he is a member of Assembly and
probably now in Albany; all were well
and I opened this to take out the handbill
to show a person who was an invalid and who
has in consequence of its removal bought a quantity
of mustard seed.
Tell Sidney & Richard I have just seen
the N. American Review of my Discourse, let them
take my Discourse and tell me where my antagonist
has left himself vulnerable, and help me.

Dear Sir,
Various occupations have pre-
vented me answering your letter; but when the
time shall arrive I will do the act you required
of me with much pleasure. I think Lucetta
and the children had better reside at Brooklyn,
during your absence. Then they will all be com-
pany for my father, who is at present rather
solitary. If they will remain at St. Haven until
after my admission in May, I will go on with them,
and I do not think they can be ready to leave St. Haven
before that time. Edwards is a plain honest man
& your voyage and residence will be pleasant
with such a person. I trust & hope & believe your
residence in Mexico will be pleasant & profitable,
I think the experiment worth making, that there
is every thing to gain and nothing to lose. You
potally will sail from St. Alcans. In that
port you must take leave from this city.
May

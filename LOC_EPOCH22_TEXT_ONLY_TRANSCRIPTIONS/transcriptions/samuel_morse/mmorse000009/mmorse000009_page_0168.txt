My dear Vielgy
I am almost tempted to say, I do not know whether it
will give you any pleasure to hear from me; and, although I feel that
my absence is unworthy of attention, yet I cannot help feeling a
good deal disappointed that my dear husband does not write of himself.
It seems as if you had been absent an age and as if months had
intervened since I had heard from you -- I eagerly looked for a letter
from my dear Vielgy this morning but looked in vain; I hope to receive
one tomorrow -- I have not been so well of late, have been a little grieved
and restless at night and unable to sleep, in bed without a painful
effort -- I have felt rather distressed from wanting so indifferently and
have been too ready to indulge in groundless fears and apprehensions
of my situation -- I should feel better if I could have resolution to go
abroad and take exercise in the open air every day, but it is a hard
matter to quit the fine old little Charles every day, grown more attached
to me and does not feel happy at my sight. He sleeps with me,
he is so affectionate and pleasant in the morning that I cannot
consent to be parted from him, especially as my dear husband and confidence is
absent Thursday morning to my great joy heard from my dear Vielgy. I feel much better every
way I talk with them to the business with the High Court, only it could not have been
done to your satisfaction -- I talk here a good deal of care and perplexity, and is doing all he
can to extricate himself from his embarrassments -- you find us a good one night just
toward 10 o'clock a.m. a pleasant dinner -- I suppose you will come up the last week in January -- I want to see
you very much, but think on many accounts I should prefer having you at home if possible
during the early part of my confinement, the little children need a parents control and care
at table etc. -- their quarrellings are very kind and indigent and imperfectly and poorly as
their Mother discharges her duty towards them, they would take the advantage of her absence --
I think you for reminding me of my duty towards them -- I feel the importance of your kind
interests she is now disposed to be passionate when she wishes -- and sends a
good deal of congratulations -- Charles is very healthy this winter, he is much beloved by
all the family and by all our friends -- He has heard from Nancy through a letter
only since our letter was sent -- I think that you must have been confined in
few weeks since and lost her baby -- Joseph expects his father and mother will come on in
May and Nancy with them -- many Hillers has a leave of absence from
May 1st -- If you think they want to know when you are coming home --
you should know if you think they want to know when you are coming home --
you should know if you think they want to know when you will be home --

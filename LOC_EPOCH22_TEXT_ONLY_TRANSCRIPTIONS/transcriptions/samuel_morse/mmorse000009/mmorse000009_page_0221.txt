New York. July 13th 1825.
My Dear Parents,
I expect to finish Prof. Filliman's
picture this week so as to be able to bring it
with me to N.Haven on Saturday, and hope
to stay with you till Tuesday evening. I have
been wholly occupied in running after Lafayette,
have obtained two settings from him, and have
completed with him; as soon as this dread-
ful hot weather is over I shall commence
the large picture of him. – I long to see
my dear little ones, I hope they bear this
weather well, tell Nancy I shall bring the
comb & fan which she spoke of when I return.
Kindly bear the dear children for me, and remem-
ber me kindly to Miss Eder, and the young
ladies of der charge, and to all friends, in great
haste but with affection as ever
Yr obedient Servt
Sam. J.W. Mosse.

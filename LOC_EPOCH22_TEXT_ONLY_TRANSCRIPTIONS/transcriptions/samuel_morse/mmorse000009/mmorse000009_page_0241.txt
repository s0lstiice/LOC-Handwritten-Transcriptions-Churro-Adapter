New York Nov. 11th 1825.
My Dear Parents,
We are all well and pursuing our
avocations as usual; Cousin Sarah Lansing
leaves N.York for Utica tomorrow, she has
been so long from home that she feels that she
must not remain any longer absent, she wishes
me to thank you for your invitation to her, to
visit you, and regret exceedingly that she cannot
see you at N.Haven, and hopes to visit you per
haps another season. — She sends a little
present of various little things to Susan, Charles,
and Finley.— I like cousin Sarah very much
I think she is a fine woman, and has an amiable
and affectionate disposition.— Cousin Susan is
doing well, and her infant, she thanks you for your
congratulations, it was born on the Grand Canal
celebration day, and I tell her she must put Grand
canal in the name somewhere, perhaps call him Jacob
Grand Canal Stout but she calls him Edward Carpenter
Stout. — 97879 Kisses to the dear Children, I long to see them
and you all, but dare not think of it, Remember me to Nancy, to the
family, neighbors and friends in general. In haste but with affection as ever
Yr Son. S.F.B. Morse.

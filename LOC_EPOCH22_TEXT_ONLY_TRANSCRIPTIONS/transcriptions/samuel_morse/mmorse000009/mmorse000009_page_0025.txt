(Read this by yourself.) New-York Jan 6th 1824.
My Dearest Wife,
Charles has given up the idea of visiting New
Haven at present, as he has but a little time left to
prepare for his examination in May, he will then
come up he says and pay you a visit, he thinks Susan
had better stay till then, and then he will accompany
her home I think so too if Father can shan him so
long from Concord. I had made my arrange-
ments to come up to morrow to spend a few days
in hopes that he would accompany me, but since
he declines I shall defer it for the present, unless
you say at home "home," as far as my business is
concerned I might be at present in N. Haven quite as
well as in N.York. I am waiting for news from Wash-
ington which will decide whether I go to Mexico or not;
I have several things to way bow, when I come up to
N.Haven I shall see if I cannot make some connec-
tion with Mr. Augur so as to bring the marble machine
into profitable operation I think a company might
be formed to advantage and that I might be so con-
nected with it by allowing the sole rise of my invention
and by superintending the designing department
that I may be able after all to settle down quietly
in N.Haven; I read your letter yesterday on the envelop
of Col. Haynes letter and was glad, very glad, to hear
that Mr. Augur has come out clear from the Townsend

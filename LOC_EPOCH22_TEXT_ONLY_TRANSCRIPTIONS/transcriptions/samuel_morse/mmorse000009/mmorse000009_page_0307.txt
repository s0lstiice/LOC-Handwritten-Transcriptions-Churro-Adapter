Albany July 4. 1828
Your favor of the 1st inst. is received.
I send you money on the Mechanics' & Farmers Bank
of this city for one hundred dollars, payable to your order
being the same due for Judge Curtis District. This is not convenient for me, just now to spare the money for the
present. I will therefore, delay this expense till I visit New
York, which I expect to do, soon after the term begins
to you. I am happy to learn that the Contract plans you
as I shall, generally be placed.
I read the article in the North Carolina
Review, to which you refer, with some interest, & I
shall read with much more interest, your reply to
it. I am not a subscriber to the Journal of Commerce,
&c &c not taken at our reading-room. I must there-
fore, request the favor of you to send me the papers
or papers containing your defense as the Chambers
appears. 977921

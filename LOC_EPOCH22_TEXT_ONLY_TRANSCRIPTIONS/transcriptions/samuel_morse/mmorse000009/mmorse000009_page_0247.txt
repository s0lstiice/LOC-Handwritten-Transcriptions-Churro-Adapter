New-York Nov. 23. 1825.
My Dear Mother,
In your last letter you requested me
to send $21,,50 to pay Dr. Smith; I did not see any
possibility of doing it, but having rec'd $50 unexpectedly.
I have paid over $22,, to W. Converse for you,
which you can credit me for --- I send also, if
M. Converse goes by the Steam boat a pot of gin-
ger, which I thought was sent by M. Goodsell,
this latter is a present from me --- If Mr.
C. cannot take it, it will come by Steam boat or
Packet. --- W. Converse is waiting to go
so I cannot add more Love to all. I hope the
dear children for me, and give kind re-
gards to Nancy. I hope she is getting better
and you also.
All well and doing well
Yr Affy son
Sam: F.B. Morse

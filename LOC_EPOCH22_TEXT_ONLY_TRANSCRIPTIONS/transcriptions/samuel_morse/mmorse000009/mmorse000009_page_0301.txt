New York Jan 2d 1828.
Dear Mother,
In your letter yesterday
and wish you in return all a Happy new
year," I have but a moment to write, so
shall put down whatever comes uppermost.-
I am busily employed: you will be gratified
to hear that I have a commission to paint
the Historical Picture which Edwards saw me
begin (and which I had put by because I could
not afford to paint it,) for 500 dollars, and
a copy of my Una & Prince Arthur for
$150.- I also begin Judge Platt's Portrait
for M. Kent Esq this morning for 100. I have
already completed one of Judge Platt for $60.
and a copy of the Columbus for the Corporation
of the city for $75.- also two small land-
sapes for Gen. Ledyard of Cazenovia for $15.-
each.- do have a little patience, and I will
clear my way, if I can't be rich; these monies
will not be received immediately, the larger sums

New Haven Decr 17th 1824
My Dearest Husband,
I received your dear letters by Mr. Jackson with
much pleasure and should have written you last week if I could
have found a leisure moment. I have had a good deal to attend
to, of late, Caroline went to Stamford to keep Thanksgiving and was
about a week there. We should have been very glad to have had you with us
at the anniversary.asting, Penniman and Lincoln died with us. I
should have been glad to have asked more of our acquaintance among
the thrifty but my own situation and Caroline's absence rendered it
impossible -- We are all well, the children are in excellent health, but
many of our neighbors are sick, Chauncey Goodrich has been very
ill with the dropsy but is convalescent, and several more are sick
with the bronchial -- Little Charles talks a great deal about you, he always
every day new words. he says "New York, for the Mutt Doctor. House
make books," and many other words the children were delighted with
their Edwards present of books -- they have been lent to all the
children in the neighborhood -- Our society are much pleased with
Mr. Bacon, at their last meeting Father was present, 68 voted
in favor of giving him a call, 20 were opposed on the ground of
Mr. W's being only young not having reached his 28th year, and not
having had sufficient time to hear him -- the opposition is very
trifling -- it seems to be so -- he wrote the piece signed LL
in Christian Spectator on a Lady's Work Basket, all you can
please with -- it was addressed to Elizabeth Hooker -- Mr. Cornelius
who was here last week on his way to Somers informed me of
the fact -- so excuse this errand -- it will show you I have
not forgotten you -- I hear but seldom from your dear Kinley but
suppose you are much occupied, I look forward to next month
with the hope of seeing my dear husband for a little season --

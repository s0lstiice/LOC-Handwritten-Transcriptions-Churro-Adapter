American Academy
of the Fine Arts 14 January
1826
Sir
I have the honor to inform you
that at the late election held
for officers of this Institution
to serve for the ensuing year
you were chosen a President.
You will therefore please to
attend a meeting on Saturday
next at 4. o'clock to organize
the new Board.
Very respectfully
Your obedient
A. M. Robertson
Secretary
P. F. B. Morse Esq.

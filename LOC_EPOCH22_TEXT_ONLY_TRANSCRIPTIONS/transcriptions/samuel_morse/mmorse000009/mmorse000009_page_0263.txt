New York Feb. 17. 1826.
My Dear Parents,
Write a hasty line by Mr.
Gocelyn who goes in the morning. —
Please deliver to him the canvas and
stretching frame which I had prepared for Dr.
Eves, he has purchased them of me.
As to Nathan's request of the loan of 200.
dollars it is utterly impossible to comply with it, my money as fast
as it comes in, is necessary to pay my debts and bear
the every day expences of my profession. —
Did you get the things, apples, trunk, &c.
sent by Mr Converse? from one of your letters
I think there must be one of your letters missing in
which you say you give particulars of Uncle Salisbury's
death. I was much surprised to hear of it, I think
it must be a very afflicting event to Aunt. —
I send by Mr Gocelyn the Stone for Lucetta's
grave, he will see it properly placed. —
Rip the dear children and give love
to all friends. my
97894
In great haste, Your affectionate Son
Samuel B. Moreau

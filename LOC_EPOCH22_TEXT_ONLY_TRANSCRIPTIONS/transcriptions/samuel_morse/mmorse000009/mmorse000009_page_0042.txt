Washington March 6. 1824
My nomination not having been
confirmed until the 4th inst. has prevented an
earlier answer to your letter of the 25th ult.
Previous to the receipt of your letter
I had agreed to accept the services of Mr. Hazard
as my private secretary.
It would certainly afford me great
pleasure to promote your other objects, as far
as it may be in my power to do so. I have not
yet received my Commission nor had any interview
with the President or Secretary of State on the subject, or
can not at this time say what facilities should
be authorized to afford you.
Gentlemen however come to this
Country as attached to Foreign legations, who
bear their own expenses, & are required to perform
no duty. Their only advantage is a free passage,
and being considered as a part of the legation.

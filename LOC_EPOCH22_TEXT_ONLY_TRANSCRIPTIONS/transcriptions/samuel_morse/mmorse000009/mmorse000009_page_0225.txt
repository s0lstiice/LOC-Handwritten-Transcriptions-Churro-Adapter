1825 C1843
New York Sep 10th 1825.
Dear Brother
I wrote you several days since saying that
I had received a letter from Professor John Maclean,
of Princeton N. T. requesting to know your
views for a portrait of the "head size" as he
terms it. - To day one of the tutors of the college
called on me to know why the letter had not been
answered. I told him that I had written you I was
waiting your answer, as I did not wish to
burden Mr Maclean with the portage of a letter
merely to say that I did not know. - I told him
further that I would write again to you &
request you to communicate directly with
Professor Maclean on the subject. He says that
it is a portrait of Dr Horack that they want,
for the college, as he has been a benefactor to
the institution. -
The prize essays have done coming in. I have
a small cart load of them. What shall I do with
them.

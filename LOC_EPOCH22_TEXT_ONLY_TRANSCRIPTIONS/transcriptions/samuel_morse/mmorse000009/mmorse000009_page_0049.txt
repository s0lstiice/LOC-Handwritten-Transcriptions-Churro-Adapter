March 10th 1824.
Chas. March 23d.
Dear Sir,
According to my promise
when I last saw you in N-York, I drop
you a line to say that I have this momt.
rec'd a line from Hon. N. Edwards the
Minister to Mexico Politely offering me
a situation in the location; which I think
I shall accept; as the legation he informs
me will probably sail from N. Orleans;
I shall be obliged to bear my own expences
there to join it; and also my expences from
Vera Cruz to Mexico. – My Passage
from New Orleans to Vera Cruz, and
the security of being attached belonging to the legation
while in Mexico is secured to me, but
what is a matter of some importance
to me just now, I shall be at much
expence. – If, therefore, any arrange-
ments on this score can be made with
W. Rosewood & yourself – I should be
happy to render you services equi-

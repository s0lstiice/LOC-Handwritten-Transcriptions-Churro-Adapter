me. — The younger artists have formed a
association at the Academy and elected me
President, we meet in the evenings of 3 days
to draw, and it has been conducted thus
with such success, as to have trebled the number
of our association and excited the attention, and affection
of the community; there is a spirit of harmony
among the artists, every one says what never before
was said, and which augurs well for the success of
the artist. The artists are pleased to attribute it to
my exertions, and I find in them expressions and
feelings of respect which have been very
interesting to me. I have had, however, in producing
a pleasant state of things I think there was the preparation
to the state of mind of the artists themselves; I find a liberal
feeling in the younger part of them, and a refinement of
manners which will redeem the character of the art from the
degradation to which a few dissipated interlopers, have tamper
with it. A literary society, admission
to which must be by unanimous vote, and to which many
respectable literary characters of the city have been denied ad-
mission, have chosen me a member, together with W. Hillhouse
and W. Longfellow, poets. — these indicate good feeling towards me
to say the least and in the end will be of advantage. I have no doubt
God gloria! —
Yr Aff. Son Sam P. Mose

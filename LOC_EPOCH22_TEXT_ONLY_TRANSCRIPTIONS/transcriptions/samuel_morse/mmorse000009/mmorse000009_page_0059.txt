I have taken an office No 6—
old ship under March Benson, near
Pearl St. The very name is associated
with yellow fever. It is a good stand, as
they say, in St. York.
I have not seen the point you mentioned.
Smith will soon finish Gunnet, who is
now at home.—
I had a letter from father this week. He and a
right hand man, Kinis, have been to Patgumett.
Uncle Lyon is dead and left a large family
behind almost, I may say, wholly destitute of
property. If I succeed I must take one
of the sons into my office, but success
you know, is doubtful. "Is not so much
to command success?" Remember me
with affection to all the family.
S.F.B. Man

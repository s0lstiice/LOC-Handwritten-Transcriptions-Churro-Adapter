New York April 9. 1824.
My Dear Sir.
I receive the letter of Credit from
my friend Mr Harmony who is well known in
Mexico for Six Hundred Dollars - the use of
any part of it - in case you should have opportunity of
investing it as contemplated - from this subject it is
not necessary for me to enter into particulars - you
understand fully my wishes - and there is mutual
confidence existing between us - I shall be somewhat
impatient to hear from you - and on the look out in
due time for evidence of your judicious and intimateative
selections - put me in charge fully from you - and
with best wishes for your health and success -
Believe me very truly
Y M O O F P
Charles Marsh.
Have the goodness to place your signature in the letter
of Credit - where I have made a pencil mark - to guard
against an imperferer or being made off - Should it
by accident fall into other hands - This is a mercantile
form - and the letter is not complete without your signature
there.
Sam. T. B. Morse Esq.
I will thank you to acknowledge receipt of this Letter

10 dollars also, and expect to be dunned for some
debts of long standing, but I must say to my creditors
have patience with me and I will pay you all; I
have almost completed Miss Horis picture for which
I shall receive $250.— but 100 must immediately be
transmitted to London to M. Bromfield, and the
other 150 will just about pay the greater debts I owe
in N-York; I am determined (Providence permitting)
to contract no new debts, but the most pressing domestic
necessary debts such as fuel & food, not even clothing
until I see every great debt paid that I now owe
and this I hope to accomplish this winter.— I am
paining Gov. Clinton for an engraver $60.— Lafayette

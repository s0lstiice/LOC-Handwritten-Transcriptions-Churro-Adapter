These Communications have to my knowledge
read by several artists in this State, as the Papers
containing them have sent for to your City, and
as they succeeded your formation into a society
I sometimes thought they were found in some
way or other useful — after your society was
formed I did it least expect some small
attention — They have paid to other artists of this
City, but not to me, not even a Circular till
now — It is true I am but an indifferent artist
and I neither wish nor expect your society
to alter its opinion of, or change its conduct
towards me — I wish the society all possible

Remember me very kindly to Aunt Salisbury and
thank her, for her generous hospitality to me when in
Boston; and give my love to my dear cousin Elizabeth
Weese, I shall soon forget her, I hope we shall
see her in NewHaven why can't you bring her on?
To cousin Hazard Sproudia if he is in Boston, give
my love, and to all kind friends in Boston or Casle-
town give my regards.
In haste but as ever
Your affectionate Son
Finley.

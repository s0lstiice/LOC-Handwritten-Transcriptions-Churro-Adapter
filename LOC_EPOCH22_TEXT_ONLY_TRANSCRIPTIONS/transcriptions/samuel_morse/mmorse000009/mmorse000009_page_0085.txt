I have been amiously waiting for a letter from
you, my dear wife, but have been dis-appointed I conclude
you had heard of my detention here soon enough, to have
written and I see it before this. I think of you
all at home and long to see you, but dare not indulge
myself in thinking too minutely of home, lest my
resolution should almost fail me of continuing my
journey; but I shall go forward trusting, I know
whom I have believed, I have experienced his go
and mercy, and can unreservedly I hope trust him
for the future.
May you all experience the protecting care
of our covenant God and may he preserve us all
to a happy meeting again in his good time.
With my Dear Susan & Charles many times, and
my affectionate love to father & mother Nancy and
all neighbors & friends.
Ever my dear, dear wife,
Your affectionate husband
Finley

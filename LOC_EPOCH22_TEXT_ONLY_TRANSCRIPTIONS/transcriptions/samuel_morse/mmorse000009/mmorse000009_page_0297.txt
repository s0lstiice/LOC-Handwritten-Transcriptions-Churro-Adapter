Although he said you live in N.Y. Capitol St.
and this is the place that I shall direct my letter
and I should pay the postage but I know not whether
you live in that place or not now. I should go to
the office but he is not in their office
now and where he is I know not. And of course
I must get information of your respecting your
terms & the price of board I wish to know
the highest and lowest price of board in
order to make some calculations respecting the
sum of money that I must obtain. Or It is
my wish to get into some respectable house
to board and I also wish to know how long a time
is necessary for me to earn to get this art. Or
if you should not object to I think me I should
think of going there in the last of Autumn
or the first of winter. I have with an
answer as soon as you mime this and allige
your humble Servt. I should pay the
postage as I mentioned above if I was certain the
letter would reach you.
Sic I am yours with respects
Philo Clappe
Despied June 1st 1827
Mass. Franklin County.

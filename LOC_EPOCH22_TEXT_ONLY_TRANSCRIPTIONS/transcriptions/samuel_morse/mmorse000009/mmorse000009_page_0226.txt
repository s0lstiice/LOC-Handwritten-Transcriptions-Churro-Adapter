The letter by Mr Brinkerhoff came
but no peaches. He had carried them by mistake to his
house & forgotten to bring them in with him. Will
bring me a basket of fresh peaches from his own
Garden on Monday to pay for them.
I shall not come up to New Haven till
fortnight or three weeks from this. When I come, I want
to spend three or four weeks, & Mr Dodd is not yet
sufficiently initiated.
Richard sends in names continually. About 40 this week. The whole number since he commenced
this tour 184. He A letter received this morning says
Direct to me at Champlain, what I find by Morris gazetter,
the best authority, is in Clinton N.Y.
Love to all,
In haste
Your affeessed brother
S C More

New York April 5th 1826.
My Dear Mother,
By Mr. Converse who goes to New
Haven this afternoon I drop you this hasty
line, just to say that I received yours of the
2d of April yesterday. I immediately called
on Mr. Jappan as you requested; he took the
list which I made out, and said he would
show it to Mr. Jappan, as she had the whole
management of that part of the business. I
have not heard from him since. You put the sofa
down among the furniture to be disposed of, I
had rather not part with that, as I shall want
it here. Every thing of that kind you do not
wish for your own use, send down to me I shall
eithe use it myself or dispose of it at auction.
I cannot come to N.Haven, as I am to move myself
this season, and I am otherwise very much engaged.

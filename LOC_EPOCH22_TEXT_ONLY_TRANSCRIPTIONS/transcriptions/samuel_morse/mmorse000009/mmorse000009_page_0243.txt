N.York Novr. 18, 1825.
My Dear Parents,
I received your letter by Mr. Goodsell;
I was very sorry to hear of dear mother's continued illness; I feel almost tempted to break away from New-York, and return to N.Haven if I thought by so doing I could be of the least service to her in relieving her cares; and Nancy too I feel exceedingly for her, she has been very kind to my dear little ones, and I can only pray, that, if it is not in my power to show her how much gratitude I feel towards her, by some more substantial token, than mere thanks, that He in whom is all fullness of blessing, will reward her abundantly here and hereafter;

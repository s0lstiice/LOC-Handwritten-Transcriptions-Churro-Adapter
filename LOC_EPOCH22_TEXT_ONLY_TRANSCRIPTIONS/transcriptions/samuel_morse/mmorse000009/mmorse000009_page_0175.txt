Most claims on our gratitude never can be satisfied
But that with to my eye outshines even this is his

send him from the line of duty. Points out -
But I like that my professional scrupulousness is
carrying me into liberty - even in a friendly district -
You must excuse me on this subject - as I am
myself among the most enthusiastic admirers of the
Hero of New Orleans -
I need not say that we all long
to see you in Charleston - Mother says she gets indulgences
to hope and we all desire for - that nothing could
afford us so much satisfaction as to have Doctor
Lucas at your side & your little family spending a week
in amongst us - Little Susan should come on if but to see
her own little wing & Perpetually sitting on our
front room mantel piece - Remember us affectionately
to all & to Uncle Grant made whenever you write - Your
own family are I suppose by this time with you in New
Jerk - I wish you all the success which you can enjoy.
Your affectionate
Clifford Dudley

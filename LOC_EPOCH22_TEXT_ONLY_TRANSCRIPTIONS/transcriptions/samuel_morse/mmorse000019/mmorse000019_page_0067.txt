is to know whether you would have
any objection to my exhibiting your
telegraphs, in the principal towns in
this, and the Western States, in connection
with experiments in Galvanism, and
electro magnetism. I propose obtaining one
of Professor Locke's "Organised batteries," and
to exhibit your telegraphs, I would only
require your apparatus for marking, or
recording the communications if you
have no objection to my making such
use of your invention, please let me
know where I could obtain such
machinery as I would require, and the
cost of it.

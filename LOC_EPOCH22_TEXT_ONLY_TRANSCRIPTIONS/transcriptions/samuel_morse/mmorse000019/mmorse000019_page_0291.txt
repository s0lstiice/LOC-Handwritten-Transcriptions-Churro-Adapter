Baltimore July 14th 1845
Sir
I learn that it is in contemplation
to extend the line of Telegraph to New York, and
in anticipation of such a result, I beg leave, respectfully,
to present myself as a candidate to your
favorable consideration as an Assistant to the
Baltimore Station.
Accompanying this application
will be found testimonials as to character and
business capacity, and if I should be successful
in obtaining the appointment I promise to discharge
my duties with fidelity and conscientiousness.
I remain most
Respectfully
Your obedient Servt
Geo H. Cogers
Professor S. B. Moree
U.S.
Sgt. Telegraph

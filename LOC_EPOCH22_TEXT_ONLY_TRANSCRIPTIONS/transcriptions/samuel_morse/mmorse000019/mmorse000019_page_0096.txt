Washington Dec. 6th 1844.
Sir, I have the honor to state that in the
letter I had the honor to address to the Depart-
ment under date Sept. 27th 1837 in reply to
queries addressed me by the Department respect-
ing Telegraphs I have this passage, "The cost of
"printing, in the first instance, of a Telegraphic Dic-
"tionary, should also be taken into the account, as
"each Officer of the Government as well as many
"others would require a copy should this mode of
"Telegraphic Communication go into effect. This Dic-
"tionary would contain a full vocabulary of all
"the words in common use in the English language
"with the numbers regularly affixed to each
"word." — One of the proprietors of the Elec-
tric magnetic telegraph has now in the press a
Telegraphic Vocabular, or Dictionary of this
character, printing at his own cost, and he has
proposed to distribute a copy of his Dictionary
containing of two copies to each of the members of
Congress at the cost price of one dollar & twenty-five
cents each copy , and to take in payment a quan-
tity of the surplus wire which remains on hand
from the experimental essay with the Telegraph &
at the market valuation.

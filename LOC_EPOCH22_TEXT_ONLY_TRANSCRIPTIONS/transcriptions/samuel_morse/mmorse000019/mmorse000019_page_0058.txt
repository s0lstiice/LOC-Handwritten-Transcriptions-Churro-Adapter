My dear Sir
Your returned favor of yesterday includes a check on the Bank of Washington is part of it
and I might not perhaps have written to day, would not put me right with you as to what I said to my last
about the proposition coming in. I addressed to Mr Smith not coming in to the proposition which I then gave you
1217 tails approbation. although I had Mr Smith's letter to that effect, and after I left you room in Washington
I spent most of the evening with him to accomplish it still I did not refuse to the letter he sent me, suggesting he concluded
it was for some wise purpose he felt just then did not like to come in to it, it might be to suit his will
alter his plan with his Boston concern, but when he left me at the Boat he seemed to think something may
get be done to suit all. The amount of it is, President, your enterprise Stuart will not gain reward
did it begin to be appreciated in a small degree does not The Pres without distinction of party run in Baltimore
have promised to advocate its construction throughout the Union. The attention & kindness of Major Rogers
& Tait and their concern in due course I think give a favorable impression who I do hope that the "American"
will act with discretion & dignity, then they will prevail they need more than present counsel.
I am yours truly in haste
David Burnside

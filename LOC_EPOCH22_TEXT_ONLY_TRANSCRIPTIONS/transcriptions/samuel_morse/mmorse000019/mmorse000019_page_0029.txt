Prof. L.F.B.Morse
My Dear Sir
I send you herewith a copy of a series of results
obtained with 2 different sizes of plates as conductors to be used in
crossing reverse - The batteries used were 8 caps of your smallest size on
one ligne and for the same through put.
I made several other series of experiments, but these I must rely on
for uniformity & accuracy.
You will see from instructing table on the last page that
the distance along the wires should be 3 times greater than
that from shore to shore across the stream - At least that 4 times the
distance does not give any increase of power
I intend to repeat all these experiments under more favorable circum-
stances and will communicate to you the result
Very Respectfully
S.G. Gale
N.Y. Nov 5 1844

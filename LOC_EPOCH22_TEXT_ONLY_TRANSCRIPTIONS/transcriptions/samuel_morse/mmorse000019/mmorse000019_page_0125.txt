I suppose very little will be done
in respect to the Telegraph Bill until
the Court of Inquiry is in Session, upon its final
decision in each house upon an advisable

It is still uncertain how soon I can

I hope to get away by Christmas. But
new operations at home seem constantly
writing to delay me.

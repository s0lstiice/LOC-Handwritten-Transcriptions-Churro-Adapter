Prof J. F. B. Morse
Baltimore Feb 15th 1845
My dear Sir
I have been applied to by Mr. S. H.
Rogers for a letter of recommendation to you
in the event of your wishing to employ appoints
in telegraphic matters after Congress shall have
made appropriate laws for continuation of
the system. Mr Rogers has been for some
years a clerk in the Bank, my way door
heugh tour, in which I keep my account, and
I have seen a good deal of him. I shall always
found him diligent active and assaying,
and I have no doubt would devote himself
self zealously and carefully to his duties
should his present wishes be gratified.
I am with my kin regards
Yours Sth most Beloved
J. H. Belk

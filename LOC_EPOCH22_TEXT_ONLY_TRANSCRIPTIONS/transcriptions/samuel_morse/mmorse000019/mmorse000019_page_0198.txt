I am exceedingly confined in my business have not been

from Washington. We have manufactured and sold
since my return about 10,000 lb. of Sugar of Lead and
we have now ready for market 10,000 more.
Please remember me to Mr Elbourn's Family - I should

your hand should your time permit.
Yours Truly

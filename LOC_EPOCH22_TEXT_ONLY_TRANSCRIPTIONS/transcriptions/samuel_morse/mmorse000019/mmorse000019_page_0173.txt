Agreement with David Barbauk
The undersigned proprietors of More's Patent for
Constructing and using an Electric Magnetic Telegraph
in consideration of the past services of David Barbauk
of Baltimore in the State of Maryland, in promoting
the interest of said proprietors, hereby agree and
contract to give said Barbauk the refusal for eight
months from the date of this instrument, of a conveyance
of the exclusive right to construct and use said
Telegraph from the city of New York to the city of
[Buffalo in the State of New York by way of Albany
and such intermediate towns as lie within ten miles
of [the City of Worcester in the State of Massachusetts
Nassau, Syracuse & Rochester on the route from
[Buffalo in the State of New York to Albany the road on the east
side of the North river nearest to said cities, on
the route between New York & Albany, and
from Albany to the city of Boston along the rail
road most direct between the two cities not deviating
beyond ten miles on either side of said rail
road, and on the following conditions, to wit,
If said Barbauk shall pay said proprietors at
any time within the eight months aforesaid a sum
at the rate of fifty dollars per mile measured by
the length of one wire stretched along the line aforesaid,
and between the cities aforesaid, and the payment of
said sum shall be made to each of the proprietors
aforesaid in their respective proportions, and their
respective receipt for the same shall be shown to
them before the conveyance aforesaid shall be
made or required by said Barbauk or his Assignees,

Feb. 10th 1845
To the Hon the Mayor & Corporation of the
City of Baltimore.
The Petition of the undersigned Super-
intendent Elec. Mag. Pel. for United States respectfully
leaves forth that he desires, leave to your Hon. body to per-
manently continue to extend the Posts and
other fixtures already constructed for supporting the wires
for the Elec. Mag. Telegraph, and also to extend them
to such directions through the city as may be deemed desirable without
planting of said posts in the street more than a common lamp post.
and as it duly bound will ever pray a
desirable
from time to time necessary
in extending the Telegraph Posts &c.
Washington Feb. 10. 1845.

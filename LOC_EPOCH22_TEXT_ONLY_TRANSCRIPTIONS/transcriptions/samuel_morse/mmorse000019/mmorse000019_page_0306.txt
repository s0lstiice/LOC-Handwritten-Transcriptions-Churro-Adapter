In the mean time while waiting anxiously
for that 70 million of appropriation for the telegraph
from Congress I would ask what benefit
is to result from the election of the telegraph
from Albany to Boston, or in those cases to
the proprietors? I am very much in want
of information as to your views as to the
success of the telegraph and as to the des
pise of it in case he does not take hold
of it at this Session.
I am as one unwilling to let it linger
along in this way until the novelty is worn
off or an improvement or substitute is made
when all will be lost, Already a mode
of trans mitting packages is invented that
will succeed and we are every day losing
very precious time. Some is it that Colt
is so successful? and that we suffer such
delays! Is it something like an appropriation
for the purchase inserted by the Coin of Loops to
means in their title, Is Mr Smith with
you, If so what in the name of Common
sense is he doing that an appropriation is
not called for, Please answer
Feb 22 1845
Strawell
Geo Keil

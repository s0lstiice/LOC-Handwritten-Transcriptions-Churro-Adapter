Washington, Wash. Feb. 28. 1843.
I have the honor to state that the balance
of the approb. of $30,000 by my consent
rendered to the Dept. on the 16 inst. in 41st. City
Against this balance there are at the present
moment outstanding demands of between 5 & 8000
dollrs. In view of the expenditure of the approb.
in the I have deemed it necessary to apprise
those who have now the charge of the Treasury
that they will all be discharged on the 1 Feb. next
or they will not be the means for their pay
after that time.
Very Respectfully
Yr O B
H. G. McBill
Col J. Kearney
Supr.

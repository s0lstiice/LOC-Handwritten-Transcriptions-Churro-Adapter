Baltimore Oct 7th 1844.
Dear Sir
I have indeed the account, and vouchers
for your examination, and have returned the same
by a charge to new as of 44270. - Then on some
other bills which should have properly come in to this office,
but have not yet been handed in viz:
St Carby for and 16 about - ƒ54.00
On arm'd due Jordan - which I will take from
Mr. Cornell sent, if it be correct - 8.33
McSister, for Diffusion in Steam - 4.00
Mr. Proctor for Hard wax about - 2.00
Mr. Wilson for casting weights for instruments 2.50
Coad & Glenn for lumber 2.00
And Mr. Hattie for 6th Coppen casting 6.00
for 5 Sept. 1844, 16th Sept. 1844, 78.53
Then on some articles made a charge direct without any
accompanying vouchers to save a small quantity of bills. - The cost
paid Mr. Cleveland for White washing it was given him as job
after work to help him a little, and perhaps if he
had not been so busy, as it would have cost more to have
paid others in the day time.
I had your letter in relation to the Win
and by following your direction, came at the result correctly
the difference is just 25 pcts. or 1/4 - and it goes this afternoon,
I took my son up with me, I attended to it personally,
and will place it to your credit.
Yours respectfully & truly
David Taberbank

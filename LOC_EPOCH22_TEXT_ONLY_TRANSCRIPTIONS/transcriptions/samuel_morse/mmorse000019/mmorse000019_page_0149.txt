[Duluth, Minn, 1838, Apr. 18, L.A. Sykes, recommendation of P. Soule]
Illinois A. J. Soc. 28th Decr.
Sir
From what I can learn from
the newspapers, and from information
delivered by furnished me by your brother
(Ed. Thomson) I am persuaded you will
soon require the services of a considerable
number of assistants, in order to
prosecute your contemplated telegraphic
project -- I am induced, therefore, to
offer myself as a candidate for a
situation of some kind --
As to qualifications I will trouble you with
no other statement than, that I am a
son of civil engineer, having had about
six years' practice as such in the states of
Ill. St. J. and Mich. I send you a certificate,
and take the liberty of directing for any
further information on this subject, to the
Hon. Messrs. Pat B. Hunt of Mich, and
L. Kinpatrick of St. J.
Prof. Morse
99981
Yours
16th April
Peter Soule --

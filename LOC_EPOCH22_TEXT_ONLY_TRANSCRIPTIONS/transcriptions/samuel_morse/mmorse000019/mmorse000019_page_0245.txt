Baltimore Jan 20th 1848
My dear Sir
You second letter of this, did not
come to hand till this morning, and I hasten to make
out the wishes as you desire – As I said in my letter
of Saturday (which I hope you have) I should have
properly advised you of the balance in order that you
might have known what sum it required. But the
truth is, I knew from what you had told me that the funds
was low, and I started on that soon, lest you might think
I had no regard for your failing. So the Professors will
please pass on for causing him addition of anxiety.
The War stands on the
booths exactly as you suggest, both in relation to Mr Smith
& myself. – And I long to see him come on. He is
held manfully and the concern will as I even
then go itself out straight get.
And I trust it will all come
out straight get.
Your respectfully & truly
David Durbinck.
Prof. Samuel F. Morse.

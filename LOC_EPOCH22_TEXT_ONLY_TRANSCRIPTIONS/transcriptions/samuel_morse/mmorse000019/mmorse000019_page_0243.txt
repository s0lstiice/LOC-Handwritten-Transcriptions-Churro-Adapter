Give much love to all. How comes on your matter with
Cousin? I hope it will be settled soon on all accounts,
for life is not so long that you can waste much more time
on his account. — Senator Woodbury gave a discourse
before the Institute a few nights ago, in the Hall of the
House of Reps. in which he lauded the Telegraph in
the highest terms, and thought I had gone a step beyond
Franklin! The popularity of the Telegraph increases
rather than declines.—
The Ellsworths send a great deal of kind remem-
bance to you, and Annie a great deal of love to
Catherine.—
Good bye Y Affectionate
Cousin
Sam. F.B.Mass.

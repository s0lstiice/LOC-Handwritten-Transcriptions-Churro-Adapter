Agreement with David Barbank.
The undersigned proprietors of Morse patent for
constructing and using an Electric Magnetic Telegraph,
in consideration of the past services of David Barbank
of Baltimore in the State of Maryland in promoting
the interests of said proprietors, hereby agree and
contract to give said Barbank the refusal for eight
months from the date of this instrument, of a conven-
ance of the exclusive right to construct and use said
Telegraph from the city of New York to the city of
Buffalo in the State of New York, by way of Albany
and such intermediate towns as lie within ten miles
of the rail road which passes through Schenectady
Utica, Syracuse, Rochester, on the route from
Albany to Buffalo and along the road on the
east side of the North river nearest to said river,
on the route between New York & Albany, and
from Albany to the city of Boston along the rail
road most direct between the two cities, not deviating
beyond ten miles on either side of said rail
road, and on the following conditions to wit:
If said Barbank shall pay said proprietors at any
time within the eight months of said a sum at the
rate of fifty dollars per mile measured by the length of
one wire stretched along the lines of said, and between
the cities of said & the payment of said sum shall be
made to each of the proprietors as aforesaid in their respective
proportions, and their respective receipts for
the same shall be shown to them before the conveyance
aforesaid shall be made or required by said
Barbank or his Assignees, then said right to be conveyed
shall be conveyed, and shall be the exclusive
right to construct and use all desired lines between
said points and intermediate points within the
limits of ten miles on either side of said route.

Washington Octr. 20th 1844
Sir,
Your letter of the 30th of August 1844 forwarded
to me by my friend the Rev. D. Savers, and enclosing
the Prospectus of the plan & objects of the Pyro Egypt-
ian Society of London, I have just received, and I
thank you for the confidence bestowed upon me by the
Iron Society of which you are the Secretary, in appoint-
ing me agent of its Agents in N.York. I must nevertheless
decline the honor in consequence of the reasons
that, My important duties as the Superintendent of Telegraphs
for the U. State, make it necessary that I should pass
the principal part of my time in this City and not in
N. York.
I have, there are other doublets in N. York whose services
in Gunfiring the projectiles of your Society would be far more efficient than mine
can possibly be, from their more direct interest in them.
But while I decline the Agency of the Society for the
City of N.York, it will give me pleasure if I can render
it any service.
I remain Sir
Very Respectfully
Yr Ob. Servt
Sam. F. B. Morse
Pres. of the National Academy
of Design.

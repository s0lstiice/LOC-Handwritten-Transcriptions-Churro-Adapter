Dear Sir
I have this morning effected a sale of the
nitrates aided, at 3 1/2. Philadelphia 40th and we to
retain the Carboy which by the way will not be lost, as
in saltnessphosphate they can be exchanged (as for as
they go) instead of paying for others. And if you
please, you may send me what you have on
hand in Washington, to be left at the "Batt Street
depot," advising me of the time of shipment, I
will pay for the transportation, I can then deliver all
at once.
I am inclined to think
this a tolerable good sale for the Telegraph, as it has
held so many + so long till now without success.
I hope you will accomplish what you
all desire respecting the Telegraph, and that a
bright day is now dawning.
Please present my respects to Mrs Smith and Tail.
and believe me
Yours truly
David Barback.
Washington D.C.
100093
Tusshon Genl F. P. Mosse.

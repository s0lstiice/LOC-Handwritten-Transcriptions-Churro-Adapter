Philadelphia
Washington DC
Dec 28 1844
We have a letter from an Eastern
inquirer stating that he has been applied to by Mayor
Whitaker in behalf of the Russian Government for information
regarding the Russian Telegraph, and he is to get full information
from Professor Whitstone who has established a Telegraph
on the Great Western Railway. Our partner says that we
misunderstood Professor Whitstone is the original inventor of the
Telegraph which Mr. Whitstone claims to have originated
it would be most unfair to deal with him
in generalities particularly since he is on fellow countryman,
and, any thing about the Richmond & Western
Railway being effected by Americans it would be very
desirable to have the American Telegraph instead of
the English one on the line of the road. Therefore please
put yourself in communication with Professor Whitstone and
tell him the circumstances – The Railway is 400 miles
long, ask him what apparatus is required and what
expenses is necessary. Plans and drawings and full details
of the System of apparatus to be put in operation as
possible.

Dear Sir
I have read with great admiration and delight, the various accounts which have been published of your wonderful discoveries in the magnetic telegraph, enabling you to annihilate time and space, and giving to the people of this great Country, the means of leaving, instantly, at the two extremities of the Union, all the important transactions at the seat of government, enabling the merchant to transmit with perfect accuracy and at trifling expense, important intelligence from Boston to New Orleans, with the rapidity of lightning. I saw an exhibit ion , on a limited scale, of your telegraph by E. Cornell at this place last summer, and was delighted with the simplicity and rapidity with which letters could be made, and I am astonished that scientific men in Europe should not have seen the best advantages of your telegraph, over any of the plans now in use either in England, France, or Russia. As an American, I rejoice that you met with no better reception when abroad,

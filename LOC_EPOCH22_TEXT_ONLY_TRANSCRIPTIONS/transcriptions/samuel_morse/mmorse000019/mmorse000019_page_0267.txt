Treasury Department
January 30th 1845.
For Cotton of the 28th & 30th Districts on
record
The Department approves of the course advised
by you in regard to the former lack of force applied in the
Telégraphs, and has no objection to continuing the service
as desired by you, of the two assistant Superintendents
"at least extremity of the time to protect the
public property" & Congress any amount of the sum
appropriated remains unexpended. Whatever is
obtained from compensation must come
from the Treasury Department fully
Your obedient Servant
Geo: Wm. H. B.
Secretary of the Treasury
Page 1 F. B. House
Dep't of the Treasury
Hastings

Newport Nov 30/1844
Professor S. B. Morse
Washington City
Sir
My old friend Mr George Valley spoke to you
same days ago of Specimen of Photography, or Daguerreotype
on paper. We had the pleasure of meeting in 1839 with
Said Specimens taken by me in France but you had
unfortunately left the city. I bought in England the
Patent from the inventor Mr Henry Fox Talbot whom
you likely know as belonging to the Scientific world.

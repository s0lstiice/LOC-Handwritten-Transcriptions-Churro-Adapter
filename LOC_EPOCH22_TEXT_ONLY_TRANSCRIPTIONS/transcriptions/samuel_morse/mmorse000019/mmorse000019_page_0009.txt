The undersigned, prosecution of Morris Patent for
constructing and using an Electo Magnetic Telegraph,
in consideration of the best services of said Burnbank
to promote the interests of said proprietors, hereby
agrees and contract to give said Burnbank the
exclusive right to construct and use said Telegraph from the city
of New York to the city of Buffalo in the State of
New York by way of Albany and such intermediate
towns as may within ten miles from the usual
route of travel between said points and from
said Albany to the city of Boston, and intermediate towns laying as above specified or said
route, not deviating beyond ten miles on either
side of the direct or usual route, and in the
following conditions viz - if said Burnbank shall
have said proprietors at any time within the year
except succeeding the date hereof, at the rate of
fifty dollars per mile for the length of the Telegraph
between New York City and Albany - and between
Albany and the city of Boston - and twenty five
dollars per mile between Albany and the
city of Buffalo - the payment to be made from
the convenience of said Burnbank, or his Assignees - said
right to be enjoyed, shall be the exclusive right to construct
and use all desired lines between said points, and

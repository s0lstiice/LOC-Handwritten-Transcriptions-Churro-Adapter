An Act to extend the Electro-
magnetic Telegraph
Be it enacted by the Senate and House
of Representatives of the United States of
America in Congress assembled,
That the sum of thousand
dollars, be, and is hereby, appropriated,
out of any moneys in the Treasury, not
otherwise appropriated, for extending the
line of Electro-Magnetic Telegraph from
Baltimore to the City of New York, under
the Superintendence of Professor Samuel
F. B. Morse, in pursuance of and in
conformity with the directions contained
in the Act, approved on the 5th March
1843 entitled 'An act to test the practicability
of the establishment of a System of Electro-
magnetic telegraphs by the United States'
Section 2. And be it further enacted, That
the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is
hereby, authorized to pay to the proprietors
of said Telegraph out of any moneys in
the Treasury not otherwise appropriated,
the sum of dollars per month,
for the use of said Telegraph by the
United States, to be paid to said pro-
prietors by installments as the work shall be
completed and approved by the Secretary
of the Treasury.

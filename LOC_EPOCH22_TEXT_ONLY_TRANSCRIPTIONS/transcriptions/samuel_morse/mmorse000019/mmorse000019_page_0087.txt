Office Philadelphia Fayette
Philadelphia December 5. 1861
Professor Muir
Philadelphia
We wish to ascertain from you
whether you would be willing to furnish us each
day with a letter containing the preceding, as
Washington up to the time of the closing of the
Lincoln at Baltimore in this City. We furnish
this to the Patriot the information up to the time
of the issue of that paper, but prefer leaving it
up to the last moment. Will you leave the
Produce to advise us when you will be kind
enough to furnish it and in what form
Very Respectfully
From Me
C. G. Childs Co

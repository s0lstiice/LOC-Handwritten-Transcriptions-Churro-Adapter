Mechanics Bank of Baltimore
February 15th 1845.
Dear Sir:
I learn that Mr John H Rogers
intends applying for a situation in the Telegraphic
Department now under your charge, and altho'
a stranger to you, I cannot but feel that facts known
to me will not act to his prejudice, and take pleasure
in stating that since 1838 he has been an officer of
this Bank, and has discharged the several duties
assigned him to the entire satisfaction of those with
whom he has long been so agreeably connected.
Consider Mr Rogers capacity fully
adequate to the discharge of the duties of any post
in the Bank which might require strict integrity,
deliberity and accuracy.
I regret exceedingly
the probability of parting with a faithful, agreeable
and well tried officer, and do so only, in the hope
that the compensation allowed may probably exceed
that which is allotted to the post so long honorably
held by him, and thereby allow him to maintain
his young and interesting family in a manner
somewhat more comfortable than his present means
appear to justify.

Boston dec. 27th 1844.
Dear Sir,
His Excellency the "Ministre de l Interieur" in France
gave lately orders for a strict examination of
the Electro-Magnetic Telegraphs for the purpose of
establishing some of them in France. This examination
will contain the different kinds of Electro-Magnetic
Telegraphs now in operation in the United States and
in England. As I have witnessed your operations
and I have convinced myself of their superiority
I would desire to learn of you if, from the
time that a patent has been granted to you
you have not made some improvement in
your useful invention. I suppose that you
have sent in Paris the specifications of your
telegraph but if you have not done so or
if you have made some improvements
as I said above, I would be very much obliged
to you if you will be so kind to send me
the plans of your Machinery and also the
explanations.
I have been but in Boston by His Excellency

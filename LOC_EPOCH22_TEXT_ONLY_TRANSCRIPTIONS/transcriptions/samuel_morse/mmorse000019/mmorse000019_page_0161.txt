St. F. P. Lines Comp
Waterbury Dec 30th 1844
Mr. Sir
I take the liberty of addressing you
in relation to the furnishing of Wire for your Magnetic Telegraph
I have noticed by the papers lately that it was in contemplation
to construct a Telegraph from New York to Boston and that it
had already commenced it has occurred to us that prospects
would have been made a advertisement in the papers and had escaped
our notice, and that you were now having your Wire from some
other source. We are now putting up some own machinery and should
be happy to receive your order for Wire if you should have occasion
to use any, if not asking too much we should be happy to hear from
you, what the prospects are for having the Telegraph continued, and whether
one is being constructed from New York to Boston.
Respectfully Your friend
Braddock Birmingham Algh

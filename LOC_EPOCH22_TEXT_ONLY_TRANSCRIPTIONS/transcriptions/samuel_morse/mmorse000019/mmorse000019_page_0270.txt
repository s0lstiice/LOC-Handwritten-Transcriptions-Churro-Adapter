Mr. Button
though not particularly acquainted
with Mr. Chester Bull, there is doubt, from
what I do know of him, or rather from the
terms in which Mr. Button speaks of him,
that Mr. Bull is well qualified for the employ-
ment he solicits; and I hope his application
may meet with favor.
Very truly yours
William H. Seward
From 22. Wolski June 24.
I cordially concur in the foregoing recommendation
of Mr. Bull.
N. S. Phelps

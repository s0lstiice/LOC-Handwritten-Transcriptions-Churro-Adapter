We have had letters from Dr. Romeype & Mr Van Schaick
concerning you, will have comforted us much since receiving
them, we don't know but we have expressed ourselves in
our letters, in answer to your last, a little stronger than we
ought, in regard to your political feelings & conduct. I find
others, who have returned, feel pretty much as you do but
it will be remembered, that your situation as an artist is
different from theirs -- It is your business to leave politics
to politicians -- The solek the Artist -- But if you are in
France, these cautions will not probably be necessary,
as you will have no temptation to enter into any
political Disappoint.
The proceedings of the Hartford Convention will
probably reach you before this through the public papers.
The condition of all parties, think well of them as wise friends
and contrarians -- The changes in our country since you
left it, have been great & disastrous beyond what you
can well conceive -- I am waiting worse & worse --are
likely to continue, for long as the present administration
it is thought will continue -- A change of administration
it is thought will be immediately followed by an honorable
peace.
There have been no material changes in town since
I last wrote you -- Your brothers were both well a short time since.
All at home are well -- Your mama & me, Nancy & Caroline make our
whole family this winter Betsy lives with Ely. Tanner, who has married
my brother's grandson, who was with us in Carolina.
Before you return I will fail to call on those in London from
whom you have received attention, take a respectful leave of them
in season to give them time to write me, if they choose. Difficult
late letters from them --

Liverpool 4 April 1814
My Dear Sir
I duly received your return favour
of 19 ultit also one for your Hon r Father
which I will endeavor to safely deliver
I am sorry to say that your
Painting has not arrived, our vessel
Clear out tomorrow & to my regret it will now
be too late for it to go by this opportunity, I
shall therefore request Mr Coates Short to
have it Houd in a safe dry ware House
to hold it subject to your future order
May God bless you
admirably
Truly yours
H. H. Wilden

are extremely careful that Company you keep. I hope you will keep
yourself from the Gay, the Disputation of the Prophane Reminiscences dear
to your early Education, & how very much depends on the manner
in which you spend & improve the present moment, that an eternity of
happiness or misery will be the consequence. I intrust you to think much
of the all important truths contained in your Bible & pray to meet
your God in peace. that if we are not permitted to see each other in
this world we may meet in that better World above & spend an
eternity in celebrating the praises of redeeming Love. This is the daily
prayer of your affectionate parents for your own other love make it
your own or it will be in vain for us to make it. I have been confined
the greater part of the Winter with the Ague in my face hair had
two teeth extracted but still wear a bandage of any face your paper
wishes me to tell you that he wrote you a fortnight ago by the
way of Halifax, by a Cartel, and sent the Bills mentioned to
Mr Broomfield, to whom he wrote also. We wish you to have
a letter from Mr Alston & Mr West with respect to your con
tinuance another year.— we wish a description of your house, to Ex
ford & Bristol &c &c and what pieces of Painting you are engaged in
at present.— Your Friends are continually asking about you and
are pleased to hear of your success Mr James Frothingham has taken
the likeness of your Father it is thought to be very good wish
we could have the likeness of Miss Hannah Moore & Mrs Wilberforce
& Dr Buchanan. Your Father Nancy Betsey &c join with me in
love to you Miss Russell Mr Evans Mr O Brown &c a great many
others beg to be remembered to you. We all want much to
hear from you much often than we do it is longer than it
ever has been. I must now bid you good bye my dear Son &c remain
your affectionate E. A. Betsey

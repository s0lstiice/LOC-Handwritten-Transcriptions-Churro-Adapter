Dartmouth Oct 14. 1814
My dear Sir
As there may be some difficulty
in forwarding the Gun, you may leave
that behind and send on the Pistols and
dick only well packed in a box. For
the Pistols you may give £4 and for
the dick £2. We now calculate to
sail on Thursday next. There is a
heavy penalty laid by God on those
persons who carry letters out of this
Kingdom without paying the postage.
This is an exorbitant and tyrannical
tax, and like many other regulations
which bear the form of law is put
forth in the spirit of despotism.
Yours M. Van Schaick

my Dear Son
This Day is the Sabbath of the Lord our God we have attended divine
worship heard your good father expound from a portion of the word of life
in the internal affections of worship I have rested myself alone in the parlour to
converse a little with you on your present situation, as I suppose this letter will
find you in a land where this holy day is abolished and where there is none
of the means of religious instruction that you have formerly enjoyed let me
ask you my son how this deprivation when you are so satisfied to have it now
is it a trial to you? do you long to get through with that part of your education
in your art that obliges you to be separated from privileges which you
truly ought to I hope will always hold dear, you dear as your immortal soul
I beg you to answer these questions in your next and let me know how you
spend your Sabbath, for I hope you will always remember the Sabbath Day
to keep it holy, wherever in the province of God your lot may be cast
and whatever thorn around you may grow so contrary think to Oh my Dear son
what shall I say to you? how shall I write you as to induce you to make
the one thing needful your chief concern shall I set before
you the flatting unsatisfying nature of all things here below does not every
day experience say more on this subject than I can in a column, what an
victorist do they not take to them silver things and fly at a Bird? What
is honour or fame hear what Pur Thatch says on this subject.
"Honours a Pass of dying Breath
that men expose their Blood
"And Venture everlasting Death
"To Gain this living Good
oh! then my Dear Child take that honour that cometh from God only
by a due observance of all his commandments let them not be
previous unto you but like the pious patriot extime all his command
ments concerning all things to be right, and hate every false way
that shall you be happy in life and Death through an Endless Eternity.

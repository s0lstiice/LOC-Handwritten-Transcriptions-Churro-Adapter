W. Sam. & B. Morse
Dear Sir
I have just sent your second
letter of yesterday, I should have replied to the
first one of Friday which came to hand on
Saturday morning, but that, expecting to be
in your Neighborhood on Monday, intended
calling upon you I put the Money you
requested into my Pocket for the Purpose of
hanging it to you – Disappointed yesterday
I intended it to day, but lest I should still
defeat it I now enclose a Bank Note
for Ten Pounds. I am sorry if you have
afford any inconvenience – as you mention
to be for your immediate want I cannot but
bring to your Recollection how short a time has
elapsed since payment of the 20th. Should your
current Expenses continue in the same Ratio
the Amount you will have occasion for will

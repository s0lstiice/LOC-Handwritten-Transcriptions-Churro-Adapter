June 6th
The Milo arrived on the day before yesterday
has brought your painting, but I believe not
from Easter cast -- We know not what has become
of the latter -- E. Goddard has not yet called on us
nor informed us what he has done with the box which
you entrusted to his care -- Some one from him
has intimated that, he P. he had sent it back to
you to London -- There seems to be a fatality attending our getting it -- We will hope to receive
it by the Galen -- The painting that has arrived
is at the bottom of the cargo, so we shall not get it
till the last of the week -- We intend immediately,
to have it framed, (though to your friends -- perhaps -- if judicious people advise it) exhibited for
your benefit, as Mr Sergents is for his -- If we
had your cast, the painting you sent by Mr. Blake
(of which we have nothing since the return of the others) with you had contemplated
sending together, they would be worth exhibiting here -- I afterwards they might be sent
to Philadelphia -- Your paintings have been spoken of in the Port Folio, Analytic Review, & in some
newspapers (I think in a work published by Rev.
Tim.o Alden, entitled "A collection of American
Epitaphs & Inscriptions," in 5 small vol.s &c.) -- I
send you for your amusement when you
have looked them over, your exchange them for
with Mr. Taylor, for some publication of his, that
will be valuable to me, I regret him to send it
out to me -- I save $4.50 for Alden's work -- I have
another set, if you will write to keep one, which I will
send you, or you may take it when you come home.
Mr. Pratt wishes you to visit her at Liverpool.
You must judge whether it will be expedient to
take such a journey -- We are glad to hear that
Mr. Wood has been your patron --

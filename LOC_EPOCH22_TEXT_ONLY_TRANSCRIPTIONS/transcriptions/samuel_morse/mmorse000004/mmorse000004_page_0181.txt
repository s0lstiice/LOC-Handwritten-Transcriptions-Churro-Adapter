Please tell Mr. Allston that I
received his last letter & that the
smell of the front has already
gone out of the room.
As I expected, I got the medal
on Saturday evening & have heard
that some handsome compliments
were paid my drawing by different
R. Rs. -- I rather think I shall
have an invitation to Mr Brown's
on Christmas & as you will be in
town I hope you can go also &
Mr & Mrs A -- I hope
you have some unsold
paintings to bring with
you to London to show us
what you have been about.
No letters from home for a long,
long time. -- I rather think John
is a little sick of the war. --
It is the general opinion of the
Americans here that peace is con-
cluding. -- Tell Mr Allston that
Lane (whom I meet frequently at the
Academy) is always very particular.

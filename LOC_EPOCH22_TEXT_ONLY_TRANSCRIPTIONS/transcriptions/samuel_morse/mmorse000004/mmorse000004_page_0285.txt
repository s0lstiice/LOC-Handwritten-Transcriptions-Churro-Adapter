I hope the time is not distant when Artists in your profession, & of the first class, will be honorably patronised & supported. In this case you can come & live with us, what would give us much satisfaction. We expect that Rich? will settle in his profession in this town -- He has acquired a good reputation at Littlefield -- we hope will be an ornament to his profession. Rich? also maintains a respectable standing in his class at Andover, is hope fully pious, very amiable & affectionate, & we must still be an able & faithful minister of the Gospel. It is our daily prayer that all our children may make it their chief object in their respective professions & in the use of their natural acquired talents, to promote the glory of God, & the good of man.
Your good mother, who is sitting by me, suggests that you had better send up, without frames, some of your best pictures (after favorable exhibition in England) to be exhibited in Boston, Phil? & perhaps N.Y. for your benefit -- Mr Sergeant has led the way in this business & made it honorable -- He is doing very well with his pictures -- Mr Alston might perhaps do well to send his pictures for the like purpose.

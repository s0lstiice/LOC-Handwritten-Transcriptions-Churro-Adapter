Mr. Samuel F. B. Morse
Dear Sir,
I received a letter from my brother
George, in which he desired me to request you to finish
the two pictures, that you are doing for him; that
I may take them with me, when I go to America.
I think I shall embark about the last of
next month or the beginning of August.
If they are already done, I should like
to send them by a friend, who goes out in the
Gulch. He wished me to help you a small
balance, which I will do. Waiting an answer,
I remain dear Sir
Yr. M. H. H. S. H. H.
Joseph H. Hayward
P.S. Please to address to care of John Bowfield
Manchester 1
Mr. J. H. H.

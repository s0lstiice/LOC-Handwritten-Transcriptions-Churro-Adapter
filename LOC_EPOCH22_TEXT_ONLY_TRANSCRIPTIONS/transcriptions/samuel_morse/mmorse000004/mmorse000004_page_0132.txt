been any extra Payments, but if there have been
extra Payments this sum will be so much the more
reduced -- you will therefore perceive that there
is a Deficiency for the current year, which I am
sorry to perceive there is a list of unpaid Debts
to the amount of £125: 16. -- all of which were
supposed to be provided for & discharged out of the
weekly Payments, to say nothing of what you may
have obtained from the Product of your own
Efforts -- this I am sorry to perceive, because it
discourses that your Expenses are augmented beyond
your Means -- and as to the property which you
place against the Debts as a Means of discharging
them, I fear that, except the 2 Landscapes sold, no
part of it will be realized to you, & perhaps even the
amount of these two may be doubtful, for I have
often observed that Pictures are an entire most
delicate subject to be sold.

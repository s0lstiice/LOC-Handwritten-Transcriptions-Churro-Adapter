My Dearest
I am concerned that it has not
been in my power since to preface the
inclosed letter and the accompanying parcel
for your worthy father. - That however they
will still be in time. - I should have
been glad of an opportunity of expressing to
you personally my regard & friendly wishes
previously to your departure. I must now
certen myself with the assurance that
I shall always reflect with pleasure on our
intereourse infrequent as it has been and
shall be most happy to render it, and that
I trust it will please a gracious providence
to afford you an early and happy meeting
with your friends at the other side of the
Atlantic. Mr Macaulay writes in
kindest remembrance Your ever faithful
Z Macaulay
26 Bunter Lane
96944 20 July 1875

you are surrounded & to which you are exposed, think of
these things - Remember your Creator - Let his fear be
before your eyes - Call to mind the advice of your
affectionate parents - how deeply they are concerned
for you - Daily read your Bible - neglect not to open
your morning & evening sacrifice to God, from a devout
heart - Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy - In-
clude yourself from improper company on that sacred
day - If you can find a suitable place of worship,
attend upon it steadily & devoutly, I get what
good you can from it. Will God preserve your
son, in health, from sin, prosper you in your professional
pursuits, & return you in safety, to your affectionate
parents & numerous friends.
I have written you on the subject of bringing home
all your paintings & of the importance of finishing your large
picture, in order that you may exhibit them in Boston
on your return, I am thus only raising something for your
support - as Mr Sergeant has set you the example of
led the way, by exhibiting his "Landing of the Fathers,"
by locked at 25th each. We think you might in this
way do something clever, & perhaps make a beginning
for a gallery of paintings in Boston, & awaken in
that town, & in N. Eng'l a spirit of emulation, not
to be out-doned in patronage of the fine arts, by
Brit. a or N. York - Now peace has come, they
may feel better able to do it - At least you had
better come as well prepared as you can for
executing such a plan - We wish, if practicable
to have our children planted near us, if they can
do it to advantage - We hope Edw. D will settle
in this town or Boston -
On second thought, I have concluded to send this
letter by Mr Wedder, as you will have one by the
Galen, & I shall not have time to write another
by him as my time is much occupied always at this
season, as we have in addition to the usual fast (on
the 6th Apr.) a national thanksgiving on the 13th -

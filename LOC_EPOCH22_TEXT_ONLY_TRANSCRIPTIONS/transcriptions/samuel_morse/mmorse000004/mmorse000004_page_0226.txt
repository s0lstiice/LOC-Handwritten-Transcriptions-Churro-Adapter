Mr Sam. G. B. Morse
Dear Sir,
Noticing the Reason which
has interfered with your calling upon me, as I had
looked for, I now enclose the Twenty Pounds
requested in your Letter of 16th. of this Month
requesting to be at your Post of the Town, I then call
upon you, I further entreat upon the Situation
you have mentioned until I have the pleasure
of your call -- in mean time however I cannot but
express how extremely grieved I have been on
the very melancholy Event to which you refer.
I pray present me with friendly Regards to Mr. Heston
& pray that I must truly condescend to call upon him -- it has
been continued in my Mind to call upon him for some days past the higher to permitted --
you will very sincerely
H: Bromfield

I have got along since I have been in England by
borrowing one, of my different Friends, but I shall
be deprived of that indulgence on returning home.
Lieut. Vinromt promised to execute the order, &
to pay for it himself, and he would receive the
money in America whenever I could make it
convenient. it will be shipped for Boston
from France; so do not be alarmed if you
should open a long box and see a figure in it.
I thought I never should have a better opportu-
nity, and was unwilling to let it pass. The price
is not to exceed 30 louisines. This sum I con-
sider as borrowed from you and will refund it
you from my first profit should it be necessary.

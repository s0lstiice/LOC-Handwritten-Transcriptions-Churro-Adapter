The minds of both your brothers have been very
serious for some time past. I hope they will both
have secured for themselves the good part which
shall not be taken away from them -- We
earnestly wish you would join them in this
choice -- We are glad to notice that you
have in viene in pursuing your favorite
art "the glory of God, the glory of man" --
Thee undoubtedly will be your object. --
Our first wish is that our children
may be good; then great eminent in
their professions -- If they cannot attain
both, we far prefer the former --
We notice have preserved your seat as a
curiosity. You have mentioned in former letters that
you were studying for some great picture -- what was
your subject? Have you begun it? -- Moses sitting
on the Rock, we thought a good subject -- one you thought
of here -- On Thursday I saw Mr Sergenth picture
the Landing of the Fathers -- I think it a fine painting --
It does honor to the Painter & to our Country --
This exhibition is a good beginning -- 25th admission --
many visitors.
Your Mama & family with Miss Russell, I your
other friends, join in love to you -- Your brave
& I wish to be kindly remembered to Mrs Alston
& to Mrs Leslie -- we had hoped you would come home
together this fall -- summer & heat fall --

44 Broadway New York Decr 27. 1814
My dear Sir
I regret exceedingly that want of
time has prevented me from answering your
of the 10th before this time.
Except of my unfeigned thanks for your
affectionate Christian Congratulation on my
safe return home. May for me that God
may make me useful more than ever to
his Church.
The enclosed letter of my friend Nathan
Schaick will give you the information con
cerning your Son which you desire. He
has been intimately acquainted with your
son for a considerable time. You may
rely on his Account, as he is not only a Gen
tleman of unquestionable integrity, but

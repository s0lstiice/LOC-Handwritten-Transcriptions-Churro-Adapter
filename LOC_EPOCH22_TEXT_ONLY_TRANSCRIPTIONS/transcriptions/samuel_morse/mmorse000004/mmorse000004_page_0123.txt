Bristol Sept. 23d 1814
My Dear Parents,
This will probably go by the same vessel
which carried my last; I find she has not yet sailed on
account of the wind, and unless it changes whilst I
am about this I shall go down to her. I am as
usual in excellent health & spirits, I have not yet
received any answer to my letters by Dr. Hayward & Cushing,
requesting leave to stay a short time in Paris, I am
making every preparation to go, the probable rout will
be first to Portsmouth, from thence by packet to
Havre de Grace, and from thence to Paris, this is the
shortest rout from Bristol to Paris, unless your
letter in answer should arrive and say otherwise
I shall be off in a week or two, they are all I am
waiting for. Since writing my last there has been
a most gallant action, between the brave little Wasp, and
the British slop of War Moon, the result of which
is that the Avon was sunk, the Carthian came up
at the close of the action just in time to take out the
crew of the Avon, during which time the Wasp made
her escape, some say the Wasp also sunk but this is
not well authenticated, and indeed is not believed,
the night was dark and the was soon out of sight.
The action was off Kinsale & Cape Clear on the 1st Inst.
There has been an action off Waterford in Ireland, for two
or three hours, seen from the shore, the result was, one of
the ships was sunk, the other escaped.

Remember more such as your picture of Mrs Payne & Barathia and any
others which may do you credit, little collabour, and try and think
to mind the landscapes for Dr Bartlett, of your Aunt Salisbury, the
portrait of Mr Russell, for Miss Russell, the seeing these pictures, in the
houses of these persons will go a great way in recommending you as an
artist. – Several gentlemen have expressed a strong desire to see for them
selves, your performances. You must be at some pains to see these things
sent to us, or they will not get here of themselves, Eliza Goddard has returned
I hear but has not called on us, although particularly requested by, the letter
I am writing will go in the Vespeill that carries Mrs Pratt and her Daughter
to Liverpool, Mr George Codman goes in her, and will proceed directly to
London, and be the bearer of this, he is Brother to Mr John Codman, the
minister, he is a young physician, was educated in Edinburgh Mr
Pratt, & Mary, will be glad to see you, should you have occasion to visit
Liverpool, on your way back to this country, or go there in quest of business,
your papa informed you that Mrs Eliza Backwood was dead he died in a
quick consumption, Mrs B with her oldest and youngest Child are to re
turn to her Fathers in Philadelphia, the other children to his Father, his furniture
was all sold to day I presume. – Mr John Soley is likewise very ill in
feard will not live long. I was very sorry to find by your letter that the
excellent Mr Thornton was dead. Improve the acquaintance of Mr Wilber
force while you have the opportunity as the time may not be long that
you may have the chance as he too may die I was glad to find that you
had not so far let your feelings run away with you in regard to Mr Wilber
force as to leave the benefit of his advice & instructions to wish you to make no one your
enemy. Your affectionate mother C D Morre

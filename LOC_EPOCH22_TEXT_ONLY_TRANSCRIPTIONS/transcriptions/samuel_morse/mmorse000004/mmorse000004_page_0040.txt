4 orders to have it sent by the first of opportunity. Mr Wilder will
tell you that he came near taking out my great picture of Hercules to you, it seems as though it is destined nothing of mine
shall reach you; I packed it up at a moment's warning and
sent it to Liverpool to go by the cartel, and I found it arrive
the day after she had sailed, I hope it will not be long, for
before both the boxes will have an opportunity of reaching
you. I am exceedingly sorry you have forgotten a passage
in my former letters where I wished you not to feel anxious if
you did not hear from me as often as you had done, I take
the reason, that opportunities were less frequent, more circuitous, and attended with greater interruptions, I tell you that
I should write at least once in three weeks, and that you
must attribute it to any thing but neglect on my part; Your
last letter has hurt me considerably, for owing to some accident
or other my letters have miscarried, and you will aid me
with neglect, and fear that I am not as industrious or correct
as formerly; I know you don't wish to hurt me but I can't help feeling hurt when I think that my friends
have not the confidence which I thought they had in me, that
some interruptions, (which all complain of, and are natural to
a state of warfare,) having prevented letters which I have
written from being received, instead of making allowances for
these things to have them attributed to a falling off in
industry & attention, wounds me a great deal. -- Mrs Allston to
her great surprise received just such a letter from her friends, &
it hurt her so that she was ill in consequence -- I have written
long letters and as frequent as formerly, but you must allow
that having long letters miscarry, which have taken so much time
to write, is but slender encouragement to continue them. --

Cheltenham, 5th Augt 1814.
Dear Sir,
Your favor of 6th inst. has reached me
here, where, unless some particular call shall occasion
my going to London, it is probable I shall remain
yet some time longer. Although the Extract given from
your Father's Letter appostolates your continuance in
England for a further Period, it is problematical
whether an inference may thence be drawn that he
would consent to your going to Paris -- It would not
be willing to sacrifice much for your improvement, and it behoves you to consider how far his financial means may enable him, in addition to what he
has already done for you, will facilitate to furnish the requisite means of enabling you to go with comfort.
For my own part I cannot presume to advise or even
to judge upon the subject. Mr. Austone will best be
able to judge of the benefit likely to result from such
an undertaking, and combining his opinion with what
you imagine of your Father's means doubt you suppose
will be his feelings upon the occasion, you must
form a conclusion for your own guidance.
In a letter written to your Father since being here

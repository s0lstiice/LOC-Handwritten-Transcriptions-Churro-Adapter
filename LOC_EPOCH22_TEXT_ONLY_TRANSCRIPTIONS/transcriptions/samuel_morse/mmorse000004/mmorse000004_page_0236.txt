My Dear Son
Your last letter was dated Oct 12th of Bristol your intention there
was to go to Paris in a short time from that date, we hope you went, and that
the letter we have received from you making the request for leave to go, have been
received by you & retired from mind, and left you at liberty to pursue your own
course – We now have the heart felt pleasure of congratulating you on
the return of peace between our Country & great Britain may it never again
be interrupted but may both countries bring the things that make for honor
& love as Brethren – Your Friends here are often asking whether we shall
not see you in the course of the Summer or Fall since peace has come, we
answer we hope so, we all long to see you very much indeed. We hope to receive
your pictures, viz. the Heracles by Mr James Knell, portrait for Shipley
& a handsome Landscape for Dr Bartlett & the Plaster Cast if not broke
to pieces are it gets here, by the first good opportunity that we may get
some of your improvements in your Art which will be a great gratification
to all your Friends. I hope you are going on with your large picture
& that you will be able to bring it home with you after gaining
the 200 guineas for its Exhibition. You must try all in your power to
clear what money you can in the way of your profession to help yourself,
as we are becoming very poor in consequence of what we have done
& are doing for our Children & others, we have been put to a vast deal
of trouble in our pecuniary concerns, they do not end with the War
we live quite Economically only four in family, your Papa & myself having
no condition make our present family, We hope to hear from you every
often till we see you, when you return we wish you to bring some excellent black or
Corbeau cloth to make your good Father & Brothers each a suit of Clothes,
Your papa also wishes you to get made a handsome black cloth Cook
for him one that will fit you he thinks he'll put him before I attend to this

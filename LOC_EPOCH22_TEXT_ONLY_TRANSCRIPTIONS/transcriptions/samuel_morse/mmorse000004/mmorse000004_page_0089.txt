Mr King received me as I wished, with
undiminished kindness; and was greatly
pleased with the pictures. He has not
however seen the large one; which, to
my agreeable surprise, I have been
selected from various quarters to exhibit, and
that he without my laying given the
least intimation of such a design. I have
taken Merrick's holly stall (a very large
room) for this purpose, and shall probably
open it in the course of next week. Perhaps you will be surprised to hear
that I have been retouching it. I have
just completed a fortnight's hard work
upon it, and have the satisfaction to
add that I have been more seldom
better satisfied than with my present
labour. I have repainted the greater
part of the figures, indeed those of all
the principal figures, excepting the Dead Man,
with powerful & positive colours; and added
double strength to the shadows & every figure

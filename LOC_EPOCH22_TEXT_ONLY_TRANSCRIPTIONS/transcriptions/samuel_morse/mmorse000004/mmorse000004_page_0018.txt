Last year 14,000.—
Nanny Rayner is dead. She left her property,
between 5 & 10,000 Dollars, to George Bartlett.—
Elizabeth Hooker, the daughter of Mr & Mrs Hooker
who were with Papa & Mama in Carolina, is now
living with us. She is about 10 years old, and is a very
lively, figgetty, horty-torty kind of a baby.—
Cousin Betsey Breeze has spent the winter in Boston
with Aunt Salisbury. She is well, and all our Utica relations
Grandmama is at Utica.— Grandpapa Morse has been
confined to the house by a blouse which he received in a
fall. He will never perfectly recover.— Uncle Jonathan talks
of selling his farm and removing into the Western country
under Wilkinson. One of Uncle Calvin's sons is in the army
Elisha Goddard has not arrived in America. We
have been waiting with great impatience to see your statue.
Write us more frequently.— Your friends here constantly inquire
after you.—
I am your affectionate brother
Sidney E. Morse.

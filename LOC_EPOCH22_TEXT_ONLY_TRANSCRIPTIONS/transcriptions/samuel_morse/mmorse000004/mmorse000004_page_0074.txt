June 13. 1814
Dear Sir Since writing the enclosed young Mr
Jack has informed us that he is about to embark for
Eng. I kindly offers to take charge of a large
parcel for you from Wilberforce Mr Taylor
I gladly improve the favorable offer to
send you & them, cast a copy of my Ap-
peal This book perhaps you had better keep
pretty much to yourself There has been
a most violent answer to it assembled to
Mr John Howell the effect of which will
be destroyed by its own violence I will
send it if I had a copy It may be sent
to Mr Alston -- H. S. it be sent to him you
may lend him the Appeal -- I commit it
to your direction If you wish you may
open the parcel for Mr Taylor & examine
the No. 7 of the Panoptist which I have sent
him If you reside any time at Bristol
or indeed wherever you reside I wish you to
write us once a month at least & let us
know particularly what you are doing, & the
state of your health I must transmit your letter
post free to Mr Rich. Brid Liverpool who will find
them for you -- or to Sam. Wm Esq. London himself better informed

My dear Sir,
I forget to tell you
how to use the Asphaltum. If
you mix it with oil it will
never dry. You must thin it
with spirits of turpentine to the
consistency that you wish and, when so thinned, put it
into a small gallipot. When
thus prepared, you may use
it freely with any other
colours on your palette, their
being ground in oil notwithstanding.
The Asphaltum was prepared
by Rowny, and dries well
without sugar of lead.
Yrs sincerely
W. Allston
Tuesday Evening
18 Febry.

New York December 14th 1814.
My dear Sir
Having just heard of a good opportunity to write to you via Lisbon
I avail myself of it to enclose to you in bill of exchange on London
for twenty five pounds sterling. The other I shall enclose to your father from
whom I have just received a letter informing me of his receipt of your order
for £24-12. I could not procure exactly that amount, and therefore beg you will
add to the books already purchased the works of William Tillinghast Lond
1740. Ed. in 10th Edition with his life by Birch in 1742. which I find by
the Catalogues may be purchased for seven shillings. -- I will make no
apology for adding the following books to the Catalogue of those which you
have already had the goodness to procure because your kind offer makes me
confident you will not consider it as too great a liberty. -- Critici Sacri seu
Dottissimum virorum in SS. Biblia Annotationes et Tractatus. Lond 1660 et
Amstelodami 1798. I do not know exactly the No of Vols. tho I believe 9. -- The
works of the Rev. C. Carneid John Lightfoot, D D. Revis'd & corrected by George Bright
D.D. Lond 1684. 2 vol. Fol. -- If the war continuing you will oblige me by having
them carefully packed in a light case I sent out by land yesterday in
the spring under convoy to the care of Mr Robert Foster of Montreal
for Mr Henry Fisher. Mr Fisher is a merchant of this city & Mr Frostle
his correspondent will forward them to him, perhaps free of cost since
Dr Rommy's books which have just arrived have been much damaged
from being packed so that the salt water could penetrate to them,
and if mine are sent to Montreal they will be transported a considerable
distance by land & exposed to danger of being robbed.
I am sorry so long a time has elapsed between the payment of the
books and the reimbursement of your money. Is there not some
quicker way of transmitting money to you?

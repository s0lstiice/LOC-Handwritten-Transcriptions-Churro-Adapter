W S G B. Messr
London
Bristol 5 Feb 7 1815
Your Sirs
I expected to have heard from you
since your return to London, long before this, particularly
when the result of the negotiation at Ghent was known
I thought I should have received a line from you
congratulating me on the event.
My family continue in a sad state of health
Hellen & Theodora are all confined to their
beds. The former is given very weak and cannot converse
long with us, he is perfectly resigned and has no
wish to continue longer than 1 pleases the Lord. She
requests to be kindly remembered to you and Mr
& Mrs Allston. Hellen is a good deal better and Mr King
has given her permission to get up a short time. They
Theodora he says has recovered more rapidly than
he anticipated and he has hopes with less injury
than he had reason to believe would be the case, in
in a few weeks more she may probably be able to leave
her bed. Mr Virgyn is tolerable well and Theodora is

Mr. J. intends to add a postscript herself, and would have done so to night had it not been for the lateness of the hour.
She says, for fear I should not have an opportunity in the morning, you will say that I send my kindest good wishes and congratulations.
Have the goodness to present my respectful compliments to your father, for whose happiness on account of your return I most sincerely rejoice, and believe that Mrs. J. & myself are as well. Your warmly attached, & affectionate friend.
Sam F. Jarvis
Sam F. B. Morse Esq.
My dear friend
It was merely to add my congratulations to those of my husband that I begged room for a postscript & to say that I hope it will not be a very long time before we may have the happiness of presenting them in our own house.
Mr. J. has left me nothing to say excepting to repeat the opinion that I am your affectionate friend.
Sarah M. J.

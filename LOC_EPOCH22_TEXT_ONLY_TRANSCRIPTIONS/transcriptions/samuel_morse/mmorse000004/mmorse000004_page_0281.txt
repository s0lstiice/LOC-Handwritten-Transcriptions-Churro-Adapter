I wish to hear from you on the subject of religion, or your own views
on this all important subject, believe you attend church, and that is going
on in the Religious World write us on these subjects, as much as you
can spare time for, We were much pleased with your letter giving an
account of the Death of Mrs Aston as discouraging a tender of prosperity
in the affliction of that family which was great. We need not tell you that
we take no comparison more satisfaction in commending and approving
and having others that are good people like him commended & approve
the conduct of our sons than in having the slightest reason to curse
or condemn them, and we acknowledge them with gratitude to our Heavenly
Father for his protecting & restraining grace granted you all that you
have afforded us the highest satisfaction that we are capable of enjoying
next to having the assurance of your all having been the subjects of
the renewing and sanctifying grace of God, which we hope if it be the will
of God we may yet live to see or hear of it. – In perfect consistency with
the feelings towards you all above described we may and ought to
tell you and that with the greatest plainness of any thing that we
seem improper in any part of your conduct either in a civil social

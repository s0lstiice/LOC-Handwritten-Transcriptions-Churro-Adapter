New York May 4th 1814
My dear Sir,
I wrote to you by the pacific last year, but not having
since heard from you, fear that you did not receive my letter.
Mr. Jarvis two months ago presented me a son, and to day
we set out for Saybrook to show the little stranger to
his grandparents & Aunts. I have only time therefore
to say that your friends remember you with affection
and that there is one in Saybrook who thinks of you more
than you imagine, & more than she knows herself.
I give you this hint, my dear Sir, because I feel a strong
friendship for you as well as for and wish that all those
little impediments which hinder persons of real worth from
forming a union may be removed. I would certainly aim
giving you hopes which might end in disappointment

St. Louis
I called as you requested me on
Miss Cropper, Benson & I enquired respecting
your Box, the first time I called I was told
the box had been taken away by Mr Godard
but as they were not very certain, I was very
desirous to call again, & I now learn that the Box
is still in their Warehouse & I have no doubt
is the same which you alluded to, it is directed to
your father, to Elisha Godard & to the case of
Cropper Benson & is about two feet long &
not quite as broad = Mr Willem had said in the
cartel previous to my arrival, of course I cannot
inform you respecting the Box containing your Miscals.
the box was between the Corners of the
corner of the house, a careful inspection
of the house would have revealed
the box
to be in the cellar
of the house
and not in the
warehouse
of the house

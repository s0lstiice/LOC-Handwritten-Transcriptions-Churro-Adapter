Will you be so good in your next letters as to repeat all former orders for books, &c. (as many I am confident from your late letter must have miscarried) that I may in season get every thing together for home; you had better not write me after the end of June or beginning of July as letters may be detained, and I shall endeavor to get away as near the beginning of Sept. as I conveniently can.
About my finances I don't know how they will hold out in the various great and small expenses of returning home, I calculate they will exceed considerably what I have got left; should this be the case I will refund to you what I expend on my own account the rest of course will be yours.

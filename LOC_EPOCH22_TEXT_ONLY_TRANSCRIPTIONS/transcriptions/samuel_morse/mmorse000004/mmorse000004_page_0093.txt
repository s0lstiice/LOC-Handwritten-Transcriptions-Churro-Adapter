has a large number of nieces & nephews and many who are dependent
communities on her bounty, and who she doubtless feels it her duty to assist
to the utmost of her means; it would therefore not be correct to say any
thing to her on such a subject, In reading your last letter to her the
other day, I omitted what you mention with respect to asking her to
assist you, but in the course of conversation I mentioned your desire
to be patronized by your Friends in America. She thought you would
do much better to look to your own exertions, and if you did not get
a picture ready for next May Exhibition, why you might the May
following, and in the mean time by taking portraits & painting small
sictures be getting together something clever, which you would en
joy so much the more for being of your own earning. She requested
me with great solicitude to press upon you the great importance it was
to you, to renew to yourself the friendship of Mr. West, this patronage we

Liverpool Aug. 4th 8th 1815.
My Dear Parents,
I have arrived thus far on my way
home, I left London the 5th and arrived in this
place yesterday the 7th at which time, within an
hour, 4 years ago I landed in England, I have
not yet determined by what vessel to return, I
have a choice of a great many, the Ceres is the
first that sails, but I do not like her accommodation
the Liverpool Packet sails about the 25th and
as she has always been a favorite ship with me
it is not improbable I may return in her.
I have seen Capt. Pratt and family, by whom
I have been rec'd with great attention, some of
them (the young ones) have grown quite out of my
recollection, I need not tell you with what pleas-
ure I saw my old acquaintance Miss Mary,
she has grown quite a fine polished girl, Charles-
town has lost one of its greatest attractions in losing
her. I have nothing to say as I shall see
you so soon, I shall probably see Mr. Goddard
to day have not yet seen him.
I am with Mr. & Mrs. Wathey, who have
shown and are showing me every possible attention.
They desire particular remembrance to you.
Excuse my haste Truly yours
Saw: my B. Mosse

and receive it kindly let your letters be in future fraught with the
same tender affectionate expressions of duty and love and all that is good
that they Used to be, before you had your mind so soured by the state of
publick affairs, let your letters, and your actions, both show that you are
of a truly gratefull disposition, and be ready to fulfill any Commission
that your parents may request of you, even if you should have to stand
still in your Art till you had accomplished it, to me, were it in your
place the heart felt pleasure I should feel from having done a kind
act, to oblige my parents would more than make amends for
all the time spent in executing it. we do not intend to be troublesome

It is all important that every patent I have should
be original. If it should be known that I have empraved
either from Copie, when Original, or from, it would operate against
my work. I want an engraving from the late celebrated Mr
Hancock's portrait, which I am told is in Boston, & hopes
to procure of his widow. Have the goodness to give to
me & certain whether it favour will be granted to me
by it being sent here for a few weeks till the engraver is
done with it. If it cannot be done, I beg you will to copy
it of the same size of Adams's, or 1/2 cloth head in large
as like as hands required, for to save time, &c you will do Adam
for.
Not being able to afford it, prevents me from offering you
more than half of your present price; of course I shall consider
your compliance as a great favour.
I have just had a conversation with your friend Mr.
King who also agrees to paint for me for the work at half
price. -- He promises to write to you in a day or two.
The two pictures I shall want packed in a good box
endorse your two of my engravings which please to
accept as a token of my respect.

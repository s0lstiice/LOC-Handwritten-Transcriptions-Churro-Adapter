I have the Presid.t's approbation of my plan for
the benefit of the Indians -- The prospects are
at present good, as I am reasonably expect.
I send you this by Mr Wm H. Mitchell,
a Yale scholar from Charleston, now a student
in divinity for the Episcopal Syn. -- I wish
to introduce you to him, as a pious young
man, a fellow lodger with me for a day or
two -- His funds being short, I have lent
him $15 -- with he will pay to you -- You
may inform me when you receive it, I
will remit it with any you may send me
Remember me affectionately to Richd. -- &
to all Charleston friends, as you see them
God bless you, prosper you, my dear son
from every affectionate
father J. Mosse
I presume you will have heard of the death
of your good Grandfather on the 29th ult. through
the hands of a spy from your mama. He was a
good man -- his spirit I trust now rests in heaven.

New York Jan 14th 1820
My dear wife
comfortable home from Albany 163 miles
from Albany I received your letter of the 8th
I am well at our pleasant home at half past
2 in the afternoon. I have not been abroad this
morning. I shall leave my letter open till I have
seen the decision of the Board of Commissioners
at Washington. I must wait till I arrive at
Washington. I presume you did not avail yourself of the
opportunity of writing me by May 11th. Am glad you have received
from the 6th. you may probably receive from Houston $38. La paper parcel.
Tell Cousin I shall see Kirk Spencer
the morning. I shall do what he requests. I will
write him from Washington what he wishes to
know of the results of my several pleasant interviews with Mr. Crawford. He sent me his pamphlet by mail 10 days before I left home. I went
to the office in P.C. some where I sent another
post to see it. Mr. H. has examined topography, the
maps for the new Atlas. The new edition of
the large type is highly spoken of by good judges.
The property in that work is certainly good.

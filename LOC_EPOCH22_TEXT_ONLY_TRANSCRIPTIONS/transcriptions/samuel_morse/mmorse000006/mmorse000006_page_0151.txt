John's Island Nov. 25. 1819.
My dear Brother,
Agreeably to my promise that I
would write you on my arrival, I inform you
that I arrived on Tuesday after a passage of 11
days. I called at Dr. Palmer's who was not at
home but effected next week from Beaufort, I
was shown the way to Aunt Finley's, where I saw
Rush - Mary & Anna & Edwards - Aunt was at her
sister's - next door, who was just confiried - She came
in & I dined there - went with Rush to Mr. Legard's
who was going next day to the island. He
invited me to breakfast next morning &
I concluded to go with my baggage, deferring
visits in Charleston till I was settled. Aunt was
well, & all inquire when you are coming on. With
me let me know. - I have left Henry's bed & at
the Store of D.W. Hall No 132. East Bay
he haste, join affect brother Rich T. Marks

My dear Son, I wrote you two days ago by
mail & now drop a very short line by
Captn Webber -- Your short letter announ-
cing your arrival in the only one we
have received from you since you left St. John's.
You may well suppose we are extremely
anxious to hear from you -- whether your bag-
gage arrived safe -- & whether you are
engaged in your business -- with what
sweet greetings I think you must have written us
knowing our anxiety -- that your letters must
have miscarried -- It is important to our
happiness, in the absence of you all, to hear
often from you -- & to know of all your affairs.
My rhymemtion has not yet left me &
I fear will not soon -- If I keep confined,
I am comfortable a great part of the 24
hours -- Your mother is well -- but feels
a good deal of that "sickness" with Hope
"deferred" always occasions. -- My mail
letter will inform you of particulars

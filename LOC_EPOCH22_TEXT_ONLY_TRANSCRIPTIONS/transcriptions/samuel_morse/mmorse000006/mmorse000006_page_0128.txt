In this case I wish I possessed a little more

I have not heard from Elizabeth since I
wrote her mentioning it impossible for me
to return this fall, as we had both fondly
hoped. but I have, I trust, made every
neccessary apology to the good girl for
my unavoidably long absence.
Not long since I wrote her, & told her
I should immediately write you, with
a request, if you visited Potomac that you
would take her miniature to match
mine, which she has now in her possession.

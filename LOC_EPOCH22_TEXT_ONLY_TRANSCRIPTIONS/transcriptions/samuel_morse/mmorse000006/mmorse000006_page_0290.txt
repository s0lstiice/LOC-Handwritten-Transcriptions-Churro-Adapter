I wish Edw'd to enclose to me to meet me at N.Y. in
immediately on receiving this, the first of the 2000
three bills - reserving the other two - I can displace
it in N.Y. better than in Boston. Write me, that
best. You have sent on to this city by my request,
I to whom sent - I expect no act. If my order does - I
with Edwards to get the first part of the new Bridge-
ment in readiness as soon as possible that it may
be put to use. I expect the whole finished, under our in-
spection before our removal - If you can see
Mr. Steepon & Capt. Spillet on the subject of the
sale of the house, I urge their assistance in
enabling us to sell it. If so, be glad. You will
of course be disposing of articles of our country
if you have opportunity to make your business
whatever you can sell for its value
you had better sell enough of our favorite articles - Probably Mr. Tracy can dispose of the
valuable furniture, stable and yard
with is better fitted to the house than his will be -
You will think of all these things.
I cannot exactly tell when I shall be at home, but
shall write you on my way - I shall come home with
a new set of feelings for the scene will be trying. I hope
I shall be enabled to bear it - I have written
instructions & advised to be ready to go with us on
our removal to N. Haven - there will be enough
of us to take a stage by ourselves. I shall write
to Mr. Spillett & Rich before I leave the city of Boston. Know
the fate of the family & of myself. I must stop. Remember my respects to all
my friends - Love to the family - I will bless you my dear wife & family at home

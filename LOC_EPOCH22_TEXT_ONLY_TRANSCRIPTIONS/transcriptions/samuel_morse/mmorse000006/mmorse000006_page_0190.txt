My dear wife
After a very comfortable ride, half way on runners, & half way on wheels, I arrived
here in little more than 7 hours after I left you & feel even a little fatigued -- my cough is evidently
leaving me -- if I shall ride as comfortable in future, I doubt not my health will
essentialy serve my purpose. I have spent the event with Mr Goodrich at
my lodgings -- Mr Waldo was engaged in business, he does not leave -- I dared not go out in the evening -- We start at
5 O'clock in the morning -- I have a tire in my room -- I shall have one to set up by --
I feel comfortable as to matters of the City Council at home, after what I have done -- I am
prepared for the issue of the City Council -- I shall send you money or a check
by Mr Thurston -- It is time I was in bed -- I go out tonight -- Love to your family --
97289
Buffalo Skin is an admirable defender of the body -- I can't do without it --
your affectionate husband
97289
Mr. Goodrich

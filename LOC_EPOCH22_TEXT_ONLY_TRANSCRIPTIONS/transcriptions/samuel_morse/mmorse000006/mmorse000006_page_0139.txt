Charlestown, Oct 26.1819.
Dear Mother,
Finley takes his desk to the South,
(as I should suppose he would) & whether it is easier
for you to remain without a desk this winter, as
heretofore; or for me to either to be without, or
purchase a new one you shall judge. A new one
costs 10 or 12 dollars, & I had rather purchase next
year. I think I must have one. Let me know
done by writing or by sending the desk —
The ship South-Boston goes 10th Nov.— Probably
I shall go in her. Papa wrote last from N. Haven
wh. he left on Saturday for N. York— His health
better, his spirit, 'as he receded from the
land of troubles.' But I hope he will remain
& not return here this great while— I wish
when he came back he would find a man
settled here: & I only fear he will come back
too soon —
I left your letter yesterday at Amstons,
Please to give the enclosed to Mr Gibbs

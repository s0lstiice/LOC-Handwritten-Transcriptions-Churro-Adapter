Boston, April 5, 1819:
My Dear Sir,
I send you herewith a copy of
our late Panoplist Circular, which I wish you
to communicate, or cause to be communicated
to all the members of the Theological Seminary.
This can be done, I presume, at some meeting,
when nearly all will be present.
I wish to be informed, within a fortnight
from the present time, how many of
the Students will take subscription papers
in ensuing vacation, & how many copies of
the four numbers of the present volume they
would wish to take with them, to leave with
subscribers. It is to be remembered, that all
the numbers taken must be carefully returned,
if not subscribed for. Any number wanted,
both of the Panoplist & Herald, can be furnished
with allowance will be made of 25 cents
for every subscriber obtained for Pan. & 50
cents if the money is returned with the name
& 10 cents for every subscriber to the Herald, &
20 cents if the money is brought with the name.

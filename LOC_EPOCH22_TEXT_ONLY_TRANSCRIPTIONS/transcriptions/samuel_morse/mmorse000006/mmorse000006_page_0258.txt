Competition
On this delicate subject I have only to say, that I feel to great an interest in accomplishing
the object in view that I shall readily spend my own time & ask only that the necessary
expenses of the tour be paid by the Govt. What these will probably be the President & Secretary
have better means for estimating than myself. I have intimated I think it indispensable that
I should have a companion. My age, state of health & the nature & labor of the employment all require it. As
the President has suggested, I think a vehicle prepared for the purpose to be drawn by one able horse, will
be the best convenience. I could with this horse vehicle if consignled, might be purchased by the property

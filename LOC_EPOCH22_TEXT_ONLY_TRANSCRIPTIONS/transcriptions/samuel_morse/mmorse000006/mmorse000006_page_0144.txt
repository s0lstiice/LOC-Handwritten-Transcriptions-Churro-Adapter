vious. -- Mr Hay preached last Sabbath 3 times to great accept-
ance, the audience were solemn and seemed much affected, I
was myself exceedingly pleased with his manner, style, and doc-
trine and think him well calculated to unite the good friends
of this Society, and I find that the friends of both sides
as far as I have heard, speak in high terms of him; now my dear Father, is not here an indication in pro-
vidence of union in this Society, and union too complete that
very influence, which we now feel it our duty to oppose, will
those men be likely to retain influence, under such doctrine
and with a phalanx against them composed of all the friends
and well disposed in the Society; let the Lord send by this hand
of him whom he will send, it does appear to me as plain as

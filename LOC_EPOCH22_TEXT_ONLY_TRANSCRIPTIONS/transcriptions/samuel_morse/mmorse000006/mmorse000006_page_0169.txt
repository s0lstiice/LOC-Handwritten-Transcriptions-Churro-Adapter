Remember me affectionately to all
Yr Affectionate Son

P.S. I really forgot to ask how the great folks
come on in Charleston, I hope they will pardon my re-
glect and my slighting them by putting them at the tail of
the letter: -- The President certainly must have forgotten
his accustomed politeness for he never has asked after Lot
Pool, John Soley, the Dean -- not James Warren the shoemaker, nor
Black Polidore nor Caleb Sweetser. -- unpardonable neglect. --

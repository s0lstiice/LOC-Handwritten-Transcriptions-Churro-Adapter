And now what I most earnestly desire of my Dear Husband is that
he would not through the slightest Optical in their way but do every
thing to show a noble Generous Spirit have no Content with them about
the pretty Business of the repairs which we have from time to time made on the
house and let them have all the advantages remitting from them in some
way or other if they should be adjudged you; Generously order them to
the poor of the Church or laid out as a fund in the Savings Bank
for their Use I would even do this to the the Interest that I draw from
them on the Moneys due from them at different times I am sure the fine
Proposition made on the Mind of your Friends in this Place by one who
would be vastly more favourable of your Conduct to them than if you would
rigidly stand out for your rights let them be put in the Wrong altogether
I hope my Dear you will direct by your Heavenly Father in the right way to
terminate this unpleasant Business as Speedily as possible and that you will do
nothing to delay them in their Course the sooner we are free from them the bet
ter I am aware of more persuade of this therefore get home as soon as
possible or give liberty to have them proceed without you as you shall think best Mr. Say said
they were to write you to day I suppose you will have a number of letters from
them at this juncture – Mr. Grant says you had better inquire at Lisbon how
on your way home about a vessel he says they came with freight not more than two
or three times a year and then in the Spring and thinks they had better engage
there to take our goods on their return from Boston as a part of their
freight Back this method will be support for us. I am like wise even they
can be stored safely when they arrive at N.Y. Edwards is hand at the
Abingment think he shall make something of it before he is done he wishes
if you come through Woodstock that you should procure all the Biographical
information you can of Fathner that on a sketch may be prepared for the

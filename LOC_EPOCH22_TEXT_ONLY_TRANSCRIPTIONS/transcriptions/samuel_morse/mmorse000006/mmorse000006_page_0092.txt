July 3d 1879
Dear Sons, I enclose an open letter to
the Professory for you to read, deal with
them. It will inform you of the state
of things at present. When I know more
I will write you. - Esg. F. will be home
next week - write by him - We will also
write you on his return - My for-
tunt has not come - take care that it
be not lost - If it has been left in-
form me whom I enquire where it
has been left. - Does Rich? go to
Beverly? I think he is? speaks as
often as he has opp? for him to
pay his expenses - It will be
full as well as profitable to him -
I hope he will write many of
our sons this summer -
Your mother sends a bundle to
Edw. Jr. for -
We expect Finley & Luricka in
a few hours - I quite long to see them
- all well, I send love -
Your affectionate
Father J. Morce
I communicated my
commission to visit the Indians to
them yesterday, I shall probably
undertake it to fulfill the commission
this fall.

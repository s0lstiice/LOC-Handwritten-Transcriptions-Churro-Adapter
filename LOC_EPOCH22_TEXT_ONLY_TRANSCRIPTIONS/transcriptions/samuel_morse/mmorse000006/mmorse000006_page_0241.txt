Washington Jan 22d 1820
My dear Wife,
As I promised I will to you again today – I am now lodged very comfort-
ably & hospitably at Rev Dr Lawry's one of the Ministers in this city (for whom I will preach tomorrow PM) who about 14 yrs ago
was at our house. He is a very pleasant man – has a pleasant family – it is a great
relief to know I already feel the benefit of the change, to my health – I am evidently
better to day –
I have had to day a most satisfactory interview with the Secy of War, & Col Stilts
Steph Calthous & Adams – The former is the principal man I have to do with – By
the bye the Mrs Calhous, with whom we are acquainted, who was so kind to Mrs Bird
is here (Mrs C, mother in law I believe) – & I am to drink tea with them this afternoon.
Both the Secretaries enter cordially into my plan – The business of the Stockbridge Indians
(who have gone home) is yet in good train – Lame here in good time & circumstances to help them

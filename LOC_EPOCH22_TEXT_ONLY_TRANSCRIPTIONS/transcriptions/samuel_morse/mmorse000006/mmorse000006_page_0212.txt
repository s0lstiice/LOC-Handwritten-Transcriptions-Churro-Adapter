I went immediately to Aunt Finley’s, the moment
they saw me they all burst into tears I were silent; I never felt
more depressed than at this time, jaded out by a long, fatiguing
steeples ride, finding Aunt and little cousin in a different
house; in black; Aunt so altered I should scarcely have
known her, every thing reminding me of Uncle’s absence, this
too combined with the absence of my dear Lucretia, at Mr.
Munro’s, the room where we slept, and dined, and supper
the places where we walked, all, all made me; dear wife,
feel most miserably, I felt by anticipation, what might
take place, were we taken from each other by death, Oh!
may this day be far removed in mercy, but especially
dearest may we prepare at any time to say, “the Lord save
and the Lord hath taken away, blessed be the name of the
Lord.” I went early to bed, slept soundly 12 hours &
waked entirely refreshed and well, every one says I have
grown more fleshy, I have not an ache or pain; what
shall we render to the Lord for all his benefits?”
I have got excellent rooms over Dr. Bux-
baum’s Apothecary shop, it is as we had heard, he lost
his wife and partner in business, and was at death’s door
himself with yellow fever; the rooms are very conven-

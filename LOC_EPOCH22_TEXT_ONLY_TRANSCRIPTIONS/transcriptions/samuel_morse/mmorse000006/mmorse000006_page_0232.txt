Now for I had written then Professor Haddock & his brother came in
he saw his picture but could not take it at present and knew not when he should
be able to pay for it as the Funds of Dartmouth College were so demanded as not to
put it in the power of the officers to realize anything of their Salaries that him
we should remove from this place in April he said he would send for the
picture before that and pay for it when he could. I received your parcel by Mr
Hurston but I have not had any money if he was to send it I do not wish it
but if you sent it by him I will apply for it I have not applied to any one to loan
me any money do I intend to if I can avoid it but then you must not expect
me to pay any debts I cant pay Ethelridge Mulip I receive same from some where or other I think you will do well to advertise
your house as soon as you return if not before we are all well and send for
me any news but Mr Evarts will take your Glass Book case for $20 of Mr L2
will let him if he does not at Mr Tay probably will & the Pide Board for $20
this is more than can be obtained for them in any other way the large table in the
Study I told Mr E he might have for $55 as all these things will suffer by
removal so much as to make it best to dig for of all we can. Catherine Eder
has just been in & Denver to be remembered to you Martha has a bad cold
Raney E is better the rest well I went for Abby & Mr Salisbury Children to
spend the day they did not chuse to come of course have not seen
them since you left us. — I have not been out since you left me even over
the risk of the door. I wrote Rich and a long letter giving him my
opinion of the Qualifications necessary for a young lady to select him that
would make him a suitable Wife I hope he will regard them so
as not foolishly to engage himself to some silly pretty Girl of a Girl
that may strike his Eye. I am your affectionate wife E D

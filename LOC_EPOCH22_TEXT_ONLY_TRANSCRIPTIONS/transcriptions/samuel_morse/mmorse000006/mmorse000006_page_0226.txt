John's Island Jan 17, 1820.
My dear Brother,
I was extremely happy to hear
by Mr Legare of your arrival & thank you for
your note. I am impatient to see you. I have received
a letter from Edwards dated Dec 19. in which he refers
me to you for information of Passa's & your
own plans. He had a letter that morning from Texas
who was well & the babe tho' been troubled
with colds. I presume you have later intelligence
however. If an opportunity offers I will come to Charleston
to see you, but in the mean time I will not delay
communicating a plan by which I think we can have
a much more agreeable interview. Mr Legare suggested
soon after I came the hope that it would be
little intermission to your business to come over on
Saturday, spend Sabbath & return early in the week. He
repeated it this morning, I knowing that I was going
to write you, requested me to say to you that it would

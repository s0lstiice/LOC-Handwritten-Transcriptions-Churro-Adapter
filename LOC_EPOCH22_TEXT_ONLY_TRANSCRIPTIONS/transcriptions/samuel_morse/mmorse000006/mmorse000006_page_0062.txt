Apr. 16. 1829
Dear son, Your of yesterday re- there is an act
of Venezuela in the last No. of Rev Cyclo. which I have in the annexed we leave of the other places
you name in S. A. & W. Georgia will not
all the necessary information, I have already
done Georgia shall take Florida, Alabama next & as to Michigan on the W. of the
Mt. then Missouri Arkansas &c. declare
U.S. with a general act of the Congress
to the Pacific -- the Spanish claim to
S.-- as you have divided them -- perhaps last
the W. Indies & other Amer. isles -- A few
weeks more will complete the whole --
I shall have little more for you to do but
to make the Index which will be in Japan
the middle of May -- I send your sketches
as far as done -- examine & send me back
(if you dont come home as your Mother & me
wont) by Mr C. You can proceed with
the New Al. (which must be the same size
as the last) as you will have the first sheet
of U.S. I of the whole of Vol. II. in the sheet sent
for the Index. I think it will best to have
the two Continents (exclusive of introduction)
about the same size as the new Al. -- about the same
no of pages -- the remainder printed for Japan
It is just to admit it to be a reading book --

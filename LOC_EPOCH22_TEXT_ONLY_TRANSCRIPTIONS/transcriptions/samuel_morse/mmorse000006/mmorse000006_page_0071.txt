Charleston April 1819
My Dear Son
I write you a few lines by Mr. Drews, your pastor preached
yesterday at Charleston for Mr. Homer that Decham might be superseded
In Council preached here three times yesterday. I hope our people
Will be well pleased with him as to give him a call, but I do not know
that this will be the case your father wishes him to settle on a
place for a year or two or less to give him the opportunity to reconcile
the two parties so as to have them all united in Mr. C and also to enable
him to settle all his affairs in this region so as to quit with the
least disadvantage to all concerned. I hope Mr. C will if applied to by
them, make this stipulation that Mr. M shall do this. Your father has
probably informed you that 20 of the church who met in regular
Church meeting (the meeting consisting of 30) voted by Ballot to
propon to your father to call a mutual council and choose a Com-
mittee of seven to make out their charges against their pastor with
their proofs to be first submitted to him, and then to the Council if
he agrees to it, if not they mean to have an extra-legal Council
the Committee are the three opposing Deacons with Dr. Warren John Riley
Jehah Mead & Lot Pool, who has gotten to be a great Big man
because he can be as Impudent as you please to the pastor, these
men are now engaged in looking up all the Zint that they can
collect to justify themselves in the course in which they have
procured and here is your poor mother almost Disttracted, failing herself
and her Husband in the hands of those who are acting like Bolivar
Years (in their opinion) toward him. I hope you will come down
next week and see us and that you will not refuse to visit your
poor afflicted father in the arrangement of his geography. Do long to

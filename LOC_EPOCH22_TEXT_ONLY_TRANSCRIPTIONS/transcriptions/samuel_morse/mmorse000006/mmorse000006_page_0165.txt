My Dear, Mr. Morse,
I sailed from Boston on Saturday, 4 days after you left there
and arrived here after a passage of 12 days. I sailed on Mrs Finley and
presented her with the letter which you favored me with of your family
well she invited me to take her with her and said she would send and
provide a boarding plan for me in Charleston to town, as she could not accom-
modate me in her own family; she succeeded in obtaining me a situation
with Mr Holmes in Lynch's Lane – Sent to the P.C. and found a letter
of direction from you – Agreeable to your orders I have carried your
Paint loss, Guns, do, Writing Desk & the 2 judges of bills, to Mrs Finleys together
with the Barrel of Apples – The other 2 cases are not taken out of
the Ship yet, but I have spoken to Mr J. Leland of Brothers & Nashville
Store them till you come, I shall take them as soon as they can be got in
Boston –
Mr J. F. B. Morse Esq
Sam. C. B. M. Esq
97275
Charleston Decr. 4th 1819
Theater has been pleasant since my arrival
and business is increasing – I have not been seen your Brother
yet nor any one that I knew before, I understand that the Ship J. Morton
had a passage of about 10 days from Boston I shall wait every in port
& hope I shall see you soon
Your Affectionate friend & Servt
Henry C. Stuart

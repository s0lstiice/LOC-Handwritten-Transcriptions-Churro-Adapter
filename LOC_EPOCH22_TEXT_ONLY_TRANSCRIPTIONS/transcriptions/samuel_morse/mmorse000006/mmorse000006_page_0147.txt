Brother Moore I was heartily sorry
to disappoint you, for you obliged
me much. I was absent from Conway
when your line arrived here, otherwise
I should have sent you the money by
Stotts. I find it impossible to get money
even from those who are owing it.
Honesty has compelled to disoblige
my friends. The incident five dollars
had it but in my power I should
before. I write in haste – The
mail is just going out. I
shall write Br. Stotts soon.
Remember me to my Comrades
etc.
Conway Nov. 9th 1819.
Yours affectionately
L. Hamilton

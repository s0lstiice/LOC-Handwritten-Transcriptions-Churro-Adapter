audience occasioned not an unpleasant moment —
I am extremely pleased with it — There is certainly very consi-
erable likeness, & the more I examine it the better pleased I am
but I do not think the likeness quite as striking as I could have
wished — with regard to the painting, you know, nothing can
exceed it — as a painting alone I would have given much
more than it cost me, & considered myself fortunate in me-
eting with it — Some of my friends think the likeness extrem
strong — I am satisfied it is the best ever taken of my Daughter —
I should be much gratified to see the fine Picture
of Mr Washington Allston, but must decline the purchase
of it — It has always been my intention to procure a piece
of Mr Allston's painting & I am sure I shall do so even though
it cost more than the present one — but I would greatly
prefer some of his figures to a Landscape, & would wish
more over to have one of his last productions as
every one will constantly improve — I certainly shall
at some future day apply to him — I assure you Sir, I feel
much flattered & gratified at your kind letter in assisting
me to procure these fine paintings — it will induce me
to take the liberty of getting your assistance at some time
hence, which otherwise I should not have ventured upon
With regard to Mr Fisher's painting of the fall
of Niagara, I wish you when you next write, to have
the goodness to state to me his price & the length & breadth
of the picture & will then determine upon it — In the
mean time receive my very best thanks for the prefer
you have been pleased to give me — His fame has reached
me — it is as loud as that Cataract — Believe me
Dear Sir, with much respect & consideration —
Your obliged subser
John H. Allston

I have not received any money lately of sufficient importance
to send you, but hope to send a remittance soon; I shall not
make so much this season as last, from a variety of causes all
of which I cannot explain in a letter, one is that Lucretia's
illness has caused a considerable expenditure of time to me
and there have been several pictures which I painted last
year that I have redeemed by painting them over again.
Will you be so kind as to inquire of Mr. Arthur Benjamin in
Broad st. Boston whether he has obtained for me the Kremlitz
White which he was to get from Germany for me; I hope he
has; I found a small quantity in Charleston and have sent for
more from Germany through a German who informed me there it was
manufactured, it is at a place called Kremlitz in Silesia,
which is famous for its manufactories of this pigment; I
tell Mr. Benjamin of this fact, as he was ignorant of
place where it was to be obtained. -- Has not my last
figure been heard of yet? I sensibly feel the want of it.
Please give my love to all; will write again soon; this is
written in great haste. -- The Surf-Times arrived safe some
time since 344 days passage, we got the bundle of kinns, but no
books; I shall go and inquire again particularly for them. --
Good bye. Lucretia sends a great deal of love.
Remember me particularly to Mr Alston, shall write him very
soon; have not got his picture.

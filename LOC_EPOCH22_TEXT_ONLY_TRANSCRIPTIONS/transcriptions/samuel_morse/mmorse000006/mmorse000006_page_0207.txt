My Dear Husband
This day and tomorrow I presume you will spend in New
York if nothing has intervened to delay your arrival there agreeable
to your letter of the 6th from Albany which was received last Monday.
We were much pleased to find you had succeeded agreeable to your
expectations and sincerely pray that if the Undertaking is calculated
to promote the Glory of God as we trust it is he will graciously smile
on it and bless it. – This day took up the Newspaper and the
first thing that caught my eye was the death of our much loved
and excellent Father Geer my dear Husband he has entered into
his rest with respect to him we may say He is well he hangs
above the wicked care from troubling and where the weary
are at rest may we be daily prospering to follow him and all
who through faith and patience are inheriting the Promises
the paper says he died the 29 of Decr aged 94, this was the third
week day before you left home why none of the family should
have let us know it is astonishing to us all I should have
thought they would have sent an purpose but perhaps they
are anxious to satisfy us when known. – We have not heard a word from Finley or Richard since you left
us but are expecting to soon I had a letter from Secretia last week
she and little Susan were well though the latter had been sick with
a cold I say they send a great deal of love to me all and Grandpa
in particular and that little Susan sends many kisses & that she
is the best company in the world they think in their family

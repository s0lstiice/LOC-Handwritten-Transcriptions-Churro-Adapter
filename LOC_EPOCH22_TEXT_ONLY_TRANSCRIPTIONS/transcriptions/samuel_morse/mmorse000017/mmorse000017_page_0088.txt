in regard to my acting in such Department under your control

made either useful to every Branch of the State
Government or to the whole State. I have informed you, that since you first heard my
illustrations, that by improvements in Machinery
and long practice in knitting, I have rendered the
Attainment of Perspective a simple and easy affair
and could do much more to improve & expedite the facilities
of its Machinery if I were permanently attached and
continued by Authority in a Public Institution.
It is true the Council of the Nat'l H. appointed
a committee to examine my apparatus & method of
Instruction, they attended to that Duty and their Report
tho' not approved & accepted in a manner gratifying
to me, they furnished me with a Copy from their Minutes
which with their former kind offer of a Room to Lecture
in has proved an availling though in circumstances beyond
their control (the winter Technique School in blazing so
as to leave no room) but if you feel confidence in
my zeal & Talent, you situation as professor at the
University enables you to open that Department

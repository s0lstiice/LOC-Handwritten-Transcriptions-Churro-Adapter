Baltimore April 12th 1845,
Pass for more
Dear Sir
I have heard nothing from N
York in relation to these oranges things for you.
In relation to the interest of Mr Smith
in the Telegraph, I thought I would make a sug-
gestion if it could be brought about, and that is, if I
can make an arrangement with him for one
1/8th or 1/4 of his portion here – do you or not,
know of some who would like to purchase the
balance? If so – perhaps I could offset this.
Yours Respectfully
David Burkart.

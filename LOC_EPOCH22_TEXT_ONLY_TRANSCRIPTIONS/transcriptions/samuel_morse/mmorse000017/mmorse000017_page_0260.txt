Baltimore May 28th 1844
Professor S. J. B. Morse
Dear Sir:
The electric tele-
-graph being now in successful operation,
you will find it necessary to appoint an
assistant at Baltimore, provided Mr Vail
leaves this for the station at Washington City.
Will you do the favor to inform me what
time you will require my service, and the
probable amount of salary allowed by the
government.
My engagement with you, will govern
some negotiations now pending between
Stockton Falls, and myself in reference
to my agency for them.
The service I now perform of being up
at 12 o'clock at night waiting the arrival
of the Eastern Mail, and dispatching
the Southern Mail at 2 o'clock in the morning,
is rather too arduous for a married
man; inasmuch as the duty has to be performed

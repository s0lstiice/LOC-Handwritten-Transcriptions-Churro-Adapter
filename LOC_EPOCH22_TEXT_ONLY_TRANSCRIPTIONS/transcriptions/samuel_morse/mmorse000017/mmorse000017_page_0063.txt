Baltimore Feb 27th 1844.
Dear Sir:
The Rugs & Boxes -- have come to
day I have put them in to the so that you
will probably receive them tomorrow's freight train.
I have paid Mr Brown bill of freight 9,000 of
Wharfage 1,000 -- & Cargoage to Depot 100 more, in
all $11,000 -- I wrote you one day last
week, but have not got the pleasure of reply --
no doubt your business prevents.
Yours truly
David Burchardt.

April 27. 1861. 60 pairs, nitrate and twice used. McCull at
Bladensburg. Much perplexed in the morning to arrange con-
nections, but about 30 minutes past 10 found all night
connected till 12 then disconnected and tried long en-
route to the Junction 22 miles, had slight indications
for a few moments when all action ceased. Mccornell
in instruction to he was going to the Junction in 4th light
train to see that the proper connection was made there,
about 5 o'clock but at 1/2 past 5 no indications
of connection very feeble spark, and deflection of
needle two or three degrees, to 6 no more favorable
indications. Either the rain affected it, the battery is too weak
or there is defective connection somewhere in the line. At 6 o'clock no indications, tried chance of poles and
various changes but all to no purpose.

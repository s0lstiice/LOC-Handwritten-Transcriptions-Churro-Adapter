New York May 1 1864
To Sam C. B. Mallory
Super. of Elec Mag. Telegraphs in the U.S.
Sir I hereby transmit to you the right and power
of an attorney to act in all matters relative to my
interest in the Elec. Mag. Telegraph of the United States
as you may deem proper and your acts, whether for sale
or any other disposal of the patent for said Telegraph, will
be considered by me, valid as my own
I have the honor to be
Your Obd. Servant
Leonard D Gale
215 Eighth Avenue
City of New York

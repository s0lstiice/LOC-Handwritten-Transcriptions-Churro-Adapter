Passport Money
Dear Sir
Baltimore May 10th 1844
I wrote on Saturday regarding the sale of the
lead pipe, and was in hopes to have the pleasure of a reply by
the return of mail. I have an opportunity to sell this
lead pipe, as had - as I stated in my last to you - but
as it is different from what we had hoped, I thought I
would rather consult you again before parting with it -
I find it impossible to sell it for any thing but lead, and
have offered every inducement I can to purchase it but
it is some other purpose but it being so small I think they
decline - Mr Avery says he put down a pipe
200 feet long for drawing off the waste water, of a fall
of 8 or 9 feet I found the tube too small, consequently
took it up - put down a pipe of Fathoms pipe
which he makes answer the purpose.
I will thank you for any advice you may
please to communicate, although as you remarked you
only expect what the market will pay.
Mr Avery is industriously at work, I came out this
morning - and he remarked, as I am writing you, that he
finds in own hacking, much bad wire that is barred &
bodily wound & he has to cut out but his work ofspan
the progress will.
Yours Truly
David P. Barbour

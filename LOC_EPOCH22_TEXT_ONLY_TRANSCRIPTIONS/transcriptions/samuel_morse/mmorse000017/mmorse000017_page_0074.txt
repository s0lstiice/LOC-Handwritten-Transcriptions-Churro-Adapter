I here sent a copy of receipts C. D. R. D2 and E2 with the
following Note. Mr Kirk and myself paid for rent of rooms in
hotel du Matinain Madame Durand 1st Month 220 francs being
110 francs each. Mr Kirk's lodging room was one story higher
up and at a lower rent than mine, but one parlour was in
common. When Mr Kirk left Paris Feb 25. 15 days before
me my rent was higher for the few days I remained in consequence of having to pay for the whole parlour instead of
half and a higher proportion rent for my lodging room.
From 26. Feb. to 14 March my bill for board
and rent which I have duly receipted is $2.04„15
and the bill from 1st Feb. to 26 that for board and
rent is $2.14„40 cents. Also duly receipted. They are
long but if you wish a copy of them I can have them
copied for you.

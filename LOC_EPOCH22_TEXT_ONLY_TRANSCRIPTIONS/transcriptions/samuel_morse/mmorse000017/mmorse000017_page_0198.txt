that the Telegraph is in successful operation for
22 miles to the Junction of the Annapolis road

of Mr Frelinghuysen as V. Pres. was written, sent
on, and the receipt acknowledged back, in 2 minutes
and 1 second, a distance of 44 miles.— The news was
spread all over Washington, one hour & 4 minutes
before the cars containing the news by express trains.
In about a fortnight I hope to be in Baltimore
and a communication will be established between
the two cities.—
Good bye I am almost asleep from overtax-
tion so course abrupt closing. Love to Catherine
Hinley, and the Children and to Richard, Louisa and
family.—
Your affectionate brother
Sam J. D. Mosby

New York Feb 7 Tuesday 1844
Prof Mose
Dear Sir I have just
finited my business. Having shipped
all the reeds. 1 Box of sundry articles from
the Labratory. 2 boxes of acids (Nitric & Suluric)
& have got the Mercury, which I have also
boxed up with the acids, as Dr Gale and
other considered it safer to do so than
take it by hand. They all go on Board
the St Rochester for Baltimore, old Slip
Mr Briggs agent to the care of Mr Burbank
8 commerce St Baltimore. The vessel leaves
this morning to tomorrow morning I
return to Washington. I loaned $6.00 of Mr
Breeze having left some of my fund at home
not thinking that I would want it. please
pay him that sum and I will settle it with
you
Yours most Obt Ser
Alfred Vail

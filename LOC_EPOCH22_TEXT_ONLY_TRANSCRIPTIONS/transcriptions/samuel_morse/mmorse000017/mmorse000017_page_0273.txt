Washington May 30. 1844
Having Confidence in
your Mechanical Skill & Judgment
I have taken the Liberty of requesting
you to favor me with your
Opinion of the Electrical Floating
Dock, Boston Ways for Naval
purposes and the general ad
vantage it possesses over the
Excavated Dock. By favouring
me with an answer to the
above request you will much
obliged
S.B. Morse
Major G. T. Crawford
99585

Baltimore May 25 1844.
My dear Sir –
If it would be perfectly con
venient for your friend Hon. Dr Ellsworth to forward to a
connexion of mine, Edward Little Esq., under his frank,
one of his late reports with the enclosed letter, I should
be greatly obliged – it is such valuable documents,
that Mr L. would like to pursue – I have given the
direction on the letter. – Although I am a stranger
to Dr Ellsworth, yet if you think it practicable,
you must please say to him, that I would be happy
to attend to any commands of his own, which purchasing
any little articles, or otherwise, for his family – as sometimes
goods are purchased for them there in Washington.
I have taken off a sketch of a few menly as a minute
and notice that the court as expressed to me, if I am correct, in § 974.22 is in my hands – which sum, or any part
thereof, I am sure shall be duly honoured. As you
are coming in so soon, I have not sent the case Hepner,
your truly
David Burbank.

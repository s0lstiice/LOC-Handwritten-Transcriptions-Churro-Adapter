Washington Apr. 29./44
P.O. J. Smith. Engr.
Sir,
The experiments thus far
with the Telegraph having been
succesful, I have thought it desirable
to apply to Congress for the means of
extending it further than Baltimore
say Phil:' or N.York. There is but
little time, to arrange terms, and I
wish to know if you as one of the
Proprietors, whether you will consent
to my saying that if Congress will
grant such means of extension of the
Telegraph, the Government shall
pay to the proprietors for the use of
it such sum as in their judgment
they may think just. - A speedy an-
swer will oblige Yr. & most afft respect
S.F.B. Morse.

Washington, Jan. 19th 1844.
Si I have the honor to report that, in compliance with my
instructions from the Department of War , to ascertain whether
any damages or loss, and if so the extent, has been sustained by the
"Govt." growing out of any contract or agreement entered into between
"Mr. Smith & Faltham & brothers to supply the Telegraphy of pipe under
"Surrells Contract," I have gone into an examination of the quality of
the pipe delivered by F.O.J. Smith the contractor with Mr. P. Faltham
and brothers to furnish lead pipe for the Telegraphic Conductors, and
I find it defective in many points, but particularly in the destructive
effect that has been produced upon the insulation of the wires by the
hot process of manufacture. The wires will should be taken out of the
pipe, be rewarmed, and returned to the pipe. From the report of
my Assistant the expense of these repairs will fall within the sum of
Twice hundred dollars but as this sum is saved to the Government
between Mr. Smith and the the Telegraphs, and credited to the United States,
on the final settlement of their bill for pipe, the aggregate sum allowed
for lead pipe is not exceeded. Part of the damage which the pipe and
wires have sustained ought to be borne by the Messrs. Faltham & they
relinquish Fifty dollars. The principal damage sustained is one of
that a kind not chargeable to their neglect, but to a great degree
to the neglect of my Assistant Prof. Fitcher who has since been dismissed
for all the pipe has been delivered under these circumstances I ask permission to pay
the balance of their account.
Respectfully I am
Yr Obd Servt
Geo. W. Mason
Secy of the War
To the Hon. John C. Spencer
99373
Secy of the Navy.

To Alfred Vail At the Relay House.
Sir
Supposing that you are at the Relay House & have
made arrangements for the Reprise, I will put the
Battery in action at the usual time this morning.
It will be gratifying to know that the magnet operates,
from that distance, even if you are not ready to com-
municate regularly. I will overhaul my apparatus
so as to have all things ready, whenever you choose to
connect. Connect Ground & last wire, till further notice.
I had no intelligence after you did connect it yesterday, I
presume that Cornell did not connect at the station at
12 o’clock. I am sorry you left so listless. Spirit it were
more extensive. We must make it so. Truly Yours
STB More Supdt Elec May 2nd

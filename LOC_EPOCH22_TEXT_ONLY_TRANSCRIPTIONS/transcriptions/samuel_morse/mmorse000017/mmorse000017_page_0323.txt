1st We propose to sell to the Government the whole
Patent, for a definite sum.
2d We will sell to Genl. the use of the Telegraph
for its own purposes, leaving to us the right to
sell rights to others.
3d We will sell rights at so much per mile,
4th Let Government go on and construct, and
leave it to Genl. to say what compensation shall be given
us for the use of it.
Very Respectfully
Yr Obser
S.J. Morse
Alfred Vail
L D Gale by
to Atty. S.J. Morse

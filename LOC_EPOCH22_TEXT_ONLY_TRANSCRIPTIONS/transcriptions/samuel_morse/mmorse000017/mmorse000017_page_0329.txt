Baltimore June 7th 1844
Dear Sir
I saw Mr. Stella of Solomons on
his return, & enquired as to the nitrous acid, he
does not seem to understand what state it is in,
or what it may be considered or called, after using it
for your purpose -- but will like to know --
I said to him -- that the last is too weak, here
replies, that the last is of the same as this other for
market sent, and is of the usual fair "commercial
strength," & he can increase it 1/2 as strong again perhaps
more so -- for 20 to 25c while he thinks might
suit your purpose very well --
If it will be convenient, please let me
know at what sum you would be willing to sell
your part of the interest, being on -- & in the
Telegraph, as I might by having a reasonable
time, accomplish what you desire, as well as
benefit myself a little -- also if you please and an
opportunity presents that I might some how be useful in future
operations -- I shall be grateful if you will bear me in mind,
so far as you may deem suitable -- Yours truly
David B. Braden,

As this letter will not be the last, and it is long enough, I hope.
Pray God, that my exertions may be successful, as I do pray
that your health, & cheerfulness may never forsake you; my
dear friend, addio.
Your
P. L. Barynemi
To Samuel J. D. Mosby at
Washington.
P.S. The first operations of the Christian Alliance will be to get up an
edition of at least 5000. copies of d'Aubigné's History of the Reformation. I have
already translated, into Italian, the first volume; and shall complete the rest within
sea. 2. Do you see a large edition of Mr. Civi's History of the Nof in Italy. This
work is already translated into Italian, and is now circulating in Italy, even in
France, by the care, & with the money of a General in the British Army, established
Repository of Bibles, & other Books in every town, & village leading into
Italy, & make arrangements for the introduction of them into Italy, & for the open
establishment of Schools, & the planting of Missions in the Italian free territories, & Islands, &
whenever Students are to be found in large numbers.

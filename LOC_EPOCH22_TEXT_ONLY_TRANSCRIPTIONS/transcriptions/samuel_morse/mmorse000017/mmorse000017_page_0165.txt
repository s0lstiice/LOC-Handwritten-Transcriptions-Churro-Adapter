Memoranda
April 12th. Tested 10 miles distance; 20 cups moved the magnet
freely; 30 cups, strongly.
" 15th. On putting the battery together this morning at 9. a.m.
found the circuit closed, so that the magnet held down
the lever; tried 20 pairs without effect, 25 pairs moved
the magnet freely; and 30 cups strongly. Inference, the
circuit is closed at or near the Baltimore extremity.
Inference proved correct, it was closed 2 miles
this side Baltimore, where the trial was made on the
12th inst. -

The riots in Philadelphia in
which lives were lost and a
Catholic church was burnt
occurred in May 1844, beginning
on Monday May 6th and continuing
several days. The Telegraph was
employed between Baltimore and
Washington by the Philadelphia
Police asking for the General
the aid of the United States troops to suppress the
Government, and important measures,
been adapted by means of the Tele-
graph, for their final suppression.
This is believed to be the first use
of the Electric Telegraph for Police
purposes, and anticipated the English
use for telegraphic purposes, by
several months. The first use in
England being August 28th 1844
to arrest three thieves arrested
Samuel the murderer was arrested by Telegraph Friday Jan 9. 3. 1845.

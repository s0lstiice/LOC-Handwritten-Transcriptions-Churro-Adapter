Baltimore April 12th 1844
Prof: S. F. B. Morse
Dear Sir,
Yours of 10th and I have
received, and pleased to hear that my "Tele-
graphic dispatch" was understood.
I have read with great pleasure the articles
on your Telegraph experiments which no
doubt is satisfactory to your friends.
We are looking anxiously for the comple-
tion of your line to this City.
I called on Dr. Dunbar to day but was
unsuccessful in seeing him - I expect to
visit him tomorrow, and shall present
respects to D.C.
I will be in Washington on Monday
morning next, and it will afford me pleas-
ure to meet you.
Very respectfully
Your faithful Servt
Henry J. Rogers
P.S. When you visit this City, I can be
found at the ticket office of Baltimore
& Ohio Rail Road; & any communication
from Washer left with Mr. Stehmins or either
of the Conductors will be forwarded to me.

October
16
Mr. Drummond called while they were waiting for
Mr. Mason, but as he did not come, Dr. J. Edmunds
McBriant hastened to them without him. He
sent to have gone in Mason's chair. Mason
did want to change from his comfortable & comfortable
position in bed to inquire if I would take the character of a woman who is to be sold. Just as
we got there a steam engine train commenced
and all they ever heard it continued & ended
up in the afternoon. We went around town with
no idea where we would go. We went to the little
hotel to consult on a proper manner of drawing
the people for at the approaching meeting of Congress
in the District of Columbia, the president of
Government. -- Dr. J. Edmunds, Mr. Drummond
& myself came again next Saturday
evening in the night. It still about eleven o'clock
and continued to draw our much -- Mr. Smith
called before he went off for Marble -- I found
another room to Mr. King's. -- Dress and clothing
of Mr. King's house were sold in the Evening --
Mr. King went to the office -- sent for Mr. Mason
to go with him to Mount Vernon to settle the two
or three days' interest -- Mr. King going to London
to find out the papers of the drawings of West Indian
Plants to Delisleton, to have engrossed with the
amount of the little island in London where he
was born -- After dinner Mr. Smith went to town
to inquire if the vessel was going to London

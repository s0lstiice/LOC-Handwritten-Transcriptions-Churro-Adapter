City of Washington - Territory of

Respctf to Mr. Thomson
Tuesday, Dec. 2nd - Mr. T. Potter called here & brought
a letter to Dr. In from Col. Lee - mentioning that
Mr. Washington has consented to give up the keys
of his late house to be placed on a monument
intended to be erected by the Congress in the Capitol
& requesting information respecting the General's
two houses building near the Capitol, the money
paid to the undertaker from having all gone
thru my husband's hands, he having intermingled
them as a friend.
Saturday - 1st - Some men from but two cars October
the wind over - Blake found the remainder of the
day - ordered the baggage to go to the Capitol
when Mr. Scott sent for Dr. In to go to the Commanding
office. - Part of the Load of hay arrived from
$25.000 - as soon as it was loaded - went to bed
Mr. T. Potter, who with her daughter Eleanor were very
rich. - Went after Dinner to the General's Houses

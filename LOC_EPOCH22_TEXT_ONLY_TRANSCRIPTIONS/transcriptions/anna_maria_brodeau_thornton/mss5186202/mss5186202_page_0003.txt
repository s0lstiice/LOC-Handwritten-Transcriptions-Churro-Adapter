January 1800. – City of Washington
Wednesday January 1st. At plenary Day – With
appearance of Snow – Mr. Foster called and
sent a letter from Genl. Thomson to
Wilson to take to Mr. Vernon at St. Lawrence
inclusively a letter from W. Machague at Philadelphia
with an express to put in the Canal at Baltimore which
had been in Philadelphia some months for collection.
I saw in the evening wrote to W. Machague
to obtain leave to send a few letters. – Sunday
January 4th – I was five clear Day – I
sent out to go to our farm – went as far as Georg
town & found the roads so bad we returned. –
Before we set out rented a room in our former
house on Square 33 – the Mr. & the
rate of $11.00 annum – she to quit it when ever
we came out the whole. – Have obtained
notice that he could not have his half of it
after the present quarter (from Christmas Day
till the 25 March) at the same rate as heretofore
sent her up to the farm in the afternoon from her house.

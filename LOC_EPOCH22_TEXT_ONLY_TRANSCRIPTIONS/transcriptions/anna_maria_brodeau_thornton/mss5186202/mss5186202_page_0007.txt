Saturday 11th a very fine day & the roads very good.
Mr. Peter intended the could not ride with us being
being obliged to go to see Mr. Hyltett who is every day
theman I go went to George Town called on Mr. Mason
& Mr. Hyltett who were with him -- Called at Mr. Johnson
who had rode out with Mr. Mason -- saw Mr. Johnson
Mr. Johnson died 8th of July 1817 -- Mr. Johnson
is gone in Lexington this morning to Mr. Colvin adjoining
to the house in occupation -- drank tea at Mr. Colvin
about one hour after our return I came home from
Mr. Vernon -- I came up with Mr. Greenbough and
dined in Alexandria at Mr. Colvin's --
Sunday 12th -- a very fine day as pleasant as a spring day
after breakfast Mr. Peter called & mentioned that his
wife & children are there for 3000 the carriage for her
& I accompanied him to the Capitol, the grounds
and Mr. Lewis house -- the latter being locked we entered
by the kitchen Window & went all over it -- It is a very
pleasant roomy house but the oval drawing room is
spired by the Corners of the ceiling & twin Pithos which
decoration the shape of the room -- Mr. Peter dinner
with us & returned home early in the afternoon some
of her children not being well -- In the evening Dr
Write to Mr. Gardner including his letter to Mr. Mason from
the painted balls requesting a vacuum in a school
Monday 13th -- a very beautiful day -- Went to
visit Mr. Mason -- with a letter from Mason to Mr. Colvin
requesting a supply of fertilizer for Mr. Washington -- Mr.
Colvin with the old former friends has built a very excellent
log house eight stones high on the Eastern Branch
and carries on the factory to a large extent --
about two o'clock Dr. L came from the office -- Mr.
Washington & Mr. Colvin came with him, the latter having
to Dinner -- In the Evening Mr. Washington came in
& mentioned that he did not think Mr. Blodgett's two
houses (one of which we were to have the refusal of) would
go on, as his son had since he went to Philad. become
on Washington's fortress of the money he had received
from selling Wood, & which he had meant to appropriate
to purchasing Planting &c -- Mr. Mason was employed
to make the windows Sashes &c -- & besides them a
small pine table & a pair of chairs &c &c &c -- I wrote a letter to Mr. Blodgett requesting him to purchase
for me a piano & the carpet & the music of the house

When I took Mr Miller & family to live with me
I felt that I would be a comfort & necessity to us
& I had no reason to be displeased with them, on
the contrary -- but I found it too much trouble
to provide for them, or rather I thought it difficult
to satisfy a gentleman -- & besides Mr. Miller's family
was increasing -- another reason that operated on my
mind, which I own was pride, was I found that though
all the trouble they took in keeping house for
me, it came directed to me at Mr Miller's, --
I shall never forget Mr. Miller's kindness in many
instances -- I then thought that if only Lady in question
with me I should be better satisfied & preferred to
Mr. J. H. Green & live with me -- & I thought as they
were alone & I alone without relatives here we could
live together very comfortably & without any being
so anxious about my table &c -- but difficulties
arose with the servants -- I then proposed their
keeping house & my living with them for the rent
& they managed our settle -- I except for some little
jealousies on both parts I think we could have done
very well -- but circumstances induced me to
sell my house -- & an farther -- St. Katherine
& Mrs. Tabbert to a house -- near Mr. Larmond --
where they are living very comfortably --

October
1820
Sept. 26th says he plays on the violin in miniature.
He cannot hold office in the election & has a vote
here also.
Thursday
9th
1820
Mr. Davis called -- Dr. J. has a bad cold & I do not quite
with us to Wilkinnins -- He went just at dark & I
was there now an hour before any one came
Mr. J. has a bad cold & I do not quite know
whether I have seen him since he began to employ
Mr. Wilkinnins, Mr. Wadsworth, Mr. G. Bushnell &c. &c.
They had four of them Wilkinnins' board -- among them
Mr. Lambdin did stand up and speak -- I am sure he
is well planted -- I am not only two times of time
since he was obliged to stay at home.

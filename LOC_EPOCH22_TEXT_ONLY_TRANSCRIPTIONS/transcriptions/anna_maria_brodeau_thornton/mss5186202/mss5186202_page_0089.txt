another few days -- The weather has been delightful
for some time past -- Poor Mellett had the morning --
He is a great loss both as a public & private man --
He leaves a wife & seven children. -- Mr. Fordert
stopped at the door, Dr. J went into him to church
at the Capitol -- He afterwards went into the
office -- returned to dinner -- Mr. Washington continued
him to dinner, but he was going a sketching --
Mr. Mellett went out, entertained
to dinner -- Dr. J went to Mr. White. -- In the
evening the gentlemen were all drawing pictures --
I playing when the Harper entered -- Naval
establishment -- Did not get to bed till near one --
The new manager went up to the farm to see it to
let in on Monday -- Dr. J went to deliver Mr. Bettis
general instructions at his farm in Virginia. --
And the drawing room, prepared for Mr. Wells to leave
in as he expected Mr. Harper to be this morning --
Mr. Betts making sketches of Dr. J's plans for new
ministerial or private office -- Mr. Washington came to
4th for his pictures & about one o'clock Mr. Harper
came -- Mr. Betts went out & returned to dinner --
Dr. J went all the way & did not return till Evening
was much fatigued --
Dr. J had his house at the door to go to the farm
but the clouds had the appearance of rain & he
put it off -- Walked with Mr. Mellett to Mr. Bowen
Mr. D. M. Ray & Lewis came, they walked so that we
were obliged to ask them to stop to dinner -- Mr. Washington
came to us for his picture -- as it began to rain it
was late & I so invited him also -- Mr. Mellett went
to town & did not return till Evening -- I stayed
a little -- Mr. Bowen went before tea -- Mr. D. M. Bowen
went in our carriage -- Went to bed a little earlier
than common --
Monday 28th -- Boy came from farm with butter &
Mr. Wells painted a little -- Mr. Betts went to town
in the Evening & called an author which Mr. Mellett
was present to & Mr. Wells went home --
Mr. Betts got up in the office of me he recollected
called from & composed by a black man
in New England -- Went to bed about eleven --
Dr. J went to the office -- Mr. Bledgett to town -- Mr.
Fult returned about 10 o'clock -- Mr. Wells came
to office -- No man came to go to the farm --
Mr. Betts came to see Mr. Wells -- Neither do Mr. Bowen
& Boy & I a handsome young man --
But I going to inquire how Mr. Bledgett family are --
Mr. Bledgett returned to dinner -- Mr. Fult went
to town returned in the Evening --

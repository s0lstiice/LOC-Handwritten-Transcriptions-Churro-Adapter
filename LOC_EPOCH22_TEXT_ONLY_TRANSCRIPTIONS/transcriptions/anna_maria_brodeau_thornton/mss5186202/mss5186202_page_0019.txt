March
then went to W. Burnes -- to meet Washington
Turner, Whitsnay &c. -- In the morning
Mr. Forrest & Mr. Lane called -- I never but of
failures among of the principal Houses in
Baltimore have spoken of some in Philadelphia.
Mr. Forrest called again in the evening in his
way home -- We are just about half way
from the Capitol to Georgetown -- and he has
almost every day
Dr. J. sold off about ten calves for Mr. Farmer
sent for up to the farm in Prince -- Now
in my barn all day -- and in the Evening --
after Mr. Forrest went who took tea with me --
Nothing in particular -- as few gentlemen love
something making for their advantage. I am
to attempt to have the Fredericksville mail delivered
at Washington. Mr. Forrest called in of both tea with
us -- and the Correspondence from the World displays
which is a very entertaining Work, being a
collection of abridgements of travels &c. Voyages --
after breakfast I went to Mr. King for the
plate of the squares in which we have lots all
of which I copied -- after dinner Mr. Brent called
with two letters for Dr. J. having just returned
from Washington.

July 27th
Before me breakfasted and breakfasted in the morning of July 27th was introduced yesterday, with Genl Wilkinsson, to the Court, opposite the courthouse, came to the door and walked down to go with them to the hospital, he directed them who he applied to to go in but did not go with them — as they returned they called to see the plans of the hospital if he was not injured if he not go down. — 5. 8. 19. 1861
Mr. and at three o'clock to Mr. Lewis — about an hour and a half came down to Mr. Duffins office he was there Mr. Duffins, Mr. Caswell, of this young man in — we went thence to Mr. Lewis' office gentleman of course Lewis.
July 28th
I went to the office — and worked all the morning after dinner on all orders to George Lewis — went to a few stores — as we returned stopped at Mr. Duffins office sent the carriage home — and then came back walked back stopped a little while at Mr. Duffins office I came early to go to Court stopped to get a certificate of Mr. Duffins death, and that the persons from whom I got the bearer of testimony, are the true heirs to the stopped at the Court office to get Mr. Wilkins to grant him as a certificate of found letters from his father in law Mr. Tanner, with which he returned home. I came a letter from Mr. Caswell stating Mr. Caswell does not pay Mr. Lewis in consequence of his stopping payment for a short time.
July 29th
I got breakfasted I went out upon after dinner rode out stopped to Court to Mr. Wilkinsson's office returned to my office from Mr. Duffins — went on my journey stopped at Washington went on my journey stopped at Mr. Wilkinsson's office with his family were gone to dine at Mr. Wilkinsson's office stopped to Mr. Wilkinsson's office who had been very ill — We came to the corner of the street I stopped for Mr. Wilkinsson as we went home we stopped at Mr. Duffins office — I went home

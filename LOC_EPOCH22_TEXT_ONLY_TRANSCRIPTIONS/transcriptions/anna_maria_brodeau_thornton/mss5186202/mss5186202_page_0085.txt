Mr Fuld & Washington, walked to Genessee Town --
I went in the barrack, & on the way back in
Mr Braggett & left him in Town. -- Went to
four times -- when we returned found a note from
Mr Houston saying they would all come in tomorrow
to go the Assembly, and the little jacket of which I
wrote the pattern -- Washington came to dinner --
Leharrys & Roberts here in the evening --
sent for to town to get some groceries & a longer
time for U.S. to meet to the door, Mr Adams's
son in New York camp dead -- Mr Braggett's. With
Samuel & Miss Elizabeth came about two o'clock --
U.S. did not go to the dinner -- as we had
had dinner we went to sleep, Miss Houston came
to go with us, went & slept here with Miss Elizabeth
& Miss Houston slept here also, & not Mr Braggett
had a room at the tavern. -- Mr Braggett came
after dinner also Mr Smith Mason. -- He went
up to see Mr Stanbury. Braggett to sleep at the
Tavern to accommodate Mr Houston. --
We had a very pleasant evening -- there were
39 Ladies & a great many gentlemen -- We
came home at nine o'clock -- Mama had a
bad cold & did not go with us.

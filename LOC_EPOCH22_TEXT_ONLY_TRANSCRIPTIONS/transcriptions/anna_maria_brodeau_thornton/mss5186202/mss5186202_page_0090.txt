Mr Field would have come early & returned to breakfast.
He wrote to the Revd. Dr. Gardner & to Majr.
Brady. - Both wrote to Mr. Tanner. - Mr. Brady
wanted to rent our land again. -
Mr. Field & Dr. S. went to the River. -
Mr. Tucker went out to visit a Mr. Brown & Miss
Hayden. - Mr. Boy called while he was gone a little
- Mr. Forrest came. - Mr. Field came home
after 3 hrs. gone to bed. -
Mr. Tucker came before we had breakfasted. -
We began game, & I invited him to stay dinner
but Mr. Field went with him. - Dr. S. went fishing
at Mr. Boursley's. - Mr. Boy left with us. -
W. D. S. walked to town in the evening without
game. -

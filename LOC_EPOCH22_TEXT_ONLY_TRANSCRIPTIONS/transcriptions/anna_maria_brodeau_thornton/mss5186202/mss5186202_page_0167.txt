Mr Talbot & I have separated after living
together about six years -- It has all this
been a most painful trial to me -- and since
I left the House I have been destitute and
not knowing whether I have done right or wrong
& from the pain of quitting for even my old
residence of so many years & home --
I only pray you that if I had done what was
right (what at I was advised to do) I might have
remitted it, & if wrong that I might be forgiven.
On Tuesday over the Sale -- & almost all the articles
were sacrificed -- as I feared they would be --
I stood at Mrs Talbott's & Mullins at the interval
of my quitting the House & coming to Kalerama
my new home -- where I hope & expect to meet
with kindness & a quiet Home -- God grant it! --
I acknowledge that I have regretted quitting with
Mrs T. & Mary (who have been very kind in respecting
me in my preparations) & think sometimes that
I might have made some arrangement more to their
convenience & advantage -- but perhaps all is for the
best -- I will try to think so!
Prayer -- present preparation -- must be
any occupation

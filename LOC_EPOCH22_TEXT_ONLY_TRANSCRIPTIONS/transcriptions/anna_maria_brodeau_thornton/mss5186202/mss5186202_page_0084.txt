December
1812
12
Mr. Spencer came, & considered
himself at liberty to quit the farm, as he says he
engaged from the 1st Dec. not from Christmas as we
understood. — Mr. Bledsoe went to Long Island — Mr
Trent & Mr. Thibodeaux were here in the evening.
— I went to the office. — We walked to a shop
near the Hotel, when we returned found Mr. Spencer
at an house, went over to Mr. Spencer's & took Mr. Spencer
with us to Mr. Craggs's House, to see the Chimney
piece of a particular house. — Mr. Bledsoe went in
search of Mr. Harbison & did not come in till we
had just done dinner — He went afterwards to Long
Island. — Sent a copy of this letter to Charles
Baltimore. — D. S. playing at Dungeons with Mr.
Spencer.

Thursday 11th. — About 1000 families on the
other side of the river, 1 mile on this side of Fortin River
Branties are to be seen — We stopped at
Reed's, instead of blacks to breakfast where
they had a little boy very ill — Dr. Strickler
overtook us a few miles from blacks having
come out of their route to do us — They rode
on with us to me Strickler's, Daniels was wounded
in the left of road having no accommodation
at Strickler's they did not like the favor to give us
some bacon, eggs & sundry things — 18000 Strickler
would much to persuade us to go to Salem with
them but they said we would have to go to
them in 3 or 4 days they at this place 2 miles
from Salem & 27 1/2 Miles Strickler's there are many
persons of Brattles & Strickler's
Rode till 10 o'clock in the night to Mr Strickler's
the latter in travelling as a favor I was not
much pleased at our arrival — We had a bed
at the head of the house, the room being occupied
previously to our arrival — We started in a quarter
diminish nothing himself, & gives nothing to ladies
except occasionally a little wine and liquor
& water — He has no engines & finds no necessity
for strong drink — and in some parts they could
live without Whisky.

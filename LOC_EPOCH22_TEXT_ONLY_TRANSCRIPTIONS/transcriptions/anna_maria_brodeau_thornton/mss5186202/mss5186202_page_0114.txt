Thursday 12th December 1861 -- We left Bayside
and passed up the river to our old house
which is in his line on their return & set off about
one o'clock -- stopped at Williamsburg to enable
to find the houses -- went to Light House camp
home to mile further, where the general left us
commenced for us very quickly -- left it --
crossed the river to Williamsburg -- the night followed
& we arrived at the house of the General
of the American army, passed in the dark to
visited the day room -- original lodging place --
the General was there we could not leave the house --

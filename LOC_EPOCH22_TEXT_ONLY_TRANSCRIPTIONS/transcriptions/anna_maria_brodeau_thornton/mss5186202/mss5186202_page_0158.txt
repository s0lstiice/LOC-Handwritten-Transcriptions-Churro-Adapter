Old Tanner too low stretched along
and yelping much did sleep
and with his trembling joints did shake
the rattles in his sleep:
Thus he seemed to have indeed
The rattles to their sides
Thus dogs can dream like gentlemen
all the they have no souls.
Orson Welles
"Heart of Miles" Correspondent
1 Oct 1862

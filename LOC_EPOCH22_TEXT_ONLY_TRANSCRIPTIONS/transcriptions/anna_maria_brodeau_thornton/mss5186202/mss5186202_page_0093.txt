Tuesday June 17th 1805
Set off from Washington about eleven o’clock
in an horse carrying to Washington landing
around at Mc Dallton in Alexandria about
one — Mr Rigaud Mry Stephens waited on
us — We dined at Mc Dallton called at the Treasury
and set out about 4 o’clock with our companion
of Voyage (2d day’s leave) arrived at Baltimore
con sidered about 16 miles a little after four o’clock
passed over the bridge (had had 2 good butter) passed through Baltimore
which is Stafford Court House about 28 miles
from Baltimore about eleven — passed the Potomac
on the Potomac branch, which has some fine bright
water in it, some good mills situated on the top
of a rocky mountain looking over — continued on to Frederickburg
a very pleasant looking place with neat
houses & gardens — The day being pleasant
and horses able to continue on we went 20
miles further, in all 47 miles and came to
an comfortable, dry House — Here we
leave the Stage at all business for an comfortable
lodging & poor fare — Thursday 19th started
about 8 o’clock ride from Williamsburg, a bridge
being broken down, obliged to go by new market
much out of our road intended to breakfast there

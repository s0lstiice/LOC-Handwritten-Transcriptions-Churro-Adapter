Thursday 21st Rain. — morning I was called before the Rain
9-1, and for 20 minutes
Saturday 23rd Sunday 24th morning 8-9, and 10-11, Mr Kingfument to
paid and doubled for supplying. — Monday I walked to
town, and went to Mr Taylor's, John came this morning
from St. George's in the Harbour from Swansea
Saturday 25th, Sunday 26th, — Mr Watson an engineer stabbed by a
man at Tunney's Inn — We went to the Town
after dinner and returned coming to Town
Sunday 27th, and Monday 28th, — Reading & watching
Tuesday 29th pleasant in the morning, broke a window to shut out
the rain, and went to the Town, and returned home, and went to
the Town in the evening, and went to bed at 10-11

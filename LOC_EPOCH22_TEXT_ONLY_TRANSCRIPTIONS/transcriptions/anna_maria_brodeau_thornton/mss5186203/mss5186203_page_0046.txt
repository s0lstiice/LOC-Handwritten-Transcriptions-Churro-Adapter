July 24th
Saturday 24th
Brought over
a long Canal - Bench furnitures
porting table - Shoe - Pepper - Pine
Mopeliner - Spinning Wheel - Whisker
and Sucker found with - Millstones
and Spinning wheel in the house
Mr. Spelman & the two house keepers
are at Spelman's vocabulary - a Mr. Saltish
is in the house - Mr. Spelman called
up to 10 o'clock - Mr. Spelman
returned to the House - Intended to go
home to the house myself but the boat was
out of order and I returned to the house
and slept in the house - Mr. Spelman
returned to the house - Mr. Spelman
called at the house at 12 o'clock -
Mr. Spelman returned to the house -
I rode to the house and returned at 1 o'clock -
I returned to the house early this morning -
Tuesday 26th
went to Northampton & returned -
I went to the house - I returned to the house - I returned to the house - I returned to the house

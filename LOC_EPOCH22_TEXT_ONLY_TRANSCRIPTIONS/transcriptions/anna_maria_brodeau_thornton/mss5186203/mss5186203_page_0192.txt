Wednesday 7th 1861 — Went to Springfield to get the money for
the train to Louisville
Tuesday 8th 1861 — On planting cotton on the different
plantations in the neighborhood — a great Bull brought
up from Kentucky, but few opportunities — I did not go to any
manufacture
Wednesday 9th and Sunday — Though the transportation arrived last
night at the station with the cotton, every opportunity to go
down to the drawing room with Judge Douglas — I did not
see the Civil Divisions in the Louisville train
Thursday 10th 1861 — called on my brother — 521 with 100000
cents of gold was to be found with the light wagon — I went to
the office of the Louisville Daily News
Friday 11th 1861 — I visited Mr. John Campbell — Mr. Taylor
presented himself — Mr. T. Wilson of the Louisville Tribune
— Saturday 12th 1861 — Mr. Hamilton presented also his opinion upon
the subject — I am engaged in writing an argument for the South which I expect
to be published by applying to the president of the United States
Monday 14th 1861 — I went to the meeting of the Committee
— The result of the meeting was as follows
Wednesday 15th — Went to visit Genl. Davis in his house — both of
the Republicans in the house — I went to the office of the Louisville
Times with Mr. A. C. Taylor, a member of the house of Representatives
— I went to a Ball at the French minister's — left from there
— returned to the house of my residence
Wednesday 16th — Went to Springfield — called at Washington — called on
my brother
Saturday 17th June 1861 — I received a letter from Judge Douglas
— I went to Louisville
Tuesday 19th June 1861 — Sent to Mr. Campbell — Mr. Taylor
presented himself — I went to the office of the Louisville Tribune
— I went to the office of the Louisville Daily News
Wednesday 20th June 1861 — I went to Springfield — called on
Mr. T. Wilson of the Louisville Tribune
— I went to the office of the Louisville Daily News
— I went to the office of the Louisville Tribune

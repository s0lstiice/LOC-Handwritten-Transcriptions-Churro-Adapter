Good Friday
25— Washington— 5 P.M. went to Williams on the steamship
Washington— Washington— 6 P.M. went to Williams on the steamship
Washington— 8 P.M. returned to Williams and not orders
Saturday 25th— 8 P.M. going to England— went to Williams & returned to Philadelphia— was here until 10 P.M. and went to bed
Monday 27th— 8 P.M. went to Philadelphia— went to bed
Tuesday 28th— 8 P.M. went to Philadelphia— went to bed
Wednesday 29th— 8 P.M. went to Philadelphia— went to bed
Thursday 30th— 8 P.M. went to Philadelphia— went to bed
Friday 31st— 8 P.M. went to Philadelphia— went to bed

Saturday 14th, 1817, at 11 o'clock in the forenoon, Mr. John Taylor, acting for the Committee of the House of Commons, in the matter of the Bill of Mr. Speaker, presented a petition from the inhabitants of the parish of St. Nicholas, in the county of Norfolk, complaining of the want of water in the parish, and requesting that the Committee would be pleased to order the Bill of Mr. Speaker to be read a second time in the House of Commons. The petitioners stated that the parish of St. Nicholas was situated in the parish of Norwich, and that the parish of Norwich was situated in the county of Norfolk. They complained of the want of water in the parish, and requested that the Committee would be pleased to order the Bill of Mr. Speaker to be read a second time in the House of Commons.

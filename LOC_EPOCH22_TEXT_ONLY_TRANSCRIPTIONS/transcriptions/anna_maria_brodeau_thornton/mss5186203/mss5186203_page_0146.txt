Rules for good behavior, &c.
Never whisper in company. Conversation is common
ground to which all persons present have a right to claim
their share.
Never offer the ear of your equal by reproachful
language, let your remarks be seasoned with friendly
admirements.
Never receive nor retail scandal. In scandal as in
other things the receiver is as much as the thief.
When in company never talk long, in that case if you
don't please, you will not tire their patience.
Inattention to persons approaching is a high crime in the
presence of a stranger, and is generally a high ignorant off-
spring of some vain, self-conceited, self-loving pedant;
who values his mind more than its worth.
Never interrupt any speaker, give time for a finish.

I suppose you are commencing your preparations for
leaving - though you cannot have many to make, keep on
with your work of improving the prisoners' quarters - I sometimes wonder how you can find
time to write a letter to the editor of your own paper during the summer. I have received
"Saturday evening" in the center of Boston of enthusiasm. There is considerable
originality in the piece - but an elementary defect in the treatment of the subject, that often its practical ability
turns to dull thought into activity.

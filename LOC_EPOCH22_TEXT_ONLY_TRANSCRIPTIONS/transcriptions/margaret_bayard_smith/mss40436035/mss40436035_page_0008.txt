Thank you very dear sister for your prompt
a kind response to my last letter & your affectional
sympathy on my roughed feelings of pleasure & anxiety
received your letter yesterday, after Julia had
left me a fine to her own home to which I
was accompanied her & was her installed in her own
room & received by her husband & his family with
unexpressed affection & gratitude – Her new home is
a plain & humble one, but with I am certain from
its comfortable & happy manner as a means of
an elegant one will be – Perhaps more so for our
canal one proportioned to our riches – our troubles
with our engagements – Whatever the condition in life
may be – it gives a pleasure and no equality between
that the position in which the same – difference in
our money & wealth in dignity. Compare with our
own commencement, separated as I was from friends
& settled in this desert of a place with most every
limited means – Julia abounds in comforts, convenience
& friends – She is perfectly contented & happy & I know
in every respect more than she expected – Our friends
have been very kind – Had Mrs. Beechfield been my
mother she could not have felt or have shown
interest – She was so anxious to have a suitable home
for the family – that she took great pains to force
one into bloom – in the morning of the day I brought
down a huge bunch of green house flowers, tender
and beautiful, & another article which she thought might be
useful to Julia or present of a new hand
or new working case of Kelerine – one of our most beautiful
livermore orchis – a splendid white Japanese Camellia roses –
blue flowers and the most beautiful heart
shaped ornament

We have heard recently of Mrs Anderson. She is better her residence on the sea together with our bathing house proved very beneficial to her.
I have not seen Brooks S. for over a month. I hear he is in Phila. I hope you go to Philadelphia till Caroline is there her antiphritis trouble. I believe early next month
Barclive has lost his youngest daughter. little Julia R. about a month ago.
This is Philadelphia both day. Do you remember what an entertaining hour it was to me.
I feel weak and am worried with writing this letter which I so doubt I have
will every one in reading it but I would not longer delay hoping to think from

May has just been in my room and requests her love most kindly remembrances
you and to Anna I need I assure you how tenderly and affectionately you are ever
longest, night and day my dear sister by your own material visits 7th.
Friday October 15th 1847
Daily have I been expecting a letter from
my dear Sister from neither of whom I have received answer to my two last to you, I wrote
a month ago to Sister 3 weeks since I have just received yours of 17th 2 before I sent
it aside I will write you a few lines, not knowing when I shall be able to write
again The symptoms when I last wrote were so abated that I really felt hope of a fair
that recovery at least. But a week or ten days since, Disreath came on a very sore
mouth his again deserted me I have since returned still, in this worst symptom
was kept in check, & the progress of disease is slow I not attended with
constant, though occasional pain on the General however Dr S says he never saw
any one more comfortable & less suffering in a similar situation My Anna
continues always gentle patient often cheerful & never depressed or disponding

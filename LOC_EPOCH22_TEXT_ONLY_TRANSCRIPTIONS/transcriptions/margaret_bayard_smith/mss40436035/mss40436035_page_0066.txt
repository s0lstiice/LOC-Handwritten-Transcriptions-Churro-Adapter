I have been wanting to write to you
my dear Sister - but I could not - my pen will
not move unless carried onwards by a current of
thought, or impulse of feeling - unless as a means
machine - a useless implement - I neither like to
waste or receive such mechanical letters - or as I often
call them daily letters, written as a task - Task
is the uniformity & monotony of my course of life
that new or striking incidents never occur to quicken
the flow of thought or feeling - yet I am tempted
to be grateful & I hope I am for this and instilled
tranquility - that usefulness & agitation have subsided
into quietude, though accompanied as it is by
an indifference that deadens all my faculties -
My dear Bayard has been very anxious that I should
resume my pen, knowing the delightful service both in
it & to satisfy him, I made the effort but it was
a fruitless one - the leader dullness of my intellect
could not be shaken off - With it ever be - I have
recently had two letters from Mrs. Hale, urging it
on me as a duty to meet any mind in earnest

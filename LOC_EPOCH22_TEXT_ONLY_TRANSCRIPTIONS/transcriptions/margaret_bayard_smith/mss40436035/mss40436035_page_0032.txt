Wednesday afternoon March 23 -- 1837
I received your letter this day & I felt inclined to
set down immediately after it several sentiments to you -- but
could not. Your dear Anna was very anxious & I was obliged
to her to be her guide if possible. The fair weather
enabled us to return to Boston on our return from the
visit to the West -- I am sure that every one of us

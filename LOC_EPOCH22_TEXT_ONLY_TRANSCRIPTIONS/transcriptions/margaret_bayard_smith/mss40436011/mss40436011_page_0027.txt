Dear Ladies you are kind
and feing I would they,
But narrow fits my mind
and shoots what I would say,
But I the truth with all truth
nor even my faults will hide
Oh think upon my tender youth
And don't too hardly chide —
A watch without a name
To misery was I born
I never a father knew
Never heard his name
And as in years I grew
Blessed for my mother's shame,
If mother I could call
One who abandoned me,
And throw me next of kin
On the cold lap of charity —

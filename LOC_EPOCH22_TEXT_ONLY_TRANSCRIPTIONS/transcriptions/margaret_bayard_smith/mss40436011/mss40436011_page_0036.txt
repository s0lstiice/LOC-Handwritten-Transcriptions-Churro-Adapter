Robin red-breast to Anne
For thee sweet Maid my strain shall flow
Who saved my life and sooth'd my woe
For when forlorn and lost I lay,
With bleeding wings along the way
You raised me drooping from the plain
And bode me live to sing again.
Oh generous Pity was thy flame,
Inspired, the bosom of the dame,
In her bright form thou lov'dst to
Her eyes thy mansion plainly tell;
And gentle pity, oh! declare,
That thou on earth a shrine more fair
Sweet maid with gratitude I bring.
Thy kindness how shall I return?
O' when my wounded pinions heal,
And in my breast new strength I
My master says he'll quit his charge
And let me rove the fields at large

Notes on management
of Green House Plants —
Geraniums, are very culture, requiring
a sort of loam with a little peat must
have little water when not in flower
They require more light than ever
green plants with coriaceous leaves & woody
stems, such as the myrtle & camellia, when
they grow large & strong, they should be
cut down, or cut off, for the smaller the plant
in general, the more vigorous the flowering
leaves — The seeds should be sown in
soil of light earth, no sooner as germination
is over they come up a seedling, for seed
may be transplanted into soil of the smaller
size. Seedlings from seeds sown early
in the season will flower the succeeding
autumn, & none will be later than one
year in showing their flowers. To obtain
new sets another way is to put plants home
where they will be influenced by other plants
in flower, produce to same effect.

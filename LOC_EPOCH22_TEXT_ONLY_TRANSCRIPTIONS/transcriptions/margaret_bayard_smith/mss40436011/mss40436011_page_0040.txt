Once upon a time there was a king
of Sparta whose name was Menelaus
who had a very beautiful wife whose
name was Helen she was considered
as the most beautiful woman in Sparta.
Pyjam was the king of Troy who had
a son whose name was Paris he went to
the court of Menelaus who received
him very hospitably Paris returned the
hospitality by running away with his
wife and that occasioned a war between
the Trojans and the Spartans Menelaus
invited a great many Princes to
fight against the Trojans with him.
Ulysses was the wisest man in Greece
Agamemnon was the chief Prince.

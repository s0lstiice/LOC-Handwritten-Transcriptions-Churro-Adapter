Thirty 3d July
There are nine parts of speech first the
Article which is used before nouns to
show the gender and case 2 Noun or
Substantive which denotes the name
of any person place or thing the
name of persons or places are proper
nouns the names of things are common
nouns nouns have gender case and
number a pronoun is a word which
stands instead of a noun and must
agree with its antecedent in number
gender and case on an adjective or
that part of speech which is placed
before a noun to describe its quality

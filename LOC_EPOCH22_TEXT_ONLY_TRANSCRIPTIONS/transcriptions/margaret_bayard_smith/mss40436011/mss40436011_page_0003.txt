When winter comes to rule the varied year
And all the charms of nature are withdrawn
Beside your social heart let this offer
Around your social heart may ever joy
That friendship or affection can bestow,
Not oft dear Anna do we find
a tender heart, a cultivated soul
When around you may thus firmly place
Love & friendship with constant
So beguile the weary hour
By their sweet & magic power
Let this little present prove
Moments of my love
This case dear Ann is meant to be
A little proof of love from me,
The only value it can claim
It draws from them & whence it came
But to your young & tender heart,
It with warmth & charm impart,
Far beyond the nearest glow
Which gold or jewels could bestow.
And as you tread life's dangerous way
This truth you'll learn from every day,
The greatest blessing heaven can send,
Is a true & tender friend,
In your dear Ann may Heaven bestow
This richest treasure found below,
Hail evening most propitious hour
To love & friendships gentle power
When the full a swelling heart
Can its tenderst thoughts impart
When the noisy day is over
When its cares people no more,
Seated round the blazing hearth
We yield to social joy 2 months,
Far from earth's pomp & show
Which no real joy bestows,
We can never pleasure find,
In the cultivation of the mind;
From off earth to moon
With state the past unfolds display
Fancy future hours array
With 2 fancy's various heavens
That bepuls these tranquil hours,
Let the muse shake chain the car
And meet me with sympathy tears
Or Heber's page such scenes display,
As oars they on the poets lay,
In vain the pen descriptive art
Would trace the feelings of my heart,

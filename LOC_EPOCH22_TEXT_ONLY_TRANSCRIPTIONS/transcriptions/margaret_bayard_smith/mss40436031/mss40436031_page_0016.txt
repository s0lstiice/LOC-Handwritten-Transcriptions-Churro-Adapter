from an unwelcome companion, think of both evils
that I have difficulty in overcoming. I never before found
it so hard to do anything in this duty; in every other case, has
not contented me. I am now in a state of great anxiety
about the condition of the Confederacy, it seems like disease
of the constitution which may be alleviated, but never
reinstituted. You know in some many families there
have been complicated with how many individuals intimately
connected, but never before have I been found one,
with whom I found it so impossible to approximate or
compromise. There is certain something in our manners
which enables all the leaders of my department to exercise
their authority over their men without any difficulty
or opposition. I do not understand why the people of

Since January 30th 1820. Sunday
Here I am seated by the side of a blazing fire, in my own dear
room, or come from all interruption, (for my family never think of giving
my bed in Sunday) withdrawn from all the cares of life, enjoying
that silence, that solitude, that communion with my God— with my
own heart, & with the friends I best love, which I so precisely
to my soul, & which seem to lift me above the world & all its noise,
& all its pain & pleasures. When we assembled at breakfast at the family
table yesterday, the fact of our being left myself to attend to family business
is as well known as a friend.— What a matter we have had? Spoke

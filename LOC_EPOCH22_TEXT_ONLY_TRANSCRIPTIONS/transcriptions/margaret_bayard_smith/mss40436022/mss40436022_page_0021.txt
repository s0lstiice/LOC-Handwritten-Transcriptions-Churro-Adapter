It is both difficult & painful I suppose you my dear Mary
realize that three months have elapsed since I last wrote
you - many times have I proposed it - but so many
things have divided & engrossed my attention - that at last
really did not feel certain whether I had written to
you or not - About a week after we moved out her
habit still unsettled - I one day took up my pen to write
& Sister the humor of a Crime home conspiring together
wrote her down than a short note - it contained an
account of our situation, my prospects of health - I knew
it was a kind of letter that would interest you - & not
knowing if I should find so favorable a mood & disposition
to write again - I requested Sister to forward it to
you - intending to write to you more particularly
very soon. I presume she has not - probably deferring it
a little while to write herself - & then after the delay con-
sidering it inexpedient from the idea that I had written
to you subsequently. - Your latter my dear Sister has
caused me from a kind of temporary forgetfulness or
neglect - forget you indeed I have not - but my neglect
has arisen from procrastination. - with a family of
20 & superintend - you will admit there is some apology for
some calls at times unduly interfering with others. - Dear
these cares too often take the place of still more sacred
interests! - My babe is almost three months old - She
is now lying on the floor by my side using her little
limbs with great activity. Her nurse is occupied & the
other three little ones are at play in the nursery -

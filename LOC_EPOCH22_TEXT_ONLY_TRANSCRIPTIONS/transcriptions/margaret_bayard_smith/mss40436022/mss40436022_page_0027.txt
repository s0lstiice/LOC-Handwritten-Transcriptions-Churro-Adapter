dearest of sisters! Was ever any one more happy? What a
rock stone of fond & sweet recollections is laid up in
my memory for years to come -- if life should be protracted --
every night when I lay my head on my pillow, I think
over some conversation -- some scene, that never leaves me with
my sisters. -- Not at any period without some lively interest being
excited. -- I feel as if I had been dwelling in the hazy
regions & was now restored to the common walks of life.
Not one dark spot, -- not even a cloud -- shades
the brightness of recollected affection -- I suppose I should

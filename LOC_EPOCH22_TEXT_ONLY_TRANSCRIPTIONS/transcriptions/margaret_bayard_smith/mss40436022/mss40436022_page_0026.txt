road, which goes over no mountains, & descends

country, whose fields & meadows are now rich &
very beautiful, but certainly afford no views either
grand or picturesque -- I always grew weary of
travelling on turnpikes -- I tired as I am, I was
often caught in rough rocks & roaring torrents,
high mountains & steep ravines, & such a
monotonously comfortable journey. -- Had I

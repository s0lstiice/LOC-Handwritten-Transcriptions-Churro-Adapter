I was tempted to pass two days the succeeding week, a go
with Caroline & Crawford to any public in the Theatre -- Mrs
Clare & Van Derven, at least 15, or 20 other smart men
present with as much more or pleasure that they said we
must have a night smart Crawford party. -- Caroline returned
into the country with us, after having John more than
a week with her. While she was here Bayard & Dyittle
from the Irish Patrick arrived & brought out with them Dr
Pool & Sam Vernon -- Such an occasion of being
made as quite gay -- Mr Vernon too, passed one afternoon
& evening with us & brought Rembrandt Beal out with
him -- for years I have not passed a more delightful
evening. Mr O'Call, is very entertaining, when conversing
on the fine arts. -- This day week, Ann & I returned
to the City with Caroline & it was not until yesterday
I returned -- 2 weeks from home. It was a week I enjoyed
very much -- I every day saw some of Mr C's best friends
& became quite intimate with several. One night I went
to Mrs Adams drawing room, with my two young ladies
& four young gentlemen. Another evening had a charming
social party at home, viz, Mr C -- He enjoyed
himself exceedingly, it was the first time these two united
he has had company -- He looked perfectly well -- as
this is a thing which no one will believe, except those
who see him -- his friends in Liverpool, are very desirous
that he should give a Grand Ball & invite all corps,
to all the soldiers & chargers & then to seem with
their own eyes, they might perhaps believe that
he is no longer a sick man. Mrs C feels afraid
of the experiment -- he has now been secluded from
society for 16 or 17 months -- & she knows not what effects
his return might have. It is to be determined in a day or two
that such people would not like Thomas, he so immediately
comes to our aid in his transportation -- soon to Caroline I am sure

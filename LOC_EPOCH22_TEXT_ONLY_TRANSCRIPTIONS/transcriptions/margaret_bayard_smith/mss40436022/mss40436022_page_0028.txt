to see you -- I hope you will be safe and well
when you have read this dear sister -- please to send
it to Maria -- I cannot write seperately -- it is not
difficult I have written this much --
I hope my dear Maria you reached home in safety, relieved from the
wind and found your family well -- Let me hear some from you -- I am
your loving son
Geo. B. H. S.

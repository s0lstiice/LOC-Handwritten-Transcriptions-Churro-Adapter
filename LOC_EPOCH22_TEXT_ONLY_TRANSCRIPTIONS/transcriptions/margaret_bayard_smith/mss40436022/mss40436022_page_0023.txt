Countryside, & felt their value enhanced in contrast with them
with the city -- Yet I have not felt that glow of sensibility
& that elevation of heart & vigor of mind which I sometimes
feel -- The difference I experience I think very much owing
to the effect of nursing -- This is the period in which I pay
the penalty of maternity. -- It is not the whole of the time
but quite part of it -- my nervous system is unpleasantly
affected -- At this time however I have left to detract my
sprits from with either of the others -- as this is the most
firm & robust child I have had -- She is active & forward
& has been perfectly healthy from her birth -- My others
have been delicate. --
Elizabeth has stayed a month with
her Aunt -- her absence was a relief -- as I could not
have attended to her if she is now at an age that re-
quires a great deal of attention -- The duty is becoming
more & more interesting -- and audacious too -- Already the
effects of a sinful nature shew themselves amidst so much
innocence & loveless -- & the painful task of correction is occa-
sionally necessary. -- I expect to spend most of the morning in
Mr. B.'s library with Elizabeth -- & Ellen too who we are
going to take from school. -- I think I can now have my
time sufficiently at command to superintend & keep her
occupied -- I cherish the hope that she will become a lovely
girl -- amiable & improved -- & that the table will be a de-
lightful one -- I watch & aid the development of her
character -- I look forward to her being my companion
& friend -- & a valuable friend to the younger children --
You see how my family confides my
thoughts within doors -- I cannot now describe the ob-
jects without Mr. B. has done a great deal towards
improving the appearance of the house & ground & furnishes

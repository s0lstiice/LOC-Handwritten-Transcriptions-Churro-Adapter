I have been two mornings writing this letter and am now so fatigued
by sleep, that I must close it. Although I have many things
left unsaid -- by most affectionate remembrance to all friends
but love to my dear Papa, whom I hope will bring Manassas
here next winter, mother is coming with you on the summer trip
to the dear children. Julia is quite well, she has made a
baby room in one corner of the parlour, where her little boy, her
little table, her little rocking chair and doll are all placed in due
order with the window curtain drawn round a few more mats
and a little black pillowcase with her name -- she is the very picture of health
but does not speak a single sentence -- address dear sister
and most affectionately M H S make

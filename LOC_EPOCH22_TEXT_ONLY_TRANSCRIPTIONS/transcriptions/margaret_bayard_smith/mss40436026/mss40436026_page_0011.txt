My good Betsy, continues to do well. always on coming home of
an evening, I find the tea-table set, the candles light & a good
service you could keep on eve, at this period. Mrs S, enjoys his tea so
much, that it gives a double relish to mine. Poor Betsy, not
withstanding her stupidity, makes a nice kind of biscuit, she is always
delighted, when I ask her to make them, & if I give any of them
to the young men, she says "have now myself, why you give
away what I make for you self". Betsy too, desirous of trying
her hand, made me this evening some very good short cakes. --
What strange information to send 200 mile!
17th April 1817
New Brunswick
N.J.
This morning Mrs P's sent her carriage for us & we went to
church, where a good sermon, was preached in a small but re-
table congregt. After church, Ch. T. & Mr Foster came home
as, & I received them same ceremony in my chamber -- Do I have
but little more to say, I will not begin another sheet. Mr Patterson was
on Friday & was quite well. Remember us to all our dear friends & bid
not to forget us -- Farewell my dear sisters. --

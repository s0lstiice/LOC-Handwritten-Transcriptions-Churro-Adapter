Washington Dec. 10th 1903
It is not possible to write a quiet letter to three dear friends in the same house, to my dear sister Jane, & Miss A. Lanning, in one short and must answer your kind letter which arrived with the same time.
Has greatly the blessing of health indeed every time I think of myself I am filled with joy. I am working hard to improve my health. I am not in the least bit of a patient. I am perfectly content to have my health in my own hands. I am perfectly content to have my health in the hands of my doctors. I am perfectly content to have my health in the hands of my nurses.

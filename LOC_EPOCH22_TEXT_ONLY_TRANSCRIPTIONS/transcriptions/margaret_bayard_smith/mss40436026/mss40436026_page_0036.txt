& prosperity. It would seem as if the noblest part
of the soul, its highest faculties, lay dormant in
a state of prosperity & were only drawn forth
by the trials of adversity, & it is only those who
have been afflicted who can truly sympathise with
others, "For the human heart is like that tree, which
yield not its balsam to heal the wounds of others
until itself is wounded." But to you my dear
sister the ways of providence need no vindication
your heart has long felt that the government of
God is that of a father, & that we are so ignorant
of the effects of his dispensations as the
new-born infant when forced by a fond mother
to swallow some bitter potion. -- It is by placing
myself in this relation of an infant to its parent
that I solve or rather acquire in all these
ways of Providence which seem darker & more
vicious. After Fanny's long confinement & your
close attendance in her sick room, you will be
enjoy the coming season with a far higher relief
than those who have watched its slow progress
& and breathe & walk again:
"See the wretch who long has toiled
"On the thorny bed of pain,
"At length repair his weary rest;
"The meanest flower of the common sun, the air, the
"The simplest note that swells
"To him one opening Paradise

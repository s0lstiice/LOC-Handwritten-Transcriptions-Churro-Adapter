Kentucky April 12
I have just received a letter from both of my dear
sisters who have not been from me since I received
this. It will be necessary to answer them
separately. Their letter to Mr. Mulford have not
yet arrived & I suppose he has not yet arrived.
Indeed my dear sister your long absence failed
me with all I imagined some anxiety. I have
not felt for a very long time such thinking or
much of you in the day made me dream of you at
night, but my dreams were not as lucid as yours
for you visited my dreams in circumstances that
more melancholy than you describe the wealth
to be. I have now a long letter by me which I
wrote to you last Monday, it has been all the
work in the world to piece together for a memory
to turn which now a days seldom occurs, being so busy
that the box cannot be opened. Over turning about
the painful & tortuous sufferings she endures; I was as
impressed from this letter which caused your silence, as in past
letters the way in which you described her situation

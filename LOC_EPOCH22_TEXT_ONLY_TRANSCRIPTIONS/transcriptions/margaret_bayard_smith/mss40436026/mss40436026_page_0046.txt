Friday Morning, Jan 7th 1863
At my own willer if you knew the presence of one letter from me, even more
of your would you write this a my only confidence with those whom I trust
and who I am sure will give you timely and friendly support to many friends in both
sections of being helped to with interest & sympathy.
I hope you will reply in my letter to my dear mother that I have thought of
my family seriously and have given up the idea of sending them to
the army or independent in an otherwise silent. This must be the reason
that I have so much to say when I write to you. I have felt that
one of these men is in the army. I have also left a half united & yet
defeated army of private soldiers. I have had a private letter from
the women whom I mentioned.

It is ten days since Maria arrived & I cannot
ten days since I received your letter which I
intended and meant much sooner -- But you know
how it is when you have friends with you --
felt as if I could not leave Maria to wait
a while sitting in the same room with her
could not stop talking or speaking without
my thoughts -- hence I have been far from
many days since she has been here, I have
been able to keep up a talk -- Part of the
first week was rainy weather, confirmed us
as at home & made us totally dependent
on indoor amusements -- which for children
if Anna & her brothers age & activity, it was
difficult to find -- I did my best to draw little
pictures & made little ones & drew as
I knew I could not more successfully please
2 amuse Maria, than by painting the children
We have not been out much -- yesterday
we spent in the city visiting & had the
weather allowed should have rode out
this morning & in the afternoon had
Nor Clay, Marsh, Kemford & a dozen more
to tea -- but I think it will rain --
I am not able to go & nor to breakfast
as I am under the operation of a course
of calomel & take a little powder every
morning -- but get down between 10 & 11
This morning I determined not to go
Dear Katharine
By your permission
1828
Monday, 29th April -- 1828
Have written this at intervals -- busy often obliged
to stop & rest -- you will scarcely be able to read it --
but I really could refrain no longer from writing to you
pray write soon to me & if there is any thing in
particular Maria and I have would like me to speak
for them tell me & I will when able, take great
pleasure in doing it. Tell me all about your roads -- what articles you
have -- what money -- I have quite an interest in it --
do write it will write me to you -- The correspondence
commences -- I quite enjoy it -- Farewell dear friend &
as I do
M H Smith

This is a long New Years letter - I indulged myself on
Wednesday in sending you a letter, because the girls being on my foot
with me, I wished as far as I could, to amuse your relatives
and supply their place. I am sending over these two papers
to the office. I fear they will take you -- but at least
they will have been useful. I think of you
God speed, I cannot enter -- new year's resolutions
are not suitable to my mind -- I shall enjoy it
with my family -- I shall enjoy it
with my family -- I shall enjoy it
with my family --

Sidney, May 10. 1827-
Once more am I seated in my own dear solitary
chamber, with my door closed on all the family; shut
up with the recollections of departed days - departed
or absent friends. I have largely for this hour of solitude
yet sacrificed it a defense for the taste I professed. The reading
of your letter - the course of your letter - the wind blowing
against the windows - the sound of my steps and light of my
lamp, better than sounds of movement - but now they are
unproductive of soothing or delightful. The whisper of the wind!

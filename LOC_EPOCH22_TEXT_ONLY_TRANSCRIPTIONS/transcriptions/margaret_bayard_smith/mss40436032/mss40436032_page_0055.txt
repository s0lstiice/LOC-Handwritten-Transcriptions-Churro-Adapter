First of May. 1828
With me, a quiet day celebrated by all the
Youth of the District, where I suppose there
are at this moment more than a dozen
queens - Anna went into the city, with such
a large basket of flowers for her friend Esther Smith,
who is to be Queen of the City, besides many balls
that she might make him impersonate Floro, lavish
on her passion on the youthful queen Esther S.
the daughter of the Caricier of the Bank, in a most
amiable part I became this winter very intimate to
make with Anna. She yesterday went to a ball
to come in today & accompany her to the Ball
this evening. I shall be merry this month indeed
the queen is to have 24 months of honor & 24
floras - The floras, beautiful little girls, grow
about 8 pounds - Insipid amusement; covered with flowers
gardenias, & carrying my basket of flowers, which they
throw in the face of the Queen, in her attention
at one end of the great department room, is an
elevated platform covered with green cloths, on which
is a miniature throne, formed of branches of trees
thorns, & hung with the richest & most beautiful
flowers - In this grave is the Throne, with seats
round it for the members of honor, who are elected in
the uniform with the Queen, who is elected in
a crown of thorns. - Sent to December the whole
ceremony would take my letter - And yet it is so
charming it is perhaps better with deserving men
an thing else than unity of sentiment. Every large school
in the District have their elaborate queens -
Ballarat & the country round absolutely emptied of
flowers - Until this year & never have been scarce,
the early ones of spring, failed to give of summer
& of flowers. But this year every one to the tendency

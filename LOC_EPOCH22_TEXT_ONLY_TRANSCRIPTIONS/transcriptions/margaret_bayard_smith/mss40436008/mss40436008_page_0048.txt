Moral Philosophy may as well be applied
to private life, as to one of the highest and most public
employment, since every man carries with himself, his
whole system (or part of it) man- y (or human)
character. Other authors have written of ordinary real
persons, but I write of myself -- Party, prejudice, ignorance,
and I write of myself -- And in doing so have
this advantage over them, however unfair or
not to learning -- that never any man treated of any
subject he better understood than what
I have undertaken, scarcely that never any man
benefited further with his matter, nor better or more
distinctly stated the facts & consequences of it, nor ever
more exactly or fully arrived at the end he pursued
by himself -- To finish it I need bring only ridicule to
the work & that is there, the most humane experience
that is ever where it can find -- I speak truth
not so much as I would -- but as much as I
know

Chesster May 22. 1860
I received your letter, my affectionate friend,
on Tuesday, and I certainly should have
answered on that day, had I not neglected
to leave Washington the next day
to the city express that would have carried
it. Accordingly I started on Wednesday
morning, rushed on being no time Station
in my return to Philadelphia. But judging of my
surprise at meeting, on arrival here
last night, a proclamation prohibiting the
passage into the city of any person from
Baltimore, who had not left that place
15 days previously -- and which tended
to appear to the world, was that the proclamation bears date the very day of my
leaving Baltimore. Then then I am, and
have to my perfect satisfaction I must
say 15 days, is the almost still struggle
of my business during that period.

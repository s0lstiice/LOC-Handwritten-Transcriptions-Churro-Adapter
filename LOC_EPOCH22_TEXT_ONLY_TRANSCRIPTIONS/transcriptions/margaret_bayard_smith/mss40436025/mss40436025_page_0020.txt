Sister & Brother talked of the improvements that could be made, how
every thing might be altered for the better &c which I think plainly
indicates their love of the country. But Brother I believe is quite unsettled
as to his future residence, the more it to depend on Mr Pintard, they
would never leave this place for more than 3 weeks at a time. I cannot
but hope that this plan will be resisted, for I am too selfish to wish them
far from me, though it might be more agreeable- Lewis is really a fine
little fellow & deserves all that has been said of him. He is so delighted with
the country, that it is difficult to keep him in the house; so that I should
not be surprised were he to loose much of what he has acquired, & his grand
Safer is so extravagantly fond of him that there is some danger of his being a
sports child. Old Mr & Mrs F. are I think quite an agreeable couple,
they do all in their power to render their house so too. I had no idea of
staying here more than 2 or 3 days, but Brother has gone off this morning
& left me, I suppose for the week. I have no doubt of spending it very pleasantly
and to tell the truth I would rather go to New York in a day or two-
Sister treats me with so much confidence & affection, that it is impossible
not to love her. Her appearance is not altered in the least, in even her
way of dressing, I you would scarcely believe she has been away, her manner

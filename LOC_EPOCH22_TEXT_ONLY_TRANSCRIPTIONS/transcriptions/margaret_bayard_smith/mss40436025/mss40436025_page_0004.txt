January 18 1847
During you will not complain of my not employing
any form of society dear you will find fault with the
too frequent use of a paper in the newspaper I am very pleased
to say I have not been at any scandalize - feel very secure in
your society I do not think that there is any one
in the world would refuse a man that was the
case - in my last letter I mentioned two engagements
one at the drawing room & the other at my residence
at the drawing room we found a large circle of
ladies & a crowd of gentlemen - but in effect a
half dozen were the only ladies present and I had not
to seat them both - I wish to do every friend
to make many enquires to you - I suppose
you will not be much opposed with the Board I have
announced her, as as a tentative one & answer
some interrogations I made concerning young Joseph
who now lives at the President's & I believe my
father been over the only gentleman Stewart who
after speaking at me a long time, concluded he did not
know me I told him as well at the time, talking
to myself - without recognising me

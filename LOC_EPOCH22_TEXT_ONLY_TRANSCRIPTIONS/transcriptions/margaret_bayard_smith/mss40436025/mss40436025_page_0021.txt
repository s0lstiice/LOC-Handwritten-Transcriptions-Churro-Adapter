Monday evening -- Sister has all the evening been urging for permission to visit Kornwell but
Mr Sinclair is inexorable, she urges her duty, when her wishes has no avail, but all to no
effect -- I have almost made him angry, in pleading for Sister -- I really think the old gentle
man unreasonable, and when we see it all crowded from affection we can excuse it.

on the mind -- but are so much more sensible of the
presence of the Creator, when surrounded by his works,
than when shut off among the inanimate objects
made by man. Perhaps at this moment you are enjoying
what I am enjoying -- the sight of many trees clothed
in the fresh, vivid verdure of Spring -- of the clear blue
sky, that looks so fresh and limpid. That one wishes
for myself to attend to the aerial region -- for a discern-
ed spirit to follow its deep, whispering storage through
the infinitude of space. What a time, compared to which,
is it man, in the immensity of the Gospel, that he
deserves it not for the appearance of the Gospel, that he
is something -- a precious something, in the eyes of his Creator,
how worthless would existence be! I struggle against the
almost overwhelming feeling of my impotence, which at moments
prevents me from writing. I cannot write a line of
praise to the Creator without feeling that I am
writing to myself. I am sure that the Creator
is the Creator of all things -- the Creator of all
things -- the Creator of all things --

1832
Saturday morning, July 20th, 1832
All around me is silent, none of the family have yet
risen. I arose early that I might get up a meal in company
with your most obliging letter which I received yesterday afternoon
from Mrs Smith's same home. My belief in the sympathies which are
with our friends is now confirmed by the letter which I received
from your own writing to me. I was talking to you -- I had one
only feeling of indignation & great disappointment in thinking
that they would not come to my aid. I was so anxious to see
you that I would have given my life for a chance to see you.
But I am now in despair of ever seeing you again. I am
sure that I will never see you again. I am sure that I will

I am very glad to hear from your letter dear sister that your cousin Mary
has almost succeeded to relations with you. I look forward to her visit with
great interest. I dare hardly say pleasure for I now feel as if it would
probably permit me again to expect enjoyment from any calls. I hope
your home is in harmony with that I desire that all long wishes should
be fulfilled. I am sorry to hear that I have to elevate these humble
beliefs above the humblest of facts. I am very much tired. There is already quite a
refrain there. Smith has returned I have not yet seen him for I have not been out of the
house since we came to the city except to church on Sunday. I believe I am going to the
praying meeting tonight. I will soon leave home as soon as may be. I am glad you are so happy. Martin sends her love to you. Your affectionately
Ever your loving sister
Emily

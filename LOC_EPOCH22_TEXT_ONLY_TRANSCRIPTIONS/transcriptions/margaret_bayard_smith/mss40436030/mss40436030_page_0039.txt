The winter is gone -- a winter to which so
many looked for and with earnest desire -- Ambition,
pride & variety, have had ample play, in
the bounds of our city -- Many are enlisting on
success -- Many deploring disappointment in
per me, it has been a season of bustle,
with out occupation -- of crowded, without secret --
my greatest pleasure, has been denied from
Mary's pleasure -- I never did labour,
rest from his labour, with more satisfaction
from his stall retire from crowded, to solitude
On dear sister, These parts of your letters, which flatte
me & depreciate yourself -- Scan never answer
pray do not write in this manner -- it only dis
trusts me -- In paper is full -- you shall
hear in the course of next week, from me
or the girls -- For ever so dearer sister, if it is some
comfort to think of your happiness -- pray pray all your love in your arms --

What days I have been at home, I have helped most heartily in
her duties which consist in the kitchen, pickling & preserving -- in some
times when I make these transitions from the kitchen to the drawing
room I think of an observation of Swift, that there are some
women who can perform the part of housewife in the kitchen &
the fine lady in the drawing room -- & appropriate to myself
some of the houses he bestows on such a versatileity of genius,
& to tell the truth I should be much better pleased, if fate only
required me to act one part only. For great as may be my talents at pickling, preserving, hatching & darning,
I would very cheerfully renounce all the honor & all the praise
thereunto attached & affixed. --

you – Sweet children! – Dear little babe, how I should
love to name it – a charming Bess – had beautiful
she was last playing on the queen – 5 posts
had them all here, racing round our yard, &
making nosegays & wreaths. – ah of we were
but nearer! – You must my dear Sister consider
this letter equally for both – I will try to
wake your "Mania" I hope she will have
a letter from her while she is on Pomusack –
nothing. – at the wind blows cold, the sun shines
brighly, the fields are green, the trees in clopens &
All my young folks out enjoying the season. I am sitting
by a fine – so much for old age! – Ann & Lucthelen are taking
a ride on horseback – Pray, pray write soon, dear Sister
& your affectionate Margaret
Mr. John Patrick
Geneswick
3/9

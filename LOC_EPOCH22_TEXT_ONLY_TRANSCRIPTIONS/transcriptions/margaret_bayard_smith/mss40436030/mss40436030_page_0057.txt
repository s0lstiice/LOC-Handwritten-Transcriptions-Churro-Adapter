I want to send you a pair of shoes, that I hope founded at home - as a small remembrance of us - but I am afraid they are not presentable - not fit to go as a gentleman. I will consider about it.
I will write to Sylletton by his Uncle, when you have him till him the news of it.
Try him in - I am ashamed to see you such a servant - excuse it. With esteem to me - I long to hear from you again and know how you all are. Give my love to every heart wishes to know.

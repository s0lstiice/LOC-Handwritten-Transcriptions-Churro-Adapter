A Friend.
Friendship is like the Cobler's shoe
That gives time to slip in unity
But Love, is like the Cobler's nail
That pierces through the soul and all.
"An, do you remember how Eugene once told us
that if we went at noon day to the bottom of a
deep pit, we should be able to see the stars,
which on the land ground are invisible. Even
so from the depths of grief, sorrow, watchful
sleep and dying -- The blessed apparitions of
heaven made themselves visible to our eyes."
Eugene train.
Richmond April 15. 1862.
To Virginia
Off with your eye shall fondly trace,
Each simple line I write to thee;
What for the time that I in the place
Oh! then Virginia think on me.
When pleasant spectacle in thine eye
When every scene is fair to thee,
Then swift the happy moment fly,
Oh! then Virginia think on me.
The life, the light, my Heaven defend,
Ye should I then by its stern decree;
He want a faithful friend,
Oh! then Virginia think on me.

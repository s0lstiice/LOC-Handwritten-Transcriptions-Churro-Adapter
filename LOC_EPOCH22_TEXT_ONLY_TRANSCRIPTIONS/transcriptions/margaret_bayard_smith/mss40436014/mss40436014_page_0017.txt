I came at the evening hour -- when the star of love
shines upth' from the azure heavens -- when the most
smiles of the moon fall gently on the shadowy waves
below -- and infuse my spirits with the meditative
broom. I came at the midnight hour when all is
dark around and all is silent there -- and when the
Whip-poor-will is heard -- or Chanticleer's more cheer-
ing voice comes early from the night bounding bower --
gone with rupture on the setting sun -- I watch his
golden beams as they sink a hill on the wood -- ascend
bells and listen with delight to the ocean waves as
they foam and dash against the rocky shore -- I
wander where the forest rises in all its grand and
impressive majesty -- and dear is the night wing
of the winds as they whistle round some isolated
tailing -- I love the thunder's tone -- and the
lightning's flash amid the sleeping gloom -- and
pray where the cateract whirled through the unuttered
voice of nature in agony, mine are the autumnal
thins, the withered leaf -- the fatigued hunter's justice
in decay -- I am the soul of song -- I am music
over the most enduring charm -- and I pour from
the father and the father's spirit which often and with the
heart -- and love for owns my influence -- I not only
create its enthusiasm -- but give it constancy to its reflec-
tions emotion -- If genius too, I am the chosen com-
fiance -- not only the lover and the poet are mine --
but I dwell in the clopine hills where wisdom and
sincerity hold their court -- and there I have my mystic
gods and their virtues. To destiny also, I give an
energy and zeal -- I am not a dark and gloomy power,
but a magnificent of noble and strong, pensive man --
born to captivate but the feeling heart!

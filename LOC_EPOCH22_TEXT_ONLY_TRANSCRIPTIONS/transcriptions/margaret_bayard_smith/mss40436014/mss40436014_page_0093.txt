Or yes the spirit of this land,
And bright liberty!
And who is this laid by the side?
Many a gain appears,
She, was a young and happy bride,
Washed in at Charleston's bay.
The tender fragile form she was,
In hopes her life to save,
From an angelical, distant shore
Whisked by the ocean wave,
To this our warm and sunny land,
To this our southern sky,
Trusting in an so soft and bland
Religion, would not die.
Another Mary, young and fair,
Far from her land of birth,
Has come, this sacred vault to share,
And lie on foreign earth!
Truly in June -- in fortune's bright,
Her youth was in its glow,
The hopes had never felt a slight,
Yet here -- the line is down!
But these were sweet eyes -- flowers
Flourished from their home;
Orchids, they in their native homes
Bought have escaped such doom.
Ah no! See where beside them lies,
A lively notice one!
The bloodied beneath our country's cross
And died where it had been.
She never left her beloved home
But green and flourishing there
For God, to be enlaid to earth,
Far from a parent's care.

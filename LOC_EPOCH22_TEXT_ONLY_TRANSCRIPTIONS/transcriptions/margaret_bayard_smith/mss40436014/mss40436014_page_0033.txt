BRING FLOWERS.
Bring flowers, young flowers for the fatal board,
Bring flowers the cup in the wine is poured to,
Bring flowers! they are springing up now and out
From their death plants out on the southern pole,
And the touch of the sun beam bathes withed the rose
To deck the hall where the bright rose flowers.
Bring flowers to their in the compass’d path—
It both shakes stones with his strong mouth!
The converse with the spirits of nature back,
The wines he crushed in his blessed trunk,
The turf loth and where he won the day—
Bring flowers to die in the conqueror’s way!
Bring flowers to the captive’s lonely cell,
They have tales of the joyous words to tell,
Of the free blue streams and the glowing shore
And the bright world shut from his languid eyes
They will bear him a thought of the sunny home,
And a dream of his youth— bring him flowers with flowers!
Bring flowers, fresh flowers, for the bride to wear!
They were given to death in her shining hair.
She is leaving the house of her childhood’s earth,
She has bid farewell to her father’s heart.
Her place is now by another’s side—
Bring flowers for the bride of the fair young bride!
Bring flowers, pale flowers, on the line to shed,
Nation for the blood of the early dead!
For this through its leaves bath the whole nation blood,
On this in the words was the earliest murder.
Though they smile in peace for what never was ours,
They are love’s last gift— bring ye flowers, pale flowers!
Bring flowers to the shrine where we have laid our prayer,
They are nature’s offering, their place is here!
They speak of hope to the fainting heart,
With a voice of promise they come and go,
They speak in dust through the soldier home,
They break bath in glory— bring flowers, bright flowers!

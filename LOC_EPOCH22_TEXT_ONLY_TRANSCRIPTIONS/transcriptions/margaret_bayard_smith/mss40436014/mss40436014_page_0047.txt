On, while an immense multitude
of ships, these twenty plains,
and twenty-five ships of light
and distant signals.
But, this just the thrilling note
which fell the approach of one;
Orion, which stands of the late
landing from shore.
For armament alone was useless,
And our ships of shipping only;
With discipline and loyalty of
the battling mains portery.
Burke's spite is well at home that
from our freedom comes
Princeps dies from the plains
whose peace and prosperity.
And gratitude inspires the stern
that busts from every heart,
In power unparted from on high
To repel the foreign's dart.
The immortals carry sires and sons
Or mortal foot to land,
United into life to save the stream
Before the name of foe.
"Praised be the God" -- The deity thrones
Rocked letters, and never falling,
Until with daylight parting flame,
Is softly whispering.
But silence falls on the noisy wing
From the peaceful land,
While, amidst rising amid the storm
The mighty guardians stand.
Washington - January 1st, 1855.

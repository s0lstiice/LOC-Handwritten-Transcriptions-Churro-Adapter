There is a word that rends the heart.
Which all have and all must say,
Which tears the bond of love apart,
And leaves the union of life apart
And then when with all broken springs
Back into life, and earth returns
Light as the wood-lark on the spring
That escaped word may think it said.
But as the regiment over the dead
Of war which makes us ourly own
That all our tender joys are gone
Which bids us have one step alone
Along the path of pain
Which is barred by the porting, broken
When the soul puts the shell of death
And never struggles with life again.
Command's the bare of love to flow
Or what hath been its joy below.
It is a word that breaks the bond apart.
Of youthful hearts in happy hours
Which makes the world a wilderness
Levont of order, - and flowers
The faded leaves between the ground,
The dead leaves creeping round
Shadows the ruined towers
Where once the rose and Lily grew,
And sparkled in the morning dew.
It is a word which scores every one
The fondly hoped would last for age
Which fills the light of beauty eyes
That shaves all her smiles away
That leaves affection on her lips
And leaves with grief her breast of
praise.
What is the word which all must say?
Oh! every heart and tongue can tell.
It is that fatal word - Ruinwell.
(Written a few days previous to L.H.s. going to Hotchkiss Field for 2 years.)
Zimmer Rhymer

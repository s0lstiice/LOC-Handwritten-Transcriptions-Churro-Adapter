The noise—the opening—the change to his presence,
fell like a leaden weight beneath his hidden face.
The dead will raise to him that deep blue eye,
which denounced despair on earthly agency.
Her long fair hair lay floating over his arms,
but with the unutterable of desolation it changed;
hence beat that broken silence upon his image divested
of all that feeling seemed almost unselfish!
With what a roar the thunder of that signal-gong!
It told him's present— and he knew that turn
again—again that form he made for flight,
which mutely obeyed, imploringly ceased!
And Willams to the trench and to the line,
One moment passed— as if to pass no more;
Still that fine line earth broke their line,
Still that old frockhead turned— is turned gone?
Staten, April 5th 1832.
Lives for a Ladies Oblivion.
Bright as the wreath the mission weaves
around the sleeping knee,
Bright as the beam that must leaves
on fields of Alpine snow,
Like bubbles bursting into air,
On rapid mountain streams,
Fleeting as there, and still as fair
In life's illusion dreams.
And yet to such frail hopes we cling
Still blindly under them line,
Through daily, hourly parthings,
And fadings in our view.
Still courting to our vain embrace
The shadows as they rise,
Still wander in the fruitless chase,
We sink the thane time his!
July—1825.

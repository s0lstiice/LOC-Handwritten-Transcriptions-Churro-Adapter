Go call the patriot from his grave,
And see him awful rise,
And see the beauty of the brave
Who paid the sacrifice
Of life to freedom's holy laws,
With martyr's in Spurian's cause:
Is he upon the little field
Where thousands lay before,
And on the broken vanquisher's yield,
And hear the wild prayer,
That shall the changes, chief and mean
In the long march from year to year.
I would not give these old dreams,
(For fortune may play them or)
And pour of matching dollars plains
From earth a river below,
For country, name of Wharton
The world may never understand nor save.
Known of parted times; long be
Oily water and beguile
The dull homes of reality
With one attractive smile.
Telling a pleasant cup for me
From fountains of antiquity.
Living September 2nd 1862.
Madame Wharton's friends, being aware of her passionate love for George found means to send him to her
premises. The night of a power change in the American government, and flat-topping down, from her imprudent
desire to be under the happy shelter of any political party, I was happy from my infancy, with my flowers and my
brother in the American cabinet of a prison with books and flowers, I can forget any other misfortunes
and the injustice of mankind.
Living April 4th 1863.

To my Washington's glorious name,
Make the wild song:
Oh may this love immortal flame!
Fill every heart and tongue.
May love what mortal thought can reach?
That mortal organ display:
Conjugations alone, which
Can wander thus away.
He left his ancient throne on high,
Off the bright realms of bliss,
And came to earth to bless and die,
Has ever love thus died?
Dear God, while we adoring pay
On humble hands to thee,
Our every heart and soul proclaim
The Father and Son.
I may have loved the blissful theme
Of every heart and tongue,
But I have loved that charming name,
And from the heart I send
My heart's devotion.

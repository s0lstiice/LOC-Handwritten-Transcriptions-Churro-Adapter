I think on them when the young moon is breaking
In reference to light.
I have at the earliest thought of my waking
only fast at night.
I think on them when day light is declining
how in the world
I think on them when its last ray goes shining
on nature's red.
And when on summer evening hour is comming
Our favorite star,
I think if one who watched with me its leaving,
never desired for.
When Labor's cries, and all are sleeping round me
I passively.
How often has the silent midnight found me
Thinking on them!
And still, in all that memory tries to measure,
They from I see,
In every little grief, or hoped for pleasure,
I think on them.
Sidney, 1827.

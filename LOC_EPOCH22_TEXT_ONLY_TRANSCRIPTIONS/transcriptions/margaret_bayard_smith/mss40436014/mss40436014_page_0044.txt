The Randal's Wife
My voice is in mine as Robert's!
The lock is in my heart,
The thorn is my resting place,
And yet I must depart,
With in my soul's demands -- too strong --
For pierced is its chain,
All women of thy sex, dear Friend!
Yet even though my fly rain!
Thou wilt mine eye from him, Robert's!
Thou wilt my life blood flow,
But to the blunderer's victory,
And entirely to me go!
A little while between our hearts
The shadowy gulf must lie,
Yet leave me for their consuming
hell, still I remain!
Had I thy dear one on my chest,
My spirit they detain,
I know that from thine agony
Is none that knowing mine
Will hinder --weep not! not the pay,
The bitter can that I left --
Oh! say what is, and yet a joy
That I thy love's support!
But rather thee! but thy thought of death
Is mine in action --
The voice that must be silent soon,
Would speak to thee once more,
That thou mayst hear its lingering on
Though years of after life,
Is taken in nothing here
Even from thy love of Christ.
I bless thee for the restful heart,
The tender and true time,
Where mine both found the happiest rest
That ever fond woman's heart;
I bless thee, faithful friend and guide,
In my own thy treasure there,

I love a world of romance
I love at times to be
Where all that is seems but a trance,
And thought relativity
Where the world for any hardship
And living men to me is dear.
It is pretty, the dwelling then
Or remains of the past,
Among the years, and stones and men,
That time hath not in cast
The divine harmony of age,
The gay airs of the later age
The white plum'd sons of chivalry,
The stately forms of yore,
The master antique or papery,
The bed or tomb of bones
The turning under the babies' eyes
The soundings of the quiet grave
I love to dwell with fantasy
And find in visions where
Some mighty spirit marching by
Before the winged steeds
Or lamenting loudly, pathos or near
When enthrall words are nothing more
Or by the my butterf'd tower
Or planted the ancient hall,
Where beauty thrones from part and time
To dance and festival,
And many a twinkling foot was gay
That long in dust hath passed away
When many a stately robe and hair
Disguised its final story
And the red wine-red had the charm
Of love or bitter wrong
I love to see these walls once more,
And revel on the ancient floor

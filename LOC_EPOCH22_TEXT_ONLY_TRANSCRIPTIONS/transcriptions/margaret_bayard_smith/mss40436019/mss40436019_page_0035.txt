1847
The city of Rome which was founded by Romulus in
the year of the Olympiad under 712 years before the
Christian era, was situated on the promontory of the
Isola Tiberina, which is bounded on the north by the
Rome river, and on the south by the Tiber river, and
on the east by the Forum Romanum, and on the
west by the Palatine hill. It was only founded into a
modern city of seven provinces each inhabited by a distinct
race of men, except the inhabitants of Rome, the Romans,
from whom the provinces were named, and who respectively
conquered their provinces, until they had made them
part of the Roman Empire.
The government of Rome was at first hereditary,
and continued to be so for two years; at the termination
of this period, Tarquin died, and the last of the
kingdom was capitated, and with him every image of
royalty was abolished.

[The following is a portion of a letter written by George B. Smith to his brother, Senator Thos. B. Smith, concerning the occupation of filling the blank leaves in the office of the United States Mint. The letter discusses the importance of preserving and improving the office, and mentions that the proper employment of time constitutes one's chief happiness.]
May this book prove a valuable acquisition to the office, and on the occupation of filling its blank leaves, may she find both preservation and improvement, and bear that the proper employment of time constitutes one's chief happiness.
Accept my thanks, my sister and my family for your gift has proved a treasure.
Your instruction and amusement lend to sure no wishing pleasure.

How shall I adore Thee, Author
of this universe & all this good to man,
To attain the height & depth of Thy
divine ways, all human thought
come short - Supreme of Thys!
April 10th 1823 New York
Behold then hast made my days as
a handbreadth, & my age is as nothing before
Thee: verily every man in his best esteem
is as vanity - Surely every one wasthought
in vain shew - all are disquieted
in vain. Man is like unto vanity, his
days are as a shadow that passeth away.
I am a stranger on the earth!
How many are the days of Thy servant?
Let my sentence come from Thy presence.
Deal bountifully with Thy servant,
that I may live & keep Thy commandments.
Open then mine eyes, that I may behold
the wonderful things of Thy law.
Attend unto my cry, - give ear unto
my prayer, that goeth not out of my spirit
life. Thou hast loved my heart - Thou
hast visited me in the night - Thou
hast loved me - I am distressed That my
heart shall not tranquill.
Hold up my garrisons in Thy paths,
that my footsteps slip not - I will call
upon Thee & Thou wilt hear me, in
thine thine ear unto me & hear me -
Show me Thy marvellous marvellous
loving kindness - Oh Thou that savest by
the right hand, those who put their trust
in Thee - Sheep me as the apple of
eye - hide me under the shadow

1st Corinthians. chap 14th

Is not this as plain an
illustration of the supremacy of
God to Christ as words can give?
Paul is promulgating the
Gospel to a heathen people
and surely he himself did
not believe he taught, or could
teach them any doctrine
essential to salvation _ If he
taught truth he would have
explained it as clearly as
he does other essential doctrines
2 among the gentiles he might
have done it without any apparent
resistance of the church public
opinion, since the idea of
the incarnation of their
Gods was familiar to
the greeks & romans &
He would not on this point
have offended their prejudices
or popular faith _ When
addressing the Jews, this would
have been the case, for the
reputation they expected, was a
man, inspired by God 2 sent by
him to teach a guide & governor
Even then Christ called himself
the Son of God, they took up
stones to stone them, & he
remained with them, why?
2 was far from damning the

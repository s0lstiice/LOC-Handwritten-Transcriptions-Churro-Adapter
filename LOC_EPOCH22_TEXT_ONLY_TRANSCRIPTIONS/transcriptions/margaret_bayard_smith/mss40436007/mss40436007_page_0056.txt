I never forget thy word for precepts,
for with them thou hast purchased me
I am thine, save me! --
I know oh Lord that thy judgments
are right and that thou in faithfulness
hast afflicted me, -- let I pray thee
thy merciful kindness be for my comfort,
according to thy promise -- let thy
tender mercies come unto me, that I
may live -- let my heart be sound in
thy statutes -- that I be not ashamed
How many one the days of thy servant
my soul is continually in thy hand.
Hold me up & I shall be safe.
Guard them upon me & be merciful
unto me, as thou wast to do to those
who trusted in thee -- Make thy
face to shine upon thy servant,
My help cometh from the Lord, which
made heaven & earth -- He will not
safer thy foot to be moved -- He
that keepeth thee will not slumber
nor sleep -- The Lord is thy keeper
& thy shadow in the day -- He
shall not let thy foot to be moved
at night -- The Lord shall preserve
thou from all evil -- He shall preserve thy
soul -- The Lord shall preserve
thy going out & thy coming in, from
this time forth & even for evermore.
Trust them in the Lord, Oh my
soul & be not cast down, neither
be affrighted -- The dark deep & clouds
compaeth thee around --
Trust in the Lord oh my soul!!

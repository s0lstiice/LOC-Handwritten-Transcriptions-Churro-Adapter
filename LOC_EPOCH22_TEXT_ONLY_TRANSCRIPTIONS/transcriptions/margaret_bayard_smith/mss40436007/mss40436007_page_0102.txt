I have been confined to the house for a month by indisposition and the Doctor says that anything too
agitating will produce a delirium. I am sure that if you do not come I shall be quite sick -- and
you will have to answer for it -- so be done to come. Father said he was going to see you to persuade
you, if he could, to come -- and if he fails, I am sure that this letter will do no good.

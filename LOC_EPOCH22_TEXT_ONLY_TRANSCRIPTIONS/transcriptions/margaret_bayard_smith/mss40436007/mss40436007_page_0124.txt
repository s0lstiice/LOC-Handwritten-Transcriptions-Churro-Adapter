January - 1833 -
15th. A year has elapsed, since I was
excluded as I am now excluded in this
chamber - alone with my god & the
spirit of my beloved one. I cannot indeed
recall her image - it will not return
though I do all I can in this silence &
stillness to recover a glimpse of sweet
form - her voice - her manner - I am
surrounded by precisely the same objects -

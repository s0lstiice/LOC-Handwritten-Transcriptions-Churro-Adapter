The letter of Mr. Jefferson was written
soon after the Attack upon him by the
"native Virginian"; and when there was a
strong expectation of a War between Russia
and Turkey:-- This explains some Allusion
in them.
From Mr. Jefferson to Mr. Adams.
Monticello, June 1st 1822.
It is very long my dear Sir, since I
have written to you.-- My Dislocated
Wrist is now become so stiff that I write
slowly and with pain; and, therefore,
write as little as I can. -- Yet it is due
to mutual Friendship to ask once in a
While how do you do? -- The Papers tell
us that General Starke is off at the age
of Ninety three. -- He still lives,
at about the same age, Cheerful, and
Plenish as a Grass-hopper and so
much without memory that he scarcely

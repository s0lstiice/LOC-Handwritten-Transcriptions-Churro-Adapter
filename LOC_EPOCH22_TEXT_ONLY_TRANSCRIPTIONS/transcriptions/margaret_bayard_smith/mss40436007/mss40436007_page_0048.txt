Sleep.
"Praise be to him who invented Sleep." Sancho Panza
When baling Sleep its soothing influence sheds,
And lulls in blissful dreams our aching heads,
No longer then by toil or pain oppress
We sink in sweet repose and quiet rest.
For then removed from every anxious care,
We breathe in brighter worlds a purer air,
Range fancy's fairy realms on wing sublime
Free and unfetter'd by the lapse of time.
Mute, in peaceful slumber, long and deep,
The sage-the Shepherd-and their Sovereign sleep,
No longer then the Ambition and the Brave
Pant for the Victor's laurel or the grave-
Een Hated sleeps! yet guiltless of the blow
Which waking dooms to death his hapless foe,
And hopeless Love-forgets its dark despair
In vision roos and wins the beautious fair.

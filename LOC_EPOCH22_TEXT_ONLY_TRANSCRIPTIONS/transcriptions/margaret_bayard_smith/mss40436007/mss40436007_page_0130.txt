October 24th 1839.
Another anniversary of my annus
heavenly birth-day! Born into im-
mortal happiness - Freed from the
meany, weary load of human life -
Bereaved of her, how almost insuffer-
able to me - What shall I do to
lighten the intolerable load? -
Oh that it would please God to
re-write us -
I want to write a memoir
of my Anna - that may serve as
a little record of her short but precious
life - but I cannot -
The event that has made my
life a blank - must leave
this page a blank -

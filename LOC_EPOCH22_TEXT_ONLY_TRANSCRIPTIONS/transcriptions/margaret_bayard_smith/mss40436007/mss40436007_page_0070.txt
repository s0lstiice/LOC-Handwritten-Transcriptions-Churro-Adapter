Power of Christ
And he said unto them, ye shall
drink indeed of my cup 2 be baptized
with the Baptism, I am baptized with, but
to sit on my right hand 2 on my left, is
not mine to give, but it shall be given to
them, for whom it is prepared by my father.
But without faith, it is impossible
to please Him - for he that cometh
to God, must believe that he is, 2 that
he is the rewarder of them that diligently
seek him."
That what is here meant by faith?
The apostle tells us,
"Faith is the substance of things hoped for,
the evidence of things not seen."
"Through faith we understand that the
world is made, framed by the word of God
so that things which are seen, were not
made of things which do appear."
"It is not mine to give - yet in another
place I find these words,
"For the father judgeth no man, but
hath committed all judgment into his
son."- John 5th
and again - "This day shalt thou receive
me in Paradise."
These oppositions of text to text, so often
found distress me - What is truth?

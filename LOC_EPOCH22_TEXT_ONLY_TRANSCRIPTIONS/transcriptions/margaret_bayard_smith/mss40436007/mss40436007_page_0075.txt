'Tis thine, almighty saviour, thine
To form the heart anew.
'Tis thine, the passions to me call
And upwards bid them rise;
And make the scales of error fall
From reason's darkened eyes.
To chase the shades of death away
Inmost at hopes to give—
This beam of heaven, this vital ray,
'Tis thine alone to give.
Oh! change these erring hearts of
ours
And give them life divine! —
Then shall our passions and powers
Almighty Lord be thine.

Help me to watch & pray & strive

Increase my faith, increase my hope
When doubts & fears arose prevail
And hear my fainting spirit up
Or soon my strength must fail.
When sin temptations first set my heart
Or leave my feet aside
My god thy powerful aid impart
My guardian & my guide!
Oh keep me in thy heavenly way
And bid this darkness flee
Oh never, never let me stray

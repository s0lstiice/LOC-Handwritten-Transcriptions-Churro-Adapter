Let me this day, record they continued good news to me to my heavenly father! Another winter has gone, another spring has come. In this interval three old & three young persons in the families of my nearest kindred have been taken from this life, whilst my dear husband & children are spared. These, my strongest ties to life, remain unbroken. Health, peace, competence are added to these blessings of the heart. My winter has been passed without cares or afflictions, unmarshalled by any intense personal interest, event or high enjoyment. It has been a quiet comfortable season -- my spirits indeed have been often depressed & life has often felt even a bastion to me. I have wished for the release which my friends & relatives have had -- There was no special cause for this weariness of life -- but like one whose appetite has been more than satisfied, who turns away from even a luxuriously spread board with feelings of satiety & disgust I turn from the feast of life. I have had enough -- no desire is felt for more. Does this arise from a diseased state of mind -- the want of which of life? Are the digestive faculties of intellect impaired -- are the sensibilities of the heart deadened? alas I fear they are -- that some powerful moral stimulus is necessary to restore their tone & activity --

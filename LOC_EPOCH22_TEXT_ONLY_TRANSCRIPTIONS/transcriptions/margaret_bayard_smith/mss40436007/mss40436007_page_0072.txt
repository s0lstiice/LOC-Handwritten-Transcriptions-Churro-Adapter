"There be some that trouble you
now, many who prevent the gospel of
Christ-but if people they shall not
trouble me-in instead of listening to
the interpretations of Men-I ask you
to the Scriptures-at present I
only need the Gospel of Jesus Christ brought
with a particular reference to the
nature & character of Christ & endeavour
to discover what Jesus himself taught
what his disciples during his life, achieved
& what after his death they taught
what Paul & the apostles taught.
My present conviction is, & for many
years has been, that Christ is a divine
being, the only begotten Son of God,
sent by God, to reclaim men from
idolatry & sin & to teach them a
pure & spiritual religion-a pure
perfect system of morals their lives
may before been taught-they by
him, God created this world, that to him
God gave this world & that at his
final consummation, when his allotted
task shall be fulfilled, he will gather
these creatures, he shall yield up
this power into the hands of his
God, & on God & his father & our
Father-in which I at Paul fully agree
in Corinthians chap 15-
But as good & wise men have
in all ages of the church, given different
interpretations to the same texts, notwithstanding
This point I have arrayed contradictory
texts one against another-a to one
justification have answered a figurative & to
another a literal sense, I will once more
meddle over the scriptures in reference to
This & endeavour mere sincerity, doctrine
unpartially & more humbly to discover
truth-I feel my own efforts to be as inefficuous
as those of a blind man groping
his way through dense forests, full
of obstructions & dangers.- Spirit of truth
shine into my mind!
My beloved Son in whom I am well
pleased-Matthew-
"The only begotten son, which is in the
bosom of the father-John-
"The brightness of his father's glory-The
capable image of his person"- (Surely even
this passage so much urged by Trinitarians does
not mark identity-The same words are
familiarly used, in relation to men-
"He is his capably image-he is his glory
His pride-he surpasses his father- and
common expressions-we must take
language in its admitted & familiar sense
to not give it in scripture a different
meaning, from what it was in human affairs
"He thought it no robbery to be equal to

Nothing would any one think of

June. 6th 1823.
When thee I seek Protecting pow'r
Betsy wait wishes still'd
And may this consecrated hour
With better hopes be fill'd!
I pray love the power of thought lest 'd
Do thee my thoughts would soar
They survey o'er my life has flowed
That mercy I adore
In each event of life her faithful
My ruling hand I see
Each blessing to my soul extended
Because bestowed by thee
When gladness wings my favour'd hair
They live my thoughts shall fall
Resigned when storms of sorrow live
To meet they sovereign will
My lifted eye without a tear
The gathering storm shall see
My steadfast heart shall know when
That heart shall rest on thee
Shelton Sarah Williams

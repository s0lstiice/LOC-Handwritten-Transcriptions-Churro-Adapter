To my dear Mother
With the first Viollets of the Spring 1823.
With what delight in childhood's hour
I've watched the Spring's advancing foot,
With her who loved to mark and trace
Sweet nature's every charm and grace—
Each leaf and every bud that burst
Bore hail and every bud that burst
Were hail with rapture as the first
But to the viollets humble shade
Were our first visits always made,
Searching the earliest bud to find
To shield it from the stormy wind
That soon it might rise and bloom
And yield to the its sweet perfume.

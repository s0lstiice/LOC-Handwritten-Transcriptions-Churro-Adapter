Be a Lady on the Death of her daughter—
Weep not my mother—weep not thus for me
Life's feverish dream & transient pain are o'er
Oh rather should thy Anna weep for thee,
And wanderer still, upon earth's weary shore!
Weep not my mother—on the spot where lie
The dust and ashes of a form you love,
But from the cold grave, turn your streaming eyes
And see your daughter, with her God above.
Weep not my mother—that my youthful part,
So soon was finished, on this earthly scene;
That the fond hopes of your maternal heart,
Burst no more,—than of they ever had been!
Since not with all your sweet indulgent care
Could I with a father's and a husband's love
Could half the joy and happiness prepare,
Could I with all the love of a mother's breast
And all the heart of a child's desire

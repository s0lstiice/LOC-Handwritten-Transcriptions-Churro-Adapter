1812. The King's Commissioners for the
Government of the Province of Canada, in
consequence of the necessity of providing
for the support of the troops in that
Province, have issued a proclamation
to the inhabitants of the Province of
Canada, prohibiting them from
exporting any of the produce of their
lands, or any of the produce of their
manufactures, except in such
quantities as they may be enabled
to supply the wants of the troops
stationed in the Province of Canada.

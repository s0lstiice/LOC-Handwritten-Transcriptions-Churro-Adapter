Fortuneous, happy old age
An age that mellts, in unprejudiced decay
And glides in modest innocence away
Where peaceful day, reverence endears
And night congratulatory conscience cheers
The general favourite, as the general friend
Such age there is & who shall with its end
See the condemnation, misfortune done.
Johnson. [1800]

Can it be possible, that when I resign
this frail, fœretish being, I shall still
find myself in conscious existence?
When the last spark of agency has
consumed that dim memory, to those
that knew me, and the few who loved
me; when the cold, stiffened, un
animated, ghastly form is wrapped into
the earth, to be the prey of inquisitive
reptiles, and to become in time a broken
stool, shall I yet be warm in life,
warm and keen, enjoying and enjoying?
Is your venerable sage, and holy flame
only, is there probability in your
conceptions, truth in your stories
of another world beyond death; or
are they all alike, brutish visions,
and fabricated fables? If there is another
life, it must be for the just, the benevolent,
the amiable, and the humane;
what a flattering idea, then, is a
world to come! Would to God I was
famously believed it, as Fortuitously much
it! There I should meet an aged parent,
now at rest from the many
buffetings of an evil world, against
which he is so long struggling. There I should
meet the friend, the disinterested friend
see a ponderous poor, or labourer, caught

found at home. The Smiths were an eager audience, but
Sam, James & myself used a stud poker, and played chess
when alone; but this was not often the case, as family affairs
were always interrupted by a few members of opposite sex. It
was pleasant to be in the neighbourhood. I received with the
greatest pleasure an invitation from Mr. Smith to dine, and
entered into a bright house. The curtains were drawn down,
the coffee placed near the fire, and a comfortable tea-table
arranged on one side for tea-place, and comfortable
chairs were the appearance of the room; Sam was
the host, and the seat of the table occupied by him, I knew some
little about him, his name, little that I could would
learn. He was a great fellow creature and a kind of President

Sydney, March 25th 1816 -
I have passed what is called a gay winter, that is I have been
at many card parties, balls &c &c have not played one evening
cards scarcely one hour alone - But has this been partly,
as this been the life of pleasure recalled to how
falsely, or called I have fully experienced - Blessed is
Sam with every circumstance conducive to national happiness,
have I enjoyed, Sam - so that now life, called
a gay life, has rendered the little people of Providence worthy
& unimproved - I have had health to have my dear husband
& children, I have had the society of a dear & much esteemed
friend both because I enjoy domestic comfort which
fortune could bestow - & also how little have I enjoyed,
how little improved my time & circumstances, to have
lived the last 5 months I can not fee on a single
day what I have passed, or rather interest or duty
would have dictated - Several myself with thinking it
was the unavoidable consequence of living in a city a
praying for society, yet I cannot acknowledge I might have
done better, - How unfavorable to the performance of
duty, to the improvement of mind, to the pleasure &
the imagination, to the exercise of the affection, in
a residence in the city, when subject to the interruptions
of company - When I come in the morning, I
immediately breakfasted & before I had arranged the
parties cleaned away the breakfast table, attended
to the children, dressed for the day a quiet friend

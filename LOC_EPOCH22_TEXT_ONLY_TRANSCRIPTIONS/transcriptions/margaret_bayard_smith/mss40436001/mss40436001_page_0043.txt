From honest sincerity & Truth &
truth only has been the object of my search.
If my mind has rejected some of the articles
of faith in which I have been educated, it has
not been because I could not understand them
but because I believed them incompatible with
the wisdom & the goodness of God. Never
would my heart consent to the doctrine that
for the crime of one man millions & millions
of beings should suffer & that to atonement, should
not alone be given to love a merciful
a God who affronts affronts such a flagrant equality
refugient in it to my moral sense of right,
that an innocent being should suffer for
the guilt of even man, with all his depravity,
would not accept such a sacrifice, every feeling
of justice & benevolence rebels against it,
but above all others, my whole soul, my heart
my mind rejects the doctrine, that an man
that be condemned to eternal misery, who does
not accept of salvation thru the sacrifice
of the holy & just Jesus, that God who
God has made it impossible that this
should be known, should suffer. May God of
believers creatures thus condemned -- thus unjustly
condemned -- All gracious & all honest men is it
possible that doctrines so opposed to every nature
could be embraced for truth!!! I set not up reason
to judge of these doctrines, if my intellect
came from my incapability to understand them,
I would submit, as I now do, the many difficulties
which are incomprehensible to my understanding,
for instance I knew not what then art, from
whence then comest, how long then hast endured,
or how long then shalt endure -- why man
was not made more perfect -- why pain, was
permitted -- there in many other things I do not
understand -- never help, I believe in the existence
of God perfectly as I believe in my own existence
in the infinite Power, goodness & wisdom, is perfectly,
perfectly, that however incomprehensible
the system of the universe appears to my understanding
yet my mind feels the perfect certainty
that it is the univers univers -- But as to
the doctrines of original sin, & universal
condemnation except thru the atonement
of Christ, these doctrines, my heart, & understanding
absolutely reject. -- There is a something within
me, I know not by what name to call it,
Moral sense, a conscience, but whatever called,

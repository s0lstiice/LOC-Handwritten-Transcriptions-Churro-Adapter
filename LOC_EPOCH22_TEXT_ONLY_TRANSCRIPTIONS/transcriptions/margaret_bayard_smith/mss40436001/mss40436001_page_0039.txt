praise & thank thee, O my Providence!
above all others let me thank thee for this—for which
would health, friends, fortune, children I could have
avoided, if from evil and infidelity men alone, instead of
blessings they would have been turned into tormenters—
the true miserablest in the human heart—How little
unto itself it is in God did I know in
own! I thought myself so strong that I was safe
with them alarmed at the danger which should
befall me—But how much did I find myself
in peril—thundering storm, conflict on every
hand—But tranquility & security are now
more mine—Evidences from heaven are my
most precious—hence faith not in my own
judgment but on thee, gracious
Protector I shall depend. May this sentiment of dependence,
this confidence in my own faultless, innocent
mind, but may it sin to desire a doctor in
my heart & draw me nearer & nearer to thee!
Oh for these warm feelings of devotion, that intimate
enterance, these delightful aspirations of the soul to God.
Whither have they flown?—Ye sin which
have my heart place in the second rank of my living
kindnesses, the beloved husband & precious children
that are still opened to me, invaluable
treasures, far dearer to me than any other blessing
Heaven has bestowed! The spirit of my heart
is recalled by reason & reflection; for what would
age be to me, had not the same kind Parent, endowed
me with certain principles affecting the heart, a
temper & disposition—Had I yielded to vice, as had
I neglected my duties, I could not have made me happy,
Virtue alone in happiness below!
That I have been endowed with virtuous dispositions,
that I have been peculiarly enabled to fulfill all
my duties, to be useful to my family & to many
of my fellow creatures; that I have been made
an instrument to guide others in the path of virtue,
to alleviate their sorrows, & to protect
even a single individual from poverty & vice, this
my duty has neither the affliction of my example
encouraged the weak & wavering, — these indeed
are blessings, for above all others—there indeed are
the gifts of divine goodness; I never did my heart
more deeply & sincerely feel that it is from God
they are derived than since the recent trial I
have met with.— How good is God!—to devote
happiness to the performance of our duty—How
truly have I been during the last three months &
never at any period of my life have I been more
constantly, or actively engaged in promoting the comfort
& welfare of so great a number of individuals.

can I regret my non-conformity with those I
believe in Error? But there is an error
which is a source of such pure elevated
happiness, that foreign world I participate of
it. Their error is harmless, -- any it is advantageous.
Therefore I wish them not to conform to me.
But they are different with them, they look
upon me as in eminent danger. For
their sake, more than for my own I would
we believed better.
What was the sentiments which expressed
my dear father in the hour of dissolution.
Trust in God. And do I not trust in
the same God. -- Oh yes beauty, daily, do I trust
in him, for all I have, I all I hope!
The hope of immortality. -- And is not that
my hope. -- Oh yes my hope hereafter in my
constitution now. -- What would this hope be
if it were not for this hope. vain & worthless.
Should I wish for a prolongation of life to my child.
If I thought it would all way remain an
infant? -- How could I wish for life, if it
was confined to this infancy of existence.
Dear father, you have given to your
father & my father in our father's house
we shall meet again. You have reached Rome
before your child, but soon with my journey to
be over 2 from we shall meet to part no more.
And has he then passed to another state of existence,
I shall see him no more on this earth! -- How
incredible does it seem, that such a change has taken
place & that every thing remains the same.
That I am separated for life from a dear & honored
parent & that my heart does not more visibly feel it.
Heavenly Father, if I am in the wrong, do
rectify my judgment. If I am in the darkness of
error, enlighten me with the light of truth.
But if night, give me strength & resolution
to withstand error, to maintain the night.

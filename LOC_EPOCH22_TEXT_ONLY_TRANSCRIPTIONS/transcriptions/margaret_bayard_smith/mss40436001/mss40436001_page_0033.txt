But to health I know of nothing which has such a tendency to produce selfishness & contentment, as order & regularity or enable in the distributor of time, either every hour of the day has its occupation to never find any hour tedious, me tedious, but when we have not what to devote time to it is inextricably obscene; or even having enough to do, if we do not divide it by some means into two or more portions, which in most instances I calculate, which in most instances, which in most instances is important, which most profitable, with the time of action accomplished by nothing remains for us but uselessness & defeat & failure.
How different, when each succeeding hour brings with it, its own peculiar occupation, to render it the more able to fill the most necessary to that hour, patience for content.
Have I not in fact any kind of time, with more satisfaction to myself than the hour in which I came home in my way, and as this interval of time has been marked by no government convenience, it has been debased in certain sections for me, I think it must be owing to the regularity methodical manner in which I have lived, step by step, hour by hour, in precisely the same way.

45
Sent 26. Tuesday
Before leaving for Mt. I ordered a long bunch of
leaves of the valley to be sent E.H.W. on Sunday when
she was expected to return, and put in a card,
"with affectionate greetings, from Huilbertge & Charlie", and
also wrote her a letter,
Sent 20. Tuesday
returned from Mt.
found a notice that E.H.W. had called me in
telephone Sunday afternoon and left word I
was to call her up right away.
Called up the house and Randall's answered
and a moment later, E.H.W.
In a voice filled with emotion she thanked me
for the letter and for the flowers, saying she
had part of them around the picture of Woodrow
in her room and won the rest. She asked me
to come up to divide, and said she felt terribly
about missing me at Portsmouth and that if a
"hint" was considered necessary by me (referring
to my letter) she would have sent me out every
turn she turned through.

Oct 31. Tuesday
Sent 1800 "forty years in work, Sec" also the
original of trans. Linnigs letter on Ammonia
of which I had a copy made by the
scrib book. Also wrote till a letter.
7 o'clock with me at 5 PM. Miss Charles Warren had
written her asking if he could bring her father out. She
arrived at 5 and met me at the door returning from
a drive with her mother & sister. I sat with
fundolable while she went up to the drawing room and
had a 15 minute talk with her before Col. Blen
arrived.
She said Woolley, after half an hour had met
her brother Williams and begged him to see her
and beg her to send Davis a letter, but she
told him she had made up her mind to do
nothing more and then Gen. Glass and I
agreed with her.

I told her she could always call on me for
advice, and that I had no friend of opinion,
I merely would tell her how any matter
affected me, and she could feel free to accept
my judgment or not, as the responsibility
was on her. She seemed very much influenced
at this.
I told her I would be glad to trust my
advice as to Davis in wrestling and that
she could tell Lowley I had advised her,
but she said that would not be fair to
me, that she must take the responsibility
unless she wanted all the advice she
could get.

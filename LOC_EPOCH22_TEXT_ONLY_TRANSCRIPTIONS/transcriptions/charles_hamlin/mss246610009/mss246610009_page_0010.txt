Aug 13. Wed.
We arrived in Baltimore and I left Mrs. in her
machine at about 9.15 am. We went back
to Raleigh St., turning by Mrs Beasley in
Brookline, we visited Mr. Wilson and Mrs Wilson
went over the house and said it were the
most charming house she had ever seen.
I had intended to leave them there but
they begged me to go in with them to
Magnolia, so I finally compromised by
going to Salem with them, returning by
the 1.05 to Boston.
We had enjoyed their visit with us
immensely and we felt very sorry when
they left us.

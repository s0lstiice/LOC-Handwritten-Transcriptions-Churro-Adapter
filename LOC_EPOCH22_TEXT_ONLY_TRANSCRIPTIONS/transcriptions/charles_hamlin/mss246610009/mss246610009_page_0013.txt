Aug. 17. Sunday
Received a delightful letter from Mr Wilson
asking me, among other things, to write her
my opinion of President Coolidges acceptance
address, delivered last Thursday evening.
The letter was dated Friday Aug. 15 from
Lincolnville. I shall at once forward Coolidges
letter and write her frankly about it.

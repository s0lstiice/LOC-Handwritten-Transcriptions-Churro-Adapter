Mr John Houghton, ambassador to Germany,
entirely with me.
He said there was danger of war between
France & U.S.B and was very bitter at France.
He claimed not to believe in the League of
Valence but said he favored strongly an
agreement between U.S. & U.S.B. except for Alaska to establish
and to effect when arbitration,
& not attack one another, neither Russia to
involved force or any naval forces.

creation of the League and art X.

the League and would not do so unless
as a full member with representation in
the council.

was merely bluffing and that in our
heart, he knows that, without the League,
there would be war again, whether in the

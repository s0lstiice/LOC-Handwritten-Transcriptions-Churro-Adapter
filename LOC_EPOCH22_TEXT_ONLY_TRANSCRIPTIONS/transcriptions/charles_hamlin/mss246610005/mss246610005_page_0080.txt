Feb 12. Thursday
Mr P M Harding told Strauss & myself he
had hatched it at night w. Houston; that
H. had divided with Miller & they sent over
our Harding & hatched over Platt; that
H. agreed that our man must be a
Republican; that he told them of his letter
w. MeAdoors; that H. suggested another
Man man, but Ed H demanded him no

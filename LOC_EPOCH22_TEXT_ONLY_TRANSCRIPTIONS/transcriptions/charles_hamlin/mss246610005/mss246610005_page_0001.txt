Yesterday Rev. H. Todd and State Williams had ordered
the Court of a N.W. City bank to write and lead
both him and his Exam er had reported that a Mr.
Cookle (brother of the Cookle who tried to defeat
Louis for Commissioner) was a borough bank lead
banker & he ordered him to have the note
written up; that Cookle said the loan was a
good one & well secured & that he saw no
reason for doing this; that Louis said if he
did not receive the note he would have his
bank drawn for frequent examinations.
Last week Louis wrote Elias a letter protesting
against allowing branches in W. Indies, Egypt
etc. on State City bank; his letter referred to
Board as granting these applications without
due consideration of the Banker's business
branches. I at once wrote Louis that the
Board gave more careful consideration to
this application than to any other at least
one hundred in its etc.
Mr. Solisried Louis in meditating a
better attack on N. City bank and also on the
Board, in case he should finally make
his combination. His vindictiveness is
simply monstrous!

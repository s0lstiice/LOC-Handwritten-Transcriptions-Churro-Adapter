Nov 19. 1861.
Gov H. said he was told on good authority
that the President had offered Sec of J. to Barney
Barnett and that Norman Davis was in
N.Y. trying to persuade Barney to decline it
on grounds that there would be a fierce
fight to prevent his confirmation. This
seems incomprehensible to me.
I received a long telegram from James
Brown & Benin Bkrs Co. N.Y.:-
"The country is to be heartily congratulated
if responsible men like this morning are
correct in stating you have been named
to succeed Mr. Ellson as Sec. of the Treasury.
while I appreciate that your leaving
the Board and adding to the Secretary's
work will increase your burden and throw
greater individual responsibility upon
you and that your might hinder to
continue your present construction work,
in the public interest I strongly
urge you to accept what I consider
the most important cabinet post
by the willow of the U.S. during
the next year and a half.
James Brown
at Brown Brothers & Co.

council asking recommendation as to leaving
states to mail rates to prevent discrimination by
members between etc. I objected on grounds no
necessity for creating commercial rates
as no discrimination existing between and it
have no power over schedule. I recommended
leave by members business. Letter had been
sent & agreed finally to let it stand in
despite misunderstanding next to bind any
member.

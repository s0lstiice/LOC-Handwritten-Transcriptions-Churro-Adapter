Feb 26. Sat. (until)
Crommerson has been very sick on 2nd day.
He has continued exceedingly weak in
weighting him. His appendix has also
burst - Dr. Mitchell refused to operate.
We called on him at 4.30 and cost had
a brief minutes talk with him.
Feb 27. Sunday
Bertie called us up Birmingham who said
he had heard from his Herriman and
he said yes; that he had said it was a
hasty to let that mark go out and asked
if the club was to sign it; that it was
decided to have the cycle, leave them
on it at its meeting tomorrow.
Bertie said the club will have no
connection with it, if it accepted
his P's franchise.

Recorded letter from Glass on Kent on Collett:
April 27, 1926
"Before going to Washington I called on the
President in your behalf and may say
that his attitude seemed to be very
encouraging, although he did not outright
commit himself. He assured me, on his chief
Senator Brand, about your welfare, and
I am sure I satisfied him completely
in that sense. Moreover, I tried to
empower him with the determinability of
court action in the matter. As soon as
I can conveniently come down, I will
see the President.

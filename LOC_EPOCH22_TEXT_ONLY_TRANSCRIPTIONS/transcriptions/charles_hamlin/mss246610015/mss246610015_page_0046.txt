Wednesday
attended meeting of Division of Mechanics
at 11.30. finished writing at 12.30.
2 P.M.
attended funeral of Rev. String.
a extended military ob. cannons.
He was a Senior - a Hamilton army
banquet. His place will be almost
imperishable to God.
3. P.M.
took train to Providence & train 7.45
The line from Providence to New Bedford
where I met Burtis & Collette Stiles
at 10 P.M. Ented, thing turning attended

61
Wednesday
wrote Catherine Grant a broad & butter letter.
asked her to come down some day next
week.
Wednesday
wrote Lady Annie - expressed our respect for
her to lunch with her.
Tuesday
Portia called up Catherine Grant at 8 P.M. to
see if she could come down to me this week
but she said she could not as Alexander was
off on a hunting trip & would probably return
in two days. We had not met a
letter to the Common Club yesterday, thinking
I was in Washington, she said she would
surely come down when we left there.
Wednesday
attended directors meeting of Mechanics. Portia
at noon we all went to the walkhall Inn
founded in 1806 and Robert Allen one of the
directors gave us a dinner. After dinner
he drove us about 10 miles to his
house in an old stage coach, owned by
him since, driven by some horses.

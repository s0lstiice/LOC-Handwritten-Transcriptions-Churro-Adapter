Nov 4. Sunday
The Revd. Hurd said he & his Alexander had
went to give a small dinner at the Chilten Club
and afterwards attend a Colerock in which celebrate,
music, dance etc at Roxburgh given by an old
means. incidently this is to be a Reuben
club. Catherine told me last week that
Alexander would probably vote for Homer
& Colerock, however, Catherine will be steadfast
and vote for Emmit.
In afternoon called on him Hattaway in his
Bedford and in his Havenside gate at
rainbow. They talked constantly on topics
more of Catherine than and almost
entirely between hands of Alexander. Hurd
sayd he was sufficiently Colbert &
did not appreciate his work.
Anne Hunter when this 2 weeks ago had
she saw a good deal of Alexander at
Volunt. He is curmde and thought too many
disciplinable & inconvenient.

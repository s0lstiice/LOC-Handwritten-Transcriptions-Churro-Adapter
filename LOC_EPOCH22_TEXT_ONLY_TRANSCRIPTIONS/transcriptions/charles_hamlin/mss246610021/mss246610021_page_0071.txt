At this Pattern, then Governor
left with his mess that he
begged me to hold out the promise
to Mr. Cockbe.
Aug. 24, Lord
Douglas called to get copies of his letter
to Board of April 9th giving views
for 6 1/2 rates. I told him that he must
refer to Elgin; that otherwise I had
universally given a copy to Elgin
which I returned to him request (in
his letter of 172) I did not feel
I could put to press him on a copy now deal
with his letter. Board answered ready to
do so, but I had sent a copy to
Elgin and it was too late to
determine whether he should
give it out or not.

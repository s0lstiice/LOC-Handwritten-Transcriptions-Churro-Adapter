(.1929) New York City, February 14, 1929
Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System
New York City
Dear Sirs:
I am sending you the enclosed letter from Governor
Young of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. The
letter is dated February 12, 1929.
It is a very important letter and I would like to
have it returned to me as soon as possible.
Very truly yours,
Charles E. Smith
Acting Governor
Federal Reserve Bank of New York

(.1929) New York City, February 14, 1939
Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System
New York City
Sir:
During the past week the directors of the
New York City Federal Reserve Bank have met
with Governor Young of the Federal Reserve
Bank of New York. The directors discussed
the discount rate, the Federal Reserve Board's
letter to Governor Norman of the Bank of
England, and the Federal Advisory Council's
letter to the directors.
Discount rates were also discussed.
See - Discount rates.
Feb. 2, 1929. 143
Gov. Young reported that Gov. Harding, Gov. Norris and Gov.
McDougall, and for a short time F.R. Agent Heath, were at
the N.Y. directors meeting; that Gov. Norman was not at
the meeting.
Gov. Young said he had several conferences with Gov. Norman at
the F.R. Bank, at which, I understood him to say, Governors
Harding and Norris were also present; that Gov. Norman said
the Bank of England would shortly increase its rates by 1 1/2;
that Gov. Norman would be in Washington Wednesday.
Feb. 2, 1929. 145
Miller reminds C.S.H. that Gov. Norman at lunch in Washington told
the Board he had come to pay his respects to Gov. Harrison
of F.R. Bank of New York, - never mentioning Gov' Young
or the F.R. Board.
Feb. 11, 1929. 166
C.S.H. stays in his office until 5:30 hoping to hear from the
N.Y. directors who were meeting, Eddy said, to answer Board's
credit warning letter.
Eddy told me he had heard, indirectly, that they had adjourned without
taking any action on discount rates.

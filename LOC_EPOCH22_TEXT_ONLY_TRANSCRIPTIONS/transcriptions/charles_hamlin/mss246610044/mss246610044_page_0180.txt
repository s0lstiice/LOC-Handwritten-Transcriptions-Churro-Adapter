National Grange
C.S.H. presides at dinner to, given by Carnegie Endowment at
Carleton Hotel.
C.S.H. delivers address over radio.
Under Secretary of State Clark, Gov. Young, and Louis Faber,
Worthmaster spoke.
The dinner was delicious and we had a good orchestra.
C.S.H. spoke of his farming experiences and later Faber challenged
him to a contest in milking cows.
C.S.H. said he used to milk cows but he never could stand a cow
switching his tail across his face. The audience quickly saw
his error, and C.S.H. replied there was nothing extraordinary
about that as the cow evidently was an "Irish bull".
H.P.H. invited the Mattapoisett Grange to come to the house in
Mattapoisett to listen over the radio, and C.S.H. told the
Grange they were listening eagerly to the speeches.
Unfortunately not a word could be heard!

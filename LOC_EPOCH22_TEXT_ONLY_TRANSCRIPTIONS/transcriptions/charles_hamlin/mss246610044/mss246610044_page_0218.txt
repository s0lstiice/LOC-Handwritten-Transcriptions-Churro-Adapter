Aug. 28, 1928. 19, 20
James M. Metcalf, Surveyor, marked the bounds of the sold property.
O.S.H. called up Brownell and asked him to draw deed. Give Metcalf
our certificate of title to give Brownell.
Aug. 29, 1928. 21
Dr. and Mrs. Stoddard called.
O.S.H. explained status as to title to land lying under Beacon
Street, -- the town owned 1/3 and we owned 2/3 minus 1/32.
O.S.H. said we would convey in the deed all of our right, title,
and interest in the fee under Beaconstreet between the lots
sold to him.

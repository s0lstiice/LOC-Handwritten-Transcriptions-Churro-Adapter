He does not believe Prohibition can ever be

that it can not be enforced.
He chose very quietly and nominally absent
the Roman Catholic Church, and said it
was only comparatively recently that Roman
Catholics were nominated by any official,
and that he believed public opinion would
have a more favorable attitude.
He made a tremendous confession to me by
his conduct of mind and Christian character.

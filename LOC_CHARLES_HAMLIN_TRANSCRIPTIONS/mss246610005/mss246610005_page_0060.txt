Wednesday June 9
In afternoon I am in council room and stayed 20
minutes. He talked about Kans. City Branch
bank & said one of the directors selected by
the Kans. C. Bk was a most bitter partisan
& most obnoxious enemy of President.
He said he placed the Kans. Bk well to
dominated by their disciple of April. City
as a branch bank & we ought to be very
cambul as to whom we alabanted.
He never mentioned the City Bank.
As he was going I asked him on to the
outreach of the Hitchcock- Underwood
content by Minority leader in this
Senate. He said it was very close with
the channel favoring Underwood; that
he felt bound to vote for Underwood
not only all, of his personal friends but
for him of long standing or for his
ability, but been a staunch supporter of
the Admister; that he had given great
happiness when Admister was being embezzled,
was radically opposed to many of its

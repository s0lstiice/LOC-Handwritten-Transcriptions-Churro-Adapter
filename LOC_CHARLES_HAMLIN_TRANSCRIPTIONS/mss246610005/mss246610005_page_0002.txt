Board voted to author. S.F. to embarking Edm. Elliott
(written in line of Presidt) as its vote to induce
State law to join M. system. Bd of Directors
unanimously begged us to do this. Dr Miller voted
No. said all this a great friend of Elliott he
did not appoint him a Director a salary
for any purpose.
April 4. Friday
When minutes had Miller asked to have
in ballot an explanatory col. said that
he had voted for Elliott but belief that
no director should have a salary except it.
the Board voted that this question
as to Elliott was resolved by ballot
considerate -- as to col. Elliott must
resign as Director.
I objected to Miller's voting as it was
not true as he voted against it.
Miller insisted & I then moved to remove
our vote col. was carried.
In this moved to refuse author. to allignment
Elliott.
I told Miller I was willing to leave matter
in abeyance if he was, but if he had
this decision in mind criticizing Bd. absolutely,
3
be not selling it now, I should vote to select it
now & should vote to reject the absolute.
Fairly Miller changed his mind, so that
he explained his reason for voting against
Elliott for reason that he was a director etc.
I said I had no objection to his thus
explaining his adverse vote & warned my
motion. The records were accordingly
acknowledged.
April 5. Sat.
En Ht. said Miller still insisted on ballot
into records a vote exactly like the one
obtained explaining that while he had
voted for the motion, yet he thought no
director should receive a salary etc.
En Ht. said he should at once tell him the
records were acknowledged & did not be changed;
that the Board stated that he voted for
Elliott's removal ballot etc.
I can not understand why Miller is so
persistant at baliborging our records. He
widently does not want Elliott to know
that he voted against him.
(He is a strange character!)

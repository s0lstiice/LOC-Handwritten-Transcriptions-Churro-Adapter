78
meeting on Gen. Hardings election
4.30 this P.M.
we also insisted Chairman to withdraw a
schedule showing these rate changes.
When the schedule was sent in I found
that N.Y. had set up the certificate
rates but had put up rates on bonds
secured by sub bonds & vesting notes
& had advanced 70 day commercial
paper from 4 3/4 to 5 1/4 %.
N.Y. but up home secured by sub bonds &
vest. Notes 1/4 % and 90 D corn. Baker 1/2 %
while Boston put up bearer secured by sub
bonds & vest. Notes 1/2 % and corn. 90 D
home 1/4 %.
The meeting was called on 11 am & we were
informed that the delay of money would
be late & w. us. when we got thru S. said
the Sec. had to go to the cabinet meeting
& Left B. had to go to State Dept., and as
Gen. H was away we hastened at
until 4.30.

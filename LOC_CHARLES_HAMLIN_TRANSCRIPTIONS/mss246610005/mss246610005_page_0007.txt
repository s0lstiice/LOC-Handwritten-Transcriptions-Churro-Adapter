12
May 27. Tuesday.
sent for Callis. told him for myself to consider his
negotiation & likely solve the difficulties -- said
it was a great opportunity for him.
Later see Elan came in & I told him about
it & said I thought Callis would stay.
Also said if he did I would recommend
him for Vice Fin. Comptor. See Elan on
Monday asked me if he could speak
a French, and I told Callis today that
if he made good many chances for
improvement might be obtained for him & that
I might ask him later to give him a place
in your Fin. Comptor -- I did not tell him
Elans had mentioned this.
May 26. Monday
David w. McPherson Ruddock. The Joh.
Ambassador was there & told me that
the Emperor had ordered a gold coin to
be made which he was to present to
Bertie in ceremony, w. the ceremony at
Fortierhaven last & the 26th July.
Some days ago he told me that May,
Archie and Maxwell were to be decreted
by the Emperors.

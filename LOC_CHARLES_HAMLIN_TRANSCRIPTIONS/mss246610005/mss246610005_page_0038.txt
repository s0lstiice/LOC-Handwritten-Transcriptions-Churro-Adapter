Nov 22. Sat.
went on to see Sec. Glass: told him the statement
on N.Y. trains & N.Y. steam. were absolutely
false -- that no one had advertised
out w. regard to Secretary's list. that if
anyone had I still at not have gone
to him about it. I said I was particularly
angry at N.Y. trains of care regarding &
declared we. of ill health. Glass said
when he was returning from wrote the
hathles said he was suffering from
cancer of the stomach. I begged him
to say nothing to me in respect of the
Sec list -- that all I wanted was to
assert him that I was receiving no
oil behind his back. He seemed very
much pleased that I had looked
with him as it evidently pleased him
much of the feeling that the little
House was working behind his back.
I told him that the newspaper
statements were audently imbied
and I believed some Italian trains
attending N.Y. trains was behind
the whole matter.
75
Dr Coughlin wrote me saying he had just
hurriedly a letter urging me on the
Sec list & endorsed Hurmly's reply --
a very short formal receipt & acknowledgement.
My letter. I wrote Dr C. upon Tuesday
him on his letter & stating as to balance
& the papers statements. I also praised
Selfbangerville absolutely and asked Dr. Haggerty
nothing of his politics.

Played billiards w. Sen. Sanborn, Clinton

my appointment as Sec. of Treasury:
I told him there were nothing to be done:
that I thought Selfbangerville would be
appointed, that he had made a very
good record as Chief Ass. Secy, that he
would receive a good salary and that the
only honorable citizen I had heard

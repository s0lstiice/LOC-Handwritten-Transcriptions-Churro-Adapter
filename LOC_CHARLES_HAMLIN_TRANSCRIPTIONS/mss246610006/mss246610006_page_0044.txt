85
1921
April 25
President Harding called in at White House:
asked if John Milton of N.Y. would be
good man for N.B.C.; in at same place
Presidt said will send his name to senate
tomorrow. This is first time Board has
ever been consulted about an appointment
by Presidt!
Presidt H. said Miller is a fine man and
doesn't know a damn about politics;
you Democrats run every thing & Miller
turns back of your big name every
day & wants not to retain yourself.
(Not sure who he addressed to Board is to
knowing.)
also said will be glad if honorable to
have slight reduction of rates but he
hoped Board will do nothing who will
cause any more inflation; reminded
Gov H. of his letter to him at Marine
& said his views had not changed.
Also said Sen. Smith (R.D.) was a wild
man & that farmers must have
their land just as all others.

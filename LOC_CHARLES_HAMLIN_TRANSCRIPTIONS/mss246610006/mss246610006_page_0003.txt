April 4. Sunday
Lincoln's called & stayed hour with
the District Attorney & his Willingdon.
He sent a short friend of his and
sent a letter with Sec. Colby a few days
before his departure on Sec. of State & that
Colby spoke very bitterly and contempt-
-uously about the President and his
policy.
April 6 Tuesday
Justice Higginbotham called & said this
Court had a substantial relation in
some delicate involving large oil
properties & asked me my opinion
of Delano. I strongly advised him
but felt it my duty to tell him the
President did not like him on account of
this letter he wrote 4 years ago about him
& embarking his whole family to Chicago
to vote against him. I also told him
about his own motto put over the
door of the M Board.
I told him I felt it to be my duty
to tell him all these facts and
hein said that in spite of these
facts I had convinced him to stand
for myself on our Board, and added
that in many others he could not,

Details of observation.
Lieut. Selig, consulted Dr. Chute of Boston
who examined me and said I had a
distinct enlargement of the prostate
gland; he drew off 8 oz of residual
waters. He said he would hardly feel
less saying that an observation was
absolutely necessary at this stage, yet
that if I wanted one he would not
discarded me. He told me to come
in again during the winter and he
would examine me again.

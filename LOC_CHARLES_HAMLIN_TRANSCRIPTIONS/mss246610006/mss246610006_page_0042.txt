1921
I firmly believe Harding has no serious
policy & that he will weight words
in his message, relying to build his
two Legacies & thus inconciliable
at the same time.
My Recollection is that sooner or later
the U.S. will break with England &
France and will to all intents and
purposes side w. Germany.
Compliments Churchill, wife and
daughter, twice as well with you.

& Keller then secured to raise rates on
Man. Cattle at Boston from 5 1/2 to 6 1/2.

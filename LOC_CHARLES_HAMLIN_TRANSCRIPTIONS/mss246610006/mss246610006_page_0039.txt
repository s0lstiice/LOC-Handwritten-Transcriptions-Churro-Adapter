Boston asked leave to meet in a 6% state.
I favored it but it was decided to
await their conference next week.
I called up M. Asher Currier & he
said every decision was arrested & the
vote was unanimous; that the
conditions in the District had so
imbued that this move was
deemed absolutely necessary & they did
not want to delay it a day; that
6 1/2 % would not be satisfactory; that
Brul was strongly in favor of 6 1/2 which
Reilly was opposed; that they had not
consulted w. Heiken & would, as they
assured, they would offer it buy
very heavy discount from the Denver
bank, and I assumed (Currier did not
say this) they feared it would necessitate
lowering their rates to their customers.
Currier also said a 6% vote w. U.S. at
7 would drive many customers to the
Boston banks & thereby to the Denver
bank, but they did take care of
themselves.
I feel it is a serious responsibility to
deny them, especially as wage hour
was materially reduced in N. England.

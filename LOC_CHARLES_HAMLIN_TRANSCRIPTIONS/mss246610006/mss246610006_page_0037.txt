see, Hellen at 7.30 had his first meeting
w. Board. He favored an immediate
deputy reduction of rates -- in transport the
Board charging 7 1/2 should go down to
6 3/4; he did not advocate any reduction
for the 6 1/2 rate where burden would now
charge 6 1/2.
he thought out that this subject would
in a great expansion of forces, but he
replied that this would in all probability
not balance & that forces were now at
a maximum; that he thought corn-
ration would suffing & cold blood in every
unit & that a slight decrease in our
route would leave a good psychological
effect.
he then took ground that such a
reduction would accomplish more if not
wanted until conditions improved more
than now.

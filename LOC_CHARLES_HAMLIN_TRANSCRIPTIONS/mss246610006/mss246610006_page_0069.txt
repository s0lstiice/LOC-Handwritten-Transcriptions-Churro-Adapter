During the week from to Mar. 20, a report of
N.Y. bankers came before Board and asked
to have the Bankers acceptance regulations
repealed and the bank loans
liberally paid. Mr. Kessler of the Milwaukee of N.Y.
came down with them. They wanted and
that customers were gradually turning to
sterling bills because of the harshness of
our regulations. They said foreign bankers
looked askance at our bankers bills
because they never could tell whether they
were eligible under our regulations,
Mr. Kessler agreed entirely with their
statements. The Comm. consisted of DeLong
Wauberg, Brodrick and Kent.

Dr Chute said the final operation could
be done in about two days.
Finally, after about 2 weeks he said
he was ready & Dr Justin said I was
in such skilful did consider him the
point of view of diet that he had
no fear of the result. Dr Justin however
had to go to a conventon at Atlanta
City & Dr Chute said he intended to
wait until his return - that the
delay would be beneficial rather than
otherwise, so it was worth while to
wait a week more to Berlin surgeon
& somewhat to await.
About the 3rd day after the first
operation I was able to sit up in a
chair & or whirled out onto the
terrace. Sitting up was very uncomfortable
as the tube was very irritating. Later
I began to walk - a small bottle very
sewed in under my dressing gown.
I found I could walk a long in bed
without discomfort but sitting in a
chair was very intolerant.
During all this time Sedatives - barbiturates
were given me in the day time, &
suppose to allow the irritation of the
tube, and also at night, they made
me very drowsy in the day & did
not operate very well at night.
Finally at my request they omitted
them by day, much to my relief.
My nights were awful - terrible dreams
& every 2 hours I had to be awakened
to take my water. I dreaded night
& this lasted up to my leaving the
hospital. It was very difficult
to sleep as the lung tube made me lay
in awkward position, I was bothered
with pillows which were very
uncomfortable at first but became
almost torture in the night when
my position would have to be changed.
I had two nurses - Miss Carr on the
night & Miss Hartnett for the day.
The latter was wonderful - kind &
sympathetic; the former, very able
but not very sympathetic.

1920
Mar 28. Sunday
Justice Clark of U.S. Sup. Ct told him in confidence about
the Sec. of War matter. He was very much
shocked & said there were influences
operating on President who were of great menace;
that who will or not, the President was cut
off from outside world & he saw and heard
letters not given to him through brokers
propelled success. He did not think
Webster had as much influence as
formerly, but there is different opinion
here. He thought outside influence
affected Washington tumultuously & Exaggerated.
He said he felt certain he could be
seen; he did not say directly how he
could see but he left no doubt in my
mind and that he had originally given
to Lee Baker about me & that Baker had
told him the matter was settled.
He thought present conditions were very
critical; he evidently found that the President
was not leaving a free hand, and was
not fit to have one in his present position.
He was very enthusiastic about Gen. Cox

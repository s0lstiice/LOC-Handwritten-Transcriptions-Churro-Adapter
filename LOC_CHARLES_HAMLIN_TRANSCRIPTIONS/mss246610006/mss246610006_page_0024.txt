March 3.1921
The Compt. sent another letter to the Board
announcing Platt's quietus as to whether he
had examined any Wall Street money
early June of 1820. It gave a list of
banks examined, but there were all
18 companies.
Platt stated that in 1820 quite a number
of N.Y. Wall companies favored examination
of the Compt. & he thought this was
probably the reason why he had not
examined them a second time.
At this meeting the sitting said that a
corner of the Church Bank directors could
be located that though the Board and 2
directors still is indicted for constituting
to obtain funds of the Bank on their
own credit; but that though they were in an
asylum half deminished, and the other
two had founded their magnificence,
and that the directors did not dare
to touch the matter at this time
but the insolence would inevitably
be revived.

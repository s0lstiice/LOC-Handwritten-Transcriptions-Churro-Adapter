5. This bill would provide for the unification of the banking
structure and for general control and regulation of all banks by the
Federal Monetary Authority. All banks would be required to carry
reserves equal to all deposits payable within 60 days, and 5 percent
reserves against all other deposits. In order to make sufficient
currency available for such reserves, the Federal Monetary Authority
would be required to purchase from banks and individuals United States
bonds
Government, municipal bonds, or bank assets and would be allowed
to rediscount bank assets.
6. The Federal Monetary Authority would be required to buy
and sell domestic gold and silver at a price fixed by it and it
would be allowed to trade in other gold and silver, foreign exchange,
and obligations of foreign governments.
7. The declared policy of the bill would be to raise wages and
wholesale prices (the wholesale price index of the Bureau of Labor
Statistics) to the 1926 level and to maintain full employment. When
it would be necessary to expand the currency in order to effect this
end the Federal Monetary Authority would be authorized to pay ordinary
and emergency Government expenditures by the issue of Treasury notes,
in addition to amounts elsewhere provided for in the bill. The same

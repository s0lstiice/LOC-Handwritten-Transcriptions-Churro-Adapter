8. The Federal Monetary Authority would be required to establish

of money necessary to be in circulation in demand deposits to maintain
full productivity of the individual and collective resources of the

money should be made to promote a constant elevation in the general
average standard of living as the productive capacity and
facilities of the Nation would permit.

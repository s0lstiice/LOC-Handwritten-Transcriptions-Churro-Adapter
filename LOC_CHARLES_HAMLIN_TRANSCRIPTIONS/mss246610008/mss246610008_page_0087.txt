Mr. Platt introduced a resolution at the meeting
today declaring that the Board had heard
indirectly that the directors of Messrs of
Cleveland had leased part of the
facilities of the Messrs to the Railroad. That
court for use during the Naval convention;
that in the opinion of the Board it should
not have been made as it is likely
to establish a precedent which may
brieff severe criticism upon the Messrs
of Cleveland & upon the Messrs system.
The resolution concluded by declaring that
a copy be sent to the directors of the
Messrs of Cleveland and that after the
national election, a circular be sent
to each Messrs to the end that leases
of part of the premises of the respective
Messrs shall not be made on the
use of any political committee.

It was very hard on me to say that, in view
of Miller's personal idiosyncracies, but I
thought I leaned backward, to prevent
any suspicion of personal prejudice.
Miller hates to work, but on this whole he
has done some good work. In effecting him
I have got at the gist of many serious
problems. His normal tendency is to say
No to everything; he has no idea of
martial administration. If he were not
re-appointed some Halterman would in
all probability be given the place. On the
whole, therefore, I attach most importance
to his economic fitness. Listen to his personal
inbetweens, and I know I can watch him.

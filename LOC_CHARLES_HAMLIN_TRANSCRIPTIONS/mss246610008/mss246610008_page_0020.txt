W. 24. Sunday
Mr. & Mrs John Davis and his wife,
and Theodore Lincoln of Providence
finished with us.
Davis talked very frankly as to
his chances for the nomination
for President. He said he had told
me west Va friends that he must
decidedly would not enter the
seat as a Republican candidate.
(under the laws of W. Va must often
states delegates can not be selected
by a candidate under the candidate
bill in his official papers)
He said he was not his personal
constituent counsel of H. Morgan
but that he had handled much
of his banking and commercial
interests
He frankly said he could be nominated
only as a commercial candidate;
that he was not seeking it, and
that if nominated he would
accept only as a matter of
honorable duty.

He said that during his time Johnson
wanted a boat to go abroad but
the state Dept would not give it as it
was suspected that he wanted to go
out to attempt to unseat him. He
confessed at Paris in the Mexican Civil
conflict that he would not attempt it
and a boat was given him and his
attorney; that he sailed from Halifax
and on the day of sailing he gave out
an interview that he was going abroad
for this very purpose; that he told
Wilson that he would not return to
the United States until he was
assured that he would be called to him
to assist him in his work.
He said he would not go abroad
as a candidate for President because
he would not be able to give the
time required for such a campaign.
He said he would not go abroad
as a candidate for Vice President
because he would not be able to
give the time required for such a
campaign.
He said he would not go abroad
as a candidate for Senator because
he would not be able to give the
time required for such a campaign.

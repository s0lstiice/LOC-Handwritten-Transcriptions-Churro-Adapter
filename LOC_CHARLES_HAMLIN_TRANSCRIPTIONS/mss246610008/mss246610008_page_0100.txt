June 17
received letter from Presdt Clark of Union of
N.C. Carolina promising my address to the
State and enclosing a check for $2000 for
expenses etc., at once replied in a polite note
stating the check saying I never have accepted
a cent, even for expenses, but a necessity, an
invitation from him. I considered a demand.
Perhaps this may cause the Trustee to solicit
that State in another way in which they
could have helped me for my time and
trouble. I never expect, however, to hear from
them again.

Mr. 4. Sunday
at 7 P.M. being Bache called out in telephone from
Cleveland Ohio; said he had been requested to act
as temporary post bearer at the Cleveland funeral
but nothing said about Ladies, and that he
would not, of course, come in unless she was
represented. He asked if I would use remuneration
if Ladies were represented. I said it would be
better to ask Bolling. He hesitated somewhat
as to this, and asked if I could use Cleveland
number. I said yes and he then asked
me to ask Bolling, and wait him.
I called out Bolling who said he did not
know but will go up State St. and ask him
Wilson. I told him Wilson said as the chair was so
limited Ladies were not expected.
I said to Bolling - why not have me with
paper surely that the best information
I can get is that on account of the limited
space, Ladies are not represented, or that
Mr. Bolling says this (I made this myself
writing to Shields Mrs. W. Dunn being quoted
directly.)
Bolling seemed rather annoyed & said,
almost tranquilly, say anything you please,

Mr. 5. Sunday
Dr. Wyoming gave to the associated press a copy
of the list of honorary pall bearers and
my name did not appear on it! I am
at a loss to understand this, but assume
it was some mistake.
I called up Wyoming & he said it was a stupid
error, that my name was the first
one he ever mentioned.

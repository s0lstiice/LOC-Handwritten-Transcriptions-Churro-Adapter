June 7.
Conyn adjourning & the Fiddler will failed.
tried w. Gen. Duncan. sat beside wife of
Egyptian Minister. Had good talk with
her about Egypt. She said she enjoyed
the subject with me immensely and asked
me to send her letter to HPH.
CH asked how to address her (she is the
wife of King Isad).
She said to use simply Madame; that
she found that she must make calls
here which she never did in her own
country; that she wrote the King who
whilst that of this was the custom she
should conform to it but not to use
her letters.
Has slept on N.Y. this am. She said the
Revely agent at Albany said he would be
glad to sign a contract with her for
the Revels stage!
CH is spending Sunday with Mrs J.P.
Watson at Highland Falls, N.Y.
News Agency of London is also visiting
them.

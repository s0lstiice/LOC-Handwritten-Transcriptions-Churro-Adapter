Jun. 2, 1861.
Dr Hiller asked the Board to give him Mr. Reeve
agent Butler of the Mass. of Genl F. a leave of
absence with bull pay in order that he
might go with the Robinson of Los Angeles to
attend the meetings of the Reformation Comm.
the Robinson was invited, with Em Davies,
and another by the Reformer Comm., but state
debt having no authority of locality to take
part in it. Cott objected 1) No authority to
attend an organization of the Methodist in any
part of any such purpose; 2) Congress barred a
law forbidding the President to send any money
abroad without direct authority of Congress
3) Hiller insisted we could send Breck
arranging some other nominal purpose!
For C went to see Sec. Hiller and returned
saying that he did not object to, that
the cabinet had considered the matter
and were most scrupulous in their decision
that nothing should be done which might
embarrass the Adams.

Estlund Meyer called me up and said she was
making a return visit & she wanted me to act as
one of the executors and also, as I remember,
the chandlers Hall, was now an executor

with my duties on the Board. I advised her
simply to appoint a Trust Co. as executor and
in addition any friend she desired.
I then called up Mr Hall, who some time
ago told me she was an executor, and told
her of about talk with Estlund and she was
very gratified.
Then sat among I turned with her MacVigil

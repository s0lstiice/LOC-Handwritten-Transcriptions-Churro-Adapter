Wednesday
President Wilson died at 11.15 am. He had
laid in a bed from about 2 weeks ago and it
was only a question of time.
In the Arm Belfor and I went to St James
church to hear an eulogy by Kennedy,
one of the Kings Chalilians priests. He
lunched with us on Saturday. It was the
ablest sermon I ever heard; in fact, as
the old old said a tribute to President Wilson
which, by eloquence and oratory surpassed
anything I have ever heard. He said Wilson
went to his cross visibly on died from
Christ for the Peace of the world &
that there misguided persons who
threw him with no ability defying
God, himself.

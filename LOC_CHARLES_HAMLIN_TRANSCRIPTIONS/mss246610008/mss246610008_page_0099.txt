The Trustees of the University are all elected
by the Legislature.
What I can not understand, however, is
that they did not wish or intend to put
me a degree, in which they could have
invited me to deliver the Commencement
Address, -- the sole speaker.
They went 2,500 present; my address
was received with great applause and
many of the Trustees warmly congratulated
me.
In the evening at Raleigh, Mr Daniels
had Judge Clackson and Ex-Judge Bibb (N.)
at supper and they both warmly

June 12. Thursday
Friend w. Rutledge began; her daughter Hall
and her daughter were also there.
I asked her Hall for the facts as to what
Rutledge said about her visit. (Suffolk, June 17, 1777)
Mr Hall said he went told her that she had
seen Bancroft in connection with the occasion of
her visit to her Willem. To talk over the
permanent burial place of Mr. Willem.
Mr Hall said he went evidently did not
know she was a cousin of Mr. Calhoun.
Mr H. said later she saw Mr. Calhoun
who told him he had not seen her before
or any woman in Washington for a year!
I at once said that Rutledge told me
he went said she had gone out to the
cathedral w. her Willem & Mr. Calhoun.
Rutledge said, Yes I said that and it was
not true but what he went said was
very true but what he went said was
made no difference!
Rutledge began in a dangerous malingering!
Mr Hall also said that he went said
later some one said she was practically

April 30
The Board received word that N.Y. Commissioners
at Ministers meeting today to lower rates
to 4 1/2 to take effect at 3 P.M. today.
The Cyc. Com. satisfied them.
CW calls up Gen Harding in Boston. He said
he had heard from, and asked if this
was the result of the hearing of the
Board as communicated to the N.Y. Board.
CW said No, we merely said that if the
selectors in their judgment ordered
and asked authority on their selection
the Bd will adjust it, and that they
should also to Boston & Phila.
Gen Harding is Board which object of Boston did not
act on several days yet.
CW said No, would not object.

Gen H. then asked if the Board observed their

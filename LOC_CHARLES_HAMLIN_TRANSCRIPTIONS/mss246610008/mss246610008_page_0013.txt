Let me have the following facts:1. Theodore

3. His wife would state in the morning.
4. Theodore had much to do in the

The various dates above should be

I am satisfied that Swelain gave Archie
his position to secure Theodore's favor
and 7. in asking him to do so so,
committed an act of very questionable
property, to say the least.
I think also that Sinclair had given

wife and that she said to him and that
she did not know why, or not to return
them.
The broken state that the above facts
conclude will prevent Theodore from
being nominated for Gov. of N.Y. I should
blunt they would!
I feel that the conduct of Theodore
is just as responsible as that of
Denver.
This week the Senate passed a motion
demanding Denver's resignation. Coolidge
whisked that was the business which
is true, but Coolidge strongly accepts
the entire responsibility for returning
Denver.
I think the Republican still merely have
slated the opinion of the Senate that
in view of the great charges against
Denver he should at once resign, even
assuming that in fact he was not
guilty of any positive misconduct.

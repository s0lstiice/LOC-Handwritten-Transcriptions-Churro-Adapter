June 3. 1861.
Dear Sirs:
I am sending you, under separate
envelope, a copy of the distinguished
article entitled "The Constitutional
system". I am sure you will find
it most interesting reading.
I enjoyed the dinner last evening
immensely, but my finger was somewhat
tremulous, when, in the way from
Berlin, I pointed out that
while one of my seats was black,
the other was brown, and, worst
still, the brown seat had a quite
incredible hole in it! Bertie
said every one must have noticed
it, but I doubt for a moment whether
you did, as you told me of
discussing higher things.
Ever truly yours,
With
entire special mensong.

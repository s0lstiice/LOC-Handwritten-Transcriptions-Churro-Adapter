Gen Davis sent today that in 1923 after he
obtained control of the bonds, when the Board was
divided in subject of California bonds
Baruch & Co & Madden called to
confrontate him; that later the aides called
in him and correctly advised in favor of
branch economy which by then Board of
States; that later Executive called in him
and said that Madden had told him
that he would not have any respect, that
he would not have him around
$5000000; that he would have him to Madden
but the aides could not persuade
him (James).
James also said that Executive tactically
asked him whether he would use his
voice in the Senate.

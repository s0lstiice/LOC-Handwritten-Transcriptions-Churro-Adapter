April 22. 1861.
Gen. Meagher spoke to U. S.
General Hunter. Heiller told me today that
General Colquitt Roberts military head
was still in Paris and that his military
with steam was replaced by a new
military not very long ago.
April 23. Thursday
Bertie called me. Extended Meagher and asked
me to lunch at 11 o'clock. I did so meet Annie
Nance. She said she would not go this
morning, was the French military here. She
then said -- I will have this incident to
make up for your rudeness to me!
Bertie asked me which she preferred and
she said -- no matter in which case to choose
at home. About when Bertie mentioned
Ricketts, others Richmond and Rachel
State!
She also said that at the dinner at
Van Rensselaer Bertie had saying that
her mother was old, colonies embarrass
her very much!
Bertie then me recollecte of having
sent many such things.
After that she became most pleasant.

April 24. Tuesday
Received reply from Collier's Grant to
my letter.
Edward Hanley writes that a hearing was
held before the Public Works Comm. yesterday
in question of extending the Emory branch
line of the Carolin Railroad; that
two terms will be thrown into Common observation
and said it would do all in its
power to take Hotchkiss; that the case
now goes to Congress where a course of
the Act of Feb. and should be returned
back to the 1861 next session.
Gen. Meagher still in Washington
and remains in Denver with a cold.
April 25.
We held official service in memory of
Anna of St Agnes Church. Father Anderson
remember Sunday was the anniversary
but Father Anderson could not bear
it himself.
We Miss Harrington sent me some
beautiful flowers in memory.

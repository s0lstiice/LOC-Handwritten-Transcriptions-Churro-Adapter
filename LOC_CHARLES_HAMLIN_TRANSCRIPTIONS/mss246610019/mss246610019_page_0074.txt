July 29, 1872
Council asked Sir Elan who said he hoped
would call on him at 2.30 and he agreed
and to come at 5. Sir Elan had not told
me he had made this advertisement.
In 1st place Sir Elan was with me in
Glens at the Railway, Sir Stewart
called up and said that a new
plan was under discussion of half
credit to Bk of England by Bk of Ireland
& The System; that Bk of Ireland could
have 25 millions of sterling but we were
obliged to take much more; that
his division could vote enormous debts
on us and he would advise us.
On way to Sir Elan I met Sir Raleigh
and told him of Sir H. Macnaghten.

Dec 28. Monday
Served as one of Comr at dinner to
celebrate birthday of Woodrow Wilson at
Mayflower Hotel. Delance crowded.
Dr Sam Cleat and Nathan Baker spoke.
The address was very fine, especially
Cleat's.
Dr Miller was there!
(About 20 cents)
Sam Wilson said he originally asked Sam Paul
or to Miller's solicitor and Paul recommended
him as an independent, not even suggesting
that he was a Democrat, etc. Sam Wilson
believes he is most decidedly not.

Dec. 31. Thursday.
at 3 P.M. Rev. Slings and Rev Norman came
before the Board and Rev N. answered questions
and talked for 1 1/2 hours.
Rev N. said that unemployment was still bad
in England this is a little improved; that there
were 1,200,000 out of work; that normally,
however, there always had been services
unemployed; that emigration was still
only partial; that most of those who were
out of work went not bit to emigrate;
that it cost about $200 per man.
He said there were a point signs of industrial
revival, and that other food prices were:
1. the successful maintenance of the
gold standard at rates not over 5%
when everyone predicted 7 or 8 1/2%
2. The Panama Canal
3. The settlement of the North Sea oil
barrenberg oil
4. After other things, the coal situation was
bad.
He said prices had fallen only a little
since the gold standard was established,
but that they must fall later.

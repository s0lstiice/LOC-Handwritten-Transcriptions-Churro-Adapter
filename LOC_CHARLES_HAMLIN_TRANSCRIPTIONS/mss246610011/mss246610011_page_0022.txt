42
Sat. Nov 21
Randolph called me up about 4 P.M. to say
that Mr. Balling had died early in the
morning; that they were going to Laythwaites
Va. tomorrow & that he would be in
Manhattan
I at once wrote Collett a note and brought up
some flowers to her. I saw Randolph
but only for a minute.
I then telegraphed Berthe at Albany.
Wed. Nov 24.
Bertrand wrote about 8 o'clock that night
dated Nov. 2.
Collett replied that he had heard
to an interview with Secretary of
the Treasury regarding the sale of
the United States bonds to the
United States Government for
the sum of $100 million, and offered
to sell the United States bonds
for $15 per bond.
Lord. Sutton, Platt & Cunningham - Angl
colt. Rev C. J. Smith N.O.
43
Platt moved that it was sense of Board
that the 38 millions of Govt. Sec. be allowed
to run off.
Colt opposed as a substitute a motion that
the Govt. should be given time to report as
of Nov 2, but decided on immediate meeting
of the Genl. Staff Council to consider whether
change could be made without calling
a change in Genl. Staff officers.
The vote carried, all voting in favor
of Platt.
Then Platt moved that question of raising
off of the 38 millions be one of the
questions considered at meeting with
the Council. voted unanimously
Harriet Hamlin wrote me that she is
to be obligated when Sat. for fall
studies.
Wed. Nov 25
Colt writes Pursuit confidential stating address
of Austin Chamberlain and Lord Cecil in
the World Court, delivered between British
club, Sept 7, 1925. (see Sec. Bk for reply
of letter.)

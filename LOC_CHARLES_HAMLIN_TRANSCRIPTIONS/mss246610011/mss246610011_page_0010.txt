19
Andrew was very bitter towards the Adams on
not giving better terms to France. He said he was
ashamed of our country's attitude in demanding
augmented, at least of the two armaments
debt. He showed me an interview he
intended to give out and asked my opinion
of it. I told him, without expressing any
opinion on the merits, it would certainly
be regarded as a blow in the face of
the Adams and that he should not
publish it without the full knowledge
that it would be so regarded.

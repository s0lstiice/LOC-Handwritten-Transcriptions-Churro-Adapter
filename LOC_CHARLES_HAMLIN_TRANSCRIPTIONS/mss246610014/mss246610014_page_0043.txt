1100-24. Thursday
We took Thanksgiving dinner with Haines
and Jane at Harman.
Nov 25 Tuesday
We lunched with Catharine Grant to celebrate
the birth day, one of the children said it
was her 38th. In Delano came in from
Washington, leaving Washington dinner on
route with Col. Ching in Carver.
After dinner I tried Catharine's voice -- it is
a low contralto. I played over my barrel
volume on till to look out.
I also sent her several pictures of Anna
on till to cheer her up, one to be colored
as a Christmas present to Berthe.
She exhibited my own color booklet as well
as her pictures. She is to smuggle it in
in the telegraph she is rebuilding
Nov 26
In Delano and Catharine went to lunch
here but Mr. J. came down with

Nov 27. Sunday
Left at 12.00 am for New Bedford. leaving 12.45 on
Boston. at 3. went for lunch to Chilson club
where we met Mr Henry Spurr and Mr
John C Gray; then we drive to West Hills
to look at the tremendous over Amass park.
then we drive back & called on Rev Many.
Young House were there and said he had
joined the Steel club -- he was elected into
the DKE in the second group.
We then called on Belk. Laurence and then on
the Horatio Curtis and then on Dick in less
apartment.
Berthe was to dine w Mr Many & I look at the
Federal to wash.
Nov 30 will
have 1.05 train for N.Y. with Delano, to
attend Carnegie Institute meeting. Went to Union
Club. In many had telegrams call on the
Hansens.

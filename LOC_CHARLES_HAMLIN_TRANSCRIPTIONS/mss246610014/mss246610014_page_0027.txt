Sent 18. Sunday
We went to church at St Peters. The congregation had
a brief tribute to Rev & Mrs Prenger and them
formally dedicated his time. When I thought
of our wedding time & of the many sentiments
connected w. Albany, the time felt like to my
eye. In the afternoon we had a pleasant
call on Robert & Miss Prenger.
At seven we dined with the Rees and met
Miss Rev & Miss Willis of Balt.
We then went to the Capitol and sat
in the balcony & listened to George
Washington at the dedication of the new
Lincoln Hotel. The music was
wonderful, and the Rees have done
wonderful work in building the whole
thing through.
Left Albany in mid-night time.
Sent 19. Sunday
Sent 2 hours w. Rev H and cousin at
Washington. Cousin set us had heard a
rumor that Mr Willis was responsible
for the strike in the Chicago train
wh. caused the major. At the Board
to institute Tim Chil. 3 1/2 % rate.

May 3. Sunday
The Nat. Pen Club gave me 6 tickets for a tripartite
performance of two new movies belong "Lemoy".
We invited Rachel Hale & Estelle Doyle.
Rachel Hale & Estelle Doyle danced a minuet at the
Women's Den Club. Rev H transmitted the motion.
The performance was long but wonderful.
Miller has been testifying before H.R. Bannery
& Civil Court. He attacked them that
appraisers & made them think the trust
always obliterated them.
I shall leave into the record of them in
the inconveniences.
He now told the Court the trust on the Record
was to render calm as against the
regretta of two other Members.
May 6 Sunday
We called on Rachel Hale.
May 7 & 8
Miller continued testimony.
May 8.
We carried a motion & send it to Delano who
begged to come to the Pen Club dinner.

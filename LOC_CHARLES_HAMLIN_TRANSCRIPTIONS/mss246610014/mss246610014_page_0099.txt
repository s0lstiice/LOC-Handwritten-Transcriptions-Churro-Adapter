July 28
Ned comes in late. He is made absent with Patten
on next Wednesday in Berlin.
Rachel Hall writes me a charming letter on to EastIndia.
found it at Common Club. July 20 in my return to
Westward.
July 29.
Ned & I went home well on a conference with
West. Civil Co.
Catherine had come over with the Kostas
she made of us. and of course was very fond
but the necessity of her came very soon.
we have talked with Catherine Friend
and she sends me well wishes to her mother
to Calcutta. July 4th.
We just missed the train. not with a
little strain to Shields & them exchanged on
Washington.
July 30
Readed work. 11 am
W3. Rie W, Rosamond, the Times and Auditors
west Berlin.

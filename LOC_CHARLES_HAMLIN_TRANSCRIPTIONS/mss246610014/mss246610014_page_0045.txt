Dec 7. Wed.
We drove out to see Col. Philip Helm at Talenew
Park Sanitarium. The weather in very good
shape.

Dec 8. Monday
We took with us assistant of Civil C. directors and Mr.
Winston and Heath and superintendent of stores where
he was and not exhibited. All were named as
unanimously except that Willis asked to be
voted as not voting and said he would
give a new prompt vote for not
voting later.
Dec 9. Tuesday
at Charlotte N. C. we arrived at 8.40 and went to
the train. We arrived at 10 am we went to
the Bank and attended first meeting of
directors of the Branch followed by a
meeting of the Richmond Board held
there instead of at Richmond. Then lasted
until 1.15. at 1.30 the evening train
put on a lunch at the hotel. About 50
were present & cost delivered an address.
The preceding officer felt him strong
on his right and cost on his left!

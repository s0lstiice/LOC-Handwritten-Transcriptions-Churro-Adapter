TOPIC - DESIRABILITY OF UNIFORM PROCEDURE
IN HANDLING TELEGRAPHIC TRANSFERS
OF BANK BALANCES IN ROUND AMOUNTS
FOR ACCOUNT OF NONMEMBER CLEARING
BANKS OVER COMMERCIAL WIRES AT
EXPENSE OF SUCH BANKS.
DALLAS
Governor McKinney explained that under the proce-
dure now in effect, Federal Reserve banks are prohibited from
handling telegraphic transfers of funds to, or from, nonmem-
ber clearing banks. He also stated that in the opinion of
the Dallas bank, permission to make these transfers would be
a great convenience to nonmember banks, and that he felt they
should be granted the same privileges with respect to tele-
graphic transfers of funds as was accorded to member banks.

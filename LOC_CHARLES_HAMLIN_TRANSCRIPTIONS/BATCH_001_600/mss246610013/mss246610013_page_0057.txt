This morning another letter came from
his lawyer in Baltimore:
1st March. 1927
My dear Charlie
I am sorry if my letter of
the 25th hurt you, but nothing was built up
from my thought; and I keep at that one
branchline rested when to begin a foundation
that it could stand forever.
You asked me to read your last three
letters to Mrs Penniston saying your mind
have been "greatly misinterpreted". It was
after reading these three letters that I
wrote you as I did; so you must see
that what I said were based entirely on
your own statement.
Mrs Penniston has just telephoned me
that the matter has been closed between
you and her lawyer; so we need not

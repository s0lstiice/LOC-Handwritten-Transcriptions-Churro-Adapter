Aug 26. Received telegraph letter from Mrs Wilson,
Aug 29. Friday
He will call me up from N.Y. for Rev. Strong, says
Wilson Young has called asking of Mr. Jay,
now in England, can remain there a few weeks
as he wishes to consult with him. To Mrs's question
call said Jay would act absolutely unofficially
& could not and would not break the Mob
in the system in the slightest degree. Call said
Rev C. was willing. Mrs told call that if the
President authorized Jay to remain over unofficially
he, U.S., would offer no objection.

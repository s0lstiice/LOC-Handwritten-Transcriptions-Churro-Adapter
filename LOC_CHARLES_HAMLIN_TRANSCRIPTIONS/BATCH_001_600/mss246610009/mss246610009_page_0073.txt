Will told me the Southshanger and railing at
the Bethel Church were finished and only the
courtesy remained; that even suggested moving
the body of the President at once but that she
preferred to wait until everything was
finished.
I told her I was more and more distressed
at the thought of leaving the body permanently
interred there; that Oliver and his family
would soon disabuse while the Cathedral
would so in forever.
She said she felt exactly as I did.

had informal conference w. Council of Bank Directors
of Bank Irving, Archer & Wood. Irving presented a motion
of proposed agreement w. Bank of England to use
as soon the correspondent and Agent of said Bank of
N.Y. in purchasing for, bills of exchange in ten
or 14 of Oct. He explained that if we appointed a
joint stock bank we would have no assurance
that sums to credit of said New Banks would be
paid, as this would require a guarantee
from Bank of Eng. who, today abroad, controls England's
gold supply, and that in it would be better
to appoint the Bank of England directly.

immense influx of cotton, wheat & wholesale

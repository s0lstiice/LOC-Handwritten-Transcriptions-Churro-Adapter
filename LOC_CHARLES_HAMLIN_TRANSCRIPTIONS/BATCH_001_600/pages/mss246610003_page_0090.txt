June 28 cont'd.
String. Reb. printed articles published at my request
by Guillaume Hunt, at direction of Sec. Lanning
criticizing pamphlet of Dr Martin Pencil
attacking Credit Wilsons foreign policy,
David W. Cuyg. Pardee, Sec. Lanning, in my
saying the Catholic vote was against
Wilsons one, of Mexican policy, said this
was the assuried name but that real
name was a secret agreement with
Germany to give the Pohle lumbered
jurisdiction of Germany over the war.
Said he did not know this but was
satisfied it was so. He added he had no
doubt at proper time Cannon could
make to accord better treatment to
Catholics but that we had better wait
until nearer the election to do this.
Said also he wanted to hasten the
reputation of Cannon but it did not
be done.

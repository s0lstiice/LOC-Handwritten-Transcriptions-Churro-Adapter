Aug 8. Tuesday
Sec U. said he would let me in on about 11:30 AM.
I forgot to note at the time that Delano said
when Smith Bell gives authority to Bel to change the
Respective votes was considered by Bel to be treasonable
that he had worried Miller for ten volumes. I am
assured to see him reply & Delano should have
a telegram from Miller saying that he
absolutely favored the Bel. Delano never
laid the Bel. as to this and the telegram was
never shown us.

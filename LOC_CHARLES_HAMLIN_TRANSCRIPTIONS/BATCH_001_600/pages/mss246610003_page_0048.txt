19 Nov 1860
Write told me since then that there had been a consultation
with Judge Daniels & Miller (not in Council) with his letter;
but Cartin advised them that he thought it better
to create new districts up to 12 census w. it being to
present existing districts down to 8; that ought not to
be, but have election for 20 years was a thin
point, and that that should the County elect or
abolished to 8 it would be 3 years before such.
It did finally settle the matter; he also said
that in his opinion the grounds stated by Council for
indisputing were very weak and unconvincing,
and that it could be fully to attempt to change
districts under the Act was practically unavailing
in the matter.
Thus it appears that a majority of the Bel has
had practically a caucus w. Cartin w. and letting
the other 3 members know anything about it.
while the Court did clearly consult w Council
to call in Miller also was a most extraordinary
overruling. I asked Wellman if he did not
know of any such caucus nor was I
well notified or given an opportunity to consult
Cartin.
Yesterday we sent a stiff letter to the ultimate
Boe board on Brodhead's about. Today Miller
and Brodhead's told him he did not consider
anything in about all indicated a serious
undertake, as the Baker might be liquidating
& if so it would letter disagree with us to the
end of the present term of our redistricting.
Miller said that the Bel ought to take a weak
brand of beer. It had good baker, but that
would disappear & that would also elude the Bel
Boe.

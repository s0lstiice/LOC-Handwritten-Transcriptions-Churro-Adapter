11
May 20. Tuesday
Cates telephoned well believe not to call on
Sel. unless Sel. wrote and asked him to
come. I said I could not understand
why L. did not go direct to Selig & show
how he was being thwarted by Crossley
& Shawell. Cates said L. had been told
he must do all his business w. Crossley
& Shawell.
I said at such a delay one in Treasury for
Labour etc. can see Elans any time
on request of matter so out of importance
& asked if L. had ever made such a
request & had been refused. He said he
had but she had no instance.
I told all this to Elans.
I am puzzled to think that Elans letter
to Dudley (see Sec. 12k) was ill timed;
he wrote for him by Cowley. Can not
agree w. Elans that L. letter to him was
obscure; it was couched in technical
language & merely stated facts.

98
Had very bad cold in head, at least all
day w. some remission.
Dec 11.
Monday.
at home all day. Still remembered
in H telegraphed me that every one
conceded now that I would be the
next Secretary - that one of Leffingwell's
launched supporters - Ray Baker the
Director of the Mint - would have
to be appointed. Baker is a great friend
of immutability & he must have got this
news from him.
Dec 12
Wednesday.
at home all day but remission
normal.
Dec 13 Sat.
at home all day. In evening gave dinner
to Mr Whitelaw Reed; present Justice Olles
Colby - Miss Boardman. Gen. Oliver Ellsworth,
Mr Low & Mr Ellsworth (son of Judge Ellsworth).
99
after dinner L. of D. was discussed. I said the
Senate had relinquished every obligation in the
covenant of L. of D. & relegated it to all
other matters to future action of Congress in any
particular case, by Act a joint resolution
- thus, in effect abdicating its real
making Power and leaving it over to
the action of future Congress. To my surprise
Justice Colby almost violently threw
our country from, attacked the League
comments & defended the resolutions.
I said as it should now the Senate
said you I don't think so & that the
most charitable view of its performance
was that in effect it was an absolute
abdication to voting.
Justice Colby, however, thought there was
no inconsistency between the obligations
of the covenant and the resolutions.
He said the U.S. has no constitutional
power to bind future congresses to go to
war. I at once replied we had done
this on to Panama, Nicaragua and all
of America (Minor hostilities). He replied
that we had an interest there. I said
so have we in Mexico for many years.

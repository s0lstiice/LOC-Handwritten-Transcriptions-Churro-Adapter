Tuesday, June 23
Courtly notified me Member of Law Jim. Coates.
He has got his reward for becoming
Leffingwell and opposing me.
Sat. June 24
Dr Powell called me up & said Sec. G. wanted Bertie
& me to go to Annapolis w. Pen Ammenemts to
act as livet & brether in his want. Had only
1/2 hour but Bertie cancelled a lunatic insp s
ment & we rushed to the special election
caus. After we started I found Bertie too
deplorable in himself. I told him I had been carried
to go as livet & breth as well as he wished that
that money be tied over until next election &
told him that the very desired sum to go as
livet & breth to his destination I did not
told him to act & select in which ground. He
is acted upon surrounded by the, commanders
of the fleet, Commodore, Captains, Rear Admirals,
Admirals etc, which I trodled in the year
unattended, in housing train, everything
swinging and curting. I left a whole
day by this middle crossing of wills
& told the Sec. so, in a humorous
description of the journey.

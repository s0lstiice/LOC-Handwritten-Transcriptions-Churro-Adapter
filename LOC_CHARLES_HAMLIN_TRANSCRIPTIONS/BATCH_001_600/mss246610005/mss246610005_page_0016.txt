He said he thought Wilson ought to
help Roberts over this issue by
some revolutionary interpretation of
his moral doctrine, turning to
allies he normally did not think
his necessary.
He said letter is nothing about Wilson
but I am certain that he does not like
him.
This speech was a strong one. He said
however the U.S. should have continued
the war sooner.

Had long letter with Miller. To my acquaintance
he said he had no doubt and never had
any against property of our sending abd
munitions of war; also that he had no
doubt that acceptance for war expenses
were eligible for indirect by the Reel board.
This seems to be absolutely reverse of the
position at N.Y. meeting of June 18th
shown by my letter to Delano prior
to N.Y. meeting which, let us say, was shown Miller
accented to by him before study.
He also addressed multiliterate class in
Germany - related experiences of discounting
by Henry, officer - attacked the Jefferson
the landed genius - said the military
claims & the industrial magnates had
joined hands. Said his sympathites
were more w. Daniel than with Germany.
I was perfectly amazed at this letter.

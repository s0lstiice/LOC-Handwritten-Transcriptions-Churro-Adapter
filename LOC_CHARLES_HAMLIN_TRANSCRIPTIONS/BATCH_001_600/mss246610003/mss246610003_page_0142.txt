Harding told me that he would not admit
that the circular dated July 26 of his bank stating that
his resignation should be accepted at once in settlement
for service, however was authenticated. I asked him
if he were not aware of this & he earnestly replied
that the fact was on his mail box list. He deliberately
declared not.
Harding also said he feared trouble over his
designate; that his associates wanted
relatives but had put none relative. He
then exhibited a wanted. He said he had
written Delano & Warburg for a conference
Sunday in N.Y. He added that Warburg
was so virtuous he found trouble.
I told Harding my room was ready for
him & that I looked he could meet
in San Francisco. He suggested that I
take w. me the pictures of Dean & N.H
senators & I said I should be glad
to do this.
He added that it was his desire to keep
down all difficulties in the Board until
after the election; that out of loyalty
to the President he felt it his duty to
act out the law whether the associates
might feel, but he seemed very much
disturbed.

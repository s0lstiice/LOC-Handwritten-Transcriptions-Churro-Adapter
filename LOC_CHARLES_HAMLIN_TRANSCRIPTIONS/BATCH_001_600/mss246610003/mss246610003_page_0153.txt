Rich. Hines Agent wrote me a letter saying his letter
of July 16 was not intended as a criticism
of either myself or the Board, but merely to
inform us of the current lots, etc.
Told Miller of this who said he would at
once send his letter to Rich saying he cannot
withdraw his letter to me & Harding as he had
suggested. Miller strongly urged me to
agree to this and I finally said I would
& could not answer Rich's letter until
he had replied to Miller's letter.
Miller said unless Rich's letter was withdrawn
I would feel obliged to dismiss him

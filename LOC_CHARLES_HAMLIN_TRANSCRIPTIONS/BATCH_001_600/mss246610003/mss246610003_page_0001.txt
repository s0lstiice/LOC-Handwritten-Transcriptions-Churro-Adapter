1915.
June 17.
Williams told me the other day that Sec. Madden
had decided to raise some more Aldershot Ireland
notes to a seven bank. He said he thought
this a great mistake. I agreed. To my mind
it is a torpedo fired at the National Reserve
act and will encourage Congress to extend
this act again which would be tantamount
to a substitution of the Nat. Res. Act.
February.
Nothing official to write about except that
Sec Madden at one time wanted to force
Richmond to raise a lower rate. The
other members have the ground that
although their power was clear it should
only be exercised after careful consultation
with the bank.

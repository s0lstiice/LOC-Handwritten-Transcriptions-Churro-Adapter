Pan American Financial Congress

May 23. Sunday we gave lunch to Madame &
Miss Aldao the wife & daughter of Dr Aldao
a delegate from Argentina. Rebuffed to whom
our Ambassador Sherman gave out a
letter. Dr Aldao & I went to a lunch at
the August. Ambassary but later came back
to our lunch.

May 27.
Berke gave a lunch to the ladies of the
foreign delegates. Mrs Begun, Mrs Hamilton Mrs
Burkson and others were present.
May 25. Tuesday.
I gave an address in the Nat. Rev Act to
the Convention. Received w. great applause
and is to be printed in Spanish &
English.

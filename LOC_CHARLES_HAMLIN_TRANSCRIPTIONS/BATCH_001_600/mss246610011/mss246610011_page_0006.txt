Sat Sept 19

Sunday Sept 20
drove from Ditz to Verdun -- about 40 miles -- and
returned, went over Fort Vaux and saw the captured
bridge.
Left 4.20 for Luxembourg -- where we arrived
at 6.30. Hotel Branau -- very comfortable.

Monday Sept 22
I called on Mr. Philpotts in U.S. and made offer
for 3.30. He asked me both to leave the U.S.
and P. at 5.
He sent me a two months to leave a card in
the name of U.S. Lt. of Belgium. He said if I wanted
to see him personally he would be glad to arrange
for a meeting. He said he could not speak
English, so I said I would merely leave a
card out of courtesy. I drove down and
got my card to a door keeper who asked me
to leave a seat in the reception room. I told
him I did not want to trouble the Gen
but merely to have my respect. In a few
minutes a sentimen came in, -- probably his
secretary, who said the Gen asked him to
sing that he was just going to an important
conference & requested extremely he could not
see me now, but would be delighted to
see me at any time preferable to me
leaving. I explained that I was
leaving early for Antwerpen. The Gen. was
very agreeable and said he was in a
meet with the Belgian debt comm. to
Washington.

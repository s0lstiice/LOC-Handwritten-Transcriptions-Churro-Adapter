I then called on Mrs Andrews carnegie who was

wonderfully confirmed the & she believed very

to have a good talk with me.
at 8 P.M. we dined with Edith; she was
alone as Miss Bancroft & Miss Johnson
were dining out. We went over the writings
& letters of the Evill and she really
seemed informed. We explained to her
about Hindrick Bryan letters to her
about Anna, and she did not seem a
bit disturbed by them. She told us some
unshakable stories about their troubles
with servants in Denver. She said she
would probably leave Denver next
Monday for Paris as the leave of absence
was only for one month. She
said she probably would be back in
Washington by the last of October and
begged Bertie to stay with her until
she settled as to an apartment or
hotel.

Andrew said he was the officer who wrote
on Harriet's men-o'-war, criticizing the
admiral for the conduct of the crew; that he
changed his clothes 2 or 3 times each day;
that when working in deck he slept on a
berth that was cold; that he often went
to bed at night and the breast was covered with
misture of urine!
Monday Sept 28
A clear cold morning. Moderate rain.
Clean sun shining day but very cold.
Tuesday Sept 29
Very stormy - heavy sea - canvas all around decks.
In afternoon became clean & cold.
Wednesday Sept 30
A clear, very cold day
Thursday Oct 1
Clear & very cold. Landed Halifax 4 P.M.
Left 5.20

Sat. Oct 3.
Break. at 6.15 am.

Landed about 10 am.
Left 1 P.M., limited by Providence.
Left motor at Pens. & reached Mill. at

Conf. Andrews spoke of the trouble w. Sec. MacVoyage.
He said the Vastie did not like him and
finally would not even speak to him; that
he was a Prof. at Howard & Aldrich asked
him to act as Sec. of the Military Comm.
in Russell's place recommend; that he got
leave from Howard on this Sunday;
that the Vastie was very jealous of
Aldrich & gave no help to the Comm.; that
Aldrich wanted him to go to the Chicago
Convention to get a blank underwriting the
Aldrich Military Comm. bill; that the
Vastie flatly refused to allow him to
go and that he remained till resigned.

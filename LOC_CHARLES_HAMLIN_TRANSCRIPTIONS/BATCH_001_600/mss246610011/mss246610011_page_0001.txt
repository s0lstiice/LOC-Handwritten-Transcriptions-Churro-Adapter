1. Sept. 12.
Dr Rubbin and Dr Caterbeil arrived last evening
but left for Paris this afternoon late via
Lausanne. He left a dress with Mrs Wilson
and went to lunch at the Institut. I asked
him for some prescription. He said I had
interstitial endometitis and gave me a prescription.
In Arm. went to assembly. Returning I drove
out to Collier villa and left the article on
the Mercury by a Mr. Grattan attacking the
Walter Hill Pass myth, -- which Harriet
sent to us.
We lunched quietly at the Hotel and then
made some calls.
In the evening we went to a dinner given
by Governor Schie to us. It was a family
dinner, the others present being Mr. Jackson
attached to the U. of N. staff, Dr Guleck a
memoriam, and Miss Endmont the Johnsons
writer.

63
Oct 29. Monday
called up Catherine Ernest to meet the mail Car
at times from 1 p.m. saying I would call and see
her. She said she would be at home and
invited me to dine with her at 6.30. I
said I would come out and asked if she
would go with me to call on Lady Ames. She
said she would.
I called about 4.30 and she not expected me
to Lady Ames and took a delightful car.
She gave me tea and asked me well on home.
Relating Alexander & her children lived together
at 6.30 and I left about 7.10 in automobile
train to catch the 10th express at Beek. Bay.
while Catherine was driving I had a friend
state w. Alexander about business home; he
sent calling me to Hamilton and I arrived
and your father has called me Charlie
for 30 or 40 years; I called your Alexander
for many years & went to extreme. Bertie
and I look on your aunt Catherine as a
young girl brother & sister so please, at least in
the home of the family, call me Charlie.
He seemed very much relieved & agreed with
me consider it respectful. I said absolutely
yes, and when I left he so addressed me.

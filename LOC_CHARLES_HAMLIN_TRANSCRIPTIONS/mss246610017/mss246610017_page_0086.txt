Governor Harrison said that Mr. Cotton's talk was
official and that Ogden Mills is very much excited over
the relations between the State and Treasury Departments
and the Federal Reserve Board in these matters.
Wednesday, April 16, 1930:
Governor Harrison continued his discussion of foreign
matters and the International Settlements Bank; that no
conclusion was reached, and he went back to have a meeting
with his Directors tomorrow morning, when he would advise
us fully.
C.S.H. took the position that the mere fact that the
statutes of the International Settlements Bank provided for
approval of issues by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York
did not give that Bank authority to pass upon any issues as
a condition precedent to their offering in the United States.

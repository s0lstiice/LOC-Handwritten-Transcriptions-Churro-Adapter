TOPIC - DESIRABILITY OF UNIFORM ACTION IN
HANDLING BILL OF LADING DRAFTS
COVERING INTERSTATE LIQUOR SHIP-
MENTS RECEIVED FOR COLLECTION.
(See Federal Reserve Board's
letter X-9188.) DALLAS
The Federal Reserve Board's letter X-9188, of
April 26, 1935, calls attention to the provisions of Sec-
tion 239 of the Criminal Code of the United States, which
makes it unlawful for a railroad company, or express com-
pany, or other person in connection with the transportation
of intoxicating liquor in interstate commerce, to collect
the purchase price thereof, or act as the agent for the
buyer, or the seller, for the purpose of buying, or sell-
ing, or completing the sale thereof.

of the states, and that in some cases, drafts with bills of
lading attached covering interstate shipments of liquor,

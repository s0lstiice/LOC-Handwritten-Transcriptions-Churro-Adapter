Second Day's Session, Tuesday, May 28, 1935
Morning
Meeting was called to order at 10:05 o'clock a.
m. with the same attendance as at the previous day's session.
Governor Harrison presided.
TOPIC - REPORT OF INSURANCE COMMITTEE
The report of the Insurance Committee was submit-
ted to the Conference, and, after discussion, it was
VOTED to accept and approve the report.

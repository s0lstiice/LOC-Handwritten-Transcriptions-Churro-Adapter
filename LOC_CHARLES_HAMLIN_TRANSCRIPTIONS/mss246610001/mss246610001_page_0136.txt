July 25. Shore in Augusta, and Bangor Maine
with Bryan and Cushman, in the
Maine gubern. campaign. It was in
doubt whether to speak w. Bryan
but a letter from W. Hughes Chair-
man of the Maine Dem. St. Comm and also
a member of the National Comm decided
me. Hughes wrote that almost he had
supported Bryan in 1896 & 1900 yet he
said it was a great error to invite
him to Maine - that he was not
consulted - that the nomination came
from Fred Plaited of Augusta. He begged
me to come as a Gold Democrat to
show the people of Maine that the
two wings were trying to git together and
to offset any bad impression Bryan
might make.
Bryan did not allude to me courage
almost he hit the "money train" as he
called it, hard.
At Bangor, where we addressed 5000
people, after the rally Bryan went
to bed while his underclothes were
being dried & sent for me to come to
his room. He talked to me for over an
hour - he said he did not expect me
did he want the nomination in 1904

but that he was determined that no one
should have it who did not support
the ticket in 1896 - He seemed to ignore
1900 but made 1896 the ticket.
He told me to tell Mr Clancy that he
did not consider it possible for the
nominee to go to him - Clancy - that he was
too closely connected with Trusts and
Corporations. He seemed to have some
personal figure against C. He said that
of course it was possible for the people
to seize upon C's other qualities and demand
his nomination but that it was not likely.
I told him that he need not worry
about Mr. Clancy - that he was not a
candidate and even should from being
put in the position of being out; that
the only contingency which might bring Mr.
Clancy before the public would be
the overpowering wishes of the people.
I further added that if such a
contingency should arise and his name
should ever come before Bryan for
appointment, it would be to remember
that Clancy had done more for him

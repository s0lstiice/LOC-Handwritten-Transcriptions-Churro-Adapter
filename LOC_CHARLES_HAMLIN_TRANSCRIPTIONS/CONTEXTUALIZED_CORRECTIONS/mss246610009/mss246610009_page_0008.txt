Aug. 8. Tuesday
Charles R. Crane and his quiet Miss Taylor,
granddaughter of President Taylor came to luncheon.
Mr. Wilson told me about Bishop Kinsolving,
who told him Bayard at a tea in week. some
time ago that he did not care to be represented
to Mrs. Wilson (who was at the tea), and that
he had refused to marry her to President Wilson.
Mr. Wilson said Bishop Kinsolving had been years
promised her family, and I think she said,
had married one of them, and that not
knowing Bishop Harding, and not wanting
to insult Smith, she wrote and obtained
Bishop Harding's consent to have Kinsolving
obligate; that she wrote and asked him,
explaining that while she would very much
like to marry his wife to be present, the
wedding had to be restricted to the immediate
family & that if she could not unite her;
that Bishop K. wrote saying he would be
delighted to marry them; that recently was
reported when he, Brit. Shot, notwithstanding,
he had publicly announced the fact in Rhode;

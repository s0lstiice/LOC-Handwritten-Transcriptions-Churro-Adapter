(United. In. 18)
Nov 10. Sunday
and during 12-15 leave from one Battling to
Boston.
at 4 PM went out to Catherine Grant
to attend her christening of Pat and
Christabel Grant, cats are Redbottles and
her brother, Redbottles to Christabel;
Elizabeth archibald was Redbottles to Pat. I
do not remember who was Christabel.
Her son's Christ was two christenings.
Christophelis birthday on July 22.
there were about 30 people present.
Mr. Kilburn, Col. Cheney, Mr. Christ, Mr. H.
Clint Conderwell, Mr. Struble, Mr. James
Turner and others.
Believe I could have written better
if I had not been so over to bed
with Col. Cheney.
I had a very short talk with Catherine. She
said Alexander was almost in a state of
melancholia, she practically said he had
lost money. I comforted him as much as I
could, and told him almost every expense
was on him himself now.
Had a short talk with Alexander who
sounded very much broken. He remembered,
in one case he had done poorly, -- in certain
said "I think I should have to eat dirt for
rest of my life."
at her christening Mary Battle looked rather
wary and worn out, although long escaped
similarty during her recovery.
I told Catherine that now, being a Red
bottles of Christabel, I had an absolute
right to write to her whenever I desired,
whatever she answered may make matters
more difficult in her old manner
and said "the blood is absolutely hers."
The whole affair was built up between
Catherine and me that her character stood
and found him guilty to turning a boy
and a girl. Their demands were right
about all.

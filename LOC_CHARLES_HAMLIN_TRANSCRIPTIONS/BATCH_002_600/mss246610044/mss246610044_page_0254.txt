Open Market Committee
Young, Owen D.
Gov. Young and James have conference in N.Y. at Woolley's office with,
Rayburn and Harrison.
Harrison wanted to ease the market but Young and the others would
not consent.
Harrison opposed easing thru acceptances but Young waved him aside.
Gov. Young is sure that if Gov. Strong resigns, Owen D. Young et als
will never elect Harrison Governor.
The N.Y. directors refused to allow the executive officers to buy
up to 100 millions of Govt. securities in an emergency, as the
Board gave authority, except with consent of Woolley, Rayburn and
Owen D. Young.

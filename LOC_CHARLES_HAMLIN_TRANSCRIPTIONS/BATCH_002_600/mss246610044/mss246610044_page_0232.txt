Warren, Charles (Cont'd.)
Warren said it was generally believed in New York that Mrs. Wilson
did not like Col. House and that she was the real cause
of the break; that Bishop Atwood told him that Col. House
wired Mrs. Wilson asking if it would be agreeable to her to
have him come down to her funeral, and that he had seen a
reply telegram from Mrs. Wilson or Bolling stating it would
not be agreeable.

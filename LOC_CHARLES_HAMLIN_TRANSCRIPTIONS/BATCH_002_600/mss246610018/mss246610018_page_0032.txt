Aug 29. Monday
Mr. Platt arrived on a visit
at 4.30 we went to the Department.
Before us stood two young called out and
with his fellow wanted Platt to meet
him in N.Y. tomorrow morning. Platt said
he could not & as was formally announced
he should meet him in Philadelphia at the
Philadelphia Hotel N.Y. Thursday at 3 PM
He said that with such blood in his veins as
Rubelato had in his I would meet
and offered him two words. Providence
or a long walk to N.Y. exclusively
Christian character in N.Y. Rochester
Rubelato etc., as Commerce advances
with a salary of $100,000 per year.
He said he required alone to
embarking tomorrow. I said if Mr. Rubelato
left I would respect his wishes & leave
the matter of his meeting with
Platt decided by mutual agreement.

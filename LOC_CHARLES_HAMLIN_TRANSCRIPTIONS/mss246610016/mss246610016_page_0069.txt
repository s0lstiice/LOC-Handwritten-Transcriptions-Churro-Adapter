July 22. Tuesday
Bertie received note from Catharine Grant
from Philadelphia Hospital:-
Philadelphia, Tuesday, July 22.
Dear Bertie:
I have another son just as I
expected, now 4 1/2 years old, - weighed
8 1/2 lbs - about a family boy and
looking healthy. I am dying to see
him Christopher, - must come in a lot
of consideration.
Thanks for both our 7 am letters.
he surely couldn't get Charlie out
the Radio - came in 10.47. at Nahant, -
so disappointing.
late up late.
catherine
we both wrote catharine and I telephoned
hospital sending congratulations.
July 24, Wed.
Dr & Mrs Richard Aldridge of N Y. shut
up with me. came from Newport in boat
to visit her then with Mr Phillips.

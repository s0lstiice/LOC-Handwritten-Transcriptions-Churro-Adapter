We dined at Mr & Mrs Wood at their
Bedford, Calvin Nielson was there.
Gertrude Hayes sent me a letter --& tells
of women in love for a birth day present.
Aug 30. Friday
My 68th birthday.
Dr North Laurence sent me a card. Harriet and
June have run some pleasant miles & expenses
very little money returns back.
Had also a letter from Anna Celeste Hahn
Have had no word from Catherine Grant.

Sat 23. Sunday
Richard & Mrs Hemelander called & left
Return back to Porte.
Sat 24. Tuesday
Write Rachel Hall a long, troublesome
account of Rochester visit. Kept no copy
in my Bk. She received a nice letter from
Mary Hall thanking me for wedding present
of $100 in gold.
Sat 17
Henry Cunningham writes. Reems. Connolly has
returned from Curtil & is now for Curtil train,
with a 2 months Cts of any land.
Sat 27. Monday
not called. Henry Cunningham on Sabbath home;
had he tried to get him yesterday he would
have been in Salem; could he have looked to
make an alternative for today, I did
not think he had to go back. Thursday not certain
to leave but a week; that he was so
angry with about his money he would
supply, or if cold he different be and

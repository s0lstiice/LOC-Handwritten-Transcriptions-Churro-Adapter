July 24. Sunday
Amidst the tumult launched with us.
He said a Chicago lawyer was now
representable on Ante even Maty Stem
to Butler, Scholzwell or Lee Kellogg.
It seemed to him almost possible
that Lee Kellogg should have been
from the North West.
He seemed to think Horner was not
exceedingly well.
He said he notified Horner on the
same night he did not care to
continue as Amb. to U.S.B.; that
subsequently Horner expressed regret
and intimated he would leave his
place to have had him continue.
He said he did not know which
Horner will have done, but I am
sure no chance.
He said Horner was a cold
blended man.

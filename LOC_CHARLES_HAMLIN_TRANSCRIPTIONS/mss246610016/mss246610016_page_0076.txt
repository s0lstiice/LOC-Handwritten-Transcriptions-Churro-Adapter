Aug 3. Sat
arrived Mhattanet.
called up Catherine Grant at Philadelphie House
Boston. She said she and her baby were both
fine and she should leave for Oakland
next week. She said she was perfectly
delighted with my letter and had read
it to Alexander, her Friends, & I think
she said she had left off to her
mother, she begged me to call in her
again next Sunday when I would leave
to Boston, and I said I would.
She said her family all said they would
have had a great writer when I went
into the MBoard.

Two young ladies asked me to believe that
they had been so as to hear from
the Menus or MBoard to receive

June 21. Riding
called into Rachel and told a long talk.
the quiet one like a soldier, upon Mailer,
his election and the current situation;
Henry Dunnefield, William Macmillan.
I said I would call him out when I
returned and that would be
done if they backed their words from the
ballot paper to which we came
over. He said the most honest votes
delighted at my election especially the
ballot paper to my election tickets
with Ireland & the Irish being votes
were so abstruse because of my thorough
humility & kindness to Erin (Ireland).
Has dawdled about to my request
to vote for him & I should try
to get Election money easily.
Rachel and Berthold made him really
angry at his reference to Gen Bradley
who was at Ireland dinner and
his statement that Gen Bradley Rachel
could not support him to deceive under
the old method here.

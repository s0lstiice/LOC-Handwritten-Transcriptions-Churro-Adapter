1928. 47
Oct. 12, 1928. 55
Catherine brought Miss Abby Howland over to tea.
She told C.S.H. the reason she had not answered his letter before this
was that it was so brilliant and fascinating that she felt
she ought to try and write an interesting and carefully written
answer somewhat near the level of his; that she had been so
overwhelmed with the details of the new house she had not been
able to even write her mother; that if C.S.H., however, would
be content with a simple, uninteresting letter she would gladly
write.
C.S.H. said this would be perfectly satisfactory, and that if he did not
hear from her soon he might send her a printed statement of
different facts and all she would have to do would be to place

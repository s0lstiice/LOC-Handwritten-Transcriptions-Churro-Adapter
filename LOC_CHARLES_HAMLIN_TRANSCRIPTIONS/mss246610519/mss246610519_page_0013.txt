FEDERAL RESERVE
BOARD
To Mr. Goldenweiser
From Mr. Thorne W.P.G
Subject: Mr. Hamlin's inquiry
Date April 3, 1935
Under the War Debt Funding Agreement and the Hoover
Moratorium, the British Government was obligated to pay the United
States $85,670,765 on June 15 and $117,670,765 on December 15, 1934.
Had these two payments been made, they would have been equivalent
to £40,748,074 at the current rates of exchange; and a budget deficit
of £33,186,074 would have resulted for the last fiscal year instead
of the £7,562,000 surplus shown in the figures published in the New
York Times on March 31.

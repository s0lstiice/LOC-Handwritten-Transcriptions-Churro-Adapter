Accordingly, it is my opinion that your ownership of shares of
stock of the Eccles Investment Company does not cause you to be a
stockholder of any bank, banking institution or trust company within
either the letter or the spirit of the above quoted provision of
section 10 of the Federal Reserve Act, and that, under the provisions
of such section, you are duly qualified as a member of the Federal
Reserve Board.

In the absence of any other purchaser for your stock in the
First Security Corporation of Ogden, you sold such stock to the
Eccles Investment Company at a considerable financial sacrifice and
severed all of your connections as officer, director or stockholder
in the First Security Corporation of Ogden.

father's estate for the benefit of your mother and his nine children,

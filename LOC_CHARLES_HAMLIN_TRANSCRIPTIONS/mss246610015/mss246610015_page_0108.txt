May 4, Sunday
Inaugurate Day
Weather cloudy, about noon a heavy rain
set in lasting all day and checking the
ceremonies, we sat out in the heavy
seat by 1/2 hour but finally gave it up.
considering the methods employed in the
creation, I am almost tempted to say,
"The end of a perfect day!" but it of course
symbolizes with the disinterestedness
of thousands of people who cannot tell.

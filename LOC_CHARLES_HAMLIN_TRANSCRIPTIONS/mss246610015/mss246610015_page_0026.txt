1861
Aug 28. Tuesday
Enfield boys collected me out from railway platform
Berks ; could round out get women at via
Hart Springs ; went to Wakefield and stood real
high Coys from Kansas ; went to Harrold's
to very attractive town, but they did not have
an ice cream ; met two Roland cattle
Smith Owners.
21
Aug 29. Sunday
Mr James H. Metcalf, surveyor, called and
marked the bounds of his new property.
I called up Mr Brownell, County Treasurer
and asked him to draw the deed.
I gave Metcalf his certificate of title and
his sale agreement to give to Brownell.
In afternoon Mr Thomas Stoddard called and
later I went over the property with
him. I explained to him the doubt
as to title little to Beale et. - that the
town owned 1/2 and we owned 1/2 - 1/32 ;
that in the deed we owned survey
all of our right, title, & interest in the lot
of the road ; believe our lots, to him.
Aug 30. Tuesday
My 67th birthday.
Catharine Grant landed with me and
gave me 2 boats ; 1. a deputy was killed by
2nd steam ; 2. Hattie rather enjoyed it. by
Woolhane.
At my 67th birthday. June 30, 1861

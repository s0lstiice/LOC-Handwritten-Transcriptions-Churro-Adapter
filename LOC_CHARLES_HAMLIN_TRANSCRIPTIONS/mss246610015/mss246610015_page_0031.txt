Sept 23. Sunday
Catherine Grant dined with me, also her Lieutenant,
and her King and her Pastor, but the Colonel's
guests. Alexander Grant had a cold and could
not come.
On leaving Catherine asked me to write her and
sent one of my delightful letters and I said I
would and that in writing out she should
began with her "Friendmother" which she said
she would. She stayed with deck watching of
Bertie & said she looked she would surely
attend her duty with her whenever she
came up to Berte. She also said it was
not beyond her mind of possibility that
she might be cast at Vaillancourt or elsewhere
with another place. I told her I would write her
a birthday letter.

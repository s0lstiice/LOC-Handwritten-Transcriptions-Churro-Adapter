July 22. Thursday.
This morning I found a message from Gen. McVoy
in Baltimore:
"I am very much pleased with the conference
I had with the gentlemen of this city. The
result of the conference was such that I
am sure you would be satisfied."
He refers to Washington.
Charles Tyler called me up and said that
Mr. Howard, Stockton had had been in brief
string letters sent to Wash. in my behalf.
He said he had told them that Washington did
not intend to make any additional loans
expressly authorized. He said he should get
in touch w. Gen. McVoy. He also said he
would not go to Gen. Hayes as if he thought
he could work in any office of his he

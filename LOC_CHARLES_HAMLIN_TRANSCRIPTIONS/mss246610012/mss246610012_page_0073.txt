The coffin was then placed in the crypt

We left us and went to wait for the train
and finally we drove away and I went back
to M.M. to come back tomorrow with Bertie
on the burial at 11.45 am.
This morning at 10.30 I attended the meeting
of the stockholders of the M.M. of Boston and
delivered an address.
Wed 30. Sat.
We went up to Forest Hills cemetery and at
11.45 am. Annie's ashes were buried in our
lot. Harriet & Jennie, Nick and Joseph were
present. Bertie brought up some rally
but M.M. and related it on the ground as
did also Harriet & Jennie.

Wednesday
I voted the straight line ticket; for Walsh
and Easter, neither of whom was likely to do
so, as they were both truerhomen to
William Walsh as to day N. and Easter voted
against William in 1916.
In evening we dined with Arthur Pearl
and heard the election returns at the
Secrety office.
Wed. Cord.
By 187,000
Fuller was reelected over Easter, and
Walsh defeated Butler by over 50,000.
Fuller is a Catholic and has been
a liberal contributor to Catholic
charities. Daniel, Joseph is staunch
with Harriet that he was going to
vote for Fuller. Daniel is an Irishman
and a staunch democrat!
Left for Washington in evening.

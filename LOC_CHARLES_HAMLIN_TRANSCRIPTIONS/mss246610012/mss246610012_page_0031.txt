June 25. Friday
Mr. Roberts to answer on Conf. Report on Medical
bill; refuse at once to conf. Cannon and instructs
Conf. Cannon to stand by the Bill as it stands
in the H.R.
Elans calls at Platt and says Street will
never yield.
Platt said the Comptroller could turn State
Seal, provided he would try to persuade
Elans to yield on his objection to the House
army.
The trouble is, however, that Elans could
have to accept his collar back or it
hands the H.R. which Elans certainly
would never do.
Elans told Cox he would find out
today about his nobility and would
let him know what he learned.
The Steg (Heller) on board Iowa received
L.D.F.'s from Zell, Howard, and one
other captain.

59
June 27. Thursday
We dine with Edelton; he and his wife will
also start.
Edelton said about two weeks ago Dr. Ruelles
wanted him to take a trip to Japan and that
Randelph was enthusiastic about Japan;
but Mr. J. engaged him from the East on the
S.S. (a pleasure); that they offered to take
at $300; that she made him travel and was
about to turn on when Randolph suddenly
changed his mind and became almost
paria stricken at the thought of going;
that he called in Dr. Robbins who said that
being as he did about it, it would be
better not to go, so she gave it up.
He said she now planned to sail on
England about July 20, and went to
Barnett's in Scotland and later to
Brattle.
He said this moment my name was
sent in the cold air. Gen. Stevenson to do
me interest to have me thrown
completely.

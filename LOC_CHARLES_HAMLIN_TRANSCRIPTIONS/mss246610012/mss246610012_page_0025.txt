June 20. Sunday
Gov H. Harding called at the House.
He said he had talked with Dr North who told
him he would be 70 years old in July; that he
had not consented and would not think of
accepting a position on the MBoard; that even
if he were desired to accept such a position,
which he was not, nothing could persuade
him to go in a Board of which James was
a member.
Gov H said Currier had a useful idea, but no
money at all, that wing might have served
one in need. Gov H said the only one he
would think of in this connection would be
claiming Cox, former Governor of Mass. and
now well President of Corpus Christi; that the
corporal wing bank was still officious
and thoroughly Corps might want to unload
Cox into the MBoard. He said, however
that Cox could not afford to give such a
salary of $25000 he was now selling;
that he could have had the Senatorial chair
but felt the need of making money.

Act 26. Tuesday
Anna's birth day, a sad day.
Act 29. Tuesday
went to Wild Hills Cemetery with Ned at
3 P.M. Anna's body in the casket was
there, having been taken from the Willing
home. It was in the Willing home.
The attendant asked I wanted to view
her remains and I said I held that
I ought to make intimate.
Her body was then opened and I
looked at the body through the glass
top. It was in a very fair state of
preservation, which, unaltered but
unbelievably recognisable, it was really
quite of a shock to me how I knew
she was not there; that she was
alive and with us.
They then told me they would have
to open the casket and take out
the metal lining which could not
be burned; that they would recloset
it back into the body, removing the
metal lining and placing the body
back in the wooden casket.

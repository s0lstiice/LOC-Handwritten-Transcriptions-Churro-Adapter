June 26. Sat.
Sen. Elkins calls me up at 3. P.M at the
House. He said he went to see Millen and
had a brusque talk with him; that Millen
gave him his word he would leave the
matter up to me with Coolidge and said
unbidenterally, he had not the slightest
doubt my name would be sent in at
once.
June 28. Monday
Edith drove me to Fort Humphreys with Mrs
Galt and Nolbi Rollins.
She said she was to sail in the Regulator
on July 17.
July 29. Tuesday
Gov C. Winter. He told me his health was
in terrible form; that his doctors recommended
it and had reduced it to normal size: that
he had some medicine also which reduced
it; that he would sell it to anyone but would
not watch what it was!

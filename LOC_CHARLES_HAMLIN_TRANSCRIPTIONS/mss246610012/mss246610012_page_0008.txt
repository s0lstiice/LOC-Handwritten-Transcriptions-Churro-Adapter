May 24. Monday
Riggle called me up to 2:00 P.M. I told him
I had talked w. Platt when said it was
too late to do anything. He agreed it was
separate line did not be introduced
and taken up in the Hall. I said the 8th
with the 3d would storm them at 6 o'clock
and when requested by Gen. Bradley I came
to the front.
I added that I must not be on the
Board at that time or my line exposed
and 10. He seemed generally astonished and
said it did not be that courage could do
otherwise it would break me. He added
that he intended to get Gen. Dodge's orders
and some of Gen. Grant's orders.
I said I had not a shot not left a
bullet on my own batteries, but, if course,
my friends were at liberty to do what
they will, but without any support
from me, directly or indirectly.

Platt told me this am he thought the Tyler
curved track taken up after regarding my
obligation, but that he had talked with
many of them and they were unanimously
in favor of my position, especially Gen.
Worth and Gen. Custer of Cleveland;
that they all agreed the Fuddlers would be
invincible.
Gen C told me today and told him that
Sebree was being considered for the
Hall. We agreed, however, that Sebree
was absurd, as Col. Custer could never be
relied upon in the Senate, as an officer
of the Army, by Gen. Grant, who, clearly
the President, could not relieve.

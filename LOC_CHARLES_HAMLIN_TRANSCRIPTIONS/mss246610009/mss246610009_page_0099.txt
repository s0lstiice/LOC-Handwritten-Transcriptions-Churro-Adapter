and all of us could go to the convention in
our cars, as her car had to go the station.
He also said Dr Alderman had brought

We went up to house at 6.15 and found Randolph
sick in bed with neurasthenia and he could not
go to the convention.

In the smoking room, Baruch said it was
the Kin Klex civil col. defeated one Party.
He also said the campaign was completely
manipulated, no one absolutely dug out to
elect Davis, but each for himself; that he
wanted an intense campaign for Senate
nominations, which they told him would cost
$750,000, and which he agreed to pay, but
no attempt was made to do this.
Baruch explained that the movement in the
convention against the Kin Klex clan started
with Protestants, while the vote in the
convention against naming the clan
started with the Catholics.
Baruch and all agreed that Meadwo would
have taken defeated had he run.
Left Berle and C.B.W. at 5.40 at about 10 P.M.

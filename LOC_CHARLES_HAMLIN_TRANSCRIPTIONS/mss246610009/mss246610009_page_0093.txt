I told Mrs. of the two historians mentioned
by Newton Baker as select men for writing
an American hist. They were -- John Sheneer
Barnett of Smith college, and Prof. Guerard
of the Rice Institute, Texas.
I wrote Prof. Iannus about them and he
replied that Barnett is a steady, accurate,
candid historian; has written some books,
articles of the text book sort, but well done;
has made no brilliant or outstanding
contributions to the literature of history;
has been seen of the inner. Historical circle
and has given satisfaction in that capacity.
He said that Guerard is an abolitionist
critic; has written on much civiliza-
tion and has ably handled history of
larger scale than Barnett; that he is
said to be clever, even brilliant, but heretical
substantial; that how he will stand
20 years from now, in the opinion of
those whose opinions count, seems to be
still an open question.

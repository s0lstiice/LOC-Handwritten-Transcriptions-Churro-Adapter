Aug 11. Sunday
Charles R. Grant lunched with me, with his
daughter, his daughter's husband.
What well to Wedde to hear Davis's acceptance
there.
It was very ably done, still judged, successful
style, but it was well up to the degree of
Nation & the Ku Klux clan.

as to the League, that the door was
closed but he did not make the
League an issue. I was heavily disheart-

he had judgment and intelligence. He

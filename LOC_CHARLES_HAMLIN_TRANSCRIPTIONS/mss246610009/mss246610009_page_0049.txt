Oct 26. Sunday. Anna 24 years old today.
Berlin went in balloting. Water = 53 1/2.
Anna received telegram from Ellis Wilson:
"Until you come here and we could celebrate
the happy day together, here and every
good will. CWSW."
To which, Anna having gone to Berlin for the
day, Berlin returned:
"Thanks for lovely telegram. Matted is in
balloting in slight of slurring protest of
Tallies and Modes. Temperatures of water
53 degrees. Let so look forward to me visit
with you and join in best love.
Anna Harwell."
Oct 27. Monday
Berlin received letter from CWSW. She asked him to give
me to give her one of my pictures.
Left for Wash.
Met Dr Woolley in State at N.Y. (Pittsburg). He
seemed very convinced that Coolidge would not
receive a majority of the electoral votes, and
claimed to feel there was a fair chance
that Davis would; he said he felt that the
99
word that could be given to Davis seemed to be
thrown into the air when Davis himself
actually be elected.
He said he felt there was not much of any
chance that Davis could carry N.Y. and he
was not sure what he said or did not say, I felt that he
was thought Roosevelt would carry N.Y.
Richard Crane was with him in South
Virginia.
Oct 28. Tuesday
Called up Randolph who said I was expected
to deliver this evening. I told him Woolley
was here and also what Berlin said as to
a letter. He replied that CWSW called up
Woolley, Saturday, I think, and selected
the question, saying she could not mention
the 2 of N. after Davis's interview of Oct 20;
that she had already done much for
Davis; she sent him a telegram on his
nominations, went to the Independence rally,
subscribed to the campaign etc.
Randolph said she had also consulted with
others and all advised him to do nothing
but take up the matter, and that he represented
the incident as closed. He said she would
explain it to me in detail this evening.

Nov 23. Sunday
Sent six packets of used playing cards received
from the Somerset club, to Mrs Wilson with a
letter in which incidentally told how I had
admitted Berlin. I had ordered them at her
request.
Called on Mrs Chandler Holt; she said she had suffered
from anemia for over two years; that two weeks
ago she went to the Rushfield Hospital for a
thorough examination, tests etc.; that the test
latter told her state was all right but that her
condition was anemia; that tomorrow she went to
go to bed on at least a month and take a
prescribed course of treatment.

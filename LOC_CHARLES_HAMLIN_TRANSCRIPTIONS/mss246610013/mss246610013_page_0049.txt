Feb 25. Thursday (cubit)
Berlin understands us, her W. and meets
her wishes and her Granger. Her W. was
very smart and cordial, and took Berlin
to the Arm. club. Just as Berlin was
leaving her Granger asked Berlin and
asked to dine with her in her office
with her W. and Dr Davis, two friends,
and her wishes immediately begged her
to accept saying she would call
for me and dear her out. And get
on her way to the lunch she had
left that cruel letter on me!
In the afternoon her wishes called us
Berlin to see if we would dine out
with her but Berlin quietly said
we could not accept it.

Her W. old doubt me out to dinner just able
she had practically accused me of disloyalty
to her wishes and herself, surely shows
my misunderstanding!
We called up Adam & Mrs Helen and they
came over at 8.30 and spent two evening.
I told Collette Helen briefly over two telephones
what had befallen me & she was almost
shrieking.
I send to them my letter to her W. and her
wishes and Collette Helen said it was a
cruel letter and absolutely unjust.
They said they did not think her W.
really intended to publicly disloyalty
but her letter stands on itself.
I send them a long letter to her W. I told
her how I had begged dear her wishes, and
Collette and Berlin begged me to substitute
Dear Collette and end "affectionately" yours
as ever letters read.
They also advised making this letter
very short and disinfused and not
showing any anger except in a quiet
disinfused way.

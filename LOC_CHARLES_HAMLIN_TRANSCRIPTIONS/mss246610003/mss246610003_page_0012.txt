27
June 5. Saturday
Palmer, director of U. S. Fed Res Bk and Staats
Sec. An agent came down complaining of Gov
String for not appointing a cashier as
authorized by Act of Congress. See Meador
that our then President would be very much
misused if we went to remove Staats.
Gov String complained that Staats went
to the Senate the proposed cashier and
later him to build out for 12000 salary.
We had an informal hearing and Harding
& Delano were appointed a Court informally
to handle state vehicle matter.
Stoppard in at Charles Warrens office.
In shearing of Atty Ems opinion as to
status of Reserve Bd he said Ems was
over strung up in it which the Atty Em
strung out because See Meador deplored
so vigorously to it, and that Ems was
dull over Ems-Warrens' problem. This shows
that Sec M. - unknown to Reserve Board
had been shown this opinion before it was
sent to us and had had it changed - an
extraordinary thing.
23
July 20. Tuesday.
Had conference w. Sec Meador, Atty Em Eugene
& Post St. En. Burleson in legal office as to
status of Fowler against issue of his Res
notes who later might be used for
purchase of Iowa Military.
Drafted letter to Atty Em asking opinion
as to law. Atty Em said Res Bd did not
ask opinion from him. Finally said see of
Measuring could use his advice as to law
to enable him to vote on any problem
in Res Bd as Sec of Treasury.
Said eleven State officers of Res Bk were
not officers of U.S. and that Res Bk
were not Gov bonds. As the Post
said the callalool offered by Res notes
were legitimate notes not bonds nor were
minute purchases - Atty. said clearly
we could not refuse to issue but Res
notes nor loans to future use when
offered.
Also said if Res Bk were asked to discount
acceptances bond in Iowa minute
purchases for myself, if asked, give
opinion to Sec. Tries to judge him as to
his vote on Board.
act 3 favored turning our action on the
Pete but I said Board must take action.

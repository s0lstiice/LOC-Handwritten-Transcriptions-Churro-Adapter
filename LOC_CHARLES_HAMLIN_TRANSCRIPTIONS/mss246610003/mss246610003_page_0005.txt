March 26 to May 2.
Left with Beebe & Anna for California. Went to
Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco where
we stayed about 2 weeks. Made 4 addresses
in San Francisco and then visited every
Western city in that district; then went
home visiting other Res Bns.

May 2. Sunday
Arrived home.
Laid in Sec. McAdoo: found him sick but very
wake and much worried over Resps. Bents sent
May 7. Thursday.
Have had several talks w. Secy. He is
still very weak and I think is worried
about the Resps. Bks matter. I have advised
him strongly against laying the case and
said if a compromise were suggested to
severly arrange it. I also strongly advised
him to think of himself in their matter
and to check Williams. I think he
would have to do this but his loyalty
to Williams prevents.
Today Benders asked for an interview
at the Lodge we were together over
an hour He said he was satisfied
Williams was absolutely in the wrong
& that the President could suggest
his approval of a letter by the Govt
council to W. advising him that he
should await our finalities etc. He said
this could leave among all the integrity
of the Bill & do away with a deal

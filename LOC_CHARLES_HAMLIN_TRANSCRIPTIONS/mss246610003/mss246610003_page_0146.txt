Wednesday Aug 23
Evening of Res. 344 meet in Berlin. Went up to
meet them. Met Harding & Delano there. told
Harding of my letter to him & my proposed
letter to Rich. Said would send Rich letter
to him when it came back from beach. &
if he thought it wise to hold it back
until Miller & Ed next Tuesday (M. was
to stop off at Miami. tomorrow or Sat.) he
would be so free to do so.
He said he was very indignant at Rich's
letter; that he believed that if any bankers
had spoken to Rich -- Ed would have "the
banshee"; that he had already givenwald
every opportunity to speak of the matter
but that he had not mentioned it.
Delano also said he did not beleive
any bankers had made such remarks
to Rich.
They both evidently thought the matter
was not of any consequence.
I told them if I sent the proposed
letter to Rich & he gave me the name
of any bankers, I should probably feel
it my duty to inform the women
sensators & Representatives of the matter.
They both thought Rich was simply
playing politics.
Harding said he had talked to Williams
287
who was present; He said he felt satisfied Williams
would vote for the Senate when the matter
eventuated.
Thursday Aug 24
Toward my letter to Harding & Rich on my
desk last night & told for want of
time I desired them both & sent them to Downing
& instructed him to deliver to Harding his
letter & to have him the Rich letter &
to mail the latter unless Harding could
not send it until meeting next
Tuesday.
Read in K.Y. June. This am. that the conf.
committee had rejected our proposed
Amendments. That the Senate may be
issued as held the amendment accepted
most of the others as passed by
Senate.
Last week, just before I left on vacation,
probably Thursday Aug 17, Sec. M.
told me only he & the President could
induce them to accept about
Amendments & that he was not clear
enough on its merits to undertake
to do this.
I explained the wisdom of the Amend
ments & he did not feel secure enough of
its success President to insist upon it.
a great mistake, in my opinion, as I
looked upon it.

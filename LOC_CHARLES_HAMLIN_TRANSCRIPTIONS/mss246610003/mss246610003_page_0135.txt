Aug. 3. Thursday
8 am called up Sen. Hollis. He said my name was
not reached yesterday as there was a row varied
by Sen. Brown in connection with confirming from
Home Board; that he had my name formally
before Senate & it is now in Calender; that
there was a full Court meeting at which
the Rebut. members were present: that I
received the unanimous vote of the
Court; that all passed out on the subject
minus; that there was not the slightest
doubt of my nomination confirmation;
that he will surely have me enfranchised
before next Tuesday and tomorrow earlier.
Harding said he had made a new draft
of Article to Sec. 16 stippling in the words
"at Pair" but promising that State Bys
under regulations of Res Board could
not dispute a vote Res Bys but clearly
proportions. Said he had shown it to Secy
& had sent it to Elans.
Sec. M. said he had called up Sen
Powerset to hurry my confirmation; he
said it will surely go through as
soon as an vote session could be had.

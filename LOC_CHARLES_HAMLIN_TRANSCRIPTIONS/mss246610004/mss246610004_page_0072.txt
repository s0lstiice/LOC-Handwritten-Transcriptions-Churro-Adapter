Sunday, Dec 29
Write, etc. Downing - send no copies in bills, or
billions:
Dec. 29, 1917
Dear Mr Secretary:
I ought to tell you when you
were at the house Christmas day that Bunn
negotiated with Mrs Hamlin two small jobs as a
Christmas present. The articles are of little
substance value and I assume there is no
objection to them receiving them. I am looking
forward to seeing nothing when you arrive
and we can have another form of total
capitulation.
Sincerely yours
CDS

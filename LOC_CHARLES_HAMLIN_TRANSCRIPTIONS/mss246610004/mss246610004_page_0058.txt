May 3. Thursday
Gen H. said Lord Curilbfe had decided to go west to meet bankers & that Sec H. asked me to go with him as an ex Env of Board. The banks were Rich. St Louis, Chicago, Cleveland & Boston & suggested Delano or St L & Ohio & I would meet him at Cleveland.
Later H said Sec H. wanted me to have whole journey as Delano did not speak it so I agreed to do so.
PM. Lord Curilbfe took tea with us at our house.

May 4. Tuesday
Lord Curilbfe called & said will leave to cut out Boston for present as must be in N.Y. Thursday May 10. He said he would surely go there before leaving U.S.
The itinerary is:
Sat. May 5. to Richmond
Sund. May 6. to St Louis
Monday May 7. dinner St L.
Tuesday May 8. lunch & dinner Chicago
Wednesday May 9. lunch Cleveland
Thursday May 10. N.Y.
May 5. Sat.
left Wash. 2 P.M. for Richmond with Lord Curilbfe, Combs, Wm. Ervin Strong & Sherman Allen.
arrived Richmond 7.30. Revolt out to Williams house a Co. of militia guarded house. Had dinner about 15 guests including Gen. Stuart who was placed on left of Lieutenant Lord Curilbfe being given place of honor.

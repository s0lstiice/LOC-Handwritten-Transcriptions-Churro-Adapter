Feb 17. Monday
Gov. H. said that some time ago Corbett
Williams asked if he would care to give
him an opportunity from which he could include
in Heiman Co. supplying RR substation; that
it would require 3 millions of dollars
and that there was a certainty of
very large profits. Gov H said he did
not care to go into it. told him he was
established at Williams suggestion as he
was an officer of the RR administration
which would be subject only to bills
from the Company.
In Calchin's opinion the Railroad Comm (Council) had
not w. Bradly Palmell as to Mr Stock Publ.
act. Calchin's called last week and
said the Rail Road Comm had better
to do most of the things covered by our
bill & were ready to begin at once if
we desired it, pending action on our
bill by Congress. Palmell advised us
to accept this offer as a move by
the Railroad Comm under its power to
prevent unfair competition would scan the
fraud, promoters & molest the money
Gov. Evan.
Feb 18. Tuesday
Sec. Elson should w. us. I told him
as he had been dead & he would
attend at 2 o'clock and go ahead.
Feb 19.
Palmell gave me a draft of letter
for Elson to send to Judge Carr asking
him to order a new election. I suggested a new
election where Elson refers to Judge
as one requiring the large business
as well as the small business to
appoint some responsibility by
unpublished statements.
I told Palmell there would undoubtedly
be many protests & we must be
sure Sec. G. & Laffingwell understood
this & Bradley obtained our action.
I called up Laffingwell & he said
he unhesitatingly opposed. I then
made an attempt for him to take
w. Palmell & P. went around to his
office.

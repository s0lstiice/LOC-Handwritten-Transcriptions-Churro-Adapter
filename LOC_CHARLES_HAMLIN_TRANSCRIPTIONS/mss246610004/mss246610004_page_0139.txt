Wednesday June 7
Miller announced that he was going to
Cumberland S.C. for a week or ten days;
he did not ask why, it would be curious what
for me to have him so nor did he give any
reason for going. Later I discovered that
the funds went also young, which explains
the matter. Miller seems to have no sense
of obligation or duty either to his associates
or to the Govt. Nothing but debt necessity
will warrant him in leaving the post
of duty, adding to the burdens of Govt. himself.
Gen H. told me Gen P.D. that Miller said to him
that he might possibly go in to Savannah
Ga & subject the M.R.S. Agency there and
asked whether it would not be all
right to put in a voucher for his
expenses over Wash. to Savannah. Return!
Gen H told him it would not be all right.
Miller is so strong I believe he will select
way to pick this manly from the Govt.
I am slowly but steadily making the
conclusion that Miller should be asked
to resign -- I stood up on him several
times in the past when Secretary
wanted to get rid of him but I shall
never defend him again.

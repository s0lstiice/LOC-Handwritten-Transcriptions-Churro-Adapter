Jan 9. Thursday
Gov H said Elson had just told him that the
President was not altogether satisfied w.
costly as money advances in the other
seat and that Lanning had suggested by
cable that Strauss be sent over to Mr.
Strohmann & M Board. Gov H did not
like this: he said the Board ought not
to be represented at all as it had no
contact over Orange exchange as McAdoo
had taken that to himself by delegation
from the Board; that if Strauss is to go
he should be given result of abominable
by our Board in order to represent
the Treasury & not the Board.
I agreed fully & said if he repeated the
Board he might bind it in some
way wh. might Stewart come down
politics. Gov H said it could be absurd
to have B&I represented by men who
had served only 2 months & knew
little of its institutions or politics.

Dec. 3. Monday
Sec. H. said the Job. Comm want to induce
Army, cab. to invest in China along with
Jacksonist & that all Secs long & millions of
State Dept had sent together a very foolish
diplomatic diet in China subject; that if
Army, cabinetists insisted so also Gov.
Grant could insist on doing same & we still
have to loan to H. B. & Ranch the correspondence.
Also said Grant will not run to China to
take responsibility the 570 ad vol. dealers in
Chinese rubies.
Said that he thought I would be a
good man to go, with Nicholson, to
Williams. I said under present condition
very member of the Board should be
at his post: that if I should leave
me one old but what old might do
as Admin. will not have a majority.
He seemed to agree with me.

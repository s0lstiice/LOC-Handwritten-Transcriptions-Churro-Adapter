76 Sat. Jan 5
learned that Sherman had called in Wash.
& that he, Gen H. & Wauberg had a
conference and that they had decided
that we ought not to join with John
in loan to China except in cooperation
with the other great Powers.
Wauberg is acting more for interest
of business than for the U.S. in this
matter. His action in not notifying
me of the meeting was discrediting.
Yours
Lincoln gives to Baum Migata by Wauberg,
we told Baum to consult w. Strong
on Attorney matter & on money, purchased
by Baum M.
Baum M. should briefly to effect that in
outlook might be possible to have cash
dealings with Bank of N.Y. & Bank of Johnson
that E.S.B owned Johnson 150 millions
etc.
Till the members of the Baum M. left
seemed to have joining in loan.
7 Monday
Baum Migata called at 4.30. Studle ran
apart on him w. Strong for tomorrow
at 10.30. He said Bank of Johnson had
called him authority to sign the
agreement he had heretofore.
I pointed out that his absence to "147
inventary movement during and after
the war" might be considered to relate to
gold exports and it so wild leave to be
decided by Sec. H. He said it was not
intended to relate to gold exports but to
military circulation and said he wanted
merely some understanding with the
Bank of N.Y. and M. Board.
He also said about 20 millions of gold
bursts in N.Y. had been owned by
elements who had sold them in N.Y.
and that Bank of Johnson would be glad to
buy them. He asked if there were now
circulated by Americans, whether they
had made this money. I referred him
to Gen. Strong.
Wed. Jan 8.
Baum Migata had conference with Gen Strong
at my office.
In late afternoon called with Baum Migata at
Shorham to tell him I was going to Boston.
He said he had reached an understanding
with Gen Strong as to the Bank of Johnson and M.
Bank of N.Y. & that Gen S. would pledge at Co
Exile Court.
T. P.M. Debt on Boston
Wed. Jan 9
Shake at dinner of Mass State Bankers
awards. Gov. Bennett.

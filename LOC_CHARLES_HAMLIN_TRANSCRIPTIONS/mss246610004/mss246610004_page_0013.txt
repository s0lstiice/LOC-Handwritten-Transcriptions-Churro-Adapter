Dec 5. Tuesday.
W. H. Res. Agents all day.
Smith: Rich. Austin - Bonneville.
I walked him w. Rev H. He said Webb & Delano
continually urged him last summer to go to Presditt
to demand salary, & that finally he agreed to
go in express confidence that they should let the
Presditt elect in their opinion & should be notified
to the Board. He said that when conference
finished he was not quite satisfied w. the
way they chose up me to President, so he
would be tempted Presditt would clearly understand
they intended it to be for their interest of
the system that I should be notified.
Skakling of nut. of Dec 7 turned w. me after
three hours w. Presditt I asked Rev H if my
memory was correct that Del. and I was
a candidate for Gen. He said he thought
Del. and I was not a candidate, but he
had temporarily sent to Gen. that we should
have next Saturday Gen. should be notified
Gen. He said however he thought that
Delano felt that W. Irving a Gen. could
not get it & that Gen. Delano would.

25
Mr. and myself Berton & I sit side by side.
Has not since Mr. Dr. Grayson called and said he
want with the President election night at Sheldon
town; that he returned to leave a liberal
work in his hand; that all returns were
telegraphed to him by himself from the
Executive offices; that all votes were
quoted except the President; that finally
he made them all relay 20 resolutions
on 2 papers he brought with them:
that he read the unfavorable messages
to myself in council discussing them
already -- that finally he ordered them
all to be sent himself.

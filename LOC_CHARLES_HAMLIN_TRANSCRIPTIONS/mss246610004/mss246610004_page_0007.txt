Sec. Cecil Strong Hill called out of and
asked if he would come right away to
see me. I of course said yes. The came
over.
He was very much excited & said he might
as well make a clean break of it -- that
there was tremendous excitement in England
& France over the Res. Board's statement.
That it was whistled down that it originated
out of a return plot to induce the President
to mediate for peace. That Warburg and
Kuhn took the while at the bottom of it.
That it was now said over there that there
was a German offensive w. the U.S. and
the submarine activity was being carried
on with the exclusion of the U.S. -- that
of course he did not believe this but
merely was letting me what was being
said over there.

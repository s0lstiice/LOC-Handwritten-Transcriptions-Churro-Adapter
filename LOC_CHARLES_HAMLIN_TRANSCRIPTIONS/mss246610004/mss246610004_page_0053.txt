April 23. Monday.
Sir, Lord Curleiffe called on me. He said he was in
the Board and I was in service but
He said I did not ask him to come in. After
our meeting was over Harding suggested
that we send in word to the Secy that
we would wait in his room for Lord
Curleiffe. I suggested that we should not
know that Lord Curleiffe could to see us.
Then some one suggested that if he came
to Harding & asked to meet us, Harding
could take him to each of us in
our own rooms.
Finally we adjourned and Harding
said if Curleiffe called he would send
for us.
Later Curleiffe did call and we
went sent on and met him very
briefly.

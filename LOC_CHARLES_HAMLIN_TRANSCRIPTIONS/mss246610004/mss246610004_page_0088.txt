June 29.
Left Gen Mts
June 30
arrived Mts
July 2. Went w. Mayor Ashbey to Providence to meet
Vicecount Ishii, Amb. of Japan, with him
were the Vicecount and Col. Tomkins
and Dr Candee, Secret Service agent
of State Dept.
He & I were our guests.
3. Held receipt at Mtts for them. About
250 attended. In evening Capt. Scott & with
Fort Rodman dined w. us.
4. It and Acting Tw. Coolidge and Mayor
Ashbey called early in morning with
3 of Evan Scott. We then went to
middle of N. Bed. bridge where there
was a fine military display.
The Vicecount delivered an address
as also it for Coolidge & myself.
Then we went to garden of Capt. Colby's
and Amb. placed a croquet on it.
Then lunch at Colby's Inn & march to
State Armory School. Ishii, Coolidge, Rev
Dr Reed it all shady, also my self. A
beautiful sight.
In evening Gen Yamas Wild dined w. us.
Amb. received from naval camp
at Marion & then went to Long
Anderson house in Brookline.
Dr C.M. Bertie & I motored to Boston & dined
w. Anderson at Somerset Club to meet Ishii
& Vicecount.
Anderson told me he was very much shocked
that he was expected to have the secret
service man Candee "threatened" him
as a secret. I thought great success when
I said we had him in our hands & at
our table He is an insufferable cad!
July 6. Motored back to Mts.
7 Sunday. Took train for Wash. at
Providence. Ishii & party on train.
Candee told me how rude Anderson had
been to him; at first refused to receive
him in his house; finally gave him a
room and sent all meals to him in his
room. Another row over Somerset club
discussion. A. said he would not be permitted
to enter club; C. said did not expect to
but could devote to and furnish w. Ambassadors
whilst he did.
Next day, July 7. Ishii went to Col. House
at Manchester.

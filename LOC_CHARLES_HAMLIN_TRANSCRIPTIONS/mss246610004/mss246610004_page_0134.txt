264 Jun 23
Sen. Elam asked me confidentially about a
reported financial statement of Congress
Williams as to a newspaper correspondence
who admitted that he had been asked
$250 to attack him & prevent his congressmen.
I said I thought it very unsatisfactory to
publish it and that the House cannot
ever to give the information to his
senate friends. He said there were two
services equally & he should suggest to
the Comptroller not to publish it.
Mr Compt. however, did publish it. See
several books.
Sen. G. also said he suggested to Compt. not
to recommend a guaranty of bank
deposits in his annual report saying
that while he rather agreed with it
in principle, it could not be used
during his service and would only
create the danger. He said the Compt.
however went ahead & made the
recommendation. He said he had no
honor to order him as he should
direct to congress.
He was very much pleased at the
Compt. He sued or constantly kept suing
him & annoying him with petty matters
of detail. I told him he ought to act
early, to put him under the microscope
This seemed to please him utterly he did
not specifically say so.

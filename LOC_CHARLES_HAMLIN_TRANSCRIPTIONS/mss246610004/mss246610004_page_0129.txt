Jan 16.
Harding said Elan had about decided to ask
Burdt to send in Dawes name for N.Rd:
that Court Williams said he loved Dawes
very much & that he was a good man on
the place; that Williams had found a
statute passed in 1896 providing that War
officials might hold well on appointment of
lieutenant until successor appointed.
Harding said Warburg is crazy to go abroad
at his own expense & make a report on
N.Road in exchange for $5000; that
he (H) asked Dawes about it and he
(H) thought this put the idea into
Dawes head of going himself and that
he mentioned huddled weirs and "arranged"
X.
Williams told me his whole family was
going with him. This will be a great
outing on the Stearns family -- all of
the friends of the N.Road!
Jun. 17
in reading over the circulated letter I found one
dated Jan 4 to Erw Mc Donald of Chicago from Erw
Harding stating among other things that the N.Rd
had recommended to Congress a National charter
for banks to do a foreign business; that he
did not believe a Government bank should
establish Branches in foreign countries to do a
general banking business; that it should not
direct its powers upon foreign exchange in a
large way on the score of profit; that the Bank
should not be competitors of private Banks.
I was surprised at this letter since, it
assumes that Banks are Trust Banks.
I originally thought they were but
Illinois and its allies have ruled contrary.
However, I believe Banks were established
for the very purpose of enriching a
number of banks when use, in the
public interest.

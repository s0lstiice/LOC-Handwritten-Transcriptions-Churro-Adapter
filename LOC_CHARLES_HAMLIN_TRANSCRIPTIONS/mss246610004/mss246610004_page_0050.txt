Mar 22. Thursday
Very Hr informed Board that he had
decided to invest 50 millions of Certificates of
Indebtedness at 2 1/2% and assured us to offer
them to Res Banks. He said he could sell
them at that price anyway but wished
to give Res boys the first opportunity.
Wouldn't better opportunity than such rate
yield at 2 1/2% and at such price the Res
boys could sell them to other banks. He
could not deny however that the Res
banks sold easily since this is not an
investment -- the Certificates running only
for 3 more. He said the Boys would be
very angry -- I said if so the reason
will be that such an asset will have
a material effect upon public bond
interest & enable them to be placed
say at 3 interest or at 3 1/2% or up
which he denied them.
He insisted on our holding up the
matter until he could talk with Mr
Selig -- who was at cabinet meeting.

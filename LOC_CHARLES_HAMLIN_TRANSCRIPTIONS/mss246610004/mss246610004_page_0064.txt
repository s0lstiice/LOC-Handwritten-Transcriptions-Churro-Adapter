Sent 19 or 20.
Rev. Aspin of Boston called and stood with me
20 minutes. Said he came down to talk
with Sec. McAdoo as to new loan. Heen
went to Wauberg just before our meeting
& Wauberg was in town that night.
Aug 21 Harding told me that Wauberg
said he never told him that he had been
obtained $500000 per year by the Nat.
Shawmut Bank, that he had a family
& relatives to support. He was in doubt
what to do. Rev H. said it was exceedingly
that Aspin did not consult me as
chairman of the Boston Comell. Kahn,
as Wauberg did not mention the
matter to me I wrote him a letter
asking what the odds were. Two
days later he told me he had
the letter -- that Aspin consulted
me confidentially. That of course
he supposed he had also told
me, that Aspin was not going to
accept it. etc.

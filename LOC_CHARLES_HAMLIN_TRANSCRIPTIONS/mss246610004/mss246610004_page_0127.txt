Jan. 15. Wed.
Slavin told me at Board meeting that the Trust
had called asking him to come over to Paris to
advise the Treasury on coupon. w. Place letter.
He said the call said that the Trust said
that on their went only four left on Board
he would only a short absence would be
necessary.
Harding told me Slavin spoke of it to him
same day as so and then admitted that he
had known of it for 10 days but had never
mentioned it; he was very indignant &
said it was a job put into by Strauss &
Barnett. That Slavin was crazy to go & he
believed fixed up the deal. We both agreed
that it was a great sin to let Slavin
leave the Board at this critical time.
It told me that he had not been well
for some time; that he could not sleep
& found the was on the point of a
nervous breakdown; that he had
intended going off for a 3 weeks but
that Slavin going would prevent
this. I urged him to go & said I would
do the extra work gladly.
I told him it was his duty to go to Paris

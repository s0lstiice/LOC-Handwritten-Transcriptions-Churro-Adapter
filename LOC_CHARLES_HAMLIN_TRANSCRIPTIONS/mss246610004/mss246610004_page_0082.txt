Tuesday May 17
Cab. Smith Council sworn in. President designated
me as Chairman & those present abstained
when Sec M. said me my designation.
Berlin was only woman present.
I told Sec M. the members thought it
unfavorable to appoint Chairman & he
thought their decision was in the whole
world. I told him I would ask Council to
appoint Chairman until he could
told w. them as to honorable coordinate
w. Tim. Board.
We then met in my office and organized
directing Selden Vice. Sec. & Nobbounty
often Officers, Clerks, local Councils etc.
honorary. Also made funds, my Sec.
lunch. distributing officers.

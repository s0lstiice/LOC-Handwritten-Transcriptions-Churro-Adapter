March 24. 1861.
Meeting of Curr.
Members proceeded and seriously discussed
N.Y. Res Bank bill. It passed a vote
objecting to being asked to subscribe
to new 2 1/2 %er bills without being consulted
as to their value.
While the Bank was clearly wrong
it was most unpalatable for the
Secy to attack it in his deed.
The Secy also said he wanted to
have law amended so that Res
Board could appoint a majority
101
of the Direction of the Res Banks.
Board considered this and every additional
member opposed it.
I said I favored it in principle as
very likely well in the future but
at present time depreated it would
cause better opposition &
might defeat our efforts.
It ultimately could change whole fact
and in time of war leave the present
nothing short of surrender of money
or part of banks will warrant
such drastic action. To say that
the Res Bank, all the central &
reservoir of the system were to be
in a minority of 1/3 of Directors
will be enough to condemn it
except in extraordinary circumstances.

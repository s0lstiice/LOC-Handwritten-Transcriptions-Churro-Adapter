Friday, 20 October 1812.
I hoped the I scarcely expected to hear from you
my dearest sister, since the battle of the wedding. The Government
occasions in your household affairs must have hastened
you out of it sometime after, as well as before this event.
By this time she is constituted in the usual order. The
present will not feel settled unless Mason has had his pay
out. Many have been here in consequence of their absence
only three days. We did the utmost that our
very limited means allowed to promote their pleasure,
but as their expectations of pleasure were from
soaring upon which we had no control, I can only
say that they were deeply entitled. Mrs. Boyd called
on the President, but did not wish to see any of
the other gentlemen attached to the government, though
never in his own state he said he was in the hands of
cruelty on men in office, he called himself on Northumberland
but did not find her at home, to the great disappointment
of poor Mary; their stay was too short to allow

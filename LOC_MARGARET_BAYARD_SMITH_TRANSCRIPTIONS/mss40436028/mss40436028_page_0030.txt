Robinson Emesae a am now reading the tales of the castle
to Spain for the second time -- I however read only such tales
which suit their age & meet those in which love is
only sentiment -- They almost know nothing of real
life -- I have long dispreferred, geographical, historical knowledge
do it often happens that I have tasted more
than I have read. -- Do you wish these tales returned
if you do, I will send them to Maria -- If not I will send
them which they can use freely and read the whole. They
are in course of the next year, or 18 months, you could share
my joy in departed maps I would thank you -- our income
is now limited to one penny per meal, so that I have to do
very economical -- I am not on any account deficient
in money -- I would like to send them to Maria -- I have sold
several tracts of the best historical works --
I have several times thought that at the present period Gibbon's
works would have great effect on his education & of his studies at
Lansanne would be so useful as amusing, his perseverance & industry are
marvelous, your best toy works, they I am sure would charm your
students & particularly your revolutions of Rome, revolutions of
Sweden & of Portugal -- this way of seeing the most
interesting epochs of history, renders him one of the most
interesting historians I have ever met within living
days -- all my memoirs too are charming, the girls would be delighted
to read them -- I have thought of sending them to
Mrs Miller, I have omitted mentioning her address
as I had to hurry the last page of my letter
to you -- I am now sending to you -- M. H. Smith

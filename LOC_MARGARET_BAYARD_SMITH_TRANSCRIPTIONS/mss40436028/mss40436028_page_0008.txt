10 minutes & close at - but now a days I have no leisure
moment - The children, myself, my needle & house hold
cares leave me no time for book or pen - I am bound
with the exception of one day had a cold, already a
searing week - but still the more work another week
had gone - How rapid is the flight of time - and
how few worthy does it leave of its baggage -
But this is only the seed time of existence, if
you have not seen the winter -
My dear Harriet
My love to your family -
Yours affectionately
M. H Smith

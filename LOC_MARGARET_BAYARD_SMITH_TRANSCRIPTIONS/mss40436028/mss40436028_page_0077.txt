obliged to give up reading by candle light -- my eyes ache

I have ample employ in knitting yarn stocking
as we have 3 women & 4 men to death, & I doubt
at their stockings -- Ann & Susan have learned to knit
2 pairs each but a pair --
What do you think of the charge in Maria's
situation -- Mrs B's sale of his city house was a very
great surprise to me -- I must fear it is not
agreeable either to Maria or Mary -- but I only
conjecture so, for Maria, in her letter mentions
many advantages she hopes to derive from the change -- But
finding her familiarity to new public society, & having no
heard her describe how necessary society was to the
health of her mind & the equality of her spirits, I
am fearful she will suffer very seriously from the deprivation
for an winter at all seasons, but unfortunately in winter
she will find distance an imperfable obstacle to our
coming society. -- She expects a visit from you before
they remove & I hope to hear from you to all
this fortunates which cannot fail to interest where
we tend to love -- Perceiving they did not charge extra
postage for your envelope I have ventured to send
you one -- I hope they will be as careful to send
it me again, for if they charge you with a double letter
you shall have a double letter next time, or only a single
one to pay for --

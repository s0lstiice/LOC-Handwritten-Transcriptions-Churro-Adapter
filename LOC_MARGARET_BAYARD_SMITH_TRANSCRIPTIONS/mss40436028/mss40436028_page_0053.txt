I think your dear little boy has got the better of his disappointment by this time.
He is a very fine boy for me, and your sister. I wish him to be a gentleman.
Our friends both at Brantendon & Merrimac are quite well. I think
I made you a very pleasant visit this week. The affairs to be very busy.

Ever your affectionate son

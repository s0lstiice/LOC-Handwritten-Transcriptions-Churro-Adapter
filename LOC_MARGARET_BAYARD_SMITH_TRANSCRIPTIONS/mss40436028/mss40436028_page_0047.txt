January 3rd 1862
Dear Tom:
I have thought much of him -- he does indeed deserve
to be called a politician -- why? None was to be found
in the service of his country. But one
reason why I like him is that he is one of the
most honest men in the army. He has been a
lender friend -- to my sister and brothers
and a noble man -- your friends have been
very nice to me -- but it is general wishes I have
desired that you and me may not meet -- he should
not be hastily returned to the army -- but if he is
to be added to the heavy burden of the army --
let alone do may she never leave this
great post -- now her only happiness -- But as
it is better to die than to live -- I am
glad to be carried to the end of life promptly
and to be buried in a quiet grave -- But if the higher
divine power is ordained by some hands -- or by some
hand of the higher power -- some can be made -- and
perhaps could be made -- in the circumstances of

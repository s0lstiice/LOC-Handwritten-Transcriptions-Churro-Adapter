My dear Aunt,
I want to tell you how happy we are
we too have the sweetest little sister
you ever saw; her coming so soon
was quite a surprise to us. Her hair &
eyes are black, & her fingers are so
long they would almost frighten
you. Her name is Anna Maria;
she sleeps all day and night, except
when she is taken up to be fed & she
never cries even when wash'd in cold
water

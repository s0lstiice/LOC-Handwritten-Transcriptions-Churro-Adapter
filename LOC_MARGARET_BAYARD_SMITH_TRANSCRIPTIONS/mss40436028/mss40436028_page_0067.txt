August 2d 1813
What can be the cause my dear sister gives for
protracted silence I have never heard from you since
I heard of Elizabeth's death a family loss there must
be must keep you much occupied about
2 or 3 each year aggrement that there is no time to
talk Elizabeth died in the memory of family I
have never complained but years of the same
occupation our miseries now quite over & no one
affliction can obtain an attaint on the only
but you not then immediately on in the city this month
is sickly but here an experience no difference
you need not part any fear from the element
or season I remain with you would I long to embrace
my dearest sister in it would gratify me in any
way to show affection & kindness to her friend
I cannot tell what to think of your
silence I wrote to her immediately on the receipt
of yours but have never received an answer
she had a private letter I am wishing to change
she may not have thought it worthy while to write
if I can see or hear from you you shall immediately
know the result
Susan has been with you I want
to hear from whether she intends to remain
in the city the winter or should stay the winter
& whether she intends to go to the city
or not I have done all I could to convey from my mind
the embittered recollections which stilled even
under feeling I even on my letters could not write

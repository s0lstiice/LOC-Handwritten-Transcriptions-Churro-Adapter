From among even the times
That bitterly were shed
In the warmth of the spirit
Or another year was spent!
And thus is every year
Prophetic of the doom
That darkness lies in spirit
On its path toward the tomb;
And thus earth coming year
Will but the silent pastor,
Until the turner's shell round
That time shall be no more.
Now hath not marked the crimson clouds
Which herald the morning sun—
While the green leaves to fresh winds bowe
And the bright day has just begun?
Now is on earth hope like this
As crimson clouds its own wings
Draw us to scenes of pleasant idyll,
With brief and pure imaginings!
Thus have the hours of Gladness been
As childhood's calls and youthful love—
Passes as a wind lass's watchful tone,
And clouds are ever changing now!
And from the songs which our own brethren
By fire in youth life's buoyant fire,
I turn to fader garlands scattering
Of those time gone, and Memory—
Belan fountain of light! my native land on high
Bright with a glory that shall never fade
Now upon truth! without a veil or shade
My holy spirit meets the Spirit's eye.
There dwells the soul in its eternal power
Gasping no longer for life's feeble breath;
But penitential in Heaven its glorious presence
With pitying eye beholds yet fear not death.
This world is but the rugged way
Which lead us to the bright abode
Of Peace above;
So let us choose that narrow way
Which leads no traveller astray
From realms of love!
Our cradle is the planting place
In life we run the onward race
And reach the goal
When in the mansions of the blest
Death leaves to its eternal rest
The weary soul.

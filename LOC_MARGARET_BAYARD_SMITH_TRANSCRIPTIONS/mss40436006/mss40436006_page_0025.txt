9. In memory of His Excellency
Jefferson, the Lord of this dear breast
And severally calm, thy love retreat,
For long in the weary frame for rest
That not a moment show overt
Thine, nothing terrible in death,
I am but a child of one note away
And sleep at night without a breath
A breath upon till dawn of day.
Is not a night without a moon,
Though gloaming impenetrable surround,
Or like the brim of an eternal fountain
A hushed sleep forever in the ground.
The darkest clouds give lightnings birth
The pearl is formed in heaven's bed;
The germ imperishable in earth,
Springs from its grave as from the dead.
In small the value of the fact:
For weakness down, but aided with power
The ancient seed shall have the dust
Of glorious and immortal flower.
But at their dead, must we despair
For you forever from our lot?
That all amends in them at not.
Is sin while then who love thee love
The parable Satan of our heart;
But though God to thee shall give
Of all their want and all their old.
If all their sweet, when from their eyes
The latest beam of heavenly light,
If all their art upon truth dawning
They spirit toward Jefferson Montgomery
Spirit of Joy! I will call upon thee
With thy trembling step upon the anxious stride
That thoul bind me thy smile and every
For thou must be the end of life's tragedy
That leaves me at home, me all gay or thorned up
If you put your and little fellow
They laughter oppress the weary heart,
And leaves it to languish and melancholy
Spirit of Peace! depart from the Sky
With thy calm peace look and the promise of rest
And let the power of thy love take up
With the throb of thy gentle breath
For Daughter of Heaven, the heaven fall,
They will not, and will not bend to thy power;
In thy arms though sweet in charms will coil
And it shall my spirit - Heaven, away!
Spirit of Love! obey my voice!
And let my steps to thy fairy throne,
And crown me now with thy brightest flowers;
Obligations! thy name too swiftly passes
For you the captain shall fall by chain,
And many a heart by thy smile betrayed
Shall sigh for its freedom but sigh in vain
Spirit of Hope! from thy bright cloud bend,
Or power can thy endless charm destroy,
If thou wilt ever my love attend
They will be with me thy brightest smiles and joy.
Spirit of Beauty! thy guardian hand
Shall shield me from ever earth's bon season,
I feel not the anguish to day my bones,

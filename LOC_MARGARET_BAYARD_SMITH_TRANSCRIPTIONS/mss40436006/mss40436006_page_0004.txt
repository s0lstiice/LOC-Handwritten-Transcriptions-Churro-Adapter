The croallow twitting from the shore-built ship.
The rocks shrill clarion, or the echoing horn,
To more shall rouse them from their toilsy bed.
For them up more the blazing breath shall burn.
Or busy house-wife solly her evening care.
No children run to lose their sire's return.
Or climb his knees the crowded hips to share.
Cyphed out the barrier to their sickly yield.
Their narrow off the stubborn globe has broke:
How jealous did the drive their team afield:
How hard the woods beneath their iron a sturdy stroke:
Let not ambition mock their useful soil.
Their homely joye, and destiny observe:
No Grandeur wear with a disdaining smile.
In short and simple annals of the sport.
The beast of brakelry, the pomp of power,
All that their beauty, all that wealth can give.
Shout alike the inevitable hour:
The paths of glory lead but to the grave.
For you ye proud, impotent to these the fault.
The Memory over their tomb no trophies have.
Where thro' the long-drawn wyle and fretful vault.
The speaking anthem swells the note of praise.
Can stolid arm or animated breast
Back to its mansion call the flicking breath?
Can Honour's voice provoke the silent dust.
Or Flattery seek the dull cold roar of death?
Perhaps in this neglected spot is laid
Some heart once pregnant with celestial fire.
Hamlet, that the veil of empire might have away'd.
Or which'd to watch the living line.

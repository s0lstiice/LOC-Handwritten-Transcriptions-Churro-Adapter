Reason theology
The first & original being in whom all by whom the universe exists
is denominated by Zoroaster Time without bounds: From the
operation of the infinite time were produced from all eternity
two secondary principles Emanuel and Thiriman each of them
possessed of the power of creation but each disposed by his
innate will nature to exercise them with different degrees - The
principle of good is constantly bombarded in light that of evil
buried in darkness - The wise benevolent Emanuel created
man capable of virtue and abundantly furnished his
habitation with the material of usefulness - by his vigilant
providence the motion of the planets, the order of the heavens,
& the temperate mixture of the climate are preserved. But
the malice of Thiriman has long since violated the
harmony of his works. Since this fatal eruption the
most minute particles of good & evil are intimately blended
together. The rankest poison springs out amidst the most
palatable plants deluges, earthquakes - conflagrations attest
the conflict of wisdom, & the little world of man is per
petually shaken by vice & misfortune - While the rest of
mankind are led away under the chains of their infernal
enemy the faithful Persian alone preserves his religious
adoration for his protector Emanuel, fights under his
banners in the full confidence that he shall share the
triumph of his glory in the last day when the enlightened
vision of goodness will render the power of Emanuel supreme
to that of his rival who will sink into his native
darkness & leave virtue triumphant to preserve the peace
& harmony of the universe

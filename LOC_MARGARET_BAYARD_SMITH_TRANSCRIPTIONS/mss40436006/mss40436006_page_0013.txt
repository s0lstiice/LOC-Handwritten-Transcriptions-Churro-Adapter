Say why was man so eminently raised
Around the vast creation? Why admit
This life and death to doubt his priority on
With thoughts beyond the limits of his frame,
That that the Crown lent might send him faith,
In night of mortal and immortal prisons,
As on a boardship theatre to run
The neat scenes of justice, to court
This genious aim to all divine deeds,
To shelve each partial purpose from his breast,
And through the midst of prison and of error
Find this the tapering light of chance and pain,
To hold his course unfaltering, while the voice
Of Truth and Justice on the steep ascent
Of Nature calls him to his high reward,
The approving smile of Heaven, the blessed beam
In mortal loometh this unquenched hope
That breathes from day to day sublime things,
And makes profession why depart the soul
Thrice from the track and journey of her time
Do you not the good she knows not?

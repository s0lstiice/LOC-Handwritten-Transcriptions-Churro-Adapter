The silence of my soul from the country she
left, what accents thrilled upon the ear
Like distant echo, tremulously low!
How peace is felt not heard, the million-fold
Replies in sympathizing own language -- tears!
But when the expressive breath is near,
He comes with whispers still, that touches not
The outward sense, but sounds within the heart
As solemn requiem to the pattering soul.
He watched her breathing through the night
Her breathing soft and low
As in the breast the wave of life
Rests heaving up and down.
So silently we seemed to speak --
Her silent moves about
As we had lent her half our power
To see her living out.
But when the moon came down I saw
That still with early showers
Her quiet eyesless lids she had
Borrowed man's than ours.
Then the form of Heaven's love was broken
Then was a trembling presence in her eye
That spoke inevitably things! it seemed as if
The emotions outside of mortality
Have clung to her spirit as it passed asleep --
Slowly the bank away till allying life
From her last impeded to the vital tide,
And the still hour returned our feeble throb
To love's last presence -- All unconscious gazed
At the slight movement of her panting breath
Whilst heard no sound of grief, for faith was strong
In the sad hearts that crowded round her couch.
Hope had enshrouded her standard to their view
Beyond the gloriouy confines of the grave.
Then thought they of the resurrection soon
Then a new glorious sun shall kindle life
In these cold relics, there are given its light
I question nature in the gayest hour --
The night of Death comes not in negligence
To those whom briefest day of life is
With him who tells of an eternal season
When every nature takes to endless bliss --

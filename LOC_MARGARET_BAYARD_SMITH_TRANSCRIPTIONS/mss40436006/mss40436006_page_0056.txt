Winter her slow step falls from day to day
Death's banner is heavy on her dullling brow
Yet with all the family flying to earth and day
I am content to live with God's not now --
Not while the blooming of the joyous Spring
Shall the sun set our souls burning to heaven,
Not while the birt's such busy gladness rings
Not while bright flowers lave my youthful mouth
Shall me grant God's lift up my struggling brow
I am content to die -- but He not now!
The spring hath ripened into Summer time,
The season's musical boundary is past,
The glorious sun has reached its turning point,
Oh! must this glimmer of beauty be the last?
Let me not perish while in land and sea
With silent steps the Lord of light move on,
Not while the murmurs of the mountain bee
Greet me with dull ear with music in its tone;
Pale darkness dim my eye, and clouds my brow
I am content to die -- but He not now!

There is a time when the soul is sick,
And the heart has more than it could be feeling:
If for the eye death sees the picture it had,
And the hair still flows in solemn streams:
There is a time, to the pure mind even,
To wander from earth and fix on Heaven.
There is a moment - to which we stand
Beside the couch, and watch the pillow
If one we love and grasp the hand,
This soon must part beneath the pillow -
This is a moment our fair would see
And follow the loved one to the Skies.
I have seen old age in its final decay,
By the winds of eight winter months;
I have seen gay youth in the bloom of May
With all its brightest nerves blasted
And I have felt within me then
I wish to parole the abode of men.
We stood a circle of mourning friends,
On the left of a dear fond parent we stood;
Her smile, it is one to spirit renewed
But the smile remains on his, into grief changed;
Fill the tea of life shall rent sorrow
I cannot forget that moment never.
I've seen him gone to his own bed
And the words in their island caves were opening
All was tranquil come as the sleep of the dead
When twilight came over - yet scarcely divesting
Of other to the eye - leave the bright eyes of even
That hour to direct the departure to Heaven!

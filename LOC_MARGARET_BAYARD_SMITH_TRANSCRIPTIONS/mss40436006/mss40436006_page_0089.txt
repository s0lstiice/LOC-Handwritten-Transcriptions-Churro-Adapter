Therefore glorify God, in your body and in your mind;

The glory of God is in our body and in our mind when
the powers of our bodies, the faculties of our minds,
the actions of our hearts are all united to him
authority in them, and to his glory as the end of all
our thoughts and actions, when our inward disposition
towards him corresponds in their order, to the union
of light in which his blessed character is presented
to our contemplation; when not only the duties which
we owe to himself are faithfully and heartily fulfilled,
but the duties which we owe to our fellow creatures
in the various circumstances of life are done
as to the Lord, and not to men.
It is right indeed that every creature should be
honored before God; and it is perfectly reasonable
that a careful and quiet nature should be thus
humbled. There is no price, indeed, to be found
in the universe except in the heart of patience
and virtue, that there is of all places it might
least be found. There is pride in earth. There
is pride in hell. There is no pride in heaven.
It was pride, in the form of ambition, that originally
reduced man from his creator allegiance to God.
Pride was thus the first principle of transgression,
and it is pride in all the variety of kinds, that still
maintains and cherishes the spirit of rebellion.
This pride must be subdued. This spirit of slavery
of self dependence, of self sufficiency must be broken.
The lofty looks of man must be humbled, and
the haughtiness of man must be broken down,
and the Lord alone must be exalted.
The first sentiment of genuine Christian humility
is an abasing union of guilt and an utter renunciation

And memory brings the light
Of other days around me
The joys I feel of childhood's years
The words of love then spoken
The eyes that shone now dim'd gone

Bound on
My holy Father! come not near
The peril of my heart to hear;
In part to mortal ear I tell
The grief that in this brave soul dwells,
The thoughts that the wishes will I own,
That wander throug' this burning brain!
That fellow being, why should I
Before thee speak imploringly?
There sorrow than murder I believe
When can his bitter worm forgive,
Or guilt unite the contrite soul
That peace which comes from heaven alone!
Oh! let me speak that prayer hour
In common with a higher power.
The world's grief and I'll hardly bend
To my sinningly Father's Preach,
To him for mercy I'll appeal,
To him my infant soul reveals.
The knowe the heart that he has made
By each alternate passion away &
How can forgive it, for he knows
Its want, its weakness I it were!
By his protecting hand I blest
Now dwells night I sink to rest
And sleep his sheltering wing beneath
Though there the last dark sleep of death!

passed the heart stands to thee in the relation

The calm retreat, the silent shade
With prayer and praise agree,
And seen by thy sweet bounty made
For those who follow thee!
There if thy Spirit touch the soul
And give sin no more abide
Oh! with what peace and joy and love
The communion with thy God!
Then like the nightingale she pours
Her solitary song
His acts in a picture of his song
He sighs for human praise.
Father! I should have blessed thy name
In purest uncorrupted prayer;
But when my eyes were fixed upon
That which my lips have uttered thy name
My foolish heart has worshipped here
Father! while in thy presence I pray
It may that heart be wholly thine;
Then will it suit with thy love
Like pungent incense rise above
His worship, solely at thy shrine
For almost idol I have known Christian that will not
Help me to tear it from its throne
Christianship only thee.
My dear Redeemer and my heart
I read my duty in thy word
But in thy life the love appears
Deeper still in living character.
Such was the truth I sought the truth
Such deference to thy Father's will
Such love and worship to divine
Immortal Creator I make them mine.
Colosseum, the midnight air
With thousands of thy prayers
The desert thy temptations knew
My conflict thy victory too
Be thou my fortress, make me brave
Offer up thy gracious image here
Then Christ the Judge shall own my name
Among the followers of the Lamb.

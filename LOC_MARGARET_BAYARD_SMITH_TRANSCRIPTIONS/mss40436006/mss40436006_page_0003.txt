They written in a Country Church-yard
The curfew tolls the knell of parting day
The leaving boat roars slowly over the sea
The placid man homeward sails his weary way
And leaves the world to darkness and to me
Five yards the gleaming landscape on the night
But all the air is solemn stillness holds
Place where the battle wheels has turning flight
And droopy skirlings toll the distant fields
Save that from pondering man's muddled brow
The roaring oar dies to the moon complain

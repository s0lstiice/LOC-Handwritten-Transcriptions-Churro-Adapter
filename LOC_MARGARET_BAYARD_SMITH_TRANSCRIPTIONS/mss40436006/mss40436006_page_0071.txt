"The pearl of great Price"
2 "Withen greet the pilgrim stranger"
Wandering in this darksome waste,
Why so lonesly unattended
Whither, Whither dost thou haste?
From the pride of wealth and splendor,
From the gay fantastick round,
I would fly -- with no defender --
Save the Friend, with Virtues found,
I no boon from Fortune could --
Charms me not Golconda's mines --
Wisdom's gem is all I seek for --
I would bend me at her shrine
Anower! Then not that Wisdom's treasures --
On the bed of Ocean lie --
Deep beneath the Waters Willows --
And, concealed, from human eye --
On the rocky bed of Ocean --
Sparkle gems of every die --
In their mossy cell they glitter --
And under the Eagles eye --

In every limb that trembles to the time
I hear the voice of God among the trees;
With thee in safety I conclude I walk;
With thee in power I tread the path
In every creature I see thy forming power
In every word thy penetrating voice.
They hope, they shall animate my discovery
They pray, they shall guide me, and they from continual
Thou shalt not rest, crowned by all alarums,
From evanescing cares, from gloomy tombs free
And feel myself omnipotent in them.
Thus when the last the dying hour draws nigh,
And earth's mercy before my trembling eyes,
When trembling on the doubtful edge of fate
I stand and deliberate my view to either state;
Shall me to quit this transient scene
With decent triumph and a look serene;
Shall me to fix my ardent hopes on high
And having loved to thee, in thee to die.
Calm is the parting hour
When death with sovereign power
Throws on the righteous soul his heavy chain;
He doubts not God's attend,
While round his head one band
But peace celestial mutes the body's pain.
The over the links of earth
Part, and his final birth
To perfect solitude with captivated eyes,
Belkins a vale of tears
In cloud and shadow appears
Before the heaven-bright fulld of premier lies
His friends have round and weep
In thine like an infant's plea
The chattering bitterness of death peals on
And on his eye the glare
Isle, and the spirits there
Flashes for me -- and all of life is done.
How able to like the breath
Of morning was that death!
Is agony her torturing thought was thine?
But what a help am I
Plays round their lips the while
And how the heart in's own will that hour is fair.
O, may my fortunes tread
This path, by which I lead
But God's own day star, till I sink in dust;
And when I lay me down
To sleep, I may the crown
Shine on my eye that circle round the sun.

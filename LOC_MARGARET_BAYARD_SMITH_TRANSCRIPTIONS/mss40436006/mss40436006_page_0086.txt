The religion of the Hindoo is described in the
Shaster which contains the substance of the Vedan
their most holy book which the priests alone are
allowed to possess - It states that in the beginning
god being alone resolved to create the world in a
small bubble so that it expanded & made the firmament, he then separated the four elements from each
other & formed the earth the sea, the air & the heavens
bodies, he then created a man & afterwards a woman
to be his companion, they gave birth to four sons &
for these four women were created to be their wives
from them the earth was peopled with a race who
for some time remained hence was supposed their
creator, but in process of time they became very
wicked & practiced all things of deceit so that the
earth destroyed all that lived upon the earth by a deluge
& receiving, notwithstanding their wickedness, their souls
into his own bosom, thus ended the first age of the
world - That the plan of the creator might not
be destroyed, at the command of god, these other beings,
more perfect than the first, sprung up from the ground,
they immediately began to worship their creator, they were
made the delegates of god's power, to Benuaw the foot
of their beings was given the power to create, to bind
the second, the power to preserve & to hinder in the 3d,
the power to destroy - in process of time the race which
they produced became wicked & Benuaw being taken
up into heaven, they were swept away by a mighty
wind which shook the foundations of earth - so ended
the second age of the world -
A few who were better than the rest having escaped the fire
of the wind again peopled the earth with a race which
being wicked in their turn were swallowed by the earth - opening
's doors upon them at two time Vishnu was taken
into heaven & ended the third age - If present Hind
seerin the destroyer is alone left upon the earth, at the end
of this age which is to be longer than either of the others
he is to ascend into heaven & carry with him the souls
of all who have existed upon the earth - the earth is
then to be entirely destroyed by fire, as the earth will
have been destroyed alternately by each of the elements
of which it is composed & at the last all struggling
together will reduce it to its original confusion -
Though there is no mention made of image worship
in the Shaster, yet the Hindus generally worship them
some of the idols of which there are many worship the
first object they are after worship, indeed the whole
religion is very much degenerated from its original state
they tell the most absurd stories of the five heads of
Brahma, of the divisions & even immersions of Vish
nu of the tricks & deceit of Brahma, Their present
religion presents a disquieting & melancholy picture of
the wickedness of human nature & the weakness of man
unintensified by revelation - Though the doctrine
of Transmigration is not found in the Shaster yet
many of them believe in it as extensively that they
will not even lend upon credit, but they should desire
some end from its dwelling, but content themselves with
eating the leaves & plants others eat meat & some even
eat animals, among some of the acts they are hospital
established to receive such animals & insects as are sick
around a low idol is supposed themselves - Some sects
believe in a future state others disbelieve it entirely

Why is my spirit sad?
Because it is passing each succeeding year
With something that is used to hold me more down
That I am not what I once seemed;
Because the spirit that once seemed to fill
This empty limbus and the lower gale
Is vacant, void, empty!
Why is my spirit sad?
Because no more within me does the soul dwell
Thoughts fresh as flowers that fill the mountain air
Or the innocent delights
Because I am weary of the strife
That with its fewest trains the springs of life
Stirring the day from night!
Why is my spirit sad?
Dear! ye did not know the lost, the dead,
The land with me of green paths to tread,
The laughter of young romance;
Ye never strove with me beneath memorials,
Or saw the rich light of their tender eyes
The solemnity of their glance!
Why is my spirit sad?
I have not that beautiful been borne away
Are not the noble hearts turned to elin
Withered in root and stem?
I see that there is no one who looks on me
The radiant joy of youth on round me goes
But not -- but not like them!
Another spirit hath paralysed
In the darkness of the past
And the shadows of the present
In our future years we cast
And a solemn dawn a gloom
In the memory of that flame
And many happy hands
The death knells it hath sung
And the smiling cry of youth
It hath blotted is with care
And the bright and sunny brow
It hath darkened with despair
And every thought and feeling
It hath forever changed
And heart that loved the sweet
It hath for age estranged
From the wings of the glad spirit
It hath forever crushed
And the voice that most was loved
It hath forever broken
And the glories of the grave
Are hanging in the bower
There happiness and love
Will dance away the hours
How many were the hopes
That were most fondly nursed
When from the womb of time
A mother's eye has burst

When the I sail, Protecting Stars
Be my vain wishes fulfilled,
And every step committed here
With better hopes be fulfilled.
They have the power of thought hidden
In this my thought would come
This money for my life I have offered
That money I adore.
In each event of life how plain
They ruling hand I see
Each blessing to my soul more dear
Because bestowed by thee
When Gladness rings our favorite hour
They have my thought still still
Preserving when Storms of sorrow leave
To meet this evening will.
My lifted eye without a tear
The gathering storm shall see
The steadfast heart shall know no fear
This heart shall rest on thee.
The smiling spring comes in regaining
And sends winter openly after;
How desolate then are the falling water
And how bemoaned are the young trees;
And in the mountain break forth the morning
The evening golds the ocean's swell
All creatures gay in the sun's returning
And I leave in one bonnie Bell.
The flowing spring leads away summer
And yellow autumn passes near
Then in his turn comes gloomy winter
Till smiling spring again appears
Thus season's changing life returning
Old time and nature their changes tell,
But never changing still unchanging
I adore my bonnie Bell.

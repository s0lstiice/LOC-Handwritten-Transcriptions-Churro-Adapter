It must be so: 'tis not for self
That we so tremble on the brink;
And striving to secure the gulf
Apt clung to Being's sovereign link,
Oh! in that future let us think!
To hold each heart the heart that shares
With them the immortal waters drink,
And soul in soul grow deathless there.
Let fate do her worst there an relics of joy,
Bright beams of the past, which she cannot destroy
While which come in the night time of sorrow I fear
To bring back the features which joy used to wear!
Long I long for my heart with such memories full'd
Like the tree in which roses have once been distill'd
You may break you may ruin the rose if you will
But the scent of the rose will hang round it still
When though life unblest we live,
Bringing all that made life dear
Should some one to our end to love
In days of childhood breathe the strain
Of how wellknown breaths the strain
Of many thoughts that long have slept
Thumbling former smiles again
In faded eyes that long have swept
As a beam on the face of the water mingling glow
While the tide flows in coldness and darkness below
In the cheek bright as tinged with a warm evening smile
Though the cold heart to ruin run away the whole
Our fatal remembrance one sorrow which thaws
Its blest shades alike on our joy and our woe
In which life nothing darker nor brighter can bring
For which joy has no balm nor affliction no string
Oh! this thought in the midst of enjoyment will stay
Like a dead leafless branch in the summer's country
The beams of the warm sun play round it in vain
It may smile in its light but it blooms not again
It is not the tear at the moment shed
When the cold turf has just been laid on him
That can tell how beloved was the soul that fled
Or how deep in our hearts we deplore him
Is the tear thrown many a long day except
Through a life by his side all shaded,
Is the sad remembrance fondly kept
When all other griefs are faded
To wander on the world's hard denizen
With now to love us now whom we can love
Now that with kindred consciousness endured
Would smile the lips if we were not
If all that followed flattered might or mused
This is to be alone this, this in solitude

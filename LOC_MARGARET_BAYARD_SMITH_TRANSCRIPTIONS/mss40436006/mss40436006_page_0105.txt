The pure design of Benevolence is to bestow happiness
upon others, but its intrinsic Reward is bringing happiness home.
No one single quality is so endearing in
the intercourse of human life as good nature. Talents excite more admiration; Wisdom more respect and Virtue more esteem: but with admiration even is not to mingle and fear with
respect; while esteem the always honorable is often cold but good
nature gives pleasure without any alloy, ease confidence and
haziness companionship without the harm of obligation or the exertion of gratitude.
G.

So there in the abyss
So there among the adamantine spheres
Whirling and whirling thru the boundless void,
Sought that with half our energy can fall
The human beam, as when Brutus son
Refugient from the stroke of Bucolio fate
amid the crowd of patriots, & his arm
Altoft extending, like eternal fire
When guilt brings down the thunder, calls aloud
On Tully's name, & shock the crimson sword
Of justice in his rapit volonist'd eye,
Since born the father of his country, Hail!
For to the tyrant prostrate on the dust
And Rome again is free! Thus thought the father
Of Caesar's life in various pomp array'd
While the great legions of the Senate stand
From her fathers throne supreme
Sent down to other law such as on earth
Moses &c. he knew most powerful, to promote
The weal of all his works, the gracious end
Of his divine empire.
The Hornet
At the close of the day, when the banner is still,
And mortals the secrets of forgetfulness prove,
When none night but the torrent is heard on the hill,
And nought but the nightingale's song in the grove;
Who, that bears
A human form, has not often felt
How dear are all those ties that which bind
In gentleness together, and how sweet
Thy force, let Fortune's wayward hand the while
Be kind or cruel? With the faithful youth
Why the cold arm of sin whom long he loved
So often fills his arms, so often draws
His lonely footsteps, silent and unseen,
To pay the mournful tribute of his tears!
He will tell thee that the wealth of worlds
Should never seduce he bosom to foreign
Those sacred hours, when stealing from the noise
Of care and envy, sweet remembrance soothes
With virtue's tender looks his aching breast
And turns his tears to captum

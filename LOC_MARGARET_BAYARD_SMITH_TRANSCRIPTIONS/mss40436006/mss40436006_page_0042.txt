Our waking dreams are futile. How I dreamed
(Of things impossible, could sleep de morte)
Of joyous prosperity in perpetual change!
Of stable pleasures in the toiling man;
Steadfast provision in the stores of life!
How easily were my mortuary trance broken
With gorgeous spectacles of fortune and peace!
For behind joy in earthly perspective!
Bless! unblemished bliss! second words and aim!
Invaluable heaven to divine science!
The noble mansion of the rights of Brown!
I clasp'd the phantom, and I found them air.
Celestial Prosperity! where is the stop?
To mid earth, one shames the god's funds,
And one alone, to make her own amends
For absent heaven -- the heaven of a friend.
My own heart must break, acrimoniously, soft
Each other's pillow to divine despond.
Oh! for a closer walk with God
And calm a heavenly frame
A light to shine under me on the road
That leads into the Lamb.
Where is the blessedness I knew
When first I sought the Lord?
When is the soul reviving day
Of Jesus and his word!
Return O! heavenly One return
Sweet messenger of God!
I hate the sins that made the morn
Blood from thee from my breast.
The nearest idol I have known
Whether that idol be
Help me to leave it from its throne
And worship none but thee --
So shall my walk be clean with God's
balm and pure my frame
And give a bright salute on the road
To lead me to the Lamb.

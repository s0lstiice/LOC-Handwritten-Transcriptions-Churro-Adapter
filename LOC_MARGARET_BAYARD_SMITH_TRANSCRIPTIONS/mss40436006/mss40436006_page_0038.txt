Oh! memory when thy gleam is cast
Upon the track of hundret years
Bewailing joys that long have passed
And left this world, a world of tears
Why did thou vainly linger still
In lilacs decayed and blushing flowers
When the young heart had lost the thistle
That was so sweet in earlier hours.
Thine is a vast and wide domain
The lot the dead enfeoff this grave,
And pass before the mind again
In times which death hath quite away,
Again the gentle is becoming bright
As once it beam when life was young
Began their voices with delight
As in departed hours they sung.
And yet its anguish to awake
And feel that then not but a dream
Buried like his who never spoke
Did stand within the mocking stream
Go, go it carries the unloved heart
With thorns of heart's agony
To haunt the joy it once possessed
Whilst dies the meter in the sky.
What dead white in their glimmer swim
Those in the freshness of their spring
When on the sunny time of sun
The bird of summer drenched his wing
Or when is that bitter worm
Of heart dreamed and worn, each despair
Rebuts the calm, the peaceful grave
He went among the flowering thorn.
In when the weary traveller gains
The height of some cloaking hill
The heart nerves of earth, the plain
The eye his home from distant earth
Thems when the abstruse religion awakes
By faith his mansion in the sky
The only his fawning strength gives
And every his speed to reach the grave
The thought of home his spirit shares
To move he moves for trouble part
For any future trial fear
So he may rest content at last
For there he says I am to dwell
With Jesus in the realms of day
There I shall bid my cares farewell
And he will wipe my tears away
Jesus on thee our hope depend
To lead us on to thine abode
Despised one hour will make amends
For all our trial while on the road.

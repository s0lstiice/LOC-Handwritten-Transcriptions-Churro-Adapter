In consequence of an ancient prophecy among the Chinese, that
an extraordinary person should appear in the west, magnificent
arms went from China in search of him; they returned about
the middle of the first Christian century, having proceeded
as far as Persia, and being responsible with the consent
of the people, they slept, and not daring to return without
having performed their mission, they carried back with
them the worship of the white religion, which prevails
through China and Siam. We are told too that about
the year that our various oppressors upon earth, that divine
commander changed his name which signified was vengeance
to one which expressed peace.

There in disorder, dark and wild,
He sees the future once so high,
Which mortal vanity had piled
As emblems of eternity!
And deemed his stately piles, whose frown
Throned in their majesty sublime,
Would stand unshaken by the storm
That gathered round the brow of time,
Now desolate and dying year!
Earth's brightest pleasures fade like thine,
Like evening shadows die appear,
And leave the spirit to repent
The stream of life that used to pour
In fresh and sparkling waters on,
While fate stood watching on the shore,
And numbered all the moments gone.
Where both the morning splendour flown,
Which danced upon that crystal stream?
Where are the joys to childhood known,
When life is an enchanting dream?
Enclosed in the starless night
Which destiny has overspread,
Circled upon the trackless fight,
Where the dark wing of time hath spread
And left its eventide
Of sorrow— heaviness and grief—
And thus divested of its pride,
It withers like the yellow leaf—
Oh, such is life's autumnal hour,
When its plumage of its summer bloom
And such is life's autumnal hour,
Which heralds man into the tomb!—
Florio
They saw their life depart who are not dead!
Why wander without thought their tombs around
In infant sleep? Did angel there?
Shrouded, rolled up in dust, eternal fire?
They saw they go, they live a life on earth
Unbelievable and from an eye
Of nature let heaven pity fall
On me, now pretty numbered with the dead.

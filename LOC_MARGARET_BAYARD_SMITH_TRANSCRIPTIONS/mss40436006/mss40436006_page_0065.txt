I would not live away; no, welcome the tomb
Since Jesus hath lain there I had not its gloom
Then sweet to my rest till to bid me miss
To quit him in triumph descending the skies.
Who, who would live away, away from his God,
Away from your heaven, that Christendom abode,
Where the rivers of pleasure flow on the bright plains
And the multitude of glory eternally reigns:
Where the saints of all ages in harmony meet,
Then divinest and brotherest transports of joy;
While the anthems of praise unceasingly roll,
And the smile of the Lord is the flush of the sun!
I could any longing spirit see
In strong immortal wing,
And reach thy palace in the stars
My Savium and my King!
There thousands worship at thy feet
And there, divine employ.
The triumphs of thy love repeat
In songs of endless joy.
There on a Throne, low dawning bright,
The exalted Jesus shines
And turns ineffable delight
To all the heavenly minds.
And perfect joy and love dencease
And own the realms of peace.
So not the fear of the moment from sin
That I had put him out of his
That can tell how blessed are the souls that follow
Or how deep in sin I have been
O that those lips had language!
My father's voice! how often does
His audience on my lonely hours
Like Meeting plants on wings of sleep
Or die to the unconscious flowers
This gentle time, even standing by
And years of sin and sorrow free
And leave me at my father's knee
Portrait of a man's head and shoulders, facing right, in silhouette.

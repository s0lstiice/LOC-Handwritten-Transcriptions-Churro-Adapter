Like a star that lights the billows
Strikes the abyss with rays so bright;
Pilgrim, there may find him treasure
That not hide from human sight
Dive beneath the floating billows
Shrive awhile the soul with
Waves have left inexpress'd, its lustre
Time can never make it dim
Lamp of Heaven to guide thee onward
What the Storms and tempests come
Twill thy path way clear before thee
Shining brighter o'er thy tomb.
S.B.S.
The post at Belleislean Chapel: John
Find God within its sacred bounds
Belleislean found appears
And helpless crowds are rocking there
To wash away their tears
From every hill and every valley
The sick, the lame, the blind
Deserted groups are approaching the British
And their respect most final
But one poor sinewy soul remains
Stretched in near Belleisle's path
The fate the soul reviving stroke
And hear the waters roll
But has not strength within himself
To bear his limbs along
Faintling he lies upon the brink
Amid the encroaching throng
But glorious hour! when breaking o'er
The sacred found he says
He caught a glimpse of Jesus there
To drive his fears away
At Jesus' call he came to plunge
Within the healing wave

Addressed to the Infant Co. of St. Philip built a few days after he left
by St. Gellert, at Vienna July 1816
Little strange, tiny self
come and answer for myself
How could I make the feelings known
In a language if they none;
But cannot then venture to tell me
All the chances that might have
done that spark of Heavenly flame,
Which now remains this immortal flame,
That its talent fullness still
From the Universal Soul!
This same spark, perhaps since then
May have glowed the greatest men
Who have graced the Roman stage
During many a former age
Was perhaps, illumin'd by Plato
Rome's Aristote,-- but,
And a lengthy scroll of names
Which the offers of History claims
For its Pet, -- With renounced
But with another immortal cross! Holy Spark, thy deathless flame
Seems another thing, the same
In each exit, then has passed
From the Roman Magazine
Then most plunged in Tellier's steam
Which offered thy previous Dream
Thus our race remains with The
of Personal Identity
Holy Spark! thy public fire
Still still quieter and vague
Than are places in ancient story
Or that bring up modern Greece
It may be thy Palms now
Transmitting here and there,
To remain in future ages
Poets, Patriots, and Sages;
And as then at present not
Shrined within a Roman heart,
May it be thy Infants fate
To become both good and great!
May his Godlike views continue
Being Bless of all his race
And may he true greatness find
In being quietly useful to mankind

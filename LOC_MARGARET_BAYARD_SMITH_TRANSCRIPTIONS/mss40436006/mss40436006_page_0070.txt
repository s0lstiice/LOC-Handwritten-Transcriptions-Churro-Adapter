The Forget-me-not.
There is a Flower Store so well
That grows within my garden plot
They will give you the name of
The lovely Blue Forget-me-not.
It is not within the rich Man's palate
But near the honest peasant's bed
Where grows that lovely Flower we call
The modest Blue Forget-me-not.
It does not break a rich perfume
The world's glory, it has not yet,
It doesn't want a warmer climate
The brilliant Blue Forget-me-not.
Their life lies here in this simple Flower
For ever be it name Forget-me-not
In prosperity or adversity leave
The humble Blue Forget-me-not.
And should I die an early death
Let no false bearer bring that
But let there spring from my grave
The humble Blue Forget-me-not.
A.M.H.S.

My soul is dark - Oh quickly string
The harp I yet can touch to hear
And let thy gentle finger flow
He melting murmurs on mine ear
If in this heart a hope be dear
That must shull charm it forth again
If in their eyes there but a tear
I will flow and cease to burn my brain -
But let the storm be cold and deep
For let thy note of joy be first,
I tell thee, ministerial, I must weep
Is ever this heavy heart will burst;
For it hath been by sorrow made,
And acted in spleenly silence long;
And now to drown'd to know the worst,
And break at once in gaiety to song.
Sun of the sleepless! melancholy star!
Whose tearful beams glow tumultuously for
That shames the darkness, there cannot be found
How like art thou to joy remembered well!
So glances the past, the light of other days,
Which shines, but cannot not with its memories
Of night - train sorrow patches to shadow,
Distinct, but distant - clear - but, oh, how cold!
Oh! unatched away in beauty's bloom,
In thee shall rest in ponderous tomb;
But on thy buff shall ever rear
Three lions the earliest of the year;
And the wild aspasp were in tender gloom:
And oft by you blue gushing stream
Shall wander from her drooping head
And feel deep thought with many a dream,
And lingering pause and listfully bend;
And watcheth: as of her steps disturbed the dead!
away! we know that tears are vain
That death nor hell nor heaven disdies:
Will this unteach us to complain?
Or make one mourned with the life?
And thou - who tell'd of me to forget,
They looks are even, thine eyes are wet -
In that high world, which lies beyond
Our own, enwrapping love endear,
If there the cherished heart be fond,
The eye the same, except in tears -
How welcome those untroubled spheres!
You sweet this very hour to die!
To come from earth and find all fear
Lost in thy light - Eternity!

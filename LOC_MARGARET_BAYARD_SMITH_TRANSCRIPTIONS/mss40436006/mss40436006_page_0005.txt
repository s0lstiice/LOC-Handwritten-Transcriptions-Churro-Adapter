But knowledge to their eyes her ample page
Rich with the sports of time still never enroll.
Chill Penury represses their noble rage,
That opens the general current of the soul.
Full many a gem of purest ray shines,
The dark unpolished caves of ocean bear:
Full many a flower is born to blush unseen,
And raise the monarchs on the desert air.
Some village- Champion , that with dauntless breast
The little Tyrant of his fields withstood:
Some rude inglorious Nobility may rest:
Some Cromwell quibbles of his country's blood.
The applause of nothing reaches to command
The threads of pain and ruin to despoil.
De scatters plenty over a smiling land.
And read their bestry in a nation's eyes.
Their lot forbade: nor circumscrub'd alone
Their growing virtues, but their crimes confined;
Forbade to waste through slaughter to a throne
And shut the gates of mercy on mankind.
The struggling phantoms of conscious truth to hide
To quench the blushing fires of ingratitude.
Or bear up the shrine of Luxury and Pride
With its incense kindled at the Muse's flame.
Turn from the maddening crowd's ignoble Scythe
Their sober wishes never learned to stray:
Along the old requisition rule of life
They keep the noiseless tenor of their way.
Yet even their bones from insult to protect
Some frail memorial still erected rise:
With uncounted chimeras and shapeless sculpture decked.
Inspires the passing tribute of a sigh.

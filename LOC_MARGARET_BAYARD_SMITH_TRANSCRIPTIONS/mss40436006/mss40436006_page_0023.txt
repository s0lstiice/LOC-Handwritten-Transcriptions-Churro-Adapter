It is not the tear at this moment shed
When the cotile turf has just been laid in time
That can tell how beloved is the soul that died
Or how deep in our hearts we deplore him:
'Tis the tear thrown, many a long day swept
Through a life by his lap all shielded,
'Tis the sad remembrance fondly kept
When all other griefs are faded.
Oh! thus shall we mourn, his memory's light
While it shines through our hearts will impower them
Your worth shall look fairer, & truth more bright
When we think how he lived but to love them.
Like the gale, that sings along
Beds of oriental flowers,
In the grateful breath of song,
That once was heard in happier homes:
Filled with balm, the gale sighs on
Though the flowers have sunk in death;
So when Pleasure's dream is gone,
Its memory lives in music's breath!
Mine -- oh! how faint how weak

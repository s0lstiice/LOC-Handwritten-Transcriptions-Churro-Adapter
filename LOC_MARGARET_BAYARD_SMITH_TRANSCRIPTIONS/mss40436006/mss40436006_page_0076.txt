Of course, their religious places were surrounded with oak trees,

The writing of Price represent on the most lively colours the
ignorance, the errors and the uncertainty of the ancient philosophy with regard to the immortality of the soul. When
this is considered of carrying their arguments against the fear
of God, they conclude as an obvious though melancholy fact,
that the fatal stroke of our separation arises from
the calamities of life and that there can no longer exist
who can no longer exist. Yet they saw a few angels who found
a man exalted and consults about of human nature --
though their reason unassisted by revelation proved too weak to reach the sublime truth of immortality and
immigration prompted by vanity often misled them.
When they examine the actions of their mental powers
when they examine the various faculties of memory, fear
and judgment when they felt the desire of fame which
which transported them far beyond the bounds of death
and glory they felt unwilling to conform themselves
with the verdict of the fates, or to suppose that a being
of so dignified a nature could be confined to a narrow

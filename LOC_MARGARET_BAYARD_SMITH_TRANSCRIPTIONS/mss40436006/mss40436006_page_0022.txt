Oh breathe not his name let it rest in the shade
Shut out cold and unconcerned his cries are loud
And silent as dark be the tears that we shed
For the night dew that falls on the grave in his tomb
But the night due that falls through in silence I hope
Shall brighten with medicine the years where he sleeps
And the tears that we shed, though in secret it rolls,
Shall long keep his memory green in our souls –
To a beam on the face of the waters may glow
While the tide runs in darkness and darkness below
Is the light may be bright'd with a warm memory
Though the old heart be warm now darkly the soul
Has fatal remembrance, one sorrow that thaws
It shall shine like a star on our eyes
Or what life nothing darker or brighter can bring
For which joy has no calm I affix no nothing!
Oh! thus thought in the midst of enjoyment will stay
Like a dead, leafless branch in the summer brightest rays
The beams of the warm sun may round it in vain
It may smile in his light but it blows not again –
How dear to me the hour when twilight die
And sun-beams melt along the silent sea
For then sweet dreams of other days arise
And my memory recalls the other night to their
Shade as I watch the line of clouds that play
Along the smooth waves and the evening mist
I long to hear that golden breath of night
And think it would lead to some bright place of rest –
While gazing on the moon's light
At moment from her smile I turn'd
To look at still that more bright
In love and distant glory burn'd
But too far
Such peaceful star
To me to feel its warming flame
That more dear
That mild other
Which now our planet smiling owns
Thy many stars be thou mine own
If full brighter eyes unheeded play
I'll have those moon light alone
Which help my home, and guide my way
The day had sunk in slim showers
But midnight now, with lustre mock
Themselves all the purest flower
Like here that lightest moonlight check
Devoid while
The moon's smile
Play'd on a stream, in dimpling bliss
The moon looks
In many books
The book's can see no moon but this
And thus I thought over fortune's sun
For many a lover look to thee
While I feel there is but one
For many in the world for me

Themselves the shelter of an angel's wing;
Or expectant over things to which their time
Is met their dead, in silent water and gloom.
This poor but mighty, broken humanity
For whom Charity stands his the latest effusion of virtue
The lingering infant, struggling on her knee,
Does a his many millions owe tribute
No matter how quite spent his labour or instinct
He joy ennobled brothers and sisters meet,
In each for others welfare humble opinion:
The social home, amidst which might be heard
Each tells the stories that he sees and hears;

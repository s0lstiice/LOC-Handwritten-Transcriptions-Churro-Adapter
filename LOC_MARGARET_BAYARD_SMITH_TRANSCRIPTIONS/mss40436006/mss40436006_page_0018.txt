Far from my thoughts vain words would begin
Let one religious hour alone
Thine fleet and sense I would be free
And holy communion, Lord, with thee.
My heart grows warm with holy fire
My spirit finds with pure desire
To feel thine influence from above
And taste the bounties of thy love.
When I can say my God is mine
When I can see the glory shine
I'll tread the world beneath my feet
And all that men call evil I grant
Send comfort down from thy right hand
To cheer us in this barren land
And in thy temple let me see
A glimpse of love, a glimpse of thee.
But military thought, with boy's toy vigilance
My certain vigil on the post remains mine;
Commencing slowly with his ordinary guard
And moving on the following guard that he
Is done thoroughly before this time
Being in the rank organization I will
At this point midnight though the awful noon,
When on my bed, for watchful and faithful
Guard and unworried, while I await
With all over me, and in forgetfulness,
I only wait to watch the watch hour.
This light gives me to myself, I go to the guard
Of death I feel myself being on my behalf,
Now saying the words words of comfort,
He cannot now are few in the midst of life
They fail to do finish, yet a little
With the last dying particle will fall
And then, in silence, without whispering.
From them and thoughts of them and state
That will dictate the way. We have now
With a small portion of what men will leave
To hold communion, for even now the knife
The separating knife I feel divide
The two sides that kindle my soul to earth.
Do I must die I feel that I must die;
And though do me life been dark & dreary
I have hope for me has still not to deliver
That disappointment still promises for Handshamonts,
Yet I do feel my soul accord within me
Do I contemplate the dim twilight of death
The shuddering word, the awful blank eternity.

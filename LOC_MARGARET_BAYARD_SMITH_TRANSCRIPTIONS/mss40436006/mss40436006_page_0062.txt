I hear thee speak of the bitter land
Then callist its children a happy band
Molien, where is that enchant'd shore?
Shall we not seek it? I weep no more!
Is it where the flowers of the orange blow?
And the fine-fir cast through the myrtle boughs?
Not there, not there! my child?
Eye hath not seen it any gentle boy!
Ear hath not heard its deep songs of joy!
Deams cannot picture a world so fair,
Sorrow & death may not enter there;
Time does not tread on it, for life's bloom
Is beyond the clouds I beyond the tomb
It is there! it is there! my child
Where are ye with whom in life I started,
With companions of my golden days?
If one dead exchanged from one departed,
I have like mourning clouds, a thousand ways.
I have not seen your friend and brother
Has he received thee and on earth none other
Can be the void in my love broken still.
When is she whose looks were love's prettiest?
Love & gladness I no longer see!
She is gone & since that hour of sadness
Future seems her shadow to me.
Where am I? -- Life's current swiftly flows
Brings thee welcome warning of release;
Death with death, ah! Whither am I going?
Is it well -- my spirit parts in peace.

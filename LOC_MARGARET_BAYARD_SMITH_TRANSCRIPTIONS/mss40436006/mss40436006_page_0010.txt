At length, some fifty warmed the master's breast.
Times then his handhold first received a guest.
Long speaking opens the door, with jealous eye.
And bows he returns in the shadowing pair.
One funeral jagged light the naked walls.
Bird nations' frowned through their lumbus necks.
Point of the corset seat with eager care.
(That handily grafted) served them both to dine.
And, when the thousand spirit appeared to cease.
A unity reverencing bid them part in peace.
With still remark, the pondering baronet mused.
For one so sick, a long so poor and rude:
And why should not (with their tenacity in origin)
Love the old wealth a thousand years behind?
But, what new marks of wonder soon took place.
In every setting feature of his face.
When, from his cell, the young companion bore
That cup, the generous landlord's own'd before.
That paid profusely, with the gracious bowl.
The blasted brightness of this Christian soul:
But now, the clouds in airy tumult fly;
The sun, emerging, fixes an agonized eye.
And, glittering as they tremble, down the day.
The weather courts them, from the poor retreat:
And the last master bolts the weary gate.
With brave they watch, the pilgrims' dream wrought
With all the turmoil of uncertain thought.
The panting acts without their cause affright
Turn their a vice and swim'd a madness here.
Desiring that, and judging this, be goes.
Lost and confounded with the various shows.
Those nights dim shades again involve the sky.
Again the wanderers want a place to lie.
Again they search, and find a lodging near.
The cell improved arrived, the mansion next.
And neither poorly low, nor ugly great.

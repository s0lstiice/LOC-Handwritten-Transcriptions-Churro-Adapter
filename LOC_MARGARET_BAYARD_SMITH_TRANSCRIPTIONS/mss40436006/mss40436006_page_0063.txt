Let me to thy throne fly
While the waves of trouble roll,
While the tempest still is high:
Take me to my Saviour, trust,
Till the storm of life is past,
Safe into heaven guide
And receive my soul at last!
Other refuge have I none,
Strange as helpless soul in thee,
Lean on thy love me not alone
Still support and comfort me:
But my trust on thee is stayed
All my hopes from thee I bring
Over my defenceless head
With the standards of thy wing!
Then what the voice from heaven declares
To those in peril or die?
Relieved from all their earthly cares,
They'll still reign with him on high.
Then why lament departed friend,
Or shake at death's alarms?
'Tis but the voice that from heaven
Tells all men to his arms.
Thee dearest idol I have known
Whither that idol be
Help me to leave it from thy throne
And worship only thee!!
When mourning o'er some one I have,
Which comes all this was a friend
And from her voice her hand her smile
Levends me for a little while;
Then Saviour, greet the tears I shed
For those did I weep o'er Lazarus dead.

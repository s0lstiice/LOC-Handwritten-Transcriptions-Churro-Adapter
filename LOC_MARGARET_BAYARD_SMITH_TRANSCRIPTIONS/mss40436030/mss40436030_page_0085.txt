When ever you feel your spirits sinking pray look yourselves
up and write to me how out all your heart. Mine is large
enough to receive it and will tenderly sympathize in all your grief
Oh you can not hate as much as I do that we were never
together but we must submit to the will of God
I have not heard from Maria this great while but
it well I expect to visit her soon. I hope she will be equal
favored in on former occasions. I feel anxious but not
alarmed about her. Try and keep your own mind tranquil
I will give you early intelligence.
I hope my good husband is now with you enjoying your
cheerful presence with our dear little son by him. I am almost
impatient for a letter. It is well your letter came this morning.
made me I had counted on one from my husband.
Brother J. has not yet reached home. I had a few lines of
Petty this morning she is anxiously looking for him. Cornelia
and all her children returned to day so that Petty will
feel doubtly anxious for his husband I wish much to visit her
but I am so anxious to get all things so arranged that I can
leave home. Give my kind love to home and the girls to my
husband (if he is well and strong) and to cousin. Believe, affectionately I hope you
may your soon be well and happy. Your truly. J N.

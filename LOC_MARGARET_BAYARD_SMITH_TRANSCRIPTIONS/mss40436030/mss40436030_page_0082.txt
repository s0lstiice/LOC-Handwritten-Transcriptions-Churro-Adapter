Paris Adams is a most agreeable addition
to our society -- I have met her at Bath twice
& always convinced of her -- she is a woman of
fine talents, convenes often about 2 p.m. every
& is winning in her manner. I have had a
great deal of agreeable morning company, long
visits & rational conversation. Just good Genl
Bethmson passed one social evening as, after
Correa expressed to come in to save money
& we had a most agreeable interesting
& amusing philosophical chat -- chat.
I most earnestly hope nothing will prevent
Mr. Dear Mr Th, from paying his promised visit --
There are few papers in the world, my heart
would be so neglected to see. I think brother
ish stay 2 or 3 weeks longer, the moment our
only chamber is vacated, I shall be impatient
to he becomes its tenant, & I rather think
you will have a letter from Ann, that Mary
may come along. -- I wish as young fellows to wait
until June, but I am afraid Williams Patience
will not hold out. But I do not know, &
only conjecture their wishes. -- Did Steh give
Stat Julia this winter commanded young
lady -- She made he grand entrance, at
a splendid look given by Mrs Newville &
was voted me again last evening -- Mrs Middlesex
was very attentive to her & too in modesty
apprehension seemed to admire her -- She looked
annually pretty & behaved modestly, but not

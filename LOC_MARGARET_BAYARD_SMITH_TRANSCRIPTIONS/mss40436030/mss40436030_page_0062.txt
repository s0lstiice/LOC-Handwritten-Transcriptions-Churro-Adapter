Such reflections often lead me to the conclusion
that the doctrine of materialism is greater hate
than I wish it to be. — But I check a
train of thought which would rob me of the
soothing, the elevated, the sublime, you begin to
question the immortality of the soul. — The time
will come, yes I well hope that it will
be so, when leaving the chains of flesh
& sense & leaving this prison house I glory
its faculties with no more he employed a nerve
"When we shall see a heaven,
"All me damned, or wish'd heaven
"And even fewer, find sweet employ
"In that eternal world of joy."
"The glorious hour, oh let me die,
"I shall be near a tributary God
"And feel a sense no more controul
"The holy transports of my soul.
In that my faith would become more stable
in hopes more animated — my devotion more
ardent. But alas! I am at times, perhaps
from God — I am sometimes full of doubt &
doubting too as to the nature of my soul & the
purposes of its creator.—
Your last letter very beloved sister, which I
received this day which, while sitting alone in
my chamber, on a sad & gloomy & tumultuous
to you since.

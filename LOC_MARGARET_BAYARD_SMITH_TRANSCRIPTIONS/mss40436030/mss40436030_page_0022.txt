If I may I will write to you direct under this letter
having waited for more than a month for it I feel obliged
to state (I have been several times) I take no
part in public opinion and a mild and quiet abstinence
from any public work is best for me, for nothing
so effectual breaks the spirit as a long series of
public duties with so much care & attention as immediately
to put the matter of a letter and men out of
their way. I have not yet read the writings of
the late Mr. Gladstone, but I am sure he would
not have written a line to me, for nothing

Sidney. - Mar. 26-1816
I received your long look'd & long wish'd for letter
a week ago my dear sister & intended immediately
answering it I was preparing to describe to you the
health of our dear Boy, when he was suddenly
served with the bowel complaint, in so violent
a manner, that had it not been checked I do
not think he could have lived two days. I never
saw a child so instantly & intensely charged as
he was in two hours.- Every word of your dear
letter accorded to me, I thought I was about meeting
the same misfortune & I devoutly prayed to
our heavenly father to inspire me with that comfort
which I breathed through out your letters. - Oh it
is not till we loose or fear to loose an object
dear to us, that we feel all its value & all
our fondness for "blest joys brighten on they take
their flight." I immediately administered sanding
first one drop & in half an hour another
I boiled a piece of fat beef (having no matter
with a little sie) & stewed it & fed him with
it all day & night, giving him as much
of the oil as I could - The sanding am stuck'd
with the violence of the complaint & the oil of beef
carried it off, sat all that night

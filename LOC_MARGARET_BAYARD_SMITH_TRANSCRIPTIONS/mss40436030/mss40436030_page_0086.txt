Sunday 1st Feb. 1819
Until no longer want for letters from Birmingham
day after day for more than two weeks have
we expected a letter from Annie to her brother was
the last she mentioned that Jane was ill, but
I did not suppose her every ill as Dystheron supposed
coming on -- Every day have one been lucky
for him -- I hoped he would bring a large
packet of letters -- both Jane hopes, I have not
off writing; but we are now so anxious, that
I write merely to hear why none of your dear
family have written -- Every day we talk
of your -- wonder how you are to if Jane
has recovered, if Dystheron is not well, and
in how painful a suspense about those whom
we dearly love -- I beg some one of you
to write immediately on the receipt of this --
never to be so long silent again --
[As for us, we are thank a kind
friendess all well -- Dear little Annie is
I think better than she was before her illness,
we have again resumed our regular occupations
2 times Jasper cheerful -- I hope not accidently
by -- we have until the last 3 days had
most obightful weather, so warm that we could
often sit out the windows -- we improved this
chermony season by walking in and out every day,
finlly there would get be bad weather
prengn to break as active doors at wont --
Jane with the girls passed many pleasant
morning's with our friends in the city -- have
had more company than usual in the county --
GEORGIA
Mr Astley, Mr Wally, Mr Ousplant
Mr Buck, Mr Wally, Mr Ousplant

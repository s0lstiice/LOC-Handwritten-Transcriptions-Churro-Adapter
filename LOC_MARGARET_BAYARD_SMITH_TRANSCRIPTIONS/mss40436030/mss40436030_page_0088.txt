with cheerful Hope.
I folded my letter before the opposite page was
dry & have blotted my letter sodden had time
to write another, I would not send so slovenly
a one -- Mrs, & I shall join you in the most
affectual wishes & hopes of good news &
that save from those we all so dearly love
your innocent kindness. MRS.

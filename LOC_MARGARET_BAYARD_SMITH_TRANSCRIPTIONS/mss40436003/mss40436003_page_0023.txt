Thursday
I spent most of the morning in
reading Haysells History - the
port I am now reading is ex-
ternally interesting. In the
afternoon Mama, Uncle, Susan
& myself took a delightful ride
and Ellison spent the evening
with us. Friday
Mama & myself went to the City,
we went to see Mrs Bishop, Mrs
Clarke, Mrs Seaton & Mrs Thorton
& Mrs Newell - I received a
long letter from Rose Wait. Miss
Buean her cousin, James Dougherty
& James William spent the evening
with us - Saturday
I was busy all the morning do-
ing Stockings, that most delightful
occupation - In the afternoon I
commenced reading the "Memoirs
of Madame Genlis" there is two
other volumes - Mr Ruggles and
Mr Boulware spent the afternoon
writing with us - I did not go
down until tea time.
Sunday 12th
Mama was very well I did not
go to church, Sister A. J. & myself
went. William Ellis & Frank
Taylor came out while we were
at dinner & spent the afternoon -
Mr Strastou & Mr Bochran also
came out. After tea I read aloud
the book of "Get" to Mama.
Monday
I read "Madame Genlis Memoirs"
aloud to Mama most of the morn-
ing - Catherine Smith & D. Batts came
in to tea as for a short time in the
afternoon - I read & served in the
evening.

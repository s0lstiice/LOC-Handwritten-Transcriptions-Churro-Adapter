Saturday
It rained all the morning
but cleared up before dinner.
we had a visit from old Mr
Brackenridge. - we quitted all
the morning. I read Madame
de Genlis in the afternoon, &
finished it after tea; it is a
book with which I am very
much pleased. - I found it
interesting & very amusing.
(Sunday 10th)
We all went in to church and
heard a Mr Smith (from Phila-
delphia) preach. - if he is generally liked, it is probable he will
be called - he is quite a young man,
we were all very much pleased
with him. After church I returned
home with Helen - Hettys does not
seem well at all - she intends going
next week - Helen & the captain.
came out with me in the afternoon in their
carriage - they could not stay to
tea - Mama & the girls went in
to the City to hear Mr Blagden -
Mr Ellison James Toughey spent
the evening with us. Mr E. Trill as
of Mrs Abraham Bradley's death -
(Monday)
I was busy quilting until two. Mama
visited Mrs Julia's Bayard went in to
Mrs Bradley's funeral - It was after
five when they returned & just
then Mrs St. Smith's carriage drove
up to the door - They stayed but a
very short time I wrote a very long letter to
Rose Whit (Tuesday)
We began another quilting & as it was
a rainy day, we were not interrupted & quitted all the morning and
evening. (Wednesday)
Another rainy day! There seems an
end to the rain - I have a read.

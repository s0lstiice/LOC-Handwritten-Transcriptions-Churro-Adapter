it is the first snow we have

I wrote a letter to E. Boyd,
and copied some for Papa.
I went abroad in the evening.
Tuesday
Went abroad in most of the
morning. In the afternoon
When I walked -- She
spent the evening with us
as did, also Mr King, Mr
H. Smith & W. Eliot.
Wednesday
A very rainy day -- I served all
the morning on my place in
fine dress -- I went abroad in
the evening. + 8th
Thursday
A very fine day -- I served all
the morning on my dress -- In
the afternoon Bayard & I went
to see E. Smith -- Mrs Stanton
& Mrs Smith, Mrs Graham
& Bolly balls spent the evening
Friday
I served until one o'clock --
When I went with Papa &
Maria to pay some visits --
Mr Hillbach brought our
piano home -- we were
all delighted again to have
it, it seems to be in excellent
order -- Susan, H.B. & I walked
in the afternoon -- I spent the
evening at Mrs Hewell's --

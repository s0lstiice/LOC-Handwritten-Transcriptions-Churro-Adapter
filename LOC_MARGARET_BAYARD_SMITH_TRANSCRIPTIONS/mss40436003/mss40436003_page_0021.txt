Julia knew my likeness immediately & thought it a very excellent one -- Mr. King presented it to me and as it was finished he brought it home. Uncle was very much pleased with Mr. King & his paintings. Julia returned home -- I was occupied all the morning copying music. I read the "Barkeret" -- in the afternoon.
Friday I was very busy during all the morning -- after dinner, myself, my sister Anna, Julia & myself rode over to see Mrs. Magy & Mrs. Ingham -- but they had all left the country & returned to the City -- after I finished the "Barkeret" -- I found it much more interesting than I expected.
Saturday Uncle & I went to the City -- we went with Papa to Georgetown -- on our return I went to see Betty & Helen & stayed with them until three o'clock -- when I went to see Mrs. Houston for a short time -- after dinner I went to see Miss Queen & old Mrs. Williams. I commenced reading the "Mechanics of the Prince's Sham Battle".
Sunday -- 6th We all went up to Rockbridge Church, where we heard Mr. Breckenridge preach a funeral sermon -- the Church was quite crowded -- a number of the gentlemen had to stand.

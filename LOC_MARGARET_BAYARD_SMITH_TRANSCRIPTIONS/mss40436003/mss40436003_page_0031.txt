Thursday
A bright, brilliant day! which
I was very glad to see. For an
hour & a half after breakfast I
practiced - then sewed, wrote &
read until dinner - I finished
the second volume of Horsell's
Ancient History". After dinner we
went out to see Mrs Hand, who has
lately returned from New England
she has a very fine baby & is very
well - we returned home to tea
after which I read "Coleraine"
aloud, we finished it. I like
it very much indeed, the inter-
est is kept up throughout - and
though rather romantic for
the present day. I like it very
much & think on the whole it
is a very well written book.
Wednesday
A very stormy day. I cleaned up in
the afternoon - I commenced reading
"Patience", a novel by Miss
Edgeworth - I read aloud all
the evening.
Thursday
A very fine day - Bagard went
to the Races - Mrs Agg. Mrs
Blackford & Mr Ellison spent
the evening with us. Mr & Mrs
Hand paid us a short visit
in the afternoon.
Friday
I was busy most of the morning
clean stocking. I read "Patience"
aloud in the afternoon & evening.
Saturday
The weather still continues
very fine - for a wonder! I went
to the bits & spent the morning
with Helen - very pleasantly.

Saturday
Another delightful full day --
Susan, Helen & myself went
a short swing about one o'clock --
Mr Wood came to see us in
the afternoon & Mr Ellison
in the evening.
Sunday
Susan, Helen, Lloyd & myself
walked down to hear Mr
John's preach in the morn-
ing -- I received a letter from
Ketty -- In the afternoon we
went to our own church -- Gen
& Mrs Macomb came from
from church with us & said
to us --
Monday
A very cloudy day -- we were
very busy during all day -- Mrs
Ferstond & Julie paid us quite a
long visit -- Mrs Branch had
a large party but as the evening
was so bad we did not go.
Tuesday
Another very disagreeable day,
I was busy writing most of the
morning -- I had sketches of a
naval life -- all the afternoon --

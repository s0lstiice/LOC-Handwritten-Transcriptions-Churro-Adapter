In the afternoon, Miss Lydia,
Susan & I took a walk and
had a chat visit at Mr
Doughty's - Thursday
I was busy most of the morn-
ing making cake - Betty &
the captain rode out to sea
in the afternoon -
Friday
I was very busy seeing all the morn-
ing - spent the practiced afternoon
I worked in my watch guard in the
evening - Susan & myself went ashore
dinner to see Mrs Dunn out but did
not stay to tea.
Saturday
I went & read in the morning -
George Burford came in the afternoon
& stayed all night, he brought
me a note from Louisa, begging
me to come & stay a week with her
on Ruggles & the Boulevard, spent
the evening with us.
Sunday 15th August
We went to our church as usual & heard Mr Bolton preach.
after church we went to Mrs
Thomson's - Young Mrs Blackford
paid us a short visit in the
afternoon - James Doughty spent the
evening with us.
Monday
I was busy until 11 o'clock
making cake - at which time it
commenced raining & ran it
all the afternoon we had no
company of course.
Tuesday
It rained nearly all the morning -
we had a long visit from old Mr

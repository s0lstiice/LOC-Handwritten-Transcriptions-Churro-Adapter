Sunday 24th July
We went to church as usual, but
had a very indifferent sermon.
Mr Smith walked to and from
towns with us after church;
where we saw Mrs. Bemford
Louisa -- we received a letter
from Mama saying that she
would probably be with us
in the afternoon -- the carriage
went in for her immediately
after dinner. Mr Smith rose
but I spent the afternoon &
evening with us -- Mama and
Susan got home about six.
We were all very glad to
see them home again, cousin
Julia was better. A very pleasant
day -- The moon is ten 85 --
Monday
I was very busy all the morning
breaking ads. -- In the afternoon
I read "Lady Morgan" and took
a very pleasant walk -- Capt. and
Mrs Doug. L., Miss Mann and
James spent the evening with us.
The city was very quiet, there
was less dinners, a ball and a
fallen queen.
Tuesday
Mrs. Thornton & Mrs. Berdican spent
the day with us -- we read aloud
"The Rivels" quite an interesting
tale -- it was quite warm, but
we had a very pleasant day.
Wednesday
I read nearly all the morning
finished Lady Morgan's France
it is quite an interesting work.

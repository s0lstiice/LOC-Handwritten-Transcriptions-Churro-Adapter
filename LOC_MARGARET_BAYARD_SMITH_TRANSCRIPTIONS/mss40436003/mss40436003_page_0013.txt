dusk. Sunday - 25 July
Helen and myself went to St Johns
church -- we found it very warm --
This has been a very long warm
spell -- we have heard of a
number of deaths occasioned
by the heat. I received a long
letter from E. Boyd. We took a
very pleasant walk in the evening.
Monday
I went to see the Randolphs in
the morning -- Mama came for
me in the evening & I returned
home after having had a most
agreeable visit.
Tuesday
I copied several things for
Mama & wrote in my journal
which occupied me until
dinner. Mr. & Mrs. Agg paid as
a visit in the afternoon, but
did not stay to tea -- Mr & Mrs
Ingham, Mr & Mrs Clarke and
Mr H. Smith spent the after-
noon & evening with us. Mr
S. stayed very late.
Wednesday
I was busy serving & reading
Rapel until dinner -- Mrs
Thomson, her mother & Mr
Higgins spent the afternoon and
evening with us -- Mr H. was
quite agreeable -- he asked me
if I would come & sit for him
as he wished to take my life --
I made it a present to him --
of course accepted his offer --
Thursday
I was busy serving & reading all
the morning --

Thursday
I went into Mr Russell's
about twelve - I was detain-
ed there by a violent storm
- all day.
Friday
I was out shopping, visiting
most of the morning - In
the afternoon went to see
the hand of Mr's Hettle -
Helen & Lloyd spent the
evening with us.
Saturday
I was busy during most of the
morning - In the afternoon I
practiced - Mrs Swerden, Mr
Baggles, Dr Collins, Mr Ellison
& Mr Wood spent the evening
with us - I had a dreadful
tooth-ache all the evening
therefore did not enjoy myself
very much -
Tuesday 19th.
A dismal rainy day, I did
not go to church but read
& wrote all the morning -

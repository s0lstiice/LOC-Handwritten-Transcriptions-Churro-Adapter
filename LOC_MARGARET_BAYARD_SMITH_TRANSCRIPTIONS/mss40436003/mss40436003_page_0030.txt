Thursday
Still raining, stifling! - however
even as we were very much
interested in "Old Mortality,"
& our work. The time passed
very pleasantly.
Friday
A dear day at last! Mama
& Julia went to the City.
We had a long visit from Mr
Wood - Esther Smith sent us
out "Blarence" - a new novel by
Mrs Settgenie - we commenced
reading it after tea.
Saturday
A most delightful day. I went to
the City. I went to see Louise
& her cousin Miss Baldwin
who has lately arrived from New
Haven - she appears to be a very
pleasing girl & I think I shall like
her very much - when I left Helena
I did a little shopping & spent
the rest of the morning with Aunt
Shannon. We read "Blarence," about
in the evening.
Sunday 27th.
William was so sick, he was not able
to drive - We then for all walked
up to Rock buck church. Where we
heard the Ash preach a very interest-
ing sermon - the day was very
damp & unpleasant & of course our
walk was not very pleasant. I wrote
in my journal all the afternoon.
Mr Ellison spent the evening with us.
Monday
Another dismal rainy day. I worked
& practiced in the morning & read
"Blarence" aloud in the evening.
Received a long note from Helen.

Thursday
I commenced Nipps Ancient
History, & read it for two hours,
& then read the Memoirs of
Madame Montenton until
diviner time - Esther & Catherine
Smith rode out in the after-
noon; Mr Wood & Mr Kend
spent the evening with.
Friday
I finished Madame de Monte-
non, which I liked very much
after dinner read "The Wild
Irish Girl," along to Mama, I
also finished that, it is very
interesting - no company.
Saturday
I went into Miss Stewart's to
spend a few days - we took a
ride in the afternoon, had
no company in the evening -
Tuesday - 11th
I went with Mr F. to St. Johns
church - Mama went home
with Mrs Bemford to dine, &
Louisa stayed with us. In the
afternoon we went up to Rab-
orama, Mama returned with
us & stayed all night.
Monday
I wrote a letter to C. Boyd, after
breakfast - at twelve Mrs J.
Mann & I went to Miss Scaturs,
they all seem very glad that
Julia has returned home -
I spent the day with her in
the afternoon she returned
with me to Miss Towns -
where we found Louisa, We
spent the evening with us.
Tuesday
Quite a rainy morning, I was busy
sewing all the morning making
a new dress - we rode in the
afternoon - In the evening Mr

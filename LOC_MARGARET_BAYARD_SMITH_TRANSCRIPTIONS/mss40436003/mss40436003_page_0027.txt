Immediately after dinner
Mr Ellison & James Doughty
came to walk with us.
We went up to Mrs Houghton's
The Ladies had all gone
out - Mr Ellison was in
very fine spirits, in fact
we were all very gay. in
our walk we met with a
very pretty little spring, near
which was a rustic seat on
which we rested ourselves
while we drank from the
limpid stream. It would
have been very romantic
had we been in nine pensive
mood - We returned home to
tea, after having had a very
agreeable walk.
Wednesday
Susan & I went to the City in
the morning. We went to see
Mrs Lamb from Boston - Esther
Smith, Mrs Thorne, Misses
Thorn & The Merrills. Mr & Mrs
Browning & Miss Benson
came out in the morning. I
received a long letter from E.P.
B--
Thursday
Mama, Uncle & Julia went to
the City. I read & served most
of the morning - Mrs Beale paid
us quite a long visit. Directly
after dinner the two Mrs
Yosts & Mr L. Hardell came
out. They stayed until after
my clock - we had a very
pleasant evening.

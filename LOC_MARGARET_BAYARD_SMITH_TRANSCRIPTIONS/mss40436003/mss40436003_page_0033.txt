Helen, Mr L. Hewell & myself
took a delightful walk in the
afternoon. Friday
Helen & I went on the Avenue
in the morning & went to see
Mrs Stone &c returned
home with Papa. At sun-
set Susan, Julia, Bayard
& myself rode up to Mrs Aggs
young Mrs Blackford gave
us home very sweet music
We spent a very agreeable eve-
ning -- on our return home
we met Mrs Ellison & James
Doughty, who had been at
our house & came up to
walk home with us -- we
had a very merry walk home.
Saturday
I was reading "Paternage," nearly
all the morning -- I finished
it, & like it very much -- Sister
Ann, Susan, Bayard & myself
spent the evening at Mrs
McKinnis. I received a letter
from Rose Wilt yesterday.
Sunday 31st
We all came into church as
usual & heard Mr Smiths
preach -- after church we went
to Mrs Thornton's where I saw
Louise -- Mr Ellison spent the
evening with us.
Monday 1st November
We came out into the city as
we about leaving the house
cleaned -- she said all night
with Mrs Thornton. We were
busy all the morning preparing
for moving -- no company --

Monday 8th.
As I was not quite well I
did not go to church -- Maria
went to see poor Mrs Ransom
in the afternoon.
Tuesday
I was busy sewing until two
o'clock, then Mad Russell's
History stood to arrange.
Monday, Papa & Susan went
to see Mrs Ingham in the after-
noon; Julia went over to Mrs
Doughty's & stayed to tea -- when
the returned home at nine
Mrs Doughty, Miss Maria the
two young gentlemen, Mr
Hoad & Mr Ellison accompanied
her & stayed with us
until near eleven.
Tuesday
A very pleasant city day, I
went to the City again to sit
I sat in from nine until one --
the City was particularly agreeable
I then went to see Mrs Hunter
for a short time -- after which
I went to Mrs Newell's -- When
Papa called for me, I was
surprised to see Uncle John
from Philadelphia in the door-
frame -- as there was no room
for me, I stayed with the girls.
late in the afternoon Helen &
I took a walk; Betty & the boys
went in the evening to a
concert, Helen & I were too
much fatigued with our
walk -- Wednesday
We spent our morning very
pleasantly in sewing & convers-
ing -- Papa called for me at
three & returned home, the
girls wanted me very much
to stay longer with them.

Sidney July 1st 1830
Thursday
A very warm day I spent my
morning in reading, writing
& reading Lady Morgan's France.
The Ball finished paid us a short
visit in the afternoon, and the
two Mrs Blackford's spent the
evening with us. Young Mrs B-
played & sang a great deal for
which I had quite a pleasant evening.
Friday
We all went to the city, Miss
Ann & Julia spent the day with
Mrs Gilles. I went to see E Smith
& Mrs Howell. I was very glad
to see Kitty, after so long an
absence. I spent most of the morn-
ing with her. I received a letter
from E. Boyd -- we had no
company -- a very warm day.

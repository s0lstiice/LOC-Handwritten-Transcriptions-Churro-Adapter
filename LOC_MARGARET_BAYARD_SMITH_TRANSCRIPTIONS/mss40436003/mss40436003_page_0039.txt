Saturday
Mrs B. Belt stayed with us
until after dinner - Mrs Gillespie
spent the day with us. Hettie
came in after dinner - Mr
King spent part of the evening
with us.
Sunday
Another dismal day! I never
knew any weather like this.
We all went to church and
heard Mr Smith preach.
Mama & the girls went in
the afternoon but I did not.
Helen came & stayed most of
the evening.
Monday
It rained heavily all day. I was
bush all the morning making
cake - Mr Wood & Mr Bulfinch
came in after tea.
Tuesday
It rained heavily all the morn-
ing. I went & served until din-
ner -
Wednesday
I was very busy serving in my
silk dress all the morning. In
the afternoon I took a short walk
with Hettie -
Thursday

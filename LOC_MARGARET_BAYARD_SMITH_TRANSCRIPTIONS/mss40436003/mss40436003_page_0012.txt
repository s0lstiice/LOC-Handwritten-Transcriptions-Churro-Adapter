Batch preach - after church
we went to Mrs Thomson's
& Mrs Howell's - The girls
wanted me to stay with them
very much. We had no
Company I fasted a long night - 15th
Monday
Very busy during all the morning. I had the Duchess de la
Pallière aboard to remain in
the afternoon; the weather
still continued very warm.
Tuesday
I sewed & read "Raphael"; before
sunrise. In the afternoon I
practised & read "Divergent"
Manus & Susan rode down
to the Mary Road & brought
Mrs Colston & Mrs Tucker home
with us - the Thermometer has
been very high 88 - in the Shade -
Wednesday
I went into the City with Pope,
& spent the day with Hattly & Sister
Hattly & Mrs R. Howell brought
me home (in the carriage) in
the afternoon - they stayed to
tea with - I spent a very pleasant day.
Mrs Ellison came to visit us.
Thursday
I read Haggard's "The King" nearly all
the morning - Mama & the ladies
rode in the afternoon - I read "Dev-
erness" - In the evening I played
backgammon with Hazard.
Friday
I sewed Ladies' Kuffs during the
morning - I read "Divergent", in the afternoon I received a note from Hattly
begging me to come in.
Saturday
Went into the City in the morning
to stay with Hattly until Sunday afternoon - We spent our morning,

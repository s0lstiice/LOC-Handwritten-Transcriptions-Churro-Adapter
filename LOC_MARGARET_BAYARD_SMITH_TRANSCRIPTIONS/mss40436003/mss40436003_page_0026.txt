Thursday
I espied music most of the
morning. Mrs Lanch came to
see Mrs Bonford & Mrs Stagg
Miss Mechlin. Bayard came
up to dinner -- soon after which we
went to see the Houses. The event
the walk is quite short; we
spent a very pleasant evening.
Friday
It rained all day. Louise & I did
not like it much as we had
intended to pay several visits
that morning. I served, copied
music & read aloud in the morn-
ing; I read all the evening.
Saturday
A very damp, cloudy day, it rained
mostly all the time, we had no
company of course -- I read aloud
all the afternoon & evening.
Sunday 26th
We went to church in the morn-
ing, & to Mrs Thorton's, where I
saw Warren & the girls. In the
afternoon I returned home, after
being about twelve days, and
having had a delightful visit.
Mrs Bonford, Louise & Ruth returned
with me, they stayed to tea.
Monday
I was busy writing & sewing all
the morning. Mrs Ellison spent
the evening with us.
Tuesday
I was sleeping all the morn-
ing in espying music.

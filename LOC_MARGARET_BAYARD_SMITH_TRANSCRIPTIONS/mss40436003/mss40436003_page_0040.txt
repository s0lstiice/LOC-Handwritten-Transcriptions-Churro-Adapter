Friday
I was out most of the morn-
ing visiting & shopping - Mrs
Houston, Miss Brodie, Mrs
Temple & Mr Ellison
spent the evening with us
it was very agreeable, and I
appeared to be a very sweet woman.
Saturday
I was busy during until two
when we all went to our
new museum, accompanied
by Mrs Houston, Mr Hewett
& Mr Wood. We were quite
surprised to find that in so
short a time, they had formed
so good & interesting a
collection. In the after-
noon Helen & I took a walk.
I returned home with her
& spent the evening - it was
as usual very agreeable time.
Sunday 6th.
The girls, Helen & myself
went to Mr John's church
the day was very unpleasant
& I did not go out in the
afternoon - Bapt. Dougherty &
James came in, but did
not stay long after tea - I
read "Beck's Remains," & the
Bible all the evening.

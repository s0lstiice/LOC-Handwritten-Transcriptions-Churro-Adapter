Saturday
I sweated most of the morn-
ing, finished my new deck
In the afternoon read the
new number of the "Museum".
Miss Lydia English & Mr Wood
spent the evening with us
Sunday 12th.
A very pleasant day. Mama
Julie, Beyond myself went
to the Unitarian Church,
Mr Palfrey gave us an ex-
cellent sermon - after ser-
vice we went to see Mrs
Seaton. I wrote in my
journal & read all the
afternoon, the rest went
to church. Mama & myself
spent the evening at Mrs Thra-
wn'd, as she sent for us. No
ling was there.
Monday
I was damp & cloudy day. I wrote
until one o'clock then went
with Mama to pay some visits in
Genessee town. It rained all the
afternoon I practiced until
near dark. Lloyd Russell spent
part of the evening with us. after
which we read aloud "Maurice
& Berthea" - it is very interesting
Tuesday
A dismal rainy day! I was very
much occupied in doing all
the morning - Mr Ellison paid
us a long visit - Mr Taylor and
his son spent part of the evening
with us
Wednesday
A clear cold day! Mama, Julia
& myself, went up to Holorama

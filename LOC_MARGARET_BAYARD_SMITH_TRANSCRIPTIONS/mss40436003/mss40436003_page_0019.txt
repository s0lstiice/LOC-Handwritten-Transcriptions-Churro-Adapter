Monday
Holly brought Susan home and
spent the day with us. The
Captain came out in the after-
noon, they left us before dark -
Tuesday
I was very busy all the morning
making coke - Julie Sexton came
out with Papa to spend some
days with us. Bayard went to
Halorama to George & stayed all
night - Mr Ellison spent the
evening with us. Mrs Agg & Mrs
Blackford paid us a short visit
in the afternoon -
Wednesday
Julie Sexton played & sang a good
deal, then wrote a letter while I
copied music - Some effort divine
Mr Ellison came for Julie to take
a ride on horseback, (as had been
agreed the night before) Bayard
accompanied them - they returned
home just before dark - Julie
sung & played a great deal for
us after that -
Thursday
About twelve o'clock Mrs Sexton
sent out a servant & gig for Julie -
We were very sorry to part with
her & her going was very un-
expected. Matron, Sister Jennie & Julia
sat the evening with Mrs Houston -
Miss F. Flanagan & her brother Randall
& Mrs Thomas, rode out soon after
divine & spent the afternoon -
Friday
I was busy during most of the morn-
ing - Matron & Uncle John rode in the
afternoon, Susan, Bayard & Julie
spent the evening at Mrs Agg's -

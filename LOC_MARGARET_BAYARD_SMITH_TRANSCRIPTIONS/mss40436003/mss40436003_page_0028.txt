Friday Oct 8th
I commenced making a pair
of cordets I was very busy
during all the morning
In the afternoon we took a
very pleasant walk. Mr
Ruggles & Mr Word spent
the evening with us.
Saturday
I finished my ensets at
three o'clock. In the after-
noon took a short walk &
was here 7 o'clock. No company
Sunday 3rd
We all went to church at usual
& heard the Colton preach a very
good sermon. after church we
went to Mrs Thornson's.
Monday
Mrs Deane came down to see her
as -- We commenced the quilting
of the breakfast & were all very busy
at it all the morning. Mrs Laph
& her brother Mr Elliot paid us
a short visit in the afternoon
Mr & Mrs Smith & Miss Ryan
spent the evening with us.
Tuesday
Sister Ann & I went to the City. we
went to see Mrs Gillis, Mrs Thom-
son & the Newells. I letty was
very busy preparing to go to Phila-
delphia. No company
Wednesday
We were busy quilting all the
morning & evening. It is very
pleasant work I think.
Thursday
A very fine day. We were occupied
again the whole day quilting.
Friday
A very rainy day, we had a fine
time for work & began another
quilt. Mama read aloud to us

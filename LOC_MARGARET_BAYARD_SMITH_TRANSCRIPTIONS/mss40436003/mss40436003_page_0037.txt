Monday
I was busy all the morning
making cake -- bed -- and Born-
ford, Lucy, Louise, Ruth, Helen,
and Mr Hagan & I have spent
the evening with us -- We
had a very gay & agreeable eve-
ning -- Mr Lyon also spent the
evening, he told us that he
intended to leave Washing ton
the next day -- as I was very
anxious, I sent him to take a letter to Mr
Wit for me -- I sat up after
the family had all retired
to rest & wrote quite a long
letter --
Tuesday
A very delightful day -- Uncle
was so much more that he
had to keep his room -- Mr
Lyon came to bed as far well.
I went shopping with Helly &
Helen -- Sister Ann, Susan, Bay-
ard & myself went down & spent
the evening with Mrs Gillis
& her little -- the both very
well & seems very happy --
We had quite a pleasant
evening, Mr Ellison who we
sent rode home with us.
Wednesday
The weather is now delightful
but it is too warm for it to
last long, I am afraid -- Uncle
still keeps his room & the doctor
attends him --

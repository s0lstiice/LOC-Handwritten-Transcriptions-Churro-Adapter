De Jones, who is an old acquaintance of Buck's, arrived with us.
Monday
I saved part of the morning & then read the Prince's Lamballie -- This great revolution in France has excited a great deal of excitement throughout our country -- Bayard read us a long account of it in the evening -- It is supposed the present King will abdicite his Throne -- We are all very anxious to hear further accounts from Paris. -- X
Tuesday
Sister & Julia went to the City I was very busy during until two, after which I read the
Prince's Lamballie -- in the afternoon I find it -- it is extremely interesting & I am very much pleased with it. It carried very distinctly all the afternoon & evening.
Wednesday
I read all the morning Russell's Ancient History -- It was very interesting. I read all the afternoon after -- I read of the celebrated battle of Marathon -- Thermopylae -- Salamis -- Platæa & Mycale -- in all of which the Persians conquered the Persians -- we had another rainstorm, it commenced at three & rained in torrents until sunset -- then rained gently all night.

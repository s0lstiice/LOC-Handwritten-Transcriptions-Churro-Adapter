Friday
Mama, Sister & myself went to
the City in the morning - just
as we were going, George &
James Bonfort came to spend
the day - I went to see Helen
& at twelve o'clock went to Mr
King's to sit - In the afternoon
Saban played a good deal
for George & James - just as
we sat down to tea, all the
Doughty's came over & spent
the evening - we had a very
agreeable evening.
Saturday
I slept & read all the morn-
ing - In the afternoon we took
a ride & then went to Mrs
Agg's where we spent a very
pleasant evening - I had a
very lightful ride home by
moonlight -
Sunday - 1st August.
They all went to church
except me as it was my turn
to stay home - there was a
thunder gath in the afternoon
& a great deal of rain for a
short time. Mr Ellison spent
the evening with us.
Monday
Sister A. & Sister went to the
City - I made cake - then sewed
2nd Mad Hassel until dinner - read
"Vicilia" in the afternoon - it
rained all the afternoon & evening.
Tuesday
It rained nearly all day - I went
to Rose Wait - Mama rode into
the City in the afternoon and
brought the two Misses Randolph
out to spend a few days with us.
Mr & Mrs Agg & Mrs Delanti spent
the evening with us -

Thursday
I was very much occupied
all the morning drawing - Mr
King, Helen & Mrs Wood spent
the evening with us.
Friday
A dismal rainy day! I spent
the whole morning & afternoon
on my desk which I finished.
Saturday
Miss Lydia English spent the
morning with us -- I went
to the church with
other visitors came, when I

James Doughty came in the
afternoon & stayed to tea but
could not spend the evening
Mama, Papa, Bayard & myself
went to a party at Mrs Smith-

part brilliant & pleasant.

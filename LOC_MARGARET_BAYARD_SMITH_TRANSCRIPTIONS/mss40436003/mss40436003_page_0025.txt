Saturday
In the morning I practiced, sun-
ed & read aloud, we took a
long walk in the afternoon -
as we had no company we
read aloud in the evening.
Sunday 19th
Mrs B. Louise & myself went to
Dr Lannie's church; after which
we went to Mrs Thoutou's, I
saw mama & the girls. Bayard
returned to Helena with us.
In the afternoon Mrs B. Louise
Benita, Bayard, Henry & myself
walked down to the river & jump-
ed we returned home to tea.
Monday
Bayard left us after breakfast -
I practiced, sewed & read aloud in
the morning - In the afternoon
we went down to Mrs Lull's we
stayed to tea but returned before nine
o'clock - we have had fine sunshine
today
I applied myself for some time
Louis & I took a long delightful
ramble in the woods - The Misses
Van Bliks came up about one
o'clock & spent the afternoon &
evening - from office dinner
Helen & Lady walked up, Mrs
Hall & Mechlin & Bayard also
came & spent the evening. We all
walked in the garden & gathered
beautiful bouquets, then returned
to the drawing room & had some
music - after tea Louise & I
played cottillions & we danced
we then played some very amusing
games - away over dinner
to enjoy themselves & I cannot
spend a delightful evening.
Wednesday
I still continue very cold I spent
the morning in enjoying music &
reading aloud to Miss Bonford

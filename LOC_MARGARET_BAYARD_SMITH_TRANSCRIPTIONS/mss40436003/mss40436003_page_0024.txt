Tuesday
I read Madame Feuilis aloud to
Beame until one o'clock - then
stood until dinner, in the after-
noon I evening I read Mad Feuilis.
Wednesday
A very fine day, at length I went to
pay my long intended visit to Hakan
I went for a few minutes to see
Helen before I went to H. I was
very much surprised when she
told me that Lloyd had re-
turned home. Louise seemed
very glad to see me, we spent
our morning in reading & sewing.
In the afternoon Mrs B. Louise
& myself went to the City, we
spent the evening at Mrs Stan-
blocks - we spent quite an agreeable
evening, Dr. Hall was there, he
lent me the Romance of the City,
by Steele -
Thursday (The next)
I practiced after breakfast for an
hour, then read to Mrs B. for
some time Romance of History -
We spent the evening at Mrs W.
Jones's by invitation, there was
a good deal of company, I we-
spent a very merry & pleasant eve-
ning.
Friday
There has been such a change
in the, & it is so cold, we can
scarcely keep ourselves warm -
After breakfast Louise & I took
a walk & went to the Mill to be
weighed, I was very much aston-
ished when I found that I
weighed 110! - we then went to
see Miss Davis; she lent us some
very pretty music. I practiced after
dinner I went aloud in the
evening - We are very much
pleased with the Romance of
History -

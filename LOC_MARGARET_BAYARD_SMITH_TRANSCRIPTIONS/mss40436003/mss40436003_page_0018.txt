In the afternoon Mr English came
for Miss Lydia, we were all very
happy is part with her.
Wednesday
A very fine day. The rain has made
a great change in the weather. It is
quite cool now & very much like
Autumn. Jason & I went over in the
morning to Mrs Dougltys, we had
quite a pleasant visit. We invited
them to spend the evening with us
which they all did, also the Misses
Queen, Mrs Hard & Mrs Ellison.
Thursday
I went to the City in the morning.
I first went to the Hattys & Helen, then
Mrs Thornton, Ben King, did some
shopping, & spent the rest of the
morning very agreeably at Mrs
Leaton's -- Mr & Mrs Clarke, Mrs
Thompson, Mrs Williams & De Colliis
spent the afternoon with us.
In the evening I began to work a purse.
Friday
Mama went to the City in the morn-
ing. I went to E. Boyd. I read
"Franklin," in the afternoon -- no
company -- Saturday
Susan & myself went to the City in
the morning -- I went to Mr. Thig's
& sat for an hour -- we spent
most of the morning with Kitty.
Susan did not return home, but
agreed to stay at Mrs. Russell's
until Monday -- Mrs. Thorton
& her mother spent the afternoon
with us.
Sunday 22nd

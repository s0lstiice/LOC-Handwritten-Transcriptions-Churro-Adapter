Elizabeth since she has been here, has been in poor
health & not in bad health though constantly very
restless. Her journey to Pittsburg will not give
her strength. Maria desires to be most patient
to you & regrets it is entirely out of her power & is
to you a request a single page arrangement & not action
from me but a kind of sending a nervous affection which
she does not receive from the whole ensuing day.
She has just come out of her room -- she looks pale & fatigued
but expects riding into the city with Elizabeth until she has
some writing with me the post would give me -- for it was all
for me to receive the fine -- I have told you all I had to
say that I imagined would interest you -- now pray write soon &
the same -- tell me all about yourself & your house -- your
grape & flowers -- every thing that interests you will interest
you. I am affectionately yours MHS and
your affectionate sister MHS

in gospel truth, never to feel unkindness to those who

to confirm my heart to my life to the same gospel standard, without
which in faith & practice I never dare to hope never shall reach
for other foundation than the revealed will of God. These sincere
endeavours are mine -- but alas they are made & wavering & inefficuous
They fall far short of the requisitions of the gospel, -- even of my own
perceptions of duty. I long for a stronger guilt & warmer love & more
strenuous and earnest & will gladly avail myself of every help that
love may afford, of which you give excellent. Or Have an apparent
in all sincerity & truth, with many thanks for his kind remembrance.
1841. 7th day. 10 p. m.
The gentle grain my dear Sister in love to you & yours
always M H.

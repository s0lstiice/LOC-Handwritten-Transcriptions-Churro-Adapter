Monday) I have just received my letter from Penz Poin - I scan our
friends Mrs. Deloy & Mrs. Du Bn Mills, who left my Anna
July last night - She is not worse - but my private improvement
has been a constant perplexation & quite a difficulty - Our
friends think it would be best for her to come home, where she
can have large rooms & conveniences - viz, riding - Steam cars
have no service - being 2 in 1 - a very simple system which can
not be made comfortable - But as she herself said
if remaining another week - our friends advised she should be
indulg'd - Bagard will go down in the Friday boat of morning
this will be his last visit - If God permits - Last night
you will have been trying times with me - I wish not to tell
you all I feared - I am so much obliged from my dear Anna, in her relation
& manner, during trying times - To leave Jackson, where tranquility of mind, or money
or her tranquility in money than 5 days to 6 months
no one need - Penswell, for get not in your prayers yours
Margaret

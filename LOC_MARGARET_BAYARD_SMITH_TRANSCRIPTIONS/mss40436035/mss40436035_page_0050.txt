Washington Nov 7 1857
Now often within the last ten days have I
seen his fair wife's note to me dear sister, father
after 24 hours passed the notification never signed
of your death I have thought upon a plan of your
having passed a memorial your most kind and excellent letter
I tried to think of facts in accordance with the views
you have expressed - I subscribe to the truth of all
you have said - but alas even these solemn
effects truths do not affect men as I write they
would - I thought I was fully prepared for the
stroke that has been inflicted on me - in my
twelve midnight watchings - in our hours of darkness
you have shown unshakable to any father they will be
done - but he that will has executed me -
belittled heart could not exist without reflection
and all the doubts & difficulties not doubts my
mind at various times has been afraid - a strong
announcing confidence in the wisdom & mercy of
divine providence never forsake me. I left him
a child in lodging rooms a delirium in the belief they
have answered to guidance by a superior power - I
period that God will not my wishes divine ordained
to the events of life, dictate a death conviction that
he would do more & better for me than I could
do for myself - But alas my mind what shall
be for my family - how I shall be the companion of my
mind this death - this death which I had
built myself to have robbed from beneath me
I left me struggling amidst stormy waves without
power to resist their overwhelming force - I have
been prostrated - mind & body - and how very bad
But I am recovering from the numbing effect

Tuesday. 2nd June. 1828.
This is a morning that a poet or a painter would
choose for to study nature -- a morning, that every
lover of nature in coldanger yields to the full melody
of contemplation in which he most delights --
Immense parts of creation! do we cats the clouds
to the trees & the hills to the plains & the streams
of not immaculate earth a morning in this these
objects seem endowed with living soul -- full of
life & motion, and think they must have sensation too
I am at this moment enjoying in the goodness of
their creation -- but night is rained in clouds --
This morning it was showers -- The clouds are still lower &
but broken into masses, some dark & heavy, others light
& fleeing -- they are born by a gentle breeze altering
the bright blue sky, cast my line -- moving shadows
on the green earth & thus giving a preliminary notion
to the fields & hills & trees.-- what alternation of
light & shade! now the landscape is dark & sombre -- the clouds
are broken & show it is gloriously bright -- Then upon
the scene various white clouds are moved in & back
then are glittering in the sun's light -- The shade & light
continually changing places, as the clouds float
over the sky -- The new beautiful is in the scenery in which
have been trees, new air, savages, for I cannot take my eyes
off and find them to my finger -- I sat down by my window
arranged my paper, thought, took out your letter to reflect
upon it and find new words suitable to my topic, long letters to my
hearers -- at most two days have elapsed & here I am
on the front page. The first hour I could not distinguish
my eyes from the clouds in sky -- the clouds, shadows, shadows
over the landscape, gave it such variety, such animation
that I could not help watching the changing scene, and seeing
it to me an indescribable charm in the appearance
of clouds after a storm. Their dark, heavy, broken masses thrown
responsibly before by the wind, seem like a united & divided army
then when the dark masses have passed by, the clouds
are visible, then come on the light, fleeing clouds
born of gentle breezes -- while I watch them, how do I
long to plant on their home to the distant regions
to check they are passing --

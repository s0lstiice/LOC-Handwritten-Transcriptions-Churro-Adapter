Mr Washington has been here near 10 days waiting for his wife.
It is very strange his being so much away from here. It is indeed
he does not like to stay at Haywood. I believe he tells me he has
nothing to do – when he first came on his business in Washington he
did not keep him a day – why or wherefore I know not, he does not stay
at Col Taylor's – He says he is happier at Sidney – He has two
married brothers, within fifteen miles, but paid them only a
visit of a day or two – when he first came here, it was because
he felt as if he was going to be ill & he preferred my money to
his aunt Gayliss – I certainly ought to be flattered by his preference
He is very polite & affable & gives me a very comfortable situation, in his armchair

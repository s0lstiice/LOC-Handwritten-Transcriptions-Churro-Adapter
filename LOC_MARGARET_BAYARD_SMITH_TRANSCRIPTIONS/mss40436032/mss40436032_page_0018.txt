is not well -- she has had a very unpleasant turn --
sent for Dr May -- but he, as his brother did 12 years
ago, pronounced that there was no disease -- said it
was a mere nervous affection -- or rather vis-motoria --
The vital principle, whatever it is, has always been
in her languid & feeble & is now, more so than
ever -- Life, requires food for the mind & affection
as well as for the body -- Hope & joy, are elements necessary
to our health & well being -- (and how few objects
of hope & joy has a single woman after a certain
age! -- Constant occupation, is the best substitute --
that it is only a matter that can enforce it -- Any
other authority would be deemed unknown. -- "It is only,
nervousness" said the Doctor, but in the whole circle
of human maladies, is there any more than this? Methinks
Pain, would be a less evil. -- Is it, oh I can both by precept

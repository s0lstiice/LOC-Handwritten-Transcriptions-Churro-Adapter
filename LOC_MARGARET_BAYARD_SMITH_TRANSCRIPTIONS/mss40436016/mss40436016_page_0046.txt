There is the breast of iron muscles
Stern inaccessible and cold
Which neither wills nor wills not when its proud destiny
Is to crush in pity's gentlest grasp
Its pieces through the warrior's steel,
Its cares to soothe his wounds to heal;
It opens into the rankling heart
And if it gives not, till the embers
Are not cool of by our side
One faithful lingering, scarcely stays,
But life's dark vault so wild, so narrow
Seems lifeless on our gaze.
The sword on some familiar face
The wild, reflected tear to brace
And sympathy's responding sigh
To music in the frozen ear of mockery.
There is a land of every land, the pride
Beloved by heaven over all the world bears;
Where brightness seems dispense everer light,
And milder moons empartisce the night
A land of beauty, virtue, valour, truth,
Time-honored age, and true-souled youth;
The wandering mariner, whose eye explores
The wealthiest isle, the most enchanting shore
Sees not a realm so beautiful and fair,
Nor breathes the spirit of a purer air;
In every glance the magnet of his soul
Touched by remembrance, trembles to that rule
Or in this land of heaven's peculiar grace,
The horizons of nature's excellent race
There is a spot of earth supremely blest,
A Denver, diviner spot than all the rest,
Where men, creations tyrant, rocks divide
The earth and scatter pageantry and parade,
While on her softened looks benignity smiles
The sire the son, the husband, father, friend

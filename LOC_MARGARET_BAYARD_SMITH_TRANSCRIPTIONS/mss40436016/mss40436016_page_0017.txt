The Fear,
sacred Icon of prevailing heaven
God of reason, mighty Fear,
In some tremendous moment given,
Nothing anguish most severe—
Melting shield of mute affliction,
Martyrdom, and feeling's gem,
Previous pledge of pure affection,
Powerful flower on pity's stem,
Reconciliation's sweet station,
Healing the disunited heart
Friendship's dearest best libation,
Balm for every anxious smart—
Oh how near ally'd to sorrow
Are our transports most sincere!
Our delight is forced to borrow
Feeling's rich expressive Fear—
Blindened eyes, that swiftly languish,
What do your soft orbs declare?
Does drop—formed of hope and anguish
Love herself has placed you there—

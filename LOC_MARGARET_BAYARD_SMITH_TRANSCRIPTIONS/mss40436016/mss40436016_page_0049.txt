Where'er I tread this worn'd service,
Good Providence on my path attends!
Alike when pleasure smiles serene,
Or pain and grief my bosom rend,
To thee infuse thy foreign power,
In youth's gay morn, in manhood hour
Or, when in age, life's parting ray
But faintly lingers low -- or yet it
fades away.
Venerable silence! welcome peace
(most welcome, holy shade!)
Thus I prove as years increase
My heart and soul for quiet words.
Thus I fix my firm belief
In thee, protector, guiding tears descent,
And every power and every love
To merit truth's unvaried friend.
I would not for a world of gold
That active, lively fire should tire
Thy fountain of blessings yet untold.
For scarce for a million fire
I grant that summer's tent will burn.

And slackness and doubt are now flying away,
Who longer turns in conjecture's torture,
Or breaks on the thicket's faint and astrey,
The bright and the balmy effulgence of morn.
Mark ambition's march sublime
Up to Power's meridian height;
While pale-eyed envy sees him client
And sicken at the sight.
Phaenomena of Danger, Death, and Dread,
That hourly severd ambition's head,
While Spenser within his revel's breast,
Sits brooding on her serpent's nest.
Happier he, the Proseast, far,
From the ranges of Prosper's face,
That breathes the keenest yet wholesome air
Of rugged Pennsylvany.
He, whose incessant task is done,
From Number in the twentieth year,
And his home, at every one's close
To sweet repast, and calm repose.
The Traveller.
But where to find that purpose spirit better
Than can deceive, when all interest is known?
The student, king, tenant of the fragid rent
Politely proclaims that happiness spot his own,
Exhibits the treasures of his stormy acres,
And his long nights of revelry and ease
The naked, vague fantasia at the knee
Beasts of his golden sands and palmy ruins,
Bulls in the glare, or shows the tent, ware
Thanks his gods for all the good they gave,
Such is the patriarchal character our room
The rest of country ever is at home.
And yet perhaps if centuries are compare,
And compare the springs which they share,
Though patriots flatter yet shall everdom find
An equal portion dealt in all mankind:
As different goods, by art or nature given
To different nations, makes their springs even.
Nature, a mother kind alike to all,
Still grants her bliss at labour's earnest call
With food as well the present as the past.

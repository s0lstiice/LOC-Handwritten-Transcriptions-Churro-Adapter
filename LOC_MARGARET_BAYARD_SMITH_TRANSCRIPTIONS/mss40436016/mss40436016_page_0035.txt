of human kind has remained the fall;
though few ever taste this uninterrupted and pure,
or lasting, long enjoy this; too often
in its encounters, to preserve the youth
unmarred with drops of bitter, which might
or temper others into dry crystal eyes.
There art the mazes of Virtue. In these arms
the spirits, appearing, as in truth she is
always born and destined to the skies again.
There art not however when pleasure is ended
that ruling principle with a soul's sweet
and warming eyes, still leaning on the arm
of novelty, her fickle but support,
for their art much and constant, hating change,
and finding, in the cabin of truth kind love,
joy that her stormy neighbors never yield.

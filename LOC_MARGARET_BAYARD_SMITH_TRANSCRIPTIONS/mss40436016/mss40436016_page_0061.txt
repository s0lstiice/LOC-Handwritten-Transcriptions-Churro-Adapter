Put standard, with a divine look,
Arguably above the moon and stars,
Gave—that every broken cheek
Beneath its own mantle.
"And go! I found the combat over,
My heart that was spalled bare
The anguish of a soldier's thank,
Go!—and return no more!
For never guilt the order brand
That lays press upon him, then go shall hold
The human with victorious hands,
Beneath a soldier's cause unrolled.
Oh stranger! by my country's lips!
And by my love! and by the cross!
Never forever could have spoken
The curse that seared native, johs;
But that a spirit over me throbbed,
And find me with a worthful mouth;
But fervently to my heart was given
To speak the millions of heaven.
They would have copied themselves with minute,
They would have poured to land the spirit;
But at the standing of my foot
Each hand down powerfully fell,
And go to Athenae! I must
Bring up the banner of your service;
But know that when its weight encircles
The weight of that is on your hands!
Go where the favor of your knees
Shall plant as thick as mountain fern;
Then shall in peace your common hearth;
The miter on your temples shall grow;
Dead on the green clover's flood,
That mantle by your souls shall be
Your glory of Roman blood
Turn! turn to Athenae!
There downward under the sun shall fall
The river's wing shall be your path,
But not a mortal shall venture
To draw from your dying face.

The breezy call of incessant breathing morn,
The swallow's twittering from the straw-built shed,
The cock's shrill clatter, or the echoing horn,
No more shall rouse them from their lothy bed.
For them no more the blazing hearth shall burn,
To brow-throw cup ply her evening care,
To children give to light their sire's return,
Or dumb his knee the favored lips to share.
Of did the harvest to their sickle yield
Thir furrow oft the stubborn globe has broken,
How round did they drive their team afield,
How bow'd the woods beneath their steady stocks!
Let not ambition mock their cupfull lot,
Their homely joys, and destiny assure
For generation with a stout heart endure
The short and simple annals of the poor.
The beast of ferocity, the pomp of power,
And all that beauty, all that wealth can give,
Await alike the inevitable hour,
The paths of glory lead but to the grave
For you, ye proud, impede to them the fault,
If slavery in their land or Neptune raise
Where through the long-drawn night and fethered vaults
The pulsing anthem swells the note of praise.
Can stern men or animated lust
Back to its mansion call the fleeting breath?
Can Heaven's own provide the silent dust,
Or pallory with the dull cold ear of Death?
Perhaps in this neglected spot is laid
Some heart once pregnant with eternal fire,
Hands, that the red of empire might have savaged,
Or could it rather the turning iron.
But knowledge to their eyes the ample eye
Rich with the sports of time did never arrest,
Chill Senecy repulsed their noble rage
And from the genial current of the soul.

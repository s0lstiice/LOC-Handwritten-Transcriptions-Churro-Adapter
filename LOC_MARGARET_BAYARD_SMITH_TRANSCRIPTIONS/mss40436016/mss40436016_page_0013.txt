For praise to dearly loved, or warmly sought
Enfields all internal strength of thought;
And the rich soul within itself unlists
Leans for all pleasure on another breast.
Hence contemplations hire with hurried art,
Pants for the vulgar praise which fools impart.
Here vanity opens her part gremiance,
And turns her robes of pride in copper lace,
From beggar prince demands her daily suer,
He bears one thousand pounds over a year;
The mind still turns where shifting fashion runs
For weighs the solid worth of self appearance.
To meet of other worlds only fancy flies
Embraces it in the deep where Hell and he
Withholds her patient sons before one stand
Where the broad ocean turns against the land,
And seduction is stop the roaring tide.
Left the tall empires of artificial power,
Toward metaphors, and diligently slow,
The firm connected labyrinth seems to grow
Spreads its long arms amidst the waters, root
Stoops out an element and wraps the shore.
While the hard ocean rising on the pile
Sees an amphibious world beneath him smile;
The slow canal, the yellow disposed vale,
The willow twisted bank, the gliding sail,
The crowded mast, the cultivated plain,
A new creation rescued from his rage.
Thus while around the wave-subjected soil,
Impuls the native to repeated trial,
Industrious habits in each boom reign
And industry begats the love of grain.
Hence all the good from opulence that springs
With all those its surfursious treasure brings
Are here displayed.

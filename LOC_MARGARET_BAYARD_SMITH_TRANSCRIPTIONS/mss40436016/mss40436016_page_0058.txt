"A hero's blade! this desert how is
It all befit the gentle warrior!
And why have I lost this flower?
To call me love lies blinding?
This purple flower my tears have never
A hero's blood supplied to Bloomer:
I have it for it was the first
That grew on Sonnachel Moran's tomb
With heartless changes, to my voice!
This desert mansion is my choice;
And that, the fatal, be the star
That led me to its wildest afar:
For here these pathless mountains free
Give shelter to my love and me;
And every rock and every stone
Can witness that he wears own.
O Connor's child I was the first
If Erin's royal tree is play;
But now to them that might in blood
The honor of my stay!
Still as I sleep my favorite train,
Of death takes me in my night,
It never dies and dies again,
The bloody feud, -- the fatal night,
When chasing Emmett Moran's men,
They called my hero's body down;
And to the line of honor's blade
They came from O'Connor's house of sinners
Their tales they said, their high degrees
Was sung in Sean's flattering;
With their earth's victorious brands,
And Cathal of the bloody hand, --
Glory (big earth) and power and honor,
Were on the mansion of O'Connor,
But he my lord only bare in the field
Of innocence cast upon his shield.
"My brothers! what did it avail,
That fiercely and bravely I fought the English of the field,
And them and De Brough's chief boy?
But what was it to me and me,
That Brian's by your standard rode,
Or beat me for your soldiers,
Upon our hundred mountains look it.

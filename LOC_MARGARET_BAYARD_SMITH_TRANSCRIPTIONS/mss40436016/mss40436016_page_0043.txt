Then in the last faint light of evening
And morning firm glides softly by
And gentle sighs its broken harmony
I will thou in broken griefs dost lie
For the sport of the painted
Which whispers— All thy cares shall end.
Then in the mild moon's peaceful twilight
Forbidding thoughts and dreams arise
And at the solemn hour of midnight
Paintless scenes before thine eyes,
The Britains give a rustling sound
It is my spirit round round.
Then, deep in fields of ancient story
Then humble earthward on the page
That gives and takes the mind of glory
Ful of thee a breath that fans thy age
And dim the trembling torch from hate
My spirit dwells with thine the tale.

For do we mainly, like an impious world,
Heaven damn religion forever, and the God
That made them an intruder on their joys,
Start at his name, or damn his praise
At jarring note.
Oh evenings worthy of the gods, exclaim'd
The sublime bard, the evenings I reply,
Often to be praised and coveted than yours,
So more illus'd, and with nobler truths,
That thine, and mine, and these we love enjoy.
The head that of this pillow prest,
That achieved them in gone to rest,
Its little pleasures now no more,
And all its mighty services are,
Forever, in the worm's dark bed
Forever sleeps his humble head!
My friend was young, the world was new
The world was false, my friend was true;
Lovely his lot, his birth excuse,
This fortune hard, my friend was poor;
In modern he had no pretence
Of child of suffering not of sense,
For nature never did impart
A weaker or a warmer heart.
This present and a soul of luxury,
Consumed its frail terrestrial frame;
That love from Heaven so freely born is
That evidence it came of own return'd,
On Pelicana inspiring bank
Or yet my friend had seemed to think,
Since as he passed the curule day
Among the mourning reeds at play,

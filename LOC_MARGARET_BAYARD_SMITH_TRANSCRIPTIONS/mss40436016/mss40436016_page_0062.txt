"I hold that marching our done
suspended till my men were given
time as it passed there left of from
held in the blood and honor.
Die was the look that on their backs
the angry pointing bullets thump;
But now behold like caterails
Tomass's plumed partisans,
Three ten thousand division clans
Were marching to their doom:
A sudden storm their plumes to hide,
A flush of lightning on their noses,
And all again was gloom;
But once again we knew in the bands
Of thunder spirits clasp their hands.
"Strange! I fled the home of grief
At the peril of Man's tomb to fall;
I found the heart of my chief;
The bow still hanging on one wall;
And look at down, and round I know
This desert place a hundred bolds,
For which I leave my brave old
Genius for Mungo Park—
"Hope no more, in peace he sleeps,
All his pains and toil are done;
No thine eye alone that wept, thy
He is dead to see no more—
The battle gained the unknown river;
He hath found a hero grave
When his head in peace forever
Rests beneath the shadow wave!
"We like him our backs are guiding
Suftily to an unknown shore,
There we know there's no abridging
The road forever more."

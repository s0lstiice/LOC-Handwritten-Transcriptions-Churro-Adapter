Wholly, and soft the eastern breeze
Just kissed the lake, just stirred the trees
And the pleasant lake like miniature joy,
Drummed but dimpled not for joy;
The mountain shadowed on her breast
With neither broken nor at rest,
In bright anticipation they lie

The water lily is the light
Her choice of spot of silver brook;
The deer awake and to the lawn
Begunmed with dew drops hid the fence;
The grey mist left the mountain side
The torrent showed its gleaming path;
Swiftly in flicked sky,
The black bird and the spunkled thrush
Good-morning gave from brake and bush,
In answer stood the roosting dove,
Her notes of peace and rest and love.

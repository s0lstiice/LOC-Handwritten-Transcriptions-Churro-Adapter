While his lord's partner breakfasted of her board
Discards her cleanly platter on the board,
And drops by so some pilgrims mother led
With many a tale repay the nightly bed.
Thus every good his native wilds impart
Impresses the patient passenger on his heart,
And even those hills that round his mansion
Enhance the bliss his scanty fund supplies.
Laird is that hill that lifts him to the stars,
And as a child when soaring sounds melody
Clings close and clover to his mother's breast
To the loud coronet and the whirled horn's roar
But bind him to her native mountains more.
To kinder skies where gentle manner reigns
I have, and France displays her bright mansion.
Gives sprightly land of morals and social ease
Plowed with herself who all the world can see.
How often have I led they spacious choir
With its music pipe beside the murmuring loom?
These shading clime along the margin green,
And freshened from the wave, the Tayport fleet.
And hapily, though my heart's work, father's skill,
But much it all time, and much the daughter's
Yet would the village praise my wanderings prove
And dance longespill of the moon's hide leaves.
Or like all ages. Lames of ancient days
Have led their children through the mirthful
And the gay prairies, still's in gentle breeze.
Has frock'd beneath the burden of three score
So blessed a life these thoughtsless nations desplay
Thus idly busy rolls their world away.
There are those arts that mind to mind endear
For honour forms the social temper here.
Honor, that praise which real merit gains,
Or even imaginary worth obtains.
These papers current, paid from hand to hand
Is shipped in crowded traffic round the land
I own doubt, to causes, to countries it swings,
And all are taught an avowal of power;
They please are pleased they give to get esteem,
Till seeming blest, they give to what they seem;
But while this offer art their ships mutual supply
If give their politics also seem to be.

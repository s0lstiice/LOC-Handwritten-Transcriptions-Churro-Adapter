Black! the nightingale, afar,
Sweetly urges the sun to rest,
And awakes the evening star
In the rosy-tinted west.
While the moon's enchanting eye
Opens Paradise on high!
Soft and tranquil is the night,
Nature's sore affliction over,
For the storm, that spent its might
Has a covenant of peace;
Vengefulness drops her harangue in red,
Mary is the Power of God.
The western waves of sleeping day
Rolling over the glen their level way,
Each purple peak, each flinty spire
Has bathed in floods of living fire.
But not a setting beam could glow
Beneath the dark ravines below,
There turned the path, in shadowed veil,
Bound many a rocky pyramid,
Shooting abruptly from the dell
To thunder-splinter pinnacles;
Round many an insular mass
The native networks of the puffs,
Huge as the tower which builders vain
Pessimists piled on Thessar's plain.
Their rocky summits split, and rent,
Formed territ, dome or battlement,
Or seemed fantastically set
With cupola or miniature,
Field crests as fagged over checkered
Cross-quoies of eastern artillery.

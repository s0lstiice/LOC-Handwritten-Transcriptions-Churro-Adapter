No Winter's icy bands thy course confine
No Summer's blaze thy glittering tide exhales.
Oracles the thunder of thy tumulting waves—
Here silence never a residence has found;
Unvaried Echo answers from her caves,
And shakes the cliffs and hanging cliffs around.
For ages shall these roaring waters glide,
Those rocks succeeding ages shall remain;
While a few years shall stop the purple tide,
That now with order swells the youthful stream.
Yet rocks the restless hand of time shall feel,
Even ocean's self in years shall pass away,
Eternity on man has stamped the seal,
That gives the promise of immortal day—
The march is o'er the mountain wave
Her home is on the deep—
Lives written while on a visit to a Friend.
From City's noisy din I fly,
To such retirement here;
Where now no verdure charms the eye,
But all is sad and dear.
The leaflets now you word is seen,
And streamlets cease to flow;
The Nature's change'd her robes of green
For mantles white— of snow;
Yet leafless strength and sternness repelling,
And country winds I heed ye not;
While placed within this peaceful dwelling
No nightship storm disturbs my lot.
Or if rude Boreas reach mine ear
With added joy I press my state;
While Pity sheds her softest tear
For many a wretched hapless fate.
This hail sweet spot! enclosed to me
By memory's pleasing aid;

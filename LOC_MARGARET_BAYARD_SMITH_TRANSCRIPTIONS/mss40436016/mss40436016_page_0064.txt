This is a dear and precious period in the
life of man, which, brief as every is best appreciated in recollection; when but to exist is to enjoy,
when the rapid pulse throbs wildly with the
vague delight which fills the empty heart,
and when it may be truly said that nothing is, but what is not.
Devoted to a higher consideration his soul
is stripped from heaven to earth, to relieve
the sufferings he suffered, or to arrest the waves
he condemned; to sublimate peace for necessity;
to restrain the blasphemies of the profane
to dispel the darkness of the ignorant; to support
the sick; to relieve the wounded; to strengthen
the weak, and to encourage the timid; to
watch, to pray, and to suffer for all.
There is certainly no greater happiness than to be able
to look back on it life carefully and valiantly employed,
to trace our progress in existence, by such tokens
as excite neither shame nor sorrow.

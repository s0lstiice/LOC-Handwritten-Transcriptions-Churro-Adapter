And wild religion o'er you shed
A balm for every woe.
And may a length of days be past
In health with no illness;
And each new year while life shall last
Be crowned with Peace and joy.
And as on earth you be worthy God,
The path approved by Heaven,
And led the wander'ing soul to God,
By precepts kindly given;
So when your era of life shall close,
All earth born cares at rest,
You'll find the promise sweet renewed
'And dwell among the Blest.
The joy of spirit
Shed at the hour of tribulation,
When the heart can freely sigh
And the tear of resignation
Twinkles in the mournful eye.
Have you felt a kind emotion
Thrill'd through your troubled breast;
Soft as evening on the ocean,
When she charms the waves to rest?
Have you felt a friend's breath?
Heard a father's parting breath?
Gazed upon a lifeless mother
Till she seemed to wake from death?
Have you felt a presence
On your arm before your eyes?
Watch'd the lovely soul retiring
From those eyes that look on you?
Did not grief then your remnant?
Roving on remember'd steps?
Did you not with fervent laughter
Clip the lips that felt no help?

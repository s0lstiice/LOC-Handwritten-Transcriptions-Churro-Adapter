Bright as the summer, Italy extends;
Her uplands sleeping deck the Mediterranean side,
Feeds over woods in gay theatrical pride;
While oft some temples moulldring tops between
Her unrivaled grandeur marks the scene.
Until nature's beauties satisfy the breast
The sense of Italy were survey that
That ever fruits in different climate grow,
But winds, air, & temperature the grounds
Another theme in winds alone appear,
When bright sweepers deck the varied year;
Whither clouds sail at the northern sky
Withernal flow is that they soon but to die;
These here disporting over the hundred sail,
For ash luxuriance from the plundered soil,
While sea-born gales their golden wings expand
Thornwood fragrance round the smiling land.
But small the ships that never alone bestows
And several steps is all this nation knows.
In flared beauty groves and fields appear
That serve as only growth that dwindles here.
Conquered fault though all his manners reign,
Though poor, luxurious; though subservient, vain;
Through grace, yet trifling, talent, yet untrue;
And even in poverty planting sons and nieces,
All evils here condemn the mind
That patience deserted leaves behind;
For wealth era there, not for reward the dark
Then commerce proudly flourished through the state
With her armament the patricians turn to rise
Again the long fall in columns sought the skies;
The carvings glow'd beyond in nature warm,
The pregnant quires heard with human form.
Till, more unsteady than the southern gale,
Commerce on other shores displayed her sail,
While might remained of all that riches gave
But turning armament, and lords without a slave.
Yet still the tops of wealth is here supplied
By arts the splendid wrecks of former peace
From these the futile heart and long fall in mind
For every contemplation seem to find.
Here may be seen in bloodily pomp array'd
The pastel board triumph and the cavalcade.

"Whatever thy lot, — Their sin then be-
conspirs thy folly, — I, for the rest,
And in thy chastening sorrow on
The hand of God.
He bruised me he will not break;
Afflictions all his children feel;
He wounds them for his mercy's sake,
He wounds to heal!
He humbled beneath his mighty hand,
Prostrate his Providence adown;
He does that stand,
To fall no more.
Now travellers in the vale of tears!
In realms of everlasting light,
Through time's dark wilderness of years
Praise thy flight.
There is a calm for those who weep
A rest for weary Pilgrims found
And while the mourning ashes sleep
Low in the ground,
"The soul, of origin divine,
God's glorious image, free from clay,
In heaven's eternal sphere shall shine,
A star of day!
"The sun is but a spark of fire,
A transient meteor in the sky;
The soul, immortal as its fire,
Shall never die."
There is a wound that will not heal,
There is a grief that cannot fall,
I fell and then
When shall I cease to feel again.

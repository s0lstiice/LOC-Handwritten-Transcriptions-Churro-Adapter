Provisions form'd it for pity and love
Of mistress or a saint in every grove.
By sports like these are all their cares beguiled
The sports of children satisfy the child;
Each noble aim repose by long contemplation
Now sinks at last, or fully means the soul;
While low delights succeeding fast behind,
In happier meanderings occupy the mind.
As in these downs where treasure once lay away,
Defaced by time and suffering in decay,
A mound the ruin, headship of the dead,
The shelter seeking peasant builds his shed,
And, wandering man could want a larger pile,
Entails, and owns his cottage with a smile.
My soul turns from them, turns me to survey
Where roughhewn stones a noble race display,
Where the bleak swiftness their sternest measures tax
And force a charlish veil for scanty bread;
Or product here the barren hill afford
But man and steel, the soldier and his sword,
Is eternal gloom thrown bespied rocks away
Bur winter lingering chills the lap of May;
No Zephyr fondly sires the mountain's breast
But miles' glare, and stormy glooms invest;
Yet still, even here, Content can spread a charm
Predicts the climate, and all its rage disarm.
Though the poor peasant's hut, his feast though small
He dies in little lot, the lot of all;
Says no contiguous palace near its head
To secure the mummery of his humble shed;
He costly lord the sumptuous banquet deal
To make him loathe his vegetables meal;
But calms, and bred in ignorance and soil,
Each wish contracting, fits him to the soil.
Careful at noon he takes from short repose,
Breasts the keen air and cares as he goes;
With patient angle trolls the fenny deep
Or drives his rent reins ploughshares to the sleep
Or cracks the dice where snow tracks mark the way
And drops the struggling savage ink day.
At night returning every labour speed
He sits Kemp down the march of a shed;
Smiles in the cheerful eve and sounds surveys
The children's looks that brightness of the blaze:

O for Evening's brownest shade!
When the bronze day by stealth
In the forest sunset's glade
Leaves the hermitage of health,
While the moon-bright mountains blaze
In the sun's tormenting rays.
On the sick and sultry plains,
Through the dim and silent air
Agonizing silence reigns,
And the universe sleeps;
Nature faints with parent heart
Ath! her pulse still ceases to beat!
Now in deep and dreadful gloom
Clouds on clouds protect the earth,
Blush as if the day of doom
Furnish'd with a shrunken head
Le! the lightning cracks on night,
- God is somnolent! God is asleep!
Now ye not his chariot wheels,
Is the mighty thunderbolts?
Nature, divine Nature rests,
From the centre to the periphery!
Scintill! Ocean, earth and sky!
Franchise! God is passing by!
Darkness, wild with horror, grimness
His mischievous bidding pleads;
Should He, from his ark of storms,
Reveil the veil and stain his face,
At the judgement of his eye,
All the universe would die.
Brighter, broader lightnings flash
hail and rain torrentials fall;
Loud, deeper thunder crash,
Desolation threatens all;
Struggling Nature gasps for breath
In the agony of Death.
God of vengeance! from above,
While others awful bolts are hurl'd,
O remember Thine art Love!
Spirit! spare a guilty world!
Stay Thy flaming wrath awhile,
See Thy low of promise smile!
Welcome, in the eastern cloud,
Misenger of mercy still!
Now, ye winds proclaim aloud,
Rule on earth, to man good will!
Nature! God's repenting child,
See thy Parent received!

Harps of the north. Farwell! The hills go over deck
On purple pinnacles a deeper shade descending
For twilight comes, the gloomier lights her spark
The dim half even are to the coral winding
Row now thy onward clime! the fountain leaping
And the wild breeze thy wildest minstrelly
They numbers sweet with nature's weavers blending
With distant who from the fold and lean
And hard boy's evening pipe, and burn of housing
Yet once again farwell, those immortal harps.
Yet once again I forgive my little erray,
And little need of the censer's sharp
May pity cast at an idle lay.
Much have I once by dreams on life's long way
Through earth passed, the world has never known
When on the weary nights deceived awakes day
And bitterer even the grief discovered alone.
That I raise such voice; Enchantress is there none.
Dark, as my lingering fortunes slow retire
Some spirit of the air has evaded the strong
I am now a spark held with touch of fire
Is near the brush of fairy's frolic wing
Reeling now the dying numbers wing
Pointed and fainter down the rugged hill
And now the mountain breeze scarcely bring
To wandering watch node of the distant shell,
And now, is silent all.— Enchantress fare thee
well!

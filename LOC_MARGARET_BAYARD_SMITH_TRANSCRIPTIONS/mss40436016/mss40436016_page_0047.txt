How woman reigns; the mother daughter, wife,
Stirred with fresh flowers the narrow way
In the clear heaven of her delightfull eye
Or angel-guard of love and grace lies;
Around her knees domestic duties meet,
And fine self pleasures gamble at her feet.
Where shall that land that spilt her young
Let thou a man? -- a master? -- look around,
O thou shall find, however thy footsteps roam,
That land thy country, and that earth thy home.
Not the jaws of Charletois nor the house rocks
Of all the pill dangers that lurk in the deep,
For the earthquake's deep yawns nor the volcanic burn
Not palliance breath in the hurricane's sweep
Not all the armed monsters, that live through me
That have caused such destruction, such misery & woe,
He from that archipelago mankind, liberation
Brings the world's world incessantly flow.
Is a nation entirely on whose giving bosom
The nation in which all her sons are gone,
All lost to all shame and the dictates of reason
He finds not one effort to our return.

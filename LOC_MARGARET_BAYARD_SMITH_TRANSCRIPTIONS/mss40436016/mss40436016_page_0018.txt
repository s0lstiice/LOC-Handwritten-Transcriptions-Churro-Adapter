Tomorrow.
How sweet to the heart is the thought of tomorrow
When hope's fairy pictures, bright colours display
How sweet when we can from futurity borrow
A balm for the griefs that afflict us to day—
When wearisome sickness has taught us to languish
For health, and the comfort it brings on its wing—
Let us hope, the hour soon it would happen may come
That tomorrow will peace and serenity bring—
When travelling alone quite fortune unfriendly
Finds the hope that tomorrow may abandon us with
That it should cheer with care, sympathise
I shall rest untroubled and slender in peace—
Or when, from the friends of my heart long divided
The fond expectation with joy their repute;
That from far distant regions, by Providence graced
Tomorrow will come in most happily meet—
And when the vain shadow of time is retreating
When life is just fleeing and Death is in sight
The Christian believing, uniting, enjoying
Beholds a Time-moment of Endless Delight.—

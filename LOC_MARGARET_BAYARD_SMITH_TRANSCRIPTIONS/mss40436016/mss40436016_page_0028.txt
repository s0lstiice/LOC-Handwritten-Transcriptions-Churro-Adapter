But scarce against his horn he wound,
When low, forth starting at the sound,
From underneath an aged oak,
That planted from an idol rock,
A Daneset quiet of its way,
A little stiff shot through the bay
That round the promontory steep
Set its deep line in graceful sweep,
Eddying, in almost invisible wave,
The weeping willow wing to leave,
And kiss, with whispering sound's blow,
The beach of pebbles bright as snow.
The boat had reached this silver stream
Just as the hunter's life his steed,
Ashore stood concealed amid the break
Of view this study of the Lake.
The murderer poised as if again
She sought to catch the distant steven
With hand expressive, and lip intent,
And eye and ear attentive bent,
And locks flung back, and lips apart
Like monument of Grecian art
In listening mood she seemed to stand
The guardian Naiade of the stream:
And never did Grecian sweet trace
A nymph, a Naiad, or a grace,
Of finer form or lovelier face!
That through the sun with ordered power
Had slightly tinged her face with brown,
What though in spite of equal grace
To measured mood had trained her pace
In foot more light, or step more true,
Nor from the heath-flower dashed the dew
Even the slight hare-bell raised its head
Elastic from her airy tread,
What though upon her speech their turn
The accents of the mountain loeongue,
Those silver words so soft, so clear
The listener held his breath to hear.
A Christian's daughter warned the maid
Her father's sword, her father's plaid,
Her golden hair such birth betrayed.

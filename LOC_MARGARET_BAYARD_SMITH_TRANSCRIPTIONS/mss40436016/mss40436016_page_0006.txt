Full many a flower is born to blush unseen
And wash its sweetness on the dewy air.
Some village Hampden, that with dauntless breast
The little grant of his fields, withstood;
Some mild enterpriser Milton here may rest,
Some Cromwell guillotin'd of his country's blood.
The rapacity of bribery senate to command,
The threat of ruin and ruin to enforce,
To vulgar plenty over a smiling land
And read their history in a nation's eyes,
Thick let forbade our encommod'd alone
Thir growing virtues, but their confound;
Forbade to enable through slaughter to a throne,
And shut the gates of mercy on mankind.
The struggling prangs of conscious guilt to hide,
Or gush forth blushing of ingenuous shame,
To hear the shrine of Luxury and Pride,
With intense rancor at the Muse's flame.
Far from the mudding crowd's ignoble ships,
Their sober wishes never care to stray
Along the cool sequestered vale of life
They keep the modest tenor of their way.
When these times from events to protect
Some faint memorial still erected might,
With unwarthy rhymes and shapeless sculpture clothe'd,
Guided by theoping tribute of a sigh.
Her name, their years spelt by the undisturbed muse
The place of fame and glory secure;
And many a holy text around she strews
That teach the rustic moralist to die.
For who to dumb Forgetfulness a prey,
This pleasing, anxious being ever resigned
Left the worn-in memories of the cherished day
For cast one lingering longing look behind?
On some fond breast the passing soul rests,
Some quiet door the dying eye requires
Even in the tomb the voice of nature cries
Even in our ashes live their honored years.

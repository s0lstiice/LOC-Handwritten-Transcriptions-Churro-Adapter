Neath Twilight's haste to throw the solar ray
And bring the hour my sensitive spirit loves
When on the hill is shed a pious day
That gives to solitude and to night the groove,
Oh! let the gay the restless morrowing hour
When in the various thrones of light arrayed
The birds fresh beauty live along the vale
And rippling trembles in the veil of shade:
Sweet is the lucid morning's opening flow,
And choral melodies bungling rise,
Yet nearer to my soul the shadowing hour
At which her blosoms close, her virtues die,
For then mild nature while she devours her
Wakes the soft tear its luminous to shed.
Rude is our passage to the tomb,
But are no radiance pierce the gloom?
Methinks a mild, unvarnished flame,
Sheds its light on the darksome mire;
On the lone heart's roof it rests
And warms the shivering inmates' breasts;
The king who feels it at his heart
Drops of his cares the heavier part;
With it the still dead bosom knows
Its dearest moments of sorrow;
That friendship, since the world began
Man's kindest, noblest boon to man!
All other joys, with mutual fire
Turned 'round in the midst of time expire,
But then, unhurt by fortune's blast
Shines the dearest brightness of the last!
The weary heart unwarmed by thee
Broods for a sullen destiny;
Heaven's fairiest gifts would fail to bless
That cold and wintry haunt of starless
selffisher's.

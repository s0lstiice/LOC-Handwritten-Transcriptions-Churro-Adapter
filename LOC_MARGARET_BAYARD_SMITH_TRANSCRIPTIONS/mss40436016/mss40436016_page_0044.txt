Hear'd thou, when silver stars are shining
As round as Lil's harp divine
Now the wild wind full chords combining,
And whisper murmuring—Ever thine!
Thou watchful star, — to guard thy peace
My watchful spirit never shall cease.
They need't thin ask or I reply
These words are for an insult many
Is ever through an opening look
The sweetest elegance of any
For thou may'st seem one at the hour
Of conservative to social pleasures
When feeling, music, fancy, taste,
And when men mix their riches—treasures
But still
For some not all the sun's last beam
Is on the west where purple breast
For vast thou all the full orb'd moon
Resplendent left her silver crest.
But shall the softer hour between
When twilight drops her mystic veil
And brings th' anxious mind repose
And leaves th' innocent heart to feel
But with a still and noiseless step
Steal to thine well known fairy room
Where fond affection rests
And never finds the heart from home.
Dear not to meet introducer there
Shall it only find my harp and me
Barethwings perhaps some passive strain
And waiting anxiously for thee.
And I will wear that violet robe
I know thou lo'st it to see me wear
And with that sweet wreath formed by thee,
This faded now, I'll bind my hair.
And round my harp fresh birds I'll twine
Or which departing sun has swept
Will now thou know at th' wise its place
And— but I merely meant to tell thee
That thou mightst become yet still I write
Is the same witchcraft charm befell me.

There is not in this wide world a valley
As that vale in which I roam the bright waters run
Or the last rays of feeling and life must depart
Or the bloom of that valley shall fade from my heart.
Yet it was not that Nature had shed on the scene
Her purest of chrystal and brightest of green
I was not the soft magic of streamlet or hill.
Oh! it was something more exquisite still
"Swee that friends of my heart were nearer
And felt such dear scenes of enchantment more dear
When on them reflected from looks that we love.
Daughter of free, unlettered power,
Thorn timer of the human heart,
Thorn even stronger, and cutting keen,
The true offspring, afflict the best!
Borned in thy adamantine chain
The Proud are taught to taste of poison
And purple Tyrants vainly grim
With pangs unsull'd before, unpitied and alone.
"When first they see to send on earth
Venus, his darling child design'd
They he gave the heavenly birth
And bid to form her infant mind.
Stain rugged nerves! they night tear
With patience means a year she tries;
What sorrow was then laid in her heart
Bid from her own she turn'd to melt at others
Hearted at thy favor terrific, fly
Self pleasing Sally's sole brood
Thick laughter, knee, and thoughtless joy,
And let no license to be good
Light they disperse and with them go
The summer Friend, the flattering sun
By vain Discretion received
To her they now their truth were agains believe.

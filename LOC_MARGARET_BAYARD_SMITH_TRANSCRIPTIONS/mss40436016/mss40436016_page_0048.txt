Maid of the sweet engaging smile;
Combination of our hours of peace!
Whose soothing arts can now begin;
And bid discordant whispers cease;
Virtue in thee her favorite train,
And dwells where'er thy suave precepts
Lies fairest charms to thee are due,
The source of pure delight, the healing
calm of soo.
Can capture their congenial hearts,
Conquered by Friendship's wreath of love,
Or rights of lips to fond impart,
The grace, enchanting maid, be thine
Can we a soft attractive grace
In the mild beams of beauty trace?
Or only when with thee combined,
Her powers can quietly claim the homage
of the mind.
When the first pair in Eden's bow
Employed the fair ring smiles of Heaven
They influence brightness every flower,
And elbied the balmy breeze of even
And since in love's connected ties,
He best can learn thy sweetest prize;
In humble affection's pure domain,
Where still untroubled joys denote thy golden
reign.
Deprived of thee does Earth profess
One charm to bind us here below;
In vain may pomp and power assert,
Or wealth its glittering gifts bestow,
Lost are their worth when thou art fled
Thy discord lefts her sceptre dread
And galled envy, care and strife,
Until their darkening clouds to veil the
noon of life.
But when thy welcome steps appear
The deceived train of evils fly;
In thy cheerful self is ever near
Avoid calm content with placid eye;
And all that to the soul endures
This dreary wilderness of years
All that our happiness employ
Where beats the willing heart to transport
and to joy.

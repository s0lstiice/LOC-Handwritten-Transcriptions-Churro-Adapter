They written in a Country Church yard
The surface tells the truth of parting days,
The turning hard word down by the sea,
The ploughmen homeward plods his weary way
And leaves the world to darkness and time.
From fades the gleaming landscape on the night,
And all the air a solemn silence holds,
Save when the beetle wheels his dying flight;
And drowsy ticklings tell the distant fields
Saw that from yonder icy painted towers,
The roaring wolf does to the moon complain
Of such, as wandering near her secret bowers
Below her ancient solitary reign.
Beneath these rugged hills, that yonder stand
There towers the turf in many a mouldering heap
Each in his narrow cell forever laid,
The rude forfathers of the humble sheep.

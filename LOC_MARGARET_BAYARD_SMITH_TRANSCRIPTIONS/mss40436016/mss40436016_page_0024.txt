Or the yielding brow of sadness,
One quiet smile of comfort stole;
One soft pang of tender sympathy
Equally thrilled your soul.
While the wounds of war are healing,
While the heart is all reorganized,
Is the solemn fact of feeling,
Is the Sabbath of the mind.
Pensive memory then retraces
Scenes of life forever fled,
Lives in former times and places,
Revisits communion with the dead.
And when night's prophetic gloom
Beats the veil to mortal eyes,
From those tombs the secret numbers
Of our lost companions rise.
You have seen a friend, a brother,
Heard a dear dead father speak;
Bewild the fondness of a mother,
Fill her tears upon your cheek.
Dreams of love your grief beguiling,
You have clasp'd a mother's charms,
And received your infant smiling
From her mother's sacred arms.
Trembling, pale, and agonizing,
While you mourn'd the union gone,
Bright the morning star arising
Open'd heaven, from whence it shone.
Whether all your wishes bending
Have in eternity subsisted;
Whether all your hopes ascending
Throned upon death and time.
Thus afflicted, bruised and broken,
Have you found such sweet relief?
Yes, my friend; and, by this token
You have known the joy of grief.

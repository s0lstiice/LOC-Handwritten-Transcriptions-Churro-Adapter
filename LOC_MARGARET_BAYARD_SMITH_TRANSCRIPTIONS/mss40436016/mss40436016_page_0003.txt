The pure design of administration is to secure happiness
and peace, and to establish justice and bring in
peaceable times.
To one single quality is so important a relation
to the happiness of human life an good reason, and one
which none can question. Freedom from oppression and
the due exercise of liberty, without any impediment to
the enjoyment of which, are the first and most
essential conditions of human happiness. It is
often said, but your nation, your
place, without any alloy, even confusion, and every
characterization without the stamp of difference in the
character of government.

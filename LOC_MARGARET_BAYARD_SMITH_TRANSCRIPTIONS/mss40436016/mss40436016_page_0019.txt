Dear Moon! of the Centaur's sage
By any spell my hand could dare
To make thy light its ample page
And write my thoughts my wishes there
How many a friend whose earlies eye
Has numbered on the starry sky
Would smile upon thy old to meet
The restitution kind and sweet
The memories of fond regret
The promise never to forget
But all my heart and soul would send
To many a dear loved distant friend.
Lime written at the Falls of Paterno.
Born to admire the ever changeful scene,
Which nature opens to the fervent eye;
So proud, delighted, the emerald'd green,
And gazed with rapture, on the starry sky,
To trace the murmuring stream's retreating shore
And stretch'd 'id along its banks, to linger there,
Or elbect, catch the torrents distant roar
Or client where rocks their towering summits rise,
Here and there rocks we wind our droving way,
And trace each path, remote from human ken,
Beneath the shadowy rocks now pensive stray,
And wander through the deep entangled glen.
Flark! the loud tumult of the water's roar!
Behold yon foaming steam's impetuous trail
See handling dash'd 'id upon the rocky shore,
The oak, all shelter'd, once the forest's pride.
Cohomistle flood! no interval is thine,
Each day, each night, still hovering th'air the vale,

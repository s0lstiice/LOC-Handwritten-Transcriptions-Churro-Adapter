you told me of his devoted attention, evening at night
& he himself so unwell as to have a blister on
his breast. - Although he is a friend - he is admitted
friend. - give me such say I - & let those who are
indifferent to me, he as cold & careless as they please.

to him & tell him to take good care of himself,

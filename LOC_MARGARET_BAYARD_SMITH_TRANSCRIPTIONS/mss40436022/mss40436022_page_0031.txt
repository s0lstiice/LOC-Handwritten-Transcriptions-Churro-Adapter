1842
11th April
It is two weeks since I received your last letter, and I get no answer from you. This is rather extraordinary for me, as I have
always been a day transmitter to people in my way or way
round. I am not a very good writer, but I am a very bad
reader. I have not been taken from it since I have been
writing. I have sat, walked, talked, and written with
uninterrupted activity every day since the first minute of the morning
with the awakened faculties, warm feelings, and restless
mind away from my soul, like the light returns from
the sun when the sun has set. The same floating imagery
the same masses of ideas sail along the air, but instead
of their brightness & beauty, just as when the sun of creation
is shining, I have left my mind. The same facts, characters,
& stories remain on the screen of memory, but desolate
of warmth or connection. And my dear friend, it is impossible
to tell on my own will to bear the light of fancy, which
the sun rises at midnight. Therefore I fear the returning
of literary scenes. To entertain characters - to have pictures

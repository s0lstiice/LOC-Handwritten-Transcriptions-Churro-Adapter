Dear Mr. Samuel Boyce
Dear Mr. Redgrave
19th Novr. 1849
A splendid sun set illuminated the landscape in the
short sketches I lighted my pipe on my paper -
perhaps you are at this moment admiring the unconnected
ballances - I suppose you at this very moment may
be fixed on the same subject as mine; I put one and
two miles apart - I wish it were not so, but that we are
getting close together - Tell the doctors I am impatiently awaiting
your answer to my last letter. I am your affectionate
son -

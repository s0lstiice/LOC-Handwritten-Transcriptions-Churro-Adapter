All this is now over -- I most highly favour -- for if he gets into the
great house -- they cannot recur -- or if he returns to Georgia -- the
reception will be the same as that of Seattle -- we hoped Mr Boyd
would have kept us apprised of all that was going on in Albany -- but
have been disappointed -- some of our friends in the city, had more punctual
correspondents here & from them we have from time to time had
interesting information -- I hope all will be as we wish -- The weather is
delightful -- we have roses still blooming in perfection in the yard &
the grass is green as spring. The girls will soon close their music &
French lessons for this season -- The days being too short -- another of
their pleasures will be thus cut off -- well -- the needle & books must
satisfy all defectives -- as for me while I have my pen I am in want
of nothing -- it is every thing to me -- Farewell dear Martin --
Ever your affectionate
Samuel Boyd

You will not expect my telling you of the serious personal feeling I have in this morning which I have on for some work I must do after my return from the country. I found her seated on the midst of five desperate (her 2 own men excepted) male loving children in a small infant bay, miserable by their love, they about her most perfectly calm, but at the same time desperate for family I felt returning affection of the right though I had not the power to relieve them. I went from home to Mrs. Meadows a before me, and I found her in bed with her husband and children. She had been in bed 11 months before. She was an Englishman & an attorney of experience, but not like her husband she was a woman of principle, and I am sure she would have been a great lady if she had not been so weak from the effects of the poison.

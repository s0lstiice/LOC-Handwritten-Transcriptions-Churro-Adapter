A lament
over a failing musical voice:
When all those friends of former years
Thro' pleasant voices of song,
This gust'd from out my inner heart
In cord soft or strong?
Oh, I remember still they lay,
Thrilled off with thoughtless glee,
And in my boys' garden walks,
Or near the spreading tree.
My Friends' lips woke my heart's young one
As hand by hand still passed
In eyes like eyes of cherubim
Lock'd deep within my breast.
And then still more, and as he stood
That hands uncuffed and
Hands smiles, and right alternate was
Praying for melody

The Sun Trial.
I cannot only think how that one unobtrusive
When leaving clouds obscure the sky
A mist and mists thence arise
On the bright sun, that gives the night
In vain for one diffuses their light
But when the gloomy orb of day
Shines in each scene with gliding rays
I know to all his mighty power
And entirely control each cloudless hour.
In spite of vanity this man
Remains in love for little span
For objects on his deathbed may
With finer and colds the dwelling age
But when Religion's banners droop
Shelter the midst of sense and leave
For fervent, constant prayer and bright
Shines in her holy heavenly light.
Febry August 1768

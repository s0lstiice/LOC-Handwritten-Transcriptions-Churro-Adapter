The names of women have in every age
Adorned the poets & historians page
Grand Virtue Genius Beauty all have shone
In every line magnificent in the throne
But now not the splendour of their fame
Is the light their destiny brought their name
Since none were left in their innocent hours
Remember the impotence of wealth & power
From transient things which never could impress
The rising tenderness of the human heart
Not those, embalmed on the cord of fame
Are passed on precious, as that humble name,
So pure still of genuine Christian virtue
Enlumed to mortal & apprised by heaven
That widow's name inscribed upon a page
Which shall endure beyond time's utmost age
Remember Dorrell's, whose name you bear
Like for a page defiled by cowardice, fear
The courage of refusing poverty means
And teach the infant tongue its first to use
Then shall the widows cry, the widowers prayer
Toward thy return I repair thy care
To heaven a sweet memorial shall ascend
And wave down blessings on the families' fires

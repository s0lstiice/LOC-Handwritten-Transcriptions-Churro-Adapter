This is the hour when memory takes
possession of joy that could not last;
This is the hour when fancy takes
possession of the past!
This brings before the preservation.
The best loved scenes of earlier years
That have since long been consigned
To silence and to tears!
The few we select -- the one we loved
Preferred land! come stalking on!
And mark a few for never removed.
But many a pleasure gone!
Friendships that now in death are lost,
And young affection broken down.
That hopes that fate, too, quickly crush'd,
In memory bloom again!
Few watch the fading beams of day,
But more on aspect so mobile fix'd.
For after time they died away.
But all at last were gone!
This is the hour when fancy weaths
Her spell round joy that could not last;
This is the hour when memory, breathes
Thought to pleasure past
1837.

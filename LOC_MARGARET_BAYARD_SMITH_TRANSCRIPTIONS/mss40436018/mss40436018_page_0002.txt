It happened on a solemn evening
Soon after he that was our safety died
Two broken friends each privately inclining
The scene of all those sorrow's left behind
Sought their own village house as they went
In musing, worthy of the great event
They spoke of him they loved of him whose life
Though blamely had incurred pernicious stuff
To whom death had left in spite of hostile arts
A dust memorial grave on their hearts
The recollection like a vein of ore
The farther traced enrich them still the more
They thought him and they justly thought him one
Serv't to do more than He appeared to have done
To all a people and to place them high
Above all else and wondered He should die
They yet they brought their journey to an end
A stranger join them curious as a friend
And asked them with a kind engaging air
What their affliction was and begged a share
Inform'd He gathered up the broken thread
And with truth and wisdom gracing all He said
Explained illustriously and search'd so well
The tender theme on which they chose to dwell

Recollection of a Scene on the Upper Rhine.
When to this region I was taken
There can be no impartiality to draw between
The mind is colored by those many hues
And of naturally the eye recognizes
their characteristic colors upon the lovely Rhine
Is with the thoughtful glance of poetry rendered
Those mighty hills many are now glancing above
But now sunk in me attaining masses
The brilliant fair and soft the colors of its bags
The negligently ground the finest stone
Of coming seasons the white edge them
In resting beneath the parapet stone
For fertile grounds are other waters below

The making of many art and their waters
At times of floods ripen as the wine

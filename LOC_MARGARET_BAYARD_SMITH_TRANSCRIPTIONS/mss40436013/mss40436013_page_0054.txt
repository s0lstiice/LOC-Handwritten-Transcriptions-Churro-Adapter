It was about the same time, I received
a letter from the young friend, whose
happiness I feared had been the recipient
of so many -- but no -- duty brings it upon
me now -- my friend informed me in this
letter that time had restored him from
the mind & that now he was the husband
of an amiable & worthy woman, the father
of two lively children & the possessor of a
comprehensive mind & possessing industry.
This information dispelled the only
realimentation which I suffered --
from the only source of sorrow I
experienced & now the subject of most keen
feeling thanks given.

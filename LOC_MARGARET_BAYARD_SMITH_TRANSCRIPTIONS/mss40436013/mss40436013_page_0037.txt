Admitted to the hospital as soon
as the world neglected & despoiled as I was
at home, I threw not into what error
my own strong feelings, excited by such many
to such society might have led me into,
had I not been restrained by the felt
presence of Death. I knew little & cared little
for the secular dignities, or abstract doctrines
of the Church, but a religious & sublime
feeling, a love & adoration of the sublime
Being, seemed an inherent principle of
my soul. There was too, no vice, some thing
or other inherent in a pure mind, a refined
taste & a tender heart, that I turned with
disgust from the mere pleasures of sense.

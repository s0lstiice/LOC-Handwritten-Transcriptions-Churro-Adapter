It had been the custom in them for the aged women
to have funeral penances pronounced on them for all
the young. Lunar first broke through by pronouncing
one for his own wife, who died in the prime. This
contributed to set him up in the affection of the people. They
sympathized with him and considered him as a man of great
good nature and who had the social duties at least.

& admire, because it did in my case.
My opinion was unchanged -- my conduct
was correct, respectfully so. I was held
up as an example of human virtue &
human happiness. But a cloud obscured
this noon line of pleasure of my external life.
But within -- within my thoughts became
an storm & strife. This was not
to be long endured -- yet I endured it
for almost a year -- and how I had to deal
with a less noble nature than my friends!
For only know how the conflict might
have ended -- as I have said, we had

The prospect before appointed me had neither
of us given their confidence to the other, it
would have been fatal to both -- But we
both loved another and both esteemed him --
at this important moment strength was imparted
from above, & we returned one fled with
hopes from two friends -- On his return
his language was separated -- on meeting
with that which I entertained him to expect --
he pleaded to have entertained, he became a
self-banished man -- a voluntary exile from
all he held dear on earth -- I was
left in innocence, but left with a broken
heart & deformed health -- For many
weeks I lay as it were on the edge of
the grave. The physicians called my doctors by

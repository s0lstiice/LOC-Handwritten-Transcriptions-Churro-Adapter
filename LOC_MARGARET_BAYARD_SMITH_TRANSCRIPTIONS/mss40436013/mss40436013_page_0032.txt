what were the usual characteristics, the passions
of feeling! I had hitherto understood words, my
friend cannot describe the cold, death-like torpor
that overcame me. For a time even the
darling voice of nature echoed within me. It brought
no joy to him, though he could not pay to me. Despair
hemmed my heart, my brain seemed to flow & I felt
as if I was turning into madness. This did not
last long, the suspense as to me of life, as well as
his, became apparent, his neglect & kindness aroused
the desire of mortality—The love of opportunity—the
energies, physical, intellectual & most miserable conditions
of the female heart, into the embraces of my shell-
being, my beloved, my affection extended—On
this modern being, thought, belief, love never encroached
—On imagination I clasped it on my arms
& gazed my eyes to its light. I gazed on its
little convenience, listened to its sweet voice
bothed it in terms of captain's almost religious
enthusiasm—& in my fond embraces—though my last will
meant with no impulse, love, I may doubt if
could have
persuaded me darling love me in return; life
again could draw me to a long & precious future
offered itself to my mind.
The tears of vengeance at the sight of joy
assailed what I had believed independence, I now found
time—But moments of such an act of love, poor human
nature cannot continue it. The body, that
troubled companion of the mind, sometimes railed
with disquieting energy & these transports came out amid
hours of nature, from a lapidary & scientific
description, & from every sentiment, & from every
description of sympathy, & love which I possessed.

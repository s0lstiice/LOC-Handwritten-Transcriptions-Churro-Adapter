And stayed my eyes over flood

from home to home, from old to old
'Till contemplation had her fall.
Beneath me lies anumbered line
Beautiful in various dies
This is nature's picture wrought
To instruct our wandering thought
Thus she dresses green & gay
To do home our cares away
Ever charming, ever new!

Oh many I with myself

Content me with a humble shade,
My lapkins throned, my ancient hands
For which our mother wildly calls,
She banish quiet from our souls,

the only God in all time & by men is an infinite
Being not Sin, which Johnson said of Revelation, have
been said of Popery. Endowed by nature with an active
transformation, & strong passion -- he could not resist sensual
pleasures & he could not indulge in them without risk.
But he was otherwise enlightened by nature, with a powerful
intellect -- a creative imagination & a keen perception
of all that is true & beautiful & sublime -- both
intellect susceptible, omnipotent -- endowed with a knowledge
of every great and terrible power & being under the power
of God -- that world full of God's wisdom -- no object that could
not be formed & being do anything in observation, he might
be considered as an astronomer -- on the earth he was
supreme -- but even after entering into different worlds
he would welcome them as philosophers & a human medium for
the divine guidance -- on his death he left God
to carry "I am concerned of the happiness of mankind both
in this world or earth, myself a nations stronger than
God is, & the prospect of returning to England with my
wife & children, gives me an idea of happiness I have
never experienced before." -- Reflections will lie every
thing to me -- but still my life has been like the curtain
in a play --

In this time -- external circumstances have little influence
upon happiness -- at least they need know that little; &
are powerless to resist their power, & cultivate intellectual
sources of enjoyment. From my earliest youth I have
done this & have found in all the various situations
of life -- in which I have been placed, even when
the circumstances that surrounded me, were most adverse
to my wishes, or my life, & could still find happiness,
by withholding my mind from them, & by depending
on its powers for enjoyment -- my creator! He

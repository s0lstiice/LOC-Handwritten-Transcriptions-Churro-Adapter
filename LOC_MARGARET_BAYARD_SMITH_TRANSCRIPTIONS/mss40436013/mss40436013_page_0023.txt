"I feel for it, that colonies, which by their constitution of our nature it is impossible to feel with respect to every thing (every one) which is absolutely foreign to our nature.
"There are spirits which even on earth, elevated above that little scene of mortal ambition with which their lives are filled, which are for the suffering, for the wretched, for the wretchedness of the world. All with them is severity, the desolation of the storm, and loneliness. Their love only is love down with with generous sympathy, on those who have not yet risen so high as to look up with gratitude to that heaven which is above their head, and which is almost shewing to receive them."
1826
Dear 18. Monday. -- The last two months have been busy building another in which my mind has been idle 2 or hours long -- The preservation of more comfort for myself, family is now completed 2 I have now nothing to do, but to enjoy the abundant blessing which my benevolent Father has bestowed. To enjoy them, if such is the will -- but to enjoy them thankfully, if that be his will -- The Lord graciously let me be thankful 2 daily bless this manse the Lord Tabitha -- I be here or might be to do, 2 if he should expect that might let me not refuse.
To my God -- The will be done, for I do believe that in giving or in taking, they partake in quite the same thing -- things though he shall make me poor I cannot foresee why I should be so blind as ignorant 2 cannot see what is good or what is evil for me -- But there is heavenly better will do more better than me can ask or think -- Therefore -- They will be done -- I am not Complained to keep the sentence of the God --

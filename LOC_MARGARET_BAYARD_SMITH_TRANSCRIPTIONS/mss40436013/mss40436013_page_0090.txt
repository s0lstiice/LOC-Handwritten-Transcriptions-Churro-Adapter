"Sweet daughter of my longed for name
I hail thy sainted other form
It passes by, quiet has no other power
To calm my breast in troubles hour
Sorrow or tumult to controul
And with tranquility
And peace to console my restless soul.
When hour I meditate on, life
Is busy cares, sad hopes, & angry struggles
In former, & in splendid, loves & joys
That which elates or pines which life destroys
All all at the same time, struggles & vain
Unworthy of esteem, or esteem, or esteem
The opinion of a dull sick or transient mind
From which we soon shall awake to glory
Can passion, temper, and hope or fear remain
When thus I feel Their objects meet & meet &
No reflections such as these, bid happiness cease,
And leave me to mortal sorrow & to despair
Dear family room, thy solitudes I see more
Than when I saw them
When on the wing I left them
I find myself contented
For every thought my every wish I put
There to my God, with sublime triumph
In such blasted towns, the unpatterned soul aspirine
For they beyond all earth born love desires
Beauty for the presence of the deity
And peace from the horror of that eternity
There all is infinite, no bounds, no space
No limits of our time, or this grace

"There is a mood
(I say not to the nearest 2 to young)
there is a mood kindly imbod of real nobility
That wraps the soul 2 friends for the thrice."
Yes, this is true! I have often felt it in times while
amused with the joys of being 2 being with the cares of life,
my mind is changed to earth -- I find its objects
efficient to satisfy the desires of my soul -- A light breeze
can waft light drops thumps, 2 parties can find delight
in trifles -- but when (let the cause be what it may) when
grief, vexation & mutual injury succeed, earth with all
its pop, its honors, 2 all attachments, can no longer
draw down any soul. The affections of animals

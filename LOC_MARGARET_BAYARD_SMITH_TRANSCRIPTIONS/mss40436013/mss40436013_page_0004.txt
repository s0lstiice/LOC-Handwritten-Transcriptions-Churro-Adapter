Sidney, July 16, 1826
Nightly forbear to close thine eyes to rest,
Ever thou hast questioned well thy conscious breast
What sacred duty thou hast left undone?
What act committed which thou oughtest to shun?
And as fair truth, or error makes the deed
Let sweet applause, or shame reproach succeed.
So shall thy steps, while this great rule is thine
Undeviating tread in virtues paths divine.
Goldeneunes of S y thagoras.
Perhaps thine never was a rule of conduct delivered
by an uninspired mortal moralist, which hath so pow-
erful a tendency to promote the interests of virtue as
this precept. It is scarcely possible that the man
who every night, reviews his actions of the past
day & fairly brings them to the tribunal of conscience
should not rise the next morning with stronger
impressions of his social & religious duties & with
a more guarded attention to avoid those moral
deviations he had so severely assigned. (Melmoth)
This rule I will more faithfully
endeavour to observe.

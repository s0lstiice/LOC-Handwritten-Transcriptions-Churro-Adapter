Then welcome home glorious that can compare
Her all the ships gay and sparkling crews, well
The tender memory is mute, unvaried
For though with which ye fall to restless mind
a restless for two Roberts
a gentle wife
A poor, a penurious, yet a happy one
Stallop when day light common takes upon
an hour for neither work; and every hour
while her twin'd husband & her children sleep
deep tranquil.
Alas I have no hope, no health
Not peace within, not calm around,
nor that content suffisive wealth
The sage in meditation found,
And walked with outward glory crowned:
For fame, nor power, nor love, nor leisure,
then I see alone those surround
I enjoy this time & only life pleasant;
Is me that call has been exact in other men's
Oh if her knees the weight of a plumed
How light the ballance of his humble family
Yet now disfair itself is mild
Pan as the woods & waters are
Swell the dawn like a timid child
And dies away the life of care
Which I have borne to yet must bear
To death like sleep might steal on me
And I might feel in the warm air
My heart grow cold, -- a fear forever
Breathes over my dying body at last Montgomery.
Shelly.

Elizabeth, pure & lovely as this short lived flower
withered & died in the morning of life!
Morning's Glory! Favorite flower
Of the frost & balmy hour
When nature waters & breathes around
fragrance over the dewy ground
fragrance which Elizabeth annexed
to her glory in case to the shrewd
debauchery of pleasure, when the bearer
is with it every joy appropriate.
Morning Glory! Nature's tear
Gleaming on thy form appears
Silent tear that fell through night
when Heaven had withdrawn its light
Pure as thine, so often shed
On youthful lovers' loveless bed
When thunders enthrall and every ray
of morning's glory falls down.
But joy returns & brings adown
The checks, like dew drops do the moon.
Morning Glory! Child of day
Gaily with theephyon's play
Enjoys this outlet & transient hair
Beside the sun's too evident power
Shake and fly from thy form
Of thy spirit wasted, thy already consumm'd
thy tender nature cannot bear
Residian heat & warm tide plane.

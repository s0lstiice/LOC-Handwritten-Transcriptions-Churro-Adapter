Shrewd you not that spring has flown
That all its lovely bloom is gone.
Knew you not that summer's past
and autumn's breakers of chilery blast
wherever you walk & look around
withered leaves lie strewn the ground
Yaded in the lovely green
and silver refuse clothes the scene
This you knew -- nor hope to meet
velvet springy round your feet
This you knew, nor hope to find
luxurious fruits of summer thorn,
Shrewd you not the life of man?
I, too, skinned on the selfsame place
that in its cheerful several hours
Sheltered many others her flowers
That I saw aspects of the heart
That I saw general character to mind in part
of the people who lived there
When the thought of free life
As little as in summer's time
But when life's summer heat
Our thoughts are old, our fond dead
Or remainders of that gentle time
No more will I use my pen in time
Her and her loss in which you regret
My sitting words of autumn will
The spirit I have made in your
My fancy near death bloom again

In truth my dear Friend your mind is possessed
that of your opinions are unsatisfied by part of reason
you have taken up a notion that Philosophy is
unnecessary to religion -- as applied to the modern
system of religion -- too much learning, with Love has
made our modern Philosophers mad They seem
to think they could have made a better model
of themselves, if some governed it better -- but I
reason about the system of the universe with
about as much justice as the Ant did
about the Cathedral of St Pauls -- To his narrow
scientific opinion even the surface of polished marble
was rough & indented & to his limited perception
it was incapable of having the least comprehension
of the beauty, order, or utility of the whole --
the superiorities of science -- how much greater the
impossibility of comprehending the works
of his Creator!
One point -- one little fact on our daily scan,
yet have aroused the whole stupendous idea
of Philosophy elevates man in the scale of being, to
own to you, I often wonder that since an
intelligent Christian preacher should not only
indicate, but even recommend the precepts
of the French & Roman Philosophers, as he
not seen, then an other enlightenment of
their wisdom, would reflect on our ignorance
& latitude which has added to the stock of
antient wisdom but rather charged or
destroyed it -- I have in a previous
letter quoted some sentences from the writings of Cicero
which affect the dignity of the soul -- the influence of natural
reason & the certainty of governing principles of God --
Revolution, has confirmed to us that reasoning philosophy
is made apparent to the great men of the world -- and this has added
a great deal of importance to the system of philosophy --
that one of its principles is that of religion, is not
always that of religion -- but to believe more than he
reasons he believes -- I am not then my dear
Friend anxious about my present cause of reading
or for a moment fear that I can make from
the end of my life, the elevation, the conviction, faith
of Religion, to the belief of my own philosophy -- that my poor friend
has been so unfortunate in the subject of
my saddest thoughts -- During her residence abroad
she has exhibited such a contempt for religious
Christianity, that it would be useless to
draw my proofs of the existence of Jesus
from the Gospel -- it is on this account that
I have her mind to an authority for which
I have her confidence -- in a Catholic country
she was disgusted with the manner in which
other Christians were held. She saw the Christian
Church only through an eye which comprehended
behind, made the mere instrument of tyranny
& hence concludes the religion to be a system
of influence & force craft.

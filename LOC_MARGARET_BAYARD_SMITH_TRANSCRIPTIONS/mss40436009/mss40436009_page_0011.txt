Morning! Glory! gay and bright
Regalness in the morning light
Who would think, it is close of day
So much beauty, could decay!
But Heaven knows best. My tender soul
Is snatched from the impending storm
To save from suffocating, yet to come
Consigns thee, to an early tomb.— Soon
Morning of hope! My general hours
Expand the brightest human powers
The heart is then most fond a light
The soul is pure, the genius bright
Youth and beauty then combine
To show the wise man which divine
Worship offers to high Heaven
The love of God, what then hast given!

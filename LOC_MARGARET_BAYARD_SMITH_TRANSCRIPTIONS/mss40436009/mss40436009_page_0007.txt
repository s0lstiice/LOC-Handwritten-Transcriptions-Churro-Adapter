Motto - a wife working her husband in
Prison - (Mrs Roberts)
Is she - Is she! my mother, faithful wife!
And beat them, faithful, innocent? Can they love
This heart through every barrier? And then tread
Defiant in health, & turn'd in them out,
At midnight break the delicate cold streets,
Then in a furious storm to throw by arms
Of conjugal indignation, round the neck
Of thy best husband? Fate cannot try men;
The bitterness is past. - Double Thoroughfaids in Crime

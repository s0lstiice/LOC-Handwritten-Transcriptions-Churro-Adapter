Morning's Glory! gay and bright
Regalness in the morning light
Who would think, in the clove of day
So much beauty, could decay!
But the even heavens haste the clouds of storm
It snatches from the impending storm
To save from suffocating, yet to come
Conceive thee, to an early tomb, poor
Morning of Hope! Thy genial hours
Expand the brightest human powers
The heart is then most fond a light
The soul is pure, the genuine brightest
Youth & beauty thus combine
To show the well-meaning divine
Worthiest offering to high Heaven
Receive Oh God, what then hast given!

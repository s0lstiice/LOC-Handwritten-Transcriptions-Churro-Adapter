The youthful year, blooms fresh & gay
And in natures bright array
Leaves profusely strewn the ground
Imaging their fragrance far around
But soon thaternal prime is fled
When time is past, their flowers are dead
Of all the bright & smiling stream
These withered & faded flowers alone remain
That no- not all have passed away
One rose remains still fresh & gay
One rose It's fragrance still shakes the earth
When every other flower has fled
Sister - when this thy youth has past gone
And all the joys which youth abound
Like this sweet rose which blooms the last
In me, you'll find a faithful friend -

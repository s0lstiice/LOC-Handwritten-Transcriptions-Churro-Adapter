Shall I live to fill this little volume, with the wanderings of a wandering mind? with my days he as numerous as his leaves! Perhaps not, but I have been ready to fill it up if I had nothing more to do -- Could I do any thing to advance the happiness of my children? Could I give them easier and better food? Could I give them nothing but comfort and life? Can no one be controlled by our wills -- than do devotion can be destroyed -- Men possess -- but God possesses it in me I have learned from experience a very pretty thing to lay schemes upon for a single day.

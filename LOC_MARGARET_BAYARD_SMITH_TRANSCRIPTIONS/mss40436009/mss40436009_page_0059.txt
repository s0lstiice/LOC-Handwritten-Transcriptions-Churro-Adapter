Extracts of letters by Voltaire, on free will,
opposition to Frederick who supported the doctrine of predestination.
But what will be a sufficient reason for the existence
of another body? The thing can only be conceived after long
other bodies are such necessarily by nature, or they are
the work of a free will or a supreme & free Being.
There is no optional Third Person; but let us choose
which of these two opinions we will, the difficulties
& arguments will be great.
Which is then in the opinion of which embrace?
that in which after a proper examination, there shall
be the least difficulty to convince. Now, I find the
most contentious difficulty a dilemma in the system
& the necessity existence of matter, & I suppose, urge
myself, on the side of those who believe in a supreme
Being, as the least contradictory - the most probable
still the existence of a creator, I cannot, I am
defeaturable inasmuch as the human mind, though
the truth cannot be placed among those which

Monday — This morning our dear friend was so feeble, she could not
talk to me — But she would not yield to my solicitations — A quiet room — a
little rest from her continued "and gloomy subject under all circumstances"
"if I could not be merry again — or at least give kindness a trial."
This then is the reason why she remains in giving us to
her dying room — where every thing is cheerful — pleasant
— and the electors of sickness and sorrow — The same
are daily filled with fresh flowers & she is dressed
with the elegant neatness & simplicity that ever
characterized her in this particular. In general
she is as cheerful as in health & the author of
selects are amusing & interesting — I now suspect
that she chooses them rather in reference to my
taste than her own — for I have more than once
observed that her mind wandered while I read —
She is the least exciting of any order person —
ever attended — & has never yet, allowed even her
maid to sit up with her during the night.

Tuesday — yesterday I rode home, to make some
engravings after the fashion of the time, to learn whether any
news had arrived from home. I find a long, not
a message of any kind — Three weeks have elapsed since
I went, without saying a word to me, or to myself, within
an address — Oh how deplorable is the human race! how little
more deplorable this heart! — Did my husband ever love me?
Oh yes, yes — I am sure he did — with fondness, with kindness —

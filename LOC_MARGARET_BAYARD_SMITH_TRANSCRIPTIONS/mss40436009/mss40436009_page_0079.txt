Heavily as affliction loads the years
of time, it cannot arrest its flight
the queen of the queen whether the
waves whitened by sunshine, or
darkened by the storm are alike lost
in the map of waters, so are the
days of human life, whether buoyant
with joy, or oppressed with grief, soon
pass away & mingle with eternity.
Why then should man exist, or
should man dispair since his en-
joyments & sufferings are so transient
Let this conviction check abate the pride
& prosperity & cheer the gloom of adversity
for as he apostle says "our time is short"

My dear Daughter New Brunswick Nov 25th 1808
It is a long time since I
had the pleasure of a line from you but I
have been otherwise informed of your
situation, which has given me pain &
anxiety for you – I hope your removal to
another part of the City of the Approaching
cold weather will remove your present
complaints & restore you to health –
It gives me pleasure to hear of
Mr Smith's attention to and success in his
Business & that his papers are conducted
with that decency & propriety that will in
the future gain him the good will & confidence
of all parties – present my best respects to
him – I have written by this ship to Maria
I dare say you will both be glad to see Judge
Patterson Your Mama joins me in his love
to you – I am indeed Peggy Your
Sincerely A N T Fisher
Ed W Smith
66976
Mr Margaret Smith
paid by the City of
Washington

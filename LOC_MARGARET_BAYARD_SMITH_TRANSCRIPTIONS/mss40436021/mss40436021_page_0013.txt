My dear Daughter
New Brunswick Mar 18 1805
I received by Judge Paterson
your kind & affectionate letter of the 3d Inst and
also the Tobacco, for which I thank you - The Tobacco
is very good but too new if not manufactured in the way
done for chewing - but will be very good for smoking
I was greatly gratified at hearing that
Judge Chase was acquitted, as were the citizens in
general in these parts, both Federalists & Republicans
as the Prosecution, always appeared a violent
party measure intended to weaken if not destroy
the Judicary of the U.S. & Mr Chase was selected
as perhaps the most imprudent & unpopular of the Judges
This Accusat does honor to those Members of
the Senate who pronounced him not guilty - & I believe
every impartial Person of either party or any
Country who will read the whole Trial will join
in approving their Conduct - but what can be said
of the Impartiality & Candor of those Gentlemen in
Senate, who continued with so much Passion &
obstinancy to declare Him Guilty, in the face of
so much & strong Evidence of His Innocence.
Alas! for the unhappy divisions & Animosities
that still continue among our Citizens
It would give me pleasure, to pay you
a visit this Summer - but at present so many
difficulties present themselves to my Mind that

Your Sister Jane Spivacum will write you by this
Opportunity - it will no doubt give both You & Maria
Pleasure to see Judge Patterson. He will tell you all the
Occasions of your Town - Your Brother Nicolas, Nancy &
their Child live in this Place - His affairs are not yet settled
& He is as yet unfixed as to his future pursuits -
Johannat Rodgers is with us, it is uncertain whether
He will continue thro' the Winter - a return at the close
of Warm Weather - Your Mama is well & joins me in
Love to Mr Smith - Maria - Yourself to You.
I am my dear Daughter
Your sincerely affectionate father
John Spivacum

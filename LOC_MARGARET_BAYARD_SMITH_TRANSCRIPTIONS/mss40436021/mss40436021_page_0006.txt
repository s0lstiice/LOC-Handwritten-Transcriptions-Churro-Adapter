My dear Daughter
New Brunswick July 27th 1821
In my letter of June 27th to your
Sister Maria, in answer to hers of June 21st. I promised my
next should be to you - I have before me your affectionate
letter of June 12th. It certainly gives me great pleasure
to hear that Mr Smith is succeeding so well in Business
& that both He and You are respected & taken so much notice
of by the Inhabitants, may these blessings be continued
to you both - And may You have gratefullness in the
receipt thereof, to God your bountiful Benefactor -
When the Mercies of God & the bounties of His Providence
are received with humility & gratitude and more especially
when they are multiplied & flow to us thro the channels of
covenant Love from our Blessed Lord & Saviour
Jesus Christ, then indeed they are sweet & comfortable
But alas! how apt are we to receive the Gift & forget the giver

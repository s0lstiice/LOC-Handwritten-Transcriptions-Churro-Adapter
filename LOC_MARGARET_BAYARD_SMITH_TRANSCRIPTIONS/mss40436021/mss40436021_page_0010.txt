My dear Daughter, New Orleans Jan 24th 1803
I should long en have not answered
your kind & Affy Letter of Octt 16th had just heard
from You, so frequently by your Letters to your
Sisters – be informed my dear daughter I feel the
tenderest Parental Affection for You – You are so
in my thoughts & Endeavour daily to remember You
in my Prayers to God – that He would preserve
& keep You in his Fear & Love – that Your
Husband, & dear Child, may be truly the Children of
God – I am very desirous of seeing your Daughter
& giving Her my Blessing – but above all – that both
you Mr Smith & Julia may receive the Blessing
of our Lord & Saviour Jesus Christ – to Him I hope You
have devoted this dear Child, relying on His Mercy
& Grace to make it an heir of Eternal Life
Oh! my dear Daughter how vain is Philosophy & worldly
Wisdom when compared to the Wisdom to is from Adam
the Apostle Paul tells us – that the Religion of Christ
was to the learned Greek & Jewish kno ws & to the Jews
a stumbling block – but to those who experienced the
Power of it, both Greeks & Jews – The Power of God
to Salvation – Alas! that this plain Philosophy should
sett itself up against Revelation – & that our poor
Unenlightened Minds should assume the prerogative
of God – We are by this Philosophy to believe nothing
but what is made clear to our reason & what we
can fully comprehend – but can We scan Omnipo ne
can We Account for One of the Laws of Nature can we
by our Reason Account for the production of a Sword

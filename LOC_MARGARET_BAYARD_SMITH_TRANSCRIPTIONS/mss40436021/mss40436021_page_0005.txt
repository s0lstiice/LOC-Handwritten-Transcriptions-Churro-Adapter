your short experience has no doubt frequently led you to contemplate
this Life as a State of Probation, & that we are but in the infancy
& due Existence – There is a Glorious prize still before us, & We are directed by the
unerring Word of divine Inspiration – By our Beloved Lord – to strive to enter in at the first Gate – to
strive the good fight of faith – to lay aside every weight – & the Sin that most easily begets – to run the
tree that is set before us looking to Jesus as the Author & Finisher of our Faith –
If We sell our Faces & Remain Hard – We must expect to
encounter difficulties, Losses & Tributes – Our Beloved Lord never
promised His children great temporal Happiness & prosperity,
but on the other hand warned them by telling them “in
“the World You shall have tribulation but He of good cheer
I have overcome the World” Yet blessed be God even in this
World the true children of God enjoy the blessings of Life
with abundantly more relish & satisfaction than the World
The receives all the Mercies He enjoys as coming from the
hands of a kind & beneficent Parent – His heart overflows
with Gratitude & Love & even the afflictions that beset
Him, when sanctified He counts among His greatest
Mercies – I am happy to hear that you have a Church
& Regular Worship on the Sabbath, I trust You will not fail
to frequent it – Your Sisters write You by this Opportunity
as customary – I have long Christies, gives you all the
Information in this place affords – to them I refer you
to Mr Smith to put me down as a subscriber to His Pastor
Altho You know I am not a Friend to His Politics –
You may expect to hear from me in a short time
when I intend enclosing You some Money –
Your Mama joins me in Love to You & Mr Smith
wishing You both Health & Happiness
I am My dear daughter
Your Affectionate Father
John Bayard
P.S. Enclosed You have a Bank Note & please to receive on it $100

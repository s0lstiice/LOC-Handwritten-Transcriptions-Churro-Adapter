I cannot determine it is very uncertain whether I shall
be appointed to attend the Genl Assembly of our Church
at Philadl this Spring - I should that ever take place
I fear I would not be able to undertake so long a journey
inside I am one of the board of Directors of a Turnpike
Road from Trenton to this Place to will be begun
this Spring & will require my attendance - but I have
not made up my Mind - when I do I will write you.
Your kind invitation in to Mr Smith joins has considerable
weight with me & I am sure he is convinced, that it
is my duty, I will undertake it - Maria is very anxious
on the occasion & my other friends generally advise me
to undertake the Journey
I was sorry to hear of the Loss of your Young
tree, but I see you have a Nursery advertised near
George town of a great variety of grafted conicalated
tree where perhaps you may be supplied
All Friends here are well - Maria went
Yesterday with us to see Mr John Foster Foggy, who spent
the day with us to stay with them a Week -
Your Mama joins me in aff & regards to
You, Mr Smith, the Children & your cousin James
from my dear Margaret
Your affectionate Father
John Bayard

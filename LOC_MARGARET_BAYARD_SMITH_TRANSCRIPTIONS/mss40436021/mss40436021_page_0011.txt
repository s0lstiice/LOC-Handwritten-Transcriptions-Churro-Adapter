It has given me great uneasiness to hear of your
situation indistinction, occasioned by the Geor g ian Flag
& that it has continued so long - That one time resolved to
undertake a Journey to Washington in order to see you
& if agreeable to Young Mr Smith to bring you with
me to Brunswick, but from the Advice of Friends
& the danger of encountering the inclemency of the
season at my advanced State of Life I have given
it up - Yet I am very anxious, that as soon as
You can possibly undertake the Journey - You should
come to Brunswick as I believe it will contribute
to the restoration of your Health & be a great Gratitude
to Your Friends & connections, Oblige me the Pleasure of
my
only seeing You but my little Grand Daughter - I suppose
from the Multiplicity of Business it will not be convenient
for Mr Smith to accompany you but perhaps He
may find leisure to come for you when Your Health
shall be established - if not perhaps I will undertake to conduct
You Back. Your Mama & family are well & join
Me in Love to You - Mr Smith & Child -
I am My dear Daughter
Your sincerely Aff ly Father
John Bayard

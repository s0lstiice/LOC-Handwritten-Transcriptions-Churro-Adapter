& almost idolized leader. Thus the virtues of party spirit are
somewhat mutilated, may I even entertained towards him
the most kindly dispositions, however he may be not only for
politically but personally, friendly to my husband, yet I could
not but believe that he was an ambitious & violent demagogue
born & vulgar in his manner, audacious & rude in his appearance — for each had the public journal & private conversation

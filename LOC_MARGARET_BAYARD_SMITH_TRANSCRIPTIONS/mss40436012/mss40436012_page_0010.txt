fire and brimstone. It will not be the habitations of the

burned to the ground; for it is by fire, that this sin
has usually been punished. The then gave many instances from scripture history in which destruction by fire
of cities, dwellings & persons, had been the consequence
of violating the fourth commandment.
At the time this sermon was preached, the most
remote apprehension did not exist of a British army
ever reaching Washington, although war was impending.
The predictions were verified -- The Capitol -- the President's House
& every building belonging to the government were destroyed & that
by fire. Mrs Madison told me, that on her return to the city, after
the British had left it, she was standing one day at her sisters
door, for she had no house of her own but until one was provided
by the public residence with her sister & while them, looking on the
devastation that spread around, Mrs. Buchanan, passing along,
she called to him & said, I little thought Sir, when I heard
this remarkable threatening sermon of yours, that its denunciation
would so soon be realized," Oh Madam," he replied "I trust
this chastening of the Lord, may not be in vain."
I am afraid the good man's hopes were never realized
for as far as I could collect, there was not for many, many years
afterwards any change in the observance of the Sabbath

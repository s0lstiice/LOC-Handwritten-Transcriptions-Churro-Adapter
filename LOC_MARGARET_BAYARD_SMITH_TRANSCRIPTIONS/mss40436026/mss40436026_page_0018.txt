to build on so slight a foundation. But you
knew I always desired Maria for a member of
Senators. Mr. T. has no children, as of a respectable mind
& stands of a marked disposition; and if fancy favors
him, what objection can there be -- I have not
given Maria a hint of this, as I know
that it would produce awkwardness in
the city.
I cannot repeat too often, do not let the dear
Selden forget me.
I shall be very happy to hear of Nicholas being settled
by his old suit. More enterprise in industry should
strongly recommend this place, as I am persuaded a
form in any profession or business would here find
encouragement to suit either side of great requests
wealth can never be acquired.

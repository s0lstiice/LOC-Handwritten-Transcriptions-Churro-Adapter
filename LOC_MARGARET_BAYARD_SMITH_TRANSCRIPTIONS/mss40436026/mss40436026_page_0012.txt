This has been the most gloomy day I have experienced since here; it has neither hail, rain, snow, nor a thick fog & constant darkness, has covered the ground which fell a day or two ago, to land, the Smiths and I went out the whole day, exploring the narrow land which was between four & five. I sat at my work during the morning & brought more of the land than I had the time to do. I went to bed at 10 o'clock, and slept very soundly. I must sincerely bid adieu. We were together. How thought I of India ever more, we would select one for an interesting work, & she should read to me, while I worked, with Mauanne and Scutia on a little bench by my side, & the night read one of her little stories, or we might select Colloquial French. I knew not how it is, while I write I find myself in a delightful solitude, I write for myself and I think only of home.

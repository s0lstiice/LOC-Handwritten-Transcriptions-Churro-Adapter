in which I could gratify him so much, as in reverence
of his sister happiness, he is very anxious for both, especially
Isaac, who is now at an interesting a point of life,
a point which may fix her future character & views.
She is now 20, but has as yet been treated more like
a child than woman. To oppose those of true refinement
& which I think I should confine for real character, I consider
her means of improving her mind, & give her occasions
to exercise her own judgement, & to yield to the natural
sightliness of her disposition, to make her favorite
happiness a friend; have indeed been favorite abstinence with
me, I hoped for her to arrive. I am certain Mary
did not mean that her sister should be wanting in affection
& I know that it is contrary to her own expression

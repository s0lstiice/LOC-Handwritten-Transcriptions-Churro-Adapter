My dear Sir,
I have received your letter of the 1st inst. and am
very much obliged to you for the information it
contains. I am sorry to hear that the King has
not yet returned from the West Indies, as I am
sure he will not neglect the interests of the
United States, and will give them the attention
which they deserve.
I am your obedient servant,

advantage of retirement & study
from Pitt's letter
The study of things is much above our
comprehension, renders the sciences of this
world very trifling on our opinion &
when we can enjoy the pleasure
of living enroled in the immensity
of space one cannot think about what
is passing in the streets of Paris.
Study has its great advantage,
it enables us to live in peace with
ourselves & deliver us from the external
trivial & ineluctable imposts.
Do you know what
is most difficult in the great world?
to reach the end of the day without
being sensible of ennuiæ?
"If I knew, I would believe
myself when I am so completely deaf
to you. I have only began to live, since
I sought for peace a gardener in
retirement. Every other mode of life
would now be intolerable -- I am so
necessary for you, it would be death
to me --
Encircled
Corcyades was a disciple of Encircled,
the fate of his master becoming
to him the danger of offering himself
a celebrated opinion to turn into
themselves to the drama ambitions
of them some of that glory Scribeles
had already acquired & Scribeles was
then acquiring. He was very young
when he engaged in the undertaking
but he had every ground for which
should ensure success, devoted from
his early years to literature, devoted
to the best masters, experienced in
the deepest philosophy & sufficiently
discerning in each element of men's
passions, tendencies to every metaphysical
system, to every philosophical doctrine
to all the finest feelings of humanity,
to every uniformity of sentiment
in the life of all Nature &
Art, had read with each other to
form his excellent mind it is no
wonder if he now became the delight
of ornament of his country; what
perhaps is a more solid honor to
him he entertained an early friendship
with Scribeles who was twelve years
younger than himself & remained
about our years. This friendship founded
on the firmest principles of mutual affection
& supported by a constantly of manners

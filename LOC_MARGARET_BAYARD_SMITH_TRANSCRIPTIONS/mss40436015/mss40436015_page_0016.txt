Snow storm
The sky is saddened with the gathering storm,
Thro' the husk'd air, the whitening showers descend,
At first thin waving; till at last the flakes
Fall broad, a wide a foot, dimming the day
With a continual flow.

Winter Scenes
No longer autumn's glowing red,
Upon our forest hills is shed
Away has passed the heather veil
That bloomed so rich on Reed-path fell.
The sheep before the pinching heaven,
To sheltered down & dale are driven,
Where yet some faded herbage prime
And yet a watery sun beam shines.

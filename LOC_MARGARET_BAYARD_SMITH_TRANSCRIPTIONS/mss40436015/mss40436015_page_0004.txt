Upon my word, I am sometimes tempted to
think, that one may make the world allow
for it whether as one please, if one can but
be steady in one opinion. It is but being the
beloved for it; that all I & I have the
power to oblige those around me to do with it
will not appear to me that one opinion is
not so, -- is it possible that I can
make their very feelings to be of
such importance to all the family.
Clarence

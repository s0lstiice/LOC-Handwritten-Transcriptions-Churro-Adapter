The Forum
It was a scene in the age of Ro-
man greatness, of unparalleled splendour
& magnificence. It was bordered on
both sides with temples, & lined with statues
It terminated with triumphal arches
& was bounded, here by the Palatine hill
with the imperial palace residence gat-
tering on its summit & there, by the
Capitol with its ascending ranges of porticos
& temples. Thus it presented one of the
most exquisite exhibitions that eyes could behold
of these superb monuments, the memorials
of their greatness & the trophies of
their fathers, the Roman people assem-
bled to exercise their sovereign power
& to decide the fate of Rome, of
horns & of nations.

Sunday Schools - St Charles Borromeo
Among many of the excellent institutions of this
great & good man, which still remain, is that of
Sunday Schools; & it is both novel & affecting to be-
hold on that day the vast area of that of the
Cathedral of Milan, filled with children, forming two
great divisions of boys & girls, ranged opposite to each
other, & those again subdivided into classes, accord-
ing to their age & capacity, drawn up between
the pillars long ranges of pillars of the immense
tribe.
Two or more instructors attend each
class & direct their questions & explanation
to many little individual without distinction.
A clergyman attends each class, accompanied
by one or more laymen for the boys & by
persons of the first distinction. Tables are placed
in the recesses for writing. This admirable
institution is not confined to the Cathedral of
Milan even to Milan. The poor archbishop
extended it to every part of his immense diocese
& it is established in all the parochial
churches of the Milanese & many in the
neighbourhood.

Sabbath
Hail Sabbath! Day of mercy, peace in rest!
From our Lord and Saviour is a neverless, spirit.
The human race, The wheel, the law, violent
Pole thought, no more; Our God's sentiments still
Much quiet spreads her wing, invisible;
And when their count self silent are the fields
Through whose sweet paths the Lord gives tomorrow freely
To him he may use, a banquet yields
Each abundant meal
A provision of beauty to his eye.
The little boys are with him, dancing flowers
Or playing the toy number golden flies
So by the Daisy's side he spreads the dawn
Renewing friendship with the birding flowers.
In this great temple offering thankful joy
Is from the infants great in good words
birth soul attains to nature's harmonies,
Sometime cheerful as a sporting child.
Count Law, Roffynes

They reached the church. The door before his
hand gave way. Behind in his train, descent
Straight to the tomb -- There he knelt
And bowed his face upon the sepulchre
Weeping aloud.

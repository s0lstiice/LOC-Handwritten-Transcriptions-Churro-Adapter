of lofty public yet in affection mild
and tender, as a young mother's joy,
Of use it strange if at such sympathy
The feelings which within his breast resided
And which he had thought should give forth like flowers
After cold winds of night, where gentle gales
Restore the genial sun.
Rodericus.

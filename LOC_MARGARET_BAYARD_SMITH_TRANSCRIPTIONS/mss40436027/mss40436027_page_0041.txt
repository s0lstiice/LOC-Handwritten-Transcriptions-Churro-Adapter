operations -- which will keep me busy for many months --
we have planted out this spring 280 or 300 pine apple trees
& much to put out at least 700 or 1000 real trees
from our own nursery. We have sold more than 100 doll.
worth of wheat, hinders retaining enough to supply the
family with flour for several months. -- I send you
some Siberian corn -- you may have these seeds in the
summer & a year, in July they have flour -- I planted some
of the 4th of July & gathered a fine mess in 50 days
from that time -- notwithstanding that during that season we
had but one shovell -- which greatly restricted it. I likewise
sowd the Siberian turnip seed -- this is turned entirely
on the account of the tope, which are very abundant &
may be cut once in the autumn & give 8 or 10 turns
during the spring & summer -- They are very much finer
than cabbage sprouts & are so abundant that they answer for the early food of cows & I
sowd them pennately for my good brother & why I remember
is so fond of Queens. -- They are sowed like common
turnips & turned to about 12 inches apart -- sowed
beginning of August -- I can think of nothing else in
this way for you -- being a young beginner -- I
surely thank you to send me at some time either the
convolvus miner, & balsam apple, the drought killed
all of the latter which I had last summer. I shall look
up for some pieces of my own -- the children cakes
letter, the I have left most in the City -- Do not give
up this plan -- I like to know how your children are
clothed --

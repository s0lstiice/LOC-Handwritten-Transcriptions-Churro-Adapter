Washington - Oct. 18th -
I really must break a memorandum book to among the
notes note down when I answer the letter I receive. I cannot
remember when I last wrote to you concerning which
I have answered Francis letter or in fact what I said when
I did write. Francis says therefore I have given my interest in it to you
for the same things two or three times over - that time without
me I have been ready to let you have it at once in anything the
interests me is much as my previous letter over it -
probably I often expressed to some friends here are among

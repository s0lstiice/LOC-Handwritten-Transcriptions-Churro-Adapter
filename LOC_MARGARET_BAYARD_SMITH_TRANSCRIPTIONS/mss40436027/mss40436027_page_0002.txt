February 26-1805-
Why my sister do you not write to me often? you know not how painful I feel this seeming neglect & this is not the first time that I have addressed
my heart, or made me shed bitter tears. I sometimes think that absence,
& perhaps our difference in regard to certain points, have in some degree
deserved your affection for me, you are together with father, with the children
& men near & dear friends, thus surrounded by those you love, your heart
& thoughts are thus much occupied to think much of me who am so far away. As for me, out of my own family I cannot in this
place name one that I can call friend or even when I must care for. -- At present I sometimes scarcely speak to Mrs Smith or ever
see them though in the day & when my visit is extended to her, it is from the arrangement of the day only that I meet with my
hearty welcome reception. The only dear relations so far from absence
whom I affectionately speak to, the experience of many lay between
me, that no where can I ever meet with friends so beloved
& so worthy to be beloved, friends on whom I may rely with
equal confidence & where sincerity I can never doubt; when
affection & esteem are cemented together to same blood, same
& friends; this can be more or durable or perfect. I cannot
tell you how impatiently I always await the arrival
of the mail, when I think it is time to hear from them
well, & how disappointed I feel when no letters arrive --
It is a very, very long time since you have written to me
& Harri's letters are proving every day less frequent, do
& pray now, scarcely know how you are all situated or
employed, since Dup's illness my anxiety to hear from
home is greatly increased. I have not seen for a
few days, part quite well having taken cold in my
heart & perhaps that is the reason of my feeling an

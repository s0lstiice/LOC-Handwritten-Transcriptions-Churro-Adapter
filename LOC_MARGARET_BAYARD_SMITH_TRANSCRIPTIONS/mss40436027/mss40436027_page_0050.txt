Sidney - May 17. 1803-
Betsy Johnson & Mr Smith have gone to take a
long walk to a mile at least 5 miles from hence, so
after finishing the childrens lessons I supposed devoting a long
morning to letter writing; but the morning has almost gone
making my butter, attending my poultry, entertaining my young
gardener, you do know it has done me the honor I desired
for my hero- But I am anxious to answer my dear sister
for my hero, 2 The many other things yet remain of my days
work, this hour shall be appropriated to her - I really rejoice
that she could have intireed her welcome to stranger met,
you would visit us more frequently. I particulate in
all your Pleasures, but in none so fully as those
you derive from your children - I think Mary Ann has
been very diligent in her studies; she has need a great
deal 2 I rejoice in her taste for books, she does not resemble
Margaret, but suffers weakness of which to those circumstances
- But tell, that her Papa used often to reproach
me for giving too much of my time to reading an
used to promise great things for her what an excellent
house keeper she should be at 15, what fine, middling
pies, basters & wagons she should make, saying that
woman could never be a great woman in her profession, or
cooking was one of the greatest accomplishments an
woman could have - are his prophetic fulfilled, 2 does
Mary take this part of family business? Many and
many has commended Beaufort to me, Aunt Maria must
be a better to visit us at Washington. If we had a
large house here I really cannot conceive any thing
which would give me more delight than to
have Mary, Sally & Jane, with the young gentlemen
here. - What charming walking & riding to which would
we not have - provided we had a carriage. - well
who knows but some of these days we may have a
little richer & have a larger estate & carriage in
the bargain - this is better The last will come first, I
am so well I do not think I am not able to take long walks as I used to

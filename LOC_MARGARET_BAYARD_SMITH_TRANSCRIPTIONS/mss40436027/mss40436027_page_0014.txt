My dear children are very well. John makes no progress in
the family of spirits but in his bodily movements and intelligence I
have been able to give him some useful duties which he has not been free
from in his situation of being so completely left in sight that he is supposed
to have a chance of escape. He is so comfortable and enjoys his health
I hope that by this time all your little fleet have received from
their late admirers I am looking every day for a letter from them
announcing an addition to Breach's family, there is not a doubt
that he is of much utility to your family. I feel sure he was supposed
to affectionate, but at present I am sorry for him. I have not seen
your letter from Gen. Scott. Remember me affectionately to
Cafin & to my other friends. Your Brother never forgets anything without
particulars after him. If you will always remain with me I will send
you a letter. Washington has been on the subject lately, I must admit it is apparent
to the world that here is both interest and money to be made. The road
she would be very badly in opinion among the people if ever you came here. You would not have the thanks of Washington and
the country if I helped her to remain here. The means she should be
able to do it if she had the proper spirit of it.
Ever your affectionate
son
Franklin
to Mr. and Mrs. Washington

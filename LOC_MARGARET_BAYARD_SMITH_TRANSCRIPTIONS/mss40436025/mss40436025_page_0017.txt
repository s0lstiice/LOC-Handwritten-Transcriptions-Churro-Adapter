Mr. Kirkbride's
Philadelphia
May 8th 1861
From the 1st Genl. Govr. in Command of the Army of the United States
to Genl. Grant, Genl. in Chief of the Union Armies
My dear Grant:
I have the honor to forward to you the enclosed letter from Genl. Lee
which I have received from the hands of Genl. H. W. Slocum, Genl. in
Chief of the Army of the Potomac. Genl. Lee states that he has received
the letter from you of the 2d instant and has read it with great attention.
He says he is deeply impressed with the importance of the subject
and earnestly desires that you will take the matter in hand and
endeavor to secure the prompt and complete restoration of the
Union. He says he is confident that the Union will be restored
and that the Government of the United States will be established
on the basis of the Constitution and the laws of the United States.

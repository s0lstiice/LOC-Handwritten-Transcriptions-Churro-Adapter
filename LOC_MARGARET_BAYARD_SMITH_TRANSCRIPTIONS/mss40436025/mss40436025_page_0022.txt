This evening after tea, sister & I took a pleasant walk, &c. we conversed all the
about their future plans. The Desire has tenderest love to you, & bids me tell you
if she could, she would visit you before the week is over. Mrs & Mrs P. Desire
me to remember them. It is with reluctance bid you good night. lie my
Brother very much & are rather dull without him. adieu adieu —
Dr Munroch (R.H.)
to Mrs & Mr Munroch
Mr Washington

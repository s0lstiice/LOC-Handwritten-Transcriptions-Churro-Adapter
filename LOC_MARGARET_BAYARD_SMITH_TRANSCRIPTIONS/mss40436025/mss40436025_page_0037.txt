Monday night, Jan. 6. 1801.
differently & truly my dear sisters, I have had no time to
write, from the time I got up which took up most of my time
until I got to bed, my time is engrossed or rather wasted by
company. To be sure, these are the hardships, I more
visiting goes forward than at any other time. I intended
writing to you, the day after the nation, but being confined
to my room, every minute of the morning was occupied by incidents,
I dined at Gros Ventres & Mr Brown spent the evening
here. Every day, I generally from the same cause, I have
been prevented writing. Julia's Father & sisters arrived
last week. Interior & Eleanor are very different, but both
claiming, quite from morning, to night they are whispering,
laughing, romping, or challenging, in all of which they make
me join; so that I have no time for reading or writing.
Leaving, with fine somewhat better, as Mrs Robinson
is to attend Julia & her sisters, I propose to, to make
a vast progress in French. The girls talk, read & write it

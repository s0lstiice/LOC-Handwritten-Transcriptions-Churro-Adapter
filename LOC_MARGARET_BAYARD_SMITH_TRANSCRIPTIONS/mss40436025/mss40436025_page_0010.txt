Philadelphia, March 19, 1797.
Yes, my heart spoke the language of prophetic feeling,
when it called you my best friend -- for whatever love, with
so much spontaneous goodness, the apitited and overwhelmed
heart of another to let it rest and to be loyally. You have
acted with more reverence, friendship and tenderness than I
expected -- with all that I could! -- do not misconceive me.
I knew you to be benevolent, I knew you to feel the force of
friendship. I knew you to be tender. But in a case of such
infinite importance, I could not bring myself to believe,
that you could assume such an intrepid command of feeling,
and vigor of reflection, as, in the course of a few hours, to form
a decision on which was to depend the happiness of beloved
sister.
Pardon me for my misapplied distress of your ability
to exert so instantaneously all the energy of your mind.

Directions for the cultivation of the Muskmelon.
At first two smooth, or seed-leaves appear above ground, then
two rough or vine leaves, afterwards in the middle of these, the
third leaf rough & leaf-like appears, as soon as it dies off, it
must be taken off; for if left it would become the main
stock of the vine & ruin a great length, whereas if on its
first appearance it be taken off, instead of one, the plant
will shoot forth two lateral branches; the third leafy &
which appears on each of these, must be likewise destroyed,
This method will prevent a great extension of the vine,
The fruit will thus derive all that nourishment from
the plants, which would in the other case be expended
on the stalk & leaves. — When the fruit is the
size of an egg, the best should be selected & the root
cut off; when it is more than half grown, a stick,
stone or piece of wood, should be put under each
Melon & each day the melon should be turned, but
with great care, lest it should be broken from the
Stock.—

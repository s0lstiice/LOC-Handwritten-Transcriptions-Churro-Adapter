In precious vase or cup of gold
Was thy Sister's to impart
But a treasure never old
Could she profit by thy heart
Nature in her beautiful form
Nature in her noblest swells
Nature in her tenderest flames
Nature in her harmonious shell
Is in all her forms & shapes
The a magic charm bestows
Which sweet transports can not
For every tender feeling heart.

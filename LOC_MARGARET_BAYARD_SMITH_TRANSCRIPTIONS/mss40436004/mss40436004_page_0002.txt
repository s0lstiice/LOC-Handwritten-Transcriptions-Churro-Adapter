in him genius, strong, brilliant & unlimited, a good mind indignant at
suppression, possessing clear perceptions of truth, which he boldly declared.—
But, a disposition weak & feeble, often sacrificing his principles for fear, a
nearly courting that power, which while he continued, he desired.—
Voltaire was born the 20 of February 1694. The fortune left by his
father, was doubtly advantageous, procuring him a liberal education, & giving
him that independence which secured liberty of principle, never obliging him
to sacrifice that liberty to a patron, or the prejudices of society. The youth
free from the affronts,ensions of poverty, preserved him from servility, from
that restless & suspicious irritability, of which his desire of fame afterwards
made him guilty. While at college his future greatness, was predicted
by his teacher, at that early age his generous persuasion was discovered, when
Odipus was performed he was only 21y. The boldness with which he
bothered through the fathers of superstition & declaring against tyranny,
frequent banished him from his country & once confined him to the
Bastile.—

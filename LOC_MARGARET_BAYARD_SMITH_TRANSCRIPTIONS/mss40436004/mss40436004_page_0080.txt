entirely centered at home. And I have been, happily
as the tenderest cares, as the unbounded confidence & the valued
esteem of a beloved husband could possibly make me; The
society & affection of my excellent sister, have added greatly
to my pleasure; for without her, Schofield have been
solitary & indolent. About the 20 of September the
weather changed from dry & disagreeable to cold & rainy
weather. The health was the consequence, 2 days, for
10 days confined to my bed; since then, none of the
family have been quite well. It is now a year
since we were settled in this house, in a few days
we shall move to a new house, & we will be in a situation
unaccountably different from the one in which we are
now situated. With my apparent improvement in health
& my prattitude & diligence the future increase, I may
the closer of every year find me more worthy of the
providence of my God.
D'un cœur qui t'aime,
Mon Dieu, qui peut troubler ta paix?
Il cherche en toute ta volonté suprême,
Il ne se cherche jamais.
Pour la terre, sans le ciel même,
Quel autre bonheur que la tranquille paix
D'un cœur qui t'aime?
Washington.
On the 29th of November 1801 my dear daughter was given
birth. Gracious God, the creator of our infant, enable me so
to perform the duties of a mother, that she may be virtuous
& happy herself, & a source of good & happiness to others.
Enable me so to repelate the warm affections of my heart,
that may be even ready to receive them sublime to the principles
of religion & the dictates of reason.

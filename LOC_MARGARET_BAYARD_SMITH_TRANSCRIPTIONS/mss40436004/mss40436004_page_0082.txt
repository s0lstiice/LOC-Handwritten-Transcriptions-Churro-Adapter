Then part that all which fortune has tested
I render this transient and mortal stage
To spirits like weary and fatiguing roads
And other alike infirmity and age
That serves of fancied pleasure wont I have
This little race of brothers to alter
And pass the short remainder of my days
The patriarch's parent, and the daughters' friend.
Delusive dreams return to gild my years
And rise again in all your forms of fair
Rejection now for hospitable officers
And grief away'd by solitude and care.
Order just heaven! but where the heart is true
The human drip of bitterness will still
Not can we have the privilege to mourn
Till we have lost the faculty to feel.
Religion, come, then daughter of the skies
And gently left thy salutary roof
Or let the daring argument of sighs
For Tolstoy tax the justice of my God.
Oh! make me then All-singing power resigned
They awful fiat humbly to receive
And oh! forgive the weakness of a mind
Which feels as mortals and as such must grieve
"In oft when heaven has granted the spiritual
It pours a disfected flood of mental day.
Left unaided by posterity from her study converse
Does reason rule with once collected force?
Is the rich game of genius turn once bright
Liter stars which throve burst from the night?
Are the sweet energies of thought impotent?
With tender transport on the tumultuous breast,
To fire to laughter, flights to impetuous mind?
Then why were Homer, Milton, Shakspeare, Hind??"
"Hobo, like a dove had bound at her breast
I'll chain by iron from that place of rest,
And thoughts wide range the humming wanderer goes
But fables or secret symbols of hope
Her friendly sprays amid the desert flame
To rest her feet and smooth her ruffled plains.

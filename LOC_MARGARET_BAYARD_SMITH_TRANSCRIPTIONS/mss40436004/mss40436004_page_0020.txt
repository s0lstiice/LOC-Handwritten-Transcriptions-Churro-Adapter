The Persian Empire under Zoroaster taught that there was one
universe being whom they called Anathras, & two divine principles,
opposite in their nature, one productive of good, the other of evil.
That after a war of 6000 yrs between these two principles in which
they should alternately conquer, the good principle should triumph
and that all mankind should speak the same language
live in peace together, and that this was to be effected by the
supreme being, or mediator.—
The continental believe God to be the principle of life, from
which all other beings emanate: that he is the creator of the
Universe which he governs: that he is not the author of evil
but of good alone: that the human mind is of celestial
origin that when it descends from the body, it reunites with
that being, from whence it proceeded. Render the sublime
being, they worship in former times as possessed of immortal
but not by the sacrifice of animals, or raising of Temples, but with the
eye lifted up towards heaven, and with the mind free from criminal
injurious & voluntary action to God, was deemed a religious sacrifice
& the sacrifice was honored amongst them. The devout individuals are
obstinate by superstition, for what but superstition is that worship which
inflicts torture on ourselves or others, to please the God of Love. The bramans
obstinately their bodies, to continue the most cruel & lingering.—
Among the Egyptians, the religion of Philostratus was divided into two
sects, one addressed to the vulgar, the other confined to a select num-
ber of the finest. The vulgar or common religion, was the most degraded
& irrational superstition, the worship of men, animals & objects
the sacred idol of Egypt appeared to be, that matter was the first
principle of all things & that materials for existence were found
in chaos, this under the name of night, was worshipped by them.
Besides this material principle, the Egyptians admitted, an active
& intelligent power, eternally connected with the chaotic mass, by
whose energy the elements were separated, bodies formed & which
continues to promote over the universe, being the efficient cause of all
effects, they seem to have believed in the immortality of the soul,
although they thought it passed thro' other bodies, before it returned
to the body.— The Celts, by which is meant those who inhabited
the north of Europe like all other ancient nations, had their

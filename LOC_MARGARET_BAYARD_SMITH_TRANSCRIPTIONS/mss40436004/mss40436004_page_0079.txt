When instead of calling only Christians my brethren, my
enefice when instead of believing the love a favor of God
confines to these few individuals of the human species, when
instead of believing the largest portion of mankind condemned
to eternal misery, when instead of these strong restraints,
my expansive heart, has entreated men of all ages &
all countries as my brethren, when I have candidly
said "Our Father who art in heaven", thus abiding them
as the kind parent of all mankind. -- How sublime, how
boundless was the rapture I felt, when in imagination
I have transported myself to another state of existence, &
believe the imyade of beings who have successfully passed
this earth from backing in the sunshine of divine Love
& rejoicing in their prolonged existence. Gracious God at
such moments how has my soul revolted from the gloomy
horror of that Hell, which Superstition has created!
But this state of mind, is perfectly yielding to all
the perfectionies of doubt & all the fears of transgressing my
daily self being involved in error. My early attachment
to this religion, & the pleasure of enthusiastic devotion,
the excellence & superiority of its moral precepts, the holy
& benevolent character of Jesus Christ, all combine to render
to me this system of Religion, a belief of which it seems
to me almost impossible to give up, but it is with
invincible repugnance, I should suppose the title of Christian
to be applied to me, because I am not a Christian.

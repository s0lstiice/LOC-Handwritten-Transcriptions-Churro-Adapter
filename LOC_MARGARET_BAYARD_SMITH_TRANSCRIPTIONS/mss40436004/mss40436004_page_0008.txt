Inscription for a Bust of Dante
To this was he, with firm and even step,
Who had the grace of fortune. God thou mark
each step, which I shall mention to the prince of men.
This car was made deaf, when twenty thousand round
the head and shield. This is the rigid eye
where pity remains glowed, as this the heart
That none so prone in death, worthy to die,
This life condemned him, let his dying hour
Oppressed him fit to live; yet Dante was:
Then only mean, then powerful, to his fate
The seat of genius, and amid the course
Of Time, by Death arrested from Death
Prevented the need of glory that he sought
to be a man in dignity. Southey

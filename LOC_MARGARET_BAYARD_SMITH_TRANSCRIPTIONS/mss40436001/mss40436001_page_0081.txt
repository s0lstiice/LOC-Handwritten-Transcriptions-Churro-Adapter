I never before have met in one family, four
children of equal unattainable disposition to each
other, at the same time in stage. Mary is
13, Matty 8, Julia 6, Susan 5 -- every one remains
on course to advance to acquire knowledge; Regan alone
is most excellent temper. Mary & Matty are endowed
with perfectual human energy, good humor, quiet
and activity. -- They seem perfectly happy, & there is no
evil they are not willing, they ambition to do, as
as for their sisters, neither necessity or election
makes them willing to forget them. Julia is very
2 good humored, but so easily influenced that it
is impossible to employ so great abundance of
activity, or animal spirits, as to form her attention.
Susan is violent & quick in her temper, but gives a
good humored & easily engaged character for a long time.
She has a remarkably tender, fond, considerate disposition,
she would willingly put the whole day in my
life, struggling to bring me out of her troubles
& suffering, to be helped & founded in a calm
antidote remedy.

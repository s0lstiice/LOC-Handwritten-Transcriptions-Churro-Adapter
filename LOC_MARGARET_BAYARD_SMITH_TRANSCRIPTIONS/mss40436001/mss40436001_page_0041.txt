April 3d. He have had scarcely a pleasant day since
we came into the country, however I never
since I came to continental soil & high winds have
kept me almost entirely within doors. During this time
I have enjoyed a delightful state of mind, especially
active & contented I have been able to give to children
via Mary's Baths from whose necessity of an evening
twist I have seen human & contrived from their
latter part have for the first time experienced
universals enjoyment of music. This excellent thing
with it an ever-increasing improvement in health
& prospects, but I cannot bear to leave of the situation
to have been obliged to send them from me & I cannot
take action to let myself neglected in every thing.
This evening I feel better & have rested in every
pleasantly with my little family. They were
complaining in the afternoon in another manner at
twilight I called them in & brought fine bread
on the hearth, they took their accustomed seats
around the apple or apple were distributed, but
John would not be contented unless I allowed
(Phillip a black boy) to partake of it in his
2 offices; as I found he had been annoying the
girls with stories I consented to his being one
of the party, but I might from some judgement
of him & his family esteem the character of
his servants, or so much importance in their effects
on the children, that I take even of principle of
getting well acquainted with them, even to
the relinquishment of a little dignity or authority.
I first entertained my little audience with a tale of national but
interesting incidents & was much pleased to find that it
excited much more attention & opinion than Phillips'
story of horses & the Grant, which however I did not quite
find I had quite & it was ludicrous & the conversation
was not improving circumstances -- I believe told it with great
satisfaction & I believed no objection. But my little one, who
had put on one of the dresses found in my place & every
morning, during my talks, became restless & totally inattentive

When they come, I collect them all around me, I find
here the hour says their lessons, in reading or telling
I show the little ones to play, when their cakes
finished their lessons, the little ones are called in
to my fires; I make it a rule that they should
all have seats, as I remember how weary I used
to be of standing -- when this little circle
seated around me, I make each repeat one of
Watts hymns, & explain in a familiar way
the precepts contained in them. There is no mode
of instructing I have found more useful than that
of asking the children questions concerning what
they have read -- of rectifying their ideas & language
after teaching them these excellent hymns, which
contain all the principles of Christianity, enough
in the most pleasant style, I sometimes read them
a story which is instructive & entertaining, &
inverses with them in with a familiar & amusing
manner, that they receive, my instructions without
turning it. I make patient inquiries of each
concerning the past week, in order to know how
they have passed it -- what faults they have committed,
enjoining on each the necessity of telling the truth; I have
found this an excellent mode, & I am to be assured
we had the best effect on her children, making
them more obedient to her & kind, to each other.
After this little course of amusing instruction is over
they all go to play, & eat fruit, cakes, & eggs, &
something of the kind is distributed. This summer
I have a swing in the yard & a little wagon
both of which afford great pleasure.
May I be enabled to frequenture in this pleasant
& instructing the poor children in this right neighborhood,
I am disposed to make myself more acquainted
with the poor families around me & to increase
the number of my relations. If I always enjoyed
the delightful discontent of seat, I feel at this moment
how easy, how pleasant would this duty prove; --
but often when disgraced in others, a when inde-
cent & inaction I find it difficult to perform --
During the three weeks I have been here, I
feel more contented with myself than I have
long been; for 10 days, I have daily given Watts
& Fulton lessons, they only knew their letters very
frequently, Watts has learned several hymns &
talks great delight in my instruction --
Heavenly Father most gratefully do thank thee, that
I am here acknowledge three weeks, happiness & usefulness
themselves.

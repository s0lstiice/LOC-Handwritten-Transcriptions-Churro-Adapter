Sunday
28 Once more does the sun shine forth bright and
unclouded - how merciful God is to nature's affrays
I too feel the influence of the cheering sun's electric
air - I cannot to have read all the morning,
but the morning has gone, & I have not opened
a book. - After dinner my scholars came, I quite
know their lessons I heard them say their hymns, but
I felt not that part, which enabled me to
intest them in their duty as I usually am
now in the garden picking some melons for them
when James called me in to return my to the house
and found a poor little boy of four years old
when a traveler had picked up on the road &
left here - I felt extremely interested for the
child & if going to be neglected, either means
endeared to discover his parents, one of the
persons I applied to thought he knew him
to be a citizen son & promised to give immediate
information - I went while I thought little of
being home & tried of sweet means & I succeeded & put him
to bed after supper - where he soon but all
his cares in sound sleep - alas! not in his
parents, I felt much for them - before I
went to bed my anxiety was achieved - the father
came for him, & went gladly did I restore the
child to his arms - it was a light moon light
night & James & I watched & I passed the night in
evening reading. Other affairs in town
Monday
29 A stormy day - The first we have had since May! I was
quite delighted at the prospect - It poured down torrent
of rain, the wind blew loud & cold - we huddled a
cheerful fire, I covered a table with baby rugs &
placed the children at it; till my work & James
sented himself beside me with Clarissa, which
he read aloud. - The day passed charmingly -
At twilight, or rather by fine light I danced with
the children while James played on the flute -
when they were tired they sat down & the fire to
eat supper, while James & I walked aim & arm
about the room, listening to the falling rain, the
loud wind, which seemed to add new charms to
our cheerful house & comfortable room - he danced
ten & ran 1 spent the children to bed, resumed
my needle & James his book, while pretty stood
by the table & saved. I do not needless for
to me than a year passing as agreeable & pleasant
a day & James says, he enjoyed it too much ever
to forget the 29th of April he have agreed to unite
to each other in the day next year -
In nature all they shows & forms
for feeling, genuine hearts have charmed
when summer smiles, the intelligent heat
& winter haunts the long dark night
in gusty storms.

While hence at home, my narrowest hour
Somewhat of manner and a man;
Though varying wishes, rather a fear,
Reversed the progress of these years.
Yet new days, weeks, a month but seem
The recollection of a dream;
So still we glide, down to the sea,
Of fathomless eternity.
Sydney, March 24th, 1807.
The winter has been a most distressing three months
to the city; I have again returned to the favourite retreat,
and now returned let me address to my memory
for the incidents that have occurred, to my death
for the sentiments & emotions it has enflamed; &
to my hours for the manner in which they have been
passed let me cast up the events recent
with my conscience let me do it faithfully, remembering
that it will be examined by a father & a judge.
Oh my beloved, my former, my father, & God
shall I recount the virtues, whose shall I begin
the enumeration of all they bring kindness, how shall
I dispense blessings from heaven, since all
are so great in the inducement of my heart determine
I for above all others have to thank thee
for preserving me from the trying temptations
to which I have been exposed. -- That these have
presented in the form of crime, that the flames
were not permitted to consume me. that he watch of
this weak heart was subdued; that the led by
circumstance to the very edge of the precipice,
thy protecting hand watched me from the danger
which I had voluntarily encountered. -- What an immense
struggle for power involved itself throughout all my friends!

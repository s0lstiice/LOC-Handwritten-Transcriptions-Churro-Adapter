The cheerful & busy home, one remembered with
gratitude. -- I have but once left my house
to pass an evening in society. Have lived as much
secluded from company as while there, after resuming
my family affairs in the morning, I devoted the
rest of the day, to my children, Mary a matron,
and in the evening I read -- this was my time
employed. I had left trouble from servants
at Christmas, I got a very good servant, where
situation I did all in my power to render comfortable & advantageous -- On that holiday,
I tried to make them happy with one who
were not happy at home -- I sought not among
the midst a gay for the companionship of my friend,
but among the poor & unfortunate -- my little companion consisted of 6 or 7 children & 2 worthy women
In the morning I rose with a determination of heart,
a tendresse & lapitude I was leachful I could
contribute but little to the happiness of the day,
but I soon caught the gaiety of my little flock
by entering heartily into their plays & amusement
forget myself -- I have never seen human
beings happier than these children & not
was the effect of sympathy between them
even three two unfortunate women forget
a while their troubles & mine as gay
as children. --

Off a little after Christmas I became acquainted
with a widow woman, who with three children
were suffering the greatest extremes of poverty
I have though he winter endeavored to alleviate
her sufferings, I have taken her eldest daughter,
I have likewise provided an excellent place
for her youngest. That this woman was ill used
increased my compassion for her, it was a new
motive for taking the daughters from her.
Already I feel but more than ever of
a mother's regret that her disposition is so
amiable & her manners so pleasing, to
win my affection, as I can hardly discharge
my duties towards her with so much more
ease & pleasure -- and may I do it faithfully,
may she enabled to continue as I have
begun. --

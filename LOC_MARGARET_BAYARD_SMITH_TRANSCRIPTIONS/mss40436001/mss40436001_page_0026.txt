says, thus far & no farther shall then come.—
Why then has nature given me a mind so
active & ingenious, an imagination so untrammeled
& free, qualities are not necessary to the discharge
of female duties? But why this agony, it
cannot charge my destiny as rather my own, I must
see a woman! I have only to try to describe
my duties faithfully.— How delightful would
be even table & quiet full scope to the powers
of mind, to plunge into all the actions, the sensations
the strong contradictions of life—to meet with certain
stars & objects that would call forth exercise
all the noblest qualities of the soul.— But
what to what end and how are these thoughts, I cannot
say, that mountains about me rise and spread in
horridness in this wild valley at its foot—
well, the valley has its charms & when I do not raise my
eyes to the summit of the mountain, I am befoged entirely
in calling the flowers that are scattered in my path.
I have often said & said with truth, & at this moment
say, I never knew any woman so happy as myself. But
what to a reflecting mind is the happiness which human
life can afford; Its comforts, its joys, its splendours, are
often mitigated, or magnified, that the immortal soul
turns with disgust from them, finding them a spectacle
for its desire to an individual & more individual existence.— I have every thing which this
world can give, it is impossible in this life for
me to have more source of happiness, a yet I
feel within me the capacity of being happier.—
Does not this speak the soul to be of higher origin
than the body, does not this prove that it is destined
to a distinct existence.— If we believe in the
goodness of God, it does prove this—in this case
is found whatever is necessary for the well-being
& enjoyment of the body, but can the soul find
in the whole world of human life, objects that
can satisfy its desires, objects that can exercise
its faculties? The labour of the body can obtain
the sustenance clothing & shelter necessary to the
preservation of animal life, but the most active
& entertaining soul cannot desired objects vast enough
to exercise all its abilities, or give scope to all its
energies. These are the reasons why I am
content with my present situation, I
have not the power to acquire

more, I have not the means of acquiring
more, I have not the opportunity of acquiring
more, I have not the inclination to acquire

It is implanted in my nature by God, I neither
the impurities of education nor voluntary effort
could ever eradicate it. This internal monitor,
this voice of God has instilled me, it has
brought conviction to my mind, in vain in
every life did I try to oppose it, in vain
did I try to oppose it, it has triumphed! —
Oh now how clear, how good, how perfect
over the universe seem, how admirable its
author, — The creator & the father of mankind!
who extends alike to all his children his
loving kindness, & Protecting power — In admiring
my heavenly father, my heart can embrace
all mankind as his children, — His sect, no
nation, no age, can divide them from my love;
Oh how sweet is it to adore a God, so wise,
so good, so just, so powerful! Oh how contrary
to honor that be in our creator & preserver.
What dreadful ideas once intruded themselves
on my mind, — and is it possible that I could ever
for an instant believe that this God, and
hence millions of millions of beings for the
transgressors of one! — That he could
could accept of the suffering of the innocent as an
abomination for the guilty! — That he would countenance
the knowledge of this plan of salvation to
a few individuals & promote miserable & heinous
eternal misery for an unworthy ignorant &
unmerited sinner! Did I believe these things,
for even in childhood when my life commenced these
things as I was taught, my heart & conscience rebelled.
I may still be in error, if not on the worthy
father, pity thy child, & hast impart the
light of truth, to one who earnestly humbly
requests it. But if I am right, give me
strength to maintain the truth, I may no motive
other of pain & pleasure, of honor or of disgrace,
of life or death ever induce me to severance from
it.

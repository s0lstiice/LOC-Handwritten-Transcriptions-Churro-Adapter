had the company of my dear husband after a much altered
home and quiet life to accompany, and not very much of the
defence of my own home, had I obtained his heart to make
it more than a mere mistress of the house, I would have
been the subject, perhaps, of the children and not of them as
they were their parents' property. The children must not be

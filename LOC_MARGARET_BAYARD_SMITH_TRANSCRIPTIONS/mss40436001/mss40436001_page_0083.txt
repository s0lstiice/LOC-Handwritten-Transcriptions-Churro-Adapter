I knew a pastor who turned atheist because the Prior
of his convent obliged him to mention within the walls
of his cloister that the Virgin Mary was born in sin
& that in the Calvonne he was obliged to support
the contrary. This man said very truly, my religion
is false; if my religion is false what is beyond all
dispute the best in the world, earnest virtue, if the
mother of falsehood, there can be no such thing as
religion & no such thing as a god.

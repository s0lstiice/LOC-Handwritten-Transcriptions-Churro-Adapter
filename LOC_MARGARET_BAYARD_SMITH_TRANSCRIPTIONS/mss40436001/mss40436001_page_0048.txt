He expressed his satisfaction in leaving his children so happily comfortable & his wishes for my establishment in life. He spoke of the trials he had experienced with the greatest deliberacy & Christian forbearance & I spoke in words equal to his, though he made no requisition upon me. His mind was free from care for myself. I should have wanted assistance to assist him. That my highest wish was to see his last moments peaceful & secure. There was an uncommon frankness & seriousness in his manner. I expressed a wish to remain with him in spiritual comfort, and when he felt sufficiently strong to listen, I earnestly prayed that he might be enabled to make his peace. He was now very useful to me in the intercourse of his thoughts. Even in depression, said he, your noble thought will attend you. I observed the dependence of the mind on the body that no so little a state has must necessarily suffer.

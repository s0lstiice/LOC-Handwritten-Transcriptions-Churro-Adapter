When the sun shines, the nestling oaks, the darkened trees
over the barren rock, can smile a charm with unvaried beauty,
but, when his rays are obscured by clouds, or by the shadow of
night, with the vivid coloring of the rose, the sparkling of
the diamonds; date to beauties of nature are lost to our sight.
Thus it is with life. When the mind is languid in
the heat of peace, solitude, domestic care, & even pain
are enlivened by the sunshine of the mind's work; but if the
infatuation of melancholy, or the gloom of distress, prevail, nor many
friends, however, is there an impartial benefactor.—
I scarcely know any night more deplorable, than that
of genius, conscious with a responsibility, condemned to the
earring of a more intimateish existence for the body, while the energies
of the mind, debased by poverty, slavery & dependence,
& condemned to association with men of unformed & utterly
deformable, one not to want, to himself— But this
is but too often the case with those who the representatives
of nature, are the offspring of fortune.

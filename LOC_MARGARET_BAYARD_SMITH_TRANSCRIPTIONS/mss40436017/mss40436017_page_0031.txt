Mountain, in Pike County, in this State
in a country very thickly settled, at the house
of a justice of Peace. Through the night
I was kept awake by what I conceived to
be a jubilee of dogs, assembled to bay the
moon. But I was told in the morning, that
what disturbed me, was only the common
barking of wolves, which nobody there
regarded. When I entered the Hall of Just-

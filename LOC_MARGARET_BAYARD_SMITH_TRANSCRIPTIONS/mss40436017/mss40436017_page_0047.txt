It is ordered that the first day of the month of
February in the year of our Lord one thousand
seven hundred and seventy-five and the first day
of the month of February in the year of our
Lord one thousand seven hundred and seventy-six
shall be declared as a holiday for the purpose
of the United States of America.
Given under my hand and authority as
above.
Samuel D. P. Smith
Secretary of War
United States of America

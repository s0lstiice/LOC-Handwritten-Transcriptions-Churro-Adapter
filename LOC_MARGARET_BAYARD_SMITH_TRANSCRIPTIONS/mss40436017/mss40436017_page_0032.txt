Examining in America.
At length I touch the happy sphere
of liberty and virtue dear,
Where man's liberty and power to claim
Themselves within the social frame,
Its grand system combination,
Themselves independent, pure and soul?
From every wild elliptic star
That, shooting with a devouring fire,
Kindled by heaven's arraying in
Its soft bath into chaos hurl'd
The systems of the ancient world!
The warrior here, in arms no more,
Thinks of the tail, the conflict over,
And gliding in the night, they own
For health and all, one and soon,
Smiles on the dusky rocks that hide
His sleeping sword's memorable prize!
While peace with sunny cheeks of tail,
Walks on the free unloved soul,
Effacing with her splendour slain
The drops that war had sprinkled there!
Thrice happy land! where he who flies
From the dark Isles of other ships,
From room or wants unnumbered woes,
May shelter him in proud refuge!
Hope sings along the yellow sand,
His welcome to a patriot land;
Once, the mighty wood receives
The stranger in its world of leaves,
Which soon their bower glory yields
To the warm thatch and autumn field;

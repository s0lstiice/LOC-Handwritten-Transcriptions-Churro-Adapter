responsible for the decisions of the

execution of the judgments of the
Court of Arbitration, and for the
payment of the judgments of the
Court of Arbitration.
The said Commissioner is to
have the right to appear in person
before the Court of Arbitration,
and to give evidence in person
before the Court of Arbitration.

1st of 26th November 1861
August
When I received my instruction I understood that I was to
take command of the 1st Division of the Army of the
Mississippi and to organize it into a division of the
Army of the Mississippi. I have since been in the
office of the Secretary of War and have received
no further instructions as to the organization of
the Army of the Mississippi.
When I received my instruction I understood that I was to
take command of the 1st Division, Army of the
Mississippi and to organize it into a division of the
Army of the Missouri. I have since been in the office of the Secretary of War and have received
no further instructions as to the organization of
the Missouri Army.
When I received my instruction I understood that I was to
take command of the 11th Corps, Army of the
Mississippi and to organize it into a corps of the
Army of the Mississippi. I have since been in the office of the Secretary of War and have received
no further instructions as to the organization of the
Mississippi Army.
When I received my instruction I understood that I was to
take command of the 2nd Division, Army of the
Mississippi and to organize it into a division of the
Army. I have since been in the office of the Secretary of War and have received
no further instructions as to the organization of this Army.
When I received my instruction I understood that I was to
take command of the 3rd Division, Army of the
Mississippi and to organize it into a division of the
Army.

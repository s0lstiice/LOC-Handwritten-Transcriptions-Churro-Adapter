Thermal river, then is the centre of the
Union. It is between the extremes of hot
and cold. It is not so far to the south, as
to be unfriendly to grass, nor so far north
as to have the portion of the summer
consumed in the length and severity of the winter.
It waters the soil, and mits in that climate,
which is most congenial to English grains,
and miserable to the cultivation of them.
It is the river, more than any other, in my opinion,
which must in the natural progress of
things connect by land and navigation
(now nearly completed 180 measured miles of
Fort Cumberland, at the expense of private
building, raised by private subscription), the
Atlantic States with the vast region which
is populating beyond all exception, to the
inclusives of it. It is designated by two for
the seat of the congress, and must soon its
extensive course through a rich and pop-
ulous country, become in time the grand
emperium of North America.

looking now at nature & at nature's God
see the medium of his own glory mingling
its new form, as one views the bright
sun & the beautiful landscape, through
a window glass, which reflects upon
the objects (or reflections) in colour
yet their own —
but when his mental sight shall
be cleared from the errors into which
it is now immersed — when he dare
look at paper & figures shall
be dispensed with the light of truth
in his heart & the light of reason
in his mind — he shall then see
nature & nature's god, in all
their beauty & their brightness —
when they will realize that they
he lost in the Infinite himself —

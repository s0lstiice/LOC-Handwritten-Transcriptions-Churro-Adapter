All other loves may fail they may decay,
Our faults or follies may disappear,
The friend in whom we fondly trust,
From us his changeful heart to wean;
Or we ourselves may change our kind,
And love the love that is our old stand,
Or lingering pain, or phisick care,
Or the love of beauty's young face,
Or love may gaze with altered eye,
When beauty's young attractions fly,
Or when the heart's old fire dies,

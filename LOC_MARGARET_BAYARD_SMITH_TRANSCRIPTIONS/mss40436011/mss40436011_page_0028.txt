a mother, crimes I must not tell
or I could unfail
would make your eyes with pity
your heart with indignation cold,
The morning I could no where see
The whom I had named
alas she had forsaken me
And not a sad tear Sally clunged,
Cold, cold the twenty blast blow
to find to dust, no fire to warm
I cast me down upon the snow,
hard pelted by the windy storm
Oh how I wept, my hands & feet
to think a mother used me so,
My thought I surely would have died
And they I did without it too
to home, no food, no clothes had I,
my mother wretched name & bone,
the neighbors kindness could I try,
That name would drive me from the door
Happily the child with Janony blessed
at last by old & hunger passed by
I wander'd to the night's long burn,
Beneath no roof I found my bed,
But in the street I lay me down
That morning with the sun's rose
at Panama doors I asked in vain
An shelter turn, hunger'd & froze
Some food & shelter to obtain
Ye kindly ones who never knew,
The agony of such an hour,
Pity I save may claim of you,
For yielding to the tempter fewer
A silver spoon, unseen I hid
Beneath my wet & lather'd clothes,
And the full oft my conscience hid,
Hunger a human's tender & true

Withdraw the occupations of
men, terminate their desires, exis-
tence is a burthen & the iteration
of memory a torment.
The difficulties & hardships
of human life, are supposed to
detract from the goodness of God;
yet many of the fastidious men
devise for themselves are fraught
with difficulty & danger. The
great inventor of the game of
human life, knew well how
to accommodate the players, the
chances are matter of complaint:
But if there were remained
The game itself would no longer amuse
the parties. In devising or in executing
a plan, in being carried on the tide of
emotion & sentiment, the mind seems
& unfolds its being & enjoy itself. Even
where the end & the object are known
to be of little avail, the talent &
the fancy are often intensely applied,
& business or play may amuse them
quite. We only desire respose to recruit
our limited & our wasting force: when
business fatigues, amusement is often
but a change of occupation. We are
not always unapplying, even when we
complain. There is a kind of affliction
which makes an agreeable state of
the mind, & lamentation itself, is
sometimes an expression of pleasure.
The painter & the poet have
laid hold of this handle & find
among the means of entertainment
a favorable reception for works
that are composed to awaken our
affairs.

the Verb is that part of speech which
denotes action or being
the Adverb is that part of speech
which is added to the verb relating
to some circumstance to show time or
place
N is added to a when it stands before a
word beginning with a vowel.
a or an is never used in the plural.
The is used either in the plural or singular.
The possessive case is marked by adding an
apostrophe and s to a noun, as Boyards hat
or by the preposition of before the noun
as the hat of Boyard

Prepositions serve to connect words
with one another, and to show the
relation between them. They are for
the most part, put before nouns and
pronouns as, "He went from London to
York," "She is above disguises They are
instructed by him."

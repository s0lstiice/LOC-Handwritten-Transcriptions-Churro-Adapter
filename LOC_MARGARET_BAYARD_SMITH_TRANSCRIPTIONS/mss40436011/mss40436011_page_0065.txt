Scott's Liquid Blue Dye
Take four ounces of the best Spanish
Indigo finely powdered add thee to one Drachm
of powder'd Arsenic put them into a large
Glass Vessel or Stone Jar, pour thecon gradually
one pound of oil of Vitriol when the effervescence
ceases add thereto gradually one pint of
Water stir the mixture with a wooden
Spatula, while adding the Water, which
must be done cautiously as the Water
being added to the oil of Vitriol creates
a great heat. —

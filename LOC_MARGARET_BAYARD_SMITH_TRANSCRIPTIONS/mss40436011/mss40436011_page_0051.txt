There are nine parts of speech.
first the Article which stands before
the noun to show the number and case
The Noun or Substantive is that part
of speech which denotes the name of any
place person or thing; the name of places
or persons are proper nouns, but the name
of things are common nouns
The Pronoun is a word which stands instead
of a noun and must agree with its antec-
dent in number gender and case
The Adjective is that part of speech which
describes the quality colour and number
it has three degrees of comparison, the
positive, comparative, and superlative

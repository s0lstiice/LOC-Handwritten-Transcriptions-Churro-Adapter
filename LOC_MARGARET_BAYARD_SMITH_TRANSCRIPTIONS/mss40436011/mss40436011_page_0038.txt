Cyrus
Cyrus was the son of Cambyses
king of Persia, he used to go to a
school to be taught justice and to throw
the javelin and shoot the bow he was
twelve years old when he went to see
his grand father Astyges king of the Medes
and his mother Mandane went with
him once at dinner his grand father put
several dishes of meat before him and
Cyrus asked him if they were for him
and his grand father said that they
were and that he might do what he
pleased with them and Cyrus said
this is for you because you serve my grand
father well this is for you because you

Not a day passes my dearest sister, which I do
not think of you I hold a kind of conversation with you for
hours sometimes I converse with you. I send without
the use of pen a paper from my thoughts will send them
away to you -- I cannot tell you why I have not written --
certain not from the want of leisure -- for more time than
seven months I have on my hands -- after a man's
degree -- Every day I resolve to remove myself to more modern
quarters & to employ myself more regularly in writing

me & let me feel your every feeling -- & oh, think by

I embrace you & your dear children -- with my whole
heart I embrace you -- & if I could I would comfort you --
My husband, my children & I am in most lively pain --
in every feeling -- in every word -- The utmost affliction &
distaste -- Terence's Margaret

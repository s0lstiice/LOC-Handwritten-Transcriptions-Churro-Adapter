Monday afternoon - June 10th 1861
I have neither within this hour, nor since yesterday received
Raynard's letter - The long agony of which my answer to
beloved friend has been released from a state of suffering
& in transition from mortality, with all its pain & infirmities
to a state of perfect & ascending felicity - And need you
consolation my sister for an event that has brought with
a glorious change on one you loved so long & so well?
Was you for what is his gain - is your loss - a loss
irreparable in this life? - Oh what a strong & tender tie
is broken - how must it loosen the heart held in life,
but then it forms a new tie to that invisible world
to which we must all follow - would that I could
comfort you my more than sister - my dear man-
tenal friend - but I am a poor comforter - I cannot
at this moment share with you your grief - but
one thought could I suggest that is not more familiar
to your mind than to mine - your undoubling father
your strong, high hopes, are the only support for the
bowed down spirit. The only balm for the wounded
heart. But how soothing, may how cheering must
be your recollections - what a slow & painful passage
has our beloved friend, and from time to eternity -
retaining all his faculties - all his affections to the
last moment - his heart beat, even after his lungs had
ceased their motion - love, kindness remained animal like
his last look rested on his wife & children - these objects
of strong affection - who had cheered & comforted him
declining age & wretchedness his hours of suffering & languor -
these were the last objects, on which he gazed - and when
they were lost to his sight, the vision which inspired

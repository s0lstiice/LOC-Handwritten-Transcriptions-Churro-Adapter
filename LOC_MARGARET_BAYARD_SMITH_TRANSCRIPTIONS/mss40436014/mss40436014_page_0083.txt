The pretty splendor remains,
That hallowed a sick man's heart
And the beautiful form of innocent truth
In languid sound as still.
And the spirit of other ages
Seated by fancy's foot,
The altar before the trembling thoughts,
Which we neither see nor dream of
In the pleasant land of the South!
Along a lost edition,
Rememberable State!
Two lands of liberty, and liberty and song,
Two men of the brave and true!
And I on this solemn shore!
And I for they France and my little home!
I will never return them more
From all it is ever land of the South!
Long gone from memory.
February August 12th 1832.
The Death Bed.
He watched her breathing though the night,
Her breathing soft and low,
From her bed, the wave of life,
Kept hearing to and fro.
He could not remain to speak
So slowly more & about,
He rushed but her death on power,
In the line closing out.
Our mother had told me once,
But I had not thought of her death,
And I thought her dying when she slept,
And sleeping when she died.
In that the more came true and true,
And still with the only chosen,
Her quiet spirit stood the bed,
Better even than ever!
Henry August 15th 1832.

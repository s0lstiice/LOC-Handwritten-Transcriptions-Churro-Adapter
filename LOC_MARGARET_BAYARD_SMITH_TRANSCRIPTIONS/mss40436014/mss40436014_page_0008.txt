The port lies his early tomb,
And right his dignified eye!
Now, — his heart burn'd on Homer's flame,
Or rests with Solon's match of skill,
By love's fancy-haunted dream,
Or among his wood-notes will!
Now but the child of genius knows
How dearly any such fortune won —
By faded thanks — by lost respect —
By life extinguished, are given!
Then does not girdle the wealth of Time
Upon her Steely's grave to lie;
Like spring's first flowers, the past came,
in Hopeful and to die!
Cambst. Bemfords.
- Halorama -
A Doge for Monge Park.
Hope no more in peace he sleeps,
All his pains and toils are over,
Is thine eye alone that watches,
His soul's rest to keep, no more?
He hath passed the unknown river,
He hath found a land of peace,
There his head in peace forever
Lies beneath the clothing waves!
We like him, our backs are pointing
Swiftly to an unknown shore,
There we know there's no bidding,
There is not fortune more.
But though this mighty ocean
Is of Heaven & earth & sea!
Then can't still the wild waves mutiny
At our hopes enflled on them!

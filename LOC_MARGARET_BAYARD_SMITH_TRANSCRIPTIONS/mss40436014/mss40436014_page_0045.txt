In the momentful secrets of thy soul,
In thy sorrow, in thy prayer.
I bless thee for kind looks and words,
I bless thee on my path who drew
In all the love in those deep eyes,
A glance I over hear!
On the voice that never to mine replied
But in kindly times of cheer,
In every spring of happiness
My heart hath rested here!
I bless thee for the last rich brow
From which affection tries
The right to pass on death with thee,
In peril by thy side!
And yet more for the glimmering hope
Even in those moments given—
Did not thy spirit ever drift
The light of mine to Heaven?
Now be thine!— Oh know we not
Our path must lead to this?
A shadow and a trembling still
Were mingled with our step!
He pledged to our young hearts, when stains
Were dark upon the sky,
In full deep knowledge of their task—
To suffer and to die!
Be strong! I leave the living voice
Of that my marriage friend,
With the thousand echoes of the hills,
With the torrents pouring flood,
A spirit may the leaves to swell,
I spoken on the air,
To render the patient from repose;
The painting from despair.
Dear it and bear it on my love!
By joyously endure!
But monuments must be altered yet,
Involate and pure!

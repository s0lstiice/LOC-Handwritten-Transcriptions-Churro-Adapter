"And you give the Thieves a full and free pardon?
I do prohibit I find my treasure untouched in
them, and Athene, if your majesty will
follow me, the treasure shall be restored to you!
The King and all his nobility followed the
cobbler to the mans of the St. Hermanns. There,
cringing his eyes towards heaven, Athene muttered
some words, which were supposed by
the spectators to be magical conspirations, but
which were in reality the prayers and thanks
giving of a sincere and pure heart to God
for this wonderful deliverance. When his
prayer was finished, he pointed to the wall,
and requested that his majesty
would order his attendants to dig there. The
work was hardly begun, when the whole forty
chests were found in the same state as
when stolen, with the treasure's seal upon
them still unbroken.
The King's joy knew no bounds; he embraced
Athene, and immediately appointed him
his chief strategist, assigned to him an
apartment in the palace, and declared
that he should marry his only daughter, as
it was his duty to provide the man whom
God had so magnificently favored, and had
made instrumental in restoring the treasure
to his long-lost home. The young princess, who was more
beautiful than the moon, was satisfied
with her father's choice; for her mind was
fixed with religion and virtue, and she had
been to virtue beyond all earthly qualities
the purity and honesty which she believed
Athene to possess. The royal work was carried
into execution in one day. The wheel of
fortune was completely turned. The morning
had found Athene in a crowded hotel, rising
from a very bad, in the expectation of losing
her life; but the evening he was the lord of
real palace, and married to the only daughter

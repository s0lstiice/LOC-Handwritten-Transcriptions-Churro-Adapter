For a closer walk with God,
Reason and heavenly science
Is left to those upon the road
That leads me to the Lamb!
Where is the philosopher's Stone
When first I knew the Lord?
Where is the refreshing stream
Of Jesus and his Word?
What peaceful home! then enjoy'd!
How sweet their union my soul!
But they have left me behind me,
The world I can never tell.
Return, O Christ, give I return,
Sweet union, peace, joy, and rest!
I hope the road that made the mountain,
And drove thee from my heart.
The dearest idol I have known,
That I have ever known.
What'er that idol be,
Help me to bear it from day to time,
And worship only thee!
So still may I walk the close with God,
Reason and science my guide,
So pure light shall meet the road
That leads me to the Lamb.
(Baptist)
Washington March 1892.
Light of heaven above burning
Guides on the shades of earth,
Born and by thy love created,
Defy all the clouds that hide
The new heaven and earth beneath,
In one deeped darkness green,
Battling all the night of nature,
Our eyes light on thee up.
While we wait for thine appearing,
Life and joy thy beams impart,

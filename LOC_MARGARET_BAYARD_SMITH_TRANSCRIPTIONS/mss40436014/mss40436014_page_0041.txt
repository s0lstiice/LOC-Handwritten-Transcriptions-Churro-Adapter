The happy inhabitants of the province of
Schleman in Prussia had enjoyed for many years
uninterrupted peace under a wise and benevolent
Prince Therain, always anxious for the felicity
of his people, limited his own power by wise
laws, and took to heart an example of justice
should be executed by his Ministers, the commerce
and industry of agricultures and the arts, which he
advanced with liberality and punishments with
punishment and by these means has established
security, tranquility and happiness on a firm
foundations. While in the thoughts of their felicity, prosperity,
with tender gratitude they blessed their God,
and they were so proud among them by the arrival
of the peaceful intelligence that Sarmatia the
Prince of Bohemia, even appears to extend his empire,
had already advanced with a numerous army to the
province of Schleman, intending to subjugate and
annex it to his conquests.

for his people, thus threatened with the extirpation

Where places where they dwelling sit,
Are left unto us desolate.
We miss them when the board is spread,
We miss them when the prayer is said;
Before our dreams their eyes appear
In still and momental silence.
But they are where their Empress reigns
Trouble no more the heart and brain;
The sadness of their asking love
Does not our Father's house alone.
I am at rest, and I am free,
He dwells in immortal spheres!
Under the poplars I stand,
And among the broken houses I stand.
But, by your life of holy faith,
And by your joyful hope in death,
Guide me! Will our souls separate?
The soul's wealth is found once more!
It is not the first time I have written to you
That I am in a state of great anxiety
As to the future of the students in the
Boarding School. I have been
much distressed to see how little
progress has been made in the
Instruction of the students.
It is not that I do not believe
that the students will be
successful in their efforts to
obtain a position in the
Government, but I am
very anxious that they should
not be disappointed in their
expectations.

To the Women!
From every thousands of the Sky!
Marvel not, or honor yourself
That you were deafened:
For, even in this deluder,
To me oft has your power, or place,
Unceasingly throned and supported.
Perfect gone power! when by your might
You leave the mild waves, veered and white,
Like movements in your weather,
Longing between them, and thus deep,
Which lies the seaman and his crew's sleep,
Save me from Earth's opening path!
Graceful your play! when round the Source
Where Beauty's all-spring, and whitest flower,
Is wreath'd the sea dark rocks stand,
And gentlest whispers lightly break the
Heavens between, I lift, amid that music,
And stir her within them.
But, thoughts like these are futile with
And you can give for Justice Justice
The power we have not, whence!
Go, go! cannot all these your flights?
All insuperable to the light of sight;
Though audible to the mind.
The sun, -- thus one, and where it goes;
The sea, -- we mark its self, and then
The moon, -- here wax and wane;
The stars, -- more distant than moon well,
The heavens' constant paths are dull, --
But you this sacred science.
Grateful, honest'st, thoughtful things!
Who mock all our comingsings,
Like spirits in a dream;
What restless, vain sounds, out of the
Heaven's heart, who takes such high
Unmanageable theme?

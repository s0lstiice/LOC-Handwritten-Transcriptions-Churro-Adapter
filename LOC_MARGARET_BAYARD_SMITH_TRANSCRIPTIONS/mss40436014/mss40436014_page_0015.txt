Madam
Most worthy of estimation, after long consider-
ation and much meditated reflections, of the great
reputation you possess in the nation, I have a strong
inclination to become your relations. On your appro-
bation of this declaration, I shall make a preparation
to remove my situation to a more convenient place
to justify any accusation, and if such situation is worthy
of preservation, and can claim commendation, it will
be an augmentation beyond all calculation of the
joy and exultation of your
Dear Reformers
Samuel Coleridge
The Answer.
Sir,
I praised your orations, with much deliberation,
and a little consideration, at the great institution,
if you make inquiries, to show such consideration,
or is slight a foundation. But after examination,
and serious contemplation, I suppose your
anniversary was the fruit of meditation, or had sprung
from oration, to display your education, by endless
enumeration, or rather multiplication of words,
of the same termination, though of great variety,
in each respective signification.
Now without disputesions, your laborious application,
to seditions on occupation, deserves commendation,
and thinking emulations, sufficient gratification,
I am without hesitation, yours
Many affectionation
Samuel Coleridge

From the throne of my soul,
Let me be thy throne fly,
While the Whirlwind roar me off
While the tempest still is high.
Take me, my Saviour! hide,
Tell the death of life is past,
Safe into the haven guide,
I receive my soul at last!
Other refuge have I none
Stronger my helpless soul can trust
Save, O Jesus! here am I alone!
Still support and comfort me!
All my trust on thee is stayed,
All my help from thee I bring
Beyond my defenses lies heed
With the shadow of thy wing.
Then, O Christ! let all I want,
More than all in thee I find.
Have the fallen, cheer the spent,
Heal the sick, and bid the dying
Just and holy is thy name,
I am all denigration!
Bile and guilt of sin I am
Then art full of truth and grace.
Charitable peace with thee is found
Grace to pardon all my sin;
Let the healing streams abound;
Let me feel them flow within.
Then I hope the fountain art,
Truly let me take of thee!
Guiding them up within my heart
Give to all eternity!
Washington March 12: 1792.

Our friend was thunderstruck. He stood thus
without being able to move or speak, reflecting
on his misfortunes, and grieving above all,
that his wife, whom he so loved, had by her
wisdom and self-interest, brought him to such a
foolish determination. Full of these sad thoughts,
he exclaimed aloud, Oh woman! woman! -
that is more beneficial to the happiness of
man than the poisonous dragon of the desert!
The last only had been needed by the Jewett's
wife, who, disgraced by those clowns, which

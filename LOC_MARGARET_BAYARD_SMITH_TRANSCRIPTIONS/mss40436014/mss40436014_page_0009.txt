The setting sun
They setting sun that setting sun!
That sunset, since first the race began,
Of years has its golden beam,
Which are, as they have never been.
That setting sun! full many a page
With Dwight upon its golden way,
With sweet, according thought at times,
In every age, and every time!
Yet sweet it marks thee, sinking slow
The ocean's fables covers beneath,
And when the horizon might be done,
To see thee rise, sweet setting sun!
When my father's feet to play,
And I to his, I am evening gay!
I understand, though I am now,
Shadows like thee, sweet setting sun!
August 1899.

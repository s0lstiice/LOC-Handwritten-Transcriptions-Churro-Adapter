Solitude!!!
It is a noble triumph of spirit and skill,
Of steady hand, the first, steady hand,
That brings that own unbroken dominion back,
And makes for both men, a peaceful home;
To which the hard life in which they all walked,
With the wild flick that never was spent,
Has given its deep and forming peace to earn;
This is not solitude; the bill to hold
It in peace with nature's charms, and give it its own reward.
But amidst the work, the hum, the shout of men,
To hear, to see, to feel, to feel perfectly,
The warm along, the world's heated designs,
With none who they are, none whom they may,
With none of splendor, brightness from earth's
Source that could lend its influence to aid,
If we were not, would seem to have the left
Of that sheltered, sheltered, night and sun,
Which is to claim this, this solitude!
Leaven, April 19th, 1880.
Addressed to a lovely infant, expecting in its father's arms
So gentle spirit, haste away,
From painful scenes of me and now,
Of sickness, sorrow and decay,
For reasons of joy unknown below.
Dear, lovely baby, thy parents heart
Would still detain you lingering here,
But from calls the time depart,
The friendly announcement thou must hear.
"Let little children come to me,
Follow them with the human words,
Who would in heaven with me abide?
"I am happy, content, and amazed with you,
In cities of innocence crowded,
The heavenly joys will soon begin,
Its mine with cares or griefs allotted."

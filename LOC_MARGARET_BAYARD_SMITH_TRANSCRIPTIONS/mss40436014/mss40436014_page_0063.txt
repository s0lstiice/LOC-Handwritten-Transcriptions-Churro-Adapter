His calm luminous eye;
Some ancient prophet, whispering yet
Of terrible destiny;
Some heart's deep language, where the fire
Of quenchless faith smolders;
For ever greater and I know
That one flaming hour.
And silent stood his children by,
Watching their own breath
Before the solemn vaults,
Of thought to enveloping death:
Silent yet did not each young heart
With love and reverence melt?
Oh! that he thus fair girls and fled
That home where he rests!
Washington January, 1832.
Behold a meeting scene
If only here, and thence offer its worth.
It was an eve of Hellenus' shadow mood.
The sun's fields bathed in Lybresh's silver light,
Rest ready for the royal gathering bands;
And all the land slept soundly. Betime seemed
In silent contemplation for adieu
Its Maker. him and them, the aged leaf
Fell from its followers, nothing in the grove;
But, as if fate's rude hand throbbed on his end,
On vale and lake, on wood and mountain height,
With present wing outspread, sat Hellenus' thought,
Conversing with himself. Steeper looked forth,
From out her wooden hermitage, and smiled;
And up the east, unclouded, rose the moon
With all her stars, gazing on earth extended,
Of the sea from under watching there.

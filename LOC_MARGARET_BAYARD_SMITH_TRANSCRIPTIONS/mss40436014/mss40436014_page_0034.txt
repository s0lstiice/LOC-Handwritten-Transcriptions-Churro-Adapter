My hommes courant la carrière
Des gardes et des miliciens
Il n'est mis en vue baron
A leur plus réclames clairs.
De mon corps petits et mobile,
Il me semble que des vents
Chassent pour moi mes lettres,
Mais dans un soulier mieux peint.
Je serais d'elle admis à toutes ces salles de glaces,
Vous vous le souvenez de la promenade
Est dans les coins de nos amis.
To man's suffering, say the grim
He stands the highest hill of fame,
He dies the death and he has
To gain, by death, a deathless name.
Of most fought fields, and heroes won,
The memory lives while they pass,
Plant in our bosoms love,
Be written on detective dogs.
But so poor little woman kind
The need of glory is denied:
With an earnest of her confidence,
The lovely virtues are their pride:
Yet not words can utter fame,
Lending where rail existence ends:
And great temples hold their name
The lessons of suffering friends.
January August 1829

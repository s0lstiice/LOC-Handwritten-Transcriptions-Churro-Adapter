For a young ladies Album
Go forth - then little book!
Go forth - like the honey bee -
And gather they doce from the fether flowers
May enrich their hearts and enrich their powers
Give some of their sweets to thee!
Go forth - then little book!
Go forth - like the provident ant
And gather they stores in the summer days,
When fruits are ripening in sunny rays
Then may come a winter of woe!
Go forth - then little book!
Go forth - and gather they store -
Of many a token which friendship leaves here
May hang to the heart if they were be dear,
When the friendship of friend is no name.
Seating - May - 1828.
That spark of heavenly flame!
Will, I quit this printed page;
Trembling, despising, struggling,
Of the grain, the bright being;
Beast, from matter, create thy stride,
And let one languish into life.
Think! they whisper, - angels say
Jesus Spirit, come and go!
What is this absent soul within?
Stands my senses, stands my sight,
Breasts my spirit, breathing breath,
Tell me, they say, can this be death?
The world recedes, it dissolves!
That's a scene on my eyes, my ears
With sounds sounds sounds!
Send, send your wings! I implore, I pray!
Opportune! where is thy victory?
O death! where is thy thing?
Washington - March 18th - 1832.

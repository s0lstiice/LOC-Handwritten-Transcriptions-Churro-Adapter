Lines, by a Cashmerian Jordanian.
When shall we there meet again?
When shall we there meet again?
Or shall gloomy hope expire,
Or shall unwept love retire,
Or shall death and sorrow reign,
Or shall we there meet again?
The in distant lands we left,
Buried beneath a hostile sky,
The weep deep between us is,
Friendship shall under our souls,
Still in fancy's rich domain,
Or shall we there meet again?
When the dreams of life are fled,
When its wasted lamps are dead,
When in cold oblivion's shade,
Beauty's power and form are laid,
Where immortal spirits reign
There shall we there meet again.
-1827-
Good memory's flower of prime age,
Remits thy hard one hour to come;
When in death's narrow bed I lie,
The bloom around my heart be gone.
And if some tender faithful friend
Should bid by love approach the spot,
And on thy flowers admiring bend,
Thine cry for me, forget me not!

Inexultation to Rain, from the German
Decent, decret, shower!
They liquid treasures o'er the meadows pour,
And raise such flowering flowers,
For here that late, in blooming pride,
Adorned the lovely valley's side,
By moistening air implores.
For below, round which the woodline gag
Its foliage turns in graceful play,
Deflected droops, and seems to say,
Decent, decret, shower!
Oh, let not then the woodybine fade,
But quickly grant thy fostering aid,
And thy returning power,
To every plant thy care extends,
And bath in pleasure -- as decret,
Oh, bolting showers!
For herds that sick the mountain's brow,
And those that range the plain beloveth
With thy aid implores:
Enfected by the noontide rays
Or hill and dell thy dropsing stray,
And heavenward burn the languid eyes
That asks for thee, shower!
Oh, let not nature plead in vain
Let not the flower that decks the plain,
For vain thy art implores;
But haste thy favor by becoming time,
Recover the hide, restore the spring,
On earth her emerald mantle flings,
And soft decret, shower!
While Heathen, by smirking Plenty crowned,
Shell scatter all her stores around,
And hail thy general power.

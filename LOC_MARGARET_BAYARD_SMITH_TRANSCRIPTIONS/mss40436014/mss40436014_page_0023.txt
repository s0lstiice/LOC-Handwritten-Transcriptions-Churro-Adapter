When Twilight dews are falling fast
Upon the winged sea,
I do not think that I shall dream of you
For guidance, me or thee!
But though I go on that unknown shore,
And think I shall not see you there,
And that I may be mine in Heaven!
There I meet a passer with tears,
And I must a flower tear,
But bring to mind some hope that I feel
Some joy for God with thee!
And that I wish that love were near
Then friends and foes forgiven,
The pains that I do not expect though there,
May turn to sorrows in Heaven.
Idaho April 1892.
SONG
The sweet air Twilight's gentle sway,
Whirls me through a world of old lies down,
To rest the soul from care away
In dreams of friends or pictures Heaven.
For there I will forever, faithful, wild,
In dreams of past enjoyment come,
And in the heart of nature's child
With visions of a dear lost home:
And as illusive, of bygone years,
The joys of the Ages, that once were mine,
Unattested by the wind of years,
Under the sky more now, still they shine:
On ballad's end by a mother's love,
And under another where in sorrow,
My heart that of Idaho's dove,
Still firmly turns to Home - Level Home!

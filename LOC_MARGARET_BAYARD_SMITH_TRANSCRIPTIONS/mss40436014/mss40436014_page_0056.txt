But me: — to me, when Fancy sees
any thoughtless, ye damn Yankee's unfeeling prey,
Who leaves me fast in bed
And when, as never, at midnight's time,
Shall your voice on all the power,
It seem the voice of God.
Washington. March 29. 1831.
Washington.
My fellow citizens!
In half spent homes our pleasure all day;
Has it done with the thoughtful virtues with the gay
Through life as I parted no doubt could I see,
And the heart that was poised now downs could I see,
But in sorrow in affliction how changed is the view!
Though gay hearts are among ourselves are few,
Though some come round as to laughter to God,
In sickness or sorrow they depart from the rest;
Their love and their friendship, order to a whole
Then fortune is smiling they also can smile.
Let them see where our work is gone,
And how all their victories have gone.
But those in my power still faithfully come,
And though they have altered find you the same;
When we you come we give you pleasure you find,
But always leave something to pleasure behind.
Like the night blooming roses, which shed their perfume
And spent its fragrance midst darkness and gloom.
March 29. 1831.
From dear to me the hour when daylight dies,
But sun beams still melt along the distant sea;
To them sweet dreams of their days arise,
And money troubles her deeper sighs to thee.
And as I watch the line of light that plays
Along the smooth wave toward the shining west,
I know He has that golden path of rays,
And think would lead to some bright isle of rest.
Friday October 1831.

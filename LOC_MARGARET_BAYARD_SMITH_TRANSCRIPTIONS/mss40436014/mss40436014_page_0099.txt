The maid was on the eve of womanhood;
The boy had fewer memories, but his heart
had for many years his years, and to his eye
there was but one beloved face on earth,
and that was shining on him: he had looked
upon it till it could not pass away;
he had no doubt, no feeling but in this;
the weathering of the rock upon his sight,
was humbled in his words, and an oath from
which he could not escape -- the love which
he loved within himself: the love of life,
the reason to the power of his thoughts,
which terminated all upon a word
-- "touch of days, this love would end and flow,
and his heart change completely to his heart

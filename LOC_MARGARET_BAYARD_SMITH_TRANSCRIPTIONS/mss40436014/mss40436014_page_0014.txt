Why mother, why have I been so rude,
Why have I been so rude to you?
My heart is broken the babe in her quiet beauty
That would meet me for safety, my toy no more,
Is here lying at the cabinet door.
— Come then with me to the window's edge
And we'll think the graves of the earliest age.
— I pray mother give from her home angel
That I know that my brothers are there at play!
— Know they are gathering the poppy's pills,
And the long fawn hides by the sparkling well

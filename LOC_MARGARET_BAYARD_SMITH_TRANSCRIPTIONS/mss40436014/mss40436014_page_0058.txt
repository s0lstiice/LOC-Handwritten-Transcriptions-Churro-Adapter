January 26 1831.
The Spirit of the Spring.
Spirit of the soul's birth!
They gaze from unseen fields over air and earth,
While budding words pour forth their youth
In aspiration breathing modes:
I see upon the flood clouds
The spectacles of the universe;
The hills and hills of hills alone;
And Nature starting from her throne,
To meet her bridgeborn springs!
Washington January 1832.

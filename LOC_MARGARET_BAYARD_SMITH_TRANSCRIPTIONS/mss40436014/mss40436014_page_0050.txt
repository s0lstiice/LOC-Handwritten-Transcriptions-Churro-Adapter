Bright shines the moon on the mountain's brow,
Bright on the waves on the stream;
Bright shines the sun on the temple's stone!
Who could it the mirror with all her beams
Or shine on me, then holy sun,
With blessings on thy wings, once!
And bid the night of grief begin,
And dry the tear-dust from mine eyes.
I cannot, will not comfort you,
When I am not here to fill the cup;
Some sorrow, come, the past arrest,
Some day, spread its net, my memory dies,
Rise on the cheerful heart of mine,
And let me now thy glory die!
But bid me use, and bid me shine,
The child of happiness and thee.
Sidney September 20. 1831.

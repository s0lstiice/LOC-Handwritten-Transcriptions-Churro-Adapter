Queen—for the Court's own's gone,
With flowers we deck'd her knee;
We tended her courts this day of pain,
In her grave we have deck'd a tomb.
Remember her presence delights our hearts
In friendship's flower breath chain
A link is broken and is fast
That can never be felted again;
Then mourn and weep, for the tear full fast
Her beauty now with could save
The loveliest dreamer, the founder and best
From the cold and envious grave.
Regret, regret! she dwells both on high
From celestial realms above
She is looking on those who still are dear
On the home of her only love;
The spirit is turning round our path
Regard us from every ill
And this union by our mutual eyes
She is with us and more as still.
Then expire, rejoice! she dwells both on high
Let hope every heart
From innumerable griefs we shall meet again
And never again shall part.
—Burnett.—
On the decline of imagination as life advances.—
"Why have ye linger'd on your way so long,
Bright visions, who were wont to hear my calls,
And with the harmony of dance and song
Rest round my dreaming couch a federal
When are ye gone, with all your eyes of light,
And where the flowering voice tends to hear,
Then, though in the silent watches of the night,
Or disguised like an angel in disguise
Is fly not with the rapid wing of time,
But with your ancient resting finally stay,
And while the latter dreams, that one in silence
In years of higher hope, have flown away
With the odours of after dreams,
Give your last touches to the dying day."

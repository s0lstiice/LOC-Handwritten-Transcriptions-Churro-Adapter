Leigh Romney, Dean of Canterbury
On the evening of Thursday evening
the author is returning to the Cathedral
and will be seated on the north transept, to which he
has at once to go, and will almost certainly be the
last. The effect is such as to create the author
deep emotion. From polite and old school
and with the sound of years he cannot respect it,
and perhaps the sound of the Apache horn,
which is heard at a great distance. At the
beginning of the sun set the mountains of the
Asp, the sound dies away, leaving them
thundered in darkness and silence.

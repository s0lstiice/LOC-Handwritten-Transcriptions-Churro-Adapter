Or Reason's feeble ray
In ever blooming prospects rise
Unconscious of decay.
Lord, send a beam of light divine
To guide our upward way aim
With one revolving touch of thine,
Our languid hearts inflame.
Then, shall on faith's sublimest wing
Our ardent wishes rise
To those bright scenes, where Pleasure's flame
Immortal in the skies.—
Anake my soul I with the sun
In daily course of duty run
Shake off dull clothe, & early rise
To join thy morning sacrifice.
The deem thy moment time, that past
Live this day, as if it were thy lot
To improve thy talents, in thy care
And for the last great day prepare.
Let all thy converse be sincere
Thy conscience on the noon day clear
Wake, & lift up thyself my heart
And with the angels bear a part,
Who at night lay, unmeasured sing
Glory to thee, eternal King.
I awake, I awake ye heavenly choir
May your devotion, mine, inspire
That I like you my eye may find
Like you may on my God attend
Direct, central, suggest this day
All, I design, or do— or say
That all my powers, with all their might
In thy sole glory may unite.

Few watch the fading streaks of light
But move on hopes as quickly flown.
Till after Cent they fade away
Till all at last they are gone.
This is the hour when fancy weathes
Visions of joy that could not last.
This is the hour when Memory breathes
A sigh to pleasure past.
J.B.R.
By day, June 18th 1825.
On finding a bunch of Flowers in my apartments.
Sometime spring Day, with laughing sun,
Twist April showers this morning
Cam= trapping in, with boughs of green
My mantle shelf adorning.
The little Viz with spring of green,
Spring Flowers had sweetly blended
The modest weathings we are fond of
Which flora now interwined.
But thanks to Elfin, nymph or Swan
I love the modest graces
Reflected from these simple Flowers
Within my Crystal Vase.
S.B.S.

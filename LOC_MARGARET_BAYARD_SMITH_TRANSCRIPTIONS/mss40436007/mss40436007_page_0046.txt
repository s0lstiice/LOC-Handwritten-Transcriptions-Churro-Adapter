Brunswick October 18th 1829.
The strong hold of Friendship is lasting,
And firm as a rock in the Heart;
But Love is delusive swaying,
And owns all its beauty to art.
Tho' it beams like a brilliant Lightning,
It shines over its own distant tomb,
Like the flash of the Heaven's redlightning,
Encircling the darkness and gloom.
Let me claim then a spot in your bosom
To Friendship and Memory dear;
Where the buds of remembrance may blossom,
Their fragrance embalmed with a tear.
The pleasures of life are but fleeting,
They pass like the rays of Spring,
And the heart that with anguish is beating
May soon throb with agony's string.
Let me hail then the joys which have broken
The dark recollections of sorrow,
But this record, alas! is the token
Of long desperation tomorrow.

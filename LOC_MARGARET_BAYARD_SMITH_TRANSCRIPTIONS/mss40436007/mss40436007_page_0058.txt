On the sudden death of an infant.
from Mrs. F.S.R. as to her fine Mr B.
Since it is necessary to touch on this
subject let me open my heart to
you, Time has brought no balm
to my wounds. On that awful
night it seemed to me that the
Deity resumed his gift without
the aid of secondary causes and
I felt as if I were in the immedi-
ate presence of God - I dared not
resist - I dared not hesitate to yield
my babe to his Almighty Father
The walled sentiments of my hus-
band with the aid of his example
supported these feelings - but as
the awful event was removed from
me by the lapse of time, I looked

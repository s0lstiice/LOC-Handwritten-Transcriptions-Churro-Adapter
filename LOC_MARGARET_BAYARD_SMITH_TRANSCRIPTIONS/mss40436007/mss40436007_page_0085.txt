June 1840
Belief does not depend on conviction -- else would I believe in
their doctrines which are continually preached, as essential to Salvation --
for if they can do me no good, they can do me no harm & I know it is safest to
believe -- but I cannot. -- It is not so much my reason, that rejects them, as
a certain internal power or principle which not only persuades but convinces
me, in opposition to the force of education -- in opposition to that self-intuited
self-love which leads one to seek self-instruction & to part with
faith, knowledge or hope for gold, given in opposition to these powerful
motives. -- This internal principle convinces me it cannot be true -- that the
happiness of the immortal soul depends on belief or Truth. -- This cannot be --
Can this internal monitor deceive me -- is it not the voice of God in
my soul. Calvinist is it not that Natural Law, of which St Paul
declares, "The law written in their hearts, Their conscience also being within them, They
knows the measureable accuracy of their actions." And is not this law written in the
hearts, an obligatory, or more obligatory than any other law? For by whom is it
written -- by divine who made the heart -- the Creator -- of them I deny this
primary law -- expressed by God himself -- can I disbelieve him by so doing? If the origin of this law I
can have no doubt -- human opinion has no hand in it -- it is not written, on the
gospels alone, in a language unknown to but a few individuals translated by men in to different
languages -- therefore subject to minute interpretation -- and on many important
points, are now assumed by the most eminent and enlightened Christian teachers that these
have been gross misinterpretations of his writings -- and his writings have been
subjected to the interference of human ignorance & prejudice in proportion -- Is it not safer

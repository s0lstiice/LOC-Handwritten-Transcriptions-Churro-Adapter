I cannot describe to you the agonized
feelings with which I saw the little
circumstances connected with the
existence of my child daily vanishing
from my eyes. This they are
all gone and the recollection of his
loveliness only remains in the memory
of his parents. But here is our
consolation - "His soul pleased the Lord
therefore was he taken away lest wickedness
should alter his understanding, or
deceit corrupt his soul."
It is my constant desire that my
friends should not participate in
my grief. pardon this trespass -
I cannot touch lightly on this subject
but I can, and must avoid it
J. S.
Happiness found in Religion, & in
the felt presence of God
There be many who say, who will shew us any
good? Lord left those at the light of Thy con-
tinence upon us
I then can't put gladness in my heart, more than
in the time that their come & their wise in-
creased.
I wish both lay me down in peace & sleep,
for thou Lord, only makest me dwell in
safety.
Oh if I trust thy sovereign skill
and bow submissive to thy will
Sickness & death shall with speed
go bring me Lord, at last to thee.
How long shall earth allowing toys
Detain my heart & eyes.
The garden of immortal joys
And strangers to the since
of these transient scenes, will soon decay
They fade upon the sight
And quickly with their brightest day
Be lost in endless night
Their brightest day! - alas how vain!
with conscious sighs we own,
while clouds of sorrow, came & pour
O'er shade the smiling moon
Oh could our thoughts & wishes fly
above these gloomy shades
to those bright worlds beyond the sky
Which sorrow never invaded.
There, joys unseen by mortal eyes

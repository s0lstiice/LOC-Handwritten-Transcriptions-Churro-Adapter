March
1831
faith says St Paul in his epistle
to the Hebrews, "is in the confident expectation
of things hoped for, 2 the conviction of
things not seen" - Boden's translation -
It includes a belief of the existence
of God 2 the new world of the life to come,
for every true son of Abraham; He that cometh
to God, must believe that he is, 2 that
he is in the new world of them that diligently
seek him".
Abraham's faith was trust in God
an undoubting confidence in his promises,
a confidence as perfect as strong, that he would
not grieve with his only son, in fault apparent
that God's promises respecting his posterity
would be fulfilled. The accordance to the
burthens of reason or law of nature. That
fulfillment confirmed impossible in case of
Jesus death - yet his trust in God, remained
the obstacle human reason to nature's law
refused - Oh for such a faith in
God as one of Christian faith - we, was
it the faith - without which we are
now told, we cannot be saved -
I have a confident expectation of Divine
belief in the immensity of the world -
I believe in God - that he is the creator,
the governor - The governor - The Judge -
The heavenly father of all his creatures -
I trust in God - I feel a pure and entire
reliance on his wisdom, his power 2 his
mercy. My trust is strong enough to
enable me to cry with all my heart -
"My will be done" - yes, whatever that
will may be - poverty, sickness, sorrow, death
whatever it is, they will, 2 not mine
on my God be done - I would not
have it otherwise - I require that
I am not at my own disposal -
but that in all things, under a shield
in leading always, I am guided 2 governed
not by my own will, but by thine
My Father, who art in heaven -
But after all - have I saving faith?
Christ says, that not your heart be troubled
2 why? ye believe in God - And
If ye believe in him, believe in me, believe
that I go to prepare a place - believe
that he sent me - this I do believe

Believe what? - Let me search the
holy scriptures 2 do them the holy spirit
of God, so enlighten my mind that I
may discern the truth 2 not mistake
the interpretations of man, for
the word of God. - I desire 2 most
patiently implore that I may believe
that which is necessary for the salvation
of my soul 2 that in my search for
truth I may not be misled by the
judgment of men, or by any previously
conceived opinion - or by the opinions
of any sect of Christians - or any
man, however wise 2 learned - but
realizing it to be a matter on
which the salvation of my soul depends,
I may be swayed by no motive or prejudice
whatever - It is a concern between
my soul 2 my creator - with which my
fellows and others have nothing to do. -
Therefore if I am right, in the sight of
God - it is of no consequence, as far
as relates to myself, whether I am right or
wrong in the opinion of men - Let me not
then be influenced by what men think - but
look only to my God - !

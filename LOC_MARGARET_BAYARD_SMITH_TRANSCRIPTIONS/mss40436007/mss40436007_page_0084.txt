from pride of reason — free
me from the influences of
human affections — even from
these which are most pure
& legitimate — let me love
no Thing — & no being better
than I love the truth.
I do confess that Jesus is the
son of God — & that God so
loved the world, that he gave
his son to be the propitiation
for our sins —
There is no fear in love — Oh
my God I love & reverence
thee, & I have no fear that
thou wilt punish me for
unintentional error — I desire
truth — but if I find it not,
pardon thy wandering servant
thou have pity on thy wanderings
& lead him into the right way —
& when in the right
He impart thy strength, "still in
the night to stay" — undetermined
by fear of human opinion —
unsuaded by human hopes
or desires —

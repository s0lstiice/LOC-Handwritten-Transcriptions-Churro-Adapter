But sometimes to our surprise
A flower unnoticed would arise
The one we thought would soonest blow
Had still to bear the frost and snow.
Oh with what fond and eager haste
The first flower on thy breast was placed,
That breast where all the virtues meet
And blest religion finds her seat.
But she who loved the opening spring
And loved to see its flowers to bring
She whom we loved is now no more
Our earthly hopes for her are over.
Oh! can we ever cease to sigh
For those sweet hours, alas, gone by.
Those days of pleasure and of joy
And happiness without alloy
Oh no! in memory they dwell
To be "remember'd oft and well"
Time can her image nier efface
But death for her remembrance trace.
Let the sweet humble widow's fate
Be emblematic of her state.
Perhaps you did not think that she,
The youngest of our little band,
That she the first would gather, be
By your heavenly Father's hand.
Yes she has first been called away
To the bright realms of endless day
Like the fair flower, this left below
The stormy wind and winter's snow,
Removed to that celestial home
Where earthly blisses can never come
She has exchanged a world of light
For one of glory and of light
And on her Heavenly Father's breast,
Her soul enjoys eternal rest.
The sentiment of filial and sisterly affection
Which animates these simple lines, I know will
be sufficient to recommend them to my sweet home,

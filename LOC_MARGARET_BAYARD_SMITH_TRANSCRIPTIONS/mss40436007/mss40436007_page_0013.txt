Man on the waters! and, purple and bright,
Bursts on the billows the flashing of light;
On the glad waves, like a shield of the Sun,
Or the talliped goes, gallantly on;
Tall is the breeze she unbores her sail,
And her cannon thunders onward, like hope, in the sail;
The winds come around her, in murmur and song,
And the waves rejoice, as they bear her along;
See she looks up to the golden edged clouds,
And the sailer rings gaily aloft in the clouds?
Onward she glides, amid ripple and spray,
Oye, the waters, away, and away!
Bright as the visions of youth, as they part,
Passing away, like a dream of the heart!
Who as the beautiful pageant sweeps by
Music around her, and shines on high-
Causes to think, amid glitter and glow,
Oh! their be hearts that are breaking below!
Right on the waves! and the moon is on high,

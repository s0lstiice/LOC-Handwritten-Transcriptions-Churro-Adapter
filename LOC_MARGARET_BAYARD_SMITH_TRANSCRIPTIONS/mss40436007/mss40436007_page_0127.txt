With my only sympathizer, in
a heart sublime to the well-
willers is ready to obey the call.
Lord! I am thine. Dispose of me
according to thy good pleasure. I pray
not for life -- or even thy answer
to life but most directly, earnestly
humble would I pray, for thy sympathy
and strengthening influences to enable
me to help thy commandments.
These two great commandments
which the blessed Jesus gave me -- to love God with all our heart
and mind and strength, to love our neighbor as ourselves -- have
been my guiding light in the heat, and
evil -- even hostile passion which
has subdued me and should give
me liberty to use my creator for
good in time.
But that my heart was filled, was
inflamed with thy divine love -- it
would earthy selfish affection melt in
its fervent heat, like gold in
the furnace to be poured from its
founder -- But my heart is cold -- cold
for some, or human feeling, the
wandering, in information of
thy children -- Claims then my
heart from every secret sin -- none
can be hid from thee -- Oh my Lord! I
am thy creature -- wilt thou not
have mercy on the creature of thy will
perpetuated by thy power -- governs
thou hast in myself what have I?
Nothing -- my mind cannot change
nothing -- my will cannot change
nothing -- my heart cannot change
nothing -- my mind cannot change
nothing -- my will cannot change
nothing -- my heart cannot change --
the events of a world, how
ignorant, how helpless as an infant,
my little one, he observes as an
infant -- Lord I am thine -- thy
child -- Lord I am thine -- my joy --
my hope, my love -- my joy --
Dear refuge of my soul -- my soul
on thee, when sorrow may
on thee when waves of trouble roll
on thy family like the waves
of the sea -- I tell each my grief
for thee alone cannot heal
thy love alone can give relief
for every pain I feel --
But do not let thy loving hands
seal thy name to me --
The springs of comfort seem to fail
and all my hopes die --
Yet gracious God where art thou? flee!
Draw out thy only trust

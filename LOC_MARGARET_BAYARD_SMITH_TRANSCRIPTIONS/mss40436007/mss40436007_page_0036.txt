That on its youthful, venial hour,
Sportive Fancy strews her flowers;
That young affections of the heart,
Their genial power to mind impart?
Then, the thoughts as freely rise,
To warling birds in summer skies,
When this inspiring warmth has fled,
Our thoughts are chilled, our fancy dead.
Then vain dear girl what you require,
Fancy denies her kindling fire.
I feel the approach of Autumn roll,
Its chilling influence on my soul.
Well be it so — I'll not repine,
While Autumn richer store is sowed;
The richest store kind heaven sends,
Domestic love, and faithful friends.
— Louisa, when your spring is flown,
Your summer days and joys are gone,
When cold autumn days shall come,
Be yours as dear and calm a home,
A husband suited to your mind
Is true, so tender, and so kind
That when your youth has passed away,
And your maturing decay,
This love like some bright evergreen,
Shall screen, and cheer life's winter scene.
Louisa, round your cheerful hearth
May you enjoy as harmless youth
With objects to your heart as dear
As those you've often met with, here.
And when from us you're far away
Sometimes recall the cheerful day
When gathered round the quilling flame
Our hopes, our fears, our tasks, the same.
The years may pass, and sorrows come,
Do not forget the evening hour
When Sidney's neighbours used to meet,
And pass their time in converse sweet.
No yet forget, how oft you've stayed
On sunny hills, — through forest shade

dear angel sister Agnes to you?
It was written during her sickness

Mr Tailey will convey it to her if
you will give it to him. He
is staying with dear Father and
playing the home game to him
with his quiet & gentle attention
and plain dispositions. My dear
Father seemed to look with abso-

Ayer's friends. He said it would
bring home the solemn truth
of this bereavement with so
much more force & conviction to
his mind. Have you seen him
yet? I think you had better
wire to him than go to see
him just now.

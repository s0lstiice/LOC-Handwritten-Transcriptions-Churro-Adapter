The last winter has been a
weary, a very weary a sleepless
one to me. — But the winter
is gone & Spring & summer
have returned & restored to the
earth all its bloom & beauty —
once more in the recreation
of my own chamber, free from
all interruption, I can yield my
mind to the contemplation
of nature & communion with
the God of nature — Yes, great
Creator, thou dost manifest
thyself in thy works, &
while in this tabernacle
of clay, I cannot see thee face
to face, I can see thy power,
& thy love & thy wisdom in
these thy glorious works.— When
no sound is heard but the sound
of words & waters — no lights
seen but the waters of thine
almighty power, I feel indeed
in thine holy presence!
The walls of churches & the
voice of preachers, do not thus
sensibly make me feel thy
presence; nay, they seem to confine
& often debase my soul, filling
it with doubts & difficulties.—
Nature is thine own
majestic Temple, in which my
soul can worship & adore Thee
free from all doubt— all difficulty
— By man may minister
to all the language of men — but
the divine language of thy creation
cannot & never be mistaken.

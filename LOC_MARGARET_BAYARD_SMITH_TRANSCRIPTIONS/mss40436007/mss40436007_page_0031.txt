O say not the Rose is a summer flower.
Which blooms in the sunshine alone:
Or it can boast a magical power.
When the buds of the Summer are gone.
On the brown slender stem, when it gracefully grew
When May with her blushing was here,
It still finds a place, with Autumn's cold day
When the Storms of the Winter are near.

The last day of the year.
Taste thee Well: Then art gone, the last evening sun,
Has set on the grave of the year;
And the showers which fall, are Nature's Farewell,
And the tribute we give - is a tear.
Thou art gone and art numbered with days that are past
And with joys that will never return;
The friends we still grieve for, lie buried with thee
Time spares us - but leaves us to mourn.
Yet the new-year may peace and serenity bring,
The past has been cloaked with care,
For a germ is imixed in each sorrowing breath,
Deep rooted - will ever be there.
Is a Blossom which fell from the garden of love,
When it was watered with Heaven's first dew -
Faith scattered each bud which she gathered from thence
Hopes sprang from the ground when they grew.

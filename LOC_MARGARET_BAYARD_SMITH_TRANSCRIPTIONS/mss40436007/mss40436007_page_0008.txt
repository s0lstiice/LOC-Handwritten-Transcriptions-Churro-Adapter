Thus is hardly a principle of the
human mind more extensive in its
influence; & none more necessary or
useful than gratitude. Accustomed
as we are to appropriate the word to signify, almost exclusively, a disposition to
reciprocate favours between individuals,
we will hear with surprise -- that it
is the only true foundation of our duty
towards our Creator & towards our fellow-men -- that it is essential to the existence of
our Society -- & is the source of patriotism
of filial & paternal affection, & friendship.

by the auxiliary & kindred passions of love,
pity & interest. Although the idea of

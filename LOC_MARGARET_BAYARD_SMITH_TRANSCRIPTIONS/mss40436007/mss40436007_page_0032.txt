The Evening Hour.
This is the hour when memory wakes
Visions of joy that could not last
This is the hour when Memory takes
A survey of the past.
She brings before the passing mind
The ballad scenes of earlier years
And friends that long have been consigned
To silence and to tears.
The few are liked, the few are loved
A sacred band come stealing on;
And many a form for hence remanded
And many a pleasant one.
Friendships that now in death are broken
And young affections broken chain
And hopes that fate too quickly crushed
In memory boom again.

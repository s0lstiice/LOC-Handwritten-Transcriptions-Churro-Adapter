By faith, Isaac & Jacob & Joseph
2 Moses were acceptable &
justified.
By faith the walls of Jericho
fell down.
By faith the Harlot Rahab
forswore not, & David &
Samuel & a host
of others, who are given as
examples of the saving efficacy
of Faith.
But the question remains what
was this faith?
The answer to which
by these examples, is, faith - is a
belief & trust in God.
More is required of me -
we are ordered to look to Jesus
as the author & finisher of
our faith.
"By him, therefore let us offer
the sacrifice of praise to God continue
- ally" Hebrews. 13th v. 15th -
"By him" - says he sanctifies - not, to him
"I believe in God" - says Jesus - "believe
likewise in me"
Believe that I am sent by the father
that I am going to the father
"and at that day, (after he is gone to
the father) ye shall ask in my name
& I say unto you, I will pray the
father for you."
"For the father himself loveth you
because I have loosed you
"I came forth from the father & am
come unto the world, & again I leave
the world & go to the father."
His disciples said unto him, "ow, new
ideas hast thou plainly - now are we
sure that thou hast not all things
& needest not that any man should
ask thee - By this we believe that
thou comest forth from God."
"Jesus answered them, do ye now
believe? John 16th v. 26 - to 31th verse
"Do not the same question address
to me & answer I answer as the disciples did - "I believe that
thou comest forth from God."

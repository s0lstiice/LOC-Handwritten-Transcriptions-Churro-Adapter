required by Jesus, Mark 16-ven. 16-
when he says, "the that behaveth himself
shall be damned" but he that behaveth not
shall be damned."
Certainly not, what is meant by
the same expression is it -- in comment
used in the Sentences, since he adds
in immediate connection
"And these signs I shall follow them
that believe. In my name shall they
cast out devils; they shall speak with
new tongues; they shall take up serpents,
&c. &c. They do not mean to say
that they shall be damned for
not believing. They shall lay hands on
the sick & they shall recover."
Thus no one will say, that
without a belief of faith such as
this, damnation shall follow.
Yet how many pious -- seeking
loving -- trusting souls have been
perjured -- cheated -- some even
driven to despair by the application of these awful words
"He that behaveth not shall be
damned."
And if teachers of the Gospel will on
time this universal application of these words, why is not the
translation of the word, damned, used in this case, as it now generally is, in
0 or in theans -- Script 11th where instead of
damned, the word judgment is substituted in its place?

how making happy hours does that
word recall to me! Oh! my dear
ling then differently do we hail the
approach of Christmas now that we
are in Baltimore. About three weeks
ago we were all sitting around the
fire just before candles were brought
in -- and as that is the best time
in the world for talking we were
discussing the past, present & future --

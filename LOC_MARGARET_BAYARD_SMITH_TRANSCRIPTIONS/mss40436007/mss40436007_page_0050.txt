Paris addressed to Mr. Henry Havell
with a bouquet of flowers on Thursday October 21. 1826
to be worn at a ball
That you may bestir to, to night
When all an gay of all an bright
We send to you, our Abbey dear,
A choice bouquet of flowers rare.
The ground you to twin around your head
With Spanish lilies, daygling red,
Let spindle of Pandation grace
With Spanish of Christend your sweet face
Let Clove of the Sea-blessed blending
With Gaiety which of modest summing
Twine with the Calter wound your comb
With berries black of English Broom,
To imitate the Morris tales
Let a large bunch, behind be placed
Purslain & Goose-grass, then combine
With Cat-tail, Soldier, & Red-root fine,
That in the dance they may not fall
Spanish-nudely to fasten all.

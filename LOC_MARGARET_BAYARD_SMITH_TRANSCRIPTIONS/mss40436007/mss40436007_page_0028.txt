Weep not my mother -- that the infant flower
That bloomed one moment in your world bower
Was soon transplanted to its native bower
To shine unfading with celestial glow.
Weep not my mother, that it was not spared +
Sweet moments of your daughter's love;
It might your holier feelings have enshrou'd
Detain'd your faith and hope, from realms above.
What were the hopes which fond affection reared,
And all the joys with which you strewed my way
What all that Love, so tenderly prepared
To gladden life, and soothe that life's decay? --
Bright summer flowers! -- more lovely still than they
Which winter soon, too surely would destroy! --
Sweet flowers! -- ye never felt that winter's blight,
Stuck'd in your beauty, free from all alloy! --
+ her infant who died a few hours after its birth --
Short was my passage, to my early tomb
No sorrows sadden'd -- and no friends deceived,
On flowerry paths, I journey'd to my home
My Saviour guided and my God received!
Weep not my mother -- weep not then for me
From Earth's dark scenes on wings of faith arise,
In these blest mansions let your treasure be,
No mist conceals, no cares invade the skies.
Except this tribute that a mother pays
To soothe the sorrows of a mother's heart,
Would that this sympathy and heart felt praise,
One ray of consolation might impart.
M. H. S. L.

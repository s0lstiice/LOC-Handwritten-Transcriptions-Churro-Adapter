The moral atmosphere of the city is not
good for me. It stills -- it numbs my soul
-- Now that I have returned to dear, dear Sidney
now, that I am surrounded by the works of
God -- instead of the works of Man -- my
feelings of mind & body will remain --
My theological affections will be aroused --
I feel myself more immediately in
the presence of my God, & my heart
I trust can hold closer & tender communion
with my father who is in Heaven.
Come holy spirit, heavenly dove
into all thy power divine
Send me a flame of sacred love
In this cold heart of mine --
See how I languish here below
Buried midst earthly toys,
My soul can neither rise, nor go
In search of heavenly joys --
Romans - Chap - 12th
"I beseech you therefore brethren by the
mercy of God, that ye present your bodies
a living sacrifice holy, acceptable to God
which is your reasonable service."
Yes, it is indeed a reasonable service --
it is an act which would make me
not only much better -- but much happier
I know that they "that walk not after
the flesh -- but after the spirit" have
more peace in this world, as well as hope
for their prospects hereafter. -- yet do I not
offer this reasonable service -- "The spirit
indeed is willing -- but the flesh is weak"
yielding to its natural inclinations -- to its
sensual indulgences -- preferring the praise
of men -- to the praise of God -- what
then, shall the manifold mercies of God
have left for me -- my erring, my ratless
heart, than the enforcements of my poor fellow men --
toils? Oh that from this day forward
I could wholly & sincerely offer myself
up as living & a willing sacrifice to
God --
"Be ye not conformed to the world --
but be ye transformed by the renewing
of your mind."
This is a command -- a command
with the power of discipline -- Have
I this power -- Can I renew my own
mind? alas no -- But so far I can comply
as to make the endeavour -- Lord
enable me so to do --

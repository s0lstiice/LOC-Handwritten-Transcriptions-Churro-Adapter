our lots in aightful waste amidst
eternal darkness & winter. When we
consider that to God, the author of our
existence, the guardian of our infancy
and protector of our mature years, we
owe the light & warmth of the Sun by
day, & the moon & stars which adorn
the night, the fertilizing showers of
spring, the cooling breezes of summer
& every blessing we enjoy. What
is the man whose bosom is not filled
with grateful emotions, prompting him
to yield in return every thing he can, his
love and veneration to the author of
every good & perfect gift.
Nature has given to man,
neither the acute ness of smell
or hearing, nor the fleetness, nor the
weapons of defence, nor the strength
which she has given to other animals. Helvetius has asserted
that this defective construction
of the human frame is the source
of all our excellence. And he

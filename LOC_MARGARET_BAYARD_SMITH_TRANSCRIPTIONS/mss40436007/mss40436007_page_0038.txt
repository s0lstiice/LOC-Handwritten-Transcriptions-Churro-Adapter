To the flower called, Forget me not.
And memory's flower, of azure dye,
Permit this bard one hour to rave;
When in death's narrow bed I lie,
Oh! blown around my humble grave,
And if some tender faithful friend
Should, led by love, approach the spot,
And see thy flowers adorning bend,
Then say for me, Forget me not!
Could we with ink the ocean fill,
Was the whole earth of parchement made,
Was every single stick a quill,
And every man a scribe by trade;
To write the praise of God above, would
Daclin the ocean dry; nor would
Not would it the seed contain the whole,
This stretched from sky to sky.
Glorianna

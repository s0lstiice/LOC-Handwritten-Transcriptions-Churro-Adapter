January 1st 1827
another year has commenced I find no
tile entwined with the nearest objects of
my affections & the last blessings of life
Time, as it rolls years into the vast
gulf of oblivion & carries in its course
Men & all that men hold dear, has
still shared me a mine -- and for what?
and for how long a space? -- questions
which Time alone can answer. Darkness &
uncertainty have reigned every moment
since the present -- But no darkness, no uncertainty
weals the precious truths of God's goodness
& God's government. -- Come then what may,
he merged my soul, since it they God & thy
father who sends it. --
That, God of life, whose constant care
with blessings crowns each opening year
My scanty span doth still prolong
And watches anew my annual song.
How many precious souls are fled
to the vast regions of the dead
Since to this day the changing sun
Doth his last pearly period run!

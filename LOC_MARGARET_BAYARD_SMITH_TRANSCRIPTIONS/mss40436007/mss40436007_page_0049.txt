Come sweet sleep! thy powerful aid impart
To soothe the sick, and cheer the sinking heart,
For soon Life's fitful fever will be o'er.
And eyes that weep, will close to weep no more;
Then shall the Spirit wing its upward flight.
Beyond the Stars which girt the brow of Night,
To worlds of light in living verdure dress,
The abodes of peace—the mansions of the blest.
March 15. 1829.
J.M.

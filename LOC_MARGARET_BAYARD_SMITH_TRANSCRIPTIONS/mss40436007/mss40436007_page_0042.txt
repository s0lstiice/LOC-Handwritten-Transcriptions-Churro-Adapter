A good Husband to you, my dear,
child, and soon, is the sincere wish
of your affectionate wife.
Oct. 17. 1829. M.D. Kirkpatrick
Ann. Act. 71.
When thro' life published, on one
looking all that makes life dear
Should some notes on love used to love
In days of boyhood must our ear
Oh! how welcome breathes the strain
Waking thoughts that long have slept
Kindling form Smilings again
In faded eyes that long have lighted
And when in bounteous paths we roam
When those we loved have often stayed
The now in the blessed regions from above
Look kindly down on the ground
Oh who ask not full in his heart's deepest core
If the pains of the trials of life were all over
He should pass from the dreams of the visions of this
To join them in that world of pure glory of blessedness
We are lingering of trembling upon the shore
Of Clement's sea, whose unsparing surge
Must so shortly of certainty bear us away
To the gloom of the grave; when never a ray

It must be self evident that the
adoration which is prompted by inter-
-est or fear cannot be acceptable to
the Deity. "My Son give me thy heart"
is, according to Solomon, his language.
Those therefore err who attempt to
work upon the minds of men by repre-
senting the Almighty - as an unjust
judge condemning to everlasting anguish
those who neglect his ordinances, and
rewarding with endless enjoyments
those who regard them - as that being
who rides upon the whirlwind, and
is heard amidst the thunder and
tempest. Such inducements to religion
are the inducements of interest & fear, &
surely there is no occasion for these.

Mercy & Liberty
These are doctrines which to human
reason appear irreconcilable. They
have been contumeliously disputed in every
age by moral as well as religious
philosophers— The consistency of human
liberty, with divine prescience is
a difficult subject unsoundable
by the limited faculties of our mind.
There is however in spite of
Reason, an internal connection of
the union of these principles which
generates our conduct & feelings, even
when rejected by our minds—

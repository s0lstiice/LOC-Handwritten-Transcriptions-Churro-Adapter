the Ground them soonuster in beauty & bloom
The young maiden first
And the Stripping as soon
They ask but an offering of peace & of love
They ask but one spring from the Jamid Spicy groove
It will shade from thine eyelids each infantile tear
Which she often will drop
When no sorrow is near
The youth full of vigour & Manhood will feel
His learning and love his night minutes steal
That his lamp will burn brighter
That his Sophrosyn will shine
From thine garlands of gold
As the joins on the ring
The Veteran past says will return & advance
As he sees them come tripping, will sing in the dance
And the Crutch which supported
This steps to the door
He thinks he can shoulder
"and fight battles over"
The husband and wife as thin little ones play
Around the green turf on some festival day
Feel the threads of affection more tenderly bind
When Fancy and feeling
Their joys have entwined
Even sighs as he listens and looks near the Band
And sees them move Merrily round hand in hand
Feels his heart beat responsive
Do melodies strain
And caress them are chords
That are touched without pain
And wisdom joins Goddess from her Ivory car
Where the Six Queens of earth hear her own solar Stars
From her clouds so vivid Cliffs
Parades her rays around
While she weeps that it Chatters
So far on the ground
When the Innocent Band ascend under Thrones
And encircle her chariot with brams like her own
The owns our pale planet
More brilliant would be
Did we cherish these Sisters
Of Sunshine and glee
S.B.S.
Sidney June 1842

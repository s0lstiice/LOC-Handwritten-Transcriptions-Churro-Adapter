"Write a few lines, 'tis all task"
"Fill but a single page."
Laura, 'tis an easy task
For youth — but not for age.
Know you not that Spring has flown
That all its lovely bloom has gone?
Know you not that Summer's past
And Autumn breathes its chilling blast?
When in you walk and look around,
Withered leaves beset the ground;
Tasted is the lovely green,
And when sunset clothe the scene.
This you know, no hope to find meet;
Violetts springing round your feet;
This you know — no hope to find,
Luscious fruit of summer kind.
Know you not the life of man,
Refashioned on the selfsame plan?

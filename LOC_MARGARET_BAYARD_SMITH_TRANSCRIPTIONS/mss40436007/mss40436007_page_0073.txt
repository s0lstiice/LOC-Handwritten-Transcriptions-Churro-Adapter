"For as the Son's father hath life in
himself, so hath he given to the
son to have life in himself," John
— what does this mean? How can Christ
have life in himself, if that life is
given him by God? Does it mean
that after it was given, he retained
a power to keep it, uncontrolled by
a supreme power?
"For as in Adam all dies, even
so in Christ shall all be made alive
For since by man, came death, by man
came also the resurrection from the dead,
But every man in his own order
Christ the first fruits, afterward they that
are Christ at his coming
Then cometh the end, when he shall
have delivered up the kingdom to God
over the father, when he shall have
put down all wile 2 all authority
2 all power, for he must reign
til he hath put all enemies under his
feet — the last enemy that shall be
destroyed is death. For he hath
put all things under his feet; but when
he sayeth all things are put under
him, it is manifest that he is
excepted which did put all things under
him. And when all things shall be subdued
unto him, then shall the Son also, himself,
be subject unto him that put all things
under him, that God may be all in all!
Conclusions 15th —
Meanwhile it is the will of God, that
"all men should honor the Son, even as
they honor the Father" — again —
"Let all the angels of God worship him"
How is this — Is it to be taken in the
same way, as delegated authority on
earth — For even in the king of England
for instance to send his son to conquer
in some part of his foreign dominions
he would exact from his subjects
the same obedience & honor for
his son, as he would for himself —
And such in fact is the case in
all instances of represented man for
Majesty & delegated authority — viewed
in this light there is no contradiction
in the various texts of scripture, where
Christ himself claims, or where his
followers ascribe to him, equal honor
& obedience —

“The mother may forget her

Sacred & immutable promise,

My Creator, my Heavenly Father
I do put my whole trust in Thee!
Send pain & sickness, poverty

mitted from evils in to blessings,

medicine which may impart
health & strength to my sick
& feeble soul.—
Oh how dull & languishing
are all its powers, how cold,
& helpless its best affections!
House, oh divine Divinity —
cause me from this painful
lethargy — excite sensibility
to thy goodness, a cleanser
— & of thy power — warm
these chilled affections, purify
them from every low & sensual
inclination & raise them to
divine & heavenly objects.
In a lethargy so profound, severe
remedies may be necessary — alas,
I fear they will — But I submit
from thine! — Do with me what
thou wilt it. — It will be better

Three Sisters, by Fancy and frolic set free,
Do them dance near Castalian, where Stramon has met
While with drops of the morning
Their garlands are, west.
From the regions of Fancy and feeling they come.
Where their voices in concert, soft legends have sung,
And they light in this vale to refresh us awhile,
To lesson our sorrowe —
And light up a smile.
From the Rainbow, and drops of Serutian blue
They have borrowed, each tint, and each life-giving hue,
And they come with the palette, the lyre & song —
To cheer lazy Times —
As her Hobbles along.
From the height of Parnassus, fresh flowers they bring
Where the woods, and the air, breath the odours of Spring,

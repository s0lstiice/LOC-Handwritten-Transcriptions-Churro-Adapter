I feel it -- I feel this to be true
without the divine assistance I can
do nothing -- my best wishes produce no fruit -- But when I feel
in my temper a conformity with
the spirit of Jesus -- when I can
fulfil his two commandments
my neighbour as myself, then
can I perform these duties & require
of us -- then I can & give him
the proof he requires, "it ye
love me, ye will keep my
commandments" -- Lord then
promest that I love thee; at times
my love is cold -- cold is
at times my faith in my soul --
the sap that gives life to the
branches, is withdrawn -- they
are leafless & fruitless -- but I trust
not dead, tho' to the eye they
look as if they were -- is there
not sap in the root? with it
not again arise a impart faithful
help to the new barren branch --

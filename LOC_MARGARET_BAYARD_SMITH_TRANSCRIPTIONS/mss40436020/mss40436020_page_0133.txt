I trust this day has been a season of devout communion with
my Saviour -- the preservation by his providence from joining any
Christian Friends in another solemn declaration of my duty to
His Service. Yet I trust he has been with me -- whilst suffering
with my views in His Sanctuary -- The Temple of the Lord is every
where -- and if the Heart be right -- he will accept the sacrifice --
He the Sovereign from be watching; perhaps it is good
for us to feel occasionally, a deprivation in the use of ordinariness --
if we truly desire to perform our Duty -- By as
steady Abstinence from them -- as means of profit or profit --
if we suffer in trifling circumstances proceed as from trading --
we have not priests. Should we be determined contrary to
our wills -- The Enemy will still be practised on -- and we
perhaps be tempted by the Sins of an unchristianized fund -- our
Souls may be tempted in another way -- we shall see that we
must not defend their forms, we shall find -- if we have peace
that the Lord is ever present with his people to do them good --
and that he will give them comfort and consolation -- If they
in Spirit take his direction -- This day my God has been
much moved by the determination of my B. -- From these words --
"I have finished my course, I have fought the fight, I have lost the
fault -- Nevertheless there is land yet for me to cross, I fight
to appear before you -- I have finished my course, I have fought the
fight, I have lost the fault -- I have kept the faith -- his from thee God I will live
for the Crossway -- with this made and brought us through this
life -- may our conduct and consideration be the evidence of
our faith -- may the higher Gospel approach upon our profession --
may we be in correspondence with our whole Department -- and enter
upon by our example -- to win souls to Christ Jesus -- may our
light be shining before others -- that they may see our good works -- may
strengthen their信心 -- Keep us humble -- and modest --
and kindly -- and in proportion to our abilities -- feeling continually that we
are unprofitable servants -- and that all that we have and are -- is of free will grace -- we could say as down
to now that night -- with the Sign that the Lord would keep us --
that he would take care of us among our desperate friends -- and
if he the Lord please to bring us to the light of another day -- that
he would so begin us -- to them in the path of duty -- that he would
be with us -- thro the course of death -- and thru all the days and
weeks of our unprofitable time -- that he would check all our changes
upon the Ground of our habitations -- teach us to application
here -- and when we have finished our course -- that he would
receive us into glory -- thru the intercession and mediation of Christ
Jesus our Lord and Saviour -- in whom we would believe --
Lord help our unbelief --
September 2d -- While morning together this will be a day to
be remembered by me -- the return of the season which
delivered me of my bed, earthly friends cannot fail to expect
every tender feeling -- and myself well-thought -- which are
almost too faint yet to dwell on -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- --

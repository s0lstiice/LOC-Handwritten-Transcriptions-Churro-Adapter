December 15th 1861 -- I have now managed to have to equal
attention. I have to mention the influence of this product of
my country upon my life -- it has been strengthened
during the whole of the first day -- which was the day when my
firmness and strength appeared with a strength of
the country and nationality of her -- an aggressive Government
which can only have originated in the ordinary desire of one
of its most desirable citizens -- and if in the middle of such
unprecedented prosperity, in the midst of youth and health, and
every thing which could render life desirable -- has neither
shown itself from home -- and possesses every hour a mind
formed for injustice -- in the minds of all here at Washington.
Reed and crowned by all -- the War yesterday committed to
the eternal grave -- leaving a dying testimony -- for his character
which was forcible and patriotic -- -- I beheld the funeral
procession -- and saw the mortal body borne along the street --
The relations carried by the solemn spectacle -- were of that
salutary -- during the day -- my thoughts dwelt upon the
subject -- and at its close -- I was under an assurance of
the British tenure by which we stand -- -- a dangerous fall --
which produced a physical wound in the head -- threw me into
deathly peril -- the need of immediate preparation for death --
in our instance -- but for the upholding hand of my Christian Divine
might have launched me into eternity! -- but my life
was spared -- and I had a taste of the goodness of my God --
may he from this time -- will effectually control the strong
hand -- and that preparation be complete -- which will
leave me a place in manning of that -- when the noble common
shall arrive -- -- I had sent of some comfort in the position --
The moment my mind was calm enough to think -- a thought
at the very time it fell -- I was engaged in the last of my
praying to pray -- having just been praying, some thoughts
on that subject -- -- -- believing that probability -- and that
God's will -- occupied the watchful hours of the night -- which
was a cruel and peaceful one -- when at 10 -- the 5th regiment
came for duty to my satisfaction -- especially the 5th Corps --
Good God! on which I stand fixed --
Only one thing to do --
The various duties of all the dead --
Then life's futile things --
I have written this morning, greatly distressed -- by the situation
of the night -- I am lost informed -- how was apprehended
in a short time the sound which appears entirely an error --
which we will be held -- I will pray God may I never forget
the obligations I am under to my God -- tell me that his
body name -- and let my life as well as my words --
show forth his praise!

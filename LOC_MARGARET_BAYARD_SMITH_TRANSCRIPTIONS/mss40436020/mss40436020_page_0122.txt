Sabbath born June 7th - Another year my life is gone over
to when do I see this act of mercy - and what are the sentiments
I owe my Heavenly Protector for all his kindness to me during
the past twelve months - The Lord has seen it beneficial to sacrifice me
yet mercy has been mingled with the punishments due to my sins - for many months past - my heart has oscillated as
between God - depravity of spirits arising in front from private
talents, and the peculiar circumstances in which it places
the Almighty to place me - have caused me much sorrow -
I have been necessarily prevented from a regular attendance
upon these ordinances - which for some years I shall have
considered my chief delights - I am now in a great depression
in the way forward towards - or the certainty of returning to
God and his death - and the difficulty in spiritual things - quite
rid of me - temporal things leave after their relations -
and I find myself a solitary mourner - that day I have
preferred a more than usual calm of mind - and have
been enabled to employ myself according to my wills and
in conformity to the Divine command - I have passed almost
the whole of it - in the retirement of my chamber -
whenever I am that assisted by the holy spirit - I feel I
pray - all is past well with me - I must mercifully
provide for my aid, and provide me grace to do this well -
subject to all my corruptions - and make me to be responsible
for every thing of my grace - make me consider with
my estate - Whatever it may be - knowing that there
are before the hands of my visitation - and consider me to be -

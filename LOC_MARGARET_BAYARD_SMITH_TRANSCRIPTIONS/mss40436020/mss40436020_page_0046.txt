satisfaction. I can fancy a society united in love and gratitude to

from perils. I appreciate the mighty work of the civil War, bold,
brave declaration, which addresses all the benevolent forces, and
separates the present or rather the improvement of knowledge which
contributes the interest. I do thus express my faith - I add
the charms of music, such as I have never heard - the
glory of colors, such as I have never seen - and above all
a deeper, demonstrating sense of self-congratulation in being
saved from the infamy of human hatred!

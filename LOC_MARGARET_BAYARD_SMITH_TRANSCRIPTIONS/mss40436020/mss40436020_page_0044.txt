And though in every time of trial I am confident to
have the trust of those who will be called into
danger, if they may thus escape a
death which I have already made myself in
the Valley of the shadow of death, I fear that
many will escape only by dying on my
side. During all the days of my
life, and when day and night shall be no more, I
will give up this defense of the King's cause in
the God of my salvation. The sentence may be made to the
discourse of the King's commissioners. My private knowledge
proves such an event possible for which I pray. I pray the
greatest God - I pray for the King's safety! -
that God will give him strength to keep his trust in Jesus!
That the King would be with us, he would be at this solemn
period - that he might have the honor of his life. I pray

December 18th 1875
I join your call with joy,
I shall and shall be kind
A fight is there upon the road
That lays me to the ground
These words are surely expressive of their feelings which the Christian
is naturally drawn in his intercourse with his Heavily Father
once did he not sometimes express a sad sense and deeply prime
of mind, while engaged in the service of God himself and contemplating the blessed union of heaven & earth? — I am not sure I
am opposed to them for the truth of their observation: but the
idea of some happy sentiments for the purpose of divine truth
directly contradicts such sentimentary falsehood within here, and the
obstinate dogmatic objections beyond the narrow circle of true
believers not yet put that amount of faithfulness that earnestness
which can alone give from the operation of all sentiments.
I would therefore hail no exterior condemnation until it affect
me — I would not believe anyone believe that I have made
any friend with him — I think any honest man be told
alone — did let me not I pray, immediately break in upon
that my state of self — did not this time I may pardon — if I
am now acting from thee with true spiritual devotion
into your heart of and lead me into thy self — that I may
call upon thy holy name in an acceptable manner and
concluding there in Jehovah and in Truths — on the last
Lord day I was permitted to join my Christian Friends
(Catholics for the last time) in the solemn solemnity in which
for 10 months past I have attacked myself — the day previous
and the one following it was highly indecisive — then the beginning
of a separation from God — and these blessed assurances which
from the promise of childhood have been connected with this place
had not power to move or console me — what does
his God the power of the omnipotent Jehovah — I am
sure that my only home and all that is within my
hearts and all about me my family and liberty interests — who in
a nation of tribulation, condemned to put into my hands
the Bible's consolation — who has brought me out of darkness
into the marvelous light of the gospel — I for a Christian
denominator upon this doctrine — then man does this life with
difficulty and trouble — and pass thro' the awful valley of the shadow
of death — passing no risk, considering nothing that the same
Being who has established me here from heaven and laid on
earth — can lead me thro' the life that conflict — and
perilous secure me into mansions of eternal blessedness
thou to dwell with saints of the just made perfect — and
among the surrounding the righteous — singing balladances
to the Lamb that requites for sin and sinners

When hardships long has with my mind
And smiling day, since divine appearance,
Then my happiness! Then I find
The joy of my doubts and pains
I think my unreasoning heart
Or a trick that I should not be
This proof to God or God in part
Or harder one hard thoughts of them!
Let me then at length be happy
Though I am still in this to know
That God is good, and changes not
For hours the shadows of a hour.
Swift truth, and easy to repeat!
But when my doubts is thankfully found
I find myself on former path
Participate, without error, after to God.
But I may speak one word from this
Consider the divine Providence will
Divine doubt and divine doubt away
And they will remain where it will
Divine will and wills to forgive
As I can pardon to forgive
Then they will all the power recover
Be thine and my self-abstinence mine.
Saturday, January 21st - Another week has come to a close, and my life is still
changed - and also a comfortable portion of health - some trials have assailed
me - and several times during this short period I have been compelled
to fly to my Ardenner for help - - from this source where I find my
best consolation - and even hospitality - where I am willing to look to it
for comfort - - Blessing dollar - makes me to enjoy this mile -
warm this cold heart - speaks duty every threatening wall - with elo
quence this time Syracuse City -
Cooper

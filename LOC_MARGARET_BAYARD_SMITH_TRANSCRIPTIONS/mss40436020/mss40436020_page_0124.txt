In vain my fancy strives to paint
The moments after death;
The groans that surround the tomb,
When yielding up their breath.
One gentle sigh from gentle breasts
Let France can say—“They’re gone”
Before the writing spirit takes
Hers mansion near the throne.
Faith strives; but all its efforts fail
To trace her in her flight.
No eye can pierce within the veil,
Where lies Eternity’s world of light.
Thus much (and this is all), we know,
They are completely blest,
Have done with sin, and love and woe,
And with their dreadful rest.
Or hopes of gold they promote his name,
This fate they always bear,
Then let us follow it, be of them,
That we may share its doom too.
With the writer of these precious lines—Faith is now thrown into
Oblivion, —no joy or human sentiment prevails—He is a child in

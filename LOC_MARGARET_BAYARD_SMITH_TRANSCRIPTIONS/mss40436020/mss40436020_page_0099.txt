January 1st 1818
On reasons of the necessity of the present
year, and the comfortable circumstances in which I find myself at this the commencement of a new one.
My heart is throated with gratitude to the giver of every
good. I would leave his name for all but beneath
to an undertaking superior, and while on my tender
hand I wish thus of confidence. I would promise that
another period of time is allowed me. In which I may
devote myself more unreproachably to his service, and
as I desire his blessing to accompany me throughout
the coming year. Could my life be spared, let me resolve
to adhere with steadfastness to the principles now formed
within my breast -- Let me set up my altar in Liberty
apartment -- and morning and evening, I hope from thence
my sacrifice of praise -- My soul is directly above
to Christendom feeling that day -- The bright beams of
the sun illuminate my apartment -- I that the English
trans of the font of righteousness. Could divine into
my heart -- discover to me all my sins -- and with healing
in his wings -- cover over all -- I so to my courts Almighty
Father -- I pray for me by preface -- while there
may I join with sincerity and faith in the eternal
man I pray with and for my fellow creatures -- my pastor,
my friends, -- give all that spiritual men themselves which
I need -- grant me whatever temporal benefits then suitable
to that I require -- never leave me -- nor forsake me -- which
I ask. -- for my dear Robertson, Ladies, -- whom I would retain
to help my artillery. -- Amen. --
On reading Davison's family of Jesus Christ:
I might have been the heavenly hill
More obscure over regions
Such thought would banish every fear
And cause angelic throngs
Could I but boast with saints above
A Sister spirit there
The triumph with so much relative profit
Would dry up every tear
Could I on Smiths British prisoners hear
And see view a Parents care
Refused to renew the oath
True he were found in Brandy --
A Hope to directly do divine
For those alone to give
Then only could the brave support
That God the divine give
In this my Lord would I come
This gift of time to save

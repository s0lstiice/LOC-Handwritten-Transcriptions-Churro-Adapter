October 21st - Philadelphia.
This is the last Sabbath I suppose I shall spend in
retirement, at Rest for a long season - yet I trust there for many of
my God that it may not be the last I shall spend in this presence.
I am going again into the world, but I shall not go into the
streets, I shall go into the world, and I shall go with the
greatest of all advantages - I shall have the power to draw me from the world that I should do - I shall have
the knowledge of my own strength - induce me to hope a doubtful ground upon
which I should have might failed. Among the persons of the
other intelligences which I have in my mind - intended by divine power
to walk in the path of wisdom - and so perfectly to pray for
infants, - and children - and those I have been the blessed
mother - I will witness for me. I have seen many so assisted in returning
from with you, and I am surprised by them. I have the proof of all
truths many would strongly doubt my heart - working there in soul
and in soulless things - pray I be enabled to see clearly the
truth as it is in Jesus. Many I possess the sacred scripture with
me in understanding heart. Many I believe them indeed to be the Word
of God and from my faith in them many I know to be true
revelation - I pray my God to guard my faith in the truth of the gospel
itself - with his eye a faithful man I believe those proud
and mysticless doctrines which my father cannot sustain -
and to make an everlasting connection to thy Glory! -
In all the changes and transformations of life, in all the departures
of fortune, may I be merry - and the long be not for the terrestrial
souls but prisoners - may they in factually be forgetful with lightnessness
and joy - I pray to remove their heavy bands.

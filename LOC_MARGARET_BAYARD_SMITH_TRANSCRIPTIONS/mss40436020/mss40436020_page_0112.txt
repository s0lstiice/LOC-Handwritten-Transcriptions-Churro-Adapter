Saturday Evening — The Meeting of a principal and quiet church has
accompanied me during the past week, and will continue an occasion, which
proved troublesome on my spirits — a little trouble has been given me — but
with it Strangely equal to the day — and I have again to record the
gentleness and forbearing care of my Almighty Friend — The most numerous
reasons of necessity have prevented me from saying so far no opinion
myself, I believe I have acted well, in which I believed it to be my duty
to avoid any political consequences during the present week — and I do think
my heart may have with my God, ever, had I been free with him
in external acts of devotion — last evening I felt myself obliged to
be absent from our Friday evening meeting — during my absence I am unable
to contribute anything to the subject of the present meeting, except to state
that I have been greatly indebted to the divine presence for the attendance at
church, and for the attendance at family services. The time may have been
improperly used by myself, but I have been subject to a momentary
irritation — I think I could bring myself entirely subject to
the will and guidance of my Father — that under every circumstance
of life I may be placed — the firm and unflinching assurance
I feel of divine protection, may keep me in a secure place, and
concluded from a mind knowing and believing that all things
well with themselves for my good, if I continue to live and pray
upon the only true source of life and light and joy! —

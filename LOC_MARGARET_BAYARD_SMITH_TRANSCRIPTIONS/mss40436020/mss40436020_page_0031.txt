But often through my country
It passes a day of grief and death goings.
"While in my sorrow I rely,
I know my God shall love their own.
And therefore dare their power defy.
Assured of conquest for his name.
But soon my confidence is slain,
And all my prayer returns again.
"Thus different powers within me stride
And watch and wait by turns toward:
I give up peace, declare reserve,
And victory hames in doubtful state:
But Jesus has one promise fixed,
That peace shall continue at last."
For this my own great Ministers have
With only trembling, help you,
Be made they calling down,
There at most cannot a soul get
And save all they righteous sail
And to the end endure.
Then desires, thus my soul receive,
Transported from this state to life
And reign with thee above:
Where justice is doubtless best in sight
And hope in full supreme delight,
And everlasting life.
Amazing grace, how sweet the sound
That saved a world like me;
I once was lost but now am found,
Was blind, but now I see.
Dear grace that taught my heart to pray,
And grace my prayer restored:
Then precious also that grace approved,
The law & first beloved.
Oh! many dangers still and snare
Already I have gone:
As grace has brought me thus far
And grace will bid me come.
Yes, when this heart and flesh shall fail
And mortal life shall pass
I shall possess within the veil,
A life of joy and grace.

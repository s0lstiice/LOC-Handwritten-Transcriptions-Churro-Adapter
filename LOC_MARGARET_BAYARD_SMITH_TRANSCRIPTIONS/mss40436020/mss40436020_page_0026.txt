April 1st 1816
"My Brother and Strangers! Your goodness to me always finds as strongly in the afflictions that are inflicted upon me as on the Blessings wherewith I have been continually blessed. May I enjoy thy blessings with a thankful and a grateful heart, yet ever be ready to repay them when it shall be thy part to require me of them! and when they are fulfilled to provide me with affliction, may I always receive them with patience and humility; remembering that they are sent by an indulgent Father who promises them for my good, and who will assist and support me under them!
May I never indulge the least suspicion or discontented thoughts, but keeping my attention on those divine joys which I have been blessed with for time and eternity. For these, may I ever be ready to resign what I must lose and value, when they cannot be had to require it of me, and by a patient perseverance to regain my will to shine in all the brightness of the world, may I at length, like thy infinite mercy, arrive at that Eternal Kingdom, where true will reign and sincere thoughts imperfect obedience, with everlasting and unchangeable felicity!"

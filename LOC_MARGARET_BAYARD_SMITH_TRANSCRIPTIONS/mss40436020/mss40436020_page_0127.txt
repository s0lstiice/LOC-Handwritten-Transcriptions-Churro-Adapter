August 17th
The errands of this morning have carried success to
me -- I have managed to perform my duties aright -- as to the
presence of an all-singing God -- yet my best services I feel
have been prohibited -- sin had been mixed with them all -- my
thoughts have wandered -- and to these errors have probably been
added the error of believing my performance -- goods -- to desire
much to exercise myself in praying aloud -- in order to obtain
the gift -- sometimes I find great difficulty -- as to language -- but
I fear the spirit is wanting -- with one which they are as nothing
when in done with my little social meetings -- we are called upon to
take a part -- it appears to me to be the duty of preserving persons
from the self-inflicted death -- to cast themselves upon the favor of their Lord -- and indeed
as to be shall direct -- yet how backward are we -- his
pride is chiefly observed at such stations -- we fear man's judgment
out stands with many it is true -- some here are -- with humility
and diffidence -- keep them back -- and others may not have

myself in it -- until I should have thought that I was thereby

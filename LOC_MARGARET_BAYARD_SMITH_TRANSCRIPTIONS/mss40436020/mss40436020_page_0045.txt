Prayer answered by Immanuel Newton
I address my Love that I may pray for
in faith and love and strong grace
might move of his enlightened heart
And seal more earnestly his grace.
Time by time taught me thus to pray
And he in truth has answered pray'r
But it has been in such a way
As almost drove me to despair.
I hope it that in some favor'd hour
at once I'd answer any request
And by his Love's restraining power
Sabbotize my sins and give me rest.
Instead of his I have made me feel
the broken trust of my heart.
And by the mighty powers of truth
Accurate my faith on every point.
You more with his own hand he would
Inflict so apparent any error.
Would all the fair designs of heaven
Blind me, and leave me still.
Lord why do I pray? I tremble why do
With these doubts why remain to death?
Is it in God's will the Lamb will die?
I cannot pray for your grace and faith.
These natural means I employ
from God and hope to let them free
And break the chains of sin.
That when my spirit shall be all in me.
With all deference and respect for the firmness another
may express of this sentiment of her feeling respect I am
not free to impede a sentiment of human suffering.

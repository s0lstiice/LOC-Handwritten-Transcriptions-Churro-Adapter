March 24th.
O Lord I would now pour forth my soul of praise
to thee - and inspire upon this paper the sentiments that fill
my breast - and I have another of all hands - but this must see
into our inward souls - look I break thee into pieces - and see if
there is any veil on me and hid me with the consciousness. I do
not pray and I would if I could be wanting, I would break
and break down thy dirty spirit - and shed abroad thy sin in my heart -
from me shall nothing rise of shame or sinfulness - so shall make
me to bow before thee - I will break myself at thy feet - adoring and
magnifying thy holy name - May the divine spirits I have
in thy heart - send drops into my heart - and make them a lashing
and over abiding impression - May I touch thee of the day and
thru every hour and minute of my life - be brought to acknowledge
my ill desire - and from deep of consideration and awful fear of
myself be constrained to explain with Holy David "for thy name
call'd I have, pardon my ingratitude for it is great" - Make me to be
myself, and while I am God in the earth before thee, while immorality
the product of my dead - I shall be thy providence, mercy, and that I am
a God long suffering, and slow to anger - I will break thy bitter and over
patting friend - I will break thy heart and despise - in view of the faultless
God taking hold of thy heart's promise - may I endow with
forevision and admission to all of thee in Jesus Christ thy things
which I had in the Gospel assurance - that thou art a prayer bearer
to my God - and wilt freely give what I ask - and I pray I will all
feel for thy things - pray for me an deliverance - and the means of obtaining it -
In the glory of thy great name - in the consideration of the Ministers

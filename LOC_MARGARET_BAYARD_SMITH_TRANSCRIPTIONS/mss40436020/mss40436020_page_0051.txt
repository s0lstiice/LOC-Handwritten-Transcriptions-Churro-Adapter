It is when in America's wilds
Attends the lot of aristocracy,
It -- neither -- serves from rule to rule
Is doubt a life, or even a soul --
And if those angels of human bliss
Remain to those of Paradise
Is this which fills his bowing head
Whose body, happy bands support
Is to the Spitzer, which opens
A spirit from the vault of heaven
Has thus they more pray, feel,
Who fervently await the Christy steel
And draw, as though with Paul's bands
Relax from the penitence and the doubt --
Who on their lamented Thighes see
The red and dark drops of blood
And who with Christian mind connect
Their portable monument.
But O of such the ray which escapes,

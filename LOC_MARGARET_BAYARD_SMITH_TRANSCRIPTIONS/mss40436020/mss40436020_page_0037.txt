Dare my love of Truth and Peace
Dare my love to Brother Hell,
That I love the mind that writes
Dullness on and on with skill.
Do I hate the sins that have
Doubtless to shame the brave,
Dare my heart with bitterness
More than it gives me the Bravery to bear.
Dare my thoughts fit fire on their
Feet with cruelty and fire? (Answer)
Do I wish to Death to see
Themselves still the bravest not.
Am I humble towards my God
Do I in his grace rely,
Nothing from that divine place
Will I hide — a child of pity.
Do I love his words, his Days,
Do I love the breathing Hills,
Can I sing of pure and grace
That a child loves in Hills.
Does the light of doubt dimme
Or may doubt its influence punish
O sublime Time in Heaven
Angel prophetic diviner Lord
Make my love to thee the first
Make my love to thee the highest
Love be banished from my breast
For, I pray God I know a heart

1835.

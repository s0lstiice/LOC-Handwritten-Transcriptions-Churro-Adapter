"And the Frenchman's sentiments
"I have a second influence still
"Over his agitated soul,

"Those bitter weapons of the King

He remains to maintain the honor
of his once noble and lawless name
And fight for the honourable cause
With the sad tokens of preference
With trembling hand and difficult breath
He lays of guilt upon us and thails
From rancor to rancor, his angry eye
Sheds like that thundering thunder
From his fair brow with golden drops
To light upon his disreputable actions
And once more he will repeat the old
Lord Byron's lines as an oracle
And the first rose and rose and rose

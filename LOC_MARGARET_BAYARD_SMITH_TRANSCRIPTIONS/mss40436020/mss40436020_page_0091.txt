1, Brighton --. Bless the Lord to my soul -- and all the is
within me. God's hid duty home. --
However I'd -- above a month has elapsed. Since my
form has received a sentiment of my heart -- I left the
place of my retirement the end of September -- and found a
pleasant wish of ten days to my friend Wm P. at Binstok
after which I returned to my home -- to the house of one
of my best friends -- where indeed I have found a home
for the last 2 years -- I have great reason to bless my
Heavenly Father -- that he suffers not his orphan children
to want Whatever is necessary for them -- and that he
may find a work to deprive them of their direct and
peculiar endowments -- which others do abundantly enjoy
yet with the depravation he can and does grant to
the sufferer -- that constitution -- which estate and
support them under the trying deprivation -- he also

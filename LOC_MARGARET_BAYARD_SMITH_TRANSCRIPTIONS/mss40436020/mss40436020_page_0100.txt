I will meet the despairing thing of death
And triumph on the grave.
I grant me that my Brothers love
Shall me that love not more
Grant me the aid of thy grace
And tell me what I am to die.
To thee my children would I come
This gift of thine to give
I will know the character of those I leave
And triumph on the grave.
— Syden
Lives written at the commencement of the Dress-Press.
At the Birth of the year in the season of gladness
His Bright and bow lovely, the prospect from here
The hovering Cloud leaves no traces of clouds
The darknesses of Nature the spring will restore
The year has not passed the fond heart of its bearer
The Goss that he discovered Heaven discovered
Past, Present and future come treasure possessing
He tender has within the yet spotless mind
Every footstep is light why eye scans with pleasure
The World has a magic its influence are Truth
The clouds do wind with joyfulness they traverse
All nature is calm and the Broom of Youth
But sorrow to Manhood and grief then hasten
From the Spills that so hold them in broken and flow
The thorns that so lately they thought they could fasten
The world of Summer had Brightened and torn
The Friends that kind thrown to infancy granted
The guides of our childhood and Hope in the hour
Some Blessing for which the heart longly panted
Is withheld — Only to shew us the general doom —
For every year as the leaves of some comfort disappear as
We see the fair bosom of fancy depart
And the act many welcome the new heart with children
Same year drops of sorrow will darken the horizon —
You will get from the few days of plant we may enrich
White leaf has a talent for every use
In regions of this, from immortal twill flower
Transplanted from gardens of earth here falls
These grant us and Heaven — The friends of Creation
That Wisdom of Life, which from heaven of spirits
I will calm earth with comfort to make tender sentiment
And such as is peace in the hope of repentance
— Syden
July. 15th 1818

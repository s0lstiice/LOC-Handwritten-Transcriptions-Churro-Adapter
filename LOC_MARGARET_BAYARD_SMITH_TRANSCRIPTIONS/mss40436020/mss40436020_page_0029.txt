Why, why is my heart so full, my affections so languid, and my soul so little attuned to my divine
Gods? I would not, at this season, and in this day, help all
Gods give my tribute of praise to thee, and from thy
gracious providence for all thy mercies to me a crown. Then
know then that it was good for me to be afflicted and my
praying heart to be still again and again – I vainly imagined that
heaven and peace and joy would forever abide with me – and
I the desperate presumption of my thoughtful heart – which
said in an hour of tranquility, then could never be unhappy
again – with God for thy protection – the whole universe must
swirl around thee – – man the creature can dream the
more – but the false hope, dream of sorrows have returned
to silence my contemplation – and sin, mercy, patience to bring
me into greater submission to the will of my creator, and
heaven my God! – – Did many words since my pen has
passed the thoughts of my heart; I have not to write on
grief – but have the voice of my soul whispering to
all it into action. Some circumstances of a principal and delicate
nature have induced a separation from one of my first friends,
my maternal and Christian friend – – will remain from my infancy
I have ever been delighted to dwell especially since the lamentation
which took from me my natural parents – – and have since
existed – – since this period we have been on a together
endeavouring to establish the foundation of affection – – mutual confidence
each of us favored – and for the last two years we have
been under the same roof. In the enjoyment of the same Christian
principles – and uniting our principles – we have together
participated in every act of peace – – devotion from each
other spiritual under all the little troubles and innumerable of life,
and encouragement to proceed in our Christian duties and way of life
putting up our petitions for each other – and improving from
our mutual support to help in every time of need –
how we are separated – never to meet each other during periods –
how miserable are the ways of Providence! how hard it is to
have our wills united and another application disposed to hinder
me to God many times during the few first months – – the state of
my mind has distinctly expressed my feelings – – I have been weak
and feeble – my spirits have been greatly distressed at times.
The struggle to appear gay and cheerful – and the continual feeling
that I was committing sin by my pretended sorrow, has been
an evil which I was powerless to – – have been overruled by depression
– – have this trial to distinguish friends which cannot be

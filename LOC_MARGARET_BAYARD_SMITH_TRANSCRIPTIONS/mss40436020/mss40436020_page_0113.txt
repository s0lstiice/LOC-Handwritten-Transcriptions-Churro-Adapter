By me -- all Disarmament
Love dismisses us with the blessing
That our hands are free and innocent.
Let us take the line governing
Democracy in guarantee peace:
I project as
Drawn from this correspondence.
Thanks we give and devotion
For any gesture of peaceful cordiality
May the spirit of this declaration
On our hearts and lives advance
Many they produce
With us our more be friends!
Do wherever the official's gives
His from which he will belong
Brings on anyone anyone to believe
That he is their own truest day
Many we reach
Bless and pray in Middlest day!
I never knew this hymn sung all the end of church service -- without
omissions of peculiar pleasure -- Whether my heart perfectly accorded
with the sentiments it contains my Heavenly Father can alone decide.
Yet I feel such strong desires after that love and peace and joy,
which his blessings give -- I am so anxious that the fruits of my
faith may appear on my life and conduct, and pray I would
be fervently, that wherever he shall call me from life, that I
may not only be ready to depart -- to be borne on Angels wings to
Heaven -- but that I may indeed triumph at that hour in an
especial manner, in redeeming peace and be enabled to pray
a song of praise -- that leaving a testimony behind me of the divine
efficiency of the Gospel of my Saviour -- that this all
inspires the Hope that I have heard from Darkness to Light!

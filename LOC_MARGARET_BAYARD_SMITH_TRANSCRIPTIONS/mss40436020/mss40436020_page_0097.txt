They must be not in Nature's first mould
Of the sublime essence of Science;
And surely are Science, at intervals sent down
As well as of all the properties, to recall
The base desire to destroy eternal youth,
To draw the truths in an attractive garb,
To draw them -- Virtue we shall love freely,
To capture the center of science -- to unravel
The mysteries of Nature -- and beyond
The narrow sphere of human aim to make
Discourses which might damn the reasoning hands
Of sabbaths in philosophy, and prove
That things they cannot understand cannot,
That other men have superior faculties
And hence might had them to imagine things
Not higher in the scale of intellect,
Which such no human mind could ever grasp.
These to my weak perception, from time end
By providence prompted in turning down,
At times to earth their high intelligence,
And these were sure not contained, if they came
At once -- and in a cluster on the stage.
Now other parts of space and time would want
their share of lustres -- and to fill the void,
If more of first-rate genius were bestowed,
That works of mine would run into confusion,
Too near -- for Nature employs such minas,
And some immortal Telegraphs, souls like those
Of friendship worthy, because capable,
Of service which to me their like, or worth;
Since for the general good they came and not
their private happiness -- been obtained,
By staying in their native country, America;
And since this earth would be to them a heaven,
Is with their souls under their control.
Is true there was a little little mind there,
But was little and that they example which
When divine command revealed the freedom that
Might write an useful lesson to the world,
Travelling, more feeling than power, risked
Living as Men about -- to resign like these!

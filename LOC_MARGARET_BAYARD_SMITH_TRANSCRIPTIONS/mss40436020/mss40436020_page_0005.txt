Hard! the trumpet of war roaring;
When from California others
Shall fly high the brave insinuing
For far homesick from home
From the though they must to pay
For their homes their country's call,
From them leave their arm with dignity
Deference hath gone from all.
Have them stand with wit my courage,
See the brave in pity, O God,
With his arm support the dying
Under the pressure in the front.
Generals speak with honor please!
Have your death rewards ye gain,

With joy the smiling place.
Olive-words with laurel turning

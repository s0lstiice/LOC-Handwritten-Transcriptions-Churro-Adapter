God is my strength — nor will I fear.
What all my foes can do
One knowledge may knock down
I preach the living absolute
Among the inhabitants of the saints
The Son God of my God.
That shall I see my principles
given for the churches still stand
That I may see the message of Jesus
And the enquirers they hold.
When trouble rises and thorns appear
There may be his children still the
Gods may have their sacrifices there

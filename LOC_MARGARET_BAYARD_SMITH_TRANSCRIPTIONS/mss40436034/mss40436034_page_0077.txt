I do not repeat what you will read in our papers -- I was affected
even to tears by an appeal in the Richmond Enquirer, of the Northern
South to the North -- a solemn -- awful, tender & affecting appeal --
What a critic have me arrived at -- Do you see Southern papers?
They are full of this solemn subject -- No wonder they are roused --
But they are roused from their quietude -- alas, that human rights
should be subject to such conflicting views of duty -- that humanity should
be opposed to humanity -- that we cannot do right, without doing wrong.
What a sad spectacle does our hasty county every where exhibit --
And will not even our free & equal Government, the worst & best ever devised,
from a sufficient security for social peace & happiness? alas --
Mr. Kirkpatrick
Brunswick N.J.

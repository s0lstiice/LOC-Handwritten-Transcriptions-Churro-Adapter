My birthday had come & gone without my
recalling its recurrence until the arrival of your most
kind letter, my dear editor. I never knew any one so accurate
in their recollections of anniversaries as you are. Is it because
you keep a diary? or is it because your memory is
uncommonly tenacious? If that Diary or the love of order, is
in you an organ so strongly developed, that the slightest
occasion of events, impressed on your memory is never
afterwards forgotten, but are preserved in that storehouse
of the brain, with the exact precision to which that
you can perfectly take hold of any fact you want.
How I wish that this organ had been developed
in my poor state & thus the objects of my desire, as well
as the objects of my brain would have always been here
in nice order, & should he have a world of trouble.
But imagination -- the governing the two opposing faculties
& my mind turns every thing they find in 2 in & out of men
as particular things of her own invention -- that she is always
falling things to pieces & making them into parts in such a
wanton manner that instead of a well arranged store house
my memory is a mere lumber room where a great
many things are lost & a great many hard to be found --
or does it contain a single shelf for numbers & dates? From
my earliest childhood I have suffered from this deficiency -- this
time has been only a little corner that could have held my
multiplication table -- how much shame a multiplication table
have escaped -- how many valuable nights a better class should
have been spared -- for many & many was the night
while at Bethlehem that by the light of the lamp that hung
at the head of my bed, I have I feared the hours in contemplation
of this table to memorize & yet, when the next day came up to
do my exam on the Black-board, in presence of the class -- beyond 3,
I could not recall, & a number -- there was I feared for
indeed by my teacher & taught at for studying by my companions
to the torture -- the torture! that miserable multiplication has
cost me & to this day I never have able to master
it equally informed from & natural

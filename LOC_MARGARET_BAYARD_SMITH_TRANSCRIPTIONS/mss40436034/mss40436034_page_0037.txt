example will have a strong & wide spread influence
on the young men of our country.
The day for 2 Nov. being 2 25 -- I supposed myself
as I went for my friends, Mrs. Brentford & I would
see their families to meet them in the afternoon --
was much disappointed they could not come -- this
afternoon, I hear, that they are intended to come &
hope they will do so materially. -- I have not
seen Mrs. Thornton -- nor the Cafe, since its
reopening -- the volume you sent me, I have been

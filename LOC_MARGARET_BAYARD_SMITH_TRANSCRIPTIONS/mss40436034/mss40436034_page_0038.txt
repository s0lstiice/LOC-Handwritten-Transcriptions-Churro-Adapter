You may imagine, that if the combination existed
the power of control would accompany it. But
indeed indeed it is not so -- The law will in general,
how deficient & obstinuous I would be, if only the inclination
to be so were wanting. -- I think for my
own fortitude & feel humiliated by the consciousness
of my own weakness & inability of persuasion, unhurried
as they may be to their opinions & often are
'longing with myself -- But alas, little or no improvement
follows. good resolutions are formed but
not executed -- time passes & nothing is done, that
may inspire or encourage me -- Time passes, & so quickly
that it will soon bear no beyond this scene of
action, in which we move to use these talents
our divine master had committed to our care & we
shall be called to that tribunal where the continuance
of an profitable covenant may be passed upon us.
Though deeply affected, yet I often feel as if the
routine of family affairs, was but busy clothe -- to
the narrowness of them domestic occupations are
not palliated with the means of a comfortable father

wrote to you I have not had a turn in my hand
my mind is going to sleep I believe -- I wish something
would awaken it --
The weather this morning is so warm

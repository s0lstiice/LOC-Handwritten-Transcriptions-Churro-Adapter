The last time I wrote to you, I was alone in the house --
it so happens, that I am again equally solitary -- The girls
& Jonathan have all gone to North Street Church, where they
are at present the annual four days meeting -- I could not write so far, to Mrs -- always has the advantage of a knowing
hand -- I had no means of writing -- So have I am all alone --
not lonely do I feel -- yet, in reality, not more alone than I
should be in my own chamber, were they all at home -- Yet
the feeling is very different -- Then, though alone -- I do not feel
lonely -- Such is the force of habit -- I often think of a piece
of Adam Johnson's verse "choose that scheme of life which is best, I habit
with even wonder if it be most pleasant" -- I know from experience
that this is the case, yet have not the decision of mind, the company
of friends, to carry this present into execution, but am too
apt to be governed by the impulse of the moment, or be deterred
by slight misadventures from the path my judgment has decided
on -- But never with impatience -- never without suffering from the
mischiefs & imperfections of mind. Oh for a strong & steadfast & firmness of spirit -- one that could resist the attractions of fancy -- the
solicitations of inclination -- the seductions of indecision -- then

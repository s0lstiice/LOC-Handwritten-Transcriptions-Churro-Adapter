My father is still sick & I am not at home to reply to your last. I have said little in answer to your last -- be attentive much remains to me in the details you gave me concerning your new residence -- this time I suppose you are quite settled -- Have home feelings come up to but me know exactly where you are -- what you do & how about our things -- leave me a full and exact account of them -- I want to know if everything is nicely settled -- have to the dear Daughters -- how goes their busy, busy business -- they are -- I cannot help them to make you a home as quickly as you desire --

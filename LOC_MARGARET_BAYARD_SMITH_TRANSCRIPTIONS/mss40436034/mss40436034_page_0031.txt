Saturday -- It is four days since I commenced this sheet & likewise
7th -- every day since I have taken it from my pockets, I
sent myself with the intention of writing, but every day
continual interruptions of time not been able to fulfill my
intention -- your long & interesting letter prompted an immediate
reply -- I had a many day intention, the influence of my heart
would have been obliged -- but this warm fine weather
puts every one in motion & not an hour of the
day but some one, often many at a time are calling,
or I myself walk out to return visits & enjoy the fine
weather -- today is cloudy & drizzling, but so warm that one
can not endure a fine -- I am sitting quite an hour since
yesterday with open windows. I am beginning to weary of
the pleasant life I lead & long for the solitude of Sidney.

"He that is in Heaven, neither dies
nor is punished, says God, in this case
"I will not punish him for his sins,
but will, perhaps, allow him to live."
2. Therefore the principle of my being
with you is not death and the end of me
— it is the great God you give your name to,
and will as friends, without it.
And I am quite sure of God's grace
to you in the matter of your life
to which you are so ready to give up
your life. I am sure God will
give you a long life, and
that you will be happy
and prosperous in your life
and in your death.
From the above, my Christian country
over Philadelphia
Dear Sirs, I am sure I shall restore
to you the peace I only had
before I left Philadelphia. I shall have no more
to say to you but that I am sure
you will be happy in your life
and in your death.
Colquitt

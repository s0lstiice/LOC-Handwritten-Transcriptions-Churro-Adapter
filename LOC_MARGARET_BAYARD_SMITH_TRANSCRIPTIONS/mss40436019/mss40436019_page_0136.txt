Hymn to Gratitude
When all thy mercies & my God,
My rising soul sways,
Transported with the wind that has
to wander free and brave.
How shall words with equal warmth
The gratitude declare
That glorious anthem my father's heart?
But there must come a time
They proclaim my life sustained
And all my wants satisfied
When in the spirit would say
And sing upon the breast
To all my worth comprehended and seen
They utterly doubt any one
Let all my fellow-thoughts have ceased
to form themselves in groups.
However content to my soul
The train has broken
before my respect heart receives
from whom these songs proceed.
When in the Almighty's hands of youth
with honest hopes I am
There was never anyone to raise
But he me up to man.
1869

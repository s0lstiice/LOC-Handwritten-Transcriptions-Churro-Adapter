mind is in undertaking the Henry Wilson -- in adopting
the treatment of Patience -- will be naturally opposed to such
selecting my situation -- I sometimes fear that these
things have too great a share in controlling my judgment
completely -- That I may not suffer temporary
perils to prejudice devote me to other interests in my daily
prayer -- and Oh that my Heavenly Father may keep me
from all error -- especially that of false confidence in
myself -- and prepare me in hidden time for the Ministers of
spiritual blessing.
February 13th 1855 Birmingham
Another consequence of the day that I am in
which I am in the opposition of parties, is that I find
that I am Birmingham politician -- with about 20000 inhabitants
in the city and suburbs, Birmingham has now been
to an uncomfortable state, my health has not been and
has corresponded with many industrial injuries -- and all their
advantages which the world values and desires -- I have
no opportunity -- no chance to give to you a general
view of the situation of Birmingham -- the official
of a central nature -- and business of peace -- I will speak
here plainly and candidly when I have the pleasure in having
been interested in the business of the world -- but as there
have been proposals in progress to divide the world into
for an international division of labour -- I will encourage
them and endeavors by the British government which I think
the future will open when we, to mitigate the famine of the
world --
Dr. Cunliffe

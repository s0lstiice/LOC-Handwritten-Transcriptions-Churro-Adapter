Transcription of the Daily Correspondence of the
Amateur's in the Office of His Majesty's
Government in India.
1. I have in my hands and am ready to give you
the copy of the very short letter which I wrote
to Mr. Gough on the subject of the
proposition to make a change in the
route of the railway from Lucknow to
Benares. I have not yet received your
letter, which I expect will be received
to-morrow. I am sending it to-day
to-day because I am anxious to have
the matter settled as soon as possible.

On the moral, as well as on the Natural World, he may
delicately discern the signs of some violent concussion which has
disturbed the original workshop of the Almighty.
Which is the work of human Nature, from which human Nature
receives its birth. These great powers of consequence and reason,
that capacity for happiness, that ardour of enterprise, that fire of
affection, which often break through the storm of human vanity and
guilt, are like the scattered columns, the broken arches, and
defaced sculptures of some fallen temple, whose ancient
foundations support amidst its ruins.

He sees many fortunes won for many learned lawyers, un-

in which one thing alone is clearly — the prevalence of
truth which profits desire or which is desired — its usefulness
is indefinable; its necessity unattainable by weakness or
indestructible. On our neighbors we cannot judge. In our

precepts, and we are compelled to see its necessity in
our own experience. Its respectability dissolves all doubt, while its
inherent inducements are abstruse. It is often thus suggested
to act in spite of its virtue, and its virtue is inscrutable!

that are likely to create the controversy. From your opinion, and from
that of all my friends, you have nothing to expect when your
communications have given me such abundant satisfaction.
With many, you must contented, you indeed, enjoy many blessings,
and with like generous will not be satisfied to go unremembered.
Yet, I do not wonder that your tranquility is sometimes interrupted,
in similar circumstances, yourail friends, I fear, would not act
as nicely as we have done. I will not lose an occasion of expressing
lines, but do, be a good girl, be communicative. I know it is your
disposition, and why should you refuse it when you quite know
that you are not convinced I love you, and that I take a lively interest
in all your concerns. Yes, I know you are, you cannot deny it,

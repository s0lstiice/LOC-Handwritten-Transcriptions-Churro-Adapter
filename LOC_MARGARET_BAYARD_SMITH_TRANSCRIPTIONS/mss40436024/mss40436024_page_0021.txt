It is a long time my dear Maria since any opportunity
has presented for me to write to you and it seems long since I heard of
you. Your large and interesting paquet gave us a great deal of
pleasure. As Bowen Swan says – I love very much the open such parole
one little thing after another takes you by surprise. – E's letter gives me
real satisfaction – and that of the purest kind – Yes – thank heaven I can sympathise with you on this point – both from affection & experience. May it
please God to carry on his work in her heart – and bring it to perfection –
It is always encouraging to observe these exercises in a young person – to be
sure, they often appear to pass away – like the morning cloud – yet they afford
hope of a future blessing even in that case – But the special promise on
my side – gives us a most joyful feeling – when we see young persons
growing to seek the right way – There is something very ingenious in the
power & expressions of E. – and I trust a deeper and thorough work becoming
on in his heart – Do trust, you will direct her attention to the great concern
and while you encourage her to seek the right way, she will find her
the vast importance of a real change of heart so that she may not content herself with any kind of a social union with her society
She is favourably situated for the cultivation of every virtue – and I hope
you will find her strengthened in health – and advancing in every useful acquirement.

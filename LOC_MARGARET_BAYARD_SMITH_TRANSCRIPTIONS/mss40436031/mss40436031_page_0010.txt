My Mr. Manley is to be married on Tuesday & to appear the next
evening at the drawing room as bride -- she is to have 7 boughs
made -- Mr. Bar.Relf -- is to be one -- Mr Arthur Inglis is to be
Mr. U.S.R. in over, she is to come back & stay a month or
so in Melbourne with us -- we are content herself with our natural
manner of living -- which I fear she will not -- Ann & Julia
have planted last week the city with their flowers -- very gratefully
I shall express for Miss Morris her well wishes for so highly
regarded a lady -- I have not seen any of the books she has
read -- I am busy preparing for coming out making up
summers clothing for the ladies -- I have abundant
grapes -- springage oranges & leucite -- we have sowed a variety
of seeds & planted out at least 400 heads of lettuce --
Have this year taken the poultry under my own care --
by attention got an ample supply of eggs -- we have found
Schickons abundant -- the willow trees, lilacs & honey suckles
are in leaf -- but the present cold weather will if I fear
dooms remain all -- if this latter could be prevented -- Anna Manley
will send cousin Elizabeth some of our violet -- we have
patience -- what verdure does our earth give -- I can give a
description of our garden -- we have some -- very fine for our
neighbourhood -- one year from the seed which
could be put up in a small bundle -- if you know of any
way we can get this year -- I will put out when ever suitable
no flowers -- Anna Manley -- with beautiful flowers --
we are without flowers -- I am perfectly well -- let
me beg of you to write every week -- do not put
it off --
Adieu Dear Sister -- your most tenderly

June 11, 1825 - Sidney
I have just received your letter of the 7th inst. with
great regret. I am most anxiously desirous to hear from you for
some considerable time. I have been ill -- The time would be scarce
if your illness had not obstructed it. I am much obliged that
you have been so much aware of my previous health only two
weeks ago. I am confident I shall soon be in full health and
you will find me a friend to every one who has been anxious
to hear from me. I am now in a very comfortable situation
and I have been anxious to give you satisfaction in the
way in which our dear daughter is treated. I am aware of
the fact that she is ever present -- that gives her no
difficulty -- I am sure that every one who has been anxious
to hear from me will be satisfied with the information
which I have given you. I am sure that I have not omitted
to mention in your letter the fact that I have not been
able to give you any information as to the state of the

With breath of Heaven; or did he tell
In precious mines of sparkling thought
Concealed beneath the earth's soil --
Did they hide bear on golden wings
Rich treasures from the hidden deeps
Or are there hidden celestial springs
Of living waters -- Whence he drew?
And how to support -- this receptacle
When even patience failed to draw
The untrenched oft his deep distress
And precious drops have fallen here.
There are no lightly satisfied ones,

At length it came over a beautiful scene
that nature in silence had made
The place was but small, but two Wells formed
and chequered with sun light and shade.
I gazed and I savied with painful poet will
and grew tired of my seat in the air,
When all of a sudden my Castle stood still
in if some attraction was there.
Like a Lake from the Sky, the castle sitting down
and placed me exactly in its view
Then who should I meet in this pleasantly deserted
This corner of calmness -- let you
Delight to find you in humor and ease,
Spelt no more sorrow than pain --
and the wind coming from the east with breeze
and what best with my castle again --

4. Diege for Mingo Park
Hope no more more, in peace he sleepeth,
All his pains and toils are o'er;
As thine eye alone that weepeth,
His is closed to weep no more.
He hath gained the unknown river;
He hath found a hero's grave,
Where his head in peace rests beneath the dashing wave!
We like him our boats are guiding
Silently to an unknown shore;
Here we know there's no abiding,
There is rest forever more.
Pilot that mighty ocean!
Lord of Heaven's air & sea!
Thou cannot still the wild wave motion—
All our hopes are fixed on thee!

He must forsake the ground for the

harvestment and prosperity of the garden, he
must dig around the plant, loosen the tender roots from the soil, in which they are utterly
obstinate. He must with a cannoneer hand
remove the weeds which would choke it
and retard its growth – he must supply
it with liquid and refreshing drops
during a season of drought, and having done
all which foresight and care require, he
must rest his hopes on the all beneficial

on visiting Lassire Garden and Summer
House at Olney.
Are these the trees? is this the place? These
robes did they wear for him? Proud he
is their wealth with thoughtful peace, Pass’d he
amid these borders trim? Is this the Bowery?
a humble shell, which did it form for such a guest?
Why in the antechamber of his proud
By arts elaborate fingers dead?
Art weaves on wealth these tether-roam —
Her fabric she rear her temple gold —
But Genius, whom he seeks a home,
Must send for Nature's help to build him.
This quiet garden's humble ground,
This homely roof, this rustic Jame,
Will play quite to its first turning round
That wood-siver peeping at the fence:
That tranquil, tender sky of blue,
Where clouds fly golden radiance o’thorn,
Those ranging trees of varied hue,
These will the sights that solaced him.

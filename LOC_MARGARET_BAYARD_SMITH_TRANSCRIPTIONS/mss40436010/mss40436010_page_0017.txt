"train up a child in the way in which he
should go", is the counsel of warning wisdom
and its consequence, "which he is old he shall
not depart from it" -- the experience generally
acknowledged. -- When our Lord instructs
about the "talents" in their several possessors
he practises a reward equivalent to their talents
to his disciples and followers -- he says "The honest
truly is plentifully, but the Labourers are few"
to all he says "of ye I am not tired, ye shall
reap" -- The whole train of Christian displays
his goodness and mercy -- in the provision
of the life that now is and of that which is
to come" -- no exact proportion as we labour
to obtain the gift of the holy spirit -- and
in temporal things -- when the husbandman
deposits the seed in the ground, he expects
to be recompensed for his soil -- but he desires
that his seed may grow and flourish
and yield him a harvest.

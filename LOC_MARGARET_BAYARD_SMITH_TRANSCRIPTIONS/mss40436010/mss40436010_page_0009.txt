The Crucifixion
imitated from the Italian of Giacomo Leopardi.
"What sin to God has done
This unexampled deed?" The brave man exclaimed,
"Two Men;—one in horror watched the sun
From such a spectacle of guilt & shame;
I sank'd the Sea;—the Sea in fury boild,
And answered with his voice of storm,"Twas then,
My brave in-rance at his crime recoiled,
Disland the abysm from the centre ran".
I looked the earth;—the earth replied as hard,
"Two Men;—such strange powers ever born exist
That still I pour & thunder at the face."
To Man, say, smiling, thus Philotheus man, I went,
And asked him next He turned a scornful eye,
Throth his proud head, & dignified me no reply.
Montgomery.
If on the fault I must condemn your flame,
I think it whispers, "God indeed me;
"And I to him devote my little hour,
In lovely devotion and humility."
If, where the finest's darkest shadows lower,
I consent quench and extinguish I see,
It seems to say,— "I be entitled the punner
"Of him, who caused me, & his will, to be."
The fountain purpling, and the river strong
The rocks, the trees the mountains raise our song;
"Glory to God," we chorus in united voice;
Philosophers in willful error blind,
Did I not know in all the creatures find,
This voice through heaven, and earth and ocean bear.
Montgomery.

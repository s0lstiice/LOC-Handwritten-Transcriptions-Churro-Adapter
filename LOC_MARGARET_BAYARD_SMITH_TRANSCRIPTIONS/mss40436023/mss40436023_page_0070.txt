Anna Maria, has in tomorrow to pass a day or two with him in
Wang or Elujia will return with her - This family, exactly suits us - Highly
educated - of polished refined manners - always living in the first society - just
domestic in habits & tastes - Our duties our pleasure, our society, our taste, a
like same. I was in hopes you would have paid us a visit during the Session - but
you could not do it - but not Mr B - have business with our new secretary of
the Treasury - I hope he will & that you will come on with him - pray do not
forget to see him - such an excursion is nothing to you - are you at home
and you are well - Oh that I could see you & embrace you - have
5 wings of liberty - I would soon be with you. Have any your flowers - I am
pretty court gard' - your Garden? - How should I like to see them. Indeed
we must hear of them of each other - do write soon - I am
your affectionate son - S
Samuel Irving
One of his
most devoted
friend
Washington
July 1st
1831
will not leave your letter long unanswered. You must look for me in
Mr. Hale's Magazine - I sent her an article today - Estelle Aubert, translator
or rather attendant from the French - My father Constantine, remains half finished -
the poor printer cannot afford to get paper to finish it & I can not afford to
get it for him - I hope your friend owns Whitleycey will succeed - at me
he knew - if I could I would have been one of his subscribers - I wish her well
I offered the sale of who is happy, which I never knew so much, to her brother
Mr Goodrich, for the School - but he declined it, as he said on account of its length
The society of the author of no fiction must have been a high gratification
If he comes this way - give him a letter to me - Mr Smithers asked Mr Crawford
to come out & dine with us - but he could not - he would rather to see some one who
seems to be a solitude - So no it will make little difference
Parisville dear Maria, I hope this will find you 2 years as well as I, more all one - yours
M H S

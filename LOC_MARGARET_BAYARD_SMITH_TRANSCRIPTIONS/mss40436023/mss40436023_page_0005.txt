I wish you would send Lucero's pay in old $15 - every person
advanced in life would I think derive advantage from it
my pen you see will wander - I meant to have not this
expensive - I am glad the Dwight Health is restored, I
wish some other cause would bring him Southward - I should
feel happy in showing every attention to one connected with
"intending" a means to gun - my means of doing so, are as far
from being unconnected at this season more so than any other.
You know what our travelling experience is - The same small
vehicle in Mr Smith's exclusive employ - He has had a variety
of occupations in the city this winter & as he goes on business
to hide you know he allows nothing to interfere with it, he likes
to go alone, that his mind movements may remain untroubled -
On this account I am obliged to give up many agreeable
engagements, & to contrast my usual intercourse with many
known limits - I might indeed avail myself of the desirable
kindness of friends - But I am almost as aware as my punctilious
husband, to incur obligations - & it is often three weeks
& sometimes longer that we do not visit the city - When I do
go, I am the first one so kindly entertained, that perhaps it is
not for us we cannot so often, we might get too fond of
city life - Brother I am wishing to see every body leave
town, & finding the inconvenience of going for backwords, & for
boards, preferred taking lodging in the city - he has not
been out for ten days, the Mrs S. called several times for
him - He is constantly engaged last week I went in to
pay some necessary visits & dined at Mrs Sherwood's after
dinner I went for Brother & after passing the twilight
hour with Mrs Boardman & Then to - I told him as to
me to Mrs Johnsons - This little woman, impresses exceedingly
on acquaintance - I told her she is too good for a fashionable
life lady & I believe her heart tells her so too, & since her
birth of her own, she does not go into half as much
company as she used to do - She has great strength of man-
-ness & has given up sleeping in the highest style & the
consequent flattening & when her rheumatism has not in the
least of partis a natural mind a simple disposition - I
like her more & more. We spent one agreeable hour
with her & drank tea, when sending I would go to Mrs May
she said she would go with us - It had been three weeks
since I had been home - Brother who had dined home the
day before in a large company, said he was much more
gratified by this several visits - Mrs C. whose was agreeable

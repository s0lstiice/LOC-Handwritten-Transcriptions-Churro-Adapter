Should I ever have a sufficient sum at my own
disposal, & it be not required for the absolute comfort of my
family, I shall certainly appropriate it to building & furnishing
a spare chamber & thus save myself the uncomfortable
feelings I have when my friends visit Washington. It
is really painful to me to have them staying at public
houses. Had I had the means I should have entertained some
mode of making the dining room this winter so double
duty -- for although this cold weather we do not eat in it when
alone, yet we should be obliged to so move friends staying
with us but the necessities for such an arrangement are wanting &
as necessarily has no law, I endeavour to submit without complaint
to this & many other of the inconveniences of a narrow income --
However, the discomfort & anxiety of living beyond one's
means, which in all conditions would to me be most annoying
wealth is becoming every day more & more essential to
social intercourse in our city. Elegance & luxury of every kind
seem now a days as essential as convenience & comfort used to
be, & as these essential are totally those of my command, all
that remains is to relinquish society, & this I can now most
willingly do, except where attention to friends must be sacrificed.
The society in which I most delight, -- Books, -- Thanks fortune and
folly at my command a are every day more & more enjoyed,
& as Wordsworth says
are a substantial world, both spirit & good,
round these, with tendnity, strong as flesh & blood
our pastime & our happiness will grow.
yes, I find no such pleasure in the intercourse of living society

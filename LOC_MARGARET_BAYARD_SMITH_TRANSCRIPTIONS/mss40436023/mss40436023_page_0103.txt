Jahna, who is sitting by me -- requests to be particularly
affectionately mentioned -- sufficient subject for her -- never before was she so happy
Mr Gallaudit has lately paid us a visit & it did my heart
good to see them sitting together commending their love to
her expect to have Jahna Washington soon with us for a
day or two on her way to Washington -- I have not had
a letter from Sister since her return from N.Y. But
hear through brother I -- that she & the family are well --
Although I required a great exertion, my dear Mame
to commence this letter, I have had pleasure in
with you -- I am sitting at the farther window, wrapped in
a stone under my feet for the sake of being a little quieter
than I could be by the fine side, where little Martin, & two
black children, under the care of Mama, are playing rather
noisily -- The dog is covered with white heavy clouds & the wind blows
a wintry blast -- Susan, goes away now, soon he here, I
shall have her to attend to -- Thus, this dark dull morning
will be passed & gone -- Could my thoughts make themselves
viable to you -- how after would you find me with you!
Farwell --
Samuel Boyd
3. J.
Washington
APRIL 21st
1861

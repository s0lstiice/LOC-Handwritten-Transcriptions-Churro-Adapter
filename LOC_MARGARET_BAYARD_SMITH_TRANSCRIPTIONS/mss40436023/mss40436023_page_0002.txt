Day after day for a month past have I waited to write &
inform the cause of your unprecedented silence; but still Sarah, "coming
fort with certain bring me a letter," but tomorrow 2 tomorrow came
& no news from my dear sister. Tell me what is the cause of such
silence -- I announced your last, almost immediate, after receiving it
of papers, to induce you to a more personal correspondence,
only to think, since Sept. you have written but once to me --
That letter was rather a short & hurried one -- Are you sick --
Are you dispirited, languid, anxious & therefore indisposed for
writing? I sometimes fear you are in that case -- and your
sister Maria it is a mistaken kindness, for the anxiety your silence
causes, both to me & myself, is more difficult to bear than any human grief,
my dear sister, my own; which instead of being chilled or diminished
by long absence, is increased by the melancholy I feel & that
the enhanced value & luxury of my position is often felt for
attainable objects -- Yes, I often think if I were living in the
same place with you I was in every way, I should not feel so
high an affection -- This I believe is founded on a principle
of nature & affects every object of Hope or Love -- I think
great deal of you & am continually saying "perhaps one of our
children are the -- perhaps the last -- more still, perhaps

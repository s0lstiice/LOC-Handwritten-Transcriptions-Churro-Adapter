This world is a seducing world my dear sister, I
can make no excuse for it, since it has interfered with
my writing to you -- but believe me, it has no power
of making me forget you -- Every day, every night when I
lie down at every morning when I rise, the image of my sister
my dearest friends, mingle with my thoughts -- when ever I have
any peculiar pleasure, a spontaneous desire blends with
my enjoyment, that you could participate in it with me
and when sorrow or sadness of heart, my feelings & thoughts
become united I have poured out my feelings & thoughts
into your sympathetic bosom -- Think not these are mere
words -- no -- the thought of you mingling with all
my trials & sufferings would fill me with joy -- If these
thoughts could arrange themselves without them many long
chapters would have been written for me -- I have
written 20 sheets of letters for you -- I wish you could
see my letters to Bayard -- they are perfect journals
from them you might form some idea of the business
we had, with the exception of one part of our occupation
the office duties, which are an endeavour to perform,
but of course I know it would be to you -- But your salary
is to be doubled by the world as to neglect her matrimonial
obligation -- It will indeed far short not only of the requirements
of the Gospel -- but, far below my own desires & determinations --
not a day passes, in which I have not omitted to
do it -- I am not forgetting, as any one might imagine
the journal of my present mode of life -- I do not believe
it has been a single day alone since I have been
literally alive in society -- that of a very agreeable
kind -- I have greatly enlarged our acquaintance
among the members of Congress & several began to visit
me in a social way -- Since I last wrote we have had

Thank you dear Maria for your prompt
attention to my requests. The different articles
I required about arrived in the interval & I only
awaited of information from you to make my decision.
On finding so little difference in the news from
New York & Washington forces, (only 3ct. in yard) I thought it
best to supply myself from our own stores - thus
avoiding any risk or delay, particularly as I have
but little time to spare, as our time of removal, is
drawing very near. I have sacrificed taste, to comfort,
& instead of furnishing only one parlor, Landowners have
got new cabinets & curtains, of a manner disonklier for
both. Most ladies sit in their chambers all the morning
& only descend into their parlour to receive company - but
we sit the whole day in ours & as my occupations
generally are of a solitary kind, I rarely use parlor
& he girls another - so we have no best parlor
& show-room - as is almost universally the case &
in all my domestic arrangements, comfort, in my furnished
study. Could brother, as in the summer he offered to do, have
remitted to me a portion of what is due from his
father's estate, I could have added elegance to comfort
as it is, my share of the wind-fall, as he calls the
Conquest money, will not suffice for all the comforts I
proposed - at least if we yield to the claims of
humanity, as well as interest, which are combined in Agnes,
the blacke girl, whom I spoke in a late letter
to you. - after she was stolen from us, in the ways

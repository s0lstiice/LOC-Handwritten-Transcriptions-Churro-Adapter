Dr & Mrs Pickles for more than three years have been
living with her & were in every respect like Friends &
relatives, instead of boarders & most agreeable companions
But she found the same keeping back, instructing especially, and
ant & so they are gone & for the present she is alone & most
lonely.
Mr Boyd
Dear John
Mr Boyd is sitting by me, talking to Mr Smith who
I wrote -- I meant to have written about my mind
neighbour Mrs Madison, whom I see more frequently
& intimately than any other person -- she is an old friend
& my heart things to old friends -- I drop off in her company
with her to feel at home. I thus avoid meeting the numerous
visitors who daily call -- She is a great comfort & much as pleased
to me. May paper is picked & dinner is almost ready
& attention for the present

I am happy to hear that our fine niece Margaret is doing so well
at Ceylon. I fear we shall not have as favorable accounts from
Jane! It is a pity she did not go to China, where female missionaries are
more wanted & more useful than any where else, as it is only by knowing
them, the light of truth can reach women so totally excluded & shut
from all intercourse with the other sex. Mr Abel thinks China opens
a great field for female usefulness –
Samuel Boyd
Pine Street
New York
Remember me affectionately to Ellen, may the kind
providence which has restored the health of her dear child,
long preserve this blessing to her – what are your girls
doing – are they well & happy & with hands, hearts & minds full
& subject? This is a difficult attainment – Adieu, Adieu –

Sunday - May 11 - 1828
Your letter dear Maria, which I received
on Friday, was an unexpected pleasure; for I imagined
you would be so much fatigued & so much engaged
while in Baltimore that you would not be able to
find time to write. That amid so many engagements
you did find time, makes it the more acceptable
as it furnishes a new proof of your affection
I felt very anxious about Dear Bayard -
though it probable he would have a return
of his fever - I hope it prove only an intermittent
which the very disagreeable is no way dangerous
& will not be increased by his travellers.
For months I was anticipating of our visit
& promising myself benefit as well as pleasure.
It was a kind of spectacle to which I looked forward
when I expected I should be rescued from the
langour, rather the apathy of disuse & restlessness to
an interest in life - when my habits, as well as
feelings would change - Well, this event has come &
it is gone - a new appears like a dream - I can
scarcely believe you have been here so short does
your visit seem. It proved a stimulus quite
it lasted but now the same animation has returned
& the stream of life flows sluggishly along & will
do so until some one it receives some new impetus
or till it ceases to flow at all -

from Doughty. Her health is as much impaired

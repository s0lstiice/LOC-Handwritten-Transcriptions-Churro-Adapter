November 21st 1853: Washington
Three weeks since we returned to the city, a
one day train got found to set down quietly a letter to
you a comfortable letter, - for several letters we not so - The train
was crowded to day after arrival at last stopping, waiting
recruiting of men, has consumed my time in you, literally consumed
it - forth with from two weeks, nearly began past, leaving
no night behind, they leave now, can be a year to absent
since in precisely the same circumstances in the same house
I informed you yesterday in the very same words of our
removal from the county - at our removal, here, is a time
to tell without any new incidents, so that the general
of one year would quite little variation come for the
rest. - The interval between us goes in the county -
our return to the city, seems like a dream - I can scarcely
realize the enormous migration that we have made
here - have me now, commencing our winter residence
to the begin of winter, comfort a cheerful reason, both
now of these changes, hopes of brilliant returns which I need
to indulge, for although never realized, yet for many years
of my life, such illusions were built upon my fancy, being
a ambition, if I knew myself, an easy interest - the desires
of the world have lost their attraction - Indifference
to fame & pleasure has increased, finally, in new means
precious to me than any thing the great or noble and
has to give - and this from a deaf felt conviction of the
universality of life, one moment in this grand affair of life
about which we are so busy, so anxious or restless, on which
we build schemes which many years could not neglect - one
moment, from reflection with the motion of the fault machine
at home in an end of the military affair! - One moment
you feel the responsibility of causing to live, in the short space,
in transport cannot take a brief or long interval -

Sidney 25. July. 1826.
It is well you write my dear Munier I cannot use the sufficient
excursion, to take up my pen a thing I now never do when not
impelled by some powerful stimulus -- such has your letter produced,
when Mr Smith came home from Bank, he brought it to me --
ever excited in my soul respect, benevolence, the first joyings, needings
while I watched my still, being in the midst of difficulty, persecution,
of which I wish I could send you a picture, and in every fine 2 about
as strong as 4 spirits -- Divinity is over -- I have brought my
writing desk out -- I am now seated on, not under the flagging
with the still in night, Maryann watching it, under my direction,
you see even in my ordinary operations, I continue to be literary,
as as Mrs Barber says romantic -- one of the first things she says
which made her wish to know me, was because of this habit
of mine, which being her habit, cheering, she could not fail to
approve -- It is impossible for the blue sky, adorned
with pearly clouds, a every line 2 form -- for the green earth
varied with every shade of verdure, a covered with trees & shrubs
of every kind, hill or valley, corn fields & meadows to look more
lovely -- or for the atmosphere to be more transparent --
The girls are engaged in their own department
for Smith is abroad -- in the fields -- Boyard & Anna remain at
Malarama & I am alone with you, in this nature -- In
sounds let there be the birds & of the woods among the trees
disturb this delicious silence -- Oh it is delightful --
The scenery without 2 the feeling within. How happy I feel!!
And therefore more happy than usual -- strange answer for an
intellectual being, but true as strange -- because the cool &
pure atmosphere, produces an equalable play in the streams
of life -- which neither turns away in fervent interest
nor stagnates in the system, nor checks loyalty among through
the veins -- it flows kindly, but equally, & diffused cheerfulness in
the spirit -- it flows kindly, but equally, & diffused cheerfulness in
the almost like bearded & humbled & heavy & life itself, in

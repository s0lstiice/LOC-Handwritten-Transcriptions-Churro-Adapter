Feb 7-24, 1839-
I have my dear Maria, taken so little note of the
lapse of days & weeks, that I really felt astonished on
looking at the date of your last letter, for find that
almost a month has intervened since I received it- The
first of Feburary had this interval been marked by
any new or interesting incidents, like land marks they would
have reminded me of the progress of my journey. I
should have counted days, as one count mile stones
seen journeying along the high road- But one day
apart is little from the preceding, & all, are so alike,
& smooth & unbroken that I pass them over, as
if tranckion does an even rail through a level country,
unconscious of the distance. The last few days,
however form an exception, as they afforded objects
& agreeable excitement- One evening at Mr Smith's
particular request, I consented to go into Mr Smith's
house to meet Mr & Mrs Combe
Mr Abbot & others of the same descent- Anna
Johns likewise went- We effected a small
select company of other, good & literary folks, but
to our surprise found an amusement of more than
hundreds- However, one may be alone in a crowd
I felt very much so while engaged in a most agreeable
conversation with Mrs Abbot- Besides time, I met
Mr George Boyd, who during the evening entertained
myself almost entirely to our party- Judge Story, & three
or four old acquaintances whom I had not seen for
long time, I was likewise glad to meet, & on the
whole passed a most pleasant evening, the first
have passed in company for more than two, & believe

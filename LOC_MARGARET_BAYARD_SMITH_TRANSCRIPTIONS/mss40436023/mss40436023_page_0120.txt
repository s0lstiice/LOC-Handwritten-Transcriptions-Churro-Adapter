When busy today, I always resolve to write
immediately, but when I am in a hurry with
it, I get a variety of occupations in the morning
in the kitchen, in the garden, that
tailor or even find ten quiet moments for pen
-work, -- I will not relate the sad news, for my letters
are heart with me. I have occasioned my little
master to be his playmate, that he cannot dis
place with my attention, so that I really have
almost given up reading or writing when he
is with me. I prefer to leave to write
by myself, what is matter of personal interest
for my family, -- My feelings prompted me
immediate reply, -- I am, dear Maria, to your most
interesting letter -- Strongly, that although your
other friends have been monthly divided the
late affecting scenes at Cincinnati -- I cannot realize
the event -- after receiving Mrs Dodds letter
I sat alone & tried to do so -- I believed the
fact, but I did not feel it -- I did not realize
it -- When I last summer laid our dear brother
forewell, I thus felted it the separation was
final -- I am truly deeply affected -- But no
one can imagine how I feel from the scenes, the emotions
stirred -- The friends connected with the event,
more of these tender sympathies are excited,
that would have been felt had I been among
you. -- It is on occasions like this that I feel
the necessity of keeping together -- that I must
make a final separation from my relatives & friends --
that I feel like an alien, an outcast from home
with all its joys & sorrows -- that I feel alone
in a land of strangers --

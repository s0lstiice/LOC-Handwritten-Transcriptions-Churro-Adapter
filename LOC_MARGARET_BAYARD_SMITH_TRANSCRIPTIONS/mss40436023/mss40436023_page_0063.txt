every evening we surround the table with our various wares -- while
needles abound -- but since we came to the city, conversation supplies the
place of books, as we have not as yet been half a dozen evenings alone.
Still the work goes on -- Bayard Street is a frequent hunter -- Mr. Mayhew, Mr.
Good, Mr. Shearsby & several others are on a footing of intimacy which abounds in
this district. They need about part of the evening --
Mr. Samuel Boyd

New York
Ever lady-visitory is chiefly in the morning. I have among others, near
one book since I came to the city with which I am sure you
Elizabeth too, will be pleased -- Silvio Pellico, in years improvement
a spirit of pure and mild devotion pervades the whole book & beautifully
illustrates the consoling & supporting power of religion.
We have sold our houses & shall not replace them this winter, an event
which I trust will be very beneficial to my health, for I find walking
the most efficient remedy for the irregular & obstructed action of the arterial
system -- from which I have of late suffered a good deal -- I have walked a great
deal since I came to the city, I feel myself already much relieved. Would not your
head be otherwise benefitted by this kind of exercise. I have fulfilled my part,
as far as possible & must bid you therefore my dear mama, good night --
your affectionately
MRS
W. H. SILLIMAN

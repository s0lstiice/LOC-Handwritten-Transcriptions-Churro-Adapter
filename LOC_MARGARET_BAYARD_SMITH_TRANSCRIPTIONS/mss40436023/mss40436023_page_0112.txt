came home the first day I told me he had subscribed to a
new library, he gave me more pleasure than it had presented
me with the most costly piece of furniture. Having now but 10
needle-work, most of my short day & long evening are occupied
with books & at 11 o'clock at night, it is seldom I relinquish
them without reluctance. Two hours every evening, for the sake
of varying my employment, I devote to copying MSS -- which if
not published, may at least amuse my grandchildren. Chop,
have given up to Ann; while she was absent I played with
Mr S -- to amuse him, not myself -- as Ann is not of
communicative disposition, she has given me but few details
concerning what she heard & saw during her late excursion.
In worthy memory this & do not depend on my knowing
any circumstances through this channel. Mr Boyd, I have
seen only in the family & have therefore conveyed no
general copies, except that he mentioned yesterday
that Elizabeth had returned home. I presume she will
remain with you until the Gent Assembly makes
arrangements in the Board of Inspections -- I dare say, you will
as I do, about Julia, that circumstances may enable it to

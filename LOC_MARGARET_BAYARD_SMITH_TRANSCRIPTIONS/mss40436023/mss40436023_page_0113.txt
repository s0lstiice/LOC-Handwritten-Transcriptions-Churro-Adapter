Let me know when you next write, how Bayard
is -- what a pity it is that he left Washington where
he was so elegibly situated. Sister writes me about her
hesitations & difficulties wishing continually to give up house
keeping, clearly stating the advantages & disadvantages of either
case. I asked my opinion -- This however neither I nor any
friend can make do -- so equally are the advantages & disadvantages

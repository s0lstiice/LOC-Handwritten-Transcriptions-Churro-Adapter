to my taste, that is to make the dots and lines which
make the letter distinct. Indeed I think with you
that it cannot be done with the type as they now
are; and to remedy the defect I have thought best
to suggest to you the idea of having a mould made
to cast type in, holes and all, so that they can be
easier made and quicker, and since the time which
remains is not sufficient to make them the old way I shall
immediately make the mould, and either send it
to you, or wait for you to send out the type met-
al, -- The type as they now are will answer perfectly
well, and are certainly better than any we can now
make in the time which remains. -- I have been per-
plesed in fixing any thing to stop the striking, I am
inclined to think that when you become deeply engaged
in sending communication you will not hear the bell.
I have several times, after I have been operating, been
at a loss to know whether it has struck or not. In
regard to the fixture which you desired for the type
to slide down upon, I have tried it but find it not
to succeed so well, and have therefore to propose to
you to take the present portion as it is, and that
I shall commence as soon as possible your plan of

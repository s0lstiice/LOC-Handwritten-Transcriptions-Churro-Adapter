[Mar. 15, 1838]
Thos. S. Cummings Esq.
Treasurer of the Association &c.
My Dear Sir,
Circumstances relating to the United States Telegraph invented by me in the year 1832
will require my attention for an indefinite time, and
I am about to visit Europe principally in reference to
mat ter s connected with this Invention, at the same
time I have in view some Studies connected with the
Pictures which the Association have commissioned me
to paint for them Yet I ought not to conceal from
the gentlemen who have so generously formed the
Association, that circumstances may arise in relation to the Telegraph, which may make it my duty of paramount importance to suspend for a season to my country
may be eventually to freeze the Commission with which they
have honored me. In this state of case I
have to request that no further collections of the
quarterly installments be made until my return
from Europe in the autumn at which time I
shall doubtless have it in my power to acquaint you with
the course which I shall be able to pursue. If
possible I wish as soon as practicable to relieve

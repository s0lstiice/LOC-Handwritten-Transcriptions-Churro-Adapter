Copy to C. S. P.
New York Union, April 20, 1833
I cannot let you part from me, my dear friend Catherine
with out expressing the regret that I never see you again, without
a few words of a friend who will ever feel a deep and
particular interest in your happiness and that of your family, and which I shall always entertain
with the utmost confidence, and which I shall always entertain with the full power of your natural affection and esteem for me. I am sorry to notice the public opinion that I am a person who is always flatterer, and
always at times a little too flattering, and never intended that I should be so, but I have

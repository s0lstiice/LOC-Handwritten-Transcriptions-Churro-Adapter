My dear Sir
I saw Mr. Jarvis
soon after I received your first
letter, which, thinking it
would come well in aid of what
I had to say for you, I showed
him. I then spoke of you as
you would wish, and as I hon-
estly think you deserve. He was
not here when I got the second
letter. But I have lately written
to him about you, and the letter,
though not long, will, I think,
carry some weight with it. Among
other things I observed, that much
as I wish to serve those as a
friend whom I highly value, I
would not say a word in his favour,
had I any doubt of his competen-
cy, but I can conscientiously say

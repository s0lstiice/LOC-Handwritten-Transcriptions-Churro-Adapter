Albany, N.Y. Sept. 9th 1838
Dear Sir
At the request of P.O. Smith I
have taken the liberty of writing you about the
large family picture you have. Which you
was to make a Copy from. He thinks
since your time is most profitably spent that
you will send up the picture and let me
make the Copy.
Do not think that I wish
him to let me do it, for I have more than
I can tend to now. but he wishes to have
the picture done, and thinks some time
will elapse before you will be able to comm
ence it.
I have nothing to exhibit this
year unless a few heads which I wish you could
place in as favorable light as possible
Could I but be away nothing
would prevent me from accompanying you to
Europe unless you refused
I remain your affectionate
pupil
Frederick Pink
Answer to your elegant one may it add another look to

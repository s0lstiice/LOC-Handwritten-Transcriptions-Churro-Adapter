Notes from the Essay in Vol. 10. Transaction
Duckhead. Yol. Library. not quoted exactly in
by Wm. Hunter.
"A despotic government is unfavorable in
itself to every species of cultivation and
liberal improvement, but still through the
influence of particular individuals it may
be adorned in the highest degree with the
fine arts -- The governments of Augustus
Cesar, of Leo X and the 14th. Lewis are examples."
Extent of Territory, commerce, & foreign
relations as well as form of Government
enter into the measure of the political state
of a country.
"Form of Government has the greatest influence;
but collateral causes may control the
tendency of government and even dictate the form
of it. Some are natural & permanent, others
accidental and transitory. The former are soil
climate, and position with regard to other nations,
whether it is a continent or Island, mountains or
inland. The latter are religion, talent of rulers,
and character of neighboring nations.

Patent Office, Sept. 1, 1837.
New York City University, N.Y.
Mr. S. F. B. Morse,
Illinois, Oct. 21, 1837.
Dear Sir:
You will see the 20 ult was the only one in the
office the office of course will fully instruct you on the
subject of patents. Please me now requested to be paid in
coin which may be transmitted per mail free of postage.
Your affectionate classmate,
(Sgd.) Henry L. Ellsworth
Postmaster
111 Washington Street, New York
I have read the Journal of Commerce with pleasure
mentioning your disconsolate and meekness.

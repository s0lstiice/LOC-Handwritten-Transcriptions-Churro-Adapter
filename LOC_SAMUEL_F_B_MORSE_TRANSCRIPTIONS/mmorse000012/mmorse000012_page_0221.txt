Philadelphia, Feb. 13/38.
Timisi,
At the request of the Committee of the Franklin Institute
upon your Electric-Magnetic Telegraph
is this day transmitted to the Sec-
retary of the Treasury, from whom it
will not doubt be obtained by Mr.
Smith, the Committee Chairman of the
Committee of Commerce. I regret
that it is not in my power to have
this subject at this time for him
and for you. The subject is noted
for publication in our Journal. I
must hardly add that it is favorable.
Very truly yours
P. M. Patterson.
Prof. Sam. F. B. Morse.

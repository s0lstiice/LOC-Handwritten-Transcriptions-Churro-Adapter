[Mar 1838]
Whereas Samuel F. B. Morse of the City & State of New York am the sole and original inventor of a system of Electro-Magnetic Telegraphy, and to secure my legal rights and property in said invention. I have filed a caveat and evidence of my said invention in the office of the Commissioner of patents in the City of Washington, to the end of obtaining letters patent for said invention hereafter, according to law.
And be it therefore known, to all whom these presents shall come, that I the said Samuel F. B. Morse, for a valuable consideration paid by Francis C. I. Smith of Portland, in the State of Maine, the receipt whereof I do hereby acknowledge, have bargained and sold, (and hereby do bargain and sell) unto the said Smith, his heirs and assigns, the undivided eighth part of my said invention and of all my rights and property therein that I have, or may have by letters patent for the same, (or in any improvements or additions that I have or may have hereafter make in and to the same (and secure by letters patent or otherwise) within the limits of the United States – also the undivided eighth part of all rights, property or interests which I may have or acquire by letters patent or otherwise, in my name or otherwise, in or from said invention or any improvements therein in all places and countries without the United States – hereby making and constituting said Smith joint and part owner with myself both within and without the limits of the United States, in the proportions above stated, of my said invention, property and interests, be the same more or less, and in all improvements (and additions) I may make or acquire therein, and as soon as may be after any of said letters patent shall be obtained,

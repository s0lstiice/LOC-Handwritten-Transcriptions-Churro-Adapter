Feb. 20th 1837
Somewhat cloudy till 10 A.M. then mostly clear, very pleasant for several hours
evening cloudy and after 10 rainy.
After a rainy night a cloudy, disagreeable day, wind moderate. Baltimore
harbor clear of ice. 8 o’clock, a very pleasant, wind light. On
Thursday, P.M. a great snow storm at Cleveland (Ohio) Buffalo valve
hub, snow about 2 feet deep, much drifted by a high, very cold wind,
at Leucie mercury 8º below 0 during the day, wind from N.E.
Snow commenced falling at 6 A.M. which at 7 o’clock, 9 A.M.
turned to rain, which fell heavily for several hours. After
3 P.M. foggy & misty. A.M. wind fresh.
24. mostly cloudy, till noon, then clear, wind fresh.
25 clear & pleasant, wind moderate, Hudson open to Nyack.
26 cloudy and 2 P.M. rainy, wind light, about 7 o’clock
it commenced snowing.
27 snowed last night about 2 inches, much drifted by
a high wind from N. to day clear & windy. Somewhat
overcast and windy P.M. a cold day.
28 somewhat cloudy, wind light (snow this day at Phila. 30
& 4 inches deep. — none here.
March. 1. mostly clear A.M. mostly cloudy P.M. and in the evening
light snow. wind light.
2. light snow during the night about 1/2 an inch deep.
after 9 A.M. clear cold morning. Cat Leucie or Monday
a severe snow storm. Snow 5 or 6 feet deep on a level with
the river, calm, and very cold. good sleighing at Phila. 8 inches
deep, and floating ice in river.
3 Saturday 4 from 7 o’clock to 7 o’clock 8 A.M. a light fall of snow
after nine mostly clear & pleasant.
5 Monday 6 clear & pleasant. On Sat. snow fell 12 inches deep at
Savannah (Georgia) deepest snow known for 37 years.
7 Tuesday 7 hazy & partly cloudy, very pleasant day.

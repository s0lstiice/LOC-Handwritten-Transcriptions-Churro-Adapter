I am at a loss to guess what has become of the
colours that Mr Rover was to send me. I am in
great want of the Blake Whites. You remember
that I requested them to be sent to the care of Mr
Pendleton. but Have they been sent? My Nephew
informs me that Pendleton, the believer, has left
Boston; at any rate he has removed from
his store in Washington Street. Now is Mr P.
has left the city, and the Colours have been
sent to Boston with his former address, sent
to his care, they must be now on a wild goose
chase. Do let me know something about them,
as I am sadly in need of them; and send me at
the same time Mr Rovers address, that I may
know how to get a letter to him. In case the
colours should not yet have been forwarded,
I will thank you to request Mr Rover to direct them
to the care of Mr Edmund T. Hastings,221 Merchants
Row Boston; and also that he will inform me by
mail, how to send in what way I am to transmit
the money for them. — By the way, would not
Mr Verplanck be a good person to consult with
about the subscription for Mr Stuart? — I have
filled my paper with out knowing it.
Yours affectionately, Wa. Allston.

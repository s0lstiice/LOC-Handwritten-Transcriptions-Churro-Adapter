Climate produces degrees of sensibility,
music on an English & Italian audience
would produce very different effects. when
there is most sensibility the true art must be
most profitable. Climate of India by
innocorating the constitution prepares it for
those engagements of an effeminaté and
luxurious character which is known to
prevail there. Their poetry & music are
principally of that character. Love is
the general theme of their poetry; Calid-
as Sacontala is a happy specimen of it.
The Greeks of Asia Minor in their poetry
and music are examples of the influence of their a relaxing climate. The
Lydian measure of music was remarkable
for its soft effeminacy; Dryden happily
alludes to it in Alexander's feast.
Society gives in Lydian measures
from the boot to the top of the head.

New York Sept. 26. 1836.
My Dear Susan,
I have got the 25 dollars for you
which you asked for your clothing this write,
but am waiting for a safe and convenient oppor-
tunity to send it to you. I have just been to Mr.
Bushrod Bircher store but not finding him in
town I shall try again tomorrow, and then de-
posit it with him for you: this will perhaps
be the best way. — I paid him the other
day your bill for Mr. Birch. —
Mr. Jarleton was here on Saturday, she
seemed quite disappointed in not seeing you,
she desired her love to you. She has gone to Alabama
by land. Give my love to your brothers. How do
they do? Tell Charles he must write me and
let me know how he passed the vacation and
how little Timley is?
Remember me to Aunt Salisbury & Cousins
and all New Haven friends. — I am in great haste
but as ever Your Affectionate
Sister
Sam. J. B. Mosher
I find I have begun this letter at
the end.

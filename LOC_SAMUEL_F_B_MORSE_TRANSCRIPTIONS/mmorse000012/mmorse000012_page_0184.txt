Spindletop Jan. 13th
Dear Brother,
Mr. Alfred Vail is just going in to N.York and will
return on Monday morning; The machinery is at length complete
and we have thrown it to the Missouri people with great
delight. It is the talk of all the people round, and the principal
inhabitants of Newark made a special expedition on Friday to
see it. The success is complete. We have tried the experiment of
sending a pretty full letter, which I got up from the members given
me, transmitted through two miles of wire and deciphered with but
a single unimportant error. — I am staying only to perfect a
modification of my fortune, and hope to see you on Saturday or
on the earliest on Wednesday when I shall tell you about it.
The matter looks well now, and I desire to feel grateful to him
who gives success, and he always surprised for any disappointment
which he in Infinite wisdom may have in store.—
I long to hear also about your doing in the upper story.
give my regards to them all. Love to Richard and family.
Your great brother & wife Brotten
Sam. P. Brotten

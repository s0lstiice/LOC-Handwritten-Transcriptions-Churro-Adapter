Mr. Morde
Dear Sir
Your kind letter was received a short time since with much pleasure, and
allow me in the first place to thank you for the Books which you have kindly enough to send me
I have read it very attentively, and am much pleased with it I am indeed trying to learn domestic
Economy which if the present state of affairs continues will be very necessary to me, as but little dependance
can be placed upon wealth, which we daily see to many ruined by unprovided and unlooked for calamities – It has come very home to me my
Father being a business man, and hearing constantly of the distress of the Mercantile Community; I only
have been sad and sick at heart ever since I came from New York – Pa became so worried that he made
himself sick, and was nearly convinced that he would not live when Mother was taken quite sick which so
alarmed him that he forgot his independence and was very shortly afterwards – he is now quite
recovered and I am sending him a letter to him which I hope will give him some satisfaction

Painting like her sister arts Poetry and Music has
for its end the production of intellectual pleasure;
instruction is doubtless principal doubled with pleasure, and it is an
object inferior to that of giving pleasure; Painting is
capable of giving instruction in a less degree than Poetry
and in a greater degree than Music. J. Beattie draws the
conclusion that "the end of Poetry is to please," and adds "verses
"if pleasing may be poetical, though they convey little or no
"instruction, but verses whose sole merit is that they convey
"instruction are not poetical." If it is the object of Painting
to please, the question naturally occurs, whom to please is it every body? This we know is impossible; is it the

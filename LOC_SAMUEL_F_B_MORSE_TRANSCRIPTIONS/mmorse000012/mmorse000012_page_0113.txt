My dear Sam Adams
Dear Sir,
I have the honor to acknowledge your receipt of
your letter of the 12th ult. and to inform you that
the General Court of the Commonwealth of
Massachusetts, assembled at Boston, on the
1st instant, in obedience to the order of the
last session of the General Court, appointed
the 1st of this month, for the purpose of
appointing a committee to meet with the
President of the United States, to consider
the measures of the United States in relation
to the colonies, and to report to the General
Court, the result of their conference, and to
recommend such measures as they might
deem advisable to the General Court.
The committee appointed to meet with
the President of the United States, and to
report to the General Court, are as follows:
Samuel Adams, President of the Common-
wealth of Massachusetts, and Governor of
the United States.
John Adams, Judge of the Court of
Appeals, in the United States.
Thomas Hancock, Judge of the Court of
Appeals, in the United States.
Samuel Eliot, Judge of the Court of
Appeals, in the United States.
Samuel Sewall, Judge of the Court of
Appeals, in the United States.

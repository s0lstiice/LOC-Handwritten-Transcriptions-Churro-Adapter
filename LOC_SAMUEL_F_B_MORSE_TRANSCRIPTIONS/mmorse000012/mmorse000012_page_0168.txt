Nov. 13. 1837. At York to Vail, I announced to Vail an experiment
with ten miles of wire perfectly satisfactory, and also
Answer him respecting an inquiry whether an Ex-
periment could not be made at Spindwell.
I say "With respect to an exper at Spindwell
" Dr Gale thinks there will be no difficulty in
" transporting a couple of miles of wire wound
" off on one of the other peeks, with this we could
" perform all that is necessary to show the
" efficiency of the Polez. and the Dr is willing to
" accompany me out -- so the new room may
" be prepared, if you think best and we will talk
" from your quarters to your brother house. I can
" bring out the dictionary when I come."

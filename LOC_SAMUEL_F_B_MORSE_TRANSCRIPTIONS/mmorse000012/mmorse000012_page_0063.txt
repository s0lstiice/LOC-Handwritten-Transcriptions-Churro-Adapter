There are at Pittsburgh some persons who know Clavering
very well, and who probably are able to give you some
interesting information concerning him. I mention
particularly a German apothecary, I believe his name
is Braun though I do not feel quite sure. The person
I mean may however be easily ascertained by applying
to Mr Leo Wolf, a young merchant at Pittsburgh
who during my short residence in that town introduced
me to him for the purpose of giving me the information
I desired concerning Clavering. You have probably
acquaintances in Pittsburgh who could easily collect
what information may be obtained there.

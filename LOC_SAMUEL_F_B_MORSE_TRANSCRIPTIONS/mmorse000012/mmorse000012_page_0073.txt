Mr. Mason. N.York Sept 15th 1836.
Dear lady,
Please to inform the bearer of this letter to the
questions he will ask you concerning me and likewise all the
knowledge of Lovet Thomson and Jennet Kitch, for I am
sure you can tell him all, and it is very curious that he
denies it, but he did own he was in Montreal at the time. If
you do not know all the particulars to tell them ask Mr. Lamb and
Mr Simonds and they will tell you every word for they all know
and your name shall never be mentioned. From your mo Ob. Gunn
England to Mr. Dickinson May Sept. 16th 1836.
Francis M. Parkinson

Copy of Pencil Memorandum, on back of Paper containing my Signals
which were handed in to Att'y Genl. Si. J. Campbell.
1st For the Type
2nd The mode of placing them at one place to determine the character
of the Sign written at a distant place.
3rd The mode of setting in motion & of stopping at pleasure
the recording of signals at any distant place without the intervention
of a second person at either extremity of the Telegraph
4th The adaptation of Clock Machinery to the moving instrument
to regulate its motion
5th The adaptation of mechanism to the use of a Single circuit.
6th The system of Signs or Characters representing
letters words and figures.
I abandon all claim to Machinery of which the several
parts have been described in any publication in Great Britain.
I abandon all claim to use magnetic needles at any point for
purposes of writing signs sounding alarms or detecting breaks
of the wires.
Copy of part in ink on back of P.
Specimen of new system of Signs, for the inspection only of the
Attorney General of her Majesty Sir John Campbell.
The subject of this memorandum was to enable me in the
second hearing before the Att'y Genl. to test how far my opponents
were acquainted with my invention as they pretended from
any publication that had been made. They could not indeed not
answer them. The abandonment part was advised by Mr. Smith
The first was perfectly a safe one for no Machinery had been
described.
The second, I demanded at lest it might seem at least to give leave to
use my signals, the invention for which I had always claimed, I
made no such offer, nor was there occasion for it for the Att'y Genl.
divided against me from other causes, best known to himself.
S.B.B.Morse
Pokepsui March 13th 1848.

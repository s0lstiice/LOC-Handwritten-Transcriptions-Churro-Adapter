Since my return,
I have been very busily engaged in finishing
the machine, and have now got it so far com-
pleted, as to assure you of its being sent to the
City this week. I have been entirely "non plus" with
the battery, and how to remedy the evil I know
not. On my return, I procured more Sulphuric -- but
found that, although the action of the solution
on the plate was as good as ever -- that is, that
the Sulphate of Copper was as readily decomposed,
yet, the Magnetic effect was not so great, or which
is the same thing, the electric effect -- I washed the
plate, thoroughly cleaning them several times, and
renewed the solution, with no better effect -- then
I tried Sulphuric acid, both to try the effect of it
upon the magnet, and also to clean the copper
plates, but without success. -- There was however
on every trial a feeble effect upon the magnet, and
a very small spark visible on breaking the circuit.
After spending two days in this way, and using
almost 3 lbs of Sulphur -- I resolved to send it together
with the Machine to you, that you might with
the assistance of Dr. Gale discover the cause, and ap-
ply the remedy -- After all my experience of two days
the only cause I can assign for its acting thus, is that
the copper plate is covered with a minute film of copper
which I cannot get off. -- Perhaps it may be some
thing else -- Our large battery never served me this
time, and I am afraid it will not do so again. I am
your very obliged servant
Samuel F. B. Morse

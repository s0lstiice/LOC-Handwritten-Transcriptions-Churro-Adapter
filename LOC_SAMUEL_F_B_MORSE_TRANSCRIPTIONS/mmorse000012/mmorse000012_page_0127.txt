Sant F. B. Morse Exp
New York
New York June 19th 1837
My dear Sir
Your letter of the 14 inst. enclosing Fifty six
dollars, came safely to hand, yesterday
I have ascertain that the Stuart family have their
pictures by the father to dispose of One the head of an Irish Harpist
which they sent, I think, at $300 - another, the head of a lady $150 - and
a third, a female head, a which but little progress had been made $6000
This last is the only one that comes near the item in hand - Jane thinks
it would be very valuable to an Artist - shall I undertake this &
send it for your Academy? or shall I request Jane to paint a
picture or two?
I have a very fine copy by her of the "Studios
by Hendyke" from the Venetian collection, and also a small copy of the
Magdela by Corregio. I know she would consider herself well
paid, for two similar copies, with the dress you omitted.
I shall take great pleasure in attending to your wishes
in this matter & please let me have them soon.
Jane has had no orders lately. My pictures are her last,
& they were sent home full two months since
With respect esteem
Yours
It's
H S Potter

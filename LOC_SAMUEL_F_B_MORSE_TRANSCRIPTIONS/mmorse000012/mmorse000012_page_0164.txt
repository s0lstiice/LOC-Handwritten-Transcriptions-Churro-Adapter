New York 3 Nov. 1837
Dear Sir
Mr George Deas has come to this city for the
purposes of studying the beautiful art to which you have
devoted yourself. He wishes as is natural, for one acquaintance
with one who is so high in the profession as you are, & whose
counsel would be so valuable. Will you allow me
to solicit for him for your assistance & advice. I have
not the pleasure of his acquaintance, but several of
his family are the friends of mine, and my Sisters
as well as myself will feel obliged by your attention
to this request.
With esteem & regard
Your truly
Peter Augustus Cay
Samuel F. B. Morse Esq.

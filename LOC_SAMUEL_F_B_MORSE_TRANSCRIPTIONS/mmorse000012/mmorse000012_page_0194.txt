New York July 21st 1838.
Dear Sir:
Your long silence with regard to my
letter to you, dated in May, but respecting the
National Academy of Design in connection
with myself, has been a matter of much concern
to me. I have been waiting patiently, though
anxiously, for many months, and it now seems
that you must altogether have forgotten it or
have decided to give me no answer. If the
former, I hope this note will prove salutary,
in reminding you of an answer naturally
expected; if the latter, you will much oblige me
by affording me of such conclusion.
Yours very respectfully
S. D. B. Morff Layton
37 Fisherman's B.

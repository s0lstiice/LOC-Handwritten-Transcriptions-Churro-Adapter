When the article alluded to appeared in the
Mirror, I had not made up my mind on the subject

accept more than a commission for one picture,
should I conclude in the affirmative; and that I had
formed this impression (as is well known to my friends

Congress: that I did not send my answer to the
subject down to the Committee until the third of this
month [February]; and that, in that answer I have
altogether declined the proposed Commission:
that I did not rectify the article at the time it
first appeared, because I could not have done so

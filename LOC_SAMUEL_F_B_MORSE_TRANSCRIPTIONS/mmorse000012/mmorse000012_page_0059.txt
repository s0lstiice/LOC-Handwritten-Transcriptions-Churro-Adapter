to secure New York to Bellor in order to procure a publisher for the same. At this
request being without the means of defraying
the travelling expenses, I lent him 30 Dollars
which was as much as my circumstances would
allow me, and which, together with his Boarding,
he intended to return as soon as possible.
The whole account would amount to
$75.00. This I might receive if you would
please to allow me of blanching work
when printed as many copies as would be
worth that sum. Since my circumstances
will not allow to disregard expenses of this
nature, and since, there is in my opinion,
the best prospect for a quick and extensive sale of the Book, I can hardly
be in danger of being misunderstood in
this matter.
I remain respectfully yours
Resident Friend
D. Hammeren
To F. Bellor
Professor in the
City of the York.

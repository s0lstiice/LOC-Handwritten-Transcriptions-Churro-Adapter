Spedwell Oct 14th 1834 Saturday evening
Prof. More
Dear Sir I received yours of
the 11th in the 10th and am much pleased to learn of the
succsess of a contract if our water experiment succeeds I presume nothing can be done in that way until we have made a trial. I regret much that you have as myself been disappointed in not obtaining the wine. I was fearful the reels would not be finished in time for you, and myself to use since the receipt of your favour, to retain the two that were finished until the remaining two were complete, which is now the case, and will be sent to you on Monday with direction for them to be left in the case of the Governor.
I have some directions to give you in regard to opening the boxes. You will find on the sides of the top cover four screens, two on each side by which the uppermost cover is fastened.
When they are taken out you can lift the top off, and you will find the reels in order for immediate use. I would suggest to you to have them remain in the lower story of the University (Perhaps you can obtain Prof. Beckers room) as they will be very heavy and difficult to take up and down from this Hall as soon after they are filled with wine. I am very busy with our Machines. I find there is considerable work to do upon them. But we have not got very well with them and I see nothing in the way of completing them next week

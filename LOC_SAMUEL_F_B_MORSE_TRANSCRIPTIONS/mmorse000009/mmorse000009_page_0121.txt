I will let you know if I go this after-
noon, before I close this letter.
2 o'Clock
I have concluded to go this after-
noon to Providence, and shall leave this
hot city at 3 o'Clock. I think I can
employ myself as profitably where I am
going, as elsewhere; I have no time for
more words: good bye, love to all,
Yr Aff Son Finley.

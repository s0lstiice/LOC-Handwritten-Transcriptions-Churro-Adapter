New Haven Nov 12
1846
My Dear Papa
I want to see you very much.
When are you coming home? Mr & Mrs
Converse are going to New York day after
tomorrow, and they will take this little
letter along with Grandfather's to
you. I did not write this myself
but Cousin writes it for me. You must
come soon, very soon, to see us. Grandma
says you must come to take a Thanks-
giving dinner with us, we expect Mr-
Penny, mom & Cushing - Do come Papa -
Dear Papa said (when he was at home) that
I must write him a letter, & now I
am trying to dictate one. - Henry
& Finley are well. - Dear grandmama's eyes
are sore be - I hope they will be quite
well soon. Jason does his work pretty
good. I have made a couple of little
needle books. & I shall try to make
you a purse. Please to give my love
to Uncle Edwards, Richard & Charles
I hope they will come to see me soon

Isakkin Court
Levocett oct-13, 1824.
Dear Sir there has been some communication
between your self and our Revd. Pastor
Mr Colburn Respecting my son Erastus Field
studying with you for the purpose of
Learning the art of Painting, your
last letter informs us that if my son
is in N.york by the Middle of Novr
it will be in season, on account of
the Coldness of the season, if your
convenience would admit of his being
there sooner, but if not, by the love
of Providence he will be there by
that Time, I expect he will go from
this Place to Albany & then down the
North river until the river should be
frozen you will determine whether it
will be necessary for you to write
again or not. your obedient &
Humble Servt
Erastus Field
Mr Samuel H. Mann
N. Heavens
97819 Cant

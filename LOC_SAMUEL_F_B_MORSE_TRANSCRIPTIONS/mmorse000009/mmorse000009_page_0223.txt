Thursday noon Aug. 12. 1825
My Dear Parents,
I write a line just to say that I called
the day before yesterday on Uncle Arthur, I saw him
but he looked very ill, he had just been tapped
by Dr. Post, and many gallons of water taken from
him, he said he felt better; I called to-day and
learn that Dr. Post has given it as his opinion
that he cannot live the day out; he may live a
few days but the Dr. says he cannot survive; I don't
know whether mother or you can leave home
at this time but if you can I know it would be
a great gratification to them at Mrs. Stout's, I am
in hopes you will get this so early as to prepare to
come in the boat the even. you receive this. (In day)
I have concluded not to go to N.Haven
so soon as I had intended; I think I shall finish
the picture of the little girl here. — Rip the children
for me, and love to family & friends My Aff Son
In haste
Sam. F. B. Morse

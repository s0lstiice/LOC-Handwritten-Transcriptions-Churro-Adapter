(my 17 June 1824)
Dear Edwds
I wrote you two days since I fail to attend to the business of preparing
the Prospectus of such a Geo f as we can
prepare at leisure, & have a copyright
of 10 p r et. It will, in my opinion,
the most lucrative of any in which we
can engage – I am ready to take the
laboring out in the business – & I can
do it, as my health is now good & shall have
the leisure necessary – Send your ideas
in form as soon as you can, say by the mid-
de of next week, as I expect to go to Hartford
soon, for 10 days or a fortnight to
superintend the printing of the Kit – I have
prepared – Will write by Mr McGuire
as m today – We have a letter from
Lucas in Boston – All well in Boston – going
to Concord Monday –
Love as usual
Y. S. father Morse

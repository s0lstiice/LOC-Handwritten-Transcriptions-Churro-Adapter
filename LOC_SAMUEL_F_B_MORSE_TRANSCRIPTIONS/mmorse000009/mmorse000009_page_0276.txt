I have not written you on account of such a
pressure of business in various ways, that I cannot
find a moment's leisure, I have just got settled
after moving, my No. is now 124 Broadway
corner of Cedar St. opposite City Hotel; I board at
the National Hotel. I am now called away
by 50 people. Rise my dear little ones, I feel
depressed often that I must spend so much of my
time from them; Love to all. —
I will write again soon
Y Affectionate Son as ever
Sam. J. S. Morgan

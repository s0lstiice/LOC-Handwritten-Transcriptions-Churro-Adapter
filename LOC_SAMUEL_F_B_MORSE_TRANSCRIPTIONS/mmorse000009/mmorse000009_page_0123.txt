Concord N.H. July 25th 1824
Dear Parents.
I arrived safely here on Monday evening, and found them all well to my great joy, after 3 weeks of anxious suspense; Lucretia pleaded guilty in not writing to me, it arose from thoughtlessness, I told her that she could calculate the difference between 18 cents and 18 dollars and that a letter costing but 18 Cts postage would probably have saved 18 dollars to me. On the whole, however, I think I shall make it advantageous, by going to Portsmouth where they are anxious to have me home, and it is of little consequence whether I paint in Utica or Portsmouth until Octr which is the season to return to N.York. In N.York I am encouraged to think I shall do well, I had a Portrait (a full length of a child of Mr. Hone's of N.York) engaged to be painted while on the passage to Providence, I shall present it in N.York.
The children are healthy Susan is much sunburnt, and not so pretty as she was, her hair has been cut short and looks like a shoe brush made of a fox's tail, but will look better when it grows longer, and when she is confined to the house by the winter, Charles has the whooping cough very mildly and looks very well.

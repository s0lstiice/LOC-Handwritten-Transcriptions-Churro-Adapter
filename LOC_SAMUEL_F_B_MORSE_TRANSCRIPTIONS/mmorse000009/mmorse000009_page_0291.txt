My Lectures were delivered before the New York Athenaeum
March 20th - 27th - April 3d & 12th - 1826. - in Chapel of Col. College
Prof. James Freeman Dana's Lectures on Electric Magnetism Jan. 11th, 15th, 18th and 22nd - 1827. - The New York American
Jan. 9th 1827. - Prof. Dana also delivered Lectures on
Electricity Feb. 1st, 5th and 8th 1827.
Prof. Dana died on the 15th of April 1827, a short
obituary notice is in N.York American of Monday evening
April 16th 1827. - I was one of his pall bearers. - See also Allen's Biographical Dictionary Article
Dana, James Freeman. -
The N.Y. American of March 19th 1827 only a few weeks
before Prof. Dana's death contains an advertisement of
Lectures on Chemistry to be delivered by him. -
Prof. Dana published "an Epitome of Chemical
Philosophy." [Enquire for it, at Blanchards No. 85 Washington
near Fulton St. up stairs]

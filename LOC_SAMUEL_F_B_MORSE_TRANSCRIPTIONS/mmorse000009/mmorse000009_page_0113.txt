[Jan. 1824]
St. Boat Court July 10th
Monday morning to
New Haven
Dear Family
I am so far on my
way but York - Had it been hospitable
I would stop at St. but I must
be at home soon - I will come up
with Ted - Lucette is very well
& so are the children altho they
are at least Sue has the choosiest
lunch - The grass for on it does
not go as tough as a lion - he goes
every where with every body pretty
black by this I fear nothing -

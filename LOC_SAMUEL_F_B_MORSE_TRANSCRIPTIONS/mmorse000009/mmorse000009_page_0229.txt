Mr S.F.B. Morse
De Sir
I called this day, on Capt Van
Dyke of the ship Crisis, who informed me that all goods
not taken from his vessel, by monday next, would be
sent to the publick store in Broad Street, if therefore
you think best to forward me your bill of lading &
invoice by return of mail, which will reach me on
monday morning, I will then call again and take
the box from the vessel.
Yours Respectfully
I Tracy
N.B. If the things are not taken from the vessel, I suppose they will lie
safely at the publick store till you come in town. Perhaps, however, if they
are bulky articles, you may be charged in this case with double cost &c &c
&c. - Lettress from Richard show that he is doing well. He says he
preaches every Sabbath & often. Hope you have answered Professor
MacLean's letter about the portrait of Dr Hosack. Be attentive
Finley.
S.F. Morse

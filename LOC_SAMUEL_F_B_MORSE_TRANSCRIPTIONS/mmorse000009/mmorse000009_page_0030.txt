Dear Parents,
I open Kelley's letter to say that two letters have just
been received from Richard, from Durham, Syracuse Co. The
victoring 46 and the other 21 subscribers, about half
of whom paid in advance $70, so that there are no
drawbacks on account of risk of collections. His
expenses continue to be trifling. His bill for the last
three or four days he states 80 or 90 cents per lb. 3 for cents
tobacco, 31 cents contribution &c. Let nothing be said
of this on any account, for if I find that it has
been communicated to any one except Ducretia and
to her in our strict confidence, I shall stop writing,
and keep all my bargains business to myself, as perhaps which
perhaps would have been most prudent in the present
case, but I could not.
Your affecte for
Sincerely
Samuel C. More

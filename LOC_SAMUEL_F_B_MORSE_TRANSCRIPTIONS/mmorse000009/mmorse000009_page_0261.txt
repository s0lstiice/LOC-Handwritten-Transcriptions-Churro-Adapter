National Academy of the Arts
of Design
New York June 19 1826
Resolved that a Committee of six be appointed
for the purpose of drafting a Code of Bye Laws
for the regulation of the National academy of the
Arts of Design, and that the same be prepared
and completed, on or before the 1st day in July
1826
Whenceupon the following gentlemen
were duly appointed the said Com
Mr Morse
- Dimick
- Suydam
- Peale
- Haverick
- Towne

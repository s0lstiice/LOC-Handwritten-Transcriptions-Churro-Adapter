There is a cellar and cellar kitchen, 2 rooms 18 by

I shall occupy the house in the following manner;
The kitchen and garret finished room I shall let to a
man and his wife, who shall have them rent free
for taking care of the house, cleaning and washing
the house, attending the door, doing my washing, cooking
my breakfast and tea and occasionally (perhaps
dinner) The lower front room I shall furnish
as my sitting parlour, to receive visitors, and to re-
tire to, after the labors of the day, to study & write, &c.

I have just scratched this in great haste to re-
lieve the minds of my dear family, and will write as
often as I find anything to communicate; I shall go
to the Church in the Representatives chamber this morning
I cannot bear the total disregard of the Sabbath, and of
everything religious which is so apparent all around me
I long for the time to sit down in quiet N.Haven.
I tell father that what he heard with respect to Mr Adams
kindness to an orthodox Chr, cannot be correct, I had a conversa-
tion with Dr. Sevall; he informed me that W. A. owned a pew
in Mr. Baker's Chr and one in the Episcopal Chr, & one in the Unitarian, but he
uniformly attended the latter, and gave his support to that Chr, &
it was well known that he is a thorough Unitarian, no one
here pretends to contradict it, but I will write particularly
on this subject to Father before I leave Washington.
With my dear Susan & Charles for me, and give
my love to all at home, write me and let me know how you
all are; you will have time to write me here once or twice.
In haste but with undiminished affection my
dear wife your husband
Stiles.
Rememberance to all the neighbors & friends

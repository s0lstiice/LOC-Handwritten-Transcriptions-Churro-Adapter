New York Feb 7th 1824 (4)
Dear Parents,
In regard to Richardson & Lord's charge of $93.75
they are undoubtedly in the right if they have credited
you with $1871.50, the whole amount due for copyright
on the 7500 copies of the Atlas. R. & L. accepted my draft
on them for $100 in June last, and charged it to the
Atlas account. If they now credit you with the
whole of what was due on that account, it is
certainly right that they should charge you for what
they paid me. If they had credited you with only half,
it is not right that they should charge you with what
they paid on my half. You doubtless recollect the circumstances under which they accepted my draft. They were unwilling at first to pay any part in cash, on the ground
that there was an unsettled account between you & them, &
that the money which they owed for Atlas ought to be held
by them on account of the balance due by you on that
account. We then wrote to them that we wish'd to keep our accounts
distinct, that I had nothing to do with your accounts, & that it was
not right that my half should be withheld. They accordingly
accepted my draft on them for $100 in favor of Gaylord P.
Tyler, and paid the money to them. - As far as you and
I are concerned this account was settled on the 14th of
Octr. by my paying you the difference between
$100 and my portion of the copyright, which was $62.50
making the difference which I paid you or rather allowed you
in the settlement of the account $37.50.

Gen. N. Edwards
Respected Sir,
It is no small alleviation
to the painful feelings of Mrs. Morse &
myself, occasioned by the departure of our
son, that he goes as a member of your
suite, & is one of your family. Your goodness
& indulging him with these valued
privileges, & the kind manner in which
you have conferred them, are deeply felt
& gratefully acknowledged by us all. I trust,
Sir, you will find the disposition & services
of my son such, as will not disappoint your
expectations. He has had
the advantages of a good & extensive education,
which have been valued & improved
by him. He goes from us with an unimpeached
moral character, the continuance
of which is secured by his Christian
profession, which he has maintained
& adorned for the last five years of his life.

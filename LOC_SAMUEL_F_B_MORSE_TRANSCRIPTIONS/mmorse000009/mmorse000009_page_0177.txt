My affectionately beloved Son,
Mysterious are the ways of Providence.
My heart is in pain & deeply sorrowful while I am
nomme to you the sudden & unexpected death of
your dear & deservedly loved wife. Her decease
proved to be an affliction of the heart, invariable
had it been known - Dr Smith, acting accompany-
ing this will explain all you will desire to know
on this subject - I wrote you yesterday that she
was convalescent - so she then appeared, I notice
Dr pronounced - the wards - about 5 August - day
8.15.15. to have her bed made as usual, was unusually
cheerful & social - spoke of the pleasure of being
with her dear husband in N.Y. for long - stepped
into bed herself fell back with a struggle on her
pillow her eyes were immediately fixed by
paleness of death immediately - on entering her
countermand in 5 minutes more her mortal
life terminated. It happened that just at that
moment I was entering her chamber door with
Charles in my arms to pay her my usual visit
to pray with her - The house met me apprised,
calling for help - your brother the family, our
neighbours, full of the tenderest sympathy & kindness,
& the night hour doctors, thronged the house in a
few minutes every thing was done that could be
done to save her life - But her appointed time had
come & no earthly skill or power could stay the
hand of death. It was the Lord who gave her
to you - the chiefest of all your earthly blessings

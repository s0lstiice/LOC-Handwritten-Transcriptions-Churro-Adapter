Kentford Aug 21. 1824
My dear Son
I have been here since Tuesday
I hope to return to N.Y. on Monday (my birth
day) having completed as far as it can be done,
my work for the Cooks -- It has been, as they
have conducted it, a perplexing business --
but -- I hope all will have a favorable issue.
This hope you know, is ever cherished by
Your father -- Miss Ceder with the three
Miss Matthews, Miss Adams left us for
N.Y. on Monday evening after a very
sant visit -- Your uncle Calvin paid us
a short visit on Saturday -- I may goe
back by the way of your uncle Jonathan,
New Habitation in Vermont -- To Fayette
they was to pass through N.Y. this morning on
his way, rapidly to Boston to be at Court
on Wednesday --
We long much to see you, Lucinda
& the Children -- You must be here as
soon as possible -- Fisher is here -- taking
the custom you w'd have had a
month ago -- There are some waiting for
you -- enough perhaps to take up all the
time you will have to spare -- There
are some you know at N.Y. -- The
fruit is now beginning to be ripe, so
we want you & the Children to enjoy
it.
My health is very good considering how much I am called to do
& to bear -- I have great cause to be
thankful.
Col. Trumbull is here with his
picture, I gave with it to N.Y. next week,
I spent last evening with him at Mr
Wade worths very pleasantly, he enquired
kindly after you -- I can't add. Remember me very
affectionately to my children, & to my father
Walker & the family -- Your affectionate Son
Moore

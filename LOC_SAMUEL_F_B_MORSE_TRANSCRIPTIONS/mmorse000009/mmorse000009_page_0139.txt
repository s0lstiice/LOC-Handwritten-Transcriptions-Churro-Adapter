Should Mr. Field commence his studies with
you, you will find him a young man, of rather retiring,
but amiable, modest deportment, correct in his habits, &
faithful & trusty in his business.
He is possessed of rather a slender constitution, &
perhaps, there may be some days that he will not be able
to attend to business.
Should we conclude to come, perhaps you may
wish to state more definitely than in your last letter,
the time you would like to have him arrive at N.Y.
& give some directions where he can find you after
he arrives & any further particulars, that you may think
of service to him &c.
Please to state also whether you wish for
a definite answer &c if so, where you wish it to be directed.
With respect, Sir,
Yrs. Mts: to yours, Servt.
Genus Calhoun.
Mr. Sam'l F. D. More.

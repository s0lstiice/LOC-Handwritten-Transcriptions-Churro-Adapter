New York June 11. 1825.
My Dear Parents,
I write a line by Pick, who
goes to day to N-Haven; I am well as usual
and very busy, at least as much as the
weather will permit; I find I have not the
strength I had before I painted the Congr's
Hall, but still I think I am better than I
have been for a long time.— I long to
see my dear little ones, how do they do? Does
Susan improve, and does Charles continue
docile, and pleasant dispositioned? I shall
come and see them as soon as I can leave
my painting with advantage which I can
not possibly now do? As to sending money
I have been disappointed in receiving money
from those who owe me, or I should have

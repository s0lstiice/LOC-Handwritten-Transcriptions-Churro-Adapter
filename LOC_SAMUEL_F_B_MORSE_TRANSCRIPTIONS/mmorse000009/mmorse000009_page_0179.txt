I expect this will reach you on Saturday, the day after the one we have appointed
for the funeral, when you will have been in Washington a week, & hope will have
made such progress in your business as that you will soon be able to return & bring Nancy
with you – Cushing is to write her today to be ready for you – You had however, another
line, when I may expect you – You need not hurry home – nothing here requires it – we
are all well & every thing will be taken good care of. Give yourself no concern on that
act – Finish your business as well as you will be able to do it, after receiving this sad
news – Regd what David did on the death of Child – 2 Sam 12. 15. to 24th – let his example
instruct you on this occasion –
I have written your father Walker &c by letters at New York – the latter to attend the
funeral on Friday –
I enclose this to our worthy friend Genl. Renfroalen to whom you will hand your reply
to it – As I have not time to write him, give my regards to him – & thank him for his communication
reel? this morning –
All join in tenderest sympathies & love for you, with your afflicted & affectionate
father
Ed: Morre

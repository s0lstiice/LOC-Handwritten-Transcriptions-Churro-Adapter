hypocrisy, ignorance, stupidity, folly united with
pride, vainness & meanness, to be cheated by almost
every man you meet, to eat & drink, not much
good things as you have been used to, but such
as you can find, to meet continually with
little vexations, & yet to go through the
country contented, cheerful, happy & without
any difficulty worth naming. But now formidable as some of these things may look, on
paper, one may still get along very well
by taking things quietly, watching favorable
opportunities & making the best of everything.
And when a journey is over it makes very
little difference whether it was performed in
a steam-boat or an Egyptian land-jay, on a
stage-coach or on a mule, whether one slept
in a palace or under a broach, on a soft feather-
bed, with clean sheets, & an elegant counterpane
& curtain or with only a coarse blanket spread
on the ground, whether you have dined at

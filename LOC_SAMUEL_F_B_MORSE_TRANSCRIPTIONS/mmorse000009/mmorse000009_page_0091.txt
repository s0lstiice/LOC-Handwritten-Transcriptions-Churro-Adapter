Washington April 23. 1824
Friday
My Dearest Wife,
I have seen the Pres't of U.S. and
the Sec'y of State and had conversations with them
on the subject of the detention of the legation; the
President told me explicitly that there would be a
delay of 5 or 6 weeks at least, and perhaps of
some months; it was intimated to me by high
authority that in all probability it might be thongh
necessary to send the Ship of Legation without
the Minister for the present, in that case, it is
not improbable we may sail from New York,
Mr Mason I know disliked the Proposal so much
that he had almost determined to go down the
Ohio & Mississippi to N Orleans in order to avoid
being on board longer than possible, The Sec'y of
the Navy told me that a Hood of War would have
been at Mr Edw's command if he had wished it
but as he wrote the vessel to come up to the city
of N Orleans, no other than the one chosen

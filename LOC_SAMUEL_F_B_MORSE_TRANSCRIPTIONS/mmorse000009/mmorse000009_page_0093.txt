The Pres.t sent a Message this morning to the House of R.
informing them that he had sent a Messenger to Mr. Calhoun
to delay his departure till further orders. The Messengers
from the Pres. and House of R. left town this morning
on their errand. — This business has caused a great
excitement here, some of the members told me that
nothing had occurred for many years which had excited
so much feeling.

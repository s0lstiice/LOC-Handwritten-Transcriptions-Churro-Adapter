When you can, let me have a line from
you. Mrs Cogdell is also anxious to hear
that I have heard from you, myself
direct. -- We have both been blessed
as to health & may with gratitude
say with every other comfort also, by
our descent from Mrs. -- if I cannot
obtain independent confidence by
my daily confinement and labour
at least -- enough to support us in
our situation, by excellent diligence
Should I go to the North this Summer
I shall hope to see you either in
New York or perhaps N. Haven.
Heaven bless your presence again.
Yr truly John S. Cogdell
22 Apr. 1826.

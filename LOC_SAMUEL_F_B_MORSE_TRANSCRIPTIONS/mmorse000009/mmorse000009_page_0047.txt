3. Where can I join your Suite?
March 11th 1824.
Copy of 2d letter.
Since writing you in addition to what I wrote in my letter
of yesterday, this thought has occurred to me;
On the supposition that you have decided to
sail from N-Orleans a national ship will of
course be ordered there to secieve you, perhaps
from N-York, or at least from some northern
port; if this should be the case, would it not
be possible for me to obtain leave, as one of
your suite, to join you at N-Orleans in
this ship. If an arrangement of this kind can
be made without difficulty
I leave this subject with you, Sir, persuaded from
your kindness thus far, that all will be done
for me in your power to relieve me. I am
in my behalf, with the government to promote
my views. I cannot close without
repeating my thanks for your polite
offers, & begging you to be assured of
the high respect and esteem of
your humble servant

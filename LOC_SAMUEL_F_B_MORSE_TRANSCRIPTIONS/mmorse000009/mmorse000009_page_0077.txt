New York April 15th 1844
Dear Sidney,
You will probably arrive in N.O.
at the most delightful part of the season. May be
frequently all you are perfectly healthy, & at this
time it is not unusual for a doctor not to have
a single patient, & truly not a sufferer. I was sorry
the girls did not carry longer & blame myself
for not urging it. But you so took me by surprise
that I had not my usual coolness and sobriety.
Lucetta & the children & you & Mr. Wills go on
in June about the 6th I best wishes to her to
die - Susan has gone & I think will come
to N.Y. and we shall start from there in a
boat from St. H. besides Uncle Edgar will never
consent to let little Sue go off without seeing her
He said he'd rather see her than all the rest of you
This will be pleasant for Lucetta & will gratify her
friends here - Smith will make a very excellent
likeness - He has improved it wonderfully, & it
will be equal to those of Durand's & perfectly
true. I shall send you one to St. Orleans to carry
to Mexico. I may send you a present of a
few handkerchiefs finished & colored at Staten
Islands, as a present for my friend Mr. Gann to
them

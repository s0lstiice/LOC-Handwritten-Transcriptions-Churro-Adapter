now and I think in very good health. Sister Susan is as
hearty as ever, and will probably be married this fall, perhaps
while I am here, there is now no doubt of her engagement
to Mr. Pickering, they will live at Greenland near Ports-
mouth where Mr. P. will build him a handsome house, and
when he owns considerable property, he is considered a
wealthy man, and is certainly a very amiable, pleasant
man. I do not feel very well to-day, having a little of a
sore throat, and an inclination to diarrhoea; but hope I shall
be well to-morrow. I am looking for something to do
here, which if I find I shall stay a few days, if not, I shall
go to Portsmouth and Paint a while there and stay in this
region until it is time to return to N.York which I shall
probably do with Lucretia & the children by the way of
Albany. Please write to me in Concord and the letter
will reach me if I am in Portsmouth. All send a great deal of love in which I join
Yr Aff-son Finley

New York April 19th 1825.
My Dear Parents,
Your letter to Edw. & Rich and the
Bundle arrived safely by Miss Whittlesby; she left no
word where she stays in New-York so I cannot call on
her, — I don't understand mothers short and touchy
letter, as far as it concerns me. I wish mother would
not write when she is in such a mood, it is not right.
As to my coming to New Haven on the 25th inst.
I wrote the College Committee that it was absolutely im-
possible, and sent a verbal message to you by Mr. Joe-
clyn to the same effect, I am therefore at a loss to know
what you mean when you say that the Senior Class &
Mr. Silliman, and all will be disappointed if I don't
come, and that you will expect me certainly in
Saturday's boat; Now I understand my own business
best, and if they take the ground of being offended or
put out because I can't do impossibilities, then they may
find some one else to paint the picture, for my business
here is worth double what that picture will be to me in
New Haven, and if they take that ground, after what I have
stated to them, I shall have nothing to do with the thing.

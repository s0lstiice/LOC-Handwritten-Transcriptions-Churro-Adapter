Dr. Matthews and send love to little Susan &c.
The D was married on Monday night as was
expected -- I hope to send the money to pay
my bills soon, as soon as I can get a little
arranged and feel at home, which I am doing
fast, -- How are the little ones, has dear little Tim
ley yet broken out with the measles, I shall feel anxious
to hear from them till I know they have
gone through with that disease. -- Keep them
all for me, and do not let Susan run wild
without discipline and instruction; I do hope
Miss Peters will take her. --
In haste but with affection as ever yr Son
Sam: F: B: Morse

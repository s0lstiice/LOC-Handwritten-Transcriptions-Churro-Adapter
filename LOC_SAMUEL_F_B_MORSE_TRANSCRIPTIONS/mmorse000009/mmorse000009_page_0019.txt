Hon. S. Van Rensselaer,
Dear Sir,
The failure of my Picture of the
House of Representatives, to produce me
any thing but empty reputation, has led
me for some time to think of some enterprize
to retrieve my lost property, and enable
me to remain in the bosom of my beloved family; I had thought of a
visit to Mexico, and my conversations
with Mr. Poinsett when I had the pleasure
of meeting him in Albany, confirmed my
opinion of the eligibility of such a visit.
From the failure above mentioned I
have not even the means of undertaking
such an enterprize; I percieve by the late
Message of the Pres. that an embassy is
soon to be formed for that country;
Shall I ask the favor of your influence
with the Pres. or with the minister who
may be appointed, in procuring me some situation in the legation, that shall enable
me to accompany it free of expence and
under its protection, I seek nothing high

New-York July 24th 1824.
Saturday.
My Dear Parents.
I have rec'd no letters
from Lucretia since the 30th of last month
nor any news of her, except in a letter from
Susan Walker, which mentions that Lucretia
did not enjoy good health, but hoped that
"the jaunt to Portsmouth might do her good,"
this makes it more than three weeks since I
have had any intelligence of her; I have
nothing to do at present here, every body
leaving town and the weather excessively hot: I
have thought I would take my Painting ap-
paratus, and jump on board the Steamboat for
Providence this afternoon, go immediately to
Concord; if all are well, I can go to Albany,
or Portsmouth and pursue my profession, if

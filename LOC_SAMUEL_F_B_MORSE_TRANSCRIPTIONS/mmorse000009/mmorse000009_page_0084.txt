Washington April 20th 1824
My Dear Wife,
I am not yet relieved from anxiety on the
subject about which I wrote yesterday, but shall
without doubt know definitely to-morrow; it makes
a great noise here, and I presume will cause much
excitement through the country; a motion was made
in the House this morning that the Pres.t be requested
to prevent Mr. Edwards from leaving the country,
until the issue of this investigation was known; but
it was lost by a considerable majority; a committee
has been appointed to consider Mr. Edwards Memorial
which committee meet to-morrow morning at 10 o'clock
so that I shall know something definite to-morrow
and will write you again; from the complexion of
the Committee and the rejection of the motion to
stop Mr. Edwards I augur favorably to my not being
detained but a few days longer; Write to me
on the receipt of this, under cover to Hon. Henry W.
Dwight, and I shall probably receive it before I
leave here. — I am at lodgings at 6 doll. p.r
week in a Congressional mess of 4. 2 Kentucky
and 2 Ohio members. — I don't know that I

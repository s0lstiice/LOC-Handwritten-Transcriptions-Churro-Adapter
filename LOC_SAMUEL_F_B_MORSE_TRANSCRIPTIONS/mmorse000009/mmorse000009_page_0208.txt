When I see you which will probably be in a week from
next Tuesday night I will tell you more particularly.
I shall be able to stay with you but 2 or 3 days
for my business purposes. — I want several things from home.—
One of the darkest points in
my future prospects is the necessity of being absent so
much from my dear children, I want them round
me, but I do not see how it can be accomplished, a cloud
hangs over me in regard to them, but I will still trust a
wise and just God, I have no reason to distrust him,
he will make my way clear, and in this persuasion I
can patiently wait; his will be done — Present duty
seems plain, every thing in my profession here looks bright,
and it is clearly my duty to stay here at present. —
I am greatly fatigued with walking and
standing and it is growing late, so that I must close.
Edw. and Rich. are in the same confusion with
myself. You must not expect to hear from them
often for they have much to perplex them. — Little
Susan is well and a good girl, and is a great comfort
to me, she has fewer visits from Humpee than formerly;
the children are well; I shall bring Susan with me. —

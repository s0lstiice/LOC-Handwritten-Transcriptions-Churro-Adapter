another time when I can explain more fully than in a letter
my views on this subject, In the mean time, my path is
plain I am engaged in Painting with a view to raise the
means; Providence may order things otherwise, his will be
done! — Give my love to Cousin Elizabeth, I esteem her
very much, I think she will make a fine woman; I
dined with her sister cousin Sarah the other day who
is staying with Mrs. Stout as is also cousin Mary Brown,
Mrs Sands and Mr. Jacob Stout from Watertford were also
present so that we had quite a family of cousins. —
Ring my dear children for me many times, till
Susan that Papa expects to find her very much improved
in every thing that is good, and that he will find no black
marks in the account. — Little Charles must learn to
read and be very good and not give Grandmama & Nancy
or Cousin any trouble; and dear little Finley must learn to talk
and be a good boy, I hope his health is entirely recovered, again
Ring them for me. — I forgot to mention that the Painting room
tile a bad penny is returned again upon me, Mrs Brown & Mrs Skinner
came and placed for Mrs Whelbley that she was not able to build and that it would be an act
of great Kindness if I would release her from her engagement, and I have accordingly done so
Love to Nancys family & neighbors. Your affectionate Son ever Finley

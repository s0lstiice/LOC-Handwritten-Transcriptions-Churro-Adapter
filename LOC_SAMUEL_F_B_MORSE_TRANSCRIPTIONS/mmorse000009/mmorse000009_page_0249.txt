My Dear Parents
In answer to yours which I received this morning, I have but a moment to say one word by Mr. Joeclyn. In regard to the sum for board &c. (as you mentioned in your letter of my children; I have talked with Edw. &c and we think $300 a year will be about right; in our arrangements on this subject when I was at home we agreed on $230. which you thought was not sufficient; I have no means to make a calculation, but I think I can afford $300. if you think this right please reduce my note that amount, on the 7th of Feb'y which will be one year that they have been motherless.— As to the tax bill to W. Scott I paid it out of monies borrowed of Edwards at the time.
My dear children, and give much love to Mother &c Nancy I am glad to hear they are getting better, I long to pay a short visit home but dare not think of it now every moment is occupied.— I was never in better health. I am here but with affection as ever
Love to Richard, and regards to neighbors.
Yr Son
C H D. Mosse
1825.

immediately to prevent any difficulties, but I have not
the means and will therefore wait until the Apollo
is finished. I can then determine more decidedly;
I hope Mr. Augur will expedite the business and let
me know when I can come to superintend the finishing
of it. — Suppose I should come on Friday, shall
I come and will he have the head in such a state that
I can direct about it to advantage? I can leave my
room in the case of a very clever young man who
is well recommended and who wishes to be a pupil
of mine, he has kindly offered to take charge of it,
and receive any visitors that may come, (which
will not take up much of his time, if my experience
hitherto is any criterion,) so that I could spend a few
days in N. Haven and not be missed at all from the
city. — I want some mending up, my cloak, two coats,
some stockings, and 3 pair of boots all want more or less
patching or renewing. — Edw. D. says that he will give me
2 dollars if I will count the Geographical in sheets for him, which
are in the lumber room at N. Haven, this will dispense two things
of the expense to N. Haven. — If you say "come," write
me by the return of mail that I may make any arrange-
ments in season, I could not stay more than 3 or 4 days

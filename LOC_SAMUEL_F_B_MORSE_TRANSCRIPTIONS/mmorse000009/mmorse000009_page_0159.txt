New York Decr. 1. 1824.—
Dear Father,
On the subject of your letter I would say that Mr. M. Nutt might take the picture for 4 months from this date, allowing me one fourth part of its exhibition; but I must expressly and imperiously enjoin it on him and all who know the fact not to mention on any account whatever my being the painter of it; this I must solemnly promise.— The picture is in the Garrett of the house I believe.
As to sending $50. in a fortnight I can no more admit it than $50.000, my picture are not painted yet, nor shall I be at the receipt of any money under 6 weeks at least, indeed I have borrowed more than 50 dollars already to pay common every day expenses, and shall have to borrow more before I receive any of my own, you must not at all depend on me, for I cannot promise any thing.
In great haste but with much love to all, Lucretia children, mother &c. Your aff. son
Sam. P. B. More

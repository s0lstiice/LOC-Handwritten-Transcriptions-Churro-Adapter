Hon. H. W. Edwards, New York Jan. 16th 1824:
Dear Sir,
Shall I ask the favor of a word in my
behalf to the Pres'd of the U. States, or to the
Minister who may be appointed to Mexico, that
I may be in the family of the minister, I should
visit the appointment of Private Sec'y to the
Minister if it can be obtained, and your knowledge
of me would enable you to render me very essential service by your recommendations, for
which I shall feel under the greatest obligations.
As my Picture of Congress Wall contains
the portraits of those who voted the first acknowledgment of the Independence of the Spanish
Colonies, I am induced by this circumstance to
expect a favorable reception for it in that
country, and a remuneration which has thus
far been denied me in my time.
With the highest respect & esteem
Y. Mo: Ob: hum: serv:
Sam'l F. B: Morse

Copy March 10th 1824.
Hon. N. E. Davis.
Dear Sir,
I return you many thanks
for your polite favor of the 6th and
for your kindness in offering me a place in
the legation.
Under any other circumstances than
those in which I am unfortunately placed
I should esteem your polite offer as in the
highest degree eligible, and possessing me
the advantages which I could wish, and still
conceive the advantages for to overbalance
the disadvantages that I accept it with
many thanks.
I was under the impression that
the government bore the expenses of
the legation to & from the United States
in your letter that as you had not yet confirmed
with the Pres. & Secy of State you were unable to say what facilities you would be authorized to afford me;
The only drawback to my gratification
is the probable expense which I may incur; and this may probably be removed

New York July 15th 1824.
Dear Parents, I have no letters from Lucretia
except the one brought by Charles, which makes it that
there are 15 days without any news from her, I put
her letter to you in the Office which you have probably got by this time; I endorsed on the back that
Lucretia was probably in Portsmouth, she had though
of going with Mr Pickering Susan's beau to visit
her friends for a few days in Portsmouth, this may
account for her not answering my letter as she had
not probably received it. We are all well. I am
coming on successfully with Mr Lawrence portrait;
I find from Lucretia's letter that there
is a route from Concord to Albany lately established
fare 5 dollars, from Albany, here, 2 dollars, & 3
from here to N Haven, makes it 10 dollars from Con-
cord to N Haven, this is the cheapest route by one half
then, to return; and perhaps I shall go for them from
Albany if I should go to Utica & Coopers town.

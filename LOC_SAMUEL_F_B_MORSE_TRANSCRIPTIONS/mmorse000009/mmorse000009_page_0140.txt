Am? Sept 30th 1824
Query 1st Am. Portrait Painting.
2d Yes.
3d Just as we can agree at the end of any given time, if his
services are such to me that I can afford a compensation
to me, I will then give him liberty to go to the middle
of Nov.
He can find me by inquiring at the N.Y. Obs. Office
there is a young man to be with me to whom I shall give
board, who is considerably advanced in the art, and who
gives me all his time for the advantage he derives in
being with me.
Materials for Painting will be trifling as to amount
of expence; he will need an easel, a palette, knife
oils, colours, boards or canvas for Painting upon, pencils
chalks, and paper; and perhaps some trifling articles which
do not now occur to me.
All I have to say on the subject is that I am
not very solicitous to take a pupil, as my profession is
of such a nature that I can derive but little benefit
from them, while they must necessarily create much
anxiety; it is rather with the wish to be beneficial
to a young man of promise, that I have put myself to
have consent and not
with any view of being benefited myself.

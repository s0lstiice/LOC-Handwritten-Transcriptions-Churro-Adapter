I am sorry to inform you of the increasing illness of
Mr Whitney his disorder has taken a different turn and is
attended with considerable fever Dr Smith who seldom attends
discouragingly told Mr Silliman last week that if Mr
W. wished to make any arrangement as to his worldly affairs
now was the time for he was past medical aid
Mr Whitney's children have been threatened with the troops
they are getting better but the alarming illness of Mr W.
weighs heavily upon her spirits - Father & Mother called these
this afternoon but saw none of the family Mr Saunders
in our neighborhood is also declining rapidly - The Son too
is very lame and much depressed he thinks he is not
long for this world he feels that it is not in the
most propitious worldly circumstances to listen to happiness
I am told he has expressed this very strongly of late
I met him last week attempting to walk out on reply
to my inquiry "How is your health?" he answered "You see me as
lame old man I am going off I shall not be here long"
this was said in a serious tone and with a most disponding look
This is indeed a wail of sin sorrow and of death were it

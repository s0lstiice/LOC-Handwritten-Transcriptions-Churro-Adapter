Mr. P. informs me that board here in Mexico is about
3 dollars per day, which, as I have often told you, is a
favorable circumstance provided I find business, and
from further conversation on the probability of having business I am greatly encouraged, thus have no painters of eminence that are fond of display and of the arts;
Mr. P. said they have many splendid collections of fine pictures and if he had time he could have made a choice
selection from them. I work on Mexico by Mr.
Poinsett & the papers will be out in a few days.
Fatter must obtain it for you, it is published by Casey
of Philadelphia. Tell Father I called on Davis & Horne
they will probably let me have the money before I leave.
I have taken from them, one national calendar, and 2
dog lead pencils at $1.25 per doz. change to Father's account.
If my things are shipped immediately they will be in
N. Orleans in season for me; have them directed to me
to the care of Gilbert E. Russell & Co. N. Orleans

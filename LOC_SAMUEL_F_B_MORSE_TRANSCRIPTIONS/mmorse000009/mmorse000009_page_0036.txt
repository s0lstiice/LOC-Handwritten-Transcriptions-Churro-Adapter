Insensé or Bar man -- can scarce

Aid of the Arts -- he speaks of his
glorious pictures: this he scanned
to spreading the Subject on the Canvas
with the Skill of a celebrated Artist
who judiciously disposes his Colours
this Subject -- to produce the desired
Effect to the Best Suit
which my present literary associates all
respect or even admiration for their
Art.
Mr. Cogdell unites her regards with mine
always to your Lady & the Little Boys &
Girls -- Oh! my spirit -- how haply
a circumstance it is from me that Kind
Providence -- has heard my Prayers on
this Subject & that it may permit me to live
without them -- but may he bless those
who have & desire to have in my prayer.
I am with old attachment sincerely
Yours from
John D. Cogdell
26 June 1824

see him; On board the boat to Baltimore, I fell in with cousin
Russel Finley, who was going with Capt. Bruce and several others
on board the Georgia Packet for Charleston, which vessel lay
down the river at New Castle; he had just received his diploma
of M. D. and was returning to practice in Charleston, he appeared
quite a different character, (no radical change that I know of) but
sobbed and very much of a gentleman; I rejoice very much on
Aunt's account. — If you recollect I paid half a seat for
my baggage to Phila. (a $2 extra,) and expected I should be at much
expence all the way on account of it, but I paid nothing extra
from Phila. to Balt. and in Balt. I found a return stage to
Washington, the driver of which offered to take me & all my
baggage for one dollar less than the stage fare, which I accepte
and arrived an hour sooner than the regular stages. I
made no stay in Baltimore, of course could neither see Mrs
Appleton nor Mr Bushing, the latter to the latter I left in the
office.
I saw accidentally Major A. Dix who is to call
on me tomorrow morning with Mr. Mason Sec. of Location
with whom he is intimate, he told me that the Parapise would
not be ready to sail under a fortnight if so I shall immediately
take lodgings where I shall be at as little expence as possible.
I am now at Brown's. I saw Charles King the painter,
last week he has built him a splendid house and Painting

Pocket Gazetteers are in the same situation, but I am
willing that they should be sold, as we can't sell them
in any other way, Whiting having spent the market.
We are expecting to see mother here at the
meeting of the Bible Society. Richard will probably have
returned from the by that time. From Richard's last
letter I observe that he now collects the money in advance
at the time he obtains the subscriptions. He will probably
have a hundred or two of dollars by the time he gets back
which will come very seasonably for my relief. Hereafter
I shall enjoy it upon him to be rigid in enforcing
payment in advance, as we have well ascertained
that we can have as many subscriptions as we
want who are willing to comply with these
terms.
I am Love to Lucretia, little
Bee & Charity, Nancy & all the

Selling Emmons

New York Oct 4 1825.
Dear Cousin,
Will you do me the
favor to draw elegantly on parchment
of suitable size to frame & adorn a
parlour, the coat of arms of the
Morse family & send it without
delay to the care of your brothers here.
I am sorry for your misfortunes - hope
your hand recovered so that you can execute this. - My affectionate regards
to your much beloved & honoured parents.
Admir. Abner Morse.
P.S. I have not yet received all the documents necessary to compile the history
of our progenitors hope to soon. In the mean time I calculate you will
get the things engraved that we conversed about. viz the coat of arms & the picture of

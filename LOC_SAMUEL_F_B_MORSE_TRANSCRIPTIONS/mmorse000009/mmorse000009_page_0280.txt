most part a good girl.— All are well at home,
and I shall set out in the morning at 8 o’clock for
Wethersfield to Paint Judge Mitchell &c.— I heard
from N.York yesterday Edwards & Charles Walker
are well, all prosperous.—
Persevere in your travels, mother, as long as
you think it does you good, and tell Dick to
brush up his best bows and bring home some lady
to grace the now desolate mansion.—
I have no fears about home, I shall
let you know the worst always when I write,
so never think that matters are worse than I
represent; as to little Charles, once more, he is
as well as he ever was, or I should not leave home
as I shall to-morrow morning without the least fear
of any relapse.—
Nancy and all send love, Nancy says
she is glad you are gone, and shall be glad when
you come back again.—

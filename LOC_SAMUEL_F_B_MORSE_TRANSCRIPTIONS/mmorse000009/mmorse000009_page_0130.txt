I told him I had engaged my passage to Portsmouth, and if I was

I was careful not to eat anything solid, and drink nothing heat-

mation which extended to my eyes, and before I got to Portsmouth
I was scarcely able to see, my face being much more swollen than
when I was in N.Haven, and the lower part of my face was one
blisters, I went to Uncle Jacob Pickering's where I was imme-
dately received with every attention; I went to bed and sent for Dr. Pierpoint, who confirmed Dr. Cummins
opinion that my blood was in a highly inflamed state, and that
I had a good deal of fever, and that in all probability I
had been saved a serious inflammatory fever, by having all
its virulence drawn out upon the face; I took an emetic, and
after 2 days confinement was able to set out for Concord where
I now am, and am recruiting fast; I began Gov. Woodbery's Portrait

New York March 22d. 1826.
My Dear Parents, I drop a line by M. Gocelyn
who says he goes this morning, and send a
bottle of anchovies for father by him. I sent
by the Packet on ion last Saturday 2 barrels
of fine cider for bottling and 2 groce corks
which I hope you have received in good order
before this reaches you. Little Susan is quite
well she seems perfectly contented and happy
and grows more affectionate every day; I
stay altogether at Dr. Mathews, sleep and
have my meals there. — Dr. Mathews is
to be married again in June to a daughter
of John Hone, Esq. Sister of Mr. Hone whose
little girl I am painting, sister to Mr. Van Schaick
and sister of Mrs. S. Howland; her father
is a man of great wealth, and respectability
and it is altogether a very eligible connection
for Dr. M. I have taken a house $400 rent
of Mr. Isaac Lawrence, it is in the upper part

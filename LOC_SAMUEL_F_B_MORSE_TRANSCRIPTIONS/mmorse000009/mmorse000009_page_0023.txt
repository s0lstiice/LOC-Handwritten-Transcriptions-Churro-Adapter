Will Mr. Poinsett have the goodness to answer the
following questions relating to Mexico for the ratification of
Mr. Morse.
1 How soon is it perfectly safe for a stranger to the Southern
climate to be at Tampico on his way to Mexico?
Ans. Last of October or beginning of Nov. Bates is not safe to
triple duty on the coast.
2. What detention will he meet with at Tampico from
custom house forms, and other government restrictions?
Ans. None, a trifling present smooths all difficulties.
3 What is the distance from Tampico to Mexico, and how
many days are occupied in the journey, Ans. 300 miles
about ten to fifteen days.
4. What is the amount of expenses necessarily incurred
from the first landing till he reaches Mexico?
Ans. from $50 to $100
5. If property is acquired in Mexico, is there any facilities
for remittance?
Ans. Such opportunities are better to bring your dollars with you unless the minute gives
a discount for the U.S.
6. When can he return with safety to the coast?
Ans. any time before May, but not till a day longer than is
required in the specific country.
7. Is a minister to be sent out to Mexico this season?
Ans. I do not know.
8. If yes, is it possible for me to be so attached to the mission
as to be under its protection, and to whom must I apply for
this purpose?
97745
Ans. Apply to the President. Must understand
when and how he can be appointed.

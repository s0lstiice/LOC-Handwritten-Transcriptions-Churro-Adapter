Mr Jocelyn, I understand, is doing well with the map of
Greece at New Haven. His agents now here are not doing very well
The people have had so much of Greece that they are sick of it
The tide is at ebb here while it is rising in the country. I tell
them they had better push off into the country and leave this
market in the hands of booksellers; but there is a clashing between
agents and booksellers. It is for the interest of the agents to keep the
work out of booksellers' hands, and as Mr Jocelyn sends down all
the maps to his agents they will of course take care that the
booksellers do not get hold of them. — I hope Mr Jocelyn will
manage the whole affair wisely, and make something handsome
out of it — but it is an experiment — and I have seen enough of
experiments to prevent me from being a quainted
Remember me affectionately to Finley,
Susan Walker, Nancy, little Sue, Charley & all inquiring friends.
I am, yours,
affectionately
Sidney E. More

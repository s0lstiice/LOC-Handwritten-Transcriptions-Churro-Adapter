New York. 20 May. 1824
Dear Sir
Tuesday will accord very well with our
motions as we should like to proceed you at Brunswick
by a day. We intend going on Monday and shall
prepare the Doctor for your arrival. A boat leaves
here every day at eleven in the forenoon which
lands you at New Brunswick at three or four in
the afternoon : at what hour on Tuesday you will
find a carriage waiting for you. If you should
not see me inquire for Dr Beach's carriage. The
New Brunswick steam boats are the Bellona, and, I
believe, the Thistle. It would be well, perhaps, to
enquire after your arrival here on Tuesday morning,
at the steam Boat shop in Washington St near the Battery

Let me hear from you — has you get along in
N.Y. — the Times are changing Daily with
us — every thing is going — backwoodsy
old families die down not supplied
by their offspring — Estates are broken
up — the distributive parts are wasted
& thus — the great fabric vanishes leave
no attendent school — Strangers are
occupying the Land of the Original
Settlers — Their descendants in a
short time will not be found.
The City of Charleston — way over
the Rival of Boston — so hospitality
& so much equalled in the warmth & sincerity
of its Manners — what shall be
said of her by Cly — must not by
me be said — for I am Native here.
I do shake Waldo — by the hand for me
he is a man of our heart.

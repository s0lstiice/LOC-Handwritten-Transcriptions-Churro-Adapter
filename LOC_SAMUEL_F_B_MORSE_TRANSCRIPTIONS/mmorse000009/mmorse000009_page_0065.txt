We have reason to hope, that it is the
dominant desire of his heart, wherever
he is, or may be stationed in Providence, to
honor God, & to do good to his fellowmen.
I hope you will find him faithful &
active in the discharge of all the duties
of his new situation. In ascertaining
& discharging these duties he, I may add we,
shall be thankful for your counsel & prote-
cion. I have thought it important for, as an
anxious father, to make you acquainted with
these things respecting my son, as he is going
into your family. He is a son of aim strong affec-
tions & hopes. He leaves a family endearred to
him by the charity of a partner who deserved
& shares largely in his best earthly affect-
ions, & of two little children, of a character
& age to interest his tenderest feelings.
Committing him to the divine protec-
tion & blessing, I subscribe myself, Sir,
with high consideration
& esteem, your obedient
Servt Seth Morse
Hon. N. Edwards
Minister to Mexico
Mr S. G. Morse

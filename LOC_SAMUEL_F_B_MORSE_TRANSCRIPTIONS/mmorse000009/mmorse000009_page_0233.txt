My dear Man:
You told me Waldo requested you to inform
me of his misfortune domestic -- but you did
not inform me that his heart was also clen
ch'd me -- I have written to him this
day a little of my heart -- in sorrow and
in anger -- I spoke of all which you
would apply within for -- do will you
do so if you can get him to procure
two bottles of the same -- for you I you
can thus send them to me -- you know
I will do as much for you --
I am sorry for Waldo's total indifference
You know how I loved that man -- how
deeply his sorrow was made my own
for sko it be my fate to live away where
in this part of the world -- the desire is
strong -- his society had been calculated
on as a balm in its turn for the loss of
Friend left behind -- but a divine
left to return Religion shortly -- but send me
2 Dillons for the British Commissioners --
with your affectualities
John T. Cogdell

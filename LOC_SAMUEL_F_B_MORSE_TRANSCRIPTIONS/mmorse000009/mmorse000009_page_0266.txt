and if I am in funds you shall have it. I was
very much rejoiced to hear of the religious state of
New Haven, and most cordially say amen to your
prayer that we may all partake of the fruits of the
spirit of peace. I have constantly held myself
ready and willing to forget & forgive, the moment I
am permitted; until then I have nothing to reproach
myself with in the attitude which I have assumed
and shall maintain, towards my brothers, as long as
the course so wounding to my feelings remains un-
explained, and not the slightest expression of regret
manifested in consequence of it; if after the state
of the case with regard to my own actions and
feelings at the time they wrote that letter, which
they are informed of, they do not think it worth while
to express one single regret that they were mistaken as to my
course and state of feeling towards them at that time, I can
only say they have very different views of Christian propriety,
and common justice from mine, and until they are
enlightened on those points, or I am, there will be no com-
promise of the matter. But the boat is going and
I must say no more. Yr Aff Sam M. Mosse

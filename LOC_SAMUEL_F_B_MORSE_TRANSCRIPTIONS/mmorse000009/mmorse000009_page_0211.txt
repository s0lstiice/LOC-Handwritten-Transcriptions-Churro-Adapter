NY. May 21. 1825.
Saturday
My Dear Parents,
I arrived safely with all my
furniture the morning after I left you, and what
is remarkable, have all the articles without an
injury to the value of 25 Cents, the sofa, the dining
table, and the breakfast table particularly are safely in
their places without the slightest new scratch upon them,
my cast also, are without a single new break, so
that I have every reason to be thankful.—
I should have written you before, but have had
no breathing time till now.—
I send this by Mr. Durand, firm of North
Kiss and Durand who has in charge the bandbox
containing little Charles' new hat, and some things
also I believe with Susan left.— Mrs Eder says
with respect to coming to N.Haven that she will
not be able certainly to come before the last
of June, so that your arrangements will probably
suit all round. they are all well at

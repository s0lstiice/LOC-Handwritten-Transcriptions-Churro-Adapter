I was sorry to hear that mother is not well.
I hope she will regain her health, and long be
spared to be a blessing to her family who all
love her very, very much; I hope dear little
Finley has got over the difficulty with his
finger, and that the rest of the children are
well, I long to see them again but dare not think
of it at present. Give my love to Richard
to Nancy and all neighbors & friends.
Cousin Sarah Landing was on the point
of being with you with Richard, I arrived in
the carriage with her at the Steamboat wharf
just in time to see the boat clear from the
wharf. She hopes to be able to spend one day
with you yet. I wish you would invite her
in one of your letters to me, she will be here
about ten days longer.
In great haste, tomorrow being
Grand Canal celebration all is in confusion
Our love to mother
Yr Aff Son
Sam J. B. Mosse

N.York Dec. 19th 1825.
My Dear Parents.
Father's letter dated the 8th inst. has
this moment me . So that if it had been in my power
to have loaned the $300. asked for, it could not have been
sent in season, but the fact is I shall but barely get
along with money to be rec'd. I have not about me
more than to pay the every day expenses, and in the
mean time I have large debts to be paid off the money
my money is rec'd. — Reputation I hope and believe
will produce money in the end, but it is a kind of
stock not immediately productive. — I long to
see home for a little while but do not see any pros-
pect immediately of it. — Love to Mother, who I
am glad to hear is getting better, and Nancy, Hig'd the
dear Children for me, and give Love to Richd.
All well, In greatest haste Yr Aff
I consent to the diminution of the
note to $1400. — Son
P. V. B. More.

Concord Sunday Augt 29th 1824.
My Dear Parents,
Your letters have been duly rec'd. We are
all pretty well, the children complain a little, but we
attribute it to the weather, whooping cough, &c in Charles
to leething. We shall set out from here on Tuesday
afternoon for Londonderry in a wagon, so as to take
the stage to Boston as it passes us at a reasonable
hour on the next day, and expect to be in Boston on
Wednesday even'd. we shall probably remain there a
day, and then proceed to Hartford by the middle road,
and endeavor to be at home by Saturday night.
Fatter mentions that Fisher is in Hartford "taking
these portraits which I might have taken a month ago"
I don't exactly understand this; if 8 portraits were enga-
ged for me, and I have written to Mr. Ellsworth saying
I should be there on Commencement week, I don't
see how any of those engaged to me can employ Mr.
Fisher without violating their word & honor. If this
is the case, however I shall not be worried much, as

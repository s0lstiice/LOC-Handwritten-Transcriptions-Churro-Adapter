You cannot complain I think of my not writing
often; indeed, I claim no merit for it, for I have scarcely
anything else to do, I wish I were so much engaged that
I could write you but once a week; but I wait, and
wait, and wait, and hope that I shall have some com-
mission soon to enable me at last to pay off my
N.York scores, they are not great to be sure except for
room rent; Kiss the dear children, for me and
give my love to Sister Susan, and to Father & Mother
& Nancy and kind regards to all the good neighbors
& Friends.
Your aff. husband as ever
Sant J B. Mason

My name is John H. Davis of
Delaware College as a candidate for the
chair of Chemistry and Natural History in
that Institution - You have spoken
something of me in the Religious I have
entailed for the last few years, and will
much oblige me by addressing a note, based
upon the above intimation, to my friend
and relation Prof. Wm H. Davis, who is a
member of the faculty -
The situation is desirable on many
accounts - The duties are not oppressive, much
time will be enjoyed for study, the salary is
a thousand dollars, with a house and
garden -
I am now praying a few days at
my early home gaining my health and strength
for the labors of the coming year - I shall
return to Albany about the first of September
1843 - soon after which will occur this meeting

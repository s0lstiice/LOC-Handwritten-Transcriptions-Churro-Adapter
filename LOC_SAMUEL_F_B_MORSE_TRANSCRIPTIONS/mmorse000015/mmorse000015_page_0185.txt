Prof. Morse
Dr. Sis.
Mr I. R. Nieuwsee who is a civil Engineer,
and has for some years been engaged with my
Isolier, is desirous of obtaining employment
in your Corps of Telegraphist -- in the work
about to be done on the Washington R.R. I
take the liberty of introducing him to you, with
the appearance that in the range of my acquaintance
I know of no gentleman of more worth,
and whose skill I am confident would be most
efficiently serviceable should it suit your arrangements to employ him.
With great respect
Sam Branlin
Most truly
Geo H B Stiles
April 22. 43

Treasury Department
May 12. 1848
Sir:
The arrangement for the supply of Reels
for reading the wire for the Telegraph machine in your
letter of the 6th Instant at the terms stated, twin, eighty
Reels at Two dollars each, is approved. The employment
of the laborer to assist in experiments in the Laboratory at
seven dollars per week is also approved.
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedt Servant
J. C. Spence
Secretary of the Treasury
Prof. S. F. B. Morse
Has J. K.

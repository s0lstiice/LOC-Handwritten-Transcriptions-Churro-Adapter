Albany June 25th 1842.
My dear Sir
I thank you for your early and very satisfactory complian with
my request, I have hastily looked through the papers you have sent me
and found that they will be very useful. But it seems to me that the
occasion will warrant a notice of our eminent artists and their
choice productions To give such sketches will be a delicate duty
but nevertheless it is a duty and ought not to be shunned from my hands
to greet a form of you as a new sketch of the artist as this work
is chemical ade. If then to a delivery or your part in furnishing it
the matter may be altogether confidential and the responsibility entirely
on me it is already so evident that an addition means for good public
ends will not be felt of suspicion
Very respectfully
Your obedient servant
William H. Severson

Copy to Isaac K. Coffin, Egy.
Constitution
New York Dec. 1. 1842
8. to, 9th of 28th ulto: I have just received, and
I have only time to say in reply that there is no time
to communicate with the other Proprietors of my
Telegraph in relation to engaging your services
the present Session, but I think they will be
unnecessary as I have determined to attend to
the subject personally with others of the Proprietors
and we expect to be in Wash. in a few days
when I will have the pleasure of seeing you.
I am fully aware of the difficulties you
present and have believed that a personal
attention at Wash. on the part of some at
least of the Proprietors was indispensable
to surmount them.
In the mean time believe me
Isaac K Coffin. Egy
Washington.

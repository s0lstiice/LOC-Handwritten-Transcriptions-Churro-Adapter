Washington DC. Jan 27th 1843
Dear Sidney,
I send you Susan's letter which
I will thank you to take care of for
me. I will you would write her a line
and Catherine too occasionally, for she
will prize them very much. —
I am still in suspense, and it is
painful and trying to me, but there is no
help for it but patience. —
You can help me if a few lines
were inserted in the Journal of Commerce
and Evening Post or in the latter only,
simply stating the fact that the press
throughout the country supports the views
of the Report, and that it is evidently
the popular wish that the bill recom-
mended should pass. — If inserted send
me a copy. — In looking over the papers
with which you exchange, you will perceive
the case to be so, and if the fact be stated
in a single paragraph, it will be read here

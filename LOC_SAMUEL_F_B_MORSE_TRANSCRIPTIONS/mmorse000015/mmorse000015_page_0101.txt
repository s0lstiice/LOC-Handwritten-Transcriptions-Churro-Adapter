My old friend
Charleston 16th June 1842
In this morning -- the proceedings of Congress so far -- on your Telegraphic System: and it gave me
delight to find one who had so long forgotten me -- had been so conspicuous in interesting the
Scientific Men of England France Refrain from our Congress: Hope my friend you
may succeed by your name thence to long
long associated with our Country -- Smith
in history --
I thank you much for this reminiscence
I was surprised when from the front of
the House -- S. B. Ayrigg M C -- but
as soon as I saw D. H. B. M: I translated
without difficulty -- and felt the effect
of the Telegraphic wire --
My Model for -- the Monument of my poor Mother
Executed for me in Carrara -- has just arrived
thence in the Ship Spring of Hamilton
at New York in 4 days --
The 3 Pens as large as 6. by 4 feet high
the Monument to occupy 6. by 4 feet in
Phillips Church -- Executed the Model
in 1841: Shad it cast -- stuck it to Carrara

Washington March 13th 1843.
Not having received an answer to mine of the 10th inst.
I am compelled to leave Wash. without further delay for home, as my
new relations with the Govt. and the long absence from my duties to
various interests in N.York make it necessary that I should be there immediately
to set my affairs in order. I will proceed directly to collect the necessary preliminary
information which must precede any final action on the part of the
Hon. Secy. of the Hear. respecting the definitive instructions for making the
experiment with my Elector Mag. Pel. hoping from his generous declaration
to me that I should have every assistance & in power to give to
I shall meet in this with his approbation, and I will only add that I
presume the time spent in gathering this information will be deemed
as services rendered under the Act making the Express printer for this
object — Hoping however soon to hear more at large from
the Hon. the Secy. of the Treasury I remain most respectfully
Yr most ob. Servt
S. F. B. Morse
7. The Hon. J. C. Spencer
Secy of the Treasury.

Articles of Agreement made and concluded the tenth
day of May — in the year of our Lord one thousand eight
hundred and forty three, between Aaron Benedict of Huntington
Commissioner of the one part, and Samuel F. B. Morse of the City of New York,
for and in behalf of the United States of America of the other part,
Wilmington:
That the said Aaron Benedict — for himself, his heirs, Executors and Administrators, doth hereby con-
tract and agree with the said Samuel F. B. Morse, to supply and deliver to the party of the second part, Ten thousand pounds
of copper wire of the following description and quality, that is to say, of number sixteen in gauge, of the inch scale, unanne-
aled of pure copper, free from alloy, flaws and defects, well annealed and soft, and to correspond in all respects with the
sample furnished with the proposals, and in lengths not less than three hundred feet each, the same to be securely packed
in keys suitable for safe and convenient transportation, and to be delivered free of expense to the aforesaid Morse, at the United
States, at the Rope Walk of Ebenezer Chase, in the City of New York, at the junction of Eighth Avenue and the Bloomingdale
Road. And it is expressly understood and agreed to by the party of the first part, that in case any of said wire thus to be
delivered shall prove not to be perfect in all respects according to the true intent and meaning of these presents and to
the approval and acceptance of Professor James C. Fisher of the City of New York, the party of the first part shall by
stipulate and agree to take back such quantity or quantities of wire as the case may be and make good the deficiency
just with by other good and suitable wire of the quality and description herein before referred to. And the party

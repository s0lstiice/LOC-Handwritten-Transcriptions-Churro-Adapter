New York Feb 16th 1843.
Prof S.F.B. Morse
Dear Sir
In compliance with your
request I will state in conciseness as possible the
terms on which I should be glad to engage with you
in the construction of the Telegraph. I will 1. it meets
your stipulation superintend the entire prefabrication of the
wire from its manufacture to the placing of it in the
tubes; 2. also the placing of it in the ground I will also commit
you in the various experiments it may be necessary
to make on the last part of galvanic batteries so I will
reduce to writing a full & complete set of rules for their
best management together with rules for the management
of the Telegraph if required. I will devote my whole time
& attention to the business so as to secure a favorable result.
I shall you wish to devote upon any other business connected
with the Telegraph I will cheerfully undertake it.
As I must give up all other business & devote my whole
time & thought to it & as I have a family dependent upon
me, I do not think the sum of $1500 a year will be more
than a fair compensation.
Very respectfully yours
Abt Secrt
James Fisher

Rec'd with Philip Schuyler & P. D. Fisher
in relation to Telegraph for China and the East.
Sept. 20th made arrangements with M. Schuyler to give
him two Registers to take to China. M. Schuyler
M. Fisher and another person with myself, four
in all, to divide the profits in quarters, each receiving
one quarter, the cost of the two Registers to be deducted from my part of the profits.
I received Forty dollars from M. Schuyler to be employed immediately in making the Registers
and must receive Sixty more making 100. in all.
Expenses of Registers.
Sept. 24. of Thomas Simpson as P. bill A $4,,00

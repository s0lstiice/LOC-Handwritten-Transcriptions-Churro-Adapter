Dear Sir,
I have no Electric Magnetic Telegraph
in action to-day at 136 Nassau St. upstairs
5th story, and should be happy to see you and
any friends, at such time as may suit your con-
venience. I will be at your service
S.F. B. Morse
Sept. 3d 1842
Stanley
Morehead

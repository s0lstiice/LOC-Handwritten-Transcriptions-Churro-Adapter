Washington 31st December 1842
In compliance with your request I beg
have to submit my opinion in relation to the electric Mayne-
rie Telegraph of Joseph Morse.
I have examined its scientific principles and the mechanism
of its construction, and am satisfied that the first are correct
and the latter well combined for the purpose of obtaining the
desired results. I have also been a frequent witness of its practical
operation, and am happy to the vastity with which it writes down alone
station the words and sentences intended to be conveyed from the other.
In the transmission of intelligence through great distances physical
difficulties will probably exist with some of which any one at present
is unacquainted that any of these can be found inconvenient.
The advantage of this mode of communication over all others is, that while
its indications are transmitted with a certainty equal to that of light in
many cases between points so far separated as to be invisible from
each other; by night as well as by day; and in most gloomy as well
as in bright sunlight.
The Hon E B Atchison
Base of Representatives

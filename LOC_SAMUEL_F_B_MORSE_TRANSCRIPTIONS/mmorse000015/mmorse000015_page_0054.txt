Friday, Oct. 22. 1842
Dear Father
Necessity once more compels me to write
you for the means to make a few more necessary purchases
in the first place they board, according to the rules of the
club ought to be paid weekly $25.00 at the entrance into
it I have now been there a week & have not yet arrived to
pay next day washerman's bill $21.36 to. Then my society
for $12,000 & I have been shunned for that. I was told
I need not pay till next term but am misinformed; then
I want oil, a pair of scissors,reek Testament (about 75 cents),
an Election book (25 cents) some blanks for compositions &
Translations, and $2.50 to pay for what I borrowed; before
I could find any club to board, I was obliged to take
4 meals at an eating house, Uncle R came here & told me
he could not take me for something of at all, so I told
he came nothing so that I had found a club, for I had
then, but going around there I found my vacancy in
it (the club) had been filled by another member of it
who had been absent, so I determined to go to another
at last I succeeded in finding Mr Akeman who has one
vacancy, which I took $1.75 for week in the price of board &
very good board it is most of the members are church
members & the strictest order is observed at table.

Treasury Department
July 18. 1845
Sir:
Your letter of the 16th instant asking to be furnished with Four thousand dollars, to meet expenses of the
construction of constructing Electric Magnetic Telegraphs is received. As your last account rendered to the
9th instant, exhibits a balance of twenty nine thousand dollars, the Treasurer has been directed to transmit you a draft for Two
thousand dollars, and you will be pleased to state whether this sum will not answer for the present
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedient Servant
Geo. S. Davenport
Secretary of the Treasury
Ref 2 Sam F B Mace
Superintendent of E.M. Telegraph
New York

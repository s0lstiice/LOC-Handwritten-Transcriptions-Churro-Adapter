S. P. B. House. Watertown August 26th 1843
Bot of Benedict & Brantham Alg Co
No 16 Copiagut Nine 701th 234th 238.34
Mr. Biv
About you have Bill of Wine which we send agreeable
to your request, or rather not having heard from you in reply
to our last, we presumed you expected it as we then mentioned
we should send it and if we haven't from you.
We consider this lot of Wine equal to any that has or can be drawn
and presume you will upon examination find it so.
It will go by the Boat on Monday evening
Respectfully re
Benedict & Brantham Alg Co
for S. P. Mitchell Ch.

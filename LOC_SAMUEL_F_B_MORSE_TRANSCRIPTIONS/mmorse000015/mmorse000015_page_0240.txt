Treasury Department
June 15th 1843.
Sir:
It appearing by your letter of the 15th instant
that you intend, as a provision in the provision of the
Telgraphic lines, that a less quantity of cotton time
will be required than was at first supposed and I
recommend that twenty five hundred pounds of that
article already secured should be sold, under state
that you are authorized to dispose of the surplus cotton
time as soon as, at the best terms that can be obtained,
and will charge yourself with the proceeds in your
monthly account of expenditures
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedt Servant
Alb C. Johnson
Actg Sec of the Treasury
Respectfultly
F.B. Mall
Superintendant of Telegraphs
Mad Jax

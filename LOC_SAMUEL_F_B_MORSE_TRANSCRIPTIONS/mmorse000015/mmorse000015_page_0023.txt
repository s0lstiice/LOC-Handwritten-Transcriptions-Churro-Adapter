Morse shall furnish two Registers complete to be used and
disposed of as before stated; but if they are not sold but re-
turned then the sum of one hundred dollars is to be repaid the
said Schuyler provided said Registers are returned to the said
Morse in good condition.
It is also expressly understood that the two Registers thus
constructed shall be at the sole disposal of the said Schuyler,
but shall not be sold or used for the purposes of Telegraphic
Communication in the United States, or in other countries in
which the said Morse has a patent for the same.
In witness whereof we have hereunto set
our hands and seals this 22d day of Sept. A.D. 1842.
Schuyler
Sam: F: B: Morse

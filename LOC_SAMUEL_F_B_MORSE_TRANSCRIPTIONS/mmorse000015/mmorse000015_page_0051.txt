American Institute 15th Fair
In Nautical Committee
New York Oct 20th 1842
Extract from the Minutes
Resolved that the thanks of
the Committee are due and are hereby extended to
Professor Morse and his assistant Dr Fisher for the
profusitude and exertions displayed by them in ac-
ceding to the wishes of the Committee and placing
at their disposal the Magnetic Telegraph to comm-
municate between the Battery & Governor Island during
the experiments on the 18th Inst -
Resolved that a copy of the above reso-
lution be forwarded to them signed by the Chairman
and Secretary with a request that they be per-
mitted to publish the same -
Geo. C. De Kay
Chairman of
N. Comtee
Joseph Cooldin
Secy

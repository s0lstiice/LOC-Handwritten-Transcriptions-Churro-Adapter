I stayed here during vacation, as Uncle R thought
it would not be worth while to go to New York for so short
a time as two weeks, the fare being $10 down I have
been quite well, last term I was sick once or twice.
I like to be where I can get plenty of exercise, I intend to
be a farmer if possible, nothing would be better than
to be in possession of a good farm and have plenty of work.
Fin it seems is now a printer Uncle Sid wrote last week
that I am set up a column & a half of last week's Observer
From your affectionate Son
Charles

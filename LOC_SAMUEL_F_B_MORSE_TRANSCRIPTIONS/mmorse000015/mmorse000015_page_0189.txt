Adjutant Office Baltimore April 24th 1863
Prof. S.J. Bellmore.
Deansu.
I was happy to be informed by Mr. McLean the
Resident of the Company that the Board had acted favorably
upon the request I had the pleasure of making to them recently
upon the line of the Wash. Branch Rail Road and that he
had apprised you of the success of your request as recommended
by me.
I take the liberty now of mentioning to you the name of a gentleman
who was formerly an assistant in the service of this Company
and who being at present out of employment would be glad to
assist you (as you not have already engaged some one for
that purpose) in superintending the execution of the work which
the construction of the Telegraph will require. I have already
given Mr. Tierman, the gentleman referred to, a general letter of
recommendation in his quitting the service, which expresses my
opinion of his character and qualifications so fully, that I have
been willing to forward it to you, and I write this line merely to confirm
all that I have therein stated of him, and to say how much pleasure it
will give me to hear that his services can be made available to you
upon the work in question.
When the work in question will be completed they will also be disposed of

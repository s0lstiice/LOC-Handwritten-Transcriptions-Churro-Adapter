NY July 24th 1843
I have the honor to enclose a duplicate copy of
the contract for trenching between Baltimore and Wash-
ington for the laying down of my telegraphic wire, duly executed
in accordance with the appropriation of the Department.
I have also the gratification to report that the contract
for the wire has been faithfully fulfilled, that the first wire
with its entire part of Baltimore to Washington
and two carriages of the whole 100 miles is completed, that
experiments made upon 43 miles have been resorted to the most
satisfactory manner, and that the whole work is proceeding
with every prospect of successful issue.
I have the honor to be
With sincere respect
Y. M. Students
S.F. B. Morse.

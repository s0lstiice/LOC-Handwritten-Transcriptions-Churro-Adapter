be your object to have all ready by the opening
of next Congress, -- if so there must be no small
difficulty. -- Father has been absent a week on a visit to
Boston my Mother is well and sends her kind regards
to your old Master and Brother. -- I hope you are
not aware of the manner of my family by the birth

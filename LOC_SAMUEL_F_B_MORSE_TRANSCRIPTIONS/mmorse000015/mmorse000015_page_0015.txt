attention of the House to the subject by a Resolution of my
opinion early in the Session. I wrote you some time after, regretting
a delay of action on the part of the Govt. in the hope that
time before this I could show them the Telegraph in Wash.
but just as I am ready I find that Congress will adjourn before
I can reach Wash., and put the instrument in order for the
inspection. – Will it be possible before Congress rises to appropriate
the funds for the erection of the Telegraph in Washington
and a small sum say $3500 dollars to put the Telegraph in
operation for the inspection of Congress the next Session. If Congress
will grant this sum, I will engage to have a complete
Telegraph between the Post house in the Capitol & the Treasury
and so that instantaneous communication can be had between
these points at pleasure at any time of day or night in
clear or rainy weather, and ready for their inspection during the
next Session of Congress, so that the whole subject may be fairly
understood. I believe the great majority of Congress
will support the results of this mention of the Telegraph in Washington
but themselves to dwell for a moment on the consequences of instant
communication of intelligence from one part of the Union to another
& which is no longer a problem to the United States, which my investigation
renders certain, I would not hesitate to pass all the acts necessary
to secure its to the Govt. – I ask not this until they have thoroughly
examined its merits, but will they not assist me in placing the
matter fairly before them? Surely, so small a sum to the Govt. in expenses
cannot reasonably be denied.

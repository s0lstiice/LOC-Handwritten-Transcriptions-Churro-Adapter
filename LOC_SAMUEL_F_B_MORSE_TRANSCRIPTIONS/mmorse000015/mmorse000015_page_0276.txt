No. 12. Portland
Mr. Smith in his letter on file, dated August 18th 1843 says
"If any thing has altered your calculation as to the
"time of commencing the trenching, I wish you would apprise
"me of it. I am preparing now to meet you order at any time
"after the middle of Sept. and before the 1st of Oct. Although
"if it were deferred until the middle of Oct. I should not
"regret it. But I am subject to your arrangements in this
"particular entirely."
Showing that the first postponement was as agreeable to
him, as to me, and would serve his convenience better
in the middle than the first of October.

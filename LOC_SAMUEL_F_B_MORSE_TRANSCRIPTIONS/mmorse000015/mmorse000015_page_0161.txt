New York March 31st 1843
Prof. Sam J. B. Morse
Dear Sir
I have received your commu-
nication of this date, informing me of the approval
by the Secretary of the Treasury of my appoint-
ment as one of your Assistants, under the act of
congress passed 3d instant authorizing you
to test the practicability of your Electromagnetic
telegraph; and report myself ready for duty.
Respectfully
Your obedient Servant
L. Gill

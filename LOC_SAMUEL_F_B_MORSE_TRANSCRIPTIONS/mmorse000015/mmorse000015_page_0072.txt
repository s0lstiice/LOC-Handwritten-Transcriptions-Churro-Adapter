At first I took board and lodgings at the Amer. Hotel at Penns-
ylvania house, where however they charged me & that is to say
16 dollars p r week, so after one week, I found a private
boarding house, (Mr. Hatcher Missouri Avenue,) for 6
that is 12 dollars p r week, where I am better accommodated
in a pleasant family and at a cheaper rate. To-morrow I
shall further reduce my expenditure, as Dr Fisher is invited
to pass the rest of his time here, at the house of Rev. W.
Rich who is his relative, and my expenses are therefore,
reduced to 6 dollars p r week. This reduction will enable
me to stay until my bill is carried through. I am told
by some members of the Senate, that it will go through the
Senate without the least delay or hesitation, and some
members of the House are declared it should pass in the
House by acclamation, and this may possibly be the case.
I find every where the most kindly feelings, and in
view of it all I cannot but recognize the special goodness
& mercy of our Heavenly Father. In his hands are all hearts,
he turns and can turn them as it pleases him, and to him
from the heart I would give all the praise and all the
glory.

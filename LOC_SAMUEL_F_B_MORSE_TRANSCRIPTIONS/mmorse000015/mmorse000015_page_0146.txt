Prof S.F.B.Morse
Dear Sir
I am desirous of being employed as
one of the Assistant Superintendents of the experiments about
to be made by the Government of the United States to Test the
practicability of Electro-magnetic Telegraphs, at a salary
of $1800. per annum
Should you see fit to give me the appointment, I
propose to superintend the Manufacture of the lead pipes
and the Preparation of the Same for laying down –
To inspect the metal and analyze the Same so as to test
its purity or impurity before it is worked – also to inspect
the pipe when manufactured – the filling the Same with
rosin and the delivery of them on board the vessel
or other vehicle for Transportation to the place
of destination.
I also propose to collect and communicate to
you such information as I shall be able to obtain
relative to the corroding power of various agents on
Maltae lead. Such as air, water & the various
Soils, etc
I have the honor to be
your obedient Servt
S.F.B.Morse
March 21st 1843.

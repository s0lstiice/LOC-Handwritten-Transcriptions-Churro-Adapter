I hardly know in what form this request of mine should be
made. Should it be by petition to Congress? or will this
letter handed into the Com't. be sufficient? If a petition
is required for party sake to be referred to the Com't. to report
the form of the petition I shall ask you to make such petition in proper form.
You know, my dear Sir, just what I wish, and I have
from your kind & friendly feeling that may count on
your aid.
If on your return you stop a day in New York
I shall be glad to show you the Telegraph Station
as it is in operation.
With due respect & esteem
Y. R. Servs
P. P. Morley.
Mr. W. W. Boardman, M. C.
Washington.
New York Aug. 10th 1842
Copy sent to office same date.
Original draft of the letter.

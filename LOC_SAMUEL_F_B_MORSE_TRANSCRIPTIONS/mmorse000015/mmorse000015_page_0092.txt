I am out of money now, and will have to
remain some time from you, to pay my little debts as postage, wishing
to $5.00 would be sufficient I think. I hope you don't think
that I have laid out my money foolishly, I am sure I
have done the best I could, it cost me quite a good deal for outfits
for 4 years, hereafter I will not have so many recessions
to please as I am pretty well settled, and will have only
a few minor wants.
I wish you would come up to see me some
time -- Prof. Hooker, Wilson, Harman & family are all well, they
are a very very kind & nice family. Selecting a very well India
late Draper's son.
Your lessons are long and hard and require nearly all my
time. Algebra especially is rather difficult to me a new beginning.
I lost to get a catalog, forget any code in as there is no calendar
as yet in the house it cost me 10 cents, raise a board for a book
for effects.
Uncle Richard cannot take me this winter very well
he would have to look out for board for myself and a young man
from the Jones' school by name gets hard in a private family at
$2.50 for week and there are others of my class board there all in
very gentlemanly set, I shall take ten here tonight and if I like it
will remain till I hear from you, I know a place where
I could board for 1.50 but there are some hard cases as
they are called, there, perhaps Uncle R. may find it convenient
after he is settled, to take me, but he can not tell how it

I am fully persuaded that if you were on here and
could inquire around a little, there would be found
a most lucrative business under government for your
heroic efforts in proving. I have shown it to many, and
they are without exception astonished at its beauty.
By the by let me know if the Observer has an
Agent here. I have been asked by one or two who have
seen the Atlas, and wish to subscribe. I could not tell
them the conditions of the Atlas.
You speak of Richard and his occasional turn
of low spirits, and think his suspicion that you
have a little tendency that way, is unfounded. I
rather agree with him and think you have of late
manifested a little of the Morse or Breese (don't
tell Cousin Sam,) disposition. I have lately had a
turn of it myself. This waiting at so much risk,
makes me question myself, am I in the path of duty?

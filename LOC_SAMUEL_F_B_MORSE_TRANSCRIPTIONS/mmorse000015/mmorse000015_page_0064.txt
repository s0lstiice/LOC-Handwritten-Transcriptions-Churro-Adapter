I am now in want of a little to keep me
in comfort & will have to pay a little charge for postage if
Uncle Sid will come up here soon perhaps he can arrange
all matters satisfactorily, Uncle Richard is expecting him.
I see by Aunt Selina's letter that Sister & Mrs. Ford have
arrived in Sanit, & that Sister is well pleased.
I have been quite unwell, I did not go out for
2 or 3 days & stood at Uncle R where Charity was very kind
& kind, I am now pretty well though at times feel rather
weak, I should like to have a high desk to stand up &
study, the Best in his lecture & as recommended from
them are in pretty general use throughout college, he states
that was hurtful to lean over a low table for any length of
time.
Our vacation will be in about 5 weeks, then I
hope to see you in New York. My studies are all easy
except Mathematics I wished I had paid more attention to
that while at Mr. Jones but he advised me not to do so
there would be time enough at college.
We are all well here in N Haven & would
like to see you but I suppose you are pretty busy now
preparing for Christmas. When you write please tell us when
Sidie did is coming. Love to Mr Uncle & Aunt State
all friends.
Yours affectionately Son
Charles (over)

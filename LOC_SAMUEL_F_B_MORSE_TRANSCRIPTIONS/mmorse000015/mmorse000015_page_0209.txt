Treasury Department
May 20th 1843
Appoint me to the temporary duties in the Telegraphic Tabernacle's office,
Rated in my last letter of the 18th instant, to wit, at an annual
rent of Four Hundred dollars, commencing on the first
of the present month
The wages allowed to Benjamin Taylor
for assistance in maintaining Holding line at Six dollars
per week are approved
Sam Tay Respectfully
Jun Hood Comt
J. C. Spencer
Secrty of the Treasury
Pres Sam C. F. B. More
Mac Jack

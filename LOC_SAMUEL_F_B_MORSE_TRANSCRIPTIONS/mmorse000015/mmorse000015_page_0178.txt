New York April 18th 1843
Prof Morse
We require 42 lbs of No 20
sspier wire to be wound with silk for the magnet
& 20 lbs of No 24. for the galvanometers. It is of great
importance that this wire should be procured immedi-
ately that the necessary experiments may be made
as soon as possible that no delay may intervene from
this cause during the preparation of the main wires
It will be well to order them immediately & have the
necessary instruments prepared.
James Christie Asst Sec

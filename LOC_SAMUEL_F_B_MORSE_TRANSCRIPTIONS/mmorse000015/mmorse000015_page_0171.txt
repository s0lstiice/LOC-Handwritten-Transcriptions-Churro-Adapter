New York April 11th 1843
Dear Sir,
Y. M. of the 7th inst. I have just received and beg you to
accept my thanks for the promptness with which my proposition
has been acted upon by the R.R. Company. The proprietors
of the Telegraph in the city have delegated full authority to
Mr Smith, Esq of Portland, one of the Proprietors, to treat with
you on the subject, and any arrangement entered into between
Mr Smith & yourself will be ratified by us all. (and below)

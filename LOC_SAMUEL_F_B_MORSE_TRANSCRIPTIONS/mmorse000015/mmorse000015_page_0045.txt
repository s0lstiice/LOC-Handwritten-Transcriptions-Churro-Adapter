I got the clock because I heard it was as cheap as I could
anywhere obtain, and cheaper than I might get one at another time.
My lessons are very long and hard, I have to study all the time I
am up till past 10 o clock. Aunt Salisbury & cousin Martin are so
very kind to me, I have taken my meals there ever since I
have been here, waiting for Uncle Richard, where can he be?
I have but one vest which is rather dirty, I cannot be wasted
with you by Mr Frisby, make me one? you can send up by Mr
Morrisons Basket. I will have to get me a lamp, candles
do not give such good light, and the grease every thing up. I
will get one from Uncle R. if he has one to sell. Give my
love to sister & brother Ed. to Uncle Charles & Aunt Kate & cousin
Remember me to John & all friends. yours in best
Charles
Monday morning This morning I called on the treasurer and got my
coal 1/2 ton and some charcoal, but he said it was against the laws
to charge it so I had to pay down $3.78 6d which from 4.55
leaves but 77 6d. I have 25 cts more owed to me, if you could
find it convenient to send $5.00 I would give you an
account of how I spend it. above my washerwoman Pels
and the Mason 37 6d I do not like to borrow. Please send
is soon as possible, dear Father, I will use it with all
its exertion in my power.
Please tell in the office where to send my proper
Linnet String now yours in haste
Charles

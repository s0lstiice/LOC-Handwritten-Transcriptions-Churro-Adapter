Baltimore April 7. 1843
Dear Sir,
I have the pleasure to acquaint you
that the Board have given me full authority to grant
you the requisite facilities for an experiment of your
telegraph upon the mail road to Washington; requiring
only that they be operated under the direction of our
Engineers in such manner as not to interfere with our
operations; and requesting from you the concurrence,
especially the recommendation of Mr. Latrobe, of the
use of the Telegraph for the benefit of the country, but
under stipulation which dispermised you may readily
grant. Have only to add, therefore, that it will give me
pleasure to confer with you upon the subject when ever
you please.
Yours

Treasury Department
May 15th 1843
Mr:
Your letter of the 9th Instant is received.
I am authorized to enter into contract with Mr Samuel
E. Garrill of the City of New York for the supply of
Sixty thousand yards of Lead Pipe for the Telegraph
at four and three quarter cents per pound at an agree-
ment cost including expense of piping line through
the pipe of Four thousand nine hundred and eighty
seven 3/4 per d Months
The form of contract sent is herewith
returned
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedt Servant
J. C. Danvener
Secretary of the Treasury
Prof. I H B Mott
New York

Boston 2d August 1843.
My dear Sir
The present serves to introduce my
friend, Mr Joseph Ames, the young artist whose
pictures you saw at my house. his pictures I am
convinced, are a better recommendation to your favor
than any I can give him.
As Mr Ames visits your city for
the purpose of seeing new works of art, I hope it
will be in your power to show him some of
your pictures. I should be particularly pleased if
he could see the copies you made of the portraits
of Rembrandt and Titian, while in Florence. —
Having never seen any genuine specimens
of the old masters, I think it would be very
gratifying to him to see them so well repeated
As my friend is a disciple of Alston I hope
you will consider him entitled to your best
commend & encouragement. I am assure you that
he is capable of appreciating and improving by them
I am your Sir, with great
regards. Truly Yours
Henry Greenough

Treasury Department
June 7th 1843
Mr:
Sam Cotten of the 3d District is received, asking
the Department to allow certain handling expenses of Mr
F. O. J Smith on business connected with the Telegraph which
you are entertaining for the United States, and representing
that Mr Smith's legal advice as rendered by you entering
into an agreement with the Baltimore & Washington Rail
Road Company, leaves the necessity for his accompanying
you to Baltimore In answer to which remark, Mr Smith
states the principle involved if legal advice was wanted
application should have been made to the Department
Mr Smith's being a proprietor of the Telegraph as related by
you, can make no difference in the mind nor out of the
description contemplated by the act of Congress
I am Very Respectfully
Your Obd Servt
Samuel F. Smith
Secy of the Treasury
Geo. Spencer
Payr S. H. B. Knox
Harper

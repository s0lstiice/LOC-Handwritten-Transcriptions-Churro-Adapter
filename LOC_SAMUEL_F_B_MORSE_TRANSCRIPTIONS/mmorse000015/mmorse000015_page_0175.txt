The greatest practical difficulty you will
ever to contend with, I should think, will
be the insulation of the wires. Twine is a
partial conductor and by making the surface
of contact sufficiently extended lateral trans-
mission will take place to some extent. The
loop however on this account can only be determined
by direct experiment with extended wires. It will
probably increase with an increasing ratio; first an
amount of the greater surface of contact and secondly
because electricity of greater tension will be required
to send the current through the longer wire.

contact it might perhaps be well to wrap around
each wire besides its continuous covering an extra

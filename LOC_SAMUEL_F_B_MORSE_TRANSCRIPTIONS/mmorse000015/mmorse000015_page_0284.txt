Treasury Department
September 7, 1843.
Mr:
It appearing by your letter of the 29th ultimo
that an additional supply of wire is needed for the
Telegraphs under your Superior direction, I am author-
ized to procure the quantity mentioned at, Seven
and one and a quarter cents per pound at an approximate cost not to exceed
Two hundred and thirty eight dollars
The discharges of the Laborers stated in
your letter and the reduction of the wages of Lewis
6 weeks are sanctioned
I am Very Respectfully
Your Obdt Servt
Mc C. Danry
Actg Secy of the Treasury
Ref'd to Samuel F. B. Morse
Superint'd of Electrical Telegraphs
Fort Jackson

I had to borrow 25cts yesterday to pay postage. I don't like
to borrow farther, if you or Uncle R's did would place $10,00
in Uncle R's hands I would give you an account how
I spent it, though I have been aware that it costs a
good deal to furnish a room & I think I have not spent
too much for it. I will soon have to get a Table &
looking glass as Mrs Whitspley will want hers soon,
I thought they to go with the room, but it was the
bureau & chairs. If you could come up here & see how
I am situated you could judge of what I needed.
Prof Worley says it is not necessary to have guardrooms
as customary except when coming from a great distance
I write in haste as I have but little time. If you
should send me any money please pay postage for I
have not got one cent. The books I use every day viz First
Testament & Eulatations, I have borrowed so get, but it is not
likely I can for much longer. Has Mr Frisby made me
a meal I really want one I have but one & that
is not over clean.
Write soon as possible
Your affectionate Son
Charlotte W Mason

Oct 9. 1842
Dear Father,
As I have an opportunity of writing you again to
town by Mr. Munson, I will not lose it. My stove & fixtures came safe
I will have to get a model to put it up for me, that is break up a
small part of the fire place. I am comfortabley situated for the winter
now or will be as soon as I get my stove up & coal which last I
will obtain from the Treasurer there is charged in any item bill.
I will give you below a list of my further expenses, $10 would not
have fitted me out quite, one of my companions from Mr. Jones
has given $15 for his fit-out and thinks it quite reasonable I believe
to have made the best use I could of my money, but let you
judge I here subscribe those occurred since my last.

Having I have just seen reason to Walker who said that he

of the $2.00, I would still owe for 6 cts, so if you sent me $3 I would

to pay weekly, if they should I would let you know I have borrowed
an oil can, but will have to get one tomorrow or next day they
come to 25 cts. I will also have to purchase a Greek Testament.
My time for writing here is very limited, and therefore you must excuse
all mistakes, in consistances, as I have no time to write deeply or clearly
to think.

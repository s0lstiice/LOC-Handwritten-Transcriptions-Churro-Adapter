Treasury Department
April 14. 1843
Your letter of the 10th instant is received
enclosing an account and vouchers of expenditures under
the act of Congress the 3rd of March 1843 "to test the practicability of the system of Electro-Magnetic Telegraphs
for the United States"
If you have charged for months salary of yourself and assistants commencing on the 10th of March and
ending on the 16th of the present month, it is not perceived how any person could properly be employed prior to the appointment of the Department at least prior to the date of your letter of the 27th of March suspending the
employment of assistants. The plan was not approved until the 14th of March. Under these circumstances I have respectfully to report our explanation from you in the subject
Paul Saul F. B. More
Sam Jackson
Samuel Spencer
Secretary of the Treasury

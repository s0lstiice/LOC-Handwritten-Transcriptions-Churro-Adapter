U.S. R. House
Watertown June 5th 1843.
Mr. Sir
We have this day sent a cart containing
805 lbs Wine we expected to have been able to have sent about
1500 lbs to day but found on examining some of the Wine that
although it was good it was not as good as we could wish
consequently we have only sent that which we knew to be first rate.
We were disappointed about receiving some Copper from Baltimore
and may not be able to send any more before about the 12th inst.
If you could give us some idea as to the quantity that will
be wanted viz whether 10 or 1500 lbs we should be glad of it
Respectfully I am
President of Bank and Mfg Co
for P. S. Mitchell Esq
Ami June 10th 1843

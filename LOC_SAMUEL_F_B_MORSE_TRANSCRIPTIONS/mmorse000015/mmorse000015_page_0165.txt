In the capacity of the Treasury has
offered to pay, as an appointment of yours as one of my
assistants under me, one of Congress for making
my own expression of my Telegraph, to your salary
is fixed at the rate of fifteen hundred
dollars per annum payable monthly.
and to be received from the date of your appointment
on the 10th of March current
which would be by the accounting
officer of the Treasury Department --
and
to be continued
His understanding that even when
time & service are to be devoted to the same
time of the Treasury, and the remuneration
of the discomfiture experienced by your
self while thus employed, are to accrue
to the benefit exclusively of the proprietors
of the Telegraph.
Very respectfully
Your obedient Servt
S. P. B. Mease.

Sam'l P. Morse Esq
Sir
The following are the terms for
which I will Contract to make your Wire
1st I will put on the first covering for $1.00 per mile you furnishing
the materials
2nd I will Crown & finish all the materials for $5.35 per mile
of the best quality cotton twine of the long size saturated in
India rubber & tar
3rd I will then Cover the four strands together for $2.15 per mile
and finish all the materials, as above
4th I will do the labour alone for $3.50 per mile
5th I will divide it into four equal parts, do the coloring, and
furnishes the material for $1.00 per mile, or the labour for 75c. per mile
6th I will wind your wire & mat the Reels for
7th I shall require four months after receiving the wire
to deliver it completed in any part of the City of New York
that you may designate — free of Charge
Respectfully Yours
Ebenazer Chase
NY

Mr. Elton Brown
you may send me forty four pounds
of pure copper wire No 20 wiregage at 35 cents per pound
& twenty lbs No 24 at 38 cents per lb the wire must be
free from flaws & well annealed.
Let me have it by the end of the week
if possible, or beginning of next week
your At Servt
Samuel B Morse
New York April 1st 1843

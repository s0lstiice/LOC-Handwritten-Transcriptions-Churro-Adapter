Watertown May 22. 1843
Prof S. C. Fisher
Rob of the Line dist 8 Bunchaw Mj C
1120 Oz Silver Soldn @ 110 $12.21
we send you above Bill of
Silver Soldn as low as we can
make it. until we understand
better. Am people that som times
make use of Silver Soldn think
that a greater proportion of Brash
Would answer a better purpose
& we intended to have sent you
a sample but have not been able
to get it down. we find difficulty
in mixing 1/3 Brash with Silver. it
goes to dry so much. Your Truly
184 Bunchaw Mj Cd

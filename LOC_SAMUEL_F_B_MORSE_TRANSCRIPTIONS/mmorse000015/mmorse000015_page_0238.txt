Treasury Department
June 13. 1848
Samuel
to:
Your letter of the 9th instant with its enclosure
is received. You are authorized to enter into contract
with Mr. Zai I Bartlett of Ka Jai, for Laying
down the Telegraph pipe and covering the
same for the distance between Baltimore and Washington
at an approximate expense of Six thousand one hundred
thirty dollars. The blank contract is herewith
returned.
I am Very Respectfully
your obedient Servant
N C Young
Acting Secy of the Treasury
Prop S.F.B. Morse
Super of Electric Magnetic Telegraph
Ka Jai

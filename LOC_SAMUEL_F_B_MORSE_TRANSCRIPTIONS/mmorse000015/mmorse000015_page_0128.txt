Washington Febry. 23. 1843.
Dear Sidney,
You will receive by the proceedings
of the House to-day that Mr Hill has passed the
House by a vote of 89 to 80. A close vote after
the expulsions raised by some of my friends in the
early part of the Session, but enough is as good as
a feast, and it is safe to say as the House is concerned
I will advise you of the progress of though
the Senate; all my anxieties are now centred
there. I write in great haste.
Very Yr Aff. brother
Saml. G. B. Moser

Dear Father,
You see I continue to improve every opportunity
I have of addressing you I would like to introduce you to
the bearer, Mr. Melanathon Wholply, son of the lady who has rented me
my room. He has kindly offered to be the bearer if any letter
or small parcel you may see fit to send me, my post if you
think of getting me would be very conveniently placed by this means
he returns Saturday at 4pm.
Mr. Richard arrived here yesterday to take possession of his house in the morning
I have joined the Unionian Society.
I will have to pay a tax of $2.00 every 3 months for which I am at liberty to draw 4 books from the library, and attend
their meetings every 3rd night to hear debates, speeches & orations
and can also debate do if I wish. Every one in Col is a member
of 1 of 3 societies, Brothers, Catholics, Unionians the Catholics
is composed almost entirely of Gentlemen, the Unionian is the
largest has consequently the greatest power & their laws are
the sweetest in the world for they require the labor to be paid
on entering, but in the Union when it is convenient but before
or at the commencement of the next term. I have had no time
to draw a book out of its library, as my studies require all my attention

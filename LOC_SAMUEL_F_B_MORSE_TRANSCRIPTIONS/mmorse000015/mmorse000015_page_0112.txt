I have been talking with some of
the Senators, and if my bill is not taken
up in the House soon, I think it will
be moved as an amendment to the Military
appropriation bill when the comes from
the House to the Senate, and so be bought
before the House for concurrence in this
way I am told I can get it before
the House if they continue to neglect
a direct action upon it. —
I have the promise of Dr. Linn
of the Senate of a copy of the maps
you wrote for, of the Oregon so soon as
they are published and laid on the
Senators desks, but they have not yet
been published. —
Thanks Cousin Samuel from me
for sending me the Observer. I hope
he will send me one every week.
Send one to Susan also, if it is not al-
ready done. —

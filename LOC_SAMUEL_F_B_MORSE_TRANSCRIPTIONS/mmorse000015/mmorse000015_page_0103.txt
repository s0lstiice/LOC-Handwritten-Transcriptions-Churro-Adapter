I confess to being a bad correspondent; for as my
associations of late years have enlarged, my correspondence also
has enlarged, and I find it impossible to avoid the imputation of
neglecting my friends, without neglecting parties that I am compelled
by necessity to perform. I am waiting the passage of my
bill that I may know how to shape my future course. If it passes
as I am encouraged to think it will, I have a work to
perform in organizing my Telegraphic system for the country
which may employ me for the rest of my life (over)
Mr M

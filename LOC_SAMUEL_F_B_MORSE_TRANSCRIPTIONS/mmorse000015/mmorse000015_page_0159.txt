New York City March 31. 1847
Mr. Alphonso Vail.
Sir. I have submitted your
appointment as one of my assistants under
the act of Congress providing for an eminence
of my Telegraph, and he has offered to it,
and your confirmation will be at the rate
of one thousand dollars per annum,
perpetual monthly. It is understood, that
your ablest time and services are to be
devoted to the perfection of the apparatus
and the results of all discourses and experi-
ments made by yourself which thus employ
me to come to the benefit and utility of the
prospectus of the Telegraph and the transfer
of your salary will commence
as soon as you shall refer yourself for service.
Very respectfully
Your obedient Servt
Geo. W. Vanderbilt

Samuel. F.B. Morse Esq.
Dear Sir,
Let me introduce to you
my young friend, Mr Stewart Hardinge, - He wish to acquire all the means of improvement
in his power, as a student of the Arts, - Knowing well
your kind feelings towards, all deserving young
men who wish to labour in the field of Art, I hereby
hastily recommend him to your kind attention,
which will be most gratefully acknowledged by
I am fixed here in Cincinnati trying to live,
though these dreadful times - Mr H, can tell
you something about me.
I remain respectfully & truly
Yours. John Cranch.
Cincinnati Oct 29t 1842.

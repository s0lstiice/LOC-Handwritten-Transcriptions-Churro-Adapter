Treasury Department
May 4. 1843
Sir:
I am authorized to enter into the con-
tract for the supply of Cotton trains, to carry the Telegraphic
line, at a rate of thirty two cents per pound, the price
quoted in your letter of the 1st instant
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedt Servant
C. D. Spence
Secrty of the Treasury
Philip Samuel F. B. Mase
Major

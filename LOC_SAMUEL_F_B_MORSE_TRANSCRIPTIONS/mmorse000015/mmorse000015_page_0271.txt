Treasury Department
August 15th 1873
Sir:
The accounts passed on in your
letter of the 10th instant have responded to the Trust
Auditors for adjustment and settlement
I am gratified with the result of
the experiments made with the Electro-Magnetic
Telegraph, and trust the Country will have reason
to be satisfied with the result of your labors
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedt Servant
Sam C. F. B. Mate
Prop. Sam C. F. B. Mate
Safety of Electro Magnetic Telegraph
Rev J. A. K
Secy of the Treasury

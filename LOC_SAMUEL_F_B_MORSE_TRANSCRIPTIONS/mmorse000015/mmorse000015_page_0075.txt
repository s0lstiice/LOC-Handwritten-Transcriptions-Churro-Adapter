New Haven Thursday 18th 42
I am happy to inform you that I have
arrived safely in this delightful city, after a passage of
forty-one days. I was doing to leave before receiving your letter
from father -- and as he died just expect to remain in
to the longer than I pass our baggage throug the Custom
house, I took the liberty to go to the Printing office, where
always gives me a hearty welcome -- especially as I
supposed much only in fulfilling for him the duties of
station in Europe. My friend Miss Laurence came
with me this morning all called at doubt if there I
had the pleasure of seeing you there -- whose contents are
very pleasing to me. I shall now remain till your coming
on, which I hope will be very soon, dearest father, I
want to tell you so much, and I hope I can finish
up to the last part of this letter -- I have returned quite
very strong -- and am desirous of getting up for Spring as
soon as possible -- Uncle Chris will give you all
(particulars concerning the Civil, by the division of
the United States) -- and all his family are knowing --

United States of America
Secretary of War
Washington, D. C.
March 21st, 1865
Major-General W. T. Sherman, Commanding
Union Army
Atlanta, Ga.
My important instructions to you
from the War Office
have been received
and I am now sending
to you the papers
which you requested
to be sent to the War
Office.
Very respectfully
I am, etc.
Brigadier-General
George B. McClellan
Chief of Staff
United States Army
Washington, D. C.

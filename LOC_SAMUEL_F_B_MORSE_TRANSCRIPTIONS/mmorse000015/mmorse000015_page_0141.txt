If you will be held accountable for all moneys
paid on your application, you will find it necessary, to be ex-
ceedingly careful to take vouchers in duplicate for all sums
expended by you. You will be required to account month-
by at least for the Amounts received, and no advances or
payments will be made while there shall remain any Undis-
creetable Sum unaccounted for. It may be well for you
to devote one of your assistants, the duties of a disburting
agent, and of keeping the accounts of the Experiment.

Washington D.C. Jan 7. 6th 1843.
Dear Sidney,
I sent you a copy of the Report on
the Telegraph a day or two since. — I was in hopes of
having it called up to-day but the House refused to go
into Com'tte of the whole on the State of the Union so it is
deferred, the first time they go into Com'tte of the whole on
The S. of Union it will probably be called up and be decided
upon. Every thing looks favorable, but I do not discount
myself to beanguine, for I know not what may be done
secretly against it. I shall believe it passed when the six-
signature of the Pres't is affixed to it and not before.—
I meant to have asked you in my last to send me
the Observer and a copy of your last maps; send me them
under cover to Genl. W. W. Boardman, or to Genl. C. G. Ferris. I
wish to show them as specimens of Georgraphy.—
Are there any letters from Susan for me? if so
send them also.—
I have no news to give you.—
Truly J. A. F. Brethec
Finley.
How is Charles?
and Finley?
and Splendidous?
98991 Love to them all and to Sister Catherine, and to all friends
and relatives, in and out of the Telegraphic Postour.—

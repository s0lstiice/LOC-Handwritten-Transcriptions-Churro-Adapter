17th. While June 20th 1842
W. L. Crawford.
My Dear Sir,
In reply to your letter of the 17th of
which I have just received, I have the
honor to transmit to you the Catalogues of the Academy of Design for
the years 1841 & 1842, from the files of
which, especially that of the present year
you will learn the general plan, character, &
condition of the National Academy of Design in addition to this information in relation to the
state of the fine arts in this city. It gives me pleasure to state that it is desirous of
giving notice on the introduction to the Academy of several years since Philadelphia took the lead in the encouragement of the Fine Arts; her Academy of Fine
Arts was the first established in the U.S. and for several years the spirit which animated some of her citizens gave her the name of the Athens of America.
BCA

Treasury Department
May 27th, 1863
Mr:
I am unable to determine at letter under the instruc-
tions to authorize payment of the Land Office mentioned
in your letter of the 25th instant requiring my approval, as you
have omitted to state how long they will probably be required
Reconstruction requires the approval of the Department for
work of any kind for more than a month. If they could
be turned to some other use in the employment of the necessary
time and the matter is approved of for a shorter period
your refusal of such employment is a sufficient confession
with the instructions
I am very respectfully
your obedient servant
J. C. Spencer
Secy of the Treasury
Prof. Saul F. B. More

Treasury Department
June 6, 1845
Mr:
The employment of the person referred to
in your letter of the 30th attaining "to make some parts of the
machinery of the Telegraphic instruments" at daily wages
of two dollars is approved as well as the additional
allowance of one dollar for a week made to the wages
of Benjamin F Taylor. The purchase of steam engines of
alcohol for making Varnish for the vine at an approximate
cost of one hundred thirty dollars is also sanctioned
I am Very Respectfully
Your obedt Servant
J. C. Spence
Secretary of the Treasury
Prof. Samuel H. B. Morse
New York

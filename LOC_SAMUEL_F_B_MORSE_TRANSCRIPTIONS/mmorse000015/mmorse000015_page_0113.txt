I sent you Mr Cathoun's speech a
day or two ago, and remarked on a
note of a singular character and of
some importance. I learn since
that Mr. Benton in reply showed
that the true map referred to by
Dr. Franklin was in our own Archives
and a late letter, the last Dr. Franklin
ever wrote, as I understand, confirms
the U. States claim of boundary line.
To be sure this is of little consequence now since the
matter is settled. Write me occasionally if but a
word or two.
Love to all,
Do you hear from Mr
of Charles?
Y aff. hotly ever
Finley

I should be happy of an early intervention
and an arrangement as definite as circumstances
will permit, that I may have a day before
the U. States.—
To fulfill my engagements I must leave Paris
by the 13th instant for England and the Gt. Western.
From reports I heard last evening, there is a
strong probability that my personal services will
be asked for both in the U. States and France,
but under present circumstances, any refusal of
course has the first claim upon them.—
Believe me, my dear Baron,
With sincere respect
Y. M. to the Lord
P. S. Morris

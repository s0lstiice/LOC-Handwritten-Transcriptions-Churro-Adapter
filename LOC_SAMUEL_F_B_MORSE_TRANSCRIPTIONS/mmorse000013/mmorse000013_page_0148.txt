Boston Jan 2 25
Young E. Mossey,
Sir,
I have the honor of acknowledging
the receipt of your favor of 1st to which I now
reply.
Thinking it due to the Public to my own
scientific reputation that an opinion must of the
origin of the discovery of invention on an Electro Magnetic
Telegraph which was current on board the Fulton Oct
1852 should be corrected I furnish you data to
the Editor of the Post which he was pleased to
put in his editorial department
Having no unfriendly feelings towards your letter
I wishing to avoid some wrong opinion which I thought
he entertained with regard to his claim of the discovery
I wrote a letter in which all our conversation which
took place on board ship are recapitulated, so that he is
on full possession of the fact on which my claim is
founded. I had none any intention of confessing the
right of using the instrument to myself, for I do not
think it comes a liberal scientific spirit to have been
your admitted myself of the patent law. I am thus
far distressed so far as concerns any preliminary evidence
to be drawn from the machines

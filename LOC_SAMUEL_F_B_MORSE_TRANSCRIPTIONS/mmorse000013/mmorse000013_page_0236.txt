(Copie) 29 Rue de Venneau
Dear Sir. Paris. 12. March.
1859
I cannot help expressing
a strong feeling desire, that instead
of delaying till you return in
America you will take out a
Patent for your highly scientific
gunnyle made of communication
intelligence by an Electrical
Télégraphie. You would
take measures for that Effect
at this moment – I for that
purpose take you model now
with you to London –
Your discovery is now much
desired as well as appreciated,
and it deserves our affection

23 March 1839. I arrived at the U.S. in
April and awaited with much anxiety the
formal address from Russia.
The 10th & the 15th of May, however, passed
without any intelligence and on the 18th being
somewhat impatient, and unable to undertake
any other business I wrote by the Steamer

Schedule. No. 1
Copy to the Baron Meyendorff
I desire to return to the United States immediately to
arrange some domestic affairs. My associates there have the
means of making all the instruments necessary for the July
trip immediately. I will engage either to return to Europe
myself (if it shall be deemed indispensable by the Russian Govern-
ment,) or to send a well instructed agent in 3 months after
my arrival in America and sooner if possible who shall
bring with him all things necessary for the establishment of the
line of 20 miles English and will submit to the work. The
accompanying schedule shows the probable expense of
establishing the whole line of 20 miles.

Philadelphia

Professer Hood
in charge University
of Kentucky
to the President
of the United States
from Washington
March 10th
1861
Mr. President
I have the honor to
submit to you the
following letter of
the Governor of
North Carolina, to
Dr. Jackson, who is
the inventor of the
telegraph, requesting
that he be allowed
to enter upon the
United States
Army as a private
to serve in the
Union Army.
He states that he
is a native of
North Carolina
and has served
in the army of
the United States
for several years
and is now a
professor of
Mathematics in
the University of
Kentucky.
I am, etc.
Henry A. Jackson
Secretary of War

I have been hoping to hear from you
in answer to my letter written
31 May, nearly three months since,
I have as yet no address from St. Petersburg, and
of course am unable to form any plans for the
future. They have were to have arrived by the 16th May
and it is now the last days of July making it impossible to visit
Europe this season. The delay has been very disastrous

the future, telling myself in dead silence to embark
for Russia. I hear nothing from the Baron de
Mijendorff, he probably has had no intelligence
to communicate. How will the delay affect your
plans? I have been engaged ever since my return

M. Amyot with Russian
M. Amyot engages
on his part with the Russian Govt.
First: To take the responsibility and superintendence of the preparation and instrument of
all the conductors of electricity or Galvanism which may be necessary for a distance of
20 miles of Morse's Telegraphic System.
Second To ascertain by the necessary experiments for the Govt. all matters required for securing
by insulation the said conductors, from contact or corrosion, To ascertain, the effects
of temperature on the conductibility of the conductors, and the size form and number
of batteries, and magnets, and all other scientific matters necessary to the carrying
into effect Morse's Telegraphic System.
Third: To indicate the nature and mode of signs most convenient for the rapidity of the
correspondence.

Perhaps in a little time, I may do all
these on a larger scale, in consequence of
waiting, but I do not like the delay.
The more I think of your Telegraph, the more I admire it. I speak
of it constantly as one of the most
simple and beautiful inventions of
any country or any age. I do not wonder
that the savans of the old world speak
of it so enthusiastically.
After all, the longer I live,
the more thoroughly satisfied I am that
it is of little consequence whether our lot
here is marked by prosperity or adversity.
The great thing is to have the talent, like
the bee, of extracting honey from the thorn
as well as the flower. If we are the children of God we can do this, and there is
no situation in which we cannot be happy.

Philadelphia June 1st 1839
Dear Morse
I am deeply penetrated with gratitude
to the gentlemen composing the Council of the National
Academy of Design, for their kindness in offering me
the use of one of the rooms of Exhibition for the purpose
of exhibiting my portrait of Queen Victoria. Do me the
favour of making known to them my grateful
acknowledgements.
I have committed no impropriety
in causing the contents of your letter, so honorable to
the Academy, and our valued Almoner, to be published
in our newspapers.
It gives me pleasure to say that my contest
with the St George Society is terminated. Many of the
members are angry at the award, and vent their spleen
by writing at me; but I am not inclined to reply, so
that the affair must soon die away.
With sincere esteem and affection
Your friend
The Fullry.

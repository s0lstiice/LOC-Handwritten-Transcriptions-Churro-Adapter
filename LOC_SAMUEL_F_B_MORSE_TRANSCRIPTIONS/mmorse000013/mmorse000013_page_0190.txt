Paris Feb 22d 1839.
Hon. F. C. A. Smith
My dear Sir,
I have a moment to write to be in time for the Packet of the 24th by Eider
ferry, and to give you at length a despatch of good news respecting the Telegraph. A few days ago at my usual
exhibition of the Telegraph on Tuesdays, which I have had for 2 months past, Mons. Jullien de Sar
is brought the Baron Meyendorff, the Agent of the Emperor of Russia for reporting useful discoveries to the Russian Government. The Baron was
much struck with the Telegraph, and learning from me that the Administration chief of Telegraphs here
in Paris had reported favorably, he rushed to know if I could procure the report for him and he would
at once transmit it to his Government and recommend the adoption of my Telegraph. I called on the
Administrator Mons Fog with your request as a member of Congress to have the report; I did not
find him in but left a note requesting a copy of his report. I have just received an answer from the
Fog, which although not complying (from very proper reasons which he assigned) with my request for a copy
of the report, yet gives me all that we could wish. I give you a translation of the letter entire.
Paris 20th Feb 3 1839.

Extract from an article on W. MacKinnon's new Patent law bill.
in Mechanics Magazine Feb. 1839 - p. 352.
"Finally, equity demands that those great benefactors
of the human species, -- those "kings of mind," whose exertions
are bloodless and immortal, their envoys of natural
difficulty on behalf of the whole race who apply the diverse
cranks of science to social comfort -- who invent useful machinery -- who circulate beneficial ideas or give impulsion
and premium to staple branches of natural industry
should be protected in the exercise of their talents and in
the fair enjoyment of their talents and deserve. Reward to such
men cannot be too high -- too piously guarded -- nor be
permanently secured. --

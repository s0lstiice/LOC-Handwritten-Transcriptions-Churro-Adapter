P. S. to Mr. Chamberlain Feb 22. 1839
I did not wish to close this letter till
I could tell you something more definite
regard to Russia. I have this moment left the Baron
Meyendorff and have made this general arrangement
with him. He writes immediately to Russia & I return
to America on 23d March in Gd Weather. leaving
America again if necessary so as to be in Paris
July 1st and in St. Petersburg by 15th July. I receive
my answer about 10th. So. May through the Ref.
Minister in N.Y. and if favorable is in order the
necessity of my personal Superintendent. No
arrangement could be made without it. In your
absence I could only act discretionally in view of
the Agreement and was compelled to make my arrangements
without consulting you. Now that will
you do with the agreement we have made? Will
you give me up Russia? or will you go
with me from Paris in July to St. Petersburg and
make such changes in the terms of agreement as
shall be settled to be just between us? If so what
changes will you agree to make. Please answer
this question without delay so soon as you shall reach
this, as you will perceive the necessity of an action

1839
N. Amoyt
My dear Sir
I have but a moment before
the sailing of the Liverpool Steamer to write
you a line. I have been anxiously waiting to receive
my promised address from St Petersburg. They were
to be sent me by the 10th May. It is now the 18th and
they have not arrived. I cannot now possibly pre-
pare for St Petersburg this season, I have not time, in
consequence of this delay. But I have just written to
the Baron Meyendorff to say that if the next spring
and summer will answer as well, I will be prepared
to be in St Petersburg by the 1st of May. How will this
arrangement suit you? Please see the Baron, and have
the goodness to apprize me with as little delay as possible
the result of your interview. I will write more at
length soon.
In the mean time believe me, as ever
with sincere respect
and esteem
Yr Obser
S B M.

1839, No. 4. Copy
sent by Liverpool Steam
New York May 15th 1839
Attention
Medicine to Baron Heidenhoff
(Consultation of Elect. 81. 82. 83.
My Dear Baron I am from anxiously awaiting
your reply to have given me the satisfaction by the 15th
of May that I might be able to proceed at once for my
visit to St Petersburg to make a tour of inspection of
the railway works. It is now too late to have my
visit prepared for 15th July under the circumstances
of the season. I shall every hour of the time, if possible,
make a tour of inspection of the railway works, and
have my visit ready for me at St. Petersburg at the
date of the stipulated. Now, my dear Baron, if it is not
too late to have the trial made in the present year, the
question of being defeated in the campaign may be
definitively settled. If I can be assured that the spring of
1840 will answer the views of the Russian God, I will
immediately prepare to go as soon as possible to St. Petersburg
the beginning of the month of May 1840. I am
sure you will have a pleasant visit with my friend
Baron Heidenhoff, the result of which I hope will
enable me to fulfill my engagement with the Russian
Government. I will not have the goodness to
attend to the matter of the bill as it will be
with me at the moment I leave Paris. I have applied to
an experienced millwright to give me the answers to your
two first questions regarding the expense of erecting a mill
of the character you desired, and the fees for determining
the cost of the Court of France, as soon as I receive their answers
I will have the honor of transmitting them to you. With
regard to the question of placing the greatest value upon the works of
Mr. Stevensons civil Engineer London

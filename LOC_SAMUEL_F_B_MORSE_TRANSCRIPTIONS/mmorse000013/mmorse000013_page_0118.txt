Then the electroorganic experiments of the celebrated Professor
of Bologna, Galvani, caused to be discovered by Volta in 1792
that the contraction of the muscles is owing but to the action of
electricity developed by the contact of two heterogeneous
metals and that this electricity can be promoted in a
continuous current by maintaining in contact these two
metals in an apparatus which was afterwards called
the Voltaic pile, where aerulated water is interposed between
the metallic surfaces (surfaces) is continually decomposed.
Then also this galvanic action of the pile of Volta was
not known in France until 1800, made to suspect in
1809 the possibility of establishing by it aid an electric telegraph
He gave the plan of it in the German Journal of
Scheweiger 1st Series Vol. 2. This Telegraph consisted of
35 metallic wires mantled in tubes of glass and of which
the extremities formed into a point of gold came to an end in
as many vases filled with distilled water, of which the decom-
position was put itself as often as the current was established at
the extremity. These wires represented the 25 letters of the alphabet
as well as the ten numerical figures. These resulted a combina-
tion complicated and confused which was abandoned as being imprac-
ticable. Afterwards in 1816 Dr. Coxe of Phil. announced in a note
inserted in the Annals of Philosophy of Dr. Thompson Young
p. 162 1st series that the application of galvanism will not
delay to gain birth to an electric telegraph. There his precision ended, and he did not produce any new apparatus.

An out-line of what Mr. Morse will propose
to do in relation to contracts for the use
of his Electric Telegraph.
1st. He will furnish the requisite wires for a direct
circuit -- that is, to communicate back and forth -- be-
tween any two distant points, not less than 20
miles distant, and between any more distant
points, prepaid for use -- for $500, or 2500 francs
per mile -- including therein every preparation of
wires for use and preservation, that is to say, covered with the requisite non-conductor, and encased
in iron, a lead tube, or other suitable composition
for their protection in the trench -- The trench is to
be dug at any depth that shall suit the contractor, and covered at his expense -- All other expenses
of the laying of the wires are included in the above price of 2500 francs per mile.
2d. He will furnish a Pat. Duct, for each end,
and for as many other points as may be desired, for
$200 a 1000 francs each -- and a Register, for each
end, and at any other point desired, for the same
price each.
3d. He will furnish the type for one francs
per pound.

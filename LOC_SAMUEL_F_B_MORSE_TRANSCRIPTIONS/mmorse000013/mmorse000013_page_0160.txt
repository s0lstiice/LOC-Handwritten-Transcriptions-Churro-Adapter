draw on you for such monies as I may want; a close
calculation will oblige me to draw for £200 in lieu
of £150, as I told you I should in my last, for it is
possible that I shall be compelled to stay a little
longer than I anticipated in consequence of some
prospects favorable from Russia. The Baron
Meyendorf, the Russian Government agent for
reporting to the Emperor all important discoveries
has been to see the Telegraph, he is very much
pleased with it; and says he shall report it to
his Government; He introduced to me M. Ameyet
who has proposed also an Electric Telegraph, but
upon seeing mine, he could not restrain his grati-
fication, and with his whole soul he is at work
to forward it with all who have influence. He is
the right hand man of the Baron Meyendorf, and he is
exerting all his powers to have the Russian Govern-
ment adopt my Telegraph. To the objections of the

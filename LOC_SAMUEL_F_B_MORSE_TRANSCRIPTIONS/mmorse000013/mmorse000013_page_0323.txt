Will you allow me so far to trespass on your kindness
as to request you to choose for me two lenses, such as
you can recommend; such as I have requested
my friend M. Lovering, of the firm of Mess Edward
& Co. No. 9 rue Clercy to receive them and
pay for them, and transmit them to me. If after
receiving the result which I will send you, you should
deem it worthy of an exchange, I need not say how
satisfied I should be to receive one from your own hand
either for myself personally or for the Academy of Science.
Will
Any communications at any time will reach me
through the house of Mess Edward & Co. 9 rue Clercy
or through the Ambassador of the United States.
With sentiments of the highest
respect I am your
Very truly yours
St B Morris
Sec'y of Natl. Acad.
by Great Western
Nov. 16. 1839

Prof. Morse asks the indulgence
of Mons. Daguerre.
The time, Mons. Daguerre, in
his great kindness, has fixed to show
his most interesting experiments is un-
fortunately one that will deprive
M. M. of the pleasure he anticipated
as M. M. has an engagement for the
entire Sunday of a nature that cannot
be broken. Will, Monday or any other
day be agreeable to Mons. Daguerre.
M. M. again asks pardon for
giving M. Daguerre so much trouble.
March 8. 1839.
From Samuel F. B. Morse
5 rue neuve des Mathurins

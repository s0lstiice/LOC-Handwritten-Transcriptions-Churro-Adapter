Alfred Vail, Esq
Dr. Sir,
Go back to Morristown to say this morning, that I have ordered the two frames of
the frame maker Mr. Wm. E. Conley & Essex Lts. for your
father and mothers portraits. They will be $12 each, the
prius your father cut, and they will be finished in
two or three weeks, when Mr. Conley will send them to
Speedwell, and his bill with them. Mr E. is a
faithful workman and a good democratic citizen.
Please send me on Monday if possible a pair of
rogs wheel of the size marked below to work in with
the wheel, that now moves the wire cogged wheel, which
moves the type: and also two journals (I think you call
them) at any rate you will understand from the
drawing A. All the rest I think I can manage without
difficulty. Let me see you on Tuesday morning if possible,
as you cannot go to Morristown before 9 o'clock.
The Truth as ever
S.F. B. Moree.

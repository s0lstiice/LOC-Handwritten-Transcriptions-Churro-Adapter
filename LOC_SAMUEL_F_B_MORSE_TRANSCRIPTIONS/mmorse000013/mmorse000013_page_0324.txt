Mr. Lovelady from of
My dear Sir,
I have written to Mr. Daguerre, and have requested
him to choose two lens' for me for my
Daguerotype, and have requested him to send
them to you for payment and for forwarding
them to me. Will you do me the kindness to
pay for them and safely (send as quickly as possible)
send them to me. The money will
of course be paid to your house here with
such commissions & charges as are usual.
In greatest haste but with
real esteem
Yr frined Sent
S F B M
send also 6 plates
6½ by 8½ in size

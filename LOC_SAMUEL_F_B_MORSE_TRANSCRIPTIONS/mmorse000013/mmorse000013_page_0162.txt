If such it impracticable for the reason the Telegraph
is a Government monopoly and therefore I am
wholly dependent on it. The Government takes
no commercial or social use of the Telegraph and
the reason why the St. Leads have not taken hold is
that they have not decided whether it can be attended
to. I get no answer yet from the Minister of State. Do
you think your patience would hold out as long?
I begin to doubt whether I shall remain any. Indeed
but for the aspect of things at work, the sooner I return
home the better; I do not see that I can further benefit
the concern at present here except by making it
known to the various men; this obtains honor, and profit
its merits, and profit may be a consequence at some future time. I shall, if possible, make this sort of an arrangement with the Baron if he should propose anything from Russia, viz. that I should return to America immediately and visit Russia or send an agent in
the Summer, for I must return, and arrange my affairs for the change which this Telegraph has compelled me to make. He may require an answer from St. Petersburg, and that will delay me, but I had better return and come out again if necessary, with a more perfected and compact instrument which I cannot get here situated as I am.

It is necessary for me to trouble you with
the more details of the proportion which I wish to submit to
the French Government -- and I will only add, that if
I should be surprised at any time to submit the whole of
the other details, with the exception, such as the terms
of payment, conditions of the undertaking, the mode and time
of communicating in U. S. together with satisfactory evidence
of accuracy of each, otherwise and otherwise I
might be unwilling to do so by the pleasure of the King,
or any other officer of his Government. I have with me
an in force instrument (not embodying my latest
universality) which will satisfy any person, to the
principle and practicability of what I propose to accomplish.
This I shall be most happy to exhibit to you,
up to the officers of his Majesty's Government on any time. In
the mean time, I pray you to do me the favor to direct me
in relation to the further course for me to pursue
to bring the subject, as early as possible, to the consideration of this
Magnificent Government, as if consistent with
your views of propriety to continue to pursue in the present affairs of that
Government, to enable me to make known the objects I
have in view.
I am, dear Sir, with sentiments of
your esteem, your friend
I Am
Albemarle

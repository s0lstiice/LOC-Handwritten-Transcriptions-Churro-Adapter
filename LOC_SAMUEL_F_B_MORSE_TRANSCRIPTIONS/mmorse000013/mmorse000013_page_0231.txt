Paris March 12.
1839.
My Dear Hamilton
A thousand thanks
for the kindness that
arrived I cannot make
up my mind to write
direct to Ld Melbourne, of
from any source at
myself, but for fear
of doing mischief -- If
I can think of a proper
letter, I'll send it over to you
to send it, or hast it, as
you may think best --
I wrote in haste from

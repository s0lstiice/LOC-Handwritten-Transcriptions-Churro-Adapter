New York July 8th 1839.
My Dear Sir,
A former pupil of mine Mr. Robert
Bogle of South Carolina, is about to visit Bos-
ton to see particularly the collection of your
pictures, and also the works of other artists,
and wishes of course to have the gratifica-
tion of seeing you. Mr. Bogle is a young gen-
tleman of excellent character, and amiable
conduct. He is very much attached to the
branch of art he has chosen, Portrait
Painting, and is an improving artist. He
will esteem any hints from you of the
highest value.

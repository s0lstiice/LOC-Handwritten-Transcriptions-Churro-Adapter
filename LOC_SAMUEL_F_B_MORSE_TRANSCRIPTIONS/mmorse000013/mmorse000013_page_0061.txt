Paris August, 1838.
My present instrument is very imperfect in its mechanism, and is only designed to illustrate the principle of my invention.
The vending part -- all in the box that holds the pen -- is made strong and durable, for the purpose of safety in travelling. It is all reducible to one third of the compass here exhibited, and without it all impairing its efficiency. It may be made into an ornamental piece of cabinet furniture, like a time piece, or a watch, or any thing of that kind. It is not intended to be a pocket watch, which would be inconvenient.
My invention was patented by me in 1832, though not announced until the spring of 1837. I have received letters from Mr. Stevens, late minister of the United States, to the President, Governor, as also from other persons, of the date of my discovery.

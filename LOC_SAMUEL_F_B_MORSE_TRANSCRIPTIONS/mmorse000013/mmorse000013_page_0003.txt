Speedwell May 2d 1838
Prof Morse
Dear Sir since my
return and the receipt of your letter
at 97 I have been thinking of the
best and surest and only mode of
getting the Port rule ready for you
by the 16th and it is this. That the
Port rule you have been sent out im
mediately and as soon as I can I
will make patterns for the type, send
them to you, to be cast at Mr Buckley
and sent out again to be finished. This
is the only mode of promptly getting the
thing done. Please send out the small
battling with the Port rule
In haste Yours truly
Alfred Vail.

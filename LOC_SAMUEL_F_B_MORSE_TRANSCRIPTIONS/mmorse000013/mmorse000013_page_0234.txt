29, Rue de Varennes à Paris
12 March. 1839.
Dear Mr. Clerkett-
I make no apology for
asking you to do an obliging act to
a very deserving man - Dr. Morse
is President of the National College
of Telegraph at New York - I cannot
pay you. Not there are now
two such Schools in London, I
indicate at the head of one of them,
I feel it might be very interesting
to them to see these Schools, but
more especially to get into
conversations with Mr. Dyer
also.
This incidentally enjoys
a perfectioning & rendering
Effective, the system of
Electric Telegraph - the
mechanism of communicating
Intelligence by it, is beautiful
Scientific, simple, &c. I will

New York October 25th 1893
My dear Man
It would have been a
great pleasure to me to have found you
here but I trust it will not be long
before we meet. I have just seen
your picture & can wish you nothing better
than that you may be so successful in the
exhibition of it as you have been in the
its execution. — I deserve that you have
not given the full height to the
Manjor of Canon and I trust the
alteration in yours, & that the Brent
have not cut or turned it in had a
thin taste in, they could not I should
think have committed such an error.
My wife & I have determined
to go to Philadelphia before we go to

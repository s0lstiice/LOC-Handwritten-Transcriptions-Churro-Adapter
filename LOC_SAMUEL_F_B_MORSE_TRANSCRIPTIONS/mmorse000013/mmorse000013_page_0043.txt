Paris, Maurice's hotel - room No 90.
July 12. 1838 Thursday
Dear Brother,
I arrived here Tuesday morning, having spent Sabbath
at Bolbec, very agreeably, & passed Monday at the memorable city of
Rouen. The pleasure I anticipated from a personal acquaintance with
our respected Correspondent, W. de Felice, was fully realized. But
of him this interesting family I have not time now to speak. In
addition to the pleasure of this visit, I was most agreeably surprised
to find Mr. de Felice, just at the point of going to Switzerland, he
would have gone last week, but from a slight indisposition was in-
duced to defer it -- as if on purpose for me to accompany him.
He expects to be in Paris to day or tomorrow, & to leave on Saturday
or at farthest on Monday next (as he is impatient of longer delay)
for Lausanne and Geneva by way of Dijon. &c. Mr. H. Lutterott
to whom I had a letter, has given me the outline of a tour in Switzerland
which he says may be accomplished in 3 or 4 weeks. He advises my
visiting Carlsbad near Tiflitz in Bohemia, about two days journey bey-
ond Switzerland which I may do, if my health seems to require it.
Sir. Hottinger informs me that Cousin Edwin Salisbury's letters are
addressed to Interlaken on June, which I find is in my route, so
that I shall probably have the pleasure of seeing him there.
I have not yet called on Mr. Cap, but may, before I set off for Tiflitz?
My health continues to improve. I rode all night for 2 nights on
my way, I was also a night on board the steam-boat, yet until yester-
day, had not a tinge of my old complaint, that arising to imprudence
is walking in the hot sun. I am to-day well again, I shall not care
to see anything of Paris till my return here. But enough of myself.

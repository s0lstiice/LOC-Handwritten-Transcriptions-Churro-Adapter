Master Wm. Lander came immediately to his
assistance, but not being a good swimmer
nor very strong, Charles carried him
under. Your Son Charles came immedi-
ately, so soon as he discovered their danger
related William & soon brought Master
Blake to a footing, while Wm. swam
out. But for the timely & efficient assistance
given by your Son there is reason to fear
that both boys would have perished.
We had contented to let them go to
this place, a Mill pond, only because
he had become weary of their impotency.
They will certainly never be allowed
to go again when they will be in
any sort of danger. It is a little
remarkable that, as the boys were
leaving home, help in earnest & half
in pleasantness I made your Son responsible
for the safety of his companions;
a responsibility which he very readily
accepted.

retired beyond my most sanguine hopes, by a return to
the native air: in a few months I hope to be no longer child-
ish and with a living representation of my dear boys that are
gone from me, I feel that I shall never be to the world
and myself what I have not been for a long time -- this
is selfish papier, but it is a friend -- and need no apology.
I really want to see you -- and if you
will come this way I must go your way and look at -- to see
you drop in upon me one of these days -- By the by, if you
have brought with you any good bread, flat Paris buns just
lay to one side half a dozen for me -- won't you? and
when I go to Paris I'll do the same for you. And if you
have any new wrinkles that can be transmuted to make
the paint go right just can a far from me until I am set
near you.

Belfast Sig.
Tuesday march 19. 1839
From the Board of Trade in answer to your letter of the 12th ult.
the Board of Trade will inform you that the Government
has not yet received any information of the
proceedings of the Commissioners of the
Imperial Treasury in respect of the
question of the value of the currency
and the Government will not be in a
position to give any information
until the Commissioners have
reported on the subject.
Yours etc
Wm. H. BENTHAM
Secretary of State for the Treasury

Boston, June 3d. 1839.
My Dear Sir,
I received your letter of June 2d. containing a statement of your case, which I had
previously read in the Evening Post. It seems to me so plain that I should think it would establish your claims
against all dispute. This is likewise the opinion of all with whom I have spoken on the subject.
I have not yet seen Mr. Jackson, but when I meet him I intend to speak to him on the subject.
The passage from Milton to which I referred you is in Paradise Lost, Book VI. Line 498-501.
Very Truly Your Friend
George E. Ellis.

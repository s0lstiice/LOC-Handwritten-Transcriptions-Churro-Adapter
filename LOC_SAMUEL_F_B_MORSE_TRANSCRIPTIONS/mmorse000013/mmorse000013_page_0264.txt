Extract New York May 14th 1839
My Dear,
The separation of the proprietors of the
Telegraph so that no consultation can be had is I fear doing
an injury to us all. I cannot move a step without such consultation. The Russian Engagement is doubly defeated for this
season, it must be delayed now for a year
I go tomorrow to Princeton on a visit to Prof. Henry to
have some conversation with him on particular points relating to the Telegraph. There are some necessary experiments, and I have every encouragement from him as
to the result.
I am
A Vail
Spedwell Iron Works
Morris Town.
98663
Yours very truly
Franklin B. Mason

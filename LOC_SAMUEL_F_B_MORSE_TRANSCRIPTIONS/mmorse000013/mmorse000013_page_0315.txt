Until. This is very kind to me, & this is made me happy
for, but we never give respect to a perfectly healthy man in his
place -- there is always some bitter enemy to the truth.
One little thing, dear fellow, renders my stay here, rather
pleasant, as all must desire, & as I do not want to
conceal any thing from you, I will tell you what it is.
Always at least one of your fellow men, an able, honest
man can write himself -- he seems to have thought
it quite safe to me, & though I know not, & has seldom
truly told me things to hurt my feelings -- though I have
finally located him politically, I think theology is for him, though
it is the way to treat young ladies -- still it is unfair on
to my feelings to dwell under the same roof with one who
changes his religion -- I am sure I do not see myself
only at meals as he is a clerk in the Stock Office, this requires
a little trial to me -- You see, dear fellow, I tell you all my
pretty troubles? Do not say anything about it, if you please, as
I would not wish to talk against my enemies, or speak against
their faults -- The game calling I should make a visit to
Boston, after I have been here a while, dear fellow -- My young
brother and friends seem the only fault to leave behind me --
stopping here sometimes longer, and I shall telegraph, dear
fellow, to walk in my dear mother's footsteps, to bring up
one good in my fellow men --

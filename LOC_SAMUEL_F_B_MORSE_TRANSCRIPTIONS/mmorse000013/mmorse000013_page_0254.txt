So far as I have consulted the Savans of Paris, they have suggested
to me no difficulties; I have, however, quite as much confidence in your judgment, from your valuable experience
as in that of any one I have met abroad. I think that you have pursued a course of experiments and
have discovered facts of more value to me than any that have been published abroad. I will not trouble you at
this time with my questions, until I know your engagements.
Believing me dear Sir
With the highest respect
Y. Mo, ob, Sent.
F.D. Mum

My Dear Baron
I have the honor to send you
6 copies of the Report of the Academie de l'Industrie on my Telegraphic invention.
I will do myself the honor of
waiting upon you with Mr. August
Ammonson (Caluncky) at 11 1/2 of
12 with sincere respect
Yr most obedient
servt
Samuel F. B. Morse
to the Baron Meyendorff .
Mass. 8th 1839
me neuve du matin

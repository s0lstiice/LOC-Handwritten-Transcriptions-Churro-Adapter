Prof. Morse engages to his part
with Russia.
First: To furnish the electric magnetic telegraphic apparatus of his invention according to the estimate made in the "schedule" handed in to the Russian Minister
on the 20th Feb. 1839.
Second: To return from the U. States by the 1st of July
1839 to Paris and to repair to St. Petersburg by the
15th July 1839 provided this arrangement is confirmed
by the Russian Govt. and officially signified to
him in New York by the 15th May 1839.
Third: To superintend the operation of the electric
apparatus until this action is proved satisfactory.
Fourth: To track 6 persons who may be assigned
by the Russian Govt. the management of every
part of the apparatus.

I, Samuel F. B. Morse, of the City of London,
in the County of Middlesex, Gentleman, solemnly and sincere-
ly declare, that I have invented an improvement in man-
ufacture for transmitting intelligence between distant points and
places, by means of mechanism acting conjointly with electro-
magnetism a voltaic electricity and metallic wires, and of a
system of signs representing letters, words and numerals;
and, also, for reading permanently at pleasure by the conjoint
action of said mechanism, electro magnetism in voltaic electricity
and metallic wires, said signs, at any distant points or places
to which the said means shall be extended by construction and use;
and also for producing sounds at any or all of said distant points
or places, by means of said mechanism, electro magnetism
a voltaic electricity, and metallic wires combined – That I
am the first and true inventor thereof, and that the same has
never been practised by any other person or persons saving,
to my knowledge a belief. And I further declare that it is
my intention to obtain patents in Scotland and Ireland. And
I make this declaration, conscientiously believing the same
to be true, and by virtue of the provisions of an act made
and passed in the 5th and 6th years of the reign of his late
Majesty, William 4th – intituled an act to repeal an act of the
present Session of Parliament, intituled an act for the more effec-
tual abolition of Baths and Affirmations taken and made
in various Departments of the State, and to substitute
therein in lieu thereof, and for the more entire suppression
of voluntary and extra judicial Baths and Affirmations, and
to make other provision for the abolition of unnecessary Baths
Declared at the Public
Office Southampton Buildings
This 22nd day of June
1838 Before me
Samuel F. B. Morse
Testified
Samuel F. B. Morse

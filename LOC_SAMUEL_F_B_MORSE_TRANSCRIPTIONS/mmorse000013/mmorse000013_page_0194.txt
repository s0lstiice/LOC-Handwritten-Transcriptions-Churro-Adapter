He said well, this I will immediately submit to my Government, you wish to return to the U. States, can you return to Paris by the first of July so as to be in it at Petersburg by the 15th July? I told him I thought I could. Well said he, you will return there I suppose by the 1st October to New York on the 25th of March arrive say 10th April, you will receive the answer of the Russian Government through its Minister in the United States about the 10th of May. You will embark from New York about the 1st of June, be in Paris 1st July at Petersburg 15th July. In 15 days the trench can be dug, for we have 80,000 men at command and there can be sufficient to dig the trench in 7 days if you desire it. The Emperor will then be in St. Petersburg, which will be favorable to you."

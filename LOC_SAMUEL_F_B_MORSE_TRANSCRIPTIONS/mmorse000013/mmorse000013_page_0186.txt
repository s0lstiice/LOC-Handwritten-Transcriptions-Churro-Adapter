Hockley is the President and is now at the post
but I shall spend my Summers at the North -
We have had a mild Winter and now it is like
April we hardly require fire - I still enjoy
my room in the University though not as freely
as I am in the College. I am not able to see it without use so I go in
and do much as I please with it.
I have nearly completed my course of lectures
at Muckstown and shall go there tomorrow
morning to give my last lecture but am the
feels me all will often inquire after you
and wish to be remembered.
I am obliged to close for want of time as
I have to prepare for my lecture before night
We are anxious to know has been done
Europe & what the prospect is of final success
but we will wait patiently till
we see you when we shall have more definite
information.
In the meantime believe me your most
Obedient & very humble servant
L D Gal

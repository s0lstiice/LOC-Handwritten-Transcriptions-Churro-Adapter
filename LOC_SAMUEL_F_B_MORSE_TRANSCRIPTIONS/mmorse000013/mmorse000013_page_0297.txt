Extract from the March number
of the Magazine of Popular Science for 1836.
In this Magazine is a letter to the Editor
dated Munich Dec 23, 1836, in which
are these passages, the letter begins by
stating "At a recent meeting of the Acad of Sciences of this place (Munich) Prof.
Steinheil communes, to the Section of
Math. & Nat. Philos. an extract from a
letter he had received from Prof. Gauss of
"Gottingen. &c." This letter describes experiments made with Induction and
modern magnets electrical machine apparatus. Prof. Steinheil proposes "to employ it as a galvanometer," to operate a
magnetic needle.
"Prof. Gauss has proposed applying this to telegraphic communications in as much as the bar of a second similar
to the first is placed in the magnetic field of the first, and the current in the second is excited by the
magnetic induction from the first, the current in the second will be in the same direction as that in the
first, and if the two bars are connected in series, the current in the circuit will be doubled."

My dear Sir,
Will you permit me to have a
copy of your report
on electro magnetic telegraphs
It has been requested
by a member of the Council of the U.S.
to which I have written, as a document of great
interest, the subject of establishing
an electric telegraph being before them
for consideration.
I will not say that if any expense
is incurred in making the copy, I will
most cheerfully defray it.
This day goes off till I and also
at 3 o'clock; It would give me great
pleasure to see you or any friends whom
you may send to be present.

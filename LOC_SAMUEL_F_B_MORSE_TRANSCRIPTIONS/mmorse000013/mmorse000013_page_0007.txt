If we have elected some new members, it
is only proof that we wished for change or rotation in
office; not to rebuke the President or those not reelected.
If it be doubted that we did not reelect the President, his in
firmness and the intention of the President to go abroad were
the reasons.
With regard to the members of Council, one of
them does not reside in New York and consequently cannot be
expected to perform all the necessary duties of his office; another
has been long in place, which was all that could be said against
Mr. Ingham; and (although it may not have been his fault) another
was blamed for allowing it out of 13 of his pictures to be placed
upon the line and in the best lights.
We are aware how difficult must be the task of pleasing
every one. We hope, Sir, that you will view what has been
done, more favorably, the disclaim any intention of creating
a party or any distinctions whatever, and with the best wishes
towards the members of the Academy, and yourself individually
We remain

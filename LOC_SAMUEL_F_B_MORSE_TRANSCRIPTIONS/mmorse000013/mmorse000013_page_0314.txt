My Dear Father, As London feels effects of leaving us in
memory for Boston, I believe I will avail myself of this
opportunity to send you a few lines in reply to your
last letter of the 24th inst., and leave my address
in the mail of Boston. — Your letter containing information
of the arrival of Mr. Thompson at Norfolk, arrived last
evening, and will you believe me, dear father, it made me
feel sick, though my friends are kind to me, they do
long to see you again, and so with you. — When shall
you come here, or have you come? I pray God may
heal us of double consumption for the sick. — I hope
you will get a conclusion to commence. — For all your friends
in America I am much. — Since I received your letter I have
gone to the office of the Boston newspaper, and have
written a letter to the editor of the Boston Daily Record
and the Daily American of Boston, and have
sent them copies of my letter to you. I am sure I will
hear from you soon. I am your affectionate son
and I shall always be your loving son.

I do not mention this in the way of complaint, but merely
to show that I also have been compelled to make great sacrifices
for the common good and am willing to make more
yet if necessary. If the enterprise is to be pursued, we must
all in our various ways put the shoulder to the wheel.
I wish much to see you and talk over all matters, for
it seems to me that the present state of the enterprise
in regard to Russia affects vitally the whole concern.
Excuse me at present for I have not recovered
from the effects of the voyage.
Truly your friend's Sust as ever
Sand J. B. Morse

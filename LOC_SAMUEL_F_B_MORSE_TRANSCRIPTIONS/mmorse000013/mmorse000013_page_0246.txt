Prof. Morse with
M. Amyot.
3 Prof. Morse engages on his
part with M. Amyot.
To give and to pay over to M. Amyot one
seventh part of the sum stipulated to be paid
by the Russian Government as my compensation
for the use of my Telegraphic System in the Russian
Dominion, so soon as such compensation shall have
been paid over and second to me by the Russian
Government for providing that the terms of the agreement made
this day between M. Amyot and myself shall have
been fulfilled, on the part of M. Amyot.
Paris March 1839

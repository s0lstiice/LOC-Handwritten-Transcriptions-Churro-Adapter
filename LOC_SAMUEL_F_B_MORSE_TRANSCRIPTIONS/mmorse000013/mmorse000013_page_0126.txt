Can any one but God fully understand what we express
by the term "the nature of things," there are impossibilities in the nature of things, which God cannot
do. And how do we come at any time to any knowledge of the nature of things, but from the study of facts within our own limited knowledge, facts which God is the originator of. Why cannot a dolphin live upon the land, or a lion in the water, it is contrary to their nature. What is the nature of contrast, it is opposition of extremes. Great, small; light, dark; hot, cold; good, evil; attraction, repulsion, &c. So that one extreme being given the other is in the nature of things implied. We cannot consider the North polarity of a magnet, the South polarity implicitly implied; so in the case of the cannot, the will implicitly implied will not, obedience, disobedience, Conformity to the requirements of a law, non-conformity to its requirements So that the very existence of law implies the possibility of resistance to law
and is it not the best proof the nature of the case can possibly furnish that a thing or fact existing, which is incompatible with the
existence of God, must be the result of accident or necessity.

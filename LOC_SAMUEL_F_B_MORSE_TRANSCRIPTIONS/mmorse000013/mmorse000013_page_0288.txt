1839.
1839. Copy to Baron Meyendorff
sent by Mr. Lovelock
F. Weston Aug 1. 1839. New York July 31. 1839.
My dear Baron,
I had hoped to have the gratification of a letter from you by the return of some one of the English Steamers, but hitherto I have been disappointed.
I hear nothing from St. Petersburg and the state of suspense is becoming exceedingly painful as well as disastrous to me in forming my plans for the future. In the mean time with the limited means at my command I am having made a more complete and compact Telegraphic apparatus, and shall intend visiting Washington with it the coming winter. I shall keep myself in readiness to fulfill my engagement with Russia, provided the advice from St. Petersburg shall arrive in due season.

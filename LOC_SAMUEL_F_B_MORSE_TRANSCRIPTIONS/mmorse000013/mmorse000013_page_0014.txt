The Board of
Directors of the Municipal Library Association of
this city, having learned that you intend spending
the Summer in Europe, presume to address you,
although in a foreign land, and surrounded by so
much to interest the eyes of a stranger, and particu-
larly one, who views new scenes with the
feetless, an artist only can experience.
Our aim,
is to remind you that we have indulged the
hope, since the receipt of your reply to a former
communication, on the subject, that during the
next winter, you might be waited to detain
one or more Lectures on the subject of the
Fine Arts, before our Association.
Should you return to this Country
we shall appear in our Lecture Room

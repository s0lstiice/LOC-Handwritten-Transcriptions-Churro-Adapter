Feb 13th 1839
On the 13th of February in a letter to Mr
Scientific Professor Morse writes;
"The Baron Meyendorff, the Russian govern-
ment agent for reporting to the Emperor
all important discoveries, has been to see the
Telegraph; he is very much pleased with
it; and says he shall report it to his Gov-
ernment. He introduced me to M. Amiot
who has proposed also an Electric Telegraph,
but upon seeing mine he could not re-
strain his gratification, and with his
whole soul he is at work to forward it
with all who have influence. He is the
right hand man of the Baron Meyen-
dorff, and he is exerting all his forces
to have the Russian government adopt
my telegraph. To the objections of
the various Savans who were present
yesterday at the experiment, that
the great difficulty was with the

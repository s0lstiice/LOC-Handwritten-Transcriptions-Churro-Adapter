I thank you, my dear Sir, for the kind
offer you have had the goodness to make
me of permitting me to come to see your
admirable experiments of which I shall
profit myself more willingly, so soon
as I may have the very recent distress
affliction which now occupies my mind will
permit.
accept my hearted assurances
of the distinguished consideration
of your devoted Servt
Alphonse Joy

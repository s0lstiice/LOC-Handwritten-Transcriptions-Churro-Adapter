in this respect as to take the steps here in Europe in which I
have taken. Having done what I believed to be duty I
can leave the result to the disposal of him who sees the
end from the beginning, and who does all things well.
In that anxious case does not pray upon me for I have
no care but to be found always ready for duty.
You will see that whether prosperity or adversity, as we
are accustomed to term s the opposite conditions of life, shall be
of nothing to me in this, as in every other affair, all will
be well. My brother Richard was in Europe some weeks
ago and I am intended to visit you at Gibraltar but
my health is his principal object, he, by the advice of
the physician, has gone to Naples in the middle of
France whose mineral waters are recommended in
complaints like his. I have just received a letter from
him and am happy to learn that he is materially better.
I regret to learn by your letter that Queen Albany's
health is not so good. I give my kindest regards to her
and sincere wishes that she may be restored to him health.
I wish we could all meet in Paris, but wishes are vain.

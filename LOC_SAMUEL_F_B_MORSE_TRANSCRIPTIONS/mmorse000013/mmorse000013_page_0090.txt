But besides this full length, the Muse has in this edition a view of the Statue in an
Italian bust, a view of St Peter at Rome, & the Brandts Cannon, with the land figures;
any one of which would place him in the premier class of artists. In the fine execution
of these, both we have here about the splendid talent of Smith, Leydig, Lech, Le
Brancart, Wilson, Durand, Houdin, &c. Their art at present this moment present to memory,
though widely differing. If you consider a fine compliment they pay
your dear father, I wish I have some one or two, but alas there's
a great dearth just at present. Mr Smith, I hope will bring me at least letters
from you -- as he writes the 3rd of November -- I shall begin to look for him soon
to-day. I am going away this evening to South Orleans, where I will be at the
same place of the 9th of December and I hope in three weeks, at that, my
dear father will give me a letter. I do long to see you so much, remember
that I am still among my family in the Spring, &c. I hope you will be home
before that time. But it must be very good bye, dear father, as I must depart
very soon after that time. I am with my love to the 2nd of this month, &c. & his lady, are well.
Their love & family are ever well, & return their love to you.
Ever your affectionate son
Charles Dickens

The Argument to prove
That, no Government over moral beings could exist
which does not include the idea and existence of Sin & Government
is a system of rules, for the encouragement
and suppression of good, and the suppression of wrong, law therefore, pre-
supposes and recognises the existence of wrong, which
is Sin. This is a part that can exist, proves the existence of Sin & God,
could God make a law which does not recog-
nise Sin, it would attribute Omnipotence to God.
When we say God is omnipotent we say he cannot do certain things, he cannot make 5 & 5
12, he cannot make an object black & white at the same moment, the Bible says he cannot
lie, that he cannot be tempted, nor can he tempt any one.
If it should be said God could have created a God without Sin in it, the burden of proof is upon
the asserter of such a proposition. If the it is required of me as the asserter of the opposite
proposition that God could not have created a God without Sin in it, then I have an argument in
support of my proposition, which my opponent has not, and cannot have in support of his proposition to wit, that the

The aggregate of expenses would then be as follows,
to establish an instantaneous telegraphic communication
between 12 miles - viz -
For the Patent Register - $1650, or 8,250sf
In the wire, laying, tubes & laying down - 6000, or 30,000sf
For two Patents, one at each end - 400 or 2,000sf
For two Registers - " 600 or 3,000sf
In trenching & covering the wires - 1200 or 6,000sf
For 500 pounds of taffeta at 1 pounds for lb - 100 or 500sf
For laying for 12 miles of telegraph wire - $10950 or 48,750sf
This sum divided into 15 years amounts to only,
the year rent of $716.66 or 3.582 pounds -
The probability is, that the wires once laid will
remain effective for 50 years, or more, without repair,
if necessary be done to them.
This aggregate cost will probably be found
to be less than the average cost of a single half
mile of rail-road on any route in Europe.
By referring to "American Telegraph's recent
article in 'Le Petit Match' in France", it is seen
that the average cost for 12 miles of the Rail road
from Paris to St. Germain, is $330,000 pounds per league,
& affording a length to equal to three English miles,
it would be 1,110,000 pounds per mile, or $22,000,000 for 12
miles, less than the addition of the profits of one per cent. to an
amount of $98564
This sum would cover the whole expense of establishing a telegraph
communication over the same distance, except
the cost of construction, & laying the wires, &c, &c.
It is probable that the telegraph will be established in the United States
by day they night, in
the same way as steam engines have been established in the United States
for the purpose of conveying mail to the other
countries.

I have given you the darker side of objective spirit.
This darkness enshrouds the inventor only, not the invention.
Want of time prevents me copying out the papers
relating to the Russian contract. It may perhaps
suffice to say that I engaged to leave Europe in the
Eastern, on 23rd March, was expected to arrive by
5th April, to commence the apparatus for a line of 20
miles of Telegraph, if not already commenced by my associates;
I was to receive my advices from St. Petersburg
by 10th of May. Officially recognizing the principles
of the contract and negotiating the particulars with the
Russian Minister, I engaged them to leave America
so as to reach Paris by 1st of July, and St. Petersburg by
the 15th July with my French companion Mr Amyot,

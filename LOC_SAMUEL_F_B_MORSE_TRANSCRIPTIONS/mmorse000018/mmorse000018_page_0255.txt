1866
On August 21st 1844 Wednesday tried experiments on Canal, as follows
Remarks.
The result of these experiments shows that electricity
crosses the river by this arrangement, and in quantity in
proportion to the size of the copper plates at the extremities of the Conductors. The number of cups of the battery
also influences the quantity but not to so great a degree.
The Battery was on one side of the river & the Galvanometer on the other. — Distance of the plates from each
other on the same side also affects the result.

New York - (Date) 18
Sir, The gentlemen who have visted their names
above, being desirous of admission into the Syro-Egyptian
Society, have authorized me to propose them as Non-Voting
Members. This I have great pleasure in doing, as the
Candidates are personally known either to me or to a friend
on whom I can implicitly rely: and I believe them to be
in every respect, qualified for election.
I am Sir
Your obedt. Servt.
The Society's Agent for New York.
U. S. A.
Wm. Holt Yates Esq. M.D.
Hon. Sec. of the Syro-
Egyptian Society of London.

17th July 1841
Dear Sirs,
I have the honour to acknowledge receipt of your letter of the 12th instant
and to state that I have received at the Government and Colonial Office
the papers referred to in your letter of the 20th instant
and to which I have sent a copy to the Home Office.
Very truly yours
Charles G. Lowes
Secretary of State for the Colonies

I send you a copy of Niles' Register which has a good
account of Telegraphs and of mine, but containing some errors
which ought to be corrected if unpublished.—
Give much love to my dear Susan, and her fine boy,
and to Catherine with her fine boy, & dear little Lucretia;—
I hope Charles is settled, and determined to exert all his
abilities to be useful in an honorable business.—
I think of you and your difficulties with Cousin Samuel
I wish that you could see Uncle Sidney, perhaps
a free conversation with him would set matters
right, at present, I fear an unpleasant family
breach. Uncle Sidney is a man of strict integrity and I
think when your representations are made to him, he will
perceive how unjustly and improperly you have been treated.
As it now stands Cousin has had the whole telling of the story
and with the natural bias of parents towards their children
they will think of course that you have wronged him.—
Love to Finley and to Richard & family
Truly your affectionate brother
Sam. F. B. Morse

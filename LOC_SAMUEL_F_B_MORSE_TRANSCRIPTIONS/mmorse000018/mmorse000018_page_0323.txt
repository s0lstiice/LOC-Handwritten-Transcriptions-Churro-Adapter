The following order was issued as
important news of Washington in 1844.
Is the Assistant Superintendent of the Telegraph
Washington: Alfred Vail, Esg.
Washington D.C., Oct 11th 1844.
Sir,
As there are so great interest taken, by
the citizens generally of both political parties, in
the results of the various elections occurring at
this season, you will be specially careful not to give
a partisan character to any information you
may transmit.
If you give a result, note the source
from which you obtain it. If such, you may
anyone let it be touched by a respectable
name, and preserve a record of the same.
Send no mere rumors, and as far as
practicable give only official results, or such
as both parties agree in.
Let no one announce from the
wires any intelligence as sent by Telegraph,
except such as one as you shall duly
authorize.
Very Respectfully
Yr. Ob Servt
Sam'l C.D. Mosse
Superintendent of the Electric
Telegraphs of the United States.

Washington Sept 27th 1844
Friend Mose
Dear Sir It is now almost
two week since you left us and I have had the
gratification to learn by others that you are busy
in Boston, with the Telegraph. I presume you
have been so engage as not to find time to
write. Yet a letter from you would be very
welcome. — We have got underway at the Post
office although our room is not finished yet.
after Mr Cornell left the man he engage would
do no more and we were a week relying upon
the false promises of Carpenters and had again
to move our stuff to another shop — From last
Saturday until Tuesday I was very sick, but
have now got pretty well, yet not entirely. I
have every thing ready for the Election which
comes of next Wednesday, and we intend to keep
it up late on that night, very much interest is
manifested to have all the Baltimore City returns
as soon as possible, and I trust none will be dis-
appointed — In selecting the main wires at the
post office, from the wires that run from P O to
Capitol I had some difficulty — There seemed to
be no difference by the galvanometer which two of
the four I took, all deflected the needle alike

Baltimore June 14th 1844
Dear Prof. More
The request you tele
graphed to me this morning "to correct
in the American error of last Dr Lardner
my register is not self registering" The
American has no report of Dr L lecture
but the Sun has (on Wednesday's Sun)
I called at the American left, the report
which will be in tomorrow's paper with
an editorial -- I call at the sun office
read their remark on Dr L lecture in
which he says your register is self
registering and speaks of it as the very
best -- I have seen the reporters for the
Post Pat & Sun, they say that Dr L
made no such remark as "if it was
his" alluding to you -- Get Thursday's
Sun 1st page

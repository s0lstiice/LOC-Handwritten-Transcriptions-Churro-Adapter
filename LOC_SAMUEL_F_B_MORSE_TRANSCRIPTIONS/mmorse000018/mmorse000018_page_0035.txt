Cincinnati O. June 12th 1844.
Prof. Morse
Dear Sir
You will confer a great favour
on your friend by sending me a full account
of your Electro Magnetic Telegraph. I am
at this time engaged in a series of Electro
tical and Meteorological experiments.
All the information concerning your
brilliant discoveries in Electro-Magnetism
which I have, has been gathered from
the public prints which of course is
in vain
Yours Truly
C B Sines
Sec. B. A. of M. D.

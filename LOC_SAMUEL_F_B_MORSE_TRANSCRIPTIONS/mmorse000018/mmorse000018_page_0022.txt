Washington June 11th 1864
6 o'clock Tuesday.
Dear Rail
I have but a moment to say that your faith as
a Christian will be tried as well as my own, in the blow
up which has taken place. I go before the Committee
this morning. Mr. S. Smith has written me a passion-
ate letter accusing my friend Mr. Ellsworth in the most
offensive terms. Depend upon it, such feeling cannot
cover any but the darkest designs upon us all. I am
sick at heart and can only find relief in that refuge,
which in the hour of seeming hopelessness, has appeared
so often to turn sorrow to joy. Let Him be your hope
and trust, and all will come right. I have no fear
for the result. No time to add
Truly Yours
S.D.B.Morris

Baltimore July 17th 1844
Mr Brown
Dear Sir I wish you would
tell Dr Page that the plate will be sent
in a case to him this evening or tomorrow
morning and hope they will answer
I want you to box up the blow pipe
conserv (in the room below) and send it
to us immediately. It is made of tin and
has a fuse on the top. It keeps in the room
on the floor I think where the paper is
Be at the capitol on Friday next unless
I cannot get ready, if I do not I will
tell you, I want you to put in circuit
the East and Ground, as soon as you
can and do not forget to close the circuit
at the register
Yours truly
Alfred.
MS Pack the blow pipe well

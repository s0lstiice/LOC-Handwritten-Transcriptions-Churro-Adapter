Baltimore July 10th 1844
Dear Sir,
Yours of July 1st & 2 I have duly
received; I have written in the language suggested to you to the day of this.
In answer to your query respecting the wire, I would say that the Benedict & Burnham
Manufacturing Co. at Waterbury Conn. furnished me my wire, and as excellent an article as
was ever made; I found them faithful and prompt Messrs. Brown & Elton of the same place also made
proposals; they furnished Colt with his wire.
I have on hand a quantity of wire all prepared which I am authorized to dispose of
by the Department; you can have it at a sacrifice.
You kindly propose to dedicate the vocabularies to me and ask my permission to do. I certainly grant it with pleasure, and hail your
proposal as an evidence that our intercourse in future will be established of a more amicable character.
I have consulted with Mr. Vail, and I have written to Mr. in the language suggested by you to Mr. Fry adding only the following
paragraph: "The contract will be entered into on his own responsibility and will contain a
provision that will secure to you the rights of all the proprietors in the patent." For it will be received
by you as such.
Very respectfully yours
Samuel D. Slocum
Secretary of War
U.S. War Office
Baltimore

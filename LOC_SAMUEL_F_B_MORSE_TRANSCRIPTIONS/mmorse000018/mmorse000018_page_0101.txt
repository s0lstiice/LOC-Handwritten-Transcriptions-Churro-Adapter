As to the actual cost of construction of the Telegraphic
line, this must necessarily depend on the style in which
the work is done; if no more substantial posts and
fittings are desired than on the Baltimore & Washington
line, and but two wires are wished, 350 Volts
per mile will accomplish it.
Six hundred dollars will consist of single
set of instruments for the termini, including the batteries and large
magnets; if magneto electricity is found capable
of being used, the cost of a magnetoelectrical machine
would increase the first cost, but the cost of maintenance
of batteries of generation of electricity is diminished so as to amount nearly to nothing. An
experiment I am preparing will soon test this point.
The maintenance or annual care will require
3 or 4 persons at each station to operate by turns, one
person at each station at a salary of $1000. — 3 at a salary of $500. — $1500, — $2500, to which
add $500 for expenses of acids, zinc & paper make a total of $33000.

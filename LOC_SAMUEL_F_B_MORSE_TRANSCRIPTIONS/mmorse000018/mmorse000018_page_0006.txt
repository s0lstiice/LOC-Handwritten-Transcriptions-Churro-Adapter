Philadelphia June 10th 1844
Professor F B Morse
Lincoln
I have been writing to
hear of the intention of the Telegraphic association
as regards the disposal of the funds to the government
from Alfred. But have been disappointed and
I would therefore desire you to inform me if it is
consistent with your feelings and the interest
of all concerned. (not without) that project is now
on foot and if its ultimate success is more possible
you may rest assured that although apparently
not with you my heart is there -- In my presence
at Philadelphia I lost all of my available property
and I have lifted up a pretty good heart until this year
when and other circumstances have broken up for me
to such an extent that sometimes I feel I would rather
not have been born -- The connection with the Telegraph
now is my only hope -- and hence the intense anxiety
I feel is occasioned -- You will please excuse me for saying
so much of myself -- and please do not mention it
to anyone except to and to him I have never told so
much as is contained in this letter --
I would add that although I feel reluctant to mention it
that if nothing but an appropriation is made
of a situation could be given to me for the purpose

New York August 16, 1844
Dear Sir,
The day after I last saw you
I left for Saratoga, from which place I
have just returned. I called this morning
at your office but found no communication
for me. Will you send me the statement I drew up about the Telegraph.
It contains some extracts from the reports
to Congress, of which reports I have no copies; and it may possibly be of some
use to me. Also tell me, if you can put
the proposition for the formation of a Company
for a New York and Baltimore line, in the
form I last suggested.
I remain respectfully
Your obedient Servt
Edward P. Fry

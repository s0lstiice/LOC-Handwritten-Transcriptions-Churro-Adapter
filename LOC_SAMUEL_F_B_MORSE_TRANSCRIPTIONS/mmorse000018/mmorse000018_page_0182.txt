Baltimore July 11th 1844
Dear Prof. Morn
By the experiments made today
There must be some wrong connection at your
end — For strange as it is — The East & ground
worked well. & West & Ground & East & West
but when the two independent
circuits were put on. wires at 5 worked my
register. — If you will take your time to
just trace out the wires tomorrow I think
all will come right — I could not get a
spark from the west wire when in the double
circuit showing conclusively some wrong
connection. It is not here for I have proved
it — By the last experiment I proved it to
my satisfaction — I presume you either soldered
wrong wires together, or there is some misunderstanding at the box of the big magnet — Let us
try it again tomorrow and patienty work it
out. When we can get 8 & 7 at 5 and 13 & 14
at 11 at your end all right so that you will
be able only to receive and not to write the
thing will be OK and then we can go on
Yours in haste
Alfred Vail
To E. Cornell

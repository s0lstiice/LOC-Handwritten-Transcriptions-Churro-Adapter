New York. The consent of the rail road Company
to the erection of the Telegraph to be obtained and
paid for by me in addition to the sum to
be paid to you.
I make as I explained to you this proposition,
in order that I may secure for myself the
office of agent or director of the line, as there will
be such a person needed to superintend the
postage arrangements &c as well as the
working of the telegraph, -- and I wish par-
ticularly to make such an agreement with you
in order that after I have obtained for you
the means of erecting the line, (a service worthy
of done acknowledgment) I may not, by parties
who may become interested be put aside and
the office bestowed on another.
If you agree to my proposition I would
be glad if you would write to me imme-
diately, and I will set to work without delay
With great respect
From ofst Servt
Edward P. Fry
Professor S. F. B. Morse

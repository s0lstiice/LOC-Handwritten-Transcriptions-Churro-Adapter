Morse's Electro-Magnetic Telegraph. -
The undersigned for himself and in behalf of the Ass
soci proprietors of Morse's Electromagnetic
Telegraph, informs the public that no permis-
sion has been given to any person, for the pur-
pose of Lectures, Exhibition or otherwise, to use
any instrument whatever illustrative of said
Telegraph. And all persons are hereby cautioned
against making any such exhibition, or infringing
upon the rights of the Patents of said Tele-
graph.
Sam'l F.B. Morse
original Patentee.
Telegraph Office.
135 Nassau St. New York city
August 3. 1844.

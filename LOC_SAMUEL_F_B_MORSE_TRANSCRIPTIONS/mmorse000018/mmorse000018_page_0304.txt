Boston Sept 25 1844
Dear Sir
I am told that you are on a visit
here for the purpose of making ar-
rangements for the establishment
your electric Magnetic Telegraph
My object in addressing you at
this time is to inquire if you re-
quire any assistant in the prosecu-
tion of your project. I am wholly
out of employ. Have been since
leaving the Boston House in October
I should be gratified to be engaged
in any employment which would
afford a humble subsistence for
myself & family. I would call on
you did I know you whereabouts
Very Respectfully
Yr Ob Servt
S. D. Ethridge
S. D. Ethridge

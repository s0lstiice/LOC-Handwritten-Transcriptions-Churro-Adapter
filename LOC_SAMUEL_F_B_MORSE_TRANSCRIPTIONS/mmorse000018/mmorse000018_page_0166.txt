Baltimore July 10, 1844.
Dear Sir,
I have this moment received
advices from my partners in the Telegraph,
and I am able to say, that one of the proprietors
will contract to put up a line complete from Philad o
to New York (reserving intermediate lines, but with
the limitation that they shall at no time be con-
verted into a continuous line so as to conflict with
or prejudice the main and direct line,) for Sixty
thousand dollars which shall include two circuits,
one for simultaneous use each way and instruments
at each end of each, and deliver it completed by 1st
Jan 7 1845 including the right to use the same ex-
clusively – reserving the right to use the same posts
for the construction of the intermediate lines but
never to the prejudice or injury of the main line – The
payments to be one quarter as the contracts for ma-
terials shall become due, or within thirty days after
signing of the contract: One quarter when one half
of the line shall be completed, and the remainder
when the whole line shall be delivered on the 1st day of
January next. –

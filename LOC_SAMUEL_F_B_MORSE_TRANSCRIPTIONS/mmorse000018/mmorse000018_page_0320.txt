I was in hopes Mr Smith would have sent to me by
you some of his dictionary relative to the Telegraphs
has he got them finished? -- And have you one or
two, that you can lend me, I would like to send one
to Philadelphias. I believe there are not many
outstanding bills of much importance, Mr Rogers spoke
probably to you about having the room fixed up a little.
I will get some baths, & Mr Chaloner can do a part of
it, though not otherwise engaged -- I think will come Chaloner.
I believe one thing have him concerned with an eye to
ecoming -- Mr Amy spoke about having a new stove,
I think I can save a bill by exchanging the one they now
have for an other. -- Did you hear anything in New York
as to what has become of poor Mr Cutler?
Yours truly
David Burbank.

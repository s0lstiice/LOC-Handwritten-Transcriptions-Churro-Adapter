1 have made a proposition to Mr. Burbank
to sell out my share of the Patent right for the unexpended time of the Patent for One hundred & ten
thousand Dollars, to be paid over & secured to me in 6
months from the date of proposition. I am fully aware
that this is not half what I should get, but for
the sake of having my mind free for a general supervision
of the system and to perfect it, I will face
so the prospects of greater remuneration.
You will see by the Batt. Pat. of yesterday which I sent
you, an article which will be favorable to your plans. The
feeling at the Department as regard to the Telegraph is
more enthusiastic than ever. They say God must have it.

Washington July 10 1840.
Señor
I have the honor to report for the approval
of the Department my appointment this day of
Henry J. Rogers, of Baltimore to take charge,
of the Washington terminus of the Telegraph.
Mr. Rogers is a man of excellent character
and capacity and has for some time been preparing
to disburse himself for the duties of this office. — I propose
to give him two dollars and fifty cents per month.
Very respectfully
Geo. M. Bliss
Secy of War
Sam T. B. Brown
Y. O. Secy
To Hon Geo. M. Bliss
Secy of War

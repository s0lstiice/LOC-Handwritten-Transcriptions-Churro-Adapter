Baltimore July 1st 1844
Dear Sir, In reply to your query for what
sum I would sell my share of the patent right
in the Telegraph, which amounts to one half,
frankly say that if One hundred and ten thous-
and dollars shall be secured to me in cash or
current funds in the United States or stocks at cash
value, so that I may be disposed to accept of present
value, I will assign over all my rights and
privileges in the Telegraph in the U. States.
offer it at this price, not that I estimate
the value of the invention so low, for it is perfectly demonstrable that it is not half its value,
but that I may have my own mind to be
occupied in perfecting the system, and in a
general superintendance of it, unembarrassed
by the business arrangements necessary to
secure its utmost usefulness and value.
If you are disposed to negotiate this
matter, and find me susceptible at that
price, I will give you the refusal of this offer
for four months from date, that is to say till
the 1st of November 1844, and will make

Washington D. C.
August 15th 1844.
The United States
To Henry J. Rogers, U.S.
For trial of Watchmaker oil
for use of Electro Magnetic
Telegraphs 50/100 Dolls
Red Washington August 15th 1844
of J. F. B. Morse Superintendent

Fifty cents in full of the above.

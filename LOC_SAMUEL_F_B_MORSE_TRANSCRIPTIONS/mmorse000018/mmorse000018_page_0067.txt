Baltimore June 21st 1844
Dear Prof. Thomson
I arrived safely this
morning and in about 2 hours after had
the car unloaded and Mr. Avery is now
cleaning up the dirty sines and putting
them in order (all that were dirty) -- While
I was very much engaged this morning I got
Mr Burbank to inquire about the copper dies
Mr. McKin will furnish the dies at 50cts
per lb. cut out, which is 5 cts advance on
sheet copper. This would make a great saving.
The holes in the centre Mr. McKin cannot do
But Mr Burbank can punch the holes at
a cost of $2 per day & $1.00 for a tool to punch the
holes. -- The labour will not I think exceed much
of over a day -- The acids is with Stabler & Lanby
and they will do what they can with it. Mr B
will take the acid elsewhere if SL & C will not do
the thing that's nice -- Mr Burbank has balls of
cotton thread and will do the best he can.
Mr Avery is extremely anxious to see his family
and would receive as a great favour of you

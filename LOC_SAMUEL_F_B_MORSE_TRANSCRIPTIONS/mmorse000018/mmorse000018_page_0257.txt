My dear Sir
The Mail says you want a letter to him from
me, and have my ass made up.
The reason I have delayed writing for a few days, is that
when I did so, I might have some what definite to write. I
am duly in hopes for your thing of that kind. I can
obtain you. I have spend neither time nor money, to letter
in. The great difficulty, the only difficulty in
the way, is the danger of injury from mischievous persons, &
my friends in New York have for several days been trying
to en if this can not be accomplished, or come by Insurance

in thin Charter. So the bugbear remains. I am

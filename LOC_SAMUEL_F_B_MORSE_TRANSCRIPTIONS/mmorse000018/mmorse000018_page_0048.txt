Washington
June 12, 1844
Prof Morse. Doctor.
I suppose you as very busy
at present as well as myself. Please
let me know when you will be at
leisure to consult about the machine.
I find somewhat to my surprise that
it will take about $60. worth of copper
to make the helices, independent of
the work upon them. Mr. Tonge is ready
to go to work upon them, but I should like
to see you or hear from you before he com-
mences. I thought at first I would take
this part of the expense myself, but I am
not able to undertake it in the above view
of the case
Prof. Morse.
Yrs truly
Has G Sage

Baltimore July 5th 1844
Dear Prof - Moore
I have been anxiously waiting
for some communication by Tel to day but have
been disappointed. At a little after nine this
morning I got a good battery of 56 cobs ready,
and had the circuit of East and Ground in
connection until 11 o'clock when I changed it
to West & Ground which it is now. I fear
you have not been up to the Capitol today or
that our ground circuits are out of order
I shall this evening put a copper plate we have
on hand into the ground and bring another
wire from it to the battery as security against
the one now breaking of the one we have had
in use - tomorrow I will again try the single
battery - first East & west at 10 o'clock - at
2 part 10 - East & Ground - 3 1/2 at 11 o'clock
West & Ground - The cars left this morning
before I got ready to try the battery Avery
is not here - Will you write to me in the
morning and let me know if you were at
the capitol today - I wrote to you this morn
ing
Your Most Obl Servt
Alfred Bail

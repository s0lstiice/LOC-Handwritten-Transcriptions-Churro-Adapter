Baltimore Sept 10th 1844.
The United States
To Henry J. Rogers Jr
For 31 Days Service as Assistant
Superintendent of Telegraphs
at $2.50 per diem $77.50
Baltimore September 10th 1844. Received
of Professor S. F. B. Morse Superintendent
of Telegraphs for the United States,
Seventy seven 5/100 Dollars in full for
Service as above.
Duplicates.
Henry J. Rogers
Assistant Super. of Telegraphs
for the United States.

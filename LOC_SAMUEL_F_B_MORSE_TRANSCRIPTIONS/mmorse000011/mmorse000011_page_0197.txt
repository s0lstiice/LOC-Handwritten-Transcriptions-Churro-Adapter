July 16th 1834
Geo. Blake, Esq. & Co.
Dear Sir,
Your favor of the 1st inst. in reply to mine of the 3d ult.
is just received. Of the inability to communicate the necessary funds
for a cash payment for my picture of the Louvre is the only obstacle in the way of your owning
it, perhaps the mode of payment can be so arranged as to
accommodate both of us, and enable you to purchase
a very important picture for it in Paris which I have with me at this home
was 490 francs, making the cost with duties 600
counting over 100 dollars, I have conversed with many
frame makers in this city on its value, not one has appraised it
lower than 150 dollars. This was not included in the price of the picture, as having a canvas of the same size
I intended filling it with a new work. I have affixed the price of
1200 dollars for the picture, and 900
dollars for the frame, and take your note with interest
for 1200 without the frame. With interest payable in New York in one year from the date
of the agreement. Your note will answer my purposes.
The picture as I observed in my former letter is in the most
perfect order and is finished. I should wish to see it properly
placed, and would consider it a duty to accompany it.
There is no difficulty to be apprehended in the transportation, the pic-
ture is in good condition and will be safe in transit.
Yours ever
Geo. Blake

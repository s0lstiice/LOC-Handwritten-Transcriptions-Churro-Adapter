This seems to be the return of our beautiful institutions,
not an excited operation of our beautiful institutions,
except in the supposable case of universal demoralization,
an absence of principle, and a prevalence of ignorance; and of such a state of things, there
is little prospect, while the benevolent are so active in
their various societies for the spread of religion, and
scientific knowledge. No, my dear General, you have
lived to see your favorite principles triumphant in
our country, they have withstood every enemy, and
have come out unscathed, and you have the glorious anticipation that they must and will triumph throughout the world, though all the tyrants of the earth league together to crush them.

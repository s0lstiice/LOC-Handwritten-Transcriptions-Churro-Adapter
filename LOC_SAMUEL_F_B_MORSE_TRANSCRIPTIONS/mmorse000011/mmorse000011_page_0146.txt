New Haven Nov 25th 1833.
My Dear Aunt
Mrs Beech arrived here last Saturday, and
we are all very glad to see her for she has been absent a month.
My meeting dress I have received safely also the pink
lining was not in it and as it has no cape and is very scant
I have concluded to have an English man's cloak for all the scholars wear
them, and it will last me a long time. Will you please Aunt have
me made a couple of long night gowns and a couple of flannel
petticoats. I meant to have had it done when I was at New York but
entirely forgot it, and when you send them to me please put in a dozen skeins
of fine knitting cotton and eight of white woolen yarn the reason I wish to send
Charles and myself each a pair of cotton stockings and Charles a pair of stockings
and both of them a pair of mittens. I have sent Charles a pair of stockings

I have not to mention that one day, while
in the rue Burenne - I was studying from my
own face reflected in a glass - as is often done by
young artists, when I remarked, how grand
it would be if we could invent a method of
fixing the image on the mirror. Prof. M. replied
that he had thought of it, while a pupil at Yale
college & that Professor Schleman (I think) and
himself had tried it, with a dash of nitrate of
silver on a piece of paper - but that unfortunately
it made the light dark & the shadows light,
but that if they could to reverse, we should
have a fac simile like india ink drawings?
I had they thought of using glass, as, in doing
the Daguerreotype, he would have been perfectly
anticipated - certainly the Photograph.

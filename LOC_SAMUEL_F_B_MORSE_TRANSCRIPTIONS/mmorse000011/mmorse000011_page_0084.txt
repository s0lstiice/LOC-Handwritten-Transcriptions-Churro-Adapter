convey may not be interesting to you, as relating
to two of your fellow-labourers in the Laurel
Mr. Royalter & my eldest daughter -- who have married
about three weeks since, &c are now gone to settle
in London, where Mr. P. has taken a house in
Poets Corner, Westminster -- I was sorry not to
have the pleasure of seeing you before you left Paris,

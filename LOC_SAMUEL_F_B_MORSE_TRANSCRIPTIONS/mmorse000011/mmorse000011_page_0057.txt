my Business is very plenty, but not very productive
as I get but small Prices. I paint little else but
Portraits -- I am sadly in want of a Say Figure
or Manikin, do you know of any second hand one
that I could procure for a small price, say not
over 50 dollars? -- Should you know of any, be kind
enough to let me know.
I hope you will find time to write me very
soon, and let me know what you plans are
for disposing of your Labor, in future, at any
rate send me one line, to assure me that you
are really in the Same Country with myself --
Please remember me to your Brothers if they
are with you, also Mr Walker. --
Many every Blessing attend you, my
dear Master --
Yrs Sincerely
Henry S. Pratt

very decidedly, that you now in the time of your trouble have

God is good when he takes away sin, as when he gives.
This is an indication of the fact that God has sent affliction and
trials to test and purify us, which is a matter of concern to the soul which
feels every disease however secret, and to none of which
is the heart pouring out God, may be satisfied by the least
admissions of affliction, unshaken and at peace.
That this peace may be yours, the nearest trial or the nearest danger of my dear
friend
Yr sincere friend
Sam. P. B. Moore

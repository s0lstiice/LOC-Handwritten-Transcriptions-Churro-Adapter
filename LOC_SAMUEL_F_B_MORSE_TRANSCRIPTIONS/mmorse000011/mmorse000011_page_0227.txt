I have thought that could you have been
present & obliged to hear all the remarks that
have been made on that portrait, you would
at least have had it tattered down -- and further
that you would have placed another in its stead.
I have mentioned to Mr. Soune, the bearer
that I should write to you on the subject --
and he will assure you that it is done from
good motives, as he has reason to believe.
I am Sir, respectfully
Yours to

Saturday Oct 3d 1834.
My dearest Father,
The book and your notes
arrived safely the morning of your departure,
and I wish you could have seen how pleased
I was. When I first looked at the book, I thought
it was from you, but on looking at the blank
leaf, I saw it was from the author, and I
assure you, father, I felt quite proud of it, because
I had it presented to me by the author,
which the girls told me was a great
honor. I cannot bear the thought of writing
him a note, dear father, for I have read
in his book what he says, about a young
ladies hand writing, and I am convinced
that he is very particular, therefore I shall
not like to have him scrutinize any of
my notes. I know it is proper that I should
address a line of thanks to him, and shall,
as soon as I have a good opportunity, as
I have not heard of one yet.

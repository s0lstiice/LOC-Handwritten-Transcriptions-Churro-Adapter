My Business is better now than it ever was
before in Boston -- plenty of Portraits & now and
then a Landscape, for variety, but as for Prices,
alas! "nothing to speak of" -- I am most sadly
in want of a Manakin: and Cannot afford to buy
one Should you know of any Chance to buy
one at second hand for a small price, will
you let me know of it? Mr Pendleton offers to
get me out one from Paris for 175 Dolls. But
I cannot raise so much money at present, so
unless I can find some old one. I shall still have
to guess at my draperies. -- You cannot imagine
how much I rejoice at your safe
return to your own Country. I have during your
absence watched for intelligence of you, I will not
say with a Brother's, but with a Lover's anxiety.
-- I wish to come & settle in N.York that I may
see you every day. but on account of my Dear Wife
whose Parents & connections are all here, I prefer
Boston for the present. Still she is desirous of visiting
N.York & I think I shall spend some months there
in the course of this year -- I must not forget to
tell you we have a Splendid Boy just beginning
to talk -- he is 18 months old. -- do come & you shall
see the Beauties!!! -- He are going to housekeeping
in May, or the last of April. and shall have a
room for you when you come. -- I hope you

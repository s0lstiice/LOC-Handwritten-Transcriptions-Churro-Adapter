London June 24th/1833
My dear Man,
I received your
letter of May 20th yesterday, and as
it requires an immediate answer, I
hasten to say that until I am in
America & have time to look about
me, and see my way there more
clearly than I do at present, I
cannot join in any plan of the
kind you propose.
I feel truly gratified dear
Man by the kind things you say
on the subject of my return.
I look forward to it with very
great - but not unmixed pleasure

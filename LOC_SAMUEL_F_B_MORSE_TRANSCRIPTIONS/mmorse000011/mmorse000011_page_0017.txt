Paris September 11th 1832.
My dear Sir,
I have seen the Poles who mean to go to
the U. S. They are twenty and hope to find a
ship who will carry them for 300f each. They
flatter themselves to get that sum, of the greater
part of it, from the French Committee. But
should they go to New York, do you think that
they will find means to be supported until they
have found a place to form their colony upon?
Do you think also that lands in the State of
Ohio, or elsewhere, could be granted to them
or that they will find in the sympathy of the
U. S. a little Capital to form their Settlement?
We must have a strong hope of it before we
encourage their emigration, which they have
much at heart.
Most truly and affectionately
your friend Casparus
Casparus

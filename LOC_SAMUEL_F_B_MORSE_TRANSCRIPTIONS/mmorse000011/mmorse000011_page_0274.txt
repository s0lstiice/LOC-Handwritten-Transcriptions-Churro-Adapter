12 Pine Apple Place, Boston, Mass.
London. Nov. 27th 1835
My dear Munro
I have never answered your letter in which you
were so kind as to refer to the
inquiries I made at the request of
Sir Robert Smith on the subject of
moving trains. -- He was much interested
with the complete account you give of
the process & begged me to return your
kind thanks. -- He thinks the trains
in U.S. just must be better built than
they are here & is of opinion that
London trains would not stand the
operation. -- We are all going on
at much as usual. -- I spent
two months in the summer at Portsmouth

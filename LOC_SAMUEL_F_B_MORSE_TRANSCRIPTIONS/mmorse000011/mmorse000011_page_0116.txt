I send this by my friend Mr Paulkner, as since
a fellow as ever lived you will say if you see him
that his face speaks for him so I need say no more
only if he calls on you, and you can conveniently
do show him your Pictures. I am so proud of
my master that I will he may be more
properly appreciated in this part of the Country, and
Mr Paulkner if he sees your Pictures give a good
account of you to a large Circle of Friends.
Some of them are of the right sort.
I wish I would paint a Portrait of your
and send me, I could make it of much use to
you, as a specimen, and to myself as a study.
If you will do this I will pay you for it I thank
you too.
Please remember me to your respected
Brothers, and accept the true affection and
most sincere good wishes of your Friend &upil
H. C. Pratt.
I ought to apologize for the poor penmanship of this
letter, had I time I would write it better.

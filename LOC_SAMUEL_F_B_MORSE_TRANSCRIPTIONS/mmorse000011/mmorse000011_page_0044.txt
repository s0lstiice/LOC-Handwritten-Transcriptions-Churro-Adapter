New Haven 7th Decr. 1832
My Dear Sir I am happy to congratulate you
on your safe return to your home and friends.
It is an event to which I have been looking
for many months with great satisfaction
and I hope before long to see you and
recount together some of the interesting
circumstances which have occurred since
our parting farewell in the Piazza Colonna.
Poor Cooper! it was a long farewell to him.
I at first intended to subjoin what I am now
saying to two sides of a letter which I began
May 2nd 1831 during an alarming attack of
inflammation of the lungs. I had begun
to recover after two months illness, the last of
which was severe and attended with very threatening
symptoms. I had made more exertions than I ought
to have done in riding out in a close carriage
the day before and I was compelled to break off
my letter abruptly and give up agains to a temporary
lapse from which I only escaped with great caution.

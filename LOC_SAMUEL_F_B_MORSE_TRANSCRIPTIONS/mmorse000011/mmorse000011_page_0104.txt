Paris February 28th 1833.
My dear Sir,
I am highly obliged to you for your
Kind letter and for your publication of my
observations on the present melancholy affairs
in the United States. I see with pleasure
that they have been repeated in all the
American papers, namely at Charleston
So they have been in the papers of this Country.
Inclosed you will find some late observations
of mine in the house. they will give you
an account of political matters on this side of
the Atlantic.
Most truly and affectionately
Your friend
Samuel Morse Esq.

Sept. 7th
I had written the foregoing intending to send this letter
by the packet of the 1st. But the interruption of company
prevented my completing it in season -- On the mean time
I made a little trip to Hartford and New Haven -- I remained
in Hartford for 6 days, where I was so harassed by
company professional and extra-professional that I had
hardly time to sleep. It was so much interrupted too, to
fix myself in that little city, that I began to think
that to gratify a few friends there I must do so. Some
little incidents that I there received even to me quite
startling. And to what, think you, I owe them? Chief
day is a letter of yours which some friends of mine had
had published in a Hartford newspaper -- I returned
to this city last evening, and today left Mr. Cooper's
letter and my address at Mr. Day's who is now in town
I have some very valuable acquaintances here in my
profession and out of it -- my pamphlet offensive
today I have prevented a few copies and shall
soon hear what is said of it -- that is, if anything
is said, yet it is on the whole a very unfavorable
time for fixing myself here, in just after the dis-
appearance of a severe epidemic -- Other considerations
enough with me, with which I have no right to trouble you --
Do Matter to join to Philadelphia
where he is very well received -- I have letter from
him today -- I shall start for home in one 7 days

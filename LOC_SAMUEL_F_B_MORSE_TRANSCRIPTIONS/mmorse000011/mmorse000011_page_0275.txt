Poor Earl does not rally. I
feel his constitution is quite broken
up. He is at Steepleford in Devon
with his sister Mrs Brighton.
Old Mr Maxwell is so kind and
friendly does not look much
attentive since you & I remonstrated
him in Warrenth 4 or 5 or twenty
years ago.
When you see a write to
Allston give him my love.
I heard a good deal of him from
my sister Ann who was in Boston
in the Summer. I never hear any
thing of him from himself.

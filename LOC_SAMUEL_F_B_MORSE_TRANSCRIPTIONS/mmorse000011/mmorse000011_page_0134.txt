London August 27th 1833
My dear Man/ I write in a post haste
in the midst of the bustle of preparing
for a change of country, (no telling event
to me after a residence here of 22 years)
but I lose not a moment in
saying how much your letter has
pained me by the proposal of a
public dinner on my arrival. -- It
is an hour which I can on no
account shelve. I think for one instant
of accepting. I shall I trust soon
have an opportunity of meeting all the
friends you enumerate in a quiet
& more agreeable way to myself. --
In the mean time pray offer my
thanks to the members of the National
Academy for their kind intentions
which I still always remember with

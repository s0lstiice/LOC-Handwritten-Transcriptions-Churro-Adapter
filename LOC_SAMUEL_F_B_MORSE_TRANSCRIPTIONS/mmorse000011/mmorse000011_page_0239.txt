Mrs Peters is going to walk with us to West Rock this
week, and after we have been all over the Rock, ride
home.
Sarah Gocelyn, Mr Greeley's eldest daughter who
is a day scholar here is dangerously ill. She
was prayed for in church last Sunday.
I must now go to my knitting, as it is the hour
for that branch. With love to Uncle, and Aunt,
I remain, as ever
Your affectionate daughter
Susan.

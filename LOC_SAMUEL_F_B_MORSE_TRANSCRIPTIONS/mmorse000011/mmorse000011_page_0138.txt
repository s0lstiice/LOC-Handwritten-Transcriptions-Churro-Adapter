London October 13th 1833
My Dear Sir,
I have the pleasure to acknowledge the
receipt of the diploma which the National Academy
of Design has been pleased to confer on me, and
to Express through to you the gratification I feel in
this honor. With my best wishes for the prosperity
of the institution, as well as for your success in-
dividually, to which I, My Dear Sir
Your sincere friend
J. Stuart Henderson
To Samuel J.B. Morse Esq=
President of the National academy of Design.

S B D More Esquire Alex. DC. Jan 11. 1829
My dear friend
Your obliged serv'ce comes duly to hand
and while I offer you my thanks for your attention & kindness I cannot
but impress an anxiety that you should have been inconvenient to lodge
me - I begin I am going to offer you some trouble on my account but
I need not give you mind without you think what I am about to mention
it is a subject which I am in the best mutually interested. I have had my
two large copies of Guido's Martyrdom of St Peter & Caravaggio's Entombment
in the Vatican for some time at "Bulley & Barlis" in Phil, they are about
breaking up their establishment and before removing the pictures I have
thought best to address with you - they are in fact a trouble to me - the
perpetrators of their Exhibition scarcely pay expenses & I have no time to
attend to them. My object is to get them well located & disposed of in
a church or Academy in the only place for them & if the N. Academy
will buy them they may have them for a trifle, and payable in installments
annually - you know the Caravaggio the St Peter is in a faithful copy
of the original size - I shall, I hope before long go to Europe on a short
visit & I want to dispose of them - I therefore ask you confidentially if
they are not worth 500 each - If you think your academy could purchase
them at that rate I will be willing to receive it in yearly payment
of 150, 200, or 300 as they can afford it - I will not require if they cannot
conveniently do so any thing down, but wait until real earnest for the full
payment - If a private will be done let me know if
any thing better can be done I feel in you I can confide as a brother - If the
Academy will buy them I am certain there will be difficulty about the payment
of the money. Let me hear from you as early as you can with perfect assurance

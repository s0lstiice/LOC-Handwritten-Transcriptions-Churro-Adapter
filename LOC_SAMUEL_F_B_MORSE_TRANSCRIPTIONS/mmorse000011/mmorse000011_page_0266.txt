Nov. 2. 1835
to
L. F. B. Morse Exp.
Master Charles Morse during the last month has performed
his Exercises;—excellently 50 times,—well 14 times,—tolerably 7 times,—and
2 badly times. In the several studies as follows:
He has been tardy at morning prayers 0 times, and absent 0 times,—tardy
at meals 0 times, and absent 2 times,—tardy at school 0 times, at church
0 times,—lost 3 lessons from
He has received 3 black marks, viz: 2 for want of neatness (in room, 1 in
seven, 1 in drops, in books);—1 for disorderly conduct,—0 for ungentlemanly
conduct,—0 for disobedience.
W. H. Minne

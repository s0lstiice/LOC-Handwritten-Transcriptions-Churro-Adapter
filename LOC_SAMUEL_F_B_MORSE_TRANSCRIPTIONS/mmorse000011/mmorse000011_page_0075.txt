The Bishop was quite
disappointed at not seeing
you at the Louvre on Friday
when he accompanied
some Connoisseur friends
to whom he pronounced the
right of your great picture.
— he also has a charge
against you, of causing
the same feeling by not
coming to dine with him
last Monday evening,
to a promise he thought
you made him.
Pray come to my
Is if you can as I know
you will be truly pleased.
also stop in between
services today when
you will meet Mr. Lister
her Highs & Gen. Wood the
former is your Minister
to King Leopold & is a most
distinguished patriot of
yrs & yr friend
Harriet Douglas

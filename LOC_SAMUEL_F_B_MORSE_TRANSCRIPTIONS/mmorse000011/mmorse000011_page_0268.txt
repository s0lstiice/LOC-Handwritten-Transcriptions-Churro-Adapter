May to the 15th of November - excepting some small items
for stores which I have not need an account of yet,
you will see that I have credited the Portraits
in part as I suggested to you $137.50 now - and the
$100- remaining to be credited in the next bill May 15th 1836. I will draw
at one days sight to meet a herment on the 15th inst.
The boys have both done better than usual this
term. Charles has been more studious, and generally
better behaved.
Finley seems to be brighter, if considerably
less certainty has quickness of mind, and as soon
as a little more strength will allow his mind to
steadily I think he will make respectable improvement.
He has received Mrs Skinner's commendation for
improvement in manners and habits of rectitude.
The boys are both well. With our kind
regards I remain truly yours
OD Skinner

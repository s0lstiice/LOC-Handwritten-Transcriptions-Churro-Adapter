My dear Father,
Mr Buick, and Miss Peters,
request me to write now, to you, on account of
having a large sum to pay, next Monday morning
and having been disappointed of receiving money
from another source, they find it necessary, to
request you, dear father, to send up the amount due
on my account, by Mr Peters, who leaves for
this place, on Saturday. The gentleman whom she
wishes you to send it by, is, Mr John R. Peters,
who resides, at No 25, Grove Street.
Could you not send up the money, father, besides, this on
my account, to purchase my clothing? I believe Mrs
Buick told you how much it would require. I know most
that Mrs Buick expects you to give the money to her,
dear father before she gets my clothing, & not to put it on
the bill, for a day or two since she said to me, "When
your father gives mother the money I will go and get your
clothing."

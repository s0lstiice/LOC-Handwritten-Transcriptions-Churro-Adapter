Boston April 1st 1835
An Exhibition of Paintings & other works of art
will be opened in this City early in June, to be
Conducted on a plan, which shall be more directly
Calculating to benefit the Exhibitors, than any
heretofore established here
The following inducements are offered for the Consideration
of Artists to exert their talent in favor
of this Exhibition. 1st It will be a favorable opportunity
to display their works as specimens, or
for Sale 2d There will be a Sale by Auction, at
the Galleries at some time during the Exhibition
on such Occasion the Exhibitors will have the
privilege of selling any of their Original works
contained in the Collection. 3d Each Artist whose
works make part of this Collection shall be
entitled of half the gross receipt, of certain
days or weeks of the Exhibition, the extent
of the Benefit to be regulated by the probable
value & importance of the works contributed. This,
Together with the time, selected for each contribution
Benefit shall be agreed upon (when practicable)
between the parties, at the time the pictures are
loaned, or at some time previous to the Opening of
the Galleries, in order that the time assigned to each
may be made public with the ensuing advertisement.

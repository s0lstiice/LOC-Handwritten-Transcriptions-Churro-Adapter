Salisbury Rowan County
North Carolina Dec 8. 1832.
My dear Mosso,
I saw the other day in the
National Intelligencer, a letter of Lafayette to an American on the way of quitting Europe, which was copied from the New York Observer. I conjectured that the letter was addressed to you, and consequently that you are returned home. Have I guessed right? Hoping that I have, I have determined to write you a letter. Do not think that I shall often trouble you in this way; for, living so remotely from most scenes of general interest, I do not feel myself entitled to do so. My apology at the present time must be found in my sharing largely in the extreme interest which all true Americans feel in the continuance of our glorious Union, the existence of which is at least jeopardized by the recent measures of a sister State.

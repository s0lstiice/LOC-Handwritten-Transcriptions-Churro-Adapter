My dear Sir, I receive
knowing that the Library Com-
mittee have before them the consideration of
a resolution on the expediency of employing
four artists to paint several pictures to
complete the Or the Rotunda of the Capitol,
If Congress should deem it expedient to
adopt this in favor of the measure I
should be willing to be enlisted if a great honor
to be selected as one of the artists I have
devoted twenty years of my life, seven years
in England France and Italy, studying
the Historical branch of Painting and with
reference to works of the kind proposed
and I must refer to my professional life
and character as a proof of my
ability to do honor to the commission, and to the country.
May I take the liberty to ask your
favorable recommendation to those who
have the disposal of the
commission?—
Man. J. 1834 With great respect I am
Y. M. H. Servt

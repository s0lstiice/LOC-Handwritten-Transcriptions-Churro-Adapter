I am happy to say that our friends at Savannah
are all well. Well as usual Mrs B's health is not as good
as we could wish. My little boy, who is there, has been quite ill
but is now well. Elizabeth is delightfully situated at Baltimore
Her husband is an excellent man. & in every comfortable situation,
they have one little boy. My baby is here at home, she
is a lovely little one with all the softness & gentleness of her dear
mother whom she resembles very much.
That you can be here and at Savannah I shall
be very glad to see you & to send you this when I shall probably be
about the 15th June. Please present my respects
to your brother and all friends & accept with my
continuing kindness to believe me Your sincere friend
with Kindest Regards
S B F. More Exp
New York

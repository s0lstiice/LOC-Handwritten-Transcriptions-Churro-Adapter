My Dear Sir
On my return Home I was favoured with
yours of the 26 July, & it was with the greatest regret I
found myself in an unpleasant dilemma arising from
my anxious desire of promising without Self-preservation on my
part your Interest & means, & the occurrence that you
attitude to of having transpired of one Gentleman having
made up his mind to give two thousand Dollars for
the Picture
Now my Dear Sir permit me frankly to say that
I by no means ever wish to avail myself of your necessities, & that my own conduct has been entirely regulated by my means, which at my time of Life and
with a growing family of Seven Children requiring
my aid must govern my Conduct; & I concluded that
I could compass my offer in the time specified, too
positively attended with some future inconvenience for
a short time to myself; thus promising I am alive
to the wants of others as well as my own & with
all selfishness aloof, I candidly leave to your own
judgement the consequences of my conduct

Burlington Jan. 27th 1835
My dear Sir:
Rejoice in this – we
have got $3000 & will get $4000
for Atlanta – Now begged
in that stone Georgia body who
had to give 2 Mails here since
the same – it comes in large sums
$1000 – 500 – 500 &c.
Allston has finished one picture
and will be himself again –
Have time only to rejoice
you least with the news
Very Truly J. D. Dickson
J. B. Mason

Dresden 14 July 1832
Illustration of a cityscape with a bridge and ships in the water.
To Mr. Sam. F. B. Morse Pres? of the Academy of Design in New York
Mr. Morse will please to accept the Engraving which I send to him with
Mr. Winndel as a respect due to the high consideration of his high merit.
He would oblige me very much with the drawing of his own Portrait and
Signature wherewith I have a great desire to embellish annexed Collection
of eminent Artists, it would thankfully be received as an additional
honor, and if in return I could oblige Mr. Morse in any manner
whatever I shall feel very happy to do so.
Charles Vogel de Vogelstein

Mr. Samuel F. B. Morse
to Isaac H. Townsend, Administrator on the Estate of H.B. Spynchard
For one lathe and the tools belonging thereto
The Inventory price $175.00
New Haven January 22d 1833
Received Payment by note endorsed
by S.E. Morse dated 16 January and payable
In 60 days
I H Townsend, Adm't of H.B. Spynchard
22d Jan 1833
P.S. - Now by the above you will see the purchase
of the Lathe has been completed the delay has been
caused by Mr Townsend being engaged at Court
the small things belonging to the Lathe are safely packed
I have given Mr Spugh such directions for the frame as
will I think ensure its safe transportation by tomorrow
team boat (2.3) - the whole will be in one box, delivered
at his letter - hope to see you on long, will then
tell you the reasons of my visit
Your very Respectfully
H. Spugh

New Haven 11th March 1833
Dear Sir
This will be handed to you by
Mr George Flagg a young artist with whose
acquaintance I know you will be pleased
as I have been He is one of our friends Henry
C. Flagg Esq and a promising disciple of
his uncle Allston He is recently from Boston
and you will be gratified by more direct
intelligence concerning Mr Allston than you
have probably had since your return.
Could you see some of young Flagg's performances
you could not fail to take a warm interest in
him on the bare ground of his merits whilst on
his part I am sure that any attention which
it may be convenient to show him will be
gratefully received by him.
I thank you for the exposé of matters & things
touching the two Academies. I could not say how
very flattering the things appear to me which frustrates
your intentions -- You will be surprised by every man
of common sense With Respect I remain Yours
Very Friend & Servant H. Peck
I.F.B. Mass Esqr
Duly Your Friend & Servant H. Peck

Where is our friend—(am I not justified in saying our
friend?)—Peninsular Cooper? Where is his charming
daughter? I hardly should dare tell you what he
made of her portrait. I delivered the whole picture perfect
and unaltered in the smallest degree, to Miss Delaney.
Have you time to write me whether I must visit Miss

Boston Dec 28th 1832
Samuel B. Morse Esq.
My Dear Sir, It was with feelings of immense
delight that I heard of your safe arrival in
America, and I have been hoping to see you
in Boston, knowing that you dear little Sue
was at Portsmouth (or near there) I supposed
you would visit her as soon as possible
after so long an absence, but the season
is so unpleasant for travelling, and you must
have many engagements in N. York that I fear
you will be here till Spring. I am very anxious
to see you, and the fine things you have brought
home from Italy, France, England, and I would come
to N. York at once if I thought I could leave my
business long enough. It occurred to me that
you would want a number of duplicating of the
Copies of Pictures which you have, and as it would
be rather irksome for you to copy the same things
over & over again, perhaps you might employ
me to assist you my time would not be so
valuable as yours, and I should be improved by
the exercise; Should you employ any one to assist
you do give me the preference. I saw Sam Alston
a few weeks since, he told me, to say from him, that
you must Exhibit your Copies here, He wishes me
much to see them.

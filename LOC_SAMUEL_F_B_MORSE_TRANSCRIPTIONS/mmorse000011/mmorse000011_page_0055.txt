Richmond October 26th 1842.
Dear Sir!
Answered letter from the Baron de Vogel, Painter
of the King of Saxon, I had brought with me from Dresden, not
knowing till now of your happy return from Europe. I am most sorry
that you have been deprived of this letter such a length of time. The engraving
given to me by you from the Baron I shall either find an opportunity to send them
to you as soon as I leave your Residence or if you wish, I shall bring them with me
next spring when I intend to visit New York myself. After having stayed
nearly two years in Germany, in Dresden & Hamburg, I returned to Baltimore
last September. Believing that you intended to visit Munich & Dresden yourself
I inquired in both places by all the artists there after you and should particularly
in Dresden been happy to have seen you. I had an opportunity to buy a very fine Copy of Titians Venus the original of which is at the Dresden
Gallerie, from the Gallerie Inspector Mr Smith, also an original picture of Staphorst
Naecke, representing the Holy Family, himself, and a very fine Portrait,
called the Spanish Queen by Grimmson. You would perhaps have the kindness
to tell me your opinion as for exhibiting these Printings in New York and in
that Case, if a good room might be easily procured. As my wife, I imagine
her in Dresden, can not speak yet english and wants to know more of the american
manners, I shall stay this winter with a friend of mine here in Richmond, else
I should have preferred the South. In hope that you enjoy all Health
and Happiness and that you will excuse the liberty of this long letter, saying
so much about myself, I am dear Sir, with the greatest Respect yours
most Abt & humble Servant
Carl Winndel
98135
Call of Charles Beck, Richmond

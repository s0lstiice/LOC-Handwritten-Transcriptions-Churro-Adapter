lived with us, lived with her mother at Charlestown. Harriet said that she used to love Nancy
dearly, and Nancy would often tell her, how
she had the ear of a little Susan Moses.
Harriet and myself are both natives of Charlestown.
She resided in Charlestown until a year ago,
when her parents moved to New York.
I am coming along in my music very well. I have
commented the Caliph of Bagdat, and learned
three pages. We have got to Part Third in Logie.
I like the study very much. As for drawing I am
very fond of it next Tuesday I commence a large house.
Mrs Brick has not purchased my winter
dresses yet. She does not seem to be any pretty
calicoes in New Haven. Miss Catharine Brick
has not returned, she is sick in New York.
Charles came to see me Saturday, he is very
well, and says he studies hard and has
taken three drawing lessons. I love to have
him come and see me, he enjoys himself
so much. All the little girls like him, he is so
obliging. Finley says he does not like the girls
here, he had rather play at Mr Skinner's than
come and see Sister. My health is very good
except that I am troubled with the headache
especially if I do not exercise well.
I want to see you very much, dear father and wish
you would surprise me again.

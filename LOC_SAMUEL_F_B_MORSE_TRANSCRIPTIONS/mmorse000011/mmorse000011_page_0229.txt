New York Sept 2. 1834
Dear Finley - On the 18th Sept 9ust. The Probate Court
meet in Oxford County, Maine, in which County is
Russford - There my father owned about $3000 of land
1/5 is the Children, for he died without a will. Bonds
for deeds have been given in some cases, & in some,
vales will be made - We, of age, can sign, but, there
must be a guardian there, for the Children. Now
he must be a resident & give resident sentence. Timo-
thy is administrator there & might as well be
guardian - Their interest is about 5 or 600 $ only,
& he is good & gives the Judge sentence, & acts for
all the rest of us - & therefore in this had better
act for the children - It is a small affair - but $50 or 600
in all for the time. I wish you, as you are first
entitled to be Guardian, to write to him that
you wish him to be guardian in Maine &
write a letter he can show the Judge.
I am administrator in U. C. & have received
the shares of the Infants cash there; about $8,800.
The total share will be about $18,5000 at
$4500 for each child. Is. Bet? some one must
be guardian there, for we sell the real estate, &
there must be a guardian to sign for them, but
of this here after. At present father will send your
letter to Timothy Walker in Russford, Oxford Co: Maine.
By the way - Timothy is remarkable for sobriety & good

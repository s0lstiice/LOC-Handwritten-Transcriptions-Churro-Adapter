Little Falls Decr 25. 1884
Sand F B Morp Exp.
I take the liberty of addressing you for the first
time respecting my son Frederick I should have done
so long before this but my son residing in the
city of Albany = whom I requested to go to Norfolk
and see you on his account, my instruction was
to have gone to Norfolk myself, but being so much
present with Broome & on account of Rail Road
I made it impossible for me to have at this time
I had a letter from my son in Albany some days
since enclosing your letter to him he also stated
that he had been to Norfolk to see you, but you
being absent from the city =
In answer to your letter
to my son of 10 Decr I comply with your terms
in every respect. I merely propose this I have already
Told to Mr Gay a considerable amount if you
would keep him eighteen months for the two thou-
sand dollars I should like it much as the Graham
has been considerable for me already
I should like to have you write me as soon as
you can conveniently your opinion respecting
Frederick Broome so I may hope and will be
thankful to you if you will give Frederick advice
from time to time how to do he has no Friends
In Norfolk and is a stranger
and ommit Budge Genl
Andrew Brick

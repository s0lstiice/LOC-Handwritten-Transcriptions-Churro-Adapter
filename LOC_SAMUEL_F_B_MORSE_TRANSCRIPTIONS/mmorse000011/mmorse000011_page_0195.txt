It is very true I should be glad to see
your Picture of the Louvre, & that I think the price
reasonable, provided it is finished (which it was not
when I saw it) and is in perfect order, but the fact
is at present I am short of cash having no more
than will be wanted for my annual Expenditure
in another year I should be better able to meet
such an extra expenditure by being from timely
notice better prepared to meet the demand, my
custom being to engage in such expenses as I can meet
within the year, & my funds to that end are appropriated
till next year. You did not state whether it is
prayed or how it could be delivered here securely
from yours with the greatest respect
Yr: Svt: Clarke

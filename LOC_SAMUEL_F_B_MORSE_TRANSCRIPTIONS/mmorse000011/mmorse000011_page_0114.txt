Pratt
Boston March 10th 1833.
Sams J. B. Morse Esq.
Dear Sir, I wrote you at N. York as soon as
I saw your arrival announced but as I directed
my letter only to the Post Office. I fear if you did
not receive it – My anxiety to see my Dear friend
and Master (if I cannot see him, to hear from
him,) induces me to write again. – I did think
that as your darling Susan was at Greenwich you
would have been this way to see her, ere this.
and that I should have taken you by the
hand in passing, and I still hope that you
will be in Boston soon, I hope you will
bring your fine Copy of the Old Masters, here
in the Course of the Summer & Exhibit them, Mr
Allston says you must; – I wish for an oppor-
tunity to Copy some of them – and I proposed
no my last letter to you, that as you would
want duplicate of them, & as your time is so
much more valuable than mine, and also
as it must be in some way to make more
than one Copy &c &c. that you should employ
me, to do that work for you – not that I imagine
it would be profitable for me, but for the improve-
ment in the noble Art that I should make in this
exercise –
Ever
your
faithful servant
Samuel F. B. Morse

are persecuted and hated by all the tyrants great and small of
the whole earth. You have been obliged to see one man fall
from the triumph of free Institutions, may you long be spared to see
these universal triumphs to the American cause. May I be permitted to
express my deep and sincere esteem for your services to the United States
and the preservation of the Union.
Yours respectfully

Albany August 14 1834.
My dear friend
I am truly sorry to be so often
a late upon your time but I know you will excuse me
when I tell you that I am nearly at the bottom of
every particle of white I have, and forgive the trouble
I am about to give you for my palette's sake if not
for mine by Will you do me the kindness to re-
quest the person from whom you now expect me to
get the last White of for me to send me the mouth of it
in good Krimble White and send it safely packed
up to my direction to be left at the Custom House
Albany by the No. York & District packet as early
as possible or I shall be very high 9 day — I shall
be in No. York in the course of a few weeks and will
pay him for it or remit the amount by mail &
would not have trouble again but that I do not have
the address of the person from whom you purchased
the other
You may think me made, my poor
friend that I should plant myself among other in
U. York but when I explain to you the way I have
from you will at least acquit me of that charge I
have much to say to you but will leave all alone
until we meet & I promise myself many a pleasant
hour from your society & that of the No. York
artists — Adieu very sincerely I truly
your obliged son of
John G. Chapman

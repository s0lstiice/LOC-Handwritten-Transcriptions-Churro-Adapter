All Paintings to be included in the first Exhibition must be ready in Boston by the 20th May
and must be in gilt frames, unless they are of very large size, in which case frames
of some other material will be admitted. — It is intended to make this Exhibition
annual and to commence (after this season) on the first of May & continue 4 months, but
any pictures may be removed by the owners after 2 months from the opening of the Gallery.
The Establishment will be under the direction of the undersigned, he therefore invites
those Artists to whom this Circular is addressed to forward at the expense of the Exhibition
any works which they may think proper, and he engages to take special care that the
Paintings shall be returned without injury.
Those who propose sending pictures, are requested to give notice to the undersigned as soon as
convenient that he may make arrangements accordingly.
Address Henry C. Pratt
56 Washington Street
Boston

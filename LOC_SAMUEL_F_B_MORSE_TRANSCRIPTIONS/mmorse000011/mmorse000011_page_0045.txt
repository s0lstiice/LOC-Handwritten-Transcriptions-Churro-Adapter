It was not until after six months that the symptoms
peculiar to that disorder entirely left me, since
then I have not been troubled with them at all.—
I have been glad that you prolonged your stay beyond
your first intention and should have received great
satisfaction if during this time you had just
written me a few lines.— Your opportunities
have been great and they will result in gain
to the public and I trust to yourself. — I must
express myself highly delighted with the copy of
the School of Athens. This carefully executed
and so happy in the tone of colour, as a copy in
oil, that it is a treasure to the art, in the sense
in which that copy in the Royal Academy (though
of inferior execution if I need not rightly) is
important to that institution. The copy also of
the Tintoretto which Mr Walker has is a capital
hit in colour and effect, reviving in my mind
the striking peculiarities of the great original.—

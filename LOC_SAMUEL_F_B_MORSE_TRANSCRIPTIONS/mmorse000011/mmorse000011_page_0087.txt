New York 22 Jan. 1833
Dear Sir. The next meeting of the Sketch
Club will be held at my house on Thursday
evening 24th inst. When I hope for the
pleasure of your Company. The subject
selected is the Scene at the Fountain
of Life in Bayoucar, by the late Mr
Sands vide 2d vol Tales of the Glorious
War. If preferred by any of the Members
the opening scene of that story is also
suggested.
Respectfully I am
R R Ward

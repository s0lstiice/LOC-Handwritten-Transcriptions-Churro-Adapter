Samuel T. B. Mordecai
Dear Sir, I wrote you a few days since, on
the subject of an Exhibition of Paintings. I now
send you the particulars of the Scheme, and I hope
you will think well of it. — I have sent
Copies of it to several of your Associates of the
N.A. but I wish you would take the trouble
to speak of it to any Artists you may
think proper, I think I shall be sure of a full
Collection, and I hope, a fine one, most if not
all, the other Artists will assist me —
The Situation of the Gallery will be preferable
to that of the Athenaeum, and I am quite sure
of success — I really hope you will allow
me to place your name among the Contributors.
I should be more proud of it than of any other.
The new Gallery is in rapid progress, and I
doubt not will be finished, in all next month.
I have taken the liberty to refer some of the
N.Y. Artists to you, for information respecting myself
Thinking they might wish to know whether I
were worthy to be entrusted with their property —
Do say I am honest! as far as you know —
Your Much Oblig'd Friend & Servt
Henry C. Bratts

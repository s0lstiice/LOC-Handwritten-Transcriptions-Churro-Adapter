Charleston 26th April 1834
Mr Saml. J. B. More
Dear Sir
I send you by the
Re Mr Mitchell a copy file of the Charleston Observer
containing the letter of Bishop England to Connell
for which you wrote some time ago. Your request
would have been granted earlier, but I could not obtain
a single paper copy. They were all eagerly sought as
soon as printed, and a large number of other copies
are circulated. I have folded down the hands to the
two most important articles. The file I intend to give
executing it to be returned by Mr Mitchell who will
leave St. J. for Charleston immediately after the Annive-
rsaries.
There is one fact or two on which you may rely
touching these letters. Think it is necessary to explain that
this full print and some may be understood.
Bishop England stood upon the disunities of the
Irish Catholics as a thorn in his estimation admirably
suited to awaken the sympathies of this community in be-
lay of the Church of Rome. And on his own nothing could more
effectually secure his spirit than to establish an association
under color of aiding the oppressed Catholics of Ireland. At this
instance a public meeting was called of which the letters con-
tain a copy.

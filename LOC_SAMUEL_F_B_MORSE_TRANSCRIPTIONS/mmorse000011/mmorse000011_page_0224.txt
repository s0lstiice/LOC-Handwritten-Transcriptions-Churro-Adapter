New York Nov. 26th 1834
George Clarke Edy.
My Dear Sir,
Yours of the 9th inst. I have just
received, having returned on Saturday from Albany
after an absence of nearly a fortnight.
The vehemence with which the sentiments
you have manifested in your letter in regard to my
picture, and which I fear I have led you into error in regard
to the object of mentioning the offer intended to be made
for the picture in of me . I did not mention it with the
intention of showing dissatisfaction at having closed with
the terms agreed upon with you, but simply with a view
to gratify you, in knowing that you possessed a picture
that was highly valued. Had the offer been definitely
made to me after the arrangements made with you, I should
certainly have not listened to the proposal, however advan-
tageous it might have been in a pecuniary point of view.

Albany June 6. 1834.
My Dear Sir,
It happens to be in your own most wish
in undertaking to remain there for a few days, quite, if it is
perfectly convenient to you, feel myself quite your hand
a new artist, greatly to your kind suggestion but fate.
I hope however that you will make it entirely a matter
of convenience to yourself and if you happen to be absent
at the time, quite await myself of some future
opportunity. I am in transport of fine work done for a
part of a day, and was so long away to give room when
from impression by the landscape. You were on a visit
to Washington.
Very Truly yours
[W. Ingram]

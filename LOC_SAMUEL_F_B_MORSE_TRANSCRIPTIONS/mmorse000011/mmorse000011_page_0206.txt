Albany May 4. 1854,
Monday.
My dear sir,
Having consented to the publication of
my Address on La Fayette, it has occurred to me that
there is a place in it where I may very advantageously
insert in the form of a note the letter which he addressed
to you and which was published in many of the papers
about the time you returned from Europe. There not a
paper that contains it, and if you feel no objections to
it being published in this form, I shall be greatly obliged
you if you will send me a copy of the letter, either in
print or as many suit your convenience. It shall
be most convenient for you to send me the original letter.
Quite copies it for the printer to turn the way immediately.
Allow me to thank you once more for
the picture since it has safely arrived. There has been but
one newspaper from there who have seen it, and that of
decided appreciation. A very intelligent gentleman from

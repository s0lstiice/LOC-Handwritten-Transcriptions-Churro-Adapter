I wish, father, you would write, and tell me
how to commence the note, and end it. So, father
I have commenced my drawing, and taken
two lessons. The last time I began to draw a
pump, with a bucket. Miss Collis says, that I
have a great taste for drawing, and that she
never had a scholar learn the touch of lines so
readily as I have. I do not say this to boast,
dear father, but to let you know that I am
trying hard to improve. Another new scholar
has come to day. Mr Skinner's school began
Wednesday. Charles and Finley I suppose are
very much engaged in their studies.
The little girl that you came up from New York
with is here. Her name is Harriet Gordon, and she
is very accomplished for her age, although
quite rude. Our class in Logic, and in History,
are making great progress. In History we ask
each other questions, and that makes the
recitation very interesting. My health is
very good now. I have, the new Kalff, you
have me, and it is very pretty. I hope
to be surprised this month, father, in
seeing you, shall I not?
It is almost dinner time, so I must conclude
Please write soon, dear father. I am, as ever,
yours affectionate
daughter, Susan.

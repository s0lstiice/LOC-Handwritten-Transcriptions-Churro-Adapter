J. F. Cooper, Esqr.
Dear Sir,
We have made arrangements to stay in my present quarters, by a
removal of my bed to a drier room, which obviates the principal objection
to my remaining. Will you therefore have the goodness to let the porter
who brings this, take my pictures and box.-
Shall I see you here in the course of the morning or day?
In haste but with great esteem
Y &c
S. F. B. Morse.
I hope your family are all well.
25 Rue de Surenne
Monday morng.
P. S. Mr. Habersham left his umbrella at your house, will you
let the porter bring it also.-

You said when you were here, that you would try
to come on in the spring, to work amongst us, &
to be near enough to Mr A, to aid him in
getting at his great picture. The says, it
is his purpose to open it in the same.
If it was never so important to him
to do so. No contribution to his necessities
will ever ask question about this, but to me
means Dutton, Dexter, & myself, it will be a most
gratified thing to hear that it is open
& that Mr A. is at work. Do it why you come
if you can, if only for a short time. You have
a most useful influence with Mr A. I think
it will be most grateful to you to spend it on
the present occasion. Enlargement is an easier form
of labour, please have it expedited at once, have
the bill sent, I to whom here it shall be paid, or
a draft on Mr York shall be sent of an amount
of the bill. Please have them directed to Mr
Samuel J. Hastings, Merchant, Commercial St
Boston, assisted to Mr A. at Cambridge, sending
a Bill of Lading, with bill of Mr Rover by mail
to same address when shipped. Mr C. is waiting for the
Colony. —
Ever truly for Wellington.
Genl Wm H. Harrison

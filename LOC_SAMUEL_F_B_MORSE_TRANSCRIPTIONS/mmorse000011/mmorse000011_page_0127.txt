Paris 30th April 1838
My dear Sir
At the request of Madames de
Trainaigs, I enclose you a letter from them, in which
they are desirous that I should say something about their Gallery. But to you it
would be superfluous as well as to praise the "Putens in a machine, so fine a Picture deserves. I certainly think, on the whole, it is his masterpiece excepting (perhaps) the Descent from the Cross. Is there any hope of getting their Gallery to our side of the
atlantic? If not, as I fear, it would really be an act of charity to get them off that false scent."
Very truly Your friend
Franc Barnes

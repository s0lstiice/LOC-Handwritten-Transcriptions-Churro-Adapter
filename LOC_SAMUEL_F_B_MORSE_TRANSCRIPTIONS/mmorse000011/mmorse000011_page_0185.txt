Gen Lafayette being detained
by sickness has sent to the reporter
of the Committee the following note
which the said reporter has read to
the house.
This is the last letter I received
from General Lafayette the date is
April 29th the seal I tore off and
gave it to Mr. A. W. Wilgus of Buffalo.
The General was too ill it seems even to
sign his name as usual to the letter of
his amanuensis; he died 3 days after
this letter was directed to be sent home.
New York June 20th 1834. Samuel Morse.

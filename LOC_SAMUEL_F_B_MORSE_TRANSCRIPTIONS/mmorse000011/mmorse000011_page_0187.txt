Joshua Shaw Esq.
My Dear Sir,
My letter of June 18th was duly received and I lost no time in
laying the subject of it before our Council at their first
meeting which was last evening. – I am directed by them
to say to you that the reports of any understanding between
the Penn. Acad. of Arts and the National Academy of Design
are without the least foundation. I have not received any
correspondence with any of the Members of either Academy, neither can I
learn that there is any individual correspondence relating
to any matters of the two Academies. Thus much I am satisfied to say
officially. – I will now briefly state in my individual capacity. I suppose not only by your
article in the Inquirer, but by other communications in other
papers of your city, that the artists and the Architects of the
Penn. Academy, are as they must ever be in an Institution on such
a foundation, at enmity. All experience in Europe as well
as the peculiar business of Architects does not only show no good
but has been absolutely injurious to the Arts. – It is singular
that in this country exists such minds of many well meaning and
in other respects honorable men in relation to the encouragement
and support of the arts, they look the arts with contempt and refuse to see them
prosper.

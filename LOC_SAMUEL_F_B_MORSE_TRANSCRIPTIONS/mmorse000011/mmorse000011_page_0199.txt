From Rome July 16-1834
To the Secretary of the National Academy of
Design, New York
Sir
Mr Jackson has shipped at my
request consigned to your Academy, New York
the following objects at cost. A Plaster cast by
Mr Whittall and in its packing case are enclosed
an Engraving of the Cena di Leonardo da Vinci
by Sig Solo a Roman Engraver; who begs to
present it to your Academy: and a Medal
by Grevinetti.
I am
your obedient and humble Servt
Thanks le Grio
Foreign Correspondent
of the President and Council
of the N. A. of Design

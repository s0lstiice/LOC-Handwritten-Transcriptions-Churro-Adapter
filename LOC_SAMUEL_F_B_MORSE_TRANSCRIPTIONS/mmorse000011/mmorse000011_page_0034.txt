New Haven Nov 30th 1832
My dear Sir
Your kind letter from
Paris was received late in August or rather
arrived at New Haven at that time but owing
to an absence at the West I did not see it
until the close of September. As you announced your intention returning in
October I could not send you an answer in
time to have it reach Paris before you
would leave it. But, without further
explanation let me congratulate you
on your safe return after a long & I
trust a happy tour. Your old friends
will be very happy to see you again in
New Haven & none more so than those in
The Avenue. -- I enter warmly into your
feelings respecting Mr Bospen that your
letter arrived in season I would have
taken immediate steps to accomplish
the subject which I decidedly approve. You
could hardly have been informed that
our commencement has been altered to
the 3d Wednesday of August & was already
given by when your letter arrived. We
shall I trust meet before the next
anniversary if you judge it expedient
to pursue the subject then it shall have
all the support which I can give it; a vote
you are aware I have not; a little influence
I may perhaps have.

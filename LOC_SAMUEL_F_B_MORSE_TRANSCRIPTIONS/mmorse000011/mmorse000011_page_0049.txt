convince our people that this has been wrongfully
accused as the source of Southern depression. —
Discrimination is quite out of the question. Were it not
extra-constitutional, it is impracticable. For, in
case of a resort to force on the part of the general
government — North Carolina, Georgia and Alabama would go with South Carolina by an
immense majorities of their respective populations,
and would take an active part on her side.
You may be perfectly assured of this — I know it
is so. Old sober North Carolina would give a
majority of ten to one in favor of resistance to
the tariff. — The hostility to a tariff of protection is
rooted — and whether right or wrong, cannot be destroyed by any form of reasoning, nor subdued by any
display or exertion of Force. And granting that a
tariff of protection is constitutional, and beneficial to
the nation at large, shall we hazard our national
existence to maintain it? — Profits at the North are
under a delusion in supposing that the enemies of the
tariff compose a small majority of S. Carolina only. —
That state is warmly unanimous — most of the Union
parties out of Charleston, say, "the nullifiers have acted free

Mason has taken a carriage together in and I have agreed to join
him several others are going about the same time and we hope
to form a out of caravan.
They you will present my kindest remembrance to Mrs. Wear. I hope his attention to West Point will not be too
engaging for him -- Leslie misled it in going to America and I
feel almost as much in returning to London -- He had several
(2.) instances of his guns being disputed before he left as I learned
from a source in which I put the greatest confidence and the person
told me he attributed leaving England to the quiet on that account.
You will be happy to hear that Mr Wyatt the English
captain is daily rising in reputation in his walk he has no superior
He has lately had a commission from the King of the Hellenes
(a nephew of Queen) I trust that I can give you my actions without
any danger of your shewing my letters to any body but Cooper
it is that belief only that induces me to talk away so sleepily
When you write me again will you tell me all
about Cooper and his family -- Is not Mrs Cooper very happy now? At home
with all her children safe about her and her husband's company and
her social position -- but is she well -- And to what does he say
that does he think of the world -- Does he like some of us enough
and fear that Solomon was right when he said there is nothing
new under the sun -- I am not one of our mind -- Steam and spirit in
one never flourish's for our sakes! He had a vigorous attempt made
here the other day to fly -- I was at the Champ de Mars. They climbed the
offensive at 20 sous the lead, this is not a bad investment -- Incent with
a party to look at it -- this contemptible steam affair -- a dandy who was
of our party -- hobbled his gills and quoted Hudibras -- Doubtless the
pleasure is as great "if being cheated or cheated" I am Dear Morse
your affectionate
H Greenough

at the expiration of two years, I shall have finished
my education. Our school commences, this morn,
and I know, by the appearance of things, that we are
to study hard, this term. Give my best love to
Aunt & Uncle. Charles & Finley are both well.
I hope you will excuse the shortness of the letter &
I am in great haste.
Your ever affectionate daughter
Susan W. Moses

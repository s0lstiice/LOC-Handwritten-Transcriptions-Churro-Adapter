Dates of Invention of my Telegraph
1832.) Conceived on board the Fulton,
in Oct. 1832, the idea of permanently recording signs
by voltaic electricity, to quote
one case or circuit, on a strip of paper
or metallic point regularly moved forward by clockwork,
by the movement of a lever passing through a hole in
the sign, originally for transmission
and reception, as shown in my sketchbook.
or by drawing out complete alphabet spaces, they elements of a
conventional alphabet afterwards formed, by
combining them with lines.
Dec. 1832. Formed the mould and cast the
pieces called type, representing the
10 numerals; each type was formed
with its respective member of code, for
the purpose of closing & breaking at determinate intervals, the circuit.
Sept. 1835 constructed the first crude instrument
for testing the practicability of recording by
the foregoing means. (See plate 8 description)
witnessed its operation in the N.Y. University
by many persons who testify to the fact.
Nov. 5 Dec. This instrument
was shown to my pupils, and students
in college, and many friends

Dear Brother
Mr Birch was in the city the other day
I told Louisa she would furnish Susan with the
articles she wants, if only funds were furnished.
We think it much better that they should be procured
& made at New Haven than here for various reasons.
Louisa thinks that will $30 will answer for the
things mentioned in this letter, which you had better
remit from Albany immediately.
The receipts from the Louvre are not yet
quite sufficient to pay the bal<=> of rent
$80. A bill of about $30 has been sent
in by Master Maiden lane, for articles
to you. As soon as I get funds from the
Lucre I will pay it, as it is stated in Mrs
Morse's name. All well & send love
The handwriting & composition of
your daughter much credit.
Yr affe brother
R L Morse
Nov 28. 1833
New York

[1832]
Hotel Rivoli
Dear Mme,
I have not time
to run round & see you, but
I wish to leave my passing
respects for Mr Cooper, and
so I enclose them to you
with remembrance for your-
self. Say every thing that is
grateful & respectful to Mr
C. for me, & remember me
also to his family. If you
will mention me now & then
in that pleasant circle, I should
be indebted to you, for I would
not willingly be forgotten by
a man like Cooper.

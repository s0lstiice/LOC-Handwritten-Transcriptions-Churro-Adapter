Boston March 17th 1823
My Dear Sir,
You are probably expecting to hear by this time that I have taken considerable money, by the Exhibition, if it was best for you, I trust it would be the case; but thus it is not at present –
was pleasant – Rec'd 87-67-60 Tuesday – I devote you in part why you have probably need – Monday brought with it a cold easterly rain storm, which effectually kept the Bostonians by their firesides – And $10 – Today is March, weather windy, squalls of rain pleasant sunshine, 80 80!! – On Monday Evening I slighted the Picture, & had one man come to see me on Tuesday Evening, it seems to me that I did not light at all, this Evening I propose to try my luck again but do not expect many will come until next week (which is the last of Exhibition) and then I shall send around notice –
I find that the small Hardbills wt I have left, will answer by a little addition for this purpose – Dunlap's Picture is at Salem at present, I understand it has been there some time, so that it will probably be gone soon – Mr Green tells me that the Franklin Hall in Salem is a very bad place for Pictures, he assisted Peale's Picture there says there are between 30 & 40 windows in it, without anything to stop them with – At Mr Marsh all these yesterday, who is the owner of the Christ Entrance in Jerusalem Painted by Sargent, his business was to know what course I was to pursue with your Picture, as he is bound to the South, and wishing to make arrangements so as not to interfere with each other, he sets out for Baltimore in about 12 days –

I shall call on Uncle & Aunt Bartlett
to-morrow morning and see about the Porter Va. I
talked with Aunt about getting you a bonnet; she
says, that it is no time to get a fashionable winter
bonnet in Boston now & that it would be much better
if you could get it in New York as the Bostonians get
their fashions from New York and of course much later
than we should in N.Haven; she thinks that white is
better than blue, Va Va Va which she can explain to you
much better than I can; she is willing, however, to
get any you wish if you still request it; she thinks if
you cannot wait for the new fashion, that your black
bonnet put in to proper shape with black plumes would
be as tasteful and fashionable as any you could procure,
I think so too. You had better write Aunt particularly
about it.
Aunt & Uncle send a great deal of love to
you. Give my love to Father and Mother, & grandfather

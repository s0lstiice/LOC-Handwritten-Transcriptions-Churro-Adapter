New York June 132 1823
Dear Sir
The receipt of yesterday was $4122 in the
greater part of wt. was taken in the Evening
I have paid Mrs Richards 8 dolts and 4 dolts at the C. House
for my Taverns, also 1 to Mr Durand, also 3 dolts to the
carpenters $3.50 for the Sign — 3 pay 25 cts to Hunderd
for Distributing 16 Bills I have sent round 900 —
The Baize and other Drapery cost 6.372 — for bill I have
paid 3" besides a number of small things which you will
see in the Book — I have 30 dolts for Mr Boraitter
for the rent of room, which he has never yet called for
I have covered the Frame with Drapery, and think
it looks better, The Chandelier, now in the greatest light
which catches the eye in looking at the Picture, and it
seems as tho the Frame was needless — Mr Jarvis was
here today — spoke very highly of the Picture.
The times are very dull. Fine Exhibitions are complaining
that I believe the Court of Death is doing pretty well.
I walked at Platt's, in Broadway, where I saw some very
good French Brushes. In a good variety of sizes, the Price
is from 12 1/2 to 25 cts each, I should be glad to secure a set
for myself if I had the means to do so
I feel more & more determined in my plan of going into
the Country, to Paint Landscape & Portraits. There is here

I intend writing to your Brother
Richard in answer to his, before long
you will hear before this which
orders all things wisely, to afflict me
& disappoint me in removing my
dearest friend & brother. I never knew
what affliction was before. My heart
now sometimes sinks & dies within
me when I think what has taken
place I knew he was mortal & we often
say often & sometimes on
the possibility that
death might soon
separate us. But the
anticipation of the
such an event is one
thing & realizing it is another.
You will mourn when you hear of it.
I trust you will pray that an event
apparently so inauspicious & afflictive
may be overruled for great good.
To remember me very affectionately
to Mrs. M. & to your Parents &
Brothers. May you grow in
grace & thus be happy. Yours S
P. Fisk

Mr C. Ball
[Mar. 28, 1821]
Dear Madam,
As you can spare but
200 dollars at the present time;
I will be content to take the 200
you offer now and give you my receipt;
and though it puts me to much in
convenience, I will wait as you
request for the remaining 200
until next year, and I have there-
fore enclosed a blank note for
200, which you will please to sign,
thus by giving the order you promise
on Messrs. Matthews and Bonneau,
and signing the note this unpleasant
business will be done amigably and fairly respectfully
S. P. Mosse

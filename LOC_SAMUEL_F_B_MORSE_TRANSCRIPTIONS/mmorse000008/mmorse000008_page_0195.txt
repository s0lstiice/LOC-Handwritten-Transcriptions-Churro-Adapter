Boston, March 1st 1823
My Dear Sir.
As there was some uncertainty with respect to the
Bank business when you left me, I thought perhaps you might
feel some uneasiness about it, I therefore now inform you that
I went to the City Bank on Friday morning, & found Mr. Payson,
he said that the news had not arrived, & that when it was sent
he would take care of it in the way which you proposed --
Yesterday was a very bad day here & I suppose that you
found it so -- the receipts were as follows $23175 of which
$11 were Season Tickets -- They stood 13 -- $1.624 --
I went down today with $30 to deposit -- but could not
get the Exchange -- I could not find any less than 500 Dollars Notes
I went to the Bank 800 from here it was -- Now I hope I could
probably get the Boston money next week, but it makes no
difference to them, so I shall keep my money by me until
next week --
I hope that you have had a pleasant journey home
& found your Friends all well -- The Receipts for
this day only $15413 -- Keys & $1. The reason of this
I know not -- however I hope we shall see better days ere long
I have sent out aman today with 500 handbills, & I
hope will do some good -- I have just now laid for
hastling 400 Keys $400 four dollars -- I think we
shall not make our fortunes out of them!
Please give me love to all the Family, and others who may
enquire after me -- Your Obst Lentt H. C. Batt

I am thankful ought I to be for his continual

him, and make me more ardent in his service.
Good bye dear wife business calls me.
Spring has opened delightfully, peach and plum trees
all in bloom, and all the fields clothed in green, the
weather almost uncomfortably warm; I think you
will have an early spring at the North. In the
latter part of April or May you may expect me home
as soon as I can finish my press of business.
My dear little Sue, and perhaps the little stranger, I
have a plan for building a house with I have drawn, and with
it much admired for its convenience, simplicity, and beauty.
Your aff. husband Finley.

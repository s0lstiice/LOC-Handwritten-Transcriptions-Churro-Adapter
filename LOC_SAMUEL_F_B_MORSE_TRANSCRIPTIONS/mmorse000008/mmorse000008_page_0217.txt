in Charleston. He is affixing Mr Fry for 3 or 4 weeks
In answer to my inquiries respecting his Family, he told me
that he has had another son added to his Family, about the
same time as yours, he was greatly surprised when
I informed him that you was keeping them Company
he says it is an interesting time in Charleston especially
among the youth — I went to the Chapel on Sunday Evening
but could not get in Mr Fry informed me that
numbers often went away for want of room tho the Chapel
is full twice as large as formerly
Majors Evarts Day, Cornelius Humphries have all been
here — Mr Allison told me that he would get
some of his Friends to write for your Pictures
The most common fault that is found with your
Pictures is its small size — if you could only say
that you had 10,000 feet of canvas to show. I presume
it would satisfy them — As for me I intend my
leisure time in bidding; but propose by 12 by (with yr
leave) to get some water colors, and Paint some
little designs for my amusement and improvement
I dont know that I have anything more to say at present
so I will leave the rest till next time, for the present
bidding you forever
With Respect Hesteen Lt Col 6th Srt
H C Pratt

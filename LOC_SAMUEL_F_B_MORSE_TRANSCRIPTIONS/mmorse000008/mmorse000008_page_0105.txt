My dear son, I have to communicate to you
the expected, but afflicting intelligence of the
death of your very worthy & beloved Mother
in law, Mrs Walker. The event took place on Mon-
day last. We have had few particulars of the
closing part of her life -- she died calmly & requests
that prayers might be offered for the forgiveness
of her sins through Jesus Christ. She requested
Dr. P. to be sent for -- He can safely leave her
in the hands of the Judge of all the earth, who
doth right -- I pray that the very great loss with
the family has sustained may be made up by the
bestowal of spiritual blessings. Lavinia is very
tenderly afflicted but is supported. I trust, by
the religion she professes -- We are all very
well & comfortable -- I am engaged, to live in
illness, in finishing my report -- laying in winter
wood & provisions & my own hands -- I have
my hands full --
Your last letter informed us in few words of
your arrival at Washington & of Henry's sitting
from N.York with your baggage -- We are anxious
to hear again from you -- of the arrival of
Henry & what reception you have met with
from the President & your prospects of -- What
Mr. Trumbull says about the remittance tell him
it is very important to him & to me, that he send
it before I go on to Washington -- i.e. this month

Charleston Jan 30th 1822-
Dear M r M r -
I arrived here a few
days since from Georgia where I
have been constantly employed
since I last wrote to you - I
was much pleased to find here
a letter from you - I did not send
you in the Price - because I did not
think you would remain long enough
to receive it, I will send it in time
with the Palmetto tree - which
I think - I can procure exactly as
you want it - I shall not have time
to attend to it sooner - I think it
would suit you better then, than
sooner - I have had some account
of your undertaking from the Papers
& I do most cordially wish you suc-
cess with it - I have now a joint
prospect of being able to float
in this miserable tide of life with
less difficulty than hitherto -- but as
my light may be only a blue light -
I will not be too languid -

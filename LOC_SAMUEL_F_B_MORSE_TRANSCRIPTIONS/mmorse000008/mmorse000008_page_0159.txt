New Haven Conn. Nov 19th 1822.
My Dear Mother,
Your letter of yesterday we have just received which has given us a great shock; I need not tell you that we shall feel very anxious until we hear again from you, and the state of Father's health, we fear his sickness is more serious than his ordinary dispositions, but we will hope the best; we shall expect another letter from you to-morrow, and a line every day till Father is better. We are all pretty well, Lucretia has a little of the head ache from sitting all day in your room with a charcoal fire, but will doubtless feel better in the morning; Edwards left us this morning for Boston, and will probably be in Boston soon after you receive this; he was detained partly by business of his own, and partly on Mrs. Bobb's account, who was unwilling to go in the distressed state of her sister Goodrich's family, little Edward Goodrich that beautiful boy was taken suddenly sick, and died last Thursday he was buried on Saturday.

Dedham February 13th 1821
My Dear Sir
Your kind letter of the 9th ult? was
duly received and for which I am pleased to accept my thanks. Your
Misgiving offer to receive my paintings and forward them
to Mr Wilson I accept with many thanks. You have
liberty to open them though I am not ambitious to have
them seen in company with your own productions.
Knowing that the contrast will not be favourable to
my pictures so far as regards myself I am sorry
for I know that you look with charity on the
works of your inferiors but the Public are not
so tender in their feelings or charitable in their
expressions. I have as you advised written to Mr A.
and made known to him that I had forwarded the
Paintings to you, and also that you are authorized
to receive the pay for them. any expense which
you may be at in consequence, you will deduct
from the same I have enclosed in the
box two sketches in India Ink to serve as keys
to the Pictures with notes on the margin, pointing
out the most important parts of the Pictures
thus you will then to send with the Paintings
to Mr A. – The price of the paintings you will
collect was agreed upon – that is 200 each for the
Paintings, and Mr Doggett's Bill is 150 more,
making in all five hundred & fifty dollars for the pictures
27546

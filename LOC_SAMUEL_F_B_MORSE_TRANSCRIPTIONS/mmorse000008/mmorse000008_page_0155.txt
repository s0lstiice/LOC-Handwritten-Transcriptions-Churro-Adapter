Boston Nov. 14th 1822
My dear daughter,
I thank you for your good letter of the 12th for your goodness in that you have so justly fairly
supplied the neglect of your husband I brethren We are relieved by the recovery of Bethany
& the improving health of your husband but grief to hear of the affliction of Mrs. Green which
to Adam present our tender sympathies I arrived safely, I well I of Sabbath morning
your Mother was at Charleston town attending worship there in the Morning I came over to dine
with one at the Longtans We have been there at Charleston town & Mr. Vinalls successively fine
I have been closely engaged in settling my personal prospering business with Clark who succeeded in making an arrangement which is nearly completed, that in existing circumstances, I think the best all round, which could be made, & which I have reason to hope will save me from any great loss of property by Clark
The conduct of Converse is shameful, & incompatible with integrity. He knows that it is his duty, & that it was my full expectation, expressed to him, that he should take care of that matter. There was no thought at the time it was given that Andrews would fail but enough till I get home

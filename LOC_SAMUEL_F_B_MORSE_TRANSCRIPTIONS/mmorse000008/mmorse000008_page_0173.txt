Dear Sir
Charleston 11. Janv. 1823
Your of 26th and 28th ultimo came to hand
in company yesterday I transmit to you, by this Morning's
mail, in adroff of H. Bennett in New York, the amount received from Mr. Taveau, i.e.
$447.82 by which remain, after deducting the Premium
of 3% interest on $447.82. The draft was dated to
day, was payable at ten days after date, on the first
of the 1st, and was in possession of and over. I
now send you, to guard against accidents by the
mail, the Sword of the Act which is forwarded
herewith, by Sea -- I am happy that the collection
of this Money has been so satisfactory, pray let me
have a receipt of the disquisition at the end of this
letter when the Draft shall be paid
P. T. B. More
Aug. Taveau &
Wife late Bankett
Ant Taveau
New Haven
City Court Charleston
Rudolph Hayne and Winke by the
hand of J. Winke, your husband and today
Sixteen Dollars 82/100 part of Principal and
interest due on the Sol of Defendant
given before her marriage with A Taveau.
1823

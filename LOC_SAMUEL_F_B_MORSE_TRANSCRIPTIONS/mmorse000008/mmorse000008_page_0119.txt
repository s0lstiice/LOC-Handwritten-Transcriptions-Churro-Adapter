Newhaven Dec 21st 1821
My Dear Son
I hope before I receive this your good Father will have
arrived safe and well. Your Dear Wife & little girl are well
as are all the family We are all much pleased with your
prospect of success in your Undertaking and daily pray
that the best of heavens blessings may continually attend
you even the Spirit of Grace. – I enclose a letter from An
droos of Hartford which I will thank you to hand to your
Father tell him the Atlas that Edwards men
tioned sending and which was to be sent to forselyne
by you arrived to day and altho open at one end so as
to slip in and out it came charged $4-62 1/2 cents
I intend to send it to forselyne and let him look at it and
get them at the post office to let it remain in the office
till your Father returns it was marked 10 printed sheets
they weighed it and marked it 9 1/4 ounces – young Mr Gould
has just called on your Father to request for himself & a num
ber say three or four of the Students the privilege afforded the
others of having the Geography & Atlas at 40 1/2 cents Discount
I told him I knew your Father would grant it if he were here

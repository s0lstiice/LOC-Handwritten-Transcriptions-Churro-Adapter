New Haven 13 Mar. 1891
My dear son,
We all rejoice with you most joyfully
in the many goodness of our common Lord that
he has given you & your dear
Lucylin another healthy & lovely daughter, &
us another dear grandchild she was born
precisly at 9 o’ clock in the morning Dr. M rs
Evans only attended & was ill but a few
hours I had a good night & is very comfortable
this morning Susan was introduced to her
sister this morning she knows not what to
do with her, or to say other than “Baby,” first
The little stranger has black hair, is plump &
promising we bid her a hearty welcome into
the family We have an experience made
an attentive physician & good folks in door
& around us all together shall take good care
Syderia & the children We have love enough
for them to prompt to this We all want to
see you & shall rejoice in your return but
are willing you should remain while you
have lucrative business
We have on the whole had a com-
fortable winter The weather has been un-
usually severe, changeful & trying & we have
all had our hands full of business but we
have had no severe sickness & have been satis-
fied with our domestic enjoyment & the friendly
visit of our good neighbors

I have made one slight sketch of the
Hoosac mountain, at Stockbridge we visited
the marble quarries, and to-morrow at Pans'-
borough shall visit the quarries of fine white
marble there.— I am much delighted with my
excursion thus far, to travel with such a com-
paigner as Mr Silliman I consider as highly ad-
vantageous, as well as gratifying.—
Give my love to all the family, keep
dear little Susan for me, and remember me kindly
to neighbors & friends.— W.S. wrote Mr Silliman
last week from Litchfield.— W.S. desires his
kind regards to you all,
With sincere affection and duty
Yr affectionate husband
Samuel P. Morrell

Say all expenses 1.50 for Day
Salem April 12th 1823
My Dear Sir,
I arrived here on Monday Evening, & the Picture, on Wednesday
morning, I have put it up in the Essex Coffee House, the Room is
small, but the bed that I could get in the place, I give 75 to day
for it at 44 pence week for Board. I have had the large Handbills
allied, & a 1000 small ones printed 300 of which were distributed
yesterday. I do not expect much from Salem, as I am tired they
do not take much interest in such things so that, if I
pay my expenses, and enough more to go on my way, I shall
be thankful. The Picture of France has received no injury in
moving and Putting up -- one of the Rose lovers is shot at
the carelessness of the driver -- the Printers here seem a
little more obligeing than in Boston. The Gazette (which I send
you) has just noticed it & says after he has seen the
Picture, he intends to give a more particular description
of it -- you will see by the Advertisement in this paper
that I have a cut to head it with -- it was done by Throck
in Boston -- I should have written you before that
it not best punctilily I had something to say --
It was a very unpleasant day yesterday, I slept 2-20 -- but
was not quite ready, and did not light in the Evening --
I have taken 5-12 today. The weather has been stormy more than
half of the day, but it is clear now and I intend to light this Evening
I wish you would write me immediately -- I am in haste
but with Affection and respect H. L. Platt

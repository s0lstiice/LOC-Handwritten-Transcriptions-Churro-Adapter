Concord N.H. Sept. 13th 1821.
Dear Parents,
We arrived safe in this place on Tuesday
evening after a very quiet and pleasant ride; Lucien-
tia and Susan both bore the ride very well, and are
in good health; little Sue behaved with her usual
goodness all the way. I presented your order to
Mr Fulsom at Chester, he told me there was nothing
due on the Turnpike Shares; he would write you on
the subjects you requested. – As to the loan of $1000.
from this bank, this also is abortive, not from any
want of willingness on the part of all concerned
but from absolute inability to loan it; the details
of the situation of the bank &c. I must give you
when I see you; – I shall leave Concord on
Saturday morning for Boston spend a few days in
Boston & endeavor to be home by Saturday night
of next week. – Lucertia will return with her
mother probably (who is in bad health,) and perhaps Sis-
ter Susan too. – Lucertia, and Father & mother, and all
relation & friends send a great deal of love to you, and little Sue
a great many kisses & Grand Papa & Grand mama Uncle. –
In health your affectionate Son
Wm. Kelley.

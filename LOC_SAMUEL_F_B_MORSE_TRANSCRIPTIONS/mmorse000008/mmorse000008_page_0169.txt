2nd January 1823.
Dear Sir
My having been much
more unwell for some days past
has prevented my replying to your
note of the other day.
I am so circumstanced that
I can dispense with the payment
of your note for some little time,
tho I cannot now say how long.
You may if you please let
it rest for the present, & if it
is convenient to you I will thank
you to call and see me some
time within a few days.
Respectfully I am your
Lect E. Whitney
Rev. J. More

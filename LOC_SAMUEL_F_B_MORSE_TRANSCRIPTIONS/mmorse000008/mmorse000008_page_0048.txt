Mar 22. 1821.
Mr C. Ball.
Madam.
I have just recd. a letter from
my Father in which he requests a remitt.
of as much as I can coll. in order to pay his
note of mine on interest. Not wishing
to put you to inconvenience in the im-
mediate paym't of what is due me, if
you will give him the accomp. note payable
in 6 mo. I can probably raise the money
on it and be relieved from much embar-
rassment. I leave town in 10 days, for the
north and wish a definite arrangement
of this debt.
With respect Madam
I may be send
S J B S D

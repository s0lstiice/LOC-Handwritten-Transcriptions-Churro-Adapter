Boston Oct 26th 1821
Samuel H. B. More Esq Bot
of P. Doppio &c
1 Portrait frame 13 1/2 Mounting, 275 38.00
12 1/2 pen cant discone 4.75
33.25
Bishop Boswell is to pay
35 new
Dear Sir
The above frame is shipped by
the Salem Vichet for New Haven I have seen
Mr George Clark and he told me that Dext More
was then at Bridgover & that he expected to see
him on his return and would endeavour to
make some arrangement with him respecting
the demand, It is my opinion there is no great
profit in claiming the money from that source
& if agreeable to your Father I should like to have
it remitted in some other way as it can make
but very little difference with him respecting
it
Very Respectfully
Yours &c
John Doppio

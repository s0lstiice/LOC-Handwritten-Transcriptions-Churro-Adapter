Albany August 10th 1823
My ever dear Wife,
I intended to have written you by
the boat on Saturday knowing you would feel anxious
to know how I got through my journey &c. but I
was prevented writing in season by being engaged in look-
ing out for lodgings; When I arrived in N.York I found
the packet had not arrived with my baggage, so that I
came away from N.York without even a change of any
thing, leaving Richard to send my baggage by the next
boat; Every thing arrived safely, and I have got
into my lodgings with which I am pleased, I have
a large front room on the second story 25 by 18 feet
and 12 feet high a very fine room for Painting, also a neat
little bed room with every convenience, and board &
lodging all for 6 dollars per Week which I think is
very reasonable; My landlord is an elderly Irish
gentleman with 3 daughters, once in independent
circumstances but now reduced, every thing bears the
appearance of old fashioned gentility which you
know I always liked; everything is neat and clean
and genteel; The name of the family is Carter No.

Boston April 10th 1828
My Dear Sir.
I rec'd your letter this morning, and am glad to find that you have directed me to go Eastward, as there is no vessel for Baltimore short of 8 or 10 days. I should have lost much time in waiting - Monday this week was Rainy. I rec'd only 125 on that day Tuesday, Fair Rec'd 7:75 Wednesday - Pleasant Rec'd 1362 This is more than I have taken any day before - since the 10th of March which is 1 month - According to my usual success I have another stormy day today, so that I do not expect any company - I have not deposited any intency in Bank except that I have given you notice of $13,742 at 20 cents & 26 at another - I have now with me 51-68 - I have yet to pay for the Advertising in Newspapers, wh I suppose will amount to 25 - besides the Room Rent and Board, and a former bill of Mr Doggett's wh you directed me to pay so that I dont expect that I shall collect enough, in the 2 remaining days in Boston, even should the weather be pleasant, and the only way that I know of, to do, is to engage to send Mr D. his money from Salem or elsewhere as soon as I can collect it - I am glad to hear you say that Mr Aston is coming to N.Haven think he will be pleased with the place, and cannot doubt that his company will be very beneficial and agreeable to us - On the 1st of next week I shall go to Salem, but will write you on Saturday - Please to remember me in love to your Family. I believe me. Your Most Obl Servt
Henry C. Pratt

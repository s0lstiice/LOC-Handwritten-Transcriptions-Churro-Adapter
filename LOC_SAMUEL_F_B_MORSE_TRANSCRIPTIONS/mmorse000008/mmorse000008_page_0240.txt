April 27 1823.
Dear Brother,
I was happy to see Richard as
did expect he would pass his time with me,
but he was too safely mo n ed at Mr. Staffords
before I had sight of him. Edwards too came
nigh living with me, which would have afforded
us much comfort, but Mr. S. stole him. I shall
bravely protest against this interference of
Mr. S. and have him legally chastised.
I have not seen so much of them as I expected;
but if you come here, then you come
to my house of course, for this Mr. S. has
not so much concern in your fancy works.

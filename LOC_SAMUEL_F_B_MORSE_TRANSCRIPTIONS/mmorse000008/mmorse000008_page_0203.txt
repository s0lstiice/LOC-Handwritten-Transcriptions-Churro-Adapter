Boston March 15th 1820
My Dear Sir.
I had almost concluded not to write you
today, as I have nothing to interest you, but as you have
given me directions to write twice a week, perhaps you
will expect to hear from me. I see your letter yesterday,
was glad to hear you were well, & your family,
I have not yet opened the Picture in the Box, but am
ready to do it on the 1st of next week, I find it very
difficult to light this Picture properly, the lights have
to be placed near the floor, and near the Picture, so that
you can easily conceive that it takes a great deal of
light to illuminate the upper part, but this I cannot
light it so equally as I could wish yet the lamp light
gives a very rich glow upon the Picture: besides the
Lamps for the Picture I must have 1 for the Door $1. for
the head of the stairs — The weather on Thursday was
pleasant—Receipts $9.62 1/2 Sep. 372 — On Friday it rained
very fast most of the day — Receipts 75 — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — —
In the Evening it snowed, and this morning was very slippery,
it has thawed today, and the wind is very high —
The receipt to this day are $9.60 —
I offered to pay Mr Salisbury the Postage as you directed, but he
refused to take it — I dine there tomorrow —
I have now 24 dollars by me which I expect to deposit on Monday
in the Bank. Wishing you prosperity & Happiness,
I remain your Most Obstert
S C B Pratt

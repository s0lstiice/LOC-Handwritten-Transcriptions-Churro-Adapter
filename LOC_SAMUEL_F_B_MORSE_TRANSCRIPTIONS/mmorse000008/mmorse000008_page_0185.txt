Boston Feb'y 21. 1823
Friday evening.
My Dearest Wife;
I suppose you are anxious to know
the result of the first day's exhibition of the
Pictures; information of its being open is not
generally spread, and will not probably be under
4 or 5 days. The day was fine over head & warm
so that the walking became very bad. The receipts
in all were $40.55 cents of which $11.80 went for keys
making 38 keys sold, leaving $35.75 as the receipts for the
first day, things look favorable & I think on the whole
we may anticipate something from Boston. -- The
season tickets seem to have sold well between 30 & 40 cents
I think the reduction in price from 1 doll to 50 cents
was wise, and that the entire advantages of it are
not yet experienced, all are for bringing others with
them, so that in a day or two I shall know something
of the effect. -- I have nothing new since yesterday
I have got rid of my cold I think entirely. I dine
to-day at Mr S. Foley's they inquire after you & send love

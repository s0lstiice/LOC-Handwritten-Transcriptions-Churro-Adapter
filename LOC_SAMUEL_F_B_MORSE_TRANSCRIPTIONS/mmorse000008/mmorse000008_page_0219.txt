Mr. D. C. Osborne compliments to Mr. I.
More. Mr. Osborne desires to have his
Portrait taken which would have been
of great age making the necessary calculation
for it; and at the same time making it a
good likeness in all other respects.
This reason is not to make himself younger, but
to appear to children Grand Children more
simply matched as to age with their Mother &
Grandmother. If Mr. More is at home, and
dispond to undertake the work, he will please
prepare his Canvas, & let me know when he
is ready for my attendance.
Newhaven 28 March 1828

Boston. March 12th 1823
My Dear Sir,
you will probably not be much gratified with the
account that I shall now give of the receipt of your Picture; but
we must try to look at the bright side of things. Hope for the best
If I recollect right I wrote you on Sat last. I gave you an account
up to that time. On Monday I deposited in the U. Bank $42
forty two dollars — The receipts of Monday were $50
of Tuesday $50 and this day they will probably amount to $300!!!
The Weather is warm, the walking bad, it rained fast last
night & is dark & cloudy now — M. L. Wells was here last
evening, on his way home he appeared pleased with the Painting.
H. G. Otis, & his Daughter were here yesterday also. They knew
nearly all the Portraits, without the Key — Some Charleston
people have been over, such as — Dr. Zufro & McIntire — Harris — Devers
Kettell, Robertson &c. — I am making preparations to light
the Picture, but dont know how it will succeed, I find it a
very difficult job, but I shall know soon, and will write you
on present employment, but as I have undertaken it I shall
do my best, I shall not make anything out of the Key, not even
enough to clothe me, but I do not wish to be anxious about that
knowing that I shall be provided for — I shall expect to hear from you
again soon — Please remember me to all Friends, I believe me
Yrs respectfully H. C. Pratt

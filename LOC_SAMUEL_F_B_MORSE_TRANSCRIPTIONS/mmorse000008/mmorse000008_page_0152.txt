In the vicinity of this State, and within hail
there are resident at Springfield N.Y. Howard
& Hooker; and in the City of St. John
Id. Bulkley, Ely, Rippon & Robert.
A meeting of the Clubs of 1810 (or as
many of its members as can conveniently
attend) at the next commencement, has
some months past, I have proposed it
to several of our Neighbors in this
neighborhood; they all spoke favorably
of the subject I will attend if possible
as my stay was short I hurry prompt
with you – I called on Prof. Hatch a few
moments, mention the subject to him
& he appeared pleased with it – In
fact I see nothing to hinder one of
the plaudantest meetings in the World
if we would but take a little pains to
make preparations & notify in season those
who we might reasonably expect will attend.

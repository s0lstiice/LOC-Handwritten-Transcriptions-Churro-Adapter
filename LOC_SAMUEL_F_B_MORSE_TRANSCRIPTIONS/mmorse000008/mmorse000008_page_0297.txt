less than it now costs us; I mean too that Father & Mother
shall accompany us; The house in N-Haven might be let
until we want it again at a very good rent: I wish
the subject to be well thought of, nor do I wish that part
which relates to my comfort in the society of my dearest earthly
blessings to be lightly esteemed in the account; I have a wife
and never to see her, to have children and have no influence in
forming their early habits, are privations of no small magnitude
and such as I shall try every method to remedy.
But it grows late, my dear wife, and I am
fatigued and must retire to my lonesome bed; Give my
loves to Father & Mother to Sister Susan, and kiss dear Sam
& Charley for their father; How is Susan's face since her tooth has
been drawn? Remember me to Clover Nancy Shepard & the rest of
the family and to all dear friends & neighbors in N-Haven; The
idea of leaving them is one of the most staggering reasons for
not leaving N-Haven that occurs to me. -- but again goodbye
I will write again soon --
Your affectionate husband
Finley

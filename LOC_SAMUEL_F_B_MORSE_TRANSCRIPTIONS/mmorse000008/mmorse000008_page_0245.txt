You, no doubt must be sensible of the mortification to
disappointment which this result has caused me, I know
not which way to turn, being a stranger here, and having no
credit with any one except Mr Cornelius, I went to
him this morning, and told him how I was situated, he
generously offered to let me have any amount that I wished,
and said he was glad to do anything that seemed like a
kindness to me, on account of his Friend Mr Morse,
he said. That he should have had me at his house while in
town, if it had been practicable, but he has the Crank boys
there, and a large Family. I expressd my gratitude to
him but told him that I could not have accepted such an offer
if he had made it He says his ashamed of Salem
people, for not giving more encouragement to the City
I shall borrow 220 of him, concluding that it will be the
best course that I can pursue I shall leave this ingenious
climate for N York, on Tuesday morning next in a Sloop for
New York, and I should rejoice to meet you there if it
is convenient, as you could probably be better able
than I to find a Room for the picture, but if you do not
go, please to send a line to meet me there informing me
of the fact I do not know that it will be
interesting or useful for me to say more at present,
Hoping that I may see you soon, I am Sir
Your Most Affy Servt H.B. Pratt
12th

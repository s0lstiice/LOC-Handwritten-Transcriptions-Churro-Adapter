[probably Mar. 16, 1821]
Copy of answer to Mr Ball
Mr J Ball Strange note.
Madam,
I dare not trust myself to
write the state of my feelings on
receiving your note of this morning;
suffice it to say that I can neither
take the picture back, make any
alteration in it, or abate one iota
of the Price. —
As to the charge of exorbi-
tancy, I must repel that by the
following facts; I painted the
Portrait of the Pres. for the City Hall,
Charged the Council 700 dollars; they
voted me 750. I painted a Portrait
of Miss Alston for her father, the
same size with yours and charged
him 800. dollars; he sent me 1000.
I painted another for him only one
third the size of yours and charged

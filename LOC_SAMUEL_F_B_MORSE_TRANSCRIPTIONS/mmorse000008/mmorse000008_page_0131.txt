Father preached to day for Mr Post; his health
is very good I never knew it better. Father sends
a great deal of love to you all, and many kisses for
little Grand-daughter. I wish the little Pussy for
her Father a great many times, and tell her that
Papa will come home by and by.
Good bye Dearest Wife
Write often of affectionate
Husband as ever
Finley.

Mr. Morse
Dear Sir I have returned to this place
after spending several weeks more than I had
contemplated and with much poorer success than
I had anticipated. I have exhibited the painting
in Albany Hartford and Middletown and in the
whole have received eighty two dollars and thirteen
cents that is to say in Albany $32.80 in Hartford $36.67
etc. in Middletown $12.65 etc. My expenses in the same
time have been eighty nine dollars and ninety three
cents that is to say for my passages - $18..75
" freight - 5..54
" Board - 26..03
" land carriage - 11..35
" etc putting up picture - " 86½
" oil & candles - 3..29
" land bills - 1..58
" Advertisements 4..75
" Rooms - 24..50
" lamps wicks
" and glass tapers
" tubs etc. 1..25
" cast foot stove - 12k
$89..93 which leaves me seven
dollars and eighty cents in debt after using all the
receipts instead of one half and I have in addition to
the above had to borrow $15 which I have got to pay
and only $5 on land for that purpose and by doing the
best I possibly can I shall after taking all the receipts
have to lose as good as 20 or 30 dol. (which I have used
in buying several articles which were necessary
in travelling which I should not have got if I had
stayed at home and also in buying some articles of clothing
and for mending &c.) besides my time for which if I had

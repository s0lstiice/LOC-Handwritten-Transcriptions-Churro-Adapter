New Haven Nov 24th 1821.
My very dear Husband,
Since I last wrote you I have received
two very kind letters from you one on Monday and one
yesterday morning both of which afforded me the highest
gratification, indeed even it not for the pleasure of hearing
from you my dear Finley this frequently I could not feel as an
accused to these long, and to me, very painful separations.
The time appears long already since I saw you my dear Husband
and though you have been gone but three weeks it seems
more like three months you have probably heard of the death
of our dear Mother ere this – what a sad change will
be made in our dear family – I scarcely realize it; as I
shall when I visit Corona again – the care of my mother is
so intimately connected with every thing there, that I shall
always be painfully reminded of her absence – but it is the
Lords doing – and I not only submit, but acquiesce in the
will of him, who doth all things well – I hope God that energy
has been mingled with judgment, that my strength has been
according to the day of trial – Hope the Lord of my soul
and forget not all his benefits – my prayer and desire is
that the may indeed prove a sanctifier affliction

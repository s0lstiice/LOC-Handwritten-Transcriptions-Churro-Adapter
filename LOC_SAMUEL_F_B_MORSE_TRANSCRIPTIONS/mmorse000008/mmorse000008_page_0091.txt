Boston Sept. 16th 1821.
Dearest Sister
I arrived safely in this morning at
noon, just in season to take dinner with Aunt & Uncle Salisbury; I had a very pleasant ride and
felt no inconvenience from my early rising; I should have been very glad to have had my dear Lucrce & little Sue with me, but all is as should be; this
line shall soon meet again in our own hired house at N-Haven, where I long to be; I shall leave here on Thursday morning, probably, so as to take the N.
London boat on Friday home.
I was charged 13c. in the mail stage from Concord so that you had better take the Accommodation when you come down. I suppose
you will hear from Father, at that time, you must be at Andover in order to return with him; Edward wished me to say you had better not be in Andover

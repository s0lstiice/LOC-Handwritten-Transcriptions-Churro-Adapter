April 2. 1823.
Dear Sir,
I received your
note yesterday, informing me
that you now had leisure to
attend to the painting of which
I had spoken to your father.
The opportunity I shall gladly
embrace to procure of
you a portrait of my wife.
The walks are now so unsettled
and I shall be so occupied
the remainder of this week
that my call must be deferred
til next Monday

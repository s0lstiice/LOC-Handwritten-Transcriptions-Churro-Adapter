Windsor July 17, 1822.
My dear friend,
You will doubtless be
surprised at receiving a letter from me
after so great a lapse of time since
we saw each other; but as the subject
will perhaps be interesting to you &
the rest of our Colonies residing in
this state and its vicinity, I shall
want to trouble you with a few lines

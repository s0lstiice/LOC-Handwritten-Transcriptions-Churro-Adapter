Boston March 22d 1825
Dear Sir,
I received your letter of the 18th and heartily rejoice
with you in the goodness of God. I hope your Son may be permitted
to grow up to be a comfort and joy to its Parents —
Among those who have visited your Picture, yesterday, were
Gov. Philips, Judge Quincy, Mrs (old Nick) Fuller, &c —
The Weather on Thursday was very stormy, from Morning to
night, the snow fell 4 or 5 inches — Wednesday was quite
blustering — Friday was pleasant, and very
thawy. I have taken off today — I think it will be safest for me
to pay off my bills, now before I depart any more in the bank,
Owing to the badness of the weather. I think I had better stay
here next week, and week after —
I have sent home the Recorder, which contains a
notice of yr Picture — being the same as has been copied by me,
Mr Willis called and left it here, you will observe also
that he has copied the Advertisement. I gave him a Season
Ticket for himself and family — The cause of Religion (as far
as my limited means will allow me to know), is steadily prospering
but I cannot give you particulars — I was desired to go last S. Evening
to hear an Universalist — he was a young man in every Sense
and I was ready to say with Truseli a most extravagant bad Preacher

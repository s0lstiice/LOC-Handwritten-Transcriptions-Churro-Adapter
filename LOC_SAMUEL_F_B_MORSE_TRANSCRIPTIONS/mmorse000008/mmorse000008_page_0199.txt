I don't know but I ought to make some reply to the closing part
of your letter, which I shall do by saying that I felt you were
dispensing great confidence in me when you put your Picture into
my hands. And I assure you Sir, that my honorable feelings, (solicited
from the debt of gratitude which I owe you) would naturally prompt
me to do the best for you which it is in my power, please to accept of
this assurance, I believe me to be humbly Servt
H. B. Pratt
Remember me in love to all the Family, and keep dear Little
Queen for them

I find this letter will not go till to-morrow even. so I will keep
it open to add a word if any thing material occurs.
I have just commenced a new portrait Mr Elias Kane's this will occupy me till
Monday even. and give me opportunity for other applications and may change my
plans for returning, Mr Kent has just come in and says Mr. Howe his wife is just ar-
rived but declines sitting at present, I think that I may take it in New York at a future
time, and the Chancellor's too his brother; by the way, the Chancellor will pass through
New Haven in a few days, I hope he will be invited to our hotel.
I was just now surprised by a visit from Uncle & Aunt Bruce from Utica they are
on their return to Utica. I am very much pleased with Aunt Bruce I think she is a very fine woman
Dr Rice and Lady & Mr Warner & lady are in the city, I believe they should take my station
at one of the hotels I should have all my acquaintances in the world pass in review be-
fore me in a short time. Mr. Poinsett of So. Car. is also in town.
Good bye I must close for the mail hour has arrived
Yrs as ever
Winlin
Thursday afternoon

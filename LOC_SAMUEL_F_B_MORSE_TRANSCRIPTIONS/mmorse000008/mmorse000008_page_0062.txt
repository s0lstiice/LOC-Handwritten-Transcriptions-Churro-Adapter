Pittsfield (May 25. 1821
My Dearest Wife
We are thus far on our tour safe
and sound; Mr. Hillman's health is very per-
fectly better, delicate, last night lodged at
Litchfield, Mr. S. had an excellent night and
is in good spirits -- at Litchfield I called on
Judge Reeves and sat a little while, I found Mr.
Farrar there from Andover, they all remarked me
very cordially, thanked me for her present,
enquired after the family &c. I called at
Mr Beecher's with Mr Hillman, and Judge Gould
no one at home, called with Mr S. at Dr.
Shields and stayed a few moments; I sat
a few moments also at Judge Gould's; I was
much pleased with the exterior appearance of
Litchfield saw at a distance Edwards pick-
ered pond, we left at 5 this morning, breakfasted at Norfolk, dined at Stockbridge, we
then left the Stage, and have him a wagon to go
on to Middlebury St at our leisure, we lodge here

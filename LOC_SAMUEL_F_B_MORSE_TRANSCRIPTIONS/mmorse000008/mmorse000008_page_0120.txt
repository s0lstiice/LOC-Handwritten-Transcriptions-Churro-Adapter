love to you & kind remembrance to Henry and me
I remain as ever your affectionate Brother
E D Dillow
1841 Gather to look at the National
Intelligence of Tuesday 18 Decr he will find a good price in
Favour of the Indians.—
Your Wife says she will write you on Monday next as she has written
you so much lately but she says send him a number of kisses
from myself & little Daughter Sue the latter sends a great many
kisses to Dea Grandpa — I have written a long letter to your Brother
& requesting him not to pledge himself with respect to copyright
either to C & A or L & R but leave it till your returns action

Boston Wednesday even. Feb. 19. 1821
My Dearest Wife,
Inc'd your letter of the 16th inst.
this morning, I have tried to match the cambric
by your pattern at twenty different places, and
I invariably found the same colours at all the
shops, the nearest to is a bright yellow on the
one hand, and a light bluish green on the other, of
the two I should by all means choose the green
as the yellow makes the ground of your chair look
dirty. I will not purchase till you
have time to answer this; I have purchased
your fringe at 5/- per yard a very superior
price, Aunt S. thought it was wrong as you
limited the price to 4/6 I must bear the blame
of the odd 6d. little Sue's request is complied
with I have got her knife & fork. Edwards
request is also complied with, as it respects Mun=
sells Map of Kinl. Does he wish one to pay for it?
My health is better, as I am very prudent and

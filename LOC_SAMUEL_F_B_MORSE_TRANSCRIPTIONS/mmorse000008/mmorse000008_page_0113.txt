Mr Taylor sermon this afternoon was from 2d Kings 4, 26, and she
answered It is well -- occasioned by the death of his youngest child.
Society -- about a year and a half old, whom they buried last Tuesday
after a very short illness -- this was a very sudden stroke, the father
particularly seemed bowed down under the pressure of this heavy
affliction, and he was enabled to speak with great feeling from
the above text -- how often are we reminded by the word
and providence of God, to beseech our children with are the
dearest earthly objects of affection to a Christian parent's heart,
as though an appearance them not, We ought ever to remember
how frail is the tenure of all our earthly enjoyments,
and how liable we are to have them torn from us by
death -- affliction reaches us more, the vanity of world
and the uncertainty of every thing here below, than any
thing else -- this is not our rest, we need to be continually
reminded of it, for we are all prone to make the
worlds too much our home and lose sight of heaven --
I feel very anxious to make a slight improvement of the
late chastisement of our heavenly Father in bringing me
of a dear, very dear Parent, if the fruit of this affliction
is to make sin more bitter -- to make me more afraid
& committing it in thought, word, and deed, more
heavily minded, more conformed to Him who was made
perfect thus suffering -- how shall I bless his chastising
hands! I received a kind letter of condolence from
Mary Appleton a few days since -- she expresses the hope
that you have been with me in this trial, that I have
enjoyed the sympathy of an affectionate husband and not

I am glad none of the family but one of us are on this note late or any return
We expect Edw's Friday night & hope to go Saturday 6 o'clock & get to Pompey in season &
so over I spend Sabbath in Woodstock & can now fix no time to be home -- but it will
be as early next week as possible --
Edw's may expect funds here to settle his debt I had requested $175 from Mr O -- but am glad to get
any -- If you will after receiving this address it to Hartford, to the care of Henry Read on No 9 --
The storm prevents my going abroad today & may delay my return as I mean to stay &
finish the business with C -- as far as it can be done at this time --
We hope Finley will recover strength to work at his pictures I find high respect for
him from it here -- Rich must not apply himself so closely to injure his health & spirit -- Debby
must also be cautioned --
Our affectionate regards to yourself, Harriet, & brother love & many kisses to dear little Sue & kind
remembrance to the Tracy, Nancy, Betsey & Eliza
Your affectionate father

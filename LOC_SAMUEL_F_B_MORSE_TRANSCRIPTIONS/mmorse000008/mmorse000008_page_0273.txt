Dalton knows them very well. Mrs Lovett wishes I could obtain
for her some lines of Poetry which father introduced into a sermon
preached at Woodstock being the answer of a lady to the question
"What is religion?" I told her I would mention it when I went home;
perhaps father can recollect it; religion is described in it as cheerful
not forbidding & gloomy.
If Henry is in NewHaven ask him to measure the Statue of
the Venus de Medici in feet & inches very accurately and let me know
in your letter; I want two measurements 1. the height of a perpendicular
line from the top of the head to the sole of the foot, and 2. the height
along the figure from head to foot being the height if the figure stood straight.
I wish Henry also to make particular enquiry on board the
packets and in the storehouses at N. Haven for my Hercules, for I
forgot to tell you that Mr Robertson found me it was put on
board the same packet with the Congress Hall; if so, it is missing,
but it is a kind of thing not easily lost & if search is made
for it I have no doubt it will be found, at best if it is advertised.
My I am pleasantly situated as to family, rooms, and
board, I only want enough to do. If I should not find
enough to employ me here I seriously think I shall return to
New York and sit down there, this I had rather not do at present
but it will be cheaper, for travelling is expensive, and
business very uncertain. I hear very unfavorable accounts
as to prospects of success westward. Roaming becomes more
& more insome. imperious necessity alone drives me to this
course. Don't think, by this, I am fainthearted I shall persevere

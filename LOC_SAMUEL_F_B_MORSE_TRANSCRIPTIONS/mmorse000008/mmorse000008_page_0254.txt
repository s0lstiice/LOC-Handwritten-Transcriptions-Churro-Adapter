I am rejoiced that you are in such
good spirits. You have not suffered
alone – If you go on any expedition you
must pass this way of the Richard himself.
It has no doubt in imagination had the
yellow fever – but he recovered & is now very
well indeed – We have moved over to Brooklyn
& the situation is high, airy & delightful,
after being all the day in town.

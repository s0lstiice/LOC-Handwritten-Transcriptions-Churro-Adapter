Charleston April 28th
1813
Dear Sir
About the commencement of your labour
in Charleston, Mrs Rebekah Stiles placed
in your hands an old portrait with a desire that
you would take this from a Copy, which was prom-
ised on your part, but either from the great press
of business which you had to go through in
some other cause, she has not heard whether you
have done any thing in it, and your departure
from this place without any communication (the
period of which was not known to her) gives room
to suppose you have not done so; By her request
therefore I have to beg you will be so good as to
inform me what arrangement you have made
respecting it, Should you have the original
with you, I must beg the favour of you to send
it me by an excellent opportunity which

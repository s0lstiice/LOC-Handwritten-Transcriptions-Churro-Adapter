Boston Sept. 11th 1821
Wednesday morning 6 o'clock
Dear Parents,
We all arrived safely in this place on
Friday evening. On the day we left you we rode as
far as Stafford springs and the next day to Boston;
Nancy went to Charlestown on Saturday morning and
Eden Jr. to Andover in the afternoon; we have been
staying at Aunt Bartlett's where every kindness and
attention that could be paid has been paid to
us. Sue is quite well and highly delighted
with every thing, she has not given us the least
trouble, she won the affections of all in the place;
every one says they never saw so well behaved a
child. We leave Boston in an hour for
Concord; we find the stage goes every day. I
did not see Eden Jr. after he left me on Saturday
morning so I cannot tell you what he did or to bring.
I suppose he will write you from Dover.
I will write again at Concord. Harriet Bee sends a great
deal of love, and little Sue sends a kiss for dear Grand-
Papa & Grand mama and Uncle. With suitable remem-
bance to all the family I am your aff. son Finley.

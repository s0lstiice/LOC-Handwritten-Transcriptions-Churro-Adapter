been engaged at my picture which is greatly im-
proved; Mr. Allison's suggestions have been of great
advantage to it; I complete it to-morrow fore-
noon and have it open to the public on Friday morn-
ing. -- I will tell you a secret to be kept by the
family and not to be mentioned abroad, Mr. Allison
is to be married this summer, and has positively
determined to settle in New Haven, he flatters
me by saying that the principal inducement is
my society; -- I am almost afraid, if he comes
to N.Haven that N.Haven will be too happy a place
for me; I have not seen his great picture, he is
finishing some part before I can see it, he has had
a good deal of perplexity with it some parts he has
painted over 6 or 7 times and one part 14 times. --
he will finish it soon. -- Good bye, dear Wilks, I long to
be at home again, Rip little Susan and take good care
of your heart, give my love to Walter & mother & Edward
and Richard & Nancy and the rest of the family. --
your aff. husband
Finley.

Hartford Nov. 1st 1823
Mr. Morse
Dear Sir
I expect you must be surprised at my long silence but trust you will excuse me when I say it has been because I had nothing pleasing to communicate but I trust I now have found a more favorable climate and one which will cause me to write in order that you may rejoice with me in the success which I hope to meet with. – The prospect is very flattering – the Editors of the Mirror called on me and tendered me his paper for the purpose of inserting any communication I might wish – if you see the Mirror for next month you will find in it what he says and that is but little of what I have heard – however next week will decide my destiny for this city. – I shall then go to Springfield and Northampton and on my return stop at Middletown.
My receipts in Albany were so small that I am afraid the paper will blush in telling the tale – they were barely $32,,80k 100/100!!
I did your errand to Mr. Carter he said he ordered the Me according to your direction but feared as you had not received it his letter had miscarried and that he would write again
Remember me to friends
I am Dear Sir
Yours De,
C. In Doxlittle

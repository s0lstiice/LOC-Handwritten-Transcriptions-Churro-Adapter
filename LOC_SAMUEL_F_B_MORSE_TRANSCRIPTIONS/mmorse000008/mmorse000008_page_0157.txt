Dr Lucretia Your Mother concludes to visit Andover
before she returns to be there with Edward, to assist
him in putting up his things. He will return on
Monday, and be ready to take the Stage on Tuesday, I got
home on Wednesday – This arrangement gives me
no more than sufficient time to finish my busi-
ness here, properly, as the storm has hindered
me – I am sorry for this delay, as it will pre-
vent my being present at the sitting of the Missionaries. I wish Mr Tracy to do us a favor & send to the
Mission family at this Sandwich Island a
copy of my Report, & of the News of the day. I
drop a short letter to Mr Bingham, informing
him that I present them with my kind regards
that my necessary absence prevents me
writing them as I had intended to – I am
sure he will do up the parcel nicely & give it to one
of the Missionaries –
Take good care of yourself – I may have
Lord bless you all
J M.

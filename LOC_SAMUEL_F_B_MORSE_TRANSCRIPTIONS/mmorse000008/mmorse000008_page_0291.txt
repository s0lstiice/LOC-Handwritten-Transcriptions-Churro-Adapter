New York. Nov. 15th 1823.
Saturday evening.
My Dear Wife,
After a tedious & unpleasant voyage
I arrived here at 12 o'clock to-day; just as we left
N.Havien harbor yesterday a sail from St.W began and con-
tinued without interruption till last night at 12. we made
Black rock harbor at 2 o'clock yesterday after toasting
about for 6 hours all on board deaf sick, we lay at an-
chor in B.Rock harbor till 2 o'clock this morning and ar-
rived at Byram cove at daylight, we had a pleasant ride
from there here; I feel very tired and can write but
little, I am in good spirits, have obtained a place to
day at friend Coolidge's at 2.25 and am in treaty for a fine
room in Broadway opposite Trinity Church yard for which
I shall have to pay 7 doll. P. M. but still I think
though the price is great, it is better to give it and go vig-
orously at work. but Mr. Tracy is waiting with Charles
who is well, and I can write no more, love to Father & Mother
Sister and Rip the dear Children. I want the family
picture he.
Good bye My Aff. husband
Finley.

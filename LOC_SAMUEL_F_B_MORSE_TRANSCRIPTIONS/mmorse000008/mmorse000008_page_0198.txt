I have made enquiry about Lamps. Mr Doggett says that former
Exhibitors brought them with them from E. York, I cannot get any one
indeed I buy them, and I conclude that as we shall want them somewh
it will be best to get some made, I have accordingly spoken for
6 to be made in all this week, they are to be made larger enough to
take nearly a pint of ale in the Liverpool fashion, only plain with
that which falls on one side (this is the Liverpool Directly down
upon it) & the apparent aim on one side is a piece of finished tin brush
the lamp is finished and which I expect every selection, I think that
6 will be enough, but if they should not I must get more --
This will cost each -- your direction, about the green baize
I will attend to, a lot to pay for Uncle's liberty --
I should like to know how wide you would have the barges?
My Receipt this day will not amt to a Dollar, nor is it to
be wondered at for I should think that all would stay at home, who
were not necessitate to go out --
Saturday -- Long
In the first part of this letter in was written on Thursday I mention that
the receipt for that day would not amt to $1.00 a few more however came
in the afternoon so that my account was Thurs. 3 -- Friday 9 -- Saturday
being a pleasant day $22.90 -- Thurs 10.8 -- 8 p.m.
I intend to have deposit $27.00 in the City Bank today, but midlock
the hour supposing the Bank closed at 4 o'clock I am sure I will
probably be able to deposit $40 or more on Monday --
I have thought Sir, that when I get ready to Exhibit in the Exposition will
be best to insert over the top of the Presidentment
"For 3 weeks Only -- " because that people are
under the impression that it will be here a long time, and therefore put
off coming -- I think it best for us to get our money as quick as we can
and in that way save time Responce -- If the people of Philadelphia
had known that they could have seen the Exhibition any time, we might
have spent the Exhibition for 3 months, and not collected much
more than we did in 4 days --

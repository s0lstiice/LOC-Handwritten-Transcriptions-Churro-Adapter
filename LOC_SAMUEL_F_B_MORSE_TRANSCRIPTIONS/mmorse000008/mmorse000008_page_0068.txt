Saturday morning July 14th. All doing well. I gave Maj. Howard
holding a check this morning for what was due from me $440.72. I
thought Potter told me he had made an arrangement to exchange with them
for this, but I suppose he forgot it.
Rev. J. D. Moore U.S.
Sam G. P. S. S. U. S. S.
Postmaster General
St. Louis
13th July 3

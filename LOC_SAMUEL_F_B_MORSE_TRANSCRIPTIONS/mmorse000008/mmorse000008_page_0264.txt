CONDITIONS.
1. The paper is issued weekly, on Saturday morning.
2. It is printed in the folio form, on paper of the royal size.
3. The price is three dollars a year, payable in six months, or two dollars and fifty cents, if paid in
advance.
Any person, becoming responsible for six copies, shall receive a seventh copy.
Subscriptions received at the Bookstore of JOHN P. HAVEN, No. 182 Broadway, and
at the Editors' Office, 50 Wall-street.
Aug. 7. NewYork.
Dear Brother,
Your things have arrived. I
have just taken them on board the steam boat.
hope you will receive them safely.
I enclose the key.
Let me hear from you, & send me
any commands to execute. Yp? Mr Coit
Today $44. & pass'd it to my credit.
Your affectionate brother
Rich. C. More.

This last picture, the "Impertinent Author" was
sold to Mr Hope for 50 guineas; he has made one
copy of it for Lord Carlisle & is to make another for
Mr Murray. He is now painting "Love's quarrelling"
from Moliere which promises to surpass the other.
I am still living with the Bridgens in
the old quarters. Harriet is not yet married
to Brock, (they are both too poor). Betty is
grown a shipping wench & is in good natured
as ever. I have just received a letter from
Carle who is at Rio Janeiro. He has been

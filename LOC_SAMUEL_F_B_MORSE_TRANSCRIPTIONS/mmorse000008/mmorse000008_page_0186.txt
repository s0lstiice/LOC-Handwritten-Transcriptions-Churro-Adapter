While & Aunt Salisbury are well, they came to see my pic-
ture to-day. Son Mason has been in twice to-day he
came in the 2 time with Mr. Leav & family, he is very
much pleased as he recognises nearly all in the house.
I do not know when to say I shall be at home
again, you may depend upon it is soon as I can. I think
I shall go down to Salem and arrange the business there
and then return to Boston and then home.
Good bye my Dearest Wife
Your little friend for her Father tells her she must
leave to read before I come home.
Love to all.
Your Husband
Finley

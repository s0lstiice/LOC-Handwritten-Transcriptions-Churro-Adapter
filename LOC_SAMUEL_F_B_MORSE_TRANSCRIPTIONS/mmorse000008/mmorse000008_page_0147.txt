Yesterday Evening - McReed - of Massachusetts
residing here for many Years, keeping a Shoe
Store - I concerned therein with a Mosquito
also concerned in Merchandise with a Mr.
Horsy - turning himself with his pocket
handkerchief to a large Stuffle in the
Wall of the Chamber in which he has
been laying down this before 6 o'Clock
they have up stairs to shut the Sunday
night's hard rain found him hanging
by his ear almost resting on the floor
- so that he was rescued.
Dr. McCall you will have seen by the
paper - has been shot by his own pistol
and like accident - the ball
entered the temporal lary on the right
side passed thru the upper side
(left) of the skull the bones of the
head have now separated that
his face could no longer carry
the marks of Dr. McCall.
Prayed of an Old Friend

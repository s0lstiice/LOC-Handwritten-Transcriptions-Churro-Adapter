Brockdon is married & gone to Italy,
Waller is living at 22 Charlotte Street, where
Allston had his room. Martin is getting
money & children. His pictures have become
very popular, though they are not so much
liked by the artists. They
are about the same
comparision to Turner that
a splendid Melo-drama
does to a play of Shakespeare. Not that
I mean to compare Turner to Shakespeare.
The remark is only made to explain
why they strike the public more than the
judges.

H.M. Ship Adventure, Naples Bay March 20th 1823.
My dear Friend.
I trust I need not say how gratified I am by the receipt
of your very interesting letter, and how much I regret that so few opportunities
of communicating from the port of the enemy for indeed, I have
but the moment had notice of the present occasion, and cannot therefore
enter into the long detail. I am about to trouble you with.
I rejoice extremely in the success you have already in the
beautiful Act you have adopted, and have no doubt that American sailors
will meet to your talent and exertion. Ruthy American engines, I
cannot but feel deeply anyone that has advance in Science, Literature
and Art should be commensurate with her power. Remembrance stipulation
desirable and that can only be attained by a diligent and persevering
application of Genius to immediate objects. I shall be much disappointed
if circumstances prevent any having at some time or other a tour through
the United States.
I cannot, for want of time, enter at present, on any views &
projects, but I am glad to inform you that I am rapidly completing,
after some years labour, a post public undertaking the first volume
of which has just been published by the Dominikty, but I doubt
from Malta, previous to any copy having arrived from England. It is
large & worthy work, of which I shall be proud to send over one of the
first impressions for your notice. The instrumental results are determined
by the finest instruments, & the whole detail is the fruit of much
laborious fatigue.
I return many thanks for your handsome by printed Gazetteer

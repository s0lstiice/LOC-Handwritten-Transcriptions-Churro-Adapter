My dear More/ I am in debt to you I don't
know how many letters & must throw myself
entirely on your good nature to pardon a long
silence for which I blame myself most severely.
The truth is I am the very worst of correspondents
& my aversion to writing a letter amounts almost
to honor. I hope when this reaches you
you will do me the justice to believe that
I am incapable of forgetting an old friend
with whom I have spent so many pleasant
hours. It is with the sincerest pleasure
I have heard of the domestic happiness you
are enjoying and the of your prosperity in
your profession. Will not the latter enable
you soon to come to Europe?
For my own part I have but little
hope of returning to America at present. My
lot has been of late to have gained by

Bishop Brownell & his lady, and say that they always
call on them when they come to Albany.—
Col. Baldwin has been very kind and obligeing to me
he is in high estimation in this city, and deservedly so.
Gen. Van Rensselaer called on me yesterday and
invited me to call and see him. Elkanah Watson Esq.
is not in town. I called on Mr. Chester, he was not
at home, I heard him preach to day; Mr Robert
Kalston and his two daughters Elizabeth & Abby came
up in the boat from N.York with me, he is not in
good health; Bishop Hobart was also on board and
young Stephen Salisbury of Worcester & a great many
acquaintances; there were about 250 persons in the
boat.— Dr. Rice & lady of Richmond came up in the
Friday's boat, he is also in ill health;—
I presume I shall see Mr. Tittiman soon
on his return. and yet
I can form no idea of the prospect of success
in my profession here, I shall paint one or more
gratuitously, and shall soon know, If I get enough
to employ me I shall go no farther, if not I may
visit some of the smaller interior towns.—
I feel the absence from home more than I ever
did, nothing but a sense of duty, and a full persuasion
that it is, in God's wise providence, for the best, keeps me

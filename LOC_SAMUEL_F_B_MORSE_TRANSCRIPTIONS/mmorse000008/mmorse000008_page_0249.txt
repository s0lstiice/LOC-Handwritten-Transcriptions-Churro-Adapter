in town a Mr Wallis who has taken 2 Views of P. York
and the surrounding Scenery, in Water colours, They are
to be engraved in Aquatint Price to be 12s in pairs
He has issued Proposal, and has already obtained 25 or 30
Subscribers. This I think shows that People are fond of Views.
I doubt not but you have thought from my former letters,
that I was impatient to get released from the care of your
Pictures. I confess I am somewhat so, because I find it
much more is known, and fear that so long an interval
from Painting will prove more injurious, than what I ever
anticipated, when I commenced the Exhibition, but notwithstanding
all this, I wish you to keep me here as long as you
consider it necessary for your convenience, and if my letters
are written in a moment of discontentment, you must not
think that I wish you to release me until it is convenient for
you to do so.
The receipt of this day is 3s 2½d
The 3 last days have averaged 1s 2s 5d about one half taken
in the evenings I have lighted every Evening in the week, so
that I am confined here from 7 o'clock A.M till after 10 P.M.
except the short time at my meals. You must excuse
this bad writing as my Pen is poor and I have for the present
lost my knife. Please give my love to your Family
I should be glad to hear often from you, you can be sure
by him down to the Packet now at them in the shortest letter.
I remain Yours &c H. C. Pratt

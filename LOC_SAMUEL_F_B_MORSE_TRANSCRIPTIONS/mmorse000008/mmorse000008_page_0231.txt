Boston April 1st 1823
My Dear Sir:
I have just received your letter of March 31st, and I suppose
you that I am not a little surprised to know that you are so
just now, you have probably received before this time my letter
of April 2d and I think if you are acquainted with all the
circumstances, you will permit me to stay here another week
The Snow that fell on Monday rendered the walking (and riding
too) quite intolerable, but there has been rain since and now
the Snow is almost gone -- So that it is probable that next
week will be more favorable than any yet. add it to this
that yesterday was last day so that I could not open
I concluded in the beginning of this week that I should
have to stay the next, and therefore have not sent
around my last week Handbills I have consulted
Mr Doggett he says that he would not have me lay
any stress on his opinion, contrary to your orders, but
thinks if I stay next week I shall stand a good chance
for success as in any former week. others are of the same
opinion all agree in saying that we came here in the
most unfavorable season -- that Boston people will not
not come out unless in pleasant weather -- &c &c
On the other hand I have your orders to leave this place
next week, what to do I know not. I wish to consult

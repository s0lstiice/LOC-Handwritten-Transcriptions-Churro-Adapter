Boston July 2d 1823
Sam'l T. B. More Esq.
Dear Sir
We gave to Mr Pratt your account at the time he left here the balance
of which was ninety nine dollars I then expected he
would remit it from Salem in a few days but was
disappointed, and have not heard from him since
he left Salem and the balance still remains, I have thought
it proper to give you this information
Respectfully yours &c
John Doyett Esq

Washington Dec. 11th 1821
Dear Father,
I have been delayed in sending the enclosed
by not being able to see Mr. Dwight;
I have nothing new to add with respect
to my own affairs except that I am progressing as
fast as can possibly be expected.
The choice of Chaplain yesterday by the
House I think will create a general feeling of regret
& may I not say of humiliation among true Chris-
tians of all denominations; after a hard struggle
of 4 ballotings Rev. Jared Sparks of Baltimore
was chosen their chaplain; Unitarians will triumph,
no doubt, they are playing a deep game here,
as their arch-master, the Secy of State is wholly
in their interest. -- Something should be done to
stop their career, or infidelity, (the remains of it
in our country) will soon arrange itself under this
name of Christianity in order to wound it more readily,
I am convinced that Prayer, united special prayer for
their swift destruction, will be the most powerful

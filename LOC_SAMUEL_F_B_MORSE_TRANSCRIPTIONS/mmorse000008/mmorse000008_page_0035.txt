I am still engaged in my 2d part, will we be more
longer than I had expected. I hope to finish it in all
this month -- About 1000 inst. page letter paper have
been sent to the President -- as much more is ready
I will be sent by next mail -- I then will probably
have 200 pages more -- I think it will be published
before you get home or soon after
Your bottling are to this in Ellis St. in the Gazetteer
which is far as it is finished (200 pages) I think, for them
credit, I will satisfy the public -- due prospects in various
respects are brightening -- It gives as pleasure to hear
that yours are also -- that you are likely to bring
home some business for the summer -- I have done
nothing about buying the house stand for another
reason I wish you to write us what you will have
us do -- Let think the rent too high, considering
the accommodation -- Probably they will lower the
rent, & let us have the land to cultivate, life she
want exercise -- If we could at all, I think
it must be postponed till the next season, propose
Longa -- It will depend on my success in disposing
of my property
I have received of President Day $20 for
John Tabbs, that I wish you to give to him -- It should
be passed to your credit -- I hope you will finish
in getting the $15 dollars from Mr Mitchell, or in
his absence, of his parents -- Let Henry carry the note
to him, in them -- There is a trifling one from Mr
Hoffman I believe some books to be returned, but am
not certain, others may call on him I know, I receive
the books, bring them with your baggage --
Mr Willinston mentioned a book seller, whom he
could recommend as an Agent for selling my books.
Ask him for his name -- Enquire as to his responsibility
& activity, & request the Bookeller to write me

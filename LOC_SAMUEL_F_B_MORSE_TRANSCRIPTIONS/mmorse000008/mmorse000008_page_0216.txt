Boston March 26th 1823
Dear Sir:
I received your letter of Monday, and am
glad to hear that you are not disappointed as to the
succes of your Picture – Among those who have been
here to see it: are Mrs. Foley and Family; Miss Mary
Payson came with them – Mrs B. Gorham – Dr Hodder
of Charleston, who desired a particular remembrance of you
and your family – Mr Fisher (the artist) was here
yesterday – just arrived from Charleston S.C. – he appears
in better health than I ever saw him before; they all seemed
well pleased with your Picture –
Lellys Picture arrived here 2 days since in 6 days
from Charleston – to be Exhibited after yours is gone –
Mr Dunlaps also came here the same day, but has since
gone to Providence, Mr Dorsey, who has the care of it,
is here yet, – appears to be a fine young man –
Monday of this week was fair Receipt 500 Reys "37 1/2
Tuesday also fair Receipt 900 "75 Reys 1"
Wednesday Snow Storm all day – Receipt 1000 "75 Reys "12 1/2
The Receipt of Wed. 19th was 400 Reys "25 –
I have lighted the Picture 5 Evening and have in that time
taken 1450 – I heard Mr. Corneille Beach last Sunday

New Haven Feb. 22. 1823.
My dear son,
We are glad to hear such good
news from you as to your health & prospects
particularly that your picture has received
the sanction of your friend Mr Alston.
The approbation of such a judge is worth
much. The information of the determination
of the Commissioners is highly gratifying
to us all -- We are in treaty with
Mr Condie about his paper -- with the
prospect of a satisfactory issue -- & a provision
for Rich. -- Thus far we are favorably disposed
with the smiles of Providence --
I find my note at St. J. Bank
becomes renewed on the 24 of Feb. instead
of 24 March -- as I had noted it in bank
supposing it 90 days -- instead of 60 --
It shall do well enough with it, if not
disappointed from Vermont whence I
have received nothing yet -- You have
$150 in bank -- as I have ascertained to-day
If you come home next week, it will
be in season to afford me the necessary
support -- I enclose Daggett's and (with $100)
after Rich's for 2000 for $61 -- have
written them on the subject -- I think they
will readily pay it in bankable money
at 1 p. c. to discount, what you may allow

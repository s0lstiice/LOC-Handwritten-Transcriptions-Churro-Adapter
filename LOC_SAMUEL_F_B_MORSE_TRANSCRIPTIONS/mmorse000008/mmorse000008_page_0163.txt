My dear Morse
Your letter of the 8th. have been answered earlier -- I do
not mind writing I spend little else to do -- It is my
business. -- I am unable to give you an official
reply to your proposition about the room -- Mr. Elliott is not in
town & Mr. Poinsett is on the high seas: you will soon
receive one -- I will only say in conversing with Mr. J. B.
while in Canton -- they requested me to advise you
that the time you wish the Academy it will be
opened for the annual Exhibition, spoken of by the
Rules: Show me a letter to the same effect from
Vanderlyn: Within the same reply will be given
we are at this moment exhibiting -- Passage
to the Delaware -- as you will

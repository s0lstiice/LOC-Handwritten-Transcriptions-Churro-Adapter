New-York Nov. 22d. 1823.
My Dear Wife
I shall write you a long and
particular letter as soon as I get settled,
in the mean time I can only give you a
hasty unconnected scrawl. The things
that were missing have all arrived safe,
the Paragon has not yet arrived, but will
doubtless be in to night; I thank you for
your prompt attention to your excited husbands
requests. I am glad dear little Susan is relieved
from her troublesome tooth and that she displayed
so much courage & I miss her for me, &
also little Charles, I miss them and you and
Auntie and all very much, but I dare not think
of home too much. I begin to paint on Monday
Chancellor Kent's portrait for Mrs Kent who
is in town and very much interested
for me. I think I have a pretty good

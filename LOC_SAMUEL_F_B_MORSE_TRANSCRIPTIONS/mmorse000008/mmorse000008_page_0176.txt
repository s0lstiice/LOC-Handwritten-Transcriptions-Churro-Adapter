Dear Sir
I am much obliged to you
for your polite invitation to visit
your painting of the House of Reps. and
I exceedingly regret that my departure
for Durstine at 6. o'clock in the morning
will prevent me from accepting it.
If I had arrived yesterday before evening it was my intention to have asked
the favor of seeing the picture – I hope
however soon to see it on public exhibition –
I hope your own disposition
will soon be removed, that I may
have the pleasure of seeing you at
Washington or in Massachusetts.
With the highest respect
I am Sir
your most obliged servant
Joseph Story
Mr Samuel F. B. Morse
Sunday June 26th 1823.
June 27th 1823.

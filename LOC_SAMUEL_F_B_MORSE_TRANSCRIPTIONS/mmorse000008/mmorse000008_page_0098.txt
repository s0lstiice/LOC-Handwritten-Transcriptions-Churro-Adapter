Newport 1818
The Mail robber whom you may see by this passage
another having escaped from the President was
taken & restored. The Enterprise Capt. Stearns
has taken from the Rivolis near the Havana
4 of their Vesels & only one man & since
that a vessel screw had been arrested
at Savannah having on board certain
property a Piano Forte among the rest which
had belonged to the Cosmopolite since destroyed
upon the Rocks proof having risen up
that they were severely taken from her
at sea.
My change of place I feel more and more
every month Patience and Virtue all pain
can subdue I must therefore watch & pray so
that Genl. Scott & Col. Baudet arrived just this moment in the
Stage Mr. & Mrs. Turnbull only before yesterday
— Wright has returned — I see a head
of an Bishop in Little which I have he
is about to engraving this way undertakes

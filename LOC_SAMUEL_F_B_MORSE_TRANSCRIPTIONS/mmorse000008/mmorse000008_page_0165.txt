Savannah Dec 23d 1822.
Dear Home
Your favor of the 17th
came to hand some days since - I am
glad to find that you have been so well
employed in painting large pictures
of making little hands - I hope you will
be able to give agreed account of your
calamities when I see you -- I came
on board city in November & expect
to repair until done here in Feb.
I went on in August to New York with
my two oldest boys -- took them from them
to Burlington N.Y. and placed them at
a school which I think I told you of --
Could you ever pass that way you will
oblige me by taking a prospect there
I ordained such summer to preserve
your palmetto tree -- but the weather
that we had in which I had to
take a very active part put it clean
out of my head -- as I do not speak here

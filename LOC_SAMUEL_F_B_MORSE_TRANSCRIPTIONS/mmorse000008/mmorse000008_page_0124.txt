my dear wife I can set without fear of disappoint-
ing you; I may be delayed longer, but I will inform
you more definitely another time.—
How I do long to see that dear little
girl of mine and to hear her sweet prattle;
instruct her early, my dear wife in the most
important of all concerns, teach her that there is
a great Father above, her obligations to him, and
to her Savior, kiss her often for Papa, and tell her
Papa will come back again one of these days.—
Give my love to all the family, to Dear Mother
and to brothers and all Concord friends when you
want. — Good bye my dear wife. let me
hear often from you.
Yr Aff husband Finley.
I suppose I shall see Father on Tuesday.

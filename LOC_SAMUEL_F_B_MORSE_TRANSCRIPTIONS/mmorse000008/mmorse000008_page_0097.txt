My dear & highly valued Mentor.
1st Nov. 1821.
Your kind letter I now acknowledge with many thanks
for your recollections of me. I cannot envy you my own
pleasures up on and near Lake George, but I regretted
at your having partaken of the self same delights
which it had permitted me, some few years since
even before I ever had the happiness of being Queen
to you -- I went 20 miles beyond Lake George some of the
most romantic Wild Scenery ever seen by me --
visit the Uncle of Mr. Cadell -- who since together
with his Wife have paid the debt still due by us:
and at this moment while Sarah -- the second Son
of that family is residing at my house -- in his way
to enter into business as a Merchant in Chicago in
a very flourishing Town in the State of Georgia -- he
has all my Moses Mildness -- with his swarmer
in mode -- but not the sortler in the -- he is but
20 Years of age -- has not had them advantages

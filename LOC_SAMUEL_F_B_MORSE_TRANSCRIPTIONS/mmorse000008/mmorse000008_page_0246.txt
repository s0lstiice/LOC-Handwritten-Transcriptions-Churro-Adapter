Salem May 2d 1819
My Dear Sir
When I last wrote to you, I expected to have
sailed from here on Monday last, but the Vespett did not get ready
to go until Wednesday, and then we had an East wind, on Thursd
morning the Wind was rather N. East, and we sailed, but before
we passed the Light House, it veered round completely ahead, and
blowing very fresh, and looking likely to Storm, we thought best
to put back, which we have since found for our good, as it blew a
Gale and Storms all last night, and all day today -- but there
is a prospect of a fair wind now for tomorrow Morning, when I hope
we shall be able to get under way -- Fearing that you might
feel concerned about your Picture, myself on account of delay,
I thought best to send you a line informing you of its safety --
I hope to be in N.York as soon as Monday Evening, and
also sincerly hope to meet you there, and I think you will
consider it important that you should --
My health is good, and hope that you and your Family enjoy this
inestimable Blessing, hoping soon to see you. I am Sirs
Yr Affl Servt
H.b Pratt
P.S. If Mr Morse has gone to N.York
before this I would request Mrs M. to inform him of the content of
his letter

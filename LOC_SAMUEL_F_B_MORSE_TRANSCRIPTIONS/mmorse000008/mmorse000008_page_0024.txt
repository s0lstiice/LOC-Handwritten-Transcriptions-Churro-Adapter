I presume Kentucky has kept you in-
formed of all matters interesting to you relating
to the family & friends I have considered her
as writing for the half of all. I have been en-
gaged, since my return from Washington, in pre-
paring my Report -- which will be much longer than
expected -- it is not yet finished -- I have had
many interruptions by necessary attentions to my
business affairs -- many letters to write -- we were
very more busy -- the last is going forward rapidly -- will
be a very valuable & valuable work.
Some of us will write you as a Sign -- In the
mean time your dear wife, parents, & children will
the family send you much love. Susan thinks you
have kept up in abundance every day & talks much of
dear Papa -- Remember also Henry who I hope
is doing well for himself & for you.
The Revival continues, is increasing
in College -- The Pestilence spreading in 21 Parishes &
of Hartford in this State -- It is powerful in Hartford
opening Unitarianism has shown its genuine spirit in
the settlement of the Winner over the Ab. Chr -- but
I think, overacting & the consequence will be
reaction -- Zion is evidently rising in her strength,
& glory -- the glorious things spoken of her are
coming to pass -- At such a time it is expected
that Chr should do their duty.
Remember us very affectionately to Aunt
Finley's children, & to Mrs. Palmer's young
to 1800 -- to Miss' Lepers all friends.
Your ever affectionate
fellow & brother
Wm. H. More

Salem July 23, 1823
My Dear Mr. More
Your two letters (the first en-
closing 25 dollars) have been received by me
6 during my late absence. I should have
called on you as I passed through N. Haven
but I was obliged to keep the stage & could not.
It was a source of great regret to me that
the favor which you rendered Salem
in directing your beautiful Picture to
be put here should have been a toy upon
your own funds but the fine art
have not so many patrons among us
as some other arts -- I hope you have done
better else where.
I expect to leave to Boston in will depart

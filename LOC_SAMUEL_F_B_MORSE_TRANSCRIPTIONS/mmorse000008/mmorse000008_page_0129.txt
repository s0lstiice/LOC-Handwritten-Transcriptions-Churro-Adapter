Washington Jan. 5th 1822.
My Dearest Luciece
I improve a few moments to drop a line to my dearest bitter half, to inform her of my continued
health and prosperity through the undeserved kindness and grace of our long suffering Father in Heaven; Why cannot
this wayward heart feel more sensibly, and more constantly the gratitude due for this continued uninterrupted stream of blessings; Why
lot is cast in mercy, dearest Luciece, I cannot but wonder at the goodness of God, it is indeed, unfathomable, when
I consider the innumerable sins and follies of my youth, my past ingratitude, and present insensibility, and contrast
them, with the showers of blessings, the blessings of the gospel, the blessings of a pious ancestry, of a pious education, in
the most favored country on earth; the choicest of all earthly blessings my dearest wife, these, and ten thousand hours
mercies; when I make this contrast I am dumb with wonder. What shall I render for all these blessings,

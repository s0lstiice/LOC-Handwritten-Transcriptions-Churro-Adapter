so profusely, that I instantly sent off for a surgeon, after
sending to 3 or 4 Drs. Beers at length came, and Dr. Fittsworth,
who after examining the wounds, found there was no glass in
any of them; they tied the artery and bound the hand up
with lint and bandages. The 3 first nights I scarcely slept
at all, the pain was so great; The latter night, so extreme
that 70 drops of laudanum produced no effect upon me;
about 3 o'clock in the morning after extreme pain, and no sleep,
I called Nancy to take the bandage off and put a fresh poultice
upon it; I found the hand very much swollen so that I
had a thumb but an inch long, I bathed the hand for some
time with laudanum, oil, camphor, & salt-petite, and on the
application of the fresh poultice felt so much relieved from
pain that I slept nearly all the day. The Doctor called in
the evening, and observed that the critical time was
past that every thing looked as favorable as possible the limb
was taken away and I had a good nights rest, free from pain,
I have had every attention and kindness from my dear wife,
and family from our excellent Nancy Shepherd, and from
kind neighbors and friends. My hand will not probably
be materially injured, at least not for the purposes
of my profession; the only use I make of it in painting is to

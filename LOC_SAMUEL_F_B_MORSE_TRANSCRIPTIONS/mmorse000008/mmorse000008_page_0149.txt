My dear Mom
I have understood you are closely engaged at Washington
painting the interior of the Representatives Hall: what certain Characters from each State will be seen: don't forget
on this: Mr. Thos. W. Saunders, for if Mr. L. Sth. become the
Pres. hereafter, so much the more trouble will be your
Painting in all sections of the Union.
our Building is done: built lumber Alabama to 1:
Cent about 4500: instead of the 14 or 1600 W. I. told
us when we first met him in the City Hall alas! I wish
I had never seen that Gentleman.
W. Elliott appointed M. J. B. White, W. Joshua Couler W. Tho.
Middleton myself, appointed but we have done nothing more
than advertise as you have seen in our papers. we (the
Society) have been incorporated, the Lottery has been
sold to Stett. Allan to be drawn certainty in 2 Clays,
the is to give us 7000 Dollars: half of this goes to the
Philosophical Society. The Society is now called the
Soc. of Fine Arts. & the Rules have been committed
with a view to adopt them to their Corporal Name.
We have borrowed 3000 from the U. S. Bank. paying
up all the Accr. of 2% us, -- from subscriptions &c: we
have it: about 1800: have it away before the
year 1845 or 50.

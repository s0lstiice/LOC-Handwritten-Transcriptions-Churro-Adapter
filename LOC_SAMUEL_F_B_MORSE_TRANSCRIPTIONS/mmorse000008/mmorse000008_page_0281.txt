if there were any pictures or Statuary where I could
sketch and draw it would be different, but the only place
of resort is a newspaper reading-room where I can amuse
myself with the news once a day for half an hour; then all
Albany is a blank to me, I have visited several families
who have been very polite to me for which I am thankful;
but most of my time must necessarily be in my
room where I amuse myself by reading & thinking, the only
rational way of passing the tedious time. – There it not
for the confidence I have in a covenant God that he will
make the path of duty plain before me, and that his designs in
this thwarting all my plans are meant for my good, I should
feel at this time somewhat distressed, but I know that all
will result right, either there will be enough for me to do
here, or the way will be made plain to pursue some other plan.
I shall leave Albany and return to N.York a week from
to day if there is no change in my prospects, of this however you
will be informed more definitely before that time. The more I think
of making a push at NewYork as a permanent place of residence in
my profession, the more proper it seems that it should be pretty
soon, there is now no rival that I should fear; a few

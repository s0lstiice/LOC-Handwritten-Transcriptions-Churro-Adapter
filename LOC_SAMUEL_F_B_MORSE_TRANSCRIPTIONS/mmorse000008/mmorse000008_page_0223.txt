Boston April 5th 1823
My Dear Sir
I have seen Mr Allston today, he says that when you
send him that letter I do you mention he will write you in
the mean time he says I must tell you from him not to
be discouraged, he has no doubt but you will do better as
the season advances and as you go South says that he has
spoken to some friends to write but they seem rather
dilatory about it. The they have promised to do so -- that your
Picture is very highly spoken of by all his acquaintances
and what is remarkable that his fault found with it I mentioned to him about the Gilt Frame, he agreed perfectly with me said it would not
answer at all -- would speak your Picture &c. and said that
a Gilt frame is the best for all Pictures.
Knowing that Mr A's opinion would have great weight
with you, I felt anxious to know what he would say about
it, and was glad of an opportunity of seeing him --
I think now that I shall not get the green brize untill to
hear from you again -- Mr A. advised me to stay here
next week -- I received today (Saturday) 8/7 and expect a fine
morning -- your Picture looks better I think in the Evening than in the
day time, it has more of the natural warmth, and rather
more in distinctness than by day light --
Please remember me to all Friends -- and accept the best wishes of
your humble Servt H.B. Pratt

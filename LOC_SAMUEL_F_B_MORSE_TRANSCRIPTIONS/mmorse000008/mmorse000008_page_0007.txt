to Mr. T. Pickin I have found it necessary to introduce
a considerable of the banks -- this I consider no disadvantage
to the Fell for they are still and by far the most
conspicuous object -- the banks of the river are important
and inasmuch as they give a considerable of the
character to the Scene -- by going down the river
as I have done I loose a little of the width but this
is more than compensates for by what is gained in height
this is of much importance for the height is what
visitors are most disappointed in
In my single view of the Horseshoe Fall -- I have
taken a position from which Table rock is seen in
Profile and a part of the Chasm beneath -- by doing
so -- an imposing feature for the foreground
had I stood on Table Rock where Mr Van--bio
this noble feature would have been lost --
I have paid particular attention to the form
on colour of the water -- indeed except in the
centre of the Horseshoe Fall (which is green) there is
little or no colour -- it being all foam --

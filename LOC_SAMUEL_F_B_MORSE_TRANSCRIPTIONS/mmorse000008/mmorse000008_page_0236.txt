Porter Apr 22. 1823
Mr. Mun
Dear Sir
Sends you hundt 68 dolls
for your 4k on "City Bank" -- Could not obtain
large bills, nor indeed any Commercial money
without paying a premium as your 4k would do
command me but Canton bills.
Hoping that the Ireland will answer
your profiteering
I am Drs
Yr fond servant
A. Adams
P.S. After having written the above, I found a letter to my mother
on the thin side, which I could not allie to suit your
mercedes, I thus for true itself

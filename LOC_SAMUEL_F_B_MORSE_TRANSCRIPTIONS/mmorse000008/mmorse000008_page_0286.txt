[Bristol Oct 5th]
My Dear Sir
I arrived here at 11 o'clock yesterday, and am in good health
I found my Uncle here, and stay at his house, but he goes on Monday
to Newport with his family in a visit 13 weeks, and as I prefer
visiting that place, we have concluded it best for me to go down
now with him as he is well acquainted there, and will be of
great advantage to me, and you soon come back here, after which
I do not expect very great things from either place, as they
are both very much deteriorated in business within a few years, there
are however several very rich Families among which, perhaps I may
find something to do. I do not intend to be discouraged until I have
reason to be so, and hope that will not be soon
It seems by advertisement that Strickley (the fine merchant
and painter) is now in Providence Painting Portraits and diverting
for scholars to learn Painting and Drawing
I shall finish this and send it you from Newport
[Newport Oct 9th 1823]
We came down from Bristol on Monday, and I
have (with the assistance of Uncle Mann) found a place to board
and a room to paint in, and have commenced his portrait
I expect to commence Aunt Mrs. Tommow's tomorrow. There are
2 Painters here now before me, but none that I fear
My Aunt Mann, is daughter to Mr Vernon of this place
My Aunt Mann is a wealthiest and most respectable man in town, so
that I shall be brought into notice by them, and if anything
can be done here, I shall probably stand a good chance
I have found something here, that I never heard of, this you probably have, it is
a collection of Pictures in possession of Mr Vernon, he is Uncle
to Aunt Mann, and lives with her father, and is an old bachelor
of the old Masters, and ever purchased in France
at the revolution of the Stuart and the Muster, have seen them sketched

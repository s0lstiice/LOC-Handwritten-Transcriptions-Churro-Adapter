at Havon August 6th 1825. Copy sent to the Secy of State
for filing a caveat in the Patent Office
against the granting of letters Patent to any one but myself
for a new and useful invention, which I claim as the
Original Inventor, the object of the invention is to cut or carve, in
marble or in any other substance by machinery the exact resemblance of any model of sculpture, whether statue, bust, or
any ornamented work; About 4 or 5 years since I
first conceived the feasibility of constructing a machine
for this purpose, which has been a great desideratum
in Sculpture; I gradually, as I have been, perfected
the plan, and last winter constructed a small working
model, which was completely successful; I have granted
leave to a mechanic in this city to construct a large
one which is in progress, and the success of its operation
will be determined in two or 3 months, after which
time I will make out a regular specification and apply
for letters Patent in legal form.
I find no legal form of caveat and assume that letter will be sufficient
if not I should best attend to be informed of the true way with the consideration
of the Secy of State.

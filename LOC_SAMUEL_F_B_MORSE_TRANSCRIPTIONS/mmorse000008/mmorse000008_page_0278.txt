With respect to young Longworth, I should have no objections
to take him as a pupil provided I go to New York; on what
terms I am hardly prepared now to say, but I will resolve the
subject in my mind, and see if I cannot mature a proper plan
so that I can take a number to advantage.
I am glad to hear good accounts of my dear daughter Susan
if she has been a good girl and has not grieved Mama & Grandpapa
and Grandmama by getting up "Humpys" give her papa's Kisses for he
loves good girls; if she is very good all the time Papa will bring
her something from Albany.
I am obliged to close this, as I have but
a few moments to run to the Office before the mail closes.
Good bye, I ought to tell you however I met Colonel
Wm. Allison the old gentleman from Chatham N.Y. he will
pass through N.Havens, I wish I was there to show
him some attention.
Good bye again
Your affectionate husband
Samuel B. Pynchon
Love to all at home, and neighbors.
The family where I board know Mrs Pynchon I believe,
they speak of her as being very beautiful and a belle in Schenectady
when she came from.
My health was never better.

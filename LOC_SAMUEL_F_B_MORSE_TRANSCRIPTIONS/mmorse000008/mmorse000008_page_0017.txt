Monday 19th Febry
I have drawn a plan of a house, as Papa writes
in one of his letters, which I think will exactly suit, I
think it will be very convenient, and not expensive, it is
entirely new in the construction and will enable us to
have all the improvements as to warming the rooms &c with
very little additional expence. — I shall bring it home
with me.
Write often, dear, now, for I feel more anxious every day

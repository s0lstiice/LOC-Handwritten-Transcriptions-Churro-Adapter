Boston Feb 10th 1823.
Tuesday.
My Picture arrived safely on Saturday
evening and yesterday I was engaged in unpacking and
placing it in the Room; it has not sustained the slightest
injury. -- Mr Allison has seen it and is very much
pleased with it he says it is a magnificent picture;
he has suggested some small improvements which I
can do in 2 days, and today & tomorrow I shall be
busily engaged upon the picture; curiosity is on
tiptoe to see it, but the delay of 2 days will be an
disadvantage especially as the improvement will
be of so much permanent advantage. --
I long to get home again, I have still a
slight cold on my lungs, the weather is such I only
venture abroad of necessity; when I get once through
the establishing of the picture, I shall return and
keep quiet for a little time to recruit. --
Your letter in Jocelyn's bundle is received
enclosing pattern it shall be attended to.
97648
Kiss little Sue a hundred times and affection your husband
give my love to all at home, friends & neighbors.
Finley.

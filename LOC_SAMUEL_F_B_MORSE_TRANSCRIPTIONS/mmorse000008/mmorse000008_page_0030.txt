* here I ought to mention that just as I was
leaving Charleston for the North Mr. B. called
and said she would give me a check for
600 dollars for the picture, that if I pleased, and
wished me to order the frame which was not
to exceed two hundred dollars; I declined
taking the whole, but said I should be
very glad of one half the amount 300,
which she promised to send me that very
day; she forgot it however and went out of
town without paying me, and did not return till I had left.

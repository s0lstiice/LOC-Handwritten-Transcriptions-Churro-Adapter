My dear Wife I have received your interest'd letter
of the 18th at the same time letters from the
Colt & Parish Counties inform'd me of their doing so
I have just finished a letter to them in answer which I have requested them to send
you I consent to have the installation take place without my presence in settlement of my
ans. to an arrangement about the occupation of my house to be previously made I have
added to the Council Dr Osgood & Messrs Grensuff & Homer they are the oldest, & near, & friends
to all parties I shall prepare a document to submit to the Council you & E. will advise
me as to what shall be its contents according to existing feelings & circumstances at home
I could wish certain parts of the Colt & Parish Records laid before the Council to enable them
to judge of proceedings -- it may be best to omit this I make this business as simple as possible
Jan 27 my health is a little improving I am still feeble the numbness in my feet & hands
continues the weather is cold & some days damp & do business with considerable effort -- to
comfort me in these circumstances I am very comfortable lodged at Dr. Lawries in a warm room good bed
a fine bed before Brieye, at going to bed & to do any writing a few neat kind affectionate from family I could not

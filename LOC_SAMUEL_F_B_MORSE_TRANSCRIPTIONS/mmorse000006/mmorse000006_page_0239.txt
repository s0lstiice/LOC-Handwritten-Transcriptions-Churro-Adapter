evening with the Vice President. It seems of the

-- till next mail -- You may, or may
not, let him know this, as you shall judge
best. I am too tired to sit up late --
I have heard a part of Mr Pinkney's
great speech on the slavery question. I have
heard speeches, not a few, which I have thought
abler more logical, & more eloquent -- I
was less pleased than some others. He is to continue
it on Monday -- I find here many
Gentlemen, whom I know, & am spending my
time pleasantly & hope usefully in very comfortable
lodging -- You shall soon hear from me as a
lover to all as usual -- Your aff ct husband

Charleston S.Ca 16 Octr 1859
My Dear Finley
We all rejoice with you at the
birth of your daughter and at the goodness
of God in carrying her through that try
ing event & in making her the living mo-
ther of a living child. But we lament
that we are not to have the pleasure of
seeing Tuerelia; For particular Mary had
been pleased with the thought that no one
but herself should put the little girl to
bed, to dress and undress her. How cruel
you have been to us just to make us ac-
quainted with your amiable wife and then to
take her away. But it ought to teach
us the instability of all earthly happi-
ness. I am sure I have seen enough in this
world to teach me the vanity of all earthly
comforts & to lead me to place all my
affections upon things above

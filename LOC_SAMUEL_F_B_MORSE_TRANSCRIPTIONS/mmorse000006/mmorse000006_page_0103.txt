"The Bishop's Pastor to provide a Candidate for
settlement in the ministry among them, as soon as may
be convenient; so much of my salary redeemed,
as shall pay the expense incurred for supplying
the pulpit."
Explanation.
So much of the salary of the Pastor, is to be
reserved as shall pay Candidates for settlement in
ministry in this place & Society who may be
employed by the Committee appointed for the purpose;
which Candidates will supply the pulpit by my
consent during the period for which they may be
respectively engaged. When Candidates are not
employed, the pulpit will, of course, be supplied,
as usual by the Pastor. When the Committee wish the
Pulpit for a Candidate -- it is expected they will
make it known to the Pastor, who will cheerfully
comply with their wishes, agreeably to his proposal -- The person whom they may employ -- need
not be explicitly informed that he is a Candidate, --
if in fact he is so heard of that it is understood
between the Bishop & Committee The person employed
it is presumed will be one who is in a situation to speak
as a Candidate, & who will be at liberty to settle, & the
people by united vote appoint him, I give him a call
to the Vestry & who are not allowed to speak as Candidates, the Pastor
will readily indulge them by asking them to speak for
him. This will give the desired opportunity to the Congregation
to judge of their talents & qualifications for settlement.

My dear Sir
I must beg of you to furnish me with
some answer to the inquiries about our engine with which
I am frequently assailed — I have assured the subscribers
generally that we should have an engine completely
according to the description given, or else their money should
be returned, but they appear a little tired of their promises, and
to tell the truth I am not less so of pursuing them —
Your hands I had are doubtless pretty constantly
employed, it is with reluctance therefore that I call your
attention to this unpleasant subject, but longer silence
would as ill comport with my friendship for you as with
fair honorable dealing towards the subscribers, to whom I cannot
bet feel myself in a measure responsible —
Mr Smiths with me in affectionate regards to yourself
Havertie, and in cordial congratulations on the happy event
of your becoming parents. Hearing nothing to the contrary we trust
Lucetta is doing well by the help of kind Providence will soon
be perfectly restored to usual health. — Being interrupted
remain in haste of my love
L.D. Parkhurst

Dr. Son
Charleston Aug 26 1819
Repenties was sent up last
Friday by the afternoon 30C: state hope
you have received it if not inquire
of the driver It was left at the toll
house, & the tollman says he gave
it to the driver.
We received your letter by Mr.
I am not very well had another turn
of the cholera on Sabbath morning was
unable to preach the house was shut in
forenoon I am still so feeble as that
I shall not feel able to preach next
Sabbath. I have written Dr. Murdoch
to come -- I have told him you will get a
chair & bring him -- Wish you could
come on Friday early after dinner
& be here to attend our Club meet s
at 50C. It will be a very important meet s
one that it will be perfectly proper
for you to attend -- If Dr. M. can come on
Friday, by all means come then --

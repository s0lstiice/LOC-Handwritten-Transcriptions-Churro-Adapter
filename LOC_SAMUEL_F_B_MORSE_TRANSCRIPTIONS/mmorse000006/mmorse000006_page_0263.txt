my Dear Husband
I have received two letters from you from Washington one
the 21st and the other the 22d of this month dated I was happy to find your
health was improving and your prospects brightening with respect to
the poor Indians hope you will succeed to the Utmost of your wishes
if it be the will of our heavenly Father I sent Dr Wood that you would
write him by the next mail he sent one word he had received a letter
from you in answer to his first to you and that he would bring it
up and show it me which he did in the afternoon he was very gracious
and said I disposed to have every thing done that was agreeable
to you, I was told by Mr Rettel who with his Wife spent the evening
with us in the Church meeting he proposed and advocated the
settling Mr Fay as Colleague because it was your wish and Mr
Fay whom he had consulted on the subject he requested me to
hand him the receipt of Mr L Dwight for the ten Dollars and
paid me the money and hoped that all things would be kind
if I forgot to say the Church did not consent to the proposal he said it
was necessary that Mr Fay's salary should commence as soon as possible
here as he would not probably have any more from Harvard
I wish you to show a spirit about all their little mean tricks and artifices
but as Edwards says act with great caution and dont commit
yourself to them in any thing, you have found they were not to be
trusted. I have paid S Ethernage on his smallest note $100. I have a

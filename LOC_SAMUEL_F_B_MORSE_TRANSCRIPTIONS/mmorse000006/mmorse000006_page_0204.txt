I am anxious to hear from the family
how you enjoy yourself where Edid is
whether Mr. Hawley's pamphlet
is received relating to Schools in this State
whether you have heard of the arrival
of Finley & received the bills from the
Sergeant, by P. Arthur & especially
how affairs are in our Congregation.
On all these subjects you will write
me to Washington where I hope
to be by the 20th inst.
Monday Even 606
The Senate have rejected my petition
but under circumstances which will not affect
my object, The Govt is warmly favorable
I can & will help my friends more than the
legislature could do I shall depart for
N.York at 4 o'clock tomorrow morning. It has been
snowing last night & to day I made it fine sleigh
ing hope to be in N.Y. Thursday night, & at
Phil.a on the Sabbath.
I have left a small paper parcel with
Mr. Thurston to carry to Edid I haven't succeeded
in changing the bills I may send you the 50
from N.Y. whence I shall write you after
receiving letters from you.
I think much my dear of you & the family
in your present situation you will make you
elves as comfortable as you can Let Edid
be with you as far as is just to your comfort &
to the keeping up of your spirits.

I always close without enquiring after the Charleston
herd of nobility; what are they doing now? have they turned
you into the streets yet? I see by the papers that Mr. Mead
is dead. I hope he has gone to a better place, I believe he
was a good man, though misled in his business. — I should
like to know a little of the state of affairs at home, or
your account and the family's; I hope by the blessing of
Providence we shall all be safely settled in N.Haven
next spring. — I long for the time, for I do miss my dear
wife and child and Parents & brothers very, very
much.
Again good bye, remembering me
who enquire after me.

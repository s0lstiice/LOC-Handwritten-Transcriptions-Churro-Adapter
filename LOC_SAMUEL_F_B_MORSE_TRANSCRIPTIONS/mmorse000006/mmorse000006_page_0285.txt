Washington Feb. 4th 1820
My dear son I am still here & my health feels
I expect to remain here nearly a week longer
my business is in good train but requires to
be constantly watched as conflicting interests
& influences begin to show them selves
The Govt has agreed on my Agency to
the kind of instructions with I shall receive
I wait only to have them in due form
I have the promise (if no influence shall
alter it) of all I wish
I have your painting of the President
I shall carry it with me to Mr. W. have seen the
copy which I think an excellent likeness The
family are much pleased. The daughter wishes
you to alter the color of the coat to a blue I
think the jacket to a buff color with I told her
you would readily do them in her view
it will be all she wishes Dr. Leuwall is very
attentive to me & no man could be more so
Mr. King is sick of inflammatory rheumatism
but is growing better The young painter
to whom you gave some lemons has called
on me & is very grateful I have called at
his room at Kings

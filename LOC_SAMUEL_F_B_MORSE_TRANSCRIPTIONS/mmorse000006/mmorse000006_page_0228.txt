But I fear you wont have patience to read thro'
this long letter. Give them love to Henry - Remember
me respectfully to Aunt Kinley & family
Your ever affectionate brother
Richard L. Moore
P.S. You are my nearest adviser. Will you please to read this
letter. If I have said too much or what is not proper send it
back + I will write another if you think not. I will thank
Henry to put a waxer in it & take it to the Post Office

My dear Children
Thinking it probable that Rich has
left this point to Johns Island I address this
letter to you both at Charleston. This morning we received Rich's letter of Jan 30th Finley
of the 1st inst. each containing drafts of $400
each The best the contents of your letters
were very acceptable to us. I am in a distressful
position, and will state nothing by these lines to aid in determining my business. From Richards
last letter, we had felt concern for his
situation in his school. I am glad he is
strong with him. (he has visited me)
Elliot's We hope he is
now at Johns island. I think it will be
well for him to remain there, for you
to return together unless Richard also returns
by land, as he proposes to do. We shall
have no objections to his returning to
this case, by stage & stop at the large houses
one stage when he may wish. Your mother
is in doubt whether she can let Nancy stay
till June
It is Saturday communion to morrow

The Sec'y of War
Washington Feb. 4th 1820
Dear Sir: Permit me in behalf of the Northern
Militia Society of New York from whom I have a
Commission under which I now call
to solicit your attention to them in the distribution of the grant of Congress at your other
Presidents disposal for the benefit of your
Indian Tribes. Their respectable society of many years standing, is managed by active,
intelligent, good men, among the best in our country in the prime of life. Their Society has
done much for the Indians in the Western parts of N.Y. & are desirous to do more. They
are ready to unite with the Society in Scotland in an establishment in a colony
proposed to formed of the remnants of Tribes in N.Y. & N.Y. Engr. & to assist in forming such
a colony. If they could receive, say $5000, out of the Grant above mentioned to assist in
encourage them in the attempt it would in my opinion, have a most happy effect, & secure
the making of an establishment of great importance for the benefit of the Indian
tribes. Having thus superfluously presented their idea on the subject, I submit it with entire confidence to the decision of the President of the United States
with his full consideration and choice of means

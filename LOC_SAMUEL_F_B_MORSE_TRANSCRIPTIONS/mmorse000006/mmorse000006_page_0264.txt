I wish you by no means to do anything to encourage Aunt H's family leaving Philadelphia to settle in New Haven on account of our going there it will incur too great a responsibility to be forward in promoting such a removal off er and Mrs. Halston could give us the best advice on this subject as they know better than we can what are their means I should have to live with Aunt very much but would not for any thing persuade to this step -- We must make our arrangements to go to House keeping ultimately as I am persuaded we shall not be satisfied with Boarding out long I think that you ought to look out for a House for Finley that he may go to keeping house after a few months being at Board -- shall you be able to bring home any cash from the sale of your Book? I wish you will as I wish to have a thorough settlement with all in this region at least before we quit the Ground; hope you will be particular in providing a good place in New Haven to store our goods and see about a vessel to take them from here. -- I hope Nancy P & Bettye may be able to pay their board at least in part by their attentions to us all as whenever they may be they would have to give extra help to attend to us and would have to pay them and Board them too but we shall pay them their wages and only require their Board for their assistance to the family with whom we are, they will take care of all the rooms that we occupy and help wash & iron & so on when not engaged in the latter for us or themselves there but their particular to prevent misunderstanding as

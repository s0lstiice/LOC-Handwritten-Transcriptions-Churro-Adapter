I have not yet left for St. Haven
to go up my journey to St. Haven
will next month - shall not want my
Gun spit Inspected - please say this to
Resp. to Fisk Parsons
Richd. has been with us 10 days cannot
come home badly poisoned, but is better, I will
return to U. B. Station - this week - probably
We are all in good spirits - I wish to
see you - I think you had better by all means
come down & see us - your niece & sister
& rejoice with us - bring home one to help
me - Dr. M or Dr. Porter or Mr. Stewart - I
prefer one of the Professors at this time.
I write in haste - All send love
Your affectionate
[Father] Morse

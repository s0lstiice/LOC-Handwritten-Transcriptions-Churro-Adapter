My dear son,
We have this morning received a very
affectionate & comforting letter from Finley, with
a remittance of $500- & more will be sent He
is in full business & has enough engaged for several
years constantly increasing
Affairs in the parish are taking a very
favorable turn The designs of the adversaries
have been unveiled, exposed, defeated their influence is down The Lord has

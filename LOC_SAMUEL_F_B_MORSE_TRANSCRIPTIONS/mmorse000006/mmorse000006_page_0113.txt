And he has indeed acted the part of a
son to me and a brother to the children
he has requested me to look upon my
purse as my own
I will hereby thank you my Dear
Finley for your kind offer and
if ever I stand in need of it I shall
certainly accept of it. But I must
insist upon you bringing Lucretia
with you, we are going to move in
a new house of my Brother's near
South Bay. I have a room that I
can spare to Lucretia and
yourself, and as I am going to
thin out some of my servants
I can let you have a nurse
all the Children join in this request
even Rush who although he has not
yet seen you, says that he is sure to
fall in love with both of you
The Girls have given such favourable accounts
Do let me hear from you
as soon as possible after Lucretia's
confinement and say that you will bring
her with you - Rush is with us and is preparing
to study surveying and Engineering with Maj Wilson
who has advised him to study drawing so that he
will stand in need of your assistance
Peronneau will continue at Princeton
We all unite in sincere love to your father
mother, Lucretia & your self
I Remain
your affecte Aunt
Mary Finley

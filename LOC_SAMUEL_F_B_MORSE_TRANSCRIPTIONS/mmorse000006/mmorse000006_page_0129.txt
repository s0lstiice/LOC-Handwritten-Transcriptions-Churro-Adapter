I hope, my friend, when you return
to S.C. again, you will take Plymou in
your route If you take the Steam Boat
line, I you probably will, this will be
the most expeditions rout as, after you
leave Baltimore, you take the Steam Boat
for Norfolk, from thence to Edenton
in the Stage. from Edenton to this
place, 22 miles, on the Steam Boat—
from here to Raleigh, 120 miles, is
a good line of stages; from thence to
Charleston, you are well acquainted—
I assure you, my dear friend, I should be
wonderingly happy to receive a visit from
you & your Lucetta. I hope something
may offer, to induce your remaining here
several days.

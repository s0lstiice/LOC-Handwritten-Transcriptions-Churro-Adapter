Henry is well and doing well, he is of great service
to me, and is fast improving, he is industrious, and
steady, and I hope will turn out well in the end, I
think he discovers good talents.
I feel rejoiced that things have come to such a crisis
in Charlestown, that our family will be released from
that region of trouble so soon; keep up your spirits
Mother, the Lord will show you good days according to
those in which you have seen evil; I think of New-
Haven with very pleasant anticipations, but I hope
in such a manner as I brought, not setting my affec-
tions on any of the enjoyments of this world to such a
degree that when they are taken from me, I shall re-
ceive and not be in a frame to say the Lord gave God the Lord
hath taken away blessed be the name of the Lord. I am
glad Lucretia and the dear little Susan intend meeting me

Albany, Jan 10th 1820
My dear Wife,
I had intended to have left
this City for N York this morning but am delayed
by the delay of the legislature in acting on my
Memorial. Presented on Saturday, I committed
the Com to report this morning favorably but
some objection to the acceptance of the Report
is made by two members of the Report, of course
must lie over till tomorrow, when it is believed
it will pass with much opposition. It is P also that it will pass the House
& then my object in coming here will be accomplished.
I have seen nothing of Williams
of Utica nor heard from him -- have written
him very plainly today -- I have rec'd 8 50
from Webster & Skinner, on acct of salary
which I shall send you by Mr Thurston
if I can get it changed into proper bills
to send you -- You may give it to my
Elberdsee if you don't want it for family
uses, I see that it is endorsed on my note
in his hands -- I think it probable I shall
be obliged to stay here till Wednesday as
I shall leave my letter open & close it
tomorrow for the 5 O' mail -- I am impatient to get away -- My health is
rather feeble, the cold affects me but
I shall soon be in a warmer climate.

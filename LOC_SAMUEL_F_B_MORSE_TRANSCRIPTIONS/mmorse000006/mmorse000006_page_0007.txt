Reason - Charleston friends a happy new year
No letters from you have been received since your first - on your arrival We wonder
What can be the reason - We expect every mail a letter - None from Rich. either
I just deaf this boaty gone by Sch. Mark, who I see advertises to sail to-morrow - to day we are all
well - nothing new since I last wrote by mail, & by water, a few days ago - Money was received so
scarcely as now - hope I have a bill on the way - It w.d come timely -
I have only time to say - We all send much love to you & Lucretia, I hope you are happy &
prosperous - I even left to
Father Moore

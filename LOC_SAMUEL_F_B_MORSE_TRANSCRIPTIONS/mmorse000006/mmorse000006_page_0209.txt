Edwards has received one of the pamphlets you mention in your letter from
Albany it is the one you sent he got that by going to the post office himself
We have received Mr Sargent's journal and his bill on Scotland a letter
from him the postage amounting to 8/2-44 I have put them all in your
desk he says that the Quaker Woman that you mentioned has changed
her mind since you was there and is acting against your plan with
respect to the Indians His words are "The Quaker woman of late, I understand
is discouraging the Indians about the White river country if you should go to Albany
Edwardson is much en courage'd about the Geography & will do his best I hope
he will find it as well to stay with me as to go to Andover.- Mr Brooks
who preached at the Old South preached the Lecture - and Mr Jenkins
preached last Sabbath very well I was told he called in to see
me after meeting he says the business you are gone on has lain
near his heart this ten years and that he had written to the
Pendential Committee sometime ago and that at St Louis ought to
have been the place to have had a Colledge & Mission house &c &c and
were it not for his family counting 14 Children under 17 years old
he would engage in it with all his heart he thinks your situation
very favourable to such an enterprise and hopes you will succeed
We are all well and send love hope to hear from you soon remain
as ever. Your sincerely affectionate wife E D Morse

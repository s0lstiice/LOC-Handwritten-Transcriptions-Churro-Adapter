New-York. Nov 28th 1819
Sunday.
My Dear Mother
I arrived safely last evening in this city,
much fatigued and with a severe cold upon me,
but this morning feel entirely well and in good
sprits. I found Papa at Mr. Coils looking I
feeling remarkably well, he will probably leave
N.York for N.Haven on Tuesday. I was disappoint
ed in not meeting him at N.Haven but think upon
the whole that the business of the house can be ar
ranged by us here as well as at N.Haven, I went
up into town at N.Haven while waiting for the other
boat, and in walking about, I asked what houses
were for sale. I found among others that Mr.
Woolley's house was to be disposed of and Papa
thinks that is just the thing. it would be a
fine situation for us and would make a double
house. I shall leave N.York for Washington on Thursday
in company with Mr. Caldwell of Washington. I miss
Lucetia if little Susan more than you can think, and

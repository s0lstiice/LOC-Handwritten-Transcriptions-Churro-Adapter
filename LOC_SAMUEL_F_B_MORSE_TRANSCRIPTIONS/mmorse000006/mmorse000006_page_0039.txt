City Samuel.
March 1st 1861.
President unanimously that the Kansas State Intendant be requested to admit Kansas
to the United States to permit a full length liberty to be taken for the duty of Christian and that
Missouri be requested to take all necessary measures for executing the order liberty from the arrest of the President
of the United States.
Received unanimously that the men of Kansas have united and that fifty delegates have appointed for this
Continent from the Missouri.
William Couch Jr.
Blacks of Kansas.

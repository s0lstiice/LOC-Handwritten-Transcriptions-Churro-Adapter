Your Mother took cold in returning from Andover
& I find her knowledge of your complaint & regrets
her spirits— she is not well nor is Betty, who
has a heavy cold— I am favored with health
& spirits, not with standing the pressure of
particular trials.— We all need the prayers of
the saints.— A letter from Nancy this morning informs
me that she is well & sends her love to you. No letters from either
of your brothers. We want to see you. Your Mother says it
will be a great comfort to her to have under her nursing care for
a short time, at least, but we wish you to judge what is best,
as you know your arrangements.

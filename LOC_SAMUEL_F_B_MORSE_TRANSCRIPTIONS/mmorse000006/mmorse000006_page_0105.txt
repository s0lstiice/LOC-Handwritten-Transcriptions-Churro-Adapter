Monday. Charlestown Aug. 9th 1819.—
Dear Edwards,
It is absolutely necessary, and important
that you should come down on Wednesday
next and stay till Friday morning. we want your
advice and presence, the crisis is probably come
in this ohh parish, we have no doubt of a favorable issue, you cannot be engaged in anything
at Andover of so much importance, as to prevent
your coming.— You will acknowledge it yourself
when you come, we are in good spirits, trusting in God, and
fearless for the result.— don't deny us on
any account whatever, the style will show
that this is from your Brother
Finley.

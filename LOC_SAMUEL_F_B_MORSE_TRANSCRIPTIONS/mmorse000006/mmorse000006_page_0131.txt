New Haven, 20th Sept. 1819.—
Dear Sir,
I now venture a request to
you, relying on what I have experienced of your
judiciousness in the South the coming winter, to furnish me with
Paintings. Ignorant in some degree of the incidents there held out, I am anxious for your
opinion respecting the eligibility of situation, &
probable degree of patronage that would attend
the attempt. My object now is to obtain
education in England — my wish is to pursue
the course best calculated to accomplish that
end.— I am conscious the degree of patronage
will in a measure depend upon circumstances
of Place & Taste & ability of the inhabitants,
& you having knowledge of different sections

My dear Wife
Russell Jan 7th 1820
18 miles beyond Springfield
65 from Albany, 7 o'clock
Thanks to God
Another days journey of 20 miles
has been performed & to our comfortable lodge
with kind & attentive landlord lady we are
to start for Albany 5 o'clock in the morning & there
to be there by 6 o'clock tomorrow evening & hence
you will hear from me at 2 o'clock. I have had to
the necessity to converse to day, I have, therefore,
enjoyed my thoughts -- much -- have I thought
of home & of affairs which have so much disturbed
me -- These I intend on to leave with
him, who cares for me -- in whose hands all
that we commit to him, is safe -- much more
pleasantly have I thought of the business which
before this, too, I can comfortably leave to him to
pass over or not to prosper it -- to contrast it to what
if our he shall -- I will constantly to feel that he
am but his humble instruments to be & to do just
what he will have me to do --
I forgot to give you a check for $50 -- in case according
to circumstances, I shall add it to this letter.
We were talking with Mr. Payson about Expressions
& Certions to cast the interest. You may say to him
that I am willing Mr. Adams, Mr. Furniss & Mr.
Tyler St. better men --

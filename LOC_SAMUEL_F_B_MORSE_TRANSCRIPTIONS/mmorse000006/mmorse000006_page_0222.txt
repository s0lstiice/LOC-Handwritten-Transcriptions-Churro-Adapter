Phil. Jan 17th 1820
My dear wife
I arrived here 10 o’clock Saturday evening
came over to Stewart on Friday evening spent
an hour pleasantly with Dr Rockwood next morning
I took the Stage with a pleasant Company
and came on very comfortably, arrived as above
preached yesterday The 7th of Janeway found all in
usual health at Mr R’s have a cordial welcome
as usual saw Mrs Rockwood all the
children yesterday all very well & happy Mrs
H. loves some distance from Mr R’s I have not been
to the house she is very well Mr Rakew informs
that her Gr. Dan. is near his end that he has experienced
a remarkable entire change in his religious
opinions feels himself to be the greatest of sinners
& embraces the orthodox faith in his intelligence
gives me great pleasure I saw Mr James Dwight
from Richmond Va where he saw finely well 24th
night since on his way by land to Charleston I was
glad to receive this intelligence as I had no news
in what way he was returning it is better to be on
the land than on the water at this season you have
bombastic news from him – A violent storm of snow
rain last night this morning confines me in this
hospitable mansion where all seem desirous to
make me comfortable & happy – The storm
now 12 o’clock seems to be over tomorrow I propose

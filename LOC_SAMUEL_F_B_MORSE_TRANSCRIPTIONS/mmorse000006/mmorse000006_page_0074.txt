We are in a strange and calltable state of affairs. It will be the result cannot be foreseen. The detail when
you come home: The influence, which has brought about all this is obviously Militarian. It is the business of a very few say half a dozen who if out of the way there wont hardly be found any where a more united Congregation -- so true is it that "Areinnen destroyt
much good." -- This evening we have a special meeting for prayer -- the Cal appears to be in distress -- it has
become a praying time -- A number are enjoying -- 3 joined us last communion, 2 men -- 1 female all heads of families -- We are expecting
a revival.
I have had my hands so full with preparing my geography, taking care of my flour
preparing the insidious attacks of Desavies for the last 3 months, that I have written no letters but indispensable ones -- I say this to Uncle Finley
Mr Keith & Dr Palmer as my apology for not writing them -- I present our affectionate regards to them & all other friends interested for me -- I know not but that
we can decide nothing at present.
Expecting soon to see you -- and no more -- I have written the above for the information of Dr Finley
& Palmer as well as for your benefit. We long to hear from both -- May God give you a safe passage home
we are making all things ready to receive you
All the family write in affectionate love to you both, with yester over loving father
With all my money you receive (except dollars with which I pay my mail -- which I have better send by
draft -- but one by mail -- bring the U. S. currency yourself
Moore

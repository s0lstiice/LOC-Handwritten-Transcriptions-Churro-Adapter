this & the middle of May -- May the Lord strengthen
us to fulfil it -- The Com m unity having given me
notice that they shall expect that we leave
the house the first of April -- I have written
them on the subject -- I think they are in
no great haste -- have little regard to our
feelings or accommodation -- All, however,
is of a piece -- They must have the credit
(so far as it is creditable) of acting consistently
with their conduct, in its kind, has been uniform for
the year past. I cannot help feeling very strongly
the ungrateful & unkind treatment I have received
from a part of the people & from some of my brethren.
But the Lord hath permitted it to take place
for our good & I pray for our good & as coming from
them, I desire to submit to it all without a murmur.
He is kindly telling over us all this at the moment
we need it -- my anchor here in the great object which
I have in view, & the pleasant prospects we have
before us of a quiet settlement in St. Haven -- Our
premian concerns are growing better & better
every day & I have no doubt of a full relief from
all solicitude on that head in many months. We
have many tokens that the Lord though severely

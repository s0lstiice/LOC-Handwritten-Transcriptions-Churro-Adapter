My dear Son
Your letter of 26th ult. in reply to that
given us much satisfaction as it informs us
of the recovery of our dear daughter from her illness.
Had we known of it at the time she suffered
I had prayed for her, particularly in reference to
her situation though we never forget you both in our
daily prayers I have comfort to believe that you
forget nothing in your prayers. Your plan of returning
via Baltimore we on the whole agree with
have written Nancy to wait for you. If any alteration
in your arrangements write her and
Your feelings in respect to the affairs of
this City, & the conduct of some of its members, are
natural I do not wonder that they are though the
whole spirit of their proceedings, were they fully
known, would not make you full up. The details must
be left till I see you. I can only say that their
design, with they were even cunning, almost as
if accomplishing is complete, is most wonderfully
frustrated. I am sure as they are constrained
to describe it to special Divine intervention. The
measures adopted for its execution to defeat
this purpose (which way my removal at all events
have been in every instance, such as they cannot
conceive, a yet in many instances completely successful

Suppose you are now left alone at home
as father is probably on his way to Washington, and Ed-
wards at Andover; I therefore sit down a few moments to
entertain you with the wanderings of your elder son, and to
congratulate you with the intelligence that he is thus far on
his journey (by the blessing of God) safe and well; I believe
I wrote you from Fredericksburg, Virginia, informing
you that I left Washington on the 29th day of December
in company with Mr. Silliman of N.York, brother to Professor
Silliman of N.Haven, an excellent, pious, intelligent
man, with whom I have travelled till now, we part at
this place, I shall set out to-morrow (Sunday) for Chas-
tor, and he next week for Augusta Geo. I have found
his society a great comfort and security throughout this
long, tedious, uncomfortable journey; and we now part
with mutual regret; I arrived here this morning at 3 o'clock
from Raleigh (N.C.) having rode all night, the place from which
I write is the best hotel in the city, but it is a miserable, dirty,
uncomfortable place; I find that the stage goes to-morrow for
Charleston, and then not till Tuesday again, so that I have
the choice of travelling on the Sabbath or staying in this
dirty hole 3 days doing nothing but spend in time & money.

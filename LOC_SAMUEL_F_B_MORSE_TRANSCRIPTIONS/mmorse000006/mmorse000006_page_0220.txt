Dear Father
The Geography is going on finely. The prospect of its being so
extensively introduced into the Schools of New York, requires that it
should be executed with the greatest care, and I have determined to
devote my time to it & spend all my labor upon it. I have thrown by
everything else. I shall not go to Andrews, I think till after your
removal to New Haven. I can do but one thing at a time, as
I have often said, and as experience continually proves to my own
entire conviction.
Your affectionate Son
Sidney E. Morse.
Mama says Rich is a queer creature for not wishing his
directed H. C. Morse Candidate for the Ministry, I do not think so.
I have the same feelings. I should wish to have as little fame with
my name as possible.

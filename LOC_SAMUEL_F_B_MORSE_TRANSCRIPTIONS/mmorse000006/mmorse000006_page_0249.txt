yesterday I dined with the President his only
guest for the purpose of conversation on any
business we had two or three hours together.
His views & feelings continue to be altogether
in unison with my own & some steps will
soon be taken. It will not do to hurry things
are in a good train nothing has yet occurred to dis-
courage. Yesterday I dined with Mr
Dana, Senator from Conn with a large No of
other Gentlemen among them Mr Norton, Prof.
from Cambridge. Last evening attended the President's levee, a splendid scene. The President introduced me to his Lady, richly dressed beautiful,

Now as to the Parish and Church business, I hope all things
will turn out right yet, and I can't help wishing that nothing may
occur to keep you any longer in that nest of vipers and conspirators;
I think with Edio's decidedly that on Mama's account
you should leave a place which is full of the most unpleasant
associations to all the family and retire to some place of quiet
to enjoy your old age; why not come to Charleston? here is
a fine field for usefulness, a pleasant climate especially for
persons advanced in life, and your children, here I
seriously think of settling in Charleston, Lucretia is willing
and I think it will be much more for my advantage to remain
through the year? Ricl. can find a place here if he
will, and Edio's can come on, and be Bishop or Presb, or Professor
in some of the Colleges, (for I can't think of him in a less character)

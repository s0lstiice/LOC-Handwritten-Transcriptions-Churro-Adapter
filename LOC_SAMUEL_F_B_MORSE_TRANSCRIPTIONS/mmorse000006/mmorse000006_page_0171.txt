My dear Sir Charleston Decr 17th 1819
ten days ago I arrived home safe &
well, from my very prosperous journey found
all well, excepted, at home I have full
of business to overflowing, since I came home
or sh all have written earlier -- We received your
from Baltimore & are expecting a longer
one by every mail from Washington We
wish to know how you are doing & what stay you
will probably make in that city of hope to be there
by the 10th. and if practicable if you will be likely
to stay till then & we must to be there in season
to meet you if you can find profitable business to do. I think you had better stay till I
arrive write me immediately on receiving
this, as there will be no more than time for
you to go to you & for you to answer, before
I shall be off for Albany I shall send
you with the advice of Mr Field well suited
for your lodgings in a quiet house of promise
paid for me Affairs in the East are progressing
as well as could be expected in casting
circumstances it is probable since I am certain
that Mr Tay will be my successor -- The council
which is to dismiss him have not met -- nor as

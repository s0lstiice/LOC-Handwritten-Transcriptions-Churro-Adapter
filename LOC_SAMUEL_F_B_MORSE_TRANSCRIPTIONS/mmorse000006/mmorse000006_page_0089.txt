He thinks it would effectually destroy all the
animosities in the happiest manner possible between
him and this people.
On every account, therefore, with as regards the property, & good
of the society, & your usefulness, & his own reputation, he
believes that the plan is desirable
With respect to the practicability of the plan, my
father believes that if you are willing it might easily be effected
through the instrumentality of the Professors to whom he wishes,
you would communicate this view of his feelings, & take their
opinion as it is wisdom upon it. -- This is the course with
me & his views.
If the Professors should be of opinion that this course is
impracticable and should be unwilling to use their influence
to accomplish it -- my father would say that your decision to
come to Charlestown would meet with his cordial approbation,
and that so far as his personal feelings and those of his
family are concerned, he would rather you would come here
than go to Salem. He would much prefer you to any other
man -- If the plan of colleagueship is impracticable
whether you come as colleague or sole pastor or either as
mother proposes that you should occupy the passage to either as
boarders --

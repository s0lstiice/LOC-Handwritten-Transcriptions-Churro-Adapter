Dear Sir
Pray here you during your residence
in the South, or elsewhere in the U. States ever met, or
heard of, a Minister of the name of Sherplis (pronounced Sherpliss) His father was an Englishman,
and resided in Philadelphia during the latter part of the administration of Gen: Washington;
where he painted portraits in crayons. He made
a collection of the portraits of distinguished Americans which has since, it is believed, been removed
by an Inn, to some part of the South. The likeness
of the father of a friend of mine was in that collection, the fate of which he is desirous to learn.
If you can give me any clue to a farther enquiry
you will confer a very particular favour.

I have all the attentions here, that I could wish
or am capable of enjoying. More difficulty
with me is the want of a subject to write
about. I have not yet had in one instance
witnessed nothing of the influence which we suspected might be extended here. The Cavalcade you
mentioned, is coming on from C. I have heard
nothing of it. I think they are not yet here.
I have just had a letter from Mr. Sidney - he says
the mortgage is sufficient seventy-half the
original debt having been paid.
I wish Edw. to find in the Vol of Tracts
Dr. Edw. Treatise on the Mahunka new language - & look it over, & ascertain whether he
gives a history of the tribe - or people says & anything which goes to show that they are or are not
a part of the Delaware tribe - whether their
languages are the radically the same or radically distinct. It is impossible if the fact,
to prove, that they are distinct nations having
no natural relation to each other. I will
request Edw. to send me the result of his enquiry as
early as possible.
I have no time to add God bless
you all Accept my best earthly love for
yourself - Love to Mrs. & the family of your
ever affable husband I know

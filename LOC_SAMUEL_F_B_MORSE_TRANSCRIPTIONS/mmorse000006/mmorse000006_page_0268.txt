It was no great disappointment to me -- A letter
from Dr. Quincy, of Alexandria, requests me simply
to spend a day with him before I leave this District.
I think I shall go -- It is only 4 miles
from here. I preached today, at the Capitol, & P. M. for
my friend Dr. Lawrick. You will infer from this
long letter after I that I am not very sick yet my
circulations in my system are very languid in my feet
& hands, especially what remains of them troublesome. The weather here is very changable, damp, & sultry, the
street very muddy, all which are very unhealthful
to health. I am most comfortable lodged &
as kindly treated by all the family as I were
of their near kin. Mrs L has a son twenty
old & died with us below for the first time to-
day -- we talk often & much of you -- Our dear child
Diego -- Dr. L. seems well acquainted with my trial
& feels as I could wish --
I think much of your trying situation, &
hope you will soon have release & be good
days, according to the days in which you are
afflicted. My own feelings were never so well,
& so severely tried, & by the treatment we are re-
ceiving from those from whom we ought certain,

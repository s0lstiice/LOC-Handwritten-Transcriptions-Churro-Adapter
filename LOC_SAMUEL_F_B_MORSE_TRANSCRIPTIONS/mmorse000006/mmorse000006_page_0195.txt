My dear Wife
After a comfortable journey to within
12 miles of this city, an uncomfortable one into this city, the last even in an open sleigh, a
great part of the way on bare ground, the cold
severe wind in our faces, I arrived at the
less 7 o’clock where I received as usual, a hearty welcome
& a good bed – feel, in some degree the ill effects
of last even’s ride; but hope to be relieved from
this forenoon, have seen the Govt. & most of the
Gentlemen of the Legislature, whom I wish to see
on my business, I have found, in yet everything
auspicious, & promising – equal to my expecta-
tions & I may add, wishes – tomorrow Mr Chester
carries me to Lansingburgh & Troy & to the Patroons
I have concluded to stay here till Monday morning
to speak for Mr Chester
then to proceed to N.York – where I hope to arrive
Tuesday night – then to stay 2 days – & then proceed
to Washington Let your letters written in
Boston & Office before 10 o’ clock Saturday be
directed to N.York – after that, to Washington
to be kept till called for – This direction I be-
write in the direction of the letter.
97293 Mrs Chester is better but has not been home – she has a fine daughter 6 Mo. old –

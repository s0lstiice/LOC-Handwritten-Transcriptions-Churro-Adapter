In view of things at present I should suggest this course, to acquire
excuses, and encourage in your friends the disposition to settle Mr.
F. to rejoice that your end was at length accomplished, that you
left the Chk. entitled under a good man, one whom
would build them up in the faith; and as for
them, would you were willing to think that most of
them were guided by good motives, that as for those
whom you believed to be your enemies, you forgive them
and pray God to forgive them by taking this noble
father, it does seem to me you come off with honor &
as W. — I. P. L. you get nothing but disgrace, whether
successful or unsuccessful.— No one can say you deserted your
post, you must stand acquitted from any evil that results from your
removal, your opposers have to bear all the weight of responsibility.

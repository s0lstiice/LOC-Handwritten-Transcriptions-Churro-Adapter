Charleston 20th July 1819
Sir
Your favor of the 11th
Inst. came to hand yesterday, receiving draft in my
favor on Mr. Barrell for $240, I have seen
them, but they are not present in my account
but expect to be shortly, however if they were, & off. that
is at present very scarce the last Fall was at 103 1/2 off
and in great demand, if any should be for sale before
I get the money from Barrell I will take position
and you or as directed, I hope this will find you
Mr. More & your father Mother well Mr. S. Hunter
with me in best respects to them all Mr. O is not yet
confined
Dear Sir
With esteem regard
Your Ob. Servt
Thomas Gist

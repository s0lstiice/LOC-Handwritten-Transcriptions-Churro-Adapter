Washington 21st Jan 2 1870
My dear Son I arrived here last evening af-
ter a very fatiguing ride from Phila with
sleep & in feeble health -- it was necessary
to fulfil an appointment that I should be here
yesterday -- I found here your letter from
Fredericksburg but had heard from you
by Mr James Dwight, as far on your way
to Richmond -- letters from home to
the 15th inform that all are well at Charleston
& Concord -- I long for the time when we may
all be settled together at N. Haven Mr Hay
is dismissed from his people, a letter from
Dr W -- writes me that the P. Ch. Cen lies
with him installed in my place on the
9th of Feb. -- I shall look out for a board &
place for you on my way home as I have
written you that you will it -- I think Mrs
Hazard & Mrs Rockwood will settle in N.H
by us next Summer pleasantly
I spoke kindly of you, favorable of your
faithfulness I shall look after it if you
request I send at the same time
to either land any other place

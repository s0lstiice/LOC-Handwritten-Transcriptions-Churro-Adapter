You talked of making a remittance in
dollars, they are at an advance of 5 or 6
cents -- If you send to any considerable
amount you had better get them insured --
Probably Mr Legare will give you dollars for his
books -- He could pay in them, as he has access to
the banks -- & you will have a good plea to
use, the depreciated value of their bills here --
Your better way will be to get good drafts
from Merchants on Boston merchants --
In hope of hearing from you soon I with
your anxious Mother's, Miss Edes, Long love to
your dear daughter & am, as ever
Your affectionate
father J Moser

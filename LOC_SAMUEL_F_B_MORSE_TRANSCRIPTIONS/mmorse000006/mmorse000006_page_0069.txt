Apr. 28. 1879
De son,
No letters from your brothers we are anxious
to hear send you 3 half sheets of Geo.7 Bring down
when you come next Wednesday, all the sheets of 2 1/2
vols, which have been sent The Geo.7 & Co. A may both be
finished in 6 weeks. Part of the affairs, are more
favorable They think of calling Mr. Buffum
he will not come as he is not pleased with the idea
of Collageneship I think it necessary for good reasons
for a few years Conventions such as exist cannot be
dispersed suddenly without too great a shock It is
danger to the peace of the city & important interests
out of it A gradual dissolution will be best for a
lumber All send love Your affecte
father J Moser
As certain whether a number of Arkansas' men
could be disposed of by the Students they may
engage any number to be sent after their return
they are not now ready The terms on which they
will have them would be liberal
Ask Mr Lockwood to request his father to write me
by him when he returns, & inform me whether he has sold
any of the Ch. Orator I sent him how many
If he has any money in his hands beyond what
is necessary to pay expenses & commissions, let him
send it by and on his return
Being down Richards & your own act from 19
of I know of Dr. Cooks what books he will take for
board I will send them a little to your bill

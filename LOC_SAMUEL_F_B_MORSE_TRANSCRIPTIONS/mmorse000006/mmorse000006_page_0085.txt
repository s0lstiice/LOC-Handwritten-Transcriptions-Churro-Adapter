[June 12, 1819]
My dear Edwards
I write to you in confidence
I regret that I spoke so freely of my views
when I was at your Father's the other night
It was not right to express myself thus
until I had heard all sides -- I trust
your Father will not betray my con-
fidence -- I think it my duty, in common
civility at least to let your people know
that I have arrived at Andover. In
Charleston I did not wish to see anyone
But it will be unfair if I do not allow
them to make further communications
if they wish -- You must not show this now

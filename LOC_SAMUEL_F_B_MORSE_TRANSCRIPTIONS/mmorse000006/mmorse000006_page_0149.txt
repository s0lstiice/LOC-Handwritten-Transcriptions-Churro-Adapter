Nov 19.
D. join we are waiting for the 4 pages, with a blank to fill
with I sent you by D. Porter on Saturday— hope you will it in the
1st, with sheets I came down by Mr Wheeler, yesterday— was
disappointed in receiving no letter— I enclose Richard's letter
just received— will you attend to his request, I send me the
books? — The printers are going on rapidly, I will have all
in types, of expert, in 5 or 6 days from this— I shall send the
sheets, as fast as printed— & the will deficient in your copy
when I know them— my health is rather slender— shall
be glad of assistance, if I could have that with to be accept-
able— purchase a copy of Worcester's new Gaz. in sheets
& send me as soon as you can— I shall go on immediately
with the 1st vol. of Geog. 7— & then with the Govt. & in the mean
time with editions of the 12th Abrognt. (more than half
the editn is already sold— it is supposed 2/3. will be sold before Jan. 7)
& the Ch. Orators None of them, however, at any risk at last to
no considerable amt.— I think, if I have health, I shall get
along with the assistance of Mr Sabine, who is getting to understand
the business very well— I likes it much— You will therefore
take care of your health, I direct your attention, exclusively
to your studies.— All send love Your Aff. father J M.
Remembered to Esq. F. family.

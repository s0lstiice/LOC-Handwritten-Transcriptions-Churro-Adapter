as I can for the rest of this picture also — I will delay going
to Charleston until I hear from you that both of the heads are
finished, as I wish to see both at the same time — as this is
an important letter will you oblige me by saying that you
have received it, as I shall be uneasy until I hear you
acknowledge the date — I am Sir, with the greatest
respect & consideration
Your humble serv
John H. Hotchkiss

Mr. Friend,
New Haven 20th July 1819
Excuse me for troubling you - I wish
you to say to S. I. House that I wish you to
appoint him not to consider me
as a Subscriber to the September, notwithstanding
standing I put my name to his paper -
As to the Member of Subscribers I
got, Genl. Howe will take the agency
of that part of the Congress - My
health is delicate - as usual -
with much regard
your friend
E. Puttill

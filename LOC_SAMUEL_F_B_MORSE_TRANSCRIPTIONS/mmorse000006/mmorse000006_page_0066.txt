My Dear Sir,
Notwithstanding all my solicitation
to be in season with subscription papers, &c for
the Panoptist, I am likely to be disappointed.
Your letter of the 20th came to hand today
at noon, after all means of sending to An
chorage before Monday had gone by. Otherwise I could have sent up the papers, &c by
the Flag, with perfect ease.
I now prepare a bundle containing
30 sets of Pam. L 20 of Herald = 4 first numbers of this volume. These you will oblige
me much by taking care of, in the manner which you propose. The numbers of
Panoptist which remain, you will please to preserve for future use.
I send 54 copies of the late Bulletin,
which is all I can spare. The only reason I wrote to Anchorage so long beforehand,
was, that I might have an answer seasonably enough to print as many as might
be wanted.

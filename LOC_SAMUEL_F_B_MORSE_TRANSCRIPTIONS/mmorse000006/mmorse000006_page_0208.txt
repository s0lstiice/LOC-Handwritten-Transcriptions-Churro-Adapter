Yesterday Edwards went to Dr. Worcester in the Street in Boston who told me
that the council had dismissed Mr. Jay from the ministry but said nothing
about Charlestonion, We presume that he will give his answer to
next Sabbath and have no doubt it will be in the Affirmative
We do most earnestly hope you will feel it to be in your own
duty to agreeably submit to & rejoice in it as ordered in Infinite Wisdom
and come home resolved to get away with your family as early as in the
Spring as possible this will be a real comfort to your family, and in order
to it will you enquire whether our goods can be transported direct to
Newhaven if it will be best to charter a vessel at Newhaven or here
and what the expence of transportation will probably be & where our
things can be stored and the expence of storage that we may
make our calculations accordingly also please to look out for good lodging
for us see if Mrs. Deforest can accommodate us all viz. Junius
and his wife & child till they get a house to their minds and
provision made & if we shall need three bedrooms & a Parlour &c &c. Mr. G.
Dwight & Mr. Leffingwell can no doubt advise us what is best to
be done and who are trust worthy and have some Conscience
give my love to Mrs. Dwight and tell her we shall draw largely on her
for kind & friendly advice on our removal to Newhaven and hope
she will not be backward in giving it; our Friends will be able to save as a
great deal if they are disposed without any injury to themselves
in any manner but enough of this you will pass & perhaps through Philadelphia
give a great deal of love to Aunt H. & Betsey K & Mrs. & Mr.
Ralston & family and any remaining Friends there on any were
else in particular to Mr. L Coits family who have been so very kind
to you may the Lord reward them in bitter Blessings than any we
can bestow I hope if we keep home in North that we shall have
it from them often

Fredericksburg Virginia Dec. 31st 1861.
My Dear Father,
I left Washington Wednesday last at 4 o'clock
on my way to Charleston, arrived here yesterday much fatigued and cold with a severe cold on my lungs, but
am better to day; I am in Company with Mr. Silliman of
New York brother to Prof. S. P. we stop here to day, and
shall proceed to tomorrow to Richmond stay the Sabbath, and
then to Petersburg and so on I shall be with Mr. S. till I
arrive at Fayetteville N.C. where we separate, he for Augusta
Geo. and I for Charleston -- we had a dreadful time yesterday
in the severest Snow storm I have experienced for a long
time. It is pleasant here to-day -- I direct this to Math.
supposing you will be there in a week or two -- I left the box
containing the large canvas with the Pres. had upon it at Davis'
hotel in charge of Mr. King the painter to forward to Boston,
I will thank you to see me King on the subject, and if not forwarded to Boston let it be sent to me in Charleston -- I have
as I think it will be easier transported from Charleston than from
Boston to N.Haven, -- my funds will but barely hold to Charleston,
and if I had not painted the portrait of the Pres. and
sec. 80 doll. from him for it I should have been compelled to
have borrowed to carry me through; I have however the note of Dr. Swall
for 80 doll. to be paid in a year. -- By the way, Dr. Swall with

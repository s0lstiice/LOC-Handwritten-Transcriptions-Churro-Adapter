I have not received the letter, and therefore cannot
say anything on affairs in N.Haven. — Perhaps any
purchase or lease had better be deferred until my
return in the spring, if arrangements could be
made to board Lucretia & myself & little Susan
with you for a short time previous to my returning
and so let me find Lucretia & Susan at N.Haven
when I return northward, it would perhaps be
best under existing circumstances. — I am sorry
your letter from N.Haven is missing as I have been
anxiously expecting it ever since I have been in Wash-
ington, it may be delayed and I may yet get it —
All monies, as you request, that I shall not im-
mediately want, shall be remitted, I fear I shall not
be able to send any from here; my stoppages on this
journey have consumed my funds pretty fast — as soon
as I get to Charleston I will endeavor to send you some of
what is due promptly paid to me. —
I miss my dear Lucretia & little Susan more than
you can imagine, I did not think I should find it
so hard to part from them, it is a new experiment,
and I don't care to try it again. —
I wrote you but a day or two since, telling you
all about my success with the Portrait of the Presd.

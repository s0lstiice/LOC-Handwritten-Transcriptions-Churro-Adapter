I trust Washington whence I shall write you again, & where I hope to find letters from you. I wrote
you from St. Yorks Till now I received nothing
new from Mr. Hanley on the subject of the
new Abe he approves of our plan. I wish her
to fee the aud[ptive] of the State of St. Yorks It must be
written on a large sheet of paper & sent by mail
for his inspection but it will be time enough
to do this after my return.
Mr. Ratston is sitting by my side & sends
her love to you & the family of Mrs Hanley
& Mrs R D is join her, if present. I must leave as
usual, to the family & friends of your ever affectionate
husband & brother
Thos Moser

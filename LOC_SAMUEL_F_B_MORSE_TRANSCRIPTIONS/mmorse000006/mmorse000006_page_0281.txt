Washington Feb. 4. 1820 Evenings
My dear Son I have to-day visited the President's Office & my business is drawing to a favorable close. I have been treated by both with great confidence, kindness & familiarity. I have never transacted business more pleasantly with any persons. This gives me all desire -- there is just rising & impending a conflict of interests & influences which, had a delayed a few weeks longer would probably have embarrassed if not defeated my plan -- but I had made such progress before these came into operation that they cannot now impede it. It will go strong. I hope to get away this the close of next week -- I have made as comfortable as kind hospitality can make me President Finley's friend is all attention to me -- physician & friend -- I have Finley's head & the President in a box, with I shall camp out have at New Haven -- Mr. Kalston writes me that Mrs Hazard & Mrs R. are in serious earnest about removing to N.Y. -- I am employed to look out for a house for them on my return.

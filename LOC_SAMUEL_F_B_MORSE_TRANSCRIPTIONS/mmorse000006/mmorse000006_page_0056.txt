Your can be sure has arrived, & was shipped
for your letter arrived -- Before the bill of lading -- The cost of shipping
which I shall pay, will be about 18250
dollars -- Mr Wilders letter, will keep till
you return.
A very friendly letter from Mr
Bromfield informs, that he had not funds to
pay Leslie for the Canvas -- If he has not
returned, per haps you had better make him
a remittance of £25 from Charleston -- I
have put the balance due Mr W. to that
debt in off my hands -- Prospects in respect
to all other debts are favorable -- What you
can conveniently spare as a loan will thus
afford effective relief.
A letter just received from President Coffin, mentioning
you with much regard.

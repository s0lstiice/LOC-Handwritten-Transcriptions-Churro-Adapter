We have no letter yet from you

quite anxious especially for Richard La

respecting you both We hope she is quite well
A letter from Nancy, received yesterday, informs that
you have relinquished the idea of returning
via Baltimore as the President will not be
at W. till July- Perhaps it will be best to
wait till fall before you go to Washington
We have written to Nancy to return immediately
We think, if there is a good vessel coming
to Boston you had better come all the way by
water otherwise in a Newport Packet which
may give you better accommodations you will
not stay too long We want to see you

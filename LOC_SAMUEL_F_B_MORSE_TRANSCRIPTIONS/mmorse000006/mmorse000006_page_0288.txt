My dear wife Washington City Feb. 9th 1820
Yesterday I received my comm-
mands & instructions of from the President
who are all that I wish The compensation
is not fully settled, but is in good train for
it. I will be done probably today or tomorrow
so that I shall leave the city on Saturday
to spend the sabbath in Baltimore. A theme
move on home as fast as my health &
business will allow. The weather, for several
days past, has been very mild &
pleasant, which has produced a good effect on
my health & encourages hope that warmer weather
& journeying will restore me & expect to
commence my journey some time in May. I
shall calculate to have the Company accompany me
in my Northern summer tour of about two months
& half a Tour on my Southern tour of three
or four months to commence in October.
I shall probably take a carriage & horse & driver
to be under my own control. I intend to take
the journey in the best manner to accomplish
my business & to secure my own health.
Hope you will consent to take a seat with us at
this, & Sackett Harbor, to remain among our friends

Washington Dec. 23. 1819.
My Dear Parents,
I received Pat's letter of 17th inst. this
morning, and snatch a few moments to answer it; I
have thought a good deal on the subject of staying
until the 10th Jan'y but conclude on the whole that it will
not be well; board is very high where I am, 2d class for
day, and I have no applications for Portraits except
Dr Sewall's & the copy of the Pres' for the family, the
former of which is completed, and the other commenced
and will be done this week, I have but one piece of
canvas left, and my materials, colors &c. running short,
so that I should be more than a fortnight without
any thing to do paying a great price for board, besides
this I find Henry has arrived at Charleston, and will
feel long pained until I join him; he writes me that
all my things arrived in good order, it is thus obviously
for my interest to hasten to Charleston, as I shall there
be immediately at work, and this is the more necessa-
ry as there are a fresh gang of adventurers in the
brush line, gone to Charleston before me.
You mention having written me from N.Haven

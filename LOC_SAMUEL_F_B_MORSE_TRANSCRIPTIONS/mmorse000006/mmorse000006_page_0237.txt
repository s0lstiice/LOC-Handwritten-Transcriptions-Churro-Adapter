Washington 21st Janr 1841
My dear Wife,
I arrived here last evening 5.30
& this morning was handed me your acceptable
1 King letter of the 19th in the 18th in the 18th done
from Gen Warren, the 13th. The information contained
in these letters is interesting in no common degree.
The die is cast -- The will of the Lord be done &
acquiesce with cheerfulness. The consequences as
to us will be well & I earnestly hope, to the old
& congregation also -- As to the men engaged in
objecting this change & thin measures, my opinion
of them remains unaltered. -- I shall answer Gen W's letter this evening
My last was from Phila. I remained there a day longer than expected. The stage was
full & I was not well enough to travel. On Wednesday morning I was better at 7 a.m. started in a very
cloudy stage trade on day night to Baltimore, where I arrived at 6 o'clock Thursday morning the roads in
many places bad go. I proceeded to President Munroe's office in his bed & then proceeded to his
city 36 miles in what may be called the slow stage.
This forenoon called I spent an hour with President
Munroe & opened to him my whole plan which has
his very cordial approbation in every part of it he

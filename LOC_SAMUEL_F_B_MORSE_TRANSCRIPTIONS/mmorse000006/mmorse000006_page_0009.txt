My dear son,
Your and Lucretia's letter of 22d28
all me was received this morning & relieved our minds
You will have received several letters from us within
which will relieve yours -- we must both be more
frequent in our letters -- We are glad to hear
you are in business as to -- & doing well -- I think
Rick? also is doing well -- Probably it may be
best for him to spend a month or two at
John's Island -- & return with you -- I take
a future off to make the land tax
will write him on the subject --
I will be glad to, soon as you can, to have you
insinuate a bill as large as you can -- It will
be relief just now, much needed --
Having written you several letters
lately -- & being in a great hurry this morning
I make this a short letter -- My principal
object is to let you know I have received
your -- We are all well & send much love
to you both -- & all friends as usual
Affectionately yours
father J Moser

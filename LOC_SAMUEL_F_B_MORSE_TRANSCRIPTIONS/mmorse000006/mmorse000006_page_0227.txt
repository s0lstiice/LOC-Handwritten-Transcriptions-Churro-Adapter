all the morning. I said such an interview would
be more agreeable, for you would have opportunity
to see the families who compose the congregation; I you
would see my situation. But I hope, my dear Brother,
that you will come, if I that you would not, I would
try to be more eloquent. Mr Legare's boat comes up
Wednesday or Thursday. All letters from me will reach you
left at his house in New Street with a charge to the
wench that takes care of the house to send them to the
Island to Mr Legare. Please to write me by this office
when you will come. What need of delay? Come this next
Saturday, or any day before, unless you think
a later opportunity preferable. Bring all letters from
home that you may have received.
I was at Mr Grumball's, your chapman, the
other day. He invited me to take a hunt, for deer, fox, or
for partridges with pointers. I should like to have you
with us. I go tomorrow to see his father who supervises
the largest & most complete plantation in this region, a
few miles from this.

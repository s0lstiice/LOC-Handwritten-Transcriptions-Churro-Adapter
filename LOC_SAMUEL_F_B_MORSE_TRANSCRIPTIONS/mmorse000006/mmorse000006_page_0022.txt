I find you are still introducing new things at Anacostia. I hope
you get to enjoy your new societies for I think they must be useful
though the Fish lot me there was but one class last Friday
evening. I am inclined to think there will be no suspension
on the score of punctuality. The attendance of the Props however
will be a greater excitement than anything of the kind in our
society last year. And haven't in theory, banquet in practice yet.
Mr F. tells me a fact which I hope no Anacostia student will
suffer to escape his recollection.
I am very well satisfied that Dr. R. will go to Genesee from
what I have heard what do you think, Dr. W. who is to be his
succesor? Curiosity is probably on the alert among you. If
the Dr goes will not some of our Anacostia friends be likely
to accompany him? There is a story relate of Dante, that,
being very ready in his answers, three persons to try him slide
set upon him at once with the salutations, God speed
Dante, whence comes your master Dante; Are the waters steep
Mr Dante, to which without stopping his horse, or making
the least pause he answered God speed, good years from the
fair, to the very bottom. Now though I had no intention to put
a trick upon you, you will perhaps find something of Dante's
responding, facetly to answer all my questions.

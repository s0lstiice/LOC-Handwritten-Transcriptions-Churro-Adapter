Nov. 26th In respect to your remaining another year before
you return, it must depend on the advice of Messrs West & Alston,
on your ability to support yourself, the state of affairs
between the two countries We all want much to see you,
& I will disappoint our expectations, if you do not come
at the end of the three years yet we can make the fat
enfure, if it will be greatly in your advantage in these
views of the subject, we leave it with you to determine
whether to remain another year, or to come home
the next summer.

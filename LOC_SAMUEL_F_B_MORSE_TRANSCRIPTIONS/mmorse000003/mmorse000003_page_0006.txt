and your Papa intends to get a Bill for you if he
possibly can he is much put to it to get money to purchase
one at this time as he has not received any from Quarters
where he had confidently expected it and the P&P case
remains undecided yet which is a source of a great
deal of trouble & embarrassment to us, and when it will
be settled is impossible for us to say - I hope you will be
able after this year to paint so well as to be able to continue
for some handsome prize so as to be some relief to us but
all in good time. - I wish you would give us some account
of the manner you spend your Sabbaths, who you hear preach
and how you are pleased & improved by the Discourses of
Morgan, you hear believe me my dear Son religion is of the
first importance to creatures born for Immortality learn
my Child to be wise for eternity and claim the God of
your Fathers for your everlasting Inheritance -
I have just returned from our Even'd Sat'd Lecture with a bad

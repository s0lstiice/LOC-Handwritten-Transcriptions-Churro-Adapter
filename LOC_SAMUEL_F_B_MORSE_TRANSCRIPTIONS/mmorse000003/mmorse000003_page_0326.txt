Please remember me to all Friends and relations,
especially Uncle & Aunt Salisbury, Cousin Betsy
Preece if she is with you, or if she is not, to
my Brothers, Miss Russell, Nancy & all the rest
of the family. — I had a letter a few days
since from Mr. Wm. Walker in Paris, wishing
me to procure a passport for him to come to
England which I have done & I suppose he
will soon be here. — Adieu.
Your very affectionately
JHB:
The last volume of Milner is the fifth, and is complete. —
1. Shall endeavor to paint immediately, the
portraits of some of Papa's friends for him. —
Be assured that as often as it is in my power, I will
call on, and keep up the acquaintance of Mrs.
Wilberforce & Thornton &c &c. I spent 2 days
and a night at Mr. Thornton's seat near London
about two weeks since. —
Mr. Phillips has just sent a note requesting
me to call on her, which I shall do. — Have not
yet seen her. — Let me not forget good old grand-
papa Morse, do remember me with a great deal
of affection to him Aunt More, and all relations

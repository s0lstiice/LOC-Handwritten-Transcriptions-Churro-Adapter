June 30th thus far my dear Son I had written three days ago expecting to
find it by the Ship that Mr. Justin the British Minister was to embark
for London in but a more direct way presenting by the way of Eastport
and Halifax I shall finish and send it in this way our Country is in
a trying situation but the Lord wissen and this is our greatest comfort
our Children are all in very great comfort to us We rejoice to think
that none of them is in a situation to be drafted as Soldiers in these
times Edwards has determined to begin the study of law as soon as he
has got through with being Amannensis for Dr. Dwight Richard
is to take Edwards place next year at Dr. Dwight's what he will
succeed as his Professor I know not they were both well when we
last heard about a week ago I have just returned from a visit to
Andover all your Friends enquired after you I read them some extracts
from your Letters which gave them much pleasure as the reading of
them has many others I must leave room in this letter for your
papa to add what he wishes I long my dear son to learn from you
that you have chosen that good part that I shall never be taken from
you this would rejoice our hearts more than anything that you can
conceive of Oh my dear very dear Son I have, early chosen the God of
your Fathers to be your God forever Use the means appointed for you
to pray, ponder, read daily the holy Scriptures attend constantly and devoutly
the preaching of good Ministers such as will teach you the purest
of your Church, give yourself to the Lord in a covenant union
to be forgotten this is your reasonable Service you are now at age
21 the 24 of last April let the life that the Lord has made in
you be devoted to his Service and to the promotion of his glory
in the world thus far from your affectionate Mother
C B M

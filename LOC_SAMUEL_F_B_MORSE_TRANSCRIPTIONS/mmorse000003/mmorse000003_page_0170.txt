12.10.1861
to the President of the United States
and the Secretary of War
and to the Commanding General
of the Department of the
West
I have the honor to submit
to you the enclosed letter
from Mr. E. G. Spalding
of Baltimore, Maryland
requesting a medical examination
and a certificate of his
satisfactory condition
for the purpose of enlistment
in the United States
Navy
and I am
your obedient servant
George W. McClellan
Commanding General

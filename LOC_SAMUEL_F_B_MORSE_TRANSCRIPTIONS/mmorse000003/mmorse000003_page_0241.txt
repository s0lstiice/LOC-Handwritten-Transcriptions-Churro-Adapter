London Sept. 9th 1813
Dear Mosby
I received yours of the 18th yesterday
and am glad to hear of your safe arrival &c.
I went immediately to Brown's who will
send you cargo as soon as possible.
Below you have the first bulletin of the
situation of the allies.
Genl Washington was relieved a few
days ago by Monkhouse's regiment under
the command of Col. Yuthill. Since when
much tranquility has prevailed in London
St. Castle. The Genl marched out the
day before yesterday and received his troops
in Buckingham place. He approved of
measures that had been taken by his majesty of Israel (Paul)
I communicated your dispatch to Capt.
Coate who is still besieged in Warren St.
The vigour of the enemy then, is I hope
abating. I mounted guard with him the
night before last. The retreat of his
leg and the skill with which it was
managed by the renowned Abernethy
still continues to be the subject
of much conversation in the Camps.

Charleston August 15th 1813.
Dear Brother.
I write you in great haste by Dr
Romeyn who leaves New-York in a cartel
for London for the recovery of his health.
We have had a good many loose reports
about you & your paintings lately. We were
told that the English papers claimed you
as an Englishman, and denied you that
you was born in America. We heard like
wise that your painting had obtained
the prize. We don't believe any thing till
we find it confirmed by your letters. So
write us often, especially good news. We had
a letter from Richard a few days since
he was not very well, his writing had
affected his health & spirit, but a jour-
ney in the fall will set him up again
as it did me. He wrote us that Thay
Sanford of his class was dead. In his
returning from Virginia (where he had
been keeping school) to Connecticut,

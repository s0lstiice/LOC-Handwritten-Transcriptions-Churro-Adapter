W. Samuel, 5th Horse
Bristol 10 Septt 1813
London
Dear Sir
Your esteemed letter under
date of the 8th was received on the 8th
I am pleased to find that you have made
up your mind to visit this place, founded there
is a prospect of your realising as much by your
profession as will pay the expenses of your journey.
As the employment of your talent will depend
altogether on the opinion the public may form of
your abilities in taking good likenesses there is
no possibility of saying to what extent you may be
succesful, but judging from my own opinion of
your merits I think there can be no doubt of your
getting as much employment as will pay at least
the expense of your journey here, and back to London.
A friend of mine, who came sometime since I heard
the probability of your coming to Bristol, said he would
have his likeness taken, I think he has not altered his
intentions. It appears you have now in a very actual

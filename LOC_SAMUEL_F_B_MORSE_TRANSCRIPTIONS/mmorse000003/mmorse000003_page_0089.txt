Popular opinion is becoming every
day more and more decided against Mr.
Madison will probably lose his election.
Already do his Weathercock partisans begin
to turn as they feel the wind shifting. The
Madisonian and Clintonian party at this
moment nearly equally divide the coun-
try, but every day brings good news to Clinton.
Our little Navy has done well bravely.
Besides the brilliant affair of the Constitution,
the Wash 18 guns, fought & captured
the Frolic 26 guns & superior force, I mem-
bers, but was obliged to relinquish her
prey, and surrender herself to a fleet
of the Line 74 guns.
The Americans have met with
no success in any quarter except on the
Ocean, and that is to be ascribed entirely
to our Navy, the increase of which has
always been a favorite measure with Fed-
eralists, and opposed by bitterly by present
Administration. No thanks to them therefore
for the honor we get on this way.

to Mr. West and Mr. Sheldon you will oblige me by preventing
my Respect, I find in a very fond humor this morning
not much or I should choose to write to Friends whom
I am much esteem as I do you. but believe me unchangeable
I shall write to Mrs. Bridgen by an other correspondence
but in case this does not reach you, you will hear
from me through her. I have not yet reported myself
to the Boston ians hour of Dinner and I hope I never
shall for it spoils a day, There are a great number
of Persons I wish to be remembered to but now I
to mention them my letter would be merely a
catalogue of names, therefore you will have only
the trouble by mentioning my arrival, but yet
to write the month of October I should
think would commit a great sin.
That of Gratitude for the Numerous favours
have received from them, Believe me to be
most sincerely yours
Abraham Lincoln

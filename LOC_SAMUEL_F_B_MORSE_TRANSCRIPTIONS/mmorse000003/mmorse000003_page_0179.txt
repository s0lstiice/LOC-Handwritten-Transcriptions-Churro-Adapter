My Dear Parents,
I wrote you a hasty line a few days ago, and
see? since, your letter via Halifax of March 9th
enclosed the 2d bill for 25 pounds on the Joe, in Scot
land; I write now in great haste, for indeed I find
but little time to write at all, and I feel sometimes
as though I neglected writing too long, for the days run
away so fast, that on looking at my letter list I find
I am often a week in arrears, and I scarcely finish
one, before it is time to begin another; as to my
health, it never was better, and I am in excellent
sprits; the only thing that causes me to be low spirits
at times, is my expences, I must confess though reluctantly,
that 200 pounds are not sufficient to support
me for a year; my first year exceeded it 10, or 12
pounds which I borrowed of the 2 years allowance
and this second year, I fear will run over perhaps 20;
what distresses me is, that it is unavoidable, no
one can accuse me of extravagance; on the contrary
I have pinch'd myself in my expences so much, that
I have certainly for some short time past impeded
my progress in my studies, not being able to obtain
many things on account of the expences, unless you
were here, you can have no idea of the constantly
increasing price of everything; I will give you an

Mr. Macaulay presents his
compliments to Mr. Morse
and begs to express his regret
at not having yet been so
fortunate as to meet with
him. Mr. Macaulay will be
particularly happy if
it should suit Mr. Morse
to dine with him at his
house at Clapham on
Saturday next at 5 o'clock.
Mr. Morse is fine doing
beyond the plough at the
entrance of Clapham

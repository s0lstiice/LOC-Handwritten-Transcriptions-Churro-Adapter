Mama's long letter gave me particular pleasure, some
of her observations however made me smile, especially
to reason she assigns for the contempt and hatred of Eng-
land to America; first I am inclined to doubt the fact of
there being so many infidel Americans in the country, second
if there were, there are not so many religious people
here who would take the pains to inquire, whether they
had religion or not, and third, it is not by seeing the
individual Americans that an opinion unfavorable to
us is prevalent in England; I could give a much more
plausible reason I think, and one which will not be
found to be far from the truth; It is founded in a regular
system of the British Government to belittle America, to
make it appear the most miserable country in the world, as
pickly, under a complete anarchy, a country which is
productive in none of the necessities of life; &c. &c. and all
this, for what? to prevent Emigrations, this is a fact,
too well known to be denied; have they not here travellers to

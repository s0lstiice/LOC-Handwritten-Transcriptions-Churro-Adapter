My dear Son
Your friend William Walker
having completed his medical studies
to the Genl. Stokes, visits France to spend
a few months perfect himself in his
profession. He may visit you in London
before he returns. I send a line by
him, thinking that it may reach you
in the Channel. Devotions in these times
consider the enemy of getting a
letter to you. This is a very pre-
cious one. I shall not write you
a long letter but only inform you
that we are all well. Your Grand-
ma Breeze is still with us, but ex-
pects to leave us for Naticca, in January
or January. Your Aunt Salisbury has
a fine daughter. Your Grandfather

but if it is any satisfaction to you (and I have
no doubt it will be) I know that I act I speak
completely from the heart, I have not disguised in
the slightest degree, my conscience bear me out
in every thing. I find I am three and twenty
years old, that I am neither blind nor deaf, can
hear, and I hope understand, that I have some
judgment in many things, that I can trace the cause
of that judgment to their sources; and I also have
found that I am in England, wide awake & that it
is no dream; now having found all this I begin
to exercise my judgment (which at 23 years of age
ought to have acquired a little strength,) and I do
and hear things every day & decide accordingly
and yet my brother tells me they have all the means
of knowing & I none, they are right and I am wrong,
when I see a thing I must believe it, when I hear
a sentiment I must believe I heard it.
Good night.
Marl. 14th. J. C. Dick.

Charlestown Nov 25th 1813.
My Dear Son
our State Thanksgivings in this day by the appointment of our excellent Governor Strong & your brothers are both at home & only await the pleasure of your Company to complete our little group which would make us very happy but we cannot expect uninterrupted happiness in this Vale of tears & must regret not to devote our self to it. I shall wish your good health and success in your profession in a glass of wine at dinner which I doubt not you will re
appricate by wishing ours when you receive this. I am all well and in pretty good spirits. Your paper of Edwards wrote you a few days ago by the way of Lisbon this will be handed you by Capt. Pratt who will tell you all about us he expects to leave his son Edward in England & will probably return himself in the course of a twelve months (when he comes if you don't return this year) I hope you will get him to take Charge of the three pictures I have so often mentioned that I wish you to prepare for Ship Russell Dr Bartlett & Aunt Salisbury I give you the dimensions of the pictures for Dr Bartlett in a former letter I hope my son you will attend to this request it is not necessary to tell Capt. Pratt that you are preparing these pictures for the persons I have named till they are completed and put under his care.— We are anxious to see the painter's cost but fear it will be injured before it arrives as E. Goddard has sold his ship which was to have brought it I want your Friends should be gratified by seeing your improvements in your art as soon as is convenient, Edwards & Buchard are enjoying themselves with us at home they and we cannot help troubling at your political observations we all wish you would leave politics to them who have to act in them and not trouble yourself about them

I have just seen Mr Stephen Van Rensselaer, who
you know was at College with us, and with whom I was
intimate, he has been at Edinburgh, he was very glad
to see me, he calls on me every day while I am painting,
he keeps his carriage and horses and is in the first cir-
cles here. I ride out occasionally with him, I shall begin
his portrait next week, if you could contrive to let Mr
Ralph Ingersoll know the circumstance of his being here in
London it would gratify him; Van Rensselaer speaks highly
of Ingersoll, and desired if I wrote to any one near him to re-

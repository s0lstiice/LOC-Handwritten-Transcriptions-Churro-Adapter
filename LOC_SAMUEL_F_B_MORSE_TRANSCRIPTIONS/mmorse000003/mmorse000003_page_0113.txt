of Venus & Adamis, & Mr W. advised him
by all means to paint it but not
to have the figures the size of life.
Mr Allston is going to begin the old
Gentlemans portrait very soon, he
promises himself much pleasure in
the execution of it. I am to
dine to day with Mr Brown &
spend tomorrow at (Twelfth Day) at
Mr Leckie's a neighbour of his.
I would be much obliged to you to
deduct the sum I had given you
visitot from £2911.0 and send
an order on Mr Bromfield for the
remainder as I am in want
of it, it being the awful time when
tradesmen's bills make their appearance
& I not having the wherewithal.
We are all very anxious to hear
how Mrs Vigors family are. Please
to remember all the good people of
London to them, that is Mr & Mrs
Allston & myself. Wishing you
a very happy New Year & hoping
you have had a very merry Christmas
I remain your friend
C Leslie

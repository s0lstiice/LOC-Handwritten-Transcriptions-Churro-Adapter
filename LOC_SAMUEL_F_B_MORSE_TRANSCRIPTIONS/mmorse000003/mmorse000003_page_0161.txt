Mr. S. B. Morse
Editor
In congratulating you on your Return
with your Friends from your late Succession
I was sorry to notice in your Letter which
informed of it that your gratifications
were interrupted by the indisposition of Mr. Alston,
it is with much pleasure I learn he is better.
I beg you in the Success & Honor that have
attended your Herculean Effort in the Society
of arts, as well as in the appropriate complements
lauded on your Picture at the Exhibition
by such a Judge as Mr. Wort, which I hope
may be the anticipation of future Fame
& Benefit.
For two past weeks by Payments I have
been indebted to you for Six Pounds I am
most truly Your Friend
N. Barnfield
Londone April 30th 1813.

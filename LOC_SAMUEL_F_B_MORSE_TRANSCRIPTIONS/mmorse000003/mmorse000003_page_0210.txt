We wish you to send us a miniature sketch

books pamphlets &c. but you may wish to

Lisbon & Liverpool to the care of Mr Hoodry
or Mr Breed - If letters were deported
with either of them they would find their
way to us - you C.O. send them by
some private officer to them.
I have no time to add.
We are all well & in good spirits -
I send you a great deal of love
Your affectionate father
Emose

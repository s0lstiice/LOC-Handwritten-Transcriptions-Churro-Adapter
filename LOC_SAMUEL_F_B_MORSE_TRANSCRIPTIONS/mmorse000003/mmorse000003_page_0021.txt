I have a favor to request of you which you would oblige
me very much by seeing done; I should like to have you obtain
for me a couple of pounds of Chinese Vermillion at
Mr. Gore's in Court Street Boston; you must think it strange
I should send to America for colors, but it is a fact that I
cannot find in all London any Chinese Vermillion that is good
for any thing, that with Mr. Allston brought with him, outshines
all that I have seen here, if you please you may send
me as much more as is convenient, it will not be wasted, if
you comply with this request will you ask particularly for the best
Chinese vermillion, it is in little patches of an ounce each, marked
with chinese characters, and likewise to get it at Mr. Gore's as he
sells the best.
June 16. Tuesday
Mr. Hurd call'd on me today and said that he
should leave London on Thursday, by him I shall send
this letter and with it a parcel for you, he will inform you
all about me, I have charmed him to go and give me
a full account of me, how I look &c. &c. with I hope
he will do, he goes in the Galin, there will be a number
of Boston people who go out in her, Mr. and Mrs. Higginson
(the old folks), Mr. & Mrs. Emery, Miss Tudor, and some others;
the Americans seem to be moving off, Col. Trumbull and
his lady visit New York this week.

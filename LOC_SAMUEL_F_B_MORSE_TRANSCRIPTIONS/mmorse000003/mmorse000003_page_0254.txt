It is futile for your benefit and we consent. — The reason why I am
opposite is yet on my hands, I am bound with a debt of between
five hundred dollars, some of which I have been obliged to lay
to secure the bearer of this letter, and that I am obliged to borrow money
to support the family. At some future time about the miserable war, our
property comes again to former revenue value, I am of opinion that
property comes as it goes from Government but this is uncertain. I
trust that you may know how our parents are situated, I doubt your expectations

Charlestown Oct 2. 1812
Dear Brother,
In less than an hour,
this letter must be written, carried to
Porton, find its way thro' the Post-Office,
into the letter bag & on board the
vespel bound to Halifax.-
This letter was written at Papa's
request; it is designed to be a letter
from home, & in this & in nothing
else is to be its charm.- as this
was the proposed subject of writing &
as it is now done - I have nothing
to do but to stop. At another time
& at a notice more seasonable, I
will write an answer to your kind
letter to me; as will Edw. B. to his &
Papa & Mama to theirs. I will mention
whatever things which I think it will
interest you to hear.- Adieu. R.C.Morse

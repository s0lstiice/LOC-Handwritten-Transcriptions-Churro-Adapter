6 Thus I have given you a slight account of my excursion, hoping there may be something in it which may give you pleasure. I have just finished a model in clay of a figure (the dying Hercules) my first attempt at sculpture; Mr. Alison is extremely pleased with it, he says it is better than all the things I have done since I have been in England, put together, and says I must send a cast of it home to you, & that it will convince you that I should make a painter; he says also he shall write to his friends in Boston, to call on you & see it when I send it. Mr. West also was extremely delighted with it, he said it was not merely an academic figure, but displayed mind, and thought; he could not have made me a higher compliment. Mr. West would write you, but he has been disabled from painting, or writing for a long time, with the gout in his right hand, this is a great trial to him. I am anxious to send you something to show you that I have not been idle since I have been here.

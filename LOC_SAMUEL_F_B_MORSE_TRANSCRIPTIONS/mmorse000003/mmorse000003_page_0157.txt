My dear Genl
Capt Pratt has just informed me
that a Cartel is about to sail for Halifax. I am
impressed with opportunity to drop you a line. We
wrote you largely by Mr Wilder a few weeks
since, who expected to sail from New-York in the
Pacific but I have just heard that she is not
permitted to sail. I know not why, or that it is
time. Possibly Mr W. may not go so on
parcel by him will be returned if so we
shall be sorry. As was written very fully by him
I sent you some of the newspapers of the day.
I enclose also by him a first exchange of
£25 on the Society in Scotland drawn by
Mr Foxeant. If it shall on further enquiry
be thought safe I shall enclose the second
in this letter.
We are very anxious to hear from you. Your
last letter, which we have received, was dated the 20th Sept.
almost 6 months ago at much longer interval, than has
passed without hearing from you, since you left us. We
presume you have written but your letter must have
been carried. Mr Ingram has arrived, who he says dined with you on the
5th of Decr. I should be bring letters from you.

Dear Moses
London November 22nd 1813
Receiving that this was but a
circular letter I took the liberty of opening
it to ascertain whether its contents would
warrant my making you pay its postage
to Bristol. If you could conclude to
exhibit your Harriers I beg you will let
me know as soon as possible that I may
have time to get a frame for my pictures.
In my own part I would rather you should
not exhibit, (as you may suppose) but of
course you will do as you like. I wrote
to Mr Allison on Wednesday last and the same
day Ross sent his pictures which I suppose
are arrived or something very near it by
this time. Since then I have seen Mr Coates
who spoke with great interest of him and
I gave him his pictures which I suppose
is about the same. I called
on Lansdale but he was not at home.
I dined with the Smiths a day or two
ago, they were all well and begged to be
remembered to the Allisons I yourself
with his copy and I think it will be his
best. Mr Coate I have just been to
Brompton a very
near the Smiths on account of the
air. Pon Brokeder has been confined

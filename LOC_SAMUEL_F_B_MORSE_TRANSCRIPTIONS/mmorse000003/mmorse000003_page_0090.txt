We are surrounded by enemies. The
English on the North in Canada, & on the
East, on the Ocean, on the South by the
Spaniards in Florida, where we are usur-
ping most wickedly and most meanly the
territory. & on the West by the Indians.
When the Jeffersonian dynasty com-
menced its operation, we were at peace
with the World. Our canvass whitened every
sea, the voice of prosperity was heard in all
our dwellings, -- This is exchanged for the
terror of War -- the scalping knife & tomahawk
misery at home and abroad -- Americans
are beginning to be ashamed of themselves.
Papa's affairs begin to look a little
brighter. At best but gloomy.
All desire to be remembered.
P.S. General Van Rensselaer has resigned his office
in the army. Write us by every opportunity and
I will you should have written longer but
prest it for time.
It convenient wish you to inquire if
Taylors Edition of the Poets is completed. How
many and what Poets it includes. What
the size, price, and manner of execution
of the work, and write me in your next.
Don't forget this, if you ever have opportunity,
to inquire.

Mr. Allston is getting on finely with his large picture, it
will be exhibited in Feb'y. it will make them open their
eyes, Mr. West says there is not another like him in
this country. He and Mrs A are well & desire to be remembered
to you I am at present upon a large picture 8 feet in
height by 6 1/2 the subject is the Death of Hercules, the figure
is from my little figure which I have before told you
of in a former letter, the model I shall exhibit at
the British Institution this Feb'y. Mr. West and Mr.
Allston, bestow many compliments upon me for that
figure; I shall send it to you as soon as I can.
My affectionate love to my brothers, to Grandmam
Aunt & Uncle Salisbury, Nancy & family, and remember
me to all friends.
Write me good long letters, and often; and may
an honorable and speedy peace set all things right
between these two countries.
Your very affectionate son
W: H: B.

in it, but pays me a compliment, by saying, I have
expressed the truth, and nothing but the truth, in a very clear
and proper manner, and hopes it may do good by his advice.

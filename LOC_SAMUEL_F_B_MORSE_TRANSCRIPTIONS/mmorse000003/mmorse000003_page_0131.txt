Mr S. H. B. Morse
Die
I am mortified at this late
success to send you the enclosed letter from your
father & can only offer as an apology a
state of extreme indisposition and a press-
ure of employment which my illness has
made extremely irksome -- I did not receive
the letter until my arrival in Liverpool & mis-
read it with some other papers -- I have been
entirely unable to attend to the commissions in
your father's note & enclose it to you that
you may execute it. I anticipated the pleasure of waiting on you previous to leaving
London but my unlucky situation must
prevent it -- I am Sir
your obed' Servt
J W Smith

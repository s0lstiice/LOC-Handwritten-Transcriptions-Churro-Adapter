Charleston, January 12th 1813.
Dear Brother,
We write by Mr Wilder, whom you know the car
cel mail goes by the Castle from Wilmington Delaware
we are anxious to receive letters from you Mr Wilder has
written to his friends in Boston since your last letter.
By them we learn that you were well and was prepar-
ing a large piece for the approaching exhibition. We ex-
pect to hear a more particular account daily.
I believe I informed you, that Charleston was
federal in the choice of Representatives to Congress
This State is now almost thoroughly federal. In the last
elections the fed' went to the Demo's as 2 to 1. The change
since has been much in favor of the Federalists. All the
States east of the Delaware are firmly united against
the war. These States embrace more than half of the
wealth, strength, and talents of the Union.
We have a reading room established in
Charleston, where we daily receive the papers from
all parts of the United States. This institution is doing
a great deal of good by informing the minds of the people
They have here an opportunity of looking at both sides
of the question.
96651 Richard spent his winter vacation here He was
well and in tolerable spirits. He has not yet concluded upon
a profession.

My dear Parents,
I have this moment received your letters of 25th
and 28th of September, have afforded me very great pleasure
I have heard nothing from you for a month, or part of it
I am afraid my letters in future will not be so interesting in
any particular, as my former ones, my studies occupy so much
of my time now that I shall not be able to give you in
them descriptions of what I have seen in this strange country,
as I could wish, I don't know but I must defer them till
I shall see you again; whenever I can however I will narrate
some things which occur that are novel, and that I
think will amuse you, but in general they will look in form
to the state of my health and the advancement of
my studies, the I know will interest you; I am at present
in perfect health and spirits, and Milpster says I am
making great improvements, my whole attention is fixed
to my studies, and my friends and acquaintances
think I am industrious and some too industrious, but
as long as I find my health improving I shall not think
the latter the climate agree with me extremely well
I am more fleshy than I ever was, I have gained twenty
seven pounds of flesh, which is something I mentioned
in my last letter that I should probably exceed my allowance
this year by a few pounds, but I now begin to think
that I shall not I am trying every method to be economical
and hope it will not be too long before I shall relieve
you from further expense on my account, be assured
as soon as I am able I shall endeavour to do it if I
can at all calculate, I think I shall be able to do with
another year's support at first I sent you in letter no. 15 or 16.
96683 I hope you will receive before this I should like a little of that kind of business
as possible, but am willing if required to do away things for
papa that is in my power, as I am under obligations to you

My dear Brother I am commissioned by Papa to write
you by a cartel, I only have half an hour to write. I am
told to write all I can think of.
At present our house is full of company. Dr. Dwight &
his son William Skinner of N. Haven are here with other
company. Madox is sick up stairs with a painful swell-
ing which prevents her sitting up. She hopes to get rel-
ief from it in a few hours. Edw. S. & myself accompany
Dr Dwight on his journey to Portsmouth & Munificence
Lake. We go on horseback.
Saint Salisbury & are well. Betray Brazier Uncle Ar-
thur's second daughter spends the next winter in Boston.
Edw. S. & myself spend the next twelve months at home
& in Mr. Rockwood's Office & I, to recruit & confirm
my health, in a most anxious manner.
It was quite sickly with the dysentery at N. Haven
when we left there I continue so. Three Students died
so fast has been dangerous ill. We are anxious to
hear from him. Rosseter devotes himself quite as
much to painting as to Law.

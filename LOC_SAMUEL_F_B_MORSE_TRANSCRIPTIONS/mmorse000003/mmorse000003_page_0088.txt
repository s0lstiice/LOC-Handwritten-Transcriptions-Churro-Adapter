Charleston Nov 7th 1861.
Dear Brother,
I write hastily by M Langdon who
fails tomorrow in a cartel for Europe, I wrote
you a few days since by the cartel from
Boston to London direct.
Many things of much importance
are occurring daily. But we have become so habit
accustomed to change, that when we write
every thing appears to be the same old story.
Representatives to Congress were elected
throughout this State a few days since. In
Charleston the Federal Candidate had the
majority. The probability, may be certainly
is that 19 out of the 26 to which this State is
entitled will be other Federalists or Clintonians.
This State is strongly decided in its
favour of Peace measures and Peace men.
News from the north, have been
hitherto without exception nothing but tales
of disaster, defeat, disgrace to the Americans. Our soldiers have their share of bravery
acknowledged even by the enemy, but not
without clothing, provisions, ammunition,
or any of those necessities which a wise
administration should have provided.

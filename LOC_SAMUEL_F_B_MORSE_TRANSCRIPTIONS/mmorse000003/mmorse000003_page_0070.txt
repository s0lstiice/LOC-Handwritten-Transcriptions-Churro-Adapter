A great noise appears to be made in America
respecting an apprehended alliance with France; it is
a bug bear conjured up by the federalists, and the inflammatory
pieces in the federal papers have been copied into
the English, till they almost begin to think there is a foundation
for it; I have often blushed for my countrymen on reading
some of those violent party essays & to see how men for the
sake of party will justify, or rather attempt to justify
this government this all their base and unwarrantable
proceedings; I would ask, because England is at war
with France, is it not possible to chastise England for injustice
towards us without uniting with France, must England
treat us worse than her own subjects, shall she dictate laws
to us & shall we obey, for fear of having it said by a few
selfish, interested men, that we are uniting with the great
tyrant of Europe to destroy the only remaining spot of liberty
in the old world; the object of France is to annihilate
this country, that of America to obtain redress from her, can
we not obtain redress without annihilating this country;
Great Britain would be very glad to come to our terms long before
she was in danger of annihilation

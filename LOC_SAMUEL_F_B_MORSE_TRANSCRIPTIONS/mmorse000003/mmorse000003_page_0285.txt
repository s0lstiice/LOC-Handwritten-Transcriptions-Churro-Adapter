49 London Street, Fitzroy Square
Wednesday 12th June, 1814
My dear Sir
Having a great deal of business
to attend to this morning, I can
spare but a moment for writing.
I must however wait myself of that
to thank you for the agreeable intelligence contained in your letter to Leslie: but in a particular manner
to request (for rather to advise) you not
to take a share in the intended raffle.
For this I can offer two reasons:
first, that the price of a share is so
much for you to risk upon another
uncertainty; second, that I much
fear, should you win, that the world
will may suspect (on account of
our connexion) that I was in some
way interested in it. I think
upon the whole you had better not
take one; but wait until you can
paint an a landscape equal to it yourself,
which I make no doubt, you will ere
long be able to do if you are industrious.

(Monday 18th) I shall go to the place to see the concourse of
people, for to see him executed, I know I could not bear;
but the crowd will be immense and you must expect
to hear of many lives lost, I should not be surprised
if an attempt was made to rescue him, the people are
very violent, and this country is in a very troubled state,
I will leave a place at the end of this to finish tomorrow
after his execution, and will then tell you what happens
at it; At the time Mr. Percival was shot I was at
concert in Hanover Square having had a ticket presented to
me, by a friend; I did not hear of it till after the concert
and could scarcely believe it, till the next morning I went
to the House of Commons and saw for myself there was
an immense concourse of people, who were very noisy, some
rejoicing and some crying out, Burdette forever!.
But enough of this; you will probably hear the
whole account before this reaches you; I am wholly absorbed
in the studies of my profession, it is a slow, and arduous
undertaking, I never knew till now the difficulties of the
art, and no one can duly appreciate it, unless he has
tried it, difficulties however, only increase my ardor, to
make me more determined than ever to conquer them.

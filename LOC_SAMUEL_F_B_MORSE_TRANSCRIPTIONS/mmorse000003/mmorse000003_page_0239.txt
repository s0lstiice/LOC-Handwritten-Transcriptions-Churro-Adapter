I must mention other things for Rob. to write
all well at Mr Channing's-
Your mama, brothers Nancy, Betsey, Mill
Rapelle - Mr Loats - Dr Dwight, D. Lyman
(he is here) & your friends join in love to you.
Remember to think of my Sister. (Write him
& write me alive) to Mr West - all my dear
Friends - who ask after me.
In great haste, your ever
Yesterinate father
Enos

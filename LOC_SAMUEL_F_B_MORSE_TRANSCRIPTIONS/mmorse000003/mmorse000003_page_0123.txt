My dear son
We have written you about one
of letters within the last two months by Capt
Bartels from Phila. (the Pennsylvanian in which I find an
answer to a letter I wrote to you) I believe has not yet reached
the office of the New York papers. I have not yet heard
from the gentlemen who are engaged to convey them to
you. I hope they will reach you safely.
I learned from Mr. John Breed, of another
office, direct to London, which I suppose, is
transmitting a draft on Mr. Sam Williams
for $91.11.2 for his passage to the Bromfield. — This will
exceed the necessity of Capt. Langdon's having
the $50. which I requested him to do — This
draft will exceed the sum you supposed
you would want as you expected to support
yourself after two years, with expectation
I hope you will be able to realize your
last (of Sep. 20th) mention of your
expenses nor of your having done any
thing with the Legs . — You will not fail to
write us fully by Capt. Langdon concerning
Charleston Jan 11. 1813

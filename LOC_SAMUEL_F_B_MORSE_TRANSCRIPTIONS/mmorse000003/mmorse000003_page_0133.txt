London Jan 9. 27. 1813.
My Dear Parents,
In great haste, I just enclose a hasty sketch
in Indian Ink, of myself; I send it merely to show you
I have a disposition to send when I can, any thing which
I think will give you pleasure; it is not very like, and
you must not judge of my improvement from this; it is
the first thing I have done in Indian Ink since I left home.
When there are more favorable times I will send you
a better one.
NB. Never in better health & spirits.
In very great haste
Your affectionate Son
S.B.

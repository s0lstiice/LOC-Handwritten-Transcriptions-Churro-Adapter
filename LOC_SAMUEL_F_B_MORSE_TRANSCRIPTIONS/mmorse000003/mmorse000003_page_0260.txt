P.S. I have not had time as yet to call
upon Brown, Gordon, Middleton & Mrs Budger
according to your memo and am, but shall
not forget it.
I open this once more just to say I have
received Mr Allstons letter of the 20th. I am
surprised he had not got the letter I sent him on
Wednesday. He does not mention having received
a package I sent by the Coast the day after
I arrived. I sent him the sketch of Waller's
he said he had seen it. I will attend to every thing

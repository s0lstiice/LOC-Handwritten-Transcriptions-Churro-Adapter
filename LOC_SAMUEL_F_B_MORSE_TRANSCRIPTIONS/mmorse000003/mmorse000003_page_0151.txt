Your young friends in this town of Boston
are all well, so far as I know. We
have as yet felt little of the war in
this part of the country -- but its effects
have been awful on the Canada
frontier.-- I wish we might have
peace without further bloodshed.--
I have lately written you by Mr Lor
der, who expects to sail from N.York, in
a Cartel -- I enclosed a draft on the
Society in Scotland of Sea Sergeants, for
£25 -- I sent another a short time before
for £91.11 -- on Mr Williams -- to see
whether you have rec'd them or rather
whether Mr Bromfield has rec'd them
as I sent them to him --
Mr Ingram has arrived, who says
he dieded with you the 5th Decr. -- but not a
letter from you -- you sh d not miss so good of
opportunities -- He informed us he saw your statue of the dying

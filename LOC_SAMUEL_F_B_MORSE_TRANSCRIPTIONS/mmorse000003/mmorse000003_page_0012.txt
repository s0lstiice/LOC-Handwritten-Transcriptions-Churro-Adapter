(Sunday evening) London May 1st 1812.
My dear Brothers
I have just a moment's leisure to write you
a few words in answer to your letter some time since,
I should have written you before, but my time would
not permit me, and now can write but short if
I had time I would willingly devote a great deal of
it in writing you and the rest of the family, and relate
all that would be interesting to you. It is
not for want of matter, or inclination, that I
have not written you before, but really for want
of time, I am going to write Papa to-morrow and
shall tell him minutely how I employ my time
so you will easily see the reasons of my little life.
I shall have the more to tell you when I come
home, and indeed what I have already observed
in this strange country would employ me many
months to narrate, whenever I have leisure I shall
tell you something or other.
At present I shall give you the particulars of
an affair which employs the months and ears of every
one in this country, it is the assassination of Mr. Per
ceval the prime minister of Great Britain on Monday
last, the 11th as he was entering the house of Commons he
was shot directly thro' the heart by a man of the name of
Bellingham, he was removed into a room adjoining &
died in a few minutes, his death, has caused great
perturbation, and in the towns where they are the most
victious such as Nottingham and Manchester there were
rejoicings on the occasion; the assassin Bellingham

Your papa has transmitted all but about 4000 of what he agreed
to allow you for to defray the expences of the three years We hope
Mr Brownfield has received all the Bills safely we shall be much obliged
to him to inform us of the whole amount which he has received of
us for you since you arrived We feel greatly obliged to him Mr Alston
and Mr West for their kind attentions to you hope you will be grateful
to all who have treated you with any civility & attention
whatever and let all the true civil things that may have been
wasted in your hearing pass for nothing in this way you will make
Friends of all and it is a good thing to be beloved may you by
good people & not hated by others any farther than cannot
be helped in the discharge of our duty to them attend to all the
requests made in this & former letters as much as you can I must
send you a dear papa Edwards Newark Belong all join me in
love to you we all long to see you we have just got a letter
from Richard he is well from us ever your affectionate

Mr. Appell is very well - I often asks after
you as do all your friends - Very affectiously
Mrs Hendly lies at the point of Death - probably
will not live through the day.
Your Grandma Breeze is with us, has been
through the summer, goes in the Aunt's
library are spending the summer at Windsor
Vermont - Mr & Mrs Rockwood are well they
have another son a few months old.
An Armistice has been proposed to
our Govt both by Govt Provost of Canada & Ad-
miral Sawyer, on the Halifax Station - We hope
the proposal will be decided to by our Govt. &
that the interval will be successfully im-
proved in negotiations for peace.
A national fast is to be kept to morrow in
State fair on the 25th July - Many of my
papers & books published among the Rest but I have no convenient
opportunity to send them to you. Will make up a
packet for you to send by first off ship.
Your Mama Grandma Nancy Belsey & every of
your friends, find you much love
Your affectionate
Father G. More
I write in great haste to send via Halifax
Mr Evans & family are well - Write me whether
you have received the sheets of the Geo. 9. I sent you, &
whether anything can be done about reprinting it
in Eng. in the manner proposed. Consult
Mr Smith, Mr Evans' friend on the subject of
Mr Taylor also. Perhaps Mr T. W. undertake
it, I make you a reasonable consideration. You
shall have what you can get in this way to give your future support.

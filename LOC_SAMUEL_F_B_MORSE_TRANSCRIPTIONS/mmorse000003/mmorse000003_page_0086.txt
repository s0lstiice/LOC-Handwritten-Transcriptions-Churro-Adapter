18. war against her? I answer with another question
what good it would do; in what manner can we injure
her; will the mere act of declaring war, hurt her; can
we send armies to France; can we send a navy;
has France any settlements on our continent which
we can invade; what then can we do to her; by the
act of declaring war we shall show our spirit, and
our justice, but with it, our folly; for turn the tables,
Can France injure us; yes, she can confiscate the
millions of american property in her power; now
has not our government at least in this instance shown
some wisdom; Why is it said, so much blood must
be spilled, so much property must be expended, taxes
must be levied; &c. What else can be expected from
war; England is an example herself of what deprivation
individuals will suffer for the good of the public, every
individual in the single tax upon income pays 7/10 of it to
government this is but a single tax; and cannot the
patriotic Americans bear a slight tax for the good of
their country, or will they make good the opinion the
English have of them, that the moment their purses are
touched they are ripe for rebellion. Do consider these,
things, my father, before you decide on such an important subject as this, do not let the sneers and taunts

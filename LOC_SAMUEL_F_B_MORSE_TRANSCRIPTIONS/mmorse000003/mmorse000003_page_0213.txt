and sound discriminating views, of what is truly good as they
enable you to judge correctly, what is, in what is not, to be imitated
by you, in any or all the good people you may meet
with in this very wicked world, we are to hope constantly in
mind that perfection, is not to be found in any of our fellow
Doormen but that we are called on by our bibles to follow as our
Perfect pattern, our Lord Jesus Christ who did no sin, in following
him we cannot excuse excuse and to be like him must be our daily aim
if we hope to be with him through an endless eternity. I hope

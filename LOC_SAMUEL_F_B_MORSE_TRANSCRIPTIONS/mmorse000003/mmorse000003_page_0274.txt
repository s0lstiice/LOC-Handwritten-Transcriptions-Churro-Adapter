Dear Brother,
I wrote you a long letter a few days since
by Mr. Abbott. Papa, Mama, Richard Ve have written repeatedly
within a few weeks, so that I very much fear you
will be inundated with congratulatory messages. I have
during the past year heard my long epithets to your
name and written frequently for the New Yorker, and
exited much democratic wrath. I have been loaded
with the epithets, Tory, Traitor, British partisan, &c
&c, but have happily survived. The democratic paper of this morning speaking of one
of my pieces in the Centinel, says "The fault in the Centinel deserves the severest castications. The writer
has given the strongest possible proof of his attachment to Britain, and as a punishment for his
crime he ought to be made to run the gauntlet from Maine to the Mississippi." The piece they
refer to is in the Centinel of the 24th Novr headed
"Harrison & Perry." You may have an idea of pursuing it.
Our Canada Expedition has terminated most disgracefully by the acknowledgement to all parties the jubilee

city, I were to dash like a thunderbolt upon the
purified Canadians." Bets were laid to a large amount

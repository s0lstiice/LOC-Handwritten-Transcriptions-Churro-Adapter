In Augt. next I shall have been 2 years
in England; I shall probably stay another year
and then visit Paris; Late letter from my father
gives me to understand that I may visit France
when Mr. Allston thinks it advantageous; which
will prob. be next summer a year. From
there I shall probably go to Italy; and then
I hope return home, how many years hence I
cannot now determine.
But the person waits to take this letter
to you for the present, Adieu!
with many respects to your parents & sisters.
I am as ever, with esteem
Your sincere friend
H. S. B. M.

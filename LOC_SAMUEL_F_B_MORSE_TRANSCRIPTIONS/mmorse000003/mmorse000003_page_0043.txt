My dear brother,
Your favor of the 1st in
is before me; I thank you for it. In
regard to the bank I cannot at present state
any shares, as what little property I have
lies locked up by the war, so that I cannot
command it. It should then be a speedy
peace & could I get my dies, I might
then take a few shares if to be had.
In regard to the taxes due on the ferry
field land, can you conveniently pay them
with your own, I take the arm out of
what you are to send Mama. Let me
pay her? If so I shall be obliged. If
not, I will write the Comptroller on
the subject. The business will be attended
to in season by some one, to prevent
the sacrifice of the land.
Mama has communicated to me
the information in her letters, respecting
your new college. For your friendly
feelings & assertions, I thank you as much
as if you had been successful. Your
disappointment, I appreciate affects you
more unpleasantly than me. Am sorry
to announce Dr. Miller as your President. Elect.

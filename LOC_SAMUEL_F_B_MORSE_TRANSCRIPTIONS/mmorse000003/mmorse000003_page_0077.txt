written so much on politics I will now leave them, only
adding that with this I send you some of the principle
public prints in with you may see a little for yourself,
what are the sentiments of the people, I would observe also
that the Morning Post, is considered the most scurrilous
paper in England, and has been notorious for its abuse of
America. There is a report current now in London that the Essex Amer. frigate has taken the Maid-
ston British frigate, I am all anxiety to know if it is
true, I hope with all my heart it is; nothing would raise
the American character so much in England, as doubling
that better dose: Oh! if we had but a navy, how much
contention among ourselves would it prevent.—
I was glad to hear that cousin Saml. Breese is
in the navy, I really envy him very much, I hope one
day as a painter I may be able to hand him down to
posterity as an American Nelson; I give Richard
joy on his bachelorship, and am glad to hear he acquired
himself handsomely— I understand a few days since
that Siml. Dexter Esq. was in favor of this war, I hope it is true

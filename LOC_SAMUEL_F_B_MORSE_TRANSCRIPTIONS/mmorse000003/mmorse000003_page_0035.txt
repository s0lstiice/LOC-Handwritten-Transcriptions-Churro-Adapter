The papers, with you will have opportunity to inform you of the state of parties. Your Mother has given you sound advice and respects the course you should pursue. Be the subject wholly of politics altogether. I enjoy it that you are where you are at the present time. You will do what you can without delay to support yourself, as I know not how we shall be able to procure funds to transmit to you, if we had them, how we could transmit them. The war will greatly depreciate our property. I enclose you the second exchange for $100 on Sam. Williams Esq. I sent the first about 40 days since I hope it will reach you safely. You will consequently improve every opportunity to write us as we shall give you.
It is rumored in Boston to day, that the President of the U.S. has directed Genl Dearborn to provide a vessel to carry the Yorkee to London in a few weeks if so luck would give you that vessel more particularly.
Fortune will offer frequently, I expect, from it. I go to the place, to write you I cannot now add the Parkman arrived in the Sally Ann, I gives us every good news of you -- We hear also from other quarters that you are doing well.
Resent my regards to Mrs. West, Alston, Bannister, Taylor, Bromfield, Dr. LeRome &c Your friends here are generally well, I send them love to you, Miss Rapillet

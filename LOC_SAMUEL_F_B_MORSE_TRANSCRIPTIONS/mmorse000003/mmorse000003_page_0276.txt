London Dec 22nd 1813.
Dear Moser
I should not trouble you
so soon again, had not an event
occurred which perhaps it will be well
for me to give you early information
of. — Poor W Coates long sufferings are
at last at an end — He died on
Monday morning last. He seems to have
been completely worn out by the dreadful
complication of disorders under which he has
been long groaned & to have dropped off at
last in a very easy manner, probably
without a struggle! — I saw him about
a week ago and he then appeared to me to
be better & seemed to think so himself
although he was certainly weaker than I had
ever seen him before; but as that appeared
to be mainly owing to a violent discharge
from his bowels which he had intentionally
brought on, it did not alarm him.
I understand that on Sunday he showed
some slight symptoms of delirium — I
have not yet been to his lodgings or to

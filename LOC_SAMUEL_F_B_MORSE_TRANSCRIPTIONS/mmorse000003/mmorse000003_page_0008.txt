You will be able always to find the latest Boston papers
at the American Coffee House, where it will be well for
you occasionally to call for the purpose of learning the
political state of your native country. There is a part
part of a change of our federal administration, at the
next General election New York has elected a federal
legislature. The house of Representatives in this state will
probably be federal, with the Senate in the democratic.
The season is very backward indeed. On Monday
last snow fell 12 or 15 inches deep with made good sleighs
in N. Hampshire. The Govt is completed, all but
the ministers, & Appendix. It is a great burden off my
mind. Matters move precipitously in regard to the
Parish. Theological Seminary, Mr. McCombman has
another Council to fit next Tuesday.
I shall send to Mr. Pomfret by this conveyance a draft for $400- Continue your economy it is very necessary.

have forgotten it. I spoke to Saml. Wood to write

I am obliged to close in haste. Your
friend, who are as numerous I affectionate as ever,
desire us to present you their kind regards, when
we to we write you Remember us affectionately to
Mr. & Mrs. Alston & all who ask after us. Continue to write
us often our letters gives us great comfort. Affectionately your

a scheme of a man of an opposite opinion in
politics to myself; I look to the general conse-
quence on the world at large, I find the A-
mericans disgraced, I don't ask did Mr. Madison
plan the scheme; will it not help our election;
will the Federal or democratic cause suffer
or gain in consequence? These are questions
which may be asked and answered by selfish politicians;
I ask what will Europe think of us,
will they say the democrats are beaten, the
Federalists have gained their point; No, they say
the Americans are beaten, the English have
gained their point. — I am well aware that

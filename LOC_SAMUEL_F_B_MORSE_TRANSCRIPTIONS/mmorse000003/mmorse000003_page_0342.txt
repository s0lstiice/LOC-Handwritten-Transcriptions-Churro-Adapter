[Parish Moore]
I thank you for the conveyance of
your father's very friendly letter, which I
have received with much gratification.
Since the severe weather commenced,
I have been unusually occupied; indeed in
writing this, during the last 2 days, I have
not been able to sleep, for the refreshment of
a dinner, and yet I must find time to give
a lecture on Thursday -- as you will see
by the enclosed card, perhaps, should you
mean to attend it, you may take a coach
toa previously (6 o'clock) in Southbrook
Court, where you will find me for
Mar 15 1814
J H Lister

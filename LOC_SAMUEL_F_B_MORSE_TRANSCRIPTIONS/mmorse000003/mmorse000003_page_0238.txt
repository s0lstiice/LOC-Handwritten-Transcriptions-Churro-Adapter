Both came home with me -- Dr Dwight at 10 o'clock has
since come here with us -- Dr D. preached
one of his best sermons before the Board of
Commissioners for Foreign Missions in Boston
on Thursday a full house -- 500 collected
body and the sermon being printed, if I have not
missed my morning at 10 o'clock -- other
similar articles -- Mr J. Been died
while I was at N. Haven of distress -- with
praise in that City -- I have proved in many instances
mortal -- Your Grandfather's friend at Wood-
stock generally well -- Rev. Mr. Spivak is dead
as is young Benjamin Hard, who was born yesterday -- It has been very healthy in this Town
& Boston in the last season, never finer one,
nor one more faithful, though not Mr. Bush.
The War continues very unpopular & disastrous
by land -- Movements interesting are now taking
place on the Lakes & borders of Canada -- Important
news from that quarter are hourly expected.

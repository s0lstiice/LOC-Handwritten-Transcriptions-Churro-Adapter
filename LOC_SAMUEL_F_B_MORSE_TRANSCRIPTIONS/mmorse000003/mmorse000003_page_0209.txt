The season continued remarkably fine --
It is generally healthy in this region -- nothing
new since mine of Saturday -- I forgot to mention
that a 74,1 a troop of war, are building
in this town -- will make it at present a pretty
busy place -- Mr Jones, whom I hope you
will see, will be able to communicate
verbally many things which I cannot
write -- Will you see Mr Wilberforce,
Mr Mannering -- Mr Thornton -- Dr Letham --
Mr Bender present my regards? & those --
I say to them, that my sentiments towards
the War remain the same as contained
in the extract from my sermon published
in the Daily No. of the Christian Observer -- I trust
I hope their influence will be used to
effect a speedy peace, in equitable terms.

Mr Samt F. B. Morse
Bristol
Sir,
Your letter by Mr Russell dated 29 ult was
only received, as is also your further favor of 11th ult I
am happy to learn the success of your visit to Bristol is
so encouraging as to induce you to continue there longer
than you at first contemplated. Two letters from
America for you were delivered to Mr Russell, who expected
to return this week.
In expectation that the Bill drawn by Gordon
for 22.19.10 would have been presented this morning, as it did not
appear yesterday, I referred writing you till to day in
order to know if your letter reached Mr. Addington &
Bottell I have sent to them to inquire, which has induced
them to present the Bill which is accordingly paid without
the Noting - say £22.19.- at same time I beg leave to
observe that such a charge is perfectly customary. I usual
in such cases - As you had accepted the Bill & left
no direction at Buckingham Place for its payment on
the day it became due, it was the duty of the Holder
to have it noted according to regular mode of Mercantile
Proceeding.

Charleston, Feb. 12, 1813.
Mr. S. F. B. Moore
Dear Sir
As your parents are about
victing, & I can send you a line without any expense, I just take the liberty of telling you that we are well, & am always happy to hear of your success & prosperity in your pursuits.
Tell us of the result of your expected debut in an exhibition which was at hand when you wrote lately.
Be so good as to hand the enclosed to Mr. Smith, or send it, if soon long, &
oblige your friend,
Jehs Evarts.

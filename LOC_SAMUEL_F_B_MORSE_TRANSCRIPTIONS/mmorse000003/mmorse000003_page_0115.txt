My Dear Son
With you a happy New Year. I have been
intending to write you by the two or three last opportunities
but have been prevented from cause to communicate to be met
with at present. Your last letter that we have received
is dated the 20th of September in that you informed us
of your point to Mangate & your arrival at the little port
of Dover Castle which is the first occasion that you have
written since you left home at your departure. We are all very
anxious to see some of your performances in the way of
music & mathematics and have been expecting something one
thing I have told Mr. Russell your Aunt Salisbury & Mr.
Barlett that you intend sending each of them a picture
the two latter are hoping to have but for your mantle
piece for the future they expect from you. I think
thereupon you have anything of that kind or any thing
desired in the way of your profession to send us and will
not put up with great care that Mr. Brownfield or Mr.
Samuel Williams will be able to furnish you with a safe
conveyance or Captain Langdon of this town would be
a fine opportunity for you to send us any of your perfor-
mances as he is your Friend and would put himself
to his convenience to oblige us. I want to see your Heroism
that has pleased Mr. Redding Alston so much because
he is a fine man.

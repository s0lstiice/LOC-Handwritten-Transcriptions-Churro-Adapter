very Dear Son
Soon after I had written my last letter to you we received
your agreeable favour of the 27th of April there have several Vessels
arrived since from different parts of Old England but no letters
from you since have come from London Direct letters have arrived
to others from their friends in London which mention you
and confirm the impression that your own accounts give us
that you are doing well, we long to hear the progress of the medical
picture, and are anxiously looking out for letters every
time we hear of an arrival that shall convey to us this information,
We learnt from Mr Channing that they had
long particular letters from Mrs Alston this week and
that Mrs Alston's health is not good which we are very
sorry to learn we hope however that his indisposition is
temporary and that one this reaches you he will be quite
recovered give our kind remembrance to them – I hope any

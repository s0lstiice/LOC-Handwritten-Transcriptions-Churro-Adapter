We had a very good Commencement at Yale. A small
class entered, but we shall have near 300. next year.
A medical Establishment is now connected with the
College in the large plaster house belonging to Mr. Kil-
house. The celebrated Dr. Smith of Hanover is one of the
professors & Dr. Ives too. Hamilton College near Utica,
(New York) takes many of our College Students. It is un-
der Dr. Backus, is magnificently endowed & its situation
is favourable to its being one of the first Seminaries.
Miss Welsey (the eldest) is married. Young Edwin Porter
will be soon & Rev. Welsey. Robert Kane who was here
with the Ralstons is dead. There is a Benevolent Society
in College to aid indigent Students. It is hoped this will
be an era in the History of our Literature.

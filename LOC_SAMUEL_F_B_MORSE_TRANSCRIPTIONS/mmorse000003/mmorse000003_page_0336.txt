Mr. Palmers presents his compliments to
Mr. Morse and informs him that the Charter of
New England, of which he desired a Copy, is now
ready for him, and Mr. Palmers wishes
he would call and take it away: the Expence
of copying it will be £3.3— the Charter
being a very long one.
AB. Be so good as to call between the Lanes of 12
and 4 on Mr. Voyes Jur. at this Office
Office for Trade, Whitehall
14 March 1814

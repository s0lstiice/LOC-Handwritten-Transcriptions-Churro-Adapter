hearing songs of triumph & exultation on the misfor-

mine neither has I tolerated it, but I cannot, I do
long to be at home, to be in the navy, & teach these
insolent Englishmen how to respect me. I never was
treated with such rudeness & such insolence.— Since
this unfortunate affair, they are the first to accuse
the Americans of vain boarding, & are the loudest & most
unscrupulous in practicing it themselves. Com. Rodgers
was head of their fleet on the 4th of July, was
at Bergen where he revictualled & stood 5 days in the
steamer North; they have dismasted 2 frigates after him
the President 38 & Amphitron 32.— The Essex has been
heard of here, at Galparais on the west coast of South
America. The enterprizes of our cruisers astonishes them
very much. Our blockade swarm in the channel, com-
plaints are made every day of captives made by them;
they have blockaded towns & rivers, the river Shannon
in Ireland is now blockaded by one.— The Moravian

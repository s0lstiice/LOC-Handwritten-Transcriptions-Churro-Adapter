1. Washington
Aug 17th 1862
With the Booth Express that
though an unexpected visitor
at this morning she is
determined the afternoon of
attending Mr. House and keeping
no promises if possible
she will avail herself of
2 o'clock to tomorrow, to see
her Mr. A will not
wait her leaving by one 1/2 the
half hour's grace which
she is always inclined to
profit by.
House Esq

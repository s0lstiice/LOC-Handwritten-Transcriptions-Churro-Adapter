My Dear Son
We received your good letter, dated March 11
last Tuesday, on our return from Andover where we had
been to Attend the funeral of good old Egbert Samuel
Abbot. - the good man is gone we trust to inherit the
promises in the kingdom of his Heavenly Father he has
left a very handsome property (after a Number of
legacies) to the Theological Institution in a very
indicious manner and with a determination to have it
do a great deal of good. - Next week we expect
Edward's home from M'Havan as the vacation will commence
next Wednesday Richard chooses to stay till the
Senior Class is finished in July and then come home Ed.
has been making out an Index to the first volume
of the Geography it has cost him a great deal of labour
& patient Industry which you know he is capable
of but I fear it will injure his health & Spirits to apply
himself so closely he writes for Dr Dwight five hours
every day which is enough with reviewing his Studies
Richard will probably take his place next fall as the
Amannenship. they are well as we learn from them by
letter last week. - We are much pleased to find by your
letter that your enthusiasm for Painting continues and
Charles town May 3, 1812

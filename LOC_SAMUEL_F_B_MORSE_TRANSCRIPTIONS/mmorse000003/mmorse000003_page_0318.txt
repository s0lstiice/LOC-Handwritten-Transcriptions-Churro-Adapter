they should happen to fancy my pictures I shall
succeed; if not, why, I shall not succeed; I am,
however, encouraged to hope.
I have given up the thought of visiting
Stirnburgh, it will cost too much.
I was extremely happy to hear that you
had given my brothers your Georgian Gazetteer , I
will certainly aid them all in my power, by pur-
chasing books, maps, &c. which they shall wish, or
I chance on any new work of use to them I will
purchase it and forward it. If I am prohibited from
writing or thinking of politics, I hope my brothers
will not be so ungenerous as to give me any. Every
thing is interesting from home which is of a pleasant
nature; now they certainly cannot suppose

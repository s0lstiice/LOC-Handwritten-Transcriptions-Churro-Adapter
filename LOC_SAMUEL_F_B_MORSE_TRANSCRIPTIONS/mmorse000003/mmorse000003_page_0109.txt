N.B. 3 Mr & Mrs Alston are very well and desire particular
remembrance to you. In a former letter you mention
having written to him; he desired me to say that he never
has rec'd such letter, or he should have accussed himself
of great disrespect if he had neglected to answer it.
Accompanying this are some newspapers, some
of Cobbett's, a man of no principle & a great rat=
cat, yet a man of sense & says many good things.

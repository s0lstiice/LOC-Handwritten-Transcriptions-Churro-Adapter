Directions for reading the Papers.
Russell has digested the accounts into a complete & orderly history of the war. You can see the progress of
the War in Georgia agt the Creeks -- of that on the coast by the British fleets of that in Canada. &c.
In the Centinel of Nov. 24. you may see the puffs & bragging of the war-men contrasted with
the actions. under in different columns -- also on the same subject a piece called Canada Expedition in
that of the 27th.
Also Chittenden's bold & spirited Proclamations to the Militia of the Vermont who had volunteers'd
agt Canada & on a detached piece, the vile answers.
A piece by Edw. concerning Harrison & several Political Communications.
Papa's estate advertised in the Centinel of 27th.
Mr. S. G. B. More
London.

Charleston July 13th 1813
Donson Mr Jones falls in half an
hour -- I add a short letter to the Packages
delivered on Saturday -- Mr Hillhouse
has also added a letter -- I hope all will
reach you safely -- Your friends here
take a deep interest in your success -- Dr
Goddard yesterday sent me a note con-
tain'd in an Envelope from a letter from
Eliska dated 10 or 12 days after yours
that we received with some joy another
revel -- Goddard writes thus -- "I have
gained much honor & credit, and aspire
from the Royal Academy -- by an elegant
painting of a Lying Female, opened in the
Academy" -- We don't understand this --
Does he not mean the prize you mention
from the Philadelphia Society of Arts for your
Plaster cast? or have you also received
a prize for your painting from the
R. Academy? We wish you had described

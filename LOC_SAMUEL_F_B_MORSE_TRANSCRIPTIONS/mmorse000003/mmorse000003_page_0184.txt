than to write very small and close on large thin paper, and to put

to me were exactly the reverse of this, they were written wide
apart, large, on thick paper, a great deal of sealing wax, one
or two strings, and only one side half down in two of them;
this is paying dear.
By the way I feel very much obliged to Mr Brown
and Mr Evarts for their kind remembrance of me, and wish
my best wishes to be tendered to them, if I had time would
write to them; My love to my brothers whom I wish would
write long, long, very long, & very particular letters, let me
know every thing, for I am from home, and cannot
learn what is going on there unless from them. Love also
to Grandma Ma Breese, Uncle & Aunt Salisbury, Miss
Russell, Dr Bartlett, and in short every body; to Nancy
Betsy, Caroline, & Joe. Adieu!
Will write again soon.
Mr Allston & Lady one in perfect health, and his large piece
will soon be the wonder of the world.
Young Payne the actor, is here, and has performed at Drury
Lane with the greatest applause, he is certainly in his morals
an exception to actors generally, I am acquainted with him
and am much pleased with him.
Could say much more but no time. Yours very affectionately
S: G: B: Moritz
Thos Jaspers, Jr. am in the case with the cast which I

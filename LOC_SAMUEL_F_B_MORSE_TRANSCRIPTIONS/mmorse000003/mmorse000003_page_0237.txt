My dear Son, I have time to write you but
a very few lines. Your last letter rec'd was dated
the 30th of May in which you mention that you
had begun a long & partular one, which we
are anxiously expecting. Capt. Langsdorff in a
letter to Mrs L. of the 20th June informed that you
were then well & doing well. All are safe and if
you have given us pleasure & comfort I trust
you will be enabled to persevere in a good
course & continue to be successful. We shall
calculate to see you by leave of Providence
before the War Commences at N. Haven.
You had better make your arrangements to leave
Long Island in July next, & do me all you can
to suit you. From reports which have reached
us we shall expect a good deal of you &
hope you will do what you can to deserve
the reputation you have among us.
We are all well except your Mother who
has been previously afflicted with a boil for a
week past but we trust her extreme pain
is nearly over. I attend to W.H. Conklin
your brother in every way. He is a prosperous
man. I am
Ever affy
your loving father
Wm H. Crompton
Charleston Sep. 18th 1813

Charlestown Jan 9th 1812
Dear Brother,
I write in great haste by a gentle-
man who will carry this letter direct to
London. We are all well and in good spirits.
Received letter from Richard to day. He is
well also.
Sam Breeze is now in this port. He has
just arrived from a long voyage in company
with Commodore Rogers - The squadron was
visited the common cruising places of the
British Frigates in the Atlantic, but could
find nothing - They have sent in two
prizes - worth 300 thousand dollars -
Captain Hule of the Constitution was
married a few days since to Ann Hart of
Saybrook (Conn.) - He is coming with his lady
to reside in Charleston immediately as Super-
intendent of the Navy Yard.
I have been engaged for several weeks
past in writing for the News Paper - and am forever
your
affectionate brother
Geo. Washington

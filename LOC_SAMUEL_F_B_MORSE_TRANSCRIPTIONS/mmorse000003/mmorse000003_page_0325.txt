16. I am in very good health, and I hope I feel grate-
ful for it, I have not been ill for two days together
since I have been in England; I am however of
the walking-stick order, and think I am thinner
than I was at home, they all tell me so; I ain't
so good-looking either, I am told, I have lost
my color, grown more sallow, and a face ap-
proaching to the hatchet class; but none of these
things concern me, if I can paint good looking
plump ladies and gentlemen, I shall feel satisfied.
Mr. Allston has had severe illness, but
he has happily recovered, not only his former health
but I think has got a complete new constitution
he is stouter, and more healthy than I ever saw
him before; Mr. Allston is also in good health.
They both devise particular remembrance to you.
We have had a dreadfully severe winter here
in England, such as has not been known for 22
years; when I came from Bristol the snow was up
on each side of the road as high as the top of the coach,
in many places, especially on Marlborough down,
and Hounslow heath.
The copying of your second from the Trade
office, cost three guineas, which I shall charge
to you. I have not time to copy it as you desire
for I have to lose so good an opportunity.

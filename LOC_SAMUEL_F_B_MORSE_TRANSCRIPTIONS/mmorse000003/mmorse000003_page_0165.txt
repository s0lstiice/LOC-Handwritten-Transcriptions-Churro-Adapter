Mr. Peale Mr Fairman the King & several
others have devoted much attention to this branch
of the School & I hope to see it in their hands
highly useful & improving. The drawings are of
first rate excellence which have already been pro-
duced & are studied for future composition, the
result must be invaluable. The Society of Artists
are about establishing a similar school, and
have done me the honour to elect me their
Prof s of Anatomy. Dr Chapman is Prof s to
the head s of arts. No lecture have yet been
delivered, but I shall commence in a few weeks
a professor, could you do me the favour to give me
a short account of the manner in which anatomy
is taught to the artists in London? What is
the number of Lectures delivered annually & upon
what particular subjects do the professors dwell
most minutely? If there are any publications
on this subject (except those of Charles Bell & Dr. Jones
hun) I shall be much your debtor if you can
inform me in your next how Mr West enjoys his
health & what he is doing. I am all anxiety
expectation about the hospital picture, as one
of its surgeons I have a lively interest in
the affair & sincerely hope we shall ere

Aug. 1st 26th 1813.
Since writing the above Mr. Parkinson has some
out of London and I send this by another conveyance,
I have just received four very acceptable letters
by Mr. Jones they came to me free from postage.
They all gave me great pleasure; Goddard must have
made a mistake in his information to his father, there
are no prizes to contend for at the Royal Academy
at the season of the Exhibition, of course I could not
attend to the Students for the prize; I could not
attend to the Students only, the subject is given out for next Decr. I am
referring to painting Achilles for the body of Hector. I did
not intend to paint for the prize this year but the
subject is one which does not take hold of my enthusiasms
and I have abandoned it as I think I can
employ my time better. I gave Goddard the gold
prize medal to give to you, as it was of no use
to me here, it was from the Adelphi Soc. of Arts,
I was not the Roy. Acad. Mr. West promised
me a note to you, but he is an old man and very forgetful,
and I suppose he has forgotten it, I don't
wish to remind him of it directly, but if in the course
of conversation I can convince to mention it, I will.
Mr. Allston has not rec'd your 2d letter which you mention,
he will write you, however soon he is well
as is Mr. Allston; and desire their respectful remembrance
to you; I shall not write on Mr. Allston's success
or goodness, for I should never stop, if I once began,

Charles town June 28/1812
My Dear Son
We have received your letter by the Sally Ann and rejoice to find
that you are in so good health & spirits and growing fleshy and
that you are making such Landable progress in your profession
as that in addition to your gaining so high reputation as an Artist
you will so soon be able to relieve your parents from much
more expense on your account. not that we have the least
degree been unwilling to hear this excuse but are really unable
the proficiency you have made my dear son has confirmed us in
the wisdom of having sent you to your present place of destination
and although we long to see you yet we rejoice that you are so
happily situated at so great a distance from our at present
wretched miserably distracted country whose mad orders
are plunging us into an unnecessary War with a country that
I shall always remain as doing more to spread the glorious gospel
of Jesus Christ to the benighted Heathen and those that are
preaching for lack of knowledge, than any other nation on
the Globe – our hearts bleed at every move to think of again
being at War we have not yet forgotten the wormwood & gall
of the last revolution – We hope you will steer clear of any
of the difficulties of the contest that is about to take place –
We wish you to be very prudent and guarded in all your conversations
& actions and not to make yourself a party man on either side

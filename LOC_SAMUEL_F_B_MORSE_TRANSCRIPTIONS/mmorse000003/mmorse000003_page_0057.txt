about 10 o'clock: the first thing that struck us after leaving Deal
was the sight of the towers of Dover Castle at a distance, we
slowly approached it, and from the mountain on which it
stands had a most delightful view of the town of Dover, &
the surrounding country, the sea, & the French coast. Dover
is situated in a deep valley between two high mountains
or cliffs of chalk, these are strongly fortified, so as to render them
impregnable, on one the castle stands, it is a

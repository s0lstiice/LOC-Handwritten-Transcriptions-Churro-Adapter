Br. Nov. 11th 1813.
Dear Morse,
With you receive this you
will be kind enough to call on Mr. Walker
and explain the reasons and ask him for
a note to Mr. Reeves of the Alien Office. Bear
in mind that the application is to be made
on the ground that Dr. Walker is a student
for England professes not to make use of
the arts and sciences. If you think that you
acquaintance with Mr. Houston is sufficient
to intimate to justify the request, perhaps
a letter would be better even from Mr.
Walker. Having obtained one from either you
will of course go with it to the Alien
Office and I should advise you to inquire
for Mr. Musgrave, who has always been
causally friendly and will give you even
information on the subject. With regard to
the application, as well as the fortification, I should like for-
mispir to be obtained, it is to be forwarded
to the agent at Dartmouth, whether by
you or the government depend not. That
you will easily learn here. I wrote to

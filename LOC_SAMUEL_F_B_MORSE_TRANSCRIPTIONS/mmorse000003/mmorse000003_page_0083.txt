It has been a standing toast for many years among the
officers of their navy, "An American war." Now under
these circumstances, has not our war been of service? if it
was only for the simple capture of the Guerriere; it has silenced
all those sneers, nothing has been said of blockading America with
6 frigates, when they recollect the Alert sloop of war, they cease
to persist in affirming that a sloop of war is a match for our
frigates, and find no fault with the management, or the
discipline of the Constitution, except that she had too much
to suit the taste of an Englishman.— the officers of the navy
too have had their wish, but would rather an American
should not choose for them again, the manner in which it
is to be conducted.

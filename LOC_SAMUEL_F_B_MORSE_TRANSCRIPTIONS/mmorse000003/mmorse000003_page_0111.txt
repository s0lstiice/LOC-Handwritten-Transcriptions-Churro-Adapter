London Jan 7. 5th 1813
Dear Monk,
It is so long since we have
heard from you that, unwilling to
believe ill wishes the cause of your silence,
we conclude you have become very
industrious, and employ your time
to more advantage in writing
to your friends.
I have been unwell part busy,
but I am now much fully recovered
from my indisposition, also
more of himself, though perhaps the
last belief is not to be rejoiced at.
Mr Allison & I have sent our pictures
to the Gallery. He has made good
interest to get his large one placed
at the end of one of the rooms. As
to mine it is of small consequence
where they put it. Mr Hafter
finsihing his Diana showed it to
Mr West who was (it speaks even
moderately) in raptures with it. He
unmistakably called his sort Raffaello,

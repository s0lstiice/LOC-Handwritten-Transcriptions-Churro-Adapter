15 Broadway Bow. Dec. 3 1842
My Dear Friend
I wished must to have seen
you ere this but a violent cold & diarrhea almost
amounting to dysentery must plead my excuse
I dined on Monday foot with Mr. Thornton
he thanked me very heartily for the sermon of
your father's which I sent him, he told you
address and desired me to tell you that he
would be pleased to have us together at his house
and would let us know when the Parliment
were engaged on an interesting subject when
he should be happy in our company to dinner, I
afterwards let us in to the Houses of Parle the debate
Mr. Wilberforce took my address and will if
he chooses ask me to dinner or show me
any politeness he sees fit. He expressed some concern
that your father had not informed
him of your residence in London. From confine

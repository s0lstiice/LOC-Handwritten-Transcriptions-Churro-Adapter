A letter from Mrs. Alison of Dec. 2d, mentioning your
as being well, &c. as intending to exhibit one of
your pieces, shortly after, at the British Gallery.
We had no letter from you by that spirit nor
by a matter by which Mrs. Cunningham's letter
from last week we have received was dated Sept. 20.
giving us a pleasant account of your pedestrian
excursion to Dover. We are anxious to hear
again from you with the fate of your exhibition
piece. We wish also to know whether your
expectations have been realized as to your
expenses, &c. whether you have been able
to support yourself for 8000 a year during
the third year of your residence in Eng. -- In all these I
feel sure you will be fully. I will be a great relief to you
if you can support yourself. It is probable it will
be best for you to make the excursion as you
have now arrived at a period of life when you
must expect to live independently of your Parents.

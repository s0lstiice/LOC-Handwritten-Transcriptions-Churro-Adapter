The Channing, one all well, as is Mr. and Mrs. H. Munro
who are become great favorites of mine. I think
it probable you will remain in London but
you will find your intercourse with the large
rather interested by the war and have occasion
to show the excellence of your services. I wish I
could have my opinion with you on my Dear
Alston on I did formerly and may again, I have
not yet determined what to do whether to spend
a Country campaign, go immediately to Philadelphia
or spend a month at this port where I am in
need of refreshment of brain burnt. I hope
you like your present Lodging. Steward has
given me great pleasure I think this one equal to
that of Mr. Lawrence. Please to present my love to
Mr. and Mrs Smith, Mrs S. of course and
inform them their Friends are well and that Mr
George Davis and Lady Salion me very much. Confer
to Capt Orr and hope he is up to a rate than former
ship Martha Lyman is engaged to a boatman of
property Mr. Leslie will lodge me by Callings on
Mr. and Mrs Denison and presenting to them my
genuine regards. Should write them and others by
all letters are liable to be opened on both sides
of the Atlantic. It renders it unpleasant that
your Uncle Ship Hales is not forgotten.

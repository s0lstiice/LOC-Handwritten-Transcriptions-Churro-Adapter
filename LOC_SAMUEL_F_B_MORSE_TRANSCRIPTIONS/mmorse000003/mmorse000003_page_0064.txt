Dear Son, I add a few happy lines to your brother's
just to say that we are all well -- Mr. Rudskog have been
at Darhams letter from London who have given us many
particulars concerning you, what have confided me -- Thank
you Brownfield for his kind letter of the 12th Aug. with
I have just received from London as kindest to him & Mrs
B. also very particularly to Mr. John Wilson, to whom
I feel under great obligations for their attention &
kindness to you -- Also to Dr. Lettowne whose letter I
have answered soon -- Mr. Mat. Colston's three other
elder sisters are now in Boston on a visit -- I think
Mr. Bodman has a very favorable opinion of Elisabeth,
I we don't know but she may at some future time
be our neighbor -- I have just returned from Maj.
Walker's having married Mr. Armstrong & Abigail.
They are to live in Boston -- I saw your grand father
now a few weeks time at Woodstock pretty well
Polly is married to Mr. Fox brother to Colly's husband --
We had a good commencement at N. Haven -- Richd.
deliv'd a handsome dissertation on a National Union
party -- Dr. Dwight has been here & left us on Monday
on a tour through N. Hampt & Newport -- Dr. W. Safford
accompany him -- Richd. is to be Dr. D. Amann's
this year -- Edwards is to spend the winter with us next
Spring proposes to go to Leitkopf's & study law with Judge
Reeves -- Your mama has gone to Boston & will be
about the Merrillson for you -- Mrs. Russell proposes
to present you with two pounds with me shall find
by first safe conveyances to you when we meet you

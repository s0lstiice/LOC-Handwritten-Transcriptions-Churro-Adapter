in them such insolence, that it has entirely taken the place of justice, and they assert that power gives right,
that is, because they can insult every nation, on the ocean with impunity, therefore they have a right to do it, this
principle they maintain: and when I attempt to reason with them on the injustice of such a principle, they come
off by saying, "who can hinder us from doing as we please." I may answer, Americans! the despised American!
and that America may shew Great Britain, that at least she shall not tyrannize over her, is the sincere wish of my heart, as it ought to be, of every true American,
of those who can sacrifice the pride of party for the good of their country. -- when I hear men in Boston,

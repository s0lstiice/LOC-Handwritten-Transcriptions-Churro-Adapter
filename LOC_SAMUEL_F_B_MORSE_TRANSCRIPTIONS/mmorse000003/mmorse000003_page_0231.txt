London Sep. 14th. 1803
New Eng. Coffee House
My Dear Parents,
I have but a moment by this opportunity, which I have this instant heart of
I can just say I am well in excellent spirits. I wrote you at length not long
ago: have since received a letter from you of 25 June; you had not then received letters, which
you mention in your later letters having received to-morrow morning. I set for Bristol, shall
deliver my letter to Mr. Russell was so kind as to give me. All these are well. --
last night accounts arrived from Continent no decisive battle had been fought. Gen.
Moreau unfortunately has lost both his legs by a cannon shot before Dresden. The prospect of reconciliation between GB. & us.
will write again as soon as an opportunity offers. Good bye, in the greatest possible haste.
This is by Swedish ship Neptuneus wht sails from Liverpool, next day after to-morrow
96709 Love to every body
Yr very affectionately
Sam'l B. Moser

Bathurst Rise
2 Feb 1814
Dear Sir
Being now at Bathurst Rise
& accustomed to come daily only to the
City, I should be sorry to give you
the trouble of a journey to either of
those places -- Would it suit you
however to take your dinner & a bed
with us on Saturday next -- We
shall dine at five -- You perhaps
will not dislike walking back to
town from Clifton Church on Sunday
which is in your way Whither --
I expect to be in Suffolk St.
Charing Cross for an hour or two or
some part very distant day, where
you might meet with me -- or I am

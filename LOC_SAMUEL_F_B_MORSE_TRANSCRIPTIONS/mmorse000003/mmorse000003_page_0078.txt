home and by painting portraits in America, collect enough
to bring me here again, for it is important that I should
lose no time while I am here, and I am in such a stage of advancement,
that were I now to leave this country, it would injure me
materially ever after; I could not help smiling at an observation
in your letter, that when I acquired sufficient knowledge in
my art I must return home; Michael Angelo studied 70 years and did not get sufficient knowledge; Mr. West has
studied 60 and is still learning; there is no such thing as
being taught the art of painting; to learn it a man must devote
his whole life to it, and he learns by constant practice, and
observation, and that too, by steps, imperceptibly; the as it is with the hand of a clock which moves slow, and imperceptibly towards its
point of destination, and it is with still by steady perseverance he
gains the object of his study. at length and here let me observe

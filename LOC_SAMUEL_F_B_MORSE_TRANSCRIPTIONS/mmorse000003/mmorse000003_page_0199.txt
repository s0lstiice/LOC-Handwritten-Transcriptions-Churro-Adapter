Liverpool Sept 20th 1813
Rev. Doctor More
These letters were given me by
Finley in London when I had a Clip at this place &
sent to return with him to America in which
I have got her which has kept the letter here so long
the time he probably tells of is still in Liverpool
I could not take it but it will come the
present opportunity I send the medal by Mr. Plummer
in 2nd class letters I heard from Finley yesterday he
was that moment leaving London for Bristol & it
prevented him from writing by this opportunity
I am respectfully
Your very humble Servt
Elkher Goddard

Bristol June 3rd 1844:
Respected Sir,
Your favour of the 3rd I have duly received, and I cannot
feel too grateful for the immediate attention shown to mine of the 31st.
I wait with anxiety for issue of their application to the Admiralty.
I shall probably be in London by the 1st of July, and intend being
there during the exhibition at the Gallery. I expect to exhibit his
large picture this year & after his return from Bristol, however
I have received no letter from Dr. Cuthill since he left England, but he
has requested me to inform him on his departure to send him a passport, if it could be procured,
and in case he wished to return to England I shall be happy to confer with you on the
subject when I return to London.
With sincere respect to Mrs. Thornton &
family I remain Yours Very
Yours
Thos. H. B.

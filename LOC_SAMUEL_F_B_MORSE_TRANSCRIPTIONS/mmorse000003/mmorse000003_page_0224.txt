Friend Morse
Sir
I shall am I in this
duty hold it when I shall get away am
unable to tell I have sold my ship
from under me & am destitute of means
of consequence at present The particular
object however of this letter is to inform
you that a Swedish Ship called the
Neptune is preparing for America with
all dispatch by which I shall send
your box which arrived safe here in due
season as baggage of some of my friends
who will willingly take it I have a number
of friends going in her as she is going
direct to Boston it will be a good opportu-
nity for you to send more letters if you
will send them to me I will give you

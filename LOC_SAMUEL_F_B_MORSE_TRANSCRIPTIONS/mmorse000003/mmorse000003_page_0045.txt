The aspect of our national affairs is some-
what brightened by the news of the revoca-
tion of the Orders in Council. The voice
of the hatred of the war, weaker, louder,
louder, that in its favor, weaker, weaker.
Persistence in the war I think, will
certainly change the Administration.
At any rate, I trust it will be changed.
De Witt Clinton appears to be the most
prominent candidate for President, in
opposition to Van Madison.
We rejoice with you & sister, in their
addition to your family. May your chil-
dren be blessed of God, & constitute for
a goodly testimony.
Mama, & your sister, join me
in love to you sister, all the
branches of the family around you
Your affectionate brother
Snodgrass

Mama had a letter from Sister Silas
Burg yesterday, from Windsor -- She is well in
fine spirits, as her husband had just returned
from a three weeks fatigue, but thoroughly
recovered, & is to return to Montreat, on important business.

London. May 22d 1811.
My dear Sir,
With the permission of the Honourable the East India Company
I have the honour to acquaint you with the sentiments of the Board of Directors
and myself in regard to the conduct of the late Mr. John
and concerning me I should be obliged by informing the Board of Directors
that I am now in the possession of a letter from the late Mr. John
which I have received from the East India Company
and which I am now sending to the Board of Directors
and which I am sure will give satisfaction to the Board of Directors
and myself in regard to the conduct of the late Mr. John
And I am, Sir, your most obedient and most affable Servant
John THOMAS
Secretary of the East India Company

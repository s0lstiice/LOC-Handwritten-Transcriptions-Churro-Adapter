I have at last been able to purchase Mr. Pat-
man's 200000 Frye bill to the Patman estate.
I have hope that it will in the spring, sell for
something more than I gave for this bill,
so as that I may get part of my debt due
from that estate -- Dr. Patman, Twiste, and
Died last autumn.
I am not without hope that we shall have
peace in the spring -- if not, I expect we shall
have war in the interest of liberty, we have
in May felt little of its effects. It is a very
unpopular war -- and the Administration find
it extremely difficult to carry it on -- the
new Congress, lately elected, will have
a very large & powerful minority.
If not a majority, of the War -- The
war in Europe in its progress, is overflowed with
deep interest here, it affects greatly the
affairs of our own country.
I am obliged to close respectfully -- having
been introduced -- Remember us to Mr. S W
Alston -- all friends -- Your affectionate

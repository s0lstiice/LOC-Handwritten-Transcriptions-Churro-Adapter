Richard writes that they have pulled down the
Briar Meeting house at New Haven and will build a
handsome one on its ruins -- He saw the steeple fall in
few days since. It was a sublime spectacle -- Last
year I saw the steeple of Fair haven Meeting House
fall. Nothing could equal the grandeur of the scene --
The mighty crash -- It split into a thousand pieces
as soon as it touched the ground -- I write this
letter in great haste -- I intend soon writing

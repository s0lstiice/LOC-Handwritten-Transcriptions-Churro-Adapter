Charlottesville Half
past Seven O'Clock in
the Morning Tuesday.
Dear Morse, you
I would thank to make
a small parcel of my flannel
waistcoat & drawers immediately
on the receipt of this I send
them to Mr. Brown's counting
house Adam's Court, Royal Exchange
directed to me as I am at present
in a state of extreme distress.
Pray remember that unless they
are there before four O'Clock
his counting house will be shut up.
Send them by a porter I charge
it to me -- Write me a
letter also and tell me all
the news. I expect to finish
the picture by the end of the
week & shall then by with
you! Give my love to
Mr & Mrs Allston & all the rest
of my friends, Mr & Mrs

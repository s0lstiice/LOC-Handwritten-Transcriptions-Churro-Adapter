I should consider your establishment would not be so
expensive as in London. The Toyings store stands
to me over a very fashionable Toy Shop in a public situation
which resorted to. If you decide on coming you must
inform me by return of post that they may be engaged.
Cannas Sunderland can be had here as also colors
but it may be well to bring some of the latter with you.
What have you done with your Herules? can
you bring some of your specimens of painting with
you, such as the likeness of Leslie & the copy of a head
of Vanderbilt. I hope Leslie will soon join you & I
expect you will find some pictures worth your attention
here. I should not recommend you to take lodgings at
any Inn, on your arrival, your luggage will supply place
in the coach office, and inquire the way to my house
at St James Square, but if you will inform me by

Dear Son
The last letter we received from you was dated
January 27th 1813, that is since nearly six months ago others have
received letters from their friends in London long since, then even
down to April 8th many of the letters dated in March
mention you as well. We have not heard within you have
exhibited your pictures and what has been its reception we hope
favourable We are quite anxious to hear from you direct &
wish you to have letters constantly in such a state as that
you can give and send them when ever an opportunity offers
Mr Broomfield & Bremer would have the goodness to send them
at any time as he undoubtedly knows of frequent opportunities
one way is to fly from and from hence here in limited supply
you have now been gone two years have one more to stay I
hope you will improve this last year in such a manner
as to render it unnecessary to return again - Young

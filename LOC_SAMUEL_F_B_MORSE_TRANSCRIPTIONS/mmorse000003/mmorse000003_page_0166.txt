The last annual exhibition was very
splendid for us. Some very capital landscape
were produced - Many admirable portraits, and
one or two historical pictures. The most con-
spicuous paintings were Mr Peale's large picture
of the Roman Charity (on if you please the Greek
daughter for Murphy has it so) & Mr Sully's
lady of the lake; the former was in general
most admired, the reasons of this admiration
(as I think I will not dispute the size of the pic-
ture, the figures being a large a life; the great
force of shadows which brought out very finely
the female figure, & the contrast of a fine
reverend head with a beautiful young
female countenance. In addition to these
attractions the picture was well drawn &
very tolerably painted. The defects I shall not
notice, as you will probably see the picture when
you return home. Sully's picture had in my
view, more beauties & more faults (which
you will say indeed more serious) it is now
in my parlour where I hope you will enter
it. Peale & Sully are certainly excellent painters

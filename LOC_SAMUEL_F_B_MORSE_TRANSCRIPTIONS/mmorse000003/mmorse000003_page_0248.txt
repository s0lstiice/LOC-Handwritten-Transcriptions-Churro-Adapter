I will thank you to tell your
Landlady that I shall take possession
of the room on a week from next
Monday, at the terms of on which
you agreed (12/7p/week); the rent beginning
from that day to be paid weekly.
You had better I think engage
the colourman to do the room on
Monday so as to give it a week to dry.
The colour of course as you like.
Thursday, No. 13th. 1813
Yr. man
W. H. Weston

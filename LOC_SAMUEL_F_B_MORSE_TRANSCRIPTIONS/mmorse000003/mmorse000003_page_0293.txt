I am advised to wait a few months
before I purchase another Bill for you,
You may calculate, on the sum from me
of $3000 for the 3 years - then you will either
devise means for your own support or
turn -- we want to remain out with your
to that which will be best for you, in the opinion of Messr. West & Alston -- (to whom present any
letters from either have been
in answer to two to each of them --
Your last letter rec'd. was the 14 Sep. 1817
as you were setting off for Bristol -- We
wait now with impatience to hear from
you -- Your patroness Miss Russell
has been quite unwell -- but is better -- She
is our fear rather declining -- Well at
your Uncle Salisbury -- at Mr Rockwood
Mr Channing's (who is about to be married
& Miss Gittin) at Mr Evarts & Mr Beard
(who you know is married) -- Capt. Batt
will tell all the late news from this
town -- Regards to all my friends
Who ask after me -- I have no time to
read my letter -- We all, our in love
to you -- Write often to your ever aff. father & son

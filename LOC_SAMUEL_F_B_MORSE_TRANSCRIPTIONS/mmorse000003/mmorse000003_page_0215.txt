Write on this week that he might you could send us some maps or
drawings of Virginia if that are accurate if they do not cost too much
we will be gratified by their attention. - I Dexter thanking you for
your attention to her request as it respects South Philadelphia she does
not wish you to take too much pains & trouble to get it but she
knows no one but myself & I would if you should send her one of China,
If you should get acquainted with him inform him that a very
beautiful young lady in America requested that I don't tell him
her name & I have informed him that you should send on bring
out any of your pictures either for Miss Campbell, your Aunt Salis
bury, Dr Bartlett, or any other, do not get former for them, this
as they can be had much cheaper here. Your acquaintance
are all much pleased with your letter, they think you have
been a good child in affording us this proof of your affection,
and they feel improved by the information contained in it
and much delighted with your success in your profession well
long to see you but think you much better off when you are
at home in the present state of things your father will doubtless
not add except to tell you that all your young
friend both male & female request due to give their kind
gardens to you whenever I write you and in particular Miss Smith
Mr. Evart & Mrs Brown in their Aunt Salisbury & Mr. Brockwood & Mr.
Cleland also Mr. C is very kind in procuring Bills for you the family, I must
love to remain as even your affectionate brother
Ed Delmore

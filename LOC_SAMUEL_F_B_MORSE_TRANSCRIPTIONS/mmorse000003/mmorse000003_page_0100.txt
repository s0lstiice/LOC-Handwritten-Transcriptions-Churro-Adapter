15- Broadway Dec 15th 1811
Yesterday, Received this
morning. You have the Yankee, fine
style of guessing twice, if you guess wrong
the first time. Now when you guess
that I had an invitation to dine
with Mr. Stanton, I cannot tell
if I shall be with you by 3 o'
lock. If not with you by that
time you may be off as soon
as possible. be sure in good health
and spirits go lovingly dear
C. D. Cushing

In this statement you will perceive by calculating over after me
that I have underrated rather than overrated that to prints, books
and many other necessaries such as subscriptions to Circulating libraries
(which by the way is 3 guineas a week) I have set down nothing against
them, that in my calculation I have supposed the 2 years to be up
at this present moment and, that I have not a farthing reserved
for amusement, for any case of emergency or indeed for necessaries.

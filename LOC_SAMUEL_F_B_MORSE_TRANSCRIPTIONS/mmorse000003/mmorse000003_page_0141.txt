My dear Son
Mr Wilder with whom you are
acquainted spent the last even Saturday in
the harbor Batter for N.York to tomorrow morning
with a view to take passage for Engd. in a
Bartelis boat to sail from that place. He
hopes to send letters from us & probably
in prove the opprt. He will return in about
five months & hopes to bring out for us any
of your paintings books &c He will give
you due notice of his return & I hope you
will not fail to have prepared at many
of your pieces as to gratify your friends
We shall like the painting you have taken of
yourself or the one Justice has taken of you
the case of the King's Hercules. You, however,
will best judge what is best to send for yet
get your transp. The good friend Mrs Russell
who takes a very deep interest in your success
Mr Wilder will readily
befriend you in any way

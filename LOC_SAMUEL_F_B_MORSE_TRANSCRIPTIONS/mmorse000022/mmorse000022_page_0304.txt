Dear Sir
I painted the Barn Stone Mill, in Company with Mr Miller

and I suggested that size to Miller to which he did
not seem to object particularly -
You will incident that Miller's proposition is to complete
the ten buildings this season and next, sum of which
Mr. Walcott agrees to loan for ten years - This is so
far as fairness and excellent arrangement and his
proposition thus far fair - You will observe that if Walcott
becomes responsible for 10 years rent of these buildings, that he
can have no redemptions to prevail upon any other person
to build by which the value of the loan might be impaired.
But as to the remaining three proposed to be erected by
Mr. Miller, Mr. Walcott may or may not be inclined to
loan - It is therefore for you to judge whether it
would be best for you to base your acceptance of the
proposition upon the assumption that Walcott shall
take a loan for ten years for all the buildings, or to
accept the offer and run the hazard of renting the
three remaining to himself.

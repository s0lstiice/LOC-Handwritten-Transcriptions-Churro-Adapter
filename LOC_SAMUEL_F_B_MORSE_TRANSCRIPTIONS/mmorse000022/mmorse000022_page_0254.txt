Boston, April 12, 1847
Prof. S. F. B. Morse
Is Sir:
Allow me to introduce to you
the bearer, the Rev. Henry Warren. As he
visits Brooklyn, I have requested him to
call on you, & shall be happy, if you
can show him the correspondence I have
alluded to. Mr. Warren is an uncle of
mine, & you may place in his hands an
invitation to him.
I wish I had your watch
on the Boston May Telegraph -- The only
fault to which you referred in
your note. If you will have the kindness
to let Mr. Warren bring it on, I
will return it by 4 p.m.
I made for my own
letter as you precise, & have sent
a large no. of copies of my pamphlet
but to express. Thank you for being
so kind as you are.
I am truly
Your obt. Servt
E. Warren

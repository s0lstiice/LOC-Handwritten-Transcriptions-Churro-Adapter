I feel frustrated at my situation
after so long an application to the
business, in which I am sure I can
compete, in the routine of business with
all employed on the various lines
throughout the Country. An old
gentleman sent for me to visit him,
and tell him how I was situated, of
my means, hopes and expectations.
What a subject to speak of and talk to him!
He was much shocked and said if he
could do anything for me he would and
if influence was desired he would use
it for me as far as it was worth.
Under all these circumstances I
have thought best to apply to Mr
Johnson, the Postmaster General, a warm
friend of my fathers family and
see if he could not do something

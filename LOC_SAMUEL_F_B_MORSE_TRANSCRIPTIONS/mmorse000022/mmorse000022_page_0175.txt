Mr Vail has treated me kindly
giving me twenty dollars promont
which hardly pays my board
beside the little profits accruing from
the sale of his books.
If at your leisure you would
write me a few lines and tell them
add your promise to write, after seeing
Mr Kendall, which would make quite
a long letter, for which I would feel
quite pleased and gratified.
Please give my best regards
and wishes to my friend Wood.
Hoping you enjoy good health and
prosperity.
I remain in your hands.
Very respectfully
Louis H. Puttinger

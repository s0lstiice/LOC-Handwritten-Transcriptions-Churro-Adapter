Is Professor Mars
Me 12th 1847
Dx7 San
Bi Hartig de karakt-
terz ywzd bi D Komstok ts expres om venters
or serez or sentensez, Yw wil obzur a sermy
or twente pour sent. I har ned dgt de
magnetik telegraf wil bx de most efuernt
manz or advansen de triumph or de gret
prinzip reformecun
Abet Dix munds agw, I began
ts Dent and tak abot de wroewel ed
get de magnetik telegraf and de fonnetipik
alfabet me gev.
Enklazd iz D Alfabet or
D Komstok; ts get I har added de tele-
grafik alfabet determinend mathematikale
akordeny tz de best date widm wi knot-
ig. I am not praktikale uhwented
wid de movement or de telegrafik meeen;
and wid dependens pruzent de folway.
Admiten dat de karakturz ywzd in
de telegrafik alfabet ar med dÿ respects
de wz and rapidite op formecun in de
proporcion or 1, 2 and 4, de pant bany
tehn for ywente, de cartes lin for 2 and
de long lin for 4.
Des bany granted,
sexti tr (61/7) alfabetikal sem buly me la
formd bi subgehten xL or de kombinacionz
or de abur dot and linz, dat met a number
zekval tz de konsekwit numberz from
wun tz sein inklusive and separette
tehn, tz al de purmatieczung or get de
ar susceptibl, koutency nie man dan fin-
dotz in ein similitud inkludenz obsa
dez formd or similitud dots or linz.

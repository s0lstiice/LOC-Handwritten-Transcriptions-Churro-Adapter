Brooklyn Jan 30/47
Dear Sir
Having found no opening
for immediate employment by the
Telegraph companies, I have thought
of exhibiting the Telegraph, accompanied
by such familiar explanation of the
principles of electromagnetism or as
I may be able to give, for the remainder
of the winter, in such country
places as have not been visited by any
of the wooden machines about, but in
which curiosity as to the operation of this
wonderful thing has been excited.
Is there any objection on the part
of the proprietors of the patent
to this course and if not, would
you as a friend recommend it to me?
Very respectfully
S.D. Backus

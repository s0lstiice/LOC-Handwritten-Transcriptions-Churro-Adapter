If you conclude to comply with my request

If you comply you will please write
when Telegraph paper can be obtained
and the price.
John B. Dancy
Buffalo N.Y.
I have sent a copy of this letter to
Washington and Boston for I did not
know when you would, and I wished
to receive an answer immediately.
I have marked the letters M.D on
the manuf. provided they are forwarded
to you they will not make you any additional
expenses for postage
Jm. D.

To the Commissioner of Patents
The Petition of Sam. F.B. Morse of New York State
County & City Respectfully represents;
That he has made certain improvements in
Electric Telegraphs, and that he is now engaged in making
experiments for the purpose of perfecting the same, prepara-
ting to his applying for letters Patent therefor. He therefore
prays that the Subjoined description of his invention
may be filed as a caveat in the Confidential Archives
of the Patent Office agreeably to the provisions of the
Act of Congress in that case made and provided he
having paid Twenty Dollars into the Treasury of the
United States, and otherwise complied with the require-
ments of the said act.
Sam: F:B: Morse
Washington Dec. 24th 1846.
(see Preamble)
The nature of my invention consists, First, In the applica-
tion of electricity to the marking of Telegraphs. At
least one Telegraph may be
constructed by means of
electro-magnetic signs for
transmitting intelligence
to be applied to Telegraphs
Second, In the application of electricity to the printing of characters, whether the ordinary
letters of the Alphabet or other characters.

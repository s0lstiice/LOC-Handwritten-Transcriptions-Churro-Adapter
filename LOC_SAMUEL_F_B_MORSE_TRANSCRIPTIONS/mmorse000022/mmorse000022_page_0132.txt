Toronto Feb. 27. 1847
Dear Sir Having formerly had a pastoral
acquaintance with you and your family, I
take the liberty of addressing you for information
in regard to the Canada Telegraph &c.
And I desire that it may be between you
& I (as one Yankee says) - It is that -
Have Messrs Livingston & Wells the sole
right, to connect with the states, and
use your instruments in Canada?
I have suffered that they had, and that
unless they built the Montreal line, it
could not run in connexion with our
line to the states - unless the Montreal Co
paid them for it, and I should like to
hear from you on the subject.
I was employed by Messrs L & W. to start
this line in Canada - have been very successful
and am indebted to them for my present situation

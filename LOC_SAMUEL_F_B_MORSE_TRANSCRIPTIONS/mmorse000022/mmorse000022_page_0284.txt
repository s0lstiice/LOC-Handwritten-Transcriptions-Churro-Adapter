I think the weight of the Iron wire
is too much 330 lb to the mile, the strength
is not in proportion to one of 200 lb and
again if I am correctly informed as to the reason
of the Gale on the Cape wire it has
broken down & disturbed the Posts & Caps very
much in consequence of the wire being thrown
in proportion to them the posts & Caps.
Of all this however I am not fully
satisfied but desire to get all the information
on the subject possible from those who are
in a situation to give me the correct information
from practical knowledge.
Very Respectfully Your
SS Foster
I think of calling the Board of Directors together
before long for the purpose of submitting the whole
subject to them.

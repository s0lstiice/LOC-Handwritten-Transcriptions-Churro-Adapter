Washington May 4, 1847
My dear Sir
Mr. Vail informs me that Mr. Kendall is going
on to New York & that I can write you by him if I choose --
I think I will drop you a line or two -- to ask you if you
have seen the Year Book of Facts for 1847. The section
vander the head of Electric Telegraphs there is what I call
a big lie -- as it represents you having admitted that the
invention is due to the European Electricians. Perhaps I
do not understand it right. I will transcribe the passage.
"It is stated in a Phil: journal of June last that Prof. Morse
has added to his telegraph machinery, by which he can print
at least 120 characters per minute. At the same time he allows
this invention to be due European Electricians." To
I may not understand it but it seems to me a wilful represent
ation -- I presume you can find the book at Wiley & Putnam's
a somewher if you have it not already.

have a cage I suppose the next thing will be a bird key?

your object is to understand the person right who showed it to me, (it was
in Cedar St, and it was not yourself but an older gentleman who explained)
was to know if the same power was given to each of the magnets by the
same current of electricity, or whether the multiplication of magnets
did not diminish the power. He observed that there was a diminu-
tion. I tested what battery he used. It was a quantity battery, and
I reflected suggesting to him that an intensity battery would probably give
a better result, as I had found diminution of magnetic power from a
quantity battery done in a few feet of wire, but with an intensity battery had
operated my magnet though half a mile.
Being in Washington I have not the opportunity of procuring
for you the battery you request. If you write to Mr. Edward N.
Kent Chemist 116 John street New York he will undoubtedly
furnish you with it.

Baltimore February 12th 1847
To take the liberty of addressing you on account
of a friend in Bremen who wishes to obtain some
information in regard to the telegraph invented
by you and used in this country. not being
sufficiently acquainted with this subject, you
would oblige me by recommending such publication
which you deem most proper of giving the information
desired. Our friend being a practical man and
being interested in a short line from Bremen to
Bremenhaven wants to know the cost of the
apparatus which are generally used here and
where they can be purchased, he is also desirous
of having the laws or regulations by which the
companies are governed by the rates of charges.
You will please to excuse the liberty we
have taken to ask you for information, but
they advised us to loose the Telegraph office
here.
We remain Sir
your obdts Servts
H F Von Kaphk
Professer Morse
Washington D C
and Feb. 20 referred to Vail, large
book, and save cost of instruments
$80. each. enclosed Boston Regulations.

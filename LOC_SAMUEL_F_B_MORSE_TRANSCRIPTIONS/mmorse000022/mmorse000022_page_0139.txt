The House had a few days of painful & pleasantable interest
with Mr. Exchequer -- I wish it could have been prolonged
especially for Annie as many would have been grateful to
touch her kind attention & sympathy. Poor Annie seems to
feel desolate indeed -- I fear lest she may, without her
feelings around herself too much exhausted from the great duties
of life -- in which God is discriminating her -- next to
Lafayette there is no place where she could spend
some time less sadly than at Washington -- I trust that
Heaven will grant her strength to bear her trials & make
her to come forth brighter from his furnace of affliction.
I have just learned today that with their usual disavowal
inaction & justice! Congress have voted $60000 to have the
House filled by young Powell -- He enlisted all Ohio's
strength to vote up -- New York I understand
will vote for you -- I hope however you may yet yourself
resume the need & furnish the public the most striking
commentary on their utter disregard of justice -- by
placing somewhere the "Genius of the Republic" in such
colors that shall make them blush & hang their heads to
think themselves such men --

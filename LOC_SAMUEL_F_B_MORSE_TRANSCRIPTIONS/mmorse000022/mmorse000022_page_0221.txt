Forest Home
near Portland, Me,
March 24th. 1847.
Prof. Morse,
Olin - On my return here
last evening I found your letter of the
19th inst. and have hastened to trans-
mit a copy of the original draft of your
letter to the Atty. General, as in my own
hand writing and your interlinations, are
forward it herewith. I am confident it
is a precise copy of that handed to
Sir John Campbell.
This letter states the ground of
resignation, the same as stated by you to
me at the close of each interview you
had with him - viz - a previous publication
of your invention.
I think not what mode is adopted
in the English practice of preserving the decisions
made on applications for Patents - but your ap-
plication was made in the requisite form, and
I presume is still on the files of the proper depart-
ment there, and perhaps the Atty's General
decision endorsed on the back of it.
My meditation is that after this
letter was submitted, to the Atting Genl.
you expect to get full consideration and

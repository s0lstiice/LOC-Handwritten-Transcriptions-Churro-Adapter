I told him, still more that instead of Prof Morses

your first suspicions were excited in reference to

in conversation with me personally and through
the telegraph with me and other his language
and general tenor of his letter was meant
as to induce the belief that he was not what
he formerly was and professed to be our friend —
I did not spare him — and told him that if
he chose to pursue such an equivocal course
that I wish him to consider my confidence in
him at an end. That he might go and do
what he could to the injury of our common potent
interests all I wished was to know how we were
to consider each other hereafter. At times he
was brave — Then again cut down and at

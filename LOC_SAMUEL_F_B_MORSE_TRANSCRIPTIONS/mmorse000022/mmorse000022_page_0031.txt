New York City Jan 4, 1847
Prof. Morse Dr. Sir
I addressed a letter to you some
10 or 12 days since, and having received no answer
am led to conclude that perhaps you may not
have received it, or in the press of business, overlooked it. My object as then stated is to inquire
whether you will consent to the construction and
use of suitable apparatus to illustrate the
principles of the "Electric Telegraph" to be used
in a lecture. I there mentioned that I am
the son of Charles Davis of Danville Va a Judge
of the supreme court and gave for a reference
Mr. Aspham U.S. Senator from Vermont. As
all my arrangements are in suspense until
I hear from you, you will confer a favor by
replying at your earliest convenience,
Very Respectfully
Isaac H. Davis
Prof S.F.B. Morse
Washington City

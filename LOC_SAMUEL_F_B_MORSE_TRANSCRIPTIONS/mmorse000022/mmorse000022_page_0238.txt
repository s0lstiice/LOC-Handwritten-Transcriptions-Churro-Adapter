History of proceedings in England to procure a
Patent for my Telegraph.
In the Spring of 1838 I left the United
States and arrived in London in June, for the purpose
of obtaining letters Patent for my Electro-magnetic
Telegraph system which had been publicly exhibited in the
New York City University on the Spring, and before
the Congress of the United States at Washington, on the
Summer of the year 1837-1838
I had learned before leaving the United States
that Professor Wheatstone and Mr Cooke of London had
obtained letters Patent in England for a "magnetic needle
Telegraph", based on the name implies on the deflection
of the Needle. Their Telegraph at that time required
Six Conductors between the two points of intercommuni-
cation, for a single instrument at each of the two points.
Their mode of indicating signs was by deflecting
Three needles in various ways, so as to
point to the required letter upon a diamond shaped
dial plate. It was necessary that the signal should
be observed at the instant, or it was lost, and vanished
forever.
I applied for letters Patent for my mode of giving
intelligence at a distance by electricity, differing in
all respects from Messrs Wheatstone and Cooke's mode,
having nothing in common on the whole system,
but the use of Electricity on Metallic Conductors, for
which use no one could obtain an exclusive privilege,
since this had been used for nearly One hundred Years.
My system was based on the attractive power of the
Electro-Magnet, or magnetic power by Electricity, and
applied to impart permanent signs at a distance.

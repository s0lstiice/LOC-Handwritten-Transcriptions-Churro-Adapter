Now, under what pretence of justice was I denied
a Patent for this instrument? It was secured to me in
France but a few weeks after the rejection of my applica-
tion in England. No description of the receiving
magnet can be found in any book published in
England or America, or, so far as I have learned in
any other country up to this hour!
This is the statement of my case. The
refusal to grant me a Patent was at that time
very disastrous. It was, especially discouraging to
have made a long voyage across the Atlantic in
vain, incurring great expense and loss of time,
which in their consequences produced years of
delay on the prosecution of my enterprise.
Is it asking or expecting too much, that a
special Act of Parliament should be granted me
to protect me in rights so long and so wrongfully
withheld from me?
(Signed) Samuel F.B. Morse
New York April 2d 1847

New York March 18th 1849
Dear Sir, I enclose you two Certificates of Stock
No. 218 for 65 shares and No. 219 for 50 shares, let please
cancel and instead thereof issue new certificates of the following
character
103 to Samuel B. Morse
12 to Amos Kendall
enclosing the first to me, and the other to Amos
Kendall.
Respectfully
J. Obscuit
S.S.B. morse
P.S. I have seen Mr. Chester who is one of the best opera-
tors who has been employed on the Boston line. How would
he do for the Albany office, if Mr. Carter or Mr. Johnson
comes to New York. He is a competent man and
would do well.
I learn that Messrs L. & W. were interested in
setting Mr. Chester the place that Mr. Wood has obtained
and I therefore think they are perhaps not charyable
with any wrong doing to take away Mr. Wood from our
line.

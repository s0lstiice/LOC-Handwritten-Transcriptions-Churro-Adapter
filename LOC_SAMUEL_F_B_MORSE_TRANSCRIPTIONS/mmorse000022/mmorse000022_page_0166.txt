New Lisbon Ohio March 10.1847
Prop S. J. B. More
Will you give me liberty
to give lectures upon the Magnetic Telegraph.
Will you sell a resist wire to obtain one of the
Telegraphs. Will you give me a letter
recommending me to the people of the west.
As soon as I am informed of your
intent in order to succeed. I will send
you letters informing of my self. that you may
be able to judge of I am the one worthy of
you confidence. Inform me who in this
part of State I shall have letters from
I will get them and send to you.
We have had Sentiment in all the great west
giving lectures upon Electro Magnetism
professing to have found Telegraph. I think you
Telegraph has never been in the west
and taking much interest in this great invention
I take this way to ascertain how to proceed
to find favor with you in our time
soon and you will confer a favor.
With Respect
Prop S. J. B. More
S. F. Bushman

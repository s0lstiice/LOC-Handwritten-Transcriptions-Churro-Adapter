Washington March 10th 1847
From Me
Your is duly received and in
reply to your request have to say that the paper
you speak of I have not even ever seen, not
did I know of it existence. I have seen Mr
Biglow and he has it not. I have also asked
Mr Kendall, and he says no.
Mr Rogers has gone to Richmond to procure
or see if he can procure the right of way --
The wires are up to Alexandria -- Mr Biglow
talks of going himself to England.
Please send for the works you have asked from
England for me. Those mentioned in your letter.
In haste Your sincere friend
Alfred Bail
Mail just closing

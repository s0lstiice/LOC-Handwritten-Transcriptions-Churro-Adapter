Broaden 11th Jan 1847.
Dear Sir.
As I am preparing for publication
descriptions of many of the Electric Magnetic mach-
ines which I have arranged and constructed since
the spring of 1833, being the most important portion of my experiments, before me since I obtained my patents, and as I wish to make
some statements in regard to the Electric Telegraph, I intend upon you at this time, to learn of you the state of your first experiments
in applying Electric magnetism to Telegraphic
purposes. I am aware of your investigations on board
the Packet ship in 1832, and I recollect having a
conversation with you on the subject of using
galvanic electricity for the Telegraph, at 42 Stamford
Street, of Paris in the spring of 1831, but do not
recollect that you mentioned any experiments
that you had then made in galvanic or Electric
magnetism. Please forgive me with the state of
your first experiments in Electric magnetism for
Telegraphic purposes at your earliest convenience.
I am anxious to obtain a small battery of the
kind used for the Telegraph, consisting of two
porous cups and platinum plates. If you can
get them here and send them to me, with your
bill, I will remit you the money for the same
I cannot procure the materials in Liverpool and
I wish to obtain them soon, in order to complete
some certain experiments.
Respectfully yours
Thomas Tannish

NY. March 15, 1847
In reply to yours of the 10th inst. I will say that
E. N. Kent, Chemist 116 Johnst. N.Y. is the Agent for
dispensing of my Telegraphic apparatus to
Lecturers & others.
Whatever terms you make
with him will, of course, be satisfactory to me
Resp. Yrs Bt
S. E. B. Doolittle
J D Bushman
Keeps Listings Office.

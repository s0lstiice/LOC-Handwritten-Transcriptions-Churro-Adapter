This evening is a meeting of the invention men present
in this City, who meet for the purpose of deliberating upon
the best mode to accomplish the passage of the patent law bill.
I wrote Mr. Kendall a note to apprise him of the fact,
I wish he will attend.
I expect Mr. Ellsworth & King here very soon.
Please write me a few lines in regard to the Manchester off.
The article I wrote on Telegraphs has been already published
in a New York paper, and has gone to Europe.
Will you ever cheer me
your friend
Chas. F. Eliason

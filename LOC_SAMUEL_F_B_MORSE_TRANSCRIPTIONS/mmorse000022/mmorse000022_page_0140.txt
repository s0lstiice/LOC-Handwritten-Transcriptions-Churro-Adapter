Tying Hercules put together all but the big toe which
I shall leave till you come on as we cannot tell precisely
by its appearance. It is much a mist and I think you
need for it. I hope we shall see you here this spring
at all events come see us when you visit us.
We are going to give up the house to the Depes P.O.
& shall take a small house in about a month
boarding with them till then. But I must stop
If you think you can write to Mr. Kirk will you do
so soon I let me know what he says. We all unite in
a kind remembrance to you.
Affectionately Yours
Prof. S. F. B. Morse
Goodrich Smith

Boston Mar 8. 1847
Dr. Fin:
Although I strongly to you, I am unable
to write this letter, requesting a statement
from you of the claims to Jackson set
up to your deceiving of the Telegraph. If
convenient send me also a copy of the
correspondence between you & him on this
subject. As to who I am, if Mr Hamilton
or Mr Winthrop is still in Washington, I
write upon you to one of them.
I spent six weeks in N during
the first part of the Session & boarded
at the Exchange, often I often saw you
without making your acquaintance. Think
I know agent.
By obliging me in this matter, you
will undoubtedly do good service to the cause
of truth. These papers are not to be used
in any way to your detriment.
Respectfully
Your obt. Servt.
E. Warren
Prof. Morse,
Washington D.C.

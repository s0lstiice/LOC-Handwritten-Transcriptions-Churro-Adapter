owner ship, title and property of said line; and at the same
price per share as the stock of said line shall be furnished to
parties who shall subscribe and pay therefor in cash as
required in the progress of construction. Such certificates
of stock shall be issued and delivered on the completion
of the line aforesaid and not sooner. One half part of said
price per mile shall be paid by said Smith in current money
on the completion and delivery of each section of Fifty miles
ready for use as aforesaid - and the remainder of said price
per mile shall be paid in current money on the final
completion by said Lanforth of his Assigns, of the entire
line ready for use as aforesaid. And in case said Lanforth
shall be required to construct masts of forty feet and upwards
at any points on said line to render the same safe and
reliable, then said Smith agrees to pay said Lanforth therefor,
whatever shall be just and reasonable for the extra expense
upon the Common Telegraph Posts, which said masts, and the
extra labor in putting the same up shall cost the said Lanforth.
And in case any trees alive and standing on said line can
be advantageously used in lieu of Posts said Lanforth is
authorized so to use them, by obtaining a right thereof of
the proper owner of such trees, and without any deduction
of the price per mile aforesaid. And in case either party
shall fail to observe in good faith and fulfill his said ten-
dencies, he shall forfeit and pay to the other party all
damages and losses arising to him from and in consequence of
such failure and all expenses incurred by such injured

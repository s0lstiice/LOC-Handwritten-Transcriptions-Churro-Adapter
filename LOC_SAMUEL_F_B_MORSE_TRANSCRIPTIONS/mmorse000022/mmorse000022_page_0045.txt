New York January 12, 1849
Thursday
Dr Sir
I acknowledge your letter of
11 inst, but do not understand from it
that I am to engage counsel until Mr
Smith or you shall further direct. It will
be very agreeable to me to have either of those
gentlemen associated with me.
It ought to a source of great satisfac-
tion to you to have your invention
obtained a counterfeit. What an
acknowledgment it is and what a tribute
to its merits.
Yours sincerely
Dan Lord
I. F. B. Morrie En

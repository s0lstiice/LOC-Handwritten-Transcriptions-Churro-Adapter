Baltimore May 17, 1847.
Dear Sir:
I promised when I left
you in New York I would write
in regard to the interview I proposed
having with, & at the solicitation of
Mr Robinson. Before doing so I hope
you will not suppose, as Mr Kendall
has done heretofore, that I am aiding
others in driving the owners of your
Patent into terms; but view it as you
would have done before Mr Kendall was
your Agent, that is, fair and
candid statement of facts made by
a man of honour.
Mr Robinson is decided upon
the construction of a line of telegraph
on the Richle Rail Road, and also
its extension to Washington city, I
think he has interested other parties
in the matter, and I have no doubt
but he will be able to extend it

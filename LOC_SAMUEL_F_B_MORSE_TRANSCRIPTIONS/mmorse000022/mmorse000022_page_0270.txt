To A Vail
Washington D.C.
New York Apr 26th 1847
Dear Vail -
"The Best Telegraph yet"
By the following extract of a letter from a gentleman of
distinguished mechanical science in London to his friend
in America, rec'd by the Cambria, & dated April 3. 1847
we are gratified in learning that Martin's Electric Magazine
Telegraph, as yet stands unrivalled either in Europe or
America. As our was the first to use it proves to be
the best as yet invented. I am truly grieved at Rogers'
conduct, he must be conscious of doing great injustice,
for a man that has wronged another is sure to invent
some cause for his act, if there has been none given.

injurious act, by the false adhesion that "I had cast
"him overboard" - Why what does he mean? was I not
"overboard myself?" Does he in any one else dispossess

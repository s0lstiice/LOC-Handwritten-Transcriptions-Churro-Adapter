Theof Saml J. B. Morse
In Sir
I called yesterday at your
office in this City in order to show you an instrument
which I have constructed to indicate the magnetic strength
of the Baltimore Battery. Showing the amount of power by
degree from 1. c 100 or more. I should be pleased to have
an interview with you in regard to this matter, whenever you
return to this City. at the same time I should be pleased
to communicate to you by mail any information regarding
the same you may think fit to inquire for.
At all events, on your return to this City please inform
me of your arrival & I will do myself the pleasure of
calling upon you
Very Respectfully Yours
John Henry Emerson
61. Stephen Street
New York May 9. 1845

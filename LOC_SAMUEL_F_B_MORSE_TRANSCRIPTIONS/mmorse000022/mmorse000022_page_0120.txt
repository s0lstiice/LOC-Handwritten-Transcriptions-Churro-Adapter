August 20th 1841
My dear Sir
I have just heard from Mr Wells, he informs
me he will be in New York next week and requests a
delay until his arrival in settling the Senate Commis-
sions.
Mr Wells also requests you to convene with him
me in calling a meeting of the Board of Directors of the
NY Asstl Rail Telegraph Co on business of importance
the nature of this business important he does not state
to me.
Mr Wells also requests you to examine the
Conditions of the British Prince Contract between the
Patentees and Lamyshin Wells, and determine the
basis upon which the 10 per Cent is to be paid, whether
upon the aggregate cost, of the line, or upon the Amount
which we receive, and it seems to me that it is for their reason
why Mr Wells asks a delay until he shall arrive next
week
Very Truly Your M C
Cleirgton
Prof S F More
Present

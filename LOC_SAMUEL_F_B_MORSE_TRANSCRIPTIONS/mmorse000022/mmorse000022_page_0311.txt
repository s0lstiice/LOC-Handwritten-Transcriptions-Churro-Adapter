I am now occupied with no
situation or business. Is it possible
for me to get the charge of any
office in Paterson on the new Hudson
or Western line? With the severe
difficulty I have had on this line
during its bad management & defective
workings, I feel confident on a new
and constructed line, with the aid
of George, that I could manage
a station prompt & intelligently.
I shall regard it as a special
hinder, if any such situation
comes within your knowledge, or
one is within your influence, if it
could be rescued for me.
With high regards,
I remain your obedient servant
Everett

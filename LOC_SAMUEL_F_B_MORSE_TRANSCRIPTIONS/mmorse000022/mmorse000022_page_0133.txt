1839. Superintendent of the Toronto, Hamilton and
Niagara Line of Telegraph - This is my
reason for directing, at above, that this
correspondence should be between you & I only.
I have no other line in contemplation, and I
would like to know the facts in the case -
Another thing - I have had no
experience as yet, with the iron wire, but
the idea occurs to me that it will collect
more atmospheric electricity, in the summer,
than the smaller copper wire, consequently,
more care must be taken, to keep it out of
the instruments - Can we not fix something
to carry off the excess, if any? - Perhaps
I am troubling you too much, but any other
information you can give me, on this or
any other telegraph subject, will be most
thankfully received. - Please give my compliments
to Charley Finley - I should like
to hear from him too.
Yours Obt Grant
Sam Porter
U.S. S. B. Man
W. York City

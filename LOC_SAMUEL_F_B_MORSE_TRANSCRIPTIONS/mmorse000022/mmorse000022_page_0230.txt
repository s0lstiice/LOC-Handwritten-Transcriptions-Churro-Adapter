Utica 27 March 1847
Mr. Mom
Sir. I received yours of the 25th in
which stated that I was doing wrong making
instruments without consent of the Patents, I was
not aware that I had done wrong excepting the
Instrument I made for Deet Boynton but still I thought
by the Idea of Boynton that you gave him the
exclusive right to use your Instrument for the purpose
of Influencing companies to establish Telegraph lines
If that was the case I did not suppose you
could where he got his instrument the rest of
the instruments have been for the New-York and
Buffalo line & Livinston & Wells & 2 to go out of
the United States those I supposed you had no
control off said to be for that purpose I did not
think a man would misrepresent for he must
know that you could patent him establishing
a line in the United States, I have not intended to
do wrong in any case what ever I would like
to be authorized to make I sell your instruments
at some rate, I have been solicited to go to
Montreal & go in to business there but it is difficult
for me to do so if I was so disposed I would
be glad to hear from you soon & say
what I may or may not do I have enjoyed
to make 2 more instruments to go out of
united States
100448
Your Sincerely
S.W. Chubbuck

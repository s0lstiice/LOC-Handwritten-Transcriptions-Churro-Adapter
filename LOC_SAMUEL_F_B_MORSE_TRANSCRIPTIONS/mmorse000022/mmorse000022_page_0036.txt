Hanover Hotel St. Hy. January 12th 1845
Ye part 10. V.M.
Genl,
At this Late hour I find I am not
to send any Magnets to wake my Line
with from In time to Albany.
In a conversation
with Mr barnell he refuses to let me have
that proposal for me & for which I have formed
Wien myself I Sign a paper recognising
his right to the Battust over any
other
This I should do, & now request you to
provide me with the many objections to put
the Line in to operation all other parts of
it will be ready very soon;
I am Suspiçed at the
town Mr barnell has been prompt to speak
about this business, as I suppose all would be
right in his letting the Magnets ready, and
particulary as I bought the train & paid for
it with which they are made,
Hope to hear from
you about this business at your earliest convenience
will Sign his articles of you say it
in prompt

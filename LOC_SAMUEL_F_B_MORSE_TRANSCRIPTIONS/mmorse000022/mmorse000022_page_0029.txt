named wires, and such named wire shall be of the
same size and quality and fitness as the first named.
In testimony whereof said parties have hereunto
set their hands inter changeably and in duplicate, on
the day and year aforesaid at the city of New York.
In the presence of
L. L. Sadler
Francis O. J. Smith.
David Smith
C. Goodrich Smith
We whose names are undersigned, do declare the above
contract between George M. Danforth & Francis O. J. Smith
to be a true copy from the original contract signed and
witnessed as above.
Washington Jan 7 28. 1847.

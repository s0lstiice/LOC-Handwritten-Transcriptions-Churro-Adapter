I am on tip toe to see the books sent you by your
brother. Greenough has returned again to Washington.
He could not get ready to go by the last Steamer.
There are no letters in Post office for you —
I have seen a long interesting and effective account
of the N. O. meeting in favour of subscribing to the
telegraph. The amt to be subscribed is $120,000—
Please purchase of Grape for me the Machine for
Pitching clock wheels Price I think $10 or 12 dollars.
It is for putting two wheels in the machine getting
the right pitch so that the arbor holes may be
marked by it on the plate — It was on the shelf
near the wheel cutting engines when I saw it — If
you purchase it, keep it and use it till I need it.
I wish we could be near each other, so as to
communicate freely and experiment together —
Please say to Mr Wood of the N.Y. Albany Tel office
I have procured him a copy of last years Val off
report. This year is not out yet.

multiplication of magnets is not the cause, but if it should be so,
I think I have a remedy in some modification of the magnets.
I am glad that you have dismissed Cornell from his position as
it was certainly a remarkable instance of spirit, that
could have led him to acknowledge his confusions for the duties he had assumed. There is some hope of him.
To your question respecting the cost of iron wire, you
will get a better answer from New York than I can give.
It is not necessary to have it galvanized, from some
evidence given me, I think galvanizing the wire is likely
to be injurious to it. -- Could you send me a minute of the
expenses of the line from Sept. 7. 1846 to Jan. 1. 1847? --

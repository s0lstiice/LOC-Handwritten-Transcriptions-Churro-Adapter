New York Jan 17th 1847
Post S. F. B. More
to Mr. House's printing Telegraph was
duly received but was mislaid & escaped
my immediate attention in discharge
of my duties as associate editor of the
Evening Post. I visited the office of
Mr. House, examined his instrument
with considerable attention & witnessed
its performance. So far from the statement
made of the operation of the instrument
being exaggerated, I was at the time & am
now, from a subsequent inspection, convinced
that it is understated. So far from the statement

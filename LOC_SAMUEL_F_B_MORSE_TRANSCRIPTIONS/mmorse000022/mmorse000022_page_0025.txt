And the said Morse hereby assigns and
makes over to the said Kendall one half
of the stock to him issued and to be
used as aforesaid and agrees that the
production of this agreement or a copy
thereof, certified by any Judge, Notary
Public or Mayor of a City under his
seat of office, shall be considered by
all Trustees or other issuing stock in
Telegraph companies, as conclusive evi-
dence of this agreement, unless a breach of the
agreement shall have been notified to such Trustee.
It is further mutually agreed, that as
soon after the first day of January 1848
as may be convenient, a settlement of
the amount between the said Morse and
Kendall, as Principal and Agent shall
be made according to the principles of
the agreements of tenth March 1845 and 3rd
November 1846, for the purpose of ascertain-
ing what portion of the hundred thousand dol-
lars on account of the interest of said Morse
and of the eleven thousand one hundred and
on account of the interest of said Hale, both
two hundred dollars, subject to a commission of
ten per cent, may have been realized
and what portion thereof remain to be
realized hereafter.

Boston, Mar. 19. 1847.
Dear Sir:
This morning returned from Washington, where I hoped to see you respecting the subject of my former letter. And on my arrival here, I found your kind favor of the 13th arresting me. You will have already seen by an article of mine in the Boston Daily Advertiser sent you, which I asked the correspondence. At the time I wrote you, however, I supposed the article of it had been published. I regret would I could not have seen you, as I have many things to say, which I cannot do on paper.
But to be brief, Dr Jackson has set up claims to an alluvial discovery that of Dr W.F. G. Morton, having pledged himself not to do so, as you will see by my paper in the Advertiser.

Sir,
I take the liberty to state that yesterday, Mr. Wm. Robinson, his son, and
Mr. Chapin, sailed in the Northumberland packet ship for Liverpool.
They have taken with them, two of my telegraphic instruments, with
a view of testing their future with them in Europe. From several incidents related
to the elder Robinson, who has engaged in the Telegraph in his integrity, and the
property of the Telegraph in this country could not
resonate with him, not having confidence, that he would act with integrity,
in the subject, that Mr. Robinson goes to Europe with the instruments solely in his
own responsibility, and not at all with the sanction of the inventor. The two young men I have no reason to believe
are worthy young men particularly Mr. Chapin and they are well versed in the whole management of the Telegraph. Should the Government to
whom you are accredited be disposed to show any favor pecuniary or otherwise to the inventor, in consequence of what these persons may do in relation to the Telegraphic system, while I should hope that they may be

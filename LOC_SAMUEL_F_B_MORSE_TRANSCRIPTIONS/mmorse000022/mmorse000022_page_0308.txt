My May 1st 47
Dear Sir
I have received your letter from Montreal which requires a letter from the Patents like the one enclosed, I am Can Concur, and you please add your name there to. If you can add anything in reference to our position as Directors of the Company it is not material however the Company do good, as the Canada Stockholders depend much upon our being able to carry out their business and the Company's operations have been so long as we remain in the direction of which we will do our utmost to protect their business thus the State of this letter, or at small degree that has given us the preference in the Contract, in Canada I must leave its effect on the Contract for the Halifax office, which as I informed you we were negotiating for, without a prospect of profit, more to secure the small Compensation to the American Owners of the Patent than usual profit or gain to ourselves please return by bearer as soon as I can mail it
Very Truly
Cushing

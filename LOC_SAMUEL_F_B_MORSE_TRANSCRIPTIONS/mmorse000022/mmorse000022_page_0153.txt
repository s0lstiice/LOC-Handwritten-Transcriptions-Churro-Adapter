Washington March 8th 1847
Friend Morse
I regret not seeing you
left. I supposed on account of the storm you
would not leave that night. I hope you
got through without detention but doubt it.
Your note in reference to Gorg & Canada line
I have got and am pleased to learn that I have
somthing coming from that quarter.
I see on as rendered by Mr Kendall that
I am there credited "By 1/12 part of $150 received
by Prof Morse on account of Macon & Binghampton
line $12.50 - less 20 per cent".
Will you have the goodness to give me a statement of the whole amount that Co owed the
Patentees for the Patent right. The number of
miles constructed & the rate they were to pay us
per mile,
I desire to keep the account of my interest
in patent rights and would be much obliged to
you for it.
I rec'd the following letter from Wells & Co
this morning in answer to one I wrote a few days since
New York Mar 6 1848
Mered Vail Eng
Dear Sir Yours of the 3d inst
was received this morning and in answer thereto
I have to say, the whole amount of money paid
the Patentees on the Canada line is some $4500
of this amount between $2500 and $3000 has been
paid Prof Morse and with the exception of the
$300 paid you the balance has been received by
Mr Smith

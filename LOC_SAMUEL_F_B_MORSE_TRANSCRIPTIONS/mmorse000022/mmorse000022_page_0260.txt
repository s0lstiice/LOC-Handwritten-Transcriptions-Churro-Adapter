Louis is out of employment. Mr Kendall promised

But his mother seemed to express no thanks for
my the interest I had made in getting him the
Baltimore situation, but was exceedingly disappointed
and cryed much because of his going to Baltimore,
She seemed to think he was competent to take the super-
intendence of the water office and should have I --
I told her that Louis must not expect so responsible
a situation until older -- That a young man like
him could not command the respect necessary to
conduct such an office -- They expect me to work
merely for each and every one, but I tell them
now I am myself out of a place but hope soon
to have one but not enough to support me I do
not know how I shall get along -- I hope you
are well and should be glad to hear from you
occasionally.
Your friend as ever
Alfred Bail

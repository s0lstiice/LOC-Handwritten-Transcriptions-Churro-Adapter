New York, 10th April 1847.
S. I. B. Horse Eq-
Division.
The American Art Union proposes
to have a medal executed, for distribution as prizes among
the Subscribers to that Society, as one of a Committee
to whom this matter has been referred, I am desirous
to have your views about the most appropriate designs
for the same. As you are so well acquainted
with the objects of the Art Union, it will not be necessary
for me -- I presume, to enter more into detail,
to make you understand, my wishes.
The favor of an early reply will
oblige.
Your Ht Servt
John P. Ridgway

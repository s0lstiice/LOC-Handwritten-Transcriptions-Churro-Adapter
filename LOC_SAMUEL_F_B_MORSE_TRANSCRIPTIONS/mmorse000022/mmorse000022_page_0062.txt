(To Fayette, I think )
Dear Sir,
In answer to your question respecting the
cause of the diminution of power which your bay has
experienced in working the line, it formerly required but
70 CUPS and now requires 200 and yet apparently
with less power, I have done more data before I can form
a sound judgment. It may be owing to a deterioration of
the zincs or acids employed. I should wish to know the
condition of the zincs, and whether they have been much corroded,
whether this has produced so much corroded, the batteries
too long in use, for either case there would be a cause for the
difficulty you mention. Another cause may be, a want of
care in making perfect connections in the various Offices or in
some of them. – Still another cause may be the increase
in the number of receiving magnets on the line, the receiving
magnets are made of finer wire than the main conductors, and
although a certain number may not sensible on the current in
the main conductors, yet when this fine wire is multiplied beyond a certain
quantity the diminution of current may become quite
sensible, and require greater power of battery to overcome it.

I am sorry to learn that he has the prospect
of troubles at the very onset of his career
as Sec. He has bid defiance to a portion
of the Building Committee, who want to
spend money rather faster than is agreeable
to Prof H. who says he will not certify
to Contractors bills without which they are void.
The other portion of Building Committee wear
vengence and say they will make it too
hot for him in Washington. So far has
it gone that Prof Henry was not even
invited by Building Committee to be present
at the laying of the Corner Stone and came
on and witnessed it, in the capacity of a
"looper" as one of his friends terms it. He
has therefore the prospect of a long war
during which, I venture to say, electricity
will not be thought of.
I have nothing new to say. Fred's
line is working well and doing well.
I remain Your esteemed
friend & etc
Wfred Vail

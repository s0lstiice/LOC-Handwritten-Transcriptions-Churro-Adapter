Washington March 5th 1847
Friend more
I think I saw two receiving
magnets in your room which I think will
do for two instruments formerly belonging to
U.S. and which we intend to put up at
Alexandria & Georgetown, will you send them
to me, and charge them to our Stock to
between this and Petersburg Please send them
by express – I hope Mr Chester would take the
instruments promising to him as we want them
now for the southern line, If he does I
think he ought to give cash or a good note &
agreed upon They have cost more than that to
make them, if my time is worth anything –
The wire is now going up to Bedonckburg &
we have no Battery, I have given Kent orders
for our Battery firing but it is a chance if they
can get here for several weeks. Mr Kendall
says he sent me a draft for $250 which

St. B. (Main En)
& Lie)
I have got pretty much
out of patience about our steam win &
am now hoping to work & try what can be
done, if I cannot get the Second win to
work in her better takes it down & pull it
up to her speed.
When we commm'd working from
N.Y. to B. on the 9th of Sept. we had about 75.
cases the first week well and all turned
to go smooth when the win was not better.
Now we have about 200 cases & do not
work half the time, what is the matter
I call on you to give me your best opinion on
this subject. You having more interest than any
other one man in our Success I hope you
will not deem me impertinent in calling
your attention to this subject.
Last week I was in Albany several days and
found at times the 2 wins better. Not to be Counted
on one of them, this owned Steam
Time in a day & this both wins could
be worked.

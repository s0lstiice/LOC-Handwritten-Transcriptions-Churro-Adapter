received at my hands a copy without any
inscription in it of any kind and told me
it should be deposited provided there was room
for it. I informed it would accordingly have
been done yesterday I met Mr Mill as asked
him what disposition had been made of the
Book - when to my surprise he informed me
that Mr Leaton and others were very desirous
of having it deposited and contended that it
should, But that Prof Henry refused
to let it be put with the other articles to be
deposited and consequently it is thrown out -
I requested Mr Mill to state to Prof Henry
that I considered myself injured and stated
to him also that I had long since heard
of charges which Prof Henry had made &
had also written to him - To which he had
made no reply and yet was still making
charges against me which was beneath an
honorable man - I shall now take pains
to have an understanding with him - I
will vindicate myself let what will
happen - His course is insulting and
he chooses to consider me beneath his
notice, inasmuch as he pays no regard
to my letter requesting to know wherein I
have offended -

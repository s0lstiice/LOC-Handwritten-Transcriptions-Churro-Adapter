Baltimore, Oct. 20th.
Prof. Moore:
Dear Sir: I enclose you an open letter from
Mr Colt, which Mr Vail sent me to have
transmit to you, as I was leaving Philad.-
I am very anxious, as you see, to have the San-
cient Harings line opened before the Legis-
lature commences; and hence the anxiety to
have the instrument at Philad. While Mr
Vail is there, to direct the artisans whom we
have employed -- an excellent workman
I must to hear, by Mr Rogers telegraph,
that you will favor us with testimonials by
Monday's Expiration; and that we may depend on
the magnets by or before the middle of the week.
We will beg to see you any time at
all times, on the lines of correspondence.
Yours truly
H O'Kelly

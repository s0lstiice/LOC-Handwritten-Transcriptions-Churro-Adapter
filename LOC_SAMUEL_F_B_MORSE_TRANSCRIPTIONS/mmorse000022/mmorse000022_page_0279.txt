Washington April 28th 1847
Dear Mose Your favour came to hand
this evening and I will at once try to procure
an insertion of the article you sent me, in one
of the papers. I am now temporarily Chief
operator of this office, at the enormous salary
of $600. I never took any thing so reluctantly
in my life as this offer why it will by no
means support my family and there is no
choice about it I must seek another business
if this is to remain thus It requires no
reflection a consideration I must quit this
business which I very much regret to do
I wished to pursue it for life, but I had given
my whole soul to it I still hope I may
but you see how it is and I suppose cannot
be helped something better may come when
southern line is finished It is now operating
as far as Fredericksburg and Mr Goett is
gone to put it up to Richmond
Will you tell me where Gardner Collins
is He owes me $7160 for Pamphlets Books

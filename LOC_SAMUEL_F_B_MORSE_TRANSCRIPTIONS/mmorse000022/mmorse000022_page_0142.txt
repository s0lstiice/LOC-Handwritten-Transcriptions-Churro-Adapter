Washington March 2d 1847
Friend Thoms
I have arrived at last in good
condition at home again and find all well. yesterday
Mr Ellsworth called at the office and spent some little
time made many inquiries after you. Miss E is also
in the City. They go to New York this week. The new
instrument works beautifully and Mr K advises with
me that the three now in New York should be retained
for this line. Now I hardly know how to act. But
will leave it to you, I would rather Mr Chester
should pay the cash for them than take a note
& hence I could not except the note, unless it
has a good and substantial endorser. In reference
to that, please use your own discretion. The other
3rd instrument please retain in your office
for the present as I shall be obliged to take it
for the line south, and disappoint Mr Livingston
to whom I had promised it. If Mr Chester should
take those two will you have two more made
immediately. If he does not I will thank you
to send two to Washington by express.

of your patent papers at the patent office, they

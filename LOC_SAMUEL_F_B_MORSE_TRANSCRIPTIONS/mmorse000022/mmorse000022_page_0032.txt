Rochester January 7th 1847
Dear Sir:
At the instance of a friend I wrote
A letter of introduction to you a short time
since for Messrs. S.L.Selden & associates
in the matter of your valuable invention
of Telegraphic communications, gentlemen well
known to me of good reputation and high
standing in this community
I now take the liberty of addressing
one to you on my own account -- I
would enquire whether a field might not be
open for me as Superintendent or Agent
in some important rout in the construc-
tion of a Telegraphic Line?
Since our acquaintance at Concord N.H.
in 1816 -- and on I have been extensively en-
gaged as General Agent in building my
Brothers Col. S.A.Long's Patent Frame Bridges
from One upper Stillwater Penobscot River Maine
& at numerous points interlacing between
that and Natches Mississippi.
I have recently paid some little
attention to the principles of your invention
and from what I have seen I am desirous
of becoming further acquainted with it & the

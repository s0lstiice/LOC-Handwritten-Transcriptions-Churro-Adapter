Cafe - Telegraph Office N.Y. March 14 1861
Yours respectfully
Isabellon Ex
Dr Lin
I have this morning read by
mail wholly amongst facts and justifications to
myself a proposition for my leaving which I deem
it for my interest to accept & which is important
that I should answer by mail to Morrow,
I am therefore anxious to call your earliest
attention to the subject,
I should deeply regret to have any
hasty action on my part embarrass the business
of the N.Y. & A. C. R. Lin, I wish it understood
distinctly that it is not from any dissatisfaction or
desire to leave the Co. that I think of leaving as it
is financial considerations alone that have wrought
with me in this case, I only have time to add
that I have been well satisfied with my plan and
shall continue to feel a very deep interest for
the prosperity & success of our favored line,
I shall be ready & happy to correspond with
you at any time upon this best bearer for you
to secure to prevent leakage,
In great hearts yours very sincerely
O.S. Wood

Washington, May 14th 1847
at Mills. Warren's
My dear Sir,
Your Chief Engineer in Holland,
Conrad, asks me to furnish him, if possible, with
the signs used for communication by the
Electro Magnetic Telegraphs in the United States.
My personal acquaintance with you enables
me to make an appeal to your kindness in
favour of my countryman. Would you be so
kind to point out to me the best way I can
take to get what could satisfy to his affectionate
demands? Please accept in advance my
thanks to any answer to this question, which
I hope will not cause any great trouble to you.
With sentiments of respect,
S. F. P. More, Brig. Genl.
Washington.

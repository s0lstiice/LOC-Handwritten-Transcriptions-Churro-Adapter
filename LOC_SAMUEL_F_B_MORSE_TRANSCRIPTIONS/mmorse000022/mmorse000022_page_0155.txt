Washington March 8th 1847
Friend Moore Your Telegraphic despatch
was duly received last week. Mr Truesd has written
me a note desiring the balance due him for his
work on the last instrument -- which is thirty seven
dollars, will you have the goodness to pay him that
amt out of any money you may rec'd from Livingston
& will for me & send me his receipt in full --
I told Mr Livingston that I should empower you
to receive my portion and let this answer as such
and I also pledge myself that your receipt to Myers
$120 in this matter is valid.
Had the second draft sent to me by Mr R while I
was in N.Y. come to hand Prior to Stockell would have
both been paid in full. The loss of it must account for
the delay
I saw Ken Mr Smith of the Sat off this morning & he is
well -- Mr Kendall has submitted a plan of our getting
rid of the Boston & N.Y line (otherwise Bog Smith) It is
to take his stock in the Washington & New Orleans line
for ours on the Boston line -- what do you think of it?
I think we ought to have the advantage in this bargain

Washington May 12th 1847
Dear More
Your Telegraph despatch reached
here at 11h 40 AM and I hentaled some time
to send the Book since I have here not over
10 copies left while I presume Mr Kent has 15
or 20. Supposing however that he may have with
them I concluded to send you the half dozen by
express. I will send them to Adams & Co
This afternoon.
Mr Haley passed through here a few
days since and I had a brief talk with
him about getting Register for Southern Line
I proposed to him to give me their construction
principally in order to give me an opportunity
to spend the summer north with my family
who are going on in a few days. I should
like very much if the arrangement could
be made; -- But I cannot do it, unless per
mitted to make such instrument as I can
warrant and that will be perfect and also
at most prices as will pay me for the time
spent in superintending them
Mr Kendall is again back. The line
south works very well so far but is not
long enough to show a closer foot if it has
any which I have no doubt it has
In haste I remain yours now
and ever
Alfred Vail

To the Commissioners of Patents
The Petition of Sam. F.B. Morse of New York city, County
& State of New York
Respectfully represents
That he has made certain improvements in electric
telegraphs, and that he is now engaged in making experiments for
the purpose of perfecting the same, preparatory to his applying
for Letters Patent therefor. He therefore lays that the
subjoined description of his invention may be filed as a Case
in the confidential archives of the Patent Office,
agreeably to the provisions of the act of Congress in that
case made & provided he having paid Twenty dollars
to the Treasury of the U. States, and otherwise complied
with the requirements of the said act.
S.F.B.M.

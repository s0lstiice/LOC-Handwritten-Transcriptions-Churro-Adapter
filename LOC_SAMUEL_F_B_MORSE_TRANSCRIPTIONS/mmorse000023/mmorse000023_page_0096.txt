Renewed from May 1st 1850 to May 1st 1851
New York July 3d 1847.
Dear Sir
In case you succeed in getting up a bona fide Company
which will give ample security that a line of my Telegraph shall be
constructed in the State of Texas, within two years from the date hereof
I am willing to make the same terms with you in regard to connecting
paid line with the lines North and East of the Sabine river, as I
have made with Messrs. Livingston & Welles, in regard to connection
with Telegraph lines in Canada. That is to say. On the con-
dition of your paying to me 10 percent, (payable at the time, and
in proportion of the installments paid in by Subscribers,) on the
"aggregate cost" of Construction, (that is (cost to the Company,) we
will connect the lines on this side of the Sabine, with
Your line and with no other in Texas.
Respectfully Yours
Sam: F.B. Morey
A. Beebee, Esq.
7 that is to say I have influence in the
Company R. & E. of the Sabine river it shall be
excited to bring about this last stipulation. If during
the year circumstances should occur depriving me of this
influence, I cannot of course fulfil it.

My dear Sir,
Your favor of June 11. is received. From the
conversations I have had with several of the stock
holders both here and Phila. I do not think you
stand the slightest chance of the office of President,
for in Phila they have some candidates for that
elevation, Mr. Somerville is the only one I have
been mentioned to me and Mr. Fuller one of the two who
have been mentioned to me. I suspect your name, they neglect the Patents
and Mr. Kendall is my agent whom I have engaged
from the beginning for the purpose of preventing
all of my money from this collision of feeling
with the Patents
and the Company was not identical with the rest of the
Company, I have our rights which we shall maintain
at all hazards if we find any who are disposed to oppose them. In the divided state of
feeling in the Company, I choose not personally to
interfere in any of their arrangements. Mr.

winter sets in. I am getting my domestic establishment
settled; trying my domestics; my farmer &c. shall
make some changes. I do not look for perfection in
them, nor do I expect to be free from trials. These I
shall undoubtedly have, but I look for grace to sur-
tain me in bearing any that may be affronted to me,
as yet my comforts & enjoyments far transcend all
discomforts. I am more and more persuaded God in-
tends to bless us here.
I have roughly calculated my expenses, and
my means to meet them, and allowing largely for
the former, and all reasonable uncertainties for
the latter, see my way clear.
I have some clouds and mutterings of thunder
in the horizon, (the necessary attendants I suppose
of a lightning project,) which I trust will give
no more of storm than will suffice, under him
who directs the elements, to clear the air and
make a severe and calm sunset. There are
attempts at infringement by the abettors of that
House humbug; but Smith has been put on their
trail, and he and they will fight it out.

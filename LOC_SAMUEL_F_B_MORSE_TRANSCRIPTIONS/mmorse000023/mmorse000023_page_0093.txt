To Edw. Warren, Esq. New York July 3. 1849
Dear Sir, I thank you for the copies of Y. able pamphlet in Miller's & Dr.
Morton's claim to the Prison, of Letters. One thing is manifest, that if Jackson has declared
whatever he is, I doubt however, that he does not yet have in our consideration, the immense secret
the only charitable interpretation of which will enable him from the charge of ignorance to honesty.
you can perhaps assist me obtaining some
important information having having a bearing upon my
exposure of Dr. Jackson.
Mr. Eddy may be able to inform you whether Dr.
Jackson, ever showed, to him, or to any one else, so far as he knows
claiming to have given to him the first thought of the Tele
Electric Telegraph? and if so, what was it? and
how long ago did he show it? I never heard that he had
shown one, nor does he even make one.
You mention in one of your letters that it was
shown to Mr. Eddy, and that he advised D.J. to take it in his
rocket. I doubt if any one of Mr. Eddy's letters (as he saw the whole

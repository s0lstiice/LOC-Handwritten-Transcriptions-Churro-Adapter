[To F. Testa, chargé d'affaires of Holland]
[May 18 1847]
My dear Sir,
Your favor of the 14th last
forwarded from Washington has just
reached me. In reply I would say
that I have requested my friend W.
Vail at the Telegraph Office in Washing-
ton to hand print into select for you a copy of his larger
work on my Telegraph, which I beg
you to accept at my hands, in behalf
of your friend and Compati o n the
Chef Engineer Conrad. In that work
you will not only find my signs for com-
munication, but much other matter
which I trust will be interesting to
him.
With the highest respect
My Dear Sir
Y. C. Lewis
Sam. F. B. Morse

Utica May 21. 1857
Dear Sir
On the receipt of your first packet I last week I
communicated it to Mr. H. Miller who was unable to give
me an immediate answer on account of urgent business out
of town. I informed him that I would consent to the
answer being given in all this week.
In the meantime he has sent Mr. Willett a confirmation
that he shall await the 3 additional trains, that
Miller says yes Mr. H. but he has not heard the government
granting. Miller is now making his arrangements to
raise the money for the $5000 of stock.
In your answer you had better adhere to your proposition
viz: To sell $8000 stock for cash in 10 days after notice to
Miller intent to be added from date of first dividend on
stock at seven per cent, and with an agreement to sell
$8000 more on receiving a letter to the Houses of Congress
to be certified as clear of encumbrances, with Mr. Willett's agreement
to meet them for ten years from time of completion of
Mr. Miller's guarantee to secure effect. The size of the Lots
to be determined according to your former letter by me and
the location selected by me. The houses to be finished
in a workmanlike manner according to the specifications
of Mr. Willett's and stock to be transferred when the
whole houses are completely finished and occupied. Contents
at the rate of seven per cent to be charged on the $5000

Dear Sir.
Your favor of 4th inst is received
In accordance with your wishes, we have placed on
board the Barge Clinton in the hands of the clerk the
two works on Villa Architecture ordered by you
We hope that you may receive it safely
Any further orders with which you may favor
us shall be promptly attended to
Respectfully Your aff s Servts
Thos J P. Roof
James P. Roof
New York. Decr 6th 1847

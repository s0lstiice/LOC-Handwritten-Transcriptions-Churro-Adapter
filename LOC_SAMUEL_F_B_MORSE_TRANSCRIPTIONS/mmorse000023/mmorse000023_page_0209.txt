Troy Oct 30th 1847
Dear Sir
I have been in Canada the
most of the time since I saw
you, and have to start to night
to go again to Montreal, which
is the cause of my not paying
the second payment, the funds
are collected and in the hands
of the collectors but I have not
had time to get it or attend to
it. I shall be back in a few
days and will then attend
to it, I am having a little
trouble in Canada, and the Montreal Co. think to build a parallel
line with Houses instruments
but I think I shall get along
I will explain the trouble when
I see you.
Very Respectfully
E Cornel
Prof O. F. Morse

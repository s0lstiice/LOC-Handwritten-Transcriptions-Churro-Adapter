to whom it may concern
This is to certify that the bearer
Joslyn Hutchinson Esq. has the full
approval of myself in the measures
he may take to extend the advantages of
my Telegraphic system in the Island
of Cuba, and I commend him most
cordiality to the consideration of the
authorities of the Island, asking for
him all confidence, and every facility
in carrying out his plans for that purpose.
Sam P B Morse, LL.D.
and Inventor of the
Electric Magnetic Telegraph
System.
New York Dec. 1st 1847

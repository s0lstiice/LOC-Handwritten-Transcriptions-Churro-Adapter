Dear Friend
New York Dec 14 -1847
I have been waiting Your arrival
in this City for some time to inquire if You
or Your sons would wish to take a boy and
make a farmer of him. fearing I should not
see You or have a personal interview before my
return to Vermont I thought I would drop
a few lines to inform You I have a son aged
16 Years who has a great inclination to be a
farmer & would wish a good situation
until he arrives at the age of 21 I let him
go in a duck & bagging Factory in N Jersey some
2 months ago to learn the trade but on visiting
the Factory last week I found he was not
learning anything as the establishment was not
in operation more than half of the time He has
the name there of being an honest industrious
& steady boy & I think with proper care would
make a smart man and under the control of
Prof Morse & sons would make a good
farmer, if You would wish to take him please
write a few lines by mail or direct to me at the
Telegraph Office, I expect Mr Cornell in the City
tomorrow, the wire for his line has all gone
up the River. Please except this from Your
100653 Friend J. M. Pinkham
Prof J. F. B. Morse

Washington June 30th 1847
Dear Morse yours by Mr K received
dont know directed package. I had to pay
50 cts that is rather silver. I am now getting
a new edition of the work published & you can
have any number at 17 cts each without your
profit if you choose not bound in boards.
The old edition has expired. I cannot get a
word from Mr Top who owns one $30. for books
If you can get the money you can keep it. These
lopes of it is a loss comes very heavy upon me
Cotton owns me over 75 dollts. The oil came
safe to hand. Mr Rogers of Balt wrote me
a letter after the receipt of yours to him stating
your good will in the matter of his being president
and requesting my aid I have not, shall not
ans his letter to me. He wants modesty and
honesty I have no confidence in him. I have
known too much & hear too much of him to
ever put him where he can attack and defeat
the patentee interest has he has tried to do &
as I understand is trying to do now He has
not lived long enough since his transaction
last winter I must say Mr K please
stand back a little till you prove yourself I
hope Mr Kendall will be selected or B B French
not Mr Hart I must tell you I do confidently
believe that some 15 thousand dollts must be
spent on this line to N.Y before it will be
depended upon. It is a temporary affair
from beginning to the end Yours truly
Alfred Bail

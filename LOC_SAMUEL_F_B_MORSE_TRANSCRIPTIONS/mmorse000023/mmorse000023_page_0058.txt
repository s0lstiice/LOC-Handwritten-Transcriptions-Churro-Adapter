As for the numerous "Richmonds in the field"
they will find it plain as good a right to
wear the "Red Rose" as any of them, and that
I am as able to defend it.
You say when "I suggested your name
they rejected the idea as preposterous", this of
course might be expected from those who have
their own ends to accomplish, I presume the
parties who are against the Patents are
the persons who think it so preposterous in
me to endeavour to elevate myself to the
Presidency. I am convinced of one thing, that
it is more difficult to obtain a preposterous
prominent position, than it is to fill it.
I now occupy a prominent position
before the public and the government, as any
of my opponents, and having gained it
solely by my own perseverance and indi-
stry, it is not likely that I will yield
it to mortal man. I know this much,

Washington June 23d 1847
Dear Morse
I have obtained this
information in regard to the missing
package. It is at Adams & Co office
and they say was there when you
called for it. We have no news
of interest except that the posts are
up to within 14 miles of Richmond
& the wires will all be in in about
15 day it is thought -- Mr Rogers
is out in the papers as a candidate
for the Presidency He has written me
a letter requesting my aid -- He says
you express much friendship for him
& speaks as though you would vote
for him -- We shall see what we shall
see -- Mr Piessefer is here. called to see
him last night & presented him with
letter to George. He desires to visit the
iron works in that vicinity -- I am
now publishing the rules of telegraph
charges. Can you send that of the
100555

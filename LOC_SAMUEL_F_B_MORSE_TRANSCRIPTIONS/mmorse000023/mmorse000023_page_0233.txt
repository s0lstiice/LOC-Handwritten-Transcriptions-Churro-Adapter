New York Dec. 29th
Prof. S. B. More
Defus, I called at
this office & found you were not
in the city so, I wrote agreeable
to a request made by my brother
& G. I in a letter yesterday
asking of you the favor to
write immediately to Senators
Cas, Dix, Bruce, any others you
may know asking their votes
& influence in favor of our father
David Smith &c. You can say
you understand he is a candidate
for Chaplin to Senate he is the
son of an old Revolutionary officer
twas himself in the war for a
short time the Congregational
Minister of the town. The Friends have
desired this of Father to be would
like it. I know you will be glad
to do what you can. What is done
must be done done Judge
Smith

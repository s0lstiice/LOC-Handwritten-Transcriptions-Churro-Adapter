I have had such experience of the goodness of God, of his faithful-
ness to his promise, that it appears at times that I could

without the Holy Spirit, I should depend on the next feeblest
shadow of adversity. – I rejoice that you acknowledge the
same hand in pointing you your great discovery. I have ventured
to say to some friends that you would eventually bring out
a discovery as valuable, if not more so, as the Telegraph,
when they have spoken of mine as the wonder of the age;
but it is rare that two if a family should be disfavored as to
benefit instruments of bringing before the world, such im-
portant revolutionizing inventions, and yet why should
we limit the goodness of God, and his mercy in Christ?
This is the honor, to him belongs all the glory. I long to
have you home, I think we could be of mutual help
in many other things yet to be brought out from your
own and also from my brain, to benefit the world.
God has given us competence, perhaps wealth is in

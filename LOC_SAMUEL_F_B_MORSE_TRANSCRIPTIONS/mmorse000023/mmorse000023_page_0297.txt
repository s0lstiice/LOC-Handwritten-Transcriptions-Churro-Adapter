New York Jan 15th 1855
Dear Sir-
The Telegraphic Map is done - I am on the point of sending it to the United States Office when I received yours of the 12th inst.
The excessive cold weather at the commencement of the week prevented me from finishing it earlier, as I thought the risk of taking it through some of the operations, with the temperature 20 degrees below freezing, would be greater than them the delay would be injurious to you.
On Wednesday I will purchase some paper, and employ Mr. Almond who has just printed the Missionary Octavo, to strike off the 200 impressions which you have directed.
The proofs have yet been taken, I will send the first one I get by mail to you.
Yours with great regard
Prof. S.F.B. Morse
W. A. Munson

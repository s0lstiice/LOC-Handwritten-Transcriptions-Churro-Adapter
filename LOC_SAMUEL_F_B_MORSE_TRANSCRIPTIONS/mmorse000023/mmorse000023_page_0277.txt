Andover Jan 4th 1848
Dear Sir
It is with much pleasure, to me
times, not a little proud, that I often hear your
invention of the Phonetic Telegraph spoken of with
admiration — I assure & trust that a son of
such excellent friends as your father & mother, were
to us, should thus succeed in inventing the most
important thing of the age, as it is sometimes said.
It will have a vast influence on our world
& I doubt not, under the guidance of our Redeemer,
aid in advancing his Kingdom, and hastening on
the Millennial day.
I see in one of the papers of the day
that you restrict the use of it on the Sabbath,
except one hour in the morning. The dear Sir
cannot you, for the glory of God & for the good
of our own sacred world, entirely prohibit the
use of it on that sacred day?
What man on the
face of the whole earth could confer so great a favour
on the church, and indeed on the whole world
as you are now allowed, thus to stand about
testify to the wisdom and mercy of God in

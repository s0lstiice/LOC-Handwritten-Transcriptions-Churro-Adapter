28 Baver St
Albany Dec 1 1847
Dear Sir
You have written to the Patent Office
This morning. When in New York, I made an
arrangement with Mr B Chilis a celebrated opinion
No 64 John Street to execute it. If you could
have the daguerotype left with him, I have full
confidence in his skill. The cost of the laying
on wood will be about $16, and the printing
of the requisite number of copies, including the
fine paper, about $20 more. As I said in
a former letter, I will agree to furnish you with
100 copies of the opinion for $5, you also paying
$10 towards the engraving itself, being $15 total,
as you shall, My opinion here on so
very busy, that I should prefer you giving it
to Mr Chilis. If not convenient, I will
have it done here. If I have the daguerotype
a week from home, it will do.
Very respectfully
S J Bellows
W Hunts

My dear Sir.
I have packed my Campbell the first and
have sent it by boat to Albany, addressed to you and
to be delivered to you or order at C. V. Clark's No. 96
vier Albany -- It will probably reach Albany in four
or five days -- I have paid the freight here -- so that
you will receive it at Albany free of Charge -- I would
have sent to Poughkeepsie but I was fearful that
it might receive some injury in transshipment at Albany
rudest, some one was there to attend to it --
I spoke to Miller today about the small
note of $136 -- due this month -- He will pay
it in a few days, when I will deposit it to
your credit at the Central Bank.
I presume that Stone is making you some
trouble -- I hope that you may have it in your
palm to overcome it -- In an invitation to great as
yours, it is not singular that there should be those
who will welcome to rob you of its advantages --
I have warned in time of the papers that O'Rally
and Stone contemplate establishing a line from
Brygalo to New York -- Is this serious or only
an attempt to intimidate --
Yours Truly
Thos R Walker
Oct 19, 1837

Copy to Mr. Braker
Virginia
In case you get up a bond fife Company which will
give ample security that a line of telegraph can be
built to Conestoga within two years from the date
of this letter I am willing to make the above terms with you in
respect to connecting said line with the lines already built and part of the
Virginia river, as I have made with Messrs. Samuel J. Stillwell
and Joseph C. Cannon of the Canadian Railroad & Company
in regard to connecting said line in Canada with the
young people of Western Virginia in connection with the
construction of laying to me as President on the approaches of
1860 the first telegraph line between the City of Richmond and the
Peninsula (that is the City of the Company) we will connect the
Virginia line and with no other

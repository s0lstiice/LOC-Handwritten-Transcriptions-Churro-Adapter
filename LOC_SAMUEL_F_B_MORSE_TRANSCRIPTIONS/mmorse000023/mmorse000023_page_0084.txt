Boston June 26, 1847
Prof. S. F. B. Morse,
Dr. Fin:
Your favor of the 23d was
duly received. I reply that I am quite as well
pleased at the disposition you have determined
to make of the correspondence between you and
S. F. Jackson. This will suit me just as
well. Indeed, I had rather, both on your
account & my own, that it should
be in the light in France. I would express the hope, however, that but little
time will be lost in having the whole
matter made public.
Having you seen the Detroit
Daily Advertiser of May 18th? In that, our
Burlingame -- a six alphabetic aes, who had
"rather be damned before a Duke
than saved in humble company,"
has written a long article, claiming the
Napulie Telegraph & The Telegraph for Dr
Jackson. I have not seen it, but have
written for a copy. Mr Eddy has handed it &
spoke of it to me. The object of this article is to effect something for Dr Fin.
The writer, Geo. Psott 104 Nassau St N.Y.
is agent & I hope he can give you a copy.

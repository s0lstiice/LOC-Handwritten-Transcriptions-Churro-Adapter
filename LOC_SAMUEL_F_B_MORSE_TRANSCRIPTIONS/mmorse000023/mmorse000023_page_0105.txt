Washington D.C.
July 12th 1847
My Dear Sir
Included I send you the
Contract signed signed by all the parties in this
City -- It is in Quadruplicate
After signing I procuring the signature
of Mr Smith -- Will you please to send me
two copies -- for myself & Messrs. K & E?
I should have been glad, had
a share in the profits from the "Intercast"
fallen to my lot -- I shall never the less
enjoy sincerely in your success in that
Quarter without me
Very Respectfully
S. Bigelow

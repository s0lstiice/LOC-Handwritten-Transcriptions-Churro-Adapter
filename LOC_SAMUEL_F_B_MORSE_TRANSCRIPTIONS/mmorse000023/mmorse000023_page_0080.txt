June 23, 1847-
Edw Warren, Esq
Dear Sir
Your favor respecting the letter
of D. Jackson's Correspondence, was handed me yesterday
by Mr. Peltzbuy to whom I read the whole. He will
give you his own impressions;
I decline having the Correspondence copied for
you at present only because I am preparing an
answer to a Travel Review which I have just seen in
which great use is made of Jackson's claim from
the letter which he had the impudence to send in
1839 to Mr. Elie de Beaumont & published in
the Comptes Rendus of the Academy of Sciences
of that year. In my answer (in compliance
with my promise personally made to M. Arago in
1845) I shall send him the whole correspondence
This is defence on my part, & not attack. When
I send you the correspondence for publication
it would assume the shape of attack on
my part which I shall avoid.
Your note in your last edition of the
"Letter" is unexceptionable: it states the truth.
Very Respectfully
Sam B B. Morse

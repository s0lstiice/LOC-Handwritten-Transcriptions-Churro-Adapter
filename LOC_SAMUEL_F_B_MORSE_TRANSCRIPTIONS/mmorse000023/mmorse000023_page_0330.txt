Louisville Ky Jan 27th 1846
to Prof Morse
Dear Sir
Your paper of the 21st was received yesterday. I was sorry that you allowed
your feelings to be so much aroused in the case of
contemplated difficulties between air hands &
those of O'Meilly. They held out the idea that they
that we would not pass them and we were
determined to do it. I had them notified that
we were prepared to meet them under any
circumstances. We were prepared to have a real
"hug", but when our hands overtook them they
only "gelled" a little and nine followed and
for 15 miles they were held by tile and when a
man finished his hole he ran with all his might
to get ahead, but finally on the 24th we passed
them about 80 miles from here and now we are
about 25 miles ahead of them, without the loss of
a drop of blood, and we shall be able to beat them
to Nashville if we can get the wire in hand, which
is doubtful. In putting the posts in the city yesterday
afternoon we had the misfortune to lose one
of our best workmen. The pole fell on him and
killed him instantly. We shall have the link
from Louisville to Louisville completed on 8th today
Mr Kendall is in transport at this time and I doubt

Atlanta, Sept. 18. 47.
Sir: In addition to the notices times here
before given you & your associates in connection
of one of the Telegraphic Systems patented by the
United States Government, I now again notify you
that I am perfectly with the lines of the "Atlanta
to Atlanta Telegraph", and am pledged at any time, as my associates & myself have
always been, to assign to you the shares in stock
or other interest required by your contract with
me of the 13th June, 1845 – Whenever you & your
associates Patents, or Whoever now controls
your Patent, shall petition that contract on
your part, as far as we have yet invested
hearts constituted according to your particular
plan of telegraphy.

Operation
While the Hammer I is separated from the Anvil
H, no current can proceed from the battery. But
the moment I, and H, are in contact the current of electricity takes the direction of the
arrow and passes through the chemically
prepared material at A. decomposing the salt
with which it is prepared, and making a
mark. Thus the characters of my conventional
Alphabet and other characters are at pleasure
made upon the prepared material.

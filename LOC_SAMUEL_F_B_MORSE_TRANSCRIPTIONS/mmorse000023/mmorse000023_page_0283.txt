Frankfort Ky Jan 9th 1861
To Prof. S.F.B. Morse
Dear Sir
I write to inform you that
Mr Kendall is now in Nashville and will return
again in a few days. Your letter awaits his return.
Your letter to the Herald will appear in one of our
daily papers tomorrow. It tells more than we in
this locality. We are going ahead with the line
to New Orleans. I have 25 hands on the road to
Nashville and will put on more next week. I have
10 on the road to Frankfurt, and my associate has
gangs at other parts. O'Kelly has 15 hands on the
Nashville part and I confidently expect a few
fight. My men are well armed and I think they
can do their duty. I shall be with them when the
parties get together and if any thing does occur, the
use of experts best will be appreciated by me. This
is to be lamented but if it comes we shall not back
out. I wrote a letter to you several weeks ago
relative to telegraphs in the great Manomouth levee, Please answer
me in detail, and tell me if you could not make
it convenient to be present at the levee when it
is carried in. Do say yes if you can it will be
about August
Yours as
S.F.S. Hartfurd

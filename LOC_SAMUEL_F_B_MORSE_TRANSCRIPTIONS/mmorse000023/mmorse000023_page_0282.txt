Copy Wash. Jan. 8th 1848
My dear Sir,
Yr. of 5 inst. from Boy & directed to me
at P. keeping has just reached me here, and I lose no time
in apprising you of my willingness to have the payment of
the balance due by the Boy & Montreal junction Telegraph
made easy to you by a postponement of it under the Contract
for the present. And if a definite time is necessary to be deter-
mined we can do it at a future day.
You will, of course, be willing to allow the usual
interest.
With every success
Y. O. bust
Sand. S. B. Mason
Expa Cornell Eq
Troy.

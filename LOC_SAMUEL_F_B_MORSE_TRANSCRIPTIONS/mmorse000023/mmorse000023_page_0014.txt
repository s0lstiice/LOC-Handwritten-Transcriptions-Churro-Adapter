7.R.Walker, Esq
New York May 22, 1847
Dear Sir
Yr. rec.
I have no objections to the arrangement to sell Mr Miller
$5000. stock for cash in 10 days notice, interest to be
added from date of first dividend on stock at
7 pCt. and I will agree to sell $5000. more on re-
ceiving a title to the houses & lot to be certified as
clear of encumbrances with Mr Walcott agreement
to rent them for ten years from time of completion a
with Mr Millers guarantee to same effect. The size
of the lots to be determined by you according to my former
better and you may select the location.
The houses must be finished in a substantial
workmanlike manner according to the specifications
of Mr Walcott and the stock shall be transferred
when the houses are completely finished and conveyed,
interest at the rate of 7 pCt. to be charged on the $5000
until contract is completed which must be done by the
1st of May 1848. or in the event of an omission to
fulfill in all particulars I am not to be bound
to dispose of the stock at the par value and the
agreement so far as the real estate is concerned
must be void.
I shall with these observations leave the matter to
your discretion. The stock is in demand &c. —

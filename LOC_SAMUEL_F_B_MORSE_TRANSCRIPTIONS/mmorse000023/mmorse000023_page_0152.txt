Telegraph Station
August 26/44
Prof. S.F.B. Mon
Dr Lin
I hope you will find it convenient to be at once
meeting on the 6th of Sept. and in the
morning as this is important business
to be attended to by the old Board
before the election.
I am fearful our Friends
L.D.W. are not well placed with the
Board if our business, as I understand they have told out their stock;
The report is that they are
going on with Tombesys Elbe Spattut
to construct other lines, do you
know any thing about that part of
the business.
I always expect you to be
very plain & frank with me on these
subjects.
Very respectfully your
W. Eaton

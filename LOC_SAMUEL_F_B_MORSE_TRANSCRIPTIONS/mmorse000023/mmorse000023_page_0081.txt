Copy of June 23. 1847. to Edward Warren
New York June 28th 1847.
Dear Sir,
Your favor of the 26th inst. is received.
If a publication of the character you mention
has indeed been made by a friend of D. Jackson
a different complexion is put upon the whole
matter. I have called on Mr. Batt but cannot
find that number of Detroit Advertiser. If you
can send it me I shall at once proceed to expose
the whole correspondence since I am thus put
on my defence. And if I don't settle the matter
as it regards the Telegraph, it will be because
there is no influence in truth. Is it possible
that he courts perpetual shame? -
In host but respectfully
Yb son
Sam D. P. Morse
Edward Warren, Esq.

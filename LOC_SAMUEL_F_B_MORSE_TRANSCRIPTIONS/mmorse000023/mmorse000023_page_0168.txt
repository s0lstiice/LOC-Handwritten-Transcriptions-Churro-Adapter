Po'keepsie Sept.r 12th 1847
Dear Sidney,
A rainy day confines me to the house
this afternoon from Church, having been this morning.
So I improve the occasion I quiet to write you
from my new home, and home it seems truly as
far as we are permitted to call any place this side
heaven home. From the beginning of my search for
a place, till now, I have felt God directed; indeed,
if I had any misgivings that I had not the light of
his Countenance to direct and sustain, I should
not be happy with millions at my control. The
farm gains upon my affections at every day's residence,
I am now within 24 or 5 hours of New York; shall be
within 2 1/2 hours when the R.R. is completed. I am
within 15 minutes of the village. Am so far from
the road as to be away from Stragglers and dust
and yet conveniently near for all necessary pur-
poses. – The house has no pretensions to taste, but
is substantially built, and will answer well for a
point d'appui, and with the grounds can be prac-
tically improved as means and inclination dictate.
I have furnished it substantially and neatly, so that
I can see you and yours and give you a bed, when
you visit me which I hope will be before the winter

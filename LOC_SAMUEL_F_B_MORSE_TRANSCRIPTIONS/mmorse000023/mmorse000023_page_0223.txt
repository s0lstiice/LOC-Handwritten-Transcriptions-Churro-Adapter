and what they may effect it is impossible for me to say. The
most annoying part of the matter to me is, that notwithstanding
my matters are all in the hands of agents, and I have nothing to
do with any of the arrangements I am held up by name to
the odium of the public. — Lawsuits are commenced against
them at Cincinnati, and will be in Indiana & Illinois
as well as here, and so notwithstanding all my efforts to get
along peaceably, I find the fate of Whitney before me.
I think I may be able to secure my farm, and so have a place
to retire to for the evening of my days, but even this may be de-
nied me. A few months will decide. I know where my help
is and who controls all, and rest calmly in the persuasion
that his will is best and should be done. Were you here I
could tell you a great deal I cannot write. You have
before you the fate of an inventor, and take as much pains as
you will to secure to yourself your valuable invention, make up
your mind from my experience now in addition to others that you
will be robbed of it and abused into the bargain. This is the lot
of a successful inventor or discoverer, and no precautions I believe
will save him from it, he will meet with a mixed estimate , the

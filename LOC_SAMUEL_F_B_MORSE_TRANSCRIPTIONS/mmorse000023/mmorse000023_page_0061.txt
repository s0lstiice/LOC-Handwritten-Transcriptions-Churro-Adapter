York (England) June 18th 1847.
Dear Brother,
Since I am on your 52 birthday, or rather the 52 return of your birth day, in Old York, I suppose that my case, while you, my Stephen, are in the York of the new world, developing army in a hot steam, and calculating that for every week in the year you have now lived, I have lived a year, you will still live to turn many weeks into many years. Since I last wrote, we have made great changes on our plans. — A week ago I left London with Catherine, Elizabeth & the children for Liverpool, where they all embarked with Mary Cooper (the Rev. Ebenezer Mason, or bearer the packet ship Sir Robert Peel) on Sunday the 13th inst. for New York. (Select gentlemen, Dr Burke) and his lady, & another young man with their 10, fellow passengers in the cabin, & this week, many in the 2d cabin & 297, chiefly Germans, in the storage. On her last voyage, the Sir. Robert Peel was 52 days in returning to America, but the winds are light at this season, you must not be alarmed if they do not arrive before the middle of August.

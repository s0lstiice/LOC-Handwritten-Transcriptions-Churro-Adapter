Boston July 9. 1861
Prof. More,
Dear Sir:
Your letter was duly
received & I am now reply so far as I can en-
deavoring the letter to the District Ad.
The Eddys say that
they never heard of any mail of
Dr. Jos. Telegraph (I) neither do they
think he ever had one. He once spoke
to the young Eddy about a draw-
ing in his Mem. Book, which
he said was burnt some time since
then his honor got on fire. The
letter cannot be true, but Mr Ed-
dy's senior is to be in N.Y. this
month & will call on you - towards
the last. You will be pleased
with him.
I should be extremely obliged
if you would give a review of Dr
Gay's & my pamphlets, & as soon as
possible, for we are suffering in
your City for want of such a
move. I trust I shall not be
too hard to precipitate if I have

Since your return you have undoubtedly read
the intelligence of the accident -- Deming of Dear Fancy
She died in the bed blind sustained by light regimen
faith and fully prepared to meet the dread change
from life to eternity -- It is a severe affliction to
us all yet our loss is eminently her great gain --
Reason perfectly consigns them to the great
last -- We looked to you during the tumult.
Yours Truly
W. Walker

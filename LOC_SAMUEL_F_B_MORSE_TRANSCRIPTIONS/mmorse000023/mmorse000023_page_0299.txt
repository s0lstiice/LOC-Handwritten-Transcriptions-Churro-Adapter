Copy of
Extract to Mr. Baxton Jan. 15, 1848 Wash.
By the by you have some stock in this
line. On what condition will you part with in
exchange for our line stock? I will exchange
with you 50 for 45 that is to say. I will give you
$15 dollars per share for your stock in the U.S.
& wast. line in Exchange for some or all your
stock giving you N.Y. All. & Buff. Stock for it.
Not complied with, and
consequently withdrawn by me.

Ask M. Kendall, about best mode of arranging Texas
telegraphs with M. Alfred Lucey. - appoint an interview if
possible on Thursday evening. Learn what the Amer. Tel. Co. is
doing in Texas with my patents. Propose to sell to State of Texas
through M. Lucey and by letter to Gov. Houston my right. This
may accomplish three or four purposes. Obtain something for them
(in lands perhaps,) establish my system, reward M. Lucey

Boston July 15. 1847
Prof. Mann,
Dr. Giv:
In confidence, I would
say that I now start to set out
for Europe on the 5th of August. But
before going I wish to see you. I wish
to inquire, therefore, what day
must such you will be in New York:
we may have it in our power
to arrange Dr Jackson's case together
& produce it for him in such a
way as to calm his monomania
affectually.
In haste
I am Sincerely Yours et
Alw. Waaler
P.S. I thank you for your good opinion of my pamphlet: it has been
certainly well received here.

In regard to the Manchester Canal line, I would
say that I have engaged it from Mr Kendall and
Mrs Smith has offered gratificationly his interest as
it is a matter of Science more than an object of
financial improvement.
In my letter to you of November 20th, 1839, addressed
to you at New York, gave the nature and general
plan of the Cave and I desired information in regard
to how it ought to be conducted. If you have that
letter I would be glad if you would refer to it
and answer it in detail, and if you have not
I will prepare another description.
Dr. Brighan, the proprietor of the brick, has requested
me to kindly invite to be at the Cave next
summer and spent as long a time as you can
The Dr is feeble and seldom writes or he would attend
to this improvement.
Let me hear from you.
Very respectfully
Samuel P. Shaffner
Prof. Samuel P. Billings

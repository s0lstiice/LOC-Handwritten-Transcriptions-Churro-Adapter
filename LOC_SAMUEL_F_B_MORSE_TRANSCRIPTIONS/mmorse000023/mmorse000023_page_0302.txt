To the Commissioner of Patents,
The petition of Samuel P. B. Morse of the town of Poughkeepsie,
in the County of Dutchess, 6 state of New-York respectfully
represents that your petitioner has made certain improvements
in Electric Telegraphs which he only believes have not been
known or used prior to the invention thereof by your petitioner.
He, therefore, prays that letters patent of the United States
may be granted to him therefor, acting in him, and
in his legal appearance is the exclusive right to the
same, upon the terms, and conditions expressed in
the act of Congress in the case made, and provided;
he having paid thirty dollars into the treasury of
the United States, and complied with other provisions of
the said act.
Samuel P. B. Morse
Washington January 18th 1848.
On this Twin ninth day of January in the year of our Lord
Eighteen hundred, forty eight before the Secretary, a
Justice of the peace in 2 for the City of Washington in the
District of Columbia, personally appeared the petitioners
named Samuel P. B. Morse and made solemn oath
that he hereby declares himself to be the original,
and first inventor of the improvements herein
described in constructing Electric Telegraphs and
that he does not know or believe the same was
ever before known or used and that he is a
citizen of the United States.
J. H. Toddack.
L. Race.

Dr S T B Morse
Dear Sir
"Alfred writes me," I wish
"you would correspond with Dr Morse in regard to the
"Cornell Payments and also Livingston Wells and Conduct
"that matter for me,"
"Alfred has received from the line which Cornell bought
$150. including the Commission of 20 per cent to Dr Kendall I
"do would like to know from you what amts we are entitled
"to, on that line as well as from the Canada line through
"Livingston Wells, from whom he has received $300,
"Dr Short, I would thank you to give us a statement of
"the Amts received and amt due to you from different
"lines sold out that we may learn, if we are not
"informed of all the transactions which have taken place
"in the Telegraph world, I suppose that you keep a list of
"all Sales & Contracts as made, and we fear that
"we may have missed some and therefore ask this favor
"from you, as well as any other information of interest
"which you can give us, Regretting to be obliged
"to trouble you so often
I remain Yours Sir Truly
Geo Tait

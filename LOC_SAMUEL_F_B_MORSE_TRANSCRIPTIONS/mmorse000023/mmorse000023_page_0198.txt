What size wire will it take? Would it have to
be wrought with lead or something else? Would the rocks along
the side of the cave near which the wires would run, would
they effect the galvanic current? Please give me all
this information as I want to carry out the plan. Will
our instrument at both ends require a relay Magnet?
Please do not fail to answer this in full and
tell me all that I must do to succeed.
We expect to go to work very soon now and
build the lines which we have recently contracted
for. The O'Neelly are alert and doing all they can
to arrest our progress.

Have you read the book -- I am anxious
to hear that it has arrived in good order.
I am happy to hear that Charles is about
coming to America to write. I am so glad that
if he has no more difficulty please to stay
that we shall be very happy to welcome
him at our house.
My wife's letter today. The happiness of
Charles has been lucky to become a father
and a mother -- I am sure he will make
an excellent one and I have no doubt that
Charles will make a good husband --
May this happiness be perpetual.
We are all well and with
the usual expressions of esteem and
affection I am
your affectionate
friend
Thomas N. Blanchard

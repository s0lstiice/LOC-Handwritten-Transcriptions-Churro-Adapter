Should you require my testimony in House's case I must be forbidding
to meet the requisition. You will again on that occasion the assignment
to be produced whereby the interest I once held was transferred to you.
Can we not to expect you in W. this winter? When you last wrote
me you obtained you would probably be here in January and
I would say we have an extra bed room which will be of your
service when you are disposed to use it. Send your trunk
to our house without ceremony when you arrive, it will please
us to have you do so.
I received your previous letter with the account of through
my friend Mr. Pisson of N.Y. all was right.
When you may have funds in hand for me you will please transfer
them to Mr. Pisson and at the same time send me the substance of
the transaction by mail -- let the moved transaction only be
communicated to him and whatever of explanation there may be, in the
case it is to be entertained.
With the highest esteem
Your truly
L. D. Gale
Prof J.B.G. Mason

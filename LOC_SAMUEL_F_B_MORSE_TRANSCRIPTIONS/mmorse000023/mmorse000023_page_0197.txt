Louisville Ky Oct 13th 1847
Samuel J B Moore Esq
Dear Sir
We have recently contracted
for a line or the night of the Mare's Telegraph, to be run
into the Great Monuments Cave in Ky, and I am anxious
to know from you what will be necessary to enable us to
succeed. It is a matter more for my own taste than otherwise
In order that you may the better informed me I will mention
the character of the Cave. In some parts of the cave it is
50 to 100 in width and the same in height. In other parts it
is very narrow, About 5 miles from the mouth the river often
fills the entire Cave for about 1 mile, beyond which opens
atend of about 50 ft and then you get into the most
beautiful part of the cave. It is dry, large and the crystalliza-
tions are beyond description in beauty. At the end of the
main Avenue (9 miles from mouth) there is the most often-
ed Chambers that I ever saw. It is about 10 ft in diameter
15 ft high, with large formations like the rocks above
ice at the end of houses making a connection between the
ceiling and the floor. I want an instrument in that
place, the wide running out the cave to the House about
300 Yds from its mouth. Much anxiety is often experienced
by persons going beyond the river, fearing that it might
not and fill up the Cave for that distance (1 mile) and
lyricly they have to stay in & beyond the river until
it falls. A Telegraph would remedy this danger as persons
could immediately be informed of the progress of a bearer

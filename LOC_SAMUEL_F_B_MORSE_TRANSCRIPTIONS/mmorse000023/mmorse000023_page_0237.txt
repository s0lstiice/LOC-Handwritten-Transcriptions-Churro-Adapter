American Institute
New York Dec 15th 1847.
Dear Sir.
We are now preparing our Report to the
Legislature which is required to be made
annually. We intend it shall contain
important matter in relation to general
Tire & Me Arts.
It will be a great favour if you will supply
us with any articles on the Magnetic
Telegraph with such remarks on Electricity
as you may deem proper.
The last year the legislature printed 600
copies of our Report. They will be widely
distributed. We shall in a few days
send one hundred copies to different
Societies in Europe. It embraces 550 pages
This year it will probably be increased
in Size. I have reflected on the effects of
your telegraph its facilities for disseminating
knowledge if knowledge is power which
I believe is a correct maxim it is designed
to do more to elevate man in the
scale of existence than any other invention
that the world has ever witnessed.
An early & favourable reply
will oblige the Institute your
obedient servant
J. B. Wakeman
Cor' Secy
Prof Samuel F. B. Morse

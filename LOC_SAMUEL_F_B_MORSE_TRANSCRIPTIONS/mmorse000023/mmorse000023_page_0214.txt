Utica Nov 13. 1847.
My dear sir
I waited until to day to answer your
letter from Wood, or Mr Barrock to whom I had
assisted and made the 1000 for a sum or not - but
as they have neither of them called I suppose that
they committed it - I have enclosed Pomeroy
Walker 1000 note for 1000 on cit. at eight months
interest or a sum paid at United Bank - As I
am my self one of the partners of that house it does
not become me perhaps to trumpet our own
impossibility - But suppose it to say "entre nous"
that we have not got a solitary discountable
note in any Bank, and that our business produces
the comfortable revenue of $6.000 and an per annum -
My partners did not want to list it and only con-
cluded to do so at my solicitation -
As it is not very business like to make one's business public please to consider the above statement as to the establish-
ment confidential - It is not worth while in these
days of compulsion to invest it -
Should you at any time wish to realize the sum
on the note I will cash it for you on a weekly notice
Miller has paid his note $139.47 which I
shall deposit to your credit at United Bank and for
which you can immediately draw if you wish -
The interest on the $1000. from 3d to 13th int.

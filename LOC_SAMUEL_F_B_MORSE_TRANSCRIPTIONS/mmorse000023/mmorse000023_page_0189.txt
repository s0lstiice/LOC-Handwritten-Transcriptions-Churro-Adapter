Spedwell October 5th 1847
Friend More
I reached home the day following
my leave of you, found all my friends well. Father
& George made many enquiries respecting your elegant
seat on the Hudson, concerning which I satisfied them
as well as I was able. All are gratified to learn that
you have a home, a sweet home.
I enquired in New York concerning the port auger
and found that there were none to be had. But that
A Betten & Co 187 water St who keeps an agricultural
ware House has ordered a number of all sizes from the
Patentee 110 Stage of Baltimore and expects them every
day. Pieces $4.30. to $5.30 for auger 9 & 10 inches in diameter
with length of Shank 4 to 6 feet. You had better order
them from him and say how long you wish the
handle or Shank.
I could not learn anything about the traps. But
my Brother says that the plumbers make them of
lead and that the plumber in Park Roe near St
Spring's Church can make them for you. When I
go in again which will be the last of this week I
will make enquiries again and inform you.
I met Mr Cornell at New York St off who paid
me $150 dollars the 1st Instalment. He desired me to
give him permission to my signature thereto to allow

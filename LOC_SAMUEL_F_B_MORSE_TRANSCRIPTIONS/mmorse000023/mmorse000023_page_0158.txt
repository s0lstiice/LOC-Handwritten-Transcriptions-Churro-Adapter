Sept. 4th 1847
My dear sir.
I have indeed an editorial which ap-
peared in the Gazette this morning - You may not
be aware that there has been considerable feeling
between Faxon & the Editor which has grown
out of some complaint as to the facilities permitted
the press for the public press - I have for a long
time past feared that the President of this line
was not sufficiently courteous and obliging at-
tracted in most respects an admirable officer -
Riley upon it that it will not do to run a
tilt with the press - It is a potent adversary
and speaks with million tongues - Can nothing
be done to enlist the press on your side? It appears
to me that the Co. will lose nothing by manifesting
a liberal spirit towards the press - and I fear
that they have not so far been done -
This is all strictly confidential and written
only for your observation and guidance -
When shall we be at Utica?
Yours truly

New York July 24. 1847
Dear Sir,
There seems to be some opposition in regard to the proposal made by Mr. Gardner to take 1800 letters of stock in exchange for 2 notes of 900 each, payable annually one for 2 years, the other for 3 years.
It appears that Mr. Gardner was the bearer of the notes. I omitted to give stock at the office after I heard from Mr. Lansing, gave Mr. Gardner a letter to me, which he left at the City Office, I called several times to meet him, and left notices for him to call on me, at particular hours, but I have not seen him, nor has he again called at the City Office.
As I leave the City on Monday for the Catskill Mountain house with my daughter for her health, I shall probably not see him.

Jan. 20. 48
Ref: P.L.B. Moun
D. Lin
From your letter of 15th come to hand yesterday
herewith you have stated if amount you will take from
it that division day is not near at hand
you ask how I will exchange stock -- I have told the
Mr. Vail that I will give him my proof of for 5000. R. G. & Buffalo
If he shall not conclude to do it -- I am ready to do so
write you -- at this I must confess I do not know what
the R.G. & Ph. Stock should not be worth a good deal
beyond their value whilst in the hands of enemy -- by their own damn
power -- if I do not disprove of my Ph. Stock I am
afraid to know when the money is before Long
I am anxious to have your reply to P.L.B. Let alone
write Oklahoma -- from present affairs Kansas seems to
be nearly used up -- I will be glad to have from you
at your earliest comm after leaving Bkkt. L.
Very Respectfully yours J.S. Sexton
Atcham January 20th 1848.

To the Baron de Gerott
Minister resident of his
Majesty the King of Prussia
My dear Baron
In regard to the establishment
of my Telegraphe System in Russia concerning
which we had some conversation last evening, I
would say that should it be desired by the Rus-
sian Government, I will provide an experienced
Superintendent of construction, and two experienced
operators, who shall proceed immediately to Berlin
with two of my best instruments, and await the orders
of the Russian Government to construct and put in
operation an experimental line of such length as
the Government may require. The Government shall
defray the travelling expenses of these persons from
the United States and back again, putting them under
such salary as may be mutually agreed upon, and
shall defray all expenses of instruments and cost
of construction My compensation as inventor I pro-
pose shall be thus estimated. The actual cost of
construction per mile (which in this country is about
125 dollars) per shall be ascertained and a similar
amount shall be given to me.

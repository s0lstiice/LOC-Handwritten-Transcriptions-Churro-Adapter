Washington June 18th 1847
Dear More you came to hand two days
since & I immediately sent to Adams & Co
to see what they knew about the missing
package. They will at once investigate it
and I will let you know
It seems to me that we must have
a large share of the villainy, rascality
and other properties of fallen
man at work against us all the while
Such has become the moral character
of many of our tradesmen that nothing
is dishonorable in their eyes - If they could
but put their hands in your pocket and
not be detected, to them it would be a
perfectly fair transaction - since they would
say "you had your eyes about you why
did you not have wit enough to be on your
guard?" So it is with that Downing - when
in Philad I learned that the Western
company were confinced of his want
of character & had determined to throw
him overboard - I told Mr Reed - He
has made a cat paw of O'Kelly and when
Downing is done with him, He will eat
the

Jan 7. 10. 45
Office of the New York, Albany & Buffalo Telegraph Company,
UTICA January 10th 1845
F. F. B Moon by
On
I have received a letter
from the President of the Magnetic Telegraph Company
calling for ten dollars in order that
he might be enabled to loose the
stock in the New York Direct
Line very respectfully declining
paying any money until I know
from this letter what has been
paid in & receive from learning
preliminary data to be a large stockholder
in that company I have thought
perhaps you might know all about it & as I am
very small in it I have not seemed to say
much about it -- seems to me no doubt
to know something about a line that has
been in operation over 2 years & in the
right of between 30 & 40 thousand dollars as I am told
perhaps my information is not correct
Respectfully yours
W. Fattman
P.S. I require him to hold still nothing will be heard
of the effect of the Iron Tower will be

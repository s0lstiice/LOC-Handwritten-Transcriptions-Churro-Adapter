I have thought of a plan of operations in Texas
in regard to the Telegraph. Suppose I give you
letter to Gov. Houston, and general letter authorizing
you to negotiate with the Govt. to dispose of my patents rights
to them for a consideration in money, or lands: You re-
ceiving a portion of the money or lands for your services
in negotiating the sale. Let this be the basis of an ar-
rangement with you. If this tale be acceded to by the Govt. of
Texas, it must be for valuable lands, such as we may locate
and freed from taxes tax for 10 years, or at least to the ex-
piration of my patent just extended.

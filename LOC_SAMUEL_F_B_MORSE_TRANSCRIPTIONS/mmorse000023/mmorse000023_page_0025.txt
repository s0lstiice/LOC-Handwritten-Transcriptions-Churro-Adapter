Copy Democrat Office
Rochester June 2.
Henry Wells, Esq
Sir,
I regret to inform you that the very small fa-
vor we asked of Mr. Taylor viz. a five minutes report from Buffalo
during one hour each evening has been denied on the ground that he has no
authority to grant the request. Much as I regret it, I fear the press will
be brought into collision with the Company. Our treatment has been extremely
illtutual; we pay a stipulated price for a certain length of time but we
seldom occupy over half we are entitled to and lose one fifth of the
Reports for which no deduction is made. Still when we ask to
occupy five minutes for a special purpose we are denied. The
press has determined to ask the legislature for relief. They propose
to forward petition to have the law repealed protecting the
Telegraph and in this they will be joined by the whole agricul-
cultural community who on account of the meagre accom-
modations afforded them press regard it a monopoly
Yours in haste
Henry Cook
True Copy attest
Henry Wells
Mr Cook wrote on the 2d this same day to Mr Taylor
Mr F returned an answer in favor on the 3d.

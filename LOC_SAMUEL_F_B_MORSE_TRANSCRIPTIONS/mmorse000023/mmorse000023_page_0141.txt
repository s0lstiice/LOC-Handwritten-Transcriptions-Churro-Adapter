my dear sir
Utica August 6 1837
I shall give some days leave but having
not received from Calhoun
I made every effort to bring Gardner to a
better price for the stock but unsuccessfully
Leunington has just up an additional comment
of stock for sale which he was offering at
par without buyers. This stock is still for
sale. Under all the circumstances I con-
cluded that it aimed be but to sell him the
stock for the notes at par and did so.
He takes 37 shares for which he gives the notes
for $1800 and intends on the notes making commis-
sion over 1800 and pays you the balance
on the one share. You may send me
if you please the 37 shares and I will
receive the notes.
The notes are my strong - indeed it is
one of the safest investments which could
be made.
I saw Miller yesterday - he says that Wade
is sick he will not break to give his
answer in relation to the building rent -
next week.

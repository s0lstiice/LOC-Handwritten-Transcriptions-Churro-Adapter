Troy Sat July 3rd 1848
Dear Sir I am getting my win up briskly
have already about 40 miles up and putting
up 10 miles per day I hope to complete
this line and get it to work in three weeks
I have already paid on account of the
Patent $6,200 and the balance over $4,000
will be collected so it can be paid during
this month I think over 300 miles of the
Eric + Michigan line west of Detroit is done and
in operation the balance will be done in all this
month. If the win can be got thru which has to be
collected through Canada from buffalo as soon
not able to get a supply before the close of
navigation the holes are up on 300 miles
of the line between Buffalo + Detroit and my
Weather communes putting up wins on them
this week at Buffalo
But the boudigeurre of the O'Reilly difficulty
he cannot collect even subsensititions, and I have
furnished all the win for that line without
recieving one dollar in cash from the in
doing this and putting up holes on the section
between Buffalo + Detroit which I have also paid
for, I have paid out $10,000 in cash and have
notes that are now falling due for nearly as
much more, and they must be paid when
due I therefore again appeal to you for a loan
of two thousand dollars of the money that is yet

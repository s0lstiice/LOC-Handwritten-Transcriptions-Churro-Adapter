Mr. Morin Sir
Your Letter of the 1st is at hand
and I must confess I am so made in
it would be hard for me to think that this Telegraph
line from N.Y. to Buffalo is the only one
in existence which is at fault.
I think if you look to the Willis Letter you
will find it of an old date. We look for Rochester
was sent the following communication from Buffalo
"We will send Market Report from Buffalo
if furnished as you requested. George T. Spivak,
Lease Day, 100 Remain upon account to send
Market Report to Rochester or Milan if furnished,
What more could be done."

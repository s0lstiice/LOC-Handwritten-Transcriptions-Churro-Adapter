Utica Oct 29. 1857
Dear Sir
In reply to your telegraphic communication stating
that you would take the loan of $1000 on the 1st
November, by Ass. on Common Bk. Poughkeepsie -
Exchange on Poughkeepsie is at 1/2 70. discount
here and I quite to suggest that you procure
the checking the Poughkeepsie Bank at Albany
or New York or transmit amount in one
hundred dozen notes on the Poughkeepsie
Bank which for the purpose of safety, might
be sent in halves by two mails, each is cut
in two, each note to be pasted together when
both parts should be in.
The letter way bearer could be deposited
at the Ass. on Bank at Albany, New York or
Utica -
Very Truly
Thos R. Walker
Palmer has executed a beautiful Concluding of
Annie, wonderful in a likeness beautiful in
execution - I will send you a Cast of it
by suit off. of the loan in Albany in a day or two -

As the Salinaters are protected by their Arti-
cles of Association of their own making,
and having control of half the stock, if
they are not satisfied, certainly they can
make a change to suit their purpose; and
if they are determined to run Mc Kendall,
we are aware that a defeat awaits us.
I hope however that harmony & good feel-
ing may exist, instead of any other,
in regard to the selection of officers.
For my own part I do not know what
I expect from anyone but my well tried
and true Baltimore friends after the mis-
representations that have been made un-
justly against me, to Messrs Hart,
Swain and others.
I now hold 14200 of the stock of the
Mc Telegraph Co: and I may increase
it and so that of others if the affairs
of the company indicate half of what
I had been influenced by you and
Mr Vad to believe, and which I have
personally advocated, contrary to the
opinion of some of my warm and
most attached friends, their opinion so
far seems to be the correct one; but

Philadelphia. Jan 28 1865
D. P. B. Morse Clipp. & Staff.
Washington.
Dear Sir
I have received your three letters, the former of
which I replied to from Pittsburg on the 27th ulto. I have just returned from
Harrisburg, whether I went to write the Governor's message, to Pittsburg & this
city. If you will find it in the evening Bulletin of the 5th, word
for word, & paragraphed & punctuated like the original with few exceptions, less
than the usual errors of lengthened typography, & these the fault of the
comparer, not the manuscript. It took me about ten hours. -- Of one
thing I am conscious -- I have ever aimed & prove the telegraph
equal to any emergency, and doing most skilfully its inability to transmit
lengthy documents. The Gov't message as printed in Pittsburg is, consequently, a
more correct copy than has ever been set up from the printed original. --
But I am very fatigued & out of health & better to give you
the points where offices now are open on this line
Philadelphia.

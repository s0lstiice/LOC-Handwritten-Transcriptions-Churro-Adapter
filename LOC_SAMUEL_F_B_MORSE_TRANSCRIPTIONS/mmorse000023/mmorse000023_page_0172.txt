Williamstown Sept 13/47
My dear brother,
Your kind letter I received last week with
one enclosed from husband, I should have answered it before
but the weather was so uncertain I could not definitely
say when I should leave here. It is my intention to leave
this day fortnight the 27th and will be at Poxceptie on
Tuesday the 25th. Should the weather prove unfavorable
I shall not leave until Wednesday, and be with you
on the next day. I feel much gratified, that you should
think so much of me as to invite me to visit you, and
believe, it will give me much pleasure to do so.
Accept my congratulations on your having once more a home
and I hope you will realize all the pleasure you
anticipate in having your children with you. I know
if no one that will enjoy or desire a home more than
yourself, Give my love to Susy and the boys, not
forgetting your nephew Charlie.
and accept of the same from
your Sister Kate
P.S. Please enclose my note to Husband when you write

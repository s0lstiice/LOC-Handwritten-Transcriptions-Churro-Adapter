and I challenge you on my own men to get a respectable high minded Senator of the South to say that a single dishonorable act has been adopted either towards him or the Public who uses the Telegraph.
So with the operators on the line I say the same thing, and allow me to say what I would most cheerfully advocate if I could better the designs & intentions of Men & their partions towards you & myself than to be impolite upon us I have reason to believe you have been.
You will pardon my plain frank unvarnished way of expressing myself to you, the whole subject is sticking to me & I cannot deny that I am having touched the bottom more than once.
Respectfully yours
W. Houston

Crosby, Mr. Boorman, Ve. Ie. -- The new railroad will run at the foot of the
ground (probably,) on the river and bring New York within 2 hours of us.
There is every facility for residence, good markets, churches, schools, take
it all in all I think it just the place for us all. If you should fancy
a spot on it for building, I can accomodate you; and Richard wants
20 acres reserved for him. Singular enough, this was the very spot where
Uncle Arthur found his wife; the trees are pointed out where he and
she used to ramble during their courtship. -- How are you going to
pay for it? I think I hear you ask. I pay of my own funds $10,000
cash, which is 6,500 of which I have in Bank, down on the stipings
of the papers, the rest remains on bond & mortgage, to be liquidated by
me at pleasure. I have $3,000 in real estate in Utica, guaranteed to yield
8 p. cent, and $23,000 Selby stock which Mr. Kendall says will yield
in a few months 12 p. cent. and by the first of Jan'y some $60,000 more
in Southern Selby stock which will be at par. Besides these there
are payments outright in cash for short lines, which are daily
called for, and in the month of Sept. I shall be in the receipt
of some $5 or $6,000, more, so that all things well weighed I
left safe in vesting the price of this estate in such a place, since
at worst I can get what I have agreed to pay for it, any moment,
for property is rising, and must rise on the river.
I wished much to have had you here to have your cool
judgement; but in your absence I have taken Mr. Burnings who
thinks I have a bargain. Your little son Newark lake came

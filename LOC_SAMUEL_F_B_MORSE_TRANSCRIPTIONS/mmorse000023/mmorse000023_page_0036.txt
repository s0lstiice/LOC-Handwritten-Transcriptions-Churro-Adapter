I hope you will give the matter serious
Consideration, about which we conversed on
the morning you left this for the East,
I do not wish to bring myself forward,
nor do my friends, as the competitor of
Mr. Kendall for the Presidency of the
Mo. Telegraph Co., their object being to
place an active, young, and energetic
person at the head of the concern, and
one who is familiar with the operations
of the Mo. Telegraph, and in whom the
stockholders feel that they have had the
selection, without any fear of being defeated
by the owners of the patent right; and as
several gentlemen have mentioned my name
in connexion with the above, you must be
aware that I feel much gratified at
this expression in my favour.
It is presumed that the stockholders
will prefer some person familiar with all
the operations of the Telegraph, as well as
with the business in general, & who
would devote all his time to their
service; this I will do if placed in
a position that will compensate me
for other sacrifices.

my dear sir
Upon my monday was duly no. Mr. Miller carried
the agreement perfect and defer the execution of the paper until
next week for the purpose of determining whether he can secure
the cash or give the mortgage as proposed - I have
not spoken to him because I suppose that you might
prefer to hear the money. He informs me that
he will be able to determine whether he can secure
the Cash by Tuesday. If not the paper will be drawn
on the basis proposed in your last letter -
We are all afflicted at the death of dear Family - not
on her account but our own. Her death has caused a
void in our household, which can not be filled.
When we hear the solemnity of coming upon another month may
we not give up the details of her last sickness & death -
which are interesting. Her entry into the "Valley of the
Shadows of Death" was indeed triumphal. Dear Family!
If I am not at the Cape when you arrive send
your luggage directly to my home, where we shall be
required to welcome you again -
Delectate
Very Truly yours
J.R. Walker
June 26. 1837
S. F. B. Man E.

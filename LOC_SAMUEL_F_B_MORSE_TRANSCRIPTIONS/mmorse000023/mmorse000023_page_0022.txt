I hope to make some arrangements for a home, if that
be best. Perhaps we may get a house together in some
sort, so as to be at much together as possible. There are
not many years before us at best, and therefore, I want
we should make the best use of time that is left, there
is a great deal to do yet to benefit the world, and with
God's help I will yet do more than I have done.
Of your affairs here, Richard and Mcminson keep
you advised. They seem prosperous.
The papers will tell you of the aspect of our political
relations; I presume you will feel much of the same disgust
that I feel at the party distortion of all representations
of our national measures. It seems to be a principle with
some to represent every thing done or proposed by the
opposite party as of course wrong, and they according denounced
and attribute all sorts of bad motives to their opponents.

of such preachers as the "Express," which I think pure excellence

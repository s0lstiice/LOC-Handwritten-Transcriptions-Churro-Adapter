I fear I shall find few friends to welcome me in my
return to the United States. The elements will have
scattered many in remote portions of our extended Union
while Death has withdrawn his full protection from the
Isle that I knew
It is long, very long, my dear friend since
I have received any letter from you, though I have often
sought for and expected one. I am sure, in all time
ever amid the brilliant Conceptions of your mind there
will be a fellow voyager to Europe, and an affectionate
friend. Believe me with sincere affection
Yours
Wm. Ellsworth
To Brig. S. H. B. More
City of New York
United States of America

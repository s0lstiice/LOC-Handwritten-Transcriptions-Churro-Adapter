In regard to the interesting project of carrying the Telegraph into the Cave
I wrote you an answer, referring you to Mr. Kendall, who is my Agent
with full authority to arrange all contract for the Telegraph.
When you have completed your arrangements for the use of the
Telegraph in the Cave, be so good as to apprize me at my home in
Po Keepsie, and if I can possibly leave home at the time it would
give me real pleasure to visit that wonderful natural curiosity, and
also other parts of your interesting region of country.
Very Respectfully
Y. Bk. Lent
Sam: F: B: Morse

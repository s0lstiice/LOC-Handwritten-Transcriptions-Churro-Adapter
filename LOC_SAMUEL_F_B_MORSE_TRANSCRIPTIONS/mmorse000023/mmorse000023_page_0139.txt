date about July 1847 —
Copy to M. Arago, Secretaire Perpetuel & c. &c.
My dear Sir
The bearer of this letter Mr Edward Warren
is a gentleman of high character and standing in
Boston, who has zealously espoused the cause of Dr. Morton
as the the discoverer of the Lethion. He visits Paris with
various documents, relating to the Lethion to lay before the Academy of Sciences.
I need not therefore bespeak for him a candid hearing, as I am per-
sueded he will receive it from the Board without any solicita-
tion of mine.
To Mr Warren I have also confided documents, to be placed in your hands relating to the
provisions of Dr. Charcot, Jackson, made many years since the in-
vention of the Magnetic Telegraph, to which counteract
the impressions made by his little published in the Compte
Rendu March 1839

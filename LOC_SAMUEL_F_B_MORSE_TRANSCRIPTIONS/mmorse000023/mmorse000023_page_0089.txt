Dear Mr.
Miller has effected an arrangement for the money
part of the telegraph stock purchase $5000 and I
am about drawing the agreement for the residue
As soon as you send a transfer of the stock he is
ready to pay the $5000-
He is to secure a covenant from Walcott to invest
the money for ten years, for which he has already con-
tracted for and to obtain his covenant or to guarantee
An investment on the other for the same length
of time-
I hope to see you soon- Please direct me
when you send the script as to the disposition of the
money, whether with Miller to deposit it to your credit at
one of the Banks, or to lend you as to the exchange
on the last would probably be 3 1/2 % - The annual rate
is 4 1/2 % but I would try to get it done at the above
figure this this is important-
If disposed to give credit you might draw it
when you come up - This however as you may judge
best-
Yours very truly
J. K. Walker
Atlanta June 30, 1859

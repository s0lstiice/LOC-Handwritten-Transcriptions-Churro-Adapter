Patent Office
Dec 21st 1847
My Dear Sir
your letter of 21 last this morning reached me and shall be promptly
answered.
A caveat can be renewed at the end of the year for another year
and so on, by renewing the caveat fee at the end of each year.
All caveat papers are retained continually in the secret archives
whether the caveat term has expired or not.
If you were to make application for a patent at this time it
would be probably 8 mos. before it would come up for examination
and in the mean time should you wish to have it delayed longer
than its term for the examination you could have it filed as a caveat
in the secret archives, and when you were ready (any time within the
year after it was so filed in the secret archives) you could call it up
to be examined as an application.
As you cannot conveniently attend to the construction of the instruments
I would by all means renew the caveat which is done by
sending a new fee to the office stating what is for
Mr Kendall is expected home in Christmas but may be obtained
until New Year.
With regard to the subject of your second page I will say I am willing
to hasten you will deal mercifully and kindly with me. You have been
strengthened to resist temptation in the hour of adversity and I can
surely trust you now in truth I do not suppose it best for the future
of the Telegraph that I should acquire an interest in it and hence
I am willing to be a user as it is.

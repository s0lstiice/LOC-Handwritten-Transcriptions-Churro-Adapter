Now I am not disposed to say
much at this time as to the progress of this
Line as compared with any other, but this
much I will venture my expectation on.
Take the Line from Boston 510 Miles (which
is the length of one) South towards Washington
and it has not made as many Miles as this
Line has days Line the 9th of Sept. Last
which was the time our Line opened.
Take the Line from Boston to New York
240 Miles & it has not made as many Days as
the Line from Albany to Rochester has hours
Line the first of Sept. Last & yet you have
had all the advantages of being between two
Large Cities & when all Capital & Talent
be combined within all the Talent,

this line, for which I have not
received the credit due me, other
than the remark "that any one else
could have done the same".
This they tried to fail to which can
be proven by their own letters.
During the time I was remitting
funds to Mr Kendall & to Mr O'Sullly,
I received several letters from the
former encouraging me to go on,
with the remarks that as I had re-
mitted part of the funds, and received
a large subscription that our line
from Phila to Baltimore will now
be rapidly built."
I have felt sorely grieved many
times when I find so little active
interest manifested towards placing
me in a proper position, but have
consol'd myself with the idea that

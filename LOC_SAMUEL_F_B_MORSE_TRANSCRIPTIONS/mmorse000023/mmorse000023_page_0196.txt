You may render me a service, if you can, with truth,
give me the means, (documents, papers, or conversations)
showing that Brett, alias House's Telegraph, has
been a failure in England, and that Prince Albert,
the Marquis Northampton, &c, were indignant at the
imposition practised on them, (if such be the case)
and it is natural they should be, after they had
discovered the humbug.
Louisa you will learn has another son; Richard
numbers now nine children, the number of the
Muses, and will of course be very musical. They
are all well.
If you should chance upon any practical work
on Landscape Gardening, with numerous designs, you may
get it for me. I have Whately and Loudon already.
Oct. 14th I must close in haste, I kept this open till the last
moment to add any thing of interest. I have this moment
received from Smith some of the proceedings against O'Reilly
House &c. Their career is shot if justice has its course, but
I don't know how far injustice may be permitted to prevail,
My refuge is strong & sure, it will not further
than He permits.
Good bye. Your affectionate brother
Hinley.

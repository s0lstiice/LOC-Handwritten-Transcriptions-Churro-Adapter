New York May 24th 1847
My dear Sir,
In accordance with my
promise made to you, I have the
honor to enclose to you a com-
plete history of my attempts to
introduce my Telegraph into the
Russian Empire, embracing my ne-
gotiations, and correspondence with
the Baron Alexander de Meyendorff
Consuls of State &c.
With the highest respect
and esteem
Yr Ob Servt
Sam F B Morse
To A. Speiffer, Esq.

My dear sir
Mr. Miller has been about two days not returned last
week. He has been disappointed in obtaining the money when
he expected it but in still desiring to complete the arrangement
I suggested to him that I would quite agree to an interim
but in it would assume your purpose to secure a bond
of his matters or a bond & mortgage for the money portion
of the arrangement stating that if it could be paid in
payment if you agreed to provide that it was
permissible for myself to take it, although I could not
undertake to say that it would be agreeable.
Mr Miller would like to give a bond & mortgage
which should be approved by you, for $3500 (the cash
portion) and complete the real estate portion of the bargain
$3500- and to retain the privilege of completing the
remainder $3000 by paying 1/2 cash & then making
next spring - if he can obtain satisfactory agreement to make
An addition to the first part of the proposition the
question is whether an approved bond & mortgage could
be equal to cash to you. If so there would be
no objection to you concluding the arrangement as
Miller can furnish such a security - I think unless
you might realize the money on such a security arrangement
difficultly though of this you must judge - I do not
think that it would be prudent on your part to agree to
the latter part of the proposition since much as Miller would
take no risk and it would all be on your side -

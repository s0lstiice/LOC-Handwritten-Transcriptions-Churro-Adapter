New York Dec 23d 1827
My dear Sir
Your letter I received last night.
Mr Fish was unable to complete the plan
work of the Telegraphic map in season to
bring it out with the Missionary atlas. I
regret this on your account, but the delay
seemed unavoidable. I have explained
this matter to Mr Walway, he has done
the same to Mr Studen of the Herald
and informed you from all hands. They
have consented to wait for the map until
after the first of next month.
I am now finishing the last of
the missionary maps and have done
remarkably successful. The next work
will be yours.
I received no letter from
you in Boston by the last steamer but I
am anxiously looking for one.
Yours with great regard
W.H. Munson
Sec'y. U.S. B. Mkt

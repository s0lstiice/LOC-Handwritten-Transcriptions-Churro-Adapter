New York May 19th 1847.
B. F. Copeland Esq. Boston. 33 India St.
Gentlemen,
In reply to your 18th favor of
18th inst. this morning. I would say,
that the application of the esteemed house
of Messrs Van Lencop of Constantinople, is unfortunate too late.
Two of my instruments made for the very
purpose of presentation to the Sultan, are
by this time in Constantinople; They were
procured by a young physician from the
South, high in the confidence of the Sultan,
D: Smith, a graduate of Yale College, of
eminent scientific attainments. This gentleman
wrote to Prof. Silliman, who communicated
with me on the subject.
Under these circumstances I would advise
the Messrs Van L. to make some arrangement
with D: Smith; it may be that
these parties can be of mutual benefit
to each other. As to myself as Inventor I
have no other than a moral right, to some
consult and

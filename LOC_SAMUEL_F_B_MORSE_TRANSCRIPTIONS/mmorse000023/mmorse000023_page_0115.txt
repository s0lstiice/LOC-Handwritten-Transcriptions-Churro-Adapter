DEAR SIR,
So many of my aged informants have died, and so many other sources of information perished, that
on the preservation of my single manuscript probably depends much if not most of the early history of
our families. Life is uncertain, — and I have no means of preserving my collection where it might be
accessible to transcribers. Will you, then, give this an early circulation among the Morses and their
relatives of other names, collect subscribers and lists of families for the Memorial, and then part the sheet
and enclose me the latter half, with any notes you may please to add explanatory of the history of any
on your list. Note all instances of rare genius, devoted piety, and public liberality and usefulness.
Yours respectfully,
ABNER MORSE.
Sherborn, Middlesex Co., Mass., July 1, 1848.

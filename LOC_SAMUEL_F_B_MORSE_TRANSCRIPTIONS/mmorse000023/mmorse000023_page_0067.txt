Our Stockholders go for the interest
of the Company, and want to merge
the offices of President & Superintendent
into one, at a salary of $2000 per
annum; enough to command the service
of your old friends and Assistant.
I should like to hear from you, in
regard to this matter as soon as possible,
so that I can decide upon what
I am to do and expect.
I do not care for the name of President
but give me a fair salary, and
your good wishes and friendship.
So that I can feel I have a place
in your affection, as I believe I need
had, when you expressed so many
kind regards for me and mine.
Very respectfully
Your obedient Servt
Henry J Rogers
P.S. Please advise me of your
wishes, the present is an opportunity
for serving me, and securing the
service of one who always had
your interest at heart.

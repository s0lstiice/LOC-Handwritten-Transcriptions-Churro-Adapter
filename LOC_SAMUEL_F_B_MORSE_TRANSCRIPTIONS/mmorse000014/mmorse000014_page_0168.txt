Philadelphia July 1st 1841
Dear Sir
In consequence of your request made to
me whilst in New York I have made enquiry
in relation to the charges which your numerous
correspondent "W" has made against Dr. Munro
although I have asked many I have been
unable to find any one who heard Dr. Munro
make the statement referred to. I went to hear
his sermon in connection with the Sunday evening. Although
he spoke of the doctrines of the Holy Roman Catholic Church he did not mention a word respecting
your writings. The from one review I made on the sermon I concluded that
he had been occupied in examining the writings
of modern authors who had propagated
falsehood respecting his doctrines.
My principal object (in connection with
the above) in writing to you was to inform you
that I have had a number of conversations with
Dr. Munro since my return here and I am
sure that the honest fellow will take for his
knowledge a statement of my opinions
which I am willing to give if you
will permit me to bear witness of the
truth of his proof according to the sample.

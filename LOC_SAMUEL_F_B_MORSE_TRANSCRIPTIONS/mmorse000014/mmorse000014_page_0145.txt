Constitution of a Decade of
the Italian Association Society
Whereas Italy, a country rich in the most interesting associations to the Statesman, the Scholar, and especially to the
Christian, has hitherto been neglected by that benevolence which has carried light & religious truth into the darkest
corners of the earth, and
Whereas Italy, in its present oppressed & degraded state, and as the seat of that great Apostacy which has
so long cursed the earth by its blighting despotism, is a source of alarm to the civilised world, from the instances of
the Papacy, and the present inconvenience inconvenience to Spain's chastisement in the
war against ignorance, delusion and superstition, especially
whereas Italian Christians are anxious for the moral
and intellectual regeneration of their country, and have
appealed to us for our sympathies and our aid
Therefore the whose names are hereunto subscribed
do associate to aid with our arms & our prayers in the

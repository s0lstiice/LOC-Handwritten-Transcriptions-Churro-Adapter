And now I suppose you would like to know when we have
been wandering and what we have seen and heard in the
course of our travels. Last Saturday morning we left Philadel-
phia for Baltimore and took rooms at the Exchange Hotel,
but I must say it is the best house I ever saw in; every-
thing is so neat, and the table so fine, that we are all quite
in love with it. I have a pleasant little chamber at the
door that leads into Wales, so it is very pleasant for such a
scramble as I am. We took a walk out to see the Lewis-
Dick Washington's Monument, and then I went in search of an

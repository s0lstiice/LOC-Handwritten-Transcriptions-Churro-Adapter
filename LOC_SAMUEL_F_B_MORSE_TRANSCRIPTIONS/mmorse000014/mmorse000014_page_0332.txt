No. of R. Feb. 9 '42
Dear Sir,
Since I let you now, merely to acknowledge
the rest of your communication, Shapow enclosed,
in regard to your great Invention - the Electro-Mag-
netic Telegraph; and to assure you that my
long neglect since receiving your letter has not
arisen from want of interest in the matter.
The subject is not within my province or that
of my Committee, but as a subject of deep im-
portance to the Country, it is one in which
I cannot fail to feel both interest and
pride. I shall not lose sight of it -
I am, dear Sir, with great regard esteem
Yr. obt. Servt.
J.P. Barnard
Sam'l F.B. Morse Fig.-

By the way, dear father - there has a poor woman been
making me some under garments, and I was to pay
her Wistham. & she would see to it. Will you let Charity
take them 1s 70d - and say it was for the place?
It will oblige me much if you will.
I have a flying report that my friend & school mate
Sam Shepherd of Bengal, thinks of accompanying me to
Bordeaux - so if there has a letter come to the office post marked
Washingtoon, Co - I shall begin to think it true. Please bring
on all letters that may have come - dear father. I hope
the will go - and am very anxious to ascertain the truth
of such a report. As to Lord Charles, I fear I shall
have to give him up. It seems very strange that he
has not wrote.
I shall not engage myself dear father, and indeed
he does not seem to think I would be doing myself
justice to do so. I have always been strongly opposed
to long engagements - especially of such a length as one
would necessarily be - four and even five years.
Though I think now my affection for him would not
change, by a voyage across the ocean - still I think
you engaging myself - but some improvement change
my feelings entirely, for I know I am rather fickle.
He has sent me a long letter for you, this morning
under the belief that I was to leave on the morning
but as that plan is changed I presume he will
prefer taking it back & waiting to see you himself.

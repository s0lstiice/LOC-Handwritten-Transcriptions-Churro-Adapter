Albany N.Y. March 26th 1841.
Dear Sir:
I send for by mail a copy of the
"Northern Light," the first No. of which is just finished.
Will you look at it, and if you think well of it, I think the
N.Y. Observer will take notice of such publications, your
mother can undoubtedly render us an essential service
by speaking of it. The Christian Intelligence published
a very handsome notice of it in a recent No.
I am sure I need not say to you that the
Northern Light will be kept free from all party sectarian
movements & insinuations. It will be devoted with unqualified
fidelity to liberty, Scientific purposes. I have had repeated
opportunities solicitation at different periods of my life to
connect myself with the political press, but I have always declined
all such quarter. My moral constitution is not fitted to the rough
struggle of party newspaper squabbles. I might get hardened, but I fear the process would wear

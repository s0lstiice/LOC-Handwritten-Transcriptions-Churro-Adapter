1839
In NY again
After this brilliant and
encouraging visit to England
& France, Mr Morse returned
in the month of March 1839
to the City of St. Joseph. His friends
were scattered in distant places;
Dr Galt having accepted a
Professnship in the South;
Mr Trail was dead, in Missouri,
and Mr Smith, on whose
license Mr Morse sailed,
retired to in Philadelphia. To
Mr Smith he writes, April 16:
"Think for a moment of my
situation. I left St. Joseph for
Europe to be absent."

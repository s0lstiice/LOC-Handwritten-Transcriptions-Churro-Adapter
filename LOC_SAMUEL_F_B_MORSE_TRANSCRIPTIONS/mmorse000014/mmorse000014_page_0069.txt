N. York City University March 18, 1840
My dear Baron
I have just received your letter of
the 21st December from Paris enclosed in one from A.
Angot. This is the first letter received from you since I
left Paris, and am therefore sorry to learn that you have
written to me without having been in Paris.
I have just written to Mr. Angot informing
of his confiding to your care one of my Telegraphic
observations made under his Superintendence, in order to
show to the Emperor on your visit to St Petersburg.
I have since I have returned to the United States made several
important improvements, which I regret my limited time will
not permit me to describe or send you. I have suffered
much in my primary affairs from a constant absence

Philadelphia Dec. 20th 1841
Prof. Morse
Dear Sir Your came to
hand this morning and regret you do not
find it satisfactory. I however send you
a sort of an instrument of my own drawing up
which if you do not find sufficient. I will
make it so next week when I hope to be in
the city of New York. So you may go on and
make your arrangements as you have informed
me they are to be.
I know all men by these present that Alfred
Vail of Penn Township. District of Spring Garden and
County of Philadelphia do hereby constitute Sam J.B.
Morse of the City of New York my true and lawful
Attorney for me and in my name and to my use
to make such arrangements with M Coffin in regard
to the Electro Magnetic Telegraph as may be necessary
to procure an appropriation from Congress for the pur-
pose of experimenting with the Electro Magnetic
Telegraph.
Alfred Vail

will be no embarassment thrown in the way
of your and our ultimate success in the
Telegraph. I should be very much pleased
to have you come to Philadelphia and invade your
stay at my house, and I will endeavour
to render you every assistance in my power.
My Father is frequently in New York and
you may be able to transact the whole
business through him and George. He will
convey any message to G and get the requisite
papers. You may show Father this letter
which will answer the purpose of my writing.
He, I think, always puts his name upon the
books of the Commercial Hotel Courtland St.
on the South side, near Greenwich St. within City.
Let me hear from you by return of mail
if this is satisfactory. I remain yours truly
Alfred Bait

Dear Sir
Your two favours of the 1st & 30th June were duly received, the first by Mr Durand of the Steam
cathen, a draft for £10 which is to you I bid it.
The Blue Glass could have been procured had I not your letter around Countermanning the Order.
But the Daguerreotype plates I found after repeated inquiries, and a consultation with the head of that
Department at the Polytechnic Institution, could not be safely purchased in England. Paris being
the place from which all are imported which are used here. After much reflection Mr Durand and
myself both agreed that it would be very unsafe to attempt executing your Order here -- and I request
him if he could procure Plates of great perfection to produce them then via Havre as directed in

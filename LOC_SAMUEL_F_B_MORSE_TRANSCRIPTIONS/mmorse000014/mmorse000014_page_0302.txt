By S.F. B. Morse,
Dear Sir:
Having recently returned to this
City after an absence of nine months, I deem it a duty
I should have discharged longer than this, to inform you of
the reasons why I have not fulfilled the pecuniary
obligation I am under to you for instructing me in the
use of the Daguerreotype. Notwithstanding all my efforts
I found the constant humidity of the atmosphere counter-
quent on incessant rains, baffled all my attempts
to succeed. I then relinquished that employment and
took a small school in the County which has furnished
me with a bare subsistence till now. My prospects,
however, are brightening somewhat as I have recently been
elected Classical teacher in the Cokesburg Manual
School School in this State. My labor in that Insti-
tution will commence with the 1st of February next, and
as soon as I receive the first payment of my Salary I will
forward the amount due you.
Yours with sincere regard and esteem
Charles Taylor

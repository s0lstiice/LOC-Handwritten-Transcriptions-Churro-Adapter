Adam College Nov. 17th 1840.
Prof. Morse
Dr Sir,
Your kind letter under date of
the 9th Inst. has just come to hand, having
been miscanied.
Mr. Dwight purchased but two
double convex Lenses which were far from being of
the kind -- This defect in the lenses has been the chief
cause of my trouble -- I was slow to charge my
want of success to this cause; having satisfied
myself, at length, on this point; I subjected them
to the examination of Mr. Young, who immediately
came to the same conclusion --
Should I come to NY --
could you furnish me a set in my Camera
a pair of lenses, achromatic, illuminating and
chemical Foci in one, such as to secure as
distinct results as you have been accustomed
to obtain with yours, or nearly so, -- If you can
will you be so good as to write me by return
mail, that I may visit you on before the river
navigation closes --

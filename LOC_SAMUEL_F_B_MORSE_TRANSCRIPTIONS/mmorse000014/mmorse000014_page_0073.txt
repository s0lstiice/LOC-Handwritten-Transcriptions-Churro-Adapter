1840
Montréal
C B Amyot, Pres
My Dear Sir,
Your letter of the 20th December
1839 enclosing one from the Baron Meyendorff has
been a long time on its way. I received it but a
few days ago and now take advantage of
the sailing of the G Western to reply.
I and my associates have suffered much in our pecuniary affairs in consequence
of the disappointment in relation to the Russian
contract and expenditures in England & France.
I was long kept in suspense by not receiving any
intelligence, and had prepared my affairs to leave
the U States for St Petersburg at the time fixed upon
by the Baron. From the negative of the Emperor
in connexion with the great pecuniary embarrassments of this country has resulted great delay

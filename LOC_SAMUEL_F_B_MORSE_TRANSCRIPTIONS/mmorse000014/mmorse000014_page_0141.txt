Do you ask, what form shall our efforts take, or not
to interfere with the political regulations of the country in which
our operations are to be carried on? Will missionaries be allowed
in Italy, will religious intelligence be allowed to circulate there?
What right have we to send them, contrary to the laws of that
country? Let our reply be that in pursuit of the Gospel to
every creature we obey a law superior to all human laws. Let
the attempt be made. Let us see if in obedience to a divine com-
mand, any government will oppose itself. Let us have the act
and the manner and reasons of a denial to our missionaries
of the same liberty taken by Austrian & Papal influences among
us.
But although for myself I should not propose
even for such a measure as this, and that the organization of our Society should embrace the design of sending
missionaries into Italy, yet it is not proposed as I understand it, as the principal object of our contemplated
Society. It is simply to collect the means for printing and circulating such works among the Italians which
are already in circulation here, as shall interest them in religious & scientific truth,
reform their customs of the nature of which a plan there can be no objections, American Christians will not object to it
at least in principle.

Philadelphia Aug 24th 1844
Some time since Mr Broadbent informed
me that Mr Broadbent & myself were about
to dissolve partnership, since then I have understood
that Mr Broadbent has actually taken up his
despation.
I wrote to Mr Couch over a week ago
soliciting his assistance in mentioning to you my
wishes to take Mr Broadbents place in your
establishment if it met with your approval & a
satisfactory arrangement could be made between
you & myself. I am extremely anxious to have this
city & the opportunity above mentioned appear
to me to be only one which I can embrace with
facility & if you have not a positive engagement with
any one it would be a source of great gratification
for me to form a connection with you as it
would no doubt result in a reciprocal & mutual
advantage.
I will be much pleased if you give
the matter a fair consideration & if your arrangements
for Mr Broadbents successor are not made I will be
much pleased to supply his place of the result
I will be much pleased to hear his return mail
Hoping of convenience to hear from you by return mail
I remain truly yours
Thomas J. Spivak
Ref S. H. B. Mail 98847

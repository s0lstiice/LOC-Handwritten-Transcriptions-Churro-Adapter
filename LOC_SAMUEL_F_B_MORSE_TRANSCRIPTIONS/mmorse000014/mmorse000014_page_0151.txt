Dear Sir,
I have received your letter explanation of the letter which
you wrote from Paris to me on the subject of Mr. Gouraud's
published against your expressed command, by which I am
with the intention of injuring me. I am not in your
favor. I am in the mind of my friends with the
deceitful conduct of that individual, I can easily
accept the apology of one like yourself who has had the
misfortune to suffer from the same base ingratitude.

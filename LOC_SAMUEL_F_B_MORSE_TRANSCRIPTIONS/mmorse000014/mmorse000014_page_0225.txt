Concord N.H. Aug. 17th 1821.
S. F. B. Morse Esq
Dear Sir
Mr. Wm. B. Hott of this
town a patriot painter & a worthy man, a
few months since received instruction from Mr. Plumt
of Boston in the art of taking lithographs after Mr.
Daguerre beautiful invention -- & purchased of Mr Plumt
apparently to prosecute the business -- but has been
previously disappointed in obtaining good plates
from Mr Plumt -- His plates seem covered with defects
& stutters -- which no burning, polishing or other means
can obliterate. He suspects some imposition in
Mr. P -- in order to keep the whole business in his
own hands -- & at my suggestion, will try to
get plates elsewhere -- can you consistently &
will you aid him in this particular, by selecting
some good plates for him or by remitting funds
for that purpose? Or inform him where good
plates may be had in N.Y. If so please inform

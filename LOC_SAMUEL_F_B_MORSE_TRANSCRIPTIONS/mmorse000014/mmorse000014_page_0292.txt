Now if this is not satisfactory send me a form or
I will see you in New York next week on Friday
morning at any place you may designate and
will endeavour to make it right. You ought to
know me too well, to suppose that I would say
any thing in the way of the success of the Telegraph,
even, if I myself should reap no benefit therefrom,
and certainly have the fullest confidence of your
perfect good faith and interest intentions in this
whole matter. I hope therefore you will go on
with all your might in the obtaining the prompt
aid. The Form which I have sent you I
am obliged to make myself in haste, and with
consideration so I must you will excuse me if it is
defective. There is no need of dispairing. Thealsan
or Genon will neither succeed. The Govt is thinking
about RRoad now and I fear that even yours may not
go through this term. But will eventually, we shall
see, I hope for the best.
I remain Yours Truly
Alfred Bail

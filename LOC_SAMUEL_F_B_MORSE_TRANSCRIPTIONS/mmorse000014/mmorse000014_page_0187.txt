St. Ivesnew
Oct 2nd March 27th 1841
My Dear Father
Your two kind letters were duly received - the
one made me quite first. I think on the whole I
shall not come till Mid - so please do father be at
the wharf to receive me, as I expect to leave no event.
It will be soon enough, and I do not want to be
in it. I longer than I can possibly help - you know
why. I can't. Should storm violently, why I shall be in
rather a bad fix - but I hope not - as I think she
have to wait till Thursday. Cousin She has very kindly
volunteered to take charge of me to the boat, so I
shall get along. He is fat one, and no appearance
of the invitations - but I hope they will get here
by Monday. I suppose that you do think of going
to Bath, would it not be delightful if Miss Ellinor
& Big Scrubbaard go too?
Aunt debbie love to you - says she shall not be
able to come to the wedding, because of her lameness
etc - she will write further soon. She has been very

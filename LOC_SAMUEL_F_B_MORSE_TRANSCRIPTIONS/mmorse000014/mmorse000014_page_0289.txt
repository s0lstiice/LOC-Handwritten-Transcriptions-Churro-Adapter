PS 3 Morn. 7
In from a letter
which has been received here from my
Brother Alfred I understand that he
has requested a power of attorney from
him and myself to dispose of the
Electro Magnetic Telegraph. As I am not
very definite in conveying to you the object
you wish to effect by having that power
and as he wishes me to act for him
I would ask from you an explanation of the object in view and I will
act accordingly
I am Yours Truly
George Rail
Stevensburg
Moore to Mr
Dec 9 1841

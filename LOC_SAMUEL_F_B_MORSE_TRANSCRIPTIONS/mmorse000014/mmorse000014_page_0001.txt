New York, 26th November 1839.
Dear Sir: Having received an intimation
that it would probably be agreeable to some
of the Members of the National Academy
of Design, to attend at the College of
Physicians and Surgeons, at times when
Demonstrations on the recent subject are
given before the class, they that you will
do me the favor to extend to the Academi-
cians and Associates of the Academy, an
invitation to attend at any time when
they may find it convenient -- my hour
of lecture is at one o'clock daily at the
College building 67 Broadway. Probably the
most interesting subject to the Members of
the Academy would be the exhibition
of their models, on which subject I hope to
commence early next week. I shall however
be glad to see them at any time. I am
always at the College the hour previous to my
lecture and if any gentleman of the Academy
wishing to attend will do me the favor of
calling upon me there I will furnish him
with a card of admission, which will
obviate any detention at the door of the
Lecture room. I also take pleasure in
showing you the Anatomical Museum, which
is a very excellent one.
Dr Todds has desired me to add that if any
of the Members take an interest in the subject
of Chemistry, he will be happy to see them in
this lecture room -- his hour is from five to six
O' clock daily except Saturdays. I am your obedient Servt
R. Mathis Jr

Shiloh Decr 15th 1841
My Dear Sir
Yours of the 15th came
to hand this morning, I reply to your
letter of some months since, and cannot
divine the reason you have not received
it. In that I stated my perfect willingness
to grant your request, on my own part but
as my brother is interested with me, and has
bome for me much of the expense, indeed
almost all. I could not do any thing in
the matter with out consulting him. I
reply to your letter to that effect and have
received no answer from you. I apprehend
however no objection on his part to grant
you any power in the matter which you
may desire. And I would suggest to you
the propriety of just dropping him a line to
that effect, and you may state to him
my perfect consent to any arrangement you
may be pleased to make for the good of
the whole Co.
I have recently given considerable thought
to the subject of the Telegraph, and was
intending to get permission of you, if there
is any thing to the contrary in our articles

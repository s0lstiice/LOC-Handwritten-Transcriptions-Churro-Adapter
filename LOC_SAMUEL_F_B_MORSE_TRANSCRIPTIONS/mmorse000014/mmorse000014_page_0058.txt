Phila Jan 7th 1840
Friend J F Bellmore
Dear Sir It is many a
day since I last had the pleasure of seeing
and conversing with you, and if I am not
mistaken it is as long since any communication
have been exchanged. However I trust it will
not long be so. When I last had the pleasure
of seeing you it was when on my way to Phila.
at which time you had the kindness to show me
specimen of the greatest discovery ever made with
the exception of the Electro Mag Zet. -- By the by I
have been thinking that it is time money in some
way was made out of the Zet. and I am almost
ready to order an instrument made, and to make
the proportion to you to exhibit it here. What do
you think of the plan. -- If Mr Brack will make me
a first rate most perfect machine, and as speedily as
possible, and will wait up a nine months for his
pay you may order one for me. It must be
made and finished for Phila. eyes. -- Mr Taylor has
taken several views with the Bar. which appear
very well. He is determined to excel in the art. I
must be short in my letter, and beg you will
answer soon and let me know about matters &
things in general. Please give my kind remembrance
to your brother I remain yours truly to
Alfred Bail

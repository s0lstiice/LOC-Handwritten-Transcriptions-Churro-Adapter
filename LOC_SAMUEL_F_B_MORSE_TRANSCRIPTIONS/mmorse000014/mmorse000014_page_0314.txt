Two thousand a year is not deemed in our cities a very great fortune for a family; yet with economy an artist may feel his mine at ease, and free to engage in the mental absorption of an Historical Picture.
Look now at the circumstances in which I had placed myself in occupation, consisted at most of 500 dollars p. Annium from the Association. It must be clear to any one that unless I had other means to depend upon, the enterprise must be abandoned.
I nevertheless commenced my preparations, and around the large canvas, studied my subject, "The Signing of the First Written Constitution at the Mayflower Nov. 11. 1620," not the Cabin of the Mayflower (as you have called it,) made my sketch of the groups, visited Boston, Plymouth, Provincetown, and the Cape, and library at Worcester Va, &c. of the materials for my picture. One instalment was paid in without then being delinquent, the second was paid in, then being delinquent, the third was also paid in without then being delinquent, and no new subscribers; The small sum on which I depended would not bear this diminution, and it was time for me soberly to count the cost, before I proceeded any further.

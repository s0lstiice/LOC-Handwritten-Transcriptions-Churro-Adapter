My dear Mother,
Just before I left Florida I received your letter of September 14th and had I not been
on the move and sick ever since, I should have sent you an answer before this time. I was happily situated in
Florida and without business. I may say quite influential in the Southern District. Such as I am in
every body, and was well established in business, I found my healths declining and had to flee
from all business expeditions – I went down the south side of Cuba, and to Jamaica but in the
latter place I found the island in a very precarious state, even things growing except tobacco,
who had his independence and was enjoying it his own way. In Jamaica land is cheap
and plenty, the wants of the negroes few and very easily satisfied, and why should he continue
to work in a gang under a task master, to enrich another? It is not natural – You do

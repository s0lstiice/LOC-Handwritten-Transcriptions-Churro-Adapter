[Dec. 25, 1841]
Dear Sir,
I take great pleasure
in giving, at your request, my
opinion of your head of Wash-
ington. I think it ranks in
expression among the best I
have seen. While it resem-
bles Houdon's bust, which is
generally considered the best
of Washington, you have given
into the features of the hero
thrown a spirit which better
corresponds with his character.
Houdon's is Washington asleep
in Washington on the eve
of battle. -- Therefore Cervi's
Washington is the hero transla-
ted into bad Italian. I never
was pleased with it. It looks
more like a companion in the
plot to his gunpowder plot
than the Christian soldier and
patriot, which the ill-fated sculpt-
ur was incapable of conceiving.

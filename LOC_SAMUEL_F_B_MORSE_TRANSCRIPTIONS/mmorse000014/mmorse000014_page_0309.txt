[cir. 1841]
After the piece alluded to in the Minor had appeared
the following, was prepared as a reply, but never sent
as upon second thoughts, I deemed it less ostentatious
privately to return the sums subscribed to each subscriber,
accordingly a private circular was prepared, and the res-
spective sums enclosed, with a receipt to be returned. I had
the gratification, of seeing the name of every subscriber
affixed to their respective receipts.
Sam. F. B. Morse

Address of the Directors of the Society for the Diffusion of Useful & Religious
Knowledge in Italy, to the Christian Public. —
In presenting the claims of a new Society to your attention, it is
due to you that it is the object and aim of the Society for which have emulated in
its assignment to humanity should be clearly spread before you.
The object in one word, is to express in the voice of the Society. We wish to make a concentrated effort to spread intellectual and religious light in a country hitherto entirely neglected by the philanthropy of the Christian world. Well may it ever be
wonder that a country, which has been civilized in the history of the world
once the light of the world, and the tomb of civilization, sending forth as from a channel
house its pestilential vapours into our borders

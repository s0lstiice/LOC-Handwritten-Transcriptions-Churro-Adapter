My Darling
I found my name borne as an honorary
member upon the rolls of your valuable Institution
several years ago. Since then I have not had
the pleasure of meeting you, nor of thanking
you for this mark of kindness which of
course I have attributed to your personal
friendship rather than to any other distinguished merit.
I did intend to have sent something
as a humble offering to your exhibition
but have never felt the necessity of even
production which I had previously intended for
this object. I have lately however finished
a full length portrait of Genl. Scott and
it is now in New York. I believe it is to
be engraved by Harpers &c. and it is
probably at this office. I sent it away
unwittingly for I felt that it was not
even equal to what I myself was
capable of. All this time liberty has been

Quincy
Massachusetts
Wednesday
1861
12 o'clock
night
to
the Governor
of the United States
Government
in Washington
Dear Sir:
I have the honor to
present to you the
following report of the
United States
Army in the Department of
the Missouri River.
During the past
month the enemy
have been in possession of
Fort Scott, Missouri
and have been making
excessive demands upon
the garrison for
provisions. The enemy
have also been making
excessive demands upon
the garrison for
supplies of clothing
and ammunition.
The enemy have
also been making
excessive demands upon
the garrison for
the purpose of forcing
the garrison to surrender.

As to the Telegraph I know not what to say. The
delay in finishing the apparatus on the part of Broch
is exceedingly tantalizing and vexations. He was to have
finished them more than six months ago, and I have borne
with his procrastination until I utterly despair of them
being completed. He owes rent to more than double the
amount, and he can neither pay it nor make a return in work
as he agreed. I suppose he has not the means, and I do not therefore
like to press him and yet I and all concerned are suffering
from his delay. I suppose something might be done in Wash t .
next season, if I, or some of you could go on, but I have
expended so much time in vain, there and in Europe, the I feel
almost discouraged from pressing it any further, Only, however,

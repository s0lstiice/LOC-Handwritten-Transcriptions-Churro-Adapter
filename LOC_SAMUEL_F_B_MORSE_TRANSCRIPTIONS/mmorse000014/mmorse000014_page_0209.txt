The family have been very polite to me. Still they discourage
him, by telling him he stands but a poor chance -- so long
an interval must elapse before we can be united -- and I
Shall be placed soon, in such different circumstances.
I know that he loves me truly -- and would be willing to
make any sacrifice for me. Lewis he wrote that letter, I
have received some more notes from him -- and he is willing
to have me go abroad free, and if my affection does not
change, to be engaged on my return.
Then is brother Charles? Why will you not let him come
to us with you, dear father, and leave me home to
see him? His friends and relatives would be delighted
to have him. -- So bring on your Daguerreotype -- for
then are a good many want to have their portraits
taken, by you. -- I hope you will at last call
Mell Balliett at No 4 Howard St on your way here
because they will think hard of it, if you do not.
Saint Parker too, would like to see you.
Is Uncle Liberty in St. Louis now? I heard admit
Kate, and he intended going there soon to visit.
Uncle should have had a long letter from me before
now -- but I presumed she was away from the city.
I think of her often, till her -- and love her the same as
ever. -- My health is good, and I am very happy.
I often fear I am not grateful enough to my Heavenly Father
for all his blessings done. -- Did I tell you Joseph joined the

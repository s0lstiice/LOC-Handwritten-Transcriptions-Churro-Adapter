Monday morning.
I have kept my letter open, the father in
hope Uncle Char will arrive before the post falls
off but still he disappoints us.
Uncle Richard only accompanied him to
Jersey for fun as Potash will soon be back
tomorrow. I will write you again by
the return of 9 and let you know if
Uncle Char has come thus. We are all
well this season. Please excuse this
shortness of words.
From the room off Chytn.
Isaac

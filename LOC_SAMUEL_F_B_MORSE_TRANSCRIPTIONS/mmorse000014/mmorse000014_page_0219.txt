140. Representative
Washington June 24. 1841
S. F. B. More Esq
Sir! I have known Isaac M. Coffin Esq
for many years and believe his integrity and
opportunility in any business he may attend to
may be relied on - he has had much
experience in relation to claims before the two
houses of Congress and from my knowledge of
his energetic attention to such matters, believe that
your business would derive much advantage
from his undertaking it -
Yours de
Rott: Mc Clellan.

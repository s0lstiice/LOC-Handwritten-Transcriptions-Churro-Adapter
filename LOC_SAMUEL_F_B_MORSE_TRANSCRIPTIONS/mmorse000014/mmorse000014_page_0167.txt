them out, but think I use it strongly when necessary. I put
2 quarts water to 12 pounds. I had so little practice with
you that I have to feel my way out in my judgment
in most things. With all my other troubles, I am
afflicted with what is called a "Pain around my right
first finger" the one above all others. I must want the use of
one hand to write and do every thing with the other hand.
As soon as I get to earning something I will give your ballance
paid.
Very Truly Respectfully Yours, D. G. Johnson

The American Foundation
Society for the promotion of Religious & Scientific
knowledge among the Italians. —
Object.
The object of this Society, is to give to the Italians in
Italy and other countries, Religious & Scientific knowledge
by the dissemination of books, pamphlets, & newspapers,
& established schools for their education. —
Means.
To defray the expenses of the Society, contributions
are asked from every subscriber the small sum of
Six cents per Week. —

Hon. John Blair,
Senator from Mich.
My Dear Sir,
I take the liberty of drawing your attention,
to the enclosed letter from Paris, which I cut from
The Observer of Jan. 1st. I feel that I may safely
confide to you the name of the author of that
letter. It was written to me by one of the Ministers at
the court of France. He is a property there, and he
requests not be publicly known as He sends it for publica-
tion but of course would not be publicly known as
its author. I can scarcely believe that such an outrage
on American citizens as that committed by France at the
Sandwich Islands is to be passed over without notice
by our Government because these excellent men and women
are Missionaries. If it is not demanded from France,
that if we submit to be thus treated without even remon-
strance, where is this to end? It is established that
the name of American citizen is no longer a protection from

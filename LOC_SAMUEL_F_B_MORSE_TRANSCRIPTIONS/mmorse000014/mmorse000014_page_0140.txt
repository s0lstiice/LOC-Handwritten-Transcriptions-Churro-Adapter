Have you not thought if you have not said, the doors here
are completely shut? The political door is guarded by foreign
banquets, and the strong holds within are garrisoned by
thrust bands whose
clothes are of detect the least
of their encroachment on their dark domains? Have you
not indulged in an unbelieving spirit concerning Italy? Have
you not deemed her to have, in superstition, so chained by a
spiritual despotism, so doubtly bound with a political
and ecclesiastical chains as to be beyond the reach of
redemption? If human strength alone were
our dependence, well might we despair. But our strength is
not in man but in the Arm of the Lord, in a power mighty to the pulling
down of strongholds, and in that Spirit whose breath is to consume
the chains of the captive, and to give the brightness of his coming.
But Christian friends, in the Providence of God a way
seems to be opened, and an invitation to enter upon this
very field, so forbidding, so apparently hopeless. It may
not be known to you all, that we have in our midst some
of Italy's noblest sons exiled here for the crime of patriotism
and playfulness who have suffered imprisonment, for desiring their
country's regeneration, they have dwelt with us long enough to
study our institutions, they have discovered the influence
which binds harmoniously all the interests of our republic. They
have learned that it is not in form of government, but in
the spirit of the people that freedom exists. That no
people can be free but as religious people, an educated
people. They have discovered that physical revolution is
useless without a moral revolution; they know that
superstitious people cannot be free. Hence for the first
time they ask for Italy religion, light intellectual and

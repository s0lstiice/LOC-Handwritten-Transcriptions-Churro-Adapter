will abide by your opinion -- I have wanted for some time past
one of those large black red Skirts with fringe -- that the Ladies wear
so much in their cloys. Almost every young lady has one been, & I
find they are very convenient indeed -- still as it was more a luxury
I concluded I would do without one -- seeing I must draw up on Harriet's
liberty for the Unit. I think now I can afford to get one, and dear
father, if it is not too much to ask -- some day when you are going down town
with you get one one -- I should like to have Harriet Pitts judgement
about that dress -- not so much if you would call on her -- she would
accompany you, & know with pleasure. -- I do not wish one over
seven or eight dollars -- and please take the money from Harriet. Much will
& Charles would take it to Albany -- she would send it next day by Mr
Tucker -- I feel very to trouble you -- perhaps if you give the money to Harriet,
she would give it to some one, and purchase it, without troubling you to ask
-- pay her -- I must write Harriet Charles again -- and thank her for
this very pleasant & unexpectative present -- You speak of a letter you
have sent me -- I have not yet it yet, but presume I shall be informed
when the penny post comes -- We have had very hot weather here for
a week past, but last Sunday there was a Local Thunder Showers -- & the
day the air is cool & invigorating -- Early this morning Miss O's cousin called
in a carriage & took us all out to the Rocks we rolled half way up the
Mr -- then got out & walked up to the top, & the little house there is built
then for the recreation of visitors -- We came back, Branch pleased with
our game -- The Taylors say, after I have done here -- I must come &
make them a visit & then Margaret Sherman wants me to visit her,
do you must not expect, dear father -- that Miss Davis will be ready
to return quite so soon, as part often commerce -- You had better
let me remain till cool weather -- for my health is so good here --
But we will talk about that, when you come up -- We have plenty
of fine tea -- & flowers as Mrs J. has a fine garden --

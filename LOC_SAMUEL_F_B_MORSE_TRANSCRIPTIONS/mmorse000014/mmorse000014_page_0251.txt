RECEIVED, New York, Decr. 11th 1841, from Mr. Samuel F. B. Morse, Fifteen
dollars, being the aggregate sum collected on this quarterly instalments of my subscription to a fund
raised in the year 1837, for procuring from Mr. Morse a large Historical picture, which sum is by the above pay-
ment refunded by him in full.
N.B. Clayton
Direct to me at the N.York Observer Office.

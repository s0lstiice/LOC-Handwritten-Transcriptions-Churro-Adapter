Post Office of Washington & Baltimore and that the Magnetic
line be extended from the depot in Baltimore to the Post
Office as early as practicable and that it be used at its
present location until that is effected.
That the Offices in Baltimore & Washington be kept
open for the reception and transmission of Dispatches from
8 o’clock in the morning until 10 o’clock, from one P.M.
until 3 P.M., and from five P.M. till Seven P.M. each
day Sundays excepted.
For the transmission of each Dispatch there shall
be paid in advance at the Office from which it is sent
by the applicant one quarter of one cent for each Telegraphic
character. Upon the reception of a Dispatch at either
Office it shall be the duty of the Officer to have the same
translated in a fair hand writing carefully envelopped
and sealed and the magnetic characters immediately de-
stroyed, and to place the Dispatch in the hands of the
funny post for Delivery who shall be entitled to receive
the same compensation therefor as for the delivery of
letters transmitted now by mail.
It is further ordered that the said Superintendent
and Assistants in no case communicate to or permit
to be seen by any person the contents of any Dispatch
except the individual or individuals to which it
may be addressed.

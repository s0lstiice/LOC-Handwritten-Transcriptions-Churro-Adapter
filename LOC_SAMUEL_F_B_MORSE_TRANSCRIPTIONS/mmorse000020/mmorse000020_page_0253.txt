106. Ct. Russell St
Bedford Square
Oct 17th 1845.
To, Professor Morse,
Sir,
I have been waiting
an answer to a letter sent to
Lewis Cabbitt, Capt. who is out of town
or should have written before –
Messrs Bowden & Son have requested
me to write you in doing so I beg
to state the position we are in
In submitting our Plan all wish
to know whose system we intend
to adopt could we positively state
that yours would be used we should
then have that part fully understood
and if you would leave anyone
with authority to carry it out we

The work in due course cannot be ready
for publication for perhaps six weeks yet, and
as I told Mr Kendall, I have no wish to
throw any obstacle in your way and will do
what I can to relieve you, but I certainly
think you are entirely mistaken in regard to
its producing any effect one way or the other
upon your prospects in Europe.

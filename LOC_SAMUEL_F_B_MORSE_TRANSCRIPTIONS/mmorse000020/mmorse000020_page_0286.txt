TÉLÉGRAPHIE,
Cabinet
de l'Administrateur
en chef.
Paris, le 6 novembre 1842
Mon cher Monsieur
I thank you for the kindness you have extended to me in inviting me to
visit your admirable apparatus in action. I will profit by it, and I shall
be greatly obliged if you will permit me to visit your factory. I am
constantly with my thoughts on the subject of the telegraph, and I
have been at work on a plan for transmitting intelligence by means of
the telegraph. I am sending you a copy of it, and I hope it will be
found satisfactory. I am your obedient servant,
Gen. S. Morse
Superintendent of the Telegraphs of the United States.

The Telegraph in the U. States is in rapid progress, and in a few months
I hope to communicate to you some agreeable results. I think my
system the most efficient and simplest ever devised, and I hope
it will be thus esteemed by your great nation, and by the people
of America the most distinguished in the world.
With the highest consideration
Yr Obedient Servt, G Bmore

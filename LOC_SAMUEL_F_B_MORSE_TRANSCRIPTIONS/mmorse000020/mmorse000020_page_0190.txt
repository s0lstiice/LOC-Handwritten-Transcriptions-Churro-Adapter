When you get to England you
will have to instruct Wheatstone
in the operation of your Telegraph, as
I think he will find some months
practice necessary before he can operate
an instrument to compete with yours.
I shall be on the look out for everything
relating to Telegraphy, either by
Land or Sea.
My Telegraph Dictionary will be in
from the press by first of September.
Where shall I forward one for you
Good bye, & may Heavens choicest
blessings attend you. Mrs R. desired
when I wrote to you to say Farewell
Mr. Cleveland & Louis Pantz: des
to be remembered, & may you have
prosperous gales, & a speedy voyage
Very respectfully
Your Obedient Servant
& faithful friend
Henry J. Rogers
P.S. Be sure and write me, & keep
me posted of your whereabouts, for
I want to herald your success.

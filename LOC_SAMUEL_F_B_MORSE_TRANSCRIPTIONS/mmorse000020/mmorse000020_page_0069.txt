Extra from the Journal of 7th April 1845
"Ordered, That S. B. Morse, Superintendent of
Tele. Mag." Telegraph, be authorized to rent
a room in the Telegraph in the Baltimore
Post Office building for one year, at $120.00 a year;
that he be allowed to incur expense in fitting
up the room to an amount not exceeding
$80.00 and that a sum not exceeding
$100.00 be allowed for conducting the Mag-
netic lines to said room."

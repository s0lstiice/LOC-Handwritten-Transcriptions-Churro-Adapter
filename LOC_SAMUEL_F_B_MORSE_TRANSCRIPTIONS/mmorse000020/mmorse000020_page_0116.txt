Boston. 22d May. 1845.
My dear Sir –
I received last Steamers a
letter from one of the principal London Publishers
requesting me to procure from you a description of your
Telegraph & its modes of operation, etc.
If you aid disposed to furnish me with material
I should be happy to prepare a scientific account of
the invention to be published in Weales Quarterly
Papers on Engineering, a work of the 1st Class –
I think such a mode of recording your invention
in a standard publication, will be but an act of
justice to your own fame & family,
Let me hear from you on the subject –
Yours ever true
J. W. Lewis –
Supreme Court

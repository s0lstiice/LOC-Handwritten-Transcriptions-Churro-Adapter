San Francisco July 9 '45
My dear friend
Although I was not a
debtor still I hoped for a letter - I should
not however wait to see how the answers
stood if I had found license on this to
write you - I am very comfortablely fixed
here. A Dutchman her wife a boy
next house for me where things lived.
I am well taken care of, and enjoy
myself as well as could be expected here
with your family and other dear
friends - I think however I in the hands
of President Grant has devoured me here
& I pray for his blessing - my new home
is almost done - it is large, very convenient
and good looking - so to the day - it will
contain more conveniences than
any house we ever lived in -
The climate is most excellent - the
water - land and air are satisfactory
& San Francisco is improving fast enough

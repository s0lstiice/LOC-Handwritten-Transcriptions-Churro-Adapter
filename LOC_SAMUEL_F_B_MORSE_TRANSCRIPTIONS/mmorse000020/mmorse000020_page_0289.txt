Washington Nov 2d 1845
My Dear Sir,
Your invaluable favor of the 18th ult. is at
hand, and I hope your views in England may
on this matter you, be fully realized.
Our progress on this side of the Atlantic is as
follows:
The entire line from Albany to Buffalo, a
distance of 224 miles, is under contract, and will
probably be finished in 60 to 90 days.
The line from Boston to New York is in the
same condition.
A line from Boston to Lowell and another from
Buffalo to Lockport, are nearly or quite com-
pleted.
A line is building from Louisville to Harv-
ings to be carried westwardly to Petersburg
and Wheeling.
The line from New York to Philadelphia is
already finished. House has obstructed us in
getting funds to bring it on to Baltimore;
but a letter from New York by the last
mail informs me that $700000 have been
subscribed, and it may expected the balance
would be made up by this day.
Bolt is building from New York to Long
Island.

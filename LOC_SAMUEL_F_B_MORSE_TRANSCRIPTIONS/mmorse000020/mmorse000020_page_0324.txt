Philadelphia, Dec. 23, 1845.
Professor Morse, Washington:
Dear Sir: You have doubtless learnt from me
how all the difficulty, delay & disappointment
to which myself & associates have been sub-
jected by relying on the inventor of the "Relay Mag-
nets" furnished by a fellow to whom I applied
solely because, as he was in the employ of Morris'
Telegraph, I supposed he would give me most
particularly what was right in your plan.
I learn now from Mr Paul that your instruments
are in Washington now with you -- those which
you will allow us to use, or buy. As I have written
Mr Cornell that we hold his magnets & instruments
"subject to his order," we can do nothing more till we
receive yours. Please therefore send them without
the delay of a car, to Philadelphia -- where Mr Davis
(our electric operator) can be informed by Mr Baird
on any subject useful in putting them up at
Lancaster, Hantsburg, & this city. Our Lanc.
& Hantsburg has been ready now about 3 weeks --
& all worse than lost (the time) for want of
proper instruments. Direct to Charles G. Odell,
case of Mr Bellaker, Railroad office, Philadelphia "for
Telegraph" -- if they will come right. Please mark

Washington March 4th 1845
Dear Sir
I propose to undertake the business matters
connected with the interest of yourself and associates
in the Magnetic Telegraph, except Mr. Smith,
for a commission of ten per cent on the sum
or sum which may be realized by you and them
from the sale of the Patent Right or any part
thereof to the government or any other pure
owner or purchasers, and of any profit which
may arise from the use thereof by the Trans-
missioners after defraying expenses of construction,
repairs and management, provided that if the
sum realized for your nine shares shall exceed
one hundred thousand dollars then any
commission shall be fifty per cent on said
profit, and the same ratio of commission shall
apply to the interest of your associates, Messrs
Grusse and Neil.
Intending that this business will occupy
only my whole time, I should be unwilling to
undertake it for a contingent commission or low
as ten per cent but for the hope of obtaining
an adequate compensation by involving the
sum to be realized beyond your anticipations.

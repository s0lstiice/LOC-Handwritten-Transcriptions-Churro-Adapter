A contract has been made with George
De Someroy Esq. by which he has undertaken
to get up a Company to construct the line
from New York to Boston. Much confidence
is felt in his ability and disposition to
effect the object, and it is expected that
a portion if not the whole of this line
will also be built before next January.
An arrangement has been made with Har-
ry Ridley Esqr. by which he has undertaken to
get up a western line through Pennsylvania
to St Louis bringing to St Louis and the Lakes.
Double can entertain no better he will accomplish

Dear Sirs,
You will undoubtedly ask you, what is
the reason for the omission of the
name of the Committee of the
House of Commons in the
letter to the President of the
Board of Trade. I will not
answer that question at all.
It is a question which
should be settled by the
Parliamentary Committee.
I am, Sirs, your obedient
servant,
George Washington
President of the United States of America

New York 14th April 1845.
My dear Sir
We expect to close tomorrow an arrangement
for the construction of a line of telegraph from
Washington to this place. The substantial terms
are the same as in the project I proposed at
Washington: viz. one half of the stock to the subscri-
bers. But some changes in the details are agreed
upon, such as limiting the amount of stock from
$300,000 to $400,000, naming the Trustees in the
article of agreement and leaving them to raise the
subscription, and some other immaterial points whether
the stock will be raised at once or some time
will be taken up in the operation, I am not yet informed.

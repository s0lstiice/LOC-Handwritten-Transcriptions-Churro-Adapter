The main object of the Transatlantic
Submarine Telegraph Company is by a main
Submarine line stretching across the Atlantic
(from either Milford Haven or the Western
Coast of Ireland to the point of the American
Coast of Nova Scotia or the Canadas, for
uniting on the most liberal basis all the
various Electric Telegraph Companies of
America (already stretching over some 40,000
Miles of territory) with all the various telegraph
Companies in Europe, either belonging to the
several Governments, or private Companies,
extending from Great Britain across the
Continent of Europe to the East & India,
The more fully to ensure this object the
Projectors in Europe & America engage
(apart from all special or private interest
as original Inventors or projectors of the
various Electric Submarine Telegraphs
which they have executed or are engaged
in carrying out under concessions of the
diver Governments in America stretching
from Canada on the North East
to the extremes of America on the South
and West, and from Great Britain
across Europe to the Mediterranean,
India & the Pacific Ocean.)

Washington, March 12, 1845
Mr. Secretary of War:
I have the honor to ask your early attention to an amendment
to the Civil & Diplomatic appropriation bill, setting apart
8000 dollars, to defray the expenses of the Telegraph between B.
& W. Some documents concerning which I left with the Department
yesterday. I give below a statement of the monthly expenditures
for six months, which was handed in to the Comr. of Commerce, and
which was the basis of the appropriation of 8000. In July &
Oct. there were some outstanding bills for construction which
swelled the amount of expend. in those months beyond the
average; so that 8000, p. Ann. is deemed sufficient.
I would respectfully state that the Telegraph
is suffering for want of its regular attention. My em-
ployees are men with families, and have been without pay
for a month anxiously waiting the action of Congress, &
that they may know what they are to expect. The Service
is of a peculiar kind and has required study & careful application
to acquire a knowledge of its details. One of my most
efficient assistant in Baltimore is seriously thinking of
leaving from necessity, and without him in the present state
of things I could not well spare him. With these representations
of the urgency of the case, I am sure not doubt the
earliest attention.
Very respectfully
Yours respectfully
100105
Geo. Mason, Secy of War
to Hon. Cas. Johnson P. M. B.

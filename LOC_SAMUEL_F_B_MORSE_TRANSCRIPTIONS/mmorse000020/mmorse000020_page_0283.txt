The average number of points or stops of the
11 letters represented is 3 1/2 for each letter,
and for 2 letters combined is equal to 6 1/2 points
for each pair representing the combination signals,
leaving 1 1/2 points in every signal in favor of my
combination. But as but 1 circuit is required
to make any signal instead of 2 in this advantage
in favor of my mode, most doubtless I retain your present
configuration for diversity, for economy in
lines, I thought it might be agreeable to you to know that
these signals may still be preserved, and if my system
should be adopted, and such an arrangement should
be desired.
I have my telegraphic apparatus in action at
my room, and if your time permits, I should
be much pleased to make some experiments on
this subject with you and Mr. Brequet, at such
time as may suit your convenience. I will
appoint on Thursday or Friday.
Dear Sir, With the highest consideration
Yr most obed. Servt. STAM.

Paris le 19th July 1841
From Your Eminence
I intended to send you a letter which I found yesterday
but this morning I had a visit from Mons. Masson, Referent
of the Telegraphic Department in Paris, who spoke on telegraphy &
occup'd himself with the new Telegraphy in Paris all
about the transaction -- to what happened between
Paris & Washington, Mons. Masson said -- of which I will give
you a short sketch which may show you a general
exaggeration of facts.
The French government has, all the systems of telegraphs
but the telegraph network was, that the signals passed
over an different from the system, as were employed throughout
France; and in order to have another system of telegraphy
is understood by the officers of the Telegraphic bureau, the French
government employed Mons. Pay's telegraph which is an electric magnet
revolving in two arms like the system above and makes the same sign --
that the sign passed through the electric telegraphs can be
immediately stated by the electric telegraphs.
So I am Mons. Pay's electricity as far, which works between
Paris & Washington -- it could, as I understand, be used for two arms revolving upon
a wheel dial -- and the signals made by means of levers in
Washington as in the telegraphs above, which requires considerable study
for the officer of the old system, and the government has at once a
number of officers who can be transferred from the old to the
new system without interrupting the operation of the system --
Therefore Washington, yours or nobody else's system will
be adopted.

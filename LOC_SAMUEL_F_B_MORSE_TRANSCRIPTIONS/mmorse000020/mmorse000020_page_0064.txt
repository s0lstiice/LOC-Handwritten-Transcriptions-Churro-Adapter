I see it announced in the daily papers that subscription books
are to be opened in Baltimore, Phila & in N.Y. to make up a stock
company &c but I am not informed as to anything further -- I would not
willingly absorb your time, but as I have been idle most of the time since
Oct last, I find it necessary to look for some point or prominence
not very far ahead on which I may rest my hopes. I am not willing
to enter again into the business of manufacturing for a length of years
&c, expecting to be re-called to Jefferson College in about 2 years
to take my old station again; thus, in my anxiety to make provision for myself and family, I have ventured to lay the whole
matter before you. If there is to be no tolerable prospect for me

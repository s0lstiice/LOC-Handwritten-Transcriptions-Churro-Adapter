6 Aldermanbury
4th Octr. 1845.
We have addressed two letters to you at the request
of Professor Morse, who gives us to understand previously to his
leaving England, that you would communicate with us on his behalf.
We therefore feel that, under the circumstances, we have not
been courteously treated by your taking no notice of either of our
letters: and we therefore now beg to state that unless we receive
a reply to our respective applications in the course of Monday next
we shall consider you declining having any thing to do with the
Matters, when we shall close all further negotiation with Mr. Morse
We are, Sirs,
Your very dutiful Servtts
Broadhurst
The Bates Coys

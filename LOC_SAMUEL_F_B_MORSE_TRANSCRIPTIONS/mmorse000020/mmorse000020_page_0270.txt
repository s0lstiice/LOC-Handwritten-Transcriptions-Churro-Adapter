I have added enough to show that in
the autumn of 1832 I conceived and planned an Electri Telegraph;
I will now show what that plan was
and the particulars in which it differs from all others
at that time devised.
All plans previous to the year 1832 contemplated numerous
conductors between the two termini. Reuss in Spain in 1798
planted at least 36. Salvas in Spain in 1798 was
constructed with the same number of conductors 36.
Socmmernig's in 1809 had 35. And after the discovery
of Oersted in 1820 of the deflection of the needle by a
Galvanic current Mr. Ampere in the same year suggested
a plan of which the following description is in his own
words. - autant d'ajustelles aimantées "etc." (See report
of Grad. Ind. Dins.) I am not aware of any other plans
from its earliest inception.
My plan on the contrary, contemplated but one
circuit, as will more plainly appear in considering
the next important difference which pertains to my

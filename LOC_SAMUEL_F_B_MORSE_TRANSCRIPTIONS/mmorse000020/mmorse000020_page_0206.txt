New York Aug 4. 1845.
Dear Sir.
It will afford me sincere pleasure
should you be able so to arrange your con-
templated visit to Europe, as to accompany
me on my Mission to the Courts of Sweden
and Norway, and while thus to receive,
as an inmate of my family, such kindness
and attention as I may be able to bestow.
I shall have it my power to render you
material assistance in the introduction of your
very valuable Telegraph to the various Countries
of Northern Europe.
I shall sail for Liverpool in the
"Ashtarton" packet on Aug 6th, and trusting
I shall find you on board, as "Attaché"
to the United States Legation at the Court
of Sweden," I remain with most respect
Dr J Hulb, Yours
D W Ellsworth
Chargé d'Affaires at
the Court of Sweden
P. Bosf. S.F.B. More
Sub. Elec. Maj. Telegraph

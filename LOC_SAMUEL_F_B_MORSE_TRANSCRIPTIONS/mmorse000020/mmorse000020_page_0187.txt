Dear Sir
Yours of 21st. duly received. House
seems to be very secret about his telegraph;
he has not yet applied for a patent.
You must be particular in getting your coils
made and have them at least as well made
as those of the instrument. I should make the
magnet bar of 3/4" or one inch iron. I do
not like your mode of mounting the lever.
A bar of iron so small should not have a hole
through it, and as you have it, it will not
be so steady as if the shaft (which should be
of brass) is a little extended on both sides.
Yrs Truly
Chas. G. Page
P.S. It would not be amiss to make the coils a little
longer say one inch, and an inch more in diameter

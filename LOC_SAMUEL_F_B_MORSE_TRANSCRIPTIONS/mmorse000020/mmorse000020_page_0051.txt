First
In Mr Smith's letter to P. M. Gen. March 24th 1845
he takes the following ground
He refuses no claim upon the government for 8000.
approp. for the use of Tel. for the current year, which I have
(P.M. 4/23) construction of appr. 15, accord payt. for it use
beyond the actual expense of managing it.
But if such a construction was adopted it is my right as Super-
intendent to have protested.
Answer. It appears to me this is already answered by the fact that the provisions of
the Telegraph
prictures as such are not protected by the provision of
the Appropriation Act. Mr Smith himself declares he has no claim on funds on
this act.

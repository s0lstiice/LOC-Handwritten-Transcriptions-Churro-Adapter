Dear Sir.
I just met a gentleman from Philadelphia
Mr Ch. Rath, who returns with the great
Victory, and promised to forward your enclosed
lines. I sent you yesterday a Mr. Cooper from Brussels
containing some account of my expedition of your Telegraph.
It was generally admired, and if that had been from
Mr. Wheaton, who came before me, I am sure
our management could have been used with the Belgian
Government, but I explained the necessity of
your invention, a hope when I return that, in
obtainable change to make experiments on some road. --
I am on my way to Leipzig & from there to Vienna, from
which I shall give you a long account of my
doings. --
I find a Selective Telegraph near Sir la Chapelle,
on near Mayence & Wiesbaden, all on the principles
of reading Discs, like those of Wheaton. --
The Ambassador of Austria at Brussels gave
me letters to Mitherniet, I stopped at Johannisburg
on the Rhine where I passed by, but I was not
there, and had only the pleasure to drink in his
chateau a real glass of Johannisburg. --

New York June 7th 1845
My Dear Sir,
We have not yet effected any arrangement
with the New Jersey Railroad; but we think
they will come to terms in a few days. It
is so clearly their interest that the Telegraphs
will be associated with them, I am satisfied
we have but to be patient and firm to ac-
complish all we ask.
With Mr. Butterfield we have made a
written contract and he has gone home to
organize his company.
Mr. Henry O'Keiley, who went up in the
Steamboat with Butterfield, has come back
full of the telegraphs, that he has pro-
pounded to get up the line from Philadel-
phia west to St Louis & Lake City. Mr.
Smith coming with me in recommending
that he be allowed three fourths of any
line he may cause to be built in that
direction in case our company decline fur-
ishing the fund on those terms.

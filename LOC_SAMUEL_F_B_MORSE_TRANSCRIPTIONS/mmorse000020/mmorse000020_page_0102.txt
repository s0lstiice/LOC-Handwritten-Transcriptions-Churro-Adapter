National Academy of Design
New York May 15. 1845
Samuel F. B. Morse resigns
Pres N.A.D.
Dear Sir.
In compliance with the order of
the Academy, I have to communicate to you the following
resolutions of the body, passed unanimously, at a special
Meeting held last evening (May 15th)
Resolved – That the thanks of the Academy are due,
and are hereby presented to Samuel F. B. Morse Esq
for the faithful and earnest performance of his duties as President of this Institution, from its earliest
foundation; and, that we deeply regret his unavoidable temporary absence from the City and the profession,
by which we are deprived of his valuable services
Resolved – That Mr Morde be requested to sit for
his Portrait to any artist he may select, size of
the picture to be twenty five by thirty inches and
that the Academy defray the expense thereof.
I remain very respectfully
Your Obst
Geo. F. Chapman
Secy. N.A.

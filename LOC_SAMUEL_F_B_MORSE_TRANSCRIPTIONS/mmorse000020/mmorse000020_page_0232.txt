66 Aldermanbury
September 24th 1845,
Sir,
We beg as the Solicitors and Registered promoters of the
General Commercial Telegraph Company for the satisfaction of
Professor Morse according to his wish to hand you the following
names, to whom we beg to refer you, on the part of Professor
Morse as a Guarantee for our respectability.
I am Sir
Your very obedt Servtts
for Messrs Bowden & Son
W D Dempsey
Self as Secry to the Compy.
R A Sprigg
To I Bates hesgts
Messrs Baring Brothers

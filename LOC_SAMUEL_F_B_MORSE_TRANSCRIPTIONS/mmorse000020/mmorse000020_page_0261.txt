22 Oct. 1845.
Professor Morse,
Sir,
Mr Dempsey one
of the parties to our Arrangement
is out of town on a Survey –
we expect him to return
Wednesday or Friday certain
as he that day has an Appointment
with the Attorney General as
such if he should return before
we shall immediately see him &
communicate with you –
I am Sir Your Obst
for General Conduct Telegraph Corps
Spring Field
100263
Professpr Morse
106. St Russell St
Berford St
Officer 66 Albermarle St

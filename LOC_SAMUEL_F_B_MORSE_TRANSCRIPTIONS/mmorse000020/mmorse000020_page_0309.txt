then proceed to Boston 36 hours distant. We
hope, therefore, to be in Boston on Thursday morning
the 4th. Edward is well, and we shall both
be glad to get home again. We have a large
statement made to contain four persons entirely
to ourselves, and have been as comfortable as we
could well be at sea. We are not full of
passengers, having only about 70 whereas the
ship has accommodations for about 120.
I have nothing special to add. Give my
love to Catherine & Elizabeth, and kiss the babies
for me, and remember me to Mary & if you are still
at 13 Brompton Sq. remember me to the family.
Affectually ever
Your brother
Sidney E. Morse, Esq.
Sam: F.B. Morse

Mr Rogers tell me he has written to you,
and given an account of the number of
letters which we have been able to write
per minute.
Every thing moves on as usual. There
is nothing new. Let us hear from you often.
Any new works upon telegraphs would be of
no service to me in this work, so that you
need not purchase any on my account.
I remain yours sincerely
Alfred Vail.
Note. From this passage it would seem that I had made arrange-
ments to advance one quarter of the expense of
publication of his work, to be repaid from the profits
of its sale. It is sufficient to say that no profits
were realized. -- My other occupations prevented my
attention to the matter. I never realized a penny from
the work, which was wholly under Mr Vails direction
and for his own pecuniary benefit.
J. F. B. Morse.

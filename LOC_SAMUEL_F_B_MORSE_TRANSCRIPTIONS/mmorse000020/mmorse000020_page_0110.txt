New Haven, Spring Bank,
May 19th. 1845.
Professor Morse,
Dear Sir,
Ever since you have
established the Telegraph, I have been very desirous
to know whether it had been used on the Sabbath.
Having no evidence that it has been so used, I
will take it for granted that it has not been so em-
ployed under your special direction.
But as the Government are to become interest-
ed in it (if they have not altogether become so,) I feel
peculiarly anxious that you should insist, in your
contest with them, as well as with individuals, who
may purchase or become interested in it, that it
should not be used on the Lord's Day, except in
cases of emergency say necessity or mercy—in which
instances there would be comparative speed.
I have, for many years, been using every means
in my power to prevent the desecration of that holy
day, in various ways—particularly by Mail, Steamboat,
canal Boats, Rail Roads &c. If I succeeded, I gave
you a paper relative to our K. Road being used on that
day. If not I send you one by mail.
I have spent much time, and money, for printing
postage, & traveling expenses relative to it, and I sometimes
hear that all my efforts will be lost.

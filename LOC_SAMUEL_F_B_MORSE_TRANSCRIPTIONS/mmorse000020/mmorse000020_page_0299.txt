Vienna November 14th 1845.
Dear Sir.
I received yesterday a letter from Mr. Clarenbach dated
17 Octob. Stockholm, stating that you had not gone to
St Petersburg as you intended, but returned to London.
In every moment I saw in Galibierne's message of
today, that three three Kings ready papers to the Academy
of Science concerning your Telegraph, which induced
me to believe that you are still in Paris -- and I should
have the opportunity to give you a short account before you
leave this Continent.
You keep my things in Brussels; -- from there I went to
Berlin when I arrived at the port, where many influential
offices were absent, and pushed for time I started for this
Place. The subject of Telegraph was then just before
the government and with the letters of introduction
to Prince Metternich I obtained immediately audience,
and he appointed a day to see the Telegraph; he was
exceedingly pleased, and requested me to show it to the
Minister of Finance Baron Hucholz; from that
quarter, had a long Conversation, in that subject with
him, and in few days afterwards I exhibited it to him, he
was like Prince Metternich very much pleased, and I did not
fail to show him the advantages of your own others.

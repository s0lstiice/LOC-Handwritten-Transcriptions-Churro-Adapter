Under these considerations I have taken
the liberty of asking for the foregoing
testimonial.
In conclusion I shall advertize
for pupils in monitors American a far longer class to
Commence on 15th inst.
During your trip to England give
if there is any thing new in the Telegraph
let us hear of it.
Very respectfully
Your devoted and
faithful Servant
Henry J. Rogers
P.S. Write me word about this
printing affair if you understand it –
I forwarded you a copy of the
American of this date containing
a reply to the article "Printing
Telegraph" & which has settled
the matter in this City, several persons
having conversed with me to day,
on the subject & expressed their
satisfaction by introducing the operation
of printing without type or ink.
Yours truly
H J Rogers

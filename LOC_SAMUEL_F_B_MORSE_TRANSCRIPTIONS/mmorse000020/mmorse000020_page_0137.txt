We have high winds now, quite like spring hurricans
and squalls of rain= I did at the same time
with up letter on for Charlie a month old & one
from Aunt Kate two months old. Where they remain
I cannot imagine – Well my dear father
I must close as I expect the vessel will sail
to day. Edward made a great deal of money by
the advance of the price in Bengal, but by sub-
sequent speculations finds fair to lose every
bit of it. The life of a merchant is very changable
These losses quite depress him at times – I fear this
far distant when we can come to America
again to live. Charlie sends you a kiss, and
wants to cut off another lock of hair, not thinking
one sufficient. All well in St Thomas & often
enquire after you – they send me many little
tokens of their affection. On his birth day his aunt
Helena made a pretty little pocket scarf & his aunt
Mann a little dress. They are very kind people.
I shall write by every vessel dear father – &
hope soon hear from you again. Edward desires
to remain with me as he has a little daughter

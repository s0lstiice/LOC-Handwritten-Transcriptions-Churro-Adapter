Do you recollect a person who, while under your hands
for a Daguerreotype in 1840 or, broke accidentally an eight
dollar lens? Is it many that you "visionary" in your
ideas of telegraphic communication, that person, you
may possibly recollect, took a lively interest in the mat-
ter, and made some suggestions about the propriety of
securing the matter energetically upon Congress & upon
public attention. You seemed then to feel pleased
to find a person who took so lively an interest in your
invention; and if you will see by the enclosed letter,
that that person (your humble servant) has not
lost any of his early confidence in its value. --
May you reap an adequate reward for the
glorious thought! I would thank you to inform me by return mail, by what day's train
the instruments may be effected here, addressed as above.
Yours respectfully

I have sent the 2 magnets for the Wheat-
line ordered by Mr. Wood. At his request I
made two sets screws to each magnet, for holding
the long thumb screws in a firm position. I
charged 25 p. addition for this, for each magnet
and 25 p. for packing each. He objected a little
to this at first, but I told him that 75. was
the price we had fixed upon for the magnets
made after the pattern furnished me, and if
I made additions I was obliged to charge a
reasonable price for them. Thus reasoning, he
became satisfied, but he has not heard from Mr. Eddy.

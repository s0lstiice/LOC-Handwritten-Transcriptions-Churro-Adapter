Chayama May 22 1860.
My dearasher
I have been sadly disappointed
this Winter Spring in receiving so few letters from my
friends & relations truly they seem to have had
enough of me in my last visit, and I am quite
forgetten. - From your dearest father, I have
received but two or at the most three letters,
a from brother thom - from aunts or uncles
not a line. By God's light I certainly expect
letters & it was a bitter disappointment, but
Charles was to blame in putting off letters, late
sending the things. I wrote for, & forwarding
letters (if there were any) - But I will not
take up the whole of this sheet in grumbling -
but tell you that we are all quite well in
old Suayama. I am thin having lost ten pounds since my arrival but nothing seems
to be the matter with me except debility.
Little Charles is very well - He sits by my
side at the table playing & has just scribbled

Mr. Bruguié having
been informed by Mr. Trago,
of Mr. Morse's presence
in Paris would feel very
happy for the honor of his
regaining, therefore, to take
the liberty of begging Mr. Morse,
to be so good as to favour him
with an appointment at the
time which could be of the
least inconvenience to him, he
will have great pleasure to
call upon him by that time.
Wednesday 29th of 8th 45.

New York 16th April 1845
My Dear Sir
I am sorry to inform you that our ex-
isted arrangement here has entirely failed.
Mr. Waddell agreed to our terms and was
to use the other capitalists he relied on
would do the same thing. After several
interviews, however, they came to a specific
proposition the effect of which was to leave
us one night only on the line from Wash-
ington to New York and take five nights
to themselves. I at once told them it was
unmercyous to discuss such a proposition;
that I was not authorized to part with
more than half and should not advise
you to enlarge my powers.
So we stand; but I am not discouraged.
Mr. Smith has answered with me thorough-
and professes a willingness to put all his
private means into this enterprise. I
would do it if I had any to put in
but could avail any thing.
I expect to be in Washington the day
after this reaches you and I will give
you all the particulars.
Most truly
Your friend
Amos Kendall
Pres. Senate F. B. More.

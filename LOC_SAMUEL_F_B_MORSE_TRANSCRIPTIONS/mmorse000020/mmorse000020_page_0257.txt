to Robt Walsh, U. States Consul Paris.
My Dear Sir,
London Oct. 26th 1845.
I write a hastily line just to say that I
have learned from Galjnnani's messenger of Saturday, last
that there is a commission of the French Govt. examining the various systems of Electric Telegraphs, and
that they have not yet reported. I have my telegraphs
with me, and intend visiting Paris with it, within a fortnight.
I have just written a line to M. Arago on the subject, and
I have told him that you have him a copy of my
letter to the Jos. J. of the Treasury, duplicate of which I
send you; asking you to send him one. They oppose
attending to it, which I am persuaded from personal examination
of Wheatstone's system that my system has the advantage not
only in simplicity but efficiency, and this I hope to prove
before the commission. I am prepared to prove priority
whenever that my telegraphs in extending in the U. States

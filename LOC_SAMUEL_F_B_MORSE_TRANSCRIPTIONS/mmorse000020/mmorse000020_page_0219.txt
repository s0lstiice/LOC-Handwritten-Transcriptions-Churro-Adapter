Washington Augt 24th 1845
Friend Morse
Your last letter was duly received.
Since your departure every thing has moved on
as usual. The office continuing open daily and
our receipts about the same. The wires have been
three times struck with lightning, but it so hap-
pened that it only stopped our operations for one
day. In two instances only one wire was struck.
These are the only accidents that have occurred.
Mr Rogers is well but Mrs R has been quite
ill- Cleveland Lewis & Elias are well. Mr Rogers
will have his book out in a few weeks.
I am glad to have it in my power to relieve
your mind from the erroneous impression which
you seem to entertain from the following reason,
given in your last letter, why I should not
publish the work which I have with seven
months hard labour and great expense been
getting up, namely, "Has the receiving magnet
and its necessity yet been published in any book
or paper"? I can unhesitatingly answer this
question by saying that "the receiving magnet
and its necessity" is no where described or alluded
to in the work. I studiously avoided it, and
I may repeat here what I stated in a former

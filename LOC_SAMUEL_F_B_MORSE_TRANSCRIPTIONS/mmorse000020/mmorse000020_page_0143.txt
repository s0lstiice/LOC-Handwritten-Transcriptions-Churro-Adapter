City of Albany N.Y.
June 11th. 1845-
General Robert Armstrong -
His. For the purpose of advancing the estab-
lishment of Morse's invention of an Electo
Magnetic Telegraph in England, the unders-
signed, proprietors of the invention will agree
and in this do agree, in consideration of
any sales or disposition you may make
for the use of it, to pay you thirty three per
cents of the proceeds of your sales. And in
the sales we are willing to have you exercise
your best judgment and discretion as to
the price, terms and manner of payment
from purchasers, relying on you doing
whatever shall affect, and you shall deem
but for the interest and advantage of
ourselves - but in no case shall any charge,
expenses, or debt be incurred for us or in
our name, socially or socially. And we
will at any time afford any full informa-
tion you may require to aid your efforts -

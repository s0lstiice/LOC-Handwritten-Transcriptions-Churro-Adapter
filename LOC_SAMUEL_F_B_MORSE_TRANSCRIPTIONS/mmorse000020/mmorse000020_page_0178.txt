I am just this moment in receipt of your
letter of 14th inst. and feel very thankful
for your kind testimonial in favor of my
Dictionary.
The more I operate with your Telegraph
the better I am convinced of its superiority
over any improvement that has been men-
tioned; and I am fully convinced satisfied
that we can, & will outstrip all Competitors.
The article enclosed by you will appear
in the American if occasion requires.
A certain James O. Connor has been out
in the American with a description of a
printing Telegraph, which, he claims as
original, I have told all my friends, if
he had been posted on Electric Telegraphy,
he would have made a much greater
discovery, by finding out, that, what
he claims as new, is nothing more than
the Telegraph used by Mr Bain in
England? I did not think it worthy
of further notice.
Your faithful servant
Henny J Rogers
Asst. Supr. U.S. Magnetic
Telegraphs.

They have determined & do determine,
that this great link of communication
between the old & new world shall be
freely opened in Europe to all Companies
equally & without partiality or preference.
The works will be proceeded with
so soon as the various arrangements are completed with the Governments of Europe
& the United States, and it is calculated
that the line might be opened for
communication within 2 years from
the present date.
Careful soundings have been made
for this purpose by the able Nautical
Surveyor Lieut. Many of the U.S. Navy
& preparations & Studies are in progress for
putting the Company into active Operation
during the ensuing year.

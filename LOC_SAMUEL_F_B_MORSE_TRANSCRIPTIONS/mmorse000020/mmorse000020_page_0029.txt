The U.S. Mag. Telegraph having been placed under the Direction
& Supervintendence of the Post Master General by the act of Congress
of March 3d, 1845, the Department has established the following
Regulations of times & rates of charges for postage for the transmission
of intelligence.
The Telegraph will be in operation every day (except Sundays)
from 9 A.M. till 3 P.M. and from 5 P.M. till 11 P.M. by telegraph

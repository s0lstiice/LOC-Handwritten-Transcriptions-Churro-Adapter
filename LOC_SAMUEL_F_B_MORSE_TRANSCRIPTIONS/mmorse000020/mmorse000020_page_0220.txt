Feeling an interest in the success of your visit abroad as well as a desire to do consistently what I can to forward your (to me erroneous) views, I stated the facts of the case to a number of persons whose judgement I know you to respect, among them the Hon Amos Kendall and Dr Page, the latter having read the proof sheet. They have stated to me that they cannot see what possible harm there is in its immediate publication -- Mr K in my conference with him thought it might be an advantage to publish it.
The work is not yet published and I cannot tell when it will be, I cannot accede to your proposition which a moment's reflection will convince you as impossible.
Mr Rogers and our whole force wish to be remembered to you. I trust your visit to Europe will be every way beneficial to you and our interests but knowing something of the English character & the French situation of things, I shall not be disappointed if you return as you left without accomplishing your object.

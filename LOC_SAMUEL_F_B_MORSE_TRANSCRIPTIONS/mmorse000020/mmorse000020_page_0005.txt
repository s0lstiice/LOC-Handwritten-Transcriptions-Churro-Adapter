Prof. Gale,
Dear Sir,
I send you a copy of an instrument
which Mr. Vail & myself have executed with
Amos Kendall, Esq. constituting him our Agent in
the transaction of all our business relating to the Telegraph
This course was advised by our most judicious
friends, in consequence upon particularly of the
movements of Smith who is the same as we.
Mr Kendall will devote nearly his whole time
to this matter, and we have made it as you will
see for his interest to make for us the rate
of $200,000 for the whole Patent right. We have
no risk, and have purchased the most competent man
in the country to manage such a concern, engaged
in our service, whose interest it is to secure us at
least at the rate of $200,000, and his pay is contingent
depending wholly on his success.
The power of Attorney you gave me Mr Kendall
thinks is not sufficient for me to sign your
name to the contract. I therefore ask you to have
one made in proper legal form before a Notary
with two witnesses, and transmit it if possible
by return of mail. I hope I shall see you
before many weeks in New York & I will then
have much to tell you.
Very Yr friend
S. P. B. Moser
Yr.
March 17th 1845

person. So that the power of Attys will not be needed. He will

13 Brompton Square Oct. 11th 1845.
Gentlemen. I would call your attention to the fact that
my stay in London is necessarily very limited, and
the negotiation with the Gen. Com. Telegraph company
in relation to my system of Electro Magnetic Telegraphs
must, therefore, be brought to a conclusion, one way or
the other, speedily.
I would not, however, have the Company hasten in
their decision, and if they are desirous of more time
to consider the matter, I would request them to
say so, and to name a time when they can give
me a definite answer to the proposals I made before
I left London for the North of Europe.
I have engagements in Paris which require me
to visit France as soon as possible, and if it will be
any accommodation to the Company in regard to
time, I will at once proceed to visit that country,
and await their answer till my return, although
such a course will materially affect that part
of the arrangements I had proposed, namely,
to initiate such person or persons, as the Com-
pany may designate, into the arcania of my whole
system. This will require a little time and as

Washington June 9. 1845.
Sir. In relation to the proportion of the line
allowed to any Contractor or Contractors for any
line secondary to the main lines of Telegraph,
I will authorize you by a regular power of Atta-
ney at any time you may desire it to grant for
me any proportion you may be disposed also to
grant, provided it is not more than three fourths
of any line which the Contractor or Contractors may cause to be
built, and provided our Company decline furnishing the funds on those terms.—
Alfred Vail
Pay. S.F.B.morse

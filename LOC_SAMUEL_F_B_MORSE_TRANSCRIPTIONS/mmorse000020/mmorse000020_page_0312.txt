New York Decr 3d 1848
Dear Sir
With this I send you a diagram which I
think you will find sufficiently plain to enable
you soon to understand the connection with
what they may learn from Mr. Wells, both
about the form of the bill and arrangement of the bill
I have given you this rough sketch showing
the position of the King's majesty's office
(which is to close the local account) as that
matter was wholly omitted in the Ladies book
and is an indispensable part of the telegraphic
arrangement
I have not made the references so full as to
explain the parts of the instrument in detail as I
suppose you will have some opportunity to
become familiar with them before attempting to
put a line on operation. Should they not
understand the subject, and with further
information I will cheerfully answer any questions they may make.
The troubles in the bill I send you I think
was not sent they were delayed by the manufacturer
they never sent over this evening however and will
be forwarded tomorrow.

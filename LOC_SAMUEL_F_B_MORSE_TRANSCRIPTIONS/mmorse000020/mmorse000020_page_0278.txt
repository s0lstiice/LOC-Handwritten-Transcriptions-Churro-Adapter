Paris Nov. 1. 1845
Dear Sidney,
Hotel Wagram
Rue Rivoli
I should have written you before
but my time has been so occupied that I have not
had the least time to write. — I arrived at Rouen
on Saturday evening of the day I left you,
after a delightful calm passage. We stayed over
Sunday at Boulogne and on Monday set out for
Paris in the Diligence, and riding all night
arrived here on Tuesday at 9 A.M. Finding
Maurice's hotel full, I came here, where I
found Cousin Woolsey, Louis Martha is well,
and so strong that she can bear the fatigue of
sightseeing, all day. They leave to-day for Nice.
I am still satisfied in verifying the fact
that my Telegraph is ahead of all the other
systens proposed. Wheatstone's is not adopted
here. The line from Paris to Rouen is not on
this plan but is an experimental line of the
Gov. Commission. I went to see it yesterday
with my old friend the Administrator in Chief
of the Telegraphs of France, M. Foy, who

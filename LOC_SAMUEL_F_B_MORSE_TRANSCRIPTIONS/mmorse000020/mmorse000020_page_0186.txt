I was informed that there exists here a cl. m. S. which prints
with the common letter and for books, by means of a disk upon
the rim of which are types, which rotates with the disk, and comes
to a station revolving disk where the letter required is opposite
the moving strip of paper, the letter is printed upon it by slight pressure
the printing is done like in printing.
It must reach through complications what does it gain
over yours nothing
1885
July 19

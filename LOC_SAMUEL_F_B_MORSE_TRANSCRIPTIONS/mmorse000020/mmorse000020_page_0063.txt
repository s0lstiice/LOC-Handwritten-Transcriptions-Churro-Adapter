New York April 4th 1845
Joseph Morse My Dear Sir
Yours of the 3d ult came to hand
yester eve, giving me information of the arrival of my papers
of power of attorney in due time, and of the manner in which
Mr K. is occupying himself. Thank you for the report of Prof.
Johnson; it is a most valuable document.
I am still unemployed except for a few days hence, and
should the enterprise of forming private companies for extend-
ing the Telegraph be successful, I should be glad to be a-
gain employed in it, and am confident you will do
all in your power to accomplish my desire, you will oblige

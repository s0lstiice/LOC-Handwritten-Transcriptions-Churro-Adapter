Please consult Mr. Neil as to the Western line
and see whether he objects to the terms proposed,
and also whether he is willing to offer
other lines of secondary importance, such as
Boston to Portland, to be built on the same
terms. In these cases it is proposed that the
amount to be refunded to subscribers in
case of a call to the government, shall
be 20 per cent over the cost instead of two
for one as with our Washington Co.
With great consideration
Your friend
Amos Kendall

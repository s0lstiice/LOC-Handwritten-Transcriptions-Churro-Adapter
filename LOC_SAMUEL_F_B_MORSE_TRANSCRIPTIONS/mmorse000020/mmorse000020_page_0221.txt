Russia has refused to adopt your plan of the
Electro Magnetic Telegraph, it comes from Mr
Bodiseo, so I presume you will not visit St
Petersburg— The fact is, Europe will not acknowledge
America in this case out of pride, I fear it, but
we shall see. It would give me pleasure to
learn of your whereabouts and what prospects you
see in the present or future. If any thing of interest
transpires I will write. You will probably be
informed of the unfortunate death of Mr Kendall
son's William by the papers, He was shot by John
C Keives brother in law in the street. The affair happened
about 6 P.M. Mr K was in N.Y. so the Telegraph
announced it to Mr Rogers who wrote the letter to
K which announced to him the melancholy news.
Wishing you intense uninterrupted enjoyment &
abundant success believe me to be your friend
and Servt
Alfred Vail

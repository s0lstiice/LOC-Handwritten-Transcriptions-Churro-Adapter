A line from Syracuse to Syracuse has been
disturbed by us, but I have not heard of any
thing done upon it. Some New Yorkers have undertaken to raise
funds for the entire line from New Orleans
to the first of next April.
The Buffalo line is to be extended to New York
or connect with the New York & Boston line.

on the minor lines, we are to have half the

I have had some disagreement with my associate,
but all is now going on smoothly.

Subterranean Inland Electric Telegraphs by means
of this telegraph any communication may be instantly
transmitted from London or any other place and de-
livered almost at the same instant of time at the
most distant parts of the united Kingdom or of the
Colonies.
The following are a few of the advantages
offered by this Patent
1st. The immediate communication of Government
orders and dispatches to all parts of the Empire.
2nd A. General telegraphic post office system —
uniting the chief and branch offices in London
in connection with all the offices throughout the
Kingdom at a fixed rate of charge and issued by
special messengers or the usual Post Office delivery.

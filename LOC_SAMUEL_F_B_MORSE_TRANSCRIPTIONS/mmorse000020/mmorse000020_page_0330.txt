Washington Dec. 24th 1845.
My dear Brother
Until this moment I have not had
a moment's time to write you since my return home.
I wrote you by the Britannia, however, from Halifax
so that you knew of my safe arrival there far in
a short time after I left you. I found all well in
N.York, and came on here with a little delay as
possible. Telegraph matters look very well, I can
hardly go into detail further than to say that, the
line from N.York to Phila. will probably go into operation
in a few days, and before many week the
entire line from N.Y. to Wash. will be complete.
The line from Buffalo to Lockport is in successful
operation, and the Posts from Buffalo to Albany
are all planted, they are nearly up all the distance
from Boston to N.York. – There are many other
lines all ready for operation for short distances. The
Post Master Genl. has recommended the purchase by Govt.
and before the Session is out I think some money will
be made and perhaps consummated before its close.
There are of course some crosses & difficulties, but
none of a serious character. Accounts from Austria
are most flattering; I think that Govt. is prepared

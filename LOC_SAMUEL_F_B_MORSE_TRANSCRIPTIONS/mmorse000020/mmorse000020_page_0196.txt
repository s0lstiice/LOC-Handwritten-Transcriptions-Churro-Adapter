New York, July 25. 1845.
My dear Friends.
Allow me to introduce to
you my friend, Mr. S. F.
Morse, author of the Elec-
tric Telegraph, who goes
to spend a few weeks
in Russia. You will find
an agreeable and most
worthy Christian friend.
Please to introduce him
to other Christian friends,
in St. Petersburg.
Yours truly
R. Baud.

21. Token H. Yard
Oct. 14, 1845
Gent.
On leaving my residence in Brompton St.
this morning I had not received any communication from you.
I am now in the city and not knowing if you may not
have sent me a note, this is to say that I should
deem it well to relate to the Telegraph. I shall be
happy to attend upon you at such hour as you may
think most convenient. It is of the utmost importance
that I should know without further delay what arrange-
ments are likely to be made between us.
Believe me with the greatest respect
Y. O. servt.
S. F. D. Mason
To Joseph Bowden & Son
66 Aldermanbury
Solicited to Genl. Com. Tel. 60

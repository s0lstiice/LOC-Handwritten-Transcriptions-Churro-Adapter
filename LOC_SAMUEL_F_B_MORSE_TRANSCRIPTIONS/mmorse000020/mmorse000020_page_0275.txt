I trust I may be spared the necessity of appearing in their public
as President of the United States, to displease the minds of any of their
present, should circumstances render it necessary, the
selects of the correspondence should be made
public, copies shall be transmitted to the Academy
of Sciences, and I am requested to convey my unqualified denial of
the statement in Jackson's undecipherable note to the distinguished
members of the Academy, that he is willing to admit into the Academy
the name of Mr. Elie Beaumont, from whom I received a letter which compels me to
state that the Academy is in a state of dissolution from beginning to end.

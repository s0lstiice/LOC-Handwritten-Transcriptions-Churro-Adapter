My dear Sir,
I leave with you my Telegraphic instruments, which may be
needed in case my system should be
adopted by the French Government.
And they are also witnesses for me
in France, should any attempts be
made to encroach on my invention.
The use of the electro magnet as an
attractive power, as applied to Telegraphic purposes, I claim as mine.
I was the first that thus applied
that power, and its adoption by
Wheatstone, and by the author of
the plan in operation on the Paris
& Rouen R.R., is long since
shown its practicability; and it is
this use of the electro magnet that
gives to my system its superiority

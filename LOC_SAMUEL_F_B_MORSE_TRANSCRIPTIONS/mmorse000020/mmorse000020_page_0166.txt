Baltimore July 12th 1845.
Professor Morse
Dear Sir
Enclosed I forward
a receipt for the box containing Zines,
& addressed to you as requested.
Will you do me the favor by ful-
sitting the promise made recently, to
add your testimonial to my Telegraph
Dictionary and Seamans Signal Book,
which is particularly adapted to your
Telegraph, as well as signals by Flags &c.
Mr Lucas has the M. S. in the
hands of printers and is desirous to
bring it out in advance of the opening
of the Eastern Telegraph lines, and to
facilitate its introduction I am extremely
desirous of having your testimonial
published in the introduction.
I have been engaged better than
four years on this work and the study
of the Telegraphic art, and you an
award this last 12 months has been
devoted to the study of the principles
of your unparalleled invention,

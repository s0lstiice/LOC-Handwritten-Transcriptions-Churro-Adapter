Professor Morse
U. S. Telegraph Office
Baltimore July 16th 1840.
Dear Sir,
W. S. Colt has
a model of the "American Semaphore"
which I should like you to get and pre-
sent to His Majesty Louis Phillip, in my
behalf; if you think it not too trouble-
some and worthy of such notice by you;
(or make any disposition of it you please.)
I have learned that he is constantly adding
to his collection of models, and I should
like to throw in my invention.
If you will pay the expense attending
its shipment &c. I will remit you the
amount to New York, or refund you,
on your return to the U. S.
The Semaphore tower is a new style
of architecture termed the Chrystalline by
the inventor Mr. George Hayden a worthy
mechanic of this city.
If you conclude to take the Semaphore
with you; upon your arrival in Europe
have it put in good order and handsomey
painted.

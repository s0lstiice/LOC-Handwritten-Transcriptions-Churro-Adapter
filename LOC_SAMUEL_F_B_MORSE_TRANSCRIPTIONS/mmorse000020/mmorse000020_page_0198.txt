New York, July 29. 1845
My dear Mrs Miller.
Allow me, in one word,
to commend to your kind
attention my friend,
Mr. G. F. Morse, author
of the Electric Telegraph,
who goes to Europe to spend
a few months. He is a
good man. Make him
acquainted to Mrs Michel-
son, I speak of him
to the Grand Duchess
Helen. Yours truly.
R. Baird

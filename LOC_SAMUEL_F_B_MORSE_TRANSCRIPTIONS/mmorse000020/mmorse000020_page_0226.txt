Aldermanbury
September 12th 1845.
Sir,
The very limited stay which pressure of business
permits you to make in this Kingdom preventing us from
giving you a definitive or decisive reply to the terms proposed
to us yesterday after the exposition of the system of Telegraphic
Communication already adopted in the United States and to
which we are desirous and disposed to grant in preference.
We cannot at present satisfy you further than to state that the
moment we have received communications essential to carrying
out our views an immediate intimation shall be made to you
at St. Petersburg trusting that in the interim you will give
our body the preference and permit us to make public that we
purposé adopting your system
Having of course in contemplation the additional advantage
of which you yesterday gave us private intimation and assurances,
and for which as well as the other part of your plan we take for
granted you could obtain Letters patent without molestation or
interruption from any other parties.
We are Sir
Your Obedt Servt
Bowditch Son
Secr to the Comp'y

On board the Cambria
Tuesday Dec. 2. 1845.
12 o'clock, M.
Dear Sidney,
In the hope of being in Halifax
this evening, and in time for the Britannia
which left Boston for Liverpool yesterday, I
write you a hearty line to inform you of our
progress and safety thus far. We came
out of Liverpool in a hurricane, and until
yesterday we have encountered only bad gales
and yet this morning we made the land, about
80 miles from Halifax. Our ship is a noble
sea boat, and all things considered we have had
a delightful passage, for although, it has been
as tempestuous as I ever knew it at sea, yet
the perfect ease with which our Steamer braved
the waves, and made headway against the most
violent storms, calmed all uneasy fears. The
least distance we have made in any 24 hours
is 101 miles. Yesterday we made 257 miles
and today 286 miles, up to 12 o'clock. We ex-

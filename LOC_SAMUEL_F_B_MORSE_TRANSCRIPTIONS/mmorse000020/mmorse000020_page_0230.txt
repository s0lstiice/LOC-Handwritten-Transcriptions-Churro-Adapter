London Sept. 16. 1845
13. Brompton St.
Gentlemen,
Your reply to my note of the 13th has just
been received, and the limited time which remains before
my departure for the North, occupies a complete waste
of time.
I have England in the morning for Stockholm
& St Petersburg by the way of Rotterdam, and hope
to return in about 3 weeks. I therefore
request that of the references which you offer may be made
known to Joshua Bates, Esq. of the house of Barney Brothers
& Co. who will advise me of the result. Should
they be satisfactory to him, they will be sent to me &
sent to the Commissioners of pensions, you will expect to meet with
me on my arrival at Rotterdam, as I am looking
for a very successful reception, as I am prepared to give
every necessary satisfaction to on that point, on
my own part.
In taste, but with sincere respect
Gentn. J. Obserd.
Sam: R. Brown

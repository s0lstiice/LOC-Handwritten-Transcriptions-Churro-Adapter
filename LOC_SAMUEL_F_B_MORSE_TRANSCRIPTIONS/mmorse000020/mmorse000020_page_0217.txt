I find Prince Metternich at Vienna. —
I invited the principal officer of the government of
the Ambassadress to see your Telegraph, all when
delighted, but as I said Wheatstone was before me, &
through the influence of the English Ambassadress
& the royal family he will probably establish his Telegraph there.—
This journey is highly interesting. I have access to
all Richard Establishment & Manufactories, &
when I return I will have Council in every
whole Europe.—
I send you the alphabet which I got in
at Rome.—
as soon as I hope to hear from you
Yours
Stollmann.

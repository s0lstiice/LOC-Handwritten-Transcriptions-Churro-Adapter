New York Mar 26 1845
Prof Morse
My Dear Sir,
I have just returned from the
Mayors Office and enclosed the document to Mr
Elsworth for you.
Your favor reached me yesterday but not in time to
get the document executed to send it off so I discharged
the Oathis this morning.
It has occurred to me that, in case of a partial sale
of the Telegraph to individuals, or companies, such as the
sale of a given district, and it be subsequently offered
to the Government, there should be a clause of provision
in the transfer of said partial sale so that the party pur-
chasing should be bound to give up his or their right, for an
advance of — profit on their investment when a sale of
the whole patent right could be made with the Government.
I perceive Amos Kendal has been appointed in the post office
for your city. Will not that affect his transaction of our
Business unfavorably? But on the other hand you must
have been aware of this a so my question hardly receive
any other than a negative reply.
Hoping to see you soon I remain
S.F. B. Morse Secy. Sup't Elec Mag. Telegraphs

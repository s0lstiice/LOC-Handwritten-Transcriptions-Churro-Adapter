From Galiziani's message Nov. 6.
1845. Paris.
" The laying down of wires under-
ground from the Electric Telegraph
of the Paris & Rouen R.R. to the
Ministere of the Interior is now
completed. The minister has already
made use of this mode of communica-
tion, and the service is placed at the
disposal of all the Departments of
the Govemt. wanting to send despatches
in that direction."

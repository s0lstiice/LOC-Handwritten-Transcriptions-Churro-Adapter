Copy to
Washington Dec. 29th 1845.
The Honorable Second President
Gentlemen,
Being desirous of procuring a quantity of
electro magnets immediately, and not being able to get them here in
time, I have taken the liberty, of collecting funds for
that purpose to your hands by the enclosed from Messrs.
Covran & Riggs the well known Bankers of this City. I
have written to Mr. L. Brequet, No. 4 Place de Bourse, or
Quai de l'Horloge to have them made for me, to deliver
them to your care for boarding for me, and I should
be much obliged if you will pay his bill out of the funds
sent, and send them to New York by first packet, directed
For Prof. S. F. B. Morse, Superintendent of Telegraphs,
New York Observer Office, 142 Nassau Street
New York.
With most respect
Yours obdents
Sam: F.B: Morse
Copy to Monsieur L Brequet No. 4. Place de Bourse
Dear Sir, You may remember that when in Paris, I purchased of
you two small electro magnets, at 15 francs each. I am desirous of
having immediately thirty more of the same kind; Will you have this
number made for me with as little delay as possible, have them carefully
boxed, and put into the hands of Messrs. Green & Miller, American Bankers
Paris, who have the funds ready to pay for them on delivery.

10 o'clock P. M. We have been contending all day against
as fierce a tempest of a North Westerly wind blew, so
that at this moment we are yet 25 miles from Sams-
bro light, and have not yet made it. We shall prob-
ably be in harbor at 4 or 5 o'clock in the morning. I
must, however, seal my letter for the bag so that I can
add no more. Your receipt of this will assure
you of my safe arrival in Halifax. Edward
joins me in love to you all. Good bye

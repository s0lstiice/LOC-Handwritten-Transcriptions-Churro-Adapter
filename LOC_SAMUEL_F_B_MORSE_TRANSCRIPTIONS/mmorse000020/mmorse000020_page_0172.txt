Washington Tuesday eve. Feb. 15. 45
My dear Sir
The enclosed two letters were handed me
by Daniel Campbell who said he found them in the pocket
of a trunk he had by exchange from Henry. I send them to
you supposing that you will be going out with him and
being uncertain whether if preceded to New Haven if they would
reach him. Will you be kind as to charge of them.
The weather for some days has been turning, melting killing
the thermometer this afternoon at 7 past 5 in Mr Wood's room
stood at 100º and nearly to 100. In the Patent office in the
space way between the rooms it stood at 86 and on being
carried into the library mounted up to 92. Isa you have it
not much less in New York than we have it here. 8 persons
died sunstroke today here – nothing new – I see the Commis-
sioner is not strongly for House Telegraph – I hope they will let us
know a little more about it after such pretensions.

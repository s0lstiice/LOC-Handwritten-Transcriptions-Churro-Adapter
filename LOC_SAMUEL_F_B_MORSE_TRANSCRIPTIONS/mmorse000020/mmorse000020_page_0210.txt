On the Boston line the grantor of the patent
right can to receive half the stock on the three
first wires and two thirds of that issued upon
each additional wire. The effect will be to
give them about three fifths of the stock
when the line is completed which cannot be
less than $200,000.
On the line from Washington to New Orleans
they may expect to get half the stock and on
the western line one fourth and the same
rate on many short lines.
I will not undertake to estimate the value
of these stocks; but there is no good reason
to doubt that those issued on the great lines

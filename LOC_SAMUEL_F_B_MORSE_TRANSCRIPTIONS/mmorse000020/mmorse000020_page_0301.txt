French government should make a favorable report on your
system, the matter would be easy here, but if the report should
be against you, it will influence the committee here.
A gentleman with whom I have had an interview with Bain
has been sent by this government to France & England to advocate the electric
magnetic Telegraph, he brought with him Bain's, and seems to
be very much in favor of it; but since he saw yours I have
seen the interest which it excited, he proposes to employ them
both grounds the principal stations, and Bain's at the
intermediate stations for Railroad purposes: -- he says that
Bain's works will & is exclusively simple & practical. I
have not seen it yet.
I wish you would write me all about that matter, and give
me your views how I shall proceed in case they ask for the
price, -- must can not be hoped in this part of the world. --
tells me if you make improvements. -- I shall remain here
until something positive is decided -- I intend to visit
Italy & return by the way of Paris to America, which
will not be before next spring.
Mr. Mills with whom I live altogether is exceedingly kind
& invites me in every way to further your interest.

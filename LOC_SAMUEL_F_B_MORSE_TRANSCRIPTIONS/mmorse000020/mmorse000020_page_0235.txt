Ask. What are the expectations of advantage in the es-
tablisment of a Telegraph by the Co. Is it to be used
for R.R. purposes only or for general commercial
service? Is it to be used as a Post, by the public on the
payment of a fee. — What does Mr. Wheatstone or
his agent engage to do? —
I have with me two instruments one for each terminus.
I am partially under engagement with the English Co. to dispose of them
for their use.
There is one part of instrument, I stipulate to remain
secret with each Company who negotiate with me. This secures to each
the use of it, and I think from its nature it can be kept secret.
I guarantee the following results. 1st. My instruments one
at each extremity, each with one conductor shall give the same
quantity of intelligence in a shorter time than any other system of
Telegraphs known. 2d. It shall record the intelligence in permanent
characters, and at the rate of at least 45 characters per minute
easily legible. — 3d. My instruments shall cost less than those
of any other system in use. — 4th. I will teach the use of my
system to any two persons designated by the Directors, and
dispose of one at least of my instruments for a model from which
others can be made, for a reasonable sum. or if English Comp.
purchase them of me, I will have a set complete sent from U.S.
ready for the next spring. —

When you Come to Washington tell my wife, that I am well
and that I try to be with her, as soon my business will
allow me, to set sail.
I wish you would request Mr Smith to write out an
article in my exhibit in of your Telegraph before the
Emperor des St. of Austria, he will find in the letter
to Mrs. Fleickmann a full account, and I am sure
it would interest our Americans, and reminds them that
I am yet alive.
I hope you will answer this letter, I would you
to know that I begin to think you forget such
a man like me.
I promised our Consul him Mr. Schwarz to have
your Telegraph at his house sometimes at night, when
I shall be the most influential man of the railroad
companies.
Your advice, success to you, and a happy journey
from the King's ditch. -- Thousand compliments
to all my friends here & there, and in reminding you
once more to write me I sign myself
Can of Mr. Sibley Chassy d'affection
Revere
From your true friend
Otto Fleickmann.

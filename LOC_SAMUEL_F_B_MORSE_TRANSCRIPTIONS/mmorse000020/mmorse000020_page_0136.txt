You must indeed feel desolate with the family
removed, & in a new boarding house, & I wish
I could be near your dearest father at this
time with ye little grandson, & cheer you up.
He is a sweet little fellow if I do say it, this
he cannot boast of much beauty -- I would
like to come on this season -- perhaps all you
feel a reluctance to cross the ocean, for
I cannot forget that fearful storm at sea,
when our ship's mind blew hard upon her, I
feel extremely nervous -- For from a few days
past I have had a house full -- five children
Counting mine, under six yrs of age -- two ladies
one English one German and a nurse -- then
we all going to Germany & as there are no
boats they depend upon the kindness of friends --
the vessel is leaking here & I think I find from
the German lady a letter to Edward so we
entertain by many as we could.
They have now gone on board, & I feel
begin to feel better again.

Baltimore Nov 28 1845.
Dear Sir.
Allow me to inquire if it will
be agreeable to you that I shall have some
interest in the Superintendence & construction
of Morse's Telegraph between this city and Phil-
-adelphia if it is about to be put up.
Such an intimation was suggested to me
by Prof. Morse and by Mr. Smith and also in
their opinion that it would meet your
approbation?
If my services can benefit you as well
as myself a little in this respect, I will
be obliged if you will suggest or point out
what step or application I need to make, if any,
Can you tell me when Mr. Smith will come
this way? & where a letter will reach him?
Yours very respectfully
David Burtank
100292
Now Amos Kendall
Washington DC

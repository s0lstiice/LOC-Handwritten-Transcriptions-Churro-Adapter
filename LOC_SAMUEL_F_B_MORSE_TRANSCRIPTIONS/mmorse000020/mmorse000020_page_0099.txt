I shall therefore look anxiously for a letter from him
towards the end of May answering to me the part
of his having all ready to come. Both told me in a
letter he wrote me which I received a few days
ago that he would shortly send me 20 miles more
of wire so that if we have forty miles to go upon
the kind of wire my dear Professor will be able to
instruct Mr. Both that he will be able to construct
an additional one mile and a half of wire in a leader
pipe of sufficient strength to resist the action of the
air. I suppose it would be as well to make a 1000 pipes
of these wires in length by their means and would have
two lengths of it of sufficient length and in that shape
it would be more easily transported, as in one pipe is
made and the wire entwined into it and it is twisted by some
processes by which, there could be no danger of it failing when it came to hand here.
I feel a good deal of anxiety about it.

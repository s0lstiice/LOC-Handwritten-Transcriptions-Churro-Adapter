Baltimore April 12. 1845
Dear Sir
I received a letter from Mr Smith, desiring
me to state what was the convention proper be
between himself & you, at the time of signing the Office
to bond - in relation to the bond of the Baltimore
being extended if he wished it. Founding
wrote him what was said - as well as I remember, and
also reminded him of his ready offer to the state
of my bond, when I sent that question at the time
if I wished to do so.
I don't know this document to much any way,
but I thought it no more than fair that you should know
what is going on.
I would wish that I had the
permission to try the matter from here to the Govt, more
particularly as Mr Smith seemed so ready to argue in
what you would do - when I sent him in Wash-
last - Whether you can ever divide, or not divide
would it not be as well to have something going on
to bring it on to notice? & by such movement,
accomplish bringing it speedily before the public?
Your truly
David Bantock

Sidney Morse.
I like the French; every nation has
its defects, and I could wish many changes here
but the French are a fine people. I receive a wel-
come here to which I was a perfect stranger in
England. How deep this welcome may be I cannot
say but if one must be cheated, I like to have it
done in a civil and polite way.

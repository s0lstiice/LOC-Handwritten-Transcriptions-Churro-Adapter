Washington Dec. 22d. 1845
Dear Sir Your of the 15th. night. from me in this city
just returned from Europe. In relation to the subject
matter of your letter I would say, that it will give
me great pleasure to use any influence I may possess to
get your gifted and gentlemanly brother in law Mr.
Schwartz the Consulship at Amsterdam. The first move
in the matter ought in strictness, I suppose, to come
from Mr. Ellsworth who as a Chargé d'Affaires has
under his surveillance the Consulate, but the district
of which Amsterdam is a part belongs more directly
to the Chargé d'Affaires at the Hague, Major
Savage. Mr. Ellsworth then I left him said
he should make it a point to represent the inefficiency
of the present incumbant of the Consulate at Amsterdam,
and should recommend your brother, who as a resident of Amsterdam
& highly esteemed among all classes both for his
character manners, and genius, would do honor to the country,
& I will endeavor to ascertain if

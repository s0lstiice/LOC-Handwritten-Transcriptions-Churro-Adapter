Grateful as I feel for your kindness I have also wished when in my
power to repay the obligation. You recollect perhaps I mentioned to you
professing if it suited your views discharging it so far as I could by
translating various documents, and I believe you expressed your willingness
to do it. If I may mention that I have translated about
30 or 35 pages of German and perhaps some 20 or 25 of French delinued
to yourself by Mr. Vaill. I suppose that a fair rate of compensation would
be at 30 or 40 dollars If you think this would be right you will
allow me to consider so much of my debt discharged. If too much
allow what you think proper -- It is not improbable Mr. Vaill may need
some nine translations from German or French and it would agreeable
to me to do them and apply it as he sees fit. -- Write if it meets your views.
And shall I trouble you my dear Sir with a little communication
As you will be in Paris which is so famed for its pretty things -- will
you with your well known taste select for me some little articles suitable
and agreeable to a lady say $5 or 6 wont for me to make a new Gen's
present to my wife -- and let her know it -- I don't care about trunkets
as she does not wear them -- but some real pretty adornage of furniture
and case -- memorandums book etc -- that may strike your fancy and
I know it will suit me. I suppose two or three small articles might be
got which would not hinder your progress -- if you think best put it
in one. -- If I had the gold with me now I would enclose the money

City of NewYork
July 31. 1845-
To whom it may concern.
The undersigned, proprietors of Morris
Electric Magnetic Telegraph, under Letters Patent
granted by the United States, having also an
equitable control over its use elsewhere, hereby
shun an attempt to the Instruction of a line between
any two or more points in the Island of Cuba,
by any person who will and take the same
and the advancement of this country by preference.
And cheerfully recommend every person who
may engage in the enterprise to all persons
who may have means or opportunity, assisted
with any interest in Cuba, to facilitate the work
and increase being herein & encourage an extension
of this new mode of intercommunication.
With our respective signatures
Sam'l F.B: Morse
signed also by Francis O'Hamite.

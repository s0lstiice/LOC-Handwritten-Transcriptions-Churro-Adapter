It is further ordered that the Superintendent keep

the same under the same rules and regulations now appli-

In consideration of the facilities allowed by the
Rail Road Company to the Superintendent and his As-
sistants in attending to the business of the Telegraph
it is further ordered that the free use of the Telegraph
be conceded to said company for the transmission
of communications relating to the business of this
road.

The Break Selector
The use of this instrument is to detect the
position of a break in a long line of conductors.
At many convenient distances along the line, the
conductors are brought within the house of the person
or persons whose duty it is to keep the line in re-
pair, and the wire from the left is attached to the
left wire of the instrument and the wire from the
right to the right wire. A plate of copper is also
buried in the earth and a wire or wires attached to the
instrument as in the diagram. A small battery of
2 or 3 cells must be in readiness to be attached when
the emergency requires it.

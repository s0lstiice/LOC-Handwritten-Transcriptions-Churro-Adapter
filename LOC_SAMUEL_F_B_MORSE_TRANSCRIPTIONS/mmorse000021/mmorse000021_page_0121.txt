Albany, March 23d. 1846
To S. T. B. Mose
My Dear Sir
Your of the 19th was
received I am sorry from the bottom of my heart,
that we must give up that Painting. I was always
ready to pay my share when demanded. and I
thinks more was fair, I do not recollect, Not do
I care at all about the Money, had you not
sent it by Mail I should not have received it.
You have not left us entirely. I cannot
believe it shall all of those whose youth
full zeal and labour in promoting the Arts in
this Country now abandon us. You may
have given cause for it And I know to
well and how suffered and can realise that
your feelings now must be alleviated in the
prospect of a Glorious Immortality. with
Franklin and Fulton you never can die
with very assurance of a faithful
Student I am Truly Yours T. Fink

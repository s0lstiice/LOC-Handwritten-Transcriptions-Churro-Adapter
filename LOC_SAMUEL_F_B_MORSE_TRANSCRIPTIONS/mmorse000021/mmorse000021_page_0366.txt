New York Dec 8 1861.
My Dear Sir
The interest I take in
your telegraphic labor must be my
apology for introducing upon you the subject
in regard to crossing the Hudson
river from Jersey City to N.Y. I think
it could be effected by the means of
"lead pipe", laid from six to ten feet
below the surface of the mud-bottom,
thus placing it beyond the distance
to which the heaviest anchor of a line
of battle ship can turles itself—
To lay it, I would construct a
machine for "dredging" or digging a
trench or furrow six inches wide by the
necessary depth and as it advances
removing the Mud & Sand before it—
let it lay, anxitly behind it, the pipe,
from a reel or coil containing the
necessary quantity—

I send you $80 for the instruments for our
western line; this, with the $12 I paid Clarke
for the Keys taken off at Washington, made
the $92 which the water changed you. (I also
paid Clarke $5 besides for putting on a
piece of brass on each, to bring the parts
better together -- but this is an extra matter,
on my own account.) I fervently trust
that before Friday next, we may be able
to salute you all the way from New York,

30 min. to one o'clock.
Dear Vail,
It is now 7/2 past 12 and I have nothing from
Fort Lee. Cornell is there and thus far his
assertions in regard to the well work-
ing of the line between here and
Fort Lee are not confirmed. He may
be able to account for the failure
We shall see I am anxious to know if
you were able to communicate to
Fort Lee.
I have just received a letter from
Hosp.t Bailey and Coulson from Souvenir-
Ville, and I am glad to learn of the
success of the line to that place.
The trouble is all here, and you may
judge of my anxieties to be surrounded
as I am by obstacles of the nature
which you can conceive.— They wish
me to meet them at Souvenirville
if I can, but I know no way of
hing in twenty places at a time. The
river I think will be a serious
hindrance to us.
I am preparing to go out to Newark to
meet Coulson and Bailey at 3 o'clock and
therefore must close. I shall probably take
what are left of your Pamphlets with me
for them. In haste, drop a line or send
this to Mckendall. Yours S. B. Morris

Philadelphia Exchange
Telgraph Office, Feb. 4, 1865:
Professor Morse; now at New York:
Dear Sir: Your letter about the money for
those two instruments would have answered
with the money a couple of mails ago, but that
I have thrown every dollar I could on the Phil.
& Balt. route, in the desire to pay off as quick
as possible for expenses there -- so as to
prevent any unpleasant feeling on
account of the delays we have experienced
in obtaining the means for completion, that line.
I think perhaps the remittance time today
would be somewhat longer; but it is not
more than enough, namely, enough, for me
to despatch an Agent to pay for timbers long
delivered along the line; and I have to request,
under these circumstances, that you will en-
cuse me for about one week, when I will
remit to the artist you allude to. If it
causes any inconvenience, I will see it re-
solved.

Nov. 9, 1846.
Dipped thin muslin in solution of iodide of tin. The
cloth became purple; placed the cloth upon type plate, put
a type with letter upon the cloth; connected battery through the
type, the letter was marked upon the cloth in white character;
bleaching the purple stain. Upon placing cloth on platinum
the mark was black and burned, a copper wire also marks
black, iron still blacker.
Nov. 10 Dipped clean cloth in Alkaline solution of Bodine
iron sulphide, and afterwards into Saturated solution of Sulph. Soda
the cloth now shows purple
When a type of a letter was placed upon the cloth (the cloth on
type plate) and electricity passed through type cloth & type the
letter was printed in white, leaving the white beneath the letter which
becomes more distinct as the cloth dries.

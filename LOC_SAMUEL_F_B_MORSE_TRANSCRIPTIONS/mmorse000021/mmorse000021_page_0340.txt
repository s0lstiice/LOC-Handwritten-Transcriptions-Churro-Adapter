Copy of the articles and conditions appended to the Agree-
ment between P. F. B. Mosse & Edward N. Kent, and signed by
the other Proprietors of the Telegraph.—
We the undersigned Coproprietors with Sam. T. B. Morse in
his Patent for the Electric Magnetic Telegraph, hereby ratify
the Agreement entered into between the said Mosse and Edward
N. Kent of New-York and signed by them on the thirtieth day
of Nov. 1846, regulating the making & vending of the instruments
of said Telegraph with these two modifications only
to wit:
First; That should the Government of the United States
purchase from the Proprietors of the Telegraph, their exclusive
right to the same, within the period that limits the
agreement aforesaid, to wit, three years from the first
day of Juny 1847, then this agreement shall be subject to the
approval or disapproval, the ratification or annulment
of the Government.
Second; That this agreement shall not be so construed
as to interfere with any rights of the several companies
already organized, to which companies has already
been granted the right to make and have made, the
Telegraph instruments for their own use exclusively,
nor to interfere with the privilege of said Morse or either
of his Coproprietors, to make or to have made for their
own experimental use, any improved telegraph instrument,
or model of an instrument which they have invented or may
invent, it being understood by them, however, that such instrument
shall not be multiplied or sold (within the territorial
limits assigned to the said Kent) except by the said Kent in
conformity with the terms of this agreement.—

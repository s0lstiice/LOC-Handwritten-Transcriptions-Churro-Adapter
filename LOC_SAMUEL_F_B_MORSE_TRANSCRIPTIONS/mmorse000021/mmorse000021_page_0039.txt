To N. Vail. N.York.
Dear Vail.
We are really at cross purposes. I came on
forty to see you, & confer on the very point which you
Speak of in your letter & I shall endeavors to meet you in a
few days in St. John - as soon as I can arrange all matters
here I will leave Washn & join you - I have much to
show you, & much to talk about. If the works of which
you speak are sent here in time I will take charge of
them as you request. Stottell is making 2 instruments
which I intend as model machines, Cornell has so
buried himself & befouled every thing he has touched about
them, that I have hard work to restrain my indignation
at his officious meddling with my matters, but it is
necessary to keep cool. In great haste - I must do any-
thing in regard to machines till I see you -
Yrs Sincerely
S.F.B. Mosse

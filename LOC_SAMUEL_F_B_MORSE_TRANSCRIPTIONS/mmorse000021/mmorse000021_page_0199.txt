New York May 31st 1846
Dear Father
I have this day sent on to you
Susan's picture. I followed get the man to pack
it who packed it when it went to Boston; I had
to get a box made for it; the whole expense of
box, packing & carting was $67.00 the freight for
Schooner Phoebe Eliza will be $2.75.— Uncle
Richard has gone on to Hadley & there was no
one here but me to attend to Mr Wood's letter.
The Schooner will sail from here on Monday next
the Captain says he will be in Washington by Thursday
or Friday next I directed the box to you and send
you here enclosed the receipt of the Captain of the
Schooner returns to day from Bridgeport.
We are all up at Uncle Richards now. I can not find the
little sketch you wrote me about, I have looked on everywhere
I could think of, are you sure you have not put it away?
Please write me, Father, I let us know what you are doing & how
you are getting on. Don't you want me to volunteer for Mexico to get a little
Glory!
From your affectionate Son
Charles W. Morse

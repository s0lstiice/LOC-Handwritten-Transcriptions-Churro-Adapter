To A Vail
Washington.
Hear Vail
York March 17th 1846
Your has given me much concern on various
accounts. Surely the Postmaster Genl does not think that
I am here in St York, superintending the Company's lines.
I am making instruments for our own line between Wash n
Baltimore. The circuit changes, the trunk defects, &
improvements in the Register, which it was impossible to
make in Wash n for want of mechanics, I have suspected
Mr Kendall to deceive him in this respect
if he is deceived, & I am a little surprised that you
do not address this matter, I speak it right with him, when
he questioned you. I am engaged for the public, as I
always have been, & while in their pay, feel bound to
use all my time for them. I shall immediately come on
& shall probably be with you on Friday or at earliest
Saturday I have sent the 12 copies of your book to
both : Robinson as you requested & the rest to
you by Adams & Loos Express.
In greatest haste
Yr truly as ever
S H B More
New York Aug 29 1846

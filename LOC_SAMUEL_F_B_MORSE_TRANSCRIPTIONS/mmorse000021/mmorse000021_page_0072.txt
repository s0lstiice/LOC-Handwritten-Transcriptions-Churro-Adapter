New Orleans Feb 7. 1840
Alfred Vail Esq,
Sir,
I take the liberty of
inclosing a description and sketch of an
instrument for forming the characters by
the magnetic Telegraph.
The idea occurred to me after
I had learned that a cogwheel button was now
used to form and break the connection, and be-
fore I had seen your book describing the
various other methods tried or proposed for
the same purpose. After examining the dif-
ferent contrivances which you have described,
it seems to me that the proposed is preferable
to any of them, in point of simplicity, cer-
tainty and rapidity of action. As, however,
my acquaintance with such subjects, is extreme-
ly limited, I of course very little in my
opinion and desire to obtain the views of
some one qualified to pronounce upon the
matter. It is for this reason that I have
the pleasure so far upon your time, as to re-
quest you to look at the enclosed and to
inform me, by your answer what you think
of it. If you will be so kind as to reply
your attention will be received as a favor.
Very Respectfully
Your Obt Servt
W. C. Micou.

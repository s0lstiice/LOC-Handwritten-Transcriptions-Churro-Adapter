New York March 16th 1846
My dear Sir
Have to acknowledge the receipt
of your circular - motion fifteen dollars
which some trifling though it be, was given
with the hope that fortune your funds were
anxious to see from your hands would have
proved worthy your position and have placed
you amongst the list of the quietest living
artists. As it is I can but rejoice at the
sense that has attended your invention and
application of electricity, and with the hope that
all may result for the permanent of the highest
happiness of the human family.
As I am detaining the gentleman
who handed me the letter excuse me, if I have
insaid much that I should like to indulge in
attorney and with most respect and esteem
believe me to remain
Your most obliged and
Obstinate but
Gothenburg
D. O. F. B. Madeley

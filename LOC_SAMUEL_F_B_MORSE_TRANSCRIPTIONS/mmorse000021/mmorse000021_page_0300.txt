Washington, Oct. 20th 1846
My Dear Sir,
My friends McSmith mentioned to me that
by the death of Mr. Levitz Levitz an arrangement which you
had made to repay 5000 dollars to the Sunday School of the church, had by the
lamented death of that gentleman embarrassed those arrangements and was likely to trouble you. I enclose a hundred dollars which you may return to me at your convenience. When, however, it is convenient for you to repay the half $2500,
please set off 25 to the Sunday School of the church and 25 to the fund for repairs of the building. On Saturday I received 45 dollars the first fruits of my Telegraph, that is, the first outright sale, and I send to
dedicate my portion of them to the bearer of this letter from whom I received this and all other undeserved blessings, asking your prayers that God would be pleased to give me that blessing without which wealth and honor are worse than vanity, a heart to use all that in his service remains to his office and to me
with affection and esteem
Yours
S.B.M.

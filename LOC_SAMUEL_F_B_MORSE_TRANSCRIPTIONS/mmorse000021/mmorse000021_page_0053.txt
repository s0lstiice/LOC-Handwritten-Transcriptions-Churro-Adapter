Philadelphia Jan 24th 1846
Dear Morse
On Friday I sent you by
Adams & Co Express a Receiving Magnet made
by Mr Clark & your old one. I have one now
in use (new) after the same pattern as the one
I sent you. It work beautifully indeed, I have
armed the two points of contact with pladium &
platinum which appear much better than having
both of the same metal. Will you have it taken
to Newark and put up & It would be advisable
to have the large ree magnet taken to your yard.
I shall have also constructed in a few days, two
registers for the Phil & Wilmington line. I have had
my large battery in use & reading a whole week
with the same nitric acid but with only a single
renewal of the Sulphuric. I think I can do better
still when I have (circumstances) or rather such a
condition of things as to try it. — The wires were
broken in over 40 places between this & Norristown.
The wires must be thrown together if the Company
wish it to be in daily working order.
These magnets cost thus
Making the 2 receiving magnets $20.00
Covering the wire 5.44
Copper Wire, $3.08. paladium 47 3.58
Mahogany Work 2.50
$31.52

We the undersigned, boproprietors with Samuel F.B. Morse
in his Patent for the Electric Magnetic Telegraph, hereby ratify
the Agreement entered into between the said Morse and
Edward S. Kent of New York and signed by them on the thirtieth
day of November 1846, regulating the making & vending of the
instruments of said Telegraph, with these two modifications
only, to wit
First; That should the Government of the United States
purchase from the Proprietors of the Telegraph, their exclusive
right to the same within the period that limits the
Agreement aforesaid, to wit, three years from the first day
of January 1847 / eighteen hundred & forty seven, then
this Agreement shall be subject to the approval or disapproval,
the ratification or annulment of the Government.
Second; That this agreement shall neither be so construed
as to interfere with any rights of the several companies
already organized, to which companies has already
been granted the right to make and have made, the Telegraphic
instruments for their own use exclusively, nor
to interfere with the privilege of said Morse or either of his
boproprietors, to make or to have made for their own experimen-
tial use any improved instrument or model of an instrument
which they have invented or may invent it being understood
by them, however, that such instrument shall not be multi-
plied, or sold within the territorial limits assigned by this agreement
to the said Kent, except by the said Kent in conformity
with the terms of this agreement.

a new appointment has been made on the strength
of my letter to Mr Kendall, circumstributing my sphere
to the operations of the Western line; but even in this
relation I may have it in my power to reply in some
degree, and at some times, the kind confidence reposed
in me by myself and associates, I have wedded my
heart too much to the Telegraph, and given up too often
quiet communion with my Maker and family altar.

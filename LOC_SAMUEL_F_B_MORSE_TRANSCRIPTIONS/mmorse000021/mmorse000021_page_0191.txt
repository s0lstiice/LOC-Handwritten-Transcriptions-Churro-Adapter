Treasury Department
April 25th 1846
Sir:
I am in the receipt of your letter stating
that you have intimated from Paris to the use of the
United States Telegraph, as well as the Telegraphs
directors established in the United States "infringe
of Electro Magnets," and asking whether the same
cannot be admitted free of duty.
In reply I would observe that most
parties of the Magnets referred to as may have been
actually intended for the use of the Telegraphs made
by the United States, may be admitted free of duty,
but any Magnets designed for any Telegraphs
are liable to duty.
I am Very Respectfully
Your Obdt Servt
Sam'l P Moser
Secretary of the Treasury
Prof Saul H B Moser
Washington

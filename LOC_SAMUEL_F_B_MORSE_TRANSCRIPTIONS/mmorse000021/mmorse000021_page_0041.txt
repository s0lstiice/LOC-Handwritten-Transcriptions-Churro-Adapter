Washington Jan 17th 1846
Prof Sands, F. B. More.
My Dear Sir Your favor of the 15th came
to hand last night.
I am surprised at the information you give
about our line through Newark and South
Street must be some mistake about it. My
expectation was that it would be built
through the business part of the Town,
which, however, is at some distance
from the Railroad Depot. Perhaps that
is what your informant meant, as I
am hardly convinced that Mr. Borwell
would be guilty of the folly of building
the line where it is impossible to have a
desirable station. However, this matter is
doubtless well understood by this time.
I propose being in NewYork on Tuesday
next when I hope we shall all have the
gratification of seeing the communication
complete between the two great cities.
Most truly
Your friend
Amos Kendall

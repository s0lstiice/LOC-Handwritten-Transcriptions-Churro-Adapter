In consideration of these provisions said Morse
hereby grants to the said Kent the exclusive rights
to make or cause to be made, and to vend the instruments
of said Telegraph in the city & County of NewYork,
according for the term of three years from the 1st of Janu-
ary 1847.
It is, however, understood that the price to purchasers
of the Receiving Magnet shall not exceed Eighteen
dollars, and the price of the Register shall not ex-
ceed Sixty five dollars.
It is also further understood that before these articles
shall be binding on the said Morse, the approval of
them shall be obtained of the other proprietors of the Patent
of the Electric Magnetic Telegraph, to wit: F.O.J. Smith; Alfred
Vail; & Leonard D. Gale.
Done at NewYork the 30th day of November
1846.
Sam.F.B.Morse

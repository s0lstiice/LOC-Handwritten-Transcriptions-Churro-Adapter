Mr Kendall goes tomorrow probably to Richmond
to consummate arrangements with P. R. Co which I
believe are in a fair way of being satisfactory.
I am grieved to say that many things have lately
come to my knowledge in regard to — that show
double dealing. Be on your guard. I hope it is but
appearance, and that his course may be cleared up by
subsequent events. I declare to you that I have seen so
much duplicity in those in whom I had confided as friends
that I feel in danger of entertaining suspicions of every
body. I have hitherto thought you were too much inclined
to be suspicious of people, but I no longer think so.
Keep this to yourself. It may be that appearances are de-
ceptive & I am not wrong one whom I had esteemed as a
real friend, without the clearest evidence of unfaithfulness.
Yet when appearances are against, it is right to be cautious.
Paul H B Mester.
Alfred Vail - P.S. 1st I was told that the Post Off. Committee
will probably report tomorrow Friday on the purchase of
the Telegraph, probably too against the purchase. P.S. 2nd
I addressed a note to Evening Post about a week ago,
asking them to correct their assertion concerning Kansas
praising 160 letters in a minute. I have no access to a file
of papers. Will you see the bay back now & know if they

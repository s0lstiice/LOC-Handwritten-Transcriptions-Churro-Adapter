The Dr is a noble united man. I wish you
would have it fixed. I do not like to have it
seen. It is not worth while to show it to
every body. I leave on Monday evening and
hope to be in Phila Tuesday and in N.York,
on Wednesday. I must see you in Phila
to great heart's rest
Yours as ever
Sam. F.B. Morse
12 o'clock m. P.S. I had just returned from
attending the funeral of Lewis' father.
Alfred Vail.

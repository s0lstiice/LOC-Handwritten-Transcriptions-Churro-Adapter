Washington, Sept. 15th 1846.
My Dear Sir:
It is extremely painful for me to inform you, that a serious
misunderstanding took place yesterday,
between my brother Louis, and Genl. Vail;
brought about by, (as I am indeed to
believe, from other sources than Louis),
the overbearing conduct of Genl. Vail,
because Louis was somewhat over his
time at dinner. Louis says, that,
he has borne with this ill-treatment
from Genl. Vail long enough, - and he
appeals to your protection, against
a repetition of it.

still more painful to me, because

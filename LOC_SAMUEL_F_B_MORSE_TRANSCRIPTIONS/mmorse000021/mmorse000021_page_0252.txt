In the latter case you will send $35,,00 - I have now $4,,25
for the Temple Magnet. I shall do all in my power to second
your wishes in relation to the continuation of Talboys to the 18th of
May. Envis much to see Mr Kendall & know how all our
matters stand. I have just received a letter from Mr Arthur
George, who deems as one of the proprietors, to have a written
statement from Mr Kendall, of the general condition of
our matters. Is Mr K in Wash? I see how P.O.S.
L. gets on with the Boston line!! A little less boasting
a little less self complacency, a little more confidence
in our experience, will not have been of any harm. Let
them work out of their boats since they will have things
their own way. I saw in Utica an excellent mechanic
who is making good substantial instruments for Mr Halter
for the Western Stations. His name is Churthuck, the same
who wrote to me some time ago requesting leave to lecture
& to make instruments. He is an ingenious model, un-
skiving man, & I think might be of service if we could
have him near us. I have a plan of stopping & releasing
the machinery that is new & will please you - Puzzle it out
if you can - I shall bring it on with me. I was very
much shocked at the news of little Henry Rogers death.
I shall visit Mr & Mrs Rogers tomorrow - I hope indeed
the application will be blessed with their vote. My respects to Mrs
Vail - to Louis & Mr Bailey & other friends. I shall write Louis by
this same mail. Truly your friend is ever J.K.B. More.

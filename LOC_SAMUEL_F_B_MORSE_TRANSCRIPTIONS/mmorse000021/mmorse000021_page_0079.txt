In view of the connexion of Volta, Gal-

who should object (if you do not) to
the name I propose for distinguishing
your "great invention of the age?"
You may be sure that no time will now
be lost in completing the lines from this city
to Hainsburg & Baltimore --
Before the 2nd of March, you will have the
pleasant, if life be spared, of seeing the
Monograph triumphant over time & space along
hundreds of miles of continuous or connected routes.
Yours truly, H. O'Neill

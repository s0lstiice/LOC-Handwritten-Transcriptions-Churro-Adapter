"Electrical Telegraph, if we may judge by the exclusion
by American novelty their preferances to its operations."
I think I shall have them nailed on this point.
Keep your eye out for me to bring home anything on the
Intelligent interest you think will interest me.
Cold weather is coming on. The air is comfortable,
and we are beginning to have causesets for the winter.
You will see by the paper, the storms encountered by
Great Western & Loss of Great Britain. September
is not a good month nor March, but Nov.
and the latter part of April or May are good seasons
to be on the water to come this way. You must be
impatient for rough weather home. You know nothing
yet of storms at sea, so when they come don't let them
frighten you. They are all controlled by the same
hand which holds the gentle zephyrs.
All were well at Haddon when I last heard from

March 25th 1846.
Prepared package to go by Steam Hibernia of 1st April
containing Telegraph Pamphlets for W. King, Paris, M. Arago, M. Joy,
M. Breguet, M. Vattemare of Paris; Fleischmann & M. Stiles of Vienna
with letters to M. Arago, M. Joy, M. M. Lane, M. King.
Sent to M. Hubbard Dispatch Agent Boston
by Adams & Co's Express.

Mr Rogers is preparing, & also whether in your opinion
an additional work by another hand would be useful?
I should by no means expect or wish to publish any thing
that would interfere with the usefulness of his. It may be that
the field opens is so ample that several works of the kind are
desirable I would not interfere essentially with each other. It would
afford me pleasure to do anything in my power to facilitate this
great public improvement, but I should by no means think of
attempting any thing of the kind proposed without your advised
cordial approbation.
I will thank you to let me hear from you once more on
this subject. I trust you will pardon me for the trouble
I give you.
With sentiments of great respect
I am Yours etc
A.C.R. Baldwin

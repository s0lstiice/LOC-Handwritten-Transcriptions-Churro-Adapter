Hon. Austin Hotel, N. B. January 8th 1846
11. P.M.
Dr Lie)
At this late hour
in a conversation with Mr Conell, he objects
to letting me have theiley Maypts which
he has Jnspire for me & for which I have
furnished the wine & food for St, because
I do not Sign a paper that he has done
me up recognizing his right as the Battlet
man, & which I deem it not proper for
me to do with out your approbation,
I address you thus & request
you to let me know what is to be done
at your earliest Cowing, as our time is
to be all ready for operation, Except the Maypt
the coming week & we are very doubtful
of letting it in Motion, Direct Notice, as I
Lean for home tomorrow Morning,
Respectfully Yours
J. S. Sexton
Buffalo, N.Y.

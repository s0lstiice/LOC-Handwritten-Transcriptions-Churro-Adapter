I have sent you a number of the Scientific
American with a new printing Telegraph in
it illustrated. I have read it and find it is
on a par with all the rest of the Telegraphs. It
must work in equal time by the main dependence
of two chronometers one at each Station. It is very
complicated and will never work, all that has
been done towards the test of such a thing is
simply the wood cut which you will see. Yours
is far superior. House I understand is now about
to apply for a Patent. His last being entirely
different from his former one. Mr. Livingston
told me to day that he had dropped House and
has no intention of using his instrument. -- Boston
is working to Springfield -- The people are getting
outrageous in reference to the way in which that
unenterprising thing is conducted, and I am asked
daily, why is it that the Patents to not look
into the matter. Where is Mr Kendall, and when
will he let us know what the state of things is
in regard to the line. Where is our interest? Where
is our stock? Have we any property in that line?
I should like to know. Is it not too bad.
I have not been able to look over the files of the
Evening Post but will do so, and if your correction
is noticed send you a copy.
In regard to Mr K. I think I can tell you
wherein he has been playing since I have long
had suspicion in regard to his conduct in that
quarter, but I will defer it till I reach home. I
shall start as soon as I can get two instruments
finished, which will not be until the end of next
week. I hope Mr Kent will get Mr Chubbuck
a somebody who can be a little ambitious and
work steadily and hurry if need be, --

Another objection against laying through the
low Country is the sparseness of the population
which will lead to difficulties in obtaining
subscription along the line and likewise the
low character of the resident whites in that section
who could and would destroy the line for a few
dollars without the slightest chance of being
detected. Again should the line break or get
out of order there would be great difficulties in
fixing it during the summer season the fever
of that country being fatal even to the inhabitants
should they remain out during the night.
These objections do not apply to the upper route.

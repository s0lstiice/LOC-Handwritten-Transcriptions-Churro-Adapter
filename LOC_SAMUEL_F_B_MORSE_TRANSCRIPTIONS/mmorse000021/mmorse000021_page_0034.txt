I do not think best to try Newark as a Station until
after the present plan has proved a failure. If there
is any failure, it is due undoubtedly to the bad insula-
tion of the pipe in the river. If C's act of the matter is
Correct, the line is in good working Condition from Hoboken to
Somerville & also from Hoboken to New York. It is thus strange
that it should not go through all the way. I find I am too late
for the mail to day & I will therefore add to memory.
Jan 13th
The solution of the difficulty of a current open for wires
after the Circuit was broken at Lambertville is undoubtedly
the true one - Mr Cornell had the plates stuck & connection
made showing the integrity of the line to Somerville & you will
doubtless find at Lambertville a connection with the
wire & ground. We think of turning out the Circuit the
wire in the India Rubber - Therefore you must be pre-
pared for it. Then will be best one wire from Hoboken
to N.Y. but two at first from Phila to Hoboken so that
you can use both circuits or two independent circuits
from P. to Hoboken. Then if all works right you can order
Cornell to connect the two wires from Phila to Hoboken, which
will lengthen one of the pipe to N.Y. & we can thus by the
line through - I am anxious to have the line complete before
the arrival of the Steamers so that if possible we may convey
the news by her, from here to Phila by Telegraph. If such
did be the result it will be late for us until the Bath Convention.
I shall wait until anxiety for letters starts when you will be ready. If there is
difficulty at the river, I will then go to Newark - 12 o'clock -

Abraham "Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac whom thou
lovest, -- and offer him for a burnt offering ye." -- It has always
seemed a mystery to me, how I should have been led on to the ac-
quiescence of the knowledge of Painting, with so much
sacrifice of time, and money, and though so many anxieties & per-
plexities, and then suddenly be stopped, as if a wall were built
across my path, so that I could pursue my profession no longer.
But I believe, I had grace to trust in God, persuaded that he
could and would clear up, in his own time and manner, all
the mystery that surrounded me, and now, if not greatly deceived
I have a glimpse of his wonderful, truly wonderful mercy towards
me. He has chosen thus to order events, that one mind might be
concentrated upon that intention, which he has permitted to be
born for the blessing, I trust, of the world. And he has chosen me
as the instrument, and given me the honor, and at the moment
when all has been accomplished which is essential to its success
he is ordering events as again to turn my thoughts to my almost
sacrificed Isaac, At times I am almost overwhelmed with the
thoughts of God's goodness, in making privations and affliction
the means of palpable blessings. It seems at such times that I
could never again distrust God, however incomprehensible his
dispensation towards me, and yet the heart is so deceitful I
trouble in the very exercise of faith lest it fail in the hour of
trial, and it would fail but for the gift of spiritual strength.
"Non nobis, Domine -- non nobis."

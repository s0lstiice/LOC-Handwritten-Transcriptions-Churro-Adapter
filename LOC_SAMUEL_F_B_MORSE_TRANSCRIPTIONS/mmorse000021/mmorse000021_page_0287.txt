Cornell is app'd Superintendent of the line that is of our
door operations to keep the line in order. Those who
speculate are preparing to come out. We must be prepared
to meet them. It is an attempt doubled to frighten, &
to get some money from us, to purchase him out.
I guess he will be mistaken. But we shall have the
spec's against us, because they will hope to bring us to
lower terms, if they can get up a competition therefor
will be foughted if considerable of a puffing is
forthcoming. Good bye for the present. I do hope Louis for
his own & Mother's sake will not fight against his own
interest.
July 27th 1846
Smith has his two lines in good order to Boston to stay.
We are going to buy a connection direct from Boston
to Buffalo, this afternoon. Yesterday I was in Albany
office & had communication direct from Boston.
N.Yak Oct 29, 1846
Dear Vail
I wish you to re-instate Louis Lantinghe at the
office in Wash. It is under pay & every day that he is
unemployed is only creating difficulty. When I see you
I will further explain Telegraph matters boom up
laye & look well.
Sincly Yr friend & Sent as ever
Paul H Beekman
To Vail. Wash.

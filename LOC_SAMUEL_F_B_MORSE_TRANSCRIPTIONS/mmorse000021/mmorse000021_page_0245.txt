I suppose you have heard of the distress-
ing death of Mr Rogers' little son, Henry, who
was playing with the hot-cock of a Boiler, over
their bath-tub, and afterwards fell in. The grief
next day, how heart-rending it must have been to
his parents. They have gone on a little trip, especially
on Mrs R's account, to Norfolk & Richmond Va.
for 10 or 12 days -- The Miss Polk's, brother, and
friends, "too numerous to mention," send their best
regards & wishes to you. I in much haste.
I am, with great respect,
Yours, &c
Louis Hantzinger.
Prof. S.D. Morse.

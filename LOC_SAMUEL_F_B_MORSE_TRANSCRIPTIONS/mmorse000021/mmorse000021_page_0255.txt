I wish you would write to Mr Kendall
to make a written statement to us all about
the condition of the lines.
I can't puzzle out your plan of
stepping apparatus I must wait till I see
it - cause why, you gave me no clue - I
might invent a half doz in the event of
trying to do so - Mrs Vail & family are
in New York - I am all aloney - and
lonesome - Louis Reed yours yesterday &
also did Mr Rogers who has returned, and
says your letter was a great comfort to
him and Mr R.
I wish you would see Mr Robinson
of the firm Colt & Robinson and get the
money for the books & Pamphlets I sold
them 200 pamphlets at 8s $15.00
18 Large pamphlets at 60s $10.80
$25.80 I think that is the amt. They have it. (as
much if not more, I cant afford to loose it.
We took in $91 here last month Aug) In July over $61.
I understand that the Post Master would like to continue
the Telegraph but for the heavy salaries. ( my thumb on
my nose) Your picture hangs in the window still &
I think I best to let it be till the 4th of March next
Yours friend as ever
Alfred Vail

I have yet to learn whether there is up a
foot of wire on the southern line -- Perhaps all
my hurry and care and anxiety is for nothing.
I should be glad to have some light upon
that subject, and would thank you to let me
know what is going on in regard to the line on
receipt of this -- Please say also to Mr Kendall
that if any sum is to be asked for the
entire right of way condition the Gov purchase, I desire
to be consulted. You will find the things
belonging to the lathe in your cupboard -- The
stocks do not belong to it, nor the Vice. I
Believe me to be ever sincere
Yours
Alfred Vail
+ H.J. Rogers.

Mr. Green & Co. Paris.
Paris April 20th 1846.
My dear Mr. Peabody, I am surprised that in calling on you yesterday you found me in a
copy of the letter from M. Bregeut in Paris to you of the 1st of this month. I am surprised that
you were so long without addressing me.
letter to M. Peabody of the date as you will perceive of 29th Decr. 1845 which letter
has long since been acknowledged by M. Peabody.
It will please you to have on M. Peabody for
the amount of M. Bregeut's bill for magnets and send
me the box with as little delay as possible.
I also send you a copy of the letter accompanying
the letter of Credit of Messrs. Concoran & Ripp which I sent you by the Steamer of Jan 1st 1846, and which
it would seem has been inaccurately transmitted.
Respectfully Yours
Sam W. Brown
Sup. E.M. Telegraph
for U.S. States

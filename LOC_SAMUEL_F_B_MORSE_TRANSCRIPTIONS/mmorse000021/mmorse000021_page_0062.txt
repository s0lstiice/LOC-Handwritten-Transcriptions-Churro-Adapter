Telegraph matters are looking very well
we are operating between Newark and Phila, and
the point is decided that the stock is a divided
paying stock; the stockholders are in good spirits
and prepared to push forward. We are troubled
with the river, and that is the reason we cannot
communicate direct to N.York, but hope to be
able in a few weeks.— Buffalo & Lockport

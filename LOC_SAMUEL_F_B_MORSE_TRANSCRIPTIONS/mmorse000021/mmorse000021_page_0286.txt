N York Oct 7 1846
Dear Vail
Ex of 3d have just wd on my return from
Utica I am sorry that Louis Charles b show such a
temper. He stands in his own light. I had just procured
for him at Utica the appointment to the N York office
under Mr Wood at a salary of $500. but if it is
correct, what you report, & he shows such a distrustful
& sulky disposition, I fear it will so far towards mod-
ifying the wish of the Directors to have him in their em-
ployment. I regret it exceedingly as I had every disposition
to befriend him, & to advance him as far as his own
interest & the proper claims of older persons will permit.
I shall still endeavor to have the appointment for
him, hoping that this temper will be corrected, or him
by proper reflection. If it is not, he must pursue
his own course, without any interference of mine, for
I have too much on my mind, & weightier concerns
I think of, than to study the means of curing a bad temper,
in him. I have written to your brother George to procure
the stone for you, as I have as much as I can do, to
get ready for a visit to Wash n . I wish to see you, &
Mr Kendall on matters of importance to all concerned.
I have just ordered of Stockell, 4 Registers & he has
promised me he will so on with them, with delay I
cannot say when he will have them done. Keep this
private for reasons I will tell you when I see you. Our
receipts on the Buffalo line for 23 days only (all
one month) were $2940 in odd dollars!!

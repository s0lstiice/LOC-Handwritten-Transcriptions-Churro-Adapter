These articles of Agreement between Samuel B. Morse
and Edward N. Kent both of the city of New York made
this thirtieth day of November 1846 witnesses
That the said Morse being the Inventor & Patenter of
the Electric Magnetic Telegraph doth by these articles
grant permission to the said Kent to make or cause
to be made, and to vend the Instruments of said
Telegraph called the Register, and also the Receiving
magnet on the following conditions to wit.
1st The said Kent shall have said instruments
well made in accordance with the models furnished
by said Morse;
2. Said Kent shall keep a faithful record of all the
instruments thus made and sold, and to whom sold,
which record shall be subject to inspection by said Morse
at all times;
3. Said Kent shall pay the sum of Five dollars to
the said Morse upon each Register thus made & sold
and Five dollars upon each Receiving magnet thus made
& sold at any time on demand after the sale of such
Register or Receiving magnet;
4. Said Kent shall keep on hand for sale, or with
as little delay as possible shall have ready for sale, a
sufficient supply of said Telegraphic Instruments
to furnish on demand the authorized companies

13th. 1860
Virginia, an order of the day in which it is stated
that the Governor of Virginia has directed the
Governor of the District of Columbia to arrest
the President of the United States and
the Secretary of War, and to take them to
the City Jail in Washington City, and
confine them there until further order
from the President of the United States.
It is further stated that the Governor of
Virginia has directed the Governor of
the District of Columbia to arrest the
United States Marshal and take him to
the City Jail in Washington City, and
confine him there until further order
from the President of the United States.

the United States has directed the

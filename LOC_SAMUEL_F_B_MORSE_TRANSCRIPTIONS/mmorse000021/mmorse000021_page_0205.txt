Rochester, N.Y.
May 24, 1846.
Dear Sir:
Your kind letter came to me like
a stream of water in a dry and parched land. It
whispered to my heart the sweet secret of Christian con-
centration. I cannot be mistaken. The words seemed to
come from the same spring whereon I have drawn in
other days long draughts of that peace which passeth
all understanding. It is an additional token of Hearer's
goodness for which I flow now tears of sincere gratitude.

To N Vail - Wash'n
New York Aug. 29. 1846
Dear Vail,
On my return from the West I found seventeen
letters on my table, yours among the rest, and so I have
work enough for a day or two in answering them, but I
take yours first.
My cellar experiment was not so unsuccessful
as you imagine. I succeeded to my entire satisfaction in
taking 3 inches of skin, a little of the flesh, & a trifle of bone
from the front of my left leg, & so the result, for one week's
entire leisure with my leg in a chair: The experiment was
to satisfy myself that I deem it needless to try it again,
having established beyond a doubt, that Skin flesh alone
are no match against wood, iron, &c &c. I am entirely
well of it, & enjoyed my visit to the Western States very
highly - The Western line is in excellent order, & well man-
aged: The operators, are without exception intelligent,
skilful & expert - & when the line is complete to New York,
its business will better, if not quadruple - The Stock is
good & in demand. I wish a few days more here, to
have completed a new instrument, when, if I am
needed at Washington I will come on, but if not I
should be glad to stay yet longer - Let me know, I have
several receiving Magnets, with all their pictures but not
just together - I can spare four, just as they are, for $12
dollars each - if cash is paid in advance - I say in advance
for I have been so annoyed in collecting, what is due
me, on acct of some of them, that I must make this provision

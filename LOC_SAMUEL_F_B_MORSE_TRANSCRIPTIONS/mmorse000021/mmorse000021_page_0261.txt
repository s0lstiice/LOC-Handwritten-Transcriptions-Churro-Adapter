Dear Sir.
I just received a letter from Brussels
in which I am informed, that a Company
has put up three wins between Brussels
& Malines; they are Watson's telegraph
for which they give him 30,000 livres.
further states
"Le gouvern't se decide pour lui de
morse des quil aura éprouvé les nombreux
inconvénients de celui-ci, mais il veut attendre
l'expérience."
You see from this, that something
may be desired from my visit to those
places.--
Mr. Stirling gave up her school, so intend
to take a small house and remain here
during the winter until something will turn
up profitable. --
I am glad to hear you are better & entirely
recovered from your disappearance about 2 months.
I hope to see you soon back again here.
Yours very truly
Ch L Kilburn

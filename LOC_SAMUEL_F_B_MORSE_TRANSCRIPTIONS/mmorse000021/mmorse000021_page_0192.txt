Washington April 23. 1846.
Dear Sir,
Yours of the 21st. I have received. I
should have had two or more receiving magnets for you
long since had you ordered them. But you only made
inquiries respecting them without ordering any.
I have no means of having them made here in Washington
But if you desire me to obtain two or more, I will procure them to be made here in Washington
for you in a very short time if the funds are advanced for the
purpose. (You have been so far ahead of my time in supplying me with
the necessary magnets, and satisfy yourself respecting them if you have any doubts, I have never heard of any
fault found with them, notwithstanding I have them in operation here and at Baltimore, at Phila. & Wilmington,
and they work admirably, I am perfectly satisfied of their immense excellence, I am not in a situation to take upon myself any
financial risk in supplying them. I have done already, to the full extent of my means in
advancing money for instruments to the N.Y. & Wash. Cts. and I cannot
advance any more. I can furnish you with money as you desire it
for 10 dollars at once if you pay for it immediately. If you pay for it
France specially for this purpose in this will be required for us 18 dollars a piece. If you
pay for it immediately and they will be paid for them every day. I
have already paid for them which are nearly completed. They can be finished
you will find them in a few days after you give the order,
and remit me the money.

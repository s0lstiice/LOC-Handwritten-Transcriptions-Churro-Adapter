Alto. Thom N.Y. August 25/46
S. F. B. Moore Eg
De Hi
I regret very
much you never met me town.
I wanted to see you on the subject
of our office Fixing,
I leave it in the hands of Mr
Baker & Robinson without knowing
any thing personally as to the composition
to arrange & put it in order, or work so
that exactly.
May I ask you to give your attention
to this town so far as it can be done
without inconvenience to yourself.
I am very anxious to have the time
strictly right & am obliged to go home
this Evening, come back soon.
Respectfully Yours
J. S. Taylor

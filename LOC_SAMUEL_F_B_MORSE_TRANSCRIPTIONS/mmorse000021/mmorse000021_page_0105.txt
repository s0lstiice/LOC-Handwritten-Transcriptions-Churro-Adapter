Philadelphia Telegraph Office, March 10.
Professor Morse
Dear Sir: On your return through
this city, I hope you will delay over
one train to examine the instruments
I am having made for our West End Line,
from drawings made for me by Mr.
Reid, & apparently well liked by Mr.
Vail. I think they will not fall
far short of your wishes; & that they
will not be unworthy of the name
which I propose to engrave on
them, in regard to the Inventor.
I expect that two of them will
be ready over in about 7 or 10 days. —

Jan 20, 1845.
Prof. Munro, Newark:
Dear Sir: As I have seen Kendall will not use the
power he has to make himself President of the U.S.
& Western Telegraph Company, I mentioned the matter
to Mr. French at Washington, who has sent me on
his letter papers for about 100 shares, that I
may vote for him for the stock Washington stock
holders in the choice of officers. Mr. Vail
has also signed the same paper, requesting
me to vote on his stock in the whole line: And
Mr. V. desires me to write to you, to let you
know, that you may, if you choose, write immediately
to Kentucky, enclosing your money
to vote for you in the choice of officers
directing your letter so as to be there by the first
mail after you receive this, as it will be
wanted on Wednesday (to-morrow) morning.

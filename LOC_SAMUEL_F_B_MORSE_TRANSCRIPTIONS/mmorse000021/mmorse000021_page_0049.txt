New York Saturday evening Jan'y 23d 1844
Friend More
Your letter & the enclosed
are duly received and the articles of agreement
vigned and delivered. Mr Smith being in N.Y.
I send you this morning a despatch by Telegraph
openting to your proposition in regard to the
lathe. I have one piece belonging to it here
which I brought on to get a review put to it,
& will leave it at the observer office for you
I have the receiving magnets done and almost all
but the Check work, Hekell is a slow work or
he might have been done too -- The loose time in
a thousand ways, and does not pretend to look
ahead. At Prock's I have worked every day at
the vice and bench without intermission nearly
from 8 delock A M till 10 P.M. It being saturday
night or 9 short to be there now, but they went
work on Saturday night. However I am completely
tired out with work and anxiety to get home.
The receiving magnets work most beautifully, as
I have tried them on the Albany line. I have
also put made a lightning protector for each
instrument and so arranged that a single line
protects the receiving magnet of an intermediate station
on the right and left wire. The mode of connecting
an intermediate station with a station on the right
or left in case of the breaking of a wire or the like
you will be pleased with. It is after your plan
of making changes in the connections -- I have
also a changer for the following variety of changes
of the main wire and local -- Main Closed & Local
closed -- Main Broken & Local Broken, Main
closed & Local Broken -- Main Broken & Local closed

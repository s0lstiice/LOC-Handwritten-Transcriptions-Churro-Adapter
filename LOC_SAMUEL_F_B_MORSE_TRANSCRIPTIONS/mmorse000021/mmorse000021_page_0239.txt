Washington August 29th 1846
Friend Morse
Dear Sir I did not receive
that letter you promised to write, so I
must answer it without receiving it. I
trust your unfortunate and unsuccessful
attempt to get down letter has not been
a serious affair, & that ere this you have
forgotten it. We are all well here and
keep the Telegraph (U.S. I mean) in operation
there has been no stop or delay. Can't say
the same of some other lines not fifty
miles off but receipt will not be much
this month - Port Master Gen talked
of giving it up on the first of October, but
I think he will not now, Mr Kendall
is not ready to give him a bid I have
urged Mr K to stay it off until the 1st of
January, I hope you will do all you
can to help in the matter, It will not
require must to satisfy the M General
to continue our pay until that time, I do
not know what I am to do after it is
stopped, I cannot live with $600 a year
It will not pay for my board & family -
The Observatory folks are putting up a
line and may perhaps want some
143 of your small French magnets, Will

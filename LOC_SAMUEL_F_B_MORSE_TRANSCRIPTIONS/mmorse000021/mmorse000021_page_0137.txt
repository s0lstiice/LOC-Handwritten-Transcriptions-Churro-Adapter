Sam'l F. B. Morse Bay
Washington,
In
Your favor under date of 29th Decr reached us
on the 18th January but it has only been within a few days
that we have received from Messr. Baguet the box containing
the Electric magnets ordered by you and which we have
forwarded by the first opportunity that has since passed for
New York, the Packet Ship DeLisle of Orleans, which sailed
from Havre yesterday. Though an inadvertence on our
part the box is consigned to consignment at New York, Mr. W. Thayer,
to whom we have herewith written to deliver it to you at the risk
incidental. The charges at and from Havre are to be paid by you.
According to your request we have paid Messr. Baguet the amount
of their bill any $450 -- which bill accompanies the package, and
in virtue of the order you sent we have drawn upon Messr. Richards
of London for £48.1.6 -- which balances the account as you
will perceive by the annexed statement.
We hope that the articles will reach you in good order and
trusting that you may have other and frequent opportunities of
being useful to you, in maintaining
Your affec. Servt
Samuel Coe

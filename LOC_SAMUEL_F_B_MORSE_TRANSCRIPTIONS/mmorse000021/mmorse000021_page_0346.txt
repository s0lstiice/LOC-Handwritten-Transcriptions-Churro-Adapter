New York January 21. 1846
Dear Sir
I have your letter of Nov. 29.
with the parts which I have on
1. A box containing Telegraph system in
form of cutting plan.
2. 4 special containing 4 zinc cylinders with
plate iron straps for Galvanic battery.
3. 8 top with cells, 4 of them with four
branched wire formers each with one large copper
bottle, and with four small bottles -- one half
filled.
If you see Prof. Brady in Washington
will you please say to him that I want
him a letter about obtaining one direct
Gloucester Mo. en should be glad to know
whether he owns it.
Very truly
Elias Loomis

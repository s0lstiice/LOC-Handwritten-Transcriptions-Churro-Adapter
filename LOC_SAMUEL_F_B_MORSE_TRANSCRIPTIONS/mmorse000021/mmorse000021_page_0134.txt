St. J. H. Morse Esq. Superintendent of Telegraphs
Washington Canal Joseph Morse
Dear Sir: Hobart 142 Nassau Street New York
We have received this morning from the post office at
Greenwich Ct. Paris a
case to your address shipped by us Dutchess County Captain
Richardson to the care of Ebenezer Thayer Esq. of Wall Street
New York - annexed a copy of our charge letter amounting
$10 which we took in reimbursement.
all this was done according to previous instructions which we
had received from Mr. Green Ct Paris, the manifest was
closed on 6th for Mr Thayer enclosing the bill of ladings
was already in the post office, when we received too late from
his office 8 new instructions to address the case to Joseph
Thayer to the New Observer Office 142 Nassau Street New York
for debit of $10.00. Green Ct with the charges.
We are sorry that this could not be done I being too late
havently write to you to explain this
We have sent to Mr Thayer a copy of the invoice desired
him to follow your instructions truly Your obedient
servt J B Bigguez & Co
Armiger

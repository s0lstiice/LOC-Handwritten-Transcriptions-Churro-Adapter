Brig. Genl. Samuel G. P. Breckinridge, April 4th 1846.
Sam'l G. P. Breckinridge Brig.
Washington
Sir,
Your favor under date of 29th Instant reached us on the
18th instant, but it has only been within a few days that we have received
from Messr. Bouquet the box containing the Electric Magnet ordered
by you and which we have forwarded by the first opportunity that has
since offered for New York, the Packet-ship Duchesne's Orleans, which
sailed from Havre yesterday. Through an inadvertence on our part the box
is consigned to our agent at New York, H. E. Chayes to whom we have
toward written to inform it to you of the address indicated. The charge
of it and from Havre are to be paid by you. According to our account,
we have paid Messr. Bouquet the amount of this bill, say $1450,
which bill accompanied the package, and in virtue of the order you sent
us, we have drawn upon Geo. Peabody, of London for $181.6 which balance
the account at you will provide by the annexed statement.
We hope that the articles will reach you in good order and trusting
that we may have other and frequent opportunities of being useful to you,
we remain
Sam'l G. P. Breckinridge
Brigadier General

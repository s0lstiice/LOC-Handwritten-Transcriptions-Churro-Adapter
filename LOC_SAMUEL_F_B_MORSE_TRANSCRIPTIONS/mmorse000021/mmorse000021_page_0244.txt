I feel anxious; and as the offer of Mr Paxton,
seems to be a good opportunity for my advancement,
I feel assured that you will not permit so good
an one to pass me by.
Our appropriation will expire in January next,
and then I would entirely be out of means, if
not of employment.
I should not be a stranger in New York
for there reside many of my dear Mother's con-
nections. If the Company would make me a
fair and just compensation, I would like very much
to be advanced to that termination.
If you think favorably of this matter,
I would feel much gratified, by your stating to
Mr Paxton what you know of my character and
of my qualifications for the post.

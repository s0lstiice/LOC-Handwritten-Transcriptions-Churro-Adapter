To the Trustees of the New York, Albany
and Buffalo Magnetic Telegraph Company
Gentlemen
If the stock securities to be issued
to me in your company, please
issue to Simon Kendall an amount
equal to ten per cent, including
that already issued and that to be
issued hereafter, of which this order
is to be taken as my assignment.
New York Nov. 7. 1846-
Very Respectfully Yours
S.F.B.M.

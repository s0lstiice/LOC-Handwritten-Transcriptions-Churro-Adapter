Dear Vail
Received yours of the 11th.
Let us get along as quietly as we can
with the plague till we can cut loose from
him. Be therefore, as good natured as you
can under the circumstances, and let us
act harmoniously with him till we see
the line through to Phil. then (if necessary)
we will have a reckoning with him, should
he be found on my ground -- but enough
of him at present.
Mr Comell will send you tomorrow at 2 o'clock, so that you can
get them on Wednesday morning, 50 copies
to increase your artillery. Gen C will be at
Fort Lee with 2 Registers of Receiving
magnets. He will bet with you the line
from Fort Lee to Phil -- & with me from It.
Lee to Norfolk and will be ready on Wednesday
day. You can thus follow the instruction
out for Thursday & Tuesday. If correct all
through we can of course advise by Telegraph.
Be sure on a day nothing by Telegraph which
you would not wish C. to see, under the
impression you are conversing only with me.
Anything concerning him, must be by letter, or
between us in conversation.

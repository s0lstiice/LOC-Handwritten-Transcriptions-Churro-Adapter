the line is not open to Jersey City! and that a

now getting orders of wire, (iron,) made of four
wires, which look well fitted for service, better
than single stiff wires. I am having some
specimens turned, & some finished. I hope to
get the lines right, in some way. Now, at last,
the means are coming in sufficient to drive me
a-head with this Phil. & Bath line, & complete your communications between N.Y.
& Washington. You are aware that Mr. Kendall
was die appointed by those persons in Kentucky
who were to have furnished money for the
Phil. & Bath line, but I have realized all I
said in assuring him that I could, with the
aid of friends, raise all that (or more than) is
wanted -- and the instalments are now coming in.

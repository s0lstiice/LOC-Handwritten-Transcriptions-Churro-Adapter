Washington, Jan 28, 1866.
Genl.
Knowing the interest which you take in every thing that
becomes the service of the country & its progress, I venture to
ask your good offices with the Dept. Telegraph Company to
put at my disposal the requisite facilities for making
this province of legislature between one of our principal cities, in
Conjunction with the Confederacy. I can furnish the necessary
instruments & means to carry out the thing in an excellent
way to the country & without any idea of what it may have involved in favor of the Telegraph Company, at home & of
your invention here & abroad. S.C. Walker Esq. whom I
have asked to call upon you will explain fully any
desires I have for the public by your address in
the matter which he will set before you.
Very respectfully yours
Pres. H D Bann

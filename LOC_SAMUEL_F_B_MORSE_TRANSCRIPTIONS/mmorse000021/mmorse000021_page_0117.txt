My Dear Sir,
By the date of this circular you
will receive that it was intended that you should
have received it with its enclosure long since; I was
not aware till a few days since that you had not
received it, but upon looking over some old drawers
I discovered the documents relating to the picture
in question, and found that yours & (Mr. H's or Mr.
H's) had not been sent, and also perceived by a note
in the list of subscribers that you were both absent
at the time in Europe. I take the earliest oppor-
tunity after the discovery to discharge the last
dollar of the amount received by me on account of
that picture. — With renewed thanks, and assurances
of my warm esteem
Yr friend & Servt
S. F. B. Morse
Copy
To Frederick Pink, Esq.
and
Geo. Harvey. Esq.
March. 16th 1846.

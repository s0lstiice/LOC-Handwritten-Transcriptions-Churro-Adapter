Washington Jan. 3. 1846.
Dear Vail
You ask for a more portable
die magnet, I think the one I send you will
be portable enough. I spare it with great
reluctance it present for twisted to take it
with me to New York; but Mr. Kendall has
persuaded me to let you have the use of it to
test the integrity of the line, and I have there-
fore consented. So soon as I can get my
other little one fixed, I may contrive to let
you have it altogether. I have shown it to Dr.
Page on our years day. His delighted with it,
says it is the ne plus, cant account for it,
seems as much pleased as if he had made it himself.

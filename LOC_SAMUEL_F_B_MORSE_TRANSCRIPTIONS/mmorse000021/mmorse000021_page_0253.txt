Washington DC Sept 8th 1846
Dear Mone
Your letter of the 27th
came to hand yesterday. I had no idea
that the injury you received was so great
and am glad to learn that you have got
over it so well this hot weather.
I cannot speak so well of the 2d line
from this to your city, as you do of the
western line, between our lines I think
Mr Kendall is very remiss, as the superin
tendent. Even when in Washington he does
not visit the office for day together, and
yet he should be here, and along the line,
until matters are systematical, regulated,
whether Mr Bailey or Clark will make,
they say, any more complaints, as Mr K
lays no regard to them, or if he does, they
are not regarded. I must say I am disap
pointed in his management, receiving as
he does such a large salary I know it
will not be so long. There is a good deal
of grumbling
We get along beautifully as usual with the
U.S. line, and there is nothing requiring your
presence here now, though I should be glad
to see you, I would advise you to stay away

Hartford April 16th 1846
Professor Morse
Sir
Your letter of the 13th
just has just come to hand, and I hasten to
answer it. I cannot describe the surprise,
mortification and regret which the contents and
tone of your letter occasioned me.
I will tell you plainly all I have
done, together with my motives. I think I shall
be able to convince you that I intended right, and
therefore not blamable.
The paragraph you speak of, in the Daily
Courant did receive my "approbation" but the
error shall be as promptly and fully corrected
as you desire.
I did believe there was a "U.S. Telegraphic Comp-
any" that Prof. Morse, Mr. Vail, Dr. Gale and Mr.
Smith formed that Company. and I supposed
that all the other companies were subordinates.
I also supposed that the two works, by Mr.
Vail, of which I have the agency, belonged to this
Company -- or that they had an interest in them. I
do not remember that I was told so, in so many
words -- although I have the impression that Mr
Vail said something to that effect. At any rate,
I believed such to be the fact, and I acted upon it.

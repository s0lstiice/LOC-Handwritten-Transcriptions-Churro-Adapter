Washington 23d 1846
7th
Dear Sir.
I obtained just now a letter from Paris by the late Steamer
saying that the French government is establishing a telegraphic
line between Paris & the Belgian frontier; Should that be
the case, no doubt the Belgian government would continue
that line to the German frontier.-- I am exceedingly anxious
to hear from you, perhaps, something more positive about your
success in Paris.-- I wrote Israël also Quinault to point the
matter there.-- If that proposition of yours is accepted
at Bremen I think we could come whole Europe with steam.--
If you have any thing interesting please let me know it.
Mr Steinhauer wishes to see you.
Yours
Very truly yours
Ch. L. Gilmore

Oct 7. 1845
If the current comes from Platinum plate
through the state point, through cloth & thin to
cylinder, the markings is on the upper side of
plate, strong and legible, both with Hyd.
Pot, and also with Ind. Im. but if the
poles of battery are reversed the strong
markings is on the underside with Hyd. Pot.
but nothing with Ind. Im.

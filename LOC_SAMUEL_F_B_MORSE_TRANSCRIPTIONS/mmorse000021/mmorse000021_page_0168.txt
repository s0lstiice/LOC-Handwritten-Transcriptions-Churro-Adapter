Washington
Copy to T. O. Smith April 17. 1846
Mr Smith, The enclosed woodcut has just
been handed me by McVail Obo tells me that it
was given him a few minutes ago by a gentleman
who informed him that it was taken from a bill
of Davis & Co. Boston advertising to make these
instruments that the said gentleman informed
that Mr Davis was making 100 and that he
had seen a mechanic who was making a certain
part of the machinery for McDavis for one
dollar apiece. If you are on the spot will
you not look after this matter, and ascertain
the facts in the case and if Davis is seriously
warning him, and stop him.
I have nothing of importance to communicate except that the line from Baltimore
with probably be in working order by the middle of the next week
Dr Coates
Philadelphia
F.O.J.S.
Boston
April 17. 1846
S. D. M.

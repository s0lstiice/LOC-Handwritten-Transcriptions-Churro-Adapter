Copy
"Ordered that Alfred Vail & Henry J. Rogers are
"permitted to have the use of the Telegraph wires from
"the 1st of Decr. 1846 to the 4th of March 1847, they agreeing
"to keep up the line at their own expense, and without any
"charge for their personal services, other than the proceeds
"of the Telegraph, and to hold the same subject to any
"future order of the Department. C.J.
Journalized 16th Novr. 1846.
P.O. Office Department
Novr. 25th 1846
Gentlemen
Above you have a Copy of an order made by
me on the 16th instant setting forth the terms on which
you can have the use of the U. States Elec. Mag. Telegr.
wires on the line between this city and Baltimore Md.
from the 1st Decr. 1846 to the 4th of March 1847. You
will please if the terms suit you furnish me with
your acceptance of them in writing before the 1st
proximo.
I am very respectfully
Y. Obsernt.
B. Johnson
Messr. Alfred Vail & Henry J. Rogers
Assist. Superintendt. of U.S.
Mag. Telegraph. Presnt.

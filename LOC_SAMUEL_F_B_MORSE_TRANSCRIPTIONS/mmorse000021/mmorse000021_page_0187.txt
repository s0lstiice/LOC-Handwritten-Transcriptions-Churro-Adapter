I would say that having spent 16 months
in the Republic of Mexico myself in putting
into operation and Leprinting Machinery
of different kinds I have had a good opportunity
to become acquainted with the peculiarities of
the country I have taken great pains to inform myself
in relation to introducing patent printing, the
right &c and have taken measures to introduce
some things which I think will result in
my favour if pursued
While in that country my attention was turned
to various schemes which appeared to me
could make money at if put in execution
and among others was the Magnetic Telegraph
I have not room to give you all the details
in this letter but should subject Strike
your favourable and you should deem it of sufficient importance to correspond with me here
after I will give you more particulars.
before I close allow me to make one request
and that is if you have not already secured
the right as above and should think best to do so
that you remember me and give me an opportunity to participate with you if you should find me worthy
Yours Truly Respectfully
Harry Hoyt
Copy Answer
Washington
U.S. Sep. 30, 46 I have no objection to making an arrangement
of this nature similar to those for European countries. My Agents
bear all expenses and risks, and divide half the proceeds
with me. I will sell their instruments at cost, and give them all
the information respecting the management of the whole invention.
I shall of course require testimonials and references.
Very Respectfully yours
Hoyt

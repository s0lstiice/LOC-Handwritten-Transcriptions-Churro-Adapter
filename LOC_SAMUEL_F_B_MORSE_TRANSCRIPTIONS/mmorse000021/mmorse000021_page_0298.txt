Copy to M. Welles, fin of the United States Treasury
Washington D.C. Oct. 15th 1846
To Oliver Tiffany, now in New York.
Dear Sir,
On my arrival last evening I immediately
called on Jacob Bigelow, Esq. the gentleman
whom I referred in my conversation with you,
and with whom I had made arrangements the
last winter in regard to my invention in Canada. From him I learn in general terms, that
matters in relation to the securing of a Patent in
Canada are in such forwardness that the passage of
this bill is almost certain the coming session of
Parliament. He wished me to say that there are
already many applications already to him for terms
upon which he would contract for the construction of lines, and he will be happy to
arrange with this that Company who will agree
to give the Patentee the same terms that the
New York Albany & Buffalo Company have
received their permission to construct their line.
As my agreement with Mr Bigelow places the
control in his hands, I beg to refer you to him,
and he will be pleased to confer with you on
the subject. His address is Jacob Bigelow Esq
Washington, D.C. - With respect to
the state of the telegraph, prevented my coming with
you as we had agreed upon when I left you. Mr. S. B. Morse,
Kindall, to whom I mentioned your desire to meet him in Phila. I
with write you, He says, however, that he shall not make any terms to Bigelow
with further subdivisions in the State of NY, until the difficulty relative to the present side line
are settled.
Y. H. Sent
J. H. B. Morse

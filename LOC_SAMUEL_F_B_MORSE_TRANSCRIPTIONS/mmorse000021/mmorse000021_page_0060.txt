By closing "Key for 12," the battery will operate all the magnets
and strike all the levers, the circuit being complete for the
whole battery. By closing "Key for 9 cups," the battery will
operate but 3 of the magnets, 9 cups being only in action
and not having strength to move magnet A. By closing
"Key for 6 cups," the battery will operate but 2 of the mag-
nets, 6 cups only being in action, and not having strength to move
nither A1 or A2 and so of the rest.
Sam'l P. B. Morse
New York Jan. 28th 1846
Exhibited & Explained to me
the day January 30th 1846

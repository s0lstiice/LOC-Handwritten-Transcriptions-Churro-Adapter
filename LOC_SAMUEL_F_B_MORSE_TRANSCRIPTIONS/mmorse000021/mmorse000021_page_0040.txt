There is one thing in respect to Lowell, in which you are
in error. You say he has thrown aside my magnets
"& substituted his". He never put mine in use at all, &
what he calls his, is but a clumpingification of mine, that is
I say, he has simply multiplied the parts, having 4
magnets instead of one, requiring 4 times the work &
multiplying four times the chances of derangement, chances
which his magnets oppose to a chance -- if they who
have used them are correct in their reports of them.
And for this thing he expects to take out a patent. He
cannot get it. Let us I am not right, & if he could, he will
not use it without my permission. Measures shall be
taken to stop his further interference on my ground.
You have tested me more than you are aware of in asking
me to take charge of the books from Gideons & the cost will
be heavy (taken in the case) of such a quantity. There are
several hundred weights. You ask further that I will bring
in wire from Phila. If time permits me to stop & get it, I
will endeavors to do it. Perhaps Mr Kendall will take it
when he passes this town next -- I will write about it. I
have much to say to you. I have just put in action
one of my little magnets, substituting it for the big magnet
in the Office. It works very strong & beautifully. I want
to try some experiments with you up the River. Put me in
mind when I see you. I speak hastily but truly as ever
Yours Fred L. Co.
S.H. Co. Morley

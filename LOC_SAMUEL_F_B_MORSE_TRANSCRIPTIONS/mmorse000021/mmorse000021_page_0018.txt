New York
Saturday Jan. 10th 1846.
evening 6 o'clock
Dear Rail
I write in great haste just to
say the arrangements for Monday must
be postponed till Tuesday: a wire is
broke at Haltensack bridge by the tall
mast of a sloop in faking the draw, and
will require some time in repairing
besides the getting of the Battery of
50 pairs from Somerville.
Let that regulations made for
Monday and Sunday be for Tuesday and
Wednesday. I have got wire no 30 for small
magnets & Galvanometers, and Lewis Word is
winding it. I shall see how well on
Monday. Hope to have my other Furnish
out ready by Monday noon.
In haste truly yours
S. F. B. Morel

Baltimore Feb. 23. 1846
My dear Sir,
Just as I was taking my seat
in the cars at Washington, my friend, Rev.
Wm. Smith handed me your note, in which
on behalf of the church, you notify me
of my appointment as a delegate to the
Sabbath convention to be held tomorrow
evening. I truly regret that business
which I cannot forego, compels me to be
absent in New York at the time.
But while absent in person I
trust I shall be with you in spirit, and
I wish sincerly pray that the Lord of the
Sabbath may preside at the deliberations of
the Convention, and direct it to measures

Baltimore Hotel,
Baltimore April 10th 1845
Mr. H. Moore:
Dear Sirs and Friends in hope of seeing
you or Mr Bail in this city during my
stay hereabouts - The delay in getting the
two cord twisted portions, our business for
some days together with the delays in moving
about the various steamers on their
route - eight vessels & coaches in all - at the
train station on two of which, a double row
compete to use pipe which I got from Colt
& Luten Estate Richmond will be finished
so as to enable us to complete by Wednesday
or Thursday we hope to put the finishing
stitch on by raising the last mat (now
all ready) at the north side of the Surgeon
damah. The iron cord works well to wit.
ington. I trust the solidity of the line from
Pul. to Balt. will fully demonstrate the
value of the "cord" in preventing delays from
break & saving much cost in supervision.
About the testimonies, you spoke of yesterday
- I did not exactly understand you. Clarke has only made
the two from western line, of which I showed

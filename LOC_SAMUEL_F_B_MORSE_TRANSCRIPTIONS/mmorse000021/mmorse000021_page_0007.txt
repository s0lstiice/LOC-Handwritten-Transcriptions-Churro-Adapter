In reply to this note I sent the package containing the specific 1 and drawings of the Telegraph, with my telegraphic apparatus, and drawings of the apparatus of the Baron Rothschild, to the Secretary of the Treasury.
In the summer of 1845, I made my arrangements to visit St. Petersburg for a few months to examine into the progress of the Telegraph, and determined to visit St. Petersburg for the purpose of examining with the Baron Rothschild in person the apparatus of the Telegraph, and to receive the following note enclosing a letter to the Baron Rothschild

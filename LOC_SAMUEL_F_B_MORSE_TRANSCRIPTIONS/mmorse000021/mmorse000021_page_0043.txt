Rochester, 17th January, 1846
Prof. S. F. B. Morse
Dear Sir,
Mr. William
Fickend of this place has invented an improve-
ment in the method of imparting the electricity
to the wire of the Magnetic Telegraph, and has
made application for Letters Patent. He has assigned part of his right to myself and John W.
Books of Rochester. We have made no movement towards disposing of the right Before
we do so, we wish to inquire, Do you, in case the letters patent are obtained, and the
operation prove successful, wish or feel desirous of an interest in the same?
Respectfully yours
William Wiley
By this invention one stroke of the finger is sufficient to produce each character.
The advantages of the operation, we conclude to be
1st. Increased accuracy of execution.
2d. Increased speed of do
3d. The employment of less Skilful and consequently lower priced hands, and in case that two
additional wires were required by the old system the saving of that wire and extra hands and machines
to the man.

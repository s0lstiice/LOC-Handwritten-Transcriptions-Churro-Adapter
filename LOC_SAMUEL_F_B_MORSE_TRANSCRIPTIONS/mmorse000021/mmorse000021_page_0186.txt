April 21, 1846
Middlesex Washington County Vermont
Propp & J Moore
To Sir
I take the liberty to address this Letter to
you notwithstanding I have never had the
pleasure of an acquaintance with you. The
apology I would offer is the want of information
I wish to make some inquiries in relation to your
Magnetic Telegraph (not quite its operation and
effect for of that I am already satisfied)
I would inquire whether you have secured
a right for your invention in Mexico if
so whether you have taken any measures to intro-
duce it there if not would you like to enter
in to some arrangement with some one that
has been in that country understands the
situation of the country and the language to
go there and secure the right and put it into
operation in case such a thing can be done, or
if you have already secured the right for
that Republic would you enter into an arrangement
with some proper person to go there
and try to establish a line in some part of
that country and experiment and demonstrate
the thing and if some thing can be made
(over)

Middletown Connecticut Nov. 24th 46
Col. S. B. Morse
Dear Sir
By yesterday mail in a short sheet I stated the outline of a plan which I ventured to you would
in my own expectation, give to the Magnetic Telegraph the
greatest effect possible with your present register and all
habit. Let the mode should have been expressed in
my haste description then I could deduce if what some of
what I had written so as to present the whole in one
sheet.
Let a, in the above figure, represent a wire
around which, as an axis, revolves the hollow barrel
gij made of nonconducting material. The wire is
broken within the barrel, as shown by the vacancy 3c.
This vacancy, however, can be filled by the piece 3e which
is always in contact with the wire at c and when the
piece is pushed down it makes a contact at b also,
thus completing the circuit. There is a lever moveable
around the fixed point c and its other extremity, f can be
depressed, like a key in music, by the finger. When this
is done, the spring on the plate 5 down so that the pin d,
or its suspension bar, seen at 8 in the figure will inter-
fere with the plate 4 be pushed down.

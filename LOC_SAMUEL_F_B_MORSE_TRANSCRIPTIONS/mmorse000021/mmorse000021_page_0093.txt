Washington City February 20th 1846
Between Morse
Sir I received a letter from Mr Rail
today and he requests me to send you the following
communication
With respect &c
J E Rail
Between Morse
I am now waiting patiently your return to this
City & hope you will not delay a single day as I am anxious
to return to Washington & resume my duties. Cornell has thrown
out your receiving magnet & put in his own at the time stating
on this end of the line - I take no part in the matter & shall
not share with him in any manner the responsibilities of
this end of the line - when you come in please call at the
Telegraph Office in Philadelphia & bring with you one which I
have had already correct with silk for the receiving magnets
& also for the Register magnets - they are in separate coils
and of different sized wire - Mr. Clark has also made
6 receiving magnets all of which were ordered for the 6
registers to be made and I would thank you to bring them on if
you will - Yours truly - Alfred Rail
75 Beeckman Str - N Y

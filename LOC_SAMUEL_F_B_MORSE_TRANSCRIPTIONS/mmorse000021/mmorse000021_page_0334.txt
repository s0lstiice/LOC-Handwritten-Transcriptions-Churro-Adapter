I applied immediately, desiring my notice
published as sent at once, and also to prepare
under advice a formal legal notice, requesting
that any useful member of my friends who are British
subjects join me in such notice and that it also
appear in the "Gazette by Authority."
On the 12th inst. Mr D. writes me again & sends
the enclosed from the Montreal Gazette
He mentions the names of several of my friends
who are ready at once to put $1000 each into
the stock of a Company if it is formed for a line
of Telegraph so I send you immediate action
that His Excellency shall not be before me
I applied at once, reproting at large, the
great & indispensable necessity of harmonious
cooperation with the lines in the U.Sates under
your patent directing him to exhibit my letter
to many influential friends whom I named, and
indeed to any who should take an interest in
the subject, and to offer at once for me as
your ally & promising your sanction, that any
companies which may be formed, who will
recognize your claims at the inventor and agree
in advance to allow you the same interest as is
allowed you in the U.Sates Such companies may
be organized as soon as they please that we
will all aid in procuring a patent for you, when
your claims shall not be increased and I advised
further that we together procure a Charter to
construct such lines throughout the provinces of
Upper & Lower Canada as the wants of the public
may require

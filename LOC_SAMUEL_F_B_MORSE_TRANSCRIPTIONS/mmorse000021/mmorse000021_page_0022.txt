I have heard that some wit-

ance with Mr. E. commenced with my
residence in his father's family when
I met Mr. Ellsworth morning, noon, night
and during his stay there I have never
seen any signs of the man addicted to
drinking. Indeed, this subject was a
topic of conversation, and Mr. E. said
openly and frankly, that in his country,
it has been his custom, as it was the

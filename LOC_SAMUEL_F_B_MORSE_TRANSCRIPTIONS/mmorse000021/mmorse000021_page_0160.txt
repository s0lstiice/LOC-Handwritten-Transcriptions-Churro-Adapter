Boston Mass. April 12th 1846
No 41. Essex Street
Dear Sir,
Having been informed that your painting of the House of Representatives, contains an accurate likeness of my father, Hon. Nathaniel Upsham, of M.H. I am anxious to learn something concerning it. We have no portrait of him and are very desirous of obtaining one. Will you have the goodness to inform me in whose possession this painting is? Would all to know if you have the original sketches from which the painting was made? If they are still in existence would you be willing to part with the one of my father, for which we should be willing to compensate you. Has there ever been an engraving taken from this painting?
I visited New York last week for the purpose of seeing you in relation to this subject, but learned from your father that you were in Washington. May I have the satisfaction of hearing from you soon?
Yours very truly
Prof S. F. B. Morse
Albert G. Upsham

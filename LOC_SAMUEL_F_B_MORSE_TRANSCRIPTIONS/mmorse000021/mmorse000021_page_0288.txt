Dear Mail
I am detained here, & I know not when I
shall be able to leave.
I have filed my caveat regularly at the Patent
Office for common letter printing Telegraph
I explained it to Dr P who is delighted
with it, and says to me (privately) it
is the best letter printing Telegraph yet
proposed, I shall immediately have it made
and then if the House Hunting has
not already exploded, I will challenge
him to contend with both mines first
he shall contend with my letter
printing Telegr, which will beat him
more than two to one, and then with
my present Telegr, which shall beat
him 4 to 1.
I was at the office a day or two ago &
saw your Cathie Zantzinger said you
wished to sell it. I will take it with
all the fixtures that belong to it at
the price you set one hundred & fifty
dollars if you will let me have it at
that, and deduct that amount from the
proceeds of the Telegraphs If you consent I
will have it packed up with my other things
immediately for N.York.

Baltimore August 12th 1846
Dear Professor
It is my painful duty
to inform you of the death of Mr Rogers, little
boy Harry-
It is supposed that he went to the bath
till & unknowingly turned the spigot, connecting
with the boiler used for heating the water, & in so
doing accidentally fell into the tub & was
dreadfully scalded-
It happened yesterday afternoon (Aug 11th)
about 3 o'clock He lingered until 11 o'clock this
morning when he died.
He will be buried to-morrow
Very Respectfully yours
John H. Witman

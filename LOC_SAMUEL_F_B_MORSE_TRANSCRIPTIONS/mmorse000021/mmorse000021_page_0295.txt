the President of the United States of

Constitution and the laws of the
United States of America, do order
and declare that the people of the
State of Kentucky are hereby
annexed to and form a part of the
United States of America, and
that the people thereof are
hereby made citizens of the United
States, and are entitled to all the
rights, privileges, and immunities
of citizens of the United States.
And I, the President of the United
States of America, do hereby
appoint, by and with the consent
of the Senate, John J. Crittenden,
of Kentucky, as United States
Marshal for the said State of
Kentucky, to be in the service of
the United States of America,
and to hold the said office for
the term of one year from the
date of this order.
Given at the City of Washington,
July 10, 1861.
ABRAHAM LINCOLN
President of the United States of

for Great Western 30th May
Paris 24th May 1846
Sam'l F. B. Morse Bg.
Washington
Sir,
We have received your favor of 20th ult and
although you would no doubt shortly after forwarding it
be in possession four letters of 28th Month we hand you
copy of the same by which, in case you should not have
received the original, you will perceive that your directions
regarding the electromagnets had been strictly attended to,
and that the information communicated to you by Major
Buguet was altogether erroneous and could only have arisen
from some mistake on their part. We trust the articles
were duly received, and have only to repeat that we shall
always experience pleasure in being of use to you, remaining
upon all occasions,
Sir,
Your most obedient
friend
C

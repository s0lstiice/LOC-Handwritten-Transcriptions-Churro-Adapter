I consider that it is my duty,
as the guardian of my brother, to drop
you these hasty lines, with the fervent
hope that you will be able to set
the matter right.
Mr. Vail is gone away, and has
taken Louis away from the instrument
altogether, putting Mr. Clark there,
until he returns. This is, doubtless,
intended to degrade Louis. I hardly
thought that Mr. Vail would have
carried his feelings thus far; but
I have relied upon Louis to remain
perfectly quiet; and quiet his indis-
tant feelings, until he hears from
you.
Louis has settled his accounts
with Mr. Vail, agreeably to the
order of the latter.

I am led to make this communication to
you, at this time, from seeing in the Morning Telegraph, of this city, a notice of the
destruction of the wire of the Telegraph,
recently, in New Jersey, by the passing of the
electric fluid from the circuit-jacket,
contiguous, at most here through the wire
to the post, to the earth. The wire was broken
& the post was destroyed. The conduct of
the electric current, interruptions of the

Pardon me Sir, for indicating in plain
terms my estimate of your
incomparable invention. For you Fame
will build a new Pillar in her Temple,
higher & more magnificent than
all of the others, & on the top of it inscribe the name of Morse.
I am very respectfully yours
AB Quincy.

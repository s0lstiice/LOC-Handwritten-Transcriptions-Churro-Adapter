I find it difficult to transmit your documents and
newspapers, fearing that I may put you to inconvenient expense or I would often send you such papers. The failure in reaching you of the book I sent, after many erroneous endeavors to cause it to reach you, is discouraging. If you can inform me of a convenient channel of communication it will give me great pleasure to keep you advised of anything that will interest you on this side of the water. In regard to the extension of my system in the United States there are about 1300 miles English connected by Telegraph, and these are under the control of present of Companies. The Government owns only the short experimental line of 40 miles between Baltimore and Washington. The companies find the enterprise a profitable investment of their capital, and consequently there is no doubt of its universal extension through the United States; new Contracts having been Negotiated for some thousand miles more, and to Canada, where lines are already contracted for to the distance of two or three hundred miles. A vast amount of commercial, social, legal, police, and political business is transacted by means of the Telegraph, and the beneficial effects of the Telegraph are beginning to be duly appreciated.

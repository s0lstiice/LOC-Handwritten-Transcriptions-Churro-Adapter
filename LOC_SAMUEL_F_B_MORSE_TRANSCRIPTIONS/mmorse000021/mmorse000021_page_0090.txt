Washington DC
12 Feb 1846
Resign S.F. Morse
My dear Sir
Being somewhat
in want of funds I write to ask
if it will be possible for you to lend
me the amount of my charge
against you on the Patent of $45
which is $40 – as this is my own
private matter I should esteem
it a great favor at this time.
On the reserve Kelly & Munyon
have a charge of $30 – which I think
you told me the Company were to pay
will you be good enough to try and get
this also for us and I will
your truly
J. Beechum
Sent check on Bk of Work
$40. March 6. 1846
from NY State

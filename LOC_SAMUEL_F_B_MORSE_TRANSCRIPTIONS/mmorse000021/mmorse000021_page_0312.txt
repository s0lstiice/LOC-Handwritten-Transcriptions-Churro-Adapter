New York 27th At - 46
Dear Sir:
Having it in contemplation
to give some letters on the Magnetic
Telegraph I have called at your office
several times to inquire whether what arrange-
ment it is necessary to make with
you in regard to it; but insensibly
finding the door locked, I am obliged
to adopt this as the only probable means
of satisfying the enquiry. I have for sev-
eral years been engaged in teaching
and lecturing upon the Natural
Sciences and can furnish most satisfactory
testimonials as to competency &c. Wishing to
be able to give complete explanations in
regard to all such matters, I have
thought of procuring a working model
of the Telegraph for the purpose. Will you
take the trouble to send me a note on
the subject at your earliest convenience.
Address
New York City P.O.-

8 Epsom Street
New York City Decem 5. 1846.
My Dear Sir, By a pamphlet I have recently received
through the Penny post I see that an agreement has
been entered into with John J. Haley Esq to construct
a line of Telegraph from Petersburg to New Albany
Not having any acquaintance with
Mr Haley I am induced to apply to you asking the
favor to give me a letter of introduction to that
gentleman, with a view of obtaining from him the
situation of canvassing agent throughout the
entire extent of the line for subscriptions to the stock.
My large acquaintance throughout the whole
extent of the line and my intimate intimate knowledge of
the habits and manners of the inhabitants of the
Eastern section of the Country would enable me,
I flatter myself, to place the matter before them
in such a manner as to induce an immediate
subscription to the stock.
While writing allows me to take the liberty
of saying a word in reference to the route chosen.
See you go by the way of Wilmington to Charleston.

selected route for various reasons. Wilmington

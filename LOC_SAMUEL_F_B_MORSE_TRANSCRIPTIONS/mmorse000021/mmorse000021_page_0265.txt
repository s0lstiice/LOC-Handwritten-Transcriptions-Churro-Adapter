1846
Patience Sept 11
Sir
Home from the 31 Aug was in, I entertain nothing. I fear
that the arrangement in your final with Mr Kendall would defeat
my object. I suppose I have no doubt, with myself a very high opinion
of Mr H. as being competent to conduct the management of the Company, &
might perhaps for ought it knows fall in with the arrangement, but as I should
have so short a time as the 1st of October to do all that I can, then and
not I think be time sufficient to ascertain the value of your position & to other things & in that time
such, that you can revoke it at will or not, I shall give me time
enough I would not hesitate to undertake it. And considering
profitably, no better arrangement could be entered into, than such as
now exists between yourself & him. I should be glad some how
to have a little interest in the affair so as to have something to do
with it, if it could be so.
The members of Alderman Lyceum, of which my son is
a member, are anxious of obtaining the aid of some distinguished men
in a course of lectures to enable them to erect a Hall for their meetings &
others can help them. The Militia has I believe met with good encour
agement & approbation, & it is to be hoped they may succeed well in this way,
well before the services. Many of the families belong to the Sabbath
School of the 7th Baptist Church here, who are erecting a splendid house
for Rev Dr Fuller of S.C. who has accepted their invitation to come
to their pastor soon as the building is completed. I hope from the Lyceum
anticipate some assistance from him. I would observe that they seem
to be a class of young men who, in stead of glorifying in their names, are aiming
to cultivate their minds, in the attainment of knowledge & useful arts. I
hope that you will find it convenient to oblige them. I also write them some good advice
to have in their counsels.
Very very truly
David Brabant

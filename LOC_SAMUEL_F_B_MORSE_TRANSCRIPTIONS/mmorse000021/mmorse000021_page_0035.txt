I have just got up letter of yesterday. I am glad to find
that the line is in good order to Phila that the instruments
work well. Reshain is feelings towards I for the present.
Im all wrong I think in regard to any league among
Marshall - Lexington & Cornell-de - I don't think it.
At any rate we must dismiss suspicion till we get
thru this matter. Take care specially not charge him
wrongfully which you will be apt to do in the present
circumstances, when he will gain an advantage which
he is shrewd enough to observe. Be very cautious,
more depends on it than you are aware. It takes
xxxx. I have no time to add the mail is closing
Truly yours
Samuel H B Morel
New York Jan 15 1846
Dear Rail
Suppose you have acted according to the
best of judgment but the mode you have taken (very
good under the circumstances) has embarrassed me a
great deal. I am probably suppose that the line runs thru
the city of Newark, but it is more than a mile from
the place, & would require to be houpted at at great addi-
tional delay. I cannot go thru. There are two Repositors
& two receiving magnets at Fort Lee, & Cornell has turned
(today) at 10 o clock to get them in order, one Repositor he will
put in connection with you, from Fort Lee, & the other with me
in New York, if this can open a communication with Phila

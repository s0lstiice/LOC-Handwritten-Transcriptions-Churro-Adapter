May 3. 1815.
With respect to returning home, I shall
make my arrangements to be with you, (which
my life be spared) by the end of September next
or the beginning of Oct. but it will be necessary
that I should be in Eng. again (provided, always,
Providence permit) by the Sept. following, as arrangements
which I have made will require my presence, this I will
fully explain when I meet you, the moment I get home
I wish to begin to work, so that I should like to have some
portraits bespoken in season, I shall charge 40 dollars
less than Stuart for my portraits, so that if any of
my good Friends are ready, I will begin the moment
I have said "how do ye do," to them, I wish to do as
much as possible in the year I am with you; if I

My dear son
This will be handed you
by Mr Vinton, a respectable merchant
of Boston, son-in-law to Rev. McVicker,
He will bring you letters from your
Mama & brother Edw. I wish
you to do him U. any kindness
in your power He will inform
you verbally of the state of things
here. By him, on his return send
us any thing you may wish, I
he can conveniently take for us,
unless you U. conclude to come
yourself. I wrote you via
Hallifax, a few days ago I
sent two drafts, one to L.B.B. 16.9. July
the other for £25 to Mr Bromfield
I send the 2C. Exchange by this app.

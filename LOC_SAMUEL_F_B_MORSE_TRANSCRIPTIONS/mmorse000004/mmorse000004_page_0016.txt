Dear Brother
We have received no letters from you since
November. Your last was dated in September We have improved every opportunity. We are all well in good spirits.
Mama has been troubled with a pain in her gums & face in consequence of the extraction of two teeth. Richard has spent the winter at home. He has been very dull & low-spirited. A few weeks since he went to Utica in company with cousin Sam Breeze, We have received two letters from him since. He writes in remarkably good spirits, & appears as happy as a King. - Cousin Sam has just returned from a cruise in the Congress; the third since the war, but he has met with no frigates or armed vessels of any description.
Papa has published an Appeal to the Public, on the subject of Miss H. Adams's charges. He is now publishing a pamphlet which will contain all the particulars & details of the controversy as connected with the True Reasons, and Harvard College.
Richard has not yet fixed upon his professor. I am still studying on Mr Rockwood's Office but shall go to Litchfield soon.

Mr Randolph expects to go home soon.
I dined with Mr. & Mrs. Brown a few days
since; they are well & desire time to
give their regards to you & Mr. & Mrs.
Allston. — I rejoice most cordially at the
melody of those dear friends last mentioned.
I need not say how anxious I am to
see Mr. & Mrs. Brown's last pictures. — After having
been so long enjoyed the happiness of close
intimacy with this estimable couple, I
assume you it requires no small exercise
of philosophy to make myself
contented here without them, & having
lost you into the bargain. — My love
to them & Mr. & Mrs. Virgin & family
& believe
me yours very truly
C H Leslie

There is no affliction of show in him, he endeavors to avoid the public observation rather than to court it, he generally sides about with his sister the Duchess of Oldenburg, in a perfectly plain dress, as unattracting as possible, in a common round hat and plain black, blue, or brown coat; he is very active, rises before any one in London, and with his sister takes his walk privately, by the time the sun has risen, he has already seen every thing in and near London worthy notice, & I believe, has quitted it to-day for Portsmouth where he will embark for France or Russia; If there ever was a ruler who has set a great example to the world it is Alexander, he has conquered the greatest conqueror that ever lived, he has conquered that people who have for 25 years been the scourge of Europe, not only by his prowess in the field, but by his magnanimity; he has not merely conquered them by the sword, he has gained a great victory, a victory over their feelings, provoked as Alexander must have been by the abuse of the French, to have his dominions overrun by them, and the blaze of his capital scarcely extinguished, still fresh in his memory and that of his troops; who ever possessed magnanimity like his, who fought his way to the capital of his enemy, and with all these injuries before his eyes, spared it from the vengeance of his soldiers, and with generosity unparalleled, gave them their liberty, restored them to their ancient dynasty and a new constitution; left them un plundered, or even without indemnification for the past, in short, like a Christian, forgave them all.

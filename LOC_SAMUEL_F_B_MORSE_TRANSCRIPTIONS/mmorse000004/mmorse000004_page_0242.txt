My dear son We omit no favorable oppo-
tion to write you - Long letters were sent you by
the Galen - since by our friend Mr Wilson,
via N. York, who was to go home to France
there he expected to find you - I write this
by New Packet for Liverpool with it I
send you two pamphlets, which will deeply
interest you - just published here - One on
Unitarianism in this country - which is producing
a great & good effect, on the cause of
truth - the other Remarks on the Rev. J.
Inchgrain's letters in the Quarterly Rev.
You will be much pleased with him - I
have sent them to Mr Wilberforce, to be
sent to Mr Bromfield for you, I hope
you will get them safely -
We are all well - Your brother's able
Instructor in Anatomy, Dr Warren,
good man, was buried last week, generally
camented - We fear Mr Rockwood is declining
in health - Your splendid house
has lost the sight of one of her eyes by a
cataract - Your cousin Saml. Breese, who

Cheltenham 5 Oct. 1814.
Dear Sir,
In congratulating you upon the receipt of letters from your hon. and father of so recent date as you mention, I regret that his sentiments on the subject of your going to France are not communicated, so as in case your going to carry with you the gratifying sensibilities of his approval, which has you must undoubtedly have answered by the next arrival to your letter you wrote to him by Dart Cushing, I think it will be well to wait for The Acco. which I sent to your father from which you now give me an extract, was forwarded to him by Dart Cushing, who consequently must have been arrived, I therefore wonder that you have not an answer to what you wrote by him.
By your father's calculation you will see that the sum of £191.16.8 which he estimates to be in my hands at the time of his writing, is consistent with the sum devoted to your expenses here for the winter
Ever affy
your affy Servt
Brigadier
Wm

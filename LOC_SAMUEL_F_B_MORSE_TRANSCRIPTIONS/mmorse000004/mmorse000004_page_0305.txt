My Dear Son
Your Papa is at present absent on a journey
of about 100 miles to meet the Genl. Association of Congregation
at ministers of this State. We have written you largely by
the last Vessel that left this Port for Liverpool to the particular
care of Dr George Codman Brother to the Revd. Mr John Codman
of Dorchester Mass. you know, he very kindly offered to take
charge of any letters or parcel to you as he intended going you
to London direct from Liverpool since he left this we have
received your picture of Hercules but the cast has not arrive
d yet. We are much pleased with the picture as are all
that have seen it which are not a few and the Universal
opinion is that it must be exhibited in Boston for your
benefit we have accordingly determined it shall be, and on
accordingly getting a handsome frame made for it agree
able to the pattern of Col. Sargent's frame for his famous
piece of the Landing of the fathers the depth of the frame
will be nine inches it will cost one hundred Dollars in con
sequence you will see of letting your Works be seen in advan
tageously to you as possible. We wish much that the cast had
arrived and the papers that mention them but we have not
seen any of them as yet. You have noticed last year in
the Port Polia in Company with Mr Milton Mr Littlesey
wish you to send any thing of yours that has met the ap
probation of the amateurs in the fine Arts either of sculpture
or painting E. Goddard who has at last called on us
says you have a model to cast the Hercules effigie and that
you have had a number cast for your Friends if it is not
very expensive I would wish one or two of them one to
present to Dr Bartlett your Friend Mr Sindale says the

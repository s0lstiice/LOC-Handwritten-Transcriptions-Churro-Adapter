stone house after the Gothic order, on the corner
opposite Mr. Selfingwell's. When completed, they
will be quite an addition to the town. A Medical
Institution connected with the College, has been established.
The Professors are Dr. Munson, Smith, from Hanover
Oves, Knight, and Mr. Hillman. Dr. H. was Tutor, I think,
when you were in College. He is professor of Anatomy, and
is said to be a very good lecturer. Dr Smith is said to be
the first physician in the country, now Dr Rush is dead.
The number of students is about forty. This inconsider a
good number to begin with. They occupy Mr. Hillhous's
large stone house opposite the burying ground. During
last year there was a society formed in Coll. called the
Benevolent Society of Yale College: the object of which is
to aid indigent young men of good character & talents in
obtaining a liberal education at that College. Our fund
amounts to about 2500 dollars. We hope they will soon
be greatly increased. Your father has contributed 200
dollars and your brother each of them 25. Many
people have died in N.H. within a year past. Mr. D. Knox & Co.

Mrs. J. Dwight, Gen. Merriman, Capt. Linus, and many

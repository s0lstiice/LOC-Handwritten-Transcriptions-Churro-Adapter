My dear son,
This is to go by the Galena, which sails to-morrow
to write you a long letter some weeks ago, to go by the Liverpool
packet but by mistake my letter was not put on board, it
is to go by the Galena so that you will probably receive both
together, with some pamphlets & other papers. We suppose you
are now at Paris though we have no information from you
since your letter by Dr. Romayne. We are now very anxious
to hear from you I am daily expecting letters from you
You will not fail to improve every step I to write up, & be particular
Mr Wilder expects to go to France in a
few days, via N. York, perhaps via London, & we hope will
meet you & then he will be able to communicate to you
many particulars what we cannot write He is much disposed
to be our & your friend & to patronize you. Mr.
he offers you any assistance, in away of funds by loan
take no more than is absolutely necessary for your use
as it may perplex your business both you have to run further
into debt. We have been obliged to provide ourselves more
than we wished in order to support you & your brothers in
settling your professional education but we shall be
satisfied to have thus exerted ourselves if you behave
well & succeed in your profession, & become useful to
your fellow men Your brother Edwin we have just
heard, is very well at Litchfield, wholly engaged in
his professional studies, who already acquired a high
reputation in that town His mind we are very happy
also to hear, is very seriously impressed with religious
concern for his own future happiness This is also
the case with your brother Richard, in a still greater
degree He spent the last Sabbath with us, I returned
to his studies (in which he says he has great enjoyment)
yesterday His mind is very tender, amiable & affectionate,
I we had great comfort in our interview with him
We daily think of them & you, my dear son, in our prayers
How happy would be our meeting next fall, if God
preserve us all to that time, I wish to bring us together
if all our sons should have come in the best
sense of the phrase "children of God" sons of the
Almighty, & we meet not only as parents & children
very dear to each other but also as Christians, fellow heirs

New York December 20th 1814.
My dear Sir
On the 12th inst I wrote to you for the Criterion via Lisbon
in answer to your favour of Sept. 9 which I that day received, and enclosing No. 4 of a set of Cachanges for £25. Sterling. Having another
good opportunity by Mr Chk King who will go direct to England, I embrace it to send you No. 3 of the same set. The first two numbers
I transmitted to your father, on the 15th in my letter above refer to, I requested you to purchase Chillingworth's works which would
make up the sum of £25. without considering that there might be some expenses attending the packing & transportation of the books.
I hope you will pardon this forgetfulness, and the trouble which I put you to, for my sake. Be assured that I very sensibly feel your
kindness. As you are now in France, I hope I shall hear from you often, and that you will give me an detail of your
occupations. I enlarge more upon your own private views I feel injsy. Whatever you write concerning yourself will be interesting.
Do not be afraid of Egotism; all such fear should be banished between friends. Mrs J. & our little son are well. She begs again
to be remembered to you, I unitly heartily, in the good wishes of
Dear Sir
Yours very sincerely
Sam'l A. Faris Jr.
W Samt F. B. Morse.

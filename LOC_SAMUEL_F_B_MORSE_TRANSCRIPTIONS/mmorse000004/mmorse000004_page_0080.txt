London June 24th 1814
My Dear Friend,
My last by the Van American cartel I trust you
have received before this, you see I am not ceremonious, for
without waiting an answer, I take advantage of a kind passage
to one of your letters, viz. that to hear from me at any time will
give you great pleasure; and improve a good opportunity by a
cartel to Boston, to write you a short letter.
The present time, in this part of the world, as you
will have heard before this reaches you, is a very interesting
and full of events, London was never gayer than now, it was
never so crowded with strangers of all descriptions, and more
especially of the greatest character in Europe, the Emperor
Alexander of Russia, the King of Prussia, the Marshal
Blucher, and the Kettman of the Czapsky, Count Statoff, are
all here, with a numerous tribe of foreign Princes, such
a visit from such Illustrious personages, you may well
think, produces no ordinary bustle; I have had the good
fortune to see them all a number of times, the Emperor
Alexander, I have seen 14 times; so that I am quite familiar
with his face; he is a man of good stature, stout, and handsome,
his countenance has much dignity, and is expressive
of great benevolence, his complexion is naturally fair, but
a little browned by the fatigues of his campaigns, his hair is
light and combed from the side towards the middle of his

London May 7th 1814.
Dear Morse, Mess. Port. Mothers and
myself arrived here late last night.
Being considerably fatigued by our
journey, and not equipped quite
in the English style, we shall not be
able to call up and see you either
today or tomorrow. Do come down with
friend Leslie as soon as you receive
this, if you can, I understand by Mr B.
Smith that Mr. Mellon had some letters
for me, will you have the goodness
to bring them down with you. Give
my respects to him and his lady
and remember me to our kind
Leslie. Sincerely yrs Geo Haynes
No 15 Broadway St. Thomas Ct.

I have got over it now for I would not write to you in that
mood for the world. My object in stating this is to request patronage
from some rich individual or individuals, for a year or
two longer at the rate of £250 per year, this to be advanced
to me; and if required, to return it in money, as soon as I
shall be able; or by pictures, to the amount, when I have
completed my studies. I ask for 250, because two hundred
has but barely been sufficient; and has left me nothing to
purchase prints, casts, &c. so indispensable at the present
time; if Uncle Salisbury, or Mrs. Russell, could do it, it
would be much more profitable to me than from any others.
This may seem to you an odd request, but I really do not
see any other alternative, if you wish me to be a great painter;
if you wish me to be merely a portrait painter, I can
return and obey you, but I should be miserable. Perhaps,
there would be some delicacy of feeling, in requesting such assistance,
but it might be done in the form of Commission, as
McLellan, has in some instances; for instance let some one
commission me to paint a picture for any sum, when I can
conveniently do it, and advance the sum; this is not perhaps,
strictly mercantile, but I rely on their confidence in my
integrity, I hope I have honorable feelings, and it could
be only consistent with the basest ingratitude, and total
want of honor, to deceive in such a case. But I leave
the management of this to you, whatever can be done to enable
me to prosecute to the end, my favorite studies, I shall
require see in, but do write soon and relieve me from a state
of great uneasiness. The box containing my plaster cast

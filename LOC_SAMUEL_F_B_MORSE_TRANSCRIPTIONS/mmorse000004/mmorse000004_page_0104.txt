London August 17th 1814
Dear Sea- hose of
or according to "I-hi" dear
Lorse, this is to inform you that I
have received yours of August 5th of
the two newspapers all duly.
You say you shall see me before you
go to Paris; from which I infer that
will be in London soon. Dr Irving
sent a note to you a short time ago
which I took upon me to open & forward it to
contain the information that he was just
about to depart for Paris, where he would
be very glad to execute any of your
commands. Letters are to be addressed to
him to the care of Mr Beasley, Harley Street.
Sallway called a day or two ago to see you.
For my part it will be quite impossible
for me to go to Paris with you, which
as I should like it, unless two or three
hundred of the ready, makes its appearance
in my pocket, the Lord knows how.

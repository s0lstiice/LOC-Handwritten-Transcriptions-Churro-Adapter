I believe in state quo. Your beloved Heart. I think is
at Saybrook. Do not let your Heart run away with
your head. Your Paper controversy with Miss Adams
about nothing cannot fail to terminate happily.
The Literary party, as they please to style themselves, has
found their great guns but it was so overwhelming that
it has done and is doing dreadful execution back
wards. I must congratulate you on the success that
has attended you in your professional studies and on
the character you have been enabled to maintain
when surrounded by innumerable temptations.
It is highly interesting to see the countenance of your
friends particularly your parents brighten with joy
when reading those parts of your letters or the letters
of others which relate to your moral character. How
should we all rejoice did we know that you were not only
almost but altogether a Christian, not only a moral,
but a pious man. I have a strong desire to enlarge but
time will not permit. I should esteem a letter from you
as a great favour. Dr. and your other friends at New Haven,
when I expect to return for some time, will also very
glad to hear from you. Nancy in particular sends her
with sentiments of sincere respectful affection. I am Dr. to
your friend & humble servant
Mark Bradford

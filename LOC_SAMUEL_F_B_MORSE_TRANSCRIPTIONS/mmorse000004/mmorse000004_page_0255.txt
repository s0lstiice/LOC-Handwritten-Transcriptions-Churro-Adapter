10 your late letters that you suppose I am painting a
large picture; I did think of it some time ago
and was only deterred on account of the expenses
attending it, all this I will explain to your entire
satisfaction when I see you, and why I do not
think it expedient to make an exhibition
when I return: I percieve also that you are a
little too sanguine with respect to me, and expect
a little too much from me; you must recollect I
am yet but a student, and that a picture of any
merit is not painted in a day; Experienced as
Mr West is, (and he also paints quicker than any
other artist,) yet his last large picture cost him
between 3 and 4 years constant attention; Mr
Allston was nearly 2 years in painting his large
picture; Young Haydon was 3 years painting
his large picture, is now painting another on
which he has been at work 1 year and expects
to be 2 years more on it; Leslie was 10 months
painting his picture, and my Hercules cost me
nearly a year's study; so you see that large
pictures are not the work of a moment; all these
matters we will talk over one of these days, and
all will be set right; I had better paint Miss
Rufelli's Aunt Salisbury's and Dr Bartlett's pictures
at home for a very good reason I will give you.
Love to all. I must close abruptly. With affectionate
Yr Affectionate Son
D. H.B. More.

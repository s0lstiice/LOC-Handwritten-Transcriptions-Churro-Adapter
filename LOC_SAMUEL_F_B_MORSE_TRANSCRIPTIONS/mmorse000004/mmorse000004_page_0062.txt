Cumberland June 10th 1814
My dear Son
The receipt of your very welcome letter of
May 14th together with the very interesting letters
of Mr Wilder before I Mr Alston. They were a
very great treat to us. We had had no letters or
intelligence in any way from you for about
seven months, I began to grow quite anxious.
I can now only make a short reply, as it is uncertain whether this will ever reach your hands but a short time to write you.
I wish you to thank Mr Wilder for his very acceptable letter which was deemed so important just at this time, as that Mr Everitts provided on me to give him a copy of it for the Campplot. I shall answer it soon. Present him my respectful & affectionate regards and the same to Mr Alston. I am much obliged to him for his highly satisfactory letter, I feel both him & Mrs P. for their attention to you. His account of your progress in your art of your general conduct has given us much comfort & pleasure. We hope God will continue to prosper & bless you, I keep your family in my thoughts & pray for their recovery. Mr Wilder arrived without your picture. I had arrived at Liverpool when the vessel sailed.

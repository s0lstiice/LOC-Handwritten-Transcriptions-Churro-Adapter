Mr. F. H. B. Miss:
Dear Sir,
Since receiving your machins
timed favour in March last I have been looking forward with
anxiety in hopes that I should in this have had the pleasure of
seeing you agreeable to your intention which you thus expressed to me. which anxiety was considerably increased by
the respectations & hopes which I had cherished that you
would have been able to have made some arrangement
which I might have received some small assistance of
which I still continue in the greatest want, however
I must presume you feel fully sensible of that, and I even
hope that if you should not visit Bristol you may be enabled
to do something for me without any detriment to yourself. I presume that from a knowledge of my
friends you can have no doubt but whatever advances
you may make me will be refunded promptly by them. Allow
me particularly to request you to favour me with an answer
inform me whether it is probable that you will provide
me with Bristol I give me your opinion as it respects a person
In hopes of hearing from you soon I shall write myself truly
Your obedient
Humble servant
Sam'l B. Tyler

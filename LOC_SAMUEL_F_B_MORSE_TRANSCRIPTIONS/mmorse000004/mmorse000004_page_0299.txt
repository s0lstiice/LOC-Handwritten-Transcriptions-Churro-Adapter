London, June 26th 1815.
My Dear Parents,
I just drop you a line by the Gales
to inform you that I am well; I am making
my arrangements to leave about the end of
August; my time is now very precious to me
and this must therefore be short; you must
be aware that I shall be under the necessity
of drawing in advance for many of next year
expenses such as clothing and printing materials,
all which I shall expect to refund you, as well
as what I may exceed in my proper allowance
for the 4 years, I trust it will not
be much though I fear it will be a few
pounds. There is one thing I wish to notify to
you; my friend Lieut. Newcomb of our Navy
went to Paris a short time since, I gave
him a commission to get me a large lay figure,
which will cost 30 to 100 guineas; the prices
are from 30 to 100 guineas; my reason for
deciding on such a step without previously
notifying you of it, was that they are to be obtained
no wheres but in Paris so cheap & so
good, that a dreadful campaign was about
to open in France & that the Allies threatened
to destroy every thing before them and to burn

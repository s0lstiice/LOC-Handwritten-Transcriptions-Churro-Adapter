28th Winchester Stt. 2429th 1815.
W. Sam. T. B. Morse
Bushingham plan
Dear Sir,
Your Letter of yesterday & the Package
of which it gives the Contents are with me -- and
according to your Request I enclose herein fifteen
Pounds in three Bank notes of £15 each, conceding
to the Justness of your Remonstrance, that a little
more than is necessary is less inconvenient than
not to have enough.
This will be accompanied with the six pair
blacken silk stockings, which I have thought
might be most eligible to have of two qualities.
There are three 3 p. rathen stocking & 3 p. finer than
the Charge will be included in the Amer Car.
which shall be handed to you beginning of
next Week -- two arrivals from Boston today
the Milo & Liverpool Packet producing a
Thurday that prevents it now -- a Letter from you
by one of them -- marked for Mr. T. C. Goddard, is herewith
your very truly H: Bromfield

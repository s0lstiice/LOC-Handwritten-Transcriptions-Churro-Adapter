119th Regt. N.Y. Vols.
Oct. 10. 1861.
to Genl. Grant
U.S. Genl. in Chief
At the request of the commanding officer
of the 32d Indiana Volunteers, I am
submitting the following report of the
actions of the 32d Indiana Volunteers
and the 3d Kentucky Volunteers in the
attack upon the enemy's position at
Fort Brady, near Louisville, Ky.
and the pursuit of the enemy.
Very respectfully yours,
Geo. W. B. H. H. Col. 32d Indiana Volunteers
Brig. Genl. U. S. Army

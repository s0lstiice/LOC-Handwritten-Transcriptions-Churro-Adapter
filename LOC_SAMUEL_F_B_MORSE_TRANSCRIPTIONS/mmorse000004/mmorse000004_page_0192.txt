This is the beginning of Monday Dec. 19th I have many things to say to you my son
than can be compassed in this letter but the Question of every kind that has to do with
to answer as particularly & fully & speedily as you can after receiving this Letter &c.
Why did you not mention Mr James Russell of his family in any of the three letters
ten to us from Bristol two we received yesterday dated Oct 11th Mr Russell thinks it singular that
he did not just say they were well with him if that was the case if you say nothing about
his likeness which chiefly concerns him not how to account for as you said in
a former letter you had taken it as a present for her. It would be the most gratifying
to me to hear that you could make her if it is a good like nep. I am sorry you do
feel the importance of fulfilling your engagements with more punctuality &c
must be more attentive to your Friends, on God will grant to love them. – How do
you propose to be in France not more than six months I hope and then return
London from which place or Liverpool I expect you will embark for Boston next
Summer and be sure you leave not a single debt to be paid by your self or any one
when you come back I was not a little astonished to hear you say in one of your
letters from Bristol that you had earned money enough there to pay off your debts
if cannot help asking what debts could you have to discharge with your own
earnings after receiving one thousand Dollars a year from us which we are
sure must have afforded you even by your own account of your expenses ample
means for the payment of all just and honourable Debts and I hope you will

London October 10 1849
Dear Morse, As I quit London in a few days
for America, I take this first opportunity of letting
you know it, that you may get ready your
letters direct to me at London or Portsmouth
as is convenient. My respects to Mr. Webster
Wilson, I would be happy to take letters for their
friends in America. I will only an answer
from the Transport Board to take up the line
of march. Godard has been in London, he leaves
tomorrow morning for Paris. What think you
of our arms in Canada.
S.F.D. Morse Esquire
W.J. Walker

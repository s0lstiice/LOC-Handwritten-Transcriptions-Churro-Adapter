My dear son,
This will be handed you by
Mr John W. Webster, son of Dr. Redford Webster
of Boston, a young gentleman who has just
completed his medical studies, passed an
examination before the Faculty of the University
at Cambridge. He goes to Europe with a view
to attend a course of operative surgery in the
London hospitals. On your return from France
I hope you will meet him in London, provide
this should this be the case he will inform
you particularly how aware he is of the state of
the family, as he very politely, called on us for
the purpose of ascertainings for your own sake
Mr W. will wish to see such places & objects
of curiosity & improvement as you may be
acquainted with. You will oblige him & me
by directing, 1, as far as your engagements will
admit, accompanying him to such places
& objects especially those that relate to your
own art, I show him all that civility &
kindness, which in like circumstances, you would
wish to receive yourself.
Your affectionate father & most
affectionate son
96902
Charleston, Mar. 11. 1875
Wm H. S. Moser

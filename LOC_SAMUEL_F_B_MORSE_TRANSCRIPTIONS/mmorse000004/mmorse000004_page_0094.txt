have it answered. This will enable you to paint your large contempted
picture, and paint some portraits too, to assist in supporting yourself
purchasing prints, if you do not mention whether you have received our
letters in which your Papa attests the sum he originally told you
he should allow you while during the three years you was to be in
England, which was two hundred pounds Sterling or eight hundred & eighty
& 100 Dollars, this sum he said he should raise to one thousand dollars a
year, for the three years, he has lately purchased a couple of Bills, the
amount alone three hundred dollars which when you receive will com
plete the three first years, you will not therefore have begun up
in your fourth year's allowance till you receive the next. Bills, that

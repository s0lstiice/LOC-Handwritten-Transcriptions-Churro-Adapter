Bristol, 3d May 1815
My dear Sir
I did hope before this to have given
some more favorable reply to yours of 27 ulto but
until this hour I could not make out how far
Mr Haythorne carried his notions of prudence
in the indulgence of a favorite passion, indepen-
dent of his late experience in London as to
the present valuation of pictures, he retains so
high an opinion of Mr Allston's landscape that
he declares 500 Guineas should not take it from
his wall was it once hung up there, yet with
this feeling he tells me that, having a large family
he cannot indulge his fancy at a great expense &
his respect for an artist who could point such a
picture he would not think of offering him such a
price, as would, if named by the seller, tempt him to
purchase, he did in conversation say that was it

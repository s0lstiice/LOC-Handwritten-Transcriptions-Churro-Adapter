young man's living for 600 dollars, I know who it is of whom
you speak, it is Dr Parkman, who made it his boast that
he would live for that sum, but you did not inquire how
he lived. I can tell you, he never refused an invitation
to dine, which he used to obtain often by pushing himself
into every body's company, when he did not succeed in
getting invitations, he invited himself to breakfast, dinner
or sup with some of his friends he has often walked
up to breakfast with us a distance of 3 or 4 miles,
if he failed in getting a dinner or meal at any of these
places, he either used to go without or a bit of bread

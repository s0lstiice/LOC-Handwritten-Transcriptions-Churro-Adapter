Edwards will go to Litchfield some time this summer & study. Last
if we live till next winter we shall be quite happy not having any
of you with us unless you should come & keep us Company. The
letters we write to each other are liable to be read by the officers of
the Government, we shall therefore say nothing on public affairs, in
the news papers which you doubtless see every Sunday in American
newspapers to go into particulars. – Then did I page on Aunt M r
Wilberforce? M r Thornton? Dr Lettsom? M r West? M r Alston
& other good Friends! Remember us very respectfully to them
when you see them & particularly to M r West & M r Alston
who we hope are quite well, M r & Mrs Botterill William, I have heard
is going to marry his Cousin Miss Gibbs, probably next month I
believe the family are well. – I hope you have painted the three
handsome pictures that I requested you too, one for Mrs Dr Bentlett,
one for Miss Huskill, & one for your Aunt Salisbury, I know not
when you will be able to send them such is the state of things
at present, but be sure you do not venture them till there is a
prospect of there getting safely to us, your Papa is publishing a pamphlet
which occupies much of his time at present to liberty to the Public
how he has been treated by Miss H. Adams & her Abbotson S. Higginson
& Shaw & others. When he has an opportunity he will send one or
two Bills, that your Papa has sent you lately & one small one

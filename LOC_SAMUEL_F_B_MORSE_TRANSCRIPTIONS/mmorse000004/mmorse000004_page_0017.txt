You will be astonished to hear that Mr Dexter
is the democratic candidate for Governor in opposition
to Caleb Strong. He has expressed himself in a letter to
the Electors as decidedly opposed to the whole course &
conduct of the General Government. He styles their
measures "unconstitutional, unjust, oppressive, demon-
atizing." He says, "They cannot be executed, & if they
could they would be ineffectual; that they make
perjury familiar; that they aim a fatal blow at
our prosperity; that they sacrifice our dearest rights
& interests." Yet the democrats will support him. The
great point in which he differs from the Federalists
is this. He says "it is our duty to submit implicitly to
every violation of the Constitution, and every species
of oppression." Federalism I need not tell you will
never go to advocate such Toryism.
The New Hampshire election has issued in favour of
Federalism. Our Charlestown Meeting for the Election
of Town Officers took place about a fortnight since. The
Federalists carried their ticket by a majority of 50.

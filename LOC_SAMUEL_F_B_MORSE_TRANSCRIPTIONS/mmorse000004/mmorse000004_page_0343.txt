NATIONAL PRINTS.
Joseph Delaplaine & Philip F. Livingston,
PHILADELPHIA,
PROPOSE TO PUBLISH,
(PROVIDED ONE THOUSAND SUBSCRIPTIONS ARE OBTAINED)
Two superb Engravings:
ONE OF THE
Battle at North Point, near Baltimore;
ON THE TWELFTH OF SEPTEMBER, 1814.
THE OTHER OF THE
BOMBARDMENT OF FORT M'HENRY,
ON THE THIRTEENTH OF SEPTEMBER, 1814.
FROM PICTURES FIFTEEN FEET LONG BY TEN FEET HIGH,
PAINTED BY MR. T. SULLY.
TO BE ENGRAVED BY MR. ALEXANDER LAWSON; AND PROBABLY MR. J. NEAGLE,
WHOSE ARRIVAL, FROM LONDON, IS EXPECTED SOON IN THIS COUNTRY.
THE name of SULLY is so well known, to the Publishers deem it unnecessary to say any thing
in its favour.
Mr. Sully will visit Baltimore to obtain the portraits of the principal officers, and also to make
sketches on the spot, of the different subjects, by which he will be enabled to execute his pictures
with accuracy.
CONDITIONS.
I. These Prints will be engraved in the line manner, and will be nineteen by twenty-six inches.
II. The price to subscribers will be Ten Dollars each, and to non-subscribers Twelve Dollars each. Each print to
be paid for as it is delivered.
Subscriptions will be received for either, or both, by George Smith, carver and gilder, opposite Christ Church; Neale, Willis,
and Coates, and Coale and Maxwell, Baltimore—James Evans, No. 54 Maiden Lane, New-York; John Cooke, Albany; Bittle, Fiche,
and Co, Boston; and at the office of the publishers, Philadelphia.
The first half volume of Delaplaine's Repository of the Lives and Portraits of Distinguished American Characters, will
soon make its appearance. It will contain the lives and portraits of WASHINGTON, HAMILTON, AMES, Dr. RUSH, COLUMBUS, and
AMERICAN VESSELETS, and will be embellished with an elegant vignette Title Page, designed and engraved by Fairman, and also a fron-
tispiece engraved by Lawson.

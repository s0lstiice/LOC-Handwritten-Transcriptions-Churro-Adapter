Your mama would like some grain colored silk for a gown if it can be had for
but little don't forget that your mother is no stranger & that a large pattern
fits her better than a small one, -- I hope my son will see all your Bills
discharged and leave Europe free of Debt when you embark for home your
papa has been this week getting Bills for you to the amount of five hundred
dollars which makes out your allowance from us all to one or two hundred
dollars which he hopes to be able to procure in the course of the spring
or beginning of the summer you will when you get this last have had
from your parents since you left them 4 four thousand dollars we do not
regret it, as all the accounts we have had from others as well as yourself
have afforded us much satisfaction with respect to your improvement in your
art & your otherwise good Conduct, continue & increase in well doing my son
and may that Almighty Power who said let there be light and there
was light shine into your heart with the light of divine truth and make
you his own by that news & bring way pointed out in the Gospel of his Son
Jesus Christ, there is nothing which can afford your parents so much joy
as to see their Children walking in the truth. Your Brother Rich and I
are studying Divinity at the Theological Institution at Ansonia and is increasingly
pleased with his studies his health & spirits are much improved I believe
is at Litchfield studying Law & attending the Law lectures & a
course of Theological lectures given by Mr. Buchin once a week to the young
lawyers Edwards is much Blessed with them I think they must do them good
I should feel terribly when I think of your situation my dear son as that
out from these precious means of grace was it not that I know you are

and whom I hope you forever served love, we are very anxious to hear from

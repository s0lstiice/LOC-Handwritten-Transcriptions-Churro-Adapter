Mr Sands F.B.Morris
Bristol 22 June 1815
London Dear Sir
Your favor of the 15th May
reached me on the 17th.
Aquitable to your request I had a
few days afterwards your pictures packed
up carefully, and forwarded by Canal; I
hope you have received them safely before
this. Board charge for packing is 5/6 which
with one shilling you owed him for attaining
a sketching passe the 8th October, and 2s for
taking the case to the Canal office makes
8/6 more to your debit. I have further
paid 8/9 for your third of News papers in 1813
and 14/9 for Canal carriage of a calf 8 August
last has just been brought in.
I cannot lay my hand on the copy
of the account I send'd you, and not vent
uring the exact price I was to allow you for
Hayes I cannot exactly ascertain the balance

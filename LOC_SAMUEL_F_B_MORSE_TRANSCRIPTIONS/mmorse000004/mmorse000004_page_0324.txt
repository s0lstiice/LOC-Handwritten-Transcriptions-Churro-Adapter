Bloomingdale November 13th 1812.
My dear Sir,
Having just heard of a private conveyance for this letter,
I embrace it merely to tell you that your favour of October 19th was
read and answered by me the 2d of November, I put into the of
office the 3d. I presume therefore you see it the beginning of the
last week. At the time of writing it I indulged a hope which
I have now in great measure abandoned of seeing you in
Boston this season. In that case I should have taken
charge of the books myself. As it is, I am at a loss what
to say. The Navigation from Boston is not I suppose
very safe at this inclement period of the year. On the
whole therefore, I must beg you to direct them to the care
of Henry Fisher Esq. and give them to Capt Hull. He
may probably know of some safe conveyance. There
may possibly be some officer coming to New York by
water to whose care they may be entrusted.
In hopes of soon hearing from you, and begging you
to excuse this hasty Scrawl I remain as ever
Yours truly
Saw F. Jarvis
Sam. F. B. Morse Esq.

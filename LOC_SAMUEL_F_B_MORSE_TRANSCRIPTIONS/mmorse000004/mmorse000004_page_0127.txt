London Sept 1874
Dear Moses Callway's bill (which he says
you have got) amounted to 10 pounds, 15 shps
so that there was no great balance
for Mrs Bridgen out of the money sent.
I suppose you forgot the two small frames
that he regretted. Middletons &
Gordon's bills were sent to me unfixed, but
as you will perceive I opened them intending
to send you the amounts only to save
postage, I did not think at the time of
adding them in the package which will be
the best way. I find by the
memorandum book that our account was
balanced by yourself in lead pencil, by
which it appears that you owed me
£3..2..4r after the guinea that I lost to
you had been deducted. This with 15 Shills
I paid to Callway to make up his account
& the sum of 1 Shill. I suppose have paid by
me Char. R. Leslie in lawful English money
for a wooden roller, you will find according
to Cocker amounts to £3..18..17c which
I quite believe is all I can claim from
you, I heartily sorry am I that it is no
more. I will do all I can to
sell you large frame, but it is not in
my power to buy it myself as in the first

But all this is not to the point; you must perceive, that
the object of your sending me to London, was improvement
in my art, to the extent of 200 pounds per Ann. now I should
conceive I was abusing your goodness if I did not employ
it all for that object; suppose, instead of purchasing frames,
carts, &c. paying models, &c. I should at the close of
year write you word that I had the happiness, to say that I
had expended but 100 pounds, that I had found very cheap
lodgings, but my room would not answer for painting because
it was small, had a low ceiling, and I had my bed in it, that
I lived on little and to save expence of cooking, I was my
own cook, but that most of my day was employed for that
purpose, in purchasing my food, dressing it, and clearing away
my cooking utensils; that I made out to paint with few
colors and cheap ones, and cheap tools, but that the
colors and tools were of inferior quality; suppose I should
say this in a letter, would you be satisfied with my years
work, would you not say, why did you not get a more
convenient room, when you were allowed ample means
for the purpose; and also better colors & tools; and why
did you expend your time in cooking, when you had
sufficient to pay others to do it for you? The fact is I
could live for 50 pounds a year if my only object was to live
cheap, and on the other hand if I was allowed 1000 pounds
a year I could spend it all without the least extravagance

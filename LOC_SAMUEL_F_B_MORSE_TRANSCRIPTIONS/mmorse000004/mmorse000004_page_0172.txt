By the bye I have not had
any letters from home for more
than a month. It seems the
Americans are all united & we
shall now have war in earnest
I am glad of it for many reasons
I think it will not only get
us a more speedy & permanent
peace but may tend to crush the
demons of party spirit &
strengthen our government.
I am done painting the
galleries I have finished my
drawings for the prize; thank
you for your good wishes.
I thought Mr Allston knew
how proud I am of being considered
his student. Tell him
if he thinks it worst while to
mention me at all in his letter
to Delaplaine I shall consider
it a great honor to be called

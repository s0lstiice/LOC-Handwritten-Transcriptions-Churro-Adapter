[cursive text, possibly 'note' or 'note note'] London April 6th 1814
My Dear Parents,
I write in much haste, but it is to
inform you of a most glorious event, no less
than the capture of Paris by the Allies, they
entered it last Thursday, & you may conceive the
sensations of the people of England on the
occasion; as the cartel is the first veil which
will arrive in America to tacitly give news;
I hope I shall have the great satisfaction of
learning that I am the first who shall in-
form you of this great event; the particulars
you will probably see nearly as soon as this.
I congratulate you and the rest of the good
people of the world on the occasion, Des-
jertism & usurpation are fallen, never I
hope to rise again, but what gives me the
greatest pleasure in the contemplation of
is the spirit of religion, & consequence of humanity which has constantly marked
the conduct of the Allies; their moderation
through all their unparalleled success, cannot
be too much extolled; they merit the grate-
ful esteem of the world.

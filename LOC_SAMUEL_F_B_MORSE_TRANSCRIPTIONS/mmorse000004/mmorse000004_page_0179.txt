London Decr 13th 1814
Dear Mouse
I rejoice in the prospect
of seeing you so soon I am heartily
glad of the arrangement you have
made with Mrs Bridgen. — I am
only sorry that there was a possibility
of your mistaking my meaning
or motive in writing the letter you
allude to. — We had so often
talked on the subject that I thought
it was unnecessary to explain myself
more clearly. — You know we had
long ago agreed that it would be to
our mutual interest in regard to
our studies, to have separate rooms.
I am now convinced by experience
that it is of great importance to
me to have a room entirely at my
command & you doubtless find it
the case also. — Our having things in
common mechanics is no objection at

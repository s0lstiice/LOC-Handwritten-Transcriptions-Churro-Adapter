Remittances he had made would be sufficient to pay
all your Bills & your Passage home, & also to purchase
some Books & Articles of Clothing, but during, if there
should be any Deficiency, thus I will advance it.
These are all the Particulars he has given me & I trust
your Wish that what is advanced for articles to be procured
for him be kept separate from your Own & you
will at the time of receiving it let me know to which
& how to place it.
The Revolution you have formed of returning to America
is in my opinion tedious, for the Multitude of Competitors here in the Line of your Profession is so great as to render the Chance of Success in it much greater than
altho the Field of Improvement may not be so extended.
There also you will be in the Midst of your Friends, an
Intercourse with whom will enhance the Enjoyment of
Life, while it will tend to mitigate the adverse Occasions
that must be calculated upon. The difference between
the two Countries in the Expenditure of living is not an immaterial object.
The Letter for your Father shall be forwarded when
any opportunity offers. The Galen I hope will get away
in about three weeks whether any will be going to
Boston sooner from Liverpool I am not informed.
I enclose the Twenty Pounds you have requested
Sincerely wish you success in the object of your present
Engagement remain as truly
Yours Your Friend & etc etc
C Bromfield

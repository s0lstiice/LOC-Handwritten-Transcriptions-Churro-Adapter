and it would give me great pleasure to see you one of the
professors of it. -- Why will you not let us have the pleasure
of seeing you, when we can talk over these matters at our leisure?
I am reserving the printing of my thing for your present, and as they
tell me I look best in the winter, because tallest, you see it is of great
importance that I see you at this season. -- Have you attended as often
to architecture? That is with me a favourite science, tho'
I know but little about it. I hope some time or other to be
a Gothic church erated here, and I must consider your taste
concerning the plans. That has been a Gothic Church erected at New
Haven since you were there; and it is my intention to put up a
monument in it to the memory of my father, which I wish to have
correspondent with the style of the building. As you have probably
noticed the principal monuments in England, it may be on your power
to furnish me with a design. I wish it to be attached to the wall
in the Chancel, near the Altar, and I think of some such inscription
as the following: "Hic sub altare jacent Mortales Ecavius, Abrahami, Jacobus

the Blessing of the Almighty. Without this you cannot, and ought not,
to expect to be prospered, I was glad to find you observing in your letter that your object in wishing to excel as a painter was to promote the
Glory of God if this is sincerely your aim, it is known to him who reaches all hearts,
and if he finds that your success in your Art will have a tendency to promote his
Glory, he will grant you all that prosperity, which is consistent with this
end, and with the same kind designs, will deny you that success which
you may be desirous of enjoying, as knowing far better than we poor short sighted
women can possibly pretend to, what will have this effect and what not
It is therefore our Indispensable duty to beg daily, that God will may be done
both in us and by us and that all we say and do may end in his Glory &
the best good of our own souls & the souls of our fellow mortals. – Indeed my
dear son your affectionate parents are vastly more interested in having
you profess of that honour that cometh from God, than all the facing
praising honours, that are derived from fallible man, these last,
are not to be desired as but they may have a tendency to place
us in situations to be more abundantly useful to our fellow men
but they are by no means to be sought for merely to gratify our ambition
of distinction, and the pride & vanity of our own foolish hearts, we
ought daily to be searching into our motives and making the public
of thealmest. Hearch me Oh God and know my heart try me
and know my thoughts and see if there is any wicked way in me and
lead me in the way everlasting, – Hope my son that you remember

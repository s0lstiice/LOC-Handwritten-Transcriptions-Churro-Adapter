London Sept 1814
Dear Doctor I am much obliged to you for
your long letter of the 3rd or 4th instt. as I having
but just recovered from the fatigue of reading
it I take my pen in hand to return
the compliments. -- Seriously the
occasion of my writing is poor Sallaway
who has called several times appears to be
much in want of money having been
disappointed in receiving several sums
that he expected. -- I am sure he would not be so
prepossessed of being thought impolite.
I am going on as usual.
I have finished another patient of
CK Leslie & it is by far the best likeness
I have done of that great character. --
I am now painting a Magdalen, I have
nearly done it. -- Mrs Green acts the
character for me. -- A Mr Yartt
called on me the other day when I was
out & left a letter of introduction from
Wayward to the firm. -- being the
resident patron here will call on that
gentleman as early as possible. -- The
letter was a mere note & contained nothing

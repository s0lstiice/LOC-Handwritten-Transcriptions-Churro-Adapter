Bristol 3 May 1814
My dear Mase
I am pleased to find by your esteemed lines from medical
good friend A.V. Cig. that you arrive in Bristol shortly - also the worthy
Mr Allston Lady - I shall rejoice to see you again introduced to this
charming circle of the social virtues - we all want to see you indeed we do,
in order that you shall fully come up to me in points of gracefulness
in external appearance (I make no claim to internal) I have in
reserve a very fine but which equals my own in strength & price.
Why you have not heard from me lately in my late tours to Manchester
Liverpool Portsmouth was because I could find no residence
otherwise be assured of my pleasure in that respect
The prints long since desired are the day forwarded to your
old residence where I hope they will safely find you
I have taken Lodgings near this peaceful neighborhood Portman
square I only await your joining me to complete my felicity
With regard to my friend Buckan I can say but little at present
to my friends but I can assure you my friends are not too
diverse - In fact no one more sincerely rejoice at the change than
the one who will always subscribe
my best regards joined to them
This charming family attend
late Mr Willis Oldston
My best regards to the rest of the family go in
the death of Mrs Visper's sister of New York Sunday the family go in
to dinner
Charles Green

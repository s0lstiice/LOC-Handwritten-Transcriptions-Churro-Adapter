P.S. The Paper of to-night confirms the news
of this morning; Bonaparte is no longer a
man, he has abdicated; and in
prorbility a Republican form of government will be the future government of France, if they are capable of enjoying such
a government. but no one can foresee events
there may be a long peace, or the world may
be torn worse than it yet has been. --
Revolution seems to succeed revolution so rapidly
that in looking back on our lives we seem to have
lived a thousand years; and wonders of late seem
to scorn to come alone, they come in clusters. a
P.S. 2. I have just received your letter of 20th May which
gave me much pleasure, I still intend returning by the
end of Augt though I cannot be sufficiently grateful
to you for your kind offers of encouragement & support for
another year. I shall tell you my plans when I get home.
Excuse me for not enclosing as I am in a great hurry.
Yr most affectionately
Sam Moser

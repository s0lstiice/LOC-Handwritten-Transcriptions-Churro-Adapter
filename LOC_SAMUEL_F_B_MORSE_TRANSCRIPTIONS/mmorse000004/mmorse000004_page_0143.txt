Dartmouth Oct. 13th 1814
May dear Sir
The canal will not sail
in less than 8 or 10 days. The Cape: City
informs me that New York is perfectly
inaccessible. The fortifications on both
sides the narrows are completed.
In addition to the forts on Ellis Meadow
Islands there have been otherwise built
on the West side of the Bay, new and
at Poulis Hook on the Jersey Shore.
The regular fortifications and batteries
Williams on Governor Island are renewed
even more perfect and powerful
than they were before. Besides the
three regular forts on York Island other
defences have been constructed. The
White South West Point of the Island
is covered with Guns & breastworks and
almost every pier head prepared for the
reception of Guns which we have already

our hearts refuse indeed with respect to all our dear Sons. I hope only Son
that you remember the Sabbath day to hapit holy, let it not be away
of Entertainment or business or vain vents, if you should fall into the sin of
Sabbath breaking you will have it to repent of as long as you live, but I will
hope better things of my first born Son; what has led me to mention this Sin
so particularly is, that Mr. Parkman mentioned dining at Mrs. Williams
with you, I think he said on the Sabbath. I hope this is not your practice but
that you will keep constantly in mind, that the Eye of that God who
sits in secret is constantly upon you. and that you have not forgotten
that you once learnt, that the Sabbath is to be sanctified by an
Holy resting from worldly employment, & recreations, that are
lawful on other days, and in spending the whole time in publick &
Private exercises of God's worship, except so much as is to be taken up
in the Works of Inceipity & Mercy. Your good Father meets with
a great many trials both from Enemies & false Friends; but I hope he will
be carried safely through them all. Since I began this letter a few days
ago he has been applied to to pay a debt of several thousand Dollars
which I know not how he will be able to discharge, and which he cannot
not to pay, one cent of this is in consequence of his endeavours to establish
you in the Book Store of Farnand & Mallory; but as which failed
they you know have failed like wise. Whitney & Watson have
been so embarrassed for this Debt, we know not what will be the
consequence, but shall endeavour to discharge our Duty. It is abbotely
neecery you see any Son, that you make all your Arrangements
to maintain yourself, as soon as possible and let us know when
we are released from any necessity of sending you further supplies,
your brothers are on a journey to see if they can collect some small debts

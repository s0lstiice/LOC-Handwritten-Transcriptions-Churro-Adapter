Mr Etheridge wishes you to bring him from Mr Taylor for
the last Vol. of Balmoths Dist. 7 with he has printed Bring
me from him also, the last No. of his 4th & 5th 12mo editions
of Wells' Scripture Geog. 7 for which I have paid but which
have not been sent with leaves both incomplete
Don't forget these -- You had better provide you a
memor o book & look over all my letters, I minute
my requests, them you will not be likely to forget
them -- Mr Evans (who is very well) wishes you to get him
in France or Eng. a French Bible, not exceeding a Vol.
Miss Rachel & Mrs Dorse are well -- Mrs Pratt's family
are going to Liverpool early in the Spring to remain in Eng.
We shall write by them --
Your Mama & I know join me in affectionate regards --
We all want much to see you -- Your friends generally
are anxious for your return --
Your affectionate father
Geo W Morse

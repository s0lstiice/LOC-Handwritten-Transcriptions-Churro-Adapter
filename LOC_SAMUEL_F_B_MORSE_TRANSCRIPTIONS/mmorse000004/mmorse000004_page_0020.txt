Bristol 1 April 1834
Mr. Samuel B. Morry
London
Dear Sir
I received in due course
your several esteemed favors of the 12, 21 & 28 June
the former of which Mr. Green undertook to reply to
who is at present at Manchester.
It gave me pleasure to learn that Mr. Leslie
sold his picture of Saulze at so good a price I hope
it will simulate opinion of his honest efforts
exertions & time, to endeavour even to enable the
witch of Tudor. I think I perceive a few symptoms of
amendment in him, and the request of this letter
that he must support himself in the opinion
of his friends here the best thing that could have
happened him, he will now have the pleasure
to taste the sweet of his own labour and I hope will
in reality know what true independence is, but
him not disposed to he will certainly succeed
much my having taken up so much of your time

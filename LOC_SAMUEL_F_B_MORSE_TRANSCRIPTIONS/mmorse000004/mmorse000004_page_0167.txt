Dear Son, With this I send Mr Bromfield a
Draft on Mr. William for $3817. This is the first part
of the Store, I do not allow you for this year. It will
end more as I have applied funds to purchase. It
costs me a great effort (which I however cheerfully make)
to support you & your brothers, all now abroad, I studying
your professions -- Edw. at Litchfield & Risk. at Denver -- both well in good spirits, pursuing their studies
with as much ardor, as you pursue yours -- Your parents
are comforted in the good conduct & proficiency of
their children -- fully rewarded for all our exertions
& sacrifices for your support -- We hope you
will all continue to deserve our affection. Do
nothing to give us pain -- We wish to hear
from you -- your last letter is dated June 24th. We
have very often written you the last year, I
know not how many letters -- The penninny
as well as political state of our country is
peculiarly perplexing -- there are a great
many failures in Boston almost every day
& must be many more it is expected Among
them is your acquaintance, Young Beers.

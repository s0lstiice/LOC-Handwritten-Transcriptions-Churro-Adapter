Mr Samuel F. B. Morse
Dear Sir
I have procured for our friend
Harmansinger the appointment of counsel for
the port of Bristol. In speaking of him to a
friend I mentioned Allison and yourself as being
intimately acquainted with him; when I
heard of your arrival home. As this business
preserves me now I shall postpone other
matters till I hear from you. The appointment
is actually made but before the President will resign the Commission he requires strong testimonials
to be laid before him of Mr Viggers character and capacity, both of which you
know to be eminently great and good. I

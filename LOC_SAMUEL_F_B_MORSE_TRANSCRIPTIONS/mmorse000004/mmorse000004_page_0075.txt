P.S. There is a number of British ships, 74's & frigates
in the Harbor which has for a few days past put us in a state of
considerable alarm for the 74 which is on the stocks in that town to
be launched (if not before burnt by the British) on Labor day next
The Philadelphia Company (the St. Eng. guard) were out on
guard last night at the Navy Yard in Chelsea, by order of the Govr.
Some people are removing from town brought from here are not
however alarmed -- we hope soon to have peace.
June 13, 1814

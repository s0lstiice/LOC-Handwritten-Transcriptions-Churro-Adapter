a poor return to us for our indulgence, & I hope you will

would make the resolution to leave politicians to settle the
affairs of nations, and attend Wholly to your own appro
priate Duties as a good man & a good citizen of any coun
try where providence may cast your lot. It will becomes a
young man, who is to get his living by his own exertions,
to afford the people who are to furnish him with his bread,
by appearing constantly under the power of prejudice &
disgust with respect to them; and I do therefore treat you to
devis, and let there be no more manifestation of this
kind with respect to you. You have made us very happy in
the success with which your efforts, in the line of your profession has
been crowned; With the eelat that has attended you in conse
quence, both in Europe & America, with the descriptions you have
given us of the scenes you have past through, & above all with
the good moral character that you sustain. All this rejoices
our hearts; we wish you would attend to our request, about send
ing us some of your paintings, which would much delight
us, and furnish us with some specimens to show your friends,
who are often asking & wondering that we receive nothing
from you of this kind, as others receive things from their friends
in Europe. We shall begin to feel neglected as it is going on four
years since you have been away, and we have not received any
thing but your likeness in India Ink, in all that time. Your poor
father has not received any one of all the Books or pamphlets he
has requested. I trust any dear son you will think on these things

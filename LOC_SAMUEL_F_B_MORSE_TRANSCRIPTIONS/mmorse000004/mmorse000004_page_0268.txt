Mr Sam. C. P. B. Morse
Mr. Winchester Mr. 22 May 1815
Dear Sir
Your Letter of 15th Mr. having come
to hand at a time of many engagements, having required
some attention in order to answer the Enquiry made in it,
a delay has occurred beyond what was intended.
Below is a statement of the D. P. side of your Actio from your
2 years, whereby it appears that in the year 1812 (the year in
which the Brooks were paid for) the Amo did not much exceed
that of one thous. dollars, but the following year did
considerably exceed, so that in except of the two years more
than balanced the Bill received in pay for the Brooks,
consequently, the Amo did must be considered as already
absorbed in your expenditure –
The Expenditure of the present year already amonts to £131.9.1
to which is added the sum now called for 20.—
the sum you mention as being for 60.—
and you will perceive that their united Amo 211.9.1
added to that of the preceding years 704.0.9
will exceed the four thousand dollars 915.9.10
attested by your Father – This will undoubtedly
prove a satisfaction to you, but still what you may
want for your Experiences shall be furnished, it also

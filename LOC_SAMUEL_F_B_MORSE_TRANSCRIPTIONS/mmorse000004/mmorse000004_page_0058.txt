New York May 23. 1814.
My dear Sir
On my return from Connecticut where I left most saving
and our Son, I had the pleasure of finding your letter dated
Sep. 3d. 1813 and sent by the fair American Castle. I had written
you a letter by Mr. Armstrong, who was to have sailed in the postscript
Brig. Illinois protecting the trade of this month. On my arrival
day before yesterday I was surprised to find she had not yet
sailed. I have therefore one hour in which to acknowledge
the receipt of your favour and to thank you for having procured
for me the books you mention. I cannot but hope that
we shall soon have peace so that you will be enabled to
send them. I am told the Custom House Officers in Boston are
much more polite than in New York, since here they insist
upon duties being paid on Articles brought out for private
use. You will be pleased to give your father an order
on me for the Amount. Walk on Infant Baptism I have
obtained if you should meet with Outland I will thank

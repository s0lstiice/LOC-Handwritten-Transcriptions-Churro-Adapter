Your cousin Sam'l Bruce, mid-shipman, in the Congress frigate
is noted in this harbor (as are nearly our whole little navy) to be
been in the last two days -- he is a fine young fellow and is fond
of the navy -- to-morrow we set out for N.H. Cont'd shall all tell you
from of which shore as we pass on -- he is well equipped -- we wish you
were here to go with us -- send us some of your paintings, when you
have a good ship -- The late Commodunt Lin was about middle
via Dorset's two fires, both performed well -- Your Grandson remains his
most affectionate father &c
August 21st 1814
to the care of Henry Samfield Esq</

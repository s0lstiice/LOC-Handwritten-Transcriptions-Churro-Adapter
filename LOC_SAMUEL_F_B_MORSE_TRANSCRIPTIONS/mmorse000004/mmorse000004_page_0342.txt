Philadelphia, January 9, 1816
I had the pleasure of addressing you twice the
last time through Mr. Adams, in that being furnished with
a reply I begged to know whether you would print a number of
petitions for me at seven and a half dollars each, of reasons of just
and of the object of which I have lately informed.
I have not received a letter from Mr. Adams who
mentions that he has been recently sent to Paris to petition to
you. Perhaps I may state from this that you have agreed to
give me an official position of the old gentlemen of
the Senate wanting.
It has been received a reply, which states the
members of the Senate do not want.
In every instance where the Honesty of living
I shall expect an original petition. A copy will next
answer.
I am with great respect
Dr. J. F. G. Your obedient
Joseph Belcher
to H. Moreby

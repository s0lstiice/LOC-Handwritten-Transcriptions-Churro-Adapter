from Washington dated the 19 December at New York
they arrived on after a prosperous voyage of 52 days
as part of the letter is intended for you I have with
you I have more than ever I know your feeling
and wish I could indulge them. The victory of Champion
in last in the annals of our country stands secured in the
“The old game.” Nothing has come to me that will give
an achievement to call for a fulfillment of my promise to you.
I have a letter from your father to Mr. Mason he demanded
your character professedly the 2d could not answer
and referred to me. I answered that your morals
were good, that your politics did not interfere with
your business—that you was a good artist and will
stand at the head of your profession, that your
drawing was good, your painting excellent, but
that you was not so patient, industrious as almost
all the Masters of the art ever had been, but that a man
with a clear view of what was due to your own interest and
character would soon rectify this neglected and
mistaken indulgence

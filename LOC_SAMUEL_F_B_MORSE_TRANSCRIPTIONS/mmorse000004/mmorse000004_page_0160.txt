Richard goes to Andover to-morrow to commence his theological studies
Edward Hooker & his sister Mary are here on their way to Andover
When they expect to live their Father whom you knew was settled at
Norwich he died about 20 months ago his Widow has last week
married to Eg. Favore who thid to live with Madame Phillipps
of Andover and the family of course have removed there we think
it quite a pleasant circumstance to have them so eligible settled so near
us as we have a great regard for them. We wish to know what the
plan was that you said you was maturing for our Arbitration in
regard to the Emperor of Russia. You must not be a schemer but determine on a
steady uniform course it is an old adage that a nothing
stone never gathers any Moss so a person that is driving about for
Millar to put very seldom lays of anything against a vaining day
you must be wise my son and endeavour to get into such business as
it with the Divine Blessing give you a support secure that first and
then you will be authorized to indulge your taste & exercise of your
genius in other ways that may not be immediately connected with
a living. You mention patronage from this Country but such patronage
is not known here unless you were on the spot and not then indeed
but for Value received. You must therefore make up your mind to
labour for yourself without leaning on anyone & look up to God for
his Blessing upon your endeavour this is the way your Parents
set out in life about 25 years ago they had nothing to look to for
a support but their salary which was a House 20 cords of wood,
570 dollars a year the reception & execution of the Geographies
was an experiment not then made with the Blessing of heaven on
these resources we have maintained an expensive family kept
open doors for almost all who chose to come & partake of our Host

Litchfield Ct. 20th 1811.
Dear Breant,
I arrived in Litchfield yesterday at 12 o'clock,
in perfect health, met with no accident on my passage from
St.Haven. Immediately after my arrival I engaged my
board at Mr Lords, the lady recommended by Mrs Hooker.
I have spoken to Mr Beecher for a room. He can accom-
modate me at the end of a week; mean while I
have put up at Mr Lords in the same room
with a Mr Hotchkiss a young gentleman from St.Haven
who accompanied me in the stage and who offered me
a part of his room till Mr B could accommodate one.
My board will be $2,,50 per week. Room rent I
have not yet ascertained. Hotchkiss gives $1 per week.
I presume mine may be estimated at $40 per annum.
For the privileges of the office we give $100 per annum.
This morning I attended one of Mr Goulds lectures
and was very much pleased. My situation here is much more
agreeable than I had anticipated.
Hedges of my club is here and Cook is expected to return every
day. brety of Richards club Churles, Hotchkiss, Perkins, Devereaux
of the club which graduated last years.
When I left St.Haven I thought it doubtful whether he should be able to go to Boston this
season. If he should go I would thank Richard to call at

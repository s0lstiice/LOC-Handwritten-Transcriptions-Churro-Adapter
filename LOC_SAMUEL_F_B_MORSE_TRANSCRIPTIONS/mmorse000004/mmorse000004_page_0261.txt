We know not how we shall ultimately be affected by the
late wonderful revolution in France -- Your views on
that event entirely coincide with my own -- I have
expected that Bonaparte will be established on the throne of
France, I remain the source of the world for their
iniquities -- But we can see but a little way into
futurity -- We can safely leave all in the hands
of Him who manages all things wisely -- All we have
to do is to secure his friendship & protection by
a diligent discharge of all our duties.

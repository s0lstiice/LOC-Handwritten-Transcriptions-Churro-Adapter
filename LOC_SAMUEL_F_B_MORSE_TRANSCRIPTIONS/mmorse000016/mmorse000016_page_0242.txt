Nashv. Dec. 27th 1843.
To Hon. J. C. Spencer.
I have the
honor to report that I have dismissed
Prof. James C. Fisher, one of my assist-
ants, whose salary was $1500. per an.
"My present labor require
the services of an efficient mechanical
assistant, whom I believe I have
found in Mr. Ezra Cornell, and whom
I present for the approval of the
Hon. Secretary, with a compensation
at the rate of $1000. per annum from
Dec. 27th 1843.

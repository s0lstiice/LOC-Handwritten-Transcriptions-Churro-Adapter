Exper. To ascertain whether the cross firing before experienced arise from Conduction or Induction. Open the pipe where the barret portion is, cut the wires apart and bind by threads each charred portion to its fellow, thus
If the magnet be worked by the current under these circumstances the effect is certainly owing to Conduction, and not to Induction, and if the same effect be produced as we have seen in the Yatham pipe, it doubtless arises from the same cause. The remedy consists in removing the charred portions or covering them and repeating the same experiments on the same wire and noting the results. If the covering be well varnished, and the cross firing be still observable it must arise from Induction for which there is no remedy but to remove the wire from the pipe and suspend them in the air on supports of wood.

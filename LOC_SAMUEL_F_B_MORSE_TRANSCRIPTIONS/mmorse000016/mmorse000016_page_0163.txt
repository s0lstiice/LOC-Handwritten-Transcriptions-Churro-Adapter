New York Nov 29th 1843.
Ref. S.F.B. Moser Superintendent of Telegraphs &c
Sir
The Means Pathmans have completed 1
delivered on board the schooner for Baltimore, including that
for which they have received the first instalment,
38,490. yards of pipe, amounting to about 22 miles.
They hope to complete the whole amount this week, if no
further accidents or breakages occur. They wish you to
remit to them a draft for the second 10 miles so that it
may reach here by Monday at present. The amount is
$673.20, or including the 25 per cent retained $897.50.
Dr Hale is slowly recovering but if extremely weak & thin
& our speed will have a very tedious consequence,
I hope we shall soon be through here so that we can come
on. I be at work with you there. I suppose Mr Smith
will love it all laid before we reach you. Please remember
me to him & to Mrs his I remain very respectfully
your Col Servt
James Fisher

Portland Sept 22d 1843
Dear Sir
Yours of the 18th is at hand I am glad all
goes well in other matters than Terrell contract.
I now write to say, that Bartlett power of attorney
need not be communicated at all to the Secry for it is to
authorise me to use Bartlett name, just as he would
use it, & to make known to you, that I have authority
to do all receipts will be in Bartlett name alone,
just as if he wrote them, and the letter he sent you, is
to justify you in taking receipt thus written in his
absence. My name in your account need not be
mentioned at all, neither as Bartlett agent, nor
as the recipient of the money, but all payments
are to be charged as made to him and upon his
authorised vouchers. It is to you immaterial who
makes the receipt, if you have the requisite evidence
that they are authorised.
I reciprocate the kindest remembrances of your
Cabinet remain truly yours
Francis D. Smith
Prof S J B Morse
New York City.

Copy of Ferrells letter to P.O.J. Smith giving the authority on which
he has acted.
149 Hammond St New York 28th Oct. 1863
Dear Sir
Finding myself unable to supply P.O. Merse with the
lead pipe embraced by my contract with him seasonably to fulfill
that contract you will oblige me if you will aid me in obtaining
the deficiency to his acceptance within the contract price at
the rate of seven and 1/8th cents per yard and give receipt to
him for payment thereof accordingly and in discharge of my
contract and this shall be your authority therefor.
Respectfully
Y ou ob sed
James E Ferrell

Duties
The Director of Station, is
The Responsible Officer, and Manager of the Station
All its correspondence, to regulate all the affairs of the
Station, amenable to the Director General; to be
either himself, or by his Assistant in constant atten-
dance, to receive and transmit intelligence.
An Assistant Director, To assist the Director, relieving him in his
duties at such times as shall be stipulated.
The Curator of the Laboratory, To take charge of the Laboratory to
keep the Batteries at all times ready for operation
make repairs, and alterations to instruments &c.
&c. to have charge of, and to be accountable for all
public property appertaining to his Department.
Requisitions for supplies furnished the Labora-
tory, to be approved by Director of Station.

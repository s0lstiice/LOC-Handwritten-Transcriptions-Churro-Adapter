Extracts
from Fisher's letters.—
Extract from Prof. Fisher's letter N.York Dec. 9th 1843
Prof. Morse, Superintendent of the Mag. S.S.
I have the honor to report to you that Messrs. Fathams &
brothers have made and delivered on board ship (39) thirty nine Casks
of lead pipe with the wire enclosed in them which after faithfully
proving, according to your instructions, I have accepted and
approved. + + + they have also made about 12, boats
including the wire, of which about half has been proved and
decepted but is not yet packed and delivered, but will be
in the course of the week. the proving and accepting, packing
and delivery will be completed in the first part of the week —
+ + + I shall hurry the business here as rapidly as
is consistent with perfection, etc.
In letter of Dec. 22d 1843 he says
The delay here in the completion of the contract could
not be attributed to Messrs. Fathams as they were always
in advance of us with the pipe except for two days, but
our force here was not sufficient to prove the pipe
according to your instructions any faster. We intended
to prove it faithfully and could not hurry it any
faster with safety to the success of the enterprise which
I thought of paramount importance.—
In letter of Dec 15th 1843 he says
I have the honor to report to you that the Messrs. Fathams
have completed their contract and have made 57,899 lb lead pipe which I have approved of."

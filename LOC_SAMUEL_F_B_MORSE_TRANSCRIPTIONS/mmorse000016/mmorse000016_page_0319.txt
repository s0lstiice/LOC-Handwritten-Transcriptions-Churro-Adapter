the result will prove to you that you have unjustly blamed me.
It will also save you from any additional expense on the side of digging refuse
will only be so much money thrown away. I am sorry that my present
necessities compel me to request you to remit to me the balance due me
from Government, as soon as possible & also the amount promised me by
you when I left Washington last Spring. You will of course see from
this letter that on mature reflection I do not take back my thing I
stated in my former letter. If you seemed to think I would do. I have yet
to learn why, when I am treated with manifest injustice I may not
complain of it & boldly & fairly state the truth. I have given to De
Gole the facts of which I wrote you in my last in reference to whom
I am obliged to you for your kind recommendation to him to give me
a situation in his Colony, though I do not see how you could in
conscience recommend him to employ a person who had been so
grossly & criminally neglectful, or ignorant, that you had yourself
been obliged to discharge him. I shall hope to hear from you again
& till then I remain your Alt Servt
James C Fisher M D P

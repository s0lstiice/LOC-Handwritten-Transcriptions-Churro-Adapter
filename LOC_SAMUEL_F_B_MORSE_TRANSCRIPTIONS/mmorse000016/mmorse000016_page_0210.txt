Washington Dec. 18th. 1843.
Sir, I have the honor to report that under the
contract for laying down the lead pipes for the Tel-
graph, Contract the Contract contemplates
the same to be laid down between 1st of August
and the 1st of Dec. 1843. Circumstances beyond my
control, the material facts of which, I have hereto-
fore reported to the Department, prevented the delivery
of the pipe so soon as the 1st of August.
The contractor has now laid down about 10
miles and tendered his services to advance with the
work. But I do not think it is safe or expedient
to proceed further with the work until spring. It is
impossible to superintend the work in the winter with
that care which is requisite in a new enterprise, it
being necessary to test that already completed before
proceeding further than a mile more. If the operations
of trenching are suspended the contractor may, I
hopes claim damages. He offers, however, the fellow
in proposition. If I will advance Four thousand
dollars he will suspend without claiming dam-
ages for anyatches of the Government up to
this time and proceed with the work on the 1st April

Copy of letter sent to the Department and appended to
Voucher No. 391.
149 Hammond Street
New York Oct. 28th 1843.
Dear Sir,
Finding myself unable to supply Prof. Morse
with the lead pipe embraced by my contract with him
reasonably to fulfill that contract you will oblige me if
you will aid me in obtaining the Deficiency at his accept-
ance within the contract price at the rate of seven and
one eighth cents per Yard and give receipt to him for
payment thereof accordingly and in discharge of my
contract and this shall be your authority therefor.
Respectfully
H. O. J. Smith Esq
Yr Mo. Obser'tt
James E. Serrall.
S. F. B. Morse.
Note, The above letter was drafted by M. Smitte

with them, subject to your order. All the other property in my charge
will be stored at the office. I have written two letters at different times
to you, viz on the completion of the 20, & 25 miles requesting that dishes
might be sent on to the Messrs. Thomas. I was very much mortified at
not hearing from you in reference to them. I am informed now by
De Gole that you have not received them. You will probably find
them at D. Button's to whom I owe one I understand from your
letter to direct my communications. I also wrote to him on 22d the
bills of loading requesting him to communicate to you & to Mr Smith
certain information of his relation to the work. I am assured he
did not immediately do so. As your instructions direct me to
remain at Messrs. Thomas till the last coil of pipe have been shipped
to Baltimore I shall be relieved from there on Saturday, I shall
if not Providentially prevented or not instructed differently
leave the first of the week for Baltimore, I should be glad if you
could remit to me some money to leave with my family during my
absence. I have received $22 from De Gole to pay my passage or
for which he holds my receipt. I have also sent on by him my
receipt for the last month to Messrs. Thomas bill on Ben Smith
for the pipe concerning which they would like to hear from you
at your earliest convenience
I remain very respectfully
your Hts Servt
James Fisher

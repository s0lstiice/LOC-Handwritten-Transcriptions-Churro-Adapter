Dear Sir,
Mr. Smith, who superintends
the trenching for the Telegraph, desires
me to request if you will give the use of a
double car, for the accommodation of
the men at work with him, which
may be removed to the various turns
outs as he proceeds with his work, and
serve as a depository for his tools, and a place also
in which they may sleep (at such parts
of the way as may be convenient. They
find that the convenient accommodation
has experienced great difficulty in procuring
board and lodging at the dwellings on the
way, and at this unpropitious season they
are doubly in need of all the conveniences
that can be afforded them. They wish to erect
a stove in it. — I called yesterday at the
Sub-Post Office for the purpose of learning the conditions of the transportation of my materials for
the Telegraph to various parts of the line by the
Company but had not the pleasure of seeing you.
Would you have the kindness to inform me
whether this transportation is without charge.

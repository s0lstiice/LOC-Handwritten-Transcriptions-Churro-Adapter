The Error was with Mr. Loring in writing Mr. Barback, 14 Boxes by
the Illinois, when the Bills of Lading make it 11
I should not have permitted Mr. Loring to write Mr. B. at all but
for my extreme dexterity at the time. I took my pen to write
but felt so much exhausted I was obliged to lay it down
again. I dictated writing but Mr. Loring obtained that
he had sent 14 Boxes by this vessel – I know the bills of
Lading would tell a being only much enfeebled did examine the subject any farther.
My mind has been on you & the work, daily and hourly, and
trust with the permission of Divine Providence to be able to transport my body there also. I suppose we should from your last
letter but one find you in Washington and after that I shall
enquire at the Whitmans Eagle Tavern and suppose we have come on
direct to Washington, shall we get our passage free from Bto W. – shall
we say nothing about it and pay a dear mileage? If you have
any directions to give you might leave a note for me at the Eagle
Tavern with the clerk or Bar keeper where I shall inquire.
I will try to do up your business to make it acceptable –
I cannot close without expressing my gratitude for your
many acts of kindness. Our Heavenly Father will reward you
I will try to do my duty.
I trust you will excuse this wandering from Bassinck
and believe me
Your Obd. Servt
L.D. Gale

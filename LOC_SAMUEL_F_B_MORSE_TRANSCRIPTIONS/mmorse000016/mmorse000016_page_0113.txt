P. Pug. James C. Fisher, Assistant U.S.
Sir,
An arrangement has been made with Messrs.
Tatham and Brothers for from 25 to 30 miles of lead pipe
of 3/8ths of an inch bore and weighing one pound to the yard;
the wires four in number of the four diff. colors they are to have
placed within the pipe. The pipe is to be in lengths of
from 450 to 600 feet, to be wound upon a drum of from
6 to 8 inches diameter, and six inches wide, the whole well packed in cases or
boxes for transportation; the delivery to be on board of packet
(for Baltimore,) (a canal boat) at their expense. Ten miles
are to be delivered thus prepared perfectly sound on or before
the 10th Novr. 1843. Ten miles more on or before the 15th Novr.
and the whole of the remainder on or before the 20th Novr.
The wire prepared for induction, is to be delivered by us at
Phi ca or New York at the option of the Messrs. Tatham.
All is submitted for my approval.
You will therefore proceed immediately to inspect and prove the lengths of pipe, by the air pump on
forcing pump or both, and perfecting the installation of the wire installation of the wire in their occupation
by a battery. You will remain until the 20th instant or until the pipe shall have been completely, and
successfully sent off to Baltimore to the care of D. Burkett
of 14 Commerce St. You will keep an exact account
of the property submitted for your inspection, taking the
proper receipts & memoranda for securing it from depu
dation or injury, and report to me at the office of the
Respective Commissioners
S.B.B.M. Superintendent

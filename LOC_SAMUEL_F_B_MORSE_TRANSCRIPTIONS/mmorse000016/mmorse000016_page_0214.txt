To D. L. D. Gale, and
Alfred Vail, Assistant Superintendents of Elec. Mag. Telegraphs
Washington Dec. 18th 1843.
Gentlemen:
You will, while I am absent, make the
necessary experiments to ascertain the conditions of the
Conductors in the Lead pipe, and will select such as
are sound sufficient to carry from the Patent Office to
the Capitol. If there is time after these experiments the
result of which you will carefully note, attend to the prepara-
tion for going into winter quarters, by determining where
the public property is (connected with the Telegraph,) is
and have all prepared for safe storage until the Spring.
The lead pipe must be collected for examination
during the winter months at Washington, Let me know
where it is deposited along the road in Casks.
Respectfully
Y. O. B. Servt.
Sam. F. B. Morse.
Superintendent of Elec. Mag.
Telegraphs.

Copy of the Endorsement on the Contract for Lead pipe with Mr. Seerell
To whom it may concern. Whereas I am unable from
circumstances beyond my control to furnish within the
time and terms of the within Contract the full amount
of Lead pipe therein contemplated, I do, therefore, hereby
agree and consent that said Morse shall obtain the
estimated Deficiency of from twenty five to Thirty
miles from such person or persons and upon such terms
as he may find practicable, without recourse to me for
the same.
New York city
Oct. 28th 1843
Witness
Francis O. J. Smith
James E. Seerell.
Note. This form was drafted by M. Smith. J. H. B. male

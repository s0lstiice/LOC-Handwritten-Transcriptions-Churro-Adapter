New York Oct 8 1840
Prof Morex
My Dear Sir
Although I wrote you yesterday, and commu-
nicated what information I had at the time; yet I was not at
all satisfied with what I had communicated. We had a great
snow storm here, Snow fell 7 or 8 inches, but a large portion
of it has already been reduced to a liquefying state (I am)
Jackson finished his contract of drawing fire last night, there
is about 1 mile to from and pack they expect to finish the work
tomorrow (Saturday) evening and all of us to come on the first
of the week. My health is daily improving. It seems to
have followed every thing connected with service work, and
his works -- I have never seen a well dug mine but it is
intended the night work in that damp cellar of a place.
Oh! that you had never known that man but it is
to late to lament.

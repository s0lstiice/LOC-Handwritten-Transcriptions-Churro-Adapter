Copy to Prof. J.C. Fisher Asst. Sup. of
Patents. On the other side of this letter and enclosed are orders
to the amount of $673.20 in favor of Messrs. Satham Brothers
which you will hand over to them on their signing the accompanying
receipts & which I then signed enclosed to you under cover to Hon.
H.L. Ellsworth, Com. of Patents. The delays in Albany and the illness
of Mr. Gale are embarrassing me very much. I fear Gale will not be
able to leave the express line. I fear he comes on I will
try and find in door work for him, but I shall need driving able
faithful men to look after the proper union of the joints, men
capable of bearing the cold weather if it should happen that
a cold day comes. -- Matters must be expedited at Paterson, the
contract was to be completed on the 20th ult. and if there have
been delays on this part, they ought to defray the additional expense
after that date, if my men in attendance, for I am doubly
dependent of their services, paying them for services occasioned by their
delay, and suffering for the want of their services on the road. -- Patent
matters closed up at N.York without delay, at least no more than cannot
be avoided. The barrel pipe is laid to the Relay house. But two
joints are constructed as yet, and the 3' length had water in it. We
shall have trouble with that pipe. -- From the Relay house we commence
with Paterson. It promises well. Write me in envelope to
Hon. H.L. Ellsworth, and send me such vouchers as you have, which
shall be duly audited to you. --
Respectfully yours
S.B. B.Morris
Superint. Ele. Mag. U.S.

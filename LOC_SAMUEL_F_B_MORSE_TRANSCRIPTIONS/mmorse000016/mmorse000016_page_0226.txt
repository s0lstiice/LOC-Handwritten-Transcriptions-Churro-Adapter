New York. Dec 21st 1843.
Prof. S.F.B. Morse Superintendent of Telegraph
Sir
I was this morning informed by the
means Fothams that you had written to me on the 1st of
December enclosing two drafts for $7500 made payable from
the Hon H.L. Ellsworth, & also 3 other letters in reference to
them I had yet received no answer from me, Immediately
on receiving this information I requested Mr Wm Fothams to
go with to the Post office to made search for them, as I had
repeatedly made inquiries for letters before without success
on inquiring we were informed that there were none, insisted
on or examination I after some time your letter of the 12th inst
from Baltimore was found. I then collated with Mr Fothams in the
Postmaster & after a long search I must questioning it was found
that your letter of the 10th enclosing the drafts & your letter of
the 11th inst finding fault with me for not answering the letter
& not reporting the progress of the work had been sent to
the Observer office where we proceeded to found them.
The letters were all directed to me without any specified place,
Had they been directed to me, at Messrs Fothams or at my residence
I should probably have received them without fail & much misunderstanding
being saved to all, Your instructions to me were to direct to
the case of D. Buford 14 Commerce St Baltimore, to whom I reported
wrote for you & to whose case I directed several letters for you which
were put in the office here by Messrs Fothams Clerk.

Dec. 5th 1843.
Sir,
I have the honor to report that I am proceeding with
the connection of my pipe devise for the Telegraph between
Baltimore and the Relay house inserting them into the test
boxes. But I find the process very slow with a single corps
of soldiers, and I have the proposition made me to have
a portion of the route from the Relay House towards Wash-
ington soldados at the same rate of average expense per
mile which the first section shall cost. It will not be
difficult to determine this when it shall have been com-
pleted, and as by thus doubling my force at no increased
pro rata p. mile I shall add nothing to the ultimate
expence in the aggregate, but avoid in a great degree protracting
the work at this unfavorable season, I desire permission
to make the above suggested arrangement with as little delay
as possible.
I have also to report the following names of labourers
hired by the day at one dollar p. day from the 23d of Novr. to aid
in the distribution of the pipe upon the road, W. Carman,
W. Langwell, and W. Cleveland.
I have the honor to be
Respectfully
J. H. B. More
Secy of War

Captain Fisher
is requested to take up his
position at the head of the
United States Navy Yard
at Portsmouth, Va.
and report to me at the
headquarters of the Army
at Washington, D. C., on
Wednesday, November 1st,
1863.
Very respectfully
I am, etc.
Genl. Grant
Genl. in Chief
U. S. Army
Washington, D. C.

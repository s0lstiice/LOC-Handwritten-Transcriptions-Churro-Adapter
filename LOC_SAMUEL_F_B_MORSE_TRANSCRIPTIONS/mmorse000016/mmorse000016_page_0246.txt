Treasury Department
December 29th. 1843
Sir:
In reference to the proposed modification
of the Contract made by you with Mr. Lui I Bartlett &
submitted for my consideration I have to state that while
Mr. Bartlett appeared to be the Contract has nothing
to modify and extend the Contract, but since it now
appears that he has assigned his Contract I do not see
why the Government should be limited in its dealings with
the person whom he may choose to displace. Mr. Bartlett's
Contract is now virtually at an end. If we must pay him
damages it seems to me it would be more fair & just to the
Government to do it directly and at once, than indirectly
in the hope of a modification of his original contract with
a new party, which in effect is making a new contract.
The Government is entitled to the benefits of competition
from which it would be excluded by the proposed operation
You will therefore inform Mr. Bartlett that being unable to
furnish pipe to enable him to proceed we must take
the consequences, and you will advertise in the Madison

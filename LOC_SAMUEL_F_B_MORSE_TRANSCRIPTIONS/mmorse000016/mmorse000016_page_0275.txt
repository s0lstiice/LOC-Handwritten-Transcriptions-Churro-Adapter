These presents witness, that the contract existing
between Samuel F. B. Morse, as agent of the United States,
on the one part, and Lewis Bartlett, of the other part, for
the performance of certain services therein named in
trekking and laying pipe for said Morse's telegraph,
and having date June 10, 1843, has been fully and
satisfactory performed and executed on the part of
said Bartlett, to the extent that the party of the other
part, to wit, the said Morse, in behalf of the United
States, has enabled the same to be done; but the provisions
of said contract, on the part of the United States, have
been violated to the great injury of said Bartlett -
and it is for the interest of the United States that the
further execution of the same by the said Bartlett
be postponed for the present - Now to the end that
justice may be done in the premises, and in consideration
that the United States have paid the said Bartlett
the sum of eighteen hundred and forty seven dollars
and fifty cents on so much of said contract
as the said Bartlett has fulfilled, and shall further
pay said Bartlett, on the signing of these presents
the sum of four thousand four hundred and seventy
two dollars and fifty cents, being the remainder of
the sum which the United States covenanted to pay on
the fulfillment of said contract by said Bartlett,
and for which fulfillment said Bartlett has been at
all times and still is ready, and the further sum
of three hundred dollars to be paid for expenses
& which said Bartlett will be subjected by the delay
required by the United States, as now estimated, the
Said Bartlett hereby covenants and agrees to
pay all other damages occasioned by the non-
fulfillment of said contract on the part of the United
States, and to fulfill the same on his part as originally

Expenses in Washington in 1843 while engaged in obtaining Act of Congress for Telegraph.
Took on Prof. Fisher to assist in exhibiting Telegraph, paid his expenses out of my own pocket to be charged to the Proprietor of Telegraph.
Board of Self at M. Fletcher from Dec 13th to March 10th 1843
Traveling expenses to Wash $16.50 to N.York $16.50
Prof. Fisher's board
and travelling expenses to Wash and back to N.York
Personal services time at $3.00 per day 88 days
Prof. Fisher's at 3.00 per day 88 days
Note Personal services & time commenced before this and board at Beech temperance house for a week at least
not allowed if salary is allowed.
Freight of instruments from N.Y. to Wash. day
Receipts and vouchers for work among my receipt at New York

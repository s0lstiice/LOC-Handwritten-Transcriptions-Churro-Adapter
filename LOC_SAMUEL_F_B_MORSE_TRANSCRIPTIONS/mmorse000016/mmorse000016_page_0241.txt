Philadelphia. This was afterwards reordened to your factory
in New York upon the assurance which experiment had fur-
nished you, that you could to better advantage to yourselves
and and with equal advantage to my purposes manufacture
in your new way. – The experiment, however, proves to be wholly
deceptive as well upon yourselves no doubt, as upon myself.
The consequences of this do not seem properly to attack to my
side of the Contract. –
Not doubting, however, that you will be disposed
to remedy the misfortune so far forth as is practicable
(though some months delay in the progress of Prof. Morse’s
experiment will be unavoidable,) I submit to you the
facts as discovered and will anxiously await your reply
as to what shall be done in the matter. –
With great respect
Y. Ob. Servt.
Francis O. J. Smith.
P.S. You can write me to the case of Mr. Morse under
cover to Hon. H. L. Ellsworth Commissioner of Patents with
whom Mr. Morse is staying and it will hasten the reception
of it by me. –

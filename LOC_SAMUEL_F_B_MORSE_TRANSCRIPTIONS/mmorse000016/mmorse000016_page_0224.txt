Washington Dec. 20th 1843.
Sir.
I have the honor to report that from my inability
to supply to the Contractor for trenching for the Tele-
graphic wires, at the stipulated time a sufficient quan-
tity of suitable pipe to enable him to fulfill the contract
on his part to the full extent of the proposed line, I am
under the necessity of leaving a portion of that work
unfinished until spring. But as this postponement
necessarily violates the contract on the part of the
Government I have made an arrangement with
the Contractor for trenching to postpone his further
operation which I herewith submit for the Honora-
ble Secretary's approval. The Contractor has been
prompt and efficient to fulfill his undertaking so
far as I have been able to supply pipe, and has also
been subjected to much loss of time and extra expense
in consequence. I have therefore entered into such an
agreement as I believe to be just towards him, and under
the circumstances most for the interest of the Government
and the enterprise, hoping it may meet with the ap-
probation of the Department.
With great respect
Y. Ob Servt.
Sam'l F.B.Morse
Superintendent of Electric Magnetic Telegraphs
to the Honr. John C. Spencer
Secretary of the Treasury
of the United States

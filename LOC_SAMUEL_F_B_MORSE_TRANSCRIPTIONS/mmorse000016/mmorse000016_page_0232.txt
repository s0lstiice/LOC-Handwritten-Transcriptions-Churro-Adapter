because insult is added to injury. As for a cause after all of your own creating,
I am sorry that our connection should be terminated in this manner & I must but hope
that when you review the whole matter after the excitement shall have passed
away you will regret the injustice with which you have treated me. It is true I am
dependent upon my salary for the support of those dear to me in my own life but
that God who has said I will never leave them nor forsake them & who has so long kept
me from them will yet provide for us those things which we need. When you spoke to
me some time back in reference to the repayment of the money I had borrowed from
the Anthony, I was on the point of telling you that I had borrowed it & more from him
on the strength of your promise that you would pay me for my board & time at
Washington, I know not how you may feel in reference to this matter but I feel
that I have a just & equitable claim on you & the proprietors of the telegraph
for my services, I am glad however that the money is paid to him as I would ill a man
afflict, it costs myself rather than he should suffer from his generosity to me. I have much
more to say but the situation of my wife who is now in labour makes it necessary for
me to stop for the present.
Dec 23. After a long & tedious labour Mr Fisher was able

the least notice for a cause originating with myself, I am generous in that knowing
my situation you were willing to avail yourself of my assistance to obtain that

P. O. J. Smith, Esq.
Washington Jan'y 3d 1844.
Sir
Your of the 2d inst is received and I hasten
to say that I cannot comply with your request to unite
with you in an appeal to the President from the decision
of the Secretary of the Treasury on the subject of the Bart-
lett contract. Having been instructed to open a corres-
pondence with you for an adjustment of damages which
you allege have been sustained, I should have sub-
mitted your proposition on its receipt had you
not chosen already to communicate it to the
Secretary yourself. I have as yet received no
instruction from the Secretary in relation to it.
I am still ready to receive such further propositions
as you may wish to make fortified by any affidavits
you may desire to add. In the mean time let
me say that admonished by the past, I must adhere
scrupulously to instructions, and confine my communi-
cations with you to written correspondence.
Respectfully
Yr mo. obt. Sent.
Saml. P. B. Morse
Superintendent of
Elec. Mess. Hygeia

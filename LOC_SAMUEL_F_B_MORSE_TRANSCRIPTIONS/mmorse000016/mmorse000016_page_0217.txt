In consequence of arrangements thus day made with Prof
Morse Sup. Elec. Mag. Tel. it is mutually agreed that
the work of trenching prescribed stipulation in the contract
with Lt. Bartlett be suspended until April 1st
1844, with the exception of the distance between the Capitol
and Patent Office, which shall be expedited as soon as possible the prize is delivered for that purpose.

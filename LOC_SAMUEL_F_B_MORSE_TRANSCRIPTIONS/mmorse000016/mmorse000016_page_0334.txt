I have examined the statements & facts set forth
in the preceding petition and find them to be correctly
representative and even in the request that Congress may
give the desired authority and direction for adjudicating the
damages occasioned in the Battle by the manifest lack of
his control on the part of the U. States. His part of
the Contract was fulfilled in all respects up to the
time of the order given by me for a suspension of operation.
Copy of endorsement request of me by Mr. Smith
drafted to Congress Jan. 7, 1844

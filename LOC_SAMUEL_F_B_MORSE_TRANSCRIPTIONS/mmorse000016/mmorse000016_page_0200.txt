I believe I mention to you, when
I had the pleasure of seeing you in my office, that
an objection existed in the minds of many of our
members, to granting gratuitously the use of the
road and Machinery in a manner which might
interfere with the regular operations of the Company
for purposes to which the appropriation by Congress
would be properly applicable; and if that appropriation
cannot be used to defray the expense of
transporting materials to be employed in the
construction of the improvement, I will be glad if
you will enable me to state that fact to the
Board.

Very respectfully,
Your obedient servant,

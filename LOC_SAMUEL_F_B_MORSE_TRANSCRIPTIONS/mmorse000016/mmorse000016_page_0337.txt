I am preparing (as Isaid before under every disadvantage) a
short distance between the Patent Office & Capitol, which I am
desirous of having completed as soon as possible, and by means
of it relieving the enterprise from the heavy weight that now threat
ens it.
I write in great haste, but must put you in mind of
W. Ellsworth's commission respecting the Engraving of Telegraph
for him, and the description of Telegraphy; He asks about it
very day, and looks eagerly at his letter for one from you
to him or to me on the subject. His Report is in preparation
and he will want it soon.—
I hope all are well, Catherine, the babies and all.
I don't know the name of the new comer, or comers; for I
suppose Richard & Louisa have one added by this time. Give
much love to all, and specially remember me in your
prayers, as I do you.—
Y. aff. brother ever
S.B. Mudge
If any expense attends my request, I will pay it of course

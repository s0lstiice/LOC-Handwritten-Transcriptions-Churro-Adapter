New York Jan 21st 1844.
Res V.F. B. Morse Superintendent to
Sir
On the 23d of last month I wrote to you
enclosing the duplicate receipts for delay for the half month ending
Dec 25th 1843. 1 also duplicate vouchers for money expended on acct
of the telegraph. I was in hope that you would have forwarded to me
the money immediately, as I need it very much. When I wrote that letter
I was under the impression that the cause of complaint against me
on your part, was my neglect in answering your letters & as for so
I told others to whom they were submitted could judge, on explication
of your part against me that I had made use of them improperly.
There was no other complaint against me but this, I understood
however from Dr Gile that in a letter received by him on Saturday
you complain of my letter as insulting & indecorous & state the
cause of complaint against me to be that, either through gross
criminal neglect, or ignorance I did not state the fact that the
covering of the wires was connected channel in 3 or 4 places in each
length of spike, I still not consent to any such charge a complaint
Sir being brought against me, You say also that you would not dare
to appear before the Secretary after retaining me in office under the
circumstances, how Sir I am very sorry that your memory is so short
that you cannot remember what took place in relation to these very
shorted places between you & the Warrens & Thomas before leaving New

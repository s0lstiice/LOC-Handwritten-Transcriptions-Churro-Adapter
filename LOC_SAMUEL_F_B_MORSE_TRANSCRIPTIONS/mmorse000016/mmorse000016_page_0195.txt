New York Dec 15th 1843.
Prof. S.F.B. Morse Superintendent Telegraph
Vic
I have the honor to report to you that
the Messrs Fathams have completed their contract & have
made 59,899 1/2 lbs of lead fine which I have approved of
which I have received the bill for all except 8509 1/2 lbs to be
packed today & to-morrow morning so that all will be sent off
for tomorrow's boat. Immediately on completing the packing
I will forward to you at Washington the one of Hon H.L.
Ellsworth in detailed schedule of the whole. I have been much
pleased with the disposition manifested by the Messrs Fathams
to complete the work in the best manner so that it should be
as perfect as possible. They were unfortunately prevented by
accidents over which they had no control arising from the
tremendous steam in making so small a hole being greater than
the strength of some parts of the machinery, from completing
their contract so soon as they expected to have done. They have
however expressed themselves to the utmost to complete it at the
earliest possible moment. Immediately on completing the
purging of the pipe I had the air humor carefully hocked & by
the advice of Dr. Geladent to your care of 2 Pounds of Platinum
by Harndens Express that no delay might arise from it. The bottom
pages were sent on by U.S. Chime. It now had the wire remaining
amounting to 565 lbs. wound on 4 reel & thats nailed on around

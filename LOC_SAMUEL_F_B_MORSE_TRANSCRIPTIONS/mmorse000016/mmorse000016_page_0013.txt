Ontario Sept. 22-1843
From
you of the 18th is at home. I am
glad "all goes well in other matters"
than Knott's extract.
I am quite sure, that Knott's
form of letter need not be communicated
at all to the key - for it is to authorise
me to use Knott's name, you as he
calls on it - & to make known to you, that
I have authority to do - all receipts
he will bill in Knott's name alone,
just as if he wrote them - and the
letter he sent you, is to justify you in
taking receipt of this matter in his
absence - my name in your account
need not be mentioned at all, matter
of Knott's agent, or as this misprint
of the money - but all paytts are to be
changed as needed to him, & where his
attorney otherwise he is to give me

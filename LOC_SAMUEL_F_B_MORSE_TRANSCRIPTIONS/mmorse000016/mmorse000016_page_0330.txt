Treasury Department
January 9th 1844
Sir:
Your letter of the 3d instant, submitting copies
of a correspondence between T. O Smith & myself
in reference to the ascertainment of damages sustained
under the contract with Louis Battell, is received.
In consideration of the subject it appears reason
to dissent from the conclusion thus pursued. As instructed
in my communication of the 29th ult. you will proceed
to ascertain the extent of damages, if any, sustained by
Battell or his agents under his contract for hauling
laying pipe, in consequence of any acts or omissions
on your part as the agent of the Government. The information
made in your letter to Mr Smith of the 3d instant where
the claims for damages purported by assistant is deemed
proper. I will at the same time ascertain whether any damage
has occurred, and if so, the extent, has been sustained by the
Government growing out of any contract or agreement entered

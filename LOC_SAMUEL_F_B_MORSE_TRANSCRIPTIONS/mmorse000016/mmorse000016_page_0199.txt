Office of the Baltimore & Ohio Rail
Road Company, December 15 1848
To,
Samuel P. B. Mose, Egr.
Dear Sir,
The Superintendent was
authorized to transport materials for the operations in
which you are now engaged at intermediate points
of the road where it could be done without interference
with the regular business of the road, free of charge,
but his authority did not extend to transportation
throughout the entire distance involving a different
sort of labor and responsibility, and without permission
of the Board I could not give it that extension.
I should be equally restricted in detaching from the
regular business of the road -- which usually gives
full employment to the Machinery, a car for your
exclusive use, and to be fitted up in the manner
you propose. I could do so, however, in the
case of the Board subject to the usual charge for
such privilege; and at the first meeting of the Board
I will submit your communication for their final
decision.

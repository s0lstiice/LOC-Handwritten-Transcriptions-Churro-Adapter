The U. States Government by Proclamation
Richard M. Hoe and Robert Hoe beg to notify their
friends, that although this Establishment is now owned
by Sanderson, Brothers & Co. yet they are engaged as
the sole Machinists there, and they request their old
friends to patronize the same. If such friends prefer
to correspond with them in regard to the mechanical
department of the business, and also in regard to orders,
they can do so, and their communications will be
attended to—or they may address Mr. Joseph Steele, the
receiving and paying clerk, and general agent to the
proprietors.
RICHARD M. HOE,
ROBERT HOE.

Oh, how these troubles drive all thoughts of children and brothers
and all rest of my mind except in the waking hours of the night, and
then I think of you all with sadness that I cannot add to your
enjoyment, but only to your anxiety I hope Catherine & you all
are rejoicing in a safe and happy birth of son or daughter.
Love to all; Specially remember me in your prayers, this
I may have wisdom from above to act wisely and justly and
calmly in this dire trial. This is private.
Your affec. brother
S.J.B.Morse

My heart is in the Telegraph but God has hushed me in from it, &
though greatly against my feelings and wishes I think my duty
plain and must submit. I am aware this will be a great dis-
appointment to you, but no greater than the grief and affliction
with which I have looked upon it as such upon me. Why it is
I know not.
Permit me to make one remark. Should I have nothing
more to do with the Telegraph, do not I pray you
as you regard its interest, engage Dr. F. W. D. G. relative in the
enterprise. But I would advice to open the matter to Mr. Fazi and
if practicable get him to give some extra attention to the
subject in laying out the necessary experiments to be made
a. of the winter, and in connection with Mr. Vail to construct
a wire of wires from the Patent Office to the Capital. From
the experiments and the remark of Stinchell and Man
of Wheatatea I am confident you will eventually suspend
the wires on folks in the air. Come say, will you not think
it best to send for the wires that are at Oakland, and suspend
them on folks for the line extending from the Patent Office to the
Capitol? And then if you determine to do it lay a second line
through the earth. I have injured quite the facts relations
to the charging of the covering of the wire. The charged parts
will be found in nearly all cases to be within 18 or 20 inches
of the solidum joints of the pipe. If not aware of this, please bear
it in mind and communicate it to Mr. Vail so that it
will be great service in removing the burned portions of wire.

Copy of a note to Mr. Wood, dated Dec. 22. 1843, of which Mr.
Smith complained to me that it was the cause of his difficulty in
getting the advance from the Treasury.
As Mr. Morse has consulted Mr. Wood respecting the
contract for laying down pipe, and as Mr. Wood is in the Treasury
Department where a modification of the Contract is pending
Mr. M. remarks that he does not know what is customary
as to security for advances. Mr. Morse is unacquainted with
Mr. Bartlett's responsibility, he doubtless could give security if
this is customary. Mr. Morse has submitted the
modification and recommended the advance upon the sup-
position that the advance of $3000 was to be in full for
all claim for damages for lackes suffered on the part
of the Government. If the $3000 is limited as so much
above the work actually performed including the prior
advance of $500 then Mr. Morse will have proper vou-
chers obtained.

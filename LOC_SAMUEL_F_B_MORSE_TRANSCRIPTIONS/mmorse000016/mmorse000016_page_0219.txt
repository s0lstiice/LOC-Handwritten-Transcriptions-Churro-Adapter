Relay House Dec. 20th 1843
To Hon. Louis M. Lane
Pres. of the B. & O. R. R. Co
Dear Sir
I have the honor to acknowledge the receipt
of your letter of the 15th inst. Since writing my letter to which yours
is an answer, I have thought best to discontinue operations
along the line for the winter, confining myself to Washington
in arranging for the spring so that the car requested by me
will not at present be needed. I thank you most
for the consideration and kindness intended. In relation to the transportation
of our materials upon the road I cannot say that the ap-
propriation by Congress would be properly applicable to defray
the expenses of transportation of persons, and the
R.R. Co being restricted by their charter from extending the priv-
ilege of free travel to us, I hoped that perhaps a commutation
would be made by the Company on that account in favor of free
transportation of our materials. -
In a few days I hope to have the pleasure of a
personal interview with you.
I am, dear Sir,
With great respect
Y. O. Servt
Sam. F. B. Morse
Supintendent of Electric Mag. Telegraphs

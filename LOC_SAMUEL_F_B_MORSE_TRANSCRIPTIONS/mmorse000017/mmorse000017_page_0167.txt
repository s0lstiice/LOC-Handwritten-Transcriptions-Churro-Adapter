Washington, April 16, 1844
Dear Sir
I have given an apology for not answering
before this your note of 11 inst. respecting the comm.
from Ch. Com. Clain Senate on claim of
2 S Barlett. I called as you suggested immediately
at Mr Porter and my explanation was perfectly
satisfactory to him and he expressed himself perfectly
satisfied that the matter had assumed the shape it has.
Very Respectfully
I am
your obedient Servt
Geo. B. Emery
Chief of Staff
Gilbert Rodman Engr
Chief Dep't

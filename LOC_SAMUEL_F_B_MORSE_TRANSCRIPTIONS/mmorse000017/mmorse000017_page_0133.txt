Washington March 23. 1844
Dear Sidney.
I have just received the letter you sent
from Susan with the endorsement respecting Richard's
son Richard, I hope his illness will not be serious, yet I am
preparing for a different issue. Tell them to
yield their hearts in perfect submission to the will of
God, and all will be peace. Give my love to them all
and to Catherine & the children & Sidney.
Susan says in her letter she shall return in May
with Charles, and the baby, and leaves Mr. Sind behind
to follow some time after. What arrangements can be
made for them when they arrive? I wish you would
cast in your mind and let me know what you think on
the subject. – I hope to be able to be in New-York when
they arrive, but where can she be, since you & Catherine will
be absent, and Richards house will be overflowing. –
Perhaps she may find it agreeable to board in
New Haven. –
The Rev. Mr. Spole has just said to me that in Nov. he
sent $5. to pay for the Obviance, and has just received
a bill which gives no credit for that amount. He wishes me
to mention it when I wrote you. Mr. Spole is our minister
here, from Carlisle (I think) in Penn.

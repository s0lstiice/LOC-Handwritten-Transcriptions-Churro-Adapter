not know but it might be imparted to you through your
friend Mr Ellsworth -- I would remark that since
Mr Knowles seemed to understand at once independent of
anything in the office, what was wanted -- if you can
obtain this for me I shall be under an additional
obligation.
I am sending glad to hear that your improvement
appears so encouraging -- and that your perseverance
and patience will accomplish what you desire.
Mr King says there will be some lead on the
way soon -- if no order come with it, perhaps I
as well put it away cheap as I can, & be doing
as of opportunity presents -- but I shall probably be
the pleasant spring you soon --
Yours truly
David Burtank

New York May 31st 1844-
Professor Morse
Lix - I take the liberty of handing you
one of my Circulars, by which you will see, that the
Natural Asphaltum rock, is a cement completely, impervious
to air and water, and is universally used in Europe &
also in the United States, for works which require protection
from air and dampness - Therefore I have no hesitation
in recommending the Asphaltum to you, as a material
for building Pipes to serve as a Cover for your Magnetic
Telegraph which will be the Cheapest, and the most
durable material you can use for that purpose -
I believe that the Asphaltum has been used
in England for the same purpose - I refer you to the
Engineers mentioned in my circular, and particularly to
the Chief Engineer Col J. G. Lotten in Washington, who
has published a Book on the subject of Bitumen, called
"Papers on practical Engineering, Bitumen, its varieties, pro-
perties, and uses" - and by calling on Col Lotten, you
can easily get a copy of it, which will give you all
the necessary information on the subject.
The price of the Asphaltum is $32. per 2000 lbs
and the mineral Tar $6. per 100 lbs - The proportion of
Tar necessary for working the Natural Asphaltum
is 12 lbs of Tar to 100 lbs of Asphaltum and 5 lbs
of this mixture will cover one square foot of half
an inch in thickness - from this statement you

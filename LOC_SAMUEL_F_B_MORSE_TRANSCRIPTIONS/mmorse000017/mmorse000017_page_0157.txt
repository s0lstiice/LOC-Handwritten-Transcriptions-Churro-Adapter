[Washington: 1844, Apr. 10, E. H. Foster to Secy of Treasury]
Washington April 11th 1844
Dear Sir:
By direction of the Secretary of the Treasury I
have herewith a copy of a communication from the Hon
C H Foster Chmn of the Committee of the United States Senate
acting for a refut of the claim of Em Battell, and to
support the suffrity of your actions on Mr Foster complaining
to him, that it is a suspension from Mr Smith for an unmi-
nable adjustment of the damages claimed, which has induced
a delay of the refut, which you understand is ready
to be notified fully
Your obedient Servant
Elbert Rodman
Post S. F. B. Missouri
Washington

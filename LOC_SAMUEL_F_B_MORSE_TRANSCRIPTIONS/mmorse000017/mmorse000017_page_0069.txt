Washington Feb. 29th 1844
Copy
F. O. J. Smith, Esq:
Sir,
I have examined the accounts of expenditures in Europe
which I have rendered to you, and the items you have objected to, and these
items I find class themselves under different heads; some are doubtless
justly chargeable to me; others to you, others to both the proprietors of the
Telgraph proportionally, and others to neither party as previously.
These I have drawn out under the different heads, and send you a copy
for your examination.
In connection with this account I would ask you
to explain some of your remarks, and reasons for deducting certain
sums from the remark "omitted Re."

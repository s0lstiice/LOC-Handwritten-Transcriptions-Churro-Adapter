Copy of P.O.J. Smith's Memorial to the Congress.
To the Honorable Senate and House of Representatives
of the United States.
The petition of Levi S. Bartlett of Kingston in the county
of Rockingham and State of New Hampshire respectfully represents
that on the 10th day of June last past he entered into a contract with
the U. States to do and perform certain services at a fixed rate
of compensation therein named.
Said contract is in writing and herewith submitted.
By its terms he was to be enabled to fulfill the same between
the first day of August and the 1st day of December last. But by
repeated failures on the part of the U. States as will fully appear
from correspondence herewith submitted he was delayed in the
progress of said work to his great damage until even past
the period assigned for its completion.
That having at very great expense and labor prepared
to fulfill his part of the contract as rapidly as the Agent of the
United States would furnish the means, and while progressing
therein, and after the greatest difficulties, risks, and labor, incident
to such complete fulfilment on the rail road between
Baltimore and Washington cities had been incurred by your
petitioner although not the half in distance had been completed
your petitioners received on the 18th day of December last past the
written order of the Agent of the U. States arresting him in the
future execution of the work and directing a small fractional
portion of work (not in fact covered by his contract) to be executed
under the circumstances which will appear by said written
order, a copy of which is herewith submitted marked A,
and by your petitioners answer thereto marked B.
Your petitioners willing as in said paper marked B to
accommodate himself to the request of the governments agent

Passion More Baltimore Feb 2d 1844.
My dear Sir.
I received a letter or rather certificate of shipment on board Sch y Rochester at New York dated 6th inst. - 65 bricks & 2 Boxes - one also
an accompanying note signed Atta d to former thimble Washington soon as they arrive.
I received your favor of the 9th when you left here I have done the useful in this case - I would like to know if the contract is closed for furnishing the Charleston bricks? if not, and you declined, I could have them furnished at a short notice or any other, as I have spoken to some about them - but I find they say they could not afford to deliver them at Washington much short of one dollar. - One of the parts they say is equal to about four or five common pots, and to get them this size & length it will take a whole tree. I only mention this, as if you have not engaged them, I might be likely to obtain them as soon as any one.
I notice in the "Franklin Institute" Journal, that Mr P L Badger or Isaac Hoffman of Concord, Mass, and Oliver Sheldon of New Marlborough, Mass, claim to be inventor of a steam machine, each, I would be obliged if you will look or inquire in the "Patent Office" if a specimen of either of their work is in the office - also if Isaac Dodds of Bennington, Vt. has on record at said office

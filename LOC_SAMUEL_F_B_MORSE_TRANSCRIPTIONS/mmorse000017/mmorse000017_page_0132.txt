Washington March 21. 1844.
Dear Sidney,
I am going to trouble you to compare the
accompanying copy of a letter of Mr Smith's to Messrs Tatham
& brothers lead pipe manufactures on Water st. and certify or
get them to certify that it is a true copy. It is a letter of
great importance in the claim of Smith before Congress. I
took a copy of it, before it was sent, but the Secy of State I
had better have a certified copy from N.York. I wrote
the Tathams on the 14th inst. requesting a copy but have
received no answer; and I fear they think that there is
something in it which implicates them. I assure them
that this is not the case; it is merely to show the
relation of Smith to the pipe contract. -- I think they
will not object to your comparing it with them, nor
to their or your certifying to the correctness of it.--
Smith is greatly haste, enclosing two letters*
received from Susan. If oranges and box of sweetmeats
come please forward them with as little delay as
possible.-- Telegraph matters look a little more
encouraging, I hope two or three weeks will enable me to show a successful experiment as far as
Belleville 12 miles.--
Give much love to Katherine & Richard's family
and Gilbert and little Lucetta, and to Finley.--
Yr Aff. brother as ever
Finley.
Keep the drawing of the house & building
of the Henrietta, carefully.

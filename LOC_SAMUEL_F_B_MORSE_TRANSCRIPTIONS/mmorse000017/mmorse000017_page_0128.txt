Washington March 20. 1844
Dear Sir,
In my interview yesterday with James Murray, Esq.
the Superintendent of the Rail Road, Concluding with him in relation
to the Distribution of my Posts between Wash. & Baltimore,
I understood from him that freight might be charged for
carrying them to the various termini, but from thence they would
be distributed gratuitously, but he advised to my correspondence
with you on the subject. On the belief that by your letter to
me of Decr. 15th. 1843, "the materials for my operations would
be transported between intermediate points on the road, free
of Charge," although "transportation throughout the entire distance"
would be subject to a different law, I had sent in
my estimates to the Treasury Department, (on this understanding)
[on the operation between Wash. & Baltimore,] As I sent in a very
low estimate, and am making every exertion for an economical
experiment I should be much embarrassed if a different
understanding has been put upon your permission of the
15th. Decr. 1843, I would take this occasion to say that the
Distribution of my materials for the present experience
will be much less trouble to the Company than the Distribution of my Posts between Wash. & Baltimore.
Believe me I am
With sincere respect
Y. M.obb Sent.
Sam. F. B. Morse
Supr. U.S. Navy Dept.
to Hon. Louis Mc Lane
of Baltimore and
Pres. Ohio R.R. Co:

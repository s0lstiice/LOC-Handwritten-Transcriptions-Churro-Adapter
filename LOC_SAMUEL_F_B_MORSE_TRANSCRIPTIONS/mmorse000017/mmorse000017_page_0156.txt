Senate Chamber
Office Committee on Claims
April 10th. 1844
When the Committee on Claims
is directed by the Committee of claims
respectfully to call your attention to the memorial of Gen Bartlett
which had the time to admit some time ago to your outside
petition I am aware of the multiplicity of engagements
of the Department, but the progress of business before the Committee
makes it desirable to have this case disposed of, and
Repatriate will meet your convenience to adopt an early
report
with these considerations
Your Res'd Servt
Orchard H Foster
Chmn Committee on Claims

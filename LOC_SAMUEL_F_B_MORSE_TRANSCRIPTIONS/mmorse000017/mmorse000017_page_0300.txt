"I feel that under the impressions which a reliance on Prof. Hordis's
supposed knowledge of Electic Currents gave rise to great injustice was done to Prof. Simon in accusing him of negligence in attempting the wire line and laid upon their failure to work satisfactorily in fields & I design so far as I have been thus made
the instrument of injustice by reliance upon erroneous theories, to make ample amends to him whenever an opportunity shall offer" &c. &c.
I do not write these things to find fault but to let you know what is going on - I have the same confidence in your sense of justice that I have always had.
Dr F has behaved very badly towards me, not only has he robbed me of all my plans & continuances for the manufacture of Sugar of Lead but in order to justify his glaring and palpable misconduct he has represented to his friends that I had promised him a partnership in the manufacture for the aid and improvements he had afforded and the only hold he had on me was to rob me of my improvements to build up himself.
The first and only time I ever said anything about this to Fraternal relation to my factory or business was when I learned you had discharged him and then I wrote F stating that it was possible I might be able to make some arrangement with him to take charge of my factory and accordingly when I reached home last Christmas I went directly to see Dr F to profess an arrangement when I was repulsed with a "Much obliged I can do better"
I shall be glad to hear from you soon
Respectfully
Ed Lisle

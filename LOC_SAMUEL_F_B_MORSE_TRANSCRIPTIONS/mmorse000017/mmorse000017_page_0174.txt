Prof Samuel G B Morel
Washington, D C,
Sir.
I take the liberty of addressing you
in the purpose of requesting a favor in behalf of my brother,
Richard W Hattard. He is now in Boston engaged in painting
and teaching and we are desirous of obtaining business here
for him in the instruction of a school in which a vacancy
exists. The only obstacle of any moment to our obtaining the
situation for him is the want of testimonials as to his qualifications
for the post which we are afraid we should not have
sufficient time to obtain if we write to him in regard to them.
I have therefore taken the liberty of requesting your kind
services in the matter by giving me your opinion in such
form as you see proper so as to be applicable to the purpose
we have in view. He has devoted himself entirely to drawing
& painting since he left you and has been for the last 8 months
engaged in teaching those arts in Boston. If you think you
can meet our wishes on this subject we shall regard it as
a very great favor.
As the urgency is somewhat pressing may I request so far
as to request as early a reply as your engagement will permit.
Very respectfully yours
Samuel G B Morel
Richard W Hattard
Col Comr

Pittsburgh April 18th 1844
St B Morse Exp
Sir.
Knowing by reputation your
extensive attainments - particularly in Electric Phaenomena
I have taken the liberty of thus addressing you
for some information - trusting you may not deem
it intemperate or improper. My profession is watch repairing,
in which I do a large business in connection
with my watch & jewelery store. I have now a patent
lever watch the steel works of which are all magnetised
I have for often methods of running it from by heat -
this process ad altering a part of the movement - Do you
know of any other forces that would run it the
rest? If your time & inclination would permit an
answer would confer a great favor - I must beg of you
Magnetic Telegraph I trust it may come to a successful
completition
With best wishes
Your Respectfully
W.W. Wilson

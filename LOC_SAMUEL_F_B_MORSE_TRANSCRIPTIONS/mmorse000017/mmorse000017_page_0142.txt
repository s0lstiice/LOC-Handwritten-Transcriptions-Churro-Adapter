Baltimore April 2d. 1844.
Professor S. F. B. Morse
Dear Sir,
During our late interview in this City, you informed me
that you contemplated making an experiment with your telegraph between Washington & Beltsville, in two or three weeks.
I had the pleasure this morning of seeing two of my friends (Editors of the Baltimore
American) Messrs. Dobbin & Bose who are highly pleased with all they have heard concerning the Electro-Magnetic telegraph,
and will take great pleasure in being present whenever you make a public
exhibition.
You also mentioned to me while here that the government shortly contemplated
organizing a telegraphic Corps; should they do so I shall feel under many
obligations if you can assist me towards receiving an appointment under you
as regards my knowledge of the science you have had practical information; and

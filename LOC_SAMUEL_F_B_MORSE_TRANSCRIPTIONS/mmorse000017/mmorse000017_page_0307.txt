Balt June 3d 1844
Dear Prof. More
I regret you did not come
on this morning for I want very much to see
you, and advise with you in preparing the room
but now I must do the best I can which I
will endeavour to do to the best of my judgment
every thing went well last week, and such an
excitement produced by never occuring before in
relation to any new thing brought before the public
thousands begged and pleaded to be allowed inearly
to look at the instrument. They declared they would
not say a word or stir and did't care whether they
understood or not, only they wanted to say they had
seen it. Hundreds have said that it is worth
million to us. I hope that the $50,000 you speak
of getting for it is only for the right of using the
Telegraph to New York. That in the same ratio
or more you will be paid as it is extended.
$50,000 for the whole or right is but a fraction of
its worth and I am not a little uneasy to know
that, the sum mentioned is only the weight
to extend it to N.York. Will you send me
the documents necessary to make a temporary
pamphlet with the words cut, that I may get
something up immediately to satisfy the public

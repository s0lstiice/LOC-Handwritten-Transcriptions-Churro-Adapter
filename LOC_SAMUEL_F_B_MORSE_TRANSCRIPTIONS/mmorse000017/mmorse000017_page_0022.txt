Copy of Mr Smith's memo
to
John C. Spivak. K- 1st Small Chamber. Com'd of Claims
Feb. 1844
By decision of the Board of Claims of the U.S. Senate, I submit
herewith the petition complaining papers of Lewis S. Bartlett, claiming
to be indemified for damages sustained by him in consequence of the
violence of his Contract with the U.S., and desire to be informed
whether the allegations of the petition are in conformity with the
evidence of your Department on the subject, and whether said
Bartlett's Contract had been in his part fulfilled in good faith, up to the
finish of the execution of the work undertaken by the direction
of the Govt Agent, and what kind of damages he is entitled to
claim by the non-filfillment of the Contract, or discontinuance
of the work under it by the Govt. And I will copy of any
reports or official letters which Prof. Davis may have made
to your Dep't. on the subject of the above Contract.
Your Respect. Ephraim H. Doster.

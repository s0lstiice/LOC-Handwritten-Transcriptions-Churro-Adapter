Received Washington June 4th 1844 of the
United States Five hundred dollars, being the
Sum agreed to be paid me, in the letter of the De-
partment under date of March 28th 1843 on the con-
ditions set forth in the following passage. "Your
"own compensation will be Two thousand dollars
"per Annum with the understanding that you
"are to receive when the work shall be completed
"an additional sum of Five hundred dollars,
"if the appropriation already made by Congress
"will admit of such allowance."
$500.00
Sam'l F.B: Morse
Superintendent of
Electro Magnetic
Telegraphs

+ + + The lead pipe will all be in Ball, and I
should be glad to have you dispose of it to the best ad-
vantage, done will be damaged and maybe sold for lead,
but the greater part if carefully handled & reeled will be
good as pipe and ought to sell from 5 7/4 to 6 cents per lb
at least. Please have it weighed, and stored carefully
for me, I should be glad to have it sold with a little
delay as is consistent with the advantageous disposal
of it. + + +
Washington Apr. 18th 1844
In host
Very respectfully
Yr stser
S B M
Copy. extract from letter to
D. Barbaugh Esq.
respecting sale of pipe

Professor
Baltimore June 8th 1845
Dear Sir
The Hoodside handed me the enclosed
note from the board, by which it seems they are unwilling
to make any deduction from their two bills which Germain is
to pay till I have consulted you – I believe you have them,
and shall attend to any direction you may be pleased
to give – This is one good consideration, I think this is the only amount
outstanding that is due the Corporation at this end of
the concern. So far as I know
Yours respectfully & truly
David Busbank.

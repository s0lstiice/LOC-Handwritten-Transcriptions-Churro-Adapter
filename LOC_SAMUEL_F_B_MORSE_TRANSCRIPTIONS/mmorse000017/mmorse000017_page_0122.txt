Mr. Cornell, you may proceed to have the Posts set out as far
as Baltimore, 200 feet apart except in points where in your judgment
you may think it best to have that distance increased.
Put in as much fence this purpose as you can employ to advantage
for I wish them all set certainly by the end of this week. You
may procure if you think it necessary two more Augusts for being
the holes in the earth for the Posts.
Respectfully yours
S. P. B. More
Sup't of Elec. Mag. Tel.
Washington March 18th 1844.

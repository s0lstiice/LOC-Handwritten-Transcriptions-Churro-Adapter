can relieve yourself why we shall both be satisfied. I send you therefore
to and the amount due me on acct of board & in a draft directed to me at this
place by return of mail. I have waited I think sufficiently long to enable you
to repay it without inconvenience. I am in absolute want of the money.
I remain very respectfully yours
Colt Secrt
James Fisher Jr. D. D.
De F. B. Moree

Phil a June 1st 1844-
Mr & T Bellare
Dear Sir
We have received
Mr F.O. Smith, a note, requesting us to send one copy,
of lead pipe contract, containing your original
guaranty for his performance, stating that he
desired to exhibit it to a com of Senate.
We replied, sending him a copy of the same,
and of the other Endorsements. We also took
occasion to say "We do not desire to become parties
on either side to the Controversy now unfortunately
existing between yourself and Mr. House, and desire
to avoid even the appearance of it, and
we would suggest that if our evidence is
reliably to the maintenance of your right, you
should have it in public legal form and
manner"
In reply last Mr Smith stated desired
the writer to make voluntary affidavit to certain
interrogatories which he proposed - These questions
to answer, but not under oath

Your brothers well the last and I from them. Rich-
ard is engaging with order in the translation
of late German Christine Geo B of Mt
Bos. Stuart speaks highly of requesting him
to engage in it. It promises to be both very
useful -- it also profitable -- what is in part it
is an object to interest Rich B attention.
The Trustees decided unanimously
to continue Dr Burdock in Office -- I have assigned
him his specific duties -- all is now quiet. Those
who had long since calculated on a different course are
mortified & disappointed. -- I consider the whole
as a pretty high handed affair -- The development
which was boldly made in the 18 18 B d will do good -- I
hope, stop a course of ruinous Policy of
grateful management too long practiced.
Lunetta will write you soon, I to hear
I leave it to inform you of what I omit -- dear
little Susan gives more interesting daily &
contributes her full share to the comfort & enjoyment of
the family -- we all unite in affectionate
love to you & due remembrance to Henry --
Your ever affectionate
Father Morse

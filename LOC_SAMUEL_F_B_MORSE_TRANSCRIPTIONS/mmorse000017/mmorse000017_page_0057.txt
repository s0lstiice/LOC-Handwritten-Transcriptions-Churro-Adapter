Dear Sir.
Your letter of the 6th inst has
been received, & but for my absence from this city would
have been earlier answered. Prof Fisher was an inmate
of our family for about ten months, but as a guest only.
We made no difference in our manner of living, & he was
most welcome to visit us. If any offense was incurred by
me on account of his visit, please let it be the offering of my
pittance to that Science which we both love.
Wishing you all success in your present labors.
I remain your attached friend
Charles Dick.
My Kindest remembrance to Mr Ellsworth's family.

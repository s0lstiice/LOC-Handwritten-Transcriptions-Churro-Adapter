Taffyson House Baltimore May 4. 1844
Dear Sir - I have delayed writing for a few days,
in the hope that I might find an opportunity to use the
same and as soon as I could not do any better
with it than as bad - I have offered to him where
able, if there was anything to be made on it, in dispute
shape over the price of land they would buy it - and
I find that land commands as high a price here as in any
other place - Mr. Avery laid some of it down for the
purchase of knowing if it would answer for water:- yesterday
he told me he finds it will not answer for it -
After making up about 10,000 of the first sale - 2 1/8th
I think I shall be able to get 3d for all the remainder
knowing your anxiety I desire to turn every thing to the
best possible account - I have him very particular in
regard to the sale - and as time is considerable
of an amount now ready to be taken away, perhaps
I may as well be disposing of it - I have made a
sketch of an offer that you may know how we stand - the
fight of the two last loads has not yet come in which will
probably be about the same as the other - 32 1/2 barrels
of Alphaltein got to come probably about 13d - One
barrel of Algeafatin was forwarded to you on the 30
of April, which hope you have received - I shall continue to pay the rent on the House in Whitney Street

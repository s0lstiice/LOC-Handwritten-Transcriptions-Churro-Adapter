Baltimore April 8th 1848
Professor Moser
Dear Sir
In addition to the interest which Mr Smith now in the
Telegraph in the United States, I know that he would be unwilling to exchange it for that abroad. But
if Professor Moser could persuade him to exchange his interest in the United States, which is one
quarter, he would receive such a proposition from me with a favorable consideration,
or if Smith made a distinct offer of ten thousand dollars in cash, and as much
money when Professor Moser shall have reported that he has made a successful experi-
ment between Washington & Baltimore, as far as to render the probability of
it no longer doubtful, he will relinquish to me all claim to the United States
with an addition that he be represented from all existing agreements between him and other proprietors; and if such an arrangement be effected,
I could not likely for myself, or by the opinion of a friend, obtain of
him his part of the Telegraph in France, being so to say 3/8th -- that he
would then be out of the concern entirely. Mr Smith
does not mention to me his offer with any idea of his opinion as to the
value or success of the invention, but I infer that he feels it would be a fatal
relation that to him have become incompatible. -- While on the other hand,
the time has been when the concern would have in done him to withdraw,
I have 15 days from 2 of April to accept his proposition, all of which I should
have a reasonable time, if that were not enough. -- Now I do not think I
can gain a company here to take it up -- but I thought I might as well let you
know that Dean has Mr Smith put, as you may have found it out
you would like it or not it, I think could all together this -- and if so
please let me know -- I have the modest done -- and I should
glad would be join abroad in it. I think him on my own express
liberty I am authorized from Mr Smith -- among other things myself and
know that you will not use it to my advantage --
Yours respectfully
David Bunker

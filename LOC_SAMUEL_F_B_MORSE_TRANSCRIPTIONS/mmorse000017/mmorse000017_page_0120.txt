[1804]
Baltimore March 16th 1804
Dear Sir - I have looked over my books, & the title
from I franklin to me, & of which I am your
copy - and find thats the charge of the Central
City pipe are made, the 25 Nov - & that by
my book, they were taken from the City
of place in the City on the 23d, the day following
as that is the time I made the charge
& you Tax you may probably find, by referring
to my bill - being a charge of 6 loads -150c
+ paid 3 Labourers 1.69c - I am satisfied
that is the time, as I am usually particular in
making such charge & on examination at
the time of being done. there were also
other articles added at the same time, as you
will notice by the bill - such as the 47 Pounds of
horns truly - in haste
David Burbank
I will send the Asphaltum by Monday,
without further addressing you

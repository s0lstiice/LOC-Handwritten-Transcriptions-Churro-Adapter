Dear Sir: I have the honor to
submit to you the report of the
Union Army in the Valley of the
Mississippi, and the report of the
Confederate Army in the Valley of
the Ohio River, which I have
previously sent to you.
The Union Army in the Valley of
the Mississippi has been in
active engagement with the
Confederate Army in the Valley of
the Ohio River since the 1st of
June, and has been engaged in
continual conflict with the
Confederate Army in the Valley of
the Ohio River since the 17th of
August, 1862.

Post for Mom
Baltimore March 9th 1844
Dear Sir. Your favor of yesterday is received, and in reply to the inquiry - Asphaltum, in any quantity, is to be had in this market, and that the price for Board is at 7 cents the pound - although I know, as they say that the Rail Road Company have actually paid more than that price for it. Skirting Coppen thin, - 25 cents, Stron
zan Coppen is 27 cents the pound -
In case any articles from Kentucky arrive for you, I will attend to them as you desire - Receiving your kind favor in relation to the inquiry at the Patent Office, and an very much obliged to you for it - although I don't know but I may have occasion to visit Washington pretty soon on such an account - I presume your proposals for Post was taken by some one.
Your very respectfully
David Bushawk.

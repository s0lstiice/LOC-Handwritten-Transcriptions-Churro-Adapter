My dear Sir Baltimore April 16th 1844,
Goods of yesterday is received, and with
it was in my power to wake up some of our people here
to what I really think would be benefit them; but they came
different from many other business men, and only use
when it is too late, & behind the age, I presume without
out my accomplishing the sale of all, I can make no arrangement with Mr S for a part, - How would it
answer for you to write some of your friends in New York, stating
as you may deem practicable that there may be an opportunity to obtain through one a seat of Mr S - claim in
the State - I wish it was so that you could have 2 or 3 days,
I have forwarded your letter to Bragman, it
will probably reach there in a week or 10 days, the chance for
sending thither from him is good - When you have such occasion you must command me here & I will attend to it.
I had very much some information, that a
Mr Knowles, a clerk, or rather the person who conducts
visitors about the room containing models of Machinery,
in the Patent office - bring on the first floor to the left as we
enter the building from the street - can impart - I had
a machine made for splitting & shaving out hoops from
hoop poles - and Mr H in conversation told me how it could
be made - also said there was a model in the Office of one
that would answer, and gave me the name, (name
that I have written this letter (paying my postage) but have
not yet sent to you)

Mr Cornell Washington April 4th 1864
Dear Sir,
I have been disappointed in seeing the
Secretary until to day ever since I received your
letter of the 27 March containing the extract from Mr.
H. O. J. Smiths letter, and the proposition from him in
relation to his claim before the Senate
In conversation with the Secretary he said
that he is willing to refer all the papers relating to
that subject to the two gentlemen named who shall
examine the case and report to him and he will
transmit to Congress their opinion as that of dis-
interested and impartial persons, and if they shall
not agree they shall select a third person.
Respectfully
Y: O: B: S: U.S.
Sam. P. B. Mors,

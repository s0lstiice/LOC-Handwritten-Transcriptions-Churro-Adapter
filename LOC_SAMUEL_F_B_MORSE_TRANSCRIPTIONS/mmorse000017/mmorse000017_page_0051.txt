[Feb. 23, 1844]
Dr. Mrs. Ellsworth,
I have felt some delicacy in asking you to
penny any return for the expenses, caused by my residence in your hospitable house. I have once or twice spoken of
it to Mr. Ellsworth, but he has referred it to you. Enclose a
sum which I beg you to accept for that purpose. If too much in your
estimation please be my almoner of the surplus to such deserving poor
as you may designate. If too little do not fail frankly to tell me so.
With sincere and affectionate regard
for you and yours
Your friend & Servt
S. J. B. Mason

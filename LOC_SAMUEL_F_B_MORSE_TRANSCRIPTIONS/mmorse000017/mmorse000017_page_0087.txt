Dear Sir
I never since since given to understand that
there was a drawing Department belonging or attached to the
University and that you had resigned the Professorship
and that nothing more was doing there; or that present of consideration
but a copy of the list of Professor Students published a few
days since was put into my hands, by which I dismissed my
mistake as regards your resignation, yet as there was no
drawing School in operation I visited on Dr. Darling Langson
the Chancellor who though confined to his Chamber by a
sprained or broken Arm, gave me away agreeable audience
he informed me there were no such Department in operation
at present that some of the young gentlemen occasionally took
lessons of some artist residing in the Building, in short as he
could not act on the subject, he recommended me to write
to you, for it belonged to your Department and that every
arrangement you might suggest would meet with attention.
I then waited on Mr. Dunner related my difficulties with the
Chancellor he strongly urged me to write you immediately
would write himself that deemed it necessary as you know
me well and would do all for me that was possible & proper

an elementary School, for me to experiment in it though I
was aware of the kind intentions of Mr. Homan Cummings
& Chapman &c it was found they had no room requisite to

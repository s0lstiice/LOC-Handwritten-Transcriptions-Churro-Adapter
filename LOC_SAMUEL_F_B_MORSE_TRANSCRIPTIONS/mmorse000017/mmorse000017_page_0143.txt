I may be of some service to you.
The Station at Baltimore would suit
me better than any other, inasmuch as
my family reside in this City.
If you should find it necessary to con-
nect the optical telegraph at any time
with your system, my invention and
knowledge may be of service.
I observe that your appropriation is to
be expended under the direction of the
Post Master General, and if he has the
organization of the telegraphic Corps, my
connection as Agent for the Great Southern
Mail Line will give me sufficient influence
with him through your nomination
to get an appointment.
If the foregoing meets your approbation
I should be pleased to hear from you.
I am very respectfully
Your faithful Servt
Henry J. Rogers
Baltimore

Voted, That the Sec'y be requested & authorized to acquaint
the Secretaries of the "Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge"
& of the "Religious Tract Society," with the existence & object of our
Society, by sending to each a copy of our Constitution; and
that he request them severally to inform us, whether our
Society, being instituted for a similar charitable purpose,
may be supplied with Tracts, on the same terms, similar
at the same prices for purchase, at which Subscriptions
to their respective Societies are supplied; and that he
may request each Sec'y to send one copy of each
of the books & tracts, distributed or sold by
his Society, marking on it the price for purchase, at
which our Society may be supplied with
the same; all said specimens to be paid for
by our Society, & to be for the sole use & benefit
thereof.
(Approved)
A Holmes Clerk
Nov. 4. 1806.

anxious to have you keep an account of your expenses during your
stay in Newhaven that at the end of the 4 years that you may
be able to say my whole expenses amount to so much and you will
find it very easy to do it if you will only attend to it every morning
to set down the expenses of the day when anyone arrives and
every Saturday cast it up and see how much it comes to and at
the end of every term see what the expense of the term comes to
for board tuition washing mendings shoes boots &c and how you
spend your pocket money and look up your account book and
dont look it or leave it about for other boys to see its content.
had a young man that was educated at N' Haven this week
he said the school of his expenses did not cost his father 500

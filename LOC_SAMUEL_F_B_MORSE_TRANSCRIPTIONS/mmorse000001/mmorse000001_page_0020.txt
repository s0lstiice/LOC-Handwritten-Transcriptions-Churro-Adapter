Andover Dec 29th 1804.
Dear Grandmama
I now take this opportunity of writing to you to
inform you by Professor Parsons that I am well
and have been all this term and hope I shall continue
through the term. I am extremely sorry to hear that
Nancy's Mother is dead, but we knew all must die, may
God grant that I may be prepared in my turn
that when Death may come I may die willingly.
May I treasure up the precepts of my Parents in my
heart practice them I be happy. There was a great
snow storm here last Wednesday so the trading did
not keep.
Please to give my love to all the Family.
I remain your affectionate
Grandson. Edwin Morse.
Andover Dec 30th 1804.
Dear Mama
I now embrace this opportunity to write to you to
inform you by Professor Pearson that I received Papa's
letter dated Dec 28th which informed me that I must
send Finley's books by the 1st opportunity which
I shall endeavour to do.
I thought this way of writing letters good as any because it took less trouble
& less paper. I received a letter from Finley which
informed me of the fire at New York & at Ports
month. Give my love to all the Family, and
I shall remain your
affectionate Son Edwin Morse
Andover Dec 30th 1804.
Dear Aunt,
I now take this opportunity of addressing you because you asked me in the
vacation, I should have wrote you before but I had no opportunity so you must excuse me.
You may tell Mama I have not received the
show Mr Phelps says he did not bring them.
I have got to the 12th Eneid in Gracia Minora
& to the 12th Enneid in Virgil.
Mr Newman put me into Mason to-day.
Give my love to all the Family and remain your affectionate
Nephew Edwin Morse.

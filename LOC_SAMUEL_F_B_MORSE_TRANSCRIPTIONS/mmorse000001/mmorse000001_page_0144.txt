Next spring I hope you will come on & fix
us; I long to get into the College for it
as to me now that I was not a member
of College, but sitting for college I hope next
spring will soon comes.
My whole journey from Charlestown here
cost me 2th., 18d., 24d. a great deal more than
either you or I had calculated on, I am sorry to
be so much trouble to you & the cause of so much
anxiety in you & especially in Mama, I wish
you to give my very affectionate love to my dear
brothers, & tell them they must write me I am
homesick but consider that I am farther
from home than they are 136 miles from home.
Your ever affectionate Son S. P. B. Morse.

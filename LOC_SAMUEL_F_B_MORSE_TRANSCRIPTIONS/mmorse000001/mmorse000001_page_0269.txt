I intend leaving the Springs at first
on Thursday evening next, or early Wednesday morning to
reach Baltimore on Thursday evening -- but I
write you ago before that time.
Since my arrival I have drank freely
of the waters & this morning have taken the warm
bath I meant to day to rest. I have retired to my
private room & in other respects pretty well accommodated
Dr Bradford from Albany is now at home, he will
leave the Springs tomorrow Mr Birmingham is here
from Boston with his daughter. Dr Nott will be here

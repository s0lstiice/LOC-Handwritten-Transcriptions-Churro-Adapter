Charles town May 19. 1866
By Dr Holmes. I write you a
short time since I wrote you
a line by McGuiffin Child, with Hope you
are well. My health is as good, & think better
than usual at this season. Mrs Morse &
the children, who are all at home, are very well,
as is Miss Breese (who is with us respects
to make our house her future home), & Nancy
is spared. – Things are quiet in the Parish
& town, as respects myself – there is a
very pleasing & increasing attention to
serious things, among Christians young
people – the former seem to be awake &
lively, & fond of meeting for religious
conference – the latter are thoughtful,
& disposed to instruction. I find they
seem unwilling to have me discontinue

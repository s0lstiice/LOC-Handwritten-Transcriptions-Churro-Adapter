1805.
Charlestown Sept 3d
Dear Grandpapa,
I received your letter
dated the 22d of May. I have got through
the Junior Class in my Academic Studies,
and it will take me but one year more to
fit for College. Finley is trying to enter New
Haven College, this year.
You made one Mistake
in your last letter, that was in Richard's age
he will be 11 next June 18th day, & Thomas
Rufsel if he had lived would have been 8
next May.
Papa is now going a journey &
we are going to Andover, & the house will
be left by all except Nancy.

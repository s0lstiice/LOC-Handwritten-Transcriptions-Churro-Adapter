It is understood by the subscribers, that Caleb
Bingham is to take the charge of vending the
Paraphlist for twelve dollars per month, Dr. Morse
finding paper and twine for all the the pack-
ages. Mr Bingham is to receive all the money
sent from abroad, and to be at the trouble &
expense of collecting all the money from the
subscribers in Boston; but is not to be accountable
for any bad debts. The money is to be paid
over to the treasurer. As Mr. Bingham is unacquainted
with the business, he does not consider himself
bound for more than one year.
Feb 15. 1807

Leahy Morse

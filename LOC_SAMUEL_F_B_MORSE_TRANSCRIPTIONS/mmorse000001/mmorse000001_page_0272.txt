Ballstown Spring Aug 5 1806
Lebbatt Loents
My dear wife, Our Miss Hazard arrived here
last evening to our great joy They are lodged
in a good house near us. We intend leaving this
place on Tuesday afternoon, & go on from
there to-day, on Wednesday morning, to White-
town. We are all in good health & spirits –
Mr Bartlett & Eliza have a room to themselves – Mr Hamilton, of Boston a young
merchant who came on with them, is their
gallant & very agreeable & attentive I think
I am befriended by the waters – To-morrow
We think of going to Saratoga, to try the
more powerful waters of that place –
I preached this morning 3 miles from this
for Mr Bradley a spirit of wine, I returned
& preached a 2d sermon in the house
where I lodge, to about 300 people, collected from all parts of the world.
On Friday evening we were entertained
with the eloquence of a Miss Hamilton
of whom I will tell you more, when I see

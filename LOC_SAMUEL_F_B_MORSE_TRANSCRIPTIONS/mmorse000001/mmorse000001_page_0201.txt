Nancy is making shirts for you
and desires her love to you; she is very well and
desires to see you very much, and says when
she grows rich and has time to spare, she
will come to New-Haven and see you; she says
she thinks very much about you.
Will you write me how much
you study? what are your hours for study? how
far you have advanced in them? and whether you
cannot keep up with your class by any means
whatever?
Sam Ward has got exceedingly far
in his studies; he studies very hard indeed he
and his chum come down from Cambridge
almost every evening.
In haste Your beloved Brother
Edw Cds Morse.

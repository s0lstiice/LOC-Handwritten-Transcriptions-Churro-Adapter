New Haven Jan 20 1806
My Dear Parents,
I have just the moment received
your letter dated Jan 15, in which I
find you approve of my desire of enter-
ing College. I can get up with my
class this vacation which ends on the 29 of
this month; I wish that you would come
on for I think that you could settle me
in College better; I believe I will let
Mr Stedman who is in town see your
letter & get me into College, I have
no doubt but he will satisfy me. I
am in great haste to study, therefore remem-
ber to my brothers & Nancy & excuse
my brevity & bad writing I must
now bid you Adieu. yours to
S. F. B. Morse.

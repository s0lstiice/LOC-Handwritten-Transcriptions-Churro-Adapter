New Haven Dec. 29 1805
Dear Parents
I rec'd a letter from you dated
Dec. 24 in whch you mention that I do
not write you often enough I have writ-
ten a great many letters all by post; but the
Mail is very irregular & I suppose that some
of them have been lost. You mention
also that I do not answer your questions
concerning my clothes & my other things
In my last letter in answer to Mama's
letter in whch there were a great many
questions all whch I answered in particular
as well as I could. I rec'd the 5 dolls. some
time ago by Erskine Hazard whch I had to spend
pretty soon after it got here for the freight of
my trunk, desk, &c, & truck line to carry it
from the wharf to Mr. Davis' & many other
small expenses, I have got a little money
of Mr. Howe & Beers for necessaries I have
lost my account of expenses out of my
Pocket I shall be more careful for the

APRIL
27 Thursday
Philadelphia to Rev Dr Dana Newbury, post 4 o'Clock
Charleston May 4 May 1805 by Dear Sir Home time
since sent you proposals for publishing the Pamphlet
with a few lines annexed, intimating a refusal expect-
ation that it would receive the aid of your influence
and particularly of your pen. My engagement at
the time could not allow me to be particular.
And I had hoped to have had a personal interview
with you on the subject. As this cannot
take place till the last of this month, I have
thought fit to write you more particularly.
Write, if possible to you, that you would
consider yourself as one of the Editors of this Work
the names of none of them have been published.
Some of them will wish to be concealed. Others may
not. The arrangements respecting Elections will
not be completed till the State Election when
it will be known, who will consent to be of the
number, among gentlemen at a distance. It remains
to have your cord action, Mr. Frisbie, Dr.
Peacock, Mr. Holmes, Dr. French, Dr. Farrar, Mr.
Tearas, Dr. Channing, Mr. Emerson, Mr. Beverley,
Mr. Bates and yourself among the number of
Editors, at least patrons and contributors to the
work. Trust you to confine with Dr. Potts his
other subject. I should wish to have him concerned
also, if possible. I have not time to write
him. You will see by the Palladium
yesterday that the first No. is not to come out
until the 1st July. Perpetual Success and Independence
are very favorable. I shall wish to let you fully into the
design of the Election and doubt not will meet with your complete
agreement. I wish you to prefer something for the
first No. Execute haste. With affectionate esteem
Sam Dana
96215 Your Servt
Tho. P. Mason

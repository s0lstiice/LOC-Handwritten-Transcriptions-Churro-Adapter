Dear Brother
I now write you a few lines by Mr
Nichols to inform you it is vacation with us. We are all well
except Papa who is a little unwell. I have got to the 41 verse
in the 4th Chapter of John which is very hard to me
I can assure you I write pretty poorly but as you care
only for the reading I presume you will not mind
it as you are not of that sort. We have a football which is
not out of use. The state Prison is nearly finished
I wish you all to your station not to to dislike to
be out of college it is no more than you wished when
at Annover I must in haste bid you
Your affectionate
Samt F B Morse
Adieu.
Brother.
R. Morse.
Charleston
Andover November 25th 1805

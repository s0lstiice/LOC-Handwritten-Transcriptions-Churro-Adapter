Thompson Oct. 19 1807
My Dear Brother,
We arrived here safely about
two hours ago, & feel pretty much fatigue
& therefore you cannot expect but a few
lines, Did you give that letter to John
Bartlett? I hope you feel better than you
did when we departed, at first we
shall see you again probably next
May which will soon pass away; Hope
you will be well fitted by next year
to come on with us & join your class
I feel very tired so you must accept
what I have written now & excuse my
not writing more fully. You shall hear
from me at New Heaven.
Till then Adieu
Your affectionate
Brother
Master R. C. Morse & S. F. B. Morse

the
16th of February 1865
Washington D C.
The President of the United States
to the Secretary of War
My dear Secretary of War:
It is a source of deep pleasure to me to
inform you that the President of the United
States has appointed you to be a member of
the Committee of Military Affairs. I have
always been anxious to give you the
opportunity of serving in this capacity
and I am confident that you will
render the highest satisfaction to the
President and the Government of the
United States by your services.
I am, Sir,
Very respectfully
Yours,
Abraham Lincoln
President
United States of America

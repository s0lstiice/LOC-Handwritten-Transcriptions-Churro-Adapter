Our College affairs remain in an anxious state I hope Dr. Pearson will yet be elected. If so the College may yet be preserved otherwise it must be given up.
I saw Rev. Mr Baker of Greenville at Hartford He told me that in that town were a No. of subscribers for the Pamphlet he thought probably a dozen or two but none had yet. Their No’s indeed the paper had not been returned. He professed himself very friendly to the work Mr Hyde writes very favorably of its subscribers are increasing in various directions. It is very important that the work should be supported. I wish you to bring as many of the Ministers as possible from your quarter to the next election Mr Shepard I see is to preach the sermon something good may be done at least a good man may be chosen to speak the Convention sermon
With my kindest regards to my dear Mrs. Lyman Milhous Your affectionate friend
Brother
Wm. H. Lyman

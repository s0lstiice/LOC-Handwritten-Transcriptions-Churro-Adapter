I wish you, in my behalf to interest
Mr Hyde in the Panoptist, in the manner
I mentioned in my last. If the Berkshire
Loyalty w'd take 50 or 100 & supply sub
scribers in that country, the same as in
yours, it w'd be a good thing they might
with little pains procure a number
of Subscribers I have received already
from two towns about 12- The books
shall be del'd at Southampton at 9c.
per of expence- They may have
subscribers in neighbouring towns
in Vermont, New York & Connecticut
& the profits arising from subscriptions
You may supply all who may wish
to receive their books at N.Y.C. without
regard to lines-
Mr M. & I take of visiting you in
the fall on our return from New Haven
we wish it- We join in affectionate
regards to your dear L. with your
affectionate J. Morse

My dear friend
I returned last evening from
Haven I found your letter of the 10th
in. At the time I wrote you (except the 245
sheets in the press) for delivery to your order
Mr W Butler made no application to me for
them I know not that he had been in Boston
Mr Patridge called & Mrs M had the books
prepared for him I expected him to take them
but was disappointed in his not calling
for them they are in box ready for delivery
And I shall as soon as possible
propose the remaining 245 sheets from Cambridge
as I hope they have been printed
before this If I can find another
press I will send them but as the printing is
going off I know not whether a good offer
will soon offer
I thank you for your frankness in telling me every thing about the Pamphlet
I never expected it would please
you to know that I am in the
most anxious expectation of
your reply
Ever affy yours
Wm H. C. Yorke
96306

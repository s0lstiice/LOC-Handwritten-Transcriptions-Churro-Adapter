Yale College New Haven Oct. 25, 1867.
My Dear Mother,
We arrived here safely last Wednesday, even
we are well and contented, and do not feel homesick in
the least; Our things have not yet arrived from Boston,
we expect them soon. Papa went on board the packet
for N. York. last Friday even. - & as he had a fair
wind I expect he has arrived there in this; he bought
for us before he went- 2 leafblairs, 3 common dittos
1 pair andirons, 1 lookingglass, 1 Washboard, an ax, ham-
mer, skillet, wood, &c. - I will write you more particu-
larly concerning things in my next, as I have
but very little time. Edward has got a room
very handy for him to come to mine when necessar-
ly he and his roommate (Mitchell) will sleep in my
room so there will be 4 in a room at night. Edward & I make out to sleep upon the floor till
our things come from the vessel. I took a letter
out of the office for Papa from Richard, & as he
(Papa) was gone to N. York I opened the letter and will give
it to Papa when he comes here again
My next shall be longer.
Your affectionate Son
S.F.B. Morse

Dear Sir
Ever since I received your letter of June 1st, I have been in the hands of your worthy parents, a large and rich choice in
which they inform me, I am indebted to your polite & attentive generosity. This mark of
your attention is received by me with myself with peculiar pleasure, I pray
you to accept our sincere thanks for it. It will ever add to our happiness to
hear of your welfare, & to contribute in promoting it. You will consider us
as among the number of those friends, who will be gratified by
your safe return to your native country.
I am with extreme esteem & affection, &c. your obd. to me
J. Moser
June 1st 1806

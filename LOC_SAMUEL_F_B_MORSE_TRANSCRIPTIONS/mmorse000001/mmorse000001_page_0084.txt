Charleston July 5. 1803
My dear friend
Spirit of the 26th will I trust this evening, I thank you for it
We hope you will answer favorably in respect to your election to our Society
all will however be made quite agreeable to your wishes - You need not
decide till you come down next Spring to Election unless you wish it.
This morning I sent by Mr Thompson, of Newburyport, our friends
a bundle of 100 of the first 10 of the Pamphlet directed to Mr
Williams of Northampton - You will find them there, & you will dispose of them in the best manner you
wisdom can devise - They were sent in such hurry as not to be
accompanied with a letter - It was intended a letter should follow
them immediately - I thought it best to send a sufficient no

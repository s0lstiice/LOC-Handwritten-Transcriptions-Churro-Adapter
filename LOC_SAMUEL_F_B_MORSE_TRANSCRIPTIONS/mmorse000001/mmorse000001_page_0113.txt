the great uncertainty of life and of course the importance of be-
ing always prepared for death even a sudden death as we have
not what an hour may bring forth. This we are sensible of we cannot
be too soon or too well prepared for that all important moment as
this is what we are sent into this world for, the main business
of life is to prepare for death let us not then put of these most
important concerns to an uncertain tomorrow but let us in
certain attend to the concerns of our precious saving dying
souls While we feel ourselves alive "that whether we live or
may live to the Lord or whether we die we may die to the Lord
that living or dying we may be the Lord" that this may be the
happy situation of each of us is the sincere prayer of your
own affectionate parents. I had a letter from your good
Grandmama Breen yesterday from New York she was on
her way to Whitestown with your Uncle & Aunt
Madison she desired her love to you all Your Aunt Abby
is gone to Philadelphia to spend the fall & winter with Uncle
& Aunt Hazard they do not expect ever to return to
Kreussburg again which I am very glad to hear Nancy
Shepard is gone to Amherst to see her Sister Beecky Cushing
She will be home I hope by Vacation which will soon be here
I will leave this letter for Papa to finish and shall bid you
good bye for the present Your Affectionate mother
E A M

Charleston Nov. 13. 1805
Dear Son,
though your Mama has
written you a long letter by your
cousin Enkine Hazard who goes
on in the stage tomorrow, I im-
prove an opp. by Mr Webster
& write you a hasty line -- We
are now anxious to hear from
you I have been looking daily
for a letter from you by post --
We have had but one, which
was written the day after your
arrival -- We wish to know
how you are pleased with your
situation -- what progress you
make in your studies, & every
particular concerning you

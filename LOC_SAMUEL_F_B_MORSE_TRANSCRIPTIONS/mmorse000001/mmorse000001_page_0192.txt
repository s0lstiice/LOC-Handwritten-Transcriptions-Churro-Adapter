Charleston Dec 25. 1805.
I have delayed answering your good Letter
till now, partly on account of my ill health & partly that
I might, before I wrote, witness the crisis, which has now
happened. The Presidency is filled by the Corporation,
and no doubt will be sanctioned by the Overseers.
The Hon Fisher Ames is the man. The Unitarians
(for so shall I distinguish the revolutionaries of the
College though I do not attach them this title) have made
a wise choice for their purposes. If he accepts with re-
men's doubtful, I shall consider the revolution run in
& the seat of the College, whose motto is "Christo et
Ecclesia" had better be broken and a more appro-
priate one substituted. This event will strength

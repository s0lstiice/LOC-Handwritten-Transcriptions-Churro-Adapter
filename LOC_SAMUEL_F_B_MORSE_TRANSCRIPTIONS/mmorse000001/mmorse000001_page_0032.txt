I think with you, that some things at Washington
look encouraging, & that there is sufficient prospect
of success & to animate our most vigorous exertion.
In regard to Convention affairs -- Mr Holmes
& Dr. Joyd & myself have met & agreed that the Convention
assembles at my house, on the Friday before Election
then they will determine when to hold their
Session this was Dr. Joy's motion -- I hope they will
conclude to sit in this town & expect they will
Dr. Joy was deliberate about the defence I trouble
of it to me but this I shall remove I think
it will be by all means expedient & convene as
many as possible of our brethren
who are right, at this Meeting of Convention --
I that we be prepared with a plan which will
act the feelings of all the orthodox provi-
a conciliation plan which I hope they
will agree to, shall be rejected --
I wish there may be a majority to adopt
such a plan -- that those who may reject
it may be the decedents -- You must be armed
in Panoply complete on the subject -- for you
will be placed in the front of the hettest
battle -- I wish a short pamphlet could be
written & published, will should inform the
people of the real object of the Convention
If you will commit your thoughts to paper on
the subject & transmit them to me, soon I
will if necessary add to them, & see them pub-
lished -- I think he sent a pamphlet judiciously
written w/ do great good -- It is Saturday after-
noon -- I have a sermon to write for tomorrow
I cannot therefore add only our kind regards
to Mrs. L. L. your son -- Yours Truly
Jas. G. Moser

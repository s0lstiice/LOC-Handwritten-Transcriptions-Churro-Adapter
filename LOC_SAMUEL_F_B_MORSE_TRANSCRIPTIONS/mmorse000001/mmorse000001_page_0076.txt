Charles Town June 28th 1805
My dear boys, Your letter of June 10th 1805, which
we lately received gave us great pleasure they
were the first we had received since you left us
We were sorry to hear that Edward was poisoned
He must be careful not to go in the way of
poisonous things it injures the blood We are glad to find you were contented that
none of you were on any of the bills I hope to find this to be the case at the close
of the term How gratifying it will be to hear the names of my three boys
called out among those against whose names no black mark appeared I was
in general pleased with the composition of your letters & with the hand writing
I wish you, however to take great pains with your letters I have them always
well written correctly spelt & punctuated the capital letters properly used as
much variety as possible In this last respect I think you have much
improved I wish Edward to inform

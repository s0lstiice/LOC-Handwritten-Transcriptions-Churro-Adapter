New Haven Oct 22 1805.
My Dear Parents
I arrived here yesterday safe & well
the first day I rode as far as Williams tavern &
put up there for the night the next day I rode
as far as Dwight Tavern in Wester & in the
morning it being rainy Mr Backus did not
set out to ride till late & the stage coming
to the door Mr B. told me it was a good opportunity
to send me to Hartford wh he did & I arrived
at Hartford that night & lodged at Applejits
opposite the State House he treated me very
kindly indeed wholly on account of my being
your son I was treated more like his own son
than a stranger for whch I shall always be
very much obliged to him; the next morning
I hired a house & chaise of him to carry me to North
field & arrived at Mr Marshes who was very
glad to see me & begd me to stay till S. Barr
will went whch was the next Monday for his
mother would not let him go so soon she was so
angry with Nancy (which I like to have
prayed for some time) with my words that she
will get me a son & I will be very happy
to have one for my father & mother
whch I will love & honor
96260

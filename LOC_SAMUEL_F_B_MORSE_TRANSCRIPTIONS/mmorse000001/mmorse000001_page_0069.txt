Mr. Lathrop's parents have come on
from Norwich, I will take her home
on Friday, so we shall be quite alone.
Anne was married last Saturday
evening - a very good girl, Rev-
inda Brown, has come in her place.
She is all well. Mama always
send you much love. The think of
you every day, & many times in a
day - Your good behaviour will
make us happy - This is all we ask
of you in return for the expence
we are at for your education - Don't
deny us that.
It is now bed time - Give
our love to cousin Hazard - remem-
ber us also, to Mr & Mrs Blanchard.
Your affectionate
son J. Moree

Your friend & I -- I say is once when I
offered him the paper to subscribe, he almost
said, he felt himself insulted & spruced it.
Mr Phillips put his name at the same time
for 25. The papers have informed you of
our political state. It has been cri-
cical in the extreme. But Providence
has mercifully interposed to save us a
little longer.
I gave Mr Austin's pamphlet to a
friend & not knowing it to be yours I know
where to get another, so W. send it
you -- I am ready to do what I can to
increase your Association. I hope you
will have Wisdom to move in a right
direction & to manage so as to attract
all the orthodox ministers & Chrs to
you -- Send us an account of your
proceedings for publication in the
Pamphlet. I wish you to write some-
thing on the subject. Awakening to the Chris-
ties are all well. I am in intimate to write me in
cordial affection to him & to yourself. I am
your friend
J M

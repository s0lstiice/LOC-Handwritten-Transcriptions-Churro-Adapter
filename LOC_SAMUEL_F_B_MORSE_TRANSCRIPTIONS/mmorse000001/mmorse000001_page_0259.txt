Cast Hendrick, & some other Chief to
confer on the subject I will
write you from the Springs the day
you may expect to see me at Lititz
and do that you need not wait for me
write me as soon as you receive
this to the Post Office Ballston,
whether I may expect to meet
you at the Church.
I shall see some of the Trustees
of Hampshire Society as I pass along
& know what they will do I bring
your word.

monies voted you by our Society

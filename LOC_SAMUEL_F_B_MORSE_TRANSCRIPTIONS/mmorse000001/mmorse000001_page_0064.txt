Charleston June 8th 1865
Dear Boys,
I cannot omit writing
you one line by Capt. Phillips
today - I hope you enter on
your studies, at the close of
vacation, with renewed pleasure;
I will determination
to make greater progress than
ever. Your behaviour the
last term, & while at home
gave us much pleasure I
has increased our affection
for you. I hope neither
of you, nor your brother Finley
will be found on either
of the bills at the close of
the term.

Gentlemen
My Son who is a member of Yale College, will hand over
this Letter. He will want some bibliatical books and stationery from time to
time, which I will thank you to let him have and charge them
to my account and transmit your bill quarterly or semiannually.
It would be preferable to me, if he to you, to repay you in my own
books which you will please to order as you may want them.
Should my Son want money for pocket expenses, to the amount
of three or four dollars, a quarter, or longer sum, to pay
his bills for board, we stipulate if you would furnish it to
my order, and permit me to pay the amount to your
order here, I should be obliged.
I shall send by Capt. Collins the first of October barracks,
token at commencement
I am Gentlemen with Sincerity
Your most
(signed) Morris
Charles H. Morris

Dear Sir
You expressed to me a wish the other day, to possess
a set of Ellis Harlands Historical Collections - I modestly request your generous
attention to my eldest son sometime time - As evidence of the grateful
sense I entertained of your kindness, which I had intended
manifested, I may, you to accept the copy of the abovementioned
work which accompanies this - And believe me to be with
much esteem yours
264 Sent
Jillmore
Cheslestown March. 29. 1806
Mr Homskirchs

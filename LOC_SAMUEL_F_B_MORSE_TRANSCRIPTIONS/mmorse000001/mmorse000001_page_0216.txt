You will present my kind regards
& Mr Davis, inform him that I have
read his letter, I am much obliged to
him for it. The family are all very
well -- Your brothers are very good boys,
attentive to their studies, & to their
other duties, I give us a great deal of
comfort -- We all think of you, very
affectionately every day -- By your
good behaviour, fail not, my son,
to give us all comfort in hearing
from -- I shall be contented with
your situation -- leave your fondness
for continual change, it is a bad
habit -- you must strive to break it,
or it will be ruinous to you -- Resolve
to be steady & manly in all your be-
haviour -- You are now in a situation of
an age to put away childish & toyish
things -- We all send you most love --
You have daily remembrance in our prayers -- pray
for me to pray for myself -- Remember your accountability to
God & to nothing to spend time -- Daily, ask his guidance
of your heart -- Read a portion of the Scriptures with
earnestness, forget not this advice -- When family entice you
to consent not -- Your affectionate parent
96298
Yr
Martha

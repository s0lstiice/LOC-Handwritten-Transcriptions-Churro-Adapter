Aug. 14th -- Evenings
We have all been to Saratoga to-day, I am
where we did not return this afternoon -- The
waters of that place are more powerful than those
of this place -- I drank freely of them -- without
much effect, other than to make me sleepy.
Miss Barthlett herself with Mr. Thomson
shall set out in the morning of tomorrow, in
stead of afternoon, as the stage starts at 9 O.C. -- It will
give us a little time at Schenectady.

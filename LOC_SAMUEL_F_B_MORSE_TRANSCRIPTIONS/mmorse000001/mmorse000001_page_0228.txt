Thompson Feb. 10. 1866
Yon? Sis. I left home this morning on my
way to N Haven. I hope to return this
week & keep near Sabbath at Woodstock.
If I do I shall be at Ashford, at Clark's
on Friday about 3 P.M. in the afternoon
I shd be glad to meet brother with a
slight if convenient delay to Woodstock - I must leave it with him to
come at some uncertainty, as I may
possibly be detained longer than I
expect - but the above is my arrange-
ment. I left all well at home, &
am well myself - love to brother
& family - Your affectionate son
J Mosse

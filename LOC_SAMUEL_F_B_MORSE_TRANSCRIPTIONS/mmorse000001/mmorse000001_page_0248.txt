My dear Sir,
I have received yours of the 10th inst with the books, what pay you are due to the Society for promoting & in knowledge, my official letter announcing your admittance in all the certificate of membership, what is given.
In regard to the Pamphlet, I think it will be best to send regularly 200 copies of each No. to Northampton, for the counties of Hampshire & Berkshire & for you & Mr Williams, with others, to dispose of as many as you can & return the any which may remain. Mr Hyde writes that he shall continue his 25 & probably 30 shall soon have a considerable increase of his subscribers. I have proposed that he should have all the subscribers in Berkshire County.

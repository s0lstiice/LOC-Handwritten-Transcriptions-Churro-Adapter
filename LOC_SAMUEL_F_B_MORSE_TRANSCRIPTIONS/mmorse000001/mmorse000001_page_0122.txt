Charlestown Aug. 31. 1805
Dear Sir, I have received your favor of the
27th of imagining Mr Williams meant 100
No 1 + 100 No 2 = 200 in all if not there is
a mistake it must be rectified. We shall
send 25 to Mr Hyde at 92 to St. Hampton
with liberty to take any further No he
may want I calculate (perhaps too
banguninely) that 300 will be disposed
of in (and around) Hampshire & Berkshire
counties in the course of the year by your
Society & Mr Hyde. Subscribers daily increase here & in various distant places
& the work has grown also in reputation
We hope it will not decline Those who
have produced its present reputation will
continue contributors We have received
a valuable communication from the
westward I expect from Mr Hyde
but know not his hand It is acknowledged
as received 4th in the 3d No

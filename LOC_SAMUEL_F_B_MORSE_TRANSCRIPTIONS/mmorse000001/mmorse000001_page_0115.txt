From our Editor
313, 1905
Dear Sirs,
As you are aware I am
the Editor of the
Chicago Tribune
and I am writing
to you because I
have received a
letter from a
man who says he
is a former
Union soldier
and is now a
laborer in
Chicago.
This man says
he is a member of
the Union League
and is a strong
supporter of the
Union cause.
He says he is
very much opposed
to the use of the
Negro in the army
and is a strong
supporter of the
present system of
paying the Negro
soldier.

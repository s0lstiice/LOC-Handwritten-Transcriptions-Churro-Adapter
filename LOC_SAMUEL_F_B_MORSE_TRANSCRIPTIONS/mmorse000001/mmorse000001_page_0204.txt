[1809]
Charles town Decr 27
My Dear Son
We have received your letter of the 18th of Decr and your
father has answered it by post, it seems as if you thought from your
last letter that it was not worth while to answer any letter except they
came by first this is not the thing if you know of no private
opportunity to write us and we know of a great many to write
you we shall always improve the private conveyances as it will
at least save half the expence of the correspondence and if you
attended to becoming as you ought you would find many opportunities
of writing us by private conveyance, but least you should
have for excuse that you do not write because you do not have
of a private chance We have authorized Dyer to write once a fortnight
by post and after all we are here for weeks anxious about you, that we
know not what to do owing to your not complying with our request
there is a matter between us in your two last letters, we have received but
4 letters from you since you left us I will give you the dates of them
and wish you to make a little memorandum of them in your book
that you note things in that you wish to remember. the first letter
we received was Decr 22d the next Nov 5th the next Nov 19th the
last Decr 18th now when you receive this take the book and
set down in it these dates and whenever you write a letter
to us immediately set down the date and who sent by if a private
person or if by post of the same and then you will be able to
see and let us know when you write I wish you to put down also
the dates of the letters you receive from home whenever you get them

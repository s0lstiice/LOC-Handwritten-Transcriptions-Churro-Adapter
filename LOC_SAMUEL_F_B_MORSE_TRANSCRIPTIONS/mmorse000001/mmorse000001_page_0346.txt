I would thank Popk very much to send me one of the

Boston.
Please to give our particular love to Richard
Grandmama & Aunt Abby & cousins W. & M. Rockwood
& Remember likewise to Betrey and all who inquire after us.
Mr. Billiman desires to be particularly remembered
to you & likewise Dr. & Mrs Dwight with Tutor Bright.
We are all well & please more & more with
college, we don't blow up our bell here, as they do at Cambridge,
please to give me the particulars of that adventure & wish
to know how things go on there.
I hope this letter will arrive safe
I am in haste therefore please to excuse all mistakes &
the handwriting.
Your affectionate Son
S. P. B. Morse

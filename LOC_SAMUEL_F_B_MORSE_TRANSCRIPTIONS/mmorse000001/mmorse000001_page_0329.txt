I find it very necessary to have a room in college for
Mr. Pwinning's house is so far from the Colleges that I can scarcely
hear the bell at any time, I cannot at all in the morning,
I either wake 2 hours too soon, or too late, but never in the right
time I have missed prayers three times this term, but was not
noted because it is the first of the term, I must choose me a room
in college when the time comes, which will be in six weeks, I
suppose they government, will let me take my choice now, but not
take possession of it till next term, but I should rather go into it now &
then I should not miss prayers at all.
The college still remains in excellent order & harmony,
no tricks played or any disturbances. I should like to know
how Cambridge college is situated, & whether there is any
likelihood of any of the scholars coming to this college.
My Repertory has not yet been rec'd nor any
letters from home I should to receive either or all. Please excuse all
mistakes, Blots, & every thing that is incorrect as I have
no time to correct this, or to write any more, till now 11 o'clock
on the evening, so Adieu. My respects to Grandmama &
Aunt Abby & love to my Brother, Nancy, & all the family.
I hope Mary is well & will continue so.
Remember me to all who inquire after me in very great
paste, do write me soon.
Your affectionate Son
S. B. Morse
N.B. I am as well as this weather will allow me, I have my
spring combines the hard work.

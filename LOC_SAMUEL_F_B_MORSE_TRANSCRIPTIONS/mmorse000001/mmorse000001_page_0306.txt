My dear Sir,
I have rec'd the 2d No of memories
of President Davies but too late for this No
which is nearly half finished - It is of no great
consequence, however. Probably the whole, if not
too long, may go into the next No. of ready in
season -- We begin the work early in the month
so as not to be hindered.
I have written to old Mr Cooper
for papers relative to his father, with he
will doubtless furnish, as far as he has
them -- I will forward to
you -- It will be quite convenient to
wait for payment till the beginning of
3d quarter & then to receive for 6 months.
Punctual semiannual pay will be agreeable from you.
The Subscribers are constantly increasing -- It will succeed by activity & perseverance --
once on the part of its friends, among which we
reckon yourself. It will be kept on the old Ground -- i.e. on Catholism Ground --

Whitehouse Aug 1st 1861
Saturday morning
My dear wife:
In the hearty line I wrote you from
Utica on my arrival there, I promised I would write
you more deliberately from this place. I improve the license of this morning for
the purpose.
From the necessity to Utica, a day or half a
journey, we had very decent company, the weather warm
on the road, though turnpike, not good. We were much fatigued. At the Hotel, in Utica, we happily found our friends
whose names I before mentioned to receive us. It was
a really a happy meeting -- the circumstance of your absence was the only drawback on our joy. It was a source of much regret to us all that you could not
be here. -- We came up to this place in Mr Snowden's carriage; I found Sister other children, eight
of them glad to see us. Sister is in fair condition as
you are -- the children are fine. I find Mrs. S. looks
as well as I ever saw her -- Mrs. S. them, but in good health -- Mr. A. has been a little indisposed but in
fat & portly. All are very clever -- Yesterday I was
introduced to Mr Caruthers spent several hours with
him -- I am most pleased with him -- he is a modest,
informed man -- his family are in Jersey

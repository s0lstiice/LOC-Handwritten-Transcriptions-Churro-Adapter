I intend writing you more particularly
respecting the state of Religion, &c. shortly,
We know not here what to make of
the fallings &c. in Kentucky with have
attended the remarkable revival of
religion in that quarter. We wait
& know the permanent fruits by
their fruits we must determine—we
dare not say that there is not a
good work progressing in that
part of our country, but it is mixed
& undoubtedly with an uncommon
portion of evil, & great occasion is
given to the adversary to speak
reproachfully.
I pray God preserve your
life, & continue your useful labours

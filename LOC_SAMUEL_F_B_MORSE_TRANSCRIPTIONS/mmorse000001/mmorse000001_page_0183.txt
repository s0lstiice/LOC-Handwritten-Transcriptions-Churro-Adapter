I have got me a leaf chair like Papa's which I find very
handy to write on I gave 3 dol. for it which I think is
quite a high price, as is every thing in New Haven. My
Boots must be new soled for they are quite worn out
which I suppose will come to a great price. You mention
that you hope I shall get rep with my class, I cannot
if I study night and day for they gain upon me
every day in Homer & in Horace every student advises me
to get a room in College as soon as I possibly can. If I don't
enter till next spring I shall have to be examined again
& perhaps I may be turned by which will not be very pleasing
to me; I should like to go next term & I can with honor
it would make me a great deal happier, I did not like the
proposal when you first mentioned it. I know nothing
more to write at Present. only give my affectionate
love to my brothers & tell them must write me a long
letter soon.
This is the shortest letter I shall ever write you
So Adieu.
Yours &c. S. F. B. Morse.
Rev'd Dr. Morse.

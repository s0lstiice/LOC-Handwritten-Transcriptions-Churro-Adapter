Keep your room, clothes, trunk, desk, & every thing
in neat & good order & maintain order in your
studies religious duties & every thing
Your Mama has written you so
that I have nothing further to add & give
my respects to Dr Dwight & Lady & regards
to Mr Davis, or Mr Everts, Mr Day &
Your affectionate parent
J. Morse
Preserve all the letters
you receive from us carefully
filed under Lock & key.

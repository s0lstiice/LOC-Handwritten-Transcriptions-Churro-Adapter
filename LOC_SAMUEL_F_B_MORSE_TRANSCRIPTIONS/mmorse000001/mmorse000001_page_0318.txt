Charlestown December
23. 1866.
Respected Sir
Assuming that it will
be agreeable to you to know the pre-
cise state of the property, origi-
nally belonging to the Protest-
ant Episcopal Church in Virgi-
nia, I have, with some pains,
obtained the law of that State
respecting this singular busi-
ness. — I find that it destroys
"the establishment," & asserts
that all property belonging to
the said Protestant Episcopal
Church "devolves on the good people
of this Commonwealth (i.e. Virgi-
nia) on the disputation of the
British government here, in
the same degree, in with the right
& interest of the Church was then
deprived from them," & authorizes
the overseers of the poor of any
county, "in with any globe

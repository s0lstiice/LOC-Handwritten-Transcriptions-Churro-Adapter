Cambridge May 2d 1805
Dear boys,
Your Mama Nancy I firmly
have written you & add one line in
taste, to tell you how much pleased
I am with Mr. Steuermans act of
you - Continue, my sons, to be
have well, to pursue your studies,
& diligence, & to comfort the hearts
of your fond parents. - I think Richard
had better try to keep along
with his class though older than him
there is an advantage in associating
with those who know more than
we do - his classmates will be
kind to him, & make allowance for
his youth, he disposed to help him.
I hope to come & see you at the
end of the term & bring you home.
Your affectionate parent
J. Morse

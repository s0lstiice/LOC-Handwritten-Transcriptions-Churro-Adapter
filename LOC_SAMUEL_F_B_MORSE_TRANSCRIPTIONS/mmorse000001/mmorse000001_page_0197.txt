Washington, D.C., May 12, 1865.
My dear Mr. President:
I have the honor to inform you that
the President of the United States
has appointed me to the office of
Chief of Staff, and I accept the
position with the assurance that
the duties of it will be performed
with all the zeal and energy which
are my characteristics.
I have the honor to acknowledge
the receipt of your letter of the
11th instant, in which you
expressed your regret that I
should not be present at the
convention of the party in the
House of Representatives.
I am, sir, your most obedient
and most affectionate servant,
George B. McClellan.
Major-General, U.S. Army.

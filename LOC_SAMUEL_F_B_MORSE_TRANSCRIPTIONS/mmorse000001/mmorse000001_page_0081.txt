Parent. - We have the promise of two letters a week from you, be punctual
to your engagements, that we may know what to expect from you.
We have had a letter from your Grandmother, even last week. She was not
well now in good Spirits your Uncle Grant Inarden are now at Shrewsbury,
they intend taking your Grandmother and Aunt Abby back with them to
Hartford if they can get ready to go, so as not to make it necessary
for them to return to Saginaw. - The Fourth of July is to be celebrated
here with a good deal of Parade both by Federalists & Jacobins the
former are to meet in our Meeting house their to hear an Oration which
is to be delivered by Mr. Aaron Putnam a prayer by your pastor
and on the hill at once by the monument a Standard is to be presented
to a new Company (called The Warren Washington Federalist) by
Doctor Putnam who is the president of the day, and all the gentlemen
are to dine at Petershall otherwise called Mashunk's Hall. The Ladies
are to take Part at the same place. The Jacobins are to have an
oration at the Baptist meeting house from Mr. Gleason I know nothing
more about them. The boys are forming themselves in Companies &
they have two or three companies of Drums which at some times are

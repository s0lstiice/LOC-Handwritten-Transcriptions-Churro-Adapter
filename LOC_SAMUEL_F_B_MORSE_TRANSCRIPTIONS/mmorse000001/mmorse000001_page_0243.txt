Our political affairs are in a
sad state. Gov. Long is chosen -- but
there will be a Democratic Council,
Senate, House of Representatives.
Our Legislature will consist, as it is
conjectured of 4 or 500 people -- The house
will hardly hold them all -- It will
be like the National Assembly in
France. You remain from Connecticut -- Long continues your "Steady
habits." We hope to see you early in June.
Perhaps after your visit, We may
wish to take a short journey -- We
send love to all the family.
Your affectionate Son
[Mark Twain]

Chesleytown Feb. 21. 1865
Hon'd Sir,
We heard from you verbally
by Chester Morse a short time since, were
glad to know of your welfare. The loss
of life, in my dear Mother, it appears,
is gradually sinking into its Socket- Through
the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, I
trust she is ripe for heaven. You sin
have great cause for thankfulness that
You have been preserved to a good
old age together, that you have enjoyed
such uninterrupted harmony & happiness- May, God prepare you for
the spending hour.
Mr Hayward called to-day informed
me that he is going to the Union Confederate line
morning, & offers to take a letter for you I
could not omit the opportunity of dropping you alive.
We are all as well as can be expected
all things considered - there it is about
Get - We have had a pretty warm
fight with the Yankees.

My Dear Husband
I wrote you on Sabbath but having a few lines on
Monday evening to you from Brother Arthur I thought
I would write you again and give you an Extract from
his letter respecting the Chenango turnpike though doubtless
you will have talked over the matter before this and have
known how completely you have been taken in by W.K.
"The Chenango turnpike there is but one gate on the route, the
road never will be finished. But if it was would afford no
dividend for this good reasoning there is no travelling -
Shares may be purchased at any price you choose to give - In
short my Dear Brother have nothing to do with Shares in
this turnpike there has been great Iniquity made use of by
Stockholders attempting to sell Shares at a Distance from
home they cant give them away, - thus far Arthur
now would it not be best to let W.K. know this and see if he
has honour or Common honesty enough to do something
to indemnify you for this Intolerable take in which under
the Appearance of a great deal of Religion & a sanctified
prince he has palmed upon you I am sick sick sick of this
Bicked World how much deceit is practiced in it, - let my
Dear be earnestly endeavouring after Subterfance Inconspicu-
ble The defiled & that faith not again in a better World -

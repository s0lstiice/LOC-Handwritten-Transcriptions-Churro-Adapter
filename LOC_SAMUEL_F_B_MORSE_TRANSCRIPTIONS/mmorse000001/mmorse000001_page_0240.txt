Charleston May 3d 1861
You Sir By Mr Griffin Child I
write you one line in much haste
just to say that I received yours
of Apr. 23d which gave us the
pleasing information of your re-
storations to health, & that you
intended us a visit in June we
shall be happy to see you. Come
if you please early in the month
as we may wish to take a journey
by the middle of June my health
is not good my health is not
good when the spring opens I
shall be better The rest of the
family is well Mrs M. sends love
duty I love with your affectionate
son
J. Moser

Your brothers are very diligent & make
good progress in their studies. They are
a comfort to us, as I hope you
will be by your good behaviour –
Don't fail to write us once a fortnight
by mail – take great pains with your
letters, with regard to the hand writing,
the grammar, pointing, spelling &c. –
give us all the information concerning
yourself, which you know will be inter-
esting to us. Your last letters were
too carelessly & inaccurately written.
Make no marks on the back side,
of your letters of the place where you
are – it is improper & boyish – Be
manly in your behaviour – very
respectful obliging to your instructor,
& the family where you live – Call frequent-
ly & sociably at Mrs Morse's & make as
many friends in N.H. as you can by your
good behaviour.
The family are all well & send love to you.
Your brother had two clothes with him
for a private conveyance. Thirty prisoners
are in our prisons. I have not preached to them
yet, but have visited most of them in their
cells. I shall expect to hear regularly & particularly from
you a fortnight from this time.
96283
father J. Morse

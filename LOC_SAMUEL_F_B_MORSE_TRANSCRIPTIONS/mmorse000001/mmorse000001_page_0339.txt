Gale College. N.H. August 12th. 1807.
My Dear Parents
I have just rec'd your letter of the 8th inst which informs me, you have not rec'd a letter from me this month past; I wrote a letter to you last week by mail with hope you have rec'd by this time; my time is so taken up in my studies that I scarcely find time to do any thing else; I hope therefore you will excuse me from writing so often as I should if I had not my studies to attend to; I am reviewing Geography & Greek Minora & wish to go perfect in them, therefore I employ all my time to hinder me from doing other things that I wish to do;
I am glad to hear that Edward & Richard are about to enter this college I hope Wm Walker will come on with Edward, for I have got an excellent room in college, for him & myself, the best room in our class, & yet I had the last choice, but those who have the last choice, have the privilege of taking the first vacated room, which is my case, & an excellent room has fallen to my lot.
You mention that Mama had written me a long letter; I have not yet rec'd it I hope I shall soon.
I am in good health, & spirits & am delighted with college; I long to be a Sophomore.
The influence begins to spread in town & among the students but I believe it is not dangerous; it prevails in New York very badly & some fear it will end in the Yellow Fever.
N.B. (Excuse my bad writing, mistake, Volck) In haste Yours S.F.B. Morse.

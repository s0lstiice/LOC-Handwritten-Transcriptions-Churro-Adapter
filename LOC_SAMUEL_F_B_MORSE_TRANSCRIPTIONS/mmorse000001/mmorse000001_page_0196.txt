New Haven Decr 25 1805
Dear Parents,
I again take the pleasure of writing you & hope this will meet you well.
I enjoy & have enjoyed good health since I have been here. I must again inform
you of the bad progress in my studies, in Horace I get 120 lines in a day &
my class get 160 & in Homer I get 23 lines & my class 50; I shall not keep
up with them in anything, for they go on very rapidly, every student that I know
advises me to go to college as soon as I can; I think it will be of much more advantage
to me, next term for I shall have to study night & day, in the vacancy to get up
with my class; I feel very much discontented with my situation here because I fall behind
my class every day; it makes me homesick & very unhappy.

Yale College, New Haven June 30th 1807
My Dear Parents,
I have just read your letter of the 27th inst.
and now have but one moment to answer it
I shall follow my resolutions of studying hard this term
if it is only to please you, it comes quite natural to
me now to sit down & study my lessons & not leave them
till I have got them perfectly, I have nothing now to interrupt
me in my studies, as I had at home.
I will return Gracia Minerva, & the Bible as
you request, it was necessary for me to get Gracia Minerva
at the first of the term as I had none, then I could not
borrow one; the Bible you told me to get, but as you will
send me one I can return it as well as not. You wish
me to return Gil Blas & say I can get it from the Society.
I am in: I am about to take a admission from that Society
because I have not time to attend to its exercises, & the one
in the Society Library is an English one, whereas mine in Paris
it is not so much for the books contents, as its acquainting
me more thoroughly with the language; I hope you will
let me keep it as it was recommended to me by Monsieur
Vallec as a very easy book to read for beginners;
M. Vallec says that I make great progress in
the French Language, & will teach it to me in 3 months
so that by vacation I shall have a considerable
knowledge of it.
In some haste
Your affectionate Son
S.B. Morse
P.S. I am in good health

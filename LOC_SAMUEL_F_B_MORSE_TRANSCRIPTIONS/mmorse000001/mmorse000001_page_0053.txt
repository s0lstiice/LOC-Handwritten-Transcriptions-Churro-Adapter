To Saml. Bradford Charleston April 30. 1805
Sir: I received your favor of the 2d inst.
in due course, I have delayed a reply
till I had rec'd & examined the book
you mention. It was not rec'd till Sat-
urday last & since that time I have
examined it as far as my pleasure
would allow -- I judge, not pretending, however,
to perfect exactness, that one half
of the states contained between 1200000000000000000000000000000000
of inhabitants may be found verbatim
in my geography. The portion

in the Union, which make the bulk of
the work, are almost wholly taken from
my book -- I am not aware, may have

Charleston July 22. 1866
Dear Sir,
I have received your last with
your journals
Yesterday the Select Committee
met at my house, we read your
letters & journals & on deliberation
concluded that (as I expect to be at
Whitestown sometime before the 15th
of next month) I should meet you there
& confer on the subject of the Western
mission, & if I think proper
I might pledge the Society for the
laid to aid it. I shall leave
this place on Monday next in the state
& go directly to the Springs at Balle
town, where I shall spend a few days,
not exceeding a week, for the recovery
of my health, which is very feeble there.
I shall proceed to Whitestown, where
I hope to meet you & if convenient

My dear Son,
I had enjoyed my passage
in the stage for N.Haven, but the weather
being so bad I intended to travel for
this time. I intend coming about the
middle of next month, if the travelling
admit. I have written Mr Davis to
answer with Mr Davis's opinion on the subject
of your admission to College. Perhaps they will think it best for you still
to board at Mr Davis's till I come & please
you in College myself. I think myself
the best for you. If you should be join your class let me charge
you, my son to guard agt. the influence of bad examples, & to be

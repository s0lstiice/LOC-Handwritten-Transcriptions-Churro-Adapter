Indover Jan 21 1804
Hon'd Father,
I now embrace an opportunity, by Mr Blanchard who goes tomorrow afternoon I have found your hatchet & shall bring it down next vacation. I have not got on Eaton's bill nor the Whispering Bill nor the Sardis Bill once this term but I have got 3 times on the Spelling Bill because it was in the hard name.
I have got to the Lucian Dialogues in Greek Minora but I am reviewing now I have got to the 2d Georgic in Virgil I shall send Finleys Books by Mr Blanchard if he goes in a sleigh. Finleys Class went through Sallust about a week & half ago I have gone into Tully They have been through Gracia Minora I have gone into Greek Testament. I should have sent Finley's Books down before only they couldn't carry them & Captn Philp went before we received the Letters, we did not know he was a going. I put the books into Mr Blanchards hands I would have sent them if there was any opportunity in great haste I am your affectionate Son. E. More

If you have not written, when you
receive this, write immediately
inform us whether Capt. Collins has
arrived with your trunk, desk
bundles &c We hear it is sickly
in a part of St. Haven take care
not to go into that part of it
take good care of your health.
Pay diligent attention to your
studies & resolve to be a scholar.
We are all well -- Our regards to Mr. Davis & other
friends in St. Haven.
Your affectionate
parents
All the family
send love to you.
J. Mosse

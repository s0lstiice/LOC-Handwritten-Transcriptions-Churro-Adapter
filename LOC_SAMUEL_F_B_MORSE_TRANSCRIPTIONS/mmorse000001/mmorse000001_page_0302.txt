My dear wife,
We arrived here safely half
past 7 O'clock have had a very pleasant
ride -- promising appearance of a fine
day tomorrow -- We hope to reach Kentucky
even to-morrow to C. P.Mo -- You will
not probably hear from us yet -- till
you see us, which we hope will be
Friday evening -- or Saturday noon --
I have partly engaged you a
girl, who passed with us in the stage
from Thompson to Pomfret by the name
of Becca Burrill -- She has the appearance
of a good girl -- I have promised
her 4/6 a week -- & to pay her passage to
now she may come on in the stage on
Saturday -- We are in the midst of a
company of soldiers -- noisy & confusing
-- I cannot add -- I expect not to
sleep much to night -- Take good care
of yourself -- I hope the people will
not complain -- Love to sister Abby,
the boys -- not forgetting Nancy & other
friends -- Believe your loving affectionate
husband J Mosse

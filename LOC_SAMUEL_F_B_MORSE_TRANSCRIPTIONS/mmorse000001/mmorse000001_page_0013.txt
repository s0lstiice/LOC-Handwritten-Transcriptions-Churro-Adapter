Andover, N.H. March 21st 1809
My Parents
I now have the pleasure
to inform you that I have not been unwell
this term. I have been about as far as the 130th line
in the 2d Eneid of Virgil. Finley has had to
go back as far as the 10th Book of Eneid of Vir
gil. I do not know exactly how far Richard
got in Nepos. I have got as far as the contracted verbs in greek grammar. Finley has
been as far as Acts in the greek Testament.
Richard has got through that he had to go
for a Morning Lesson. I was very glad to
hear that Elizabeth could walk, I hope she
will be able to talk by the time I come down.

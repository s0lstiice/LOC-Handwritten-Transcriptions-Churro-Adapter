I wish you tellt Wm Walker that I expect him to come
on with me next term & shall be very much disappointed
indeed, till him not to decide till I come home for commence-
ments, for if he should decide in the negative without
hearing both sides of the question, it would disappoint me
very much; I ought to have written him but I have
not had time, Please to give my love to him, Elisha Godard &
John Bartlett, & Saml. Ward I take it for granted he is com-
ing on to this college;
Give my love particularly to my brothers, to Grand-
mother, Aunt Abby, Nancy, & in short all the family.
I hope my brothers will be at home when it's vacation here,
I would write them if I could. I am in great haste as
it is past ten o'Clock even.
Please excuse all blots & mistakes,
Yours etcetera
S F B Morse
Red. Febt. 11th 1816.

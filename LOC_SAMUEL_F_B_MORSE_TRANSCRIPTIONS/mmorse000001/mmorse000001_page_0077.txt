I am much pleased with his hand writing --
You may all with due pains, will I hope you
will take, make very good writers, & very
good scholars too -- Think, my dear boys, how
much the happiness of your parents depends
on your good behavior -- Remember that
a studious life is comparatively an innocent
life -- The boy that applies himself closely
to his studies, & finds his pleasure in them,
is seldom fallen into any vicious course.
He is pleased with himself, & others are
pleased with him. But an idle boy naturally
falls into many sins -- Both make
him disaffected with himself, & he has
few or no friends. -- I wish you all to
feel the importance of forming habits
& attention to your studies, & to all your
proper business. Let no religious duty
be neglected. Cultivate a devout temper.
Always fear God who is present to see
you, & do nothing to displease him --
They live in all things -- Your parents,

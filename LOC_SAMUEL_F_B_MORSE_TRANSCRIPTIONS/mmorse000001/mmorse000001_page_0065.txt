I have no news to write you
Grandma Beech, we hear is not
very well - Lydia will leave
us very shortly - we miss your
company very much - You must
write us frequently, & long letters
& comfort us - To hear of your
good behaviour, & of your pro-
gress in your studies, will com-
fort us more than any thing
else - Don't forget to read
your bible daily, & perform
devoutly your morning &
evening devotions - Your
Mama & Many join me
in love to you both
Regards to Mr & Mrs Blan-
chard - Your affectionate parent
J. Moser

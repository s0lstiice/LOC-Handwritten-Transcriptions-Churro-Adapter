I must now hasten to conclude Papa was very sick last night but is
better to day he joins me in love to you and your Brothers & cousin
Eskimee in all which Nancy joins. Remember us also with respect
to Madame Philispe & all inquiring Friends. Lydia Lathropse
is gone home, she went last week. I told you before that Anne
is married and we have a young Woman here has in her place
where Anne is Rebecca Brown a young Dear Girl, she Used to live
at Mrs Lovells. Mrs L is going to move to Boston soon, which we
are sorry for. I can think of nothing more to say to you at present
Adieu my dear Son, and believe me ever your affectionate
Mother E Bellamy
Master S.F.B. More

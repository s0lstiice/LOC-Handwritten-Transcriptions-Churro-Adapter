was a weak infirm fellow & none make mention
of Batalis as a Poet who used to write wicked
songs & Lampoons & the name of Clancy they
put upon him because he was fierce & passionate.
He began to learn oratory very soon &
hearing that Callistratus an Orator was going
to speak one day, he desired his tutor to let
him see him, the tutor being well acquainted
with the Door keepers to easily found a
place for the Boy, who admired Calli, too,
very much & resolved to imitate him,
he had a very odd place for studying, he had
a study built on purpose for him under
ground & there he used to shave himself

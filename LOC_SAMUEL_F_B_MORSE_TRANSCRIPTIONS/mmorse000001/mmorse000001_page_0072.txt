Dear Mr. President,
After the conversation I had
with you at my house, previous to issuing
the Proposals for the Panoplist, I was
unwilling to answer your letter of April 2d, but the
concluding clause paragraph, in which
you say "I led me to hope that
I should be able to remove your
objections, & to engage such a position
from you as you could not think
laborers afford, I would give up
in contemptful respectfulness of
part in circulation by the work on
this expectation I wrote you, I had
further conversation with you on
the subject at your house. After
what had been communicated to
you on the subject, verbally in writing
& what you had said I wrote in reply, if I had no reason to expect
your assistance, I had certainly no
reason for what you wrote in said
letter. I am
Yours respectfully
Samuel F. B. Morse

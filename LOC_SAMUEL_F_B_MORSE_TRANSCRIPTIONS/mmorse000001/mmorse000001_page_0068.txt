Dear boys, We have not had the pleasure
of a line from you since you left us -- we
are quite anxious to hear how you do,
& what progress you are making in
your studies. We hope you are very
happy, & making rapid progress
in your studies. I have written
one letter to Finlay -- & one to
Edward & Richard. I hope you had
received them -- We should be glad
to hear from Cousin Hazard -- we
hope he finds himself agreeably
situated. -- Let us hear from you
all very soon & often.
I have nothing new to write
you -- Master Charles Hud, who
carries this will tell you the news
what will be most interesting to you.

We are extremtly anxious my Son that above all other things
you should make Religion your early choice as that is indeed
the one thing Godful that good part which shall never
be taken from you neglect not your Daily Devotions read
your Bible constantly & prayerfully and avoid as much as
possible all companions that have not the fear of God before
their eyes better be alone at any time than with such Company
never be absent from prayers at Candleight always be very
serious While attending don't let the folly and lightness of
others tempt you to be of your Guard at such Solomon Season
but keep always in Mind Your Accountability as I have been
for mentioned. – attend on all Dr Dwight's Sermons on
lectures and profit by all your Opportunities to get Wisdom
endeavour to be the better for all your Advantages as this is
the only way that you can Compressate & comfort your
Affectionate Parents Who cannot pray Daily & earnestly
that having a prince put into your hand to get Wisdom
you may have a dear heart given you from above to
prove it just to make all the advances you can both in
Human and Divine knowledge and this will rejoice our
hearts. – I must now bid you good night as it is late & my
hopes is full Your Papa & Brothers & Sisters join me in
love to you May heaven bless you my son Your Affectionate
mother E D Moon

Charlestown July 27th 1804
Respected Sir,
In obedience to the directions of the Massachusetts Society for Promoting
Christian knowledge, I had the honour of addressing
to you a letter by my young friend Mr
Lowell, some time ago; I have since received from your hand, a valuable discourse
containing much information desired for the
benefit of our Society; a lately, by Mr Lowell,
your excellent Charge of 1803: For these
attentions, I pray you sir, to accept my thanks.

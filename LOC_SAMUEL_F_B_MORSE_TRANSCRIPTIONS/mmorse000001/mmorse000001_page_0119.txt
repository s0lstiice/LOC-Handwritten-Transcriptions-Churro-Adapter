As the Sheriff made out the Bill of sale
& you rather receipted the goods, I supposed them secure
in his hands & did not remove them - I was
induced thus to do, also an acct of the delicate state
of Mrs P. health, unwilling to do anything to disturb
her - Things remained in this state till about 10 days ago
it was intimated that your brothers affairs were growing
worse, & the goods made over to me were not safe in his
hands. I immediately called on him, & he agreed to send part
of them to me that evening, & a part on subsequent evenings
to avoid observation, till all but the bed should be
delivered - Promised me he would keep his son locked till
he left town - He however sent the things sparingly to me
about the amount of 40 or 50 dollars - & in my absence
an officer entered & seized all his goods - & last week, he
with his family left town in the night - to day his goods
remaining have been sold at auction - I am left to pay the
last of next month 257 doll. unless you will consent to take
my place, as I hope you will, as I am very illly able, to sustain
such a loss - & I became obligated, to save the life of his wife
to continue him in business, & in confident expectation
that on your return, you could pay the note - I know
the humanity & goodness of your heart & was assured by
your brother both of your ability & willingness to assist him

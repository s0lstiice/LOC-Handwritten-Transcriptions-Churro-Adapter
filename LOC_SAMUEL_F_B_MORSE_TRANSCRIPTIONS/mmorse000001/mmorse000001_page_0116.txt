My dear boys,
Though I have nothing special to write you, I drop you one line by
Mr Eldridge who is going to London this morning to return to Londonmond
— you write by him — We had a fine refreshing rain on Saturday night —
tablett for us to we, I hope we are very thankful — I sent Friday the 2d No.
of the Pamphlet with him must let his brother read — I last even'd rec'd a
letter from your Uncle Hazard of Phila. You can tell Ensign that
all are well there — we send love to him — We think of you affectionately
every day & many times a day — Let it be your constant aim to please God
in every thing — Then you will please your fond parents, who wish for no
greater joy in this world than to see their children loving & well taken
in the truth — Nancy has not returned — Your Mama writes in love to
you all with your loving father I am

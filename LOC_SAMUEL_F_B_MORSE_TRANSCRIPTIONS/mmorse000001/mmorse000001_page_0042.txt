Charlestown March 7 1805
Dear Brothers,
As Papa is now engaged with his
Professor Parsons (who informs me that Mr Gam
net goes to-morrow) I take the pleasure
of writing to you. There has been
spring weather here & carried all the
snow away & the roads here are all
dry. I have got nothing new to tell you
only that last monday was a very busy
day here for the Jacob's; there was an
hundred & seventy two cannons fired & a
large feast at Massachusetts Hall &
200 Jacobins dined there all that
was praiseworthy in them was that
they had the best of musick the band
of musick played for them. I must
now bid you Adieu the Family
send a great deal of love to you with
me House my bad writing for I am
in Great haste.
Your affectionate
Brother
S. F. B. Mauss.

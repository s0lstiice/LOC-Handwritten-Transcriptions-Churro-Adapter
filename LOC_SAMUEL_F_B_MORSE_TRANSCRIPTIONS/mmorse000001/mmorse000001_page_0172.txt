My Dear Son
It is three weeks to day since we have heard a word
from you which makes me very anxious indeed if you are and
have been well I much wonder that you are not more regular
and punctilious in writing us than you are we depend on hearing
from you at least once a fortnight by post you must write a
long letter and answer it to the post office every other Sunday
night in season to be put in the mail and come on on Monday
morning and we should get it every other Wednesday
which would be a great pleasure to us your brothers almost
every day come to the office in expectation of getting a letter from
brother Finley and are greatly disappointed to return us that
one you cannot therefore please us all by being more punctual in
your brothers are to stay home this winter to supply the place of
Prince by taking care of the cord & supplying the young boys
bringing in wood & going of commands leaving having a man
and to save paying board for them in order to enable you to
be so far from home to get your education so you see the need
of close application on your part to render us all for our attention
to you your brothers study very closely at home and devote
things as recreation and are good boys. You must write them
on the same paper you write us occasionally, I wrote you a long
letter by Mr Nichols a fortnight ago & your brother Walt
then too. hope you have received them I heard from Philadelphia
this week that Dr Green took the sick of Washington the letter to
you on to Philadelphia with him through an intable he went

March.
1805. May come in, I have delayed authorizing
our joint letter of 25 ult. till now, partly on account of my ill health
and partly that I might leave Toronto, in hopes the crisis which
has now happened. The Presidency of [Howard] [Mellon] has
been filled by the Corporation and no doubt will be sanctioned
by the Overseers. The Hon. Fisher Ames is the man. The Unionism
forso I shall distinguish the revolutionists of the College though I
do not allow them the title (to) have made a wise choice for their
purposes. If he accepts which remains doubtful, I shall consider
the revolution complete; and the seat of the College whose motto
"Caritas et eclesiae" (Charity and the Church) had better be
broken, and a more appropriate one substituted. This event
will strengthen the revolutionists.
These events may probably be the means of founding
a new literary and religious institution on principles
and for purposes similar to those on and for which
Tarward College was founded. Athenix may arise
out of the ashes of this ancient seminary. At least such an
event is not impossible. Dr. Pearson has decided to resign
his office at the close of the vacation and he is by far the
most efficient officer belonging to that institution; and
though the revolutionist will rejoice at his resignation
he will find it no easy matter to fill his place.

would pour forth the shameless conduct of those, who have
been instruments of this perversion, and publish it to the
world. Perhaps circumstances may hereafter render it
neccessary; and we propose ample documents.

you see what a long letter I have written you do not let my
letters be seen by anyone as they are not written with that accent
which for the want of those advantages which you enjoy in my
home youth has made them very deficient in many particulars
but they convey to my children and Friend the feeling I have
of affection and love to them and that will answer for me
I hope my Dear son you will not fail to do your duty in reading
your Bible Daily & attending your private Devotions and in your
prayer for yourself forget not to pray for Blessings on your
Parents & Dear little Brothers who all love you very much
indeed together with Nancy who joins us in our prayers
for you that the best of heavens Blessings may remain
with you and you be preserved from all evil especially from
sin I shall now bid you Adieu remember
Dwight M. Davis & Mrs Davis & M. Day & Mrs Dyell
& my Daggett & Wife & old M. Sherman & Mrs Day Mama &
all who may ask you when you have heard from us and
how we do. I must bid you remain as ever
Papa & Nancy join me in love Your Affectionate
Mother E A Morett

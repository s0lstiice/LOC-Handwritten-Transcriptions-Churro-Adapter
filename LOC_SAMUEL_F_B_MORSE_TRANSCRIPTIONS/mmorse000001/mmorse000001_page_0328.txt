Yale College June 7th 1807.
My dear Parents,
I hope you will excuse my not writing
you soon, when I inform you that my time is entirely
taken up with my studies. In the morning I must rise at
5 o'clock to attend prayers, immediately after recitation, then
I must breakfast, & begin to study from 8 o'clock till 11, then recite
my forenoons lesson, which takes me an hour, at 12 I must
study French till 1, which is dinner time, directly after dinner
I must recite French to Monsieur Vallee, till 2 o'clock;
then begin to study my afternoon lesson, recite it at five,
immediately after recitation I must study another French
lesson and recite at 7 in the evening, come home at 9 o'clock & study
my morning lesson till, 10, 11, & sometimes 12 o'clock & by that
time I am prepared to sleep.
Monsieur Vallee says that I make great progress
in French & will engage to teach me in 3 months so
as to read any French book. I have bought several
French books -- one, Bayard Dictionary, 2d and 3d. Semaine
1st, 25 -- Lectures Francois -- 1st, 25 -- Likewise I have bought
me a pocket edition of the Bible to, last me this college & Longman's
1. Spence Minor, a Sett of the Geography & atlas. I am greatly in
need of Dufief, would thank you to send it on as soon as
possible. You see now I have enough to do my hands as
full as can be, not 5 minutes time to take recreation
I am determined to study, & thus far have not miss'd

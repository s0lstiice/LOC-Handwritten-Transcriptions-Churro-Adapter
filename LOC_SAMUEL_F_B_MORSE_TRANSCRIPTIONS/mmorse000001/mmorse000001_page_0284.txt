Yesterday We had several gentlemen, two Mr. Rensallaers
& a Mr. Schuyler to dine with him also Mr. Camptcham
& Mr. Sister Sprouden -- We sat down after 3 O'clock
till tea time -- quite in high style all things in
very good order & an excellent dinner -- My
next letter will be from Albany, as no mail will
leave this place, except the one that comes this, till the
one which we shall accompany on Wednesday next
when we propose leaving White town -- I am going
this morning with Mr. Utica, I will finish my
letter on my return --
Sabbath Morning Sept 96334
The eastern mail closes to-day -- I went to Utica & transacted
considerable business to advantage -- The Sergeant will meet me
to-morrow with Capt. Hendrick, at Mr. Sprouders. The mail closes
today & I have no letter from you, as I expected -- I am greatly
desirous to hear from you, not having heard a word from
home since I left it -- I am sorry I forgot to make
arrangements with you about writing me -- Mr. & Mrs. Hazard
& Watney arrived here last evening well -- We are all now
at Mr. Arthur's, a house full of us -- The weather is clear,
cool, & fine & we are all well -- Mr. Sidney did not come
last evening as we expected, he may be here to-day or tomorrow -- It is probable we shall not leave this place till
Friday's stage, as we contemplate an excursion together into
some of the neighboring towns, & possibly may go on to
Cazenovia, if Mr. S. does not come -- I shall write you
by next mail -- I expect to spend the next Sabbath in
Albany, & to leave it on Tuesday following -- You will have
time to write me there, after you receive this -- Don't fail to do it --
I hope to receive a letter here by next mail -- Love to the boys -- Remember
me affectionately to Mr. Brown, Mrs. Campbell &c. &c. forgetting your name in writing
to Mr. Rensallaers -- I am
Yours affec. &c. S. P. G. H. S.

We were much pleased with your letter and it was particular in letting
us know how you got along from place to place. We enclosed your
letter to our brothers promising it would afford them much
pleasure I hope we shall receive some letters from them to enclose to
you in this before it goes -- but if we should not have letters from them
we take it for granted they are well. They were well a week ago. -- Dear
Bapa is appointed Chaplain to the State Prison by the Governor's Council
he has in consequence an addition to his labours which you know
were very great before I am afraid it will be more than he can do
to get through with all without injuring his health. You must be very
industrious in your studies and be able to relieve him from a great many
of his cares the reason that he can get through with so much business
is that he early inbibed habits of Industry and application to
his studies which have grown up with him and makes him thus
able to be useful to his fellow men in various ways if his children
would be as useful to his fellow men in various ways as his father
and grandfather have been in their youth they must follow his example and not let their
prerious moments pass unimproved in but in early life lay upon
store a fund of good and useful knowledge now that they
have the means put into their hands lest they should not be always
thus favoured be ambitious therefore my son to excel in whatever
you undertake that is good and no doubt with the blessing of heaven
on your endeavours which I trust you will not fail to ask daily
you will succeed and be a great comfort to your parents by being a

Staten Island - Aug. 1st 1806
Tuesday 2 O'Clock PM.
My dear Wife. We have this moment
safely arrived here - where we shall
remain till tomorrow 9 O'clock -
then proceed westward - I find my
health evidently improving - The weather
is now fine for travelling - We have
had much rain - & little warm
weather - Miss Bartlett is
with me very well, a pleasant
companion - I shall take good
care of her - Abby has gone on,
& we have left Eliza at the
Springs - so that she is left alone
with me - Mr Shipman leaves
us here

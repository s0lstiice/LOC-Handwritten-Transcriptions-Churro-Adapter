I am glad to hear that land is rising
in Cash buy. Jonathan writes me that
he thinks if you will consent of selling
at Woodstock settling in that town.
You write nothing of it - Hope you will
both have wisdom to direct you in
this & all other matters.
I will endeavors to deliver
your messages to Dr Larkin Letters
All are in usual health. Finley spend
the winter at home, to help me I
presne his Studies the other two
are at Andover, well the last
we heard from them - Things
remain well in my parish - I shall
send you some few late
publications - I haven't time to add
we all send most love to you, Mama
brother & sister, & the dutiful affectionate
grandchildren who you mention are
so kind to you I matter we wish to
be affectionately remembered to Aunt
Mom - I forgot to mention in my turn that Anna
Reed has Mother's good Mother - From affectionate J M

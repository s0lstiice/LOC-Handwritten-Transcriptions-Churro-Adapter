Charleston Jan 7th 1805
Dear boys, Your letter by Mr Harman
gave us great pleasure nothing in this
world gives us more satisfaction than
to see our children belaying well
& improving their opportunities to store
their minds with useful knowledge.
We are much pleased to hear you have
made such progress in your studies.
See that you get your lessons thor-
oughly—that you fight nothing
this is the only way to make
good scholars.
Mama Abby Nancy as well
as your mama were very much
pleased with their letters. With
your reflections on Mrs Edwards
death—what is a sad event especially
to Nancy. We expect Nancy will
come of live with us as soon

not prevent. Imagine the Amount of 160 Vincent may be
profitably secured by your Society from the variety of tracts, which
have. I should wish them bound, or whatever difference there
may be between doing up the tract & binding Vincent, I will
allow it. – as the Pamphlet will contain 8 pages more than was
promised, I think it would be right in your Society to pay the
trifling expense of transportation, as the proportional increase
will increase the demand for the Pamphlist, & consequently increase
your profits, & what you will begainers on the whole? I
suggest this for your consideration. It is not of much consequence
either way. Have been & am still in fairable health, I
need a journey as much as Lever did, & hope to be benefited
by it. Mrs M – is tolerably well. She writes in cordial regards to
you & Mr L. not forgetting your son – Yours truly, J. Morel

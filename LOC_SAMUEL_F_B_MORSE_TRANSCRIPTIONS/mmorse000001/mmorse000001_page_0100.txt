To Revd Dr Lyman Hatfield
Charleston July 12, 1805
Dear Sir
Received your favor of the 2nd inst, on
my return from an excursion into the country to
recruit my health & spirit, which, under Providence,
has had the desired effect. I am gratified that your
Committee have agreed to take 100 copies of the Panoptist.
I have little doubt of your procuring subscribers enough
in Hampshire County to take the whole number. They
shall be delivered free of expense, at Northampton at
9d payable semiannually. We shall be obliged
by your expending your profits in the work, at the
same price, for distribution. I think the same num-
ber might easily be disposed of in Berkshire County,
with a little exertion, on the same terms. Some very favor-
able returns from that county, I have now received. Will
you undertake to negotiate with Mr Hyde for me. I
will be obliged to you to do it. He shall have all the
subscribers received from that County, if he, in behalf
of the Missionary Society in that County, will take
100, delivered at Northampton. I have received no returns from
Hampshire County, but have heard that Dr Lathrop has a num-
ber that he is friendly to the work. Opposition is silenced here,
at least for the present, by the appearance of the first number.
I had forgotten in my great haste, to say that I sent last
week 100 copies of the Panoptist to Hadley, by Dr Thom-
son, in Stage, which I hope have been received. I send with
this a number of subscription papers. I shall direct all
subscribers in Hampshire County to receive their books of your
100 – this moment, while I am writing, a subscription paper,
for 22 copies has been received from Mr Somroy, Worthington,
which I enclose, as belonging to you, & which will take a large
portion of your 100 at once. I hope you will want 100 more. I
think you will. I will write you longer soon. The heat, at
a lecture this evening

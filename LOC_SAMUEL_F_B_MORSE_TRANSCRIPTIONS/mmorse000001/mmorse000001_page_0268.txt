Baltimore, August 1st 1856
My dear Wife,
We arrived here yesterday to Cook's
& obtained accommodations at Lord's new house, with
the resort of the geniectest company. We are about
20 in number. General Derby his brother & his family
are among them. I know Miss Betty Malcom is here from New York the left the
family well. Abby Sands came in with us this
morning at 6 o'clock expecting to reach Sherridaville at 9 o'clock. I take the
stage at 10 for Whitehouse. They have a fine day
for travelling. Eliza Catharine are with me
they are both writing for themselves. They have both
been very good girls, & are learning to drink the
water. It will take us several days to become
acquainted with the company I am accustomed to the
place. As for myself I feel a little fatigued with
my journey, but hope here to rest & refresh myself.
I should be much happier were you with me
now. I feel lonely amidst a crowd. While travelling
the constant change of objects, employs my attention
& the exercise serves to exhilarate my spirits, but
when I am at rest distant from my books &
the scene of business my spirit flag, & I think with

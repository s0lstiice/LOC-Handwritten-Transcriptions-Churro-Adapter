4:2117, they intend writing you by this opportunity, as it is a good one
it is Mr. Loring his going on to Philadelphia and afford to take
letters for us if you will him ask him when he will pass through the
on his way back and if he tells you make a point of writing us
all a number of letters & have them ready & get the favour of him
to call up take them if you do take the trouble to ask at Mr. Loring
or Mr. Day if they have of any one going to Boston they could
frequently tell you of opportunities as they have a Brother living
in Boston Mr. Oliver Sherman I know you may remember to
have seen at our house Saml. Barrell, writes often to his Friends
that he is much pleased with his situation & is very contented. - Then
one of the Students has died of the Bilious Fever this is a solemn Warning
to those that remain to be also ready suppose my Dear Son it has
been your wish to have him in the United States instead of him? What would have been your state ask yourself this
important Question and let Conscience answer it sincerely and let
the Uncertainty of life and the Certainty of Death make you decide
above all things else to love Jesus with your whole heart. I have
begged God and beg of him to implant his divine Grace in your heart &
give you an Intrest in the perfect Righteousness of his dear Son
Jesus Christ having this Security you would be perfectly safe let happen
to you what would either life or Death you would be able to
bid all welcome as long as God was your Friend through the
mediator think much may I say on the concerns of your soul you
will never repent of having early began to seek the Lord but
if you should delay this important business you will undoubtedly
repent of it and perhaps Death is too late there will be wise
before it is too late and close that good God that no one can take
from you. This is our Daily prayer for you that you may take
this happy Choice who so you hear much on the Sabbath?

could finish my letter and send it by him and it would save postage
which comes to a great deal every Quarter as you send all your
pay post I wish you would try and find private opportunities
to write by as Mr. Stedman & Mr. Simon Dwight or anyone
that would be like to know to inform you if they hear of any
person going on to Boston and write by them. – I wish you
to be careful only send not to dirt more clothes than is necessary
as working is so dear put on two clean shirts a week two thick
clothes & one pair of stockings is enough this cold weather two
in Summer & 1 pocket handkerchief. Count your clothes when you
send them to be washed & when they come back and see that
you soon done your self a hope to be on to see you next week
if the weather and roads and other things will admit of it
let me only dear son above all other things beg of you not
to follow any of the bad examples that you may see around
you either in the schools or any one else remember everyone
must be accountable to that great and all seeing God who has
with us in secret and it will be no excuse to him that we only
did as others did who blasphemed his holy name & profaned his
holy Sabbaths or was guilty of disobeying his commands in any
other way but endeavour to have a constant sense of his presence
being always with you even when no other eye beholds you &
make this resolution your own How shall I do this or that and
and sin against God?

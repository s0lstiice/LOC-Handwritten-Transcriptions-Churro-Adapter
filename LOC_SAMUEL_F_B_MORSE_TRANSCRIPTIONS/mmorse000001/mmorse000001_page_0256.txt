Dr. David: Morse
Is respectfully informed, that the Managers
of the Boston Female System, by an unanimous Vote, have appointed
him to offer an Introductory Prayer, on the sixth celebration of their Anniver-
sary Sept. 26. 1806; and solicits an answer of acceptance to their request.
By Order.
Eliza Frottingham, Secy

advanced in your studies. I am sorry
you should let your class get before
you. I hope you are not getting into
idle habits. The vacation will give
you a fine opportunity to get up with your
class, & even to get ahead of them,
& I hope my son, you will cheerfully
& diligently improve it for
that purpose. I remember I
used to value vacations as among
the most important seasons for improvement.
I wish you to be ambitious
to among the best scholars in your
class— I presume you may be if you
have but the inclination, I will
subsequently apply yourself to your
studies. I would not have you too
anxious to go into College. I am persuaded
you are well situated as you
are—in the Spring, I will (God willing)
come on & place you in College. If
you are studious, you need not fear
an examination. I trust you will pass
it with honours to yourself.

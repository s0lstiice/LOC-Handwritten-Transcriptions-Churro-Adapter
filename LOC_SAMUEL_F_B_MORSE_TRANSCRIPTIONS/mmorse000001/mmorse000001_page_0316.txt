Nancy Shepard has been for a month
past on a visit to her sister at Amherst
we expect her home to night. All your
family are well I send you much love,
as also to the rest of the family. Remember
me very affectionately. I must now rejoice
to hear she is well, at home. I will ex-
ceeding to see her, as does Mrs Morse. We
shall expect a visit from her in due time.
I enclose her a small tract "To the Afflicted."
A violent N.E. storm has com-
menced I feel for vessels at sea.
I write in most hastened
as ever your affectionate
and faithful son
J. Morse
I have concluded to find Chester too
old for a year, the beginning of December.
I happen not to have so much money on
hand – hope he can get it in wood stock
& carry with him – to be P. beginning of
December. Wish you could get us a good
portion of butter, as Calvin disappoints us this fall.

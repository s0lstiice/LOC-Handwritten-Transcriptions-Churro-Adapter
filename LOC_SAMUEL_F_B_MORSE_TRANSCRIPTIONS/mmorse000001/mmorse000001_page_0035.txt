10 - two ought not to have voted, but
we reduce the majority to 8. We fear
they will get a President of the same
sentiments - in that case the character
of the College will be completely changed,
which we consider as an awful fault from
of Providence & we fear will be the means
of spreading extensively the most dangerous
errors. But the Great Head of the Church will
take care of his own cause. It becomes his
followers to stand in their lot, to do their
duty & leave the event.

Charlestown Jan 15. 1806
My dear Son I have received three letters
from you since my last they inform us
of your great solicitude to join your
class & get into College. We have con-
sidered your request, & though we are well
satisfied with your present situation,
& consider Mr Davis as one of the best
Instructors, & as having been of great
service to you hitherto, he have
pretty much concluded to gratify your
wishes, provided you can get up with
your class (as you can easily do, with
proper application before the end of
vacation) & you can get a steady,
direct roommate older than yourself,
such as Mr Shedman can recommend. Give my regards to Mr Ash-
man if he is in New Haven, & request
him to befriend you & me in procuring

Another event they have with great address
turned to their purpose. Mr Lovell has received & ac-
cepted a call to settle at W Boston, in place of Dr
Howard. Mr L is my friend & reputed philosopher he
wished to have me of the council to believe we have reason
to believe we'd have me of the Council been glad
to have me preach his Ordination Sermon. The canditate has
aright by custom to name half the ordaining Council. Yet
through the influence of my opposers, Mr L's urgent request was
denied to his mortification & to the Society by their Committee
contrary to all precedent, undertook, not only to appoint
else the Council, but to appoint Mr Ware to give the
charge & Mr Buckminster to give the right hand.
Their designs in all this are very apparent, to paralyze
at once compel Mr L to be on their side. I fear
he will not have fortitude enough to resist the
overwhelming torrent of influence though I have reason
to believe, his heart is not with them. The Committee tender all these
circumstances have invited me to attend the Ordination &
give with the Council, which I have of course declined. I
have detailed to you these particulars because they will
serve to give you a correct idea of my situation. When
my health is tolerably good, I have, feel as if I could bear
all this & more & that I will not gratify my opposers so
much as to quit my station. When my health fails
as it has done for a month I fear I will take part my spirits
sink & I long to be gone. My desire is to be in my own
station & in the way of my duty. I intend (by leave of
Providence) to bring myself to Philadelphia next spring

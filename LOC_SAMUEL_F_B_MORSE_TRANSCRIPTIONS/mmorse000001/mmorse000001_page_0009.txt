Andover Feb. 9 1804
Dear Parents,
As an opportunity now
offers I take the pleasure of informing you that
I received your letter & bundles safe & give
you great thanks for them, I gave that little
book to Mrs. Blanchard & she told me to thank
you for her. Our class has joined again & we
are all together in Ruge Dictums I have read considerable
in Plutarch & I am tell you the position
of Demosthenes which is as follows, History
of THE MSSINES
Demosthenes (the father of this Demosthenes) was
a citizen of great note among the Grecians & as
Theopompus writes he was surrounded (the sword
maker) because he had a great number of ser-
vants to work in that art. When Demosthenes
was but seven years old his father died &

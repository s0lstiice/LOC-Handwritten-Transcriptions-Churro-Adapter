Charleston Dec. 2d 1805
My dear Sir, I am indebted to you for two letters
the last one, last evening of the 27th ult. -- My health
has been very feeble for the last fortnight or
three weeks -- or I should have written you -- I
am now not well -- After passing through a
good deal of trouble & difficulty with the printer
& paper maker I have transferred the printing
of the People's Republic to Boston, I feel re-
lieved of a considerable portion of care -- I
expect the work will be more punctually printed
& distributed than it has been -- I feel the more
as you remark -- & we shall readily do what
we consistently can to adapt it to the taste
of your common readers but if we go too far
that way we shall loose on the other hand
& defeat our main object -- The work, to far
as I learn is very well wt. this way, & subscriptions
are increasing -- The Deficient numbers
you mention were sent on 10 days or a fortnight
ago, & I hope before this have been received --
The relations of the paper maker & printer

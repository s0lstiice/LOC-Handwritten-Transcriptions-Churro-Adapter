Charlestown Nov 21 1805.
Dear Brother:
I will now address you in a
few lines to inform you we are all well excepting
Papa.
The State-Prison is done and Mama is
going to see it with us before the Prisoners are
put in it which will be on December next
the 1st day of the month. It is Vacation now
in all its Beauties, the most pleasing Vacation
to I, ever had, Richard and I both study for
all it is Vacation and that makes it seem the
more pleasant, when I left Andover all the
sch ool were well.
I hope you will be contented with
your situation at New haven seeing when you
were at Andover you wished to go there, I would
try to bear it as well as I could and not go
into College as it is like enough if there are
any bad scholars they will try to make you
as bad as themselves.

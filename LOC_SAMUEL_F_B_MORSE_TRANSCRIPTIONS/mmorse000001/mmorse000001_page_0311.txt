Do write Dr L I wish the id of Union in Opposition to the Theological School -- I wish he could converse with
Dwight on this subject -- I know how much he desires the Union as indispensable to the maintenance of evangelical
truths in Wesleyanistes -- I have hope that if such an union could be cordially effected, & the evangelical strength of Majts concentrated,
we might yet effect bring about a counter revolution in our University, through the instrumentality of the Legislature by
an alteration of Charter -- a new board of Overseers -- Keep this Idea in your own bosom -- I cannot carry it into
your mind for the first time this evening -- I commit it to your contemplation -- Mr Quincy told me this eve

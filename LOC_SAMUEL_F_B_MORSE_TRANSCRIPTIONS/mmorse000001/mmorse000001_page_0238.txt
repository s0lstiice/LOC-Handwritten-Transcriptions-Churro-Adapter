The Panegylist is the only channel through which
we can, with effect, communicate such information
to the public from time to time, as may be necessary
to our purpose. It is the only weapon of
the kind, with our Opposers, fear. It is now
formidable, & must be made more so. I hope
you will still continue & increase your exertions
& support it. If there be a falling off
in some places, let exertions be made to make
up the Deficiency in others. Mr Baker of Gran-
ville, told me at Hartford that he believed there
were between 20 & 30 Subscribers in the several
parishes in that town whose names had not
been returned, & that he w'd see about it on
his return. Pray, collect all the information you
can on the subject, so as to be able to make arrangements
for the next year. Will not your
Society consent to purchase parts of the Panegylist,
with the profits they receive on the Work?
I wish they w'd as we have 200 on hand, & we
shall need to make great exertions to pay our
Debt. Do what you can to aid us.
My family is in tolerable health. My
repeating spring complaints are upon me, & compel
me for much service. Mrs M. writes in best
affections to both your son daughter
I self with your faithful
friend
Moore

Charlestown Dec 27th 1805.
Dear Brother:
This is the first time I have had
an opportunity of writing you, and I hope now
I shall write what you shall call a long letter.
I have been down to the State Prison
since it has been finished and all the rooms
excepting the lower cells are very comfortable.
The Prisoners are all very comfortable,
and they say they have better victuals than
they could possibly expect. One of them is sick,
but he is said to be the worst of them all. I
saw all of them at work outside of the walls
of the Prison a few days ago, in the Prison
dress which I suppose you know is red & blue.
This sick Man whom I mentioned with 5 others,
would have got out of Boston Jail, if they had
not been seasonably discovered by the Jailer.
Two of them have been put in the Prison upon suspicion of having taken stolen Goods, but
the Goods were found upon another person.

Yale College New Haven May 13th 1807
Dear Parents,
I suppose by this time you are in Philadelphia
& therefore I shall direct my letter there, I am very well, in
good spirits, & well contented with my situation, the term
to have next term begin & end. Mr Day (Tutor) set out
this morning I believe, for Washington & will continue there
during the vacation & therefore I cannot receive him as I
solicited however I shall study & get kind Mathematics.
Just now I asked Mr Twining to let me go a gaming
for this afternoon, he told me you had expressly forbidden
it & he therefore could not now I should wish to go once in
a while for I always intend to be careful, I have no
amusement now in the vacation & it would gratify me
very much if you would consent to let me go once in a
while. I suppose you would tell me that my books ought to be my
amusement, I cannot study all the time & need some exercise,
If I walk that is no amusement & if I wish to play ball or any
thing else I have no one to play with Please to write me an
answer as soon as possible. I think of nothing to write at
present. Please to give my respects to all my friends & relations who
are near me after me. I am haste Your affectionate Son
S. S. B. Morse
Wm. H. Lee & Mrs. E. H. Morse

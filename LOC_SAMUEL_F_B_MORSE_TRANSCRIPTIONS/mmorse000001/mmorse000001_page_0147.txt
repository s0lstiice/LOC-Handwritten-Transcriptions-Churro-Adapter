there is Opportunities of writing to us by private convenience and to
save and improve it. I intend sending you some Cambrick ruffles for your
three plain shirts & 2 shirts let be worn on them when your ruffles get ragged
let them be picked of and either wear them 2, though if the shirts are
good enough let them have ruffles on if you happen to have any by
you ready for the purpose which I will endeavour shall be the case.
Your Papa is not very well to day he had a tooth extracted yesterday
afternoon he will I hope be better soon Nancy is much better but
has not got down yet Mr Gardiner Spring lodged here last night he
had not seen or heard of you on his way here from New Haven being
particular in your account of your journey when you write us over
with such to know how you made out keep the best boys company
that you can find both in College and town and be a very good one
yourself that such boys may be happy to be your associates.
I must now bid you Adieu as I have to write to Aunt Abby by
Lr Green Nancy and the rest of the family send love to you from
Grain ever your
Affectionate Mother
E A More

Charles town July 17th 1805
My dear Sir, yesterday Genl Dickinson handed
me your good letter of the 10th of June I
had written you but last Friday (I believe
one letter before with you had not rec'd) I sent
by private conveyance to care of Mr Wm
Stampton enclosing 22 subscribers for Pano-
plist in Washington -- It is a comfort to
me, that the first No. meets your approbation, as
it has that of all who have read it this way
even its enemies are silenced -- I hope
you will be able to dispose of several
loo in Hampshire & Berkshire counties,
including what your society will distribute.
The work is gradually gaining ground here
amidst all the opposition of enemies &
coldness of friends -- I sincerely thank you
for your excellent hints -- they meet my
probatation, I shall be regarded, I have
not time to be particular -- I think we can
reach the work as good as the first number
I hope original matter will come from
your quarter -- Mr Taylor's hints are good -- we
are aware of our situation -- Dangers between
Lyella & Charley both Reviews will be

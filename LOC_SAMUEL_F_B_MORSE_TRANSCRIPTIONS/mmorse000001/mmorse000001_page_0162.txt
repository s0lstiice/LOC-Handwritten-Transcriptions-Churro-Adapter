My Dear Son
We had a letter from you dated
the next day after your arrival at New Haven which gave
us much pleasure. We were glad to find that you had no agreeable
a journey and so little difficulty in getting on we supposed
it would cost you more than we had contemplated as you
left Mr Backus so soon but it was perhaps best to do as you
did and go on in the Stage I was a little afraid of
your staying too long at Mr Marshes I hope you took care
not to give them any more trouble than you could not
possibly help and that you were particular in thanking
them when you left them for their kind attentions to
you be sure my son that you do not forget at all times to be
very gratefull to every body that treats you with any attention
and kindness whatever whether for your parents sake
or your own and take every opportunity of showing
that you are your feelings by being as polite and kind
to them as you can whenever you have opportunity always
keep in mind that excellent proverb "that those who
have Friends must show themselves Friends" - You have
received letters from us by Mr Green I presume before this I hope
you called on him while he & his son were in New Haven
and paid your respects to him & thank'd him for his
care of your letters & a pair of yarn socks which I sent you
by him. be sure and express your thanks to any person that

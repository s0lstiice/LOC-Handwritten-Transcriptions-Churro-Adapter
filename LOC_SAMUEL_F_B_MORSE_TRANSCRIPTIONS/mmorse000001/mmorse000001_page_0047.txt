To Dr. Good of Bedford.
Charleston Apr. 5th 1808
Dear Sir,
I have just received yours of yesterday.
The day you were here I meant frankly to
open to you the whole plan of the pamphlet
& mentioned the time when the first number
was to appear -- I concluded you understood
that the plan was determined on from
what you said I concluded you were willing
to read such foreign publications, as you
might be furnished with, I give your
opinion of them direct, at least, to such
parts as might usefully extracted & in-
cited -- that you would give your opinion
on any pieces sent for publication
which might be doubtful -- I hope, not
withstanding what you have written
me, you will at least be ready to
do so much to aid the work. I have
not time to answer your objections --
When I do you will find they remove
them I hope you will send your pieces on
the Debates & your observations on the
force for the work -- I have seen a
copy of your letter to Judge Chas Clay

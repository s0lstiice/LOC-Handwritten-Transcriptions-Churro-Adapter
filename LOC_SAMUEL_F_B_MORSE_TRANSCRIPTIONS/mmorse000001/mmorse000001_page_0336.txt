Yale College New Haven August 5th 1807.
Dear Parents,
You must excuse me for not writing you the
some time past as my time has been wholly engrossed
in my studies; I have scarcely any time for exercise, I
begin to feel the want of it very much. I hope I shall have
sufficient time in the vacation for all these things, as I have
nothing to do but to study now if I can truly say, I never stud-
yed so hard before; I begin to feel a pain in my breast &
weakness, which troubles me considerably.
To-day was the first time I declaimed in the
Chapel I was very much frightened at first but soon over-
came it; the students plainly perceive the defect in my
voice, the I think it is growing better every day, you will
be able to tell best as you have not seen me this some
time. I am very well excepting the forementioned pain
in my breast, enjoy good spirits I am more & more
delighted with the college... it is but 4 or 5 weeks now
to vacation & then I think I shall be not a little re-
joiced the scholars have drawn for choice of rooms & I
among the rest, my room mate had the first choice & I the
last, which was rather remarkable, but my choice is as
good as his, & better than the middle choice, as I have
the first room that is vacated. I have however chosen a
very good room that will suit me till next year &

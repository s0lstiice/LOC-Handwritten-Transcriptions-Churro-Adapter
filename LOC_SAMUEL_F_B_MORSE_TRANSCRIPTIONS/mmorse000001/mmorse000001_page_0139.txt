Mr. Beers' keep your penknife in your
desk, & then you will not lose it Your
Mama will send you a pair of socks by your
Dr. Green, who will carry this -- You will find
out where Dr. Green lodges I call on him very
respectfully -- I wish he to see you, not having
seen you since you were six weeks old.
He will stay but a day in St. Haven. This son
is with him who took his degree last month
at Princeton College.
The last note from your brother informed
that they were well -- Army has not yet got down
stairs, but will be well, we hope in a few days.
Estline died with my to-day -- Mr. Brown is licensed
to preach & delivered his first sermon here
this morning, to general applause -- The
State Prison is soon to be occupied by the
Criminals. Your Papa is appointed Chaplain
to the prison, & Dr. Bartlett Physician --
Chap. J. Ward, Gore, Pickman, to getter
with the Chaplain -- Physician, Visitors &
Chaplain Jackson Superintendant.
You may inform E. More that the body
he sent me have arrived in good order -- ask
him whether there I sent him, answer his
purpose, I will be sale able -- Send me the
catalogue of the clays when printed -- You
may write us once a fortnight by post -- &
offencer if you have private opportunities --
let your letters by mail be long & particu-
lar, & habituate yourself to write correctly
& in a fair & good land. -- Improve your
time diligently -- Remember it if you had time

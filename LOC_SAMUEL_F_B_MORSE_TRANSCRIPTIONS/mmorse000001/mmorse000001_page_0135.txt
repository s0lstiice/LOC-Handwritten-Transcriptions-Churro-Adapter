My Dear Son
By Capt Collins I send
you this with your trunk desk &
some bundles for Mr Backus, who
I hope you will receive safely –
We have put into your desk a pair
of slippers, which we suppose are
Mr Maxwell's you will inquire
of him if they are his, & hand them
to him – if not you may wear them
to study in this winter, if they
fit you – The bundle to Mr John
Marst, you will hand to him at
Dr. Dana's – I tell him the books
are a present from me to his
father, with I wish him to
present with my kind regards.

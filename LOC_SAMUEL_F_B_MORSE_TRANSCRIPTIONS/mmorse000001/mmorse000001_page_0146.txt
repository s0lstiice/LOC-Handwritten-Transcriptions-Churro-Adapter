Charles town at 23d 1805
My Dear Son
We had a letter from Mr. Backus last night dated
Oct 17th at Springfield he informed us that you left him at Boston
despite the care of a lady for Hartford that he had just heard
by the Stage driver that you were safely arrived at Major Ripley at
Hartford we were pleased to hear from you thus far we are now very
determined to know how you got along from there to Mr. Marshes and to
Newhaven hope you met with no uncommon difficulty getting to
your journey's end. You will receive this letter with one from your
Dada by Dr. Green & Son of Philadelphia who will spend one day
in Newhaven you will make a point of calling on him and
Asking after H, and devoting the favour of him to remember
you to your Aunt Abby & all your Philadelphia Friends I have
just seen Mr. Blanchard he has brought letters from your
Brothers who are well will send you Richard. You must write
them on the same sheet of paper that you write us and that will
save postage What money had you remaining when you got to N.H.
let us know and let us have a particular account from time to time

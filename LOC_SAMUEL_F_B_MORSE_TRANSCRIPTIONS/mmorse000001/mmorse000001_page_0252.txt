Charleston June 27. 1861
Mr Lyon has this moment
handed me your letter of the 21st We
are very happy to hear of your
safe arrival home & rejoice
with in the kindness of Provi-
dence in protecting & prospering
you. We tenderly feel for
Aunt Moor in her uncommon
trials & fervently hope as her
day of her strength will be
We are all well & send you
much love.

Charleston Oct. 1. 1866
My Son, I have only time, by brother Chris
to tell you we are all well - I have
been at St Haven Cont. as I presume
Mr Lyman informed you - We came
home, 160 miles in 30 hours - My health
was never better - & I have as much
to do as I can do - I shall not
be able to visit Woodstock this fall
having rode between 11 & 12,000 miles,
the last two months - I must now
set down to business to make up for
the time of my absence - Wish you
to write us as often as you can -
Pray is Aunt Moor come to Woodstock
yet? We want much to see her - I do
hear she is comfortable with you -
We all send Duty & love as usual -
Your affectionate Son
Joseph G. Moser

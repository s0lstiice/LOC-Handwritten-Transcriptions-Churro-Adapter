My dear Sir, I have received no answer to my
last letter to you. I regret that the books
did not go in season to answer your purpose they were ready for Butler & your
Son in law had they called for them I have
time carefully sought opportunity to send them but have found none – They are in a box
directed to Wm. Butler at White's Bookstore.
Orders are left to forward them by the first
team to Northampton Henry on Beecher
& Goddridge on Education are not yet out
of press they will do to distribute separately
& you had better make your Vol. of such as
you have – Is Vincent yet out? – I wish
to hear from you on many subjects. – If
Gov. Strong is doubtless re-elected unless it
legal votes, or too late returning, or some other
accident prevent – The Senate will probably be equally divided – It will depend on
the House what sort of Senate & Connell
we shall have – It will be a stormy Session
I think in before us – Our Nation alas how

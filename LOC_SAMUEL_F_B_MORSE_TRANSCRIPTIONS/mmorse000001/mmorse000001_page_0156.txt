My dear Son,
After what your Mama has written
I need not add much, as indeed I am too much
indisposed -- having been confined since Saturday
I am getting better, however -- I hope you
will make yourself contented in your present situation -- if Dr Dwight should
think it best to let you join your class
perhaps he will consent to let you
board at Mr Davis's for the Winter.
I may visit N.H. in the Spring, &
see you settled. In the mean time
you must bend your whole attention to
your studies, & by no means let your class
get before you. If you get into idle habits
you will be ruined -- Let us hear from you
immediately after receiving this I be as
particular as your mama requires. --
My regards to Mr Davis, whose health
I hope is better -- I remain your affectionate
Parent J. Morse

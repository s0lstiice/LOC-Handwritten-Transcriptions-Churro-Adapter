My dear Son, We have been looking for a letter
from you by the two last mails, being anxious to
know how you proceeded in your journey – We
shall hope to be informed very soon of your safe
arrival at N.Haven, & that you are agreeably sit-
uated. Your desk trunk &c with a letter are on
board Capt Collins, who I hope will reach N.Haven
this week – You will inform us as soon as
you receive it whether all the bundles
for Mr Barkur &c are safe at Mr Beers'
– You will give us a particular account of
your journey, whether you met with St Banks
or went on by stage in company with some
other person – inform us where you are lodged,
& who are your companions, & whether you
are well situated for study & who is your
Washer Woman & what books you have
got from Mr Beers' H– Keep an exact
account of all your expenses, & a journal
of the principal things you do every day.
Tell how you are pleased with New
Haven. Your things sent by Capt Collins
are to be left at Mr Beers' bookstore. I

the key of your desk is enclosed in the
letter sent by Capt Collins. I put some

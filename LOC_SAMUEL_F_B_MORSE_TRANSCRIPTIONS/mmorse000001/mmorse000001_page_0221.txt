in attending all College exercises - never

your expenses - & by all means keep
an accurate account of what you receive
& what you expend - This I expect
from you, & shall not dispense
with it. No excuse will be
need for loosing your account.
I enclose Mr Davis' letter which
you will deliver him - We are all
very anxious to hear from you.
Your brothers are very studious, attentive
good boys, & give us a great deal
of comfort. Your affectionate father
J. Monroe

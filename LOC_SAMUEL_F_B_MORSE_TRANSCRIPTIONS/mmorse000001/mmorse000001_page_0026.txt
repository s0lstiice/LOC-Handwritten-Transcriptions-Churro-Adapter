Charles Town Jan 16. 1805
Hon. I dear Sir,
I have this evening received
by Dr. Seagrave, yours of the 10th insta
with the turkey found out & thank you
for the pinkin of butter, which comes
very reasonably, as we are out
I have been purchasing at a dear
rate for some time all the stores.
We send you by Dr. Seagrave
two bottles wine, a few raisins
which are remarkably scarce, &
matinee for the comfort of your
Dear & languishing mother
I would gratify me to come
up I see you I have indeed
thought I talked of it but my
home is at Vermont with
brother Calvin to Winter &

Charlestown July 5. 1805
Dear boys, Though I wrote you two days
ago-(I sent Rich a pair of shoes) yet I
send you a line by Eskine Hazard-
Since the weather has been so very
warm -- it is so dry, windy & Dusty-
I presume you congratulate yourselves
that you are not down here, & obliged
to go back in all the heat I doubt-
You will enjoy yourselves the more
when you do come -- The house
opposite Mr White's bookstore
in Boston caught fire yesterday,
but was suppressed before it got
much head --
I send you up the beaches
of Watts, & Salem private, for you
to read -- I have sent Finley some
books also -- I wish you to keep them
very clean & nice -- cover them

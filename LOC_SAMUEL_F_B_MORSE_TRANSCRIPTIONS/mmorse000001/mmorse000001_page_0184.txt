My dear Son,
Your letter of the 18th was
received this evening & I received
our anxiety, which was great as we had
not heard from you for more than a
month before, not with standing our
defects in punctuities to write us
regularly once a fortnight by mail.
I fear my son you are too attentive to our
letters & inquiries of you -- Your Mama complains that
you are not at all particular in
answering her questions -- The next
letter you write Don't fail to read
over all our past letters to you,
& take time particularly to write
us a satisfactory answer to our
enquiries. Particularly whether
you rec'd the 5 dol. bill we sent
you by Erskine Hazard, & what
have been your expenses, & whether

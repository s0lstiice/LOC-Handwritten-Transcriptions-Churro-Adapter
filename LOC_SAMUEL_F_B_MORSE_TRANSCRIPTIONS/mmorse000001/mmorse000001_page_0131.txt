been such as have prevented my seeing you
since the decease of my honoured and

satisfy you, that my delay has not been
occasioned by a want of filial respect &
affection.
Though the weather has been
extremely warm & sultry, my journey
has been evidently conducive to my health
& that of Mrs Moser. We have had a
pleasant Commencement, & a charming
visit at Mr Marsh's where we now
are.
Today is the election, with so far, as
I can learn is likely to have favorably
to the cause of Federalism. -- I have
no special news to write you. You have
probably heard of a battle between the
English & combined fleets with Spud
favorably to the English -- We have had
the last three days, a plentiful sea-
table rain -- No sickness prevails
in this part of our country -- New York
is in great distress.
My Moser joins me in affectionate
regards to yourself, brother &
sister -- Hoping soon to see you I remain
your affectionate son
Genl. J. Moser

13 M.S.
Sec Gt Union Lit. p 36-
I have particularly, though very hastily stated the
whole business to you - that you might know my
situation - In your return I pray you to call on me
I presume your paying one hide will not oblige
you to pay all - & no one of his crediton I presume
has greater claim than myself
with esteem, & affection
I am your obedient Servant
Jed L Mosse

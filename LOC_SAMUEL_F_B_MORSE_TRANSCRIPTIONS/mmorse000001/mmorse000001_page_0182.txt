New Haven Dec. 18. 1805.
My dear Parents,
I now take my pen to write a few lines
by post, I hope this will be acceptable to you & will
find you well. I received your long letter & socks with a
letter from Aunt Abby Beeu some time ago, and on the
16 inst. a letter by post the 1st one that I have received from
you by post; in which you mention that my brothers will
stay at home this winter & am very glad for their sake
because they wish it very much when I was at
home; I feel a great affection for them and you &
long to see you. I hope my brothers will have the
pleasure of giving this to you as they wish to get
one from me very much I am glad to hear that
the corporation have chosen a President; the sickness
in New-Haven is very much abated one of the
students died yesterday of the bilious fever his name was
William B. Peck of Norwich a relation I believe
of Mr. Lathrop of the same place, he was a member
of the Junior Class his funeral was this afternoon. I am
in very good health and have been since I have been here
I shall not go out to gun any more for I know it
makes you anxious about me. There was one of the
scholars expended a few days ago for rolling logs down
stairs his name is Shelton; I am sorry Papa & Mama
have been unwell & glad that they and Nancy have
got well give my love to Nancy & tell her I will
write her as often as time will admit & also to S.D. Lead
when you see him and tell him I will write him soon
my clothes are done up neat my washing, ironing & mend-
ing are done well: the muffles that you sent on by
Erskine have not been served on the muffles on 2 of my
skirts are very ragged and have been taken off. I have
received the letters by Mrs. Webster. Thanksgiving day
I spent as I did other thanksgiving days at Charlestown

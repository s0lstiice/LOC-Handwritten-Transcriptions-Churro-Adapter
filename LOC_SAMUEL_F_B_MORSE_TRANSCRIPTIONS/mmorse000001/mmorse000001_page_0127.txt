I expect you will receive this letter.
I am your affectionate
Grandson, Edwards Morse.
Now, sir.
We are about to set out for
Phil.a tomorrow in the stage. We meant
to have gone as far as Woodstock in
our own chaise, & to have left the
chaise at home, with you till our
return but for reasons, which
I will mention when I see you
we have thought best to go by
stage from home, & pay you our
visit on our return, 4 or 5

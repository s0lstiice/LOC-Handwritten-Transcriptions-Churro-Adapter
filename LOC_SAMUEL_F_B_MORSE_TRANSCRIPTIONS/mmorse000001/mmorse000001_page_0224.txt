Dear Son
I am in great hopes that your Papa would be with
you before this or I should have written you before that but the
traveling is so bad that I have not when he will be able to see you
I hope you feel easy and respects going into College as your Papa
has presented that you should go in and join your Class and want
get the favour of Mr. Steidman to appoint you to get you a good
chair, that will not lead you astray in any way by bringing
travagant with Wood or Candies or anything else think you to
get a worker woman to do your Nails that will not change so
much as 4 1/2 Mr. Dozen artificer is a very extravagant piece
Mr. Ely says he did not pay so much by a great deal as that when
he Bar in at Havana he had his Washing done by the piece
so much by the shirt 8 1/2 for a pair of stockings 1/2 cent for a
handkerchief and so on. You must keep a Book and set down
your expences in every Artificial in it that we may see what
We pay our money for and to leave you to keep your own Acc
ounts that you may be able to make an estimate of the expense
of your Education and leave the value of money I beg that you would
be an Acceptant on your Brothers will not be able to join
you. one thing I hope that you would avoid and that is hav
ing any thing to do with raining to the buttons as they have a
great price for Truffles and on no Consideration dont have any skill
to pay there I am told that one way to find out who are able

P.S. Since writing this I have received Briefs, Georgia Minor o &
Testament, but no letter.
June 13th 1807.
It is very necessary that I should room in college as soon
as possible, I am absent from prayers & meditation very often
my roommate has not got back, my watch is out of order,
& I cannot hear the bell I wish that you could conveniently
send on my things that I shall need to room in college
such as bed & bedstead & bed clothes, & I will take care
of the rest, it is very necessary to my staying in college
that I should have a room there
In haste &c
your
son
Geo

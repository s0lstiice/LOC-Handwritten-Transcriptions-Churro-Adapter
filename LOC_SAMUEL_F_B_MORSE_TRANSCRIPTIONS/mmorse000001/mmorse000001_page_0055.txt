After these few remarks, I have to thank
you Sir for your ingeniousness & disposition to
do in this case what is just & honourable If
any thing would induce me to permit the fall
of this work under existing circumstances it would
be the manner in which you have presented the
subject for new consideration. I am ready to meet
you on this fair ground. I wish not to injure
Mr Doyle he is a stranger to me. Had he merely
made an epitome of Pinkerton with might have
been done, I conceive with advantage (I hope?
don't, however, whether Mr D. has the proper talents
to execute such a work well) I should have had
no objection to it.
It will be equitable, I conceive according to the decision of the court before
mentioned, if I receive such part of the profits of the work as is proportioned to
the matter taken from my Log. 7-1/2 or 2/3d.
to be left to Mr Bondinot, Green Litho Man
gand.
Sent the above with alterations & additions,
May 1st 1805.

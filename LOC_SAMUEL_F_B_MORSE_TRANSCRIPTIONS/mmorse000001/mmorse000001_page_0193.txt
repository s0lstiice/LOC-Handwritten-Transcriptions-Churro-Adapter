influence of this College, which has been great, must
now cease, and probably become pernicious for how
long a time to come God only knows. When I think
of the pious & venerable founders of this Institution
their prayers & tears for its prosperity, and the
present perversion of their hard-earned benefactions,
I am grieved to the heart, & I wish some competent
to the task would portray the shameless conduct
of those, who have been the instruments of
this perversion, & publish it to the world. Perhaps
circumstances may hereafter render it necessary;
and we possess ample documents.
Every day events occur which convince
a determination, to mystify & put me down,
as they call it. Till this year, I have been invited to
the "Feast of Shells" instituted to commemorate
the virtues & principles of our pious Forefathers.
This year I have been neglected, & one of their
tracts was aimed particularly at me -- They have
failed however in their object in this neglect
& I venture to find it that they have given a death-
wound to this festival. Indeed there is the most palpable
inconsistency between the design of this festival
& their conduct towards me in respect to the
College. They are active in uprooting those very
principles & virtues which they profess to celebrate. I am
astonished that the inconsistency of their conduct does not shake
them in the face
The Papalist maintains its ground & increases its reputation, circu-
lating in the press the impression that it has obtained its effect & knows not how
to counteract them.

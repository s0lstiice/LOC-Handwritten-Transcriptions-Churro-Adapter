tomorrow morning we propose to visit the
Saratoga Springs, whose waters are said to be better
than those of this place. They are only 7 miles from
Baltimore. I am now going to call on Mr Har-
per who I learn is here & will finish my letter
on my return.
I have just had a pleasant interview with Mr
Happer, I was introduced to Mrs H. who is an invalid
& if able will visit Boston before her return to Baltimore.
I hope the boys are very attentive to your & all
my wishes. I have not been a state of enjoyment. I hope
however it has not been a sort of relaxation which will
conduce to health.
Believe me my dear to be with great sincerity your affectionate
husband Morse

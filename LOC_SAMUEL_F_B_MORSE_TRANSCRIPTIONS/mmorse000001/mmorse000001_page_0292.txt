Mr Malcom's only child is quite sick -- they are
anxious about it having buried a fine child.
Tomorrow I am to dine with Mr Knowlson to
spend a lecture for him at 4 O' C. P.M. -- I have
mentioned every thing that I can recollect, except
that we have had a letter from Sam Hilarion
on his way to Magasin -- He will be back probably
next week -- may find his parents here -- I will
write in much love to you -- We talk much of your
visit -- I expect my leave of absence to be
next Sabbath -- My love to the
members of the family -- I will tell my friends, who ask after
me -- particularly to Mr. Wilkins Wood, who
I hope will be in Charlestown when you receive
this -- I am too much fatigued to add
your every affectionate
husband J. Morse
If you write me immediately after receiving this
(if it goes directly) to Northampton, to the care of Rev. Mr Williams,
I shall probably receive it I wish you to do it -- I shall
probably be at Northampton about the middle or close of
next week -- All are in bed, but myself -- Good night
Wednesday morning -- All well -- The rain this
morning may prevent getting this letter
to Utica -- I am on for the mail, with closes at 8 o'clock

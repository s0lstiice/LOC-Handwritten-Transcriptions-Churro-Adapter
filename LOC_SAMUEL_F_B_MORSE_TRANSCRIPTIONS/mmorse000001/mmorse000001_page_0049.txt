Charlestown Apr. 27th 1805
Hon Sir, I received this day week
the melancholy intelligence of
the death of my dear Mother, an
event which I have for sometime
been expecting It is a consoling
circumstance that her departure
was so easy, & that we have
but good hope, through grace
that her immortal spirit has
gone to rest in the bosom of
her God & Savioress You Sir,
& all of us have great cause
for thankfulness that she has
been preserved to you so long,
& that she has left behind
her that good name
which is better than precious

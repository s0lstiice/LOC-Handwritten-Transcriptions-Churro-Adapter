he got beat off and especially by Alexander
who order'd (after he conquerd Demosthenes)
twelve of the Orators, of whom Demosthenes
was one but after wards he said he would be
content with eight only, an ambassador was
sent to demand pardon which was readily given
by Alexander. sometime after Aristophanes
came to Athens who was a great seducer he
had a silver cup of a certain king with
which he tried to seduce Demosthenes & setting
it on the table as he dined with Demosthenes
Demosthenes ey'd it with attention
asked him how much he would give him for it
he said five talents which he took & gave him
the cup & the next night he was seized with
a Quinsey & could not speak, & as he went
into the senate house, the senate desired him
to get up & speak, but he made signs to
them that he could not speak and the people
began to ridicule him & said the Orator had
no other disease than that of a Silver Coinsey.
Soon after this he went to war with Cratinus
& the Exile Flautus

Dear Sir,
Permit me to interrupt you
by a request, which I know not to whom
else I may make, it is if it should
be in your power to learn of the Booksellers
what it will cost, (cash in hand)
to get a set of pages (stereotype) for the
New Testament, similar to those used
by the British & Foreign Bible Society
published at Cambridge, a year or two
since, will you have the goodness to inquire
about this & let me know, for as soon as
government will permit I intend to purchase
such a set for this town. The folks are in general well
"Finley Morse's Letters" are often quoted
in the Cucles I go into. Command me
in any thing with freedom.
Should Dr Buchanan (to whom I
write by same conveyance as this is sent)
or Miss More, or Mr Wilberforce publish
any thing this spring shall I ask you
forward a copy in Board the 87th
since I will pay to your Father.
Believe me yours sincerely
Samuel T. Ambler
Mr. S.B.F. morse.

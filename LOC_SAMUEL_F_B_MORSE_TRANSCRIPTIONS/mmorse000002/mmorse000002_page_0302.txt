Charleston Jan 18th 1812
My Dear Son
Your Papa and both your Brothers are at
this moment engaged in writing you by the Ship
Plato which sails to-morrow for London
I have written you a long letter which was to have
gone in the Sally Ann Captain Holmes for Liver-
pool sometime ago but we find by the papers
that she sailed this morning Your Papa has writ-
ten you a few lines by her but we kept the largest
packet to go in this vessel direct for London we
hope she will have a quick passage.-- Mr Codman
lodged here a few nights ago We read him your
letters he was much pleased with them and that
you went to church in Swallow Street he says
the minister is an excellent man and that he will
write him and introduce you to him you must be
sure and be acquainted with him and attend constantly
on his ministry Mr C says he wishes he was with
you. they plague his life out where he is but he will give
superior to them he is a good man sends his love to you
as do all your Friends whom you have mentioned in
your letters we read your letters so often to our Friends that

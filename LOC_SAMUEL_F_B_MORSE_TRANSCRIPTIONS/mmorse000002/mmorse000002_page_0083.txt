mination to pay for every thing as I got it, but
was stopped in this at the very beginning for in going to Mrs.

for a week or 2 weeks, & once he was absent 6
weeks and made no sort of provision for us. Mrs.
T is never trusted with money for us: now in such
cases I am obliged by necessity to get a thing cheap
and I have found by sad experience that a Bill

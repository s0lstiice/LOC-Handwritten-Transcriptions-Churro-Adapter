(No 2)
My beloved son
We have just rec'd your 12th
letter by Miss Lyman's from Phila (13th not rec'd 14th)
rec'd & answered last week by Brig Thomas, Liverpool)
which gave us most pleasure. The embargo
to effect last Friday & I now write, you a
hasty letter to go to New York, & then a to you
by the British Packet -- this must be our only
channel of communication to you for 90 days
possibly longer -- Your turn however will not be
stopped -- we expect letters of by Mr Parkman,
who de arrival is hourly looked for -- Your wishes
in regard to the convenience of our letters to you
by letter bag have for some time been complied with, & this too with the knowledge of
your well wishes to pay, the increased expense
by substituting water for beer with your
meals, till paid. We will not hold you obligated
to do this -- or to make any other sacrifice
than is necessary to bring your expenses within
your income, or than is consistent with sound
& correct economy. It is very gratifying to us
& a great relief to our minds to find you
determined to be an economist because useful

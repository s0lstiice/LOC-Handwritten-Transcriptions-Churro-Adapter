1809 Yale College New Haven March 3d.
My Dear Parents,
I now sit down with pleasure to write you a short letter. I have not received one from you this long time, I am trying to conjecture a reason for this long delay but cannot, I hope nothing has happened which you are afraid of informing us of, however I ought to know better than to think that any serious circumstance should happen relative to the family or the public, without immediate information of the same from some of you I am in daily expectation of some news from home I hope I shall not be deceived.
I informed you in my last letter that Wells my former roommate had lost his mother and only sister by this terrible fever which rages so much near this place, late news from Hartford informs us that he has lost his only brother in addition to these, and it was currently reported here a few days since that he himself was dead; however this was a false report, although he runs a great risk of his life, his father is the only person left of the family. This fever is really a horrid thing, a person is dead, at the farthest period 24 hours after he is taken, it is now within 12 miles of this place, and raging all over this state in every direction; if it makes its appearance in this city in a single instance, college will without doubt be dismissed, do you must not be surprised to see us home if that is the case, for there is no place in the country where we shall be free from danger.

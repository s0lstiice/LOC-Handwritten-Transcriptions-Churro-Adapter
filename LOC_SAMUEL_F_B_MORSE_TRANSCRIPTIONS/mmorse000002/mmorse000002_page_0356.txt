We observe that you do not mention Mrs. Astor in your last letter. You will take care always to
mention them particularly in future that I may inform their friends, who ask after them. Request him to write
me, I remember us particularly to them and to all others who ask after us, especially to Mrs. West. Dr. Lettsome. I hope
before this you will have delivered all your letters. You do not have delayed them so long as the gentlemen
think themselves neglected. You do not mention having received Mr. Van Hope's last fall
draft on him. It is mentioned in your letter by Miss Hyman's. I know not when I shall be able to send you more,
as we every day expect an embargo, I expect, war with Great Britain but I hope the elections of this day, will
convince our distracted government, of the inexpediency of folly of such a measure. The state of this
country was never more critical. The result of today's elections will produce almost important effect but
only on this State, but in our country. The language is strong. Peace, Gen. of War. I believe this correct. I have 3 neat copies of
your letter. I shall send you by this opportunity the proofs of the American Geog., new Edttn., as far as printed.
A few sheets will complete it, and I shall send as soon as I have offshl. -- I wish you to confer with Mr.
Smith on the subject of securing in your own name the copy right of the work in Eng. -- I engage
Badell, or some other bookseller to print the work, I make you a proper allowance. In this way
you may get something to support you it may be handomely, without depending on me. Before it is printed in Eng. -- particularly the Art. of the U. States, you
may want to receive some additions & corrections

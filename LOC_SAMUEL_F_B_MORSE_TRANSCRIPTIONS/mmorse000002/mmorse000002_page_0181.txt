I have a great deal to tell you, which I must
defer till I arrive in London
I shall deliver my letters tomorrow
to Mr. Woolsey & Mr. Thornley. Good Bye
I again assure you I am in perfect health
& in excellent spirits. Give my best
love to my brothers (who may possibly be at
home when this arrives) & to all the family,
Nancy &c. &c. to Miss Russell, Uncle &
Aunt Salisbury & in short to all inquiring
friends; Oh how I wish you knew at
moment that I am safe & well in the
England. Good Bye to write soon &
often as I shall.
Your very affectionate Son
Sam'l J. B. Mosson

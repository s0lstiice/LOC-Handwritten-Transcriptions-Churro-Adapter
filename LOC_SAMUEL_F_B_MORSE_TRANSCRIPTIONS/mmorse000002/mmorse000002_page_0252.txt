the Lord Mayor opens the fair with some ceremony
which I did not see, the lower orders of society are let
loose on those days and are allowed to amuse themselves
in any lawful way they please; their principal amusement
was swinging, the swings were large frames in which
a box capable of holding 4 or 5 is suspended, there were
upwards of 300 of these to speak within bounds, placed
round Smithfield market, in which place the fair is always held;
it almost made me dizzy to see them all
in such a variety of motion; as there was no room
to spare they were huddled up as close together as
they could get them without striking each other, another
machine was the wheel such as is sometimes seen on our
election days, & another was a wheel revolving parallel to
the ground on the circumference of which were the figures
of the heads of horses, & saddles; & in fact every
variety of motion which you can conceive of, in the centre
of the square was every thing, in the world, theatres,
luscious nature, wild beasts, & tame four footed & two, mountebanks,
buffoons, ventriloquists, dancers on the slack wire,
tumblers, fighting & swearing, pocket picking & stealing,
music & dancing & hubbub & confusion such as I never
witnessed before; the theatres were temporary buildings
all hung round with tapestry, & adorned & illuminated
on the outside with various colored lamps; strolling
players generally occupy them, & between the plays

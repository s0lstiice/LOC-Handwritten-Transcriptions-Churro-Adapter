I wrote you about a month since & did then, what you wish me now
to do - I mentioned all that is worth mentioning, which by the way is
very little about New Haven's inhabitants - Since then I have been to
New York & saw the Misses Keadleiffs & in passing through Stamford the
Mrs. Ravensfells -- The mention of the name of Bavenport would at one
time have excited in your breast emotions unutterable, but now though
there is as lively as ever, your heart requires the influence of another heart
to quieten it perturbations -- The sight of these girls brought to my mind
senses to agreeable even to return -- they were well & I suppose happy till
S. Hilthase that time is as lively & as blooming as ever -- as to the girls
that are left in New Haven I know little about them -- The Miss Suffle I never
visit & seldom meet Mary S. U. I have not even seen her in two
months Mrs. C. Mills is at present in New York when she has been all
winter as I suppose nothing & about forty being constantly -- her train -- The Miss West of
an as usual at home & when seen as lively & agreeable as ever -- last

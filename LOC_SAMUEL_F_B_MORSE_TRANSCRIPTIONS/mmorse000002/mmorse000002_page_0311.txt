Much is said about dear old Great Britain but I have no belief that the Govt. is
in earnest in the business, notwithstanding all that is said in Congress that looks that
way. Our legislature, as you will see by the Govt. speech, & the doings of the legislature
are prepared to go great lengths. They have inserted an entering wedge in regard to the
College (by calling on the Corporation for a statement of the College funds) what will be their
future course, cannot be foreseen. They keep their plans to themselves.

London. Sept.r 14. 1811
My Dear Parents,
I have just five minutes to write
you one line; a gentleman is going imme-
diately to Liverpool to sail in the Lydia, for
Newyork and has offered to take a letter form,
so, as I know it would be a pleasure to
hear from me any way I sat down to write
you this very hasty line; I am extremely
well in good spirits, industrious & econom-
ical. I thought of Sidney Edwards last
Wednesday throughout the day sometimes im-
aging him on the stage & then taking his
degree congratulate him for me on his back-
cloths, he must write me an account of
the commencement. I wrote you by the
Penn for Boston very long & particular letters
wh. I hope you have received in them.
Good bye, my love to every body
Your affectionate Son
Sam'l F. B. Mosse

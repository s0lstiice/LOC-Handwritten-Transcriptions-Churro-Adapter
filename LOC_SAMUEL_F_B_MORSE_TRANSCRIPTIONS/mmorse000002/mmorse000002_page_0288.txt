Charleston Jan 6th 1812.
Dear Brothers.
I write by Capt. Holmes who sails tomorrow morning for Liverpool. I am at present
employed in preparing Geography for Papa -- His next edition will be out in a few months
and it is as much as both of us can do, to supply the press with copy. Richard is at
New Haven & when I left there, was as well as usual, excepting, that he was at times a
little nervous. We were very much pleased with the long letters which you sent us; but were
sorry to hear by your last of the attacks of rheumatism and cholic. Mama has written
you a long letter on that subject, and given you cautions and prescriptions and antidotes
&c. It rejoiced us much that thou wert admitted into the Royal Academy & I congratulat
thee upon it, inasmuch as thou didst me

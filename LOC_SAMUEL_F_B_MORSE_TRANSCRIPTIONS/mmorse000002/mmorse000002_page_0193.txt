After a most delightful journey thro' a very beauti-
ful country, I arrived in this great city about
6 o'clock this afternoon; and am now writing you
from the York Hotel in Bridge street, Black
Hriars, within a few minutes walk of the
famous cathedral of Saint Pauls, and in full
view of it, & also within a few rods of Black
Hriars Bridge I feel perfectly happy, in
perfect good spirits, & quite at home; I also
feel increased enthusiasm, & long to begin again
to paint. -- Mr. Allston has just returned
from Mr. Wests, & Mr. West will be very
glad to see me tomorrow, his great picture
is much talked of, it is pronounced by the
greatest connoisseurs to be the best ever painted
in England, to give you an idea, what an
opinion the people generally have of it, I will
tell you what Mr. West told Mr. Allston, viz.
that the exhibition of the picture, had produced
to the British Institution, for whose benefit
it was exhibited, upwards of 9000 pounds

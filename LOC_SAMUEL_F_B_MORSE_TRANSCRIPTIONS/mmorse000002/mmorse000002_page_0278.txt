[Dec. 18, 1811]
Mr. Sam: T. B.Morse
Titchfield Stt.
Dear Sir
My engagements contrary to intention, have prevented sending the Six Payments
requested in yours of 16th inst. which are now enclosed in Six Bank Notes and in doing
so I have been led to cast my eye on the sum that have been paid & to contrast them with the
appropriation made by your Hon'rd. father for the year, consisting of the two Bills that have
been received £205.13.6 the cost of which I have the pleasure to inform is accepted.
The sum already paid including what is now enclosed amounts to £110.10.— in the course of
four Months, the Residue 95.3.6 consequently must be divided between the remaining Eight Months,

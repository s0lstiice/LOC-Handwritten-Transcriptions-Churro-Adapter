I take this opportunity, my dear Sir, to acknowledge the receipt of the "Signs of the
Times" which you were so obliging as to send me; and at the same time to return
you my thanks, not only for that instance of politeness, but also for the many decla-
rations of friendship which you have conveyed to me through the medium of
Mr. J's correspondence. -- Be assured, my dear Mr. Morse, that I feel a strong
desire to see you prosperous and happy, and a great esteem for that sincerity,
and frankness, and amiability of Character which you so uniformly exhibited
while I had the pleasure of seeing you. I go the next month to N.York, and if
I can there or elsewhere be of service to you, it will give me unsuigned satisfac-
tion. I beg you to believe that I am,
with every sentiment of esteem,
Your sincere Friend,
Sam'l H. Jarvis.
New Haven March 31st 1811.

Do not think it improper that I should have them now once in a whiles nearly
2 thirds of the scholars spend double of the money that I suppose things, & I suppose some of them do not spend so much; But this I know by experience that the more I study the less I am inclined to desire such things. I hope you will find by my expenses (of which I have kept an accurate account) that I have not been so extravagant as I was last term, & if there are any things which you think I might have dispensed with I will amend in future; in the vacation I will send you a list of my expenses
We sleep very comfortable I am warm, have plenty of clothing. We are early to bed & early to rise, which makes us healthy, wealthy & wise.
The season here is very mild we have had no snows, & in many places the grass is quite green, it seems more like spring than winter.
I enjoy myself very much, feel quite contented & very well pleased with my studies; The past term (for it is almost gone) seems to have run away very fast, & if the next passes as fast, I think I shall be at home in less than a week. Remember us to Richard in particular, I wish I had time to write to him but he must excuse me I believe as I have so little time to spare; if he feels home sick at all he must think of the great distance we are from home, & must think how he will feel when he comes on to college. Remember us particularly to Grandma Ma & Aunt Abby, & to "excusing" Mr. & Mrs. Woodroof & in short to all who are solicitous for our welfare & enquire after us.
It is almost bedtime do I think Somers is alone the christle, & bid you Good Night
Please to execute the writing of Blots
I remain your affectionate Son
S. F. B. Morse.
Rev'd W. J. Morse Charlestown

I hope you will write to Doctor Dwight on the
subject of my rooming with Washo, I shall have to
room with some of my own class who are disagreeable
fellows, unless, I can be so fortunate as to get Wan for
a roommate; I wish you would attend to this subject
as it interests me very much.
I wrote you some time since concerning some
books for Mr. James Dwight but I have heard nothing
of the fate of the letter which contained the request.
I be wished me to inform you that you need not send
them now, as he has got them from NewYork. They
were concerning the Theological Institution.
There is no news in NewHaven, I hear nothing but complaints
of the "scandalous embargo"; I have received no papers from
you if late; I should be gratified to see some of Boston
news.
Please to write us soon and often this vacation
as it will serve to heighten our spirits, as they are a
little depressed at the approach of a vacation which
we are not destined to enjoy in I find it a difficult
task to do nothing." I shall be employed in the vacation
in the Philosophical Chamber with Mr. Dwight who
is going to perform a no. of experiments in Electricity.
Next term the studies will be more difficult
than at present we shall study Dialling, Spheres,
Gracia Majora, (which by the way is a hated book here) and
Philosophy; besides an o of small duties such as, disputing
declaring, composing, &c &c. so you see that I have
my hands full.

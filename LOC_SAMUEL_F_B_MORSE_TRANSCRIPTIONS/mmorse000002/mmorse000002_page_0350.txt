unapproachable, he is a man of tender feelings, but of a mind
so noble that it soars above the slanders of his enemies,
and he expresses pity, rather than revenge, towards those
who wantonly or malice, plan to undermine his character;
no man perhaps ever passed thro' so much a wise
and none I am confident ever born up against its evidence
with more gallbladder of spirit; with a steady perseverance
in the sublimest of human professions, he has towered
on the heads of the sweres, the ridicules, the delirations
of his enemies, he has arrived at that height of fame, and
when the lustre of his works, will not fail to illuminate the
regards of barbarism and distaste, till langue de l'ambre
author be long after exists. Excuse my fervour in the praise
of this man, the is not a common man, not such a one as
geniuses, who are doomed in their life time to endure, the malice
the ridicule, and neglect of the world, and at their death
to receive the adoration of this same in consistent world, then
cannot be a stronger proof that human nature is the same in
all ages, and that men of genius are compelled to undergo
the same disappointments and to pass thro' the same routine of
calumny & abuse!

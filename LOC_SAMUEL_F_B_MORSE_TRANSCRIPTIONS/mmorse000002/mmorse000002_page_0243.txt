Last Sabbath I went to Saint Paul's & heard
every good a good sermon, but what struck me as very
singular, was the constant change in the congregation
some coming in & others going out, thro' the whole service
the sermon was at least well distributed; it reminds
me of Professor Sittman's explanation of the proverb of
boiling, when he says that on a particle of water at the
bottom of the vessel, in his lectures a particle of heat, & then
another particle of water comes, it does not till every
particle has taken its place -- Over the left entrance
into the church is the epitaph of Sir Chris. When
the celebrated architect, & builder of St. Paulo
you have probably heard of it, it is short & fulthy.
It runs thus, Subitus conditor hujus ecclesia et
annos supra nonaginta, non sibi, sed bono publico.
Lector si monumentum requisit, circumspice.
The latter sentence struck me as remarkably fine -- I have not yet visited

both may use it in a distinction, I felt
anxious to secure your correspondence while
in Europe, sensible that such a
correspondence must be highly useful to
both, provided both attain to some degree
of excellence, and must qualify us to be
more useful to our country.
Before I close this, let me charge you,
not to cast my pack before mine. This
letter is not for vulgar eyes.
I am to give my compliments to your
son.
I shall expect an answer soon.
I remain yours, with growing esteem and attachment.
Wm P. Rice
Mr S. F. B. Morse.

Receipt to make good Liquid Blacking.
Take 1 quart of sharp vinegar
1/4 oz. of Gum Arabic
1 gill of Molasses
2 ozs. Sweet oil
2 ozs. Sulphuric Acid
1/2 lb Ivory Black
Remember to pour in the Sulphuric Acid last as that
will set it a boiling, be very careful not to let any of
the Acids touch your clothes or hands, as it will burn them bad-
ly. After it is mixed you can either keep it in a bot-
tle or a bowl, the Ivory black will keep at the bottom
remember when you use it to stir it up well; the virtue
of this blacking is that it requires less brushing than
the common liquid blacking, & there is a fine gloss
remaining on the boot or shoe, it is also cheaper than
any other, 1/6 will get all that is necessary for making
a quart.

18. 3000 persons -- I had the pleasure a few
day since of witnessing one of the oddest exhibitions, perhaps, in the world; it was no other
than St. Bartholomew's fair; it is held here in
London, once a year & continues three days,
there is a ceremony on opening it by the Lord
Mayor which I did not see, at this fair the
lower orders of society are let loose, and allowed to amuse themselves in any lawful way
they please, the fair is held in Smithfield
market about the center of the city, the principle amusement appeared to be swings, 9,
there were large boxes capable of holding 5
or 6, suspended in large frames in such a
manner as to vibrate nearly thru a semicircle,
there were, to speak within bounds 300 of them,
they were placed all round the square, & it almost made me giddy, only to see them all in
motion, they were so much pressed for room, that
one of these swings would clear another but about 2 inches, and it seemed almost miraculous to me that they did not meet with more accidents than they did -- another amusement were
large wheels, about 30 or 40 feet in diameter, on

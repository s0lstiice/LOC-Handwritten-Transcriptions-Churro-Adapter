40 Guineas, and I have to find my own bedding
which will be a mattress, pillow, two pillow cases
and two pairs of sheets the difference of which will
be about 10 dollars as Capt. Stout says, this has already
been the custom, the bedding is afterwards sold when
we get to Liverpool -- I shall not have money
enough to carry me to London: I will tell you
before I leave this place what it will cost me &
where I get the money. Capt. Stout has offered to give
me any sum for a draft on your insurance. I shall fall
at Mr. Whitings & shall avail myself of his offer.
I found Capt. Stout's family in trouble on
account of the death of Mrs Marx. who died in child-
hood last Tuesday & was buried on Wednesday.
The weather here is exceedingly hot; the thermometer
was in the shade at 93 on Thursday's & on being
taken into the sun rose to 116: yesterday the thermo-
meter in the shade was at 98 -- Six people
died yesterday on account of drinking cold water
when very hot & I saw one today in the
door of a house just gone from the same cause.

Charleston July 1. 1811
Sir I take the liberty to introduce to
you my eldest Son, Sam'l Finley Morse
Morse, A.B. who visits Europe, for the
purpose of perfecting himself in the
art of painting, for which he has
a taste. His intention is first, to
place himself under the instruction
of our celebrated country man,
Sir Benj. West & afterwards to
visit France & Italy. He
he visits France during your re-
sidence there, permit me to solicit
for him your protection & patronage,
& an introduction to such artists, Men
of science as may be useful to him
& you will lay under particular obli-
gations, Sir with high consideration
Your Oldt Sentt Ledt Morse

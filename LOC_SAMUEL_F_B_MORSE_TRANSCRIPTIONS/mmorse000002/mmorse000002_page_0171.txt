Charleston July 18th 1811
Dear Sir,
I expect this will be handed you
by my eldest son, who left the country on Saturday last for London (via Liverpool) where he proposes to reside two or three years under the
direction of Sir Benj" West, other Masters in painting, for the purpose of improving himself
in that art, having chosen it as his profession. This son of mine has just entered his 21st year,
he completed his education under Dr. Dwight, at Yale College, last September, & sustains
a respectable standing in his class, & goes to your country with a fair, humble, finished moral
character in the common acceptation of that phrase. Though we have no evidence of
his piety, he has ever manifested a high veneration for the Christian religion & its Institutions.

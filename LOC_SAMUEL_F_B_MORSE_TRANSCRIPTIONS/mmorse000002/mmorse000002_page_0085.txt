Charleston July 14. 1810
Dear boys I write you a hasty line
by Mr Finly N. Wood & Mr Thomas
Hurd who are going into New York
through N. Haven. Wish you to pay
them attention by showing them the
Colleges & whatever is to be fun of
importance to them – Introduce
them to Dr. Dwight, as children of
his old friends Col. Wood & Mr
Hard. – Don't fail to do this – Tell
Dr. D. that Mr Bradstreet died
suddenly yesterday afternoon. –
I send the cloth for Finley's clothes
by Mr Wood. – I have requested him
if quite convenient to let you have
50 dolts & he leave it give
$20 to Stafford – with the other

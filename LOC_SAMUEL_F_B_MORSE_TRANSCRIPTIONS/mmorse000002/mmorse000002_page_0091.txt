I went to see Mr. F. about it and he told me
that it always happened to be out when
he called (and Mr. Twining never will advance a cent if
he can help it.) I am sorry that it should
pen so, but it was out of my pocket to
it, and I think I shall be enabled to pay
the greater part of my debt myself as I shall
find constant employment if I wish it.

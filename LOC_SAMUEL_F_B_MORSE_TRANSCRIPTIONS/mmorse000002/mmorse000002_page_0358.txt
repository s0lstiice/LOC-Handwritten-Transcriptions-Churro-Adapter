Mr. I shall put this into this letter box, to go by mail. The parcel contain-
ing the fruits of the Govt. some News papers, Pamphlets, a letter from your
former & one from Mr. Reason to Mr. Bromfields with Dr. Readings former
I shall commit to a young man who will go from Liverpool to London. This
will go via Liverpool unless stopped by the Embargo.
It is sent by the Brig Thomas, who hastens away
to avoid Embargo – I send the Govt. 2 half shillings are wanting, she shall be
sent home.
The Comdt. of the fleet
to the use of your Excellency
Brig. Thomas
Commanding
His Excellency
the Governor

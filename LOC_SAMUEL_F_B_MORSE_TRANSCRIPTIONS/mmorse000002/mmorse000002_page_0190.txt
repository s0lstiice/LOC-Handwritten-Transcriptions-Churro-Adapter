Mayor allowed us but 10 days to get to London)
we were under the disagreeable necessity of
beginning our journey. Mr. & Mrs. Allston with
myself took a post-chaise which indeed is
much more expensive than a stage-coach, but
on account of Mr. Allston's health, which you
know was not very good when in Boston (altho' she
is much benefited by her voyage,) we were obliged
to travel slowly & in this manner, it has cost
us perhaps double the sum, which it would have
done had we come in a stage coach, but necessity
obliged me to act as I have done; I found
myself in a land of strangers liable to be
cheated out of my teeth almost, & if I had gone
to London without Mr. Allston, my waiting
at a boarding house totally unacquainted with
any living creature, I should probably have
expended the difference by the time he had arrived

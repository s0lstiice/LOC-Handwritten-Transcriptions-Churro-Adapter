a few days since, and I was glad to see
them; they did not stay long enough in town
to be introduced to Dr Dwight as they
went out of town an hour or two after they
came. – Mr Wood could not make it
convenient to let me have $50.00 as he
expected to purchase a chair in Norfolk.
I should be glad if you could send it on
as soon as possible as we are in want of
money. I need the cloth for my bonnet suit
for which I am very much obliged to you
I have obeyed the orders of Mama concern-

Charlestown Jan 18th 1812.
Dear Brother,
Capt Holmes was detained a
week longer than he at first expected, and as he
went by the way of Liverpool, we troubled him only
with a single letter; the rest we send by the Plato
Richard says we shall garden you with reading
letters, but notwithstanding his remonstrance Papa
insists upon my writing you this second, although I
am obliged to do it in the parlour surrounded by
prates, and harassed by messages flying in all
directions.= Under these circumstances you can expect
nothing but apologies -- and the the have I am
sitting in the parlour in the arm chair, on
the right of the fireplace and as I hold my paper in
my hand, with my feet stretched out before the fire,
and with my body reclining in an oblique position
against the back of the chair, I am penning you a
letter, such as it is, and for the inverted portion of the
letter I beg to apologize.

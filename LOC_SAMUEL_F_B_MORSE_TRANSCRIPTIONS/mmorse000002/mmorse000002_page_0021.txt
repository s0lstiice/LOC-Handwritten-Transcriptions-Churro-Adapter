Yale College New Haven June 10. 1808
Dear Parents
I have just received your letter & parents
by Revd Mr Jackson of Philadelphia, viz. a
sett of Geographies & an atlas for Mitchell, & a
no. of Panoptist Subscription papers with a letter
Mitchell had just purchased a sett together with
an Atlas, but I believe he is about to sell them
again. We arrived here safely on Thursday
evening after we departed from Boston, we were
neither of us in the least homesick & have not
yet been. I am a little indisposed to day with
a violent headache, therefore I hope you will
exercise the brevity of this letter, I hope I shall
feel better the next time I write. I have not
yet been to see Mr Swining, Evarts, or Beers
concerning the business with you mentioned to
me I will go soon. Mr Silliman has
given me a receipt to make liquid blacking
it is the best & cheapest of any I have seen, I
will copy it here, thinking it may be of
great use to you,

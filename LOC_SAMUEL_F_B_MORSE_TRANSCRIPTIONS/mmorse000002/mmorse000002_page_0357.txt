I wish you, my son, to be very cautious how you introduce with spotlights or you may ruin
yourself in serious difficulties. You had better keep your own opinions, whatever they may be
to yourself, I not make them a subject even of consideration. It may be pretty clearly hazardous
to commit them to writing. I am so well known at present personally only in your profession as a painter
I leave you only feel at your ease in any conversation in any company. I am sending you
your name to writing this letter. I am your affectionate father & brother
Samuel F. Morse

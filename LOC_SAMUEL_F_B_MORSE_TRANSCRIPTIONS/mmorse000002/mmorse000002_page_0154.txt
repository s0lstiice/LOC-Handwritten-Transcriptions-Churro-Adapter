New York July 6th 1810
My Dear Parents,
I arrived here this morning about 5
o'clock safe & in good spirits, though not a little
fatigued with my very unpleasant journey.
After bringing you Farewell in Hartford
at nine o'clock, rested there that night, &
started the next morning for Utica, & I am
about 6 o'clock about half an hour after
the mail stage had gone for New-York. I find
my brothers well; got no letters of introduc-
tion from either Dr Dwight or Mr Silbene
found the latter had received a very bad res-
ident from the explosion of fulminating
silver, and is in great danger of losing his eye-
ight altogether; in the evening of Wednesday
went on board the packet for New-York, was
becalmed for two days, and arrived in this port
after being in great anxiety all day on Fri-
day last the Lydia, sh sail; but find she
does not sail till Tuesday the 9th.
Mr Alston arrived last evening well
and I am on board our ship to see what ac-
tions he has taken in the matter of the
protection of the Union.
I am your affectionate son
Samuel
Alston

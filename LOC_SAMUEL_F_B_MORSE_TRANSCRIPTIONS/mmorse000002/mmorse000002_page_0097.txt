College N. Haven Sept. 1st
Dear Mama.
Papa arrived here safely this week
and I need not tell you we were glad to
see him. He has mentioned to me the plan which
he proposed for my future business in life, yet
I am pleased with it, for he was determined
beforehand, to trust you to his and your will in
every thing, and when I came home I shall
endeavor to make returns for the trouble &
anxiety with you have been at my account,
by assisting Papa in his labor of pursuing
with Dooder my own business.
We have prospects of a new school
commencement; and Ball is thriving. I
almost dread the week of Comm. of the duties
of a manager in so laborious. Eliot &
Richard are very well, and doing well; to-
morrow afternoon Dr. Dwight delivers his
farewell address. I have been extremely

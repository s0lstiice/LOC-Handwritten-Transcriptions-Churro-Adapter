my allowance for a week, I put the sum in large
characters on every leaf of my account book, and at the end
of the week I sum up the expense and compare them,
if I find I have exceeded my allowance, I set down
the excess and bear it in mind the next week, so that
if I find myself inclined to purchase anything which
is not immediately necessary, I restrain myself, untill
my weeks expense falls short of my allowance, &c.
I am at present a little in debt to my account
owing to the expense of setting myself out at the begin-
ning, but hope to square my accounts at the end of the
year.
Dec. 20th
I have as yet received no intelligence from you
and therefore conclude the letters you have written
a lost; I will however wait with patience, in
This country appears to me to be in a very
bad state; I judge from the increasing disturbances
at Nottingham, and more especially from the series of
murders lately committed in this city; a few mornings
since was published an account of the murder of a family
consisting of 4 persons, and this moment there is
another account of the murder of one consisting of 3,
making the twelfth murder committed in that part
of the city within 3 months, and not one of the murderers
as yet have been discovered, altho' a reward of
more than 700 pounds or near 4000 dollars has been
offered for the discovery; the inhabitants are very much
alarmed, and hereafter I shall sleep with pistols at
the head of my bed; altho' there is little to apprehend
in this part of the city, still as I find many of my
acquaintance adopting that plan, I choose rather
to be on the safe side, and join with them

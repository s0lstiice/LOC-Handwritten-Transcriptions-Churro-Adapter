New Haven, Ct. Feb 27th March 1808
Dear Parents,
I have but a moment's time to write you a few lines by Mr Huntington who goes immediately, I received of the 12th inst. a letter to take this opportunity to answer it -- The Post is not so bad as I think it was when I wrote, he was able to attend his toller in office the next day after I wrote you, Norris is in good health at present, as fast as I can team. P. Dana is quite unwell, & is not expected to live. Eliot & myself are in very good health, & enjoy ourselves very much. You ask us if our clothes look shabbier, I think I shall need a new light suit soon by the time I come home, I have had mine mended once since I have been home, & it is almost in need of being mended again. My class have studied since this commencement. Ishamous, No 2d vol. Geography, Buckid & Horace, (for the first term,) (2 terms) review Euclid, Horace, & study 2d vol. Webster Math. & have got as far as Euclides in it, I wish when you write me you will inform me who it is that is coming from Cambridge to this college -- The large box you mention I was not able to sell, I placed in the entry & it was broken some of it was carried off for kindling wood, so I brot it in & burn it -- I have not the pamphlet you mention & likewise the paper I have not time at present to write more "multum in parvo" Abeel years to
S. H. B. Morse

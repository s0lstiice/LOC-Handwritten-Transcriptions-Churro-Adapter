I write this merely to let you hear from
me & to show you that we improve every day
& write you knowing your anxiety & hear
& desirous of satisfying you as you have
us by your frequent letters We now
are writing to hear from you as in the last
letters we were dated the 6th Nov.
I hope you have received the draft on
Mr Hope for 2000 odd pounds which
I sent by the Liverpool packet shall
send the 3d bill by the Plate on Galen
I was sorry not to have any packages
from Taylor by the Galen with the
business with him settled & a regular
channel through Mr Brownfield opened
between the Editors of the Chas Ev.
Evans Mag. & Elective Rev. & myself
that we might exchange publications
I just learn that Capt Holmes has gone
on board & must therefore close
all your friends in this region are well
& remember you affectionately I enjoy

As to your brothers, I trust, with your edu-
cation, you will be able to maintain yourself, your
parents too, &c. They need it in their old age. Probably
this necessity laid on you for exertion in industry
economy in early life will be better for you in the
end, than to be supported by your parents. In
time cases out of ten, those who begin in the world
with nothing richer & more useful men in life,
than those who inherit a large estate. I am
persuaded that you & your brothers with your
talents, education, good character, will enter the
world with as fair prospects of success as
fulness, as any young men of your age stand-
ing. We have great comfort in all our children.
It gives us particular satisfaction to find in
you all a disposition to relieve us, as fast as
soon as possible from the care of providing for
your support. Your brothers seem particularly
anxious on this subject.
We have had an unusually long & severe winter
this month particularly this year has been very cold & un
pleasant. There is a great boy of yours at the Northward which
will occasion a backward spring. Your Mama has been confined
a great part of the winter though not (a few ill turns
excepted) very sick when the spring opens, so that she can
take the air, I trust she will perfectly recover her health.
My own health, considering the labor I do include I
have had to sustain has remained very good. Your brothers
& the family have all enjoyed very good health. All take a
deep interest in what concerns you & I rejoice with us in
all the good news we hear from you.

Alexander & in 27th v. it says "both these kings hearts shall be to
do mischief" this seems to correspond with their actions at
present. Then in the 30th verse it says "for the ships of Chit-
lin shall come against him" & this passage seems most to
confirm my belief that this is a prophecy of Brionaparte
For I take "the ships of Chittlin" to mean "the ships of Eng-
land" & that this part of the prophecy & the remaining
part is yet to be fulfilled therefore I can't explain
the rest, only in the 36th verse it says "he shall exalt him-
self & magnify himself above every God" & in the 24th He shall
do that which his fathers have not done, nor his fathers fathers" &
all this seems to apply to him. I wish you would write
me your opinion of this subject & correct me when I am
wrong. Another thing concerning Brionaparte I will
mention to you; in the 13th Chap of Revelation & 18th verse
it says "Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understand-
ing count the number of the beast, for it is the number
of a man, & his number is Six hundred three score & six"
that is 666. Now Brionaparte read in Greek & spelt accor-
ding to the Italian or Corsican manner is Bonaparte
& the amount of this name is exactly 666.
I have read Newton on this subject but he supposes,
with another writer that this passage refers to one
Latinus & Lateinus the famous kings & or of the
Latinus &c. &c. I only mention this, perhaps it may be of some importance.

My dear son,
By Capt. Holmes of this town
who goes in the Sally Ann, to Liverpool, I
write you a few lines just to inform
you that we are all in usual health,
except that your Mama, who had an attack of bilious fever in Nov – has had
several relapses, has to last the air, that
I fear at this season to go abroad but is
otherwise pretty well – Your brothers are
home both at home & very well assisting
me in the new Editn of the Geo's – Richd
came home on Monday last – Edidn has
been at home three weeks – We are pro-
ceeding rapidly with the Log – If I
write you particularly, as would your
Mama & brothers also (who have letters
for you written) but that there is a
vepel going in a few days direct to
London, (the Plate, Capt Smith) by which I
shall send you the papers & letters
with which you can receive without expense &
from which you may learn fully what is passing here.

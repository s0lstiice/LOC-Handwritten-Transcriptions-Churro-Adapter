Give my particular love to Richard, I
hope he is well & doing well, I long to see him
very much, but the time I trust is fast past
on when I shall have my wish realized. I
write him a letter if time would permit me
for at this I have more time to spare than I had
last term yet I have not sufficient to write to
many friends I could wish to correspond with;
however I expect he will find as much enter-
tainment in those letters while I write to you as
in one directed particularly to him.
My studies are at present Optics in Philoso-
phy, Dialling, Homer, besides disputing, composing,
attending lectures &c &c all which I find very interesting,
and especially Mr. Day's believe who is now believing
on Electricity. Adull! my letter is full I will write more
another time.
Yours &c. Sam'l F. B. Moore.

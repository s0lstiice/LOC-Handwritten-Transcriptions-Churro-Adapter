"bout Lunnon town," but they must excuse

When I arrived at Liverpool, Mrs G. Woolley was
at Hinckley in Leicester shire with her son George, &c.
I did not see her, but am under great obligations
to Mr. Woolsey— Remember me to Mrs
Dwight & all N.Haven friends all you must
particularize for me— Good Bye.
I shall be a complete old Batchelor by the time
I come home again.— Yours affectionately

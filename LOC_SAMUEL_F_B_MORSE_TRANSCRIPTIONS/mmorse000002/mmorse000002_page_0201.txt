I am longing for the Telegraph
to hear from you. I have not received
since finishing the way to carry this has satisfied, & I understand
Wednesday, as I understand there is one going to
New York. I have just heard that a ship has arrived
this morning from Boston but did not learn her name: I hope it is the Galen; I have changed my
lodgings, since the within has been written, as I found
in making my terms the people were determined to
make as much from me as possible; & have got
lodgings at No 67 Great Fitchfield Street, where
they are very clever people, & very obliging & honest;
and instead of a guinea per week I pay only a pound
& have one little room more than in the former house
I still feel very happy & contented; I shall begin my
studies to-morrow. There has a ghost made its
appears about a few streets only from me, which has
alarmed the whole city; it appears every night with
form of shriekings & groanings; there are crowds at
the house every night; & at the they all hear the noises
none can discover from whence they come; the family have
quitte the house. I suppose it only a hoax by some
rogue; which will be trot out in time. ~ Good Bye.

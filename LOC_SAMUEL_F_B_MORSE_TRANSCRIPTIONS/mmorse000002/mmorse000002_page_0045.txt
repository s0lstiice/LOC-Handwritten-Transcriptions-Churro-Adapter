very much pleased at rooming with him; he is well, in good
spirits, & very much pleased with the College, thinks it far superior
to Cambridge. The disturbances are all over here now, & we
are restored to order again. I feel very well, & enjoy myself ex-
traordinarily, am more & more pleased with my situation in college.
I am very much pleased with "Chemistry" it is very amusing
as well as instructing, there are many very beautiful and
surprising experiments that can be performed, which are likewise very useful

P.S. I send Mr Bender the 3 last No's of Pennsylvania & Dr. Parsons &
Wood's Remons. You may read them before they are delivered. Do not
engage to keep them long. I am to Mr Bender in End writing him soon
and send for the two papers of the day. This letter I shall put into the bag the
princel. I shall send to the care of Mr. Woolsey.
The President of the United States
of the United States of America
Your obedient Servt
George Washington
President
Washington D.C.
July 18th 1792

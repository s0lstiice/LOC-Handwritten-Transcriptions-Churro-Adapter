London Septt 24th 1811
My Dear Parents,
I have just received papa's letter
of Augt 16th by the Reaper via Bristol; as the
Galen goes to morrow morning it will be out
of my power to send any thing wt you have
requested but by the next opportunity will have
all things ready & will call on Mr. Taylor
& transact the business as you direct.— I hope
you have long ere this received some of my letters
whk I suppose are at this moment in all parts
of the world; I drop't you a line at sea, by
a vessel bound to Quebec but I don't know
that you have rec'd it.— You will see by the
papers which accompany this what a report re-
specting the capture of the U.S. frigate President,
by Melampus frigate, prevails here; it is suffi-
cient to say it is not in the least credited.— In
case of war, I shall be ordered out of the coun-
try, if so, instead of returning home had I not better
go to Paris, as it is cheaper living there even than
in London, and there are great advantages there; I
only ask the question in case of war.— Mr. & Mrs.
Allston are very well; I wish in your better you

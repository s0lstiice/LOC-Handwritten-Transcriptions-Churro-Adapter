London Sept.r 20.th 1811.
My Dear Parents.
As I understand the Calen is to sail
again in a few days for Boston I improve the
opportunity of again writing you, altho I feel very
little in a writing mood today, having caught a
slight cold which has made me feel dull a little for
a few days past, but I am regaining my spirits
very fast so you must not think any thing of it, for it
would be strange if I did not feel dull sometimes
since it is constitutional; I do all I can to resist it
by applying myself to my studies, strictly; I shall
offer my drawing to the Royal Academy next
Monday week, as it then opens for the admission of
students, I will then write you whether I am successful or not. I have not delivered many of my
letters of introduction; Mr Bromfields, Dr. Luttmans
& Mr. Wests are the only ones as yet. Mr. Burder
lives 4 miles out of town at Camberwell Sir
Joseph Banks is not in town, Mr. Wilberforce lives
about 3 miles from me &c. I have not as yet found
time to call on them but certainly shall as soon as it
is in my power. I was invited a few days since
to dine at Mr. Higginsons with Mr. & Mrs Allston, whom
invitation I accepted; they are very polite & attentive to
me -- it was a party consisting of 18 Americans; it made
me feel quite at home; we sat down to dinner at 2 p.m.

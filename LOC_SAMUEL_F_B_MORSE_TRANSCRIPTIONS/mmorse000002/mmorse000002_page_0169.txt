On board the Lydia off Sandy Hook
Monday July 15th 1841.
My Dear Brothers
After lying windbound a long time in
New York we have length got under way, 14 for
sewers, and the prospect of an excellent passage
I have but a moment to write you, as the
pilot goes ashore directly: I am very well
as yet, I suppose I shall be seasick bye & bye
we are almost out sight of land; it looks strange
to me two ships in company fair wind
S.S.W. Good bye; I shall write on my
arrival in Liverpool.
Remember me to all who enquire after
me.
Your affectionate Brother
Sam'l F. B. Mosse.

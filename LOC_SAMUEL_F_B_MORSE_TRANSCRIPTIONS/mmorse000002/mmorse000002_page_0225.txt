say the custom house officers were very civil
to us & behaved very gentlemanly. I had
more difficulty at the Union office, they at first
refused to accept our passports saying that
they ought to come from the Secretary of
State but after some scolding which we bore with
becoming patience they gave us a passport to go
to London which was to be countersigned by the
Mayor of Liverpool so off we rested to the Mayors
who is a very ill natured man at least he
appeared so to me; he signed our passports
and gave us 10 days to get to London telling
us at the same time that he should put us
in prison if he found us in Liverpool after
that time. after having thus far got through
the ceremonies of a stranger Mr & Mrs Allston
with myself prepared to set out immediately
for London which is 207 miles distant from Liver-
pool. On Sunday morning therefore on account
of the time to which we were limited, we were

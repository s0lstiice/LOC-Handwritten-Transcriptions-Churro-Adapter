(Monday) New-York July 5th 1811
My Dear Parents
to the Rev. Mr. Channing & Bart,
who came with Mr. & Mrs. Altston will go
about 2 oClock home, I approve the opportunity
of writing you by them. I wrote you on Saturday last about 2 hours after my arrival &
informed you what a tedious & disagreeable
journey I had, I have felt no inconvenience
from it, but feel perfectly well, & in excellent
spirits. Capt. & Mrs Stout are very well, send
a great deal of love to you & I am very
kindly attentive to you I feel under great
obligations to them & hope one of them days
I shall be able to show them I am grateful.
[Marginal note] Capt. Stout went with me to day to get
my bedding -- I have got a mattress, a pillow
& pillow cases 2 pr. sheets & blanket all amount
ing to 18--25 this sum Capt. S. paid & I still
give him a dist: on you for the sum -- as soon as
I shall arrive in Liverpool I shall probably

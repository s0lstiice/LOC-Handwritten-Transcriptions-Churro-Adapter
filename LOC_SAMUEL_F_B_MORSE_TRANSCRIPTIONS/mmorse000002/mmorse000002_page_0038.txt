I inform you with a great deal of joy that my voice
is entirely changed; I have now spoken like other people,
I obtained it I believe only by singling I have sung on
great deal this term.
There is no news in town I believe, I see by a
New York paper that there has been a rumor that Bue-
naparte has been assassinated at Bulegne by the
Spaniards.
I hope you will not be under the necessity of writing
another letter to me; this I shall expect one, if this
miscarries like the rest of my letters which I have sent by post.
I must now bid you Adieu!
as I have little exercises to attend to
Your affectionate and ever dutiful Son
Sam'l B. Yosse

the arts are in this country. Mr. West is now
engaged upon another piece, the sketch of which
he has nearly finished; the subject is Christ be-
fore Pilate, he showed it to Mr. Allston and
myself, "because," as he said, "we were Americans,"
he had shown it to no one else. Mr. Allston told

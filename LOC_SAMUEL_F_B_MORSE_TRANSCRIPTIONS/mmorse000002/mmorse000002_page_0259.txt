My Dear Parents,
As I write this in haste I cannot
be particular; I only will inform you that
I remain in perfect health, and in high spirits;
it is some time since I heard from you
the last way via Bristol; I long to hear again
the papers mention that there will probably
be an embargo in the U. States, if so our correspondence
will be at least embarrassed
the only communication, will be via New-
York & Falmouth (Eng.) by the British Jack-
ets; I wrote you by the Galen about 3
weeks ago; and have this moment heard
she was still in the Downs; I was really
provoked; there is great deception about
vessels, they advertise for a certain day to
perhaps do not sail under a month after;
the Galen has been going and going, till I
am sick of hearing she has not gone; if I
had had any idea of her being thus delay'd
I should certainly have contrived to put
Papa's things on board; I shall go to-morrow
into the city (which I suppose you know is a
distinct part of London,) I live in Westminster,
which is three miles from the business part

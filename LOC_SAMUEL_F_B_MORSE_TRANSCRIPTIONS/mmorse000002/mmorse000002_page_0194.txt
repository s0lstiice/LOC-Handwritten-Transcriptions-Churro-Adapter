Friday 16th 1811.
This morning I called on Mr. Bromfield &
delivered to my letters he p.rd me very cordially, en
quired after you particularly & invited me to dine
with him, at 5 o’Clock, with invitation I accept
ed. Mr. Bromfield is a very pleasant uncer
monious man; they requested me to come at any
time, & they sh. always be glad to see me. I
took 20 pounds from the 100 with I deposited
in Mr. Bromfields hands for the purpose of obtain
ing things immediately necessary, I shall be under
the necessity of getting a suit of clothes, with which
to be black, as the Kings death is daily expected, &
then every individual in the kingdom is by law
required to dress in that color, & by getting a
suit now I should obtain it more reasonably
as black cloths will be in great demand at that
time & will consequently rise, a suit will cost
me about 8 pounds. I bought me a hat to day
for 28 shillings with in our money is a little over
five dollars

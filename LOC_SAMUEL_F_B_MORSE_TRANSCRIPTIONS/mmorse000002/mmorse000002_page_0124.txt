I immediately turned quarter & applied a portion of wheat

kept on frequently changing it - This morning morning when
I went to Dr. Munson, who is found, as not able to put
it off - The division of grain him he supposed it was not a bill
and divided a portion of wheatbran and bye - Today he
came & examined it - He said it was a swelling but gave it no
name - After this portion has been kept on two or three
days, he says it will break & discharge, and then get well -
He observed that it was a great pity that one of Dr. Munson's men
should be fundamentally erroneous - His inconvenience is
such arising from its situation that he hopes his bill a consideration
part of the time - The pain of the swelling I believe
is principally nerve. He is in better spirits - I have got him
some wine - He has boarded at Mrs. Haroligs - His wife
are now sent to him - His appetite is totally gone - I have
been thus particular because some parents love to know
justifications respecting their children, especially when so dear
ag. R. - I think he will get about in a few days. Perhaps, as
this will reduce him somewhat in the Divisibles for him to
take a journey to C. H. is fully of the opinion I am respecting
this the Divisibles will be contented - Perhaps you will have
time to write before he will be able to put out - I hope you
will not be concerned about him, as he will have attention
brothers & friends can quite - I fear they are not cured of the it -
Be does not complain - Your instructions last 31st February 1845

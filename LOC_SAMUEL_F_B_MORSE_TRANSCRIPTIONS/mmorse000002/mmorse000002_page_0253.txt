they would strike up & make such a noise as to drown
every word that is said, tho' the other noise would I
think be sufficient for that, I said music because
we generally call the sound of a musical instrument
music, but if you can conceive of between 20 & 30
different bands, all within hearing of each other,
all playing at once, & all different tunes, in different
time, you can have some idea of the music I mean.

Remember me particularly to Mrs. Broomfield. Apologies from
for my not writing him. I have not a moment's time to send off
to which you will at most hasten. You must not throw my letters away
not fit to be seen. Ask Mr. Spofford Wilson & West to do me the favor to collect
a fund lying in consideration of my letter to them. Deliver all your letters
& keeping up your good acquaintance. We are glad you attend this in earnest
to the arrangement with the Govt. minister of last year. I have had his character
from the Colman who will give you a letter to him by the gallant
Mr. Spofford Wilson
Colman

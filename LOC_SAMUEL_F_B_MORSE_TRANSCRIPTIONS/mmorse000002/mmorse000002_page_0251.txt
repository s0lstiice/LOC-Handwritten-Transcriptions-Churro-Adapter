most every day Mr. & Mr. Allston are in excellent
health Mr. Allston says she never enjoyed better, the other
day did her a great deal of good. There is Capt. Van
Rensselaer, who came passengers with us, who lives
a short walk from me, they are likewise very kind
to me she was a Van Rensselaer of New York state
cousin to young Van Rensselaer with whom I am acquainted
& who is in this country in Edinburgh
shall probably see him soon. I do not hear any
of Mr. Isaac Hurd, only that he is in Edinburgh, if
you want to address a letter to him I should do
it to him know I am here. I wrote you a very long
line by the return of the Lydia a few days since, as I had
no moment to write it in. I shall certainly write by every
vesper that I hear of. I went the other day to see
very singular sight, it was St. Bartholomew's fair, it

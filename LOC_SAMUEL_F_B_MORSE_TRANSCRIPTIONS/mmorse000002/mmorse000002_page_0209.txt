On the contrary I have been in very good spirits &
more enthusiastic & determined than ever in the
pursuit of my profession. Monday 29th
at 7 and breakfasted by 7 past 7 I get to work, these two or three days
past I have sat over my drawings from 7 to 10 till
5 o'clock in the afternoon which is my dining hour, after
dinner I generally walk a little, and visit Mr. Allison
who live but about 3 minutes walk from me at 49 London Street, he is very sociable & pleasant
with me, he visits me every day to talk & smoke his cigar
with me. By the way, I propose a little to inform you
how I got my sextars on shore, when we first went ashore I filled my pockets & hat as full as I could be
left the rest in the top of my trunk intending to
come & get them immediately, I came back &
took another pocket & left about 8 or 9 dozen
on the top of my clothes, I went up into the city again
& forgot the remainder until it was too late either to
take them out or hide them under the clothes, so I went
triumphing (for contraband goods subjects the whole trunk to
sueion), but the custom house officer been very good natured & clever saw them & took them up, I told him

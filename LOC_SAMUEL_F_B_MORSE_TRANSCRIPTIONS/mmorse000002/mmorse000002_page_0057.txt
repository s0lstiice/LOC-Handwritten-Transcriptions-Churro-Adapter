our account, as I think we are entirely out of danger. I expected that I had taken this fever
a few days since, as I had the symptoms very strongly; it however proved nothing more than a
violent turn of my accustomed complaint, the headache; it has now nearly left me; it has occasioned
my being absent from prayer & recitation a number of times when I regret very much.
I have left Mr. Dwight's room, as it appeared so inconvenient for us to room together, the
measure was advised by Mr. Dwight & he has recommended me to a good roommate by name
Grainke brother to the person who visited us in Charleston some time since with Dr. Durghm. I am
very much pleased with him.
I have nothing of importance to relate to you; my class about a fortnight since sent unanimous
petition to the authority, to permit us to study something in the room of Grace Majors
it was granted, and we are permitted to study Homer & that our studies are comparatively
easier than they were last term.

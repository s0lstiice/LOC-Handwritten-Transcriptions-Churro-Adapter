I shall endeavour by Monday morning to get
away from this house into lodgings, as I have been
told by my friends that a hotel is an extravagant place at
which to stay, I intended moving this morning but
was unable on account of the sickness with I men-
tioned before; it was occasioned by eating some fruit
at Mr. Bromfield's the day before. I shall take my
lodgings for a month, at 1 guinea per week & in case
I should not find cheaper ones, I must be content to
stay there; my lodgings will be No. 4 Buckingham

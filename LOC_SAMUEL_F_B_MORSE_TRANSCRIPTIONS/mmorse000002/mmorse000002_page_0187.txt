conceive my emotions on the 20th day of our passage
when we heard the Capt cry out from the mast
head, land! you may be assured I was joyful
enough, although I was by no means tired of the
voyage, for I cannot recollect a single circumstance
that was disagreeable thro' the whole voyage.
there were no quarrelsome persons on board, all
things went on smoothly and gaily; there was
no seasick one (excepting the Captains
lady) of consequence; Mr. Waitt who has crossed
the Atlantic 6 times, was sick almost every day
but excepting her, there were none who were
seasick after the 2d or 3d day for my own part
I felt a little disagreeable the 2d day on

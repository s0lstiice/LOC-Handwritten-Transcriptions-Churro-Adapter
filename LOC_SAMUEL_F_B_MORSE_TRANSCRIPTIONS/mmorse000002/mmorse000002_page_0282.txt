My Dear Friends
This will be handed over to
Mr. Signe of Boston who intends spending some
time in England, and excuse you an answer
from me, I wrote you a few weeks
since requesting the purchase of some
time with an order for the purchase of
some of your coffee and tea. I have
not been here sufficiently long to
forget the delightful time when we could
meet in the evenings with Uncle Coffin and
have a glass of wine with the Convention of that
place. I am sorry to inform you that it will
not again take place, my mind

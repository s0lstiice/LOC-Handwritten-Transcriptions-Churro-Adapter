I hope you have seen Mr Wilberforce & Mr Mac Cabeley
long before this, though you mention nothing concerning them
they will be valuable dignitaries to you. I say to Mr Wilberforce
that I have read his 2ndth Thirteenth petition to Parliament on the ground
of establishing the Propag relating to American affairs, with much pleasure, & on
approbation I will be glad to have a letter from Mr Co. St M. W. in answer to
or thus, will I have written them -- as it are unanswred.

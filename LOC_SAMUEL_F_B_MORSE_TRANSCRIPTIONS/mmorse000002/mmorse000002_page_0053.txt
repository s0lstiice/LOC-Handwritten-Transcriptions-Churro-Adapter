Landscape is the most beautiful of all scenery, as it
affords us an infinite variety of forms & colors; a land-
scape however is considered as defective unless water is
introduced in some part of it: the water by reflecting and inverting the objects
which are near its borders, occasions a pleasing vari-
ety, and gives a degree of lustre to the whole.
The human form and features are excellent
examples of variety, the oval form of the face, the
dissimilarity of the ears, eyes, nose, & smooth, & the other
membes of the body to each other, are fair proofs of this;
but I shall have occasion to mention the human
figure in an after part of this composition, I will
therefore leave it for the present.

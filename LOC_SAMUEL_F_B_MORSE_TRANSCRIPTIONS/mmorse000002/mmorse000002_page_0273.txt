My Dear Parents,
I have but a few moments, just to
say that I am well, and that I wrote a few
days since by the ship Paragon (I think) via N.York
this makes the 12th letter I have written to you since
my arrival and I have been for some time in anxious expectation of a letter from you, acquainting me that you have heard of our safety, no account has reached us to that effect, excepting that the news of the safe arrival of the ship had been received at NewYork, saying nothing of the passengers.
I hope some of my letters have reached you before this: -- This is the first letter that I have received but in future shall do it, as I think it a good plan for you well to know the order in which you receive them, and whether any are missing.
Decr. 6th
I have just recieved a line from Mrs Vose of Charlestown, and by him learn that you have heard of our arrival and that you receive well, but did not learn whether you had heard by letters from me, or some other way; Mrs Allston has also just received a letter from Mr Channing in which he mentions he has only received 1 letter, and she has written, 13, -- there seems to be a strange fatalilty attending our correspondence, you must have written to me certainly, but there has no letter reached me since the one via Bristol; in one of my letters I recommend

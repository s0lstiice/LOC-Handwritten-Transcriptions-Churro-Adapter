Yale College. New Haven June 25th 1810
My Dear Parents,
I received with humility your reproach I am
extremely sorry it should have occasioned so
many disagreeable feelings; I feel it my duty
to tell you of them debts, and indeed I could
not feel easy without the amount of my
battery bill is 12 or 13 dollars Mr. Nettleton
's Butter and is willing I should take his
likeness as part pay I shall take it on
worry and he has engaged to allow me 7 dollars
for it, my price is 5 dollars for a miniature
on ivory and I have engaged 3 or 4 at that
price, my price for profiles is 1 dollar and
every body is ready to engage me at that price

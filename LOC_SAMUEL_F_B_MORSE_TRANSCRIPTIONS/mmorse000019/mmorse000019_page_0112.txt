New York Dec 9th 1844
My dear Sir,
I am very desirous to obtain the
loan of Field's book on color which you have
deposited in the Library of the Academy and
being obliged to leave the City before I can furnish
advice an answer from you to the request this
morning I sent your Booksholder to he though
that if the Librarian would allow me to take
the Book that you would be agreeable to it
provided I would write to you & say that in
case you have any objections I would re-
turn the Book immediately I have taken
his advice & in consequence took the book with
this commandation I wish the book for
several weeks. My dear Morn how long
it is since I have had the pleasure of see-
ing you it seems an age in the hope
that so great a length of time will not pass
again without that pleasant remembrance of my old Colle-
agues

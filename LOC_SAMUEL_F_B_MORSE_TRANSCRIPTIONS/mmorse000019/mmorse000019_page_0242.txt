Washington Jan 7. 1845.
Dear Sidney.
I wrote a letter to Charles more than a
week ago which I have not burned the fate. I requested him to get a fan left at the Fancy store corner of
Bway & Courtlandt St. pay for mending it, and enclose it in a roll of six Bible Altars to be got from you
under corn to M. Ellsworth for me. There was also a letter for Miss Catherine Pattison to be put into the
Post Office. As I ought to have heard from them some days ago I fear some miscommunication. They were enclosed in an envelope to you and franked by Selah
Strong your clasp mate.
I am well, but anxiously waiting the action of Congress on the bill for extension of Telegraph. Texas drives every thing else into a corner. I have not
many fears if they will only get it up. I had a day the Russian, Spanish & Belgian Ministers to see the operation of the Telegraph; they were astonished and delighted. The Russian Minister particularly takes the deepest interest in it, and will write to this Government by next Steamer. The French Minister also came the day before yesterday, and
will write in its favor to his Government.
Has anything been done with Mr. Emerson in relation to the legacies of Charles Walker? Do write me
a line, and urge Charles to write also.

City of New York Dec 19th 1844
Prof Morse
My Dear Sir The 19th of last month
you were kind enough to lend me for a month one hundred
dollars This is the day I should return it and I have
it in my power to do so, but should be glad if
you would permit me to retain it some twenty
days longer as I have suffered repeated delays in
getting the machinery ready for my new business so that
it has carried nothing for me – Yesterday was the first day
we have been able to run the works every thing in relation
to the business looks well – I have taken the liberty
to retain the notice I shall hear from you but
in the mean time if you have need of it be kind enough
to give me two days notice & it shall be forth coming
It was a great favor for which I trust I shall not
be ungrateful – I shall be glad to hear from you
I heard the progress of Congress – but it is not judicious
to move in Telegraph business yet I suppose arrangements
are making to connect the office and
have fast office by Telegraph magnetic –
Please make my respects to Rail and Mr Ellsworth
and such as may inquire after me –
Mr desires to be remembered by you
Your Ob Serv
L P Gale

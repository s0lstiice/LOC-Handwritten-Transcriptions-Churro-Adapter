New York. Decr 28 1846
Dear Sir) When I last saw you which was last fall
I requested of you that if Congress made an appropriation
for extending the Telegraph that you would give me a job
in preparing the wire. Will you be good enough to let me
know what your prospect is in regard to its being extended
I have seen it mentioned in several papers that it will
be brought on to New York and if it should I would agree to
take the contract for preparing the wire on as favorable
terms as any one could do it for and would engage to have
it done within four months and after the first month I would
engage to have it ready to be put up so that the whole line
might be completed in the time before stated
Within a few weeks I have been (when time would permit) covering
the Baltimore wire -- and as I understand and understand
you very well know I have a practical knowledge
of the telegraph and I am confident I can have it better done than that of
the Baltimore wire -- and as for soldering and vanishing
please to give me an answer as soon as you can and
inform me in regard to the prospect for extending and to
what place -- Your compliance will much oblige
Your friend
Leviss O. Wood
S. F. B. Morse
B. Please to direct
to No 522 Broadway

Answer. Wash. Feb. 15./45
Sir
In reply to Y. of 13 inst. I would
say that in the application you propose
making to the Legislature for a Charter
for a Tel. Co. I have no objection
to your using my name in the bill provided
as you promise it involves me in no
liability, nor obligation to contract with
the proprietors of P. Charter.
There is every reason to believe
however that Congress will act on the
matter in a few days.
From Respr
W Oster
St PM
100080
to Jos. C. Potts En
State N.J.

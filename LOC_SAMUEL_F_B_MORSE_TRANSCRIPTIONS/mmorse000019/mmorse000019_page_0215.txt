Hartford Jan 6th 1846.
Dear Sir.
Mr Charles Bull by whom
this will be handed to you is known of a
relation in connection with the magnetic
telegraph. I have long acquainted with
Mr Bull & have formed him Gentlemen
& skillful in his business which he has
been in this City. I believe that he will
be found an able assistant if placed in this
department & hope that you will use your
influence in his behalf.
With very great esteem
your best
McIlwath
Thos Henry L. Branth

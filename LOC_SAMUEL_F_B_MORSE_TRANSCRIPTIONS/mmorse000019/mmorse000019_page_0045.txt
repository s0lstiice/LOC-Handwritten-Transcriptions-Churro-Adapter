The Minister of the Interior is himself president
of the Commission, M Arago is one of the members.
I would gladly take charge of any communication,
but I hope soon to return to America. Besides it
would be better perhaps to send through M. Payet at
Washington, or the American Minister or some
of your scientific friends at Paris.
I hear of McCloskey through his new publications,
and I should add, through the frequent notices and
advertisements of his old ones in the French Newspaper.
His high reputation on the Continent is undiminished.
France in my opinion enjoys a solid material
prosperity. The active strife waged here between Roman
Catholicism or Ultramontanism and the moral
and intellectual independence of France cannot
have escaped your attention. The party which bows
to Rome seems to be adding most to its numbers;
seems boldly, even themselves. Liberty of instruction which
this party claims, it is asserted means the right to
teach blind submission to Rome in matters of religion,

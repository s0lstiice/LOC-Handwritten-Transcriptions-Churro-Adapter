Baltimore, Dec. 6th 1844.
U.S. Telegraph Office
Professor Morse
Dear Sir,
In the above estimate I
have included the number of persons, that
I believe will be required if the line is
continued eastwardly.
As the business increases we may have
occasion to employ a larger number of
messengers; the Inspecto when not on the
line can assist if required in the delivery
of messages.
I thought it better to make an over esti-
mate than to fall short, for I am satis-
fied there will be a great demand for
Telegraphic Service, and to make efficient
servants the government should pay good
wages. It should be recollected that the
persons connected with the Telegraph are liable
to be called on at all hours, and the ser-
vice will be responsible and laborious.
Very respectfully
Your Obst Sent
H.S. Rogers

Copy of note to P.O.J. Smith etc.
Washington Oct. 27. 1844
Sunday evening.
I am sorry to see you on a subject of great
importance to mention morning at 9 1/2 o'clock at the
Telegraph Office. Some circumstances totally affecting
our whole interest in the Telegraph have been communi-
cated to me the last evening by a friend which re-
quires our immediate attention. I write this that
you may not leave in the morning before I see you.
Truly your friend & Servt
Samuel F. B. Morse

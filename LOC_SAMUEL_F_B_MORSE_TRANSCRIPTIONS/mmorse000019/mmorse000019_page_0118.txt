Baltimore Dec. 11th 1844
1012 P.M.
Professor Morse
Dear Sir
I have received yours
of 10th inst. enclosing check for $75 &
content have noticed. The correction
in regard to the Committees in Congress
have been made by Patriot this afternoon,
and you will also see of of Chess
Game played between this & Washington
by the aid of Telegraph.
Enclosed you will receive part of the
wire which separated at the Silver soldered
joint near Belleville. It was
broken about 3 or four feet from the
top of the post, and hung down, the
other part laid down the bank, the
rest of wire being 30 or 40 feet from the
post as though it had sprung suddenly
from it.
Very respectfully
Your obed Servt.
Henry J. Rogers
U.S. Telegraph
Yr. Supptt
U.S. Telegraph

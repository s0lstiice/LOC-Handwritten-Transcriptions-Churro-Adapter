Estimate of Acids and Consumption
of other materials daily at the U. S.
Electro Magnetic Telegraph Station,
Baltimore Dec. 11th 1844.
A large and Small Battery containing
only five cups will require daily,
1/2 pint of Sulphuric Acid at 29 pints is .04
6 pints of Nitric Acid " " " .85
Mercury to amalgamate zincs .50
Consumption of Zincs, porous cups,
and other material composing batteries } .25
Platina connecting series of cups .04
Alcohol, Soldier Solution, and solder .09
$1.77
From which takes Small Battery
containing 5 cups } 19 2/3
Making the total cost per diem of } $1.57 1/3
Large Battery Contg. 40 Cups $1.57 1/3
Five cups are used in the small Battery, and
estimated as above will cost per diem 19 2/3d.
One pint of Sulphuric Acid is estimated to
weigh 1 5/8 pounds, and costs 4 cents per pound.
A pint of Nitric Acid is estimated to weigh
1 3/4s, and costs 10 1/2 cents per pound.
The above is respectfully submitted.
Henry J. Rogers
Asst. Supt. Telegraphs
of the U. S.
To Prof. S. F. B. Morse
Superintendent of the
U. S. Telegraphs.

Philadelphia, February 1, 1865.
My dear Judge:
The Company of gentlemen
are desirous, in case Congress shall to make your
télégraph a national affair, to obtain the
right to establish it between Phila. and New
York, thru New Jersey. To this end they propose
to obtain from the Legislature, now in session
an act of incorporation, provided the right
can be purchased of you upon terms that
would suit. I am regretting to express
that sum would purchase the right to be
used for this purpose -- or what proportioned
interest in the work, you would regard as
a fair compensation for the privilege. Your
early answer will greatly oblige
Very respectfully
Jos. C. Potts.

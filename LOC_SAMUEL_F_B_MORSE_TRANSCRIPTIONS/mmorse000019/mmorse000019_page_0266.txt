Prof S. B. F. Morse
having imposed upon me the duty of addressing
you, I plant myself for that purpose, in the
hope that I may be successful in the service
to which I have been commissioned
Our object is to give a few lectures, by
gentlemen of distinction resident abroad, and our
knowledge of you sir as a gentleman and a
Lecturer is such as to warrant the belief that
if in your power, you will do us the honor of
delivering a Lecture before us at some day
suitable to yourself
Our object is to augment the
Library attached to the Association and thereby
diffuse knowledge among its members
Hoping that you will
comply with our request
I remain
Your humble servant
Wm Harden Jr
Co. Secretary
Young Literary Assn
P.S An immediate answer
is desirable
Baltimore January 30th 1845

Dear Sir
I have learned
to day that a bill has been introduced
on leave in the Senate of U.S. to incorporate
the Magnetic Telegraph Co. I am not aware
that the proprietors of the Electric Magnetic Telegraph
have petitioned for it and I have
therefore taken measures to oppose its passage
by writing our Senator to that effect, asking him
to send a bill and to write me what further
to do and I shall at the first intimation
to that effect proceed to Hartford, myself from
where I will write to you. In doing this I
may incur some consideration expense, if
I should go into it strong it will undoubtedly
be the case in which event I must be made
whole by the Company I think -- It being easier to
put down an application for an act of incorporation
than to get up one it will be to our
advantage -- and I hope that my travelly
expenses and time will by the whole cost,

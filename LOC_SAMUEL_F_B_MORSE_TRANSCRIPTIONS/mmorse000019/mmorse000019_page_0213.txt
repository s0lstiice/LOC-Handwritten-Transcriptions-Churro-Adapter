Patent Office
January 4th 1845
Dear Sir
Suffer me to introduce again, to your
acquaintance Mr Charles E. G. Morse, the young gentleman
of whom I spoke to you last summer, more than once,
he is desirous of being attached in some way, to the
Electo Magnetic Telegraph business, of your management,
& now under your control; I think him a deserving
young man, I feel more than an ordinary interest
in his obtain, a place, or something to do both
instinctively, & he desires, I must therefore urge it as
an apology in thus approaching you on his behalf
if it comforts with your present requirements, to
employ any one especially a youth, who may become
useful in this particular, I hope you will so far
afford him a trial of his adequacy that for
& I believe he will ever, & I know I shall look
upon it as a special favor
Yours Respectfully
J J Rome

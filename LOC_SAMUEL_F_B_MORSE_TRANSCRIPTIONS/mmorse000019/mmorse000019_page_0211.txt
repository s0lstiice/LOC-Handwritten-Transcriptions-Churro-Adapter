Washington Jan. 3. 1845
Sir,
I have the honor to enclose Copies of
a letter published by order of Congress, on the
subject of my Electric Magnetic Telegraph which accompanies it.
By the Report No. 17, it will be seen that the
system which I have now in successful operation
was examined in 1838 by a Commission appointed
by the Minister of the Interior, and the preference
given to mine over all others.
Precisely that the French Government are
examining the subject of Electric Telegraphs with
reference to the establishment of some one of the
systems offered to the public, I am desirous of
having the facts in regard to mine placed before the
Commission. I shall be exceedingly happy to show
to you the operation of my Telegraph, and to explain
it in all its details.
I leave the city for New York to day, to be about
a week. On my return I will have the honor of
approving your visit, and should it be agreeable to
you to witness the operation of the Telegraph I will
hold myself in readiness at any moment to do so, but you
may have the honor to visit my residence at
your convenience.
I have the honor to be with great respect
Y. M. Alphonse Praets
100024 Thin. Temp. of France
J. F. Brown
Y. M. Alphonse Praets
J. F. Brown

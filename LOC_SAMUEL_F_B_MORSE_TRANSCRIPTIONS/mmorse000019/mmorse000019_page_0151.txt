Having been personally acquainted with
Peter Soud's Easys as a Civil Engineer for several
years past about three years of which time he has
been engaged upon the same work with myself
I am enabled to speak confidently relative to
his ability & talent as an Engineer as well as of
his strict integrity, his unimpeachable moral
character While engaged with me on the
New Jersey Rail Road he had charge as first
assistant Engineer of the most important part
of the route upon which were several large &
important mechanical works of which
he had executed to my entire satisfaction as
well as to the best interest of the Company
And I take much pleasure in recommending
him as being every way qualified to take charge
of the building of Rail Roads not only from
his having had long experience as an Engineer but
his scientific mechanical talent will qualify
for the business
Samuel Soud
April 10/1888

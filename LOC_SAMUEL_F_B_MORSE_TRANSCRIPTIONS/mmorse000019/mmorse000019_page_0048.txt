Baltimore Nov 14th 1854.
Dear Sir,
Since I addressed to you in my letter of yesterday, but was not in the Office until now. I have taken off a sketch of a memorandum of the use of my life and if it is convenient, you may please forward me, say 150 to 200 off and as you desire it. I will acknowledge it by return of mail, and forward you in return the "American Republican" with the annexed figures on it. – I would remark that the Federal American are waking up him, to their interest – Mr Webster who comes out strong on that score – However humble, I desire to be one.
In relation to my movement as to the formation of a company I can see no difficulty in doing it if the proprietor will come in. – And since I left you here, I have seen others that will like an interest in it.
Your respectfully & truly
David Burbank.
Since settling the last had of a bill has come in for blacksmiths work. I am to finish that in taking it up of about 8,000 I suppose it may be pretty dry, but I have sent it, to enquiry of Mr Cornell if it be correct before paying it.

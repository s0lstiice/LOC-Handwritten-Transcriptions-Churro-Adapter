(1.) When I left N.York I had $365. dollars in my private purse. 365.
(2.) Advanced by Smith in N.York to me 200.
(3.) On acct of National Academy of Design for their purposes 400.
Note Academy account was kept entirely separate and has long since been closed up
so that (3.) need not be mixed up with these accounts. --
So also my private purse money have nothing to do with expenditure on Smiths ground.
therefore (1.) need not be mixed up with these accounts further than to mention the fact
that I had 365 dollars independent of Mr Smith. --

Washington Feb 12th 1845.
Sir,
I have made my calculations as to the
extent to which the remaining sum of the
appropriation will allow me to go, and I find
that I can continue the operations and Superinten-
dence of the Telegraph no longer than Saturday
the 15th inst. The salaries therefore if all has
connected with the Telegraph must cease after
that date. If you choose to continue your
daily Superintendence (free of charge) so far as
to guard the property of the Telegraph, I will
ride my influence in case the bill before Congress
Skall pass to have your salary allowed from
after the 15th inst. to the time when the new ap-
propriation shall enable us to put the Telegraph
in operation again. The continuance of your
Superintendence, however, must necessarily be at
your own risk, as I shall not the funds to en-
able me to continue your salary.
Very Respectfully
Y. B. Davis
C. B. Mason
Sup. of the May.
Feb.
To Henry J. Rogers, Esq
Assist. Sup. of Telegraph
Baltimore Station.

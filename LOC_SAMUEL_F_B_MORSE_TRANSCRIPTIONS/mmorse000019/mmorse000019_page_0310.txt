Washington Feb. 25th 1845
In compliance with your request I propose to you and through
you to establish an Agency for your
Magnusson Telegraph.
The results which I think may be reasonably
expected from that invention will in part be
followed up.
1. It may be made to send the foreign news,
communied as well as political, together with
the domestic news, to all the principal cities,
in every state of the Union. The consequence would
be, that the newspapers of the great cities would
be to be transmitted to distant points and would
reach but a local circulation. The mails would
thus be relieved of a large portion of the weight
they now have to carry.
2. The chief correspondence of bankers,
and merchants could also be carried on by
the telegraph.
No portion of the running mail business
would require extraordinary speed, and by putting
it on boardship, or in wagons, or by moderating
the time of transportation to the convenience
of the travelers, more than half the cost
of that service may be saved.

Instrument will cost not over 80 dollars a pair to us, but
this is exclusive of the great magnets which consume at present
2 miles of wire each. There is the some delicate iron adjustments to
them which will make each magnet cost at least 38 dollars or say
40 so that the complete apparatus for a station exclusive of battery
or mag. elec Mach. will be the whole at last, perhaps 900 dollars.
How much the M.E.M will cost cannot yet be ascertained perhaps 500
dollars each. but this is to be set down to battery expenses. Our battery
costs about $1.60 per day. so that the M.E.M. will be vastly the
cheapest.
Extract to F. O. J. Smith
Dec 30. 1844.

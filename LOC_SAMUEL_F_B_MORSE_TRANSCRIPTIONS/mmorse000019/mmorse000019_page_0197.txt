My Dear Sir
With this I have enclosed a letter
of power of Attorney directing you to act for me in all
matters relative to my interest in the Telegraph and I trust
the power is sufficiently general. Should Congress make
another appropriation I may probably find employment.
Should an additional appropriation be made I would
be glad if practicable to be engaged in it.
Dr Fisher has done me so much injury that it is not only
possible but quite probable he will be the means of ruining
all my prospects in business. He has robbed me of all
my plans and drawings and the vital parts of my land works
and he is now at work with the Zachary in Phila making
sugar of lead to at the rate of 150 lbs for annum.
In consequence of these circumstances the article has fallen ½ in
the price and will fall still more. We are making book for any
but although I think it has injured me very much I think I
should be worn out if I should take out a patent, and
by so doing publish to the world what is my plan of working
the Telegraph. I have sent you a letter from Mr Vail.

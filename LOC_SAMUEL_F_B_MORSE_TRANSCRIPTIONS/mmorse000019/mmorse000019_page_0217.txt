Washington City August 9th 1845
I write in great haste to say
that Henry's nomination is still hanging
in the trunk and I can not call it
defeated - some civil minded person
has stopped him with interference
You can you give me a letter
dated if you please to Col Benton
as to what you can in his department
appear upon a habit abroad - it
may help him -
Very sincerely yours
W H Silloway

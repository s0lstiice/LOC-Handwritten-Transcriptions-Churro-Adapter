Washington Monday 25th 1844
Prof Morse Dear Sir -
With a very bad pen and
in great haste and with cold fingers I address you
to request a remittance of $75- Mr Brown is
begging every day and I am out. We have got a
real room at the Capitol to ourselves, the instrument
is already and we have been telegraphing from
there - I think you will be pleased with the
multiplicity of the arrangement - My own opinion
regard to using the post-office station this
winter is that it would be too troublesome and
Mr Wood could render important assistance in
another way as reporter for the Telegraph - Will
you be kind enough to remit immediately Your
truly
Alfred Vail

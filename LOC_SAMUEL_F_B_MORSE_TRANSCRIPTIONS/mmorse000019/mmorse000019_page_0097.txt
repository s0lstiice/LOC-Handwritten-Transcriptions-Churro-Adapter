The expense of the number of copies necessary
to supply the members of Congress as proposed
will not exceed Five hundred dollars, and the
one on hand will not probably fall short
in value of One thousand dollars.
I would respectfully request to make
the arrangement proposed by exchanging
the necessary quantity of wire for the requisite
number of copies of the Dictionary to be distrib-
uted among the members of Congress as pro-
posed.
Very Respectfully Sir
Y. M. O. H. Servt.
Sam'l F. B. Morse
Superintendent of
Elec. Mag. Telegraphs
To the Hon: Geo. M. Bibb,
Secretary of the Treasury
of the United States.

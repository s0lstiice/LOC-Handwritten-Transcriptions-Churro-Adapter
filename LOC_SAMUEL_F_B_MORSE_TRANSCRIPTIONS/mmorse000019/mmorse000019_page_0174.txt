And this Agreement is further conditioned as
follows; in case the Government of the United States
shall elect to purchase said Telegraphs upon said route,
said Burbank and his Assignees shall be obligated
to convey the same to the United States upon such terms
and for such sums, as the same shall have cost them, with
a reasonable commission not to exceed twenty per cent
superadded thereto - if such purchase shall be de-
termined upon by the Government at any time within
one year from the date hereof.

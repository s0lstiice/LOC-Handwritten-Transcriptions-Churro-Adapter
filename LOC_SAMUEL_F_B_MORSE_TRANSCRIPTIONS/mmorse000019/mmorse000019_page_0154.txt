[Pittsmon Oct 30th 1844.]
My dear Sir
It occurs to me that in making use of some edition
of Statistic as to what the Telegraph may be expected to furnish,
and such as you may intend presenting for the con-
sideration of Congress in order to exhibit its prospective, its
income over its actual expenditure, that some such calcu-
lations as I have made in New York & Philadelphia, and
presented for the consideration of some of the business
men of those cities may serve to aid you in some useful
My calculation on based upon actual, daily trans-
actions of business of various kinds, between the cities of Boston
& New York, & of York & Philadelphia, & Baltimore.
Making however a great deduction from what I think
conceived to be, and was also admitted by them to be,
less than the actual occurrence; but I used this precaution
as you will see that I remarked to you while in New York,
that no exaggerated estimate might appear. And
some of them acknowledged, a noble truth & fact in this
enterprise of Joseph Moore was, that the more the subject is
looked into, the better it appears.
If in making use of some such calculation
as above alluded to, what I have can be of any advantage
to you, I shall be happy to forward it for your own
operation. I am daily expecting Mr Smith to arrive
Yours respectfully & truly
David Burbank.
